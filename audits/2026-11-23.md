## unclear: grdriving GRDRV-REG-022 (medium)

- File: `data/greece-driving-questions.json` (fix the source in `tools/` when the bank is built from one)
- Key: B; should be B
- Why it was checked: source
- Finding: B is the intended and correct meaning, but the stem describes the sign wrongly. The Vienna Convention end-of-speed-limit sign (C,17b) and the Greek equivalent (Ρ-33) are a white disc with no red border, a grey or black number and grey or black diagonal stripes. A red-bordered circle with "50" is the speed-limit sign itself, so a well-prepared candidate could be misled by the description.
- Fix: Rewrite the stem as: "A white circle with no red border, showing a grey "50" crossed by grey diagonal lines, means:" Keep key B. Rewrite the explanation as: "The end-of-limit sign is a white disc with no red border. Its grey diagonal lines cancel the limit shown, and the general limit for that type of road then applies."

## unclear: sgm5 SGM5-NOT-006 (medium, attempt 2 of 3)

- File: `data/sg-cmfas-res5-questions.json`
- Finding: Under MAS Notice FAA-N20 (Balanced Scorecard Framework), at least 60% of variable income is measured for representatives paid only variable income. For those paid a fixed salary plus variable income, all of it is measured, so B is correct. However, the stem never mentions the balanced scorecard, FAA-N20 or the non-sales KPIs, so 'measured' has no stated reference point. The independent examiner flagged this gap, and a well-prepared candidate cannot be sure which rule is being tested.
- Fix: Rewrite the stem as: "Under MAS Notice FAA-N20's Balanced Scorecard Framework, a representative's remuneration consists of a fixed salary plus variable income. How much of the variable income must be measured against the non-sales KPIs?" Keep the options and key B. Add to the explanation: "(By contrast, where a representative receives only variable income, at least 60% of it is measured.)"
