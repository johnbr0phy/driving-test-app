## ambiguous: dmv CA-011 (high)

- File: `data/questions.json` (fix the source in `tools/` when the bank is built from one)
- Key: C; should be C
- Why it was checked: report
- Finding: The student is right that the stem doesn't say whether the permit holder is a minor, and California sets a different age for each. A minor with an instruction permit must drive with a parent, guardian, spouse, or other adult aged 25 or older who holds a valid California license. A permit holder aged 18 or older needs only a supervising California-licensed driver aged 18 or older (Vehicle Code §12509 and the CA Driver Handbook). As worded, both A and C are defensible; C is right only for minors.
- Fix: Rewrite the stem as: "What is the minimum age for a licensed adult (other than a parent, guardian, or spouse) who supervises a permit holder under 18 in California?" Keep the key as C. Replace the explanation with: "A minor with an instruction permit must drive with a parent, guardian, spouse, or another adult aged 25 or older who holds a valid California driver's license. (Permit holders aged 18 or older only need a supervising California-licensed driver aged 18 or older.)"
