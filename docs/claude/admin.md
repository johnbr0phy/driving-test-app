# /admin tabs and analytics

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-20. Keep it current when the code changes.

**`/admin` is one page with ten tabs** (`app/admin/page.tsx`, `?tab=` in the URL, each tab loading its own API on first open through `useAdminFetch` in `components/admin/AdminTabParts.tsx`): Today (the default, below), Growth (`components/admin/GrowthTab.tsx`, `/api/admin/metrics`: KPIs, charts (new signups by day, week or month, `components/admin/PeriodBars.tsx`), study times, the visitor funnel with new visitors by source and the search and AI engines chart), New tests (Nov 15 2026, one table since Nov 16, `components/admin/NewTestsTab.tsx`, `/api/admin/new-tests`, `lib/server/newTestsAdmin.ts`, rules in `lib/newTests.ts` pinned by `tests/new-tests.test.cjs`: the 200 most recently launched tests, each counted since its launch: traffic (first visits whose first landing was one of its pages, from `funnelLandings`), signups (`signupOrigins` landing), questions answered (`analytics/questions.byExam`), Premium sales and revenue (`payments.exam`), with a total row. Above the table, "Sent to Bing" lists the latest IndexNow runs (when, after a deploy or nightly, pages, whether Bing accepted, which test landings were in it) and flags a sender that has not run in 36 hours; the table's Sent to Bing column is the first day any of the test's pages was sent and how many (records from Nov 19 2026, `lib/indexNowSent.mjs`). A test's pages are its landings (translated and state landings included), app routes and `/questions/<slug>/` pages. `funnelLandings` also keeps `src` and `cty` maps per landing (source and ISO country of first visits, since 2026-11-16), not shown yet. Launch dates are `lib/examLaunchDates.json`, written once per exam by `scripts/build-landing-dates.ts` (first commit adding its landing or bank; today when git cannot see it) and never moved), Conversion (below), A/B tests (`components/admin/ABTestsTab.tsx`: every running experiment in one place: the English paywall test (`PaywallTestCard`, from the Today tab's payload), the sign-up message test and the top prepper email against its holdout (`TopPrepperTestCard`, from the Conversion tab's payload); the Today and Conversion tabs no longer show them; `lib/server/opportunitiesPayload.ts` holds the Today tab's cached build), Teachers, Quizzes, Searches (what people type into the test search), Questions (question quality, below) and Users (`components/admin/UsersTab.tsx`, `/api/admin/users`: the 100 most recently active accounts with their country and delete, Super Amazing adoption). The old `/admin/v2`, `/admin/opportunities` and `/admin/teach` redirect to their tabs in `next.config.ts`; the header shield (`components/AdminLink.tsx`) opens `/admin`. Only the Today tab is in the page bundle; the others load with `next/dynamic` when first opened (Growth carries recharts). Every admin GET route gates with `requireAdmin()` (`lib/server/adminAuth.ts`) and serves through `cachedAdminPayload()` (`lib/server/adminCache.ts`): the last payload is kept gzipped in `adminCache/{key}` as well as in memory, so a cold serverless instance answers from one doc read; within `ttlMs` (1 to 5 minutes by route) it is a HIT, up to 24 hours it is served as STALE while a rebuild runs after the response (`after()` from `next/server`) and `useAdminFetch` polls until the fresh copy lands (the tab header says "updating"), and `?refresh=true` (the Refresh button) rebuilds before answering. The tabs are built for a phone too: a sticky tab bar, wide tables scrolling sideways with a sticky first column (`ROW` + `STICKY_COL` in `AdminTabParts.tsx`) and the Users table becoming a card list below `sm`. Long lists on every tab show their top 10 with a button for the rest (`useTopN` + `ShowAllButton` in `AdminTabParts.tsx`, `TOP_N`); use them for any new list.

The Conversion tab (`components/admin/ConversionTab.tsx`, `/api/admin/conversion`, `lib/server/conversion.ts`, the grid and pay chance in `lib/conversion.ts`) is cut down to conversion (Nov 15 2026): (1) headline tiles: revenue 30 days and purchases 7 days against the period before, pay rate when asked (buyers per account that saw a paywall, all time), purchases per 100 paywall hits and per 1,000 first visits (30 days), paywall hits; (2) **Conversion by country** (`byGroup()` by market, plus first visits from `analytics/funnel` `visit_region_*`, purchases and revenue per market), top 10; (3) **the last 10 payers**: when, what they paid in their currency and dollars, country, test, the paywall and paywall-test side and visit, the first visit's source and landing (`payments.source`/`landing`, else `signupOrigins`), days to buy and questions, tests and paywalls before paying; (4) the paywall hits chart (`analytics/paywalls.daily`); (5) **Who is closest to paying**: the 30 unpaid accounts with the best `payChance()` (questions x paywall views grid; cells under `MIN_CELL` 25 fall back to a broader one), each with its marketing consent, the emails it has had (`emailsSent`) and the email it would get next by the top-prepper and paywall-follow-up rules (a best guess, said so), plus how many accounts each would reach next. The top prepper holdout and the sign-up message test are on the A/B tests tab. A market is one of Tigertest's countries, `OTHER` or `UNKNOWN`: the country of the account's last paywall view (`users/{uid}.paywallCountry`), signup (`signupOrigins`) or payment, else its picked region, else its test's country. A buyer is measured at the moment they paid: their answer history is split at `subscription.purchasedAt` once and kept in `analytics/conversionBuyers` (`b.{uid}` = purchased at, v1 answers and tests before; purchases under 6 hours old are read again), so a build reads only new buyers' histories. Questions are `_stats` plus v2 progress from `users/{uid}.v2` (`v2Activity()`). Paywall views are distinct paywalls seen (one per location and item), not taps. `tests/conversion.test.cjs` pins the rules.

The Teachers tab (`lib/server/teachAdmin.ts`, `/api/admin/teach`) tracks the channel: tiles, a teacher funnel (saw /teach, clicked Create, reached the form, made a class) and a student funnel (opened a class link, joined, answered, finished a test), the dashboard nudge (`components/TeachNudge.tsx`: browsers shown it, copied its /teach link, tapped it, overall and by exam in `nudgeExams`, counted since 2026-10-20), every class with link views against joins, practice, answers, tests, last student activity and when the teacher last opened the class page, school LMS referrals, and a 12-week table. Browser steps come from `lib/teachTracking.ts` (once per browser, internal browsers skipped) into `analytics/teach` (total and daily, landing sources and referrer hosts with dots stored as `_`) via `/api/analytics/teach`, counted since 2026-10-19 (`TEACH_TRACKING_SINCE`); class link views also land on the class doc (`joinViews`, `joinReferrers`), and the report route stamps `teacherViews` / `teacherLastViewedAt` when the owner (not an admin) opens it. Joins, practice and tests come from the classes themselves, so they cover all history. Classes made by internal accounts are hidden.

The Quizzes tab (`lib/server/quizAdmin.ts`, `/api/admin/quizzes`, `components/admin/QuizAdminView.tsx`) tracks Make a quiz: tiles, connector to quiz (accounts with an `oauth_refresh_tokens` sign-in, by assistant from `oauth_clients.clientName`, against those who made a quiz), how quizzes were made and who can play, a 12-week table, every quiz (creator and email, source, visibility, questions, plays, named players from `quizzes/{code}/members`, answers, reports, with a Feature button on public quizzes that PATCHes `featured`) and every creator. Quizzes by internal accounts are hidden; the all-time `analytics/quizzes` counters (which include them) are in the footnote.

The visitor funnel on the Growth tab (`lib/funnel.ts`, `/api/analytics/funnel`) counts signed-out browsers once per step: first visit, first question answered, signup page opened, account created, plus the DMV state picker (`dmv_picker` shown, `dmv_state` chosen there, since 2026-10-29; a `?state=` link skips the picker and counts in neither), shown as the "Never chose a DMV state" tile. Since Nov 15 2026 a dashboard with no state sets the visitor's IP state as if they had chosen it (`/api/geo` returns `state` for US visitors from `x-vercel-ip-country-region`; `useSuggestedState` in `DmvStatePicker.tsx`; GA4 `state_selected` with `via: "geo"`), and the hero line's Change switches it. The picker shows only when there is no guess, so from then on the "Never chose a DMV state" tile counts only visitors with no IP state. Visits and first answers are also counted by the first visit's landing path (`lib/funnelLanding.ts`, one doc per UTC day in `funnelLandings/{day}`, class and quiz codes folded to `/c/*` and `/q/*`), shown under the Growth tab funnel as "First question by landing page" (pages with 10+ visitors, most visitors lost first). The funnel's after-signup "Answered a question" counts `_stats` or any `enrollments` doc for the uid (`_stats` alone misses v1 training and all v2), and every buyer counts as a paywall hit. The funnel route also counts each first visit by the visitor's Tigertest country from Vercel's `x-vercel-ip-country` (`visit_region_<US|CA|UK|IE|AU|NZ|IN|OTHER>`, since 2026-10-31) and stamps the ISO `country` on `signupOrigins/{uid}`. GA4 gets `state_selected` with `state` and `via` (`picker` or `link`) for everyone. Counts go to `analytics/funnel`; each new account's origin (`tried` first or `cold`, plus its first visit's `source` and `landing` path, kept in localStorage from the visit step) goes to `signupOrigins/{uid}`, kept out of `users/{uid}` so it never races the first load.

The Today tab (`lib/server/opportunities.ts`, `/api/admin/opportunities`, view in `components/admin/OpportunitiesView.tsx`) is the what-to-do-next page: a By country card (US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, India, then worldwide exams: exams in use, answers, learners, visitors, signups and revenue; practice filed by `regionOfTest()`, visitors and signups by IP country; tapping a row filters the exam table), every exam's practice (with a country filter, a search box and an all-time column; the per-exam table that used to sit on Growth), paywall views, purchases and revenue (what free US exams would earn at the DMV's revenue per answer feeds the free-exam note only, not a column; every exam's stats page sells Premium, in every country, at the visitor's local price), paywalls with purchases per 100 views, visitors and signups by source, weekly retention cohorts, a data-health list and generated insights. It reads three Stripe listings read-only (completed checkouts for older payments' `location`, refunds, the webhook's events). The Stripe webhook now stores `location` and `exam` on each `payments` doc (`lib/purchaseAttribution.ts`; the DMV dashboard sends `training_set_4` / `practice_test_4` for the third set and test too) and marks `charge.refunded` as `refunded` / `partially_refunded` with `refundedCents`, never revoking Premium. **English paywall test** (`lib/paywallTest.ts`, ids in `lib/paywallTestIds.ts`, test `paywallJohn1`): `PaywallModal` splits English readers 50/50 by browser (localStorage `tigertest-paywall-test`, `?paywall=john|classic` pins it) between John's chip-in `SupportPaywallModal` and the classic Premium paywall; other languages always get the classic one. Each open counts in `analytics/paywallTest` (views and distinct browsers per variant, via `/api/analytics/paywall-test`) and stamps `users/{uid}.paywallTest`; every checkout call sends `paywallVariant`, which rides the Stripe metadata onto the `payments` doc. The A/B tests tab shows purchases per 100 browsers per side with a two-proportion p-value. **Return messages** (Nov 13 2026): on both sides, half of browsers (`ret: "new"`, assigned once) get a different message the second and third time they hit a paywall and half (`"same"`) keep the first one as the comparison (`notePaywallHit()`, visits counted in the same localStorage record, a reopen within 10 minutes is the same visit, `?paywallHit=2|3` previews without counting; copy in `RETURN_HEADLINES` in `PaywallModal` and `MESSAGES` in `SupportPaywallModal`, English only). The visit and arm ride every view (`analytics/paywallTest.hits.{1,2,3}.{views,new,same}`) and checkout (`paywallHit` and `paywallReturn` on the `payments` doc), and the card lists views and purchases per visit and arm with a p-value for new against first again. At the top is a daily update written by Claude (`lib/server/dailyBrief.ts`, `claude-opus-5-5`): the cron `/api/cron/daily-brief` (weekdays 10:00 UTC, so by 7am Eastern in summer) sends the page's numbers, the teachers and schools numbers from `buildTeachAdmin()` (classes and school links new since the previous update, so a first class or first school leads the headline), plus the per-country numbers and the previous update's to the API and saves a headline and 3 to 5 bullets to `dailyBriefs/{Eastern date}`; alongside, a second call with the web search tool (`lib/server/webMentions.ts`) looks for pages talking about Tigertest since the search homepage launched (`MENTIONS_SINCE` in `lib/webMentionsSince.ts`, Nov 1 2026), keeps up to 15 links across updates in `mentions` (new ones, not in the previous day's update, are marked and fed to the brief as `newWebMentions`) and shows them under the bullets (both routes allow 300 seconds); "Write a fresh one" on the page calls `/api/admin/daily-brief`. Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` the cron skips and the page says so; the six rule-based notes are folded under it.

**School referrals** (`lib/schoolReferral.ts`, `lib/schoolHosts.ts`, `/api/analytics/school-referral`): a visit whose referrer is a school LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Moodle, Schoology, Google Classroom, Sakai) or a .edu / k12 site is reported once per browser per school and course, signed in or not (internal browsers skipped). Each host is a doc in `schoolReferrals` (visits, daily, landings with '/' stored as '|', and `courses` keyed by the course id read from the referrer path). The first visit from a new host, or a new course at a known host, emails John (`CAMPAIGN_REPLY_TO`, transactional, capped at 20 a day in `analytics/schoolReferralAlerts`): each is a teacher who posted a Tigertest link.

The "Questions by exam" table's Enrolled column counts distinct people with at least one answer per exam: one doc per (exam, person) in `enrollments` (`lib/enrollment.ts`, `/api/analytics/enrollment`), keyed `{exam}__u_{uid}` or `{exam}__b_{browserId}` for guests; signing in claims the browser's guest docs so a converted guest counts once. Accounts that predate it are seeded by `/api/admin/backfill-questions` (`enrollmentsBackfilledAt`).

`/api/admin/backfill-questions` is resumable: each call scans users for about 35s, saves its cursor and running sums in `analytics/questionsBackfillRun` (with a lease so two tabs never scan twice) and returns `done: false`; the admin pages loop through `lib/questionsBackfillClient.ts` until `done: true`. The anonymous answer counter (`/api/analytics/questions`) is capped per IP at 2,000 answers an hour and 6,000 a day (`lib/server/answerRateLimit.ts`, hashed IPs in `analyticsRateLimits`, TTL field `expiresAt`); dropped answers are tallied in `analytics/questions.rateLimited`.

**Internal accounts never count in /admin.** `lib/internal.ts` (`isInternalEmail`: `ADMIN_EMAILS`, `@johnbrophy.net`, `@stensul.com`) is applied on read by `/api/admin/metrics`, `/api/admin/users` and `/api/admin/backfill-questions` (uids from one cached Auth listing in `lib/server/internalUsers.ts`: users, payments, test requests and signup origins are dropped), and on write: an internal login marks the browser (`tigertest-internal` in localStorage, set in `AuthContext`, never cleared), after which the anonymous counters (questions answered, funnel steps, paywall hits, shares) are not sent from it even signed out. Test browsers never send them either (`isTestBrowser()`: any host but tigertest.io, so localhost and preview deployments, which write to the live Firestore; Claude's own browser, user agent `Claude/`; and `navigator.webdriver` automation), and quiz plays follow the same rule; the paywall and funnel-signup routes also drop a request whose token is internal. Counts recorded before the flag existed stay in the aggregates. **Bots never count either** (Nov 2026): every `/api/analytics/*` route, quiz plays and email clicks drop a request whose user agent `isBotUserAgent()` (`lib/botUserAgent.ts`, also the middleware's crawler list) flags: named crawlers, self-named automation (HeadlessChrome, curl...) and desktop Chrome 5 or more majors behind current stable (scraper fleets in China, Hong Kong and Singapore ran Chrome 103 to 150 and filled the By country card with fake visitors). Phones, ChromeOS and Windows 7/8 are never judged by version. Bump `CHROME_ANCHOR_*` if Chrome's cadence changes. The stats cron has its own, broader `isTest()`.
