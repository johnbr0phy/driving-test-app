# Email

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-20. Keep it current when the code changes.

### Email

**Links in any email John sends or Claude drafts** (templates, Gmail drafts, replies to users): always write the full URL, `https://www.tigertest.io/<path>`. Never a bare `tigertest.io/...`, never the apex host, never a path without the scheme. Build template links on `SITE_URL` from `lib/siteUrl.ts`. Check every link in a draft before saving it. **Never put a link in a Gmail draft made through the Gmail connector**: it rewrites every link it saves (plain text and `<a href>` alike, reply or new draft) to `https://www.google.com/url?q=...&source=gmail&ust=...`, which goes out broken. For any email with a link, give John the paste-ready text in chat instead. If a draft is ever made anyway, read it back with `get_draft` in `RAW` format and check every `href` before calling it done.

Resend, from `noreply@tigertest.io`, reply-to John. Templates are HTML strings in `lib/email-templates.ts`, one function of an `EmailVoice` (`lib/email-voice.ts`) each. The signup welcome (`/api/send-welcome-email`) is about the exam in the signup URL (`examIdFromSignupUrl`: the `?redirect=` target, else the page, else the store's `primaryExam`), and seeds `primaryExam` only when the account has none; a signup from `/teach` (`isTeacherSignupUrl`) gets `welcomeTeacher` instead and no exam is set. The sender is `lib/server/welcomeEmail.ts`, shared with the hourly `/api/cron/welcome-backstop`, which sends the welcome to every account made 15 minutes to 24 hours ago whose browser call never arrived (no `welcome` in `emailsSent`; opted-out skipped; copy from its `primaryExam`). Every signup path calls the route with `keepalive`, including Google and email signups on the connector's `/oauth/login` page. It is transactional mail, not a campaign, so it does not count in `CAMPAIGN_CRON_COUNT`. Daily campaign crons under `app/api/cron/*` (schedules in `vercel.json`) share `lib/cron-email.ts`: one Auth list call, `getEligibleUsers()` (consent, opt-out, quiet-days rule per exam; it reads only small fields first and downloads full records just for accounts that pass, plus each route's `prefilter`, usually `notSentAndNotRecent(EMAIL_KEY)`, to keep Firestore egress down), a 24h frequency cap, a once-only `emailsSent` key per campaign, and the account-wide daily budget in `lib/email-quota.ts` (bump `CAMPAIGN_CRON_COUNT` when adding a cron). `scripts/email-dryrun.mjs` shows who each cron would reach without sending.

**The question that got away** (`/api/cron/exit-question`, 18:00 UTC, key `exitQuestion`, once ever): someone whose last act was a training answer (`findAccountExit()` in `lib/exitQuestions.ts`: 3+ answers, no test passed on that exam, last act not a finished test) and who has been quiet 3 to 10 days gets that question back with every option, the answer in green, the explanation and a joke (`exitQuestionEmail()`; one copy for a miss, one for a right answer), about the exam the question came from. English readers only, DMV and v1 exams only (v2 keeps no answer times); stamps `exitQuestionEmail` {examId, questionId, isCorrect}. Paused unless `EXIT_QUESTION_CAMPAIGN=send`; paused or `?dry=1` it sends nothing and returns the eligible count and sample question ids. Its light prefilter keeps only accounts whose `lastUpdated` is 3 to 10 days old (every answer save writes it), so it downloads few full records; without it the run timed out at 300s every day until Nov 21 2026 and sent nothing.

**Top prepper** (`/api/cron/top-prepper`, 16:20 UTC, key `topPrepper`, once ever; rules in `lib/topPrepper.ts`, pinned by `tests/top-prepper.test.cjs`): an unpaid English reader who has answered 100+ questions and more than 75% of all accounts with any answer (`_stats`, read for every account so the percentile is honest), and whose latest paywall view was 36 hours to 14 days ago, gets their own numbers ("more than 92% of the people who practice here"), a well done and a Keep Practicing button to their dashboard. No upsell: no price, no locked item, no paywall link (John, Nov 14 2026). `?unlock=1` still opens the paywall on the DMV dashboard and `ExamDashboard` for any other link that wants it. About the exam of that paywall, DMV and v1 only (`_stats` has no v2 answers); class students of that exam are skipped. One account in ten (stable by uid) is held out and stamped `topPrepper.group = "holdout"` instead of mailed, only as far down the ranked list as that day's sends reached; a send stamps `group: "sent"` with `at`, `examId`, `answers`, `pct`. The A/B tests tab's "Does the top prepper email work?" card compares purchases within 7 days for the two groups. Paused unless `TOP_PREPPER_CAMPAIGN=send`; paused or `?dry=1` it sends and stamps nothing and returns the eligible count and a sample. Never state a percentile that is not computed from live data.

**Premium is only ever emailed after an action** (Nov 15 2026, John: no unprompted upsells). The one sales email is the paywall follow-up (`/api/cron/paywall-abandon`, 12:00 UTC, key `paywallAbandon`, once ever): someone not Premium who opened a locked step 1 to 36 hours ago and has 10+ answers on that exam gets "Take another look at <what they opened>" with what Premium adds on **that** exam (`EmailVoice.premiumBenefits`: the DMV's sets and tests 3 and 4, a v1 exam's locked sets and tests by name from `lib/examLocks.ts` plus its full stats, a paid v2 exam's locked drills and test) and a button to its dashboard. It covers every exam that sells Premium: `paywallExam()` in `lib/paywallAbandon.ts` maps a hit to its exam (DMV `dashboard`/`stats_page`, v1 `<family>_dashboard|_test|_set` with an `exam_` item or `<family>_stats`, v2 `<exam>_...` with a `v2_` item; results, drill and upgrade-page hits are left out), free exams (`hasPremium` false) never get it, and class students of that exam are skipped since they already have it. Top prepper (below) is the later, praise-only follow-up. The old unprompted upgrade pitch (`upgradePitch`, "What DMV Premium includes", 50+ answers, DMV only) was retired; its old copy in `functions/src/index.ts` is not deployed (no `firebase.json`). Do not add a Premium email that is not triggered by something the person did.

**Checkout recovery** (Nov 20 2026, `lib/checkoutRecovery.ts`, pinned by `tests/checkout-recovery.test.cjs`): signed-in checkouts are created with `after_expiration.recovery` (guests are not: their sign-in cookie is keyed to the original session id). When one expires unpaid after 24 hours, the Stripe webhook's `checkout.session.expired` case emails Stripe's recovery link (it reopens a copy of the same checkout for 30 days) with `checkoutRecoveryEmail()`, through `sendCronEmail` under key `checkoutRecovery`, once ever per account, campaign rules (marketing consent, not unsubscribed, not internal, not Premium, English readers only, 4-hour gap), stamping `checkoutRecovery` {sessionId, examId, at}. The paywall follow-up skips anyone sent one in the last 7 days. The Stripe webhook endpoint must have `checkout.session.expired` enabled. Opening checkout is the person's own action, so this fits the only-after-an-action rule.

**Blocked email domains** (Nov 2026, `lib/emailDomainBlock.ts`, pinned by `tests/email-domain-block.test.cjs`): some domains refuse all outside mail (school districts whose student addresses only take internal mail; `daytonpublic.com` was the first and is on `SEED_BLOCKED_DOMAINS`). Resend suppresses one bounced address at a time, so the Resend webhook `app/api/webhooks/resend` (Svix-signed, `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, events `email.bounced` and `email.delivered`) counts hard bounces per domain in `emailDomains/{domain}` (addresses stored as sha1 hashes; deliveries only counted for domains already tracked), and a domain is blocked once `BLOCK_AFTER_BOUNCES` (3) different addresses hard-bounce and none is delivered. `sendEmail()` checks it before reserving quota and returns `blockedDomain: true` (an uncertain retry is exempt), and `sendDurableEmail()` turns that into `ineligible`, so welcome mail and campaigns just skip those people. Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and the other big providers are never blocked or tracked. The Users tab on `/admin` has a "Blocked email domains" card (`components/admin/BlockedEmailDomainsCard.tsx`, `/api/admin/email-domains`) to block, allow or reset a domain by hand; a hand choice wins.

Opt-out is `users/{uid}.unsubscribed` (`lib/unsubscribe.ts`). It is checked in the audience query and again in `sendCronEmail()` right before every send, and every campaign carries RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` headers pointing at `/api/unsubscribe` (GET and one-click POST). Nothing on the client writes that field. The quizzes launch (`/api/cron/quizzes-launch`, key `quizzesLaunch2026`) is paused unless `QUIZZES_CAMPAIGN=send`; paused, the daily run returns at once without reading accounts (`?dry=1` counts the audience). The new-tests campaign (`newTests2026`) is paused unless `NEW_TESTS_CAMPAIGN=send`; while paused each daily run only logs how many people it would reach. DMV readers are split across the variants still sending in `lib/email-launch.ts` (v4 and v5; v1 and v3 retired Oct 17 via `RETIRED_VARIANTS`) (one per user, stable by uid, stamped as `emailVariants.newTests2026` on send; links go through `/api/email/click`, which records `emailClicks.newTests2026`). The split-test card on the Growth tab was removed Oct 30; the stamps are still written. Non-DMV readers get the short version, outside the test. It also asks "what should we build next?": clicks land in the `testRequests` collection via `/api/feedback/test-request` and `/request-test` (`lib/test-requests.ts`), shown on the Searches tab under Asked for by name (`lib/server/testRequestsAdmin.ts`, served with `/api/admin/searches`); a named exam emails John immediately.
