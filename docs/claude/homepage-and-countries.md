# Homepage, search and country homepages

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-20. Keep it current when the code changes.

- `/` — Tigertest hub homepage. Since Nov 2026 every English homepage (`/` and the English country homepages) opens on the search (`components/home/SearchHome.tsx`, described under `/home-v2` below, which now redirects to `/`) with the questions answered this week under the tagline, then Popular this week, the DMV state grid (US) or the country's own tests, Popular this week (6 cards) and every catalog group as short plain text links, busiest first, up to 6 a group with "+N more" to that group's anchor on `/tests` (`TestCatalogStatic`, server-rendered: every group on `/`, the country's own and worldwide tests elsewhere; Nov 15 2026 it replaced a card per test, which made the page very long), then a See all tests button. The homepage only needs a crawlable path to every landing, which the text links plus `/tests` (server-rendered, every test as a card, groups anchored by `groupAnchor()` in `lib/testCatalog.ts`) give it, the FAQ and Request a test; How it works and the Reddit quotes went, and the full card catalog lives at `/tests`. The search page's `h1` is the wordmark plus a screen-reader-only ": free practice tests for N exams". Since Nov 18 2026 the homepages in other languages (`/de`, `/fr`, `/it`, `/es`, `/pt`, `/se`, `/dk`, `/fi`, `/hu`, `/ro`, `/gr`, `/be`, and a picked language via `app/home-lang`) use the same search layout, with its words in `components/home/searchCopy.ts` (all seventeen homepage languages) and a visually hidden h1 and lead from `REGION_HOME` (the title without " | Tigertest", and `testsSub`); the old hero, How it works with US screenshots and card catalog are gone. Every non-English country homepage sends a Content-Language header (`COUNTRY_HOME_LANGUAGES` in `lib/urlLanguage.ts`, pinned by `tests/url-language.test.cjs`). "About each test" on a country page describes every driving test and then the busiest of its other tests, 8 in all (`LOCAL_TESTS_SHOWN`); until Nov 18 2026 it skipped every test in `NON_DRIVING_TESTS`. Every homepage link to a landing (cards, the country list, About each test, the JSON-LD ItemList) goes through `landingIn(test, lang)` in `lib/testCatalog.ts`, so a test with a translated landing in the page's language links that one (the Dutch CBR car test on `/nl`); since Nov 22 2026, pinned by `tests/home-landing-language.test.cjs`. "Popular this week" (six tests ranked by questions answered in the last 7 days from `analytics/questions.byExam`, `lib/server/popularTests.ts`, revalidated hourly). Catalog cards show a flag per language from `TestCatalogEntry.languages` (the DMV's es/vi/ko/zh, a v1 exam's `translations`)
- **Country (region)** (`lib/region.ts`, Oct 2026): every country has its own indexable homepage, the US at `/` and the others at `/uk`, `/ie`, `/ca`, `/au`, `/nz`, `/in`, `/ph`, `/my`, `/sg`, `/hk`, `/ae`, `/za`, `/ng`, `/ke`, `/pk`, `/es`, `/pt`, `/ro`, `/se`, `/gr`, `/dk`, `/hu`, `/be`, `/fi` and `/mt` (`app/{uk,ie,ca,au,nz,in,ph,my,sg,hk,ae,za,ng,ke,pk,es,pt,ro,se,gr,dk,hu,be,fi,mt}/page.tsx`; India, region `IN`, added Oct 30 2026 with the RTO learner's licence test, its first exam; the Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong, UAE, South Africa, Nigeria and Kenya added Oct 31 2026, each with one driving test; Pakistan, region `PK`, added Nov 1 2026 with the driving licence sign test, its first exam, more to come; Spain, Portugal, Romania, Sweden, Greece, Denmark, Hungary, Belgium, Finland and Malta added Nov 2026, each with its driving theory test; Italy, region `IT`, homepage `/it` in Italian, added Nov 12 2026 with the patente B quiz, CILS B1 Cittadinanza and CELI 2 (all in Italian); France, region `FR` (also the overseas departments GP, MQ, GF, RE, YT), homepage `/fr` in French (`app/fr/page.tsx`; `app/fr/layout.tsx` also wraps the French landings, the Belgian `/fr/examen-theorique-permis-b` included), added Nov 14 2026 with the code de la route (ETG), its first exam; Switzerland, region `CH`, homepage `/ch` in English, added Nov 16 2026 with the basic theory test in English, its first exam; `/es` is the Spain homepage in Spanish, `/pt` Portugal's in Portuguese and `/be` Belgium's in Dutch and `/gr` Greece's in Greek since Nov 2026), all rendered by `components/home/HomePage.tsx` with each other as hreflang alternates (en-US and x-default `/`, then en-GB, en-IE, en-CA, en-AU, en-NZ, en-IN and the en- tag of each newer country, `REGION_HREFLANG`), their own title, description and canonical (`REGION_HOME`, `countryHomeMetadata()`), JSON-LD in that English, and sitemap and `llms.txt` lines. Outside the US the page has that country's own h1 and lead, Popular this week counting only its own and worldwide tests, "About each test" in place of the DMV state grid, its own FAQ (`faqsFor(region)`), and a catalog without US groups until the visitor searches, with a link to `/tests`. `components/CountryPills.tsx` sits above the catalog search: on a homepage each pill is a plain `<a>` to that country's page (crawlable) whose tap saves the country and does a full page load (the client router can hold a prefetch of `/` that middleware answered with a redirect under the old country), keeping the pills where they were on screen through sessionStorage; on `/tests` they are buttons and `orderGroupsForRegion()` puts the country's groups first (a group is a country's own when any member is, so the mixed "Citizenship tests" group leads in the US, UK, Canada and Australia; on a country homepage `TestCatalogGrid` `localOnly` also drops other countries' tests inside a mixed group), then worldwide (`GLOBAL`: A+, Security+, AWS, AWS SAA, AZ-900, CCNA, PMP, Gulf RN, Salesforce Admin, CFA Level I, ACCA FA, CISA, Six Sigma Green Belt and others), then other countries, then the US. `middleware.ts` (matcher `/` and `/tests`) sets the `tt-region` cookie on a first visit from Vercel's `x-vercel-ip-country` (GB maps to UK, IN to India; a country with no tests gets US) with `tt-region-src` `geo`, `default` or, after a tap, `chosen`; a saved cookie always wins, and a non-US visitor opening `/` gets a 307 to their country page. Crawlers (user-agent match) never get a location or a redirect, so `/` is indexed as the US page. A view setting only: progress is untouched. A pick (never a location guess) is also `region` + `regionAt` in `useStore`, written straight to `users/{uid}` by `setRegion` and merged on sign-in like `primaryExam` (the newer pick wins); `CountryPills` keeps the cookie in step once the account has loaded, so a pick made on another device moves this one to that country's page the first time it learns it, and a cookie-only pick goes up to the account.
- `/home-v2` (redirects to `/` since Nov 2026) — the search-first homepage, now the top of every homepage (`components/home/SearchHome.tsx`, search in `lib/testSearch.ts`): one box over every test in every country with a flag per result (🌐 for worldwide), typo matching, DMV states ("texas permit" opens `/dmv/dashboard?state=TX`), ranked by name match, then the visitor's country, then worldwide, then this week's popularity; a tap or Enter opens the dashboard. Laid out like google.com on a phone: the site header without its logo (the page is the wordmark) and no test chip, the short footer, a large wordmark that collapses to one line while typing, a filled pill box (`.search-pill`, exempt from the global focus outline) with voice search where the browser has the Web Speech API, and under the tagline the questions answered this week (`getPopularTests().totalAnswered`, a `CountUp`), and below the box, like Google, "Trending searches" (`lib/server/trendingSearches.ts`: the searches typed at least twice in 7 days that found a test, topped up with popular test names; a tap runs the search), and above that, for anyone with progress on the device or account, "Pick up where you left off" (`hooks/useTestsInProgress.ts`: every started test with questions mastered and tests taken, the current test first; `useStartedTests` is the separate yes/no check used by Settings and the test switcher). Search drops generic words (practice, test, exam...), matches terms of 3 letters or fewer only at a word start, prefers name matches for acronyms, only offers a DMV state on a driving search (state names match as they are typed, "calif", "new y", "north car"; a bare code or alias such as "ny", "nyc" or "tx" opens that state too, after any test literally named it, like AZ-900 for "az"), keeps the last results on screen while a half-typed word matches nothing and shows "we don't have it" only once typing pauses (`SETTLE_MS`) or on Enter, and when no test has every word shows the tests with the most of them as closest matches, counted as not found (`partial`). A one-letter slip in a driving word next to a state is corrected ("california dmc"), and a search naming a state-sold family and a state, even inside a long sentence, opens that state's version ("louisiana real estate" opens `/real-estate/la/dashboard`; a state without a version offers the national test, counted as not found). Queries (sent once typing pauses, internal browsers skipped) and picks go to `/api/analytics/search` into `searchQueries/{sha1 of the query}` (count, misses, lastResults, picks, byRegion, daily) and `analytics/search`; the Searches tab on `/admin` (`components/admin/SearchesView.tsx`, `/api/admin/searches`; the old `/home-v2/searches` redirects there) is built to stay short as the collection grows: it reads only queries active in the last 90 days plus the 300 most searched ever (cached 2 minutes), groups the ways of typing one search by `searchIntentKey()` (generic words dropped, spellings folded, words sorted), counts each group over 7 days, 30 days or all time (a toggle, from `daily`), and re-runs every past miss through today's `searchAllTests()`, so a search moves from "Build these" to "Found now" by itself once its test or alias exists. Build-list rows can be marked Planned or Ignored (`searchTriage/{sha1 of the key}`, read fresh on every load) so typos and junk are handled once; plus Most searched, Latest, a 30-day chart and a filter box. Trending searches read only the last 7 days' queries (`lastAt`) for the same reason.
