### Question quality (Nov 2026)

Answer keys are what the product is judged on, so the banks are watched after they ship, in five layers. **No person is in any of these loops** (John, Nov 19 2026: he does not know these subjects and will not review banks, findings, flags or source pages). Every finding, report, flag, changed page, dated exam change and yearly review is settled by an automated run that fixes, double-checks and merges its own change or keeps the question as it is; never add a step that waits for John to judge a question. Shared types and the flag rules are in `lib/questionQuality.ts`.
- **CI** (`.github/workflows/question-banks.yml`, on every PR touching `data/`, `tools/` or the bank wiring): `tools/check_banks.py` (blocking: answer key matches its letter, four distinct options compared case-sensitively since capitalization and genotype questions differ only by case, no empty stem or explanation, v1 ids unique across every base bank, each translated bank `<bank>_<lang>.json` has only its base's ids with the same key and category, the zh DMV bank partial on purpose; v2 ids unique per exam, key indices in range, stimulus references resolve; and, through `tools/check_option_refs.py`, no stem or explanation that points at an option by position, "the second option", "the last choice", or "Choice B" in a v1 bank or a v2 stem, since options are shuffled on screen: only `lockedOrder` items and v2 explanation letters, which `relabelChoices()` rewrites, may do that), `tools/check_freshness.py`, `tools/build_exam_sources.py --check` and `build-training-parts.ts --check` (blocking), then `tools/check_answer_patterns.py --all --baseline tools/answer_patterns_baseline.json` (blocking, also in `scripts/automerge-checks.sh`): it fails a bank that gets a new answer pattern, a word whose option is the key far more often than guessing ("pick the option with WORD" right in 55%+ of the 8+ questions that offer it) a key reused word for word in a category, or 4+ keys in a row (ID order, the order training shows them) sharing a word, as RBT-SDOC-002 to -008 did; it measures what one student meets (the DMV bank per state, universal plus that state's 40) and reports marks a test-wise student gains per 50-question test (RBT's scenarios about 5 before the fix, the worst DMV state 1.3, so the DMV is left as is); the baseline holds what older banks have today (60 banks gain 2+ marks on Nov 15 2026, CNA 7.9, CDL 7.3, the security guard banks and Notary about 7: report, call, stop, decline, give way) and may only shrink, and `validate_bank.py` fails a bank with any. Next, `tools/check_option_reuse.py --all --baseline tools/option_reuse_baseline.json` (blocking, also in `scripts/automerge-checks.sh` and both validators, Nov 17 2026) fails a bank where 10%+ of questions share an exact option set with another (the "quartet" build of #876 and #877: four questions per option set, each option keyed once, so the last falls to elimination; standard formats used in 6+ questions, number answers and one shared v2 stimulus are exempt) or where picking the option that alone repeats the stem's words gains 4.5+ marks per 50 over guessing (that batch: 7 to 11.6; good banks under 3). The baseline holds PenTest+, PRINCE2 and CA real estate (echo 5 to 8), to be fixed. Codex and other agents get these rules through `AGENTS.md`. Then `tools/check_stem_length.py --all --baseline tools/stem_length_baseline.json` (blocking, also in `scripts/automerge-checks.sh`, Nov 18 2026) fails a bank whose median stem is over 20 words (site median 14; the Nov 2026 batch ran 20 to 26 from scenario wrapping and stacked qualifiers); 63 older banks over it are baselined and may only get shorter. The same tool fails an English bank where over 25% of options start with The, A or An (site median 12%; the ChatGPT-built Nov 2026 banks ran 28 to 46% and were stripped to under 5%), 24 older banks baselined; banks in another language (accents in over 10% of options) are skipped, since Hungarian and Portuguese also use "A". Then `tools/check_length_rank.py --all --baseline tools/length_rank_baseline.json` (blocking, also in `scripts/automerge-checks.sh`, Nov 22 2026) scores "pick the r-th longest option" for each length rank and fails a bank where any rank beats 40% (chance 25%): to pass the key-longest rule, writers trimmed keys just under one distractor, so "pick the second-longest" scored 50% on PALS and SHRM-CP and 63% on the worst older bank; older banks over it are baselined and may only improve. Blind answering uses `tools/blind_answers.py`, a keyless copy, since a reviewer reading the bank sees the key. Then the house-style `validate_bank.py` on changed v1 banks as a warning only, since older banks predate it. `check_banks.py --export <file>` writes the flat index of every question (about 56,000) that the audit reads.
- **Reports**: "Report this question" (`components/ReportExamQuestion.tsx`, its strings in its own table for all seventeen languages) under the explanation in DMV and registry training (`TrainingCard` `reportExamId`) and v2 drills (`DrillRunner`). Reasons: wrong answer, two answers, unclear, outdated, typo, other, plus a note and the option picked (bank index). `POST /api/questions/report` (known exam ids only, bots dropped, about 20 an hour per IP through `grantAnswers`) writes `questionReports/{auto}` with `status: open`, the reporter's uid and email (account email, or one a guest typed) and counts in `analytics/questionReports`. Reports close themselves (`closeQuestionReports()` in `lib/server/reportClose.ts`): the audit judges each the next morning; found right (or only a low-confidence doubt) closes it with `replyOutcome: "right"`; a confirmed problem keeps it open until the fix merges (`--resolve`: `"fixed"`), or until the fix run checks the source and keeps the key (`"right"`); a finding given up after 3 runs, or a question no longer in the bank, closes it with no reply. `/api/cron/report-replies` (daily 14:20 UTC, transactional, not a campaign) emails each closed report with a `replyOutcome` and an email once (`emailReporter()` in `lib/server/reportReply.ts`, in the language they reported in, `emailedAt` on the report). Quizzes keep their own report route.
- **Pick statistics**: every answered exam question, class or not, goes through `recordClassAnswerById()` to `recordQuestionPick()` (`lib/questionPicks.ts`): bank index picked (-1 for formats with no single pick), right or wrong, and whether this browser is strong on the exam (30+ answers there at 80%+, kept in localStorage `tigertest-pick-skill`). Batched to `POST /api/analytics/question-picks` every 10 s and on page hide, summed per question into `questionPicks/{examId}` (`n`, `c`, `w.{i}` wrong picks, and `s`, `sc`, `sw.{i}` for strong browsers), nothing about the sender stored, own per-IP budget. Test, internal and analytics-declined browsers and quizzes never send. Recorded from Nov 3 2026. The cron `/api/cron/question-health` (03:40 UTC) runs `flagQuestion()` over every question plus open reports and writes `globalStats/questionFlags` (worst 600 by score, counts by kind and exam): **keySuspect** (12+ strong answers and strong browsers under 50% right, or their top wrong option at 75%+ of their correct count), **splitAnswer** (40+ answers and one wrong option at 30%+ of answers and 80%+ of the correct count), **reported**. A hard or an easy question is not a problem, so "too hard" and "too easy" are no longer flags (Nov 10 2026); only the signs of a wrong or second answer are. **Flags answer themselves**: the audit's daily `--signals` run (below) judges every new flag and report the next morning and stores its decision in `questionFlagReviews/{examId}` (`FlagReview`: verdict, confidence, reason, answers at the time). `applyFlagReviews()` joins flags and reviews in the cron and the Questions tab: a flag reviewed "ok", or with only a low-confidence doubt (`reviewClears()`: the key is kept), is **cleared** (kept, folded away on /admin, out of every count) until the question's answers double or 180 days pass (`reviewFresh`), and a confirmed problem (medium or high confidence) becomes a finding with a fix PR. A question with an open report is never cleared by a review. When a finding is closed as fixed (the button, or a merged resolutions file) `resetQuestionSignals()` (`lib/server/questionFlagReviews.ts`) deletes that question's pick counts and review, since they describe the old key; closed as not a problem, `clearQuestionReview()` writes an "ok" review so it is not raised again. `tests/question-flags.test.cjs` pins the rules.
- **Freshness**: `lib/examFreshness.json` lists dated changes to real exams (outline, law, format, sunset, notice, review) with the action and source; set `done` once the bank follows it. Every exam is also due a full review 365 days after its bank last changed (`lib/landingDates.json`). `lib/examSources.json` (built by `tools/build_exam_sources.py`: the first three https URLs of every `tools/**/sources.md`, plus each freshness source, about 500) is read by `/api/cron/source-watch` (04:10 UTC, a seventh of the pages a day, `?all=1` for all): each page is cut to its sentences of 40+ characters and hashed; a page is changed when 5% or more of them differ from the baseline (files compared by bytes), kept in `sourceWatch/{sha1(url)}` until marked reviewed, which makes the latest the new baseline. Pages failing twice are listed as broken. **Nobody reads them by hand** (Nov 13 2026): `.github/workflows/source-triage.yml` (05:23 UTC Mon, Wed, Fri, after the cron; daily until Nov 20 2026, cut to save API spend) exports the changed and broken pages still watched and not yet triaged (`scripts/source-triage.ts --export`), and Claude Code reads each page against the exam's bank, facts, costs and landing and decides: noise (re-baselined at once, never shown), updated (bank sources, facts and copy fixed), replaced (an unofficial or broken URL swapped in `sources.md` for an official one), or needs_person (retried after 7 days; a second needs_person in a row keeps everything as it is and re-baselines the page, so no page waits on a person). Decisions go in `audits/sources/<date>-<run>.json` and `sourceWatch.triage`; a day with real changes opens a PR, which the run checks itself (typecheck, lint, tests, the bank checks; a PR opened with the workflow token starts no other workflow) and merges when all pass, then re-baselines those pages (`--apply --merged`); a failed check closes the PR and puts its pages back to needs_person for a retry (`--apply --failed`), and a PR merged by hand re-baselines through the push job. To re-baseline a merged decisions file whose pages were missed (as #939 on Nov 21 2026, when the merge step crashed), run the workflow by hand with `rebaseline: audits/sources/<file>.json`; that runs only the re-baseline, no triage. `sourceNeedsPerson()` (`lib/questionQuality.ts`, pinned by `tests/source-triage.test.cjs`) picks the pages the triage still has to read.
- **Claude audit** (`scripts/question-audit.ts`, from `.github/workflows/question-audit.yml`: everything on Mondays 06:17 UTC, and on the other days at 04:47 UTC only new statistical flags and student reports (`--signals`: no freshness, no sweep; a run with nothing new writes no report and opens no PR); needs the repository secrets `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_KEY`): queues open reports, the top statistical flags, time-sensitive questions (years, money, limits, "currently") in exams with a dated change within 60 days or a changed source page, and a sweep of `--sweep` (1,000 in the Action) random choice questions not audited in 90 days, so the roughly 54,000 choice questions are each re-read about once a year (raise it for a faster cycle; cost scales linearly). Each is answered blind (low effort, JSON schema output) with confidence, other defensible options and any problem; a disagreement, and every reported, flagged or freshness item, goes to a second call (high effort) with the key, explanation, evidence and the exam's `sources.md`, which returns ok, wrong_key, ambiguous, outdated, unclear or typo with the exact fix. Findings of medium or high confidence go to `audits/<date>.json` and `.md`, `questionAuditFindings/{exam__id}` (a dismissed finding is not raised again for the same verdict), `questionAudits/{runId}` and `questionAuditLog/{examId}`. When there are findings, the Action runs Claude Code (`anthropics/claude-code-action`) to check each one itself, fix the bank sources (never only the built JSON), rebuild, fix the key in every translated bank, run the checks, append what it changed, write `audits/resolutions/<date>-<run id>.json` for the findings it settled (fixed, or dismissed after checking the official rule), and opens a PR (`peter-evans/create-pull-request`), which the run checks itself (typecheck, lint, tests, the bank checks) and merges when all pass and a resolutions file was written, then applies that file with `--resolve` (a merge made with the workflow token starts no push job). John chose this on Nov 13 2026: fixes to the banks need no person. **A second reviewer comes first**: before the merge, a second Claude Code run that did not write the fixes re-checks every changed question against the exam's sources and writes `verify.json`; any objection, or no verdict, closes the PR with a comment naming what failed (fix step, second review, checks, or no finding settled), and its findings stay open. The fix step must settle every finding (fixed, or dismissed after reading the official rule); findings still open are carried into the next run's report (`attempts` on the finding doc) and after 3 runs (`MAX_ATTEMPTS`) closed with the question kept as it is (`gaveUp`). The checks are `scripts/automerge-checks.sh`. Both fix steps (this one and the source triage) may change a question only from an official source read with WebFetch (the exam owner, the government or regulator, or the primary law, standard or manual), recorded dated in the exam's `sources.md`; prep, coaching and news sites are never enough on their own. Local: `python3 -I tools/check_banks.py --export /tmp/q.json && npx tsx scripts/question-audit.ts --index /tmp/q.json --dry-run`. The index carries what the candidate sees beside the stem (a v2 item's shared stimulus title and text, a figure's alt text) and the auditor is told a v1 sign-image question's sign from `lib/signImages.ts`; before Nov 9 2026 it did not, so every passage, table, puzzle and picture question read as unanswerable (11 of that day's 29 findings). The workflow uploads `audits/` as an artifact and still opens the PR when the Claude Code step fails (it did on Nov 9, in under a second, and the report was lost with the runner). Run it by hand with `export_open` to write the findings still open to the log without auditing. To close findings from a session, commit `audits/resolutions/<date>.json` (`[{ id: "<exam>__<questionId>", status: "fixed" | "dismissed", note }]`): when it reaches main the workflow's `resolve` job runs `question-audit.ts --resolve` on it, (a fixed one closes that question's open reports with a "fixed" reply and restarts its statistics; a dismissed one closes them with a "right" reply). Since Nov 10 2026 the fix PR carries that file itself, so merging it closes its findings. Check a finding against the official source before applying it: three of that run's were wrong (Manitoba multi-lane changes, the ADI part 2 27 m eyesight distance, R.A. 8551 s. 28).
- **Search gaps** (`scripts/search-gaps.ts`, Tue, Thu, Sat from `.github/workflows/search-gaps.yml`, 07:41 UTC (daily until Nov 20 2026, cut to save API spend), same secrets): lists the searches that still find no test, using the Searches tab's own grouping (`lib/server/searchGroups.ts`, shared with `/api/admin/searches`) minus anything Planned or Ignored. Claude Code then adds `SEARCH_ALIASES` for searches that name a test we have (each with a case in `tests/test-search-aliases.test.cjs`), builds at most one new test with the `exam-bank` skill for the most-searched missing exam (2+ searches in 30 days by default; only after the skill's step 0 research finds the owner's official outline, handbook or primary law, recorded in `sources.md` and passed by `tools/check_sources.py`, then a 25-question re-check of keys against those sources), marks that search Planned so the next run does not start it again, and opens a PR. A PR that changed only `lib/testCatalog.ts` and `tests/test-search-aliases.test.cjs` (aliases, no new test) merges itself once `scripts/automerge-checks.sh` passes; a PR with a new test or any other file always waits for John. Run it by hand with `build` to force a named test.
- **Build tests** (the `/build-tests` command, `.claude/skills/build-tests/SKILL.md`; goal 1,000 tests): started by John by hand in any session, phone or desktop, `/build-tests` for 6, `/build-tests 10`, or `/build-tests id,id`; stopped by saying stop. No schedule and no GitHub workflow. The database key is never put in a cloud session (it reads outside websites): from a phone John runs `/build-tests id,id` with ids a desktop session ranked, and the desktop session records the outcomes and refreshes the sheet. `scripts/build-queue.ts` ranks `tools/queue/backlog.json` (about 300 candidates, each naming the live tests that predict it as `siblings`): a live test scores answers in 14 days / 100 + paywall views in 14 days + purchases x 40, where purchases count only from `PURCHASES_SINCE` (2026-11-10, when Premium went on sale beyond the DMV; earlier sales are nearly all DMV) to today, at most 60 days back, and each is weighted by the dollars it brought (`paymentNetUsdCents()` / the US $9.99 price, so a US sale is 1.0 and a $5 local-price sale about 0.5; rules in `lib/buildQueueScore.ts`, pinned by `tests/build-queue-score.test.cjs`); a candidate gets its siblings' mean score x `sells` plus 15 per failed search for it in 30 days, at most two picks per lead sibling; anything live, in an open PR (`buildQueue/{id}`, 21 days), failed in 14 days or twice, or skipped is held. Each pick is built with the `exam-bank` skill, blind-reviewed by a second reviewer (recorded in `tools/queue/reviews.json`), and the run opens one PR that always waits for John. It then runs `scripts/test-progress.ts` (one row per live test: questions, price, sources on file, second reviews, questions re-read by the audit, findings, reports, answers, open flags) and writes the rows to the Google Sheet "Tigertest: road to 1,000 tests" (`tools/queue/progress-sheet.json`), whose Summary tab is formulas over the All tests tab. Top the backlog up when it runs low: it does not refill itself.
- **Exam upkeep** (`.github/workflows/exam-upkeep.yml`, Wednesdays 05:41 UTC, `scripts/exam-upkeep.ts`): up to 3 tasks a week, dated changes in `lib/examFreshness.json` due or taking effect within 7 days first, then yearly reviews (exams whose `lib/landingDates.json` date and `lib/examReviews.json` review date are both over `reviewAfterDays` old). Claude Code does each from the official sources (a change sets `done` on its event, or `recheck` to a later date when the body has not published it yet; a review writes today's date in `lib/examReviews.json`), a second Claude Code run checks it, and the PR merges itself when the reviewer has no objection and `scripts/automerge-checks.sh` passes; otherwise it is closed and the tasks retried next week, and a task failed 3 times is skipped (`examUpkeep/{key}`).
- **The Questions tab** on `/admin` (`components/admin/QuestionsTab.tsx`, `/api/admin/questions`, GET only, built by `lib/server/questionHealth.ts`) is a read-only log: what was settled this week (`readHandledWeek()`), findings and student reports closed in 14 days with their outcome and whether the student was emailed, items still with the runs, and the dated exam changes the upkeep will take. No buttons. The daily brief leaves question quality out.

### Running without John (Nov 13 2026)

The aim is that John hears from the site only when a decision is his. Rules in `lib/ownerWatch.ts` (pure, pinned by `tests/owner-watch.test.cjs`), reads in `lib/server/ownerWatch.ts`.
- **Claude Code in GitHub Actions** (source triage, question audit, search gaps) runs through a wrapper step that unsets `CLAUDE_CODE_ENTRYPOINT` and `CLAUDE_CODE_ACTION` (`path_to_claude_code_executable`), because anthropics/claude-code-action#1901 makes the action's requests fail with "Credit balance is too low" on plan credit while the same key works from the plain CLI (Nov 16 2026: every triage run had failed since Nov 13). Drop the wrapper once that issue is fixed, and keep it on any new workflow that uses the action until then. Each step also passes `--disallowedTools` for Agent, Task, Workflow, Monitor, ScheduleWakeup, TaskStop and SendMessage: in the first working triage run (Nov 16 2026) Claude handed the pages to background agents and ended its turn while they ran, which ends the job, so nothing was written and the run cost $25.67 for nothing.
- **API budget** (Nov 20 2026; `lib/apiBudget.ts`, `lib/server/apiBudget.ts`, `scripts/api-budget.ts`, pinned by `tests/api-budget.test.cjs`): the Claude Console caps the organization at $200 a month, and past it every API call fails until the 1st. Every job that calls the API has a monthly share (`JOB_BUDGETS_USD`: daily brief $35, source triage $45, question audit $45, search gaps $28, exam upkeep $20, AI answer check $15) and nothing new starts once the month reaches `STOP_AT_USD` ($190). Before each Claude Code step a workflow runs `api-budget.ts check <job> <per-run cap>`, which gives the smallest of the per-run cap, the job's share left and the month left (`allowanceUsd()`); that goes to `--max-budget-usd`, and under $1 the step is skipped (the listing step reports 0 items, so nothing downstream runs). After it, `api-budget.ts record` adds `total_cost_usd` from the action's execution file to `apiSpend/{YYYY-MM}` (`totalUsd`, `jobs.<job>`). Per-run caps: triage $8, search gaps $8, audit fix $5 and verify $2, upkeep fix $8 and verify $3, AI answer check $2.50 (priced at Sonnet 5.5 rates, `SONNET_PRICES`). `question-audit.ts` prices each response at Opus 5.5 rates (`usageCostUsd()`), stops at $8 on Mondays and $2 on other days (reports, flags and freshness are queued before the sweep, so they are audited first), and records its total. The daily brief checks before writing (`briefBudgetLeft()`, $3 a run; the cron skips when it is used up) and records its two calls. A check that cannot read the ledger skips (fails closed). November 2026 counts $165 spent before the ledger (`UNTRACKED_USD`). Any new job that calls the API needs a share and the same check and record.
- **Self-merging PRs**: source triage, the question audit and exam upkeep (both after a second independent review) and alias-only search-gap PRs merge themselves when their checks pass (see Question quality). When a check or the second reviewer objects, those PRs are closed and retried, never left for John. Only new tests wait for him. `scripts/automerge-checks.sh` is the one list of those checks: add a check there when adding one to `checks.yml` or `question-banks.yml`.
- **Watchdog** (`/api/cron/watchdog`, daily 11:30 UTC): every scheduled job leaves a timestamp (`readJobHeartbeats()`: `globalStats/studyTimes.updatedAt` for aggregate-stats, `globalStats/questionFlags.updatedAt`, the newest `sourceWatch.checkedAt`, the newest `questionAudits.at` within 9 days, the newest `dailyBriefs.generatedAt` within 4 days, the newest `aiAnswerChecks.at` within 6 days). A job past its limit emails John once (transactional, `CAMPAIGN_REPLY_TO`), and again only if still broken 3 days later or another job stops (`shouldAlert()`); state and current problems in `analytics/watchdog`. List a job only when every finished run writes its timestamp, and add a heartbeat with each new nightly job. `?dry=1` reports without emailing.
- **Owner digest** (`/api/cron/owner-digest`, Mondays 12:10 UTC, `buildOwnerDigest()`): one email with only what needs a person: stopped jobs and open pull requests (GitHub's public API through `VERCEL_GIT_REPO_OWNER` / `VERCEL_GIT_REPO_SLUG`; optional `GITHUB_TOKEN` only lifts the rate limit). Question quality is never in it; its footer counts what the automation settled in 7 days (`readHandledWeek()`). A quiet week still sends "nothing needs you this week", so no email on a Monday means the digest broke. `?dry=1` returns it without sending. Neither route is a campaign, so `CAMPAIGN_CRON_COUNT` is unchanged.
- **Production errors** are read by a Claude scheduled task (daily, Vercel connector), not by code in this repo: it reads the last day's runtime errors and failed cron invocations, opens a PR for a clear bug and stays silent otherwise.
