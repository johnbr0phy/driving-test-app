- **AFOQT** (`/afoqt`, landing `/afoqt-practice-test`, `[data-theme="afoqt"]` grey flight-suit green (109 20% 30%; moved in Nov 2026 so it no longer matches TOEFL Reading and HSPT), icon `plane`, region US, hub group "GED, college, military & nursing school entrance" after asvab, borrows the ASVAB tiger, Premium, teacher-led): the U.S. Air Force Officer Qualifying Test, Form T, as the Air Force describes it on its Pearson VUE page and AFROTC Detachment 485's page (fetched Nov 2026; the AFPC Form T page, DAFMAN 36-2664 and the DTIC Form U report could not be loaded): 12 timed subtests, 516 questions in about five hours, scored as 1 to 99 composite percentiles, minimums Verbal 15 and Quantitative 10 per DAFMAN 36-2664, two attempts (three with a waiver). Modelled: the eight text subtests at about half length and the real pace, in Form T order, five options each, one section per subtest (`va` 12 in 4 min, `ar` 12 in 14, `wk` 12 in 2.5, `mk` 12 in 11, `rc` 12 in 12 with the 15-minute break after it, `ps` 10 in 5, `tr` 20 in 3.5, `ai` 10 in 4; 56 minutes), two tests with Test 2 held out, one drill per subtest (`va`, `ar`, `wk`, `mk`, `rc`, `ps`, `tr`, `ai`; domains `afoqtVerbal` ... `afoqtAviation`), `ar` and `mk` opening with shared Basics, so the dashboard is 12 steps; Premium locks Practice Test 2 and the `tr` and `ai` drills. Situational Judgment, Instrument Comprehension, Block Counting and the Self-Description Inventory are not modelled. Percent correct per subtest and overall; never an AFOQT percentile (`scoreNote`). Bank `data/v2/afoqt/questions.json` (250 original items, prefix `AFOQT-`, object form with 10 reading passages and 5 Table Reading grids, 6 drill-only items per subtest and 8 for Table Reading) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/afoqt/build.py` from `verbal.py`, `quant.py`, `reading.py`, `science.py` (key written first; all-number options with one unit sorted and locked, checked against a computed `v=`; Table Reading generated from seeded 7 x 7 grids with computed keys; unlocked keys take the least-used letter so A to E land at 20% each). Aviation Information sticks to the flight physics and aircraft parts on NASA Glenn's Beginners Guide to Aeronautics (the FAA handbooks returned 403). Sources in `tools/next100/afoqt/sources.md`.
