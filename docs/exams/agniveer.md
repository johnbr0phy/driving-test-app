- **Agniveer GD** (`/agniveer-army`, landing `/agniveer-army-cee-practice-test`, `[data-theme="agniveer"]` army olive, borrows the ASVAB tiger, region IN, hub group "India defence exams"): the Indian Army's Agniveer (General Duty) Online Common Entrance Exam, the first phase of Agnipath recruitment (2026 cycle: registration 13 February to 1 April 2026, extended to 10 April per news reports, CEE from June 2026, in 13 languages per the Army; joinindianarmy.nic.in could not be read). The GD paper as guides report it: 50 questions for 100 marks in 60 minutes, General Knowledge 15, General Science 15, Mathematics 15, Logical Reasoning 5, 2 marks each, 0.5 off per wrong answer (explained in `scoreNote` and on the landing, not scored), pass mark 35; then the rally and medical, not modelled. Two full 50-question tests in 60 minutes (one module, GK, Science, Maths, Reasoning), Test 2 held out, 9 drills (`agniveerHistory` (history, culture and the Army's battles, awards and days), `agniveerPolity`, `agniveerGeography`, `agniveerPhysics`, `agniveerChemistry`, `agniveerBiology`, `agniveerArith`, `agniveerMaths`, `agniveerReasoning`; 5/5/5/5/5/5/9/6/5 per test), so the dashboard is 11 steps.
