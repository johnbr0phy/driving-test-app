- **CogAT** (`cogat`, `/cogat`, landing `/cogat-practice-test`, `[data-theme="cogat"]` deep pine teal (174 40% 18%; the pre-assigned 168 40% 38% gave white text only 4.2:1 and every mid-lightness teal near it clashed), icon `graduation`, region US, hub group "US school tests" (with MAP Growth; `RELATED_OVERRIDES` to mapgrowth, elevenplus, ocnsw), fully free, children's test (`/cogat` in `CHILD_PATH_PREFIXES`, which also covers the landing), teacher-led, borrows the times tables tiger): Riverside Insights' Cognitive Abilities Test, Forms 7 and 8, Levels 9 to 12 (grades 3 to 6), from Riverside's CogAT Overview Brochure ("Testing Times and Descriptions": at Level 9 and up nine tests of about 10 minutes each in three batteries; Verbal and Quantitative items are text) and Overview of CogAT for Parents. Two sections that are the batteries, each 36 items in 30 minutes, its three tests in real order, 12 each (Riverside publishes times, not counts; 12 is ours), with a 5-minute break between: `verbal` (`cogatVerbalAnalogies` "puppy → dog : kitten → ?" with word options, `cogatSentenceCompletion`, `cogatVerbalClassification` "which word goes with apple, pear and plum?") and `quant` (`cogatNumberAnalogies` two complete pairs and a third to finish, `cogatNumberPuzzles` an equation with a ?, `cogatNumberSeries`). The Nonverbal Battery (Figure Matrices, Paper Folding, Figure Classification) and the picture-based primary levels are not modelled, and the landing, facts, `scoreNote` and the blueprint list say so. Percent per battery and an average, never Standard Age Scores, stanines or percentiles. Two tests, Test 2 held out; six drills of 20, one per CogAT test, no Basics, so the dashboard is 8 steps. All four-option single answer; number options are sorted and locked and the build respaces distractors with near misses so the key's slot rotates.
