# CRCST

Tigertest's sterile processing exam follows the HSPA Certified Registered Central Service Technician outline. The CBSPD CSPDT has its own exam, `cbspd`.

- Landing page `/crcst-practice-test`, app routes `/crcst/*`, state code `CRCST`.
- The bank in `data/crcst-questions.json` has 200 questions. Each of the four 50-question tests (IDs 401 to 404) includes 7 departmental, 11 decontamination, 10 preparation and packaging, 11 sterilization, 5 storage and inventory, 2 patient equipment and 4 professional development questions.
- Training Sets 1 to 3 and Practice Test 1 are free. Premium unlocks Training Sets 4 and 5 and Practice Tests 2 to 4. The five training sets use IDs 401 to 405. Tigertest's practice pass mark is 70%.
- The certification exam allows three hours for 150 questions, including 125 scored and 25 unscored. HSPA reports pass or fail. Applicants can complete 400 hours of hands-on experience before the exam or submit documentation within six months after passing provisionally.
