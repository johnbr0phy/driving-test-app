- **CTET** (`/ctet`, landing `/ctet-practice-test`, `[data-theme="ctet"]` burnt sienna, region IN, hub group "India teacher eligibility tests" (moved from "India government job exams" in Nov 2026)): CBSE Central Teacher Eligibility Test Paper I (Classes 1 to 5; real paper 150 MCQs in 150 minutes, five sections of 30, no negative marking, 60% qualifying, 55% reserved). Modelled: four 15-question sections in 15 minutes each (`cdp`, `lang`, `maths`, `evs`, 60 in 60, the real pace, no break); Language II is not modelled (chosen from 20 languages; the landing says so). Two tests, Test 2 held out, 8 drills (`ctetDev`, `ctetLearn`, `ctetComp`, `ctetLangPed`, `ctetMaths`, `ctetMathsPed`, `ctetEvs`, `ctetEvsPed`; 10/5/8/7/8/7/8/7 per test, 5 drill-only each), 10 dashboard steps. Language I uses original unseen passages and poems as shared stimuli (`CTET-P1`..`P5`). Bank `data/v2/ctet/questions.json` (160 original items, prefix `CTET-`) built by its own `tools/ctet/build.py`; sources in `tools/ctet/sources.md`.
