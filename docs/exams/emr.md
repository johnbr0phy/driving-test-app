- **EMR** (`emr`, `/emr`, landing `/emr-practice-test`, `[data-theme="emr"]` cool slate (196 10% 40%), region US, hub group "Emergency services" before EMT, Premium, borrows the EMT tiger, teacher-led (EMR courses, fire academies, workplace response teams)): the NREMT Emergency Medical Responder cognitive exam on the EMR Examination Specifications used since April 7, 2025 (2023 BLS Practice Analysis): Scene Size-up and Safety 19-23%, Primary Assessment 37-41%, Secondary Assessment 4-8%, Patient Treatment and Transport 20-24%, Operations 10-14%, pediatrics integrated; the real exam is computer adaptive, 90 to 110 items (30 unscored pilot) in 1 hour 45 minutes at Pearson VUE or OnVUE, scored 100 to 1500 with 950 to pass, three attempts 15 days apart, then a new EMR course, $88 per attempt. One linear section `emr` of 60 items in 70 minutes (the real pace), 13/12/11/4/13/7 per test, two fixed tests with Test 2 held out, 6 drills (`emrScene`, `emrAirway` and `emrCirc` (Primary Assessment split by Tigertest into airway and breathing and circulation and priorities), `emrSecondary`, `emrTreatment`, `emrOps`), 8 dashboard steps, no Basics. Formats: single (four options), select two or three (always three wrong, as NREMT writes them), build list (`order`) and options table (`matrix`); no numeric items (the EMR exam has none). Scope follows the EMR column of the National EMS Scope of Practice Model 2019 (change notices 1.0 and 2.0): no nasal or supraglottic airways, CPAP, pulse oximetry, aspirin, oral glucose, inhalers or epinephrine auto-injector (EMT and above, states may add), and naloxone only by unit-dose nasal device or auto-injector; scope items say "under the national model". Clinical figures from the NASEMSO National Model EMS Clinical Guidelines v3.0, choking and infant compressions from the 2025 AHA releases, triage from SALT (mcitriage.org). Bank `data/v2/emr/questions.json` (200 original items, plain array, ids `EMR-T1-NNN`, `EMR-T2-NNN`, `EMR-D-NNN`) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/emr/build.py` from one Python source per domain. Written against the EMT, AEMT, paramedic, CPR, lifeguard, firefighter and first aid banks and the five sample items in the EMR test plan. Sources in `tools/next100/emr/sources.md`.
