- **GCSE Combined Science** (`/gcse-science`, landing `/gcse-combined-science-practice-test`, `[data-theme="gcsescience"]` leaf green at hue 103, icon `microscope`, hub group "UK school tests", fully free): Foundation-tier GCSE Combined Science (England) on the DfE subject content. Three sections, biology, chemistry and physics, 20 items in 25 min each with 5-minute breaks; two practice tests, Test 2 held out. Six drills, one per science's paper 1 and paper 2 topics (`gcsescienceBio1`, `gcsescienceBio2`, `gcsescienceChem1`, `gcsescienceChem2`, `gcsesciencePhys1`, `gcsesciencePhys2`; 10 per test from pools of 35, 15 drill-only), `drillSize` 20. Bio1, Chem1, Phys1 and Phys2 open with Basics from `data/v2/basics/gcsescience.json` (magnification, variables, relative formula mass, balancing, substituting into and rearranging physics equations, units and prefixes, resultant force), so the dashboard is 12 steps. Every physics calculation states its equation (the 2025-2027 equation sheet), and the physics section's `reference` is the sheet.
