- **GRE** (`/gre`, landing `/gre-practice-test`, `[data-theme="gre"]` muted plum, region GLOBAL, hub group "GED, college, military & nursing school entrance"): the shorter GRE General Test (since Sept 2023). Verbal (12 in 18 min, then 15 in 23) and Quant (12 in 21 min, then 15 in 26, calculator), no break, no essay; each second section has `lower`/`upper` variants picked at 60% on the first, like SAT module 2. Two tests, Test 2 held out, 7 drills (`greTextCompletion`, `greSentenceEquivalence`, `greReading`, `greArithmetic`, `greAlgebra`, `greGeometry`, `greData`), 9 dashboard steps, free. Formats: one-blank text completion as `single`, two or three blanks as `cloze`; sentence equivalence as `multi` with `select: 2` of 6; reading as `single`, select-all `multi` (3 choices) and select-in-passage `highlight`; quantitative comparison as `single` with the four fixed choices and `lockedOrder`; numeric entry; multiple answer. Problem-solving items with all-number choices are sorted ascending and locked. Each measure is scaled 130 to 170 with `curve(..., 1)` (lower path caps near 153/155), composite "Verbal + Quant" 260 to 340. Bank `data/v2/gre/questions.json` (194 original items, 12 shared passages, ids `GRE-T<n>-<V1|V2L|V2U|Q1|Q2L|Q2U>-NN` and `GRE-D-<V|Q>-NN`) built by `tools/gre/build.py` from `tools/gre/test1.py`, `test2.py`, `drill.py` (keys authored first, shuffled by a stem seed; a reading item with no passage continues the previous item's stimulus). Sources in `tools/gre/sources.md`.
