- **HSC Maths Standard 2** (`/hsc-maths-standard`, landing `/hsc-maths-standard-2-practice-test`, `[data-theme="hscmaths"]` eucalyptus, hub group "Australia school tests", region AU, fully free): NSW Year 12, written to the NESA Mathematics Standard 11-12 Syllabus (2024), first examined in the 2027 HSC (the 2026 HSC is the last on the 2017 syllabus). Two sections like the real paper's: `s1` 15 multiple choice in 25 min and `s2` 25 short answers (mostly typed numbers) in 60 min, both with a calculator and a `reference` sheet; two practice tests with Test 2 held out; 6 drills grouping the ten Year 12 focus areas (`hscmathsAlgebra`, `hscmathsFinance`, `hscmathsMeasure`, `hscmathsNetworks`, `hscmathsBivariate`, `hscmathsProbability`; 6/8/8/6/5/7 per paper), three opening with shared Basics, 11 dashboard steps. Drill-only items (`test: 0`) are in `s2`. Networks are given as tables, no figures. 200 original items built by `tools/hscmaths/build.py` from `tools/hscmaths/q_*.py`, every answer computed (`netlib.py` for networks). Percent score, not bands.
