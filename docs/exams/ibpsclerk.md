- **IBPS Clerk** (`/ibps-clerk`, landing `/ibps-clerk-prelims-practice-test`, `[data-theme="ibpsclerk"]` deep rose, borrows the IBPS PO tiger, region IN, hub group "India government job exams"): IBPS CRP CSA (Clerk) Preliminary Examination, 100 five-option questions in 60 minutes with sectional timing (English Language 30 in 20 min, Numerical Ability 35 in 20 min, Reasoning Ability 35 in 20 min), a quarter mark off per wrong answer; pattern confirmed in the official CSA-XVI notification of 1 August 2026 (prelims October 2026; guides report 10 and 11 October), the same as CSA-XV (2025). Three sections (`eng`, `quant`, `reason`), each one timed module. Two 100-item mock tests, Test 2 held out, 9 drills, three per section (`ibpsclerkReading`, `ibpsclerkCloze`, `ibpsclerkGrammar`, `ibpsclerkNumber`, `ibpsclerkDI`, `ibpsclerkArith`, `ibpsclerkLogic`, `ibpsclerkPuzzle`, `ibpsclerkVerbal`; 12/10/8, 15/8/12, 8/15/12 per test, 3 drill-only each), so the dashboard is 11 steps. Heavier on simplification than the PO paper, no quadratic equations. Bank `data/v2/ibpsclerk/questions.json` (227 original single-answer items, prefix `IBPSCLK-`, object form with 19 stimuli) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/ibpsclerk/build.py` (computed answers asserted in the sources; warns on stems shared with the other India banks). English only, though the real exam is also offered in Hindi and regional languages (the landing says so). Score out of 100 with no negative marking; penalty and cutoffs in `scoreNote` and on the landing. Sources in `tools/next100/ibpsclerk/sources.md`.
