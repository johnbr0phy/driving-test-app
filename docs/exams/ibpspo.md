- **IBPS PO** (`/ibps-po`, landing `/ibps-po-prelims-practice-test`, `[data-theme="ibpspo"]` raspberry, region IN, hub group "India government job exams"): IBPS PO/MT Preliminary Examination, 100 five-option questions in 60 minutes with sectional timing (English Language 30 in 20 min, Quantitative Aptitude 35 in 20 min, Reasoning Ability 35 in 20 min), a quarter mark off per wrong answer. Three sections (`eng`, `quant`, `reason`), each one timed module, so there is no going back to a closed section. Two 100-item mock tests, Test 2 held out, 9 drills, three per section (`engReading`, `engCloze`, `engGrammar`, `qtNumber`, `qtDI`, `qtArith`, `rsLogic`, `rsPuzzle`, `rsVerbal`; 13/10/7, 15/10/10, 8/15/12 per test), so the dashboard is 11 steps. Bank `data/v2/ibpspo/questions.json` (225 original single-answer items, prefix `IBPSPO-`, object form with 18 stimuli: reading passages, cloze passages, para jumbles, data interpretation pipe tables and puzzles, each shared by five to eight items) built by its own `tools/ibpspo/build.py`; fixed-order items (error spotting letters, quadratic relations, inequality and syllogism conclusions, para jumble letters) use `lockedOrder`. Reasoning puzzles are text only; rupees and Indian names in word problems. Score out of 100 with no negative marking; the penalty and cutoffs are explained in `scoreNote` and on the landing. Sources in `tools/ibpspo/sources.md`.
