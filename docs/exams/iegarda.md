- **Garda Trainee** (`iegarda`, v2, Stage 1 online assessments of the Garda Trainee competition run by publicjobs for An Garda Síochána, Ireland, `/garda-trainee/*`, landing `/garda-trainee-assessment-practice-test`, `[data-theme="iegarda"]` dark slate blue-grey (192 20% 14%), region IE, hub group "Ireland licences and workplace certificates" (after iepsa), Premium (`PREMIUM_V2_IDS`), borrows the police tiger): An Garda Síochána's selection flowchart (fetched 2026-11-23) lists Stage 1 as online verbal and numerical reasoning tests and a written exercise, then an online interview (with a roleplay in 2026, publicjobs Garda Trainee 2026 page), then the Garda appointments process (Physical Competency Assessment, medical, substance misuse test, vetting); the 2024 booklet says Stage 1 is remotely proctored and ranked on an order of merit, the 2026 FAQ that the aptitude tests are English only, and publicjobs describes its verbal (statements judged against passages) and numerical (conclusions from numerical information) tests as multiple choice. No counts, times, provider or pass mark are published (the 2026 booklet could not be found), so the split is Tigertest's: section `verbal` 30 in 25 min (five original passages, four True / False / Cannot say statements, locked, and two four-option questions each: `gardaVerbalTfc` 20, `gardaVerbalInfer` 10) and section `numerical` 20 in 25 min with the calculator (four invented tables of five questions: `gardaNumPct` 10, `gardaNumCalc` 10; all-number options sorted and locked, every key computed in the source and checked against its explanation), no break; two tests, Test 2 held out; four drills (`tfc`, `pct`, `meaning`, `calc`), eight dashboard steps including Basics for True, False or Cannot Say and Percentages and Change. Percent score, goals 60 to 90, default 70; `scoreNote` says there is no pass mark and Tigertest does not estimate a rank. The written exercise and Stage 2 are not modelled; no situational judgement items (not listed for Stage 1).
