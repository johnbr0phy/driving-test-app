- **JAMB Use of English** (`/jamb-use-of-english`, landing `/jamb-use-of-english-practice-test`, `[data-theme="jambeng"]` Nigerian green, region NG, hub group "Nigeria university entrance tests"): the compulsory Use of English paper of JAMB's UTME (180 questions in 2 hours across four subjects, English 60, each subject out of 100, 400 in all). One section `eng` of 60 four-option questions in 40 minutes (the pro rata share of the 2 hours), free navigation, two practice tests with Test 2 held out, 6 drills (`jambengComprehension` 15 on three passages, `jambengCloze` 10, `jambengInterpretation` 5, `jambengVocabulary` 10, `jambengCompletion` 10, `jambengOral` 10 per test), 8 dashboard steps. The prescribed reading text (it changes by cycle; The Lekki Headmaster for 2026 per guides) is not modelled: its share goes to extra original passages, and the landing says so. Section counts follow guides (about 25/25/10), not a JAMB document; IBASS could not be read. Oral forms underline letters with `__x__` and mark stress with capital-letter syllables, judged by RP. Bank `data/v2/jambeng/questions.json` (200 original items, prefix `JAMBENG-`, object form with 12 Nigerian-set passages `JAMBENG-T1-P1`.. and cloze passages `-CZ`) built by its own `tools/next100/jambeng/build.py`. Percent score out of 100. Sources in `tools/next100/jambeng/sources.md`. Borrows the SAT tiger (since batch 5, with the other JAMB subjects).
