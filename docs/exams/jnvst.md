- **JNVST** (`/jnvst`, landing `/jnvst-class-6-practice-test`, `[data-theme="jnvst"]` school mustard, borrows the times tables tiger, region IN, hub group "India school entrance tests"): Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti's Class VI selection test for Class 5 students, on the JNVST 2027 prospectus (test 28 November 2026: 80 four-option questions for 100 marks in 2 hours, Mental Ability 20 + EVS 20 in about 60 minutes, Arithmetic 20 and Language 20 in 30 each, time adjustable; 1.25 marks each, no negative marking; qualifying 14/7/7; district merit with 75% rural and one third girls). One section `jnv`, one 80-item module in 120 minutes in the real order, two tests with Test 2 held out, 8 drills (`jnvstPatterns`, `jnvstShapes`, `jnvstNature`, `jnvstLiving`, `jnvstEvsPassage`, `jnvstNumbers`, `jnvstMeasure`, `jnvstReading`; 10/10/8/7/5/10/10/20 per test, 45 drill-only), 10 dashboard steps, free. The real Mental Ability Test is figures only; the bank writes the same reasoning as text (patterns, mirror and water images of letters and clocks, folding, counting shapes) and the directions, `scoreNote` and landing say so. EVS is 15 items plus one passage of 5, the Language Test four passages of 5, all original and written for a Class 5 reader. Bank `data/v2/jnvst/questions.json` (205 items, 13 stimuli `JNVST-T1-E`, `-T1-L1..L4`..., object form through `withStimuli()`) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/jnvst/build.py`. Kept clear of the SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, IBPS PO and CTET banks. Sources in `tools/next100/jnvst/sources.md`.
