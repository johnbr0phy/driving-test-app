# ARRT Limited Scope of Practice in Radiography (`lmrt`)

- **Limited Scope X-Ray** (`lmrt`, ARRT Limited Scope of Practice in Radiography exam, given by ARRT for state licensing agencies to limited x-ray machine operators, `/limited-scope-xray/*`, landing `/arrt-limited-scope-practice-test`): `data/limited-scope-xray-questions.json` (200 original questions on the ARRT Limited Scope content specifications, board approved January 2022 and in effect January 1, 2023: core module as patient care 20, radiation physics and radiobiology 12, radiation protection 28 (10 CFR 20.1201 eye and skin limits, 21 CFR 1020.30 HVL, 21 CFR 1020.31 mobile SSD, PBL and light localizer, NCRP 116 public limit, OSL, dose creep), image acquisition and evaluation 20, equipment operation and QA 24; procedure modules as chest 20, extremities 24, skull and sinuses 16, spine 20, podiatric 16), test IDs 44001-44004 (50 questions each, 26 core and 24 procedure: 5/3/7/5/6 and 5/6/4/5/4, Tigertest's split), sets 44001-44004 (Patient Care & Radiation Safety 60, Image Production 44, Chest & Extremities 44, Skull, Spine & Podiatric 52), state `LMRT`, prefix `LMRT-`, 75% readiness line (ARRT reports the number correct per module to the state, which sets the pass mark). The real exam: Core 100 scored + 15 pilot in 1 hr 55 min (Patient Care 18, Safety 40, Image Production 42), then the procedure modules the state assigns (Chest 20, Extremities 25, Skull/Sinuses 20, Spine 25, Podiatric 20, each + 5 pilot), Pearson VUE, no breaks between modules (ARRT 2026 Handbook for State Candidates). There is no bone densitometry module (a separate ARRT exam). No images: positioning, anatomy and evaluation in words; calculation keys (mAs, inverse and direct square, 15% rule, time) asserted in the sources. Revised specifications are planned for January 1, 2028 (CR removed, mandible added): `lib/examFreshness.json`. No item repeats a fact and answer from the `arrt` bank. Built by `tools/next100/lmrt/build.py` from `q_*.py`; sources in `tools/next100/lmrt/sources.md`.
