- **NCLEX-RN** (`/nclex`, landing `/nclex-rn-practice-test`, `[data-theme="nclex"]` clinical blue, hub group "Healthcare certification exams"): one linear section (no going back, like the CAT) of 85 items in 170 min, two practice tests with Test 2 held out, 8 drills, one per Client Needs category (`nclexCare`, `nclexSafety`, `nclexHealth`, `nclexPsych`, `nclexComfort`, `nclexPharm`, `nclexRisk`, `nclexPhys`). Bank `data/v2/nclex/questions.json` (200 original items: pools 36/26/18/18/18/32/24/28, per test 15/11/8/8/8/14/10/11, 30 drill-only) weighted by the NCSBN 2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan, built from `tools/nclex/` by `tools/build_v2_bank.py nclex`. Next Generation formats: per test one 6-item unfolding case study (`stimulusId` + `caseStep` 1 to 6 on the clinical judgment model; CS1 heart failure in Test 1, CS2 urosepsis in Test 2, CS3 DKA drill-only, cases in `tools/nclex/stimuli.json`) plus a bowtie and two each of matrix, cloze, highlight, order and numeric (dosage), and select all that apply on about a quarter of items. Percent-correct score, all or nothing per item; the real exam is pass/fail with partial credit. Its eight-expression tiger set is in `public/tigers/nclex/`.
