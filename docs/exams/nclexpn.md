- **NCLEX-PN** (`/nclex-pn`, landing `/nclex-pn-practice-test`, `[data-theme="nclexpn"]` teal, hub group "Healthcare certification exams" right after NCLEX-RN): the LPN/LVN licensure exam, built exactly like NCLEX-RN: one linear section (no going back) of 85 items in 170 min, two practice tests with Test 2 held out, 8 drills, one per Client Needs category (`pnCare`, `pnSafety`, `pnHealth`, `pnPsych`, `pnComfort`, `pnPharm`, `pnRisk`, `pnPhys`), no Basics. Bank `data/v2/nclexpn/questions.json` (200 original items, prefix `NCLEXPN-`: pools 42/26/18/24/20/26/24/20, per test 18/11/8/10/9/11/10/8, 30 drill-only) weighted by the NCSBN 2026 NCLEX-PN Test Plan (Coordinated Care 18-24%, Safety 10-16%, Health Promotion 6-12%, Psychosocial 9-15%, Basic Care 7-13%, Pharmacological Therapies 10-16%, Reduction of Risk 9-15%, Physiological Adaptation 7-13%), built from `tools/nclexpn/` by `tools/build_v2_bank.py nclexpn`. Every item is in the PN scope: the PN collects data, reinforces teaching, contributes to the plan of care, assigns tasks to AP and reports to the RN; IV push, titration and other state-limited acts are "per facility policy and state scope". Same NGN mix as the RN bank: per test one 6-item case study (CS1 in Test 1, CS2 in Test 2, CS3 drill-only, cases in `tools/nclexpn/stimuli.json`), a bowtie, two each of matrix, cloze, highlight, order and numeric (PO or IM dosage), SATA on about a quarter. Percent-correct score, all or nothing per item. No tiger set yet (falls back to the DMV tiger).
