- **Civil Service Exam** (`/civil-service-exam`, landing `/civil-service-exam-reviewer-practice-test`, `[data-theme="phcse"]` deep government blue, region PH, hub group "Philippines government and licensure exams"): the Civil Service Commission's Career Service Examination, Pen and Paper Test (CSE-PPT), as announced for the 8 March and 9 August 2026 sittings (Professional 170 items in 3 h 10 min, Subprofessional 165 in 2 h 40 min, reported from the CSC announcements; guides say 20 of each are a personal-data questionnaire; passing is a general rating of 80.00). Both levels: verbal ability (English and Filipino), numerical ability and general information (1987 Constitution, R.A. 6713, peace and human rights, environment); Professional adds analytical ability, Subprofessional clerical ability (filing, spelling). One bank serves both: Practice Test 1 is a Professional mock and Practice Test 2 (held out) a Subprofessional mock, each one 100-item module in 2 hours (30 verbal, 25 analytical or clerical, 25 numerical, 20 general information, the split Tigertest's own), 9 drills (`phcseGrammar`, `phcseVocab`, `phcseReading`, `phcseAnalytical`, `phcseClerical`, `phcseNumbers`, `phcseWordProblems`, `phcseConstitution`, `phcsePublicService`), so the dashboard is 11 steps; the clerical drill is all drill-only items. English only (Filipino verbal items not modelled), abstract reasoning as letter patterns, US spelling, pesos. All-number options are sorted and locked, as are the I/II conclusion sets. Bank `data/v2/phcse/questions.json` (245 original items, prefix `PHCSE-`) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/phcse/build.py`. Percent score; `scoreNote` says the CSC's raw-to-rating conversion is unpublished. Sources in `tools/next100/phcse/sources.md`.
