- **Praxis Core** (`/praxis-core`, landing `/praxis-core-practice-test`, `[data-theme="praxis"]` dark ochre, hub group "GED, college, military & nursing school entrance"): ETS Praxis Core Academic Skills for Educators, Reading 5713 (56 in 85 min), Writing 5723 selected response (40 in 40 min; the two essays are not modelled) and Mathematics 5733 (56 in 90 min, four-function calculator). Two practice tests of three sections at half length and the real pace (Reading 28 in 43 min, Writing 20 in 20 min, Math 28 in 45 min; 10/8/10, 5/15, 10/9/9 across the ETS categories), no break, Test 2 held out. Five options everywhere like the real tests; Writing runs usage (four underlined parts plus No error, `lockedOrder`), sentence correction (choice A repeats the original, `lockedOrder`), revision in context on a numbered draft, then research skills; Math adds select-all `multi` and typed `numeric`. 7 drills (`ideas`, `craft` = Craft + Integration, `language`, `revision`, `number`, `data`, `algebra`); `ideas`, `language` and `number` open with existing Basics skills (66 tagged items), so the dashboard is 12 steps. Bank `data/v2/praxis/questions.json` (200 original items, prefix `PRAXIS-`, 16 shared stimuli: per test a long passage, paired passages, three short passages, a data table and a revision draft) built by its own `tools/praxis/build.py` from Python-data sources. Scores are percent correct; states set their own 100 to 200 cutoffs (156/162/150 in many). Sources in `tools/praxis/sources.md`.
