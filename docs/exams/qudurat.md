- **Qudurat, the General Aptitude Test (GAT), English version** (`qudurat`, v2, free (college entrance, not in `PREMIUM_V2_IDS`), `/qudurat/*`, landing `/qudurat-practice-test` (the word Saudi students search; "gat" alone is ambiguous with CUET's GAT and others), `[data-theme="qudurat"]` vivid purple (280 70% 48%; clears every theme within 8 degrees by 15+ saturation or 8+ lightness, CUET at 280 30% 30% included), region SA (listed on the `/sa` Saudi Arabia country page beside the Saudi driving theory test), new hub group "Saudi Arabia university admission tests" with `RELATED_OVERRIDES` to sat, sehp, cuet and act, borrows the SAT tiger, teacher-led (`TEACHER_LED`, `TEACHES`: "a Qudurat (GAT) prep course")): the university admission aptitude test of Qiyas, the National Center for Assessment at ETEC (Education and Training Evaluation Commission), Saudi Arabia. Real test (ETEC's GAT page under https://etec.gov.sa/en/centers/qiyas and the English Test-Taker's Guide, both fetched 2026-11-25): 5 sections of 24 multiple-choice questions (four choices), 120 in all, in 2 hours 5 minutes, a time limit per section and no going back, no scheduled breaks, no calculator, Arabic and English, paper and computer; GAT for Humanities Tracks and GAT for Science Tracks; verbal weights analogies 17, sentence completion 7, contextual error 10, reading 21; Science Tracks quantitative weights arithmetic 23, geometry 10, algebra 4 (scientific version only), data analysis 8; norm-referenced, no pass or fail (65 average, 81+ top 5%); five attempts; valid five years. Modelled: the English Science Tracks version; two practice tests of the real 120 questions in the real 125 minutes, regrouped by section because ETEC does not publish how its five sections split (section `verbal` modules 1 to 3 of 22 at 23 minutes, section `quant` modules 1 and 2 of 27 at 28 minutes, no break); per test `qdAnalogy` 20 (15 analogies, 5 odd one out), `qdCompletion` 9, `qdContext` 12, `qdReading` 25 (7 passages), `qdArith` 28, `qdGeometry` 12, `qdAlgebra` 5, `qdData` 9 (4 data sets), with 11 quantitative comparison items per test inside arithmetic, geometry and algebra (ETEC's four fixed choices, shortened, locked in ETEC's order, key letter checked against computed values); Test 2 held out; 8 drills (`analogy` with no Basics, then `completion`, `context`, `reading`, `arithmetic`, `geometry`, `algebra`, `data`, each opening with 4 or 5 shared Basics skills used by no other drill), 17 dashboard steps; drill pools 20/15/15/25/31/15/15/15 from Test 1 plus 31 drill-only items. Contextual error items underline four words with `__..__` and lock them in sentence order. All-number options are sorted and locked (key in an end slot 52%, min and max even); text options are shuffled with the key on an A to D rotation. Score is percent correct per section and overall (`percentScale`, `overallPercent`), goals 60 to 90 (default 70); `scoreNote` quotes ETEC's ranking table and says a percent cannot become a GAT score. Not modelled: the Arabic GAT (no right-to-left support), the Humanities Tracks version, true/false statements and related terms (listed by ETEC but given no weight), and the real section order. Bank `data/v2/qudurat/questions.json` (271 original items, 24 stimuli: 16 passages and 8 data sets, object form through `withStimuli()`, ids `QUDURAT-T<n>-V<m>-NN`, `QUDURAT-T<n>-Q<m>-NN`, drill only `QUDURAT-D-V-NN`, `QUDURAT-D-Q-NN`) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/qudurat/build.py` from `verbal_t1.py`, `verbal_t2.py`, `quant_t1.py`, `quant_t2.py` and `drill.py` (every quantitative key is a Python expression the build checks; the build exits 1 on any warning).
