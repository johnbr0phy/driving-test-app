- **REET Level 1** (`/reet`, landing `/reet-level-1-practice-test`, `[data-theme="reet"]` sandstone brown (39 25% 34%), borrows the CTET tiger, region IN, hub group "India teacher eligibility tests"): the Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers, Level 1 (Classes 1 to 5), conducted by the Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan (REET 2024 notification No. 01/2024 of 12 December 2024: 150 one-mark MCQs in 2.5 hours, five parts of 30, CDP, Language I, Language II, Maths, EVS; four options per question plus a fifth OMR bubble E for not answering, a question with no bubble losing 1/3 mark and more than 10% blank disqualifying, no deduction for wrong answers, explained, not scored; 60% to qualify for general candidates, 55% for SC, OBC, MBC, EWS and ST). Modelled with all five sections, Hindi as Language I and English as Language II, each 15 questions in 15 minutes (75 in 75, the real pace), two tests with Test 2 held out, 9 drills (`reetCdp`, `reetHindi`, `reetHindiPed`, `reetEnglish`, `reetEnglishPed`, `reetMaths`, `reetMathsPed`, `reetEvs`, `reetEvsPed`; 15, 10/5, 10/5, 10/5, 10/5 per test, 51 drill-only), 11 dashboard steps. EVS includes Rajasthan's fairs, festivals, dances, crafts, wildlife and water systems (static facts). Hindi items are in Devanagari with English explanations; three Hindi and three English original passages as stimuli (`REET-H1/H2/HD`, `REET-E1/E2/ED`). Bank `data/v2/reet/questions.json` (201 original items, prefix `REET-`, object form) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/reet/build.py` (key first; all-number options sorted and locked). Kept clear of the ctet, uptet and rajpolice banks. Sources in `tools/next100/reet/sources.md`.
