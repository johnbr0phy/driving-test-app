- **REx-PN** (`/rex-pn`, landing `/rex-pn-practice-test`, `[data-theme="rexpn"]` deep purple, hub group "Canada healthcare and first aid", region CA): the Canadian practical nurse entry-to-practice exam (RPNs with CNO in Ontario, LPNs with BCCNM in BC), built like NCLEX-PN: one linear section (no going back) of 90 items in 180 min (the real exam's minimum length; the real limit is 4 hours for up to 150), two practice tests with Test 2 held out, 8 drills, one per Client Needs category (`rexpnCare`, `rexpnSafety`, `rexpnHealth`, `rexpnPsych`, `rexpnComfort`, `rexpnPharm`, `rexpnRisk`, `rexpnPhys`), no Basics. Bank `data/v2/rexpn/questions.json` (210 original items, prefix `REXPN-`: pools 42/28/20/24/20/34/23/19, per test 19/12/8/10/8/15/10/8, 30 drill-only) weighted by the NCSBN REx-PN Test Plan (effective January 2022, revised 2025: Management of Care 18-24%, Safety and Infection Control 10-16%, Health Promotion 6-12%, Psychosocial 8-14%, Basic Care 6-12%, Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies 14-20%, Reduction of Risk 8-14%, Physiological Adaptation 6-12%), built from `tools/rexpn/` by its own `tools/rexpn/build.py` (same id scheme as `build_v2_bank.py`; sources list keys first and the build shuffles options by a stem seed). Formats are the REx-PN's own: multiple choice, SATA, fill-in-the-blank calculation (5 per test, drip and pump rates, tablets, mL, mg/kg) and exhibit items (a table in `passage`); no NGN case studies. Canadian practice: SI units, Canadian drug names, UCPs/PSWs, consent, privacy and substitute decision-makers stated as varying by province. Percent-correct score, all or nothing (the real exam gives SATA partial credit from January 2026). Sources in `tools/rexpn/sources.md`.
