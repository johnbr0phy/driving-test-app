- **SSAT** (`ssat`, `/ssat`, landing `/ssat-practice-test`, `[data-theme="ssat"]` deep magenta (331 95% 26%; moved off 91 95% 26%, which clashed with Wonderlic), icon `graduation`, region US, hub group "US school tests" (after mapgrowth; `RELATED_OVERRIDES` to mapgrowth, psat, elevenplus), fully free, children's test (`/ssat` in `CHILD_PATH_PREFIXES`, which also covers the landing), teacher-led, borrows the SAT tiger): the Middle Level SSAT (students in grades 5 to 7 applying for grades 6 to 8) from E3n (admission.org, formerly The Enrollment Management Association; ssat.org redirects there), on E3n's "What's on the Middle Level SSAT?" page and the SSAT Candidate Handbook for testing year 2026-2027: an unscored 25-minute writing sample, then Quantitative 25 in 30 min, Reading 40 in 40 min, Verbal 60 in 30 min (30 synonyms, 30 analogies), Quantitative 25 in 30 min and an unscored 15-minute experimental section, two 10-minute breaks, 3 h 10 min; five choices, no calculator; +1 right, -1/4 wrong, 0 blank; scaled scores and same-grade percentiles, no pass mark. The Upper Level (grades 8 to 11) has the same format but is not modelled. Four sections in the real order at about half length and the real pace: `quant1` 13 in 16 min, `reading` 20 in 20 min (four 5-question passages, two narrative and two argument), a 10-minute break, `verbal` 30 in 15 min (15 synonyms then 15 analogies), `quant2` 12 in 14 min (`ssatArith` 10, `ssatAlgebra` 8, `ssatGeometry` 7 per test across the two quant sections); writing sample and experimental section not modelled. Two tests, Test 2 held out; seven drills of 20 (`arithmetic`, `algebra`, `geometry`, `narrative`, `argument`, `synonyms`, `analogies`, one per domain, quant drill-only items in `quant1`), arithmetic, algebra and argument opening with shared Basics, so the dashboard is 12 steps. Percent per section and overall, never SSAT scaled scores or percentiles; the quarter-point penalty is explained in `scoreNote` and on the landing, not applied.
