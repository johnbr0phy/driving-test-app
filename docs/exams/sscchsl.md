- **SSC CHSL** (`/ssc-chsl`, landing `/ssc-chsl-practice-test`, `[data-theme="sscchsl"]` slate teal, borrows the SSC CGL tiger, region IN, hub group "India government job exams"): Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Tier 1 on the 2026 notice (7 September 2026; Tier 1 30 November to 31 December 2026): 100 questions in 60 minutes, four parts of 25 (English Language, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude, General Awareness) with a sectional timer of 15 minutes each, 2 marks each and 0.50 off per wrong answer, minimum qualifying marks UR 30%, OBC/EWS 25%, others 20%. Four sections `eng`, `gi`, `qa`, `ga`, each one 25-item, 15-minute module (no going back to a closed part), two mock tests, Test 2 held out, 8 drills, two per part (`sscchslGrammar`, `sscchslVocab`, `sscchslVerbal`, `sscchslLogic`, `sscchslArith`, `sscchslAdvanced`, `sscchslHistory`, `sscchslScience`; 13/12 per part per test, 4 drill-only each), so the dashboard is 10 steps. Bank `data/v2/sscchsl/questions.json` (232 original single-answer items, prefix `SSCCHSL-`, object form with cloze stimuli `SSCCHSL-CL1/2` and reading stimuli `SSCCHSL-RC1/2`) built by `tools/next100/sscchsl/build.py`; checked against the ssccgl, rrbntpc, ibpspo and ctet banks for repeated facts and numbers. Reasoning is text only (figural items not modelled); GA is static; rupees and Indian names. Percent score; negative marking is explained in `scoreNote` and on the landing, not applied.
