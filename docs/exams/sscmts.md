Bank `data/v2/sscmts/questions.json` (212 original single-answer items, prefix `SSCMTS-`, object form with cloze stimuli `SSCMTS-CL1/2` and reading stimuli `SSCMTS-RC1/2`) built by `tools/next100/sscmts/build.py`. MTS level (10th pass); reasoning text only; GA static; rupees and Indian names. Havaldar PET/PST not modelled.
