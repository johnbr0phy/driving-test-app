- **TABE** (`/tabe`, landing `/tabe-practice-test`, right after HiSET in the "GED, college, military & nursing school entrance" group, `[data-theme="tabe"]` muted orchid (301 40% 42%, 20 points less saturated than Times Tables at 300), region US, fully free (never in `PREMIUM_V2_IDS`), borrows the GED tiger, teacher-led (adult basic education and workforce programs)): DRC's Tests of Adult Basic Education. TABE 13&14 is the form in use (the Federal Register notice of May 8, 2025 let TABE 11&12 be used for NRS reporting only until June 30, 2026), so the bank is written to DRC's TABE 13&14 Level D blueprints (AE-CCR level D, about grades 6 to 8; revisions dated Jan and Feb 2026): Reading Level D 39 items in two 50-minute parts (Key Ideas and Details 45.5%, Craft and Structure with Vocabulary 40.9%, Integration of Knowledge and Ideas 13.6%; multiple choice and evidence-based selected response), Mathematics Level D 40 items in parts of 45 and 15 minutes (Numbers and Operations 27.5%, Algebraic Concepts 27.5%, Measurement, Data and Probability 25%, Geometry 20%; multiple choice and gridded response, online Desmos calculator), Language Level D 33 items in 50 minutes, multiple choice only (Text Types and Purposes 39.4%, Conventions 27.3%, Knowledge of Language and Vocabulary 33.3%); Levels E, M, D and A, a Locator picks the level per subject, no pass mark (DRC sets no cut scores; programs do). Modelled: Level D only, three sections at the real pace, `read` 22 in 55 min (10/9/3 on five passages of four or five, one with a table, one pair of texts), `math` 20 in 30 min (6/5/5/4, typed `numeric` items with `wholeNumber` standing in for gridded response, no calculator needed, Tigertest's summary of the Level D reference sheet as `reference`), `lang` 18 in 27 min (7/5/6: two drafts with numbered sentences, then single sentences), 5-minute breaks, `testLength` "1 hr 52 min"; two tests, Test 2 held out. Percent score per section, composite `overallPercent`, goals 60 to 85, default 70; `scoreNote` says scale scores, NRS levels and grade equivalents are not modelled. 7 drills of 16 (`drillSize` 16): `keyIdeas` (`tabeReadKey`, Basics mainIdea, findingDetail, inference, causeEffect), `craft` (`tabeReadCraft` + `tabeReadIntegration`), `number` (`tabeMathNumber`, Basics negativeNumbers, fractionOps, ratios, percents, rateProblems), `algebra` (`tabeMathAlgebra`), `dataGeometry` (`tabeMathData` + `tabeMathGeometry`), `writing` (`tabeLangText`), `conventions` (`tabeLangConventions` + `tabeLangKnowledge`, Basics subjectVerb, commaSplices, pronounCase, verbTense, commonlyConfused, apostrophes), so the dashboard is 12 steps. Bank `data/v2/tabe/questions.json` (200 original items, 21 stimuli, ids `TABE-T1-NNN`, `TABE-T2-NNN`, `TABE-D-NNN`, sections Reading, Math, Language) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/tabe/build.py` from `reading.py`, `math.py`, `language.py` (`Q()` key first, rotated A to D; `L()` locked; `N()` numeric; all-number and `\frac` options sorted and locked; the build checks per-test domain counts and every `basics` tag).
