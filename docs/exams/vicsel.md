- **Victorian Selective** (`/victorian-selective`, landing `/victorian-selective-entry-practice-test`, `[data-theme="vicsel"]` violet blue, icon `graduation`, fully free, hub group "Australia school tests", region AU): the Victorian Selective Entry High Schools exam (Year 8, Year 9 entry; ACER for the Department of Education) without Writing. Two sections at the real times, no calculator, four options throughout: Mathematics and Quantitative Reasoning 40 items in 60 min (`vicselMaths` 20 then `vicselQuant` 20) and Reading and Verbal Reasoning 40 in 55 min (`vicselReading` 20 on four texts: story, article, poem, opinion piece or paired texts; then `vicselVerbal` 20); a 20-minute break after the first, as on the day. Question counts are not published, so 40 per section is a modelling choice. Two tests, Test 2 held out; percent per section and an overall that averages the two, no pass mark, goals 60 to 90, default 75. Bank `data/v2/vicsel/questions.json` (200 items, 10 stimuli `VICSEL-R1-A..`, ids `VICSEL-T1-`, `-T2-`, `-D-`) built by `python3 -I tools/vicsel/build.py` from `tools/vicsel/*.py` (keys rotated A to D by the build). Four drills of 20 (maths, quant, reading, verbal), each opening with shared Basics, so the dashboard is 10 steps. Australian spelling, metric, AUD. Teacher-led (selective entry coaching classes).
