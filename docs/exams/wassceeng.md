- **WASSCE English Language** (`/wassce-english`, landing `/wassce-english-practice-test`, `[data-theme="wassceeng"]` deep violet (275 95% 30%), region NG, hub group "West Africa school exams" (its own, right after "Nigeria university entrance tests"), free like every school exam, borrows the SAT tiger, teacher-led (SS3 English classes)): the core English Language subject of WAEC's WASSCE. WAEC's own sites do not serve the syllabus, so the format is WAEC's syllabus as published by waecsyllabus.com (updated September 5, 2026) and myschool.ng, which agree with the hosted syllabus PDF; WAEC Nigeria's FAQ confirms English and General Mathematics as the two core subjects for 2026 and 2027. Paper 1 (The Gambia, Nigeria, Sierra Leone and Liberia) is 80 four-option questions in 1 hour for 40 marks, forty lexical (everyday vocabulary and sixteen fields of activity, synonyms, antonyms, idioms and collocations, figurative usage) and forty structural; Paper 2 essay, comprehension and summary, 2 hours, 100 marks (not modelled); Paper 3 is 60 multiple-choice items in 45 minutes for 30 marks, the Test of Orals in Nigeria and Liberia (vowels and diphthongs, consonants and clusters, rhymes, word stress and syllable structure, emphatic stress and intonation, phonetic symbols) and listening comprehension in Ghana, The Gambia and Sierra Leone (not modelled); Ghana's Paper 1 (30 lexis, 20 structure, 30 literature) is not modelled. Two sections, `p1` (80 in 60 min: `wassceengFields` 16, one per field, `wassceengSynonyms` 14, `wassceengIdioms` 10, `wassceengConcord` 14, `wassceengTenses` 13, `wassceengStructure` 13) and `p3` (30 in 23 min, half at the real pace: `wassceengVowels` 10, `wassceengConsonants` 8, `wassceengRhymes` 4, `wassceengStress` 8), two practice tests with Test 2 held out, ten drills, one per domain, 12 dashboard steps, no Basics. Percent score; WAEC grades A1 to F9 and publishes no conversion, which `scoreNote` says. Oral notation as JAMB English (`__x__` underlines, stress in capitals), RP model, British spelling, West African names. Bank `data/v2/wassceeng/questions.json` (270 original items, prefix `WASSCEENG-`, plain array, 50 drill-only; syllable-count options sorted and locked) built by `python3 -I tools/next100/wassceeng/build.py` from `lexis.py`, `structure.py`, `orals.py`.
