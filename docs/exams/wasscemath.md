- **WASSCE Mathematics** (`wasscemath`, `/wassce-mathematics`, landing `/wassce-mathematics-practice-test`, `[data-theme="wasscemath"]` deep green (150 75% 22%), region NG (no Ghana region yet; the copy says it serves Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, Liberia and The Gambia), hub group "West Africa school exams" (with WASSCE English, right after the JAMB subjects), free (a school exam, never Premium), borrows the SAT tiger, teacher-led): WAEC's WASSCE General Mathematics, Mathematics (Core) in Ghana. WAEC's own sites do not serve the syllabus, so the format comes from WAEC's syllabus document as published by waecsyllabus.com (page and PDF), with identical copies on zenodo.org and rachel.core2learn.org, plus the WAEC Nigeria FAQ (English Language and General Mathematics are the two core subjects for 2026 and 2027 entries): Paper 1 is 50 multiple-choice objective questions from the common areas of the syllabus in 1 1/2 hours for 50 marks; Paper 2 is 13 essay questions, answer 10 in 2 1/2 hours for 100 marks (not modelled); silent non-programmable calculators and WAEC tables allowed; grades A1 to F9 with no published conversion, so the score is percent only. One section `p1`, 50 four-option questions in 90 minutes with the calculator, two practice tests with Test 2 held out, 9 drills (`wmNumber` 6, `wmIndices` 5, `wmSetsSequences` 5, `wmCommercial` 6, `wmAlgebra` 8, `wmMensuration` 6, `wmGeometry` 5, `wmCoordTrig` 4, `wmStatistics` 5 per test, Tigertest's split since WAEC publishes none; 11 or 12 drill-only each); number, commercial, algebra, mensuration and statistics open with shared Basics, so the dashboard is 16 steps. Topics the syllabus marks with symbols as not common to every country (matrices, financial arithmetic, binary operations, functions, completing the square, tangents, two-variable inequalities, sine and cosine rules, latitude and longitude, construction, loci, intercept theorem, bearings, calculus, grouped SD, without-replacement probability, vectors and transformation) are left out.
