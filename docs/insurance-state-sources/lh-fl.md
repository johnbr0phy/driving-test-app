### 2-15 outline weights (effective January 1, 2026)
General knowledge (67%): I Types of life policies 10; II Life riders, provisions, options, exclusions 10; III Completing the life application, underwriting, delivery 8; IV Retirement and other life concepts 5; V Types of health policies 11; VI Health policy provisions, clauses, riders 10; VII Social insurance 4; VIII Other health concepts 4; IX Field underwriting 5.
Florida statutes, rules and regulations (33%): I Common to all lines 13; II Life and annuity incl. variable products 10; III Health 10.

## Chosen form
150 items = the 150 scored items, pass 70%.
- General knowledge 100 national items: I 15, II 15, III 12, IV 7, V 17, VI 15, VII 6, VIII 6, IX 7 (the section-to-item map is in national.json "_notes.sections").
- Florida law 50: section I (20) = 8 national LH-REG items whose answers hold in Florida (2 reworded) + 12 LHFL-FLREG items; section II (15) = LHFL-FLLB (4), FLLPV (6), FLANN (5); section III (15) = LHFL-FLHB (2), FLHPV (7), FLHTY (6).
- Totals by family category: insuranceRegulation 22, generalInsurance 13, lifeBasics 11, lifePolicyTypes 11, lifeProvisions 21, annuitiesRetirement 11, healthBasics 16, healthProvisions 23, healthPolicyTypes 22.

- Excluded: LH-HPRV-005 (3-year legal action limit; Florida uses the statute of limitations, s. 627.616).
