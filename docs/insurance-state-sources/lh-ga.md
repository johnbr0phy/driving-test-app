### Outline (125 scored)
General: I Life policy types 15; II Life riders, provisions, options, exclusions 15; III Life application, underwriting, delivery, contract law 12; IV Life retirement and other concepts 8; V A&S policy types 16; VI A&S provisions, clauses, riders 15; VII Social insurance 6; VIII A&S other concepts 5; IX A&S field underwriting 8 (= 100).
Georgia: X Laws, rules and regulations pertinent to life, accident and sickness 19 (Commissioner powers, examinations, hearings, penalties; domestic/foreign/alien, stock/mutual, certificate of authority, transacting; licensing incl. temporary, nonresident, counselor; unfair trade practices incl. rebating, defamation, discrimination, misrepresentation, twisting, advertising, coercion, controlled business, claims practices, fraud; premiums and fiduciary duty; Life and Health Guaranty Association); XI Life only 3 (replacement Reg. 120-2-24 and 33-24-6.1; advertising and solicitation Regs. 120-2-11 and 120-2-31; insurable interest 33-24-6); XII A&S only 3 (individual and group A&S, Medicare supplement, LTC, LTC partnership) (= 25).

## Chosen form
125 items = 100 national picks + 25 Georgia items at the exact state weights (19 / 3 / 3). Pass 70%. Georgia offers the combined line as its own exam (12-GA-05), so no CA-style combination was needed; the single-line exams (80 each) are not modeled. Pretest items are not modeled.

National picks follow the general outline, which has the same nine areas and weights as the Texas Pearson VUE general outline, so the Texas selection was the starting point: life types 10 + annuity products 5 (I); life provisions and riders 14 (II); application, contract law, FCRA, GLBA, 1033, twisting, agent authority (III); life basics and annuity tax (IV); health types (V); health provisions and renewability (VI); Medicare, Medigap, Medicaid, SSDI (VII); COB, subrogation, workers' comp (VIII); foreign/alien insurer items 2 (Georgia X.B, kept national with Georgia stems). Changes from Texas: LH-ANN-004, LH-TYPE-016 and LH-HPRV-003 dropped (see exclusions); LH-TYPE-005 (return of premium term), LH-ANN-006 (period certain) and LH-HPRV-004 (time of payment) added in the same outline areas.

## National exclusions and overrides
Excluded (key wrong or incomplete in Georgia):
- LH-HPRV-003: keys the NAIC 15-day claim-forms deadline; Georgia's required provision gives 10 working days (O.C.G.A. 33-29-3(b)(6)).
- LH-TYPE-016 and LH-ANN-004: key "life license plus FINRA registration" for variable products; Georgia also requires a separate Variable Products agent line (exam 62, handbook #121100), so the key is incomplete.

Overrides (wording only, keys unchanged):
- LH-REG-002, LH-REG-003: stems moved from Florida and Texas to Georgia; explanations cite O.C.G.A. 33-3-1.
- LH-PROV-004: explanation states Georgia's 30-day minimum life grace period (33-25-3(a)(1)); the national text said 30 or 31 days.
- LH-PROV-005: explanation states Georgia's three-year reinstatement rule (33-25-3(a)(8)).
- LH-HPRV-001: explanation states Georgia's 7/10/30-day A&S grace minimums (33-29-3(b)(3)); the national text said 31 days for other modes.
- LH-HPRV-004: explanation states Georgia's 30-day loss-of-time payment rule (33-29-3(b)(8)); the national text said claims are paid immediately, but Georgia ties other claims to its prompt-pay statute.
- LH-LIFE-001: explanation cites 33-24-3(h) (insurable interest needed only when the contract takes effect).
- LH-GEN-017: same option change as Texas (option A "Waiver" replaced with "Indemnity", explanation rewritten) to remove a second defensible answer; no state content.

Checked and left alone: life incontestability 2 years (33-25-3(a)(2)), misstatement of age (33-25-3(a)(3)), suicide exclusion limited to 2 years and war/aviation exclusions allowed for individual policies (33-25-5(a)), individual life free look 10 days (33-25-8), group life conversion 31 days (33-27-3(a)(8)), A&S reinstatement 45 days / 10 days, notice of claim 20 days, proof of loss 90 days, legal actions 60 days to 3 years, time limit on defenses 2 years (33-29-3(b)).

## Facts considered and not used
- S.B. 109 (2025, Act 75) amended 33-25-5 so a group life policy may not exclude deaths of service members that are not caused by war. Current and verified, but the life-only section has only three items and replacement, solicitation and insurable interest are the outline's listed topics.
- Cease and desist penalties (33-6-9) and guaranty association advertising penalties (33-38-21(b)): rewritten by H.B. 1344 effective January 1, 2027, so not asked.
- The rebating exception's dollar cap per customer is not asked; the item tests the "not contingent on a sale" condition instead.
