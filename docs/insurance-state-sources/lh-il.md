### Which outline
The 2026 and 2027 outlines have the same scored counts and the same section weights. The 2027 life state outline adds two small life-only topics (G. Unfair practices, 5/236, 0-1; H. Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association, 0-1). Every state item here sits in topics present in both outlines, so the bank serves exams taken now and after January 1, 2027.

## How the law was checked (read this)
ilga.gov (the Illinois General Assembly, including the ILCS and the JCAR administrative code) could not be reached from this environment: its server sends an incomplete TLS certificate chain (Sectigo OV R40 intermediate missing) and the fetch tool got HTTP 503. Statute text was therefore read from the FindLaw mirror of the ILCS (codes.findlaw.com, each page marked "Current as of January 01, 2025"), and administrative rules from the Cornell LII mirror of the Illinois Administrative Code (law.cornell.edu/regulations/illinois/...), each page checked as real rule text with its Illinois Register history note. Current practice facts (prelicensing, CE timing, reinstatement, LTC and annuity training, moving to Illinois) were confirmed on official IDOI pages accessed 2026-10-18. Searches for 2025-2026 Public Acts touching the cited sections found none enacted (bills such as HB 2775 and HB 4330 on Medicare supplement switching stalled in Rules). 50 Ill. Adm. Code 3119 (CE) was amended effective 12/30/2025 for CE reciprocity; the statutory 24 hours with 3 hours of ethics in 5/500-35 is unchanged and is what the items use. The wirer or a reviewer with ilga.gov access should spot-check the cited sections against the official text.

## Chosen form
Illinois has no combined life and health exam: the Life and the Accident & Health lines are separate licenses, each with its own general and state exam, scored separately. Following the California precedent, the form combines what a candidate for both lines faces: 50 + 31 + 50 + 39 = 170 scored items. That exceeds 150, so it is capped at 150 keeping the national:state ratio (100:70): 150 x 100/170 = 88.2, so 88 national and 62 state. Pass 70% (the scaled 70).

National picks (88) split evenly between the two general outlines, 44 each, scaled from 50:
- Life general: types 13 (9 life types + 4 annuity products: phases, SPIA, variable annuity licensing, joint and survivor), provisions and riders 13, application/underwriting/contract law 11 (contract elements, aleatory, unilateral, representations, concealment, conditional receipt, delivery, statement of good health, insurable interest, FCRA, GLBA), retirement and other 7 (third-party ownership, viatical settlement, group conversion, key person, taxation, MEC, Roth IRA).
- Health general: types 14, provisions 13 (uniform provisions, renewability, future increase option), social insurance 5 (Medicare A/C/SNF/disability eligibility, SSDI), other concepts 5 (COB birthday rule, subrogation, workers comp, taxation, occupational vs nonoccupational), field underwriting 7 (ACA guaranteed issue, DI underwriting, twisting/replacement, utmost good faith, adhesion, estoppel, implied authority).

State items (62) at the scaled state weights, 62/70 of each section: common section 44 x 62/70 = 39 (Director 3, licensing 18, fiduciary 4, commissions and disclosure 4, marketing and claims practices 10), life only 9 x 62/70 = 8, A&H only 17 x 62/70 = 15. Within A&H: Medicare supplement 2, long-term care 3, advertising 2, minimum standards 2, group 1, Illinois HIPAA 1, unfair discrimination 1, guaranty association 1, HMO 1, LHSO 1. Within life: replacement 2, solicitation/Buyer's Guide 1, policy provisions (free look) 1, suitability and annuity training 2, illustrations 1, advertising 1.

## National overrides (text only)
- LH-PROV-004: explanation now states the Illinois grace period of 30 days or one month (215 ILCS 5/224(1)(b)). Key unchanged.

## National exclusions and checks
- LH-REG-002 (alien insurer, stem names Florida) not picked rather than overridden; the Illinois state outline does not test insurer classification.
- LH-PROV-011 (free look "varies") not picked; Illinois sets 10 days for individual life (5/224(1)(n)), written as LHIL-ILLIF-004.
- LH-HPRV-015 (15-day probationary period on a disability policy) not picked: 50 Ill. Adm. Code 2007.60(a) bars probationary periods in individual A&H policies except as the rule's sickness definition allows, so the item could mislead an Illinois candidate.
- LH-REG-012 (dividends called a savings account) not picked to avoid overlap with the Illinois advertising item LHIL-ILLIF-008.
- Checked and kept: life incontestability 2 years and reinstatement (5/224), group life conversion 31 days without evidence (5/231.1), uniform A&H provisions (grace 7/10/31, reinstatement 45/10 days, notice 20, forms 15, proof 90, legal action 60 days to 3 years), COBRA (federal), ACA guaranteed issue, Medicare and SSDI facts.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Medicare supplement birthday open enrollment (5/363(8), 45 days from the birthday, ages 65-75): FindLaw flags a version of 5/363 effective January 1, 2026 whose text could not be read, so no item asks it.
- Guaranty association dollar limits, license fees, bond amounts: dollar figures are off-limits as the asked fact.
- Viatical settlements (215 ILCS 159) and accelerated benefits (Reg. 1407): outline weight 0-1 each; skipped to keep the life section within 8 items.
