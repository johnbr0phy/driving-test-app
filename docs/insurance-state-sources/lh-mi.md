### 16-80 outline (150 items)
Insurance Regulation 17 (appointments, producer duties, commissions, licensing, CE, discipline, unfair trade practices, fraud, privacy; every topic cites MCL sections); General Insurance 15; Life Insurance Basics 15 (includes Michigan life laws: insurable interest, variable products, solicitation, guaranty association, replacement R 500.601-.606, unfair discrimination); Life Insurance Policies 8 (incl. group conversion 500.4438, universal life); Life Policy Provisions, Options and Riders 15 (entire contract 500.4014, free look 500.4015/.4073, grace 500.4012, reinstatement 500.4028, misstatement 500.4018, exclusions 500.4046); Annuities 6; Federal Tax (life, annuities, qualified plans) 5; A&H Basics 15 (incl. advertising, outline of coverage, genetic information 500.3407b); Individual A&H Policy General Provisions 7 (500.3407-.3452); Disability Income 8; Medical Plans 9% = 14 (incl. Michigan dependent age, child enrollment, newborns 500.3403); Group Health 8 (incl. small employer 500.3701, .3707, .3711); Dental 2; Senior and Special Needs 12 (Medicare, Medicare supplements 500.3803+, LTC 500.3901-.3955); Federal Tax for Health 3.

## Chosen form
150 items, the real scored count (no cap needed), pass 75%. Michigan tests life and health in one combined exam, so the combined-form rule does not apply. Because the exam is integrated rather than split into general and state parts, Michigan items were written where the outline itself cites Michigan statutes (all of Insurance Regulation, the Michigan life laws, policy provisions, dependent rules, small employer and LTC rules), and national items fill the general topics. Pretest items are not modeled.

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- LH-REG-002: stem moved from Florida to Michigan; explanation notes the certificate of authority.
- LH-PROV-004: explanation states Michigan's 1-month grace period and deduction of the overdue premium (MCL 500.4012).
- LH-PROV-008: explanation states Michigan's 2-year incontestable clause and its exceptions (MCL 500.4014).
- LH-HPRV-001: explanation states Michigan's 7/10/31-day grace minimums (MCL 500.3410).

## National items left out because of Michigan law or the outline
- LH-HPRV-006 (time limit on defenses framed as a "health" application): since 2016 PA 276, MCL 500.3408 applies to disability policies other than health insurance policies, so the stem would be arguable in Michigan.
- LH-HPRV-002 and LH-HPRV-005 (reinstatement and legal actions framed as health policies): same 2016 split in MCL 500.3411 and 500.3422; the claim-deadline and payment items (LH-HPRV-003, -004) were kept because they are framed neutrally or as disability.
- LH-HLTH-017 (birthday rule): coordination of benefits in Michigan runs through the Coordination of Benefits Act (MCL 550.251-.255), not checked here, so the national item was not used.
- LH-PROV-005 and LH-PROV-011 (reinstatement, free look) and LH-LIFE-016 (group conversion): replaced by Michigan items with the Michigan figures.
- LH-LIFE-018/-020, LH-ANN-020, LH-TYPE-024 and the business-plan items (split dollar, Section 79, 162 bonus, deferred comp): not in the 16-80 outline or lower priority for the slots available.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Civil fine amounts (MCL 500.1244, 500.2038) and guaranty association dollar limits: dollar figures not worth an item.
- DIFS director's appointment and term: MCL 500.202 still describes the "commissioner of insurance"; the office became the DIFS director by executive reorganization, so no item.
- Michigan unfair discrimination (500.2027) and privacy (500.501+): not read in full, no item.
