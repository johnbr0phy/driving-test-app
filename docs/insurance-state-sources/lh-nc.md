### Accident and Health or Sickness Agent outline (55; eff. March 2, 2026)

## Chosen form
North Carolina tests life and A&H only as two separate 55-item exams; no combined exam exists. As with California's combined form, the practice test models a candidate taking both: **110 items = Life 55 + A&H 55**, keeping each exam's general:state ratio (82 general + 28 North Carolina). 110 is under the 150 cap, so nothing is scaled. Pass 70% (the scaled pass mark used as the percent). Pretest items are not modeled.

National picks by outline area (82):
- Life types of individual life (17): lifePolicyTypes 15 (term, renewable, convertible, ROP, whole life, limited pay, modified, endowment, UL and death benefit options, variable, indexed UL, survivorship, payor) plus the 2 contract-formation items counted below.
- Annuities (5) and tax-qualified plans and taxation (part of IV): annuitiesRetirement 8.
- Life provisions (11): lifeProvisions 11 (entire contract, ownership, grace, loans, APL, incontestable, misstatement of age, beneficiaries, simultaneous death, nonforfeiture, waiver of premium).
- Other life topics (8): lifeBasics 5 (insurable interest, key person, cross purchase, group master policy, Social Security blackout period) plus legal concepts.
- Legal concepts and contract formation (life IV.I, IV.K; A&H III.G, III.H): generalInsurance 9.
- A&H policies (22): healthPolicyTypes 14 (disability income, business overhead, key person, future increase option, AD&D, major medical, corridor deductible, hospital indemnity) + healthBasics 9 (perils, DI need, HMO, capitation, PPO, POS, ACA preexisting rule, COB, Social Security disability).
- A&H provisions (11): healthProvisions 11.
- No national regulation items: neither general outline has a regulation section; all regulation is in the North Carolina portion.

North Carolina items (28) follow the two state sections: the shared common topics 16 (Commissioner, definitions, licensing, CE, marketing, privacy, guaranty association, fraternals), life-only 6, A&H-only 6.

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- LH-PROV-004: explanation states North Carolina's 31-day life grace period (G.S. 58-58-22(1)) instead of "30 or 31 days".
- LH-HPRV-001: explanation cites North Carolina's 7/10/31-day grace provision (G.S. 58-51-15).
- LH-GEN-015: explanation cites G.S. 58-3-10 (application statements are representations, not warranties).

## National exclusions
- LH-HPRV-003 (proof of loss within 90 days): North Carolina's required provision allows 180 days (G.S. 58-51-15). The key text cannot be changed, so it is excluded and LHNC-NCAH-002 tests the North Carolina rule.
- LH-PROV-005 (reinstatement "usually about three years"): North Carolina requires five years (G.S. 58-58-22(5)); tested by LHNC-NCLIF-002 instead.
- LH-PROV-011 (free look "often 10 days, varies by state"): North Carolina has no single life free-look rule (10 days is tied to the buyer's guide timing in G.S. 58-60-15, 30 days on replacements), so replaced by LHNC-NCRPL-001.
- LH-REG-002 (stem set in Florida) and the other LH-REG items: the North Carolina general outlines have no regulation section; regulation is covered by the 15 North Carolina items.
- Medicare, Medigap, Medicaid and long-term care items (LH-HTYP-017 to 028): North Carolina tests these on the separate Medicare Supplement/Long-Term Care Agent exam, not the Life or A&H outlines.
- Not selected for weighting reasons only (no conflict found): remaining items in each category.

## Checked and left alone
North Carolina's A&H provisions in G.S. 58-51-15 match the national items used: entire contract (executive officer), time limit on certain defenses (two years), grace 7/10/31, reinstatement (45th day; sickness after 10 days), legal actions (60 days to 3 years), change of occupation, misstatement of age, relation of earnings, unpaid premium. Life incontestability is two years (G.S. 58-58-22(2)) and suicide may limit benefits for no more than two years (58-58-22(4)). Variable products need a FINRA registration (G.S. 58-33-26(e)), matching LH-TYPE-017 and LH-ANN-004.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Dollar figures (civil penalty range in G.S. 58-2-70, embezzlement felony thresholds, the $50 address-change fee, guaranty association limits): dollar amounts are not asked.
- Rebate exceptions (G.S. 58-63-16, $250 value): repealed and replaced effective January 1, 2027, so an item would go stale within months.
- Renewability notice periods in G.S. 58-51-20 (a fraction of months covered): too formula-bound for one item.
