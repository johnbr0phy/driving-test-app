## Chosen form
New Jersey has no combined life and health exam, so the form combines the two real exams, as the California variant does: 83 + 88 = 171 scored items, which exceeds the 150 cap. Capped at 150 keeping the general : New Jersey ratio (100 : 71): 88 general-outline slots + 62 New Jersey-outline slots. Pass 70% (the real pass mark on both exams). Pretest items are not modeled.

The 62 New Jersey slots follow the combined New Jersey weights scaled by 62/71: laws common to all lines 44 (the common section appears on both real exams, so it is counted twice before scaling), life-only 7, health-only 11. Within the common 44: jurisdiction 7, definitions 7, licensing 14, trade practices and licensee duties 11, guaranty associations 2, fraud 3. Four of those slots are textbook items the outline itself says come from a "standard insurance textbook" and are taken from the national bank (LH-REG-001 McCarran-Ferguson; LH-REG-002 alien and LH-REG-003 foreign insurers, reworded for New Jersey; LH-REG-007 broker), so the bank is 92 national + 58 New Jersey items.

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- LH-REG-002: stem moved from Florida to New Jersey; explanation cites N.J.S.A. 17B:17-7 (alien insurer).
- LH-REG-003: stem moved from Texas to New Jersey; explanation cites N.J.S.A. 17B:17-7 and 17B:17-11 (foreign, authorized).
- LH-PROV-004: explanation states the New Jersey grace period (30 days, or one month of at least 30 days, N.J.S.A. 17B:25-3).

## National items deliberately not picked
- LH-PROV-005 (reinstatement) and LH-PROV-011 (free look): New Jersey items LHNJ-NJLIF-002 (3 years, 17B:25-9) and LHNJ-NJLIF-001 (10 days, 17B:25-2.1) test the state rule instead.
- LH-HPRV-018 (75 percent participation, "typically") and LH-HPRV-017 (COBRA 102 percent): not wrong, but New Jersey small group rules are governed by the SEH program; left out to avoid implying a New Jersey participation figure.
- LH-ANN-004 and LH-TYPE-016 (variable products licensing): New Jersey issues a separate variable life and variable annuity line of authority (N.J.S.A. 17:22A-33(a)(5)); the national wording ("life license plus securities registration") is incomplete for New Jersey.
- LH-TYPE-024 (credit life): fine, but not needed; credit life rules in N.J.A.C. 11:2-3 were not verified.

## Checked and left alone (no conflict with New Jersey law)
Life: entire contract and representations (17B:25-5), incontestability 2 years (17B:25-4), misstatement of age (17B:25-6), insurable interest at inception (17B:24-1.1(b)). Health uniform provisions in the national bank (grace 7/10/31 days, reinstatement 45 and 10 days) match 17B:26-6 and 17B:26-7.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Group life conversion: the outline cites N.J.S.A. 17B:27-1 to -25, but those sections are not in the Legislature's current statutes file, so no New Jersey group life item was written (national LH-LIFE-016, the standard 31-day conversion, is kept).
- Small employer lower bound: N.J.S.A. 17B:27A-17 says at least two but not more than 50 employees; the item asks only for the upper bound (50) to avoid the one-employee federal definition.
- Dollar penalties: only two statutory penalty caps are asked (17:22A-45 first offense $5,000; 17:33A-5 third and later violation $15,000), because the outline lists penalties and fraud, and both figures are fixed in statute (not indexed).
