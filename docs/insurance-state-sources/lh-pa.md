### Series 16-03 outline (effective 3/1/2026), weights of 150 items
1 Insurance regulation 21% (licensing, discipline, state regulation, producer regulation, unfair practices, privacy, fraud, federal law); 2 General insurance concepts 7%; 3 Life, accident and health basics 14% (insurable interest, uses of life insurance, viaticals, policy classes, producer responsibilities, field underwriting, delivery, company underwriting, perils, limited policies, exclusions, replacing health coverage); 4 Types of life policies 8%; 5 Life policy provisions, options and riders 13%; 6 Annuities 9%; 7 Individual A&H policy provisions 7%; 8 Disability income 4%; 9 Medical plans 4% (incl. HIPAA and Pennsylvania mandated benefits); 10 Group health 3%; 11 Dental 1%; 12 Senior and special needs (Medicare, Medicare supplement, LTC with Pennsylvania rules) 7%; 13 Federal tax 2%.

## Chosen form
150 items = the real scored length. Pass 70%. Because Pennsylvania integrates state law into every area, the form uses 101 national items for the general concepts and 49 Pennsylvania items placed in the areas whose outline lines cite Pennsylvania statutes or regulations (roughly a third of the outline lines do). Experimental items are not modeled.

National picks by outline area: regulation (federal law, agency, twisting, defamation, insurer classes, unfair claims) 12; general concepts and field underwriting 13; life basics (insurable interest, needs, business uses, group basics) 9; life policy types 10; provisions, options and riders 12; annuities 7 + federal tax 6 = 13; medical plans, renewability and HSA 10; A&H provisions, COBRA and ACA 10; disability, medical expense, dental, Medicare and LTC 12. Twelve national picks were swapped for same-area items keyed D to keep every answer letter between 20% and 30% (national keys are fixed, so state keys alone could not balance the form), and five state items were moved off the plain A-B-C-D rotation for the same reason.

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- LH-REG-002: stem moved from Florida to Pennsylvania.
- LH-REG-003: stem moved from Texas to Pennsylvania (Ohio insurer with a Pennsylvania certificate of authority).
- LH-REG-005: explanation cites IDA 696-A (no mingling with own funds without the insurer's express consent).
- LH-GEN-017: optionA "Waiver" replaced by "Indemnity" (waiver was defensible for the facts), explanation rewritten; same fix as the Texas form.
- LH-PROV-004: explanation states Pennsylvania's grace period of 30 days or one month (ICL 410(b)).
- LH-PROV-005: explanation states the three-year reinstatement period (ICL 410(k)).
- LH-HPRV-006: Pennsylvania's time limit on certain defenses is three years, not two (ICL 618(A)(2)), so "Three years after issue" became "Four years after issue" and the explanation cites the Pennsylvania rule.

## National items left out for Pennsylvania reasons
- LH-LIFE-001 (divorce, policy owned by the ex-wife): 20 Pa.C.S. 6111.2 revokes a revocable spouse designation made by the decedent; the item's facts are outside it, but it reads as conflicting, so it was not used.
- LH-LIFE-016, LH-PROV-011, LH-HTYP-023, LH-REG-009, LH-REG-010: the same topics are tested by Pennsylvania items (group conversion, free look, Medigap open enrollment, Chapter 51 rebating, financial institution rules).
- LH-HTYP-028 (LTC must be guaranteed renewable): not checked against Pennsylvania's Chapter 89a text, so not used.

## Checked and left alone
National items that state a rule Pennsylvania also uses: life grace (ICL 410(b)), two-year incontestability (410(c)), misstatement of age (410(e)), health grace 7/10/31, reinstatement 45 and 10 days, notice 20 days, claim forms 15 days, proof 90 days, legal actions 60 days to 3 years, change of occupation, misstatement of age (all ICL 618).

## Facts deliberately not used
- Dollar amounts (license fees, the $125 gift limit as the asked fact, guaranty association limits).
- Pennsylvania mandated benefits (mammography, serious mental illness, newborns): the outline cites them, but their statute text (40 P.S. 764b-764g, 771-775) was not retrieved from an official source in this session, so no item rests on them.
- Annuity suitability (Act 48 of 2018) and viatical settlement rules: official text not retrieved.
- The age 19 limit for children on family policies in ICL 617(A)(3): federal ACA dependent coverage to age 26 applies to major medical, so no item asks it.
