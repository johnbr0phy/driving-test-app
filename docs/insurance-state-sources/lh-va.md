### Content outline (140 scored)
1 Insurance regulation 14; 2 General insurance 8; 3 Life insurance basics 8; 4 Life insurance policies 14; 5 Life provisions, options and riders 16; 6 Annuities 8; 7 Federal tax, life and annuities 3; 8 Qualified plans 3; 9 Health insurance basics 8; 10 Individual health general provisions 16; 11 Disability income 7; 12 Medical plans 8; 13 Group health 7; 14 Dental 2; 15 Senior and special needs (Medicare, Medicare supplement, LTC) 15; 16 Federal tax, health 3.

The Virginia outline does not split a general part from a state part. Virginia law is woven through the sections, with Code and VAC citations on the line items (all of section 1, life provisions in 5, eligibility in 12.4, small group in 13.5, Medicare supplement and LTC rules in 15).

## Chosen form
140 items, the real scored count (under the 150 cap), pass 70%. Virginia offers a combined life, annuities and health exam, so no combining was needed. Prometric says each exam has a general and a state-specific portion but publishes no split; exam-prep providers commonly report 100 general + 40 Virginia for 11-01, and counting the outline lines that cite Virginia law gives about the same, so the form is 100 national picks + 40 Virginia items. Pretest items are not modeled.

National picks by outline area: regulation 4 (NAIC, implied authority, FCRA, 18 USC 1033); general insurance 10 (risk, adverse selection, contracts, conditional receipt, delivery); life basics 7 (insurable interest, needs, key person, buy-sell, group conversion, accelerated benefits); life policies 13; life provisions 10; annuities, tax and qualified plans 12; health basics and medical plans 10; individual health provisions, COBRA and ACA 16; DI, AD&D, major medical, dental, Medicare, Medigap and LTC 18.

Virginia items by outline area: regulation 12 (licensing 8, trade practices 4), life basics/field underwriting 3, life provisions 7, annuities 2, health eligibility 4, health provisions and group/small group 5, Medicare supplement and LTC 7.

## National overrides (text only)
- LH-HPRV-008: explanation said "one of 12 mandatory provisions"; Virginia requires 13 (cancellation by insured added, § 38.2-3503 provision 13), so it now cites the Virginia list.

## National exclusions
- LH-PROV-011 (generic free look), LH-PROV-004 (grace computation), LH-PROV-005 (reinstatement): the Virginia rules (10 days, 31 days, three years) are tested directly by state items.
- LH-LIFE-001: divorce and insurable interest scenario; Virginia's divorce revocation statute (§ 20-111.1) makes the facts easy to misread, so the rule is tested directly by LHVA-VALIF-005.
- LH-REG-002, LH-REG-003: stems name Florida and Texas.
- LH-HPRV-018: generic 75 percent participation figure; not a Virginia rule.

## Checked and left alone (consistent with Virginia law)
Health uniform provisions in the national bank (grace 7/10/31, reinstatement 45/10 days, notice 20 days, forms 15, proof 90, legal action 60 days to 3 years, two-year time limit on defenses) match § 38.2-3503. Life incontestability (two years, §§ 38.2-3107, 38.2-3305), misstatement of age (§ 38.2-3306), entire contract (§ 38.2-3304), group life conversion within 31 days (§ 38.2-3332), the Medigap six-month open enrollment (14VAC5-170-100) and LTC inflation protection offer (14VAC5-200-100) all match.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Guaranty association dollar limits, appointment and renewal fees, penalty amounts (§ 38.2-218): dollar figures are off-limits.
- LTC rate increase notices (§ 38.2-5206.1, 2024): on the outline but too procedural for one fair item.
