# Washington Life and Disability Producer exam: sources (all accessed 2026-10-18)

Washington licenses the life line and the disability (accident and health) line separately (RCW 48.17.170(1)(a), (b)); PSI offers each alone and as one combined "Life and Disability Producer Combo" exam. This form models the combo, which is the TigerTest life and health family's line.

All three PDFs were downloaded with curl and extracted with pdftotext; each is real text (the bulletin is 13 pages, the outline 11 pages), not a challenge page. Scratchpad copies: `wa-src/wa-cib-6616.pdf`, `wa-src/lifedas26.pdf` (+ `.txt`).

Note on pre-license education: the 8/3/2026 bulletin still says pre-license education is required for full-line exams, while the OIC pre-licensing page says the requirement ended July 23, 2023. Because the two official sources disagree, no item asks about it.

### Outline weights (150 items)
Federal laws 3; Washington laws, rules and regulations 45; general insurance concepts 6; life, accident and health basics 20; types of life policies 12; life provisions, options and riders 17; annuities 6; individual A&H provisions 10; disability income 5; medical plans 13; group health 3; seniors and special needs (Medicare, Medigap, Medicaid, LTC) 5; federal tax considerations 5.

The Washington section lists, without sub-weights: commissioner, terms and concepts, life and disability guaranty association, licensing (producer, temporary, nonresident, appointments, penalties, renewal, CE, late renewal, reporting, DRLP), marketing practices (unfair practices, compensation disclosure, rebating, twisting, misrepresentation, defamation, discrimination, receipts, advertising, public interest), producer responsibilities and compensation; life only (replacement, disclosure, illustrations, annuity suitability and training, return of policy, policy loans, settlement, grace, entire contract, incontestability, misstatement of age, limitation of liability, group life); disability only (outline of coverage, Medicare supplement, LTC education and definition, Health Care False Claim Act, health insurance reform, maternity, IRO review, child enrollment, standard provisions, free look, discrimination, HCSC/HMO continuation, newborns, children).

## Chosen form
150 items = 105 national picks + 45 Washington items, pass 70%. 150 is the real scored count, so no cap applies. Washington items split about half common to both lines (22), life only (12) and disability only (11), since the outline gives no sub-weights.

National picks by outline area: federal laws 3 (LH-REG-015 1033, LH-REG-016 GLBA and Do Not Call, LH-HLTH-009 ACA); general concepts 6; life/A&H basics 20 (insurable interest, needs and human life value, personal and business uses, adverse selection, representations, conditional receipt, delivery, FCRA, perils, DI underwriting); life policy types 12 (incl. group life); life provisions and riders 17; annuities 6; A&H uniform provisions 10; disability income 5 (incl. SSDI); medical plans 13 (incl. ACA, AD&D, hospital indemnity, dental); group health 3 (COBRA x2, COB); seniors 5; federal tax 5 (exclusion ratio, MEC, life proceeds, DI benefit taxation, HSA).

## National overrides (text only)
- LH-REG-003: stem moved from Texas to Washington; explanation cites RCW 48.05.010 and 48.05.030.
- LH-PROV-004: explanation states Washington's one month, not less than 30 days grace period (RCW 48.23.030).
- LH-PROV-011: explanation states Washington's 10-day return after receipt and the 10 percent late-refund penalty (RCW 48.23.380), replacing "often 10 days, varies by state".
- LH-PROV-016: explanation states Washington's 120-hour survival rule (RCW 11.05A.030), which reaches the same keyed answer.
- LH-HPRV-001: explanation cites RCW 48.20.062.

## National items deliberately not used
- LH-LIFE-001 (ex-spouse owner-beneficiary after divorce): Washington's nonprobate asset revocation statute (RCW 11.07.010) turns on who made the designation; dropped to avoid a second defensible answer.
- LH-PROV-005 (three-year life reinstatement): no Washington statute sets it; not asserted.
- LH-HPRV-018 (75 percent participation): underwriting custom, not Washington law.

## Facts checked and deliberately not used
- WAC 284-30-360/-370/-380 claim timelines were replaced on 2026-10-18 (WSR 26-17-089: decisions now due 30 calendar days after notice of claim instead of 15 working days after proof of loss). Too new to be reflected reliably on a 4/1/2026 outline; no item.
- RCW 48.23.030(2) lapse notice to a third-party designee takes effect January 1, 2027 (2026 c 62); not yet law, no item.
- Guaranty association, bond and PIP dollar limits: not asked.
