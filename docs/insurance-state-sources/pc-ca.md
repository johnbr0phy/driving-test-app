### Outline weights (CDI 2025 objectives)
- Property (75): General Insurance 15%, Property Insurance 15%, Personal Lines 38% (homeowners, dwelling, valuation, earthquake/CEA, FAIR Plan, NFIP, wildfire mitigation, inland marine), Commercial 30% (CPP, BPP, BI, inland marine, crime, farm, ocean marine, BOP), Pet Insurance 2%.
- Casualty (75): General Insurance 15%, Casualty Insurance 15%, Personal Lines 35% (residential liability, watercraft, personal auto incl. Prop 103, Good Driver, CAARP, CLCA, UM, TNC and vehicle sharing, umbrella), Commercial 35% (CGL, professional, D&O, EPLI, commercial auto, umbrella, workers compensation incl. SCIF and ABC test, surety).

## Chosen form
California itself offers the combined Property and Casualty exam at 150 questions (the SPEC's two-exam rule gives the same: min(75 + 75, 150) = 150). Pass 60 percent.

## Facts resting on non-statute sources
- CAWC-007 (pure comparative negligence): California Supreme Court, Li v. Yellow Cab Co. (1975) 13 Cal.3d 804; case law, not fetched. The CDI objective asks which doctrine applies in California.
- CAOTH-003 (FAIR Plan with a DIC policy): CDI property objectives III.D.2.f (official, but descriptive rather than statutory).
- CAWC-006 names the WCIRB: the statute authorizes "a rating organization"; the WCIRB as that organization is from the CDI casualty objectives II.A.1.a.iii.

## Not used
- Auto financial responsibility limits (SPEC: never ask X/Y/Z); Vehicle Code 16028 was read but not used.
- Specific wildfire moratorium bulletins: only the standing statutory rule (675.1(b)) is tested, not any particular moratorium.
- Credit-score rating ban: in Prop 103 regulations not fetched, so no item.
- Rebating: Prop 103 changed California's P&C anti-rebate rule; not verified, so no item.
