### 2-20 content outline (effective January 1, 2026)
General knowledge (64%):
- I Types of property policies 14% (HO-2 to HO-8, DP-1 to DP-3, CPP, commercial property, BOP, builders risk, cyber first-party, inland marine, NFIP, earthquake, mobile homes, watercraft, farm, windstorm)
- II Property insurance terms and related concepts 9.5%
- III Property policy provisions and contract law 8%
- IV Types of casualty policies, bonds and related terms 15% (CGL, personal and business auto, workers comp/employers liability, crime, bonds, professional liability, umbrella, BOP)
- V Casualty insurance terms and related concepts 9.5%
- VI Casualty policy provisions 8%

Florida statutes, rules and regulations (36%):
- I Common to all lines 15% (CFO, Financial Services Commission, OFR, OIR, DFS; definitions; licensing, appointments, CE, record keeping, administrative actions; fiduciary duties; FIGA; unfair trade practices incl. sliding, twisting, churning, rebating)
- II Pertinent to general lines 15% (cancellation/nonrenewal for commercial, homeowners, personal auto and casualty; proof of loss; claim payment; premium finance; hurricane coverage, windstorm, definition, deductible; wind mitigation discounts; catastrophic ground cover collapse vs sinkhole; fungus; HO-6 loss assessment; loss settlement; dispute resolution; flood; admitted, reciprocal, surplus lines, Citizens, FAJUA, WCJUA; required auto coverages, PIP, UM/UIM, auto cancellation, motorcycle; comparative negligence; equipment breakdown; adjuster practices incl. s. 627.70131; company, independent and public adjusters)
- III Pertinent to health insurance 6% (contract provisions, limited policies, major medical, DI, Med Supp, HMO, PPO, LTC, renewal and advertising disclosure)

Florida licenses the 2-20 as one combined exam (property, casualty, surety, health and marine), so no two-exam combination rule applies.

## Chosen form
150 items (the 160 scored items, capped at 150), pass 70%.
- General knowledge 64% -> 96 national items: pcGeneral 24, pcPolicy 16, pcHomeowners 12, pcAuto 18, pcCommercial 9, pcLiability 12, pcOther 5. The national bank's categories do not line up one to one with the six outline sections, so these counts approximate them: property policy types about 21 (homeowners, dwelling, commercial property and BOP, flood), property and casualty terms about 28 (pcGeneral), property and casualty provisions about 24 (pcPolicy plus PAP conditions), casualty policy types about 23 (auto, CGL, workers comp, crime, bonds, professional).
- Florida common to all lines 15% -> 23 items (FLREG, pcGeneral).
- Florida general lines 15% -> 22 items (FLPOL 6 pcPolicy, FLHO 7 pcHomeowners, FLAUT 5 pcAuto, FLLIA 1 pcLiability, FLMKT 3 pcOther).
- Florida health 6% -> 9 items (FLHLT, filed under pcOther because the P&C family has no health category).
Pretest items are not modeled.

## 2022-2023 reforms and 2026 status
- Chapter 2023-15, Laws of Florida (HB 837), https://laws.flrules.org/files/Ch_2023-015.pdf: Section 10 repeals s. 626.9373 and Section 11 repeals s. 627.428 (one-way attorney fees); adds s. 768.81(6) (greater than 50% fault bar). The 2026 Statutes return "cannot be found" for 627.428 and 626.9373, consistent with the repeal. Bill page: https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2023/837. No item asks about attorney fees directly; the repeal is background only.
- Assignment of benefits ban: s. 627.7152(13) in the 2026 text (from the December 2022 special session).
- PIP: the 2026 Florida Statutes still contain the Florida Motor Vehicle No-Fault Law (627.730-627.7405). Repeal bills in the 2026 session (SB 522, HB 769) died in committee. Secondary confirmation: Insurance Journal, 2026-05-05, https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/05/05/868507.htm. The PIP items rest on the official statute text; the news article only confirms no later change.
