### Outline (125 scored)
General (ISO, NCCI and SAA forms): I Property types of policies 22; II Property terms and concepts 15; III Property provisions and contract law 13; IV Casualty types of policies, bonds and related terms 23; V Casualty terms and concepts 15; VI Casualty policy provisions 12 (= 100).
Georgia: VII Laws, rules and regulations pertinent to all lines 18 (Commissioner powers, examinations, hearings, penalties; domestic/foreign/alien, stock/mutual, certificate of authority, transacting; licensing of agents, counselors, subagents, adjusters, surplus lines brokers; unfair trade practices incl. rebating, defamation, discrimination, misrepresentation, controlled business, advertising, coercion, commingling, fiduciary duty, sharing commissions, fees, unfair claims practices, fraud); VIII P&C rules 3-4 (cancellation and nonrenewal Reg. 120-2-53 and 33-24-44 to 47; regulation of rates 33-9; binders 33-24-33; Georgia Insurers Insolvency Pool 33-36); IX Property only 0-1 (FAIR plan 33-33); X Casualty only 3-4 (auto: defensive driving 33-9-42, uninsured motorists 33-7-11, financial responsibility, Georgia Automobile Insurance Plan; workers' compensation law).

## Chosen form
125 items = 100 national picks + 25 Georgia items: VII 18, VIII 4, IX 0, X 3 (inside the outline's ranges, total 25). Pass 70%. Georgia offers the combined line as its own exam (12-GA-06), so no combination was needed. The FAIR plan (IX, 0-1 items) is covered by national PC-OTH-014 with a Georgia explanation.

National picks: the general outline has the same six areas and weights as the Texas Pearson VUE P&C general outline, so the Texas selection of 100 is reused unchanged: terms and contract law plus provisions served by pcGeneral 26 + pcPolicy 26; types of policies by pcHomeowners 14 + pcCommercial 11 + pcOther 5 + pcAuto 10 + pcLiability 8. Every pick was rechecked against Georgia law (ISO PAP and HO forms are used in Georgia with state amendatory endorsements); no pick conflicts.

## National exclusions and overrides
Not picked (would conflict or be unverified in Georgia; none were in the Texas list either):
- PC-AUTO-030: ISO PAP cancellation reasons after 60 days differ from O.C.G.A. 33-24-45(c).
- PC-AUTO-015: says UM pays bodily injury only; Georgia UM must also cover the insured's property damage (33-7-11(a)(1)).
- PC-AUTO-018: keys "varies by state"; Georgia stacking rules not verified.
- PC-POL-025: short-rate refund on insured cancellation is not stated in 33-24-44.1, and 33-24-44(g) limits penalties on unearned premium.
- PC-AUTO-002, PC-AUTO-003, PC-AUTO-022: ISO PAP dollar figures.

Overrides (wording only, keys unchanged):
- PC-OTH-014: says Georgia property insurers must belong to the FAIR Plan association (33-33-3) instead of calling it state-run.
- PC-GEN-024: stem moved from "a US state" to Georgia; explanation cites the 33-3-1 alien insurer definition.
- PC-AUTO-017: the national explanation said UIM pays when the at-fault limits are below the insured's UIM limits, which is Georgia's optional reduced-by coverage; the Georgia explanation says underinsured protection is part of UM (33-7-11(b)(1)(D)(ii)) and pays on top of the liability limits by default.

Checked and left alone: binder until issue or 90 days agrees with PC-GEN-028; insurer cancellation refunds pro rata (33-24-44(c)); NFIP, ISO HO, DP, CPP, BOP, CGL and WC items are form-based.

## Facts considered and not used
- Dollar limits (the 25/50/25 UM minimum in 33-7-11(a)(1)(A) and the higher S.B. 121 DUI limits) are not asked as X/Y/Z (house rule); the DUI item asks the three-year duration.
- Workers' compensation applies to employers with three or more regular employees (34-9-2(a)(2)), but the only official text available offline is the 2017 Title 34 volume and Title 34 acts since then were not indexed, so no state WC item was written.
- Homeowners cancellation reasons after 60 days (33-24-46(c)(2)) and the 45-day commercial notice (33-24-47(b)) are explained but not separately asked.
