### Which outline
The 2026 and 2027 outlines have the same scored counts and the same section weights. The 2027 life state outline adds two small life-only topics (G. Unfair practices, 5/236, 0-1; H. Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association, 0-1). Every state item here sits in topics present in both outlines, so the bank serves exams taken now and after January 1, 2027.

## Chosen form
Illinois licenses Property and Casualty as separate lines, each with its own general and state exam, scored separately (the Personal Lines exam is a narrower combined line and is not modeled). Following the California precedent the form combines both lines: 50 + 30 + 50 + 37 = 167 scored items, capped at 150 keeping the national:state ratio (100:67): 150 x 100/167 = 89.8, so 90 national and 60 state. Pass 70% (the scaled 70).

National picks (90), 45 per general outline scaled from 50:
- Types of policies: property 20 (homeowners forms and coverages 10, dwelling forms 3, CPP/BPP/causes of loss/business income/extra expense 5, NFIP 1, farm 1), casualty 21 (personal auto Parts A to D 8, CGL A/B/C, claims-made, products 5, workers comp 2, crime 2, surety 1, professional 1, umbrella 1, BOP 1).
- Terms and related concepts (II of both outlines, 30 scaled to 26 plus occurrence): pcGeneral 25 + PC-LIA-005.
- Policy provisions and contract law (III of both, 25 scaled to 23): pcPolicy 20 + PAP duties after loss, non-owned auto other insurance, TRIA.

State items (60), 60/67 of each section: common section 44 x 60/67 = 39 (Director 3, licensing 17, fiduciary 4, commissions and disclosure 3, marketing and claims practices 10, Guaranty Fund 2), P&C common 10 x 60/67 = 9 (cancellation and nonrenewal 4, rescission limit 5/154 1, premium finance 1, credit information 1, hate crimes 1, fraud reporting 1), property only 3 x 60/67 = 3 (FAIR Plan, location, mine subsidence), casualty only 10 x 60/67 = 9 (auto 8, workers comp pool 1).

## National overrides (text only)
- PC-AUTO-015: explanation now notes that Illinois makes uninsured motorist property damage coverage available under 215 ILCS 5/143a, in place of the generic "varies by state" line. Key unchanged.
- PC-GEN-017: stem now says "innocent misrepresentation" and the explanation cites 5/154, because in Illinois a misrepresentation made with actual intent to deceive also defeats a policy, so "only when material" was not the whole Illinois rule. Key unchanged.

## National exclusions and checks
- PC-OTH-014 (FAIR plans, general) not picked: the Illinois FAIR Plan is tested as a state item (PCIL-ILPRP-001).
- PC-AUTO-018 (UM stacking "varies") not picked: Illinois 5/143a-2(5) bars increasing limits because of multiple vehicles, so the generic item is not the Illinois answer.
- PC-AUTO-030 (ISO PAP cancellation after 60 days for three reasons) not picked: Illinois 5/143.19 lists a longer set of permitted reasons, tested as PCIL-ILPC-003.
- Dollar-limit items (HO special limits, NFIP maximums, supplementary payment amounts) avoided where possible.
- Checked and kept: ISO HO and DP forms, PAP Parts A to D (framed as ISO forms), CGL, workers comp exclusive remedy, pro rata return on insurer cancellation, NFIP administration, TRIA, UIM limits comparison (matches 5/143a-2(4)).

## Facts deliberately not used
- Auto liability minimums (625 ILCS 5/7-203, 25/50/20 on the FindLaw copy): X/Y/Z limits are off-limits, and ilga.gov confirms 25/50/20 is unchanged (P.A. 102-982).
- Child restraint replacement coverage (5/143.32): the FindLaw page marks the section as repealed or moved, so no item.
- Mine subsidence waiver rules (5/805.1): changed by P.A. 104-519 on January 1, 2027 (renewal notice after a waiver, no deductible), so the item asks only the Fund's role.
- Personal lines nonrenewal notice days (5/143.17(a)): 30 days now, 60 days from January 1, 2027 (P.A. 104-534), so PCIL-ILPC-004 asks the stable 143.17(c) rule instead.
- Assigned risk eligibility: 625 ILCS 5/7-501's "rejected by 3 carriers" trigger applies only if carriers fail to subscribe to a plan; the Illinois Automobile Insurance Plan's own eligibility rule (an attestation of failing to obtain coverage) is in the AIPSO plan manual, which could not be read, so PCIL-ILCAS-007 asks how 7-501 apportions assignments.
- Oral premium estimate (5/155.28) applies only to producers in municipalities over 500,000; skipped as too narrow.
