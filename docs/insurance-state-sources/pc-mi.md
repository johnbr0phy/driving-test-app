### 16-81 outline (150 items)
Insurance Regulation 15 (appointments, termination, duties, commissions, licensing, CE, discipline, unfair and prohibited practices, fraud, privacy); General Insurance 16; P&C Basics 30 (principles, policy structure, common provisions, plus Michigan laws: P&C guaranty association 500.7901-.7949, mandatory fire policy provisions and appraisal 500.2833, cancellation and nonrenewal 500.2123 and 500.3020, TRIA, termination of authority 500.1209, Essential Insurance Act 500.2101-.2131); Dwelling 4; Homeowners 22; Automobile 22 (Michigan financial responsibility, MAIPF 500.3301, no-fault 500.3101 with PIP 500.3107/.3114, PPI 500.3121, residual liability 500.3131, cancellation grounds 500.3220 and notice 500.3204/.3224, rental vehicles, crash parts; PAP; commercial auto with Michigan PIP and PPI endorsements); CPP 18; BOP 9; Workers' Compensation 8 (Michigan WDCA ch. 418: exclusive remedy 418.131, covered employers 418.115, injuries 418.301, benefits 418.301-.351, second injury fund 418.521, self-insurance 418.611; federal acts; policy); Other Coverages 6 (umbrella, specialty liability, surety, surplus lines, NFIP, other policies, alternative funding, cyber).

## Chosen form
150 items, the real scored count, pass 74%. Michigan tests property and casualty together in the 16-81 exam, so the combined-form rule does not apply. Michigan items follow the outline topics that cite Michigan law; national items fill the general topics.

| pcCommercial | CPP property, crime and inland marine (about 10) + BOP 9 | 19 | 0 | 19 |

| pcOther | Other Coverages (flood, surety, TRIA, surplus lines) | 3 | 1 | 4 |

Auto carries the most Michigan items because the 16-81 auto section is mostly Michigan no-fault law as reformed by 2019 PA 21 and 22 (PIP coverage choices from July 2, 2020, Medicare opt-out, default 250/500 residual bodily injury limits with a signed form for lower limits, $3,000 mini-tort). National auto picks are limited to ISO PAP structure that Michigan policies share (Part A liability, CSL, insureds, UM/UIM definitions, collision and other than collision, non-owned autos, territory).

## National overrides (text only, key unchanged)
- PC-POL-024: explanation says Michigan also uses pro rata when the insured cancels a casualty or fire policy (MCL 500.3020(1)(a), 500.2833(1)(h)); the national text said short rate applies to insured cancellations.

## National items left out because of Michigan law
- PC-POL-025 (insured cancellation refunded short rate): conflicts with MCL 500.3020(1)(a) and 500.2833(1)(h), which require pro rata (subject to a minimum earned premium; short rate only for motorcycles, watercraft, ORVs and snowmobiles).
- PC-AUTO-030 (ISO PAP 60-day cancellation rule): Michigan uses 55 days and narrower grounds (MCL 500.3220).
- PC-AUTO-002/-003/-004/-005/-012/-013 (supplementary payment dollar figures, split-limit arithmetic, Part B medical payments): Michigan PIP replaces Part B in practice and split-limit examples invite confusion with Michigan's 2019 limits.
- PC-LIA-019/-020 (exclusive remedy, no-fault benefits): replaced by Michigan WDCA items.
- PC-OTH-014 (FAIR plans) and PC-OTH-016 (twisting): kept out for slots; Michigan's FAIR plan is the Michigan Basic Property Insurance Association (MCL 500.2901+), not checked here.

## Facts deliberately not used
- PIP medical limits other than the default (asked only as "unlimited" vs the statutory levels), replacement-services $20 a day, work loss monthly maximum (adjusted every October 1), PPI $1,000,000 cap (in an explanation only), guaranty association caps and net worth test: dollar figures, several indexed.
- Michigan Catastrophic Claims Association assessment: not in the 16-81 outline.
- Michigan homeowners Special Provisions endorsement and Michigan PAP endorsements: forms text not available from an official source, so no item.
- Rental vehicle coverage (500.3009, 500.3105(2)) and aftermarket crash parts (MCL 257.1361-.1364): listed in the outline, not read, no item.
