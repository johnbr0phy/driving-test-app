### Property Agent outline (55; eff. March 2, 2026)
I Terms and concepts 7; II Personal insurance coverages 25 (standard fire policy 4, DP-1/2/3 4, homeowners on North Carolina Rate Bureau forms adapted from ISO HO-2011 12, mobile home 1, flood 3, watercraft hull 1); III Commercial coverages 10 (BPP, causes of loss, builders risk, CPP, BOP, business income, extra expense, equipment breakdown, farm, inland marine); IV NC statutes and regulations pertinent to property 13 (Art. 1, 2, 3, 33, 39, 28, 63, G.S. 14-100, CE, Rate Bureau Art. 36, Art. 43 and 44 fire policies, Art. 45 beach area essential property, Art. 46 FAIR plan, Art. 48 guaranty association, consumer rules 11 NCAC 4 .0120-.0122, .0423, .0429).

### Casualty Agent outline (55; eff. March 2, 2026)
I Terms and concepts 7; II Personal coverages 18 (PAP on the North Carolina Rate Bureau form with NC 03 02, 03 03, 03 06, 03 15 endorsements, personal umbrella, watercraft); III Commercial coverages 15 (CGL, business auto, garage, workers' compensation and employers liability, surety and fidelity, commercial umbrella, crime, CPP, BOP); IV NC statutes and regulations pertinent to casualty 15 (common articles plus Art. 37 Motor Vehicle Reinsurance Facility, G.S. 20-279.21 and 11 NCAC 4 .0415, G.S. 20-310 cancellation, Chapter 97 workers' compensation, Art. 48).

Note: G.S. 20-310, cited in the outline for auto cancellation, has been repealed (S.L. 1993 (Reg. Sess. 1994), c. 761, s. 29); auto termination now sits in G.S. 58-36-85 and 58-37-50, which is what the items use.

## Chosen form
North Carolina offers property and casualty only as two separate 55-item exams. The form models a candidate taking both, as California's combined form does: **110 items = Property 55 + Casualty 55**, keeping each exam's general:state ratio (82 general + 28 North Carolina). Under the 150 cap. Pass 70%.

National picks by outline area (82):
- Terms and concepts (7 + 7 = 14) and the policy-structure items: pcGeneral 12 + pcPolicy 9 (declarations, insuring agreement, conditions, endorsements, ACV, coinsurance, subrogation, appraisal, mortgage clause).
- Property personal (25): pcHomeowners 15 (DP-1/2/3, HO-3, HO-4, HO-5, HO-6, HO-8, Coverage A, D, F, flood exclusion) + pcOther flood 4 (NFIP, waiting period, contents ACV, WYO) + hull 1. Standard fire policy and loss settlement are carried by pcPolicy.
- Property commercial (10) and casualty crime/package: pcCommercial 13.
- Casualty personal (18): pcAuto 13 (Parts A to D, split and single limits, insureds, temporary substitute, UM/UIM definitions, collision and OTC, non-owned excess, extended non-owned endorsement) + umbrella in pcLiability.
- Casualty commercial (15): pcLiability 12 (CGL A/B/C, occurrence and claims-made, products-completed operations, exclusions, workers' compensation Part One, exclusive remedy, no-fault, umbrella) + pcOther surety 3.

North Carolina items (28): common topics 12 (pcGeneral), fire policy and rate rules 4 (pcPolicy), Rate Bureau, coastal pool, FAIR plan and flood warning 4 (pcHomeowners), auto 4 (pcAuto), workers' compensation 3 (pcLiability), guaranty association 1 (pcOther).

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- PC-POL-012: explanation fixes a garbled sentence and adds that a North Carolina coinsurance clause needs "coinsurance contract" printed on the policy (G.S. 58-3-15).
- PC-AUTO-016: explanation cites G.S. 20-279.21(b)(3), which names each uninsured case (no insurance, limits below the minimum, insurer denies coverage or is insolvent).
- PC-AUTO-017 (added in review): the national explanation compared the at-fault limits with the insured's UIM limits; North Carolina's current definition of an underinsured highway vehicle in G.S. 20-279.21(b)(4) compares the at-fault limits with the injured person's damages, so the explanation now says that. Key unchanged.

## National exclusions
- PC-AUTO-030 (ISO PAP cancellation rule after 60 days): North Carolina limits auto terminations to the reasons in G.S. 58-36-85(b) and 58-37-50, with 15/60-day notice; tested by PCNC-NCAUT-002 instead.
- PC-AUTO-018 (UM stacking "varies by state"): North Carolina law bars combining UM limits for vehicles on one policy but allows combining across policies (G.S. 20-279.21(b)(3)), so the national key is not right for this state.
- PC-AUTO-015 (UM pays bodily injury): North Carolina UM also must include property damage (G.S. 20-279.21(b)(3)); the national key is incomplete here.
- PC-AUTO-002, -003, -012, -014, -022, -035 and the HO dollar sublimit items (PC-HO-011 to -014, -016, -029 to -032), PC-HO-025 (four families): figures from the ISO forms that could not be checked against the North Carolina Rate Bureau PAP and HO forms, which the outline names as the tested forms.
- PC-POL-028 (valued policy): North Carolina pays no more than actual cash value on a total fire loss (G.S. 58-43-10) and has no valued policy law; left out to avoid confusion.
- PC-OTH-003 (NFIP dollar limit) and PC-LIA-017 (employers liability dollar limits): dollar amounts are not asked.
- PC-OTH-014 (FAIR plans) and PC-OTH-016 (twisting): replaced by North Carolina-specific items (FAIR plan, coastal pool, licensing).
- Remaining items not selected for weighting reasons only.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Auto minimum limits (50/100/50), the $100 UM property damage exclusion, guaranty association caps, and the $50 personal lines referral cap: verified, but dollar amounts are not asked.
- Safe Driver Incentive Plan points (G.S. 58-36-65, 58-36-75, 11 NCAC 4 .0415): point values are not asked; the inexperienced operator rules also changed in 2025.
- UIM calculation (excess over the at-fault driver's limits, G.S. 20-279.21(b)(4) as amended): recently changed, with effective-date amendments in S.L. 2025-45, so not tested.
