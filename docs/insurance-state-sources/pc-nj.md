### Property outline (79)
General (50): 1.0 Types of policies 25 (DP and HO forms, mobile homes, commercial property and causes of loss, business income, extra expense, CPP, equipment breakdown, BOP, inland marine, flood, earthquake); 2.0 Insurance terms and related concepts 14; 3.0 Policy provisions and contract law 11.

### Casualty outline (91)

## Chosen form
New Jersey has no combined P&C exam, so the form combines the two real exams, as the California variant does: 79 + 91 = 170 scored items, over the 150 cap. Capped at 150 keeping the general : New Jersey ratio (100 : 70): 88 general-outline slots + 62 New Jersey-outline slots. Pass 70% (the real pass mark on both exams).

The 62 New Jersey slots follow the combined New Jersey weights scaled by 62/70: laws common to all lines 44 (the common section appears on both real exams), property-only 4, casualty-only 14. Within the common 44: jurisdiction 7, definitions 7, licensing 14, trade practices and licensee duties 11, guaranty associations 2, fraud 3. Four common slots use textbook items from the national bank (PC-GEN-024 alien insurer, reworded for New Jersey; PC-GEN-023 reinsurance; PC-GEN-027 agent represents the insurer; PC-OTH-016 twisting), so the bank is 92 national + 58 New Jersey items.

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- PC-GEN-024: stem names New Jersey instead of "a US state"; explanation cites N.J.S.A. 17B:17-7.
- PC-AUTO-015: explanation adds that New Jersey policies must include UM property damage (not for hit-and-run vehicles) with a $500 exclusion (N.J.S.A. 17:28-1.1), while many states do not require it, so option C ("in every state") stays wrong; the national line said property damage UM "varies by state".

## National exclusions (conflict or risk of conflict with New Jersey law)
- PC-AUTO-018 (stacking "varies by state"): New Jersey bars stacking (17:28-1.1(c)); tested by PCNJ-NJAUT-006 instead.
- PC-AUTO-030 (ISO PAP cancellation after 60 days): New Jersey's statute governs auto cancellation (17:29C-7, -8); tested by PCNJ-NJAUT-008 and -009.
- PC-AUTO-005 (25/50/25 split limits): reads like a New Jersey minimum, which is 35/70/25 for policies issued or renewed from January 1, 2026 (39:6A-3); left out so no stale minimum appears.
- PC-AUTO-012 and PC-AUTO-013 (Part B medical payments): New Jersey auto injury benefits run through PIP (39:6A-4), not PAP medical payments.
- PC-AUTO-007 (out-of-state limits): fine, but not needed.

## Checked and left alone
ISO homeowners and dwelling form items are framed as ISO forms and do not conflict with New Jersey law. PC-POL-024 and 025 (pro rata and short rate refunds) are stated as typical practice. PC-LIA-019 and 020 (exclusive remedy, no-fault benefits) match N.J.S.A. 34:15-8 and 34:15-7. PC-OTH-014 (FAIR plans) matches 17:37A-8.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Auto liability minimums (35/70/25 from 2026) and the basic policy's $5,000 property damage and optional $10,000 bodily injury limits: X/Y/Z and limit trivia, not asked. The one dollar item asked in auto is the $250,000 default PIP medical limit (39:6A-4.3(e)), which the outline lists (PIP benefits) and which is fixed in statute.
- PAIP, CAIP, the UEZ program and commercial deregulation (17:29AA): listed on the outline, but the current PAIP and CAIP rules (N.J.A.C. 11:3) were not verified, so no item was written.
- Workers compensation maximum weekly benefit: indexed yearly to the statewide average weekly wage (34:15-12(a)), so only the 70 percent rate is asked.
