### 17-56 outline (150 items; percent x 150)
Insurance Regulation 9; General Insurance 9; Property and Casualty Basics 13; Dwelling (2014) 6; Homeowners (2011) 14; Auto 11 (NY financial responsibility, NYAIP, no-fault 5101-5108, MVAIC, UM/SUM 3420(f), Reg 79 inspection, 3425 cancellation, 2610, PAP, commercial auto); Commercial Package Policy 11; Businessowners 8; Workers' Compensation 8 (NY WCL, NYSIF, DBL, Paid Family Leave); Other Coverages and Options 7 (umbrella, specialty liability, excess lines Reg 41, surety, NFIP, NYPIUA FAIR plan, C-MAP); Accident and Health 4 (sums to 100).

National picks: general 17 (risk, hazards, law of large numbers, risk techniques, indemnity, contract characteristics, representations, estoppel, adverse selection, reinsurance, alien insurer, apparent authority); policy basics 16 (policy parts, endorsements, open perils, proximate cause, ACV, coinsurance, other insurance, subrogation, appraisal, mortgage clause, assignment); dwelling 6 and homeowners 22 (ISO DP and HO forms, which the outline names); auto 5 (PAP Part A, split and single limits, Part D); commercial 24 (CPP conditions, BPP, causes of loss, business income, BOP, inland marine, crime); liability 12 (CGL coverages and triggers, umbrella, professional and D&O, WC Parts One and Two, exclusive remedy, no-fault benefits); other 3 (NFIP, surety, TRIA). The real exam's A&H section (4%) has no national P&C items, so all six are New York items filed under pcOther.

## Law sources
Statutes read on the official Open Legislation pages, https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/<LAW>/<section> (ISC Insurance Law, FIS Financial Services Law, WKC Workers' Compensation Law), each page's "most recent revision" date checked; curl gets a Cloudflare challenge there, so pages were read with a browser-style fetch, never from a challenge page. Regulations read on Cornell LII's mirror of 11 NYCRR with NYS Register history notes checked.

Recent changes checked: Ins. Law 3425 revised 2026-07-03 (the 45-to-60-day notice, the three-year homeowners required period and the auto cancellation grounds used here are unchanged); Ins. Law 5104 revised 2026-05-29 (adds a cap for certain at-fault drivers; the serious injury threshold used here stands); Ins. Law 3420(g) supplemental spousal liability changes on July 31, 2027, so no SSL item was written; Reg 79 (11 NYCRR 67) amended June 18, 2024, and the 14-day deferral in 67.4 reflects that text; Ins. Law 5414 (C-MAP) revised 2025-02-21 (voluntary, at least one mile from shore); Ins. Law 2118 has a version effective July 1, 2029 (affidavit filing moves to the superintendent), not used.

## National overrides (text only)
- PC-LIA-019: explanation's "exceptions vary by state" now states the New York rule (WCL 11: suit only if the employer failed to secure compensation).

## National exclusions (conflict with or superseded by New York law)
- PC-AUTO-012, PC-AUTO-013 (Part B medical payments): New York auto injury benefits run through mandatory no-fault PIP.
- PC-AUTO-018 (UM stacking "varies"): New York SUM is nonstacking; not needed.
- PC-AUTO-030 (ISO PAP cancellation after 60 days): replaced by the New York 3425 item, whose grounds differ.
- PC-AUTO-035 (TNC exclusion): New York regulates TNC coverage under VTL Article 44-B; not modeled.
- PC-LIA-017 (employers liability 100/500/100 standard limits): the New York WC policy uses a New York endorsement; not asserted.
- PC-POL-024, PC-POL-025 (pro rata and short rate refunds): left out rather than tie them to 3425/3426 return-premium rules.
- PC-OTH-014 (FAIR plans): replaced by the NYPIUA item.

## Checked and left alone
ISO HO and DP items are framed as ISO forms, which the outline lists by edition; HO vacancy 60 days matches the standard fire policy's 60 consecutive days. Alien insurer (PC-GEN-024) matches Ins. Law 107(a). Assignment needs insurer consent (PC-POL-027), consistent with the standard fire policy.

## Facts deliberately not used
- VTL 333 liability minimums and the DBL $170 weekly cap: dollar figures, not asked as X/Y/Z.
- Supplemental spousal liability (3420(g)): its opt-out rule changes July 31, 2027.
- No-fault 5104(d) cap added 2026: too new and narrow.
