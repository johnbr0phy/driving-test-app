### Series 16-06 outline (effective 3/1/2026), weights of 150 items
1 Insurance regulation 20% (licensing, discipline, state and producer regulation, unfair practices, agency termination, privacy, fraud, FCRA, 1033, GLBA, TRIA, Motor Carrier Act); 2 General insurance concepts 8%; 3 P&C basics 14% (insurable interest, damages, liability, underwriting, rating, loss valuation, negligence, policy structure and conditions, coinsurance, Pennsylvania guaranty association, standard fire policy, cancellation laws, death of named insured, binders); 4 Dwelling policy 4%; 5 Homeowners 7%; 6 Personal auto 10% (MVFRL, tort election, first-party benefits, assigned risk, UM/UIM); 7 Commercial auto 5%; 8 Commercial property 7%; 9 CGL 7%; 10 Commercial crime 2%; 14 Businessowners 3%; 11 Workers' compensation 5% (Pennsylvania Workers' Compensation Act); 12 Other P&C 8% (specialty liability, surety, umbrella, farm, mobile home, DIC, flood, FAIR Plan, risk retention groups, crop, ocean marine, aircraft, watercraft, RVs, pet insurance).

## Chosen form
150 items = the real scored length. Pass 70%. 103 national items carry the general concepts and ISO forms; 47 Pennsylvania items sit in the areas whose outline lines cite Pennsylvania law (licensing and practices, the standard fire policy and binders, residential, commercial and auto cancellation, the MVFRL, the Workers' Compensation Act, the FAIR Plan, pet insurance).

National picks by outline area: regulation and general concepts (risk, hazards, contracts, agency, underwriting, rates, insurer types, binders) 25; policy structure, conditions, valuation, coinsurance, other insurance 11; dwelling and homeowners forms 13; personal auto 13; commercial property, package, BOP, inland marine and crime 18; CGL and workers' compensation policy 13; flood, surety, ocean marine, FAIR plan, TRIA, twisting 10. Commercial auto has no dedicated national item in the family bank; its liability and physical damage concepts are carried by the PAP and CGL items.

## National overrides (text only, keys unchanged)
- PC-GEN-024: stem names Pennsylvania instead of "a US state".
- PC-OTH-014: explanation corrected for Pennsylvania, whose FAIR Plan is run by the insurers' Industry Placement Facility under the Fair Plan Act and supervised by the commissioner, not a state-run pool.

## National items left out for Pennsylvania reasons
- PC-AUTO-012 (Part B medical payments): Pennsylvania auto policies carry first-party medical benefits under 75 Pa.C.S. 1711; tested by PCPA-PAAUT-003.
- PC-AUTO-018 (stacking varies by state): Pennsylvania stacks unless waived in writing (75 Pa.C.S. 1738); tested by PCPA-PAAUT-005.
- PC-AUTO-030 (ISO cancellation after 60 days): matches Pennsylvania Article XX but is replaced by the Pennsylvania items.
- PC-POL-024 and PC-POL-025 (pro rata and short rate refunds): Pennsylvania commercial cancellations add statutory refund deadlines (Act 86 section 4); tested by PCPA-PAPOL-005.

## Checked and left alone
PC-GEN-028 (binder): Pennsylvania limits binders to 30 days (ICL 506(d)); the national item's wording does not conflict. PC-HO-019 (60-day vacancy) matches the standard fire policy's 60-day vacancy suspension. PC-AUTO-015 says property damage UM varies by state; Pennsylvania's UM is bodily injury only (75 Pa.C.S. 1731(b)). PC-LIA-019 (exclusive remedy) matches WCA 303(a).

## Facts deliberately not used
- Financial responsibility minimums (15/30/5 in 75 Pa.C.S. 1702) and the $5,000 first-party medical amount: X/Y/Z and dollar limits are not asked.
- Guaranty association dollar caps ($300,000 per claimant, $10,000 unearned premium).
- Penalty amounts (civil penalties up to $5,000 per violation).
- The 31 Pa. Code 113.81 list of commercial risk types and the Assigned Risk Plan details (75 Pa.C.S. 1741-1744): not needed for the chosen items.
