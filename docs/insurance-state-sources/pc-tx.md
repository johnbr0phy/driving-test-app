### General knowledge outline (100, eff. Sept 1, 2026)
I Types of policies 22 (HO-2..HO-8, DP-1..3, CPP/commercial property/BOP/builders risk/cyber first party, inland marine, NFIP, earthquake, mobile homes, watercraft, farm, windstorm); II Insurance terms and related concepts 15; III Policy provisions and contract law 13; IV Types of policies, bonds and related terms 23 (CGL, personal and business auto, workers comp and employers liability, crime, bonds, professional liability, umbrella, BOP); V Insurance terms and related concepts 15; VI Policy provisions 12.

### Texas state outline (30, eff. Sept 1, 2026)
I Texas statutes and rules common to P&C 18 (commissioner, insurance definitions incl. Texas Lloyds, licensing, marketing practices / unfair trade practices, agent duties); II Texas statutes and rules pertinent to P&C 12 (P&C definitions, surplus lines, rates and forms, homeowners declination/cancellation/nonrenewal, FAIR Plan, TWIA, loss settlement, liquidated demand, auto coverage, UM/UIM, PIP, financial responsibility, auto cancellation/nonrenewal, TAIPA, TNC, workers' comp definitions and benefits, P&C guaranty association, medical liability JUA).

## Chosen form
130 items = 100 national picks + 30 Texas items, pass 70%.

National picks map to the general outline: terms and contract law (II, V and part of III: 30) plus provisions (III, VI: 25) are served by pcGeneral 26 + pcPolicy 26 = 52; types of policies (I and IV: 45) by pcHomeowners 14 + pcCommercial 11 + pcOther 5 + pcAuto 10 + pcLiability 8 = 48. Texas items follow the state outline exactly: 18 under section I (all pcGeneral) and 12 under section II (pcHomeowners 4, pcAuto 4, pcLiability 2, pcOther 2).

Final counts by category (national + state): pcGeneral 26 + 18 = 44; pcPolicy 26 + 0 = 26; pcHomeowners 14 + 4 = 18; pcAuto 10 + 4 = 14; pcCommercial 11 + 0 = 11; pcLiability 8 + 2 = 10; pcOther 5 + 2 = 7.

## TDI pages (official, used for context and two explanation details)
- Home insurance guide: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/pubs/consumer/cb025.html (nonrenewal notice was 30 days before 2024, 60 days from 2024; unearned premium refunded within 15 days with a straight-proportion example; FAIR Plan two declinations; TWIA one-year claim filing)
- What is windstorm insurance: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/what-is-windstorm-insurance.html (TWIA eligibility: service area, a declination, inspection certificate)
- Workers' comp employer resources: https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wc/employer/index.html ("private employers can choose to carry workers' compensation insurance coverage, but it is not required in most cases")
- Texas Homeowners Policies (TDI report, April 12, 2018): https://www.tdi.texas.gov/reports/documents/texas-homeowners-policies-04122018.pdf (HO-A/HO-A+/HO-B/HO-C mapped to ISO HO 1/2/3/5; insurers file their own forms since 2003)

On the Texas homeowners forms (HO-A, HO-B, HO-C): no item was written. The 2026 outline tests the ISO forms (HO-2 to HO-8) in the general portion and does not list the Texas promulgated forms in the state portion, and the only official TDI description found is the 2018 report above, which is dated. The national ISO HO items are kept as written because they are framed as ISO forms.

Auto financial responsibility minimums (30/60/25, Transportation Code 601.072) were verified but, per house rules, not asked as X/Y/Z.
