### Content outline (135 scored)
1 Insurance regulation 20; 2 General insurance 12; 3 P&C basics 18 (3.4 Virginia laws: guaranty association, flood and earthquake notices, dwelling and commercial cancellation, ordinance or law and sewer backup offers, binders); 4 Dwelling policy 7; 5 Homeowners 18 (HO-3, 4, 5, 6; 38.2-2119(B)); 6 Auto 21 (6.1 Virginia laws: financial responsibility, VAIP, UM/UIM, cancellation, rental reimbursement, aftermarket parts, driver exclusions); 7 Commercial package 16; 8 BOP 7; 9 Workers compensation 9 (65.2-101, 65.2-300; assigned risk 65.2-820); 10 Other coverages 7 (umbrella, professional, surplus lines licensing, bonds, NFIP, watercraft UM, Virginia Property Insurance Association).

As with life and health, the outline has no separate state section; Virginia law is cited inside the sections.

## Chosen form
135 items, the real scored count, pass 70%. Prometric publishes no general/state split. Counting the outline lines that cite Virginia law (about 17 of the 20 regulation items plus parts of sections 3.4, 5.8, 6.1, 9 and 10) gives roughly 35, so the form is 100 national picks + 35 Virginia items. Pretest items are not modeled.

National picks by outline area: general insurance and P&C basics 31 (pcGeneral 18, pcPolicy 13, including coinsurance, other insurance, subrogation, mortgage clause, pro rata refund); dwelling and homeowners 22; personal auto 14; commercial property, crime, inland marine and BOP 17; CGL, workers compensation and umbrella 13; NFIP and surety 3.

Virginia items: regulation 16 (licensing 10, practices 6), Virginia policy laws 3 (binder, flood notice, guaranty association), homeowners 3, auto 7, workers compensation and commercial liability notice 4, residual market and surplus lines 2.

## National overrides (text only)
- PC-POL-012: explanation had a garbled phrase ("Did over should"); now reads "Carried divided by required". Key unchanged.
- PC-POL-024: explanation adds that the short-rate rule on insured cancellation is the Virginia standard fire policy rule (§ 38.2-2105, which also gives pro rata on insurer cancellation).

## National exclusions
- PC-AUTO-015: says UM pays bodily injury only; Virginia UM also covers property damage (§ 38.2-2206).
- PC-AUTO-030: ISO PAP cancellation reasons include misrepresentation; § 38.2-2212(D) allows midterm cancellation after 60 days only for license suspension, nonpayment or a move out of state.
- PC-AUTO-016: lists insurer insolvency in the uninsured vehicle definition; Virginia's § 38.2-2206(B) definition is worded differently.
- PC-AUTO-018: stacking keys "varies by state"; a Virginia form should give the Virginia answer, and § 38.2-2206(B) priority rules are too detailed for one item.
- PC-AUTO-002, -003, -014, -022: ISO PAP dollar figures and the 14-day window; Virginia uses its own amendatory and transportation expense endorsements (PP 01 99, PP 13 52), which were not checked.
- PC-POL-025: overlaps PC-POL-024.
- PC-GEN-028 and PC-OTH-014: binder and FAIR plan are tested by the Virginia items.
- PC-OTH-016: twisting, left out to make room for Virginia regulation items.
- PC-HO-024: HO-8 is not on the Virginia outline.

## Facts deliberately not used
- Financial responsibility limits (§ 46.2-472), UM property damage minimum and the $200 unknown-driver exclusion (§ 38.2-2206(A)), surplus lines broker bond: dollar figures are off-limits.
- § 38.2-2114(K) roof age rule: not effective until January 1, 2027 and not on the outline.
- Driver exclusions (§ 38.2-2204): the current text found covers only umbrella and excess exclusions, so no item.
