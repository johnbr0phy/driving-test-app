# Washington Property and Casualty Producer exam: sources (all accessed 2026-10-18)

Washington licenses property and casualty as separate lines (RCW 48.17.170(1)(c), (d)); PSI offers each alone and as one combined "Property and Casualty Producer Combo" exam. This form models the combo.

### Outline weights (150 items)
Federal 2; Washington laws, rules and regulations 45; general insurance concepts 17; P&C basics 21; dwelling policy 2; homeowners 14; personal auto 14; commercial auto 5; commercial property (incl. inland marine, crime, farm) 10; CGL 10; businessowners 6; other (specialty liability, surety, umbrella, mobile home, earthquake, flood, watercraft) 4. There is no workers' compensation section: Washington workers' compensation is a state fund (Title 51 RCW).

The Washington section lists, without sub-weights: commissioner, terms, licensing (producer, temporary, nonresident, exemptions, appointments, penalties, renewal, CE, late renewal, reporting, DRLP), marketing practices and P&C trade practices, compensation disclosure, rebating, twisting, misrepresentation, receipts, advertising, public interest, policy delivery, premium accountability and separate accounts, reply to the commissioner, display of license, surplus lines, cancellation and nonrenewal (commercial and personal, credit scoring adverse action), homeowners cancellation and nonrenewal and actual reason, applications and binders, records, place of business, address changes, producers' contracts, the P&C guaranty association; property only (overinsurance, FAIR plan); casualty only (total loss, UM/UIM, auto liability and financial responsibility, auto cancellation, assigned risk, PIP).

## Chosen form
150 items = 105 national picks + 45 Washington items, pass 70%. No cap needed.

National picks by outline area: federal 2 (PC-OTH-001, -002, NFIP); general concepts 17 (risk, perils, hazards, risk handling, indemnity, contract law, representations, concealment, estoppel, alien insurer, express and apparent authority); basics 21 (insurable interest, underwriting, rates, policy structure, open perils, indirect loss, ACV, coinsurance, other insurance, deductibles, subrogation, mortgage clause, pro rata cancellation, liberalization, valued policy, CSL); dwelling 2; homeowners 14; personal auto 14 plus 5 more PAP items standing in for commercial auto, since the national bank has no business auto items; commercial property 10 (incl. farm, crime, inland marine); CGL 10; businessowners 4 plus 2 stand-ins (common policy conditions first named insured, claims-made tail); other 4 (surety principal and obligee, umbrella, D&O).

## National overrides (text only)
- PC-POL-024: explanation states Washington's pro rata refund and 45-day payment (RCW 48.18.290(4)) and drops the national sentence that short rate applies when the insured cancels, which is wrong for Washington personal lines.
- PC-AUTO-016: explanation adds that Washington folds uninsured, hit-and-run and insolvent-insurer vehicles into underinsured motorist coverage (RCW 48.22.030(1), 48.22.040).

## National exclusions
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| PC-POL-025 | Keys short rate with a penalty when the insured cancels; RCW 48.18.300(2) requires a pro rata refund of at least 90 to 97 percent for homeowners, dwelling fire and private auto. |
| PC-AUTO-015 | Says UM pays bodily injury only; Washington's UIM also covers property damage (RCW 48.22.030(2)). |
| PC-AUTO-017 | Distractor "the at-fault driver has no insurance at all" is also correct in Washington, where an underinsured vehicle includes one with no liability insurance (RCW 48.22.030(1)). PC-AUTO-025 was used instead. |
| PC-AUTO-018 | Keys stacking as "varies by state"; Washington allows anti-stacking limits (RCW 48.22.030(5), (6)). |
| PC-AUTO-030 | ISO PAP reasons after 60 days include misrepresentation; RCW 48.18.291(2) allows only nonpayment or license suspension or revocation. |
| PC-LIA-015 to PC-LIA-020 | Workers' compensation: Washington is a monopolistic state fund state and the outline has no workers' compensation section. |

## Facts checked and deliberately not used
- WAC 284-30-360/-370/-380 claim timelines changed on 2026-10-18 (WSR 26-17-089, 30 calendar days from notice of claim); too new for a 4/1/2026 outline, no item.
- Auto financial responsibility minimums (RCW 46.29.090) and PIP benefit amounts (RCW 48.22.095, .100) were verified but, per house rules, no X/Y/Z or dollar-limit item.
- PCWA-WAHOM-003 keeps "assign a higher rating tier" as a permitted use of credit: RCW 48.18.545(1)(a)(ii)(B) treats tier placement as an adverse action that needs the notice, not a banned use, and current WAC 284-24-140 regulates insurers that use insurance scores to assign rating tiers (RCW 48.19.035).
