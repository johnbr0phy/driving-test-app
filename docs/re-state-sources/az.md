### Arizona state outline (salesperson, effective January 1, 2026), 60 items
I Regulatory framework 5; II Consumer protection laws (Consumer Fraud Act, statute of frauds, Arizona fair housing, homestead) 5; III Advertising 5; IV Arizona agency 6; V Licensee duties and obligations (disclosure, seller disclosures, conflicts, compensation, timing, cross-sale controversies) 6; VI Competencies and duties (new homes, property management, timeshare, vacant land, commercial) 6; VII Reasonable skill and care (property taxes and assessments, utility and environmental) 6; VIII Contracts 8; IX Escrow, title insurance, lending 5; X Ownership and encumbrances 5; XI Foreclosure, short sale, deed in lieu (anti-deficiency, foreclosure process) 3.

### National outline (salesperson, effective April 1, 2025), 80 items
Property 11, ownership/title 9, valuation 11, contracts and agency 16, practice 10, disclosures/environmental 9, financing/settlement 7, math 7. (Same outline as the Texas model.)

## Chosen form
140 items = 80 national picks (exact national outline weights) + 60 Arizona items (exact state outline weights), the real scored count; under the 150 cap so nothing is scaled. Pass 75%. Pretest items are not modeled. Arizona is a community property, deed-of-trust state, and the picks and overrides reflect that.

National picks by outline area: property 11 (fixtures, trade fixtures, emblements, situs, immobility, metes and bounds, plat, rectangular survey acreage, eminent domain, variance, appurtenant easement); ownership 9 (tenancy in common, community property separate-property rule, fee on limitation, tenancy at sufferance, lien priority, deed validity and delivery, special warranty deed, recording acts); valuation 11; contracts and agency 16 (adds dual agency with written consent, lawful in Arizona); practice 10 (earnest money, commingling, listing types, fair housing, vicarious liability, antitrust); disclosures 9; financing 7 (deed of trust trustee, secondary market, due-on-sale, VA, TRID, RESPA, ECOA); math 7.
