# Colorado broker exam: sources (all accessed 2026-11-18)

Colorado has no salesperson license: every entry-level licensee is a "broker" (associate broker level), so this variant models the PSI Colorado Real Estate Broker examination, which is the national plus state exam a new Colorado licensee takes.

## Official exam facts

|---|---|---|
| Regulator | Colorado Real Estate Commission and Division of Real Estate (DORA) | C.R.S. 12-10-206; PSI bulletin p. 2 |

| CE and reciprocal use | CE and reciprocal candidates take only the 74-item state portion | PSI bulletin p. 3 |

### Colorado state portion outline (74 items, PSI bulletin p. 6)
Duties and Powers of the Real Estate Commission 2; Licensing Requirements 5; Requirements Governing the Activities of Licensees 11; Additional Topics (property management and landlord tenant, water rights and well ownership, Colorado taxes, Colorado Fair Housing Act, Foreclosure Protection Act and processes) 7; Colorado Forms and Contracts 23; Record Keeping and Trust Accounts 5; Closing and Settlement 10; Brokerage Relationships 11.

### National outline (PSI, broker percentages, bulletin pp. 3 to 6)
I Property ownership 10; II Land use controls 5; III Valuation 8; IV Financing 9; V Contracts 19; VI Agency 13; VII Property disclosures 7; VIII Property management 5; IX Transfer of title 6; X Practice of real estate 12; XI Real estate calculations 6. Total 100%.

## Chosen form
The real scored form is 80 + 74 = 154 items, over the 150 cap, so it is scaled to 150 keeping the ratio: 150 x 80/154 = 77.9, so 78 national and 72 Colorado items. Pass mark 75% (60 of 80 national; the state line is 53 of 74, 71.6%, so 75% is the stricter one).

- National 78 (percent x 0.78, largest remainders): property ownership 8 (PROP-001, 002, 014, 015, 017, 026, 028; OWN-001), land use 4 (PROP-021, 022, 023, 025), valuation 6 (VAL-001, 003, 005, 014, 018, 021), financing 7 (FIN-002, 004, 005, 006, 008, 012, 013), contracts 15 (CON-001, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 009, 010, 011, 014, 015, 017, 018, 019, 020), agency 10 (CON-027, 028, 031, 033, 035, 037, 039; PRAC-006, 007, 008), disclosures 5 (DISC-001, 002, 004, 009, 011), property management 4 (PRAC-011, 019, 020; OWN-011), transfer of title 5 (OWN-017, 018, 019, 020, 024), practice 9 (PRAC-003, 005, 013, 014, 015, 016, 017, 023, 024), calculations 5 (MATH-001, 004, 005, 011, 012).
- Colorado 72 (74 scaled, largest remainders; Forms 23 to 22 and Closing 10 to 9 take the cuts): Commission COCOM 2, Licensing COLIC 5, Activities COACT 11, Additional Topics COADD 7, Forms and Contracts COFRM 22, Record Keeping and Trust COTRS 5, Closing COCLS 9, Brokerage Relationships COREL 11.

The 2025 title PDFs were not at the same addresses (404), so later acts were checked one by one:
  - HB26-1287 (Sunset Division of Real Estate), signed June 4, 2026, effective August 12, 2026, signed act https://leg.colorado.gov/bill_files/117164/download (local src/hb1287.pdf): revoked licensee waits two years (12-10-217(9)); affiliated business disclosure to the represented party at the time of referral (12-10-218(2)(b)); new 12-10-403(9) lets a broker share client confidential information with the employing broker for supervision; 12-10-217(1)(h), (i) trust duties limited to licensed functions; gender-neutral and email changes. Items COCOM-002 and COACT-009 use the amended text; no item rests on any other changed sentence. 12-10-403(2) (transaction-broker presumption) and 12-10-406 (no dual agency) are unchanged.
  - SB25-016 (Updating Escrow Disbursement Practices), signed March 26, 2025, effective August 6, 2025, https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/SB25-016 : narrows which wire transfers count as good funds under 38-35-125 and adds real-time payments. COCLS-004 tests cashier's checks, which the act does not change.
  - HB25-1249 (tenant security deposits), signed June 3, 2025, effective January 1, 2026, https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/HB25-1249 : the signed act PDF is a scan with no text, so its changes to 38-12-103 and related sections could not be read. No item tests security deposit deadlines or damages for that reason.
- Colorado Real Estate Manual (2026 chapters), Commission position statements and the Commission-approved forms (CBS1 and others) on dre.colorado.gov: every request returned 403 (curl and fetch tool), and the Wayback copies could not be reached from this environment. Search snippets confirm the current CBS1 was adopted August 5, 2025 with mandatory use from January 1, 2026, but its text was not read. So the Forms and Contracts items rest on the statutes and rules that govern the forms (C.R.S. 12-10-403(4), Rules 6.14 and 7.1 to 7.4, the C.R.S. 38-35.7 disclosures) and on deed of trust, foreclosure and title law, not on form paragraph numbers or contract deadlines.

### Left out and why
- Contract-specific dates and paragraphs (CBS1 deadlines, earnest money release, title objection mechanics, closing instructions, counterproposal form): form text not readable.
- Commission position statements (CP-xx): not readable (DRE 403).
- Security deposit return deadlines and damages: HB25-1249 text not readable.
- Water rights deeds and the well change of ownership filing: the rule lives with the State Engineer, not read; only the 38-35.7-104 well permit disclosure is used.
- Fees, fine amounts and E&O dollar minimums: excluded by brief.
- Proration items: the Colorado contract's proration convention is in the unread CBS form.
