# Florida sales associate exam: sources (all read 2026-10-17)

## Official exam facts
Source: DBPR Candidate Information Booklet, Real Estate Sales Associate Examination, "Effective January 2025"
https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/servop/testing/documents/RE%5Fsales%5Fcibs.pdf (local copy: src/cib.pdf, src/cib.txt)

- Vendor: Pearson VUE schedules, administers, scores and reports for DBPR.
- Format: 100 multiple-choice questions (A to D), 3.5 hours, closed book, calculator allowed.
- Pass: graded on 100 points; 75 or higher passes. Possible unidentified pilot items are unscored and get extra time,
  so the scored form is the 100 items.
- One exam: Chapter 475 Part I F.S. and 61J2 F.A.C. principles, practices, law and math. No separate national portion.
- Prelicense course completion is valid 2 years (also s. 475.181(2)).
- Content outline, 19 areas with weights (sum 100%):
  I Real Estate Business 1; II License Law and Qualifications 6; III License Law and Commission Rules 2;
  IV Authorized Relationships, Duties and Disclosures 7; V Brokerage Activities and Procedures 12;
  VI Violations, Penalties and Procedures 3; VII Federal and State Laws 3; VIII Property Rights, Homestead,
  Condos, Time-Sharing 8; IX Titles, Deeds, Ownership Restrictions 7; X Legal Descriptions 5; XI Contracts 12
  (incl. Florida mandatory disclosures: material defects, radon, lead, energy brochure, HOA, property tax,
  building code, CDD); XII Residential Mortgages 9; XIII Types of Mortgages and Sources of Financing 4;
  XIV Computations and Closing 6; XV Markets 1; XVI Appraisal 8; XVII Investments and Business Brokerage 2;
  XVIII Taxes 3; XIX Planning and Zoning 1.

## Form size and split (why)
100 items, mirroring the 100-question exam. Each outline area was mapped to the 8 bank categories, then split
between reused national items and Florida items by how much of the area is Florida law:
52 national (8 property, 7 ownership, 9 valuation, 6 contracts/agency, 4 practice, 2 disclosures, 11 financing,
5 math) and 48 Florida (3, 7, 1, 10, 18, 4, 1, 4). Full form: 11 / 14 / 10 / 16 / 22 / 6 / 12 / 9.
Areas II, III, IV, V, VI (30% of the exam) are almost all Florida law, which is why rePractice and
reContractsAgency carry most Florida items.

## Florida Constitution (Online Sunshine, http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?submenu=3 ; src/const.txt)
- Art. X s. 4: forced-sale protection 160 contiguous acres outside a municipality, one-half acre inside; not subject
  to devise if survived by spouse or minor child. (FLOWN-001, -002)
- Art. VII s. 6: first $25,000 exemption plus a second exemption (not school levies) now inflation-indexed. No item
  asks an exemption dollar amount for that reason.

## Proration convention
- Florida Realtors/Florida Bar "AS IS" contract, Standard K: items prorated "as of the day prior to Closing Date",
  so the buyer owns the closing day. Copy read: FloridaRealtors/FloridaBar-ASIS-4 Rev.9/15,
  https://www.consolidatedcredit.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/AS-IS-Contract-Sample.pdf (src/asis/). The 365-day
  year is stated in the FLMTH-004 stem itself rather than asserted as law.
