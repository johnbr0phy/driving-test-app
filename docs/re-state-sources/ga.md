### Georgia salesperson outline (state portion, 52 items)
I State Laws and Rules (16): unfair practices; substantive regulations; qualifications and fees; fair housing; Real Estate Education, Research and Recovery Fund; investigation and hearing process; Commission organization and procedures; required licensure.
II Real Estate Practice in Georgia (21): real estate practice; sales contracts; listings and agency; property management; community association management.
III Finance and Closing (15): finance; closing procedures.

### National outline (PSI, salesperson percentages)
I Property ownership 10; II Land use controls 5; III Valuation 8; IV Financing 10; V Contracts 19; VI Agency 13; VII Property disclosures 7; VIII Property management 3; IX Transfer of title 6; X Practice of real estate 12; XI Real estate calculations 7. Total 100%.

## Chosen form
The real scored form is 100 + 52 = 152 items, over the 150 cap, so it is scaled to 150 keeping the ratio:
150 x 100/152 = 98.7, so 99 national and 51 Georgia items. Pass mark 75% (the providers' figure for each portion).

- National 99, by outline area (percent x 0.99, rounded; Contracts takes the one-item cut because it had the largest rounding up):
  property ownership 10 (PROP-001, 002, 014, 015, 017, 026, 028; OWN-001, 008, 015), land use 5 (PROP-021 to 025),
  valuation 8 (VAL-001, 003, 005, 014, 018, 021, 026, 027), financing 10 (FIN-002, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 010, 012, 013, 014),
  contracts 18 (CON-001, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 009, 010, 011, 014 to 021; OWN-013 lease types),
  agency 13 (CON-026, 027, 028, 031, 032, 033, 034, 037, 038, 039; PRAC-006, 007, 008 listing types),
  disclosures 7 (DISC-001, 002, 004, 007, 009, 011, 018), property management 3 (PRAC-011, 020; OWN-011),
  transfer of title 6 (OWN-017, 018, 019, 020, 024; FIN-016), practice 12 (PRAC-003, 005, 013 to 019, 021, 023, 024),
  calculations 7 (MATH-001, 004, 005, 011, 012, 013, 017).
- Georgia 51 (52 scaled; 16 / 20 / 15, Practice in Georgia takes the cut):
  I State Laws and Rules 16 = GALIC 9 + GACND 7; II Practice 20 = GAAGY 8 + GAPRC 7 + GAPM 5; III Finance and Closing 15 = GAFIN 8 + GACLS 7.

## Law sources (official current text)
- GREC Rules, Chapter 520-1 (Licensure and Brokerage), Georgia Secretary of State rules site: https://rules.sos.ga.gov/gac/520-1 (local src/gac520-1.html; current text, includes Rule 520-1-.05(1)(f) effective July 1, 2025).
- Official Code of Georgia Annotated, official LexisNexis edition published under authority of the State and released by Public.Resource.Org (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 2020): https://archive.org/details/gov.ga.ocga.2024 (local src/ocga/). Volumes used: Title 10 (2024 ed., acts through the 2024 session) for 10-6A; Title 43 Ch. 33-51 (2021 ed.) for 43-40; Title 44 Ch. 8-15 (2022 ed.) for 44-14; Title 48 Ch. 1-6 (2024 ed.) for 48-6; Title 44 Ch. 1-7 (2010 ed.) for 44-1-16, 44-5-163/164, 44-6-190, 44-7-31 to 44-7-36, each checked for later amendments below.
- Later acts checked on legis.ga.gov:
  - SB 443 (2018), https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20172018/175277 : the as-passed-Senate text would change the 44-7-34 deadline from "one month" to 30 days, but trackers show it stalled in the House, so it is not cited as the source. The current rule (move-out damage list within three business days, tenant may inspect within five, deposit back within 30 days) is confirmed by the Department of Community Affairs owner letter of April 13, 2026, https://dca.georgia.gov/document/document/ehv-security-deposit-letter/download . Items cite 44-7-33(b) and 44-7-34(a) without an amending act.
  - HB 404, Safe at Home Act (2024), committee substitute https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20232024/216456 ; signed April 22, 2024, effective July 1, 2024 (Georgia Appleseed bench card, https://gaappleseed.org/wp-content/uploads/SafeAtHome_BenchCard.pdf): new 44-7-30.1 caps deposits at two months' rent. HB 404 amends 44-7-13, 44-7-14.1, 44-7-30.1 and 44-7-50 only.
  - HB 480 (2022) floor amendment, https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20212022/202319 : changes only the broker experience rule in 43-40-8(c)(4).
  - SB 458 (2026, Home Inspector Licensing Act), House substitute https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20252026/246137 : would add a "wholesaler" definition to 43-40-1, revise 43-40-2(a), 43-40-25(b)(12), (35)-(37), 43-40-29 and 43-40-30(c). Not enacted: the House 2026 Composite Status, "Session Final", May 14, 2026 (https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/document/docs/default-source/house-calendars/20252026/hcomposite8e0ed763e5394e828987c524d59e9ec6.pdf) shows Senate passage 3/6 and House passage by substitute 3/31 with no Senate agreement, no transmittal and no Act number. No item tests wholesaling.
  - HB 586 (2025), Senate Finance substitute https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20252026/236009 , signed May 2025, effective July 1, 2025: 48-6-60(3) long-term note now means principal due more than 62 months (was three years); the short-term note definition is repealed. Confirmed by Georgia DOR Informational Bulletin 2025-02 (June 27, 2025), https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document/hb-586-sb-141-informational-bulletin-2025-02/download . REGA-GAFIN-008 uses the new figure; the 48-6-61 rate ($1.50 per $500) is unchanged.
  - HB 399 (2025): new 44-7-25 (a nonresident landlord of single-family or duplex rentals must employ a Georgia-licensed broker) and a restated 43-40-29(a); the executor exemption (a)(4) and the three-transaction referral exemption (a)(9) are unchanged (Senate substitute https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/legislation/document/20252026/239199 ). Not tested.
  - SB 406 (2026, Georgia Property Owners' Bill of Rights Act, Act 715, signed May 12, 2026): new Title 43 Chapter 17A for association registration and POA foreclosure; it does not amend 43-40, 10-6A or 44-7, so no item changes.
  - Other 2026 bills on security deposits (HB 687), foreclosure notices (HB 948) and flood disclosure (HB 618) did not pass (2026 Composite Status, Session Final).
  - 10-6A: no amendment after the 2024 volume found.
- Supreme Court of Georgia, In re UPL Advisory Opinion 2003-2, 277 Ga. 472 (2003): only a Georgia attorney may close a real estate transaction.
- GREC FAQ (prelicense course not repeated when the exam is retaken after 12 months): https://grec.state.ga.us/licensed-professionals/faq/real-estate/

## Caveats
- PSI and GREC do not publish the time or pass mark; the 100 + 52 item split appears only in the bulletin's sample score form. 4 hours and 75% per portion are the course providers' figures.
- 44-14-162.2 (30-day foreclosure notice) and 44-7-36 (ten-unit exemption, covering 44-7-31, -32, -33 and -35 only, lost when a third party manages for a fee) were read in the 2022 and 2010 bound volumes. The 2025 and 2026 session status reports show no enacted change to either (HB 948, 2026, on foreclosure notices died in the Senate).
