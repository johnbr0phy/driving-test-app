# Louisiana salesperson exam: sources (all accessed 2026-11-05)

The Louisiana Real Estate Commission (LREC) licenses salespersons; Pearson VUE now administers the exam (it was PSI). LREC's proposed May 2025 minutes mention updating state questions "with Pearson Vue"; the exact switch date was not found on an LREC page.

## Official exam facts
| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor and handbook | Pearson VUE, "Louisiana Real Estate Commission and Louisiana Appraisers Board Candidate Handbook", July 2024 (#091900) | https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/091900.pdf |
| Outline | "Louisiana Real Estate Content Outlines" #091901 (04/2025): national salesperson outline effective April 1, 2025; state outline effective Sept 1, 2021 | https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/091901.pdf |
| Format | Combination exam 135 scored (80 national + 55 state) plus 10-15 pretest, 240 minutes; national only 80 + 5 pretest, 150 min; state only 55 + 5-10 pretest, 90 min | Handbook p.8 |
| Pass | Scaled 70 on each part for salespersons (75 for brokers and instructors); parts scored separately | Handbook p.9 |
| Retakes | Authorization valid one year, unlimited attempts; a passed part is kept one year and only the failed part is retaken | Handbook; LAC 46:LXVII, sections 501, 507 |
| Education | 90 prelicensing hours; 45 post-licensing within 180 days; 12 CE hours a year | La. R.S. 37:1437(C)(4), (5); LAC 46:LXVII, section 907 |

### National outline (salesperson, April 2025)
Property characteristics, legal descriptions and land use 11; ownership, transfer and recording 9; value and appraisal 11; contracts and agency 16; practice 10; disclosures and environmental 9; financing and settlement 7; math 7. Total 80.

### State outline (Sept 2021), midpoints used
Commission duties and powers 4-6 (5); licensing requirements 7-9 (8); statutory requirements governing licensee activities 13-15 (14); Louisiana civil law 13-15 (14); Louisiana law of agency 13-15 (14). Total 55.

## Chosen form
135 items = 80 national picks at the Pearson VUE national weights + 55 Louisiana items at the state outline midpoints. Pass mark 70%. Pretest items not modeled; the real exam scores the parts separately, the practice form reports one combined score.

## National picks and Louisiana exclusions
Included (80): see la-national.json. Left out because they do not fit Louisiana's civil law: joint tenancy and tenancy by the entirety (RE-OWN-002, -003), tenancy in common terms (RE-OWN-001), fee on limitation and life estates (RE-OWN-007, -008, -009), tenancy at sufferance (RE-OWN-011, Louisiana uses reconduction), warranty and quitclaim deed types, grantee signature and delivery rules (RE-OWN-017 to -020), adverse possession with a 12-year period (RE-OWN-021), easements by prescription (RE-PROP-026, -027, Louisiana servitudes), emblements and trade fixtures (RE-PROP-002, -003), deeds of trust and lien theory (RE-FIN-001, -003), equitable title (RE-CON-020), oral leases over a year (RE-CON-006, leases may be oral), net listings keyed as lawful (RE-PRAC-009), mill rates on a 40% assessment (RE-MATH-006), transaction brokers and implied subagency (RE-CON-028, -029, -032), bare fee promise (RE-CON-002).
