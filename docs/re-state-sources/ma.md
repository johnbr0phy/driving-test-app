# Massachusetts salesperson exam: sources (all accessed 2026-11-18)

The Board of Registration of Real Estate Brokers and Salespersons, in the Division of Occupational Licensure, licenses salespersons; PSI Services LLC develops and administers the exam. Applications moved to the Division's eLIPSE platform in 2025; the bulletin says passing candidates pay the license fee there and get a digital license.

### National (general) outline, salesperson percentages (PSI)
Property ownership 10; land use controls 5; valuation 8; financing 10; contracts 19; agency 13; property disclosures 7; property management 3; transfer of title 6; practice of real estate 12; real estate calculations 7. Total 100%.

### State outline (salesperson item counts as printed)
I Duties and powers of the Board 2 (examination of records; sanctions: fines, suspension and revocation); II Licensing requirements 4 (activities requiring a license; active, inactive, expired; renewal and continuing education; Board notifications); III Requirements governing licensees 13 (advertising; broker/salesperson relationship; commissions; disclosures: consumer/licensee relationship, conflict of interest, rental fee disclosures; agency and non-agency relationships; handling of documents; handling of monies; home inspectors); IV Contracts 2 (offer to purchase; purchase and sale agreement; leases; listing and buyer agreements); V Consumer protection laws 5 (property disclosures; misrepresentation; privacy; sanctions and enforcement); VI Environmental issues and hazardous materials 4 (lead; private sewage disposal; smoke and carbon monoxide detection; wetlands and rivers protection); VII Massachusetts fair housing law 4 (protected classes that differ from federal; MA exceptions; MCAD; ADA and accommodations); VIII Landlord tenant law 4 (rental deposits, receipts and statement of condition; State Sanitary Code; short-term rentals); IX Additional topics 2 (ownership; condominiums, cooperatives and timeshares; registered land). Total 40.

## Chosen form
120 items = 80 national picks at the PSI salesperson weights + 40 Massachusetts items at the exact state outline counts, the real scored form (under the 150 cap, nothing scaled). Pass mark 70%. Pretest items are not modeled. The real exam scores the two portions separately (56 of 80 and 28 of 40); the practice form reports one combined score.

## National items excluded
- RE-PRAC-009 (net listing keyed as lawful): Massachusetts prohibits net listings, 87AAA(l), 254 CMR 3.00(11)(c).
- RE-FIN-001 (deed of trust with nonjudicial sale) and RE-FIN-003 (lien theory): Massachusetts uses mortgages; the national framing does not fit.
- RE-OWN-004 (community property): Massachusetts is not a community property state (c. 209, section 1 keeps spouses' property separate).
- RE-OWN-021 (adverse possession with a 12-year period): left out to avoid a period that is not Massachusetts law; registered land cannot be taken by adverse possession at all (c. 185, section 53).
- RE-MATH-006 (40% assessment ratio, mills) and RE-PROP-024 (mills): the Massachusetts assessment and tax-rate rules were not read; left out rather than model a ratio that may not apply.
- RE-MATH-016 (transfer tax per $500 at an arbitrary rate): Massachusetts deed excise rates not read; left out.
- RE-CON-006 (Statute of Frauds for leases over one year): the Massachusetts statute (c. 259) was not read; left out.
- RE-CON-028 (transaction broker): Massachusetts calls the role a facilitator (254 CMR 3.00(13)(e)), covered by REMA-MAREQ-010.
- RE-PROP-018 to -020 (further rectangular survey math): Massachusetts does not use the survey; one survey item (RE-PROP-017) kept because the PSI outline lists it.
