# Maryland salesperson exam: sources (all accessed 2026-11-18)

The Maryland Real Estate Commission (MREC), in the Maryland Department of Labor, licenses salespersons; PSI Services LLC administers the two-part exam (national and Maryland state portions).

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|

### National outline (PSI, salesperson percentages)
I Property ownership 10; II Land use controls 5; III Valuation 8; IV Financing 10; V Contracts 19; VI Agency 13; VII Property disclosures 7; VIII Property management 3; IX Transfer of title 6; X Practice of real estate 12; XI Real estate calculations 7. Total 100% of 80 items.

### Maryland state outline (salesperson counts, total 30)
Duties and Powers of the Real Estate Commission 4 (general powers; investigations, hearings and appeals; suspensions, revocations and penalties; Guaranty Fund); Licensing Requirements 4 (activities requiring a license, change in status, renewals, continuing education, partnerships and corporations, place of business, ownership); Brokerage Relationships 7 (listing agreements, disclosure of brokerage relationship, disclosure of interest and conflict of interest, disclosures); Supervision 5 (handling trust money, recordkeeping, supervision requirements, other Maryland laws: short sale, home improvements, PIFHA); Business Conduct 6 (offers, commissions including rebates, advertising and signs, fair housing, summary suspensions and convictions); Ethics 4 (unfair inducements, dealings with other licensees, general ethics). The counts are fixed, not ranges.

## National items excluded (Maryland conflicts)
- RE-CON-026: Maryland: only the broker (or designated branch office manager) is the dual agent, and the agent who lists cannot represent both sides; separate intra-company agents (17-530.1)
- RE-CON-027: Keyed as "designated agency"; Maryland uses dual agency with intra-company agents (17-528(k), 17-530.1)
- RE-CON-028: Transaction brokers are not a Maryland brokerage relationship
- RE-PRAC-009: Keys a net listing as lawful; Maryland bars net listings (17-322(b)(11), COMAR 09.11.01.01B)
- RE-CON-022: Keys holding disputed earnest money until agreement or court only; Maryland also lets the broker distribute after a 30-day notice (17-505(a)(4), (b); COMAR 09.11.01.18)
- RE-FIN-003: Lien-theory framing; Maryland practice uses both deeds of trust and mortgages, so left out
Other national items were left out only for weight, not conflict.
