# Missouri real estate salesperson exam: sources (all accessed 2026-11-18)

The Missouri Real Estate Commission (MREC, in the Division of Professional Registration, Department of Commerce and Insurance) licenses salespersons and brokers; PSI Services LLC administers the exam (PSI has been the vendor since AMP's contract ended in 2023). Missouri uses a national portion plus a Missouri state portion, scored separately.

### National outline (salesperson weights, handbook 7/18/2024)
Property ownership 10%, land use controls 5%, valuation 8%, financing 10%, agency 13%, property disclosures 7%, contracts 19%, property management 3%, transfer of title 6%, practice of real estate 12%, real estate calculations 7%. Of 100: 10, 5, 8, 10, 13, 7, 19, 3, 6, 12, 7.

### State outline (salesperson item counts, handbook 7/18/2024)
General Rules 2 (definitions, disputes); Licenses 7 (applications, expiration and renewal, individual license and business name, broker-salesperson relationship, salesperson license and transfers, denial, fees); Educational Requirements 2 (salesperson course, continuing education); Business Conduct and Practices 17 (improper use of license and office, branch offices, clerical personnel and assistants, advertising, listings, offers, licensee's interest in transactions, earnest money and disputes, standard forms, closings and closing statements, general, commissions); Disciplinary Proceedings 6 (complaints, investigation, violations, action by the Commission, action by the Administrative Hearing Commission, penalty for practicing without a license); Brokerage Relationships 6 (disclosure of agency, types permitted, agency duties, dual agency). Total 40.

## Chosen form
140 items = 100 national picks (national outline weights, the real scored count) + 40 Missouri items (exact state outline counts). Pass mark 75% (the stricter of 70% national and 75% state). Pretest items are not modeled. The real exam scores the two portions separately; the practice form reports one combined score.

## Law sources
Statutes were read section by section on the Revisor of Statutes site (https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=339.NNN, local copies src/rsmo/), which shows the version in effect today. Rules are the Secretary of State's current Code of State Regulations PDFs (https://www.sos.mo.gov/cmsimages/adrules/csr/current/20csr/20c2250-N.pdf, local copies src/csr/).

- RSMo Chapter 339, sections 339.010 to 339.205 (license law) and 339.710 to 339.855 (brokerage relationships). 339.100 is in the version effective August 28, 2026 (its only change was offense wording in subsection 5); 339.150 and 339.780 are in versions effective August 28, 2025.
- 20 CSR 2250-1 to 2250-10 (Missouri Real Estate Commission): chapter 2 (9/30/19), 3 (5/31/21), 4 (5/31/21), 6 (9/30/19), 8 (6/30/23; 8.060 display of license rescinded July 30, 2023), 9 (9/30/19), 10 (5/31/21).
- RSMo 432.010 (statute of frauds), 442.600 (psychologically impacted property), 516.010 (ten-year limit for recovering land), 535.300 (security deposits): background and overrides only.
- Left out: every fee and fine amount (license fees, the 2,500-dollar civil penalty cap, delinquent renewal fees); the core CE course topic (named each cycle on the commission's website); the broker exam and broker-only rules; reciprocity agreements (none listed); property management escrow details beyond the outline.

## National items excluded
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| RE-PRAC-009 | Net listing keyed as lawful; 20 CSR 2250-8.090(4)(F) prohibits net listings |
| RE-CON-002 | Bare fee promise keyed as a unilateral contract; RSMo 339.780 and 339.800.6 require written brokerage agreements addressing compensation |
| RE-CON-022 | Holding disputed earnest money until agreement or court conflicts with the 60-day good-faith disbursement rule, 20 CSR 2250-8.130(2), and the 365-day treasurer rule, RSMo 339.105.4 |
| RE-CON-032 | Agency by ratification; Missouri agency arises only by written agreement and an unagreed licensee is a transaction broker, RSMo 339.720.2, 339.780 |
| RE-CON-040 | Open house disclosure timing; attending an open house and answering questions is a ministerial act, RSMo 339.710(19)(c), and Missouri sets its own disclosure points, 20 CSR 2250-8.095, 8.097 |
| RE-OWN-004 | Community property; Missouri is not a community property state |
| RE-OWN-021 | Hypothetical 7-year adverse possession period; Missouri's period is ten years, RSMo 516.010 |
| RE-MATH-006, RE-PROP-024 | Hypothetical assessment ratio and mill rate unlike Missouri's tax system |
| RE-MATH-016 | Hypothetical local transfer tax rate |
| RE-FIN-003 | Lien-theory mortgage framing; Missouri brokers' standard security instruments include trust deeds (20 CSR 2250-8.140) |
| RE-DISC-015 | "Varies by state" key; not useful on a single-state form |
| RE-DISC-016 | Duplicate of RE-CON-037 |
