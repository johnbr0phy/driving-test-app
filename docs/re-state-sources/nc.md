# North Carolina real estate broker exam (provisional broker): sources (all accessed 2026-10-18)

North Carolina issues one individual license, the broker license. A new licensee is a provisional broker (G.S. 93A-2(a2), 93A-4(a1)) until the 90-hour Postlicensing program is done, so "salesperson" wording is replaced by "broker" or "provisional broker" on this form.

### National outline (RELINC p. 17)
Real Property 9, Ownership 8, Valuation 11, Contracts & Agency 16, Real Estate Practice 14, Property Disclosures 8, Financing 7, Real Estate Math 7. Total 80.

### State outline (RELINC p. 17)
Licensure 3, Agency 16, Supervision/Compensation 4, Brokerage Practice 12, Taxes/Insurance 4, Contracts/Closing 7, Landlord/Tenant 3, Other NC Laws 11. Total 60.

## Chosen form
140 items = 80 national picks at the exact National outline counts + 60 North Carolina items at the exact State outline counts. Under the 150 cap, so no scaling. Pass 75% (the real mark on each section). Pretest items are not modeled.

National picks by outline area (bank category = NC National category, one to one):
- Real Property 9: fixtures, trade fixtures, emblements, metes and bounds, plats, one rectangular-survey item (tested nationally; explanation notes NC does not use it), eminent domain, variance, easement appurtenant.
- Ownership 8: tenancy in common, tenancy by the entirety (NC recognizes it), fee simple determinable, tenancy at sufferance, lien priority math, deed validity, delivery, recording (explanation reworded to the Conner Act).
- Valuation 11: market value, elements of value, substitution, contribution, FIRREA, reconciliation, adjustments, reproduction cost, functional obsolescence, cost approach, BPO.
- Contracts & Agency 16: unilateral/bilateral, essentials, Statute of Frauds, void contracts, specific performance, time is of the essence, counteroffers, equitable title, dual and designated agency (both lawful in NC with written authority), express agency, confidentiality, latent defect disclosure, duties to customers, agency disclosure at an open house.
- Real Estate Practice 14: practice of law, earnest money handling by a provisional broker, commingling, conversion, listing types (three), familial status, steering, blockbusting, reasonable modification, emotional support animal, vicarious liability, market allocation.
- Property Disclosures 8: lead paint (two), asbestos, radon, USTs, flood insurance, Phase I, wetlands.
- Financing 7: deed of trust trustee (NC power of sale hearing noted), secondary market, VA, Regulation Z trigger terms, TRID timing, RESPA kickbacks, ECOA.
- Math 7: acreage, cap rate value, commission, commission split, seller net, interest, depreciation.
