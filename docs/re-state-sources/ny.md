# New York real estate salesperson variant: sources

All sources accessed 2026-10-17. Local copies of the DOS PDFs and extracted text are in `src/`.

## Official exam facts

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who gives the exam | NY Department of State, Division of Licensing Services, at DOS exam sites, scheduled through eAccessNY. No outside vendor. | https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-agent (Written Exam section); https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-salesperson-exam-sites |
| Format | Multiple choice, "based on the 77-hour pre-licensing curriculum" | https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-agent ("About the Exam") |
| Time | 1 1/2 hours (90 minutes), starting after the instructions | same page |
| Scoring | Reported only as passed or failed, with no numerical score. A pass is valid for two years. | same page |
| National vs state split | None. One exam on the DOS 77-hour syllabus, which mixes general and NY-specific topics. | same page + syllabus |
| Number of questions | **75** (not published by DOS; every course provider and prep source reports 75) | Not official. Examples: colibrirealestate.com, aceableagent.com, usrealtytraining.com (search results, 2026) |
| Pass mark | **70%** (about 53 of 75). DOS does not publish it because it reports pass/fail only. | Not official. Same prep sources. |
| Eligibility | Age 18; 77-hour approved course plus the school's own proctored final; sponsoring broker; pass the state exam | https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-agent; DOS 0022-f-a Salesperson Application instructions (Rev. 02/23), https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-salesperson-applications |
| Continuing education | 22.5 hours each 2-year term: 3 fair housing, 2.5 ethics, 2 implicit bias, 2 cultural competency, 1 recent legal matters, 1 agency (2 in a salesperson's first term) | DOS 0022-f-a; RPL 441(3)(a) |

### Content outline (the only official weighting)
19 NYCRR 176.3(a) as amended effective Dec 21, 2022 (rule text: https://dos.ny.gov/educational-update-real-estate-professionals-rule-text) and the DOS
Real Estate Salesperson Syllabus 2022 (https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-salesperson-77-hour-curriculum-eff-12212022), 77 hours:

License Law and Regulations 3 (includes the Property Condition Disclosure Act and RPL 242 gas-well disclosure), Law of Agency 11 (includes independent contractor 1),
Legal Issues 10 (estates 3, liens and easements 2.5, deeds 2.5, title closing and costs 2), Contract of Sales and Leases 3, Real Estate Finance 5,
Land Use Regulations 3, Construction and Environmental Issues 5, Valuation Process and Pricing 3, Human Rights and Fair Housing 6, Real Estate Mathematics 1,
Municipal Agencies 2, Property Insurance 1, Licensee Safety 1, Taxes and Assessments 3, Condominiums and Cooperatives 4, Commercial and Investment Properties 10,
Income Tax Issues 3, Mortgage Brokerage 1, Property Management 2.

## Form size and split

75 items, one full-length form, 70% pass, matching the reported real exam. With no separate national portion, the split mirrors the syllabus hours
(about one item per hour, 77 hours scaled to 75): **39 reused national items + 36 New York items.** NYC rent regulation is only a sub-bullet of the lease hour
(and its luxury-decontrol text is out of date since the 2019 HSTPA), so no NYC rent items; statewide security-deposit rules are covered instead.

| Category | National | NY | Total | Syllabus areas it carries |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rePractice | 3 | 12 | 15 | License law, human rights / fair housing, property management |
| reContractsAgency | 6 | 8 | 14 | Law of agency (11), contracts (3) |
| reDisclosures | 4 | 7 | 11 | Environmental issues, PCDA, RPL 242, 443-a, 175.28 |
| reOwnershipTitle | 8 | 3 | 11 | Estates, deeds, liens, leases, co-ops/condos, commercial leases |
| reFinancing | 5 | 2 | 7 | Finance (5), title closing costs |
| rePropertyCharacteristics | 4 | 2 | 6 | Land use, municipal agencies, easements, encroachment |
| reValuation | 4 | 2 | 6 | Valuation (3), taxes and assessments (3), commercial |
| reMath | 5 | 0 | 5 | Math (1) plus commercial, tax and closing arithmetic |
| **Total** | **39** | **36** | **75** | |

Correct letters across the 75: A 19, B 20, C 20, D 16.

## Notes and open questions
- Question count (75) and pass mark (70%) are not on any DOS page; DOS publishes only "multiple choice", 1 1/2 hours, pass/fail. Treat both as the industry-reported figures.
- GOL 7-108(1-a) wording on scope was hard to read through the fetch tool; the one-month cap and 14-day return are the HSTPA 2019 rules as widely reported, with exceptions for rent-controlled units, seasonal rentals and owner-occupied co-ops. Worth a human glance at the official text for NYLSE-001.
- National items not listed in `excluded` were left out only for weighting, not because they conflict with NY law.
