## Chosen form
120 items = 80 national picks at the PSI salesperson weights above + 40 Ohio items at the state outline weights (4 governance, 6 licensing, 16 license law and rules, 14 agency). It mirrors the scored items; pretest items are not modeled. Pass mark 70%; the real exam scores the two portions separately (56 of 80 and 28 of 40).

### National exclusions (conflict with Ohio law)
- RE-OWN-002 (joint tenancy) and RE-OWN-003 (tenancy by the entirety): Ohio abolished tenancy by the entirety for deeds after 1985 and uses statutory survivorship tenancy (ORC 5302.20) rather than common-law joint tenancy.
- RE-OWN-004 (community property): Ohio is not a community property state.
- RE-FIN-001 (deed of trust trustee and nonjudicial sale) and RE-FIN-003 (lien theory): Ohio uses mortgages and judicial foreclosure, and its classification as lien or intermediate theory varies by source.
- RE-CON-026, -027, -028 (dual agency, designated agency, transaction broker): Ohio permits only seller, purchaser, dual and subagency (ORC 4735.53) and handles in-company sales under 4735.70 to 4735.72; these are covered by Ohio items instead.
- RE-CON-040 (open house first disclosure): Ohio's disclosure timing is specific (ORC 4735.56(D), 4735.58) and tested by Ohio items.
- RE-DISC-015 (seller disclosure "varies by state") and RE-DISC-018 (stigmatized property): Ohio prescribes its own residential property disclosure form (ORC 5302.30, OAC 1301:5-6-10); left out to avoid implying no Ohio rule.
- RE-DISC-016 duplicates RE-CON-037.

### National overrides (wording only, keys unchanged)
- RE-PRAC-002 explanation: cites ORC 4735.21 (salesperson collects money only in the broker's name and with consent) and 4735.18(A)(26) (broker trust account), replacing "exact deposit deadlines vary by state".
- RE-CON-037 explanation: cites ORC 4735.67(A) (licensee's duty to disclose known material defects not discoverable by a reasonably diligent inspection).
