### Pennsylvania salesperson state outline (effective March 16, 2026), 40 items
I Real Estate Commission 5 (duties and powers; complaints, investigations, hearings and appeal; Real Estate Recovery Fund); II Licensure 8 (qualifications, activities requiring a license, renewal and reactivation, change of employment, exemption); III Agency and Disclosure 10 (agency disclosures, duties of licensees, agency relationships, compensation); IV Regulations Governing the Activities of Licensees 11 (advertising, broker/salesperson relations, funds and accounts, prohibited conduct, suspension and revocation); V Miscellaneous 6 (property disclosures; documents, contracts and forms; Pennsylvania Human Relations Act; time-shares, planned communities and condominiums; rentals, leasing and property management).

### National outline (salesperson, effective April 1, 2025), 80 items
Property 11, ownership/title 9, valuation 11, contracts and agency 16, practice 10, disclosures/environmental 9, financing/settlement 7, math 7. Same Pearson VUE national outline as Texas.

## Chosen form
120 items = 80 national picks + 40 Pennsylvania items, the real scored counts; pretest items are not modeled. Pass 75%, the real mark on each portion (the app grades the whole form at 75%).

National picks follow the outline subarea counts: property (fixtures 4, characteristics 1, legal descriptions 2, land use controls 2, easements and encroachments 2), ownership (estates and leases 4, liens 1, deeds and recording 4), valuation (value concepts 4, appraisal process and FIRREA 2, methods, GRM, BPO and assessed value 5), contracts and agency (types 1, elements 3, performance 3, sales contract 2, agency types 2, creation and termination 2, obligations 3), practice (scope and UPL 2, listings and property management 3, fair housing 3, risk management 2), disclosures (environmental 5, disclosure duties and liability 4), financing (concepts 2, lender 1, federal 2, settlement 2), math (one per math subarea).

Notes from the text: 49 Pa. Code 35.272 still says 60 hours of pre-license education; RELRA 521(2) as amended in 2018 requires 75 for anyone who had not started, and the 2026 handbook says 75, so REPA-PALIC-001 uses the statute. RELRA 604(a) still says fines up to $1,000, but later BPOA law raised civil penalties, so no item asks for a fine amount.
