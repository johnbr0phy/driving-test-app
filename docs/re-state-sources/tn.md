# Tennessee affiliate broker exam: sources (all accessed 2026-10-18)

Tennessee's entry license is the affiliate broker license (there is no "salesperson" license). The Tennessee Real Estate Commission (TREC, in the Department of Commerce and Insurance) licenses; PSI administers the exam.

## Official exam facts

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor | PSI Services LLC | TREC "PSI & Exam Information" page, https://www.tn.gov/commerce/regboards/trec/license/psi.html |
| Bulletin | Tennessee Real Estate Candidate Information Bulletin, "Updated 7/1/2025" (the edition TREC links, bulletin 511; bulletin 509 on PSI's portal is the same text) | https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/511 (local copy src/psi511.pdf, src/raw511.txt) |

### National outline (affiliate broker weights, bulletin 7/1/2025)
Property ownership 10%, land use controls 5%, valuation 8%, financing 10%, agency 13%, property disclosures 7%, property management 3%, contracts 19%, transfer of title 6%, practice of real estate 12%, real estate calculations 7%. Of 80: 8, 4, 6, 8, 10, 6, 2, 15, 5, 10, 6.

### State outline (affiliate broker item counts, bulletin 7/1/2025)
Duties and powers of the Commission 4; licensing requirements 4; advertising and marketing 7; broker/affiliate relationships 5; handling of documents and record keeping 4; handling of trust/escrow funds 4; other improper activities and consumer protection 3; agency and disclosure issues 6; special areas of practice (timeshare, property management, commercial) 3. Total 40.

## Chosen form
120 items = 80 national picks (national outline weights) + 40 Tennessee items (exact state outline counts). Pass mark 70%. Pretest items are not modeled. The real exam scores the two parts separately; the practice form reports one combined score.

## Law sources
Tennessee's official code is published through LexisNexis (linked from TREC's laws page, https://www.tn.gov/commerce/regboards/trec/rules-and-laws/laws.html), which cannot be fetched as text. Statute text was read from LawServer (the source the PSI bulletin itself cites for the state portion), which is current through the 2024 code, and every 2025 and 2026 amendment was then checked against the enrolled public chapters on the Secretary of State's site. Rules are the official Secretary of State PDFs.

- Tenn. Code Ann. Title 62, Chapter 13 (Tennessee Real Estate Broker License Act of 1973): https://www.lawserver.com/law/state/tennessee/tn-code/tennessee_code_title_62_chapter_13 (sections 62-13-102, -112, -201, -302, -303, -309, -310, -312, -313, -321, -401 to -407; local copies src/tca/).
- Public Chapter 524 (2025, HB1330, Less is More Act): 62-13-303(d) one year to apply after the exam; 62-13-318/-319 reinstatement; 62-13-206(c) deleted. Effective July 1, 2025. https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/114/pub/pc0524.pdf
- Public Chapter 122 (2025, SB886): 62-13-303(h) broker CE for grandfathered principal brokers who supervise, effective January 1, 2026. https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/114/pub/pc0122.pdf
- Public Chapter 72 (2025, SB909): wholesaling, Tenn. Code Ann. 66-4-401 to -403. https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/114/pub/pc0072.pdf (background; not tested)
- Public Chapter 1039 (2026, HB2530), effective July 1, 2026: electronic notices (62-13-104, -112(j)(2), (l)(2), -313(b), -317), office zoning and sign wording removed from 62-13-309(a), (b), broker post-licensing education 4 years (62-13-303(h)), violent offenses added to 62-13-312(b)(12), guilty pleas in 62-13-312(f), reinstatement window in 62-13-319(c). https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/114/pub/pc1039.pdf. Because of it, no item tests office signs, zoning letters or the broker post-licensing deadline; TREC says rule changes to match are coming.
- Public Chapter 1095 (2026, SB1786): vacation lodging designated agents (62-13-104). https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/114/pub/pc1095.pdf (not tested)
- TREC Rules, Chapter 1260-01 (Licensing) and 1260-02 (Rules of Conduct), "September, 2025 (Revised)", effective September 23, 2025: https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/rules/1260/1260-01.20250923.pdf, https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/rules/1260/1260-02.20250923.pdf (index https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/rules/1260/1260.htm).
- Tenn. Code Ann. 66-32-114 (Time-Share Act rescission), 66-5-207 (stigmatized property), 66-26-103 (unregistered instruments), 29-2-101 (statute of frauds): LawServer, same caveat as above.
- TREC newsletters (legislative summaries used to find amendments): Spring 2025, Summer 2025, Fall 2025, Summer 2026, https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/commerce/documents/regboards/trec/newsletters/<Season-Year>.pdf

|---|---|
| RE-CON-002 | Bare fee promise keyed as a unilateral contract; 62-13-401 requires a written bilateral agreement for agency |
