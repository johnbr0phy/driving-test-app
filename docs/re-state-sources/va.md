## Chosen form
120 items = 80 national picks at the PSI national weights + 40 Virginia items at the exact state outline counts, i.e. the real scored form. Pretest items are not modeled.

Pass mark: the real exam has two marks (70% national, 75% state, each passed separately). One combined form cannot carry both; the minimum total that passes both parts is 86 of 120 (72%). Suggested pass percent 72; 75 if the wirer prefers the stricter state mark.
