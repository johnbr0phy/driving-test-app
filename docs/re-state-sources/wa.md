# Washington broker exam: sources (all accessed 2026-10-18)

Washington's entry license is **broker** (the salesperson license was folded into it on July 1, 2010, RCW 18.85.481). Local copies of the PSI bulletin and the statute and rule text are in the builder's scratchpad (`re-wa/src/`).

### National outline (PSI, broker weights)
Property ownership 10%, land use controls 5%, valuation 8%, financing 10%, contracts 19%, agency 13%, property disclosures 7%, property management 3%, transfer of title 6%, practice of real estate 12%, calculations 7%. On 100 items these are item counts.

### Washington state outline (broker, 30 items)
I General rules 4-5 (definitions, commission and director, investigations and sanctions, Real Estate Research Center); II Licensing requirements 8; III Statutes and rules governing licensee activity 13 (firm relationships, prohibited conduct, compensation, trust accounts, records, offices, property management, supervision and teams, advertising); III (sic) Brokerage relationships 4-5 (types of agency, disclosure, duties, written consent). The state outline covers only RCW 18.85, 18.86, 18.235 and WAC 308-124; seller disclosure, community property and landlord-tenant law are not on it.

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| RE-OWN-003 | Tenancy by the entirety does not exist in Washington (community property, RCW 26.16.030) |
| RE-OWN-021 | Hypothetical 12-year adverse possession period; Washington is 10 years (RCW 4.16.020) or 7 with color of title and taxes (RCW 7.28.070) |
| RE-MATH-006, RE-VAL-028 | Assessment ratio below market value; Washington assesses at 100% of true and fair value (RCW 84.40.030) |

## Caveats
- The PSI bulletin is dated March 28, 2024 but is still the one PSI links for the Washington Broker test in October 2026. It predates SSB 6091 (2026), so REWA-WAACT-013 (public marketing, RCW 18.86.130) may not yet be on the real exam; it is current law.
- Washington DOL publishes no current pass rate or item bank; counts and weights come only from the PSI bulletin.
