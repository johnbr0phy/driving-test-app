# French civic and Nail Tech tiger sets

Reviewed November 19, 2026. Only frcivique and nailtech change.

- French civic: navy blazer, cream shirt, burgundy scarf, French blue-white-red tricolour and a town-hall study handbook. Replaces generic site art, never the US-flag civic mascot.
- Nail Tech: teal apron, cream collared shirt, lavender emery board and coral nail polish. Replaces hairdresser comb-and-scissors artwork. Hairstylist and cosmetology mappings are unchanged; Esthetics is out of scope.

Built-in image generation made the happy bases using the grcitizen and barber style references respectively. Each other expression was an independent identity-preserving edit of its role's happy base. Prompts lock head shape, ears, stripes, muzzle, pose, paws, clothing, props and colours. Expression directions follow TIGER_ASSET_PROMPTS.md: crowned winner, confetti celebration, happy, confident closed smile, tentative closed smile with eyes open, neutral flat mouth, closed frown, worried open mouth with sweat.

All sixteen final exports were visually reviewed after Sharp palette optimisation. Face 05 has open eyes and a small tentative closed smile, with no teeth, tongue or decorations. The French flag and role props remain consistent. Each transparent PNG is 512 by 512 pixels, below 100 KB, with clear outer margins. SHA-256 fixtures pin the reviewed files.

lib/tigerAssets.ts registers both complete sets; shareImageUrl refreshes only their share-card cache keys. French translated landings, result expressions and OG cards consume the shared mappings. No exam content, pricing, access or unrelated artwork changes.
