# Water treatment and pesticide applicator tiger artwork

This change adds two complete sets. Water Treatment Operator and Wastewater
Treatment Operator share `waterop`, a utility worker with a water sample and
clipboard. Pesticide Applicator uses `pesticide`, a field worker with a
crop-scouting clipboard, protective glasses and no spraying equipment.

Both sets have eight identity-preserving expressions. The first face has a
winner's crown directly on the tiger's fur. The applicator's hat and its goggles
are absent from that face only. The second face laughs with confetti and the
third is the default happy face. Faces 04 to 07 are closed-mouth: confident, tentative, neutral
and frowning. The tentative face keeps its eyes open. Face 08 has an open
worried mouth and blue sweat. Props and clothing identify the same role in
each face. The crown and absence of headwear were checked visually. The transparent 512px PNGs are stored under `public/tigers/`.
The pinned hashes in the test fixture identify the visually reviewed exports.
