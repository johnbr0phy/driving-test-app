CDM, CFPP PRACTICE STANDARDS - AUGUST 2025
FOODSERVICE
Standard 3: Establish Continuous Quality  
Improvement
A CDM, CFPP is responsible for establishing continuous quality improvement by:
Establishing Continuous Quality Improvement Procedures for Foodservice Department 
(e.g., QAPI)
• Defining foodservice objectives, implementing necessary procedures, and monitoring 
quality indicators
• Using auditing tools and analyzing data to improve performance
PERFORMANCE INDICATORS KEY ACTIONS
1.0 Define objectives and standards
1.1 Understand the objectives and standards 
of a quality management system to ensure 
client’s needs are continually met.
• Establish clear objectives and standards 
based on regulatory requirements and 
client expectations.
• Implement Continuous Quality 
Improvement (CQI) and Quality 
Assurance and Performance 
Improvement (QAPI) Processes to 
continuously study and improve 
processes.
• Engage every staff member in the QAPI 
process to ensure a comprehensive and 
data-driven approach to maintaining 
and improving safety and quality.
• Report QAPI results as needed to the 
Interdisciplinary Team.

2.0 Implement auditing tools and determine  
effectiveness
2.1 Implement process to gather 
information related to quality standards 
being met, analyze data and determine 
process changes.
• Use auditing tools to gather information 
related to quality standards being met 
such as food acceptance/satisfaction 
surveys, taste panels, test trays, plate 
waste studies, checklists, and self-audits.
• Collect and analyze data from 
various auditing tools to evaluate the 
effectiveness of quality improvement 
efforts.
• Continuously monitor the changes 
implemented through the QAPI process 
and adjust as necessary to ensure 
ongoing compliance with quality 
standards
