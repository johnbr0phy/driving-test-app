    (function ($) {
        $(function () {
            const urlSearchParams = new URLSearchParams(window.location.search);
            const params = Object.fromEntries(urlSearchParams.entries());
            var userToken = params.usertoken ? params.usertoken : params.userToken;
            if (!userToken) {
                userToken = "";
            }

            var xhr = new XMLHttpRequest();
            var sendParams = 'userToken=' + userToken;
            xhr.open('GET', '/sso/status' + "?" + sendParams, false);

            xhr.onload = function () {
                if (userToken) {
                    window.location.replace(params.ReturnURL ? params.ReturnURL : params.ReturnUrl);
                }
                if (xhr.status === 200) {
                    var response = JSON.parse(xhr.responseText);
                    if (response.IsSignedIn) {
                        document.getElementById("logged-in").style.display = "block";
                        document.getElementById("welcome").innerHTML = "Welcome, " + response.FirstName
                        document.getElementById("updateRedirectUrl").setAttribute("href", response.UpdateRedirectURL);
                        document.getElementById("updatePasswordRedirectUrl").setAttribute("href", response.UpdatePasswordRedirectURL);
                    }
                    else{
                        document.getElementById("logged-out").style.display = "block";
                    }
                }
            };
            xhr.setRequestHeader('Cache-Control', 'no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate');
            xhr.setRequestHeader('Pragma', 'no-cache');
            xhr.setRequestHeader('Expires', '0');
            xhr.send(null);
        });
    }(jQuery));

Register Online for the CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam

To submit an application for the exam, use the convenient, secure online process.

By registering for the exam, you attest that you have read the entire CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam Candidate Handbook and agree to abide by all stated policy.




According to the CBDM
 privacy policy
, CBDM maintains confidentiality of candidate data and information.



Access the CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam Candidate Handbook 



*NOTE: The most recent version of the CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam Candidate Handbook PDF is dated September 19, 2023. If you are not seeing this version, please contact CBDM at 


exam@CBDMonline.org


 

or 800.323.1908.


To register online, you will be prompted to log into your ANFP record with your username and password. 


New customers will need to create a new account.

If you already have an ANFP account, please do not create a new one. 

If you do not know your login information, use the “forgot my password” feature or call 800.323.1908, select option 1, for assistance.

During the exam application process, you are required to read, sign and upload the  CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam Candidate Agreement to 
Terms and Conditions form
.  

Make sure you have the required transcripts, completed 
Employment Verification Form
, employer-issued job description, etc. as required for the Eligibility Pathway under which you are applying. You will be required to upload the documents during the application process.

If you will be requesting testing accommodations as qualified under guidelines set forth by the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (ADA), you must indicate such during the exam application process and follow the process as listed below.

Have payment information available. To submit the application for processing, payment must be submitted. The exam fee is $425.



When submitting exam application and payment online, payment is processed immediately. If you do not fully meet a current eligibility pathway (as determined by CBDM) within 90 days of submitting the application and fees online, credit will be issued for the payment, less a $50 processing fee. When all eligibility requirements have been met, you will then be required to reapply and submit an exam application with exam fees

 for subsequent CBDM review and approval.


Individuals choosing to retake the exam for any reason must wait 90 days before they may reschedule their exam. They must meet current eligibility requirements and submit an exam application with fees for CBDM review and approval for each retake of the exam.


Register Online 




Exam fees are not transferable to other parties/individuals, are not refundable, and cannot be canceled.

CBDM Employment Verification Form 

Individuals applying for the exam under eligibility pathways III (a & b), IV and V must meet CBDM criteria for eligible work experience.


Work Experience Criteria


This CBDM Employment Verification Form is required for Pathways III (a & b) and V exam applicants. Applicant must also submit an employer-issued position description for each Employment Verification Form submitted. When applying online, download and complete the form and submit via an upload with your online registration.


The position title on the CBDM Employment Verification Form must match the position title on the corresponding position description. 

Employment during the stated timeframe must be continuous and full-time.

The employment verification section must be completed by the immediate supervisor or Human Resources Manager. This section should not be filled out by the exam applicant.

The CBDM Employment Verification Form and position description(s) must be submitted with the exam application. 

CBDM will determine to what extent the submitted employment verification qualifies for non-commercial foodservice work experience. 

For more information, refer to the 
CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam Candidate Handbook
.



NOTE: The most recent version of the Employment Verification form is dated February 2025. If you are not seeing this version, please contact CBDM at

 


exam@CBDMonline.org


 or 800.323.1908, select option 1, for assistance.

Special Accommodations

To ensure equal opportunities for all qualified individuals, qualified exam candidates with a disability within the guidelines set forth by the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (ADA) who supply appropriate documentation will be provided reasonable
    and appropriate accommodations in order to take the exam.


Testing Accommodations Process Overview


A summary of steps required to request testing accommodations as allowed under guidelines set forth by ADA) an in compliance with CBDM policy is as follows


Submit CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam online application with fee for CBDM review and approval.  
        

Answer yes to the question if you will be requesting testing accommodations. 

Complete and sign two-
page Request for Special Examination Accommodations form
 .

Upload completed form when submitting exam application.





Special Accommodations Request Form



After CBDM sends confirmation of eligibility to sit for the exam 
and
 PSI sends e-mail notice of eligibility to schedule an appointment, submit request to PSI for testing accommodations using the PSI online form available here: 
        
Submit a request – PSI Candidate Experience (zendesk.com)

 

Please fill out the PSI online form as directed.









PSI Online Testing Accommodations Form Directions



Provide all required information including preferred testing date and location.
        

To allow processing of the request, requested testing date/time preferences must be 45 days later than date of submitting PSI online testing accommodations request form. 

Do NOT schedule an exam appointment online or by calling PSI. Doing so will result in approved accommodations not being available.




Upload the completed/signed two-page 

Request for Special Examination Accommodations form

 that
 you also submitted with your exam application. If this form is not uploaded, the request will not be processed. 



Requests are reviewed and approval is made within the guidelines set forth by ADA.


For more information on requesting special accommodations, refer to the 
CDM, CFPP Credentialing Exam Candidate Handbook
.
