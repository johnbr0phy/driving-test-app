# Independent blinded review, before explanations

All 200 answers were independently locked in blind-answers.json before access to keys or explanations. Options-only guesses were locked earlier. No bank, author implementation, mapping, or other review was inspected. This assessment uses the stems, option text, supplied primary-source manifest, fetched CBDM outline/practice standards, and FDA Food Code 2022. It is an independent model review, not a professional psychometric or clinical validation.

## Decision

**Do not approve this as a substantively representative CDM exam-preparation bank merely after fixing cues.** It can be a useful focused introductory supplement after factual and wording corrections, with honest limitations. A representative 200-item bank needs a material content rebuild, particularly nutrition and personnel. The current problem is more than the 47.5% options-only nutrition score: many questions are answerable by literal matching, and several important outline skills are absent. Matching the five headline weights does not establish depth or subdomain coverage.

The supplied practice standards are appropriate primary sources for role boundaries, responsibilities, expected processes and some explicit facts. They are not equivalent to the detailed nutrition/foodservice textbooks to which they repeatedly refer. The FDA Food Code supplies strong detailed support for the food-safety numerical items. Thus sources are not universally inadequate; the source base is uneven. More substantive source material is needed before writing specific diet, nutrient, regulatory, employment, and technical-operational questions. Do not fabricate detail from broad performance statements or require independent prescribing outside CDM scope.

## Definite factual issue and limited ambiguities

- **R108: no strictly valid option.** Intended B is raw fish followed by raw poultry, apparently relying on increasing cooking temperatures. But FDA 4-602.11(B) says a succession of different types of raw MEAT and POULTRY; the Food Code definition of MEAT expressly excludes FISH. Other options are descending temperature sequences. Replace B with raw intact beef followed by raw poultry. Source passages: foodcode.txt lines 1222–1225 and 4898–4902. The independent answer file records intended B with the defect; it is not an endorsement of validity.
- **R085: unit wording.** "One cup of the 30 g cereal" may mean cereal mass rather than carbohydrate content. Specify "30 g carbohydrate per cup" for each product. Intended half-cup yields 15 g carbohydrate.
- R054 assumes the other four shifts remain present through lunch; state their hours if desired. R068's one-cycle-per-batch wording is slightly awkward alongside two batches per cycle; clarify each batch requires one cycle duration. These do not make the intended answers seriously contestable.
- R163 is valid as a minimum question: instantaneous 165°F is the minimum although 15 seconds at that temperature also achieves safe cooking. Preserve the word minimum.
- Most other questions are unambiguous under their explicit constraints. The main weakness is excessive elimination of alternatives, not widespread multiple correct answers. For example, R057 explicitly removes every reason to reassess; R199 explicitly forbids the other three interventions. Those are valid answers but easy discriminations.

## Nutrition options-only cues and targeted correction

The supplied score is 19/40. Against my independently locked answers (not yet an author key), 17 of the 19 nutrition items where I recorded a substantive option-only cue agree; two fail, R073 and R161. This concentration is more diagnostic than random guesses. Some cues are genuine general/domain knowledge, so options-only success alone is not sufficient to reject an item.

- **R032, R050, R115:** source attribution, end-to-end audit, and compiled intake data are the only clearly evidential choices. Other options concern recipe cost, carts, pharmacy, or production and are implausible for nutrition documentation. Use four realistic record sources or handoff artifacts, with timing and provenance deciding the answer.
- **R038, R044, R047, R057, R175:** one option is uniquely accessible, practical or proportionate; rivals are remote speech, irrelevant recall, or unnecessary reassessment. Keep each option a plausible adaptation or next step; require a real case distinction between access, comprehension, ability, symptoms and prescription.
- **R007, R027, R099, R141, R147:** broad clinical review, verification, actual outcomes, before/after measures and cautious conclusions stand out against narrow/unrelated measures or overclaims. Make alternatives plausible competing interpretations or measurements, not merely omit the relevant outcome from three answers.
- **R131, R134, R148:** a precisely bounded recall method, meal preparer, and open recent-change question carry predictable interview cues. Use plausible alternatives with equal specificity; the time period or available firsthand knowledge should distinguish them.
- **R167:** speech-language pathology is the only realistic swallowing professional among occupational safety, foodservice quality, and pharmacy technician. This is legitimate recall but very weak discrimination. Use other plausible care-team roles with distinct functions and ask a bounded referral question.
- **R073/R161 are positive counterexamples:** the superficially authoritative order or consumption metric is NOT the answer because the case requires identifying a stale tray card or testing delivery. This is how contextual evidence can defeat a generic cue without making wording tricky.

Do not fix cues simply by padding distractors to equal length or replacing every item with numeric options. Improve the competing clinical/operational hypotheses and balance genuinely plausible choices.

## Missing depth and source expansion

The official nutrition outline includes BMI, laboratory/MDS/medical-record data, established screening tools, nutrient content, medical/nutrition terminology, appropriateness for diagnosis, confidentiality/client rights, and actual menu modifications for medical conditions. The bank mainly covers elementary intake arithmetic, information sourcing and fictional diet-label compatibility. Weight loss and SLP referral alone do not cover screening or therapeutic-diet implementation. Additional authoritative teaching references are needed for selected real food/nutrient comparisons, validated screening interpretation, diet terminology and safe implementation. Use approved manuals or public evidence-based sources, keeping physician/RDN responsibilities distinct.

Personnel has almost no substantive fair-employment, FMLA/USERRA, wage/personnel policy, privacy, survey interaction, correction planning or regulatory training knowledge. Supplying a seven-day contract rule and asking if three days complies does not test those competencies. Obtain applicable primary agency material (with jurisdiction and dates) or transparently limit scope rather than invent generalized law.

Foodservice has useful production arithmetic and QAPI, but little culinary technique, actual menu nutrition, technical equipment choices, or foodservice system selection. More technical foodservice sources are needed if those outline skills are to be assessed with depth.

Safety is the strongest sourced area, yet the bank barely addresses specific pathogens/illness associations, employee-health exclusion/restriction decisions, outbreak/allergy response, OSHA/SDS/HAZCOM, pest management, water/waste/ventilation and cleaning/sanitizing operating conditions. Much of the food-safety expansion can be drawn from the already available Food Code; occupational topics need their own authorities.

Business has credible calculations but limited bidding/contracts, group purchasing, inventory methods beyond basic balances, renovation/layout proposals, payroll/capital decision complexity and marketing analysis. Expand carefully rather than repeating division with new quantities.

A defensible next step is a subdomain-by-subdomain blueprint with distinct learning objectives and authoritative support, retaining the strongest current arithmetic/safety/application items and replacing the literal-match clusters. If the current task cannot support that research and rebuild, report a transparent candidate failure for representative exam preparation, with the option of publishing only as a limited introductory operations supplement. A few targeted cue edits cannot justify a broader claim.

## Final explanation and landing-page pass

After answer locking, all 200 explanations were read. All 200 intended letters match the locked answers, but this agreement does not repair R108: its explanation repeats the erroneous broadening of the meat/poultry exception to fish. Correct both the option and explanation. R085 explanation clarifies carbohydrate units that the options should themselves make explicit. No other definite numerical or scope-of-practice error was found. Explanations generally preserve screening versus diagnosis, approved substitution versus independent prescribing, and association versus causation well (especially R058, R100, R141, R167 and R186). Some explanations merely restate the supplied condition and cannot compensate for a low-information question. Broad references such as "Personnel Standard 2 supports..." often establish domain relevance rather than a uniquely sourced answer; that is acceptable for clearly supplied fictional premises, but should not be represented as evidence that the standard supplies all detailed teaching content.

Read app/cdm-practice-test/page.tsx and the CDM entry in lib/examFacts.ts without inspecting the bank. Reopened the official [CBDM exam FAQ](https://www.cbdmonline.org/exam-prep/credentialing-exam-faq) and [content outline](https://www.cbdmonline.org/exam-prep/exam-content-outline); checked the fetched owner scoring text too. The 160 total / 140 scored / 20 pretest / 180-minute format, modified Angoff wording, historical approximately 70% score statement, PSI delivery and stated outline percentages are supported. The 50-question tests are properly distinguished from the official length. Practice target is not presented as a guaranteed passing cutoff. The page already includes meaningful focused-practice/textbook limitations; do not describe it as making an explicit exhaustive-coverage claim. However, these disclaimers do not substitute for a representative substantive-content quality gate if that is the release requirement. Headline domain proportions alone cannot support a stronger coverage claim.

Final disposition: factual correction required; wording clarification recommended; **material pedagogical/source-depth rebuild required for a representative exam-preparation bank**. An accurately scoped introductory supplement is a distinct, potentially acceptable product decision, not equivalent to passing that gate. No bank or application files were edited by this reviewer.
