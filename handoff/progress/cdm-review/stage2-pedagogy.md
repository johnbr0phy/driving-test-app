# Independent blinded review, stage 2

All 200 stems and option sets reviewed; all 200 answers independently locked in blind-answers.json before any keys or explanations were opened. All numerical solutions were recomputed from stem quantities. Primary evidence read: the 17 supplied CBDM practice-standard texts (performance indicators/actions), FDA Food Code 2022 sections cited in answer rationales, and the supplied sources manifest. No author generation code or full keyed bank was inspected. Individual option cues are preserved for all 200 rows in options-guesses.json. The parent reported an options-only score of 161/200; this review did not calculate that score against keys.

## Overall finding

Substantial pedagogical rewrite is necessary. The bank primarily measures recognition of socially reasonable professional behavior, reading qualifier words, and simple arithmetic. Most qualitative questions can be answered without the stem. The exact domain weights do not establish adequate content depth or examination realism. Numerically and factually the offered intended choices generally appear sound under the stated assumptions, subject to the wording refinements below; this does not salvage weak distractors.

## Specific applicability and precision changes

- CDM-NUT-025: Require comparison to the verified current diet order. A tray card can be outdated, as NUT-012 itself teaches; a card plus diet manual is operational evidence, not independent verification of the order.
- CDM-NUT-030: Replace "all observed oral intake" with oral fluid amounts consumed throughout the day. The stem concerns a fluid record, not all solid food intake.
- CDM-NUT-039: Specify that no reliable observation or report supports the entry. CBDM Nutrition 1 expressly permits observation, EHR information and client interviews; avoid teaching that the CDM must personally observe every valid entry. Preserve attribution and uncertainty rather than converting reported data to observed fact.
- CDM-FOO-013: Specify a resident taste panel if the outcome is whether diners like the sauce. An internal staff taste panel need not establish resident acceptance.
- CDM-SAF-019: State ready-to-eat and held more than 24 hours so Food Code date marking applies. Merely "prepared refrigerated TCS" is broader than section 3-501.17.
- CDM-SAF-045: Explicitly say stand **covered** for two minutes. The Food Code requires covered standing, not merely covering during heating.
- Numerous stems have typography defects: NUT-023/033 missing spaces after commas; FOO-006 and many BUS questions missing spaces before dollar amounts. Run editorial cleanup after substantive revision.

## Systemic cue problems and actionable replacements

1. **Three explicitly deficient alternatives.** NUT-009, NUT-030, NUT-037, PER-018, PER-020, PER-038, SAF-011, SAF-024, SAF-041, BUS-004, BUS-009, BUS-019 and BUS-024 use "only," "alone," "without," or similarly self-disqualifying qualifiers. Remove these giveaways, but more importantly make each wrong option a coherent plausible action based on a real misconception. Example BUS-004: all four alternatives should include reasonable factors; make the case require accounting for yield loss, equipment capacity or labor that one tempting calculation omits, without announcing the omission.
2. **One humane or ethical choice against misconduct.** NUT-018/022/027/028/034, PER-016/021/025/031/039, BUS-008/018 have coercive, biased, fabricated or obviously unprofessional alternatives. Replace with legitimate but lower-priority actions. For NUT-022, alternatives might all preserve resident choice while differing in whether they verify compatibility, consult the right clinician or document the refusal at the appropriate stage. The stem must contain the fact that distinguishes the best next step.
3. **One cautious verification/update action against blind action.** NUT-012/023/038/039/040, FOO-008/018/030/033, PER-029/030/037, SAF-038/048, BUS-008 use the recurring rule "review/verify/update" as an answer cue. Turn these into interpretation tasks: present actual records or conflicting measurements and ask which discrepancy warrants escalation, which record controls, or what adjustment follows. All options should be professionally plausible and equally current in tone.
4. **One comprehensive answer against isolated details.** NUT-029/037, FOO-007/027/029, PER-028/040, SAF-031/032/046, BUS-004/016/019/022/024. Avoid giving only the correct choice multiple coordinated factors. Match option structure and scope. For SAF-046, all choices can contain supplier/product/lot-like identifiers, but only one links the recalled lot to the actual received case; require reading a small receiving record.
5. **Irrelevant distractors.** NUT-035 includes napkin color; FOO-003 mixes acceptance with employee turnover; PER-007 includes portion size; SAF-020 includes vendor pricing; SAF-047 offers mop water. These barely test domain knowledge. Use adjacent concepts or realistic operational alternatives: hazard types, competing scheduling changes, similar records, distinct interventions with different prerequisites.
6. **Overt SMART-goal cues.** NUT-014, FOO-017 and PER-009 offer one measurable target and three aspirations. Make all goals measurable, but only one match the actual problem, available baseline, timeframe and scope. Supply enough context to select it.
7. **Numbers often test one division with no interpretation.** Keep some foundational arithmetic, but replace excess duplicates with applied decisions using units, yield, capacity, safety margins and operational constraints. Preserve explicit assumptions and error-based distractors. FOO-040 is better because rounding full batches matters. SAF-025 usefully tests total cooling time rather than rote two-plus-four arithmetic. Do not make every calculation harder merely by adding irrelevant numbers.

## Near-repeat clusters to diversify

- PER-001/006/013/023/034: Five 40-hour FTE conversions. Keep two with meaningfully different operations; replace others with staffing coverage, turnover analysis or workload allocation using supplied rules.
- FOO-002/028/036: Three direct recipe scaling items. Keep one plain multiplier and one applied yield/capacity problem; use the freed item for an untested foodservice skill.
- NUT-010/020/026: Fraction-consumed calculations. Keep two if teaching energy versus fluid units; replace the third with a mixed-component intake calculation where consumption percentages actually differ.
- PER-005/012/027/035: Competence beats attendance/satisfaction. Keep a baseline skill demonstration and a transfer-to-work question; give the others different training barriers or evaluation evidence.
- NUT-014/019/029; FOO-017/018/039; PER-009/019/033: Repeated measurable outcomes versus process proxies. Reuse only with different substantive interpretation demands.
- FOO-003/026/037 and BUS-024: Acceptance/plate-waste evaluation. Distinguish sensory acceptance, cultural suitability, process waste and financial tradeoffs using data, rather than repeat "ask residents/measure actual outcomes."
- NUT-012/025/036 and FOO-024/035: Individual diet compliance. Introduce realistic order reconciliation, substitution or texture verification instead of generic correct-order language.
- PER-014/026/038 and NUT-031: Actions/owners/deadlines documentation. One or two are sufficient without a distinct scenario challenge.
- SAF-007/030/039: Repeated 41°F threshold. Reception, holding and thawing are different contexts, but add decisions about exceptions or corrective actions using explicitly stated conditions to avoid mere number reuse.
- BUS-001/010/017/023: Unit-cost division with different labels. At least one should require interpreting volume-normalized variance or edible yield.
