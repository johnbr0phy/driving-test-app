# EPA 609 authoring reset

The 94/100 options-only score is retained. Packet audit found no answer-key leak. The bank contains prohibited strawman distractors, including uniquely sensible safety answers paired with irrelevant, absurd or absolute alternatives. Knowledge/prior labels do not excuse those defects. No further draft or bank expansion until a small independently reviewed pilot establishes a better method.

## Before a pilot item is written

1. Name one useful, source-backed learning objective and the actual decision or distinction it tests. A changed story with the same inference is not a new objective.
2. Read the current primary passage and its scope/exceptions. Do not invent a technical alternative from memory.
3. Choose a concise stem with the discriminating condition. Do not wrap a definition in a fake scenario or stack qualifications to rescue ambiguous options.
4. Write each wrong alternative from a credible novice error or a real adjacent rule/procedure applied in the wrong circumstances. For each alternative record why a partially trained candidate might select it, the relevant real rule, and why this stem rules it out.
5. Reject an item if three plausible alternatives cannot be supported. Narrow the decision or choose a different objective; do not fill a slot with nonsense.

## Required self-review

- Could a reader identify the only reasonable, cautious, qualified, complete, professional or technically real choice without the stem? If so, revise the decision and alternatives, not only the wording.
- No correct maintenance action against dimming a display, no eyewear against hearing protection, no tested compatibility against promises applying to every vehicle, no specified interval against indefinitely neglecting maintenance.
- Alternatives need comparable form and specificity. Do not reserve “specified,” “approved,” “manufacturer,” or meaningful conditions for the key.
- Name a different condition in which each plausible action would be reasonable when feasible. This is a check against strawmen, not an obligation to make every false factual statement true.
- Verify the explanation against the actual offered alternatives. Explain the tempting error; do not mention abandoned choices.
- Run ordinary length/echo/reuse checks, but a mechanical pass cannot substitute for this read.

## Pilot and review

After the full 100-item audit, select 10–15 varied defective items across domains, not a cherry-picked set of numerical questions. Preserve original IDs and before/after evidence outside the production bank until accepted. A fresh reviewer receives independently shuffled anonymous options only, locks choices and reasons, then receives stems and finally explanations. Preserve every raw result. Small-sample success is a writing-method check, not full-bank approval; expansion must still pass full independent category review, factual checks and pedagogy review.
