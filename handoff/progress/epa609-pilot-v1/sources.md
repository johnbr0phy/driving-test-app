# EPA 609 pilot sources

Read on November 18, 2026. Current eCFR displayed currency through November 16, 2026. These sources support original questions; no provider exam, sample question, editorial Q&A or answer pool was used. The January 2025 manual is technical training narrative, not a substitute for current regulations.

- [40 CFR part 82 subpart B](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-B): 82.32(e)(1)–(5) processing, mobile service and top-off exception; 82.34(d)(1) salvage processing; 82.38 equipment certification; 82.40(c), (f) program review and technician demonstration; Appendix C 4.1.1 filter monitoring and 7.3.3 cylinder retest.
- [40 CFR 82.154](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-F/section-82.154): (c) multiple sales categories and (d) used refrigerant conditions. Consulted to avoid repeating the rejected universal purchaser claim. Pilot REG-028 instead asks the separate salvage processing condition in 82.34.
- [EPA servicing requirements](https://www.epa.gov/mvac/regulatory-requirements-mvac-system-servicing): Technology Transitions section and dated vehicle table, updated April 8, 2026. Pilot REG-015 is explicitly about that listed schedule, not a prediction of future amendments.
- [EPA 608/609 overlap](https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-and-section-609-overlap): MVAC-like off-road cab equipment distinction, updated November 16, 2026.
- [MACS January 2025 training manual](https://macsmobileairclimate.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/MACS-609-CertTrainingManual-2025.pdf): printed pages 11–12 electrical/pressure precautions and maintenance; 16 oil accounting; 22 flushing; 23 pump maintenance; 24 desiccant exposure; 25 component-specific charge instructions; 43 yf precautions; 51 charging sequence. Printed page n is PDF page n+6 (one-based). Earlier source cache: /Users/johnbrophy/tigertest-next20-progress/epa609-research/macs2025.pdf.
- [Honeywell Solstice yf SDS, version 3.4](https://www.audi.kkecomm.com/Files/Product%20assets/SDS/R-1234yf.pdf): manufacturer-authored document hosted by Audi, revised March 22, 2024. Section 4 identifies evaporative contact cooling; sections 5–6 address heavier-than-air vapor and release hazards; section 9 distinguishes physical properties. Used for physical mechanisms only, not to supersede contemporary regulations or supply medical treatment advice. The MACS first-aid wording and this SDS differ; no first-aid treatment item is included.

Every offered alternative has a locator and rationale in authoring-evidence.json. A distractor rationale is an author’s hypothesis about a novice error, not evidence of measured learner behavior. Where no valid alternate condition exists, the entry identifies the mistaken inference instead of inventing a technical rule.
