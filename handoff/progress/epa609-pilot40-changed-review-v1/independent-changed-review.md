The earlier R005/high-side-pressure correction remains resolved and is unrelated to this change.

[82.42](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-B/section-82.42) supports equipment-owner signing and small-container notice distinctions. EPA’s overlap guidance and ASE’s refrigerant FAQ support REG-017’s completed credential alternatives.

No arbitrary gas, pressure, or system-testing authorization is inferred.

J639’s standalone page also timed out; its broader system role is corroborated by the current J2842 scope and official historic title.
