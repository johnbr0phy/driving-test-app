All 100 stems and all 400 options were personally read. The bank provides broad factual coverage but is not ready as a discriminating assessment. Most items ask recognition; several useful procedural distinctions are weakened by distractors unrelated to the decision. Correct options often survive without knowing the question. The disclosed 94/100 options-only result remains a real failure of stem dependence; domain familiarity explains some successful predictions but does not excuse weak alternatives.

Labels below: **credible** means plausible confusion in this specific decision; **mixed** means some plausible alternatives plus easy eliminations; **weak** means irrelevant, absurd, absolute, or uniquely responsible wording dominates. These are editorial judgments, not measured learner psychometrics. Repair directions are proposals, not new authorized edits. Existing stem answers and options-only answers remain locked.

## Depth and overlap

Numerical application is concentrated inR031/R081;R002 is a1:1 accounting step, andR007 concerns resolution. Many safety items are individually important but require no operational discrimination with current distractors. Stronger candidates includeR008,R020,R029,R031,R045,R051,R056,R065,R066,R072 after scope repair,R076,R089,R093,R099.

Specific overlaps needing consolidation or deliberate differentiation:

- R040/R078 both select high-pressure cutoff; retrofit legal requirement differentiates them, but current options offer nearly the same task.
- R068/R018/R080/R035 cover separate stages of one yf leak-check sequence. Retain only if stages are made essential to distinguishing plausible next steps; current unsafe alternatives let the sequence be ignored.
- R032/R024/R074/R100/R054 all concern correct charging. They test different facts, but current stems disclose their respective safe action. Use actual charge/hoses/bulletin decisions rather than multiplying obvious rules.
- R017/R023/R033/R049 distinguish mixture separation, source purity, recovery and reuse. These are legitimate distinctions; options presently reduce several to generic proper-processing choices. R045 is materially deeper and should anchor this cluster.
- R073/R039/R087/R098 each distinguish identification or leak detection. R087 almost names its answer andR098 repeats why pressure cannot identify refrigerant; consolidate or require a concrete instrument-selection workflow.
- R012/R048 are different standards but both answer compatibility versus claims outside scope. Reusing limited-claim-versus-guarantee structure teaches test-taking pattern.
- R001/R037 relate oil/desiccant moisture; not duplicates. R013/R093 relate crimp preparation versus defect cause; not duplicates. R019/R070 concern different eye hazards, butR019 offers almost no discrimination.

A10–15-item authoring pilot should include important regulatory boundaries, numerical interpretation, realistic procedural next steps, and equipment-specific applicability. Give all options equal specificity and plausible rationales tied to actual novice errors. Recheck options-only stem dependence before scaling.
