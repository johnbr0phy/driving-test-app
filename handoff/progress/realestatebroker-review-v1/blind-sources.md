## Regulator conceptual text, permitted caches

Read targeted explanatory passages in dre4.txt, dre5.txt, dre6.txt, dre7.txt, dre8.txt, dre9.txt, dre10.txt, dre12.txt, dre15.txt, dre16.txt and dre22.txt. These are California regulator texts and do not establish uniform national statutory law. Review used only conceptual definitions, explicit contractual facts, valuation formulas and basic accounting. Legal outcomes remain subject to governing jurisdiction and instrument language; specific guards appear in item findings.

- [DRE4 Property](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref04.pdf): personalty/fixtures, adaptation/agreement, estates, legal descriptions, survey grid, bundle of rights.
- [DRE5 Title](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref05.pdf): undivided interests, survivorship, dominant/servient and appurtenant rights, actual/constructive notice, marketable title, abstract and divided property interests.
- [DRE6 Transfer](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref06.pdf): contract classifications, assent/indefiniteness, material fraud, remedies, option, assignment/novation, modification, offer inquiry vs counteroffer, prescribed acceptance, listing classifications, eminent domain/escheat. Statutory deadlines and California-specific capacity rules excluded.
- [DRE7 Instruments](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref07.pdf): deed vs security instruments, quitclaim, express warranty covenants, reconveyance.
- [DRE8 Escrow](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref08.pdf): neutral performance of agreed instructions, not California licensing rules.
- [DRE9 Leases](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref09.pdf): fixed vs periodic estates and lessor reversion; no notice periods imported.
- [DRE10 Agency](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref10.pdf): principal/agent, authority, attorney-in-fact, general/special scope, informed ratification, principal manifestations, actual express authority, fiduciary confidentiality and own-account conflicts, revocation/renunciation. Old MLS compensation/subagency rules were not treated as current national practice.
- [DRE12 Finance](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref12.pdf): collateral vs promise and blanket security/release clauses; no foreclosure or deficiency rules imported.
- [DRE15 Valuation](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref15.pdf): highest/best-use tests, progression/contribution/anticipation, operating expenses excluding mortgage principal/interest, occupancy-stable taxes, multipliers, economic incurability, effective age, value/cost distinction and depreciation.
- [DRE16 Assessments](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref16.pdf): benefit assessments contrasted with value-based charges. Taxonomy is not assumed universal; R018 flagged.
- [DRE22 Management](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref22.pdf): preventive maintenance, rent comparison and management operations. Demographic screening suggestions and obsolete fee/employment statements excluded.
- [DRE trust-fund update](https://dre.ca.gov/Newsroom/DRE_Updates/2026_10_06_Trust_Fund_Records.html): live read of control record, beneficiary ledger, reconciliation. Used for accounting meaning only, not mandatory nationwide records or withdrawal rules. R026/R045/R051/R082 independently reconciled.
