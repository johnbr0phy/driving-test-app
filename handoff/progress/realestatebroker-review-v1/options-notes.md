# Options-only review — locked

Personally reviewed all 200 option sets, grouped by category. Only anonymous-options.json was inspected; no stems, keys, source mappings, bank files, websites, or git were consulted. Choices are locked pending the next blind stage.

These are test-wise selections with unequal confidence, not an accuracy score. Knowledge tags explicitly acknowledge subject familiarity; prior tags acknowledge anticipated questions. None tags are unsupported selections necessitated by absent inputs. No answer balancing or source-position inference was used.

Strong options-only cues occur in environment, agency, finance, and practice: cautious conditional answers compete against implausible absolutes, preliminary information is contrasted with unjustified certainty, and actual verification competes with assurances. Several option sets expose a familiar rule directly (lead date, radon level, pension-income treatment, actual versus constructive notice). These may measure legitimate prior knowledge even without a stem, so apparent success must not automatically be labeled surface leakage.

Pure numeric questions are mostly indeterminate. A few invite number-shape or decimal-error inferences, documented individually. Stem-dependent terms and legal classifications often support plausible anticipated scenarios but cannot establish a uniquely correct answer. The locked choices intentionally preserve that uncertainty.
