# Life and Health Insurance question corrections

These are original questions. Sources checked 2026-11-28.

- The NAIC Uniform Individual Accident and Sickness Policy Provision Law, Model 180, section 3A(12), distinguishes the 60-day wait after proof is filed from the three-year limit after proof is due. This is the model for national item LH-HPRV-005. https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-180.pdf
- Ohio Revised Code 3915.05(J) requires the extended-term coverage to be in force and the original policy unsurrendered and uncanceled. LHOH-OHLIF-006 applies the three-year reinstatement period from default with evidence of insurability and payment of arrears with interest. https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3915.05
- New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance, Coverage of Young Adults in New Jersey Up to Age 31, gives the age limit and eligibility rules for LHNJ-NJHLT-003, including residence in New Jersey or full-time student status. https://nj.gov/dobi/division_consumers/du31.html

## State copies of LH-HPRV-005

In each participating state, submitting an early proof starts the waiting period. The proof deadline starts the three-year period for filing suit.
