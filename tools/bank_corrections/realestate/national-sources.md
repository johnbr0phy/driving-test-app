# Sources for the national real estate answer choices

Sources checked 2026-11-28 for the original national questions in `realestate_choices.json` and their state copies. California DRE Reference Book chapter 2 identifies the subject areas. The current outline lists ownership, valuation, financing, agency, contracts, and disclosures. [Chapter 2](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref02.pdf) and [current examination outline](https://dre.ca.gov/Examinees/SalesExamContent.html).

## Contracts, agency and brokerage

| National item | Fact checked | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RE-CON-004, RE-CON-005, RE-CON-014, RE-CON-015 | Formation requires competent parties, consent, a lawful object and consideration. The Statute of Frauds generally requires a signed writing for a real property sale. A purchaser can seek specific performance, and a time-is-of-the-essence term makes contract deadlines material. | [DRE chapter 6, Transfer of Interests in Real Property](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref06.pdf) and [chapter 20, Basic Contract Provisions and Disclosures](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref20.pdf) |
| RE-CON-026, RE-CON-031, RE-CON-037, RE-CON-039, RE-CON-040 | A listing grants authority for the agreed transaction. Agency disclosures identify whom the broker represents. Where dual agency is authorized, both principals must consent. A listing agent must deal honestly with an unrepresented buyer and disclose known material defects, including flooding. | [DRE chapter 10, Agency](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref10.pdf) and [chapter 20, disclosures](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref20.pdf) |
| RE-PRAC-003 | The DRE's second example of commingling is depositing trust funds into the broker's general account. | [DRE, Trust Funds, Commingling Prohibited](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf) |
| RE-PRAC-006 | An exclusive right-to-sell listing earns the agreed commission even when the owner procures the buyer during the listing period. Five percent of $400,000 is $20,000. | [DRE chapter 10, Exclusive Right to Sell](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref10.pdf) |
| RE-PRAC-014, RE-PRAC-016, RE-PRAC-019 | Familial status includes pregnancy. Inducing a sale for profit by invoking a protected group's entry into a neighborhood is unlawful. A landlord must allow necessary reasonable disability modifications at the tenant's expense under the Fair Housing Act. | [42 USC 3602(k)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t42/s3602/) and [42 USC 3604(a), (e), (f)(3)(A)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t42/s3604/) |
| RE-PRAC-023 | Competing brokers who agree to divide a territory allocate markets. | [Federal Trade Commission, Market Division or Customer Allocation](https://search.ftc.gov/advice-guidance/competition-guidance/guide-antitrust-laws/dealings-competitors/market-division-or-customer-allocation) |

## Finance and valuation

| National item | Fact checked | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RE-FIN-004 | The lender originates the loan in the primary market. Its later sale to Fannie Mae takes place in the secondary market. | [FHFA, Fannie Mae Annual Housing Activities Report](https://www.fhfa.gov/document/fannie-mae-ahar-2024.pdf) |
| RE-FIN-006 | A due-on-sale clause may give the lender the right to accelerate repayment after a sale or transfer. Federal law specifies protected transfers. | [12 USC 1701j-3(a), (d)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t12/s1701j-3/) |
| RE-FIN-012 | Regulation Z generally requires the consumer to receive the Closing Disclosure at least three business days before consummation. | [CFPB, 12 CFR 1026.19(f)(1)(ii)(A)](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/19/) |
| RE-VAL-001, RE-VAL-005, RE-VAL-020 | Market value differs from the amount paid and the cost of construction. An improvement contributes the increment buyers will pay. An inconvenient layout can cause functional obsolescence. | [DRE chapter 15, Appraisal and Valuation](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/refbook/ref15.pdf) |
| RE-VAL-026 | A broker price opinion estimates probable selling price. North Carolina defines a BPO and a comparative market analysis together, so the latter can also describe a broker's estimate for a lender. | [12 USC 3355(b)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-142/t12/s3355/) and [North Carolina General Statutes 93A-82](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_93A/GS_93A-82.html) |
