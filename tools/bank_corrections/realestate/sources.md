# Real estate state question corrections

These original items were checked against the following official texts on 2026-11-28. Each state's exam outline and allocation are recorded in `docs/re-state-sources/`.

| State and item | Official source, fetched 2026-11-28 | Fact checked |
|---|---|---|
| Texas, RETX-TXAGY-002 | [Texas Legislature, Occupations Code 1101.558(c)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/OC/pdf/OC.1101.pdf) | The open-house conversation must concern the property being shown. The separate lease exception requires a term shorter than one year and no contemplated sale. |
| Georgia, REGA-GACLS-005 | [Georgia Department of Revenue, Deed Fundamentals, Georgia Code 44-1-16(a)](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document/deed-fundamentals/download) | Direct questions about a death require truthful answers based on personal knowledge. An answer may be withheld if giving it would violate federal or state law or regulations. |
| Washington, REWA-WAAGY-004 | [Washington Legislature, RCW 18.86.030(1)(f)](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=18.86.030) | The pamphlet precedes the client's services agreement. For unrepresented parties, the law specifies before signing an offer or as soon as reasonably practical. |
| Colorado, RECO-COACT-003 | [Colorado Secretary of State, 4 CCR 725-1, Rule 6.10.D, effective September 30, 2026](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=12721&fileName=4%20CCR%20725-1) | Paragraph 2 gives three days to remove expired listings from the broker's media. Paragraph 3 gives the same period for written requests to syndicators. |
| California, RECA-CA-R4-MLO-PROCESSOR-CONTRACTOR-001 | [California DRE, 2026 Real Estate Law, Business and Professions Code 10166.03(c)](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/2026/relaw.pdf) | Independent contractors who process residential loans for an originator need their own MLO endorsement and a valid NMLS identifier. |
| New Jersey, RENJ-NJ-REBATE-ADVERTISEMENT-TAX-NOTICE-001 | [New Jersey Legislature, P.L. 2009 c.273, section 3](https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2008/PL09/273_.PDF) | Rebate ads must disclose the buyer's responsibility for applicable taxes and advise consultation with a tax professional. Both notices must be conspicuous and at least as large as the ad text. |
| Massachusetts, REMA-MAADD-002 | [Massachusetts General Court, chapter 183A, section 6(d)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartII/TitleI/Chapter183A/Section6) | The statement is due within ten business days of a written request upon payment of the reasonable fee. A mortgagee giving notice of intent to foreclose is exempt from the fee. |
| New York, RENY-OWN-021 | [New York Senate, RPAPL 501](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPA/501), [CPLR 212(a)](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CVP/212), and [New York courts, Montague v Yezol](https://www.nycourts.gov/Reporter/3dseries/2024/2024_24067.htm) | Successive adverse possessors may combine their periods when possession passes between them. The period is ten years and each period must meet the adverse possession requirements. |

## California answer-choice review

The following sources were checked 2026-11-28 for the California handoff items. Each shortened question retains the legal distinction tested by its original key.

| Item suffixes | Official text and rule checked |
|---|---|
| BUYER-001 through BUYER-004 | [DRE, adopted regulations 2906.1 and 2906.2](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/2026/Title10_CalCodeofRegsSecs_2906.1thru2906.3implementing_AB_2992.pdf). Term, signed renewal, the rebuttable presumption for a showing, and the role of a seller's agent. |
| MLDS-EARLY-OBLIGATION, AFFILIATE, ADVERT, ADVANCE-MODIFICATION-STAGES, ADVANCE-COMPLETED-TASK, COMP | [DRE, 2026 Real Estate Law](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/2026/relaw.pdf). Business and Professions Code 10240(a), 10132, 10159.6(b), 10085.6(a)(1), 10026(b)(4) and 10137 cover disclosure timing, supervision, team advertisements, advance fees and salesperson compensation. |
| ASSIST-AD-PREPUBLICATION, ASSIST-001 | [DRE, Guidelines for Unlicensed Assistants](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/guide_unlic_asst.pdf). A licensee reviews and approves advertising before publication. An assistant may hand out information and direct visitors to a licensee at an open house. |
| DRE-001 and DRE-002 | [DRE, Filing a Complaint](https://www.dre.ca.gov/Consumers/FileComplaint.html) and [Complaint Form Information](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/forms/re519.pdf). Jurisdiction includes unlicensed acts requiring a license. The licensing process can result in discipline and does not award a buyer damages or rescind a contract. |
| FAIR-002 | [California Civil Rights Department, Housing](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/housing/). Charging a higher deposit because of a voucher violates source-of-income protections. |
| RECOVERY-002 | [DRE, Professional Responsibility course booklet](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/professionalresponsibilitycoursebooklet.pdf). The claimant must search for assets and make reasonable efforts to collect. |

## New Jersey answer-choice review

The following sources were checked 2026-11-28 for the New Jersey handoff items.

| Item suffixes | Official text and rule checked |
|---|---|
| RG-ENTITY-002 and RG-TRUST-002 | [Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, 42:2C-27](https://pub.njleg.gov/Bills/2012/PL12/50_.HTM) and [Uniform Trust Code, 3B:31-81](https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2014/PL15/276_.PDF). Membership alone confers no authority to act for an LLC. A trust certification identifies who can sign for the trustees. |
| ATTORNEY-REVIEW-EMAIL and ATTORNEY-REVIEW-PERIOD | [New Jersey Supreme Court, Conley v. Guerrero](https://www.njcourts.gov/system/files/court-opinions/2017/a_65_15.pdf). The court approved emailed disapproval and kept the three-business-day period. |
| AGENCY-DISCLOSURE, BUYER-AGREEMENT and AGENCY-TERMINATION | [Real Estate Consumer Protection Enhancement Act, P.L. 2024 c.32](https://pub.njleg.gov/Bills/2024/AL24/32_.HTM). Agency disclosure within an agreement needs a separately titled paragraph. The law distinguishes commercial and residential purchases and requires written confirmation of termination by mutual agreement. |
| BRANCH-OFFICE-SUPERVISOR, BRANCH-IN-DWELLING and REFERRAL-LICENSE-SERVICE-LIMIT | [New Jersey Real Estate Commission, 2026 adopted rules](https://www.nj.gov/dobi/proposed/aa260120njac11_5.pdf) and [referral licensing FAQ](https://nj.gov/dobi/division_rec/licensing/referralagent.htm). Rules 11:5-4.5(b) and (c) govern branch locations and supervisors. A referral salesperson may refer prospects through the employing broker. |
| RG-CONDO-001 | [New Jersey Condominium Act](https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/codreg/pdf_regs/46_8B.pdf). The master deed and bylaws govern the condominium and treatment of structural common elements. |
| RG-POA-001 through RG-POA-003 | [Revised Durable Power of Attorney Act, chapter 109](https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2000/AL00/109_.HTM). Authority is limited to the actual grant. A power can take effect upon incapacity. The exception for good-faith use after death requires lack of actual knowledge. |
| RG-LAD-001 and RG-LAD-002 | [Division on Civil Rights, Housing Discrimination](https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-on-civil-rights-home/know-the-law/njlad/discrimination-in-housing/) and [LGBTQI+ housing rights](https://www.nj.gov/oag/dcr/downloads/fact_LGBTQI_Housing.pdf). Covered landlords and brokers cannot vary rental decisions or terms based on sexual orientation or gender identity. |
| REBATE-LENDER-NONDISCLOSURE | [New Jersey Legislature, P.L. 2009 c.273, section 2(a)(3)](https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2008/PL09/273_.PDF). A rebate must be disclosed to all parties, including a mortgage lender. |

The copy review checked the named Subdivided Lands Law, the exception for a subdivision restricted to industrial use, and the residential scope of exclusive listing agreements against [California DRE's public report guide](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/sprag.pdf) and [California real estate law excerpts](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/excerpts.pdf). The New York course-hours question asks about [salesperson licensure](https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-agent). For New Jersey, see [the commission's agency disclosure bulletin](https://www.nj.gov/dobi/bulletins/blt24_11.pdf) and [the Fair Housing Act's inclusionary zoning provisions](https://nj.gov/state/bac/planning/documents/fairhousact.pdf).

For the Louisiana oral-sale example, [Civil Code article 1839](https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=109078) gives both conditions for an oral transfer to bind the parties.
