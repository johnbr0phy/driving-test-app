# Sources for `afsp` (IRS Annual Filing Season Program: Annual Federal Tax Refresher (AFTR) course test)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the IRS's published AFTR course outline
and the Internal Revenue Code, revenue procedures, notices, fact sheets and IRS publications it
points to. No CE provider's course or test item, IRS review question, commercial course, book or
app item was used, reworded or adapted, and no provider is named. Checked 2026-11-18.

## Outline and format (official, watch these first)
- IRS, Publication 6079, "Annual Federal Tax Refresher (AFTR) Course Outline (For Preparation of
  2026 Individual 1040 Tax Returns)", Rev. 5-2026, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p6079.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18). Gave the three domains and every topic the bank follows: Domain 1 New Tax
  Law/Recent Updates (1.1 inflation adjustments from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 items .12, .29, .39, .42;
  1.2 Notice 2026-10 mileage; 1.3 Form 1099-K threshold; 1.4 $2,000 information reporting
  threshold; 1.5 dependent care credit at 50%; 1.6 adoption credit refundable $5,120; 1.7 AOTC and
  LLC SSN/EIN and math error; 1.8 30C ends for property placed in service after June 30, 2026;
  1.9 itemized deduction updates; 1.10 Schedule 1-A tips, overtime, car loan interest, seniors;
  1.11 charitable changes; 1.12 529 changes; 1.13 Trump accounts; 1.14 HSA changes), Domain 2
  General Review (2.1 filing status to 2.16 due dates and extensions) and Domain 3 Practices,
  Procedures and Professional Responsibility (3.1 identity theft to 3.8 Tax Pro Account). The
  outline sets no domain weights, so the bank's 14/26/10 per 50 split is Tigertest's.
- IRS, Publication 6012, "Annual Federal Tax Refresher (AFTR) Course Comprehension Test
  Parameters", Rev. 3-2026, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p6012.pdf (fetched 2026-11-18): each
  test has 100 multiple-choice questions with 4 answers and 1 correct answer covering all three
  domains; at most 3 continuous hours (180 minutes) with a visible countdown clock; at least 70%
  correct to pass; course and test completed by December 31 (midnight local time); every topic and
  sub-topic tested on each attempt; at most 10 lookup questions; a new version every third
  attempt. Format facts are used on the landing and in examFacts only, never as questions.
- IRS, Publication 6026, "2026 Annual Federal Tax Refresher (AFTR) Course", Rev. 3-2026,
  https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p6026.pdf (fetched 2026-11-18): the 2026 AFTR course and test
  support the 2027 AFSP; course is exactly 6 CE hours (300 minutes); offered June 1 to December
  31, 2026; at least one test question per outline topic; EAs get no AFTR credit.
- IRS, "Annual Filing Season Program frequently asked questions",
  https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/frequently-asked-questions-annual-filing-season-program
  (fetched 2026-11-18): the IRS does not give the test, approved CE providers write and run it;
  100 questions, 4 answers, 3 hours; 18 CE hours (6-hour AFTR, 10 federal tax law, 2 ethics),
  PTIN renewal and Circular 230 consent; exempt groups need 15 hours; no IRS fee; advertising rules;
  ineligibility under Rev. Proc. 2014-42.
- IRS, "Annual Filing Season Program", https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/annual-filing-season-program
  and "General requirements for the AFSP Record of Completion",
  https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/general-requirements-for-the-annual-filing-season-program-record-of-completion
  (both fetched 2026-11-18): program overview, limited representation, public directory.
- IRS, Publication 5646, AFSP CE requirements chart, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5646.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18): exempt categories and 3/10/2 = 15 hours; non-exempt 6 + 10 + 2 = 18.
- IRS, Revenue Procedure 2014-42, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-14-42.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-18): limited representation (examinations only; not appeals officers, revenue officers
  or Counsel), Record of Completion needed for the year prepared and the year of representation,
  advertising limits.

## General review rules
General review items (filing status, dependents, income, Schedule C, Schedule A, credits) follow
long-standing rules stated in IRS publications cited in each explanation (Pubs. 17, 501, 502,
504, 523, 525, 526, 530, 531, 535, 536, 544, 550, 559, 575, 583, 587, 590-A, 590-B, 596, 915,
936, 946, 970, 972, Instructions for Schedule A and Form 8867), with every 2026 dollar figure
taken from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 or the other official sources above.

## Left out on purpose
- Questions about the AFTR test itself (count, time, pass mark, attempts) and about CE provider
  rules: these are on the landing and in examFacts only.
- Phaseout rates for the tip, overtime, car loan and senior deductions (not on the IRS pages read)
  and the 400% FPL premium tax credit rule for 2026 (not confirmed on the pages read).
- Fees (the IRS charges none; providers set their own).
- Changes after the outline's May 2026 revision; the course may add them voluntarily.
