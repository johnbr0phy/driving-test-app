# HT(ASCP) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official content guideline and the
primary sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from ASCP BOC sample items, BOC study
guides, released forms, the NSH, or any commercial review book, question bank or prep site.

## Outline (ASCP Board of Certification)

- ASCP BOC, Histotechnician HT(ASCP) and HT(ASCPi) / Scientist in Histotechnology HTL(ASCP) and
  HTL(ASCPi) Examination Content Guideline (revised June 24, 2026, 6 pages), fetched 2026-11-18
  from ASCP's own content host:
  https://ascpcontentwebsite.blob.core.windows.net/boccontent/docs/default-source/explore-credentials/content-guidelines/ascp_ascpi_ht_htl_content_guideline.pdf
  One guideline covers both exams. Examination model: 100 questions in a 2-hour 30-minute time
  frame, all multiple choice with one best answer, given as computer adaptive testing (CAT);
  theoretical and procedural questions. Content areas: Fixation 15-25%, Processing 10-20%,
  Embedding/Microtomy 15-25%, Staining 30-40%, Laboratory Operations 10-15%. Regulatory
  questions are based on U.S. sources. Items marked HTL ONLY were left out of this HT bank:
  fixative and reagent chemistry principles, molecular preparation (RNase-free area, scrolls),
  enzyme histochemistry staining, IHC staining theory, antigen retrieval, control selection,
  antibody preparation and detection theory, ISH staining, refractive index of mounting media,
  and management and education principles. The guideline's footnotes say the HT exam asks IHC
  only on basic terminology, fixation and processing, and enzyme histochemistry and ISH only on
  fixation and processing; the bank follows that. Its fixative and stain lists (pages 5 and 6)
  set which reagents and stains are asked.
- ascp.org pages (About the Examination, procedures booklet) returned 403 to automated requests
  on 2026-11-18. A search of the BOC's own "About the Examination" page
  (https://www.ascp.org/boc/earn-a-credential/examination-process/about-the-examination,
  searched 2026-11-18) gives its summary: "The ASCP BOC uses a scale of 100 to 999 with a minimum
  passing score of 400." The copy states that figure as read from the BOC page's summary, the same
  way the MLT(ASCP) build did.

## Bank decisions

- The real exam is computer adaptive (100 items). Tigertest models it as four fixed 50-question
  forms, and the landing says so.
- Per 50-question test, inside the guideline's ranges: Fixation 10 (20%), Processing 7 (14%),
  Embedding/Microtomy 10 (20%), Staining 17 (34%: H&E, mounting, cytology, IHC terms and QC 6;
  carbohydrates, connective tissue, nerve and lipids 6; microorganisms, pigments, minerals, bone
  marrow and tissue components 5), Laboratory Operations 6 (12%). The split inside staining is
  Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target, not the BOC standard.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees; no state rules; no provider named.
- Duplicates: written against data/htl-questions.json (the HTL bank, same guideline, the closest
  sibling: its fixation, processing, embedding and staining facts were avoided, so the same topics
  are asked through other facts), data/ascp-mlt-questions.json and data/cst-questions.json, and
  scanned against every bank in data/ for the same key with an overlapping stem. Matches found and
  rewritten: an SDS first-aid item identical to rpt, fire class, hepatitis B vaccine timing, and the
  Gram iodine mordant item in phmedtech.
