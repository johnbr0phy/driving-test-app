# MLS(ASCP) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official content guideline and the
primary sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from ASCP BOC sample items, BOC study
guides, released forms, or any commercial review book, question bank or prep site, and no prep
provider is named.

## Outline (ASCP Board of Certification)

- ASCP BOC, Medical Laboratory Scientist MLS(ASCP) and MLS(ASCPi) Examination Content Guideline
  (revised September 25, 2025, 17 pages), fetched 2026-11-18 from ASCP's own content host:
  https://ascpcontentwebsite.blob.core.windows.net/boccontent/docs/default-source/explore-credentials/content-guidelines/ascp_ascpi_mls_content_guideline.pdf
  Examination model: 100 questions in a 2-hour 30-minute time frame, all multiple choice with one
  best answer, given as computer adaptive testing (CAT); theoretical questions (apply knowledge,
  calculate results, correlate results with disease states) and procedural questions (techniques
  and quality assurance). Content areas: Blood Banking 17-22% (blood products, blood group
  systems, blood group immunology, physiology and pathophysiology, serologic and molecular
  testing, transfusion practice); Urinalysis and Other Body Fluids 5-10%; Chemistry 17-22%
  (carbohydrates, lipids, heme derivatives, enzymes, proteins and other nitrogen-containing
  compounds, acid-base and blood gases, electrolytes, endocrinology, vitamins and nutrition,
  therapeutic drug monitoring, toxicology); Hematology 17-22% (physiology, disease states,
  laboratory testing, hemostasis); Immunology 5-10% (principles, diseases of the immune system,
  transplantation, infectious disease serology, serologic procedures); Microbiology 17-22%
  (preanalytic, bacteriology, mycobacteriology, virology, parasitology, mycology, postanalytic);
  Laboratory Operations 5-10% (quality assessment and troubleshooting, safety, laboratory
  mathematics, methodology and instrumentation, basic management principles, education
  principles). Regulatory questions are based on U.S. sources (AABB, FDA, CLIA); blood bank donor
  eligibility questions follow regulations as of June 2023. The guideline lists the calculations
  to know (transferrin saturation/UIBC/TIBC, indirect bilirubin, Friedewald LDL and non-HDL, A/G
  ratio, timed urine, creatinine clearance, Beer's law, corrected WBC with nRBCs, hemocytometer
  counts, red cell indices, absolute counts) and publishes composite reference ranges, which the
  bank uses wherever it calls a value normal or abnormal (MCHC 32-36 g/dL, CSF and seminal fluid
  ranges, semen liquefaction 30-60 minutes).
- The MLS guideline differs from the MLT one (same host, revised the same day) in its ranges:
  blood banking, chemistry, hematology and microbiology are each 17-22% (MLT 15-20 or 20-25%), and
  laboratory operations adds basic management and education principles.
- ascp.org pages (About the Examination, procedures booklet) returned 403 to automated requests,
  as they did for the MLT(ASCP) and HT(ASCP) builds. A search of the BOC's own "About the
  Examination" page (https://www.ascp.org/boc/earn-a-credential/examination-process/about-the-examination,
  searched 2026-11-18) gives its summary: "The ASCP BOC uses a scale of 100 to 999 with a minimum
  passing score of 400." The copy states that figure as read from the BOC page's summary.

## Law, regulation and standards used in questions

All eCFR text was read through the eCFR versioner API, point in time 2026-11-01, fetched
2026-11-18.

- FDA, 21 CFR 610.53 (dating periods for whole blood and blood components):
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-610/subpart-F/section-610.53
  Irradiated red cells 28 days from irradiation or original dating, whichever is shorter; open
  system deglycerolized 24 hours; closed system deglycerolized with additive 14 days; frozen red
  cells at -65 C or colder 10 years; FFP and PF24 1 year at -18 C; Liquid Plasma at 1 to 6 C, 5
  days from the end of the whole blood dating period; platelets 5 days at 20 to 24 C.
- FDA, 21 CFR 640.34 (plasma processing): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-640/subpart-D/section-640.34
  FFP placed in a freezer within 8 hours of collection (or the system's stated time).
- FDA, 21 CFR 640.54 and 640.56 (cryoprecipitated AHF): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-640/subpart-F
  An average of no less than 80 units of antihemophilic factor per final container; monthly
  potency testing of four containers.
- FDA, 21 CFR 640.24 (platelet processing), 21 CFR 630.15 (apheresis donor frequency) and
  21 CFR 606.160 (records, kept 10 years after processing or 6 months after the latest
  expiration, whichever is later): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-606
- FDA, 21 CFR 606.170 (adverse reaction file): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-606/subpart-I/section-606.170
  Fatal complications reported to CBER as soon as possible; written report within 7 days by the
  facility that did the compatibility testing.
- CMS, CLIA, 42 CFR 493.1105 (records kept at least 2 years; pathology reports 10 years),
  493.1253 (verification of accuracy, precision, reportable range and reference intervals for an
  unmodified FDA-cleared test; analytical sensitivity and specificity only for modified or
  laboratory-developed tests), 493.1451 (competency methods; semiannually in the first year,
  annually after): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-493
- OSHA, Bloodborne Pathogens, 29 CFR 1910.1030: hepatitis B vaccination made available within 10
  working days of initial assignment:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
- DOT, 49 CFR 173.199 (Category B infectious substances): triple packaging, primary receptacle,
  secondary packaging and rigid outer packaging:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-173/subpart-E/section-173.199
- CDC, Clinical Testing Guidance for HIV, page dated Aug. 17, 2026, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://www.cdc.gov/hivnexus/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html (antigen/antibody tests detect
  p24 with IgM and IgG; NATs detect RNA earliest).
- CDC, Clinical Testing and Diagnosis for Hepatitis B, page dated Nov. 2, 2026, fetched
  2026-11-18: https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html (anti-HBc persists
  for life and is absent after vaccination; IgM anti-HBc indicates infection within about 6
  months).
- CDC, Clinical Testing and Diagnosis for Lyme Disease, page dated Nov. 2, 2026, fetched
  2026-11-18: https://www.cdc.gov/lyme/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html (two-step testing;
  positive IgM results disregarded after more than 30 days of illness).
- CDC, Sexually Transmitted Infections Treatment Guidelines, Syphilis, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/syphilis.htm (reverse sequence algorithm: after a
  reactive treponemal screen and a negative nontreponemal test, a different treponemal test such
  as TP-PA adjudicates).
- Standard laboratory science facts on the guideline's topics (blood group serology and special
  reagents, urinalysis and body fluid findings, chemistry methods and interferences, hematology
  morphology, cytogenetics and flow markers, coagulation testing, immunology, microbiology
  identification and resistance, instrumentation, quality control and management). Every
  calculation is recomputed in build.py. Paid standards (AABB Standards, CLSI M100, H21, GP41)
  were not read, so the bank gives no AABB storage figures or CLSI breakpoints beyond the FDA and
  CLIA text above.

## Bank decisions

- The real exam is computer adaptive (100 items). Tigertest models it as four fixed 50-question
  forms, and the landing says so.
- Per 50-question test, inside the guideline's ranges: Blood Banking 10 (20%: products and
  transfusion 4, groups and serology 6), Urinalysis and Other Body Fluids 4 (8%), Chemistry 9
  (18%: general 5, acid-base, electrolytes, endocrine, vitamins, TDM and toxicology 4), Hematology
  10 (20%: cells and testing 6, hemostasis 4), Immunology 4 (8%), Microbiology 9 (18%),
  Laboratory Operations 4 (8%). The splits inside blood banking, chemistry and hematology are
  Tigertest's.
- Written at scientist depth: workups, interferences, correlations and method choices rather
  than single facts, to stay clear of the MLT(ASCP) bank.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target, not the BOC standard.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees; no state rules; no provider named.
- Duplicates: written against data/ascp-mlt-questions.json (closest, the same seven areas one
  level down), data/philippines-medtech-questions.json, data/ascp-ht-questions.json,
  data/ascp-pbt-questions.json, data/rpt-questions.json, data/phlebotomy-questions.json and
  data/htl-questions.json, then scanned against every v1 bank for the same key with an
  overlapping stem. About 30 items that repeated a fact and answer in phmedtech or ascpmlt
  (Bombay phenotype, Duffy and P. vivax, waxy casts, oval fat bodies, nitroprusside and
  beta-hydroxybutyrate, traumatic tap, seminal fructose, fructosamine, the Friedewald limit,
  the air bubble in a gas syringe, cold agglutinin indices, factor XIII and urea, the hepatitis B
  window and vaccine patterns, TCBS, BCYE, X and V factors, CAMP, germ tubes, the normality of
  0.5 M sulfuric acid and others) were replaced. Remaining overlaps are the same calculation type
  with different numbers (MCHC, coefficient of variation, corrected reticulocytes).
