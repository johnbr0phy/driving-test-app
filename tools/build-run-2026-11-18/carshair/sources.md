# Red Seal Hairstylist (carshair): sources

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the Red Seal Occupational Standard's
tasks and ranges of variables, Health Canada and CCOHS guidance and standard trade practice. No
Red Seal sample question, provincial study-guide item, textbook exercise or commercial practice
question was copied or paraphrased, and no training provider or product brand is named.
Checked 2026-11-18.

## Exam breakdown (official)
- Red Seal, "Hairstylist - Exam Information", https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/hairstylists/exam-information.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-18): 120 questions, all four-option multiple choice with one correct answer.
  Questions per Major Work Activity and task: A Performs common occupational skills 14 (A-1 4,
  A-2 4, A-3 3, A-4 3); B Performs hair and scalp care 11 (B-5 6, B-6 5); C Cuts hair 24 (C-7 19,
  C-8 5); D Styles hair 16 (D-9 8, D-10 8); E Performs chemical texture services on hair 17 (E-11
  10, E-12 7); F Alters hair colour 25 (F-13 9, F-14 7, F-15 9); G Performs specialized services 6
  (G-16 4, G-17 2); H Performs salon operations 7 (H-18 4, H-19 3). Question types: knowledge and
  recall 10 to 20%, procedural and application 75 to 85%, critical thinking under 10%.
- Red Seal, "Examination Weightings" (Hairstylist), https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/hairstylists/exam-weightings.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-18): MWA percentages A 12, B 9, C 20, D 13, E 14, F 21, G 5, H 6 and every task
  percentage (A-1 31% ... H-19 35%), matching the question counts above.
- Red Seal Occupational Standard: Hairstylist, https://www.red-seal.ca/_conf/assets/custom/docms/hairstylist/rsos.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18, © 2019, Cat. No. Em15-3/22-2019E-PDF, read in full as text): the task
  matrix, sub-tasks and ranges of variables used to choose topics: disinfection methods, PPE and
  safety equipment, sharps, WHMIS and SDS, PIPEDA, capes and draping, active listening,
  mentoring steps and practice conditions, learning styles, the definition of harassment; hair
  structure, growth stages, scalp disorders (head lice, ringworm, cradle cap, psoriasis,
  hypo- and hyperpigmentation, alopecia), beaded and knotted hair, health and environmental
  factors, patch, strand and elasticity tests, scalp massage benefits; guidelines, cutting
  techniques (point cutting, slide cutting, disconnection, undercutting, slithering, chunking,
  feathering), haircuts with and without elevation (tapers, fades, A-line bobs, page boy), detailing
  techniques (inverted trimmers, rocking the clipper blade, trimmer blade laid flat), beard and
  moustache styles; roller placements, pin curl stems, styling and finishing aids, thermal tool
  safety, hair additions (clip-ins, tape-ins, fusion, micro-link, sew-in); wrapping techniques
  and patterns, waving solutions (acid-balanced, buffered alkaline, alkaline, exothermic,
  endothermic), air oxidizing, relaxers (sodium hydroxide, ammonium thioglycolate, no-lye,
  smoothing and re-forming systems), pH reducer; colour types, developers 5 to 40 volume, law of
  colour, level system, oil, cream and powder lighteners, toners, colour removers, colour
  placement (foiling, blocking, freehand, stencilling); wig measurements, fibre types, depilatory
  services (waxing, sugaring, tweezing, threading), 5 and 10 volume developer for facial hair;
  business ownership models, insurance needs, target market, financial planning, professional
  behaviours and sales techniques. Appendix B lists styptic powder as a single-use item.
- Red Seal, Hairstylist trade page, https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/hairstylist.shtml (fetched
  2026-11-18): designated Red Seal in AB, BC, MB, NB, NL, NS, NT, NU, PE, QC, SK and YT. Ontario
  is not on the list.

## Exam format (official)
- Red Seal Exam Preparation Guide, https://red-seal.ca/eng/resources/exam-prep-guide.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-18): multiple choice, four choices with one correct answer, one mark per
  question, pass mark 70%, writing time four hours. Retake rules are set by each province or
  territory.
- Skilled Trades Ontario, Hairstylist trade page, https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/hairstylist/
  (fetched 2026-11-18): Ontario trade code 332A; the trade is compulsory in Ontario; Hairstylist is
  an interprovincial Red Seal trade but Ontario does not offer the Red Seal endorsement, so
  Ontario hairstylists write the provincial Certificate of Qualification exam. The copy calls this
  bank Red Seal practice and says Ontario's exam is provincial.

## Chemical and workplace safety
- CCOHS, "Hairdressers" OSH Answers, https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/occup_workplace/hairdresser.html
  (fetched 2026-11-18): hair dyes, persulfate bleaches, perm and straightening solutions and
  formaldehyde-releasing smoothing treatments as hazards; allergic contact dermatitis; local
  exhaust ventilation; gloves chosen for the product; WHMIS training, labels and SDS; ergonomics
  (adjustable chairs, anti-fatigue mats); wet floors; electrical safety near water.
- Health Canada, "Hair dyes", https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/cosmetics/hair-dyes.html
  (fetched 2026-11-18): skin test behind the ear or on the inner forearm, washed after 24 hours,
  repeated before each use; sensitivity may appear only after several uses; label warnings
  against use on eyebrows and eyelashes.
- WHMIS pictograms (flame over circle for oxidizing hazards, supplier and workplace labels):
  Canada's WHMIS rules under the Hazardous Products Regulations, as summarized in the CCOHS page
  above and the RSOS's WHMIS objective.

## Technique facts
Cutting, styling, waving, relaxing, colouring and lightening technique items (developer volume to
percentage, mixing developers, roller and pin curl placement, wrap patterns, test curls, relaxer
application order, fillers, colour removers, toning bases, clipper guard sizes in eighths of an
inch) are standard trade practice within the RSOS ranges listed above, stated at the level every
Canadian hairstyling program teaches. Where sources commonly disagree on a figure (relaxer pH
values, exact processing times, temperatures for thermal tools, underlying pigment charts level
by level), the item asks the principle or a broad band instead, and defers to the manufacturer's
directions.

## Could not confirm, or left out
- The Public Health Agency of Canada head lice page returned 404, so lice items stay at the
  RSOS level (a condition to identify); none is keyed on a lice rule.
- Health Canada's formaldehyde advisories on hair smoothing products (2010 and 2011) are archived;
  no item rests on a formaldehyde limit.
- Provincial rules (licensing, sanitation regulations for salons, disinfectant contact times,
  apprenticeship hours, fees, retakes) vary by province or territory and are not asked; the
  landing says so.
- The Red Seal pages give no publication date for the exam breakdown; the RSOS PDF is dated 2019.

## Blueprint (Tigertest)
The real exam is 120 questions; each practice test is 50, each MWA scaled by 50/120 and rounded by
largest remainder: A 6, B 5, C 10, D 7, E 7, F 10, G 2, H 3. Within an MWA the split follows the
task counts: A-1 and A-2 3, A-3 and A-4 3; C-7 8, C-8 2; E-11 4, E-12 3; F-13 4, F-14 3, F-15 3.
Pools are four times each count (200 in all), so every question appears in exactly one test.
Five training sets in MWA order, then four tests: 9 dashboard steps. Pass line 70%, the real
pass mark.
