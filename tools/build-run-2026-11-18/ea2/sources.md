# Sources for `ea2` (IRS Enrolled Agent Special Enrollment Examination, Part 2: Businesses)

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the IRS's published Part 2 content
outline and the Internal Revenue Code, Treasury regulations and IRS forms, instructions and
publications it points to. No IRS sample question, released SEE item, PSI material, commercial
review course, book or app item was used, reworded or adapted. Checked 2026-11-18.

## Outline and format (official)
- PSI Services LLC for the IRS, "Candidate Information Bulletin, Internal Revenue Service,
  Enrolled Agent Special Enrollment Examination", REVISED October 9, 2026 (48 pages),
  fetched 2026-11-18: https://test-takers.psigov.us/api/content/bulletin/12238
  It gave:
  - Part 2 Examination Content Outline with scored questions per domain: Business Entities and
    Considerations 30; Business Tax Preparation 37; Specialized Returns and Taxpayers 18 (85
    scored), with each domain's topic list (business entities, partnerships, corporations,
    S corporations; business income, expenses, deductions and credits, business assets, analysis
    of financial records, advising the business taxpayer; trust and estate income tax, exempt
    organizations, retirement plans, farmers, rental property). Used domain by domain.
  - Each part: 100 multiple-choice questions, 85 scored and 15 unscored, 3.5 hours.
  - "For exams taken between July 1, 2026-February 28, 2027, all references ... are to the
    Internal Revenue Code, Code of Federal Regulations, IRS forms, instructions, and
    publications, as amended through December 31, 2025. Unless otherwise stated, all questions
    relate to the calendar year 2025." So every item uses 2025 rules.
- IRS, "Enrolled agents frequently asked questions", fetched 2026-11-16 for ea1 and rechecked
  2026-11-18: https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/enrolled-agents/enrolled-agents-frequently-asked-questions
  Three parts, 100 questions (85 scored), 3.5 hours, scaled 200 to 800 with 500 to pass, PSI as
  vendor, the 2026-27 window, three-year carryover of passed parts.

## Modelling
- Four 50-question mock tests, the bulletin's 30/37/18 of 85 scaled to 50 as 18/22/10, split by
  Tigertest into eight topic categories: entities 4, partnerships 6, C and S corporations 8
  (domain 1); business income and expenses 9, business assets 6, records, methods and advising 7
  (domain 2); trusts, estates and exempt organizations 5, retirement plans, farmers and rentals 5
  (domain 3). Pools are exactly four times the per-test counts, so no question repeats across
  tests.
- The 70% line is Tigertest's readiness target. The real result is a scaled score with 500 to
  pass, which is not a fixed percentage.
- The real Part 2 is 100 questions (85 scored) in 3.5 hours; ours is 50 questions at the same pace.

## Left out on purpose
- Exam fees, penalty dollar amounts and indexed 2025 thresholds (section 179 limit, the
  gross receipts test amount, QBI thresholds, luxury auto limits, retirement plan dollar limits,
  standard mileage rate, 45R wage limit), and provisions starting in 2026 (the 1% corporate
  charitable floor, the $2,000 Form 1099-NEC threshold). The $600 Form 1099-NEC threshold is
  named only for 2025 payments.
- Part 3 topics (representation, Circular 230, collections), which ea3 will cover.
- The CAMT minimum tax credit item was dropped: section 53(e) text was not confirmed.
