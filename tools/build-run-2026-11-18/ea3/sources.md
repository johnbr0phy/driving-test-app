# Sources for `ea3` (IRS Enrolled Agent Special Enrollment Examination, Part 3: Representation, Practices and Procedures)

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the IRS's published Part 3 content
outline and the primary texts it points to: Treasury Department Circular 230, the Internal
Revenue Code, Treasury regulations, and IRS forms, instructions and publications. No IRS sample
question, released SEE item, PSI material, commercial review course, book or app item was
used, reworded or adapted. Checked 2026-11-18.

## Outline and format (official)
- PSI Services LLC for the IRS, "Candidate Information Bulletin, Internal Revenue Service,
  Enrolled Agent Special Enrollment Examination", REVISED October 9, 2026, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://test-takers.psigov.us/api/content/bulletin/12238
  It gave:
  - Part 3 Examination Content Outline with scored questions per domain: Practices and
    Procedures 26; Representation before the IRS 25; Specific Areas of Representation 20;
    Filing Process 14 (85 scored), with each domain's topic list (used domain by domain).
  - Each part: 100 multiple-choice questions, 85 scored and 15 unscored, 3.5 hours, on-screen
    calculator; two scheduled 10-minute breaks after questions 34 and 67, with no return to a
    finished section; closed book.
  - Scaled scores 200 to 800; the IRS passing score is 500. A pass shows only "pass"; a fail
    shows a scaled score from 200 to 499.
  - "For exams taken between July 1, 2026-February 28, 2027, all references on the examination
    are to the Internal Revenue Code, Code of Federal Regulations, IRS forms, instructions, and
    publications, as amended through December 31, 2025 ... candidates should not consider any
    legislation, court decisions, or penalty amounts after December 31, 2025."
  - Study materials named: the Code, CFR, Treasury Department Circular 230, IRS forms,
    instructions and publications.
  - Up to four attempts per part in a window; passed parts carry over three years.
- IRS, "Enrolled agents frequently asked questions", fetched 2026-11-18 (window July 1, 2026 to
  Feb. 28, 2027, no testing in March and April, four attempts per part, PSI as vendor; also
  confirms 72 CE hours per cycle and 16 a year with 2 of ethics):
  https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/enrolled-agents/enrolled-agents-frequently-asked-questions
