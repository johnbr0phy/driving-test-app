# ExCPT (NHA) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official test plan and the
primary sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from NHA study guides, NHA practice
tests, released items, or any commercial review book or prep site. Federal law only: where a
rule is set by each state (who may take phoned-in orders, technician ratios, generic
substitution), the bank says so or leaves it out.

## Outline (National Healthcareer Association)

- NHA, ExCPT Test Plan, based on the 2023 job analysis, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://info.nhanow.com/hubfs/Test%20Plans/2023%20ExCPT%20Test%20Plan.pdf
  100 scored items and 20 pretest items, 2 hours 10 minutes. Scored items by domain (domain 1
  is the technician's role, practice settings, communication, terminology and inventory):
  1 15; 2A federal requirements 9 and 2B controlled substances 6; 3A drug classification and
  interactions 8 and 3B top drugs 5; 4 Dispensing Process, as 4A Prescription and Medication
  Order Intake and Entry 13, 4B Preparing and Dispensing Prescriptions 13, 4C Calculations 7,
  4D Sterile and Non-Sterile Products, Compounding, Unit Dose, and Repackaging 10; 5 Medication
  Safety and Quality Assurance 14. Tasks and knowledge
  statements K.1 to K.164 were used category by category. Tigertest scales 15/9/6/8/5/13/13/7/10/14
  to 7/5/3/4/3/6/6/4/5/7 of 50.
- NHA media center, "Coming soon: new ExCPT certification exam study materials", fetched
  2026-11-18:
  https://info.nhanow.com/mediacenter/coming-soon-new-excpt-certification-exam-study-materials
  The new ExCPT exam (on the 2023 job analysis plan) is for exams on or after July 9, 2025, with
  study materials from March 12, 2025; NHA says the USP <795> and <797> beyond-use date changes
  have no impact on the exam.
- NHA, Certified Pharmacy Technician (CPhT) certification page, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://www.nhanow.com/certification/nha-certifications/certified-pharmacy-technician-(cpht)
  Eligibility: high school diploma or GED (or earning one within 12 months), plus a pharmacy
  technician training program within the last 5 years or 1 year (1,200 hours) of supervised
  pharmacy work within the last 3 years. Exam of 100 scored questions at a PSI testing center or
  by live remote proctoring; results online within two days. Renewal every 2 years with 20
  continuing education credits, including 1 in pharmacy law and 1 in patient safety; an expired
  certification can be reinstated within 1 year.
- NHA, annual pass rates, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/annual-pass-rates/nha-annual-pass-rates.pdf
  Scaled passing score 390 of 500 on every NHA exam. ExCPT 2024: 16,519 exams, 62.22% passed,
  44,599 active certificants. The bank's 70% line is a readiness marker, not the real rule.

## Controlled substances (DEA)

- DEA, Pharmacist's Manual, 2022 edition, fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/GDP/(DEA-DC-046R1)(EO-DEA154R1)_Pharmacist's_Manual_DEA.pdf
  DEA number letters (A, B or F for practitioners, M for mid-level practitioners), dispensing
  only to the patient or a member of the household, automated dispensing systems at long-term
  care facilities (drugs not dispensed until the system provides them), mailing (outer wrapper
  free of markings showing the contents), prescription fraud signs (directions written in full
  with no abbreviations).
- eCFR, 21 CFR parts 1300 to 1316 (versioner API, as of 2026-11-01), fetched 2026-11-18:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-11-01/title-21.xml?part=1306
  1301.76(b) theft notice in one business day and DEA Form 106 within 45 days; 1304.04(h) red C
  at least 1 inch high; 1304.11(e) biennial inventory, exact count of an opened container over
  1,000 units; 1305.05 power of attorney with two witnesses; 1305.13 Form 222 valid 60 days;
  1306.11 emergency oral Schedule II and "Authorization for Emergency Dispensing"; 1306.13
  partial fills (72 hours; 60 days for LTCF and terminally ill patients); 1306.14 and 1306.24
  label contents; 1306.15 and 1306.27 CENTRAL FILL; 1306.26 OTC Schedule V sales (pharmacist
  only, age 18, bound record); 1308.12 to 1308.15 schedules.
