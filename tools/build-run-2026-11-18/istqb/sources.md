# Sources for `istqb` (ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level, CTFL v4.0)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the learning objectives and body text of
the ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1 syllabus. No ISTQB sample exam question, member board or exam provider mock
exam, training provider question or commercial practice question was copied or reworded, and no
training or exam provider is named. Checked 2026-11-18.

## Syllabus (official, confirmed)
- ISTQB, "Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) v4.0 Overview",
  https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-foundation-level-ctfl-v4-0/ (fetched 2026-11-18):
  the certificate page. Lists the six chapters (Fundamentals of Testing; Testing Throughout the SDLC;
  Static Testing; Test Analysis and Design; Managing the Test Activities; Test Tools), the exam
  structure (40 questions, 40 points, passing score 26, 60 minutes, +25% in a non-native language)
  and the download links for the syllabus, sample exams and exam structure files.
- ISTQB, "Certified Tester Foundation Level Syllabus v4.0.1" (copyright 2024),
  https://istqb.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ISTQB_CTFL_Syllabus_v4.0.1.pdf (the page's syllabus
  download, fetched 2026-11-18). Its revision history dates v4.0.1 to 15.09.2024 as an errata of v4.0
  (21.04.2023), and Appendix C says the errata aligns learning objective wording with glossary terms.
  It gives every learning objective with its K-level (FL-1.1.1 to FL-6.2.1) and
  the body text every question is written from: test objectives, testing and debugging, testing and
  QA, error/defect/failure/root cause, the seven principles, test activities and testware,
  traceability, the two roles, generic skills, whole team approach, independence; SDLC impact, good
  practices, TDD/ATDD/BDD, DevOps, shift left, retrospectives, the five test levels, four test types,
  confirmation and regression testing, maintenance triggers; static testing value and differences,
  the ISO/IEC 20246 review activities, review roles, the four review types, success factors; EP
  (including Each Choice coverage), 2-value and 3-value BVA, decision tables (T, F, -, N/A notation,
  feasible columns as coverage items), state transition testing (all states, valid transitions
  (0-switch) and all transitions coverage), statement and branch coverage, error guessing and fault
  attacks, exploratory and session-based testing, checklist-based testing, user stories (3 C's,
  INVEST), acceptance criteria formats, ATDD; test plan content, release and iteration planning,
  entry and exit criteria (Definition of Ready and Done), the four estimation techniques with
  E = (a + 4m + b) / 6 and SD = (b - a) / 6, risk-, coverage- and requirements-based prioritization
  (including additional coverage and dependencies), the test pyramid, the testing quadrants (Q1 to
  Q4), risk likelihood, impact and level (quantitative as a product, qualitative by risk matrix),
  project and product risks, risk analysis and control, test metrics, progress and completion
  reports, communicating status, configuration management (baselines), defect management and defect
  report content; tool categories and the benefits and risks of test automation.
- No newer CTFL version was announced: the certificate page is titled "v4.0", its syllabus download
  is v4.0.1, and the ISTQB news page (https://istqb.org/news/, fetched 2026-11-18) lists new and
  updated certifications in November 2026 (usability, accessibility, game, security, expert test
  management) but no new Foundation Level syllabus.

## Exam format (official, confirmed)
- ISTQB, "Exam Structure Tables" v1.21 (9 November 2026),
  https://istqb.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ISTQB_Exam-Structure-Tables_v1.21.pdf (the page's
  "Exam Structures and Rules" download, fetched 2026-11-18): CTFL v4.0 is 40 questions worth 40
  points (one point each), passing score 26 (65%), 60 minutes, 75 minutes with the 25% extension.
  K-levels: K1 8, K2 24, K3 8, K4 0. Questions per chapter: chapter 1 8 (K1 2, K2 6), chapter 2 6
  (K1 2, K2 4), chapter 3 4 (K1 2, K2 2), chapter 4 11 (K2 6, K3 5, the K3 group being FL-4.2.1 to
  FL-4.2.4 and FL-4.5.3), chapter 5 9 (K1 1, K2 5, K3 3, the K3 group being FL-5.1.4, FL-5.1.5 and
  FL-5.5.1), chapter 6 2 (K1 1, K2 1). The bank's per-test blueprint follows these chapter counts.
- ISTQB, "Exam Structures and Rules" v1.2 (2 May 2025),
  https://istqb.org/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=3829 (fetched 2026-11-18): exam questions are
  multiple choice; every learning objective is examinable; every syllabus keyword is examinable for
  its definition at K2; questions may draw on more than one section of the syllabus.
- Format facts used on the landing only: 40 questions, 60 minutes (75 minutes in a non-native
  language), pass at 26 of 40, multiple choice. Exam delivery (test centres, online proctoring),
  languages, fees and retake rules are set by each ISTQB member board or exam provider, so the
  landing says "check with your exam provider" and states none of them. Certificate validity is
  not stated in the documents read, so the landing says nothing about it.

## What was left out
- The official sample exams (download on the certificate page) were not opened, so no question
  could be shaped by them.
- No question asks about the exam itself (counts, timing, pass mark, providers).
- The CTFL-Agile Tester and CTFL v3.1 syllabi (v3.1 has sunset) are not used.
- The bank uses six "Choose two" items, all drill-only; the two practice tests are single answer.
- Pseudocode in coverage items is Tigertest's own notation; the syllabus uses no fixed language.
