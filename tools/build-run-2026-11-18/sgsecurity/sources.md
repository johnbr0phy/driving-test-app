# Sources for `sgsecurity` (Singapore security officer licence, WSQ Basic Licensing Units)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the sources below. No assessment item
from any training provider, no PLRD refresher e-quiz item and no commercial practice question was
copied. The PLRD refresher training package is written as short Q&A notes; its facts were used, but
every stem, option set and scenario here was written fresh. Checked 2026-11-18.

## Licence requirement and the Basic Licensing Units (official)

- Singapore Police Force, "Security Progressive Wage Model Requirements" (Apply for Security
  Officer Licence), https://www.police.gov.sg/E-Services/Apply-for-Security-Officer-Licence/Security-Progressive-Wage-Model-Requirements
  (fetched 2026-11-18): a new security officer must complete four mandatory WSQ modules: Handle
  Security Incidents and Services, Provide Guard and Patrol Services, Recognise Terrorist Threats
  and Operate Basic Security Equipment; completion is mandatory for continued deployment from
  1 January 2027 for existing officers who lack the access control module; WSQ certifications are
  needed before screening with X-ray machines or metal detectors.
- Singapore Police Force, "FAQs on Enhanced Training Requirements for Security Officers",
  https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Files/E-services/SO/FAQs-on-Enhanced-Training-Requirements-for-Security-Officers-Updated.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18, PDF): since 1 January 2024 new entrant SOs must pass TSC Guard and Patrol
  (Provide Guard & Patrol Services) and TSC Access Control Management (Operate Basic Security
  Equipment), TSC Incident Response (Handle Security Incidents and Services) and TSC Threat
  Observation (Recognise Terrorist Threats) before deployment; existing SOs licensed before
  1 January 2024 must be certified in the access control module by 31 December 2026 or cannot be
  deployed from 1 January 2027 (a standalone course is enough for those who passed the old Guard
  and Patrol unit); the HSIS curriculum was enhanced from 1 January 2024; a refresher e-quiz
  (run by POLWEL) has been mandatory for licence renewal since 1 January 2024. The fee figure in
  the FAQ is not used.
- Singapore Police Force (PLRD), "Refresher E-Quiz for Security Officers: Training Package",
  https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Files/E-services/SO/Refresher-Quiz-Training-Package.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18, PDF, 17 pages; the older .ashx address returns a 404 page). PLRD's own
  summary of the key information from the Basic Licensing Units: SEC-SOP-1007-1.1 Guard and Patrol
  (taking over duty, fire alarm, metal detector and CCTV purposes, construction site PPE, delivery
  orders, suspicious mail, information not to share, call signs, searches on company instruction,
  visitors and vehicles, smoking, the guardhouse phone, emergency calls, key control, banned
  persons, lost property, covered CCTV, a chemical incident, a reporter's call, a theft at the
  gate); SEC-ICM-1003-1.1 Incident Response (duties, building layout, defects, guarding a detained
  suspect, criminal offences, unauthorised surveillance, suspicious behaviour and attire, terror
  methods, fire extinguishers and call points, 995 and 1777, sprains, cuts, burns, extinguisher
  steps, injured persons, a hose reel leak, a fire with people trapped, a bomb threat call);
  SEC-OBS-1002-1.1 Threat Observation (terrorism, lone wolf and group attacks, trends, methods,
  casing, impact, the four stages and target types, person-borne, vehicle-borne and mail IED
  signs, UAV casing, a suspicious person filming, a car circling, a suspicious parcel with the
  100 m cordon); plus employment rights, the Conduct Regulations, protection for officers (USE
  Mobile app), basic conflict management, workplace safety and health, and the PDPA. Penalty
  amounts and the wage hotline are not used.
- Security Tripartite Cluster, "Recommendations of the Security Tripartite Cluster on Enhanced
  Training Requirements for the Security Industry", 31 January 2023 (Ministry of Manpower),
  https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/employment-practices/pwm/stc-report-on-enhanced-training-requirements-jan-2023.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18, PDF): Annex B lists the four BLUs with TSC codes (SEC-SOP-1007-1.1 level 1,
  SEC-TEM-2001-1.1 level 2, SEC-ICM-1003-1.1 level 1, SEC-OBS-1002-1.1 level 1), four Statements of
  Attainment, the OBSE unit added and HSIS enhanced to cover disorderly behaviour and the PSIA
  protections, from 1 January 2024.
- PLRD, "Licensing Conditions for Security Officers" (revised as of 12 December 2024),
  https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Files/E-services/SO/SO-Licensing-Conditions-Revised-as-of-12122024.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18, PDF): condition 1 (above 60 working for a non-agency employer: medical
  certificate before deployment and yearly), condition 2 (Security PWM WSQ requirements, no job
  functions above one's grade; higher grades may do lower grades' functions), condition 3 (no more
  than two employers at one time), condition 4 (WSQ certifications for X-ray machines and for
  walk-through and handheld metal detectors), condition 6 (patrol dogs need prior written
  approval of the Licensing Officer).

## Law (official)

- Private Security Industry Act 2007 (2020 Revised Edition, current version as at 18 Nov 2026,
  last amended by Act 23 of 2025 with effect from 29 June 2026), Singapore Statutes Online,
  https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PSIA2007 (fetched 2026-11-18; provisions read through
  `?ProvIds=P13-`, `P15-` and the whole-document view): s 2 definitions (bouncer, screening,
  frisk search), s 3 (not applicable to police officers and auxiliary police officers in their
  functions), s 13 meaning of security officer, s 14 licence needed (including advertising or
  holding out), s 16 employer notifications, ss 17A to 17F protection of officers from assault,
  hurt and harassment (17E knowledge requirement), s 21 (fit and proper matters, false statements
  in an application), s 22 conditions, s 23 validity up to 5 years, s 24 revocation, suspension up
  to 6 months, immediate suspension and 14-day review, s 25 effect, s 26 appeal to the Minister
  within 14 days. Fine and penalty amounts are not used.
- Private Security Industry (Conduct) Regulations 2009 (current version as at 18 Nov 2026,
  last amended by S 590/2025), https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/PSIA2007-S170-2009 (fetched 2026-11-18):
  reg 3 no obstruction of public officers, reg 4 no unauthorised sharing of security information
  (system, layout, numbers deployed, arrangements) and its exceptions, reg 5 police report without
  undue delay on surveillance of the premises, reg 6 no tampering with security equipment, reg 7
  entry and exit of unauthorised persons at the premises it covers and handing over to a police
  officer without unnecessary delay, reg 7A on-duty conduct (no sleeping, alcohol, absence without
  valid reason, threatening or abusive language; prompt response to requests for help; no false
  representation of training). The Schedule (Code of Conduct) did not load and is not used.
  SSO limits automated requests; some provision views returned an empty 202 response and were
  retried.

## Emergencies, first aid and terrorism (official)

- Singapore Civil Defence Force, "Civil Defence Emergency Handbook", 10th edition (2026),
  https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/comm.---volunteers-(docs)/(2026)-scdf-emergency-handbook-(english).pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-18, PDF): bleeding, fractures, burns (four Cs, 10 minutes, severe over 5%),
  seizures, stroke (F.A.S.T.), sprains (R.I.C.E., 15 to 20 minutes), choking (abdominal and chest
  thrusts), CPR (at least 100 a minute, about 5 cm), AED (pacemaker, paediatric pads, battery
  prompt), fire safety (fire floor and two above, extinguisher types table, P.A.S.S., fire
  blankets, call points, hose reels, refuge floors and fire lifts, smoke inhalation, gas leaks,
  EV, solar PV and active mobility device fires, stop drop and roll), power outages and lifts,
  tremors, unruly crowds, the Public Warning System signals, chemical and biological threats,
  anthrax, bomb threats and suspicious mail, explosions, In-Place Protection, SGSecure and useful
  numbers (995, 999, 70999, 1777, 1800 752 1800 and others).
- SGSecure (Ministry of Home Affairs), "What to do during and after a terror attack",
  https://www.sgsecure.gov.sg/advice-to-stay-safe/ (fetched 2026-11-18): Run, Hide, Tell; Press,
  Tie, Tell (5 cm above the wound, windlass, tell SCDF the time tied); 999, SMS 70999, SGSecure
  app; not sharing unverified videos.

## Format of the real assessments

- The Basic Licensing Units are WSQ courses assessed by approved training providers, each leading
  to a Statement of Attainment (STC report, PLRD FAQ). Neither PLRD nor SkillsFuture Singapore
  publishes a question count, time or pass mark for the unit assessments, so the landing and
  examFacts give none; 70% is Tigertest's target, labelled so. Practical assessment is not
  modelled.
- SkillsFuture Singapore's Skills Framework for Security page
  (https://www.skillsfuture.gov.sg/skills-framework/security) renders only by script and returned
  no content; the TSC reference documents could not be read. The unit content therefore comes
  from PLRD's own BLU summary (the refresher training package) and the STC report.

## Left out on purpose

- Fines, penalty amounts, fees (the refresher e-quiz fee, course fees), PWM wage amounts and the
  CPF threshold.
- Questions about the courses or assessments themselves (which units, deadlines, the refresher
  quiz): those facts are on the landing and in examFacts only.
- The Conduct Regulations' Schedule (Code of Conduct), which did not load.
- Senior Security Officer and supervisor units (Deterrence, Remote Surveillance) and screening
  certifications beyond naming them.
