# Sources for `ita2` (Test di italiano A2 per il permesso di soggiorno UE di lungo periodo, in Italian)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Italian for Tigertest. Every notice, letter,
message, article and set of instructions was invented for this bank, with fictional names, places
and figures. No item was copied or reworded from any Prefettura or CPIA test, past paper, sample
test, textbook or practice site; none were opened. Sources fetched 2026-11-19.

## Law and exam owner: Ministero dell'Interno (Prefettura) with the Ministero dell'Istruzione (CPIA)

- Decreto 7 dicembre 2021, Ministero dell'Interno, "Modalità di svolgimento del test di conoscenza
  della lingua italiana" (Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 36 del 12 febbraio 2022), fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2022/02/12/36/sg/pdf
  - in force; replaces the D.M. 4 giugno 2010 with the same rules for the test itself. Art. 2: A2
    level, "comprendere frasi ed espressioni di uso frequente in ambiti correnti". Art. 3: request
    online to the Prefettura of the place of domicile; convocation within 60 days; the test is on
    computer (on paper on request), "strutturato sulla comprensione di brevi testi e sulla capacità
    di interazione"; pass with at least 80% of the total score; a failed test can be retaken with a
    new request. Art. 4: exemptions (A2 certificate from a recognised body, CPIA A2 course,
    integration agreement credits, Italian school diploma, university courses, certain art. 27
    workers). Art. 1: not for children under 14 or people with serious certified learning limits.
  - text cross-checked against a copy of the same decree as printed from gazzettaufficiale.it
    (https://lentepubblica.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Decreto-ministeriale-7-dicembre-2021.pdf).
- Decreto 4 giugno 2010 (Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 134 dell'11 giugno 2010), Prefettura copy, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://prefettura.interno.gov.it/sites/default/files/75/2024-08/decreto-4.6.2010-modalita-di-svolgimento.pdf
  - the original rules, same art. 2 and 3 wording and the 80% pass mark.
- Ministero dell'Interno, Dipartimento per le libertà civili e l'immigrazione, leaflet "Il test di
  lingua italiana per gli stranieri", fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://prefettura.interno.gov.it/sites/default/files/29/2024-03/brochure_test_italiano.pdf
  - who must take it (regular residence for at least five years, valid permit, age 14+), request
    and result at https://portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it, bring the letter and an ID, no certificate
    is issued for a pass, 80% to pass.
- Prefettura di Potenza, "Test di conoscenza della lingua italiana" information sheet, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://prefettura.interno.gov.it/sites/default/files/77/2024-06/test_di_conoscenza_della_lingua_italiana.doc
  - the test is held at the competent CPIA; a candidate absent without a medical certificate, or
    who fails, can book again after 90 days (that Prefettura's rule; the copy says "in molte
    prefetture").

## Format and content: Vademecum (MIUR, under Ministero dell'Interno note n. 8571 of 16 December 2010)

- "Vademecum: indicazioni tecnico-operative per la definizione dei contenuti delle prove che
  compongono il test, criteri di assegnazione del punteggio e durata del test", hosted by the
  Regione Valle d'Aosta, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://gestionewww.regione.vda.it/allegato.aspx?pk=18457
  - three prove, 60 minutes net: comprensione orale (two short texts, 10 items, 25 minutes, 30% of
    the score), comprensione scritta (two short texts, 350 to 450 words in all, 10 items, 25
    minutes, 35%), interazione scritta (one task, 10 minutes, 35%: reply to an email, postcard or
    invitation giving three pieces of information, or fill in a form). Item types: multiple
    choice, matching, true/false.
  - reading sub-skills: lettura della corrispondenza; lettura per orientarsi (signs, notices,
    timetables, menus, ads); lettura per informarsi (short articles, leaflets); lettura di
    istruzioni (safety rules, machines such as a cash or ticket machine, how to get a document).
  - domains private, public and occupational (table 4), example words (table 5) and the A2
    socio-communicative acts (table 6: greeting, thanking, inviting, asking permission, giving
    reasons, asking to repeat, simple "se piove non vengo" hypotheses). These shaped the categories.

## Modelling decisions and what is left out

- Modelled: reading (four sub-skills, 20 of 50 per test), written interaction as single-answer
  items (choose the reply, the email sentence, the form field: 6 per test), socio-communicative
  acts (6), and A2 vocabulary (8) and grammar (10) from the vademecum's tables. The vocabulary
  and grammar categories are Tigertest's addition to help learners; the real test has no separate
  grammar part. The split is Tigertest's: the vademecum weights the three prove, not topics.
- Not modelled: the listening prova and a free written reply.
- The 80% pass line is the real one, applied here to the practice questions.
- No question asks about the test itself, the permit procedure, fees or dates.
