# Sources for `itcilsb2` (CILS DUE-B2, written in Italian)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Italian for Tigertest. Every article, notice
and message was invented for this bank, with fictional names, places and figures. Nothing was
copied or reworded from CILS sample papers, past papers, keys, commercial books or practice sites.
Of the official 2017 B2 exam booklet only the section headers, task instructions and times were
read, to confirm the format; its questions were not used. Sources fetched 2026-11-19.

## Exam owner: Centro CILS, Università per Stranieri di Siena

- Centro CILS, "Livello CILS DUE-B2", fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://cils.unistrasi.it/1/79/74/Livello__CILS_DUE-B2.htm
  - B2 certifies full autonomy in Italian; minimum level for access to the Italian university
    system (annual MUR-MAECI circular); used for study and work in Italy.
- Centro CILS, "La valutazione", fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://cils.unistrasi.it/1/117/83/La_valutazione.htm
  - B1 to C2 scored out of 100; each skill scored separately and every skill must reach the
    sufficiency; failed skills can be retaken within 18 months (attestato di capitalizzazione).
- Centro CILS, "Le prove", fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://cils.unistrasi.it/1/83/15/Le_prove.htm
  - five tests: ascolto, comprensione della lettura, analisi delle strutture di comunicazione,
    produzione scritta, produzione orale.
- Centro CILS, "Come si svolgono gli esami CILS", fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://cils.unistrasi.it/1/94/28/Come_si_svolgono_gli_esami_CILS.htm
  - order of the tests in one session; oral recorded and rated centrally.
- Centro CILS, FAQ, fetched 2026-11-19: https://cils.unistrasi.it/1/210/FAQ.htm
  - certificates do not expire.
- Centro CILS, "Quaderno di esame, Livello B2, Sessione giugno 2017" (PDF), fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://cils.unistrasi.it/public/articoli/198/Quaderno%20Due%20B2.pdf
  - headers and instructions only: ascolto 3 prove; comprensione della lettura 3 prove in 50
    minutes (multiple choice on an article; choose the pieces of information present in a text;
    reconstruct a text whose parts are out of order); analisi delle strutture di comunicazione 4
    prove in 1 hour (complete a text with adjectives and pronouns; with the right verb forms; a
    multiple-choice cloze; match each expression to its communicative situation); produzione
    scritta 2 prove in 1 hour 10 minutes.
- Centro CILS guide reproduced by the Istituto Italiano di Cultura di Buenos Aires, "Procedure di
  valutazione" (PDF), fetched 2026-11-12 for itcils, used again:
  https://iicbuenosaires.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Procedure-valutazione.pdf
  - for UNO-B1 to QUATTRO-C2 the minimum to pass a skill is 11 of 20.

## Grammar and usage

- Grammar items test standard written Italian at B2 (congiuntivo and concordanza dei tempi,
  condizionale passato, futuro anteriore, passivo, forme implicite, periodo ipotetico, discorso
  indiretto, pronomi combinati e relativi, connettivi), the structures the B2 booklet's four
  structure tasks exercise. No CILS B2 sillabo page exists on the Centro CILS site map, so the list
  is Tigertest's, following standard reference grammar; items avoid contexts where both
  indicative and congiuntivo are accepted.

## Not loaded or not confirmed

- No CILS page read gives the current item counts per task; the 2017 booklet's task types and
  times are used and the landing says the format should be checked with the exam centre.
- The CELI 3 format PDFs could not be loaded.

## Modelling decisions and what is left out

- Modelled: comprensione della lettura (15 of 50 per test) and analisi delle strutture di
  comunicazione (35 of 50), as single-answer items. Text reconstruction becomes "which order" and
  "which sentence continues"; the matching task becomes "in which situation is this said".
- Not modelled: ascolto, produzione scritta, produzione orale.
- Pass line 55% matches the official 11 of 20 per skill; the real exam needs it in every skill.
- No question asks about the exam itself, fees or dates.
