# KCET (Karnataka CET, PCM) sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from the textbook chapters below. No KCET past
paper, KEA sample, NCERT exercise or example question, or coaching material was copied or reworded.

## Format: Karnataka Examinations Authority (official)
- KEA, "Information Bulletin for UGCET-2026" (Bulletin-1, 16/17 January 2026, English), the official bulletin
  hosted on the KEA website. KEA's own site (cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea and kea.kar.nic.in) refused
  connections from this build environment on 2026-11-19, so the bulletin was read from two byte-identical copies:
  https://i3c-medical-colleges-assets.blr1.digitaloceanspaces.com/2026/02/23/information_bulletin_1_ugcet_2026_17012026english.pdf
  and https://karnatakahelp.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UGCET-2026-Information-Bulletin.pdf (fetched 2026-11-19,
  `cmp` identical, 71 pages). It gave:
  - Section 3, time table: Physics and Chemistry on 23-04-2026, Mathematics and Biology on 24-04-2026; 60
    marks / questions per subject; sessions 10.30 to 11.50 am and 2.30 to 3.50 pm.
  - Subjects by course: Engineering / Technology PCM; farm science PCMB; veterinary, naturopathy and B.Sc
    Nursing PCB; B.Pharm and Pharm-D PCM or PCB.
  - "The question papers of the Common Entrance Test will be based on the First and Second PUC syllabi
    prescribed by the Department of Pre-University Education of Karnataka State"; the department's subject
    experts reviewed the PCMB syllabus and the CET-2026 syllabus was published on the KEA website.
  - "(ii) Duration, question paper and OMR answer sheets": each paper 60 marks, 80 minutes, multiple choice,
    four options, one mark each, several versions, "no negative evaluation with regard to wrong / incorrect
    answers but marks will not be awarded in respect of multiple answers for the same question".
  - Engineering rank: PCM marks in both the CET and the qualifying examination, in equal proportion.
- Calendar of events for CET-2026 (one-page extract of the bulletin), https://www.fathermuller.edu.in/nursing-college/img/pdf/update/information_ugcet_2026_KEA%20-%20B.Sc%20Nursing%20-%202026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): bulletin hosted 17-01-2026, CET on 23 and 24 April 2026.

## Syllabus: NCERT textbooks (primary text)
The KEA CET-2026 syllabus PDFs could not be loaded (KEA site unreachable, see above). Karnataka's PU colleges use
the NCERT textbooks for Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology (see the next section), so every question is
written on the chapters of the current NCERT books. Contents pages read on 2026-11-19 (all "Reprint 2026-27"):
- Physics XI Parts I and II, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keph1ps.pdf and https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/keph2ps.pdf
- Physics XII Parts I and II, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/leph1ps.pdf and https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/leph2ps.pdf
- Chemistry XI Parts I and II, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/kech1ps.pdf and https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/kech2ps.pdf
- Chemistry XII Parts I and II, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lech1ps.pdf and https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lech2ps.pdf
- Mathematics XI, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/kemh1ps.pdf
- Mathematics XII Parts I and II, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lemh1ps.pdf and https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lemh2ps.pdf
Chapter lists used: Physics XI 14 chapters (Units and Measurements to Waves), Physics XII 14 (Electric Charges and
Fields to Semiconductor Electronics); Chemistry XI 9 units (Some Basic Concepts to Hydrocarbons), Chemistry XII 10
units (Solutions to Biomolecules); Mathematics XI 14 chapters (Sets to Probability), Mathematics XII 13 chapters
(Relations and Functions to Probability). Units no longer in these books (solid state, surface chemistry, s- and
p-block, hydrogen, environmental chemistry, polymers, chemistry in everyday life, mathematical reasoning,
communication systems) are not asked.
