# ACLS (Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the American Heart Association's
published 2025 Guidelines material, algorithms and course documents listed below. None is
copied, adapted or reworded from the AHA ACLS Provider Course Exam, the precourse
self-assessment, HeartCode questions, the ACLS Provider Manual's review items, or any
commercial review book or prep site. No stem names a training provider. Drug doses and energy
levels are only those printed in the 2025 AHA algorithms; anything a local protocol sets is
worded "per local protocol" or left out.

cpr.heart.org, professional.heart.org, eccguidelines.heart.org and ahajournals.org return a
Cloudflare 403 to automated reads, so the AHA's own files were read from the same media
library on www.heart.org (identical paths under /-/media/CPR-Files/), from the AHA newsroom,
from PubMed (the guideline abstracts), and the AHA's international ACLS posters and science
summary table from the Irish Heart Foundation, an AHA International Training Center that hosts
them unchanged (each page carries the AHA copyright line).

## Course and exam format (American Heart Association)

- AHA, 2025 ACLS Product and Course Orientation, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/4700782A8F784D86BC7B41D5A2016E2F.pdf
  Three delivery options (instructor-led, HeartCode blended learning, RQI); every student must
  pass the precourse self-assessment with at least 70% before class; the course exam is given
  online at the end of the course, proctored; "Students must pass the exam with a minimum score
  of 84%"; HeartCode students take a cognitive assessment online instead of the exam; learning
  stations, megacode testing station, CPR feedback devices in skills tests.
- AHA, 2020 ACLS Provider Course (Instructor-Led Training) FAQ, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Courses-and-Kits/Healthcare-Professional/ACLS-ILT/2020-ACLS-ILT-FAQ_102120.pdf
  Open-resource exam policy (provider manual, notes, handbook, guidelines, posters; no
  discussion). This is the 2020 course FAQ, so the landing does not state the policy as
  current.
- The number of exam questions and the time limit are not on any AHA page that loaded; the
  landing does not state them.

## Guidelines (2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR and ECC)

- AHA, Highlights of the 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC (25-2500),
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/2025-documents-for-cpr-heart-edits-posting/Resuscitation-Science/252500_Hghlghts_2025ECCGuidelines.pdf
  (same file as cpr.heart.org; also linked from the AHA newsroom release). Used for: single
  Chain of Survival (6 links, recovery and survivorship), ethics (default to start CPR,
  termination of resuscitation, family presence), systems of care (ALS-trained code teams,
  on-scene resuscitation before transport, ECPR, debriefing), adult BLS (head tilt-chin lift
  if jaw thrust fails in trauma, 1 breath every 6 s in respiratory arrest, 30:2 before an
  advanced airway, visible chest rise, avoid hyper- and hypoventilation, bra adjusted not
  removed, obesity, mechanical CPR not routine, back blows then abdominal thrusts), adult ALS
  (IV first then IO, epinephrine after initial shocks fail in shockable rhythms, vasopressin no
  advantage, beta-blockers, bretylium, procainamide and sotalol uncertain, head-up CPR only in
  trials, vector change and double sequential defibrillation not established, universal TOR
  rule, synchronized cardioversion for unstable and refractory stable wide-complex
  tachycardia, 200 J or more for AF and flutter, double synchronized cardioversion uncertain,
  transvenous pacing for refractory unstable bradycardia), post-cardiac arrest care (MAP at
  least 65, temperature control at least 36 hours, 32 to 34 and 36 to 37.5 degree ranges,
  head-to-pelvis CT, POCUS, angiography before discharge, mechanical circulatory support,
  myoclonus without EEG correlate not treated, continuous EEG background, emotional distress
  screening), special circumstances (asthma ECLS and volatile anesthetics, IV calcium in
  hyperkalemic arrest not well established, hypothermia and hyperthermia cooling rates and ice
  water, LVAD compressions, resuscitative delivery by 5 minutes, ECPR in pregnancy, massive
  transfusion for amniotic fluid embolism, naloxone in arrest and at discharge, aerosol
  generating procedures), education (feedback devices, teamwork training, cognitive aids).
- AHA newsroom, "Updated CPR guidelines tackle choking response, opioid-related emergencies and
  a revised Chain of Survival", October 22, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://newsroom.heart.org/news/updated-cpr-guidelines-tackle-choking-response-opioid-related-emergencies-and-a-revised-chain-of-survival
  Publication date, single chain of survival, choking and opioid changes.
- PubMed records for Part 9 (Adult Advanced Life Support, Circulation 2025;152(16 suppl 2):
  S538-S577) and Part 7 (Adult Basic Life Support, S448-S478), fetched 2026-11-22 through the
  NCBI E-utilities:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41122884/
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41122888/
  Scope of each chapter (causative versus secondary arrhythmias, TOR rules by scope of
  practice, opioid antagonists in the BLS algorithm). The full chapters (ahajournals.org)
  could not be loaded; nothing in the bank rests on a recommendation that is only in them.

## 2025 AHA algorithms and change notices

- Adult Cardiac Arrest Algorithm (VF/pVT/Asystole/PEA), 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-ACLS-CA-250527.pdf
  CPR quality (at least 2 inches, 100 to 120/min, full recoil, change compressor every 2
  minutes, 30:2 without an advanced airway, 1 breath every 6 s with one, waveform capnography),
  biphasic 120 to 200 J per manufacturer or maximum if unknown, monophasic 360 J, epinephrine
  1 mg every 3 to 5 minutes and ASAP in asystole/PEA, amiodarone 300 then 150 mg, lidocaine
  1 to 1.5 then 0.5 to 0.75 mg/kg, the H's and T's.
- Adult Cardiac Arrest Circular Algorithm, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-ACLS-CA-Circular-250620.pdf
- Adult Bradycardia With a Pulse Algorithm, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-ACLS-Bradycardia-250514.pdf
  Rate typically under 50, compromise signs, atropine 1 mg every 3 to 5 minutes to 3 mg,
  transcutaneous pacing, dopamine 5 to 20 mcg/kg/min, epinephrine 2 to 10 mcg/min, causes.
- Adult Tachyarrhythmia With a Pulse Algorithm (accessible long description), 2025, fetched
  2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Accessible/Algorithm-ACLS-Tachyarrhythmia-LngDscrp-250726-Ed.pdf
  Rate typically 150 or more, wide QRS 0.12 s or more, vagal maneuvers and adenosine 6 then
  12 mg if regular, beta-blocker or calcium channel blocker, procainamide 20 to 50 mg/min to
  17 mg/kg (avoid with long QT or CHF), amiodarone 150 mg over 10 minutes then 1 mg/min for 6
  hours, sedate before cardioversion.
- Adult Basic Life Support Algorithm for Health Care Professionals, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-BLS-Adult-Healthcare-250701.pdf
  Breathing and pulse checked together within 10 seconds, rescue breathing with a pulse,
  naloxone, signs of puberty treat as adult.
- AHA, ACLS Provider Manual change notice, updated February 6, 2026, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Course-Updates/2026-Updates/2025_ACLS_PrvdrMnl_ChngNtc_2626.pdf
  PETCO2 over 20 mm Hg in the LVAD perfusion table, clopidogrel removed as primary
  antiplatelet, "establish vascular access" added to bradycardia support, post-arrest
  hypotension as MAP 65 or more.
- AHA, ACLS Reference Cards change notice, updated August 11, 2026, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Resources/FAQs/Course-Updates/2026-Change-Notices/2025_ACLS_RfrncCrds_ChngNtc.pdf
  Stroke hypertension panel: otherwise eligible for reperfusion except BP above 185/110.
- AHA, 2025 Handbook of ECC change notice, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Course-Updates/2026-Updates/2025_Handbook_ChngNtc_2626.pdf
- AHA, 2025 ACLS posters, international edition (JN-2788, 12/25), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://irishheart.ie/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/JN-2788_ACLS-Posters-International-English.pdf
  Acute Coronary Syndromes Algorithm (12-lead ECG within 10 minutes of first medical contact,
  oxygen if saturation under 90%, aspirin 162 to 325 mg, nitroglycerin, morphine or fentanyl,
  P2Y12 inhibitors, TIMI or GRACE risk, onset 12 hours or less, FMC-to-balloon 90 minutes or
  less, door-to-needle 30 minutes, early invasive strategy triggers), Adult Suspected Stroke
  Algorithm (last known normal, glucose, prehospital notification, bypass the ED to imaging,
  hemorrhage on imaging, thrombolytic candidate, CTA and EVT), 12-lead ECG and coronary
  anatomy, post-cardiac arrest care (SpO2 90 to 98%, PaO2 60 to 105, PCO2 35 to 45, MAP 65,
  temperature control 32 to 37.5 degrees, glucose 70 to 180, prognostication at 72 hours or
  more), Cardiac Arrest in Pregnancy Algorithm (left lateral uterine displacement when the
  fundus is at or above the umbilicus, IV above the diaphragm, stop magnesium and give calcium,
  detach fetal monitors, A to H causes), Durable LVAD Algorithm (MAP over 50, PETCO2 over 20).
- AHA, 2025 ACLS Science Summary Table (international edition), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://irishheart.ie/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-IVE-ACLS-Science-Summary-Table.pdf
  Synchronized cardioversion starting energies (narrow 100 J, monomorphic VT 100 J, AF 200 J,
  flutter 200 J; polymorphic VT and irregular wide QRS get an unsynchronized defibrillation
  dose), sotalol removed from the tachycardia algorithm, SVT renamed narrow-complex
  tachycardia, single 6-link chain, SpO2 90 to 98%, hold temperature 36 hours, tenecteplase
  added for stroke, LBBB no longer a definitive STEMI criterion, fentanyl, enoxaparin or
  fondaparinux and ACE inhibitors added in ACS, bag squeezed one third to one half for chest
  rise, no drugs down the ET tube.

## Not used or left out

- The full Part 7 and Part 9 chapters, the 2026 AHA/ASA stroke guideline and the ACC/AHA ACS
  guideline (ahajournals.org, 403). No item asks a thrombolytic dose, a stroke time window in
  hours or a P2Y12 drug choice; stroke items stay with the algorithm's steps and the 185/110
  eligibility line on the reference card.
- Compression depth upper limit, pediatric ALS doses and neonatal care: outside the adult
  ACLS course or not on a page that loaded.
- The ACLS exam itself (question count, time) is not modelled and never asked.

## Kept apart from sibling banks

Written against `cpr` (lay and BLS CPR, first aid), `paramedic`, `emt` and `ccrn`. BLS items
here are the health care professional points ACLS reviews (pulse and breathing check together,
30:2 before an advanced airway, 1 breath every 6 s with one, capnography); no first aid, no
infant CPR, no lay choking steps beyond the 2025 adult FBAO change.
