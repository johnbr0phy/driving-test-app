# PALS (Pediatric Advanced Life Support) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the American Heart Association's
and American Academy of Pediatrics' published 2025 Guidelines material, the 2025 pediatric
algorithms and the AHA course documents listed below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from
the AHA PALS Provider Course Exam, the precourse self-assessment, HeartCode questions, the
PALS Provider Manual's review items, the instructor manual's case scenarios or any commercial
review book or prep site. The case scenarios were read for clinical facts only (fluid volumes,
drug choices, signs that separate problem types); no scenario, patient or vital-sign set is
reused. No stem names a training provider. Every weight-based dose and energy was recomputed
from the 2025 algorithms.

cpr.heart.org, professional.heart.org and ahajournals.org return a Cloudflare 403 to automated
reads, so the AHA's own files were read from the same media library on www.heart.org
(identical paths under /-/media/CPR-Files/) and the guideline abstracts from PubMed.

## Course and exam format (American Heart Association)

- AHA, 2020 PALS Provider Course (Instructor-Led Training) FAQ, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Course-FAQs/2020-Course-FAQs/2020-PALS-ILT-FAQ_102120.pdf
  Course completion requirements: "Completion of the open-resource written exam with a minimum
  score of 84%"; precourse self-assessment with a score of at least 70% before the course;
  open-resource exam policy; online exams for instructor-led courses. No 2025 PALS FAQ could be
  found; the 84% minimum is also the score the AHA's 2025 ACLS orientation and 2025 BLS FAQ set
  for their course exams, so the landing states 84% as the PALS FAQ's figure.
- AHA, PALS Provider Course training memo, released October 22, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR2-Files/Course-Materials/2025-Course-Materials/Training-Memo-PALS-Provider-Course-PDF.pdf
  2025 course updated to the 2025 Guidelines; new materials only from March 1, 2026; mandatory
  precourse self-assessment continues; video prework option; updated 2025 exams from ShopCPR
  or Atlas.
- AHA, 2025 PALS product information sheet, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/2025-documents-for-cpr-heart-edits-posting/Resources/2025-PALS-PI-Sheet-(1).pdf
  Audience, three course formats (instructor-led, HeartCode PALS blended, RQI), the same
  two-year completion card, 2025 course updates (single chain of survival, FBAO changes,
  infant compression techniques).
- AHA, PALS Instructor Manual change notice, updated February 6, 2026, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Course-Updates/2026-Updates/2025_PALS_InstrctrMnl_ChngNtc_2626.pdf
  Cardiogenic shock bolus reworded to 5 to 10 mL/kg of isotonic crystalloid over 10 to 20
  minutes with reassessment.
- AHA, 2025 PALS Instructor Manual, practice case scenarios 1 to 16 (PDF dated October 20,
  2025), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/32508158BE214432B3A4DE95E7BE009F.pdf
  Read for facts only: evaluate-identify-intervene, Pediatric Assessment Triangle, primary
  (ABCDE) and SAMPLE secondary assessment; respiratory distress versus failure; upper and lower
  airway obstruction, lung tissue disease, disordered control of breathing and their
  treatments (nebulized epinephrine, dexamethasone, albuterol, ipratropium, magnesium sulfate,
  heliox limits, CPAP or noninvasive ventilation with monitoring, cuffed tube, SpO2 94% or
  more); hypovolemic shock (20 mL/kg boluses, 10 mL/kg packed red cells after 2 to 3 boluses,
  platelets and plasma), septic shock (10 to 20 mL/kg isotonic boluses, antibiotics and
  vasoactives within the first hour, epinephrine or norepinephrine), cardiogenic shock
  (inotropes, echocardiography), obstructive shock (DOPE, needle decompression before x-ray,
  chest tube, fluid as a bridge in tamponade and pulmonary embolism); stop-bolus signs; SVT,
  wide-complex tachycardia, pulseless VT and bradycardia management; post-ROSC SpO2 94% to 99%,
  fever prevention, vasoactive titration; hypoxia as the most common cause of bradycardia;
  flumazenil and seizure threshold; reporting possible abuse.
- The number of exam questions and the time limit are not on any AHA page that loaded; the
  landing does not state them.

## Guidelines (2025 AHA and AAP Guidelines for CPR and ECC)

- AHA, Highlights of the 2025 American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC (25-2500),
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/2025-documents-for-cpr-heart-edits-posting/Resuscitation-Science/252500_Hghlghts_2025ECCGuidelines.pdf
  Pediatric BLS (secondary nature of pediatric arrest, pauses under 10 seconds, heel of 1 hand
  or 2 thumb-encircling hands, 2-finger technique dropped, child FBAO 5 back blows and 5
  abdominal thrusts, infant FBAO 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts with the heel of 1 hand);
  pediatric ALS (early epinephrine in nonshockable arrest, under 3 minutes best, ETCO2 to
  monitor CPR quality, no ETCO2 cutoff alone to stop, 20 mm Hg association, diastolic 25 mm Hg
  infants and 30 mm Hg children, refractory SVT with procainamide, amiodarone or sotalol,
  post-arrest systolic and mean pressure above the 10th percentile, hypotension under the 5th,
  diastolic above the 50th in the first 6 hours, multiple prognostic modalities, cough and gag
  not established, EEG up to 72 hours, survivorship evaluation in the first year); single
  6-link chain of survival; safety huddles in pediatric ICUs; immediate and delayed debriefing.
- PubMed record for Part 8 (Pediatric Advanced Life Support, Circulation 2025;152(16 suppl 2):
  S479-S537), fetched 2026-11-22 through the NCBI E-utilities:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41122885/
  Scope: infants and children up to 18 years, excluding newborns; topics covered.
- PubMed record for Part 6 (Pediatric Basic Life Support, S424-S447), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41122891/
  Two-finger compressions eliminated, AED with a pediatric attenuator, FBAO cycles.

## 2025 pediatric algorithms (AHA and AAP)

- Pediatric Cardiac Arrest Algorithm, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-PALS-CA-250123.pdf
  Depth at least one-third of the chest, 100 to 120/min, change compressor every 2 minutes,
  15:2 two rescuers before puberty, 30:2 after puberty onset or one rescuer, 1 breath every 2
  to 3 seconds with an advanced airway, ETCO2 and invasive diastolic pressure; shocks 2 J/kg,
  4 J/kg, then at least 4 J/kg to 10 J/kg or the adult dose; epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg (0.1 mg/mL)
  to 1 mg every 3 to 5 minutes; amiodarone 5 mg/kg to 300 mg, up to 3 doses (later doses to
  150 mg), or lidocaine 1 mg/kg; ET tube or supraglottic airway; H's and T's including
  hypoglycemia.
- Pediatric Bradycardia With a Pulse Algorithm, 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-PALS-Bradycardia-250121.pdf
  Compromise signs, CPR if HR under 60, epinephrine 0.01 mg/kg, atropine 0.02 mg/kg (minimum
  0.1 mg, maximum single dose 0.5 mg, may repeat once) for vagal tone or primary AV block,
  pacing, causes.
- Pediatric Tachyarrhythmia With a Pulse Algorithm (accessible long description), 2025,
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Accessible/Algorithm-PALS-Tachyarrhythmia-LngDscrp-250729-Ed.pdf
  Sinus tachycardia versus SVT (infant 220, child 180, P waves, RR, abrupt change), narrow QRS
  0.09 s or less, adenosine 0.1 mg/kg to 6 mg then 0.2 mg/kg to 12 mg by rapid push and flush,
  synchronized cardioversion 0.5 to 1 J/kg then 2 J/kg, sedate without delaying cardioversion,
  vagal maneuvers, adenosine for regular monomorphic wide QRS, expert consultation.
- Pediatric Basic Life Support Algorithm for Health Care Professionals, Single Rescuer, 2025,
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-BLS-Ped-Single-Rescuer-wNos-250212.pdf
  Ages 1 year to puberty, breathing and pulse within 10 seconds, rescue breathing 20 to 30/min,
  CPR for HR under 60 with poor perfusion, witnessed collapse, 2 minutes before leaving if
  alone, AED use and when to stop.
- AHA, 2025 Guidelines change notice, updated February 6, 2026, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/Course-Updates/2026-Updates/2025_GL_ChngNtc_2626.pdf
  No pediatric changes affecting the bank.

## Not used or left out

- The full Part 6 and Part 8 chapters (ahajournals.org, 403). Nothing in the bank rests on a
  recommendation that is only in them; septic shock items follow the 2025 course scenarios,
  not the separate Surviving Sepsis pediatric guidelines.
- Endotracheal tube size formulas, hypotension-by-age formulas, vital sign tables, glucose
  doses and ECPR criteria: not stated in any document that loaded.
- Neonatal resuscitation (a separate course and guideline).
- The PALS exam itself (question count, time) is not modelled and never asked.

## Kept apart from sibling banks

Written against `acls` (adult algorithms), `cpr` (lay and BLS CPR, first aid), `paramedic` and
`emt`. BLS items here are the pediatric health care professional points PALS reviews; no adult
doses, no first aid, no neonatal care.
