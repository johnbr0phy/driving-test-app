## Spec and deliberate limits
V2 id osssc, prefix OSSSC; /osssc, /osssc-cre-practice-test. Premium government-job exam, with standard free-to-start tier. No v1 stateCode/idBase.
200 unique questions: Maths47, GeneralStudies47, English47, Computer35, Reasoning24. Two tests each20/20/20/15/10=85 questions, retaining the five non-Odia subject counts. Thirty additional drill-only items; test1 questions also serve drills, test2 held out. Each subset test uses a chosen90-minute timer; this is not the official full100-question paper. No Odia questions, current affairs or practical computer assessment. General studies samples stable topics rather than claiming exhaustive breadth.
The existing v2 engine has no negative-marking configuration: it grades correctness and percentScale, matching other Indian recruitment v2 banks. Our percentage therefore does NOT apply the real -0.25 penalty, normalization or qualifying rules. All limitations must appear in directions, score note, landing and facts. Search 'odisha' is an explicit shortcut to this OSSSC entry, not an equivalence with OSSC or Odisha Police.
