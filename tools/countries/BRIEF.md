# Builder brief: one country driving theory exam

You build ONE driving theory practice exam for one country, as a v1 Tigertest exam. Eight builders
run at once in the same worktree, `/Users/johnbrophy/tt-more-countries` (branch `more-countries`).
Work only there, never in `/Users/johnbrophy/driving-test-app`.

## The template

Copy the New Zealand learner licence exam, which is already live:
- `tools/next30/nzroad/` (spec.json, sources.md, q_*.py, build.py, WIRING.md)
- `lib/banks/nzroad.ts`
- `app/nz-learner-licence/*` (8 wrappers: layout, page, dashboard, drill, practice/[n], stats, test/[id] and its results)
- `app/nz-learner-licence-practice-test/page.tsx` (the landing)

Read `.claude/skills/exam-bank/SKILL.md` first and follow its house style. In short:
- Options are short phrases (20 to 35 characters on average). The key never stands out by length or tone.
- The key goes in slot `i % 4` of each segment, as `build.py` does.
- Distractors are plausible mistakes, not strawmen.
- Explanations are 1 to 3 sentences and teach the rule.
- All questions are original, never copied from an official bank or a commercial app.
- Signs are described in words (no images).
- No em-dashes anywhere. The brand is "Tigertest".
- Use the country's own spelling (licence, metres, kerb in Commonwealth countries) and its units.
- Never ask about the test itself (question count, pass mark, time, languages, booking, retakes, how
  long a pass is valid, age to sit it). Those facts go on the landing and in examFacts only. Fill the
  licence category with rules a driver lives by, or shrink it. `validate_bank.py` fails these stems.
- Don't put money figures (fines, fees) in questions. Leave out rules you cannot confirm from an official
  source, and anything the "avoid" list below names.

## Rules

- Spec: `testCount × perTest ≤ pool` for every category. Pools add up to 200. Spares, if any,
  are the last items of a pool and are training-only. Every training set has `size` ≤ 60 and at
  least that many unique questions. Use 4 to 6 sets. Keep the dashboard to 8 to 12 steps
  (sets + tests).
- Test ids are `idBase+1 ..`, and set ids are `idBase+1 ..` too, as in nzroad.
- `passPct`: choose it so `Math.ceil(passPct/100 × questionsPerTest)` equals the pass mark below.
- Category keys must be prefixed (e.g. `phSigns`), and you must grep `types/index.ts` to make sure
  none is taken. `sgSafety` is already used by the security guard exam.
- Validate with
  `python3 .claude/skills/exam-bank/scripts/validate_bank.py data/<file>.json --pool key=n --pool key=n ...`
  (one `--pool` flag per category). It must print PASS. Also grep your bank for "—" and fix any.
- The landing (copy of the NZ landing) has the following, with JSX apostrophes written as `&apos;`
  and plain `'` inside metadata and JSON-LD strings:
  - metadata;
  - the WebApplication + FAQPage JSON-LD;
  - `ExamLandingBreadcrumbs`, `ExamFacts` and `ExamRelatedTests`;
  - honest facts about the real test, each with its source.

  Wherever a figure is only "what guides say" or Tigertest's own target, say so.
- Finish by printing the validator output and listing the files you wrote.

### phlto (Philippines, LTO driver's licence written exam)
- 3 tests × 60 questions, passPct 80 (48 of 60). Source: LTO's TDC module TDC-15-S-01-2 v9-2021.
  The landing may also give the older 2008 memo (MC AHS-2008-949) figure, 40 questions with 30 to
  pass, and its date.
- Categories (per test / pool):
  - phLicence 8/25
  - phSigns 12/38
  - phMarkings 6/20
  - phRightOfWay 9/29
  - phSpeed 9/29
  - phParking 5/16
  - phSafetyLaws 8/25
  - phEmergency 3/18

  That leaves 20 spares.
- Sets:
  - 12201: phLicence + phSafetyLaws (50)
  - 12202: phSigns (38)
  - 12203: phMarkings + phRightOfWay (49)
  - 12204: phSpeed + phParking (45)
  - 12205: phEmergency (18)
- Facts:
  - RA 4136 Sec 42(b): a vehicle already turning left that has signalled has priority.
  - Ages: student permit 16, non-professional 17, professional 18. The student permit is valid for 1 year.
  - Don't claim that a student driver must have the supervisor "beside" them; that wording is not in the law.

### zak53 (South Africa, K53 learner's licence, Code 2 car)
- Code 2 learner's at 17. 4 tests × 50, passPct 80 (40). Per test: 22 rules of the road, 22 signs
  and markings, 6 vehicle controls.
- The real test is 64 random questions (RTMC spokesperson, May 2026). The section pass marks
  22/28, 23/28 and 6/8 come only from study guides, so the copy must say "guides say".
- Facts:
  - Report a crash with injuries within 24 hours, and a damage-only crash by the next working day.
  - The no-overtaking sign shows two black cars.
- Avoid:
  - Licence card renewal after the 10-year approval (uncertain).
  - Any "zero alcohol limit" claim (not law).
- Training sets: split rules and signs into several sets each, all ≤ 60.

### sgbtt (Singapore, Basic Theory Test)
- 4 × 50, passPct 90 (45). "45 to pass" is only the mark guides quote. Leave it out of the landing
  facts, or label it as what guides say.
- Source: the 2.1.2026 Basic Theory handbook (BT-ENG-2126.pdf), which includes the three-second rule.
- Avoid:
  - The alcohol limit (changed by the 2026 Act).
  - The DIPS 13/24 point thresholds (they change to 12/18 on 1 Jan 2027).
- Category key for safety is `sgRoadSafety`, labelled "Safe Driving & Road Users". Never use `sgSafety`.

### aerta (UAE, RTA Dubai theory test)
- 4 × 40, passPct 75 (30), per rta.ae service 510.
- Minimum age is disputed (17 under the federal law, 18 on RTA and u.ae). Word it non-committally,
  e.g. "check the current minimum age with your emirate's licensing authority".
- The ban on driving into wadis during floods comes from MR 178/2017, as amended in 2023.
- No fines and no black points in questions.

### ngfrsc (Nigeria, FRSC Highway Code / driving test theory)
- 4 × 50, passPct 75. Nothing official is published, so present 75% as Tigertest's own target.
- Source: the FRSC Nigeria Highway Code and the National Road Traffic Regulations 2012.
- Avoid:
  - The car highway speed limit (80 vs 90 conflict).
  - Tanker limits.
  - Learner's permit validity.

### kentsa (Kenya, NTSA driving test theory)
- 4 × 50, passPct 80, presented as Tigertest's target.
- Source: the Kenya Highway Code and the Traffic Act.
- Drink-driving (LN 12/2026): the limit is zero for PSV, commercial and school transport drivers, and
  35 µg per 100 ml of breath for other drivers.
- Avoid:
  - Camera instant fines and the second-generation smart DL (frozen by the courts).
  - Any "model town board" claim in the facts.

### mykpp (Malaysia, JPJ KPP01 theory test)
- 4 × 50, passPct 84 (42 of 50), per the JPJ KPP Gabungan FAQ. Since 29 Aug 2025 the combined test
  is 70 questions with 56 to pass (80%); the landing can say so.
- Facts:
  - An LDL holder drives only under an instructor.
  - Hazard lights are for a stopped vehicle only.
- Avoid:
  - Any "give way to the right" rule.
  - Any "sunset to sunrise" headlight rule.

### hkdrive (Hong Kong, Transport Department written test, private car)
- 7 tests × 20, passPct 80 (16 of 20). The TD confirms 20 questions, 20 minutes, 16 to pass and
  3 options. Our engine uses 4 options, so the landing must say that the real test has 3.
- Use 4 or 5 training sets so the dashboard stays at about 11 to 12 steps. Pools: 7 × perTest plus
  spares, 200 in all.
- Facts:
  - Child restraints are required for children under 8 and under 1.35 m, since 1 Nov 2025.
  - The electronic driving licence (eDL) has been valid since 15 Sep 2025.
  - The mobile device rule applies from 25 Jan 2026.
- Hong Kong drives on the left.
