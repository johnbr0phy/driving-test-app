# Sources for `hkdrive` (Hong Kong Transport Department driving written test, private car)

All 200 questions are original. No official, commercial or third-party question bank was opened or
copied. Topics and facts come from the Road Users' Code and Transport Department (TD) pages listed
below, all checked 2026-10-31. Signs and markings are described in words (no images).

## Test format (checked 2026-10-31)
- TD, "Part A Test (Written Test)":
  https://www.td.gov.hk/en/public_services/licences_and_permits/driving_test/wt/index.html
  20 multiple-choice questions, three options each, one correct; pass with 16 or more right;
  20 minutes; computerised, at the TD written test centre in Cheung Sha Wan Government Offices
  (practice kiosks there); questions mainly based on the Road Users' Code plus recent changes in
  road traffic legislation and driving rules. The page does not state languages or retake waits,
  so neither is claimed anywhere.
- The real test has 3 options; this bank uses 4 like every Tigertest bank. The landing says so.
- TD publishes no per-topic counts. The blueprint (3/4/3/3/2/2/2/1 per 20) is Tigertest's own,
  following the Code's chapters with signs and markings weighted heaviest. The landing says so.

## Main study source
- Road Users' Code, TD, effective 19 June 2020 (current edition on the TD site):
  https://www.td.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_172/road_users_code_2020_eng.pdf
  Read in full for Chapters 3, 5, 8 and 10 (text extracted locally). Facts used:
  - Ch 5 Driving: minimum age 18; eyesight, number plate at 23 m in good daylight; prescribed alcohol
    limits 22 micrograms/100 ml breath, 50 mg/100 ml blood, 67 mg/100 ml urine; screening breath
    tests at random roadblocks; refusing specimens is an offence; any concentration of six specified
    drugs (heroin, cocaine, ketamine, methamphetamine, cannabis, MDMA) is an offence; front passenger
    quarter-metre gap from dashboard.
  - Safety checks: vehicle licence on left of windscreen; tread at least 1 mm across three quarters
    of the breadth; never mix cross-ply and radial; no engine running with fuel tank cover off;
    driver assistance systems (keep hands on wheel).
  - Seat belts: private car driver responsible for all passengers; driver exemption only while
    reversing manoeuvres; no sharing; pregnancy not an automatic exemption; lap belt across hips and
    thighs; 25 + 25 km/h head-on = 50 km/h impact; airbags supplement belts; no rearward-facing child
    seat in front of an airbag; child never held on a lap.
  - Driver education: learner needs a licensed driving instructor; L-plates; no expressways; learners
    restricted to certain times and roads; probationary licence at least 12 months, P plates front
    and rear, 70 km/h maximum, no right most lane on 3+ lane expressways unless exiting right; apply
    for full licence within 3 years; driving improvement course compulsory after drink driving.
  - On the road: keep left; phone not held in hand or between head and shoulder while moving;
    footwear; leaving the vehicle (engine off, gear, parking brake); horn only to warn of danger;
    headlamp flashing means only "I am here"; silent zones; idling limit 3 minutes in any 60.
  - Stopping distance at 60 km/h: 15 m + 20 m = 35 m (dry, alert driver); wet roads double braking.
  - Speed: 50 km/h unless signed; signed limits 70, 80, 100, 110; end of higher limit marked by a
    50 km/h sign; two-second rule, four seconds in bad conditions.
  - Overtaking, dual carriageways, expressways (Road Traffic (Expressway) Regulations), tunnels
    (no main beam, no horn, not below 25 km/h, no crossing double white lines, one-tube-two-way
    limit usually 50 km/h), lane markings, road stud colours, double white lines, bus lanes,
    passing places, steep hills.
  - Junctions, right-turn pockets and the indicative right-turn arrow, nearside-to-nearside right
    turns, box junctions (right-turn exception, none for tram/light rail boxes), roundabouts (turn
    left on entry, give way to the immediate right, lane and signal rules), zebra and green man
    crossings.
  - Sharing the road: jaywalkers, pedestrian priority zones, school crossing patrols, cyclists when
    turning left, doors, large vehicles (move right before turning left, blind spot behind cab
    doors, heavy vehicles need almost 50% more distance), trams, light rail RAIL ONLY lanes,
    emergency vehicles, animals.
  - Where not to stop / park: expressways, bus stops, zigzags, tunnel areas, double and single
    yellow lines, broken yellow line on unlit roads, lit roads (lights not more than 200 m apart)
    park only in parking places, 24-hour maximum in a parking place, meters, hill parking.
  - Ch 8: sign shapes and colours, traffic lights, wig-wag lights, secondary lights, police signals
    take precedence, lane signals, direction sign colours (blue, green on expressways, white local,
    yellow temporary), road markings, red no-stopping sign post colour code.
  - Ch 10: hazard lights; no warning triangle on roads over 50 km/h; triangle about 50 m back on
    50 km/h roads; breakdown in a tunnel; chainage markers every 100 m; report injury or unreported
    damage crashes to police within 24 hours; minor damage-only crashes may move aside; 999;
    first aid basics.
- TD, "Conditions Imposed with Issue of Learner's Driving Licence" (Oct 2017):
  https://www.td.gov.hk/filemanager/en/publication/conditions%20imposed%20with%20issue%20of%20ldl%20(eng)_oct%202017.pdf
  Instructor for the vehicle class; passengers limited to the instructor, one other learner under
  instruction and examiners; white 250 mm plate with a red L and the red character for "learn",
  front and rear; permitted times Mon-Fri 6:00-7:30, 9:30-16:30, 19:30-23:30; Sat 6:00-7:30,
  9:30-23:30; Sun and public holidays 6:00-23:30. The two time items (LIC-007, LIC-008) say
  "under the standard learner licence conditions"; this is the latest English version on the TD site.

## 2025-2026 changes (checked 2026-10-31)
- Electronic driving licence, from 15 September 2025: TD eDL page
  https://www.td.gov.hk/en/public_services/licences_and_permits/edl/index.html and press release
  https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202509/15/P2025091200303.htm . Holders may show the eDL on a
  phone or carry the physical licence; HKID holders log in with iAM Smart, others with the TD
  e-Licensing Portal.
- Child restraint devices in private cars, from 1 November 2025: TD page
  https://www.td.gov.hk/en/road_safety/crd and press release
  https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202502/24/P2025022400161.htm . Children under 8 must use a
  CRD in any seat unless they are 1.35 m or taller.
- Mobile telecommunications devices in front of drivers, from 25 January 2026 (Road Traffic
  (Traffic Control) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulation 2025): TD page
  https://www.td.gov.hk/en/road_safety/mtd/ and press release
  https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202601/08/P2026010800216.htm . No more than two devices
  (phone, tablet, laptop) in front of the driver; each visible screen no more than 19 cm diagonal;
  must not block the view of the road or mirrors; passenger-only devices excluded; hand-held use
  while moving still banned.

## Deliberately avoided
- Fines, fixed penalties, test fees, driving-offence point values and thresholds (house style).
- Autotoll lanes and toll plaza details (the Code's 2020 text predates HKeToll free-flow tolling).
- The Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge right-driving road rules.
- The old "children 3 and over may use a seat belt if no restraint is available" advice and the
  "three children count as two passengers" rule, which the 1 November 2025 CRD law may affect.
- Learner licence validity, test languages and retake waiting periods (not stated on the pages read).
- Colour of the P plate (not stated in the Code text).
