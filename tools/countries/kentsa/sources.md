# Sources for `kentsa` (Kenya NTSA driving test theory)

All 200 questions are original. No official or commercial question bank was opened or copied
(several "NTSA questions and answers" uploads exist on Scribd, Stuvia and similar sites; they were
deliberately not used). Rules come from the Traffic Act (Cap. 403), its Traffic Rules and Traffic
(Minor Offences) Rules, the drink-driving limits given in the brief (LN 12/2026) and the general
guidance of the NTSA Highway Code. Signs are described in words. Checked 2026-10-31.

The NTSA website (ntsa.go.ke) is a JavaScript app that did not render for our fetch tools, and
the NTSA Highway Code PDF mirrors we found were offline (404/503), so every numbered rule below
was taken from the Traffic Act text, not from the Highway Code. Highway Code items (signals,
roundabouts, overtaking, night driving) are restricted to standard rules shared by Kenya's
left-hand-traffic, UK-derived Highway Code, with no distances or figures we could not confirm.

## Test format
- NTSA publishes no current question count or pass mark for the theory part of the driving test.
  Business Daily / Nation, "Drivers to sit written test in NTSA changes", 13 Dec 2019: NTSA
  guidelines said the written test "shall comprise a minimum of 50 multiple-choice questions" and
  learners must reach a pass mark of 80 percent; a failed theory exam is retaken in full after at
  least 21 days. https://nation.africa/kenya/news/drivers-to-sit-written-test-in-ntsa-changes-232084
- Third-party prep sites quote 20 questions, 16 to pass, 30 minutes; not official, not used.
- The practical test also uses the model town board; not part of this bank and not claimed in the
  facts (brief: avoid).
- Tigertest therefore uses 4 tests of 50 with an 80% target (40 of 50), presented as our target.
- Blueprint (8/10/8/9/6/5/4) is Tigertest's own weighting of the Highway Code topics; NTSA
  publishes no per-topic counts.

## Traffic Act (Cap. 403), section text via Sheriaplex (checked 2026-10-31; Kenya Law's own pages did not load)
- s.30 Drivers to be licensed: licence or provisional licence for the class; one licence per
  person, endorsable for several classes; owner must not permit an unlicensed driver; tell the
  Authority of changes affecting fitness; PSV and commercial drivers retested every three years.
  https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3088
- s.31 Conditions for grant: test under s.39, medical declaration, read a number plate at
  25 metres. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3089
- s.32 Provisional licence: granted so the applicant may learn to drive the class.
  (Validity "three months" in the Act; guides say 6 months under newer rules, so validity is not
  asked.) https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3090
- s.33 Ages: no licence under 16; under 18 only motorcycles; matatus and motor omnibuses need age
  over 24 and 4 years on a car or commercial licence. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3091
- s.39 Driving tests: rules of the road, road signals and signs, Highway Code, physical fitness.
  https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3097
- s.42 Speed: 50 km/h in trading centres, townships, municipalities and cities, with signs where
  it begins and ends; class limits as prescribed. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3100-section-42-speed-of-motor-vehicles
- s.44 Driving under the influence of drink or a drug; minimum 12-month disqualification.
  https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3102
- s.45 PSV driver drinking on duty, and giving a PSV driver liquor, are offences.
  https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3103
- s.45A Driving on a pavement or pedestrian walkway to avoid traffic. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3104
- s.52 Signals and signs: obey a uniformed police officer, traffic signs, stop when required;
  stop when a person in charge of cattle signals. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3111
- s.52A Parking bays: overstaying the time on the sign is an offence. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3112
- s.53 Obstruction: stopped vehicles as close to the side as possible; remove a breakdown as soon
  as possible; lights from 6.45 pm to 6.15 am; two red reflecting triangles not less than 50 m
  ahead and behind. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3114
- s.54 Racing and trials of speed. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3115
- s.56 Limitation of loads. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3117
- s.60 Pillion riding: one passenger, astride, on a proper seat. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3121
- s.66 Unattended vehicles: no engine running, take precautions against moving. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3127
- s.66A Maximum driving hours: PSV and commercial, 8 hours in any 24. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3128
- s.68 Highway Code: breach is not itself an offence but may be relied on in proceedings.
  https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3131
- s.73 Duty to stop and report: give name, address, owner and identification marks; injury to a
  person, dog or cattle, or no details given: report to police within 24 hours; passengers give
  details if required. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3147
- s.89 Bicycles: one passenger on a fitted carrier or step. https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3163
- s.103B Helmets and reflector jackets for rider and passenger; one passenger.
  https://www.sheriaplex.com/kenya-acts/3178

## Traffic (Minor Offences) Rules, LN 161 of 2016 (offence list; fines not used)
- Learner failing to exhibit L plates front and rear (Traffic Rules r.12(1)(b)); failing to carry
  and produce a licence; seat belt not worn while in motion; mobile phone use while in motion;
  speed governor in PSV and commercial vehicles; motorcycle protective gear; tinted PSV windows;
  matatu stops only at authorised places; body outside a moving vehicle; reflective triangles
  (life savers) carried and displayed. Text: https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2016/161/eng@2022-12-31
  (list read via https://hapakenya.com/2024/08/20/list-of-minor-traffic-offences-in-kenya-and-their-fines)
- Learner accompaniment and passengers: money254, "Driving with a learner sticker in Kenya"
  (qualified driver licensed for that class beside the learner; no passengers for hire; no
  pillion on a two-wheeled motorcycle). https://www.money254.co.ke/post/driving-with-a-learner-sticker-in-kenya-the-law-your-rights-the-fine-info

## Speed governors
- 80 km/h speed governors for PSVs and commercial vehicles (2003 "Michuki rules", still enforced):
  Business Daily, https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/news/commuters-pay-the-price-as-rogue-matatus-keep-off-roads-2226274
- Highway (open road) limits for private cars are reported as 100 or 110 km/h by different sources,
  so no open-road car limit is asked.

## Drink driving (per brief, verified by the fact-checkers: LN 12/2026)
- Zero alcohol for PSV, commercial and school transport drivers; 35 micrograms per 100 ml of
  breath for other drivers. Blood and urine equivalents are not asked.
- Standard, "Chirchir gazettes new rules on drunk driving, school vans":
  https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/national/article/2001522238/chirchir-gazettes-new-rules-on-drunk-driving-school-vans

## Insurance
- Third-party cover: Insurance (Motor Vehicles Third Party Risks) Act, Cap. 405 (referenced in
  Traffic Act s.103B(3)).

## Left out on purpose
- Camera instant fines and the second-generation smart DL (frozen by the courts; brief).
- Model town board in the facts (brief).
- Fines, fees, demerit points, provisional licence validity, open-road car speed limits, licence
  sub-classes (B1/B2 definitions differ between sources), warning-triangle placement figures from
  the Highway Code other than the Act's 50 m.
