# Sources for `mykpp` (Malaysia JPJ KPP01 theory test, car)

All 200 questions are original. No official JPJ question bank, KPP handbook question or commercial
app item was copied. Signs are described in words (no images). Checked 2026-10-31 unless noted.

## Test format
- JPJ KPP Gabungan FAQ (as quoted by third-party prep sites; the jpj.gov.my FAQ itself was not
  reachable by search): car KPP01 computer test 50 questions, 45 minutes, 42/50 to pass; KPP
  Gabungan (combined motorcycle + car module) from 29 August 2025: 70 questions, 1 hour 15 minutes,
  56/70 to pass. Brief's fact-check confirms 42/50 and the 29 Aug 2025 date.
  https://open-exam-prep.com/practice/my-kpp-car , https://open-exam-prep.com/practice/my-kpp-combined
- The Sun (2025): KPP01 motorcycle and car modules merged into one module "to provide additional
  emphasis on safe driving"; the article gives 1 August as the planned start. The landing uses the
  29 August 2025 date from the FAQ.
  https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/module-kpp01-for-motorcycle-car-licence-to-be-combined-from-aug-1-anthony-loke-pm13972805/
- eCentral guide: 50 questions, Bahagian A 15 (road signs), B 25, C 10 (law, roads, KEJARA), 42 to
  pass. Study guide, not JPJ: the landing says "study guides say". Blueprint per test follows it:
  15 signs + markings, 25 rules/speed/parking/defensive, 10 licence/law + emergencies.
  https://ecentral.my/ujian-komputer-lesen-memandu-jpj/

## Licensing process
- MyGOV (malaysia.gov.my) "Memohon lesen memandu": register with a JPJ-registered driving institute,
  KPP01 theory course, pass the Part 1 road law test, LDL, circuit training (6 hours) and road
  training (10 hours), JPJ circuit and road test, PDL valid 2 years (cancelled if KEJARA demerit
  points pass the limit), convert to CDL within 1 year after the 2 years.
  https://www.malaysia.gov.my/my/categories/pengangkutan/lesen-memandu/memohon-lesen-memandu
- MyGOV "Jenis lesen memandu": LDL holder may drive only under a driving institute instructor or a
  JPJ examiner (also in the brief); PDL holders display P plates front and back; minimum age 17.
  https://www.malaysia.gov.my/my/categories/pengangkutan/lesen-memandu/jenis-lesen-memandu
- The KEJARA point thresholds conflict between sources (20 vs 15) and house style bans point values,
  so no question gives a number.

## Speed limits
- National Speed Limit Order 1989 (Perintah Had Laju Kebangsaan 1989): expressways 110 km/h by
  default (some stretches 80 or 90), federal roads 90 km/h. State-road default (80 vs 90) conflicts,
  so it is not asked; neither is the town 60 km/h figure. Wikipedia "National Speed Limits
  (Malaysia)" and a 2018 parliamentary written reply (pardocs.sinarproject.org, soalan 276); March
  2024 Berita Harian: Minister Loke says 110 km/h stays.

## Alcohol, seat belts, child seats, phones
- Road Transport Act (Amendment) 2020, in force 23 October 2020: breath limit cut from 35 to 22
  micrograms per 100 ml (Mondaq / Mah-Kamariyah & Philip Koh summary). Blood figure not confirmed,
  so only the breath limit is asked.
  https://mondaq.com/rail-road-cycling/1237532/amendments-to-the-law-on-drink-driving-in-malaysia
- Child restraint system compulsory in private cars from 1 January 2020 (Motor Vehicles (Safety
  Seat-belts) (Amendment) Rules 2019; Malay Mail, FMT Nov 2025). Height/age thresholds differ by
  source (135 vs 136 cm), so no threshold is asked.
- Rear seat belts compulsory since 2009; hand-held phone use while driving is an offence (Road
  Traffic Rules). Asked only as yes/no.

## Signs and markings
- Warning signs yellow diamonds, prohibitory red circles, mandatory blue circles; green guide signs
  for tolled expressways and blue for other roads; brown for tourist attractions (Wikipedia "Road
  signs in Malaysia", Kurnia and Carsome sign guides). Malay wording BERHENTI, BERI LALUAN, IKUT
  KIRI, JALAN SEHALA, AWAS, KURANGKAN LAJU, HABIS, LORONG KECEMASAN, LALUAN BAS.
- Double yellow kerb lines = no parking at any time (Wikipedia "Yellow line (road marking)",
  Malaysia section). Single-yellow meaning varies, so not asked.
- Left turn on red only where a sign allows: the exact Malaysian sign wording was not confirmed, so
  the question does not quote it.

## Crashes and emergencies
- Road Transport Act 1987 s.52(2): report a crash at the nearest police station as soon as
  reasonably possible; lawyers quoted by the Borneo Post (27 Jan 2021) say a report later than 24
  hours without valid reason is an offence.
  https://www.theborneopost.com/2021/01/27/handle-road-accidents-the-right-way-lawyers/
- Emergency number 999 (MERS 999); 112 also works from mobiles.

## Brief rules followed
- Hazard lights only for a stopped vehicle (DEF-001, DEF-002, EMG-003).
- No "give way to the right" rule anywhere; roundabouts are "give way to traffic already on it".
- No "sunset to sunrise" headlight rule; headlights are asked as "poor visibility" and "at night".
- No fines, fees or demerit point values.
