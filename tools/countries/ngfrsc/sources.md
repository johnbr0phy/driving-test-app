# Sources for `ngfrsc` (Nigeria FRSC Highway Code / driving test theory)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the rule text below. No question was
copied from an FRSC bank, a driving school or a commercial practice app (open-exam-prep.com and
similar sites turned up in searches and were not opened). Signs are described in words (no images).
All sources were checked on 2026-10-31.

## Primary sources

1. **National Road Traffic Regulations 2012** (S.I. No. 61 of 2012, published 25 December 2012),
   FRSC copy: https://www.frsc.gov.ng/NATROADTRAFFICREGS2012.pdf (frsc.gov.ng refused direct
   connections on 2026-10-31; read from the Internet Archive capture of that exact file,
   http://web.archive.org/web/20250610023919/https://www.frsc.gov.ng/NATROADTRAFFICREGS2012.pdf,
   203 pages). Gazette listing: https://www.judy.legal/legislation/akn/ng/act/si/2012/61
   Regulations used:
   - 42(4)-(6) learner's permit: reapply no sooner than one calendar month after a failed test; no
     passenger except a licensed driver or certified instructor; licensed driver or instructor in
     the passenger seat beside the learner. (42(1) permit validity deliberately NOT used: brief.)
   - 44 classes: A motorcycle, B motor vehicle under 3 tonnes other than motorcycle/commercial,
     F agricultural machines and tractors, G articulated, H earth-moving, J physically challenged.
   - 45 fresh licence: approved driving school, learner's permit, visual acuity and medical
     fitness; private licence at not below 18; commercial at not below 26; temporary licence 60 days.
   - 46 renewal within one calendar month before expiry; driving test again if six months have
     elapsed; under 18 or unlicensed driving is an offence; annual recertification for commercial.
   - 47 disqualifications (uncontrolled epilepsy, uncontrolled diabetes, defective vision...).
   - 56 driving test content, including reading a number plate at 23 metres in daylight;
     56(3)/59 automatic-only endorsement. 57(3) medical checks incl. visual acuity, colour blindness.
   - 61 learner's sign: white background, letter L in red.
   - 119(6)-(11) required equipment (horn, jack, wheel spanner, fire extinguisher, spare tyre,
     first aid box, warning triangles or cones, insurance and roadworthiness certificates);
     private car extinguisher 1 x 2 kg, not expired; warning devices at rear and front not less
     than 45 m; triangle not removed until the vehicle is.
   - 122 lamps between sunset and sunrise; red only to the rear except white when reversing.
   - 124 single-note electric horn, no excessively loud horns. 125 two external mirrors plus an
     interior mirror. 126 seat belts front and rear for all occupants; child seats for children
     7 and below, never in the front row; driver responsible. 130 no tinted glass unless approved.
   - 152-153 speed: never above Schedule 8 or a posted sign; emergency vehicles on duty may exceed
     with sound device and lamp. Radar guns calibrated every 12 months.
   - 154-155 sirens/flashers only for named officials; obstructing a vehicle using them is an offence.
   - 156 obey officers, signs and signals; stop for pedestrians waiting at a crossing.
     157-158 damaging signs; emergency vehicles may disregard signs with siren and lamp.
   - 159-160 trafficators, arm signals (Schedule 7: when indicators are absent or faulty), signal
     before stopping, slowing suddenly, turning or moving left or right.
   - 161 do not enter an intersection without space to clear it; slow near schools and
     playgrounds; preference to children, elderly, physically challenged and visually impaired
     people at crossings; lane changes only when safe; slow down for oncoming traffic on narrow roads.
   - 162 keep right when meeting oncoming traffic; overtake on the left, except when the driver
     ahead signals left or in slow queues; slow before bends and keep right on them; no stopping
     within 8 m of a corner; push a disabled vehicle to the right side with lamps or warning signs;
     slow vehicles keep to the shoulder.
   - 163 parking prohibitions (crossings, bends, hill tops, footpaths, near lights or pedestrian
     crossings, alongside or opposite another parked vehicle, continuous white line, bus stops,
     school or hospital entrances, fire hydrants, wrong side, away from the kerb).
   - 165 level crossings: stop within 15 m but not less than 5 m from the nearest rail when warned;
     never drive through, round or under a closed or moving barrier.
   - 166 no phone calls, texts, images or browsing while moving; instructors may not phone while
     supervising; exemption for calls to emergency services.
   - 167 dangerous driving examples (tailgating, no lamps at night, engine running unattended or
     while refuelling, asleep at the wheel, no spare tyre or extinguisher, worn tyres).
     167(4) cracked windscreen. 168 doors and passengers. 169 dashboard TV off while moving.
   - 170 driving hours: no more than 5 hours continuous; 30 minutes rest every 3 hours on journeys
     over 5 hours; 8 consecutive hours rest in 24.
   - 173 pedestrian crossings (yield to a pedestrian on your half or close from the other half;
     do not pass a vehicle stopped at a crossing; pedestrians use footbridges; walk facing traffic).
   - 174, 176 alcohol and drugs, including occupying the driver's seat with the engine running;
     refusing a test is an offence. (175's figures are garbled in the gazette text and contested,
     so no alcohol limit number is asked.)
   - 177 wrongly parked vehicles ticketed and removed within 24 hours, or at once if dangerous.
   - 183-190 expressways: no livestock, no bicycles, tricycles, motorcycles under 50cc or
     pedestrians except on designated paths, no agricultural machines, loading only at designated
     stops, no stopping except in emergency, no repairs on the main carriageway, U-turns only at
     designated points, no parking on the central reserve, parking on the hard shoulder allowed.
   - 191-192 crash reporting to the nearest police station and FRSC office as soon as practicable;
     minor, serious and fatal crash classes.
   - 194-195 motorcycle and bicycle helmets and passengers.

2. **FRSC Highway Code literacy compendiums** (FRSC promotion-exam study material, which restate
   the Highway Code):
   - 2025 Compendium for MI-I, section 1 "Highway Code literacy":
     https://frsc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/MI-I-COMPENDIUM-2025.pdf
     Road and junction types; sign categories (warning, regulatory, informative; prohibitory and
     mandatory; STOP octagonal; no waiting / no stopping blue with red border; mandatory blue
     circles); traffic light meanings (red, red and amber, green, amber, green arrow); road
     markings (hatched areas, continuous lines, chevrons with continuous or broken borders, block
     markings at roundabouts); causes of crashes (human factor about 90%); WOFT daily check and
     safety parade (a pair of reflective triangles, fire extinguisher...); ABC first aid.
   - 2024 Compendium for RCs: https://frsc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/RC-COMPENDIUM-2024.pdf
     Speed limit definition; driving at night, in rain (stopping distance four or more times
     longer), fog (low beam; pull off and use hazard lights if too dense), sun glare, hills, blind
     turns, busy roads, slow traffic (legal, safe, necessary?), animals, bad roads (common sense
     speed in and out of built-up areas), convoys; defensive driving; persons with disabilities
     (FRSC or VIO stickers on front and rear windscreens); "Call 122 or 112".

3. **FRSC news release, 8 July 2021**, "FRSC Commences Computer Based Test For Applicants":
   https://frsc.gov.ng/creating-a-safer-road-use-culture-and-enhancing-the-integrity-of-the-national-drivers-license-frsc-commences-computer-based-test-for-applicants/
   (read via the Internet Archive capture of 2025-06-19). States: CBT for fresh applicants in all
   driving schools from 1 June 2021; 26 training sessions, exam number generated after session 25;
   "35 multi-choice questions" in "30 minutes"; pass mark 60%; retake after 7 days.
   FRSC toll-free number 122 is shown in the site header.

## Format decision

The builder brief fixes 4 tests x 50 questions and passPct 75 as Tigertest's own target. The FRSC
2021 release above is the only official format statement found (35 questions, 30 minutes, 60%).
The landing and examFacts state the FRSC figures with this source and call 50 questions and 75%
Tigertest's stricter practice target. FRSC publishes no per-topic weighting; the blueprint follows
the Highway Code and NRTR 2012 parts.

## Left out on purpose (brief or unconfirmed)

- Car speed limits on highways and expressways (80 vs 90 km/h conflict between sources; Schedule 8
  is an image). No numeric car limit is asked.
- Tanker and trailer speed limits.
- Learner's permit validity.
- Blood alcohol figures (NRTR 175 text is garbled; press figures differ).
- Fines, penalty points and fees (house style).
- Roundabout give-way direction stated only as "traffic already on the roundabout"; circulation
  is anticlockwise because Nigeria drives on the right.
- Arm signal shapes (Schedule 7 is an image); only when to use them is asked.
