# Sources for `phlto` (Philippines LTO driver's license written exam)

All 200 questions are original. No LTO exam paper, TDC reviewer PDF or commercial reviewer app was
copied. Rules come from the statute text on lawphil.net (all checked 2026-10-31) and the other
sources below. Signs are described in words (no images). Copy uses Philippine (US) spelling:
license, meters, curb. lto.gov.ph returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches on 2026-10-31, so LTO's
own pages were not read directly.

## Test format
- LTO Theoretical Driving Course module TDC-15-S-01-2, version 9-2021: written exam of 60 questions,
  48 correct to pass (80%). Decision supplied in the builder brief (verified by the fact-checkers);
  the module was not found online by this builder.
- LTO Memorandum Circular AHS-2008-949, 12 March 2008, "Standards for Written and Practical
  Examinations": non-professional 30 of 40, professional 45 of 60; practical exam passing score 70.
  https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/52964 (checked 2026-10-31)
- LTO publishes no per-topic counts. The blueprint (8/12/6/9/9/5/8/3 per 60) is Tigertest's own,
  with signs weighted heaviest; the landing says so.

## Licenses and permits
- Ages 16 (student permit), 17 (non-professional), 18 (professional); student permit valid 1 year:
  builder brief (fact-checked). RA 4136 Sec 30 still prints 6 months and 18, superseded by LTO rules.
- RA 4136 (Land Transportation and Traffic Code), https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1964/ra_4136_1964.html :
  Sec 19 carry the license at all times; Sec 26 not renewed = delinquent and invalid; Sec 29
  confiscation receipt good for up to 72 hours; Sec 30 student driver only when accompanied by a duly
  licensed driver, who is responsible for violations and damage. The bank never says the
  companion must sit "beside" the student (not in the law).
- RA 10930 (2017), https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10930_2017.html : license valid
  5 years from the birthdate; 10 years if no violations in the 5-year period.
- 15-hour TDC before a student permit; PDC before a non-professional license; LTO-accredited
  medical clinic; hold the student permit at least one month: consistent across secondary guides
  (TopGear PH, Carmudi, philstar 2021, TESDA BR 2020-46 for the 15 hours). Medium confidence on the
  one-month holding period (LIC-019).

## Right of way, speed, overtaking, parking (all RA 4136, lawphil, checked 2026-10-31)
- Sec 35(a) careful and prudent speed; Sec 35(b) table: cars 80 / 40 / 30 / 20 km/h, trucks and
  buses 50 / 30 / 30 / 20 (open country, through streets, city streets, crowded/school zones);
  Sec 35(c) exemptions (physicians, ambulances, officers chasing a violator).
- Sec 37 keep right; Sec 39 overtake on the left, return when safely clear, pass on the right only
  in business/residential districts with 2+ lanes one way; Sec 40 give way to the overtaker on an
  audible signal, do not speed up; Sec 41 no overtaking unless the left is clear, on a crest/curve
  with view under 500 feet, at railway crossings, at intersections unless signal/officer controlled,
  between road work signs, in no-passing zones.
- Sec 42(a) left yields to right; 42(b) yield to a vehicle already in the intersection or already
  turning left that has signaled (brief fact); 42(c) pedestrians in crosswalks; 42(d) full stop
  before a through highway or railroad crossing. Sec 43 driveway exits and through highways yield;
  Sec 49 pull to the right and stop for police/fire/ambulance with audible signal, clear of the
  intersection. Sec 44 signals by left arm or approved device (hand-signal meanings are the usual
  convention, not statute text). Sec 45 right turn from the right lane, left from nearest center.
- Sec 46 no parking within an intersection, on a crosswalk, within 6 m of curb-line intersections,
  within 4 m of a fire station driveway or fire hydrant, in front of a private driveway, double
  parking, where signs prohibit. Sec 47 stop the engine and set the hand brake. Sec 52 no driving
  or parking on sidewalks. Sec 54 no obstructing traffic, including while loading passengers.
  Sec 32 no loading beyond registered capacity. Sec 51 no hitching. Sec 34 headlights from not later
  than half an hour after sunset; dim when meeting; parking lights/flares visible 100 m.
- Sec 55 duties after a crash; may leave only if in imminent danger, reporting to the nearest
  officer, or summoning a doctor or nurse.

## Road safety laws (lawphil, checked 2026-10-31)
- RA 8750 Seat Belts Use Act: https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1999/ra_8750_1999.html
- RA 11229 Child Safety in Motor Vehicles Act (12 and below, 150 cm, front seat, never unattended):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11229_2019.html
- RA 10054 Motorcycle Helmet Act (driver and back rider, PS mark or ICC, tricycle drivers exempt):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_10054_2010.html
- RA 10666 Children's Safety on Motorcycles Act (foot pegs, waist, helmet; roads over 60 km/h):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2015/ra_10666_2015.html
- RA 10913 Anti-Distracted Driving Act (covers red-light stops, hands-free if line of sight is
  clear, emergency calls exempt, motorcycles covered): https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2016/ra_10913_2016.html
- RA 10586 Anti-Drunk and Drugged Driving Act (field sobriety on probable cause, refusal =
  confiscation and revocation, mandatory tests after injury/death crashes, confirmatory drug test):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10586_2013.html . BAC thresholds from its IRR
  (2014): 0.05% for vehicles up to 4,500 kg GVW, 0.0% for trucks, buses, motorcycles and PUVs
  (Supreme Court e-Library copy https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/63134,
  TopGear PH and Philstar explainers).
- DOTC Department Order 2007-38 (4 Oct 2007): motorcycles of 400cc and above on expressways
  (GMA News, jur.ph summary). Expressway speed limits were left out (sources conflict by road).

## Signs, markings, emergencies
- DPWH Highway Safety Design Standards Part 2, Road Signs and Pavement Markings Manual (May 2012):
  warning signs are red-bordered triangles; regulatory signs are red-bordered (prohibitory) or blue
  (mandatory) circles; STOP octagon; give way inverted triangle. Via the Wikipedia summary of the
  manual (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_signs_in_the_Philippines), checked 2026-10-31.
- LTO primer on road traffic signs and pavement markings (Carmudi, "FYI: LTO Releases Primer..."):
  broken side of a solid/broken yellow pair may pass, solid side may not; stop line before the
  pedestrian lane. https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/fyi-lto-releases-primer-on-road-traffic-signs-pavement-markings/
- Early warning devices: Letter of Instruction 229 (1974) and LTO memoranda (placement ahead of and
  behind a stalled vehicle). The 4-meter figure was not asked.
- 911 national emergency hotline (Executive Order 56, 2016).

## Deliberately avoided
- Money figures (fines, fees), expressway speed limits, number coding and other local ordinances,
  right turn on red, the LTO license restriction code letters, the RA 11235 plate rules.
