# Sources for `zak53c3` (South Africa K53 learner's licence test, Code 3, heavy vehicles)

All 200 questions are original and none is shared with the Code 2 bank (`tools/countries/zak53/`).
The general rules, signs and markings sources are the same as zak53 (see `../zak53/sources.md`):
the National Road Traffic Act 93 of 1996 and Regulations 2000, the gov.za learner's licence page,
Arrive Alive, and the SADC Road Traffic Signs Manual through Wikipedia's "Road signs in South
Africa". Checked 2026-11-03 unless noted.

## Code 3 and the heavy vehicle licences
- gov.za, "Apply for a learner's licence"
  (https://www.gov.za/services/driving-licence/apply-learners-licence): Code 3 learner's from 18;
  valid 24 months, cannot be extended; supervised driving only.
- Wikipedia, "Driving licence in South Africa" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driving_licence_in_South_Africa):
  C1 3,500 to 16,000 kg GVM, C over 16,000 kg, EC1 and EC the articulated or trailer combinations;
  old codes 10 (C1) and 14 (EC).
- The Code 3 test format is not published officially. Practice sites disagree (64 questions with
  8 controls; one says 68 with 16 controls). The landing gives guides' 28/28/8 with "guides say";
  Tigertest's mocks reuse the Code 2 bank's 22/22/6 split, 40 of 50 to pass (our own target).

## PrDP
- NRTA s32: a professional driving permit is needed for, among others, a goods vehicle over
  3,500 kg GVM (category G, from 18), passengers for reward (P, from 21), dangerous goods (D,
  from 25). Western Cape Education Department circular e44/15
  (https://wcedonline.westerncape.gov.za/circulars/circulars15/e44_15.html); Arrive Alive, "What
  is the difference between a PDP and a PrDP".

## Heavy vehicle rules
- Professional drivers' alcohol limit 0.02 g per 100 ml of blood, 0.10 mg per 1,000 ml of breath (NRTA s65).
- Goods vehicles over 9,000 kg GVM: 80 km/h maximum (reg 293; Arrive Alive, "Speed limits and the law").
- Loads secured so they cannot fall, shift or spill; GVM and axle mass limits; weighbridge directions.

## Deliberately avoided
- Vehicle dimension and load projection limits, driving hours, tachographs, specific air pressure
