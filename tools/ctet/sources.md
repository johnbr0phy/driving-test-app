# CTET Paper I sources

Checked October 2026.

- CBSE, CTET Information Bulletin and syllabus (ctet.nic.in), pattern unchanged through the February 2026
  (21st) session: Paper I for Classes 1 to 5, 150 one-mark MCQs in 2 hours 30 minutes, no negative marking,
  five sections of 30 (CDP 15 child development, 5 inclusive education and children with special needs,
  10 learning and pedagogy; Language I and Language II each 15 comprehension and 15 pedagogy of language
  development; Mathematics 15 content and 15 pedagogy; EVS 15 content and 15 pedagogy). Language I and II are
  chosen from 20 languages. February 2026 was pen-and-paper (OMR), results 30 March 2026 (press reports).
- Qualifying mark: 60% (90 of 150) per the bulletin and NCTE guidelines; reserved categories 55% (concession
  under NCTE guidelines; reported as 82 or 83 marks). Certificate validity: lifetime (NCTE decision, 2021).
- NCERT, National Curriculum Framework 2005, and its position papers on Teaching of Mathematics and Teaching of
  Indian Languages (mathematisation as the main goal, multilingualism as a resource, connecting knowledge to
  life outside school, integrated EVS for Classes 3 to 5).
- NCERT, Looking Around (EVS textbooks, Classes 3 to 5) and the EVS source book on assessment (indicators:
  observation and recording, discussion, expression, explanation, classification, questioning, analysis,
  experimentation, concern for justice and equality, cooperation).
- National Education Policy 2020 (foundational literacy and numeracy by Grade 3; NIPUN Bharat).
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (21 specified disabilities, up from 7 under the 1995 Act).
- Standard child development texts for Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, Gardner, Bruner, Chomsky, Krashen and the
  van Hiele levels.

All 160 questions, the three prose passages and the two poems are original. No CTET past paper items were
used. Language II is not modelled. Exact stems were checked against every other bank in data/ (two generic
stems that matched NAPLAN and 11+ items were rewritten).
