# DMV bank sources

The DMV bank (`data/questions.json` and its `_es`, `_vi`, `_ko`, `_zh` translations) is edited directly; it has no build script. Official sources checked when a question is fixed are listed here, dated.

## California

- https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/safe-driving-cont2/ (California DMV, California Driver's Handbook, Section 8, read 2026-11-18). "Minors and cell phones": "It is against the law for a minor to use a cell phone or electronic wireless device to answer calls and send or respond to text messages while driving." "Minors may use a cell phone to make a call for emergency assistance." Adults: "Adult drivers should only use a cell phone in hands-free mode when necessary." Used for CA-010 (stem now says "Outside of an emergency ... under 18"; the emergency option was replaced, since the handbook allows emergency calls).
- https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/getting-an-instruction-permit-and-drivers-license/ (read 2026-11-18): "When you are under 18 years old, your driver's license will have the word provisional."
