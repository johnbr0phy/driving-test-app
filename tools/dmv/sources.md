# DMV permit test: official sources

The DMV bank (`data/questions.json`, translated as `data/questions_<lang>.json`) is edited in place; there is no build script. Each state's rules come from that state's official driver handbook or vehicle code.

## California

- https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/getting-an-instruction-permit-and-drivers-license/ (California DMV, California Driver's Handbook, "Getting an Instruction Permit and Driver's License"; read 2026-11-24). Says to "Practice driving with a California-licensed driver who is at least 18 years old (25 for minors)", and that applicants under 18 practice at least 50 hours "with a California-licensed driver who is at least 25 years old". Used for CA-011 (stem now names a permit holder under 18, key 25 unchanged, explanation adds the 18+ rule), audit 2026-11-24.
