# FAA Private Pilot Airplane (PAR) sources

All 202 items are original, written from the public FAA handbooks and regulations below. Nothing is copied
from the FAA airman knowledge test bank (not public) or from the FAA sample question PDF
(`par_questions.pdf`), and nothing is taken from the Tigertest Part 107 bank (`data/part107-questions.json`).
No item needs a figure: chart symbols, METAR/TAF strings and performance numbers are written into the stem.

## Test format (checked October 2026)
- FAA ACS knowledge test table (faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/acs_knowledge_test_table.pdf):
  PAR, 60 questions, minimum age 15, 2.0 hours allotted (effective 4/24/23), passing score 70.
- FAA Community Advisory, April 2023: PAR time reduced from 2.5 to 2.0 hours.
  (The SPEC row said 2h30; that was the pre-April-2023 figure. The config uses 120 minutes.)
- Three answer choices per question (A, B, C), as on all FAA airman knowledge tests.
- Knowledge test result valid 24 calendar months (14 CFR 61.39).

## Content sources
- Private Pilot for Airplane Category ACS, FAA-S-ACS-6C (effective May 31, 2024): knowledge areas and elements.
- Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge, FAA-H-8083-25C (2023): aerodynamics, systems, instruments,
  weather theory, performance, weight and balance, navigation, aeromedical factors, ADM.
- Aviation Weather Handbook, FAA-H-8083-28A (2024; replaced AC 00-6 and AC 00-45): reports and forecasts,
  GFA replacing the textual area forecast, AIRMET/SIGMET/CWA criteria, winds aloft coding.
- Aeronautical Information Manual (current edition): airspace, airport signs and lighting, light gun
  signals, wake turbulence, weather briefings, microbursts, scuba diving guidance (8-1-2), illusions.
- 14 CFR Parts 1, 43, 61 and 91 (eCFR, current): 61.3, 61.23, 61.31, 61.39, 61.56, 61.57, 61.60, 61.113,
  43.3, 43.9, 91.3, 91.7, 91.17, 91.103, 91.107, 91.113, 91.117, 91.119, 91.121, 91.126-91.135, 91.151,
  91.155, 91.157, 91.159, 91.203, 91.205, 91.207, 91.211, 91.213, 91.215, 91.225, 91.303, 91.307, 91.403,
  91.409, 91.411, 91.413.
- 49 CFR Part 830 (NTSB notification and reporting): 830.5, 830.15.
- Aircraft Weight and Balance Handbook, FAA-H-8083-1B; Risk Management Handbook, FAA-H-8083-2A;
  FAA Aeronautical Chart User's Guide (sectional symbols, MEF, obstruction labels, MTR numbering).

## Blueprint
The FAA does not publish per-area counts for PAR. Per test: regulations 9, airspace and airport operations 8,
weather theory 7, weather services 7, aerodynamics/systems/instruments 9, performance and weight and balance 7,
navigation 7, human factors and ADM 6 (= 60). Pools (tests 1, 2 and drill-only): 9/9/11, 8/8/10, 7/7/10,
7/7/10, 9/9/12, 7/7/10, 7/7/10, 6/6/9 = 202.

## Facts deliberately avoided
BasicMed aircraft and occupant limits (the FAA Reauthorization Act of 2024 changes them, rulemaking pending),
domestic versus ICAO VFR flight plan forms, HIWAS and Flight Watch (decommissioned), parachute repack days.
