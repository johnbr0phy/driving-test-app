practice or past paper was opened or copied; only the official content and

## Design decisions

- Practice paper = 10 non-calculator + 30 calculator one-mark questions (the 25% / 75% split), 20 + 70 minutes.
  Real papers are fewer, multi-mark questions; the time is scaled, not copied.
- Domain weights per paper (no official content-area weights exist): number 9, fractions/percentages/ratio/money 12,
  measures/shape/space 12, data 7. Section A per paper: 4 / 3 / 2 / 1.
- Content item 13 (money, interest, discounts, tax, budgeting) is grouped with percentages in `fsmPercent`.
- Income tax item uses figures stated in the stem ("use these figures"), so it does not date.
- Conversion factors (1 mile ≈ 1.6 km, 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb, 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 gallon ≈ 4.55 litres) are always given.
- Content item 21 (drawing plans and elevations) cannot be drawn on screen; it is assessed as reading and naming views.

## Not confirmed

- Exact Level 2 assessment duration per awarding body other than Pearson (several revision sites say "about 2 hours";
  the landing says "many ... around 2 hours in total" and tells readers to check).
- Whether every awarding body supplies a formula sheet. The practice papers show one; the landing FAQ does not claim the
  real exam does.
- GCSE equivalence: there is no single rule (apprenticeship maths requirements for adults changed in 2025, and some
  courses insist on the GCSE), so the landing says many employers, colleges and providers accept Level 2 in place of
  GCSE grade 4, that not all do, and to check with the course or employer.
