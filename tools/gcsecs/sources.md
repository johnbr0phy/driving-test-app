# GCSE Computer Science: sources (checked 2026-10-30)

All 202 questions and the 6 Basics skills are original. No past-paper, specimen or
commercial item was copied. Topic coverage follows the DfE subject content; the paper
split follows the boards' published schemes of assessment.

- DfE, "GCSE subject content for computer science" (first published 26 Jan 2015, last
  updated 31 Mar 2025): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gcse-computer-science/gcse-subject-content-for-computer-science
  13 knowledge areas: standard algorithms (binary search, merge sort); writing and tracing
  algorithms; data types and structures (records, 1D/2D arrays); binary and hex, binary
  addition and shifts; text, sound and graphics; Boolean logic (AND, OR, NOT, truth
  tables); systems software; systems architecture (CPU, fetch-execute, storage, data
  capacity, embedded systems); networks (types, security, protocols, layers); cyber
  security; ethical, legal and environmental impacts; levels of programming language.
- AQA GCSE Computer Science 8525, scheme of assessment: Paper 1 Computational thinking and
  programming skills, 2 h, 90 marks, 50%; Paper 2 Computing concepts (including SQL),
  1 h 45 min, 90 marks, 50%. https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/computer-science/gcse/computer-science-8525/specification/scheme-of-assessment
  and key dates for June 2027 (8525/1 2 h, 8525/2 1 h 45 min): https://aqa.org.uk/subjects/computer-science/gcse/computer-science-8525/key-dates
- OCR GCSE Computer Science J277: J277/01 Computer systems and J277/02 Computational
  thinking, algorithms and programming, each 1 h 30 min, 80 marks, 50%, non-calculator;
  Paper 2 uses OCR Exam Reference Language. (Confirmed via OCR spec summaries and
  school pages, e.g. https://www.savemyexams.com/gcse/computer-science/ocr/22/revision-notes/12-exam-technique/the-exam-papers/paper-1-guidance/)
- Pearson Edexcel GCSE Computer Science 1CP2: Paper 1 Principles of Computer Science
  (written); Paper 2 Application of Computational Thinking, 2 h on-screen (Python),
  75 marks, 50%. https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/GCSE/Computer%20Science/2020/teaching-and-learning-materials/pearson-edexcel-computer-science-assessment-overview.pdf
- UK law named in the impacts items: Data Protection Act 2018 (UK GDPR principles,
  subject access, ICO enforcement), Computer Misuse Act 1990 (unauthorised access, with
  intent, unauthorised acts to impair), Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.

Board differences handled:
- Pseudocode: one neutral style (← assignment, = comparison, MOD/DIV, zero-based arrays,
  explicit ENDIF/ENDWHILE) with a reference panel on Paper 1, since AQA and OCR each have
  their own reference language.
- Test data categories: only values that are normal / boundary / erroneous under both AQA
  and OCR conventions are used (OCR's separate "invalid" category is avoided).
- Insertion sort (OCR) and SQL (AQA) appear as board-specific skills; SQL is drill-only.
- Units: stems state 1 KB = 1,000 bytes; Basics mentions 1,024 (KiB).

Not confirmed / judgement calls: Edexcel Paper 1's exact length was not rechecked (the
landing only states the on-screen Paper 2). Practice papers here are 30 auto-marked
items in 45 and 40 minutes, not the real mark totals; the landing says so.
