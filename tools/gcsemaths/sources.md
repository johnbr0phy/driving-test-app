item was copied or adapted; topic coverage and weights come from the sources
below.

## Not confirmed

- The brief suggested probability and statistics at 22-28% for Foundation.
  The AQA table and every school page found give 15% (12-18%), which also
  makes the five midpoints sum to 100, so 15% is used.
- Paper length 1 hour 30 minutes and calculator rules are from memory and the
  board specifications' summaries; the AQA scheme-of-assessment page fetched
  did not state durations.
- Grade boundary figures are secondary summaries apart from the Pearson PDF
  URL; they are only used as a rough guide with a "varies by board and year"
  caveat.
