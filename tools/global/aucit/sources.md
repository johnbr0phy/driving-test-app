# Sources: Australian citizenship test (aucit)

- **Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond**, testable section, Department of Home Affairs.
  https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/citizenship-subsite/files/our-common-bond-testable.pdf
  Downloaded 2026-10-30 (50 pages, (c) Commonwealth of Australia 2020, current text: Head of State
  "His Majesty King Charles III", Gallipoli "in Türkiye", House of Representatives "over 150 members").
  Parts: 1 Australia and its people; 2 Australia's democratic beliefs, rights and liberties;
  3 Government and the law in Australia; 4 Australian values; plus a glossary of the testable section.
  Every item is original and written from this text; the glossary (e.g. Indigenous peoples about 3%,
  Australian Human Rights Commission for racism complaints) counts as testable.
- **Test format** (book p.3 and Home Affairs "Learn about citizenship test" page, checked 2026-10-30):
  computer-based, multiple choice, in English; 20 randomly selected questions including five on
  Australian values (since 15 November 2020); pass = all five values correct AND at least 75% overall
  (15/20); 45 minutes (Home Affairs test page and its official practice test site).
  https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/citizenship/test-and-interview/learn-about-citizenship-interview-and-test/learn-about-citizenship-test
  https://citizenshippracticetest.homeaffairs.gov.au/
- **Blueprint**: Home Affairs does not publish how the 15 non-values questions are split across parts
  1 to 3. Ours is 5 / 4 / 6 (+5 values), roughly by the length of each part of the book.

Not asserted anywhere: the pledge (it sits before the testable section), fees, age rules for who sits
the test, retake rules, and anything from the non-testable "Our Australian story" section.
