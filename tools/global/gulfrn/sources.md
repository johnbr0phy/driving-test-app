# gulfrn sources (checked 2026-10-30)

## Read from the authority's own documents
- Qatar, MOPH Department of Healthcare Professions, Qualifying Examination page
  (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/QualifyingExamination.aspx): Registered General Nurse and
  Registered Midwife exam 150 MCQs, 3 hours, cut score 50%, unlimited attempts, no rounding,
  computer-based at Prometric.
- Qatar DHP "Qualifying Examination Blueprint" (NurseAndMidwiferyBlueprintAndReference.pdf):
  Registered General Nurse 150 MCQs, 3.5 hours (the page says 3), pass 50%. Nursing Fundamentals 30,
  Adult Nursing 61, Maternal-Child Nursing 44, Unit Management and Leadership 15. References: Kozier
  and Erb, Brunner and Suddarth, Lewis, Wong, Murray and McKinney, Stuart (psychiatric), Stanhope
  (community), Sullivan (leadership).
- Saudi Arabia, SCFHS "Saudi Nursing Licensing Exam (SNLE) Applicant Guide", March 2022
  (scfhs.org.sa/sites/default/files/2022-06/SNLE Applicant Guide - March 2022_0.pdf): 200 MCQs, up to
  10% pilot, two parts of 100 questions, 120 minutes each, 30-minute break, four options one best
  answer; sections Nursing Fundamentals (fundamentals, physical assessment, pharmacology, nutrition),
  Adult Nursing (medical, surgical, critical care, community, mental/psychiatric), Maternal-Child
  (maternity, gynecology, neonatal, pediatric medical and surgical), Nursing Management and
  Leadership; blueprint may vary +/-5% per category; passing score 500 on a 200 to 800 scale; up to
  four attempts a year; delivered at SCFHS-approved Prometric centres. The section percentages are an
  image in the PDF and could not be read; prep sites quote 20/40/30/10, consistent with Qatar.

## Not verified (prep and consultancy sites only, conflicting)
- DHA (Dubai): commonly quoted 150 MCQs, 3 hours, 50% for RN; others say 100 questions / 60 to 65%.
- DOH (Abu Dhabi): sources disagree on question count and pass mark; several say DOH now delivers
  through Pearson VUE rather than Prometric. The landing therefore never says which vendor DOH uses.
- MOHAP, OMSB (Oman: quoted 100 MCQs / 150 min / 60%), Kuwait MOH (quoted 150 / 170 min / 60%),
  Bahrain NHRA (Prometric's NHRA page reportedly states a 60% pass for all Bahrain licensure exams;
  the page returned 403 to us).
These are shown on the landing as "Check guide".

## Blueprint used
Per 50-question test: fundamentals 7 + pharmacology/dosage 4 (= 22%, Qatar 20%), adult 20 (cardio-
resp 7, other med-surg systems incl. critical care and perioperative 8, mental health 3, community
2 = 40%, Qatar 40.7%), maternal-child 14 (maternal/newborn 7, pediatrics 7 = 28%, Qatar 29.3%),
leadership/ethics/infection control 5 (10%, Qatar 10%).

## Clinical references used for the items
Standard texts named in the two blueprints (Kozier and Erb, Brunner and Suddarth, Wong), WHO hand
hygiene guidance (20 to 30 s rub, 40 to 60 s wash), WHO breastfeeding (6 months exclusive), WHO BMI
cut-offs, WHO HbA1c 6.5% (48 mmol/mol), current adult and pediatric resuscitation guidelines (100 to
120/min, 30:2, 15:2 two-rescuer pediatric), Holliday-Segar maintenance fluid, Naegele's rule.
All 200 items are original; none copied from any bank or recall collection.
