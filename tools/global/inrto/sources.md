# inrto: India learner's licence (LL) test sources

Checked 30 Oct 2026.

## Law the questions are written from
- Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, as amended by the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019 (India Code, indiacode.nic.in).
  Sections used: 3 (licence needed), 4 (ages: 18, or 16 for a motorcycle up to 50cc), 7(2) (guardian's written consent under 18), 5 (owner
  must not let unlicensed person drive), 10 (licence classes), 14 (LL valid 6 months; transport DL 5 years;
  non-transport DL to age 40 if issued before 30), 2(21) (LMV up to 7,500 kg), 2(12A) (golden hour), 56 (fitness
  certificate), 112 (speed limits), 128 (one pillion), 129 (helmets for everyone 4 and above, BIS standard, turban
  exemption), 130 (produce licence), 131 (unmanned level crossing), 132 (stop when signalled), 134 (accident duties,
  report within 24 hours), 134A (good Samaritan), 146 (third-party insurance), 184 (dangerous driving incl. handheld
  phones), 185 (30 mg per 100 ml), 189 (racing), 194B (seat belts, child under 14 restraint), 194D (helmet: 3 months'
  disqualification), 194E (give way to emergency vehicles), 199A (juvenile offences).
- Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989: Rule 3 (L plate red on white, instructor beside learner), Rule 11 (LL test,
  online tutorial, 60 percent pass), the 30-day wait before the driving test, Form 1 / 1A, Rule 115 (PUC), Rule 138(7)
  (child under 4 on a motorcycle: harness, helmet, 40 km/h; in force Feb 2023), Rule 139 (documents in DigiLocker /
  mParivahan), the 2020 amendment allowing a handheld device for navigation only, AHO for two-wheelers from April
  2017, HSRP.
- Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations, 2017 (G.S.R. 634(E), 23 June 2017, under Section 118), which superseded the Rules
  of the Road Regulations, 1989 (reviewer, 30 Oct 2026, text via indiacode). Verified there: hand signals reg 11(2)
  (stop, right, left anticlockwise, overtake; the slow-down signal is from the 1989 schedule only), overtaking on the
  left reg 14(2), no overtaking reg 14(5), give way to the right reg 9(2), roundabouts reg 10, no reversing into a ONE
  WAY road reg 18(3), hill roads (uphill has priority) reg 21, parking reg 22(2) (50 m of a junction, bus stop, school or
  hospital entrance, continuous yellow kerb line, double parking; wrong-side parking is not listed), horn reg 23,
  towing reg 30 (5 m, 25 km/h, no two-wheelers, retro-reflective ON TOW on both vehicles), footpaths reg 39(3).
  Earlier builder notes on the 1989 regulations: keep left, overtake on the right, pass on the left only a vehicle signalling a
  right turn, no overtaking at bends / hill brows / pedestrian crossings / when the road ahead is not clear, do not
  speed up when overtaken, turning left and right positions, give way to the right at uncontrolled junctions and to
  main-road traffic, emergency vehicles, pedestrian crossings, hand signals (schedule), parking prohibitions, one-way
  roads, towing (5 metres, ON TOW notice), horn use and silence zones, lane discipline, sudden braking, processions.
- Road signs: the schedule to the Motor Vehicles Act (mandatory, cautionary, informatory), as reproduced in IRC:67.
  Signs are described in words in the stems; shapes and colours were checked against the schedule.
- Supreme Court, Avishek Goenka v Union of India (2012): sun-control film on car glass banned.

## Test format (varies by state; used on the landing table and in examFacts)
- Delhi Transport Department, Learner's licence page (transport.delhi.gov.in/hi/node/3079): computer-based
  multiple-choice test on road signs and traffic rules, pass with 60 percent. Does not print the question count.
- Kerala MVD revised pattern from 1 Oct 2025: 30 questions (was 20), 18 to pass, 30 seconds per question
  (Onmanorama 13 Sep 2025; Kerala Kaumudi; New Indian Express). Press reports, no MVD notice found; old format 20 questions.
- 15 or 20 question formats are reported by guides but not by any official state page or reputable outlet, so the
  landing and examFacts no longer state them (reviewer removed the rows). Delhi "10 questions, 6 to pass" also unconfirmed.
- Draft CMVR amendment, Oct 2026 (Onmanorama 23 Oct 2026, Autocar India, DriveSpark, Zee Business; reports from about
  22-23 Oct): pass mark 60 to 75 percent, 30-minute tutorial. Reports disagree on whether the 75% applies to the test or
  to the tutorial assessment, and on the completion window (7 or 15 days). Draft only, 30 days for objections; not in
  force. Reviewer could not open the gazette text.

## Deliberately avoided
- Exact fine amounts (2019 set central minimums; states may raise them, Section 210A).
- Exact speed limits by vehicle and road (MoRTH notification 2018, states can set lower).
- PUC validity periods, retest waiting periods and fees (vary by state / emission norm).
