# Sources for `uklitu` (Life in the UK test)

All 200 questions are original. No commercial question bank, past paper or practice book was copied;
the official handbook fixed the topics and facts. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Test format
- GOV.UK, "Life in the UK Test": https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test
  24 questions in 45 minutes about British traditions and customs; book online at least 3 days
  ahead at one of 30+ UK test centres; study only the official "Life in the United Kingdom:
  A Guide for New Residents". Pass mark confirmed on https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test/what-happens-test (checked
  2026-10-30): "You must score 75% or more to pass the test", i.e. 18 of 24.

## Study material
- Home Office, "Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents", 3rd edition (TSO, 2013,
  reprinted since). Chapters tested: 1 The values and principles of the UK; 2 What is the UK?;
  3 A long and illustrious history; 4 A modern, thriving society; 5 The UK government, the law
  and your role. Categories here follow those chapters, with chapter 3 split at 1688/1689.
- No newer edition has been published as of 2026-10-30. The 2013 rewrite (Home Office press release,
  27 January 2013) is the current content.

## 2025-2026 changes checked
- The government's earned settlement consultation (opened November 2025, closed 12 February 2026)
  keeps the Life in the UK test as a settlement requirement, and commentators (secondary sources only) report a
  stated intention (October 2025) to refresh the test; the landing says only that the government has signalled an update. No revised handbook, syllabus or test format had been
  published on GOV.UK as of 2026-10-30. Re-check before each content update.
- Facts that changed since the 3rd edition and are handled in explanations: the monarch is King
  Charles III (anthem "God Save the King"; items say "the monarch"); the National Assembly for Wales
  became the Senedd / Welsh Parliament in 2020; the IPCC became the IOPC in 2018; the UK left the
  EU in 2020; the Elizabeth Tower name (2012). Items avoid the voting age (Scotland and Wales allow 16
  in devolved elections; UK-wide change under discussion), the English language level for
  settlement (B1 now, B2 proposed) and the number of Commonwealth members.

## Weighting
- The Home Office publishes no per-chapter question counts. Per 24-question test: values 2, what is
  the UK 1, history to 1688 4, history from 1689 5, society 6, government 6 (history 9 of 24,
  the largest share, as the brief and commonly reported test experience suggest).
