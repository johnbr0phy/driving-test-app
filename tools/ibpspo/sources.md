# IBPS PO Prelims: sources

Exam: IBPS Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers / Management Trainees (CRP PO/MT),
Preliminary Examination. Conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (ibps.in).

## Pattern modelled
- Notification: IBPS CRP PO/MT-XV (2025-26 cycle, notified July 2025; prelims held in August 2025).
  The Prelims pattern has been stable since 2020 and is the same in the 2025 notification.
- One online objective paper, 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes, with sectional timing:
  - English Language: 30 questions, 30 marks, 20 minutes
  - Quantitative Aptitude: 35 questions, 35 marks, 20 minutes
  - Reasoning Ability: 35 questions, 35 marks, 20 minutes
- Penalty: one quarter of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer; no penalty for
  a question left blank.
- Candidates must clear sectional and overall cutoffs (set per category each year). Prelims is qualifying;
  the final merit list uses Mains and interview marks only.
- Each section is answered in its own time window; a candidate cannot move to another section until its time ends
  and cannot return to a closed section.
- Five options per question.

Checked in October 2026 against the IBPS notification summary and secondary syllabus pages (Oliveboard, PW,
Adda247, PracticeMock, all agreeing on 30/35/35 and 20 minutes each, with one table typo giving Reasoning 40 marks).
The official notification PDF on ibps.in was not fetched directly; see "unverified" below.

## Question-type mix (from published analyses of the 2023 to 2025 Prelims, typical counts)
- English: one reading comprehension passage (7 to 10 questions), cloze test or fillers, para jumbles,
  error spotting, word usage and sentence improvement.
- Quant: simplification or approximation (5), missing number series (5), quadratic equations (5),
  data interpretation (10 to 15), arithmetic word problems (8 to 10).
- Reasoning: puzzles and seating arrangements (about 15 to 20 questions in sets of 5), inequalities (3 to 5),
  syllogisms (3 to 5), and miscellaneous coding-decoding, blood relations, directions, order and ranking and
  alphabet questions.

Tigertest per test: English 13 reading and para jumble, 10 cloze and fillers, 7 error spotting and sentence
improvement; Quant 15 simplification, series and quadratics, 10 data interpretation (two tables), 10 arithmetic;
Reasoning 8 inequalities and syllogisms, 15 puzzles (three sets of five), 12 coding, relations, directions and ranking.

## Originality
All passages, puzzles, data tables and questions are original. Nothing is taken from IBPS papers or from
coaching-institute question banks. No item is shared with the SSC CGL, RRB NTPC or CTET banks in data/v2/.
