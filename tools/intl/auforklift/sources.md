# auforklift sources (Australian forklift high risk work licence, class LF)

All 200 questions are original. Nothing is copied from the Safe Work Australia national
assessment instrument, any RTO's course or pre-study material, or any commercial bank.
The US bank `data/forklift-questions.json` was used as a tone reference only.

Weights: TLILIC0003 and the national instrument publish no per-topic weights, so the
blueprint (7/9/8/10/10/6 per test) is ours, weighted toward load charts, calculations and
safe operation. The 80% pass line is a practice target, not the official marking.

Unconfirmed, kept general on purpose: exact fees, forms and approach distances near power
lines (all "vary by state"); the official instrument's own marking rules.
