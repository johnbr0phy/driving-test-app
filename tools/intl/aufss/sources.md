# aufss sources (all checked 2026-10-30)

- FSANZ, Standard 3.2.2A Food Safety Management Tools (overview page and InfoBites):
  https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/standard-322a-food-safety-management-tools
  - Food safety supervisor fact sheet: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/fact-sheets/food-safety-supervisor
    (category one and two must appoint a certified FSS before a prescribed activity; certificate from an RTO or
    regulator-recognised body obtained within the past 5 years; "reasonably available" = on site or easily
    contactable; requirements came in December 2023; show certificate to an authorised officer on request)
  - Food handler training: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/food-handler-training
    (category one and two; safe handling, food contamination, cleaning and sanitising, personal hygiene; before
    handling; no certificate required for handlers)
  - Evidence tool InfoBite (PDF): https://foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-10/Std%20322A%20-%20Evidence%20tool%20Final.pdf
    (category one only; clause 12; records kept at least 3 months; alternatives such as demonstrating an activity)
- FSANZ Standard 3.2.2 Food Safety Practices and General Requirements and Standard 3.2.3 Food Premises and
  Equipment, via Safe Food Australia (FSANZ's guide): 5°C / 60°C, cooling 60 to 21°C in 2 h then 21 to 5°C in 4 h,
  rapid reheating to 60°C, thermometer accurate to +/-1°C, single-use items, notification, hand washing facilities
  (warm running water, soap, single-use towels), health and hygiene of handlers, animals and pests, 2-hour/4-hour
  rule, 48-hour exclusion after vomiting/diarrhoea. https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/publications/safefoodaustralia
- training.gov.au, SITXFSA005 Use hygienic practices for food safety (Release 1, elements 1-4):
  https://training.gov.au/TrainingComponentFiles/SIT/SITXFSA005_R1.pdf
- training.gov.au, SITXFSA006 Participate in safe food handling practices (Release 1, elements 1-6):
  https://training.gov.au/TrainingComponentFiles/SIT/SITXFSA006_R1.pdf
- NSW Food Authority, Food Safety Supervisor: https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/retail/fss-food-safety-supervisors
  (site timed out on fetch; key focus areas allergen management, safe egg handling, cleaning and sanitising, Food Act
  offences; certificate 5 years; copy kept on premises, confirmed through NSW council pages, e.g.
  https://www.federationcouncil.nsw.gov.au/Environment-Waste/Public-Health/Retail-Food-Businesses/Food-Safety-Supervisor-Requirements)
- NSW Food Authority raw egg guideline (acidify to pH 4.2 or below, keep at 5°C or below, discard within 24 hours):
  https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/media/1606 (via council copies, e.g. https://www.canadabay.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/raw_egg_guidelines.pdf)
- Food Standards Code Standard 1.2.3 / Plain English Allergen Labelling (mandatory declarations; gluten cereals wheat,
  rye, barley, oats, spelt; no celery or mustard in Australia).

Heat sanitising: FSANZ cleaning and sanitising InfoBite gives 77°C for 30 seconds (https://foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-10/cleaning%20and sanitising_FINAL.pdf), confirmed 2026-10-30.
Certificate on premises: FSANZ only says the FSS should show it on request; keeping a copy on site is a NSW rule (LAW-025 is labelled NSW).
Not modelled / unconfirmed:
75°C core cooking is state and FSANZ guidance, not a Code requirement (items say "generally" / "usual guide").
No question names a training provider. All 200 items are original.
