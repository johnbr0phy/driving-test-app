Checked 29 Oct 2026.

- Transport for NSW, Road User Handbook (English PDF, file dated 19 Feb 2026), the source for every question:
  https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-11/Road-User-Handbook-English.pdf
  (linked from https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/roads-safety-and-rules/safety-updates-for-nsw-road-users/road-user-handbook ;
  handbook in 8 community languages)
- NSW Government, Driver Knowledge Test page (online 4-6 hour course + test from 15y11m, unlimited attempts for one fee,
  retake after 12 hours per the video transcript, in-person test from 16, online languages: English, Simplified Chinese,
  Arabic, Spanish, Vietnamese, Korean, Thai, Farsi, Traditional Chinese):
  https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driver-and-rider-licences/driver-licences/driver-licence-tests/driver-knowledge-test
- Service NSW, DKT online transaction (eligibility, 12 months access, fee paid at a service centre after passing):
  https://service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/driver-knowledge-test-online
- Service NSW, Learner driver licence: https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/services/driving-and-transport/driver-and-rider-licences/learner-driver-licence
- NSW Government, Rider Knowledge Test page (states the 45-question format used by NSW knowledge tests: 15 general knowledge,
  at least 12 correct, and 30 road safety, at least 29 correct, questions drawn at random from a bank of several hundred):
  https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/driver-and-rider-licences/rider-licences/rider-tests/rider-knowledge-test
- Official practice test: https://driverpracticetest.transport.nsw.gov.au/pages/practice-tests?init=true

## Format note (uncertain)
The current car DKT page does not print the question count or pass marks. The 45-question split (15 general knowledge,
need 12; 30 road safety, need 29; 41 of 45 overall) is the format on the official Rider Knowledge Test page and is the
format every DKT guide reports for the car test. passPct 91 = 41/45.

## Key facts used from the handbook
- GLS: L (16+, min 12 months and 120 h incl. 20 night if under 25, valid 5 yrs) -> HPT (10 months on L if under 25) ->
  Driving Test (17+) -> P1 red (min 12 months, valid 18) -> P2 green (min 24 months, valid 36; suspension adds +6 months) -> full.
- Restrictions: zero BAC L/P1/P2; 90 km/h L and P1, 100 km/h P2; no phone at all L/P1/P2; P1 under 25 one passenger
  under 21 11pm-5am; P1/P2 no high-performance cars; learners not in Parramatta, Centennial, Moore Parks;
  learners must not tow, P1 light trailer to 250 kg unladen.
- Demerits (3 years): L 4, P1 4, P2 7, full 13. Double demerits: speeding, seatbelts, child restraints, phones.
- BAC: under 0.05 full; under 0.02 public passenger, heavy over 13.9 t, dangerous goods; supervisor under 0.05.
- Speed: 50 built-up default, 100 elsewhere; shared zone 10; local traffic area 40; high pedestrian activity 30/40;
  40 past school bus with flashing lights; 40 past stopped emergency/tow vehicles where limit <= 80, slow and move over above 80.
- Gaps: 3 s, 4+ s in poor conditions; bicycles 1 m (<= 60 km/h) / 1.5 m (> 60).
- Parking distances: 20 m lights intersection, 10 m otherwise; crossings 20 m before / 10 m after; bus stop 20/10;
  level crossing 20 m; hydrant 1 m; 1 m between cars; post box 3 m; double lines 3 m.
- Lanes: bus/transit/truck lanes 100 m; bicycle/tram lanes and painted islands 50 m; edge lines 100 m when turning.
- Lights: dip high beam within 200 m; headlights when a person in dark clothing can't be seen at 100 m. Tyre tread 1.5 mm.
