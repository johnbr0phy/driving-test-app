# auqld sources (Queensland learner licence written road rules test)

Fetched 29-30 Oct 2026 from qld.gov.au (TMR) and legislation.qld.gov.au. Raw page text is in dl/.

Test format and fees
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/getting/tests (updated 1 Oct 2026): 30 multiple-choice questions; PrepL 90% (27/30); written test in 2 sections, at least 9/10 giving way and 18/20 road rules and driver licence requirements; once a day, fee each attempt; pass valid 5 years; questions come from Your Keys to Driving in Queensland.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/getting/learner/getting-a-learner-licence : age 16, PrepL from 15y11m, learner licence valid 3 years; written test "will eventually be replaced with PrepL".
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/getting/learner/prepl/prepl/prepl-online-learning-and-assessment : PrepL 4-6 hours, 12 months access, 24 h wait to re-attempt final test, same cost as written test; 3 sections.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/driver-licensing/applying/learner (reviewed 13 Jan 2026): fail = wait next working day; supervisor open licence held 1 year.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/driver-licensing/fees : written road rules test and PrepL $29.70 (not used in questions).

Licensing (GLS)
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/getting/rules : L plates black L on yellow, 14.6cm, visible front and rear from 20m, 2 demerit points; 100 h incl 10 night; phone rules for learners under 25; no alcohol limit.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/getting/steps : P1 at 17, learner 1 year, HPT after 6 months on learner, logbook under 25.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/getting/learner-logbook : 100 h, 10 night; driver trainer hour = 3 hours.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/driver-licensing/applying/driver-licence-progression : P2 at 18 after 1 year P1; open after P2 2 years and age 20; 25+ goes straight to P2.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/driver-licensing/applying/provisional/p1 , /p2 , /restrictions , /p-plates : red/green P on white; peer passenger 11pm-5am (1 under 21, non-family) for P1 under 25; high-powered vehicles (130 kW/t, post-2010) for P1/P2 under 25; P1/P2 cannot supervise learners; P2 hands-free allowed.
- https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/safety/fines/demerit/about , /learner , /provisional , /open , /double , /points : 4 points/1 year learner (3 month suspension, no choice) and P (choice of 3 months or 1-year good driving behaviour, 2+ points = 6 months); open 12/3 years; points carry over; double demerits all year for repeat offences within 12 months; speeding 1/3/4/6/8 points; phone 4; seatbelt 4.
