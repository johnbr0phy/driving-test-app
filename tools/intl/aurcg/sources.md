## The unit (outline and weights)
- training.gov.au, SITHGAM022 Provide responsible gambling services, Release 1, unit file:
  https://training.gov.au/TrainingComponentFiles/SIT/SITHGAM022_R1.xml
  Application, elements 1 and 2, performance criteria 1.1 to 2.4, foundation skills.
  "Supersedes and is equivalent to SITHGAM001 Provide responsible gambling services."
  `SITHGAM022_R2.xml` returns 404, so Release 1 is the latest release; a web search for a
  successor unit found none, and providers still deliver SITHGAM022 in 2026.
- training.gov.au, SITHGAM022 Assessment Requirements, Release 1:
  https://training.gov.au/TrainingComponentFiles/SIT/SITHGAM022_AssessmentRequirements_R1.xml
  (also as .pdf). Performance evidence (self-exclusion, counselling request, venue exclusion,
  disputes/complaints, refusal of credit, under-age gambling; signage, passage of time, breaks in
  play) and knowledge evidence (the 10 signs of harm, public interest reasons, harm minimisation
  principles incl. signage inside the venue, clocks, no machine advertising, ATMs out of gaming
  areas and no credit withdrawals; signage, exclusion, confidentiality, records, roles,
  non-compliance, counselling services). Category split and per-test counts are ours: the unit
  publishes no weights.
- training.gov.au, SITHGAM001 record (superseded by SITHGAM022, minor update 9 June 2022):
  https://training.gov.au/Training/Details/SITHGAM001

## National facts used
- BetStop, the National Self-Exclusion Register (Australian Government / ACMA): online and phone
  wagering only, 3 months minimum to lifetime, launched 21 August 2023.
  https://www.betstop.gov.au/ ; VGCCC news item on the launch:
  https://www.vgccc.vic.gov.au/for-community/news-and-media/industry-news/betstop-national-self-exclusion-register-has-launched
- Gambling Help line 1800 858 858 and Gambling Help Online (free, confidential, 24/7, interpreters):
  https://www.gamblinghelponline.org.au/ ; https://www.dss.gov.au/gambling/gambling-support-services
- Interactive Gambling Act 2001 (Cth), enforced by ACMA; credit card and digital currency ban for
  online wagering from 11 June 2024 (Interactive Gambling Amendment (Credit and Other Measures) Act 2023).
- National Consumer Protection Framework for online wagering (deposit limits, activity statements,
  BetStop): https://www.dss.gov.au/gambling
- Lifeline 13 11 14, National Debt Helpline 1800 007 007, Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467.
- Productivity Commission, Gambling (2010) and Australia's Gambling Industries (1999): 5 to 10
  people affected by each person with a severe gambling problem; most people with a gambling
  problem have it with gaming machines.
- Problem Gambling Severity Index (Ferris and Wynne 2001): 9 items scored 0 to 3; 0 non-problem,
  1-2 low risk, 3-7 moderate risk, 8+ problem gambling.
- Browne et al., Assessing gambling-related harm in Victoria (VRGF, 2016): low and moderate risk
  gamblers carry most of the population's total harm.
- Probability facts computed by hand: single-zero roulette 37 numbers; C(45,6) = 8,145,060;
  two dice total 7 = 6/36; Keno draws 20 of 80.
