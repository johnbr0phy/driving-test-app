## The test
- In person at Service SA (or remote police stations): Part 1 has 8 give-way diagram questions, all 8
  must be correct; Part 2 has 42 multiple-choice questions on road rules and safe driving, 32 correct
  to pass. About 30 minutes, on screen or paper, interpreter available. Minimum age 16.
  https://dit.sa.gov.au/ontherighttrack/your_licence/getting_a_licence/licence_tests
  https://dit.sa.gov.au/ontherighttrack/your_licence/getting_a_licence/licence_tests/booking_a_learners_permit_theory_test
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/road-rules/the-drivers-handbook/obtaining-permit
- Online myLs course: enrol from 15 years 9 months, permit at 16, 12 months to finish, test is 30
  questions with 27 correct to pass, unlimited attempts.
  https://www.mylicence.sa.gov.au/myls/frequently_asked_questions
  https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/driving-and-transport/licences/learners-permit/apply/myls-course-and-theory-test
- Official practice test (14 random questions incl. give-way): https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/my-car-licence/practice-theory-test
- The Driver's Handbook (Feb 2026 PDF): https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0004/328504/drivers-handbook-south-australia-2026-02.pdf

## Graduated licensing
- L: 12 months minimum under 25, 6 months 25+; 75 hours supervised incl. 15 at night in logbook;
  L plates front and rear; supervisor beside learner with an unconditional full licence for the
  preceding 2 years; zero alcohol/drugs; no mobile phone function at all; max 100 km/h; speeding by
  10 km/h or more is a condition breach; breach = lose permit and disqualification; 4+ demerits =
  6-month disqualification.
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/my-car-licence/learners-stage
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/parents-and-supervisors/learners-permit
  https://dit.sa.gov.au/ontherighttrack/your_licence/getting_a_licence/learning_to_drive/supervised_driving
  https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/driving-and-transport/offences/disqualification-and-suspension/types-of-disqualification/learners-permit
- HPT before P1, plus VORT or CBT&A.
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/my-car-licence/the-hazard-perception-test
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/the-driving-companion/options-p1
- P1: red P on white front and rear, at least 12 months, zero alcohol, 4+ demerits, max 100 km/h,
  under 25: no driving midnight to 5 am unless supervised, max one passenger aged 16 to 20 (not
  immediate family), no high-powered vehicle; no phone use (L and P1, incl. hands-free/Bluetooth).
  https://dit.sa.gov.au/ontherighttrack/your_licence/getting_a_licence/licence_conditions/p1_conditions
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/my-car-licence/p1-provisional-licence
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/safe-driving-tips/mobile-phones
- P2: 2 years, no plates, zero alcohol, 4+ demerits, max 100 km/h, may use hands-free/Bluetooth;
  provisional licence 3 years total; full licence at 20 or older.
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/my-car-licence/p2-provisional-licence
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/the-driving-companion/provisional-conditions
- Full licence: 12+ demerits in 3 years = disqualification (3/4/5 months), or 12-month good
  behaviour option (fewer than 2 points).
  https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/driving-and-transport/offences/disqualification-and-suspension/types-of-disqualification/demerit

## Road rules (Driver's Handbook pages)
- Speed limits: 50 built-up default, 100 outside, 110 signed (not for L/P), built-up = buildings or
  street lights not over 100 m apart; school zone 25 when a child present (under 18 or student in
  uniform), 40 km/h signed school times do not replace it; 25 past stationary emergency vehicle with
  flashing blue/red, between two sets, past breakdown vehicle with flashing amber; 25 past stopped
  school bus either direction; not on the other side of a median-divided road (RTA s 83(2)).
  https://www.mylicence.sa.gov.au/road-rules/the-drivers-handbook/speed-limits
  https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/safe-driving-tips/school_safety
  https://lsc.sa.gov.au/handbook/ch12s08s05s21.php
- Koala crossing (flashing lights) 25 km/h; emu crossing (flags) 25 km/h with children present, stop
  for anyone using it. https://www.dit.sa.gov.au/ontherighttrack/driving_safely/drive_for_the_conditions/schools
- Following distance 3 seconds, about 8 or 9 car lengths at 60 km/h, more when wet, poor visibility,
  downhill, tailgated, tired, at night. https://mylicence.sa.gov.au/my-car-licence/the-hazard-perception-test/safe-following-distance
