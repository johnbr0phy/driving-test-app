# auvic: Victoria Learner Permit Knowledge Test, sources

## Primary (every question is checked against this)
- Road to Solo Driving, VicRoads / Department of Transport and Planning, cover dated September 2026
  (print code VRPIN00568 09.26; inside: "Updated March 2023"), 176 pages.
  Downloaded from the Transport Victoria media host:
  https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/stategovernc45d-cftw-production-c9ca/media/Project/TransportWebsite/Forms/road-to-solo-driving-handbook.pdf
  (linked from https://transport.vic.gov.au/road-and-active-transport/registration-and-licensing/licences/driver-history-handbooks-and-logbooks/driver-handbooks-and-logbooks ;
  vicroads.vic.gov.au and transport.vic.gov.au pages return 403 to our fetcher, so the PDF was read directly).
  Local copy and text: dl/road-to-solo-driving-handbook.pdf, dl/c.txt.
  The handbook says: "The learner permit test questions are based on this handbook."

Key facts used (handbook page numbers):
- Must be at least 16 to sit the learner permit test (p10). Test = eyesight chart + computerised multiple-choice test on road law and road safety (p11). Learner permit valid 10 years (p13). Test and HPT available in 14 languages, interpreters free (p9, p25).
- Learner conditions (p15, p26-27): L plates front and rear visible from 20 m; carry permit; full (not probationary) licence supervisor; zero BAC; no towing; car only; phone only for navigation/audio set up before driving, in a commercial holder, no touch or voice commands.
- Licence: at least 18; permit held 12 months (under 21), 6 months (21 to under 25), 3 months (25+); under 21 needs 120 h incl. 20 h night; HPT from 17 y 11 m; incomplete log book = fees lost, 6-week wait (p16-19).
- P1 red plates min 1 year, P2 green min 3 years; 21+ starts on P2; P1 one peer passenger aged 16 to under 22; PPV over 130 kW/tonne; automatic restriction; zero BAC (p21-27).
- Demerits: L/P 5+ in 12 months or 12+ in 3 years, suspension 3 months+ (p154). P licence cancelled = P period restarts; suspended = extended (p155). Carry licence if under 26 (p155). Notify address/name change within 14 days (p155-156).
- Alcohol (p71-75): full licence under .05; zero for L/P, taxi, bus, instructors; .05 doubles crash risk (p51); BAC definition; only time lowers BAC; illegal to drink while supervising; L/P first offence 0 to <.05: 3 months, .05 to <.1: 6 months; interlock 6 months (12 repeat); refusing a test: disqualified 2+ years.
- Fatigue (p57): 10 pm to 6 am, 17 hours awake, 2 hours non-stop; only sleep cures it; 15-minute power nap.
- Speed (p79-83): 50 km/h built-up default; 100 km/h outside built-up and freeways; built-up area definition (street lights not over 100 m apart for 500 m+); school 40 km/h (permanent on most 50 roads, time-based on 60/70); shared zone 10 km/h; crash risk doubles per 5 km/h over in a 60 zone (p60); doubling speed ~4x stopping distance; 67 m at 60, 142 m at 100.
- Signs and markings (p84-97), parking (p146-152), give way (p98-111), turning (p112-121), freeways and tunnels (p122-124), sharing the road incl. trams, bicycles (1 m up to 60 km/h, 1.5 m above), emergency vehicles (40 km/h passing), trucks, animals, level crossings (p125-145), penalties and impoundment (p152-155), crash responsibilities and first aid (p158-163).

## Test format (not in the handbook)
- 32 multiple-choice questions, 78% (25 of 32) to pass: Youth Central, Victorian Government
  (https://www.youthcentral.vic.gov.au/driving-and-public-transport/getting-your-ls), as returned by web search on
  2026-10-29 ("you need to get a minimum of 78 per cent on the test to pass. There are 32 questions on the test").
  The page itself returned 503 to our fetcher, so this is from the search snippet. 25/32 = 78.1%, consistent.
- Online Learner Permit Test: enrol from 15 years 11 months via myVicRoads; permit issued only from 16; 12 months to finish the
  course; after passing you visit a Customer Service Centre (VicRoads page via search snippet:
  https://www.vicroads.vic.gov.au/licences/your-ls/learner-permit-test-online/). Format details of the online course not verified.

## Not modelled / left out
- Fees (change yearly). Fine amounts. Hazard Perception Test (video, P licence stage, separate test).
- Specific sign images: signs are described in words.
