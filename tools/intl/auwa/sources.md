  Department of Transport and Major Infrastructure, Driver and Vehicle Services.
  https://transport.wa.gov.au/mediaFiles/licensing/DVS_DL_B_DriveSafeFull_o.pdf
  (full text read; every rule in the bank is from this edition unless noted below)

  https://www.transport.wa.gov.au/licensing/drivers-licence/get-a-licence/car/take-theory-test
  "at least 24 out of the 30 multiple choice questions correct to pass"; "A pass result is valid
  for 5 years"; resit needs a new fee; can sit it the same day you apply for the learner's permit;

  https://www.transport.wa.gov.au/licensing/road-rules-theory-test-quiz.asp

## Key facts used (Drive Safe 2026 section)
- Learner's permit (C class) from 16; valid 3 years (2.1). L plates front and rear; max 100 km/h;
  not in Kings Park; zero BAC (2.1.1 step 2).
- Supervisor: licensed 4+ years (2 for moped), or licensed instructor; A-condition supervisor only
  in automatic; supervisor subject to normal BAC limit and drug ban.
- HPT after 6 months on permit and age 16 years 6 months (step 3).
- 50 hours supervised including 5 at night; PDA from 17 (step 5). Learners 25+ exempt from log book.
  Research: 100 to 120 hours recommended.
- Provisional: P plates 2 years, red P first 6 months, green P remaining 18 months; first 6 months
  no driving midnight to 5 am (exemptions work/training/education) and one passenger limit
  (exemptions: experienced driver 4+ years C, immediate family, employment).
  Provisional licence issued if under 19 or not held licence 2 years.
- Novice: zero BAC until held licence 2 years. Novice Type 1 (permit and first year) 4 points;
  Type 2 (second year) 8 points; disqualification minimum 3 months; P licence cancelled.
  Full licence 12 points in 3 years: 12-15 = 3 months, 16-19 = 4, 20+ = 5. GBP 12 months,
  more than one point during it = double disqualification; elect within 21 days; P holders cannot.
  Double demerits on holiday periods. Points recorded by offence date.
- P plate offences: BAC 0.02 or more etc. lead to disqualification/cancellation.
- Change of address within 21 days.
- BAC: 0.05 general; zero for novices and others. Standard drink 10 g; raises BAC about 0.01;
  1 hour to clear one standard drink; peak up to 2 hours; only time sobers. 0.05 = 50 mg/100 mL.
- Fatigue: microsleep 3 to 5 s, 100 m+ at 100 km/h; 17-18 h awake = 0.05 BAC; worst midnight
  to 6 am; breaks at least every 2 hours.
- Anti-hoon: 45 km/h or more over the limit = reckless driving; burnouts: 48 h impound first,
  up to 3 months second, confiscation third.
- Phones: call only if mounted or hands-free; no texting; GPS only if mounted and untouched.
- Child restraints: under 7 in restraint; 0-6 months rear-facing; 6 months-<4 rear or forward with
  inbuilt harness, not front row; 4-<7 forward harness or booster, not front unless rear full of
  under 7s; driver responsible for passengers under 16.
- Tyre tread 1.5 mm; horn only to warn; windscreen top 10% tint.
- Speed: built-up default 50; outside 110; learners/towing 100. SLOMO 40 km/h past stationary
  incident response vehicle with flashing lights. Freeway not more than 20 km/h below the limit.
- Two-second rule, 4-5 s in poor conditions; double in wet. 60 km/h: 25 m reaction + 20 m braking = 45 m.
- Dip high beam within 200 m of oncoming or following; fog: low beam, fog lights only in fog.
- Keep left: 90 km/h+ or KEEP LEFT UNLESS OVERTAKING: no right lane except to turn, overtake, etc.
- Rail: stop 3 m from nearest rail if no line; no stopping within 20 m; train 1000 m+ to stop.
- Give way to bus leaving stop in built-up area if indicating with GIVE WAY sign.
- Water: 2WD no higher than bottom of vehicle; don't cross flowing water deeper than chassis.
- Warning signs yellow/black, pedestrian warning red-orange; advisory speed.
- Lines: broken may cross; single continuous may not except enter/leave/turn/U-turn; double
  continuous no overtaking; continuous on your side stay left; merge: give way to vehicle ahead.
- Single yellow edge line = no stopping. KEEP CLEAR. Zigzag lines before pedestrian crossings.
- Signals: yellow stop if safe; flashing yellow at intersection = faulty, treat as GIVE WAY;
  dark = give way; pelican flashing yellow = proceed with caution, give way to pedestrians;
  LEFT TURN ON RED PERMITTED AFTER STOPPING; no U turn at signals unless U TURN PERMITTED.
- Officer directions override signals, signs, give-way rule, markings; back to you = stop.
- Indicate at least 5 s when pulling out from kerb; hand signals; brake lights visible 60 m.
- Roundabouts: give way to all vehicles already on it; clockwise; left: left lane; right: right
  lane, signal left before exit; straight: no signal approaching, signal left before exit.
- Uncontrolled: give way to right at four-way; T: give way to through road; turning right give way
  to oncoming going straight or turning left.
- Turning: wheels straight while waiting; vehicles 7.5 m+ may cross continuous lane line.
- Overtake on left: multi-lane, vehicle turning right, police direction. Not when vehicle stopped
  at crossing, NO OVERTAKING sign, crest/bend.
- Stopping: 10 m intersection; 20 m before and 10 m after pedestrian/children's crossing and bus
  stop; 1 m hydrant; 3 m letter box; 20 m rail; double parking; 50 m / 150 m visibility.
- Parking: NO PARKING 2 minutes pick-up; ACROD bays; park left in two-way, either side one-way;
  hill: wheels towards kerb, first gear uphill reverse downhill (manual).
- Clearways: white C on red shield.
- Emergencies: hazard triangle up to 50 m built-up, up to 200 m outside; blowout firm grip,
  off accelerator; stuck accelerator neutral/clutch; brake failure pump, lower gear, park brake;
  stalled on rail leave if train; skid ABC; crash reporting: injury or > $3,000 damage or owner
  absent = police; DRSABCD; 30:2; check breathing up to 10 s; powerline stay in car, 8 m.
- Cyclists: 1 m at 60 km/h or less, 1.5 m above 60; may ride two abreast; can exceed 55 km/h.
- Pedestrians at slip lane; children's crossing flags.
- Emergency vehicles: give way, move left.
- Motorcycle passenger 8+ unless sidecar.
- Towing another vehicle: gap less than 4 m; flag if rope longer than 2 m.
- Trailer: brakes required from 750 kg GTM; 100 km/h towing.
