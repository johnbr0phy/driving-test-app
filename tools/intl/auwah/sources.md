# auwah sources (all checked 30 October 2026)

- training.gov.au, RIIWHS204E Work safely at heights, release 1 (unit and assessment requirements PDFs,
  generated 11 Feb 2025): https://training.gov.au/Training/Details/RIIWHS204E . The training.gov.au API
  (https://training.gov.au/api/training/RIIWHS204E) now marks it **Superseded**, mapped on 1 Sept 2026 to
  **RIIWHS204 Work safely at heights** (release 1, current from 2 Sept 2026), "Supersedes and is equivalent".
  Elements: identify work requirements; identify procedures and instructions; access and install equipment;
  perform work at heights; clean up. Knowledge evidence as listed in spec.json. No question count or pass mark
  is set by the unit; RTOs write their own assessment.
- Safe Work Australia, model Code of Practice: Managing the risk of falls at workplaces (Oct 2018 text, re-issued
  21 Oct 2022): https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/doc/model-code-practice-managing-risk-falls-workplaces .
  safeworkaustralia.gov.au refused automated downloads, so figures were read from the SafeWork NSW edition based
  on the model code (Aug 2019): https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0018/50076/Managing-the-risk-of-falls-at-workplaces-COP.pdf
  (regs 78 to 80 hierarchy; guardrail 900 to 1100 mm; covers 2 kN; 7/20 degree surfaces; scaffold 225/450/675 kg,
  30-day inspection, 4 m licence and handover; boom EWP 11 m licence; trestles 2 m; restraint unsuitable over 15
  degrees; catch platform 2 m beyond edge, 1 m drop; nets within 2 m; free fall 2 m; vertical lines 300 mm / 600 mm;
  ladders 120 kg, 4:1, 900 mm from top, 1 m above landing, stepladder second tread, fixed ladders 70 to 75 degrees;
  suspension intolerance, 5-minute leg-raise guidance, rescue procedures).
- Safe Work Australia, model Code of Practice: Managing the risk of falls in housing construction (Oct 2018):
  https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/doc/model-codes-practice/model-code-practice-managing-risk-falls-housing-construction ,
  read from the SafeWork NSW edition (Aug 2019): https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0020/52157/Managing-the-risk-of-falls-in-housing-construction-COP.pdf
  (scope Classes 1, 2, 10; 15 kN anchors and clearance; truss 600 mm, 1.5 m from external walls; critical angle
  26/10 degrees; 35 degree steep roofs; roof guardrail 900 mm, 450/275/100 mm gaps, infill over 26 degrees; catch
  platforms 1 m / 500 mm, 225 mm from face; fascia platforms 450 mm; studs at 450 mm as guardrail).
- Model WHS Act and Regulations (Safe Work Australia): primary duty, worker and other persons' duties, officers'
  due diligence, no charge for WHS items, notifiable incidents and site preservation, right to cease unsafe work,
  SWMS for high risk construction work over 2 m and stopping non-compliant work, general construction induction.
- Not used: any RTO's assessment, learner guide or commercial question bank. Victoria runs its own OHS laws, and
  WHS details vary by state and territory, so items say "varies" rather than assert one jurisdiction.
