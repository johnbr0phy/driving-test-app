# auwhite sources (checked 29 Oct 2026)

## The unit (training.gov.au)
- CPCWHS1001 Prepare to work safely in the construction industry, Release 2 (CPC Training Package 8.0, document generated 11 Feb 2025): https://training.gov.au/assets/CPC/CPCWHS1001_R2.pdf . Release 2 only fixes a typo; content is Release 1's.
- CPCWHS1001 Complete Release 1 (unit + assessment requirements, generated 2 Mar 2025): https://training.gov.au/assets/CPC/CPCWHS1001_Complete_R1.pdf
  - Elements: 1 legislative requirements (duty holders' roles, rights, duty of care, safe work practices); 2 hazards and risk control (risk management, hazards, PPE, controls); 3 communication and reporting (documents, designated safety personnel, signs and symbols, reporting hazards/incidents/injuries); 4 incident and emergency response (procedures, first aid, fire safety equipment).
  - Knowledge evidence used for the categories: duty of care and roles of business owners and workers; hazard and risk; the five steps in order (identify hazard, assess risk, consult and report, control hazard, review); first aid; hazards: asbestos, confined spaces, electrical (power lines, cords, equipment), excavations and trenches incl. underground services, dust, falling objects, hazardous substances and dangerous goods, hot and cold environments, manual handling, noise, plant and equipment, traffic and mobile plant, unplanned collapse, UV, working at heights incl. scaffolding, work needing a high-risk work licence; PPE incl. footwear, harnesses, respiratory protection, UV clothing and sunscreen; emergencies (chemical spill, fire, injury, structural collapse, toxic/flammable vapour, vehicle or plant accident); incidents incl. near misses; safe work practices (amenities, procedures, reporting, housekeeping, no drugs or alcohol, bullying and harassment, PPE, smoking only in designated areas, waste); signs: emergency information, fire, hazard (danger, warning), regulatory (prohibition, mandatory, limitation), danger and out-of-service tags.
  - Performance evidence: report two hazards and how to control them; select PPE; explain fire blankets, extinguishers (water, CO2, powder, foam), hose reels; explain signs; explain JSA, SWMS and SDS; explain roles of first aid officers, WHS representatives, WHS committee members, supervisors; fit eye protection, hearing protection, hard hat, hi-vis.
  - Assessment conditions: direct observation by an assessor, in person or by real-time audio-visual; range of methods incl. practical demonstration, oral and written reporting. No pass percentage (competency-based), so 80% is our readiness line.
  - Range of conditions still lists WA's OSH Act 1984; WA's Work Health and Safety Act 2020 replaced it on 31 Mar 2022 (WorkSafe WA: https://worksafe.wa.gov.au/announcements/important-changes-high-risk-work-licensing-new-work-health-and-safety-rules-begin-31-march-2022). Victoria: Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004.

## Model WHS laws (Safe Work Australia, model WHS Act 2011 and model WHS Regulations; facts used, from the current model texts at safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation)
- Act: duties not transferable; shared duties and duty to consult, cooperate and coordinate; reasonably practicable factors; PCBU primary duty; officers' due diligence; workers' and other persons' duties; offence categories 1-3; notifiable incidents (death, serious injury or illness, dangerous incident), notify immediately, written notice within 48 hours of a request, records kept 5 years, site preserved with listed exceptions; consultation triggers; HSRs elected by the work group, trained HSRs may issue PINs and direct cessation; HSC within 2 months of a request by an HSR or 5 or more workers; right to cease unsafe work and notify the PCBU; discriminatory conduct prohibited; improvement, prohibition and non-disturbance notices.
- Regulations: PCBU provides PPE free; worker must use PPE as trained and report damage; falls hierarchy and rescue procedures for fall arrest; high risk work licences (minimum age 18; scaffolding where a person or thing could fall more than 4 m; boom-type EWP with boom 11 m or more; forklift; dogging); high risk construction work list (fall more than 2 m, trench or shaft deeper than 1.5 m, adjacent to a traffic corridor in use, asbestos, confined space, etc.) and SWMS rules (prepare before work, consult, stop if not followed); general construction induction training and cards; SDS review at least every 5 years; decanted container labelling; asbestos register, Class A (friable) and Class B (non-friable) licences, 10 m2 threshold, banned tools and methods.
- Engineered stone prohibition from 1 July 2024: https://www.asbestossafety.gov.au/silicasafety/about-silica/working-silica/engineered-stone-prohibition (manufacture, supply, processing or installation of benchtops, panels and slabs; legacy stone allowed under a national framework); WorkSafe Qld: https://www.worksafe.qld.gov.au/safety-and-prevention/hazards/workplace-hazards/ban-on-engineered-stone-protecting-workers-health
- Asbestos ban 31 Dec 2003, pre-1990 buildings likely to contain asbestos: Asbestos Safety and Eradication Agency (asbestossafety.gov.au).
- Noise exposure standard 85 dB(A) LAeq,8h and 140 dB(C) peak; raise-your-voice at 1 m rule: Safe Work Australia, Managing noise and preventing hearing loss at work code of practice.
- Falls: guardrail top rail 900 to 1100 mm; ladder 4:1 and 1 m above landing; three points of contact: Safe Work Australia, Managing the risk of falls in housing construction / at workplaces codes of practice.
- Excavation: Before You Dig Australia (byda.com.au, formerly Dial Before You Dig); hand or vacuum exposure of services; spoil and plant back from the edge: Excavation work code of practice.
- Signs: AS 1319 categories (prohibition, mandatory, restriction, danger, warning, emergency information, fire). Extinguisher bands: AS/NZS 1841 (water all red, foam blue, CO2 black, dry powder white, wet chemical oatmeal). Hearing protector classes 1 to 5: AS/NZS 1270.
- UV: Cancer Council and Safe Work Australia, protection when UV index is 3 or above, SPF 30 or higher broad spectrum, reapply every 2 hours, slip slop slap seek slide.
