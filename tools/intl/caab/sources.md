# caab: Alberta Class 7 learner's knowledge test, sources

All checked 2026-10-29.

## Official
1. Driver's Guide: cars and light trucks, Spring 2026 (Government of Alberta, Transportation and
   Economic Corridors; ISBN 978-1-4601-5676-6). Published May 2026, updated June 15, 2026 (Driver
   Medical Fitness Program). Supersedes the 2023 edition.
   https://open.alberta.ca/publications/drivers-guide
   PDF: https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/387f4e8a-6c0a-456a-ab31-995aadaf1f2b/resource/5babee5c-040d-4e41-981a-04f22e839aac/download/tec-drivers-guide-2026.pdf
   Every question in the bank is taken from this guide (read in full; text in dl/gc.txt).
2. Alberta.ca, Driver's knowledge test: https://www.alberta.ca/drivers-knowledge-test
   - "has 30 multiple-choice questions", "requires a score of 25 out of 30 to pass" (83%)
   - taken at a registry agent, paid each time, once per day
   - Class 7 test offered in 25 languages (page lists Amharic ... Vietnamese), audio and oral
     versions, translator only after 3 attempts
3. Alberta.ca, Get a Class 7 learner's licence: https://www.alberta.ca/class-7-learners-licence
   - age 14+, parental consent under 18, knowledge test + vision test at a registry agent
   - supervisor 18+ with full Class 5 (non-GDL) or higher, in the front passenger seat
   - no driving 12 a.m. to 5 a.m.; zero alcohol; no more passengers than seat belts;
     8 demerit points leads to suspension; temporary licence valid 30 days
4. Alberta.ca, Impaired driving penalties: https://www.alberta.ca/impaired-driving-penalties
   - IRS ZERO: Novice 30-day suspension, 7-day vehicle seizure (used to read the guide's
     two-column layout correctly; dollar amounts not used in the bank)

## Facts used from the guide (page numbers from the 2026 guide)
- GDL p.10-11: Class 7 at 14+, hold at least 1 year (extended by suspension), supervisor fully
  licensed (non-GDL) 18+ seated beside; no driving midnight to 5 a.m.; passengers <= seat belts;
  suspended at 8+ demerits; zero alcohol/drug. Class 5-GDL at 16+, after 1 year, Class 5 road
  test; 2 years minimum probationary; cannot supervise a learner; cannot re-class to 1-4. Exit
  at 18+, after 2 years, no suspensions/violations in last 12 months; approved driver training
  course (code U) can cut probation by up to 6 months.
- Knowledge test p.11: multiple choice on computer at a registry agent, safe driving practices,
  driving laws, road signs; one test per day; 25 languages.
- Demerits p.108-110: full licence letter at 8-14, suspension at 15+ in two years, reinstated
  with 7; GDL letter at 4-7, suspension at 8+, reinstated with 3. Points table (7 leaving scene;
  6 careless, school bus, racing, 51+ over; 5 failing to stop for peace officer; 4 pedestrian
  in crosswalk, following too closely, 31-50 over; 3 stop sign, red light, 16-30 over, etc.;
  2 up to 15 over, improper turn, U-turn; GDL-only 2 points curfew, passengers over seat belts,
  unqualified supervisor). Suspensions 1 month, 3 months (within 1 year), 6 months (within 2
  years). Points removed 2 years after conviction. Defensive driving course: 3-point credit.
- Distracted driving p.91: fines and 3 demerits.
- Impaired p.111-114: IRS ZERO Novice (zero tolerance for Class 7 / 5-GDL); IRS WARN at 50 mg
  per 100 mL; IRS FAIL at 80 mg per 100 mL or impaired to any degree or refusal: 90 days no
  driving + 12 months (interlock possible), 30-day vehicle seizure. 24-hour IRS for impairment by
  a physical or medical condition, drugs or alcohol. Convictions/suspensions stay on record
  10 years.
- Speeds p.27: 50 km/h urban default, 80 km/h on a non-highway roadway outside urban areas,
  100 km/h on a provincial highway outside urban areas, 80 km/h on a provincial highway inside
  a city. School zones 30 km/h on school days 8:00-9:30, 11:30-1:30, 3:00-4:30; playground zones
  30 km/h every day 8:30 a.m. to one hour after sunset; flashing yellow pedestrian lights 30 km/h;
  60 km/h or posted (lower) past stopped emergency vehicles/tow trucks with lights flashing in the
  adjacent lane.
- Distances: parking 5 m from hydrant, stop/yield sign, crosswalk; 1.5 m from driveway; wheels
  within 50 cm of curb; stop within 3 m when no line or crosswalk; turning lane 15 m before;
  school bus stop about 20 m; follow emergency vehicles 150 m; low beams within 150 m following
  and 300 m oncoming; headlights when you cannot see 150 m; train within 500 m; railway stop sign
  5 to 15 m from nearest rail; signal-controlled crossing at least 5 m; reflective triangles
  10 to 20 m apart; seat 25 cm from wheel; head restraint centre level with top of ears, < 10 cm
  behind head; two-second following distance (four for large vehicles like motor homes); look
  12-15 s ahead urban, 20-25 s rural; mirrors every 8-12 s; load 1.5 m+ overhang needs red flag
  (30 cm) by day, red light at night; U-turn not near hill crest within 150 m (rural); child under
  6 and not over 18 kg in a child seat; trailer brakes not needed at 910 kg or less or under half
  the tow vehicle's unloaded weight; LRT up to 100 m to stop; snowplow operators pull over every
  5 to 8 km; noise in residential areas 10 p.m. to 7 a.m.; under 14 cannot drive farm equipment on
  a highway; new residents 90 days; visitors up to one year.
