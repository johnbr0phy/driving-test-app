# caauto sources (Red Seal Automotive Service Technician)

All checked 2026-10-30. No question was copied from any source; Red Seal publishes no item bank
and its self-assessment and sample questions were not used.

- red-seal.ca, Automotive Service Technician, Exam Information (Red Seal exam breakdown by MWA and task):
  https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/autoservtech/exam-information.shtml
  125 questions. A 9 (A-1 safety 4, A-2 tools and documentation 5, A-3 communication 0), B 22 (B-4..B-9:
  4/4/5/4/3/2), C 12 (C-10 8, C-11 4), D 17 (10/7), E 23 (E-14 8, E-15 6, E-16 HVAC 5, E-17 HVAC 4),
  F 23 (13/10), G 10 (6/4), H 9 (5/4). The page does not print the RSOS year.
- red-seal.ca, Automotive Service Technician, Examination Weightings:
  https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/autoservtech/exam-weightings.shtml
  A 7%, B 17%, C 10%, D 14%, E 18%, F 19%, G 8%, H 7%.
- Which RSOS: the breakdown used is the one on the two pages above. It has eight MWAs including
  MWA H "hybrid and electric vehicle (EV) systems", which is the structure of the 2023 RSOS (ESDC
  publication Em15-3-10-2023, https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2024/edsc-esdc/Em15-3-10-2023-eng.pdf).
  SkilledTradesBC notices say interprovincial exams aligned to the 2023 AST RSOS started on
  2026-01-01. I could not find an older-RSOS breakdown still published on red-seal.ca for this trade
  (the legacy URL returned 404), so the 2023 RSOS breakdown is the only one modelled.
- Red Seal Exam Preparation Guide (pass mark 70%, four hours, four-option multiple choice, one mark each):
  https://red-seal.ca/eng/resources/exam-prep-guide.shtml
- 310S is the Ontario trade code (Skilled Trades Ontario); other provinces use their own codes.
  Confirmed in review 2026-10-30: Skilled Trades Ontario's trade report is titled
  "automotive-service-technician-310S" (https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/automotive-service-technician-310S_2024_en_TradeReport.html).

Content facts were written from standard technician knowledge (Ohm's law, CAN physical layer,
OBD-II DLC pinout, refrigerant handling, brake hydraulics, HV safety practice) and cross-checked
against the RSOS task list. Provincial items (refrigerant certification, lockout rules, fees,
eligibility hours) are "varies by province" or omitted.
