# Sources: British Columbia ICBC Class 7L knowledge test (cabc)

All checked October 29, 2026.

## The test
- ICBC, "Before you take your knowledge test" (MV2654, 06/2026), tip sheet PDF:
  https://www.icbc.com/assets/en/4zwylo9j9OaOX2I7Q8Epat/knowledge-test-tip-sheet.pdf
  Passenger vehicle test: 50 questions, 40 correct to pass (80% on all tests), max 45 minutes
  (75 if taking Class 7 and 8 together). 12 languages: English, Arabic, Cantonese, Croatian, Farsi,
  French, Mandarin, Punjabi, Russian, Spanish, Vietnamese, Ukrainian; audio available. Skip each
  question once. Test ends as soon as you reach the pass count or exceed the allowed wrong answers.
- ICBC, "Preparing for your driver licensing tests, Class 5 and 7" (MV2022, 10/2026):
  https://www.partners.icbc.com/assets/5s5WTCbPfpQeitI89dtfVN/mv2022.pdf
  50 multiple-choice questions on Learn to Drive Smart; online or in office; knowledge test fee $15
  and $10 for the Class 7L photo licence; vision and medical screening first.
- ICBC newsroom, June 2026, at-home online knowledge test (Classes 5 to 8, 12 languages):
  https://icbc.com/about-icbc/newsroom/2026-06-08-online-knowledge-tests
- ICBC, Get your L: https://icbc.com/driver-licensing/new-drivers/Get-your-L
  L on or after 16th birthday; 40 of 50 to pass.

## GLP changes effective October 19, 2026 (override the handbook where they differ)
- https://icbc.com/driver-licensing/new-drivers/graduated-licensing-program-changes
- https://www.icbc.com/driver-licensing/new-drivers/Graduated-licensing
- https://icbc.com/driver-licensing/new-drivers/you-have-a-class-7l-licence
- https://icbc.com/driver-licensing/new-drivers/you-have-a-class-7-licence-and-youre-under-25
  Second road test replaced by a Driving Record Assessment. Class 5 with restrictions after 24 months
  of safe driving on the N if under 25 (18 with an ICBC-approved course taken in the L stage), 12 months
  if 25 or older; no prohibitions and no excessive-speeding or device convictions. Restriction 55
  (zero BAC/BDC) for 12 months after, restarting after a prohibition or suspension. Supervisor
  minimum age lowered from 25 to 22 (needs own Class 5 without restriction 55, sits beside you).
  L: one passenger besides the supervisor, or more if all are immediate family. L and N: zero alcohol
  and drugs, no electronic devices including hands-free, L or N sign; L: no driving midnight to 5 a.m.

## Deliberately not asked (sources conflict or change often)
- Exact learner-stage minimum before the Class 7 road test (handbook says 12 months; the current
  Graduated licensing page says 2 years, or 1 year if 25 or older).
- Age needing parental consent (handbook and June 2026 news: under 19; current GLP page: under 18).
- Fines and fees.
