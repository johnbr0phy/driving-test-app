## Weighting decision
Current exams: 11/28/30/21/10. New standard (exams in development): 12/26/34/18/10.
Per 50-question test: A 6, B 14, C 15, D 10, E 5 (12/28/30/20/10), the closest whole-number
fit to the current split that also leans toward the new one. Pools: 24/56/60/40/20.

## Facts not independently confirmed (flag for review)
- CEC rule details stated from memory without the 2024 text at hand: Class A GFCI within 1.5 m
  of a sink (CAELEC-WIR, branch circuits), heating demand 100% of first 10 kW then 75%, 100 A
  minimum service at 80 m² or more, two rod electrodes at least 3 m apart, 1 m working space.
  All are long-standing CEC rules, but a reviewer with the 2024 code should confirm.
- Three-phase colour set red, black, blue (asked as a set, not as an A/B/C order).

## Reviewer check (2026-10-30)
Confirmed against secondary sources that apply the CEC (municipal and utility load worksheets, ESA and Technical Safety BC bulletins, CEC guides): 100 A / 24 000 W minimum at 80 m² or more excluding basement (the service is the greater of this and the calculated load), heating demand 100% of first 10 kW then 75%, Class A GFCI within 1.5 m of a sink, red/black/blue phase colours. Two rods 3 m apart and 1 m working space are long-standing CEC values not contradicted anywhere; 2024 rule text itself was not available.
