# Sources for caguard (Ontario security guard licence test)

All checked 2026-10-30. No question was copied from any of them or from any commercial bank;
the official pages define topics, facts and weights only.

- Ontario.ca, Security guard and private investigator testing: 60 multiple-choice questions,
  75 minutes, online or at select DriveTest centres, training completion number needed to book.
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/security-guard-and-private-investigator-testing
- Ontario.ca, Security guard study guide: cut score 62%, 2-hour appointment incl. 75 minutes,
  results in five business days, fee listed as $66.50 + HST ($75.15), section summaries (PSISA
  in force Aug 23 2007, Training and Testing Regulation Apr 15 2010, carry and produce licence,
  uniform and prohibited items, $25,000 / 1 year penalty, patrol types, alarm types, evidence
  steps, TPA and s.494 arrest, s.25/26 force, positional asphyxia, ELFA topics).
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/security-guard-study-guide
- Ontario.ca, Training syllabus for security guards (updated July 06, 2026): 12 sections with
  suggested hours (used for the blueprint), minimum 40 h with first aid / 33.5 h without, and
  section pages 2, 3, 6, 8.
  https://www.ontario.ca/document/training-syllabus-security-guards
- Ontario.ca, Security guard or private investigator licence (individuals): age 18, entitled to
  work in Canada, clean record, CRJMC within six months, $80 fee, digital-only licence, carry a
  digital or printed copy, expiry two birthdays after issue, renew up to 90 days early, report
  a listed conviction within 5 days.
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/get-security-guard-or-private-investigator-licence
- O. Reg. 362/07 (Uniforms): SECURITY / SECURITY GUARD on chest, upper case, at least 1.5 cm,
  contrasting colour; ID tag; no police-style forage cap. https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/070362
- PSISA 2005 (prohibited terms detective, private detective, law enforcement, police, officer).
  https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/05p34
- Criminal Code ss. 25, 26, 30, 34, 35, 494 (incl. 2013 Citizen's Arrest and Self-defence Act
  amendments), 265 assault; Interpretation Act s.34 hybrid offences deemed indictable.
- Trespass to Property Act (Ontario): s.2 offence (does not leave immediately when directed),
  notice orally, in writing or by signs; red markings entry prohibited, yellow certain activities;
  s.9 arrest and delivery to police.
- OHSA (Ontario): three rights, JHSC at 20+ workers, refusal reported to supervisor; workplace
  violence and harassment policies. WHMIS 2015: 16-section SDS, supplier label elements.
- Ontario ESA overtime after 44 hours; RTA 24 hours written notice; drinking age 19; AODA 2005
  service animals and support persons; Ontario Human Rights Code grounds (gender identity 2012).
- Canadian first aid/CPR guidelines (Heart and Stroke / CSA Z1210 level): 100-120/min, 5-6 cm,
  30:2, back blows and abdominal thrusts for choking.

Not confirmed / kept out of the bank: exact test fee (official study guide says $66.50 + HST,
other sites say $35 + HST, so the landing omits it); baton/handcuff authorization details
(O. Reg. 366/07 not readable here); vehicle marking rules; raw pass count (62% of 60 = 37.2).

Review 2026-10-30: the date physical cards stopped (Sept 30, 2023 on provider sites) is not on ontario.ca, so no item states it. Charter s.10(b) explanation hedged (courts generally hold it does not bind private guards directly).
