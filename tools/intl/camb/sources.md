# Sources: Manitoba Class 5 learner knowledge test (camb)

## Primary (official)
- MPI Driver's Handbook, DVL0019, print dated 20/10/2026 (PDF metadata Oct 2026), 168 pages:
  https://www.mpi.mb.ca/en/PDFs/completehandbook.pdf (local copy and text in ./dl/handbook.pdf, ./dl/hb3.txt)
  Every rule in the bank was checked against this text. Key facts used:
  - Test appointments p.6-7: knowledge test $12, 30-minute limit, not open book, no phones;
    GDL applicants wait seven days to retest; Class 5 knowledge tests in more than 20 other languages.
  - Class Licence System p.21-22; Basic Class 5 requirements p.22.
  - GDL p.23-24: 5L min 9 months, age 16 (15 1/2 in a high school driver education course in progress /
    MPI Driver Z), parental consent under 18; supervising driver held 5F 3 years (36 months), BAC under .05,
    must not fail drug screen, only front-seat passenger; rear passengers up to working rear seatbelts;
    no towing; no Class 3 farm truck; no ORVs along/across highways; may not obtain Authorized Instruction
    Classes 1-4. 5I min 15 months: road test to enter; passenger limits 5 a.m.-midnight and midnight-5 a.m.;
    towing under 4,540 kg. 5F: zero drugs/alcohol first 36 months; may supervise after three years in Full.
  - Seatbelts and child seats p.15-20: booster until 145 cm, 36 kg or nine years old.
  - Signs p.26-35; stop positions p.36; four-way stops p.37; signals p.38-40; pavement markings p.40-43.
  - Lane changing, hand signals, curves, turning p.47-62 (signal 30 m before a turn, 150 m outside towns;
    U-turns prohibited at curves and hill crests without 150 m clear view).
  - Speed 90 km/h rural / 50 km/h urban default; four-second following distance (six on gravel) p.64-65, 115.
  - Passing p.66-67; No-Zone p.67-68; expressways p.69-70; right-of-way p.70-71.
  - Emergency/designated vehicles: 40 km/h where limit under 80, 60 km/h where 80 or more p.71-72.
  - School bus: stop at least 5 m, both directions on undivided highway p.73. Transit buses p.74-77.
  - Construction zones: speeding fines doubled p.78; zipper merge p.79.
  - Railway crossings: 5 m (restricted speed area) / 15 m (non-restricted); no parking within 30 m p.80-81.
  - Pedestrians, crosswalks (no parking/stopping within 3 m), pedestrian corridors (15 m) p.82-86.
  - School zones 30 km/h (posted under 80) or 50 km/h (80 or more); no passing within 15 min of classes p.86.
  - Cyclists: at least one metre when passing p.87-91.
  - Parking: hill parking p.101; illegal parking distances p.103 (hydrant 3 m, stop sign 9 m, fire station 6 m).
  - Equipment p.104-109: tread 1.6 mm, studded tires illegal April 30 to Oct. 1, headlights half hour
    before sunset to half hour after sunrise and when visibility under 60 m, horn heard 60 m.
  - Poor conditions p.110-120: low beam 30 m, high beam 110 m; dim 450 m oncoming / 60 m following;
    deer peak sunset to midnight and after sunrise; farm equipment max 40 km/h.
  - Emergencies p.121-126; collisions p.127-129 (report to police within seven days for serious injury etc.;
    property damage only not reportable to police; flares 60 m).
  - Impaired driving p.132-138: novice zero drugs/alcohol; 24-hour roadside suspension; Tiered ALS at .05-.079
    (72 h, 7 days with under-16 passenger, 15, 30, 60 days); three-month ALS at .08 or more, over 5 ng THC,
    refusal; cannabis must be in secure compartment.
  - Distracted driving p.114: hand-operated electronic device: three-day suspension first offence,
    seven-day each subsequent; five levels down the DSR scale.
  - Driving while suspended p.140: vehicle impounded minimum 30 days.
  - DI&C p.141-142: novice = no Full Stage licence for more than one year.
  - Driver Safety Rating p.144.
- MPI "Testing for your Class 5 licence": https://www.mpi.mb.ca/testing-for-your-class-5-licence/
  (30-minute limit, $12, English, French and 28 other languages with audio, computer-based;
  GDL seven-day retest wait; Driver Z at 15 1/2).

## Secondary (not official; used only for question count and pass mark)
- Question count of 30 multiple choice: reported by CBC (https://www.cbc.ca/1.6952180, not fetched: 403) and
  every prep site (tests.ca, try2pass.ca, licenceready.ca). MPI does not publish a question count or pass mark.
- Pass mark: prep sites split between 24 of 30 (80%) and 25 of 30 (83%). Not confirmed by MPI.
  The bank uses 80% as a practice line; flagged as uncertain.
