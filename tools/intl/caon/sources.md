# Ontario G1 (caon) sources

Read October 29, 2026. Raw chapter text saved in `src/` (fetched from ontario.ca).

## Official
- The Official MTO Driver's Handbook (ontario.ca, pages "Updated: March 31, 2026"):
  https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook
  - Getting your driver's licence (age 16, vision + knowledge test, G1 12 months or 8 with BDE,
    G1/G2 conditions, accompanying driver 4 years and BAC under .05, 400-series over 80 km/h,
    midnight to 5 a.m., G2 passenger limits for 19 and under, at least 20 months total, "Could you
    pass?" topic list, 10 days between road tests):
    https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook/getting-your-drivers-licence
  - Keeping your licence (demerit tables: novice 2/6/9 points and 60-day suspension, full 6/9/15
    and 30 days, points kept 2 years, 6 days to report name/address change):
    https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook/keeping-your-drivers-licence
  - Other ways to lose your licence (21 and under zero BAC, ADLS 90 days over .08, warn range
    .05 to .08 3 days / 7 days, novice condition violation 30 days):
    https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook/other-ways-lose-your-licence
  - Safe driving chapters (getting ready, driving along, sharing the road, intersections, stopping,
    changing directions/positions, parking, freeway, particular situations, night and weather,
    emergencies, driving efficiently): links from the handbook contents page.
  - Signs, traffic lights, pedestrian signals, pavement markings:
    https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook/signs
  - Sample knowledge test questions (multiple-choice format, 4 options):
    https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook/test-yourself-sample-knowledge-test-questions
- Get a G driver's licence: new drivers (5 years to finish graduated licensing, G1/G2 rules):
  https://www.ontario.ca/page/get-g-drivers-licence-new-drivers
- DriveTest, Knowledge tests: https://drivetest.ca/tests/knowledge-tests/
  (403 to direct fetch; content read through search-engine extracts of the official page):
  multiple choice, made by MTO, two or three sections (signs and rules of the road), 80% total
  score to pass, not timed (typically 20 to 30 minutes), G1 test in 32 languages, a retake within
  a year redoes only the sections that did not meet the standard.

## Not official, used only as context
- The widely reported 40 questions (20 signs, 20 rules) and 16 of 20 per section come from
  third-party practice sites; DriveTest does not publish a question count. Landing copy says
  "usually reported as 40" and never claims an official count.
- Fee: third-party sites give a G1 package of $158.25 (knowledge test, first road test, five-year
  licence). Not confirmed on an official page reachable here, so copy only says the application
  fee covers those three things.

## Choices
- Pass mark 80 (DriveTest total score).
- Signs section is about half the real test; here signs (14) + lights/markings (8) = 22 of 50.
- Collision reporting threshold ($5,000) asked once: it is in the handbook and on the test topic
  list ("collision reporting"). No other dollar amounts.
- Hazard: sign designs are described in words. Only shapes and colours stated in the handbook, plus
  standard Canadian symbols (stop, yield, crossbuck, do not enter red circle with white bar,
  U-turn prohibition, slippery when wet car, snowmobile green circle).
