# capal sources (checked 29 Oct 2026)

## The course and test (RCMP, Canadian Firearms Program)
- https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/firearms-safety-training-transport-and-storage/safety-courses :
  CFSC is in-class instruction by qualified instructors followed by a written and a practical test;
  anyone 12 or older can take it; first-time applicants must pass it before applying for a PAL.
  Topic list used for the categories: evolution of firearms, major parts, types and actions; basic
  safety practices; ammunition; operating actions; safe handling and carry; firing techniques;
  care of non-restricted firearms; responsibilities of owners and users; safe storage, display,
  transportation and handling.
- Pass mark 80% on each test and a 50-question written test: consistent across provincial
  delivery partners (e.g. College of the North Atlantic firearms FAQ,
  https://www.cna.nl.ca/Programs-Courses/Continuing-Education-Firearms-FAQs). RCMP does not
  publish the item count or per-chapter weights online, so the blueprint counts are ours.
- Licence facts: PAL renewed every 5 years and 28-day minimum wait for new applicants
  (RCMP Canadian residents page and form RCMP 5592); minor's licence 12 to 17.

## Law (Justice Laws, current to 2026-09-30)
- Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals Regulations, SOR/98-209:
  https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209/FullText.html
  s.1 unloaded; s.5 storage of non-restricted (unloaded + locking device / bolt removed / locked
  container; ammunition not readily accessible unless locked; s.5(2) predator control; s.5(3)
  remote wilderness); s.8 display; s.10 transport (unloaded; muzzleloader cap or flint removed;
  unattended vehicle trunk / not visible; s.10(3) wilderness); s.15 load only where discharge lawful.
- Criminal Code Part III, s.84 classes (660 mm overall, 457 mm barrel, 470 mm semi-auto centrefire).
- Bill C-21 (S.C. 2023, c. 32), Royal Assent 15 Dec 2023: handgun freeze (regulations Oct 2022),
  red flag emergency prohibition orders, yellow flag (in force 7 Mar 2025):
  https://www.publicsafety.gc.ca/cnt/cntrng-crm/frrms/c21-en.aspx and
  https://www.canada.ca/en/public-safety-canada/news/2023/12/legislation-to-reduce-gun-violence-receives-royal-assent.html
- May 2020 Order in Council prohibiting about 1,500 models: kept general on purpose (compensation
  and amnesty dates are still moving).
- Licence verification on non-restricted transfers in force 18 May 2022 (former Bill C-71).
- Migratory Birds Regulations: non-toxic shot for most migratory game birds.

- Address change: report to the Canadian Firearms Program within 30 days (licence condition;
  RCMP contact page and change-of-address guidance), confirmed in review 30 Oct 2026.
- Written test format: about 50 questions, mostly multiple choice with some true or false at
  some delivery partners; 80% on written and practical (consistent across course providers).

## Not confirmed online (worded softly in the bank)
- Hangfire wait: CAPAL-AMM-009 keys "keep it pointed safely and wait"; "at least 60 seconds" is
  only in the explanation as the usual guidance.
- Muzzle-down storage after oiling: CAPAL-CAR-005 now asks why owners do it, and mentions laying
  it flat as an alternative.
