# caqc sources (Quebec SAAQ Class 5 knowledge test)

saaq.gouv.qc.ca HTML pages sit behind a bot check (Radware) and could not be fetched directly;
the SAAQ's own PDFs under /blob/ and the Gouvernement du Quebec pages on quebec.ca were fetched
and read in full. Facts seen only in search-engine summaries of SAAQ pages are marked (summary).

## Official SAAQ PDFs (read in full, local copies in caqc/dl/)
- Quick Reference Guide, Obtaining a passenger vehicle driver's licence (Class 5), C-6963-A (25-07):
  https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/permis-conduire/reference-guide-licence-classe-5.pdf
  (phase 1 + school exam -> learner's licence; hold 10 months -> knowledge test; 12 months -> road test;
  28-day wait after a failed test; probationary licence 2 years, fewer than 4 demerit points, zero alcohol,
  passenger rule; test may be requested in Spanish, Mandarin or Arabic where applicable)
- Demerit Points, Driver's Licence: https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/publications/demerit-points-driver-licence.pdf
  (brackets 4/8/12/15, points stay 2 years, penalty 3/6/12 months, offence table)
- Accompanying Rider's Guide, Passenger Vehicle (2018):
  https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/publications/accompagning-rider-guide-vehicle.pdf
  (accompanying rider 2 years Class 5, probationary years do not count; 24 h theory + 15 h practice;
  12 modules / 15 in-car sessions; 50 hours recommended by experts)
- Road Safety Education Program course modules (English):
  https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/cours-conduite/module-1-vehicle.pdf (seat belts, 145 cm / 9 years)
  .../module-2-driver.pdf, .../module-3-environment.pdf (50 km/h default, 150 m low beams, quiz),
  .../module-5-evaluation-en.pdf (learner rules: 4 points, zero alcohol, no driving midnight to 5 a.m.),
  .../module-6-accompanied-driving.pdf, .../module-7-oea-strategy.pdf (OEA; following 4/6/8-10 s; 1 m/1.5 m),
  .../module-8-speed.pdf (excessive speeding, reaction time 1.3 s, stopping distances),
  .../module-9-sharing-road.pdf (vulnerable users, heavy vehicles, school bus 9 points, Move-Over),
  .../module-10-alcohol-drugs.pdf (0.08, THC limits, zero-alcohol penalties),
  .../module-11-fatigue-distractions-en.pdf, .../module-12-eco-driving.pdf, .../in-car-session-workbook.pdf
- Your Obligations Around Schools: https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/publications/feuillet-obligations-around-schools-school-buses.pdf
- Road Safety Is for All Ages: https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/blob/saaq/documents/publications/road-safety-all-ages.pdf
  (crosswalk colours, pedestrian lights, 25 cm airbag distance, health changes within 30 days)

## Other official
- SPVM (Montreal police), New drivers: https://spvm.qc.ca/en/Jeunesse/New-drivers- (summary): probationary
  driver 19 or under, midnight to 5 a.m.: one passenger 19 or under for the first 6 months, three for the next 6.
- SAAQ, Preparing for the knowledge and road tests (summary):
  https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/en/tourists-newcomers/driving-in-quebec/preparing-tests
  Three parts (Highway Safety Code; road signs and traffic signals; behaviours and techniques for driving a
  passenger vehicle), multiple choice, 75% required on each part; audio in French and English.
- SAAQ, Obtaining a Passenger Vehicle (Class 5) Driver's Licence (summary):
  https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/en/drivers-licences/obtaining-licence/passenger-vehicle-class-5
  At least 16; parental consent under 18; 10 months before knowledge test; 28-day retake wait.
- SAAQ, Drinking and driving, young drivers (summary):
  https://saaq.gouv.qc.ca/en/road-safety/client-groups/young-drivers/drinking-driving
  Zero alcohol for licence holders under 22 and for every learner's licence holder.

## Not confirmed / left out
- The number of questions on the live test (SAAQ does not publish it); fees; the 2-second vs 4-second
  following rule debate (we use the SAAQ OEA module figures); uphill parking wheel direction (the module says
  left with a curb, quebec.ca says toward the curb generically), so only downhill is asked.
