## Clinical content
- Heart & Stroke, heart attack signs and ASA (chew one 325 mg or two 81 mg unless allergic; not acetaminophen or ibuprofen): https://www.heartandstroke.ca/heart-disease/conditions/heart-attack and the Heart Attack Emergency Checklist PDF.
- Heart & Stroke FAST stroke signs: https://www.heartandstroke.ca/stroke/signs-of-stroke
- Canadian first aid and resuscitation guidelines (ILCOR-based consensus used by Canadian training agencies): CPR 100 to 120/min, adult depth at least 5 cm (not over about 6), child and infant one-third of chest depth (about 5 cm / about 4 cm), 30:2 for lay rescuers all ages, breaths about 1 s, pulse-free lay assessment up to 10 s, switch compressor about every 2 min, paediatric AED pads under 8 years or 25 kg, pad 2.5 cm from implanted device, tourniquet 5 to 7.5 cm above the wound, open chest wound left open or non-occlusive dressing, cold-water immersion for heat stroke, burns cooled at least 10 minutes. ILCOR CoSTR: https://www.ilcor.org
- Public Health Agency of Canada hand washing (at least 20 seconds): https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/diseases/2019-novel-coronavirus-infection/prevention-risks/hand-washing.html
- Good Samaritan Drug Overdose Act (federal, 2017): https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/opioids/about-good-samaritan-drug-overdose-act.html
- 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline (launched 30 Nov 2023): https://988.ca
- Quebec Charter of human rights and freedoms, s. 2 (duty to assist a person whose life is in peril): https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/c-12

## Choices made where sources differ
- Adult choking: Canadian agencies teach back blows plus abdominal thrusts (one teaches thrusts alone); questions use the combination and never offer "thrusts alone" as a distractor.
- Infant choking: "back blows and chest thrusts"; infant compression hand technique is not asked (two-finger vs two-thumb is changing between guideline editions).
- Burn cooling: "at least 10 minutes" (distractors are seconds), so the 20-minute UK/AU figure is not contradicted.
- No training agency is named in any question or explanation. Provincial rules (workplace kits, certificate validity, Good Samaritan wording) are "varies".
