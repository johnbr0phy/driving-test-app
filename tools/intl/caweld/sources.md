**Which breakdown:** the current one, for the **2023 RSOS** (new standard, exams in force from
February 2026). red-seal.ca's exam-information page, the Saskatchewan Feb 2026 sheet and the NL
"RSOS 2023" guide all give the same 125-question split. The older 2015-standard breakdown was
not used.

Blueprint per 50-question test (125 scaled by 0.4, rounded; D rounded up by one):
A 8 (20 -> 8.0), B 11 (28 -> 11.2: layout 5, fabrication 6), C 9 (23 -> 9.2),
D 22 (54 -> 21.6: SMAW 8, GMAW/FCAW/MCAW 7, GTAW + SAW 7). Task A-6 (communication and
mentoring) has 0 exam questions and is not covered.

Technical content is general welding practice and concept-level references to CSA W47.1
(company certification), CSA W48 (filler metals, metric classifications such as E4918), CSA W59
(welded steel construction) and CSA Z94.3 (eye and face protection). No clause numbers are cited.
Province-specific rules (fall protection heights, hot-work fire-watch times) are avoided.

Facts I could not confirm from an official page: the exam time per jurisdiction (the 4-hour
limit is from the Red Seal prep guide); the Ontario trade code 456A is from Skilled Trades
Ontario's trade list as commonly published.
