# WHMIS (cawhmis) sources

All 200 questions are original, written from the regulations and public government guidance below.
No provider's course quiz, commercial bank or sample test was copied. Checked 2026-10-30.

- Health Canada, "Amended Hazardous Products Regulations" (in force December 15, 2022; Canada Gazette II January 4, 2023; transition ended December 14, 2025; GHS Rev. 7 plus parts of Rev. 8; chemicals under pressure; aerosols Category 3; flammable gases 1A/1B with pyrophoric gases moved to 1A; significant new data documents; ingredient disclosure; narrower concentration ranges):
  https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/environmental-workplace-health/occupational-health-safety/workplace-hazardous-materials-information-system/amendments-hazardous-products-regulations.html
- Health Canada, WHMIS hub: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/environmental-workplace-health/occupational-health-safety/workplace-hazardous-materials-information-system.html
- CCOHS OSH Answers, WHMIS General (roles, laws, exclusions, CBI): https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/whmis_ghs/general.html
- CCOHS, WHMIS Pictograms (pictogram to class/category map, classes with no pictogram, red diamond border): https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/whmis_ghs/pictograms.html
- CCOHS, WHMIS Hazard Classes and Categories (Category 1 most severe, subcategory letters, gases under pressure by state): https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/whmis_ghs/hazard_classes.html
- CCOHS, WHMIS Labels (supplier and workplace label elements, bilingual, hatched border optional, 100 mL / 3 mL small containers, decanted portable containers): https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/whmis_ghs/labels.html
- CCOHS, WHMIS Safety Data Sheets (16 sections, 12 to 15 headings only, bilingual, web link not enough, 90-day significant new data, employer 3-year check in federal, BC, SK, NL, NT, NS, NU, YT): https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/whmis_ghs/sds.html
- Energy Safety Canada, 2025 WHMIS legislation note (confirms December 15, 2025 full compliance): https://www.energysafetycanada.com/resource/2025-whmis-legislation

Blueprint: WHMIS has no national exam or published weighting. Topic weights are ours, set to the core
content every WHMIS general-education course covers (roles and laws 16%, hazard classes 14%,
pictograms 18%, labels 18%, SDS 18%, safe handling and emergencies 16%). The 80% pass line is a
practice target, not an official pass mark (each employer or trainer sets its own).

Not confirmed / kept general on purpose:
- Annual training review: "at least yearly in most jurisdictions"; the exact rule varies.
- Decanted portable container exemption, laboratory labels and eyewash distances: worded as
  "varies by jurisdiction".
- Biohazardous infectious materials pictogram has a circular (not diamond) border: from Health Canada's
  set; CCOHS says only "most pictograms" have the red diamond, and training sources (safetynow.com WHMIS pictograms sheet) confirm the black circle.
