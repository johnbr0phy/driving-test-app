# cscs sources (checked 30 Oct 2026)

All 200 questions are original, written from the law and HSE guidance below. Nothing is copied
from CITB's GT100 revision book, its question bank or any commercial mock test.

## The test (CITB)
- https://www.citb.co.uk/courses-tests/health-safety-and-environment-hse-test/operatives-test :
  Operatives test, 50 questions, 45 minutes, pass mark 90% (45 of 50). Core knowledge areas listed
  as working environment, occupational health, safety, high risk activities (and environment).
- https://www.citb.co.uk/courses-and-qualifications/hse-test-and-cards/about-hse-test/ : three
  tests (Operatives, Specialists, Managers and Professionals), each 50 questions in 45 minutes;
  fee £23.50; taken at Pearson Professional Centres (group bookings and mobile testing exist).
- https://www.cscs.uk.com/frequently-asked-questions/test/ : a test pass must be within the last
  2 years when applying for or renewing a card.
- https://shop.citb.co.uk/HS-E-Test-for-Operatives-Specialists : GT100 (2019 edition) is the
  official revision material for the current test. Behavioural case-study section was removed in
  the 2019 redesign; behavioural elements are now built into the 50 questions (ARCA notice,
  https://www.arca.org.uk/news/citb-to-update-health-safety-environment-test/).
- CITB publishes no per-topic question counts. Our weights are ours: legal and management 16%,
  health and welfare 12%, occupational health 20%, general safety 20%, working at height 14%,
  excavations and confined spaces 8%, environment 10%.
- Not confirmed: whether CITB now also offers remote (online) testing; landing does not claim it.

## Law and guidance used for the questions (legislation.gov.uk and hse.gov.uk)
- Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (ss.2, 7, 8; improvement notice not less than 21 days, s.21; prohibition notice s.22)
- Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 (risk assessment, young persons reg.19)
- Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015: duty holders, construction phase plan
  (client ensures, PC/contractor draws up), notifiable project (>30 working days with >20 workers
  at once, or >500 person days; form F10), reg.22 excavations inspected at the start of every
  shift, Schedule 2 welfare. HSE L153.
- RIDDOR 2013: over-7-day injuries reported within 15 days; over-3-day recorded.
- Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012; HSE: buildings before 2000; disease 15 to 60 years latency.
- HSE construction dust (RCS) guidance; face-fit testing (INDG479).
- Control of Noise at Work Regulations 2005 (80 / 85 dB(A) action values, 87 limit).
- Control of Vibration at Work Regulations 2005 (HAVS).
- COSHH 2002; CLP pictograms (skull and crossbones = acute toxicity).
- Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992 (avoid, assess, reduce; TILE).
- PPE at Work Regulations 1992 as amended 2022 (limb (b) workers).
- Health and Safety (Safety Signs and Signals) Regulations 1996; BS EN 3 extinguisher colour bands.
- HSE HSG150 Health and safety in construction (110 V CTE, colour-coded connectors, RCDs, site transport, signallers).
- HSE HSG47 Avoiding danger from underground services; GS6 overhead power lines.
- Work at Height Regulations 2005, Schedule 2 (main guard rail at least 950 mm for construction, toe boards), Schedule 7 (scaffold inspection at intervals not exceeding 7 days); HSE INDG455 ladders (1 in 4, about 1 m above landing, top steps of stepladders).
- Confined Spaces Regulations 1997 (definition, rescue arrangements).
- Environmental Protection Act 1990 s.34 waste duty of care; registered waste carriers.
- Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) (England) Regulations 2001: secondary containment 110%.
- Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (nesting birds); Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017 (bats).
- Environment Agency, SEPA, NRW, NIEA as pollution regulators.
