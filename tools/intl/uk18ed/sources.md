  2382-26 (Amendment 4) is the current version, centres must use it from 16 April 2026; last registration 31 Dec 2029. 2382-22 (A2 + May 2023 corrigendum) registrations ended 31 July 2026.
- 2382-26 qualification handbook v1.0 (March 2026): https://www.cityandguilds.com/-/media/productdocuments/building_services_engineering/electrical_installation/2382/level_3/centre_documents/2382-26-requirements-for-electrical-installations-bs7671-2018-2026-handbook-v1-0-pdf.pdf
  Level 3 Award, GLH 35, TQT 40. One online multiple-choice test (2382-626), open book with BS 7671:2018 (Amendment 4:2026) only, no notes, sample questions or added diagrams. Duration 120 minutes. Pass/Fail, pass mark "approx. 60%".
  Test specification (60 questions): 1 scope/object/fundamental principles 4 (7%); 2 definitions 2 (3%); 3 general characteristics (Ch 31-36) 6 (10%); 4 protection for safety (Ch 41, 42, 43, 44, 46) 15 (25%); 5 selection and erection (common rules, wiring systems, protection/isolation/switching, earthing and protective conductors, other equipment, safety services, stationary secondary batteries) 14 (23%); 6 inspection and testing (Ch 64, 65) 4 (7%); 7 special installations or locations (700-753 incl. 716 PoE) 7 (12%); 8 Part 8 and the appendices 8 (13%).
  Scaled here to 50: 3 / 2 / 5 / 13 / 12 / 3 / 6 / 6.

## The standard, Amendment 4:2026
- NICEIC, Amendment 4 overview: https://niceic.com/amendment-four
  Published 15 April 2026; BS 7671:2018+A2:2022+A3:2024 withdrawn 15 October 2026. New Chapter 57 (stationary secondary batteries), Section 545 (functional earthing and functional equipotential bonding for ICT), Section 716 (PoE), major revision of 710; PNB earthing included; HRRB wording for AFDDs (care homes, HRRBs, HMOs, purpose-built student accommodation, socket circuits to 32 A); functional earth conductors pink preferred, green-and-yellow not allowed; MEIWC ring readings now r1, rn, r2; FI and C3 no longer make an EICR unsatisfactory.

## Technical content
Values used (all long-standing in the 18th Edition and unchanged by A4 as far as the sources show): Table 41.1 disconnection times (TN 0.4 s / TT 0.2 s for final circuits to 63 A with sockets or 32 A fixed; 5 s / 1 s otherwise); 30 mA additional protection for sockets to 32 A, outdoor mobile equipment to 32 A, domestic lighting; RA × IΔn ≤ 50 V and the 200 Ω stability note; Zs = U0 × Cmin / Ia with Cmin 0.95; MCB B/C/D bands; Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz, I2 ≤ 1.45 Iz; adiabatic S = √(I²t)/k; SELV 50 V AC / 120 V DC; IPXXB/IP2X and IPXXD/IP4X; Table 42.1 temperatures; 300 mA fire RCD; SPD consequences and 0.5 m / 1.0 m lead length; Table 54.7, 54.8, 54.1 sizes; gas bonding within 600 mm of the meter outlet; safe zones 150 mm; joists 50 mm; Table 52.3 1.5 mm²; Cc 0.725 (BS 3036) and 0.9 (buried); insulation resistance 500 V / 1 MΩ, 250 V / 0.5 MΩ, SPD case 250 V / 1 MΩ; RCD 300 ms at IΔn; Section 701 zones (2.25 m, 0.6 m, 12 V AC zone 0, IPX4, sockets 3 m); 702 zone 1 2 m; 704 55 V; 708 individual 30 mA RCD, 0.5 to 1.5 m, no PME; 710 groups; 722 dedicated circuit, Type B or Type A plus 6 mA DC detection, PME restrictions; voltage drop 3% / 5%.
Review (30 Oct 2026): the On-Site Guide floor-area items (not in BS 7671, so not answerable in the open-book exam) were replaced by private-supply voltage drop (6% / 8%), the 13 A fused-spur fuse and the unfused-spur count from Appendix 15.
