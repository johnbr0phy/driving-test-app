# ukadi sources (checked 29 Oct 2026)

Official (GOV.UK / DVSA):
- ADI part 1 test: https://www.gov.uk/adi-part-1-test (print view /adi-part-1-test/print). 100 MCQs in 1 h 30 min,
  4 categories of 25 (road procedure; traffic signs and signals, car control, pedestrians and mechanical knowledge;
  driving test, disabilities and the law; publications and instructional techniques). Pass 85/100 overall AND 20/25 in
  each category. Hazard perception 14 clips (one with two hazards), 57/75 to pass, taken in the same sitting.
  Revision list: Highway Code, Know your traffic signs, Driving: the essential skills, Official DVSA Guide to Learning to
  Drive, Driving Instructor's Handbook, Practical Teaching Skills for Driving Instructors, National standard for driving
  cars and light vans, National standard for driver and rider training, Driving instructor code of practice, Carrying
  out driving tests: examiner guidance (excl. sections 2-5 and 15). Retake after at least 3 working days. Pass valid
  2 years (must qualify within that time). English voiceover on request; extra time with evidence.
- Become a driving instructor: https://www.gov.uk/become-a-driving-instructor . Part 1 fee 81 GBP, part 2 111, part 3
  111, trainee licence 140, first ADI certificate 300. Parts 2 and 3 within 2 years of passing part 1; register within
  12 months; trainee licence 6 months (after part 2); standards check at least every 4 years; registration and DBS every 4 years.
- ADI part 2: https://www.gov.uk/adi-part-2-test/print . Eyesight 27 m (27.5 m old plates), about 1 hour, max 6 driving
  faults, 2 of 4 manoeuvres, 3 tell me + 2 show me, 3 attempts then part 1 again (after 2 years from first pass).
- ADI part 3: https://www.gov.uk/adi-part-3-test/print . ~45 min lesson, 17 competencies scored 0-3, 3 categories
  (lesson planning, risk management, teaching and learning strategies); 0-30 fail, 31-42 grade B, 43-51 grade A;
  risk management 7 or less = fail. Pupil cannot be an ADI or part 3 candidate.
- Car driving test: https://www.gov.uk/driving-test/print . Eyesight 20 m / 20.5 m; 1 tell me + 1 show me; ~35 min;
  independent driving with sat nav/signs; one of parallel park, bay park, pull up on right and reverse ~2 car lengths;
  emergency stop possible; pass with up to 15 driving faults and no serious/dangerous; retest at least 10 working days.
- Learning to drive: https://www.gov.uk/driving-lessons-learning-to-drive/print . Provisional at 15y9m, driving at 17
  (16 with enhanced PIP mobility), supervisor over 21 with 3 years' licence, motorway lessons with ADI in dual-control
  car, green ADI badge / pink trainee badge, uninsured up to 8 points.
- ADI code of practice: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/driving-instructor-code-of-practice/approved-driving-instructor-adi-code-of-practice
  (conduct, business dealings, advertising, complaints to ADI Registrar).
- The Highway Code: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code (introduction H1-H3; rules 91, 95, 96, 99-101, 112-116,
  119, 121, 124, 126, 129, 141, 146, 149, 155, 163, 167, 174, 181, 186, 191, 195-199, 206-207, 219, 223, 226, 232, 238,
  239, 243, 248, 249, 259, 264, 265, 275, 286, 293); typical stopping distances diagram (9 m thinking / 23 m overall at
  30 mph; 96 m at 70 mph).
- Eyesight standard (6/12 Snellen, decimal 0.5): https://www.gov.uk/driving-eyesight-rules
