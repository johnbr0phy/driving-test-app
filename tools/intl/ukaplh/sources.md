# ukaplh sources (Award for Personal Licence Holders, England and Wales)

All checked 30 October 2026. Every question is original; no awarding body's specimen or mock paper was used.

Exam format
- TQUK Level 2 Award for Personal Licence Holders (RQF) qualification specification (verve.tquk.org/files/specifications/specification_62.pdf): one unit, "Legal and social responsibilities of a personal licence holder"; 40 multiple-choice questions, 28 correct to pass; 60 minutes on TQUK's paper.
- Highfield Level 2 Award for Personal Licence Holders (RQF), 603/2597/5 (highfieldqualifications.com/products/qualifications/licensing/level-2-personal-licence-holders) and centre course outlines (e.g. trainingsafety.co.uk Highfield APLH sheet): course content list used for the categories: licensing authorities and objectives, personal licence application and duties, premises licences and operating schedules, the DPS, unauthorised licensable activities, permitted temporary activities (TENs), rights of entry, police closure powers, prohibitions on sale, alcohol strengths and effects, protection of children, responsible retailing.
- BIIAB APLH centre pages (btbl.co.uk): 40 questions, 28 to pass, about 40 minutes. Timing varies by awarding body (40 to 60 minutes), so the landing says so.

Law (legislation.gov.uk, current text)
- Licensing Act 2003, incl. s.4 objectives, s.5/5A policy and cumulative impact (5 years / 3 years), s.6-10 committees, s.47 interim authority (28 days, changed from 7 in 2010), s.107 TEN limits (15 notices / 21 days per premises; 20 / 26 applied to 2022 and 2023 only, via SI 2021/1049), s.120-135 personal licences, s.136-160 offences, s.145-153 children, s.169A closure notices (48 to 336 hours), s.176 excluded premises, s.179-180 entry, s.191 definition of alcohol (over 0.5%).
- Licensing Act 2003 (Mandatory Licensing Conditions) Order 2010 as amended 2014 (age verification ID with photo, DOB and hologram or UV; small measures 25/35 ml, 125 ml, half pint; free tap water; irresponsible promotions; permitted price = duty + VAT).
- Deregulation Act 2015 (personal licences no longer renewed; policy cycle 5 years); Live Music Act 2012 and the Legislative Reform (Entertainment Licensing) Order 2014 (500 / 1,000 audience exemptions, 8 am to 11 pm); Immigration Act 2016 (right to work; Home Office as responsible authority); Policing and Crime Act 2017 (licensing authority power to suspend up to 6 months or revoke a personal licence; cumulative impact assessments).
- Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 Part 4 (closure notices 24 hours, 48 with extension; closure orders up to 3 months, 6 max).
- Home Office, Revised Guidance issued under section 182 of the Licensing Act 2003 (gov.uk).
- Gambling Act 2005 s.282 (two category C or D machines by notification); Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005; Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 s.8; Private Security Industry Act 2001.
- UK Chief Medical Officers' Low Risk Drinking Guidelines (2016): 14 units a week over 3+ days; no alcohol in pregnancy. GOV.UK drink-drive limits (35 µg breath, 80 mg blood, 107 mg urine; Scotland 22 / 50).

Not asserted: fee amounts, fixed penalty amounts, Wales minimum unit price, EMROs, Martyn's Law (not yet in force).
