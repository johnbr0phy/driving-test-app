# ukcar sources (checked 29 Oct 2026)

## The test (GOV.UK, DVSA)
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test : from age 17 (16 if getting or applied for the higher mobility rate of PIP); provisional licence needed; booking costs £23.
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/multiple-choice-questions : 50 multiple-choice questions in 57 minutes; 3 questions on a short silent video of a normal driving situation; flag and change answers; up to 3-minute break before hazard perception.
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/hazard-perception-test : 14 clips, 15 developing hazards, up to 5 points each (75).
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/pass-mark-and-result : 43 of 50 (86%) multiple choice; 44 of 75 hazard perception; pass certificate number lasts 2 years.
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/reading-difficulty-disability-or-health-condition : English or Welsh voiceover, BSL video, extra time on request.
- https://www.gov.uk/theory-test/revision-and-practice : questions come from The Highway Code, Know Your Traffic Signs, Driving - the essential skills.
- DVSA topic list (Official DVSA Theory Test for Car Drivers): alertness; attitude; safety and your vehicle; safety margins; hazard awareness; vulnerable road users; other types of vehicle; vehicle handling; motorway rules; rules of the road; road and traffic signs; essential documents; incidents, accidents and emergencies; vehicle loading.
