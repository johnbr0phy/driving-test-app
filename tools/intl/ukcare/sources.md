Law and guidance the items rely on (England): Care Act 2014 and the Care and Support Statutory
Guidance (adult at risk definition, six safeguarding principles, ten types of abuse, Making
Safeguarding Personal); Mental Capacity Act 2005 (five principles, decision-specific test,
best interests, lasting power of attorney); Mental Health Act 1983; Equality Act 2010 (nine
protected characteristics, reasonable adjustments); Human Rights Act 1998 (Article 8);
Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014 regulation 20 (duty of
candour); Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998; Working Together to Safeguard Children;
UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 (72-hour ICO breach reporting, subject access);
Accessible Information Standard; Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, Manual Handling
Operations Regulations 1992, LOLER 1998 (six-monthly thorough examination for equipment
lifting people), COSHH 2002, RIDDOR 2013; National Infection Prevention and Control Manual for
England (PPE donning and doffing order, alginate linen bags, 48 hours symptom free);
Resuscitation Council UK adult BLS (10-second breathing check, 30:2, 100 to 120 a minute,
5 to 6 cm, choking: up to 5 back blows then up to 5 abdominal thrusts); NHS Eatwell Guide
(6 to 8 cups or glasses of fluid a day); IDDSI framework; Health and Care Act 2022 and the
Oliver McGowan Mandatory Training; STOMP; GP learning disability annual health check from 14.

Not confirmed from a primary page in this session (stated from established guidance):
the Health and Care Act 2022 wording that Oliver McGowan training is the "preferred and
recommended" package, and the 40 to 60 second handwash figure (WHO / NHS guidance).
