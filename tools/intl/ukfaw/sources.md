# ukfaw sources (all checked 2026-10-30)

- HSE, *First aid at work: The Health and Safety (First-Aid) Regulations 1981. Guidance on Regulations*,
  L74 (third edition, reissued with amendments 2024). https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/l74.pdf
  Used for: Appendix 5 (FAW course content) and Appendix 6 (EFAW content), which set the seven
  categories; Appendix 2 (suggested kit contents: three pairs of gloves, no tablets or medicines);
  Appendix 3 (suggested numbers: low hazard <25 appointed person, 25-50 one EFAW, >50 one FAW per
  100; higher hazard >50 one FAW per 50); para 74-75 (certificates valid 3 years, 2-day FAW
  requalification, annual refresher strongly recommended); appointed person role; RIDDOR pointer.
- HSE, RIDDOR (Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013),
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/riddor/ : over-seven-day injuries reported within 15 days; records kept 3 years.
- Resuscitation Council UK, 2025 Resuscitation Guidelines, Adult basic life support (published
  27 October 2025). https://www.resus.org.uk/professional-library/2025-resuscitation-guidelines/adult-basic-life-support-guidelines
  Used for: call 999 for any unresponsive person, speakerphone, agonal/panting breathing, 5-6 cm,
  100-120/min, 30:2, breaths about 1 second to make the chest rise, compression-only CPR, AED use
  by anyone, pads on bare skin, no touching during analysis, restart CPR after shock/no shock.
- Resuscitation Council UK, 2025 Resuscitation Guidelines, First aid.
  https://www.resus.org.uk/professional-library/2025-resuscitation-guidelines/first-aid-guidelines
  Used for: recovery position, choking (up to 5 back blows, up to 5 abdominal thrusts, then
  alternate and call 999; no blind finger sweeps), severe bleeding (direct pressure, haemostatic
  dressing, tourniquet 5-7 cm above and not over a joint, note the time, never loosen),
  anaphylaxis (outer thigh auto-injector, second dose after 5 minutes, lie down or sit if
  breathless), chest pain (300 mg aspirin), FAST, hypoglycaemia (15-20 g glucose, below
  4.0 mmol/L, repeat after 15 minutes, nothing by mouth if unresponsive), heatstroke (cool first,
  transfer second), hypothermia (insulate, remove wet clothes), spinal injury (minimise movement,
  manual head support, jaw thrust, airway first, no rigid collars for first-aiders), opioid poisoning,
  concussion.
- NHS (nhs.uk) pages used for points RCUK does not cover: burns (cool or lukewarm running water
  for 20 minutes, cling film, see a doctor if larger than the person's hand), nosebleeds (lean
  forward, pinch soft part 10 to 15 minutes, A&E if it will not stop), epilepsy/seizures (999 if
  over 5 minutes or first seizure), TIA (call 999 even if symptoms pass), sprains (RICE, ice up to
  20 minutes), asthma attack (1 puff every 30 to 60 seconds up to 10 puffs, 999 if no better),
  fainting (lie down, raise legs), meningitis glass test.
- Assessment format (for the landing and facts only, not for questions): awarding body
  qualification specifications for the Level 3 Award in First Aid at Work (RQF) list practical
  observation plus multiple-choice theory papers; pass marks are set per awarding body (one
  specification lists 40 questions, 70%; others two shorter papers at about 72-73%). No body is
  named in questions or copy.

All 200 questions are original. No awarding-body sample paper or commercial bank was used.

Unconfirmed / judgement calls:
- Chemical eye irrigation is keyed "at least 20 minutes" (NHS eye injuries page: run water over
  the eye for at least 20 minutes; RCUK 2025 first aid does not give a figure). Distractors are all
  1 minute or less, so a candidate taught "at least 10 minutes" still picks the key.
- Open chest wound keyed "leave it uncovered" per current ERC/RCUK first aid (leave open or a
  non-occlusive dressing); older UK manuals taught a three-sided seal, which is not offered.
- Collar and cuff for a broken upper arm and chest thrusts for a heavily pregnant choking
  casualty follow common UK first aid manuals; RCUK 2025 web pages do not spell these out, so
  both items now say "UK first aid courses/manuals" rather than citing RCUK.
- Confirmed against RCUK 2025 (fetched 2026-10-30): call 999 for any unresponsive person, 5-6 cm,
  100-120/min, 30:2, 300 mg aspirin, tourniquet 5-7 cm not over a joint, open chest wound left
  exposed, second adrenaline after 5 min, 15-20 g glucose repeated after 15 min, cool first
  transfer second, no collars outside specialist rescuers.
