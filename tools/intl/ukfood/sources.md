# Sources for `ukfood` (Level 2 Food Safety and Hygiene for Catering, UK)

All 200 questions are original. No awarding body's exam, sample paper or course book was copied;
the sources below fixed the topic coverage, figures and legal points. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Qualification structure and assessment
- Open Awards Level 2 Award in Food Safety in Catering (RQF, 603/1290/7), qualification guide:
  https://openawards.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/documents/qualifications/qualification-guide/QualificationGuide_126965.pdf
  One mandatory unit "Food Safety in Catering" (T/615/5478): personal responsibility for food safety,
  keeping yourself clean and hygienic, keeping work areas clean and hygienic, keeping food safe.
  30 MCQ, 20 of 30 to pass; content based on the NOS for food safety in catering and FSA good practice.
- Highfield Level 2 Award in Food Safety for Catering (RQF, 603/4937/2) fact sheet (topics only):
  https://centres.highfieldqualifications.com//Assets/DownloadArea/QualificationDownloads/[15042024_1449]_L2_Award_FSC_FS.pdf
- Centre listings for the Highfield award give 20 MCQ in 45 minutes with 12 or 13 of 20 to pass
  (Monmouthshire CC, Leicester College, Trainsafe); the figure differs between listings, so the
  landing says "usually 60 to 70 percent" rather than one number.

## Temperatures and law
- FSA hot holding guidance (63°C, 2-hour single display period, then reheat, chill to 8°C or discard):
  https://www.food.gov.uk/sites/default/files/media/document/hot-holding.pdf
- Food Safety and Hygiene (England) Regulations 2013, Schedule 4 (8°C chill holding, 63°C hot holding,
  tolerance periods 2 h hot / 4 h cold), and the Welsh and NI equivalents; see also the 1995
  Temperature Control Regulations text: https://legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2200/part/II/made
- Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006, Schedule 4 (reheat to not less than 82°C):
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2006/3/schedule/4/made
- FSA Safer Food, Better Business (cooking time/temperature combinations 60°C 45 min, 65°C 10 min,
  70°C 2 min, 75°C 30 s, 80°C 6 s; cool within 90 minutes; probe checks -1 to +1°C in iced water,
  99 to 101°C in boiling water; daily fridge checks): https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/safer-food-better-business
- Food Safety Act 1990 (s.14 nature/substance/quality, s.21 due diligence, s.32 powers of entry,
  s.33 obstruction, s.35 penalties): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/16/contents
- Regulation (EC) 852/2004 as retained (Article 5 HACCP; Annex II Chapter XII training).
- FSA: register a food business at least 28 days before trading: https://www.gov.uk/food-business-registration
- FSA food hygiene rating scheme (0 to 5; display compulsory in Wales and NI) and Food Standards
  Scotland FHIS (Pass / Improvement Required): https://www.food.gov.uk/safety-hygiene/food-hygiene-rating-scheme
- FSA fitness to work guidance (exclude normally for 48 hours after diarrhoea or vomiting stop naturally; report jaundice, fever, infected skin, nose or throat, and anyone at home with D&V):
  https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/fitness-to-work
- FSA E. coli O157 control of cross-contamination guidance (complex equipment such as vacuum packers
  and slicers not shared between raw and ready-to-eat): https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/e-coli-cross-contamination-guidance

## Allergens
- FSA allergen guidance for food businesses (14 allergens, sulphites above 10 mg/kg or 10 mg/L,
  non-prepacked food written or verbal with signposting): https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/allergen-guidance-for-food-businesses
- FSA prepacked for direct sale (Natasha's Law), in force 1 October 2021: https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/introduction-to-allergen-labelling-changes-ppds

## Unconfirmed / judgement calls
- Chopping board colours are a widely used convention, not law; stems say "usually".
- "Danger zone 5°C to 63°C" is the figure taught on UK courses (not a legal definition).
- 70% pass line is ours (awarding bodies use about 60 to 67%).
