# UK LGV (lorry) theory test: sources (checked 29 Oct 2026)

## The test
- https://www.gov.uk/become-lorry-bus-driver/driver-cpc-part-1-theory-test
  Part 1a multiple choice: 100 questions in 1 hour 55 minutes, pass mark 85 of 100. Questions drawn from
  The Highway Code, Know Your Traffic Signs and The Official DVSA Guide to Driving Goods Vehicles.
  Part 1b hazard perception: 19 clips, pass mark 67 of 100 (not modelled in this bank).
  Theory certificate valid 2 years from passing the first part; both parts within 2 years.
- https://www.gov.uk/become-lorry-bus-driver/fees  Part 1a GBP 26, part 1b GBP 11.
- https://www.gov.uk/become-lorry-bus-driver  5 Driver CPC tests (1 theory, 2 case studies, 3a off-road,
  3b on-road, 4 practical demonstration); over 18 with exceptions; 35 hours periodic training every 5 years;
  renew licence every 5 years and every year from 65.
- https://www.gov.uk/driver-cpc-training  35 hours every 5 years; any National course gives a National Driver
  CPC that does not let you drive professionally in Europe.
- DVSA lorry theory topic list (the official DVSA theory test book, Driving Goods Vehicles): vehicle weights and
  dimensions; drivers' hours and rest periods; braking systems; the driver; the road; accident handling;
  vehicle condition; leaving the vehicle; vehicle loading; restricted view; documents; environmental issues;
  other road users; traffic signs (carrying passengers is PCV only).

## Rules used in questions
- Drivers' hours, assimilated EU rules: https://www.gov.uk/drivers-hours/eu-rules and
  https://www.gov.uk/guidance/drivers-hours-goods-vehicles/1-eu-and-aetr-rules-on-drivers-hours
  (9 h daily, 10 h twice a week, 56 h week, 90 h fortnight, 45 min after 4.5 h, split 15+30, breaks
  under 15 min do not count, daily rest 11 h, reduced 9 h x3, split 3+9, weekly 45 h, reduced 24 h,
  compensation in one block by end of third week, 6 x 24 h, multi-manning 9 h in 30 h, ferry
  interruption twice totalling 1 h).
- GB domestic rules: https://www.gov.uk/drivers-hours/gb-domestic-rules (10 h driving, 11 h duty).
- Working time: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/drivers-hours-goods-vehicles/annex-2-working-time-rules
  (48 h average over 17 weeks, 60 h max week, 10 h night work, no more than 6 h without a break,
  30 min for 6 to 9 h, 45 min over 9 h, breaks at least 15 min).
- Tachographs: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/drivers-hours-goods-vehicles/4-tachograph-rules
  (lost card: report and apply in 7 days, drive up to 15 days with start and end printouts; one card;
  produce today plus previous 28 days; driver card download 28 days, vehicle unit 90 days; chart max
  24 h; analogue charts back to operator in 42 days; UTC; modes).
- Speed limits: https://www.gov.uk/speed-limits (over 7.5 t: England and Wales 50 single, 60 dual,
  60 motorway; Scotland 40 single, 50 dual, 60 motorway; built-up 30 England and Scotland, 20 Wales).
- Speed limiter 90 km/h (56 mph) for goods vehicles over 3.5 t: gov.uk guide for recovery operators
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/guide-for-recovery-operations/running-a-vehicle-recovery-business-driver-and-vehicle-safety-rules
- Drink-drive limits: https://www.gov.uk/drink-drive-limit (35/80/107 England, Wales, NI; 22/50/67 Scotland).
- Eyesight: https://www.gov.uk/driving-eyesight-rules (lorry and bus: 0.8 (6/7.5) best eye, 0.1 (6/60) other).
- Load securing: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/securing-loads-on-hgvs-and-goods-vehicles/2-load-securing-the-basics
  and /3-what-to-do-if-a-load-becomes-unstable-during-a-journey (forwards full weight, sideways and rearwards
  half; covers contain, not restrain; friction low; centre line and low centre of gravity; diminishing load
  effect; shifted load: slow, stop, re-secure; fallen load: 999, do not retrieve on fast roads).
- Highway Code (https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code): rules 91 (tiredness), 123 (idling),
  126 (2-second gap, double in wet, up to ten times on ice, 4 seconds for large vehicles in tunnels),
  160 to 188 (long vehicles, 1.5 m cyclists, 2 m and 10 mph horses, advanced stop lines, box junctions,
  roundabouts, mini-roundabouts), 204 (hierarchy), 221 to 222, 226 (100 m visibility), 246 (over 7.5 t
  not on verge or pavement), 249 to 250 (parking lights), 265 (right-hand lane), 276 to 277 (triangle 45 m,
  not on motorways, motorway breakdown), 283 to 286 (incidents, dangerous goods orange plates, report within
  24 hours), 294 to 299 (railway telephones, level crossing breakdown), annex 6 (1 mm tread on large
  vehicles, tyres over 10 years not on front axles, cold pressures), annex 7 (first aid: burns 20 minutes,
  CPR 5 to 6 cm about twice a second, do not remove helmets).
- Know Your Traffic Signs (DfT) for the sign descriptions.

## Not asserted
Fines, fees other than the test fees, tachograph card validity period, exact axle-weight tables.
