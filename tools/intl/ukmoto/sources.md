# ukmoto sources (checked 29 Oct 2026)

Official, GOV.UK / DVSA:
- Motorcycle theory test overview: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-theory-test
  (two parts, both must be passed; provisional licence; 16 for moped, 17 for motorcycle)
- Multiple-choice part: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-theory-test/multiple-choice-questions
  (50 questions in 57 minutes; case study of 5 questions before 5 Nov 2026, from 5 Nov 2026
  three questions on a silent video; flag/change answers; up to 3 min break before HPT)
- Hazard perception: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-theory-test/hazard-perception-test
  (14 clips, 15 developing hazards, up to 5 points each)
- Pass mark: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-theory-test/pass-mark-and-result
  (MCQ 43/50, HPT 44/75, certificate lasts 2 years)
- Revision: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-theory-test/revision-and-practice
  (Highway Code, Know your traffic signs, Riding - the essential skills)
- Languages / support: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-theory-test/reading-difficulty-disability-or-health-condition
  (voiceover English or Welsh, BSL video, extra time with evidence)
- Fees: https://www.gov.uk/driving-test-cost (theory £23; module 1 £15.50; module 2 £75 / £88.50)
- Categories and ages: https://www.gov.uk/ride-motorcycle-moped/bike-categories-ages-and-licence-requirements
  (AM 16, A1 17 up to 125cc 11kW, A2 19 up to 35kW, A 24 direct or 21 progressive after 2 years on A2)
- CBT: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-cbt (not a test; L plates, or L/D in Wales; after CBT moped at 16,
  up to 125cc 11kW at 17; pass full test within 2 years)
- Learning to ride: https://www.gov.uk/ride-motorcycle-moped/riding-learner
- Drink-drive limit: https://www.gov.uk/drink-drive-limit (E/W/NI 35 breath, 80 blood, 107 urine;
  Scotland 22, 50, 67)
- New drivers: https://www.gov.uk/penalty-points-endorsements/new-drivers (6 points in 2 years revokes)

The Highway Code (gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code):
- Rules 83-88 motorcyclists (helmet, Sikh exemption, one pillion astride facing forward, provisional
  no pillion, visibility, reflective at night, filtering slowly)
- Rules 103-158: speed limits table (30 built-up England/Scotland, 20 Wales, 60 single, 70 dual and
  motorway); rule 126 two-second gap, double in wet, up to ten times on ice; rule 149 hand-held phones;
  rule 91 15-minute break every 2 hours; rule 92 number plate at 20 metres
- Rules 159-203: rule 163 1.5 m cyclists up to 30 mph, 2 m horses under 10 mph, 2 m pedestrians;
  rule 174 box junctions; rule 186 roundabouts; rules 191-199 crossings; H2 pedestrians at junctions
- Rules 253-273 motorways: provisional motorcycle licence holders must not use motorways; keep left;
  no overtaking on left; red X; hard shoulder emergency only
- Rules 274-287: warning triangle 45 m, never on motorway; report collision to police within 24 hours;
  insurance certificate within 7 days; dangerous goods orange plates
- Annex 3: MOT at 3 years then yearly; third-party minimum insurance; VED
- Annex 5 penalties: phone 6 points; careless 3-9; speeding 3-6; drink-drive min 12 months ban;
  12 points in 3 years = disqualification; no insurance 6-8 points
- Annex 6: motorcycle tyres 1 mm across three-quarters of breadth and continuous band; mopeds visible tread
- Annex 7 first aid: don't remove motorcyclist's helmet unless essential; CPR 5-6 cm about twice a
  second; burns cool with clean water at least 20 minutes; 999 or 112

DVSA topic list for the motorcycle theory test (Official DVSA Theory Test for Motorcyclists):
alertness, attitude, safety and your motorcycle, safety margins, hazard awareness, vulnerable road
users, other types of vehicle, road conditions and motorcycle handling, motorway rules, rules of the
road, road and traffic signs, documents, incidents accidents and emergencies, motorcycle loading.
Merged into 7 categories. All items are original; nothing copied from the licensed DVSA revision bank.
Not modelled: hazard perception clips, the case study / video questions.
