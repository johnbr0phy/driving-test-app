# UK PCV (bus and coach) theory test: sources

Checked 29 Oct 2026. Questions are original; nothing copied from the licensed DVSA revision bank.

## The test
- https://www.gov.uk/become-lorry-bus-driver/driver-cpc-part-1-theory-test
  Part 1 theory: multiple choice 100 questions, 1 h 55 min, pass 85/100. Hazard perception 19 clips, pass 67/100 (not modelled).
  Both parts within 2 years of each other; certificate valid 2 years from first pass. 3 clear working days before a retake.
  Question sources: The Highway Code, Know your traffic signs, The Official DVSA Guide to Driving Buses and Coaches.
- https://www.gov.uk/driving-test-cost  Part 1a multiple choice 26 GBP, part 1b hazard perception 11 GBP.
- https://www.gov.uk/become-lorry-bus-driver  Driver CPC parts 1 to 4; 35 hours periodic training every 5 years; licence renewal every 5 years, yearly from 65.
- DVSA theory test topic list for buses and coaches (third-party mirrors, e.g. mocktheorytest.com/highway-code/bus/, theorytest.org.uk PCV categories):
  vehicle weights and dimensions, drivers' hours and rest periods, braking systems, the driver, carrying passengers, the road,
  incidents/accidents/emergencies, vehicle condition, leaving the vehicle, restricted view, essential documents,
  environmental issues, other road users, road and traffic signs. Merged into 7 categories.

## Rules asserted
- https://www.gov.uk/drivers-hours/eu-rules  9 h daily (10 h twice a week), 56 h week, 90 h fortnight, 45 min break after 4.5 h, 11 h daily rest (9 h three times), 45 h weekly rest (24 h every other week), 6 x 24 h, coach international 12 days.
  Split break 15 + 30 and split daily rest 3 + 9 are Regulation (EC) 561/2006 arts 7 and 4 (retained/assimilated).
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/drivers-hours-passenger-vehicles/1-eu-and-aetr-rules-on-drivers-hours  regular services on routes not exceeding 50 km are exempt (GB domestic rules apply).
- https://www.gov.uk/drivers-hours/gb-domestic-rules  10 h driving, 16 h spreadover, 30 min break after 5.5 h, 10 h rest (8.5 h three times a week), 24 h off every 2 weeks.
- DVLA leaflet INF177B (assets.publishing.service.gov.uk ... digital-tachograph-driver-card-information-inf177B.pdf): report lost card within 7 days; drive up to 15 days with printouts.
- https://www.gov.uk/driving-licence-categories  D1 up to 16 passenger seats, max 8 m; D more than 8 passenger seats.
- https://www.gov.uk/speed-limits  buses/coaches/minibuses: 50 single, 60 dual, 70 motorway (60 if over 12 m); built-up 30 (20 Wales).
- https://www.gov.uk/drink-drive-limit  35 ug breath / 80 mg blood (E, W, NI); 22 / 50 Scotland.
- https://www.gov.uk/driving-eyesight-rules  bus/lorry: 6/7.5 better eye, 6/60 other, 160 degree field.
- Seat belts: https://www.think.gov.uk/law/seat-belts (passengers 14+ on coaches must wear; operators must remind by signs or announcements).
- 10-year tyre ban on front axles of buses, coaches (Feb 2021): https://movingon.blog.gov.uk/2020/12/09/ban-on-tyres-over-10-years-old-for-heavy-vehicles-and-some-minibuses/
- Highway Code (gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code): rule 265 right-hand lane, 269 hard shoulder, 126 two-second gap/wet/ice, 163 cyclists 1.5 m, 215 horses 10 mph 2 m,
  H1 hierarchy, H3 turning across cyclists, 203 reversing, 223 buses pulling out, 174 box junctions, 293/299 level crossings, 112 horn 11.30 pm to 7 am,
  123 idling, 236 fog, 274 warning triangle 45 m, 275 motorway breakdown, 286 report within 24 h, Annex 7 first aid (DR ABC, burns 10 min, CPR 100-120/min), tunnels.
- Know Your Traffic Signs (DfT): circles order, triangles warn, rectangles inform; weight, height, width limits; no buses; buses and cycles only; minimum speed; clearway; national speed limit; red route lines; school bus sign.
- Speed limiter 100 km/h for buses and coaches (Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) speed limiter rules); first annual test one year after registration.

## Uncertain / not asserted
- Tachograph record-carrying period (28 vs 56 days changed in 2024-25 for international work): no question.
- Minimum ages for D/D1 (exceptions apply): no question.
- Vehicle max lengths other than the 12 m speed-limit threshold and D1 8 m: no question.
