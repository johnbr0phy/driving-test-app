# ITIL 4 Foundation (`itil`) sources

- PeopleCert ITIL 4 Foundation exam page
  (https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/it-governance-and-service-management/ITIL-1/itil-4-foundation-2565),
  checked 2026-10-30: still on sale (exam, eLearning bundles), 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes,
  closed book, minimum score 65%. No retirement notice and no mention of Version 5 on that page.
- ITIL 4 Foundation candidate syllabus (AXELOS/PeopleCert, 2019 edition and its later minor revisions):
  learning outcomes 1-7 and their marks: key concepts 5, guiding principles 6, four dimensions 2,
  service value system 1, service value chain 2, purpose and key terms of 15 practices 7, seven practices
  in detail 17 (total 40). The marks per outcome are from the syllabus as published since 2019; the current
  PDF could not be fetched on 2026-10-30, so the split was not re-verified online.
- ITIL Foundation, ITIL 4 Edition (AXELOS, 2019): definitions used in stems and keys (service, value,
  product, service offering, output, outcome, cost, risk, utility, warranty, IT asset, event, CI, change,
  incident, problem, known error, release, value stream, process), the 7 guiding principles, 4 dimensions,
  SVS and value chain purposes, practice purposes, the continual improvement model, change types,
  problem management phases, SLA requirements. Paraphrased; no sample-paper items reused.
- ITIL (Version 5): launched by PeopleCert on 12 February 2026 (ITIL Foundation Version 5; a Bridge exam for
  ITIL 4 holders from 26 February 2026; more languages from 23 April 2026). Same Foundation format (40 questions,
  60 minutes). Keeps the guiding principles, four dimensions and 34 practices; replaces the six-activity value
  chain with an eight-activity product and service lifecycle; adds AI (6C capability model) and digital product
  material. Secondary sources only (New Horizons "ITIL 4 vs ITIL (Version 5)", 2026; training-provider pages):
  some report an ITIL 4 sunset of 31 December 2027, others say no date is announced.
- PeopleCert ITIL FAQ (https://peoplecert.org/help-and-support/faq-itil), checked 2026-10-30 by the reviewer:
  "The current plan is to sunset all ITIL 4 modules on 31 December 2027"; ITIL 4 and Version 5 "will run in
  parallel for a period"; ITIL 4 qualifications are recognized as prerequisites for higher Version 5
  certifications; Version 5 Foundation stays closed-book multiple choice; 34 practices; "AI-native". It gives no
  launch date, question count or value chain detail, so the landing says "early 2026" and attributes the
  lifecycle change to training providers. Prerequisites for ITIL 4 Foundation: none stated on PeopleCert's pages.

Blueprint per 40-item test: itilConcepts 5, itilPrinciples 6, itilSystem 5 (dimensions 2, SVS 1, value chain 2),
itilPracticePurpose 7, itilChangeImprove 8 (continual improvement, change enablement, SLM), itilSupport 9
(incident, problem, service request, service desk). Drill-only items: 80 (13/13/13/13/14/14). Total 160.
