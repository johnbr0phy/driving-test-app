No 2026 change to the test frameworks was found: the 2016 frameworks remain the published specification, and the 2026 tests were taken in May 2026.

Facts inside reading texts (written for this bank, kept to well-established facts):
- Hedgehogs: nocturnal, roll into a ball, hibernate roughly November to March, travel up to about 2 km a night, 13 cm hedgehog highway hole, no milk (lactose). Common UK wildlife-charity guidance.
- Honeybees: round and waggle dances, Karl von Frisch (Austrian), 1940s research, shared the 1973 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
- Eiffel Tower over 300 metres tall (330 m).

Format decisions: both practice tests are about 60% of the real length (time cut in proportion) because a v2 section has one scale and fixed module timers, so a full-length Test 1 and a shorter Test 2 could not share one curve. One mark per item (real items are 1 to 3 marks).
