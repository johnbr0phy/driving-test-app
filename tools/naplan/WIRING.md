## CLAUDE.md (v2 exams list, after GED)
```md
- **NAPLAN Year 9** (`/naplan`, landing `/naplan-year-9-practice-test`, `[data-theme="naplan"]` green, fully free, hub group "Australia school and work certificates"): ACARA's online NAPLAN without Writing. Four sections in the real order: Reading (`napReading`, 10 + 10 in 20 + 20 min, 5 items per text), Conventions of Language (`napSpelling` then `napGrammar`, 6 + 6 per module, 12 + 12 in 15 + 15 min), Numeracy non-calculator (`numeracyNC`, `napNonCalc`, 6 in 8 min, no calculator) and Numeracy calculator (`napNumber` 5, `napMeasure` 4, `napStats` 3 per module, 12 + 12 in 25 + 25 min); `breakAfter` on Reading and Conventions only. Tailored design modelled as SAT-style module 2 (`upper`/`lower`, threshold 0.6) in Test 1; Test 2 is a fixed form held out of the drills. Each section is scaled 300 to 780 with `curve()` (easier path capped at 600) against ACARA's 2023 Year 9 cut points (Strong from 532 to 545, Exceeding 627 to 673); the composite averages Reading, Conventions and Numeracy (the two numeracy sections weighted by length); goals 480 to 700, default 540. Bank `data/v2/naplan/questions.json` (227 items, 11 stimuli `NAP-R-xx`, ids `NAP-T1-`, `NAP-T2-`, `NAP-D-`) is built from `tools/naplan/*.py` by `python3 -I tools/naplan/build.py` (its own builder; edit the sources, never the JSON). Spelling is proofreading and choice items (the audio dictation is not modelled), Australian spelling, metric, AUD. Six drills, `drillSize` 20: reading, spelling, grammar, noncalc, number and measure (measurement plus statistics); reading, grammar and number open with shared Basics, so the dashboard is 11 steps. A Year 7 sibling would be a separate config (`naplan7`) and folder.
```
