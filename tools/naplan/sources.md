## Not confirmed / modelling choices
- The real number of items per testlet is not published; module sizes here are ours (Reading 10 + 10, Conventions 12 + 12, non-calculator 6, Numeracy 12 + 12 per sitting).
- The real test has three stages; this models two (module 1, then a harder or easier module 2 chosen at 60% accuracy). Test 2 is a fixed form.
- Scale scores are an estimate from our own curve (300 to 780), read against ACARA's cut points. The easier path is capped at 600 (inside Strong), a simplification of "students on more complex pathways are more likely to score higher".
- Spelling dictation (audio) and Writing are not modelled. Drag-and-drop is replaced by tap formats (order, highlight, cloze, matrix).
