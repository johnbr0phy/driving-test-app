## Not confirmed / modelling choices
- Real items per testlet are not published; module sizes mirror the Year 9 sibling (Reading 10 + 10, Conventions 12 + 12, non-calculator 6, Numeracy 12 + 12).
- Two stages instead of three (module 1, then a harder or easier module 2 at 60% accuracy). Test 2 is a fixed form.
- Scale scores come from our own curve (260 to 740), read against ACARA's Year 7 cut points; the easier path is capped at 560 (inside Strong).
- Spelling dictation (audio) and Writing are not modelled. Drag-and-drop is replaced by tap formats (order, highlight, cloze, matrix).
- Year 7 content kept inside the v9 Year 7 curriculum: no circles, Pythagoras, index laws beyond powers and square roots, or simple interest (those are Year 8 and 9).
