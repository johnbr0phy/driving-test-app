# Sources for `aatl2` (AAT Level 2 Certificate in Accounting)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from AAT's published qualification
specification and UK bookkeeping, costing and business law practice. No AAT practice assessment,
sample assessment, past paper, textbook or commercial question bank was copied. Checked 2026-11-03.

## Qualification and assessment format (official)
- AAT, "AAT Level 2 Certificate in Accounting":
  https://www.aat.org.uk/qualifications-and-courses/accounting/level-2-certificate-accounting
  (checked 2026-11-03). Four units with their share of the overall grade: Introduction to
  Bookkeeping 25%, Principles of Bookkeeping Controls 25%, Principles of Costing 20%, The Business
  Environment 30%. SCQF Level 6 in Scotland. "Usually takes around one year", possibly six months.
  Students register with AAT through a training provider. (Fees are on this page but are left out
  of every Tigertest page.)
- AAT, "AAT Level 2 Certificate in Accounting (Q2022) qualification specification", version 5.7,
  published September 2026:
  https://www.aat.org.uk/files/AAT-Level-2-Certificate-in-Accounting-Q2022-qualification-specification.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-03):
  - Qualification number 603/6338/1; four mandatory units, 255 guided learning hours, TQT 340.
  - Section 4.3: "Three units are assessed individually in end-of-unit assessments. This
    qualification also includes a synoptic assessment".
  - Section 9: all assessments are set and marked by AAT, computer based, time-limited, available
    on demand at approved training providers and assessment venues. Question types "may include
    multiple-choice questions, numeric gap-fill questions, or question tools that replicate
    workplace activities such as making entries in a journal. In the synoptic assessment, some
    tasks will require written extended responses."
  - Section 10: overall grade Distinction 90-100%, Merit 80-89%, Pass 70-79%, Unclassified 0-69%
    or failure to pass an assessment; "AAT maintains a pass mark of 70% across all assessments".
    Unit marks are weighted by each unit's contribution to give the qualification percentage.
  - Unit test specifications (Section 11):
    - Introduction to Bookkeeping: computer based, computer marked, 1 hour 30 minutes. Learning
      outcomes 1 to 5 at 20% each (set up bookkeeping systems; customer transactions; supplier
      transactions; receipts and payments; transactions into the ledger accounts).
    - Principles of Bookkeeping Controls: computer marked, 1 hour 30 minutes. LOs 1 to 4 at 25%
      each (control accounts; bank reconciliation; the journal; trial balances).
    - Principles of Costing: computer marked, 1 hour 30 minutes. LO1 cost recording system 30%,
      LO2 cost recording techniques 40%, LO3 actual and budgeted costs and income 20%, LO4
      spreadsheet tools and techniques 10%.
    - The Business Environment: assessed only through the synoptic assessment.
  - Section 12, synoptic test specification: computer based, partially computer and partially
    human marked, 2 hours. Assessment objectives: business types 10%, finance function and
    information 13%, CSR, ethics and sustainability 14%, bookkeeping transactions and communication
    22%, control accounts and journals 10%, contract law 7%, bookkeeping systems and data security
    10%, global business environment 14%.
  - Content used for the categories: business documents, books of prime entry, ledgers, coding,
    the accounting equation and classification, digital bookkeeping benefits and drawbacks; trade,
    bulk and prompt payment discounts (PPD recorded by credit note in the discounts allowed and
    discounts received daybooks, the only method required at this level); cash book and petty cash
    (imprest and non-imprest); receivables, payables and VAT control accounts and reconciliation;
    payment methods (cash, cheque, cards, bank draft, standing order, direct debit, BACS, CHAPS,
    Faster Payments); bank reconciliation (unpresented cheques, outstanding lodgements); the journal
    (opening entries, irrecoverable debts with VAT, payroll with income tax, employer's and
    employees' NIC, pensions and voluntary deductions); errors not disclosed by the trial balance
    (commission, omission, original entry, principle, reversal, compensating) and the suspense
    account; cost classification by element, nature, behaviour and function; FIFO, LIFO, AVCO;
    time rate, overtime premium, piecework, bonuses and guaranteed minimums; overhead absorption per
    unit, labour hour and machine hour; reorder levels and buffer inventory; cost, revenue, profit
    and investment centres; manufacturing account; fixed and flexible budgets, variances and
    percentage variances; spreadsheet formulas (=SUM(A1:A10), cell references); classifications and
    sources of law, offer, acceptance, consideration, capacity, legality, void and voidable
    contracts, discharge and remedies; supply and demand, government control of the economy, the
    principles of a tax system, exchange rates and international trade; CSR, triple bottom line,
    the five fundamental principles of ethics; business entities, limited liability, taxes by
    entity, statutory books and filings, off the shelf companies, pre-incorporation contracts,
    business names; business functions, the finance function, outsourcing, communication,
    workload planning, characteristics of useful information, data security and cybersecurity.

## UK rates and rules used in items
- VAT standard rate 20%: GOV.UK, "VAT rates", https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates (checked 2026-11-03:
  standard 20%, reduced 5%, zero 0%). Every VAT item states the 20% rate in the stem.
- Confirmation statement replaced the annual return in June 2016, filed at least once every 12
  months: GOV.UK, "File your confirmation statement with Companies House",
  https://www.gov.uk/file-your-confirmation-statement-with-companies-house (general knowledge, the
  rule has not changed).
- Bank Rate is set by the Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee:
  https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy (general knowledge).
- Data Protection Act 2018 and the UK GDPR govern personal data: https://ico.org.uk (the Data
  (Use and Access) Act 2025 amends but does not replace them).
- No income tax, NIC or pension rates, thresholds or fees are asked; payroll items give the figures.

## Not modelled
- Entering transactions into ledger accounts, daybook and journal tools, drag-and-drop and gap-fill
  tasks are reduced to four-option knowledge and calculation items.
- Spreadsheet tasks in Principles of Costing (formatting, entering formulas) beyond choosing a formula.
- The synoptic assessment's written extended responses (emails, letters) and its human-marked tasks.
- The real question counts per assessment are not published by AAT, so the per-test counts follow
  the unit weightings and learning outcome weightings above (Tigertest's split).
