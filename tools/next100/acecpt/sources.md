# Sources for `acecpt` (ACE Certified Personal Trainer exam)

All 200 questions are original, written from ACE's published Personal Trainer exam content outline
and the public guidelines it names (ACSM, DHHS Physical Activity Guidelines, USDA Dietary
Guidelines, ACOG, PAR-Q+). No ACE practice test, ACE manual review question, commercial question
bank or prep course was used. Checked 2026-11-22.

Written after `nasm` (NASM CPT), which covers much of the same science. Items use ACE's own
frameworks (ACE IFT model phases, the three-zone VT1/VT2 model, five movement patterns) where the
outline points to them, and general exercise science otherwise.

## Exam outline and format (landing, FAQ and examFacts only; never in the bank)
- ACE, "ACE Personal Trainer Exam Content Outline" (2022 role delineation study, current version):
  https://www.acefitness.org/fitness-certifications/pdfs/CPT-Exam-Content-Outline.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-22). 14 tasks in four domains: I Client Onboarding and Assessments 23%, II Program
  Design and Implementation 31%, III Program Modification and Progression 27%, IV Risk Management,
  Professional Conduct, and Ethical Business Practices 19%. Task and knowledge statements used to
  split the categories. Per 50-question test here: 11 / 16 / 14 / 9 (each weight x 50 ends in .5;
  the two largest domains round up, the two smallest round down).
- ACE, "Personal Trainer Certification" page:
  https://www.acefitness.org/get-certified/personal-trainer/ (fetched 2026-11-22). NCCA-accredited
  exam: 150 multiple-choice questions, 3 hours, score of 500 or greater to pass, in person or
  online with a live remote proctor.
- ACE, "Personal Trainer Exam Options":
  https://www.acefitness.org/fitness-certifications/exam-options/personal-trainer/ (fetched
  2026-11-22). 150 questions, closed book, live proctor, score of 500 or greater; CPR/AED required
  before scheduling; retake available 4 days after the first attempt; separate non-accredited
  open-book Personal Training Certificate exam (not modelled here). Fee is quoted only as a
  bundle price, so no fee is stated on the site.
- ACE, "Schedule Your ACE Certification Exam":
  https://www.acefitness.org/fitness-certifications/schedule-your-exam/ (fetched 2026-11-22).
  Eligibility: 18 or older, high school diploma or equivalent, current CPR/AED with a live skills
  check, government photo ID.

## Standards and guidelines used for facts
- ACE, "The New ACE Integrated Fitness Training Model: What Has Changed?" (Certified, July 2020):
  https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/certified/july-2020/7580/the-new-ace-integrated-fitness-training-reg-model-what-has-changed/
  (fetched 2026-11-22). Cardiorespiratory phases Base (below VT1), Fitness (VT1 to just below
  VT2), Performance (at and above VT2); muscular phases Functional, Movement, Load/Speed; five
  movement patterns; talk test.
- ACE Personal Trainer Manual, Chapter 1, "Role and Scope of Practice":
  https://www.acefitness.org/academy/AcademyElitePDFs/ACE_PT5th_Manual_Ch1.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-22). Scope of practice table (do not diagnose, prescribe, prescribe diets or recommend
  specific supplements, treat, counsel), allied healthcare continuum, following a clinician's
  release. Read for scope only; no item copied.
- US DHHS / ODPHP, Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 2nd edition, "Top 10 Things to Know":
  https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines/top-10-things-know
  (fetched 2026-11-22). 150 to 300 minutes moderate a week, muscle strengthening 2 or more days,
  youth 60 minutes daily.
- General standards stated in the outline (ACSM preparticipation screening and risk factor
  thresholds, ACC/AHA blood pressure categories, ACOG warning signs, Dietary Guidelines added
  sugar limit, DSHEA supplement rules, negligence elements) are written as long-settled
  textbook facts and worded without paragraph citations.

## Could not confirm, and how the copy words it
- ACE does not publish the scale range or a percentage pass mark; the landing says "a scaled
  score of 500 or greater" only. The site's 70% practice threshold is Tigertest's, not ACE's.
- The scored versus unscored item split is not on ACE's pages read; not stated anywhere.
- The exam fee is not stated (ACE quotes it only inside study-program bundles).

## Left out on purpose
- No item on exam logistics, fees, CEC renewal or credential validity.
- No exact equation-based VO2 or body-fat formula beyond 220 minus age and Karvonen.
- No brand-name products or specific supplement doses.
