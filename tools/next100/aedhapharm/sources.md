# aedhapharm sources (fetched 2026-11-23)

All 200 questions are original. They were written from the published blueprints and the primary
rules below, never from an authority's question bank, a Prometric item, a recall collection or any
prep company's or app's questions.

## Exam outline and format (authorities' own documents)
- Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, "Saudi Pharmacist Licensure Examination (SPLE)
  Examination Content Guideline" (2025)
  (https://scfhs.org.sa/sites/default/files/2025-09/SPLE%20Applicant%20Guide.docx_.pdf), fetched
  2026-11-23: computer-based, 200 questions in 2 parts of 100, 120 minutes each, 30-minute break, up
  to 10% pilot items, four options one best answer; passing score 536 on a 200 to 800 scale;
  results 2 to 6 weeks after the window; up to four attempts a year; Prometric centres locally and
  abroad. Blueprint: Basic Biomedical Sciences 10%, Pharmaceutical Sciences 35%,
  Social/Behavioral/Administrative Sciences 20%, Clinical Sciences 35% (each may vary +/-5%), with
  the subtopic competencies used for the categories here.
- Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, SPLE Applicant Guide 2024
  (https://scfhs.org.sa/sites/default/files/2024-07/SPLE%20Applicant%20Guide%202024.pdf), fetched
  2026-11-23: same format; superseded by the 2025 guideline.
- Qatar Ministry of Public Health, Department of Healthcare Professions, "National Pharmacist
  Qualifying Examination Blueprint" (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Documents/NPQUB.pdf), fetched
  2026-11-23: 150 MCQs, 3.5 hours, pass 60%; Basic Biomedical Sciences 7, Pharmaceutical Sciences 38,
  Social/Behavioral/Administrative Sciences 38, Clinical Sciences 67; references include the
  Comprehensive Pharmacy Review, the APhA Complete Review and Qatar MOPH strategy documents.
- Qatar DHP Qualifying Examination page
  (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/QualifyingExamination.aspx), fetched 2026-11-23: Pharmacist 150
  MCQs, 3 hours (the blueprint PDF says 3.5), 60% cut score, unlimited attempts, no rounding,
  Prometric.
- Dubai Health Authority, "Manual for DHA Professional Assessment (Oral/CBT)", version 1.1, effective
  08/05/2025 (https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20for%20DHA%20Professional%20Assessment-Final2025533182.pdf),
  fetched 2026-11-23: licensed professionals pass a DHA assessment (CBT and/or oral) unless exempt
  under the Unified PQR; result is Pass or Fail only, no score; three attempts, then blocked for two
  years (one further attempt possible with another authority, or a fourth with DHA by declaration);
  booked through Prometric with a Sheryan eligibility ID; result uploaded within 2 working days.
  The question count, time and pass mark for the DHA pharmacist CBT are in DHA's CBT guideline,
  which could not be loaded.
- Department of Health Abu Dhabi, "Pharmacist and Pharmacy Technician Scope of Practice",
  DOH/SOP/PPTSOP/V1/2024, effective April 2024
  (https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/E160783B819C479D90E4DF8BAA108737.ashx), fetched 2026-11-23:
  technicians work under a pharmacist and must not counsel, dispense prescription-only or controlled
  medicines or keep controlled drug registers; pharmacists vaccinate adults (18+) only when trained,
  certified and permitted by DOH in a permitted pharmacy. Used for PRA-006 and PRA-007, both worded
  as DOH Abu Dhabi rules.

## Not verified (prep sites only, conflicting)
- DHA pharmacist CBT: prep sites quote 150 MCQs, 3 hours, 60%; DOH, MOHAP, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain
  pharmacist exam figures were not found on official pages. The landing shows "Check your
  authority's guide" for them and never states a number for them.

## Blueprint used
Per 50-question test, following the SCFHS 10/35/20/35 split (Qatar's 7/38/38/67 of 150 is close):
basic biomedical 5 (10%); pharmaceutical sciences 18 (36%) as medicinal chemistry, pharmacology,
toxicology and pharmacognosy 9, pharmaceutics and pharmacokinetics 5, compounding and calculations
4; social/behavioural/administrative 10 (20%) as practice, ethics, law and management 5 and
epidemiology, pharmacovigilance, biostatistics, pharmacoeconomics and drug information 5; clinical
17 (34%) as clinical pharmacokinetics, pharmacogenomics and patient assessment 3, cardiovascular,
renal and endocrine 5, infections and immunisation 4, other therapeutics (respiratory, GI, CNS,
pain, oncology, self-care, special populations) 5.

## Clinical and scientific references for the items
Standard texts named in the SCFHS and DHP reference lists (Applied Therapeutics, DiPiro, Goodman and
Gilman, Shargel, Ansel's calculations, Lippincott reviews), plus: GINA 2024 report summary
(https://ginasthma.org/reports/), the 2020 ASHP/IDSA/PIDS/SIDP vancomycin monitoring guideline
(https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/vancomycin/), FDA metformin labelling change 2016
(https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-drug-safety-communication-fda-revises-warnings-regarding-use-diabetes-medicine-metformin-certain),
WHO definition of a pharmacovigilance signal (https://www.who.int/teams/regulation-prequalification/regulation-and-safety/pharmacovigilance),
WHO vaccine storage and freeze sensitivity guidance, CPIC guidelines for CYP2C19-clopidogrel, TPMT
and HLA-B (https://cpicpgx.org/guidelines/).

## Left out on purpose
Fees, attempt rules and exam format (landing and FAQ only, never in questions); national
controlled-drug schedules, prescription validity periods and other rules that differ between
countries; vaccine schedules by age; drug prices and pharmacoeconomic currency figures beyond
arithmetic in the stem.

## Duplicate check
Every stem and key was compared with all v1 banks in data/ and every v2 bank (word-overlap of stems
with the same key). Items repeating the Philippine pharmacist (phpharm), PhMedTech, INBDE, CCRN,
Hong Kong nursing and other banks were rewritten with a different fact or new numbers.
