# Sources: Dubai RERA real estate broker exam (`aerera`)

Every question is original, written from the primary law and the Real Estate Regulatory Agency's own
practice guide. No official sample or past question, no prep-site item and no commercial question bank
was used or reworded. All sources checked 2026-11-16.

## Official (Dubai Land Department and RERA)

- Dubai Real Estate Legislation, official English compilation published by the Dubai Land Department
  (Legal Affairs Department, 2014 edition), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://rdc.dubailand.gov.ae/Assets/img/Real_Estate_Legislation_en.pdf
  Text read from the PDF. Gave the full text of: Law No. (7) of 2013 (the Land Department), Decree No. (26)
  of 2013 (Rent Disputes Settlement Centre), Law No. (9) of 2009 and Law No. (13) of 2008 (Interim Property
  Register, Art. 11 as replaced in 2009), Law No. (33) of 2008 amending Law No. (26) of 2007 (Arts. 2, 3, 4,
  9, 13, 14, 15, 25, 26, 29, 36 replaced), Law No. (26) of 2007 (landlords and tenants), Law No. (27) of
  2007 (jointly owned property), Law No. (16) of 2007 (RERA), Law No. (8) of 2007 (escrow accounts),
  Regulation No. (3) of 2006 (areas for non-UAE nationals), Bylaw No. (85) of 2006 (Real Estate Brokers
  Register) and Law No. (7) of 2006 (real property registration). The English text says the Arabic prevails.
  Bylaw 85, Art. 6(8) names the "Real Estate Brokerage test prepared by the Department" as a condition of
  registration for a Category B broker, and Art. 6(7) the accredited training courses.
- Real Estate Brokerage Practice Guide, second edition, November 2024 (DLD/RERA/RL/LP/Manual/No.6/Vr.2),
  https://dubailand.gov.ae/media/i31iv1n0/real_estate_brokerage_practice_guide_2024.pdf
  Fetched 2026-11-16. Gave: the ten ethics of the profession; smart contracts A (seller and brokerage),
  B (buyer and brokerage) and F (seller and buyer); Trakheesi (professional practice cards, office
  registration, permits, violations); the Dubai REST app (broker data, smart contracts, green list,
  Mashrooi project check); advertising permits and the permit number on every ad; the green list rule;
  classification of offices and brokers; off-plan marketing (project licensed and registered with RERA,
  escrow account, marketing contract between developer and brokerage); the amicable settlement service for
  Contract F; registration and services trustee offices; the Dubai Real Estate Brokers Program; how to get a
  broker card. The guide lists the exam domains only as sample headings (Real Estate Mathematics, Laws and
  Legislation); its sample items were not used.
- Dubai Land Department, Real Estate Broker Card (Trakheesi),
  https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-for-issuing-a-real-estate-activity-practice-card/
  Fetched 2026-11-16. Card requirements (photo, Emirates ID, Dubai Police good conduct certificate), card
  validity tied to the trade licence, steps in Trakheesi. Fees on that page are not used.
- Dubai Land Department, Training, https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/training/ fetched 2026-11-16.
  Says exams are held separately for certain categories such as brokers; gives no format.
- Dubai Legislation Portal, Law No. (7) of 2006 Concerning Real Property Registration in the Emirate of
  Dubai, https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2006/Law%20No.%20(7)%20of%202006%20Concerning%20Real%20Property%20Registration%20in%20the%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai.html
  Fetched 2026-11-16. Matches the compilation; Article 9 was later amended by Law No. (7) of 2019, whose
  changes are not asked.

## Later Dubai laws (read 2026-11-16, Dubai Legislation Portal, dlp.dubai.gov.ae, English text; the Arabic prevails)

- Law No. (6) of 2019 Concerning Ownership of Jointly Owned Real Property in the Emirate of Dubai
  (https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2019/Law%20No.%20(6)%20of%202019%20Concerning%20Ownership%20of%20Jointly%20Owned%20Real%20Property%20in%20the%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai.html),
  fetched 2026-11-16. Art. 51 repeals Law No. (27) of 2007; Art. 49 passes Owners Associations' rights to
  Management Entities. Used for the whole Joint category: Arts. 7, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 22, 25, 28, 32, 40.
- Law No. (19) of 2020 Amending Law No. (13) of 2008 (Interim Real Property Register)
  (https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2020/Law%20No.%20(19)%20of%202020%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(13)%20of%202008%20Regulating%20the%20Interim%20Real%20Property%20Register%20in%20the%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai.pdf),
  fetched 2026-11-16, text read from the PDF. New Art. 11 replaces the 2009 text: over 80% complete the
  developer may keep the contract and all payments, auction, or terminate keeping up to 40%; 60% to 80%
  up to 40%; under 60% with work started up to 25%; work not commenced for a reason beyond his control, or
  a project cancelled by RERA, means a refund of all payments (no 30% retention).
- Law No. (4) of 2019 Concerning the Real Estate Regulatory Agency
  (https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2019/Law%20No.%20(4)%20of%202019%20Concerning%20the%20Real%20Estate%20Regulatory%20Agency.html),
  fetched 2026-11-16. Art. 10 supersedes Law No. (16) of 2007 and moves lease registration to the DLD;
  Art. 5 gives RERA the audit of jointly owned property accounts, approval of advertisement content and
  licensing and registration of real property activities.

## Exam format (not published by the owner)

Neither the DLD nor the Dubai Real Estate Institute (DREI) publishes the broker test's question count, time,
pass mark or topic weights on any page that could be read. Bylaw 85 only requires passing the test the
Department prepares; the DLD's card page lists it as part of the application, with exemptions. DREI's own
site could not be loaded. Third-party guides disagree (50 to 70 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes,
70 to 75 percent), so the landing says the format is not published and 70% is Tigertest's target.
Tigertest's split follows the Bylaw's registration and conduct rules, the practice guide's topics and the
property laws that training covers.

## Left out on purpose

Fees, commission percentages, fines and penalty amounts; rent increase caps and the RERA rent index (they
change); the 2-year rent rule of the 2007 text (replaced by Law 33 of 2008); Law 7 of 2019 changes to
Article 9; newer circulars not read; Ejari procedure details;
anything about booking or sitting the test. Rules come from the 2014 compilation, the three later laws above (which replace its Law 27/2007
Joint, Law 16/2007 RERA and Art. 11 of Law 13/2008 texts) and the 2024 guide; a rule amended after
these and not in them is not asked.
