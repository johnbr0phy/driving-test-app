# Sources: UAE SIRA security guard licensing exam (Dubai), `aesira`

All checked 5 November 2026.

## Official (law and regulation)

- Law No. (12) of 2016 Regulating the Security Industry in the Emirate of Dubai, Dubai Legislation
  Portal: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2016/Law%20No.%20(12)%20of%202016.html
  - Establishes the Security Industry Regulatory Agency (SIRA); Art. 17(a) no person may provide
    security services in Dubai without a licence; Art. 7 SIRA develops or approves training
    curricula and supervises training and testing; Art. 22 nominated SIRA employees act as law
    enforcement officers who may record violations. Penalties exist (Art. 21, as replaced by Law
    No. (3) of 2018); no fine figures are used in the bank.
- Resolution No. (1) of 2026 issuing the executive regulation of Law No. (12) of 2016 (Arabic),
  issued 10 June 2026, Dubai Official Gazette issue 783, 4 July 2026, effective on publication.
  It replaces Resolution No. (1) of 2018 (Art. 31(a)).
  https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Ar%20Reference/2026/ (file "قرار رقم (1) لسنة 2026 إصدار اللائحة التنفيذية للقانون رقم (12) لسنة 2016.pdf"), read from the PDF text.
  Used in the bank (article numbers of the 2026 regulation):
  - Art. 10: individual licence types, including security guard (monitors and protects lives and
    property), watchman (khafeer, guards residential premises for the service user), money transport
    guard and driver, security systems monitor (control room), event security organiser, bodyguard,
    armed guard (assas, trained in weapon use).
  - Art. 11: individual licence conditions: full legal capacity; at least 18; valid UAE residence for
    non-nationals; not struck off the registers; basics of Arabic or English; good conduct and no
    conviction for a felony or a crime against honour or trust; qualifications per licence type;
    passes the theoretical and practical tests SIRA approves.
  - Art. 12: application documents; SIRA decides within 3 working days; an identification card is
    issued.
  - Art. 13: individual licence valid two years, renewable; renew at least 30 days before expiry.
  - Art. 15: temporary licence up to 90 days; provider shows enrolment in an accredited training
    centre; provider responsible for the individual's violations during it.
  - Art. 16 (company duties): Preventive Systems Manual; notify incidents at once and a preliminary
    report within 24 hours; keep records 5 years; no one without a valid licence; uniform as SIRA
    approves; employment contracts to SIRA; report staff data changes, including end of employment,
    within 5 days and request licence cancellation; use only contracted, registered staff; no
    non-security tasks; written notice within two days of an employee's conviction or detention for a
    felony or crime against honour or trust; 24/7 duty officer for general guarding, cash transport,
    command and control, vehicle tracking, tourist establishments, events and bodyguards; respond
    immediately to SIRA over complaints.
  - Art. 17 (individual duties): licence terms; the Preventive Systems Manual; integrity and SIRA's
    code of ethics; confidentiality; carry and clearly display the licence on duty; accurate
    information to SIRA; professional appearance and uniform; report a lost, stolen or damaged
    licence; respond immediately to SIRA over a complaint.
  - Art. 25: public events need guarding and visitor entry and exit by a SIRA-licensed provider, and
    warning and guidance signs as SIRA approves.
- Resolution No. (1) of 2018 (English, superseded July 2026, checked only for context):
  https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2018/Resolution%20No.%20(1)%20of%202018.html

## Exam format (not published by SIRA)

- SIRA's site (https://www.sira.gov.ae/en/) does not publish the individual guard test's question
  count, time, topics or pass mark. The 2026 regulation only requires passing SIRA-approved
  theoretical and practical tests.
- Guides say: a written multiple-choice test of about 60 questions in English or Arabic after the
  SIRA-accredited basic guard course, with the pass mark commonly reported around 70% (one guide
  also reports 60% sittings and a 40-question example). Sources:
  https://open-exam-prep.com/practice/ae-sira-security-guard ,
  https://flysky-certs.pages.dev/sira-security-uae/ (guides, not official; used only as "guides say").
- Training content: Gulf News (updated 5 November 2018) says SIRA's training centre runs sessions in
  English and Arabic, with fitness training and basic police, civil defence and first aid
  operations: https://gulfnews.com/uae/crime/security-regulators-ramp-up-guard-training-1.2189445
- Tigertest's choices: four 50-question tests, 70% as Tigertest's target (labelled as such on the
  landing and in examFacts), topics weighted by Tigertest across role and conduct, SIRA law,
  access control and patrols, reporting, legal limits, emergencies, fire and first aid.

## Other law and practice

- UAE criminal procedure: anyone who witnesses an offence as it happens may take the offender to the
  nearest public authority officer (Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2022, Art. 49, per a law firm
  summary: https://www.mondaq.com/crime/1511492/understanding-arrest-procedures-under-uae-criminal-procedure-law).
  The bank states the rule without an article number.
- Emergency numbers: 999 police, 998 ambulance, 997 Civil Defence, 901 Dubai Police non-emergency,
  991 DEWA electricity emergency (long-standing UAE numbers).
- First aid figures follow current international resuscitation guidelines (compressions 100 to 120
  a minute, 5 to 6 cm, 30:2; cool burns 20 minutes under running water).
- Privacy: photographing or sharing images of people without consent can be an offence under UAE
  law (Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on combating rumours and cybercrimes); stated generally,
  with no article or penalty.
