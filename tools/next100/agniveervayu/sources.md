# Agniveer Vayu (Science subjects) sources

Checked 1 November 2026.

## Official
- Indian Air Force / CASB, "Invites online applications from unmarried Indian male and female candidates for selection test
  for Agniveervayu intake 01/2027 under Agnipath scheme" (detailed notification, 3 pages; registration 12 January to
  1 February 2026, online exam 30 and 31 March 2026). The CASB portal (https://iafrecruitment.edcil.co.in) did not serve
  the PDF directly; read in full on 1 November 2026 from the copy at
  https://karnatakahelp.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Air-Force-Agniveer-Vayu-Intake-01_2027-Detailed-Notification.pdf
  Confirmed from it:
  - Para 26: Phase I online test, objective type; questions bilingual (English and Hindi) except the English paper.
  - Para 26.1: Science subjects: total duration 60 minutes; Physics, Mathematics and English as per the 10+2 CBSE syllabus.
  - Para 26.2: Other than Science subjects: 45 minutes; English (10+2 CBSE) and Reasoning and General Awareness (RAGA).
  - Para 26.3: Science and Other than Science together: 85 minutes, all four. (Not modelled.)
  - Para 26.4: one mark for each correct answer, nil for an unattempted question, 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer.
  - Para 27: marks normalised across sessions; shortlisting for Phase II is state-wise, and cut-offs may vary by state.
  - Para 28: candidates must qualify in each paper individually (Science subjects and Other than Science subjects) with
    normalised marks.
  - Paras 33 to 36: Phase II: Physical Fitness Test (1.6 km run within 7 minutes for men, 8 minutes for women; then
    10 push-ups, 10 sit-ups and 20 squats for men, 10 sit-ups and 15 squats for women, each in a set time) and
    Adaptability Tests I and II. Para 37: Phase III medical examination.
  - Educational qualification (Science subjects): Intermediate / 10+2 with Mathematics, Physics and English, 50% aggregate
    and 50% in English; or a three-year engineering diploma or two-year vocational course with the stated marks.
  - Age (intake 01/2027): born between 1 January 2006 and 1 July 2009; upper age limit 21 on enrolment.
  The notification gives no number of questions per subject.

## Question split (guides)
- 70 questions in the Science subjects test: English 20, Physics 25, Mathematics 25, 70 marks. Reported consistently by
  guides found on 1 November 2026: Self Studies (https://www.selfstudys.com/nda/agniveer-vayu/group-x/pyqs/2025),
  Oswaal Books (https://oswaalbooks.com/pages/agniveer-exam-pattern-2026), Physics Wallah
  (https://www.pw.live/defence/exams/indian-airforce-agniveer-vayu-model-paper), Testbook
  (https://testbook.com/indian-airforce-agniveer/syllabus-exam-pattern). One Self Studies page for 2023 gives 75
  questions; the 70-question split is used and labelled "guides report" on the landing and in the config.
- Easetolearn (https://easetolearn.com/course/agniveer-vayu/exam-details) repeats the official 60 minutes, marking and
  bilingual rule.

## Content
- Physics and Mathematics follow the NCERT / CBSE Class 11 and 12 syllabus; English follows the CBSE 10+2 level
  (grammar, vocabulary, comprehension). Physical constants and formulas checked against NCERT Physics Parts I and II
  (Classes 11 and 12). Every item is original; none is copied from a past paper, an official sample or a commercial
  app. Items were checked on 1 November 2026 against every India bank in `data/v2/` (ssccgl, rrbntpc, ibpspo, ctet,
  rrbgroupd, sscgd, sscchsl, sscmts, uppolice, ibpsclerk, jnvst, sbiclerk, sbipo, ibpsrrb, biharpolice, rajpolice,
  delhipolice, mppolice, jambeng, phcse) and the batch-3 banks present at the time (nda, agniveer, upscse, rrbalp,
  rpfconst, ssccpo), and rewritten where they tested the same fact with the same numbers or the same sentence.
- Money figures, office-holders and current affairs are not used.

## Review notes (1 November 2026)
- Marking arithmetic: with +1 / -0.25 and four options, a blind guess is worth 0.25 x 1 - 0.75 x 0.25 = +0.0625 marks on
  average, so the landing and scoreNote no longer say it gains nothing.
- Phase II shortlisting is described as state-wise (para 27) without the unsupported reason about vacancy allotment.
