# AP Biology (`apbio`): sources

All 200 items and all 8 shared experiments and data tables are original. No College Board released exam,
Course and Exam Description sample question, AP Classroom item or commercial prep item was used or paraphrased.
Classic findings mentioned in explanations (semiconservative replication by density labeling, the 18O tracer
result for photosynthetic oxygen, spark-discharge experiments, keystone predators) are standard introductory
content, described in our own words, never as a quoted passage.

## Format (checked 1 November 2026)
- College Board, AP Central, "AP Biology Exam": https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-biology/exam
  - Exam 3 hours. Hybrid digital: multiple choice answered in Bluebook; free-response questions viewed in
    Bluebook and handwritten in paper booklets.
  - Section I: 60 multiple-choice questions, 1 hour 30 minutes, 50% of exam score; discrete questions and
    sets of questions with a shared stimulus, typically 4 to 5 per set.
  - Section II: 6 free-response questions (2 long, 4 short), 1 hour 30 minutes, 50%.
  - Calculators permitted; reference information (formulas) provided.
- College Board, "AP Biology Course Overview" (PDF, (c) 2026):
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-biology-course-overview.pdf
  - Eight units: Chemistry of Life; Cells; Cellular Energetics; Cell Communication and Cell Cycle; Heredity;
    Gene Expression and Regulation; Natural Selection; Ecology.
  - "A four-function, scientific, or graphing calculator is allowed on both sections of the exam."
  - Sample multiple-choice questions show four choices (A) to (D).
- College Board, "AP Biology Course and Exam Description", effective fall 2025, (c) 2025, fetched 1 November 2026:
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-biology-course-and-exam-description.pdf
  - Exam information: 60 multiple-choice questions "appearing either as individual questions or in sets of
    typically 4-5 questions each"; all six science practices assessed in the multiple choice.
  - Unit weightings on the multiple-choice section: 1 Chemistry of Life 8-11%, 2 Cells 10-13%, 3 Cellular
    Energetics 12-16%, 4 Cell Communication and Cell Cycle 10-15%, 5 Heredity 8-11%, 6 Gene Expression and
    Regulation 12-16%, 7 Natural Selection 13-20%, 8 Ecology 10-15%. (Same figures on
    https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-biology/course, checked 1 November 2026.)
  - Science practice weightings on the multiple choice: Concept Explanation 25-33%, Visual Representations
    16-24%, Questions and Methods 8-14%, Representing and Describing Data 8-14%, Statistical Tests and Data
    Analysis 8-14%, Argumentation 20-26%.
  - Topic list (fall 2025 framework): 1.1 Structure of Water and Hydrogen Bonding, 1.2 Elements of Life,
    1.3 Introduction to Macromolecules, 1.4 Carbohydrates, 1.5 Lipids, 1.6 Nucleic Acids, 1.7 Proteins;
    2.1 Cell Structure and Function, 2.2 Cell Size, 2.3 Plasma Membrane, 2.4 Membrane Permeability,
    2.5 Membrane Transport, 2.6 Facilitated Diffusion, 2.7 Tonicity and Osmoregulation, 2.8 Mechanisms of
    Transport, 2.9 Cell Compartmentalization, 2.10 Origins of Cell Compartmentalization; 3.1 Enzymes,
    3.2 Environmental Impacts on Enzyme Function, 3.3 Cellular Energy, 3.4 Photosynthesis, 3.5 Cellular
    Respiration; 4.1 Cell Communication, 4.2 Introduction to Signal Transduction, 4.3 Signal Transduction
    Pathways, 4.4 Feedback, 4.5 Cell Cycle, 4.6 Regulation of Cell Cycle; 5.1 Meiosis, 5.2 Meiosis and Genetic
    Diversity, 5.3 Mendelian Genetics, 5.4 Non-Mendelian Genetics, 5.5 Environmental Effects on Phenotype;
    6.1 DNA and RNA Structure, 6.2 DNA Replication, 6.3 Transcription and RNA Processing, 6.4 Translation,
    6.5 Regulation of Gene Expression, 6.6 Gene Expression and Cell Specialization, 6.7 Mutations,
    6.8 Biotechnology; 7.1 Introduction to Natural Selection to 7.12 Origins of Life (including Artificial
    Selection, Population Genetics, Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium, Evidence of Evolution, Common Ancestry,
    Continuing Evolution, Phylogeny, Speciation, Variations in Populations); 8.1 Responses to the Environment,
    8.2 Energy Flow Through Ecosystems (biogeochemical cycles), 8.3 Population Ecology, 8.4 Effect of Density,
    8.5 Community Ecology, 8.6 Biodiversity, 8.7 Disruptions to Ecosystems. Item `skill` labels use these names.
  - Exclusion statements followed: no molecular structures of specific carbohydrate polymers, nucleotides or
    amino acids; no specific cyclin-Cdk pairs or growth factors (the cyclin item is general); replication
    enzymes named only from DNA polymerase, ligase, RNA polymerase, helicase and topoisomerase; no details of
    genetic engineering techniques beyond what PCR and electrophoresis do; no specific behavioral or
    physiological mechanisms.
  - Appendix "AP Biology Equations and Formulas": mean, standard deviation, standard error, chi-square and its
    critical-value table (p 0.05: 3.84, 5.99, 7.81, 9.49, 11.07...; p 0.01: 6.63, 9.21, 11.34, 13.28,
    15.09...), laws of probability, Hardy-Weinberg, rate, population growth (exponential and logistic), water
    potential and solute potential (-iCRT, R = 0.0831 liter bars per mole kelvin), Simpson's diversity index,
    surface area and volume. The config's reference panel is Tigertest's own summary of these formulas, not a
    copy of the sheet's layout.
- No penalty for wrong answers: AP Students FAQ,
  https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/faqs/will-i-lose-points-if-i-answer-multiple-choice-question-incorrectly
  (as cited for AP Psychology in tools/next100/appsych/sources.md; applies to all AP exams).
- AP scores are 1 to 5; the composite-to-score conversion is set each year and not published as a fixed percent.

## Modelling choices
- Section I only, full length and pace: 60 questions in 90 minutes, one module, two practice tests,
  Test 2 held out of the drills. Calculator and a practice formula sheet in the tools drawer.
- Per test by unit: 6/7/8/7/6/8/10/8 (the midpoints of the CED ranges, rounded to sum to 60).
- Per test three shared-stimulus sets of four (Test 1: catalase and temperature, a dihybrid cross with
  chi-square, rabbit population growth; Test 2: potato cores and water potential, gene expression in liver and
  muscle, moth allele frequencies). Drill-only: a signaling-pathway mutant table and finch beak data (three each).
- Science practices are spread through the items (calculations, variables and controls, data reading, claims and
  predictions); items carry the topic, not the practice, as their `skill`.
- Score is percent correct. The free-response section is not modelled.
