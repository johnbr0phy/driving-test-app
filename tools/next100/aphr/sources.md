# Sources for `aphr` (HRCI Associate Professional in Human Resources, aPHR)

All 200 questions are original, written from the HRCI aPHR Exam Content Outline and the federal
statutes and regulations it names. No HRCI sample item, past exam item or commercial prep question
was copied or reworded, and no prep provider is named. Items were checked against the `phr` and
`shrmcp` banks so none repeats one of their questions; aPHR items are shorter, entry-level
knowledge checks. Built 2026-11-22.

## Exam format (official, HRCI)
- HRCI, aPHR certification page: https://www.hrci.org/our-programs/our-certifications/aphr
  (fetched 2026-11-22): "No HR experience is required for this knowledge-based credential." Exam
  time "1 hours and 45 minutes (plus 30 minutes administration time)"; "65 scored questions + 25
  pretest questions"; computer-based at a Pearson VUE testing center or at home or office through
  OnVUE. Functional areas: Talent Acquisition 19%, Learning & Development 15%, Compensation &
  Benefits 17%, Employee Relations 24%, Compliance & Risk Management 25%. Fees are listed;
  deliberately not used in copy.
- HRCI, aPHR Exam Content Outline (PDF, "This aPHR exam content outline took effect in 2021",
  (c) 2026 HRCI):
  https://www.hrci.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/hrci_aphr-exam-content-outline.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-22): the five functional areas with the same weights and their knowledge
  statements 01.01 to 05.06 (staffing needs and job descriptions, sourcing, screening and selection
  and biases, hiring and onboarding documents, recruiting technology and metrics; orientation,
  instructional design and ADDIE, training formats, change management, LMS and evaluation; pay
  structures and adjustments, health and disability plans, fringe benefits, retirement plans,
  payroll; mission, vision and values, HR operations, SWOT, engagement, performance management and
  discipline, complaints and investigations, diversity and inclusion; employment, union, pay and
  benefits, safety and privacy laws, risk mitigation, restructuring). "Candidates are responsible
  for knowing the HR laws and regulations that are in effect at the time of their exam."
- HRCI, Certification Policies and Procedures Handbook (PDF, "Revised October 2026"):
  https://www.hrci.org/docs/default-source/pdf-documents/cert-handbook.pdf (fetched 2026-11-22):
  item types (traditional multiple choice with 3 or 4 options, multiple response, fill in the
  blank, drag and drop, scenarios); pretest questions are randomly distributed and not scored; the
  passing score is set by the modified Angoff method; scores are scaled and "The passing score for
  each exam type is set at 500"; "A scaled score of at least 500 is needed to pass." The aPHR suits
  entry-level HR practitioners, managers outside HR and people moving into HR. HRCI publishes no
  percentage passing score.

## Blueprint (Tigertest)
19/15/17/24/25 scaled to 50 per mock test: 9.5, 7.5, 8.5, 12, 12.5. Floors sum to 48; the four
half remainders tie, and the two largest areas take the extra question: Compliance & Risk
Management 13, Talent Acquisition 10, so 10/7/8/12/13. Pools are four times each count (40, 28, 32,
48, 52). passPct 70 is Tigertest's readiness line, not an HRCI figure. Multiple-response, fill-in,
drag and drop and scenario item types are written as single-answer items.

## Federal law used (as in force 2026-11-22)
- Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (EEOC; race, color, religion, sex, national origin);
  ADA Title I (reasonable accommodation, undue hardship, essential functions); GINA (2008).
- IRCA (1986) and Form I-9 instructions (USCIS): every new hire; Section 2 within three business
  days; unfair documentary practices, 8 U.S.C. 1324b(a)(6).
- Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. 101 and 201(b) (works made for hire).
- FLSA (29 U.S.C. 201 et seq.; Wage and Hour Division): 40-hour workweek of 168 hours, time and a
  half, executive exemption duties, child labor rules for 14- and 15-year-olds.
- Fair Credit Reporting Act, 15 U.S.C. 1681b(b)(2) (written disclosure and authorization).
- COBRA (18 and 36 months, 29-month disability extension, up to 102% premium); ERISA (fiduciaries,
  summary plan description, Form 5500); IRS rules on HSAs with HDHPs, health FSA forfeiture,
  401(k) catch-up and hardship safe-harbor reasons, 457(b) plans; FICA and FUTA.
- ACA (applicable large employer of 50 or more full-time employees including equivalents;
  30 hours a week; 90-day waiting period limit).
- USERRA (five-year cumulative limit with exceptions).
- NLRA Section 7, NLRB elections; WARN Act notice recipients, 20 CFR 639.6.
- OSH Act general duty and Section 11(c); 29 CFR 1904 (Forms 300, 300A, 301; first aid versus
  medical treatment, 1904.7; five-year retention, 1904.33); Hazard Communication, 29 CFR 1910.1200;
  emergency action plans, 29 CFR 1910.38.
- Drug-Free Workplace Act of 1988 (federal contractors and grantees; policy statement, no testing
  mandate); HIPAA privacy rule; Sarbanes-Oxley Act Section 806.

## Left out on purpose
- Fees, application windows, recertification and any dollar thresholds that change by rule or each
  year (FLSA salary level, contribution limits, minimum wage amounts).
- State law (final pay timing, state leave, mini-COBRA) beyond noting that it varies.
- The exam's own format, scoring and eligibility (landing page and `lib/examFacts.ts` only).
- Guides say the HRCI scaled score runs 100 to 700; not confirmed from HRCI, not used.
