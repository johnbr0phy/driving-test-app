# AP Human Geography (`aphug`): sources

All 204 items and all 21 stimuli are original. Every data table is hypothetical (lettered countries, cities,
districts and tracts with made-up figures) and is labelled "hypothetical data" in its title; maps, landscapes and a
population pyramid are described in words, also invented. No College Board released exam, Course and Exam
Description sample question, AP Classroom item or commercial prep item was used or paraphrased. Concepts, models and
standard examples (Iceland as a nation-state, the Kurds as a stateless nation, Lesotho inside South Africa, Basque as
an isolate, maquiladoras) are common course content, written in our own words.

## Format (checked 1 November 2026)

  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-human-geography/exam

  - Section I: 60 multiple-choice questions, 1 hour, 50% of the score; "individual questions and set-based

  - Section II: 3 free-response questions, 1 hour 15 minutes, 50%; question 1 no stimulus, question 2 one stimulus,

  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-human-geography/course

## Facts used in items (standard course content, checked 1 November 2026)
- UN Convention on the Law of the Sea: territorial sea 12 nautical miles, exclusive economic zone 200 nautical miles.
- UNDP Human Development Index (health, education, gross national income per person) and Gender Inequality Index
  (reproductive health; empowerment: parliamentary seats and secondary education; labor force participation):
  https://hdr.undp.org/data-center/documentation-and-downloads
- UN Sustainable Development Goals: 17 goals adopted in 2015 with targets for 2030: https://sdgs.un.org/goals
- Africa about 30.4 million sq km, Greenland about 2.17 million sq km, so Africa is about 14 times larger.
- Natural sex ratio at birth about 105 boys per 100 girls; rule of 70 for doubling time.

## Modelling choices
- Section I only, full length and pace: 60 questions in 60 minutes, one module, two practice tests, Test 2 held out
  of the drills. The free-response section is not modelled.
- Five choices per question, as in the CED and course overview samples.
- Per test, in unit order: Unit 1 6 (10%), Units 2 to 7 9 each (15%), inside every CED range.
- One stimulus set of 3 per unit per test, so 21 of 60 items (35%) use a stimulus, inside the CED's 30% to 40%.
  Test 1: 4 tables (Units 2, 4, 6, 7) and 3 described stimuli (Units 1, 3, 5); Test 2: 3 tables (Units 1, 5, 7)
  and 4 described stimuli (Units 2 pyramid, 3, 4, 6), so roughly half quantitative, half qualitative. Images, photographs and
  real maps are not modelled; maps and landscapes are described in words.
- Drill-only items: per unit one stimulus set of 3 plus 9 stand-alone items (12), so each drill pool is Test 1 plus
  12 (18 for Unit 1, 21 for the others).
- Skill tags follow the five CED skill categories (after the review, 1 November 2026: Concepts and Processes 65 (32%), Spatial Relationships 50 (25%), Scale Analysis
  32 (16%), Source Analysis 30 (15%), Data Analysis 27 (13%) across the 204 items, each inside the CED range; per
  practice test Concepts and Processes 20 and 18 of 60, Scale Analysis 8 each).
- Score is percent correct.
- Key position spread: the build seeds each option shuffle from the stem plus a fixed salt ("d"); all-number options
  are sorted ascending and locked (9 items, key in an end slot in 4).

## Overlap check (1 November 2026)
Grepped the AP World History, AP U.S. History, AP Psychology, AP Biology, AP English Language, GED, SAT, PSAT, ACT
and Praxis banks for the same concepts and facts (Mercator, push factors, remittances, Fertile Crescent,
maquiladoras, gerrymandering, supranational, creole, Sikhism, WTO, Green Revolution, redlining, Burgess). Rewritten:
a WTO item (AP World History asks which organization sets the rules of freer trade), replaced by a free trade area
item. Kept clear of AP World History's Green Revolution item (its key is the new seeds behind population growth;
here the items are about where yields rose and the criticism of costly inputs) and of AP U.S. History's suburban
growth items (no GI Bill or mortgage item here). No AP Human Geography bank existed before this one.
Review pass (1 November 2026), against every v2 bank: rewrote the HDI item (UPSC Prelims asks what the HDI
combines; now an HDI-versus-income rank item), the ethnocentrism definition (UGC NET has it; now a cultural
relativism scenario), the shifting cultivation definition (NDA has it; now where it is practiced) and the Green
Revolution crops item (UP Police asks which crop gained most; now where it did least).
