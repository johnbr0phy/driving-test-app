# AP Italian Language and Culture (`apitalian`): sources

All 210 items and all 42 stimuli are original. Every stimulus is a practice text written in Italian for
Tigertest (articles, articles with a data table, literary excerpts and promotional material); each opens
with an "Introduzione" line, as on the real exam, that says it is an original practice text and that its
places, people, organizations and data are invented. No College Board released exam, Course and Exam
Description sample question, AP Classroom item or commercial prep item was used or paraphrased. Questions
and options are in Italian (as on the real exam); explanations are in English.

## Course framework and exam format
- College Board, "AP Italian Language and Culture Course and Exam Description", effective fall 2026
  (c) 2026: https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-italian-course-and-exam-description-effective-fall-2026.pdf
  (downloaded 2026-11-12)
  - The revised course (2026-27) has six thematic units: 1 Families and Communities, 2 Language and Culture,
    3 Art and Creativity, 4 Science and Technology, 5 Contemporary Life, 6 Global Contexts (pp. 11, 89). These
    replace the six themes of the earlier framework (Families and Communities, Personal and Public Identities,
    Beauty and Aesthetics, Science and Technology, Contemporary Life, Global Challenges); the bank uses the new units.
  - Skill categories: 1 Interpretive Communication (1.A Recognize explicit meaning: 1.A.1 supporting or relevant
    details, 1.A.2 data points, patterns and trends; 1.B Interpret meaning: 1.B.1 main idea, theme or purpose,
    1.B.2 intended audience, 1.B.3 point of view, perspective, tone or attitude, 1.B.4 organizing or rhetorical
    structures and strategies, 1.B.5 meaning of words and phrases from textual cues; 1.C Synthesize and infer
    meaning: 1.C.1 connect information within or across sources, 1.C.2 synthesize meaning to draw logical
    conclusions, 1.C.3 infer implied meaning through context), 2 Interpersonal and Presentational Communication,
    3 Cultural Understanding (pp. 11, 17-18). Item `skill` labels follow the Skill Category 1 objectives.
  - Exam overview (p. 89): about 2 hours 30 minutes. Section I free response, 3 questions, 65-70 minutes, 50%
    (Project Presentation 20%, Project Q&A 15%, Argumentative Essay 15%). Section II multiple choice,
    55 questions, 80 minutes, 50%: Part A Listening 25 questions, 25%, 40 minutes; Part B Reading 30 questions,
    25%, 40 minutes.
  - How learning is assessed (p. 90): Skill Category 1 is assessed in every multiple-choice set; Skill
    Categories 2 and 3 are not directly assessed in the multiple choice, though its content is cultural and
    interdisciplinary.
  - Section II (p. 92): 55 questions in 11 sets, each set five questions on one or two audio or written texts.
    Part B (Reading): six sets across four task types, Article, Article and Chart, Literary Text, Promotional
    Material, each 1 to 2 sets a year, always 30 questions. Sets may appear in any order.
  - Unit overviews (pp. 23, 33, 43, 55, 65, 77): recommended instructional contexts per unit (e.g. Community
    Activities, Family Relationships, Urban and Rural Communities; Customs and Celebrations, Linguistics,
    Italian in the World; Architecture, Artistic Heritage, Fashion and Design, Literature, Visual and Performing
    Arts; Innovations, Personal Technology, Science in Everyday Life, Societal Impacts; Education and Career,
    Entertainment and Media, Food and Health, Pop Culture, Sports and Leisure, Travel and Tourism; Global
    Communication, Natural World, History, Policy and Planning, Transportation). Each set's topic was chosen
    from its unit's contexts.
  - Task type table (p. 13): Article and Chart and Promotional Material are "text and image"; Literary
    Text is text. Sample multiple-choice questions (pp. 95 onward) have Italian stems and four choices (A) to (D);
    they were read for format only.

## Modelling choices
- Section II Part B (Reading) only, at full length and pace: 30 questions in 40 minutes, one module, six sets of
  five, two practice tests, Test 2 held out of the drills. Part A (Listening) and Section I (project, Q&A,
  argumentative essay) are not modelled.
- Each practice test has one set per unit, in unit order, and covers all four task types: Test 1 Article 2,
  Article and Chart 2, Literary Text 1, Promotional Material 1; Test 2 Article 1, Article and Chart 2, Literary
  Text 1, Promotional Material 2.
- Drill-only items: five sets of five per unit (25), mixing the four task types. Each unit drill draws its
  Test 1 set plus its 25 drill-only items (30 questions).
- Single answer, four options, Italian stems and options, English explanations. Stimuli run 174 to 297 words.
- Cultural facts in the texts are limited to well-known, stable ones (Epiphany on 6 January and the Befana's
  sweets and sugar coal; the proverb "L'Epifania tutte le feste porta via"; musical terms of Italian origin used
  worldwide; regional dishes culurgiones (Sardinia), canederli (Trentino), orecchiette (Puglia) and 'nduja
  (Calabria); swallows and swifts belonging to different bird families; the maturità as the final secondary
  school exam). Every statistic, place, organization and person in the texts is invented and labelled so.
- Left out: real current events, real officeholders, real statistics about Italy, prices and fees, and any
  question about the exam itself.
