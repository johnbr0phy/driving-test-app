# AP Psychology (`appsych`): sources

All 200 items and all 7 study summaries and data tables are original. No College Board released exam,
course-and-exam-description sample, AP Classroom item or commercial prep item was used or paraphrased.
Studies described by name (Asch, Milgram, Harlow, Bandura, Tolman, Kohler, Lorenz, Loftus-style
misinformation, Ebbinghaus, Selye, Schachter and Singer) are standard introductory-course content,
described in our own words.

## Format (checked 31 October 2026)
- College Board, AP Central, "AP Psychology Exam": https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-psychology/exam
  - "This is a fully digital exam" (Bluebook).
  - Section I: Multiple Choice, 75 questions, 90 minutes, 66.7% of exam score.
  - Section II: Free Response, 2 questions, 70 minutes, 33.3%: Article Analysis Question (one summarized
    peer-reviewed source, 6 parts, 7 points) and Evidence-Based Question (three sources, 3 parts, 7 points).
- College Board, "AP Psychology Course Overview" (PDF, (c) 2026):
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-psychology-course-overview.pdf
  - Exam is 2 hours 40 minutes: 75 multiple-choice and 2 free-response questions.
  - Section I "assesses all content and Practices 1, 2, and 3"; AAQ 25 minutes incl. 10-minute reading
    period; EBQ 45 minutes incl. 15-minute reading period.
  - Units: Biological Bases of Behavior; Cognition; Development and Learning; Social Psychology and
    Personality; Mental and Physical Health.
  - Sample multiple-choice question shows four choices (A) to (D).
- College Board, AP Central, "AP Psychology: Course": https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-psychology/course
  - Each unit 15% to 25% of the multiple-choice section.
  - Science practices in the multiple-choice section: Concept Application 65%, Research Methods and Design 25%,
    Data Interpretation 10%; Argumentation is assessed only in the free-response section.
- Four answer choices instead of five, and 75 instead of 100 multiple-choice questions, from the 2024-25
  revision: AP Central "AP Psychology Exam Revisions"
  (https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-psychology/revisions-2024-25/exam; returned 403 to our
  fetch, content confirmed through search results quoting the page). The course overview sample item has four choices.
- No penalty for wrong answers: AP Students FAQ, "Will I lose points if I answer a multiple-choice question
  incorrectly?": https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/faqs/will-i-lose-points-if-i-answer-multiple-choice-question-incorrectly
  (no deduction since 2011; score is the number correct).
- AP scores are reported 1 to 5; the conversion from composite to AP score is set each year and not
  published as a fixed percent. Tigertest's goals are its own targets.

Not confirmed, so not stated: the number of stimulus-based question sets on the real Section I, and any
per-unit topic weights below the 15 to 25% unit range. The 2026-27 exam date is not stated on the landing
(the exam is given each May).

## Topic list used for skills
Course framework topics as taught in the 2024 redesign: Unit 1 heredity and environment, the nervous
system, the neuron and neural firing, drugs and neural firing, the brain, sleep, sensation; Unit 2
perception, thinking and decision-making, introduction to memory, encoding, storing, retrieving, forgetting,
intelligence and achievement; Unit 3 themes and methods in development, physical development, gender and
sexual orientation, cognitive development, language development, social-emotional development, classical
conditioning, operant conditioning, social, cognitive and neurological factors in learning; Unit 4
attribution and person perception, attitude formation and change, psychology of social situations,
psychodynamic and humanistic theories, social-cognitive and trait theories, motivation, emotion; Unit 5
health psychology, positive psychology, explaining and classifying disorders, selected categories of
disorders (DSM-5-TR names), treatment.

## Modelling choices
- Section I only, full length and pace: 75 questions in 90 minutes, one module, two practice tests,
  Test 2 held out of the drills.
- Per test: 11 concept-application items per unit (55) plus 20 research-methods and data items (27% of the
  test; practices 2 and 3 are 35% of the real section, and many of our unit items also involve applying
  study findings). Each unit is about 20% of the test once its research block is counted.
- Research items are their own domain (`appsychResearch`), placed four after each unit; each test has three
  shared-stimulus sets of three (study summary or data table), drill-only has one.
- Score is percent correct. The free-response section is not modelled.

## Review against the Course and Exam Description (1 November 2026)
- College Board, "AP Psychology Course and Exam Description" (effective fall 2024, (c) 2024), fetched 1 November 2026:
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-psychology-course-and-exam-description.pdf
  - Exam overview (p. 147-148): 2 hours 40 minutes; Section I 75 multiple-choice, 66.7%, 90 minutes; Section II
    2 free-response, 33.3%, 70 minutes (AAQ 16.65%, EBQ 16.65%); each unit 15-25% of the multiple choice;
    Practice 1 Concept Application 65%, Practice 2 Research Methods and Design 25%, Practice 3 Data Interpretation 10%
    (approximate). AAQ 25 minutes with a 10-minute reading period; EBQ 45 minutes with a 15-minute reading period.
    Sample multiple-choice questions show four choices (A) to (D). All figures on the landing, config and examFacts confirmed.
  - Exclusion statements followed in review: specific names of theories of emotion are out of scope (EK 4.7.A.1),
    so the Schachter-Singer and James-Lange items were replaced with display rules and facial feedback (EK 4.7.A.1,
    4.7.B.2); the psychoanalytic theory of dreams is out of scope (EK 1.5.A.4); Maslow's hierarchy is out of scope
    (EK 4.4.B.1); only the listed neurotransmitters, hormones, sleep disorders and monocular depth cues are tested;
    disorders are limited to those in Topic 5.4. Pitch theories are named place, volley and frequency theory (EK 1.6.C.2).
  - Lewin's approach and avoidance conflicts are Topic 4.6 (motivation), not Unit 5, so that Unit 5 item was replaced
    with stress and immune suppression (EK 5.1.B.1). Fluid and crystallized intelligence are EK 3.4.A.3 (adult
    cognitive development); intelligence as g or multiple abilities is EK 2.8.A.1 (no theorist named).
