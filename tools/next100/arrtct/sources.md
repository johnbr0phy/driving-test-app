# ARRT Computed Tomography bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the content outline and the primary
references below. None is copied or reworded from ARRT material, a registry review book, a prep
app or any published question set.

## Content outline (ARRT, the exam owner)

- ARRT, Computed Tomography Examination Content Specifications, board approved July 2025,
  implementation date September 1, 2026 (V 2025.09.15), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/406ac8c6-58e8-00b3-e3c1-0c312965deb2/7e8095ab-e424-496d-a5d4-d1e951d5783b/CT_Content_Specifications_2026.pdf
  165 scored questions plus 30 unscored pilot questions. Patient Care 21 (Patient Interactions and
  Management 21); Safety 21 (Radiation Safety and Dose 21); Image Production 52 (Image Formation 30,
  Image Evaluation and Archiving 22); Procedures 71 (Head, Spine, and Musculoskeletal 25; Neck and
  Chest 22; Abdomen and Pelvis 24). SI units are primary. Every category of the bank follows the
  topics of this outline (time-out, power injection options, timing bolus and bolus tracking,
  reaction treatment, adverse event reporting, external equipment, informatics, lumbosacral spine,
  cardiac gating and TAVR, esophagram, urogram, renal stone, retrograde cystogram, colonography,
  infant and young child, surgical planning).
- ARRT news, "Updated Computed Tomography Documents" (published 2025-10-31), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.arrt.org/news/article/2025/10/31/updated-ct-documents-2025
  The revised CT documents take effect September 1, 2026; biliary removed; total scored count unchanged.
- ARRT, Computed Tomography credential page, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.arrt.org/earn-arrt-credentials/credential-options/computed-tomography
  Postprimary pathway needs a supporting credential; lists the 2026 content specifications, task
  inventory, structured education and clinical experience documents.
- ARRT news, "Updated CT Standard Setting Report" (2026-08-27), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.arrt.org/news/article/2026/08/27/updated-ct-standard-setting-report-2026
  An updated passing standard takes effect September 1, 2026; results are scaled scores.

## Format (ARRT handbook)

- ARRT, 2026 Postprimary Eligibility Pathway Handbook, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/406ac8c6-58e8-00b3-e3c1-0c312965deb2/4521cd9b-c1fe-44f7-b81c-5cd8ccc12166/arrt-postprimary-handbook.pdf

## Clinical and safety references used for facts

- ACR Manual on Contrast Media (current PDF, contrast reaction cards updated September 2026),
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/americancoldf5f-acrorgf92a-productioncb02-3650/media/ACR/Files/Clinical/Contrast-Manual/ACR-Manual-on-Contrast-Media.pdf
  Prednisone 50 mg at 13, 7 and 1 hours plus diphenhydramine 50 mg 1 hour before; methylprednisolone
  32 mg at 12 and 2 hours; metformin held 48 hours only with AKI, eGFR under 30 or arterial studies
  with renal emboli risk; eGFR under 30 as the risk threshold; breastfeeding may continue (12 to 24
  hour pause optional); 20-gauge or larger preferred for 3 mL/s or more, antecubital vein preferred,
  1 to 2 mL/s through a hand vein; stop the injection when the patient reports pain or swelling;
  cold compresses first and elevation for extravasation; surgical consult on concern for severe
  injury; only power-injectable central lines; adult IM epinephrine 0.3 mg of 1 mg/mL; contrast
  warming to 37 C lowers viscosity; dialysis schedules not changed for contrast.
- U.S. Preventive Services Task Force, Lung Cancer Screening (2021), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/lung-cancer-screening
  Annual low-dose CT, ages 50 to 80, 20 pack-years, current smokers or quit within 15 years.
- FDA, Computed Tomography (CT) page (current as of 2023-05-01), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/medical-x-ray-imaging/computed-tomography-ct
  Children need pediatric settings; suspected overexposure reported through MedWatch with protocol,
  technique and displayed CTDIvol and DLP.
- CT physics, dose metrics (CTDIvol in mGy, DLP = CTDIvol x scan length in mGy-cm, effective dose
  in mSv), pitch, reconstruction, windowing, Hounsfield values (water 0, air -1000) and artifacts
  follow standard CT physics as named in the outline; anatomy and protocol facts follow standard
  cross-sectional anatomy and CT protocol practice.

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it

- The number of correct answers needed for a 75 is set by ARRT study and not published; Tigertest's
  75% is a readiness target, said so on the landing.
- Exam and reapplication fees are never stated.

## Deliberately left out

- No image items: anatomy, findings and artifacts are described in words.
- No facility-specific protocol numbers presented as universal; timing numbers are typical values
  and stems say "typical" or "commonly".
- No exam-admin questions; no commercial prep provider named.
- Facts already asked in the ARRT Radiography bank (`arrt`) are not repeated with the same answer.
