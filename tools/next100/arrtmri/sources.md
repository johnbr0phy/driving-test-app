# ARRT Magnetic Resonance Imaging bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the content outline and the primary
references below. None is copied or reworded from ARRT material, an ASRT document, a registry
review book, a prep app or any published question set.

## Content outline (ARRT, the exam owner)

- ARRT, Magnetic Resonance Imaging Examination Content Specifications, board approved January
  2024, implementation date February 1, 2025 (V 2024.03.05), the version in force, fetched
  2026-11-22:
  https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/406ac8c6-58e8-00b3-e3c1-0c312965deb2/8a6d78b2-c8bf-4a57-b824-85a9bc5a98e5/Magnetic%20Resonance%20Imaging%20Content%20Specifications%202025.pdf
  200 scored questions plus an additional 30 unscored pilot questions (the document's footnote).
  Patient Care 16 (Patient Interactions and Management 16); Safety 21 (MRI Screening and Safety
  21); Image Production 106 (Physical Principles of Image Formation 40, Sequence Parameters and
  Options 36, Data Acquisition, Processing, and Storage 30); Procedures 57 (Neurological 25, Body
  15, Musculoskeletal 17). Every category of the bank follows the topics of this outline
  (consent and legal terms, time-out, infection control, power injector timing, linear versus
  macrocyclic gadolinium, Level 1 and Level 2 MR personnel, translational and rotational force,
  magnetohydrodynamic effect, SAR, conductive loops, gradient noise and nerve stimulation,
  cryogens, zones, quench; instrumentation, Larmor equation, relaxation, spatial localization,
  k-space, the fourteen listed artifacts, quality control; TR, TE, TI, NEX, flip angle, FOV,
  matrix, ETL, bandwidth, b-value, VENC and the options list; spin echo, FSE, IR, GRE family,
  MRA/MRV, EPI, DWI, SWI, perfusion, spectroscopy, k-space filling, FFT, Nyquist,
  postprocessing, informatics; the neurological, body and musculoskeletal studies listed).
- ARRT, Magnetic Resonance Imaging credential page, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.arrt.org/earn-arrt-credentials/credential-options/mri
  MRI can be earned through the primary pathway (an MRI educational program) or the postprimary
  pathway (a supporting credential plus structured education and clinical experience); links the
  2025 content specifications, task inventory and both handbooks.
- ARRT news, "ARRT Seeks Comments on MRI Documents" (2026-08-17), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.arrt.org/news/article/2026/08/17/arrt-seeks-comments-on-mri-documents-2026

  2026, scheduled to take effect February 1, 2028. The draft keeps 200 scored questions but
  moves the counts (Patient Care 20, Safety 40, Image Production 86, Procedures 54):
  https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/406ac8c6-58e8-00b3-e3c1-0c312965deb2/813ab724-07be-44b0-b769-30c9fdb7d303/Magnetic_Resonance_Imaging_Content_Specifications_2028.pdf
  The bank follows the 2025 document; re-weight when the 2028 version is approved.

## Format (ARRT handbooks)

- ARRT, 2026 Primary Eligibility Pathway Handbook, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/406ac8c6-58e8-00b3-e3c1-0c312965deb2/e2811114-6ed8-49d3-8dd0-1f53769aa33d/arrt-primary-handbook.pdf
  MRI (primary): 200 scored + 30 pilot = 230 items, test time 230 minutes, appointment 250
  minutes (tutorial 8, NDA 2, survey 10). Scaled scores 1 to 99; a scaled score of 75 passes.
  Three attempts within three years of the first exam window.
- ARRT, 2026 Postprimary Eligibility Pathway Handbook, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/406ac8c6-58e8-00b3-e3c1-0c312965deb2/d7b6ef38-4221-44f1-9a47-56296270b336/postprimary-eligibility-pathway-handbook.pdf
  MRI (postprimary): 200 scored + 20 pilot = 220 items, test time 210 minutes, appointment 230
  minutes. Same scaled score rule and three-attempt policy. The two handbooks give different
  pilot counts and times for MRI; the copy states both, by pathway.

## Safety and contrast references used for facts

- ACR Manual on MR Safety (2024 edition), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/americancoldf5f-acrorgf92a-productioncb02-3650/media/ACR/Files/Clinical/Radiology-Safety/Manual-on-MR-Safety.pdf
  Four zones (Zone III is the MR Controlled Access Area with the console, Zone IV the scanner
  room with projectile risk); Level 1 and Level 2 MR Personnel, Level 2 supervises Level 1 and
  admits non-MR personnel after screening; MR Medical Director, MR Safety Officer, MR Safety
  Expert; ferromagnetic detection recommended before Zones III and IV; the MR Environment is
  bounded by the 0.9 mT (9 gauss) contour, the 5 gauss (0.5 mT) line being the historic
  threshold; medical code: remove the patient from Zone IV at once to a magnetically safe place
  and start BLS; Emergency Table Stop and Emergency Power Off do not ramp the field down;
  Emergency Magnet Off (quench); pads between skin and transmit coil; tattoos and RF heating;
  pregnant patients scanned in Normal Operating Mode (whole-body SAR 2 W/kg or less) when the
  benefit justifies; pregnant staff may work in Zones III and IV but not in Zone IV during
  scanning; hearing protection.
- ACR, Changes to ACR Manual on MR Safety (2024 edition, effective February 2026), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/americancoldf5f-acrorgf92a-productioncb02-3650/media/ACR/Files/Clinical/Radiology-Safety/Changes-to-ACR-Manual-on-MR-Safety.pdf
- ACR Manual on Contrast Media, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/americancoldf5f-acrorgf92a-productioncb02-3650/media/ACR/Files/Clinical/Contrast-Manual/ACR-Manual-on-Contrast-Media.pdf
  GBCA groups for NSF (group I: gadodiamide, gadopentetate dimeglumine, gadoversetamide; group II:
  gadobenate, gadobutrol, gadoterate, gadoteridol, gadopiclenol provisional, gadoxetate); renal
  screening optional before group II agents; group I contraindicated in patients at risk for
  NSF; standard dose 0.1 mmol/kg; elective GBCA MRI scheduled close before regular dialysis; eGFR
  under 30 as the at-risk threshold; gadolinium retention seen with macrocyclic agents too, no
  confirmed adverse effect; some linear agents falsely lower serum calcium assays.
- FDA, Criteria for Significant Risk Investigations of Magnetic Resonance Diagnostic Devices
  (issued June 20, 2014), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.fda.gov/media/71385/download
  Static field above 8 T (adults, children, infants over 1 month) or 4 T (neonates); whole-body
  SAR over 4 W/kg averaged over 15 minutes, head over 3.2 W/kg averaged over 10 minutes; dB/dt
  enough to cause severe discomfort or painful nerve stimulation; peak sound over 140 dB, or
  over 99 dBA with hearing protection in place.
- MR physics (Larmor frequency of hydrogen 42.58 MHz/T, so about 63.9 MHz at 1.5 T and 127.7 MHz
  at 3 T; T1 recovery to 63% and T2 decay to 37%; scan time = TR x phase steps x NEX / ETL for
  fast spin echo; SNR proportional to voxel volume and the square root of NEX and phase steps and
  inversely to the square root of bandwidth; pixel = FOV / matrix; the null point of inversion
  recovery at TI = 0.693 x T1; fat-water shift of about 3.5 ppm, about 220 Hz at 1.5 T; in and
  out of phase TEs about 4.4 and 2.2 ms at 1.5 T) follows standard MRI physics as named in the
  outline. Every calculation is computed and asserted in the q_*.py sources.

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it

- The number of correct answers needed for a 75 is set by ARRT and not published; Tigertest's
  75% is a readiness target, said so on the landing.
- Fees are never stated.
- Facility protocols vary; protocol stems say "typical", "commonly" or "usually".

## Deliberately left out

- No image items: anatomy, findings and artifacts are described in words.
- No device-specific conditional values (those come from each device's labeling).
- No exam-admin questions; no commercial prep provider named.
- Facts already asked in the ARRT Radiography (`arrt`) and ARRT CT (`arrtct`) banks are not
  repeated with the same answer (legal terms, consent, chain of infection, extravasation,
  epinephrine dose, DICOM, time-out are asked from other angles or left out).
