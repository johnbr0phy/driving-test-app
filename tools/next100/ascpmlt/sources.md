# MLT(ASCP) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official content guideline and the
primary sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from ASCP BOC sample items, BOC study
guides, released forms, or any commercial review book, question bank or prep site.

## Outline (ASCP Board of Certification)

- ASCP BOC, Medical Laboratory Technician MLT(ASCP) and MLT(ASCPi) Examination Content Guideline
  (revised September 25, 2025, 16 pages), fetched 2026-11-15 from ASCP's own content host:
  https://ascpcontentwebsite.blob.core.windows.net/boccontent/docs/default-source/explore-credentials/content-guidelines/ascp_ascpi_mlt_content_guideline.pdf
  Examination model: 100 questions in a 2-hour 30-minute time frame, all multiple choice with one
  best answer, given as computer adaptive testing (CAT); theoretical and procedural questions.
  Content areas: Blood Banking 15-20% (blood products, blood group systems, blood group
  immunology, physiology and pathophysiology, serologic and molecular testing, transfusion
  practice); Urinalysis and Other Body Fluids 5-10%; Chemistry 20-25% (carbohydrates, lipids,
  heme derivatives, enzymes, proteins and other nitrogen-containing compounds, acid-base and blood
  gases, electrolytes, endocrinology, vitamins and nutrition, therapeutic drug monitoring,
  toxicology); Hematology 20-25% (physiology, disease states, laboratory testing, hemostasis);
  Immunology 5-10%; Microbiology 15-20% (preanalytic, bacteriology, mycobacteriology, virology,
  parasitology, mycology, postanalytic); Laboratory Operations 5-10% (quality assessment and
  troubleshooting, safety, laboratory mathematics, manual and automated methodology,
  instrumentation). Regulatory questions are based on U.S. sources (AABB, FDA, CLIA). Blood bank
  donor eligibility questions follow regulations as of June 2023. The guideline lists the
  calculations examinees must know (transferrin saturation, indirect bilirubin, Friedewald LDL and
  non-HDL, A/G ratio, timed urine, creatinine clearance, Beer's law, corrected WBC with nRBCs,
  hemocytometer counts, red cell indices, absolute counts) and publishes composite reference
  ranges, which the bank uses when it quotes a normal range.
- The ascp.org pages (About the Examination, procedures booklet, statistical reports) returned 403
  to automated requests and were not read in full. A search extract of the BOC's own "About the
  Examination" page (https://www.ascp.org/boc/earn-a-credential/examination-process/about-the-examination,
  searched 2026-11-15) gives: "The ASCP BOC uses a scale of 100 to 999 with a minimum passing
  score of 400." The copy states that figure but notes it was read from the BOC page's summary.

## Law, regulation and standards used in questions

- FDA, 21 CFR Part 630 (donor eligibility), eCFR full text as of 2026-11-01, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-630
  Temperature not above 37.5 C; pulse regular, 50 to 100; hemoglobin 12.5 g/dL (female) and
  13.0 g/dL (male) for allogeneic donors, 11.0 g/dL for autologous donors; whole blood no more
  than once in 8 weeks, two-unit red cell apheresis once in 16 weeks.
- FDA, 21 CFR Part 640 (additional standards for human blood and blood products), eCFR as of
  2026-11-01, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-640
  Red cells at 1 to 6 C; platelets at 20 to 24 C with continuous gentle agitation, pH not less than
  6.2, at least 5.5 x 10^10 platelets per unit in at least 75% of units tested; plasma frozen and
  stored at -18 C or colder.
- CMS, CLIA regulations, 42 CFR Part 493, eCFR as of 2026-11-01, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-493
  493.1256 (two control materials of different concentrations at least once each day patient
  specimens are assayed); 493.1255 (calibration verification at least once every 6 months);
  493.1271 (ABO by forward and reverse grouping, storage temperature alarms, transfusion reaction
  investigation); proficiency testing subpart (80% per event for most analytes; unsatisfactory on
  two consecutive or two of three events is unsuccessful).
- CDC, Diabetes Testing, fetched 2026-11-15: https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/diabetes-testing/index.html
  A1C below 5.7% normal, 5.7 to 6.4% prediabetes, 6.5% or higher diabetes; 2-hour glucose 200 mg/dL
  or higher diabetes.
- CDC, Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories, 6th edition, page fetched
  2026-11-15: https://www.cdc.gov/labs/bmbl/index.html (biosafety levels and cabinets).
- CDC, Laboratory Response Network, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/laboratory-response-network/php/about/index.html (the network of labs for
  biological threats; the sentinel "rule out and refer" role is from the content guideline's
  item on the role of the regional laboratory and the LRN).
- OSHA, Hazard Communication Standard, 29 CFR 1910.1200:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200, fetched 2026-11-15
  (an SDS for each hazardous chemical, with hazard identification, first aid and exposure
  controls/personal protection sections).
- Standard laboratory science facts on the guideline's topics (blood group serology, urinalysis
  and body fluid findings, chemistry methods and calculations, hematology morphology and flow
  cytometry markers, coagulation testing, immunology and infectious disease serology,
  microbiology identification and resistance mechanisms). Every calculation was recomputed when
  written; the working is in each explanation. Paid standards (AABB Standards, CLSI M100, H21)
  were not read, so the bank gives no AABB storage or CLSI breakpoint figures beyond the FDA and
  CLIA text above.

## Bank decisions

- The real exam is computer adaptive (100 items). Tigertest models it as four fixed 50-question
  forms, and the landing says so.
- Per 50-question test, inside the guideline's ranges: Blood Banking 9 (18%: products and
  transfusion 4, groups and serology 5), Urinalysis and Other Body Fluids 4 (8%), Chemistry 11
  (22%: general 6, acid-base, electrolytes, endocrine, TDM and toxicology 5), Hematology 11 (22%:
  cells and testing 7, hemostasis 4), Immunology 4 (8%), Microbiology 8 (16%), Laboratory
  Operations 3 (6%). The splits inside blood banking, chemistry and hematology are Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target, not the BOC standard.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees; no state rules; no provider named.
- Duplicates: written against data/philippines-medtech-questions.json (the closest bank, the same
  seven subjects), data/ascp-pbt-questions.json, data/rpt-questions.json,
  data/phlebotomy-questions.json and data/htl-questions.json, and scanned against every bank in
  data/ for the same key with an overlapping stem. Matches in the lab family (needlestick first
  aid, hCG detection, a corrected-calcium sum with the same answer) were rewritten. The sibling
  ascpmla bank was not on disk when this bank was finished, so the integrator should scan the two
  against each other.
