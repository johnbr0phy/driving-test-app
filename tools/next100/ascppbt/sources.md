# PBT(ASCP) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official content guideline and the
primary sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from ASCP BOC sample items, BOC study
guides, released forms, or any commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (ASCP Board of Certification)

- ASCP BOC, Phlebotomy Technician PBT(ASCP) and PBT(ASCPi) Examination Content Guideline
  (revised September 25, 2025, 3 pages), fetched 2026-11-15 from ASCP's own content host:
  https://ascpcontentwebsite.blob.core.windows.net/boccontent/docs/default-source/explore-credentials/content-guidelines/ascp_ascpi_pbt_content_guideline.pdf?sfvrsn=aa1319c7_1
  Examination model: 80 multiple-choice questions with one best answer in a 2-hour time frame,
  given as computer adaptive testing (CAT); theoretical and procedural questions. Content areas:
  I. Circulatory System 5-10% (heart, arteries, veins, capillaries; types of blood, plasma,
  serum, cellular elements; terminology); II. Specimen Collection 45-50% (orders; patient
  communication, age-specific and special needs; identification; assessment and preparation;
  site selection; techniques; common tests; order of draw, venous and capillary; complications
  such as fainting, edema, hematoma, IV, mastectomy; equipment); III. Specimen Handling,
  Transport, and Processing 15-20% (acceptability, add-on tests, chain of custody, alcohol,
  forensic, newborn screening; accessioning and labeling; centrifugation and aliquoting;
  temperature, light, time, pneumatic tube); IV. Waived and Point-of-Care Testing 5-10%
  (urinalysis, hemoglobin and hematocrit, PT/INR, glucose, kit tests; instrument operation,
  troubleshooting, maintenance, internal and external QC); V. Non-Blood Specimens 5-10% (urine,
  stool, sputum, throat and nasopharyngeal swabs, breath testing for H. pylori and lactose,
  iontophoresis for sweat chloride, chain of custody; processing); VI. Laboratory Operations
  15-20% (QA, QI, proficiency testing and competency, interpersonal relations, ethics, laboratory
  regulations including ADA, CDC, CLIA, CMS, CAP, CLSI, TJC, DOT, IATA, ICD coding, ABN and test
  complexity, and safety regulations: chemical, fire, radiation and infection control). Regulatory
  questions are based on U.S. sources.
- The ascp.org pages (credential page, reading list, procedures booklet) returned 403 to
  automated requests and were not read. The ASCP BOC passing standard is therefore not confirmed
  from the owner here: guides say scores are scaled from 100 to 999 with 400 to pass, and the
  copy labels that as "guides say".

## Practice standards and law used in questions

- WHO guidelines on drawing blood: best practices in phlebotomy (2010), publication page fetched
  2026-11-15: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241599221
  (PDF downloaded the same day from the WHO IRIS link on that page.) Used for: disinfecting 2 cm
  or more and re-cleaning a touched site, entering the vein swiftly, a blood pressure cuff at 40
  to 60 mm Hg, one pair of gloves per patient, no latex glove as a tourniquet, tube table (ammonia
  with either heparin, fluoride preserving glucose up to five days), winged set and needle
  lengths, small low-vacuum tubes and 23 versus 25 gauge in children, puncturing 3 to 5 mm distal
  to the vein, immobilizing children, lancets not blades, 0.85 mm lancets for premature neonates,
  capillary order (hematology, then chemistry, then blood bank), neonatal finger punctures and
  nerve damage, vasovagal signs and management, arterial puncture with a pressure bandage and
  radial pulse check, arterial samples on crushed ice, glucometers and hepatitis B outbreaks,
  patient position (supine if possible).
- WHO, Guidance on regulations for the transport of infectious substances 2025-2026 (applicable
  from 1 October 2025), publication page fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240120822
  (PDF downloaded the same day.) Category A definition and UN 2814 / UN 2900; Category B and
  UN 3373; exempt human specimens in basic triple packaging marked "Exempt human specimen";
  primary receptacle, leakproof secondary container, outer packaging; ICAO Technical
  Instructions and IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations for air transport.
- CLSI, Order of Blood Draw: Tubes and Additives (19 March 2019), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://clsi.org/about/blog/order-of-blood-draw-tubes-and-additives/
  Order of draw and closure colors (red-speckled serum, pearl EDTA); only blood culture, glass
  non-additive or plastic serum tubes without activator before coagulation tubes.
- CDC, Sequence for Putting On and Removing PPE (poster CS250672-E), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/Toolkits-PPE-Sequence-P.pdf
  Gown, mask or respirator, goggles or face shield, gloves over the gown cuffs; removal example 1:
  gloves, goggles or face shield, gown, mask, with a respirator removed after leaving the room.
- CDC, Clinical Safety: Hand Hygiene for Healthcare Workers, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/index.html
  Alcohol hand rub until dry, around 20 seconds.
- CDC, Newborn Screening, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/newborn-screening/about/index.html
  Heel blood on a filter paper card goes to the state's newborn screening lab.
- MedlinePlus, Sweat Test for Cystic Fibrosis, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/sweat-test-for-cystic-fibrosis/
  Pilocarpine driven in by a weak current, sweat collected for 30 minutes, no creams or lotions
  for 24 hours, 60 mmol/L or more means CF is likely.
- MedlinePlus, Helicobacter Pylori (H. pylori) Tests, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/helicobacter-pylori-h-pylori-tests/
  Baseline breath sample, urea with special carbon atoms, second sample.
- NIDDK, Diagnosis of Lactose Intolerance, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/lactose-intolerance/diagnosis
  Breath hydrogen measured every 30 minutes after a lactose drink.
- CMS, Medicare Advance Written Notices of Non-coverage (MLN006266):
  https://www.cms.gov/files/document/mln006266-medicare-advance-written-notices-non-coverage.pdf (fetched 2026-11-15)
  The ABN is given before the patient receives the possibly non-covered service.
- OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
  OPIM definition; shearing or breaking needles prohibited; sharps containers closable, puncture
  resistant and leakproof; sharps injury log with device type and brand; annual exposure control
  plan review with consideration of safer devices.
- ADA.gov, Introduction to the ADA, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ada.gov/topics/intro-to-ada/
  Businesses open to the public, including health care providers, must give people with
  disabilities equal access.
- Standard textbook facts on the ASCP BOC reading list topics (circulatory anatomy and blood
  cells; common tests and their tubes; urinalysis pads; chain of infection; fire classes;
  incident reports and turnaround time). CLSI GP41, GP42 and GP44 are paid and were not read.

## Bank decisions

- The real exam is computer adaptive (80 items, difficulty set by the candidate's answers). Tigertest
  models it as four fixed 50-question forms, and the landing says so.
- Per 50-question test, inside the guideline's ranges: Circulatory System 3 (6%), Specimen
  Collection 23 (46%: orders, identification and patient preparation 6, site selection,
  technique and common tests 6, order of draw and equipment 6, complications 5), Specimen
  Handling 8 (16%), Waived and POCT 4 (8%), Non-Blood Specimens 4 (8%), Laboratory Operations 8
  (16%). The split inside Specimen Collection is Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target, not the BOC standard.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees; no state rules; no provider named.
- Duplicates: written after the RPT (AMT) bank and against data/phlebotomy-questions.json (NHA CPT),
  data/rpt-questions.json, data/rma-questions.json, data/cma-questions.json,
  data/ccma-questions.json and data/cet-questions.json, scanned for repeated fact-and-answer
  pairs; matches were rewritten.
