# Sources: ASE Automobile and Light Truck certification tests A1-A8 (ase)

Every question is original. No ASE sample question (the study guide prints some after each task
list), no Official ASE Practice Test item, no Motor Age, Delmar/Cengage or other review-manual item,
no prep-site, flashcard or forum question was used for writing or copied, not even reworded. Items
were written from ASE's task lists and the primary rules below.

What this exam is: one Tigertest exam covering the eight Automobile and Light Truck tests needed
for ASE Master Automobile Technician status (A1 to A8). A9 Light Vehicle Diesel Engines is in the
same study guide but is not required for Master status and is not modelled.

## Outline (blueprint)

- ASE, The Official ASE Study Guide, ASE Automobile Tests (A1-A9), 2026 edition ("© ASE 2026",
  "Automobile Study Guide 2026 v2"), fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://ase.com/wp-content/uploads/ASE_Automobile_Study_Guide.pdf
  Test specifications (content areas with scored question counts) and the full task list for each
  test. Used for every category and for the split of each 40-question pool:
  - A1 Engine Repair, 45 scored: general engine diagnosis 18, cylinder head and valve train 6,
    engine block 4, lubrication and cooling 8, fuel/electrical/ignition/air induction/exhaust 9.
  - A2 Automatic Transmission/Transaxle, 45: diagnosis 26 (general, mechanical/hydraulic,
    electronic), in-vehicle maintenance and repair 12, removal, inspection and installation 7.
  - A3 Manual Drive Train and Axles, 40: clutch 6, transmission 7, transaxle 7, drive shaft,
    half-shaft and U/CV joints 5, drive axle 7, four-wheel/all-wheel drive 8.
  - A4 Suspension and Steering, 40: steering 12, suspension 12, wheel alignment 11, wheels and tires 5.
  - A5 Brakes, 45: hydraulic, power assist and parking brake 19, drum 5, disc 11, electronic brake
    control (ABS, TCS, ESC) 10.
  - A6 Electrical/Electronic Systems, 50: general diagnosis 13, battery and starting 9, charging 5,
    lighting 6, body electrical 17.
  - A7 Heating and Air Conditioning, 50: HVAC and engine cooling service and diagnosis 21,
    refrigeration system components 10, operating systems and related controls 19 (the guide's
    table prints "Total 40" but its rows sum to 50, and the test page says 50 scored).
  - A8 Engine Performance, 50: general diagnosis 12, ignition 8, fuel, air induction and exhaust 9,
    emissions controls 8 (PCV 1, EGR 2, AIR and catalyst 2, EVAP 3), computerized engine controls 13.
  Tigertest pool of 40 per test, split in the same proportions (A1 16/5/4/7/8, A2 23/11/6,
  A3 6/7/7/5/7/8, A4 12/12/11/5, A5 17/4/10/9, A6 10/7/4/5/14, A7 17/8/15, A8 10/6/7/6/11).
- ASE, A Series (A1-A9) test page, fetched 2026-11-14: https://ase.com/tests/a1-a9/
  Scored questions and times per test (A1 45/75 min, A2 45/75, A3 40/60, A4 40/60, A5 45/75,
  A6 50/90, A7 50/75, A8 50/75), 10 unscored research questions on every certification test,
  recertification lengths, A1 to A8 required for Master Automobile Technician, retest every five
  years. Used for the per-test blueprint weights (rounded from 45/45/40/40/45/50/50/50).

## Format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

## Law, standards and primary references (bank content)

- 49 CFR 571.116, FMVSS 116 Motor vehicle brake fluids (Cornell LII copy of the CFR), fetched
  2026-11-14: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/571.116
  Minimum dry and wet equilibrium reflux boiling points (DOT 3 205/140 °C, DOT 4 230/155 °C,
  DOT 5 260/180 °C); DOT 5 is silicone base (SBBF) and purple; DOT 5.1 is non-silicone; container
  warning to keep the container clean and tightly closed to prevent absorption of moisture.
- 49 CFR 571.138, FMVSS 138 Tire pressure monitoring systems, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/571.138
  Warning at 25 percent below the recommended cold inflation pressure, within 20 minutes; a
  combined telltale flashes 60 to 90 seconds and then stays on for a TPMS malfunction.
- 49 CFR 571.126, FMVSS 126 Electronic stability control systems, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/571.126
  ESC brakes wheels individually to create a correcting yaw moment, uses yaw rate and steering
  input, can reduce engine torque, returns to its default mode at each ignition cycle.
- US EPA, Regulatory Requirements for MVAC System Servicing, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.epa.gov/mvac/regulatory-requirements-mvac-system-servicing
  Section 609 certification to service MVAC for consideration, certified recovery equipment,
  venting prohibited (R-744 exempt from the venting rule only), recovered refrigerant recycled
  or reclaimed before reuse, records kept 3 years.
- US EPA, Section 609 Technician Training and Certification Programs, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.epa.gov/mvac/section-609-technician-training-and-certification-programs
- US EPA, MVAC refrigerant transition and environmental impacts, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.epa.gov/mvac/refrigerant-transition-environmental-impacts
  CFC-12 ozone depleting; HFC-134a in all new US vehicles with A/C by model year 1995; HFO-1234yf
  mildly flammable (A2L), used in most new light-duty vehicles, SAE J639 unique fittings and labels.
- California Air Resources Board, On-Board Diagnostic II (OBD II) Systems fact sheet, fetched
  2026-11-14: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/fact-sheets/board-diagnostic-ii-obd-ii-systems-fact-sheet
  MIL steady for a detected fault, blinking for a fault that can damage the catalytic converter,
  readiness indicators incomplete after a repair or battery disconnect, OBD II on 1996 and newer
  gasoline cars and light trucks.
- Engineering fundamentals that every task list assumes (Ohm's law, series and parallel circuits,
  voltage drop, gear and drive ratios, pressure and vacuum readings, compression and leak-down
  interpretation, refrigerant pressure-temperature behaviour, alignment angles) are textbook
  physics and shop practice; every number in a calculation item is worked in its explanation.
  Where a figure is set by the manufacturer (torque, clearances, pressures, fluid type) the item
  says "the manufacturer's specification" and never gives a number as universal.

## Could not load or confirm

- SAE standards (J2788, J2843, J2099 and the like) are paywalled and were not read; items name
  only SAE J639 and J2844 as the EPA page does, and otherwise say "certified recovery equipment".
- ASE publishes no passing score on the test page, the FAQ or the Need to Know guide. The 70%
  line is Tigertest's readiness target and the landing says so.
- The guide's A7 table total reads 40 while its rows and the test page give 50; 50 is used.

## Deliberately left out

- Fees, registration windows, retake waits and any question about the test itself.
- A9 Light Vehicle Diesel Engines, G1, L1 to L4 and the other ASE series.
- Manufacturer-specific numbers (torque values, clearances, pressures) stated as universal.
- The Technician A / Technician B and "Both A and B" format the real tests use: Tigertest items
  are single-answer, because options are shuffled on screen.
