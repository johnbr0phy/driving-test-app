# Sources: ASE G1 Auto Maintenance and Light Repair (aseg1)

Every question is original. No ASE sample question (the G1 study guide prints some after the task
list; they were not read for writing), no Official ASE Practice Test item, no review-manual,
prep-site, flashcard or forum question was used for writing or copied, not even reworded. Items
were written from ASE's G1 task list and the primary rules below, and checked against the
Tigertest ASE A1-A8 bank (`data/ase-questions.json`) and the Red Seal automotive bank so no fact
and answer pair repeats.

## Outline (blueprint)

- ASE, The Official ASE Study Guide, Automobile Maintenance and Light Repair (G1) Test ("© ASE
  2026"), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://ase.com/wp-content/uploads/ASE_MaintenanceLtRepair_Study_Guide.pdf
  Test specification (content areas with scored question counts) and the full task list:
  A. Engine Systems 9 (16%), B. Automatic Transmission/Transaxle 4 (7%), C. Manual Drive Train
  and Axles 6 (11%), D. Suspension and Steering 13 (24%), E. Brakes 11 (20%), F. Electrical 8
  (15%), G. Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning 4 (7%); total 50 scored. G1 is 55 total /
  45 scored per the guide's "How long" table, which conflicts with its own 50-question
  specification and the test page (55 scored plus 10 unscored). The content area counts (50) are
  used for the blueprint: each Tigertest practice test has exactly those counts, and each pool is
  four times them (220 in all). Also: a Spanish/English split-screen rendering, and students in
  ASE-accredited programs who pass a set of 8 entry-level tests can earn G1 without the test.
- ASE, G1 Auto Maintenance and Light Repair test page, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://ase.com/tests/g1/
  55 scored questions plus 10 unscored research questions, 90 minutes; G1R recertification 28
  questions in 45 minutes; remote testing available; work experience required; seven content
  areas named.

## Law, standards and primary references (bank content)

- 49 CFR 571.110, FMVSS 110 Tire selection and rims (Cornell LII copy of the CFR), fetched
  2026-11-23: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/571.110
  Tire and loading information placard on the driver's side B-pillar (or the listed fallbacks),
  showing vehicle capacity weight, seating, recommended cold tire inflation pressure for front,
  rear and spare, and original tire size.
- 49 CFR 574.5, Tire identification requirements, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/574.5
  TIN of 13 symbols; plant code first; the last four digits are the week and year of manufacture
  (example "0109" is week 1 of 2009).
- 49 CFR 571.139, FMVSS 139 New pneumatic radial tires for light vehicles, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/571.139
  At least six treadwear indicators that show wear to 1/16 inch of tread; sidewall shows size,
  maximum permissible inflation pressure, maximum load rating; TIN on the outboard sidewall.
- 49 CFR 575.104, Uniform tire quality grading standards, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/575.104
  Treadwear grade is comparative (150 lasts about 1.5 times as long as 100); traction AA, A, B, C
  is straight-ahead wet braking only; temperature A, B, C, with C the FMVSS 109 minimum.
- 49 CFR 571.138, FMVSS 138 Tire pressure monitoring systems (read for the ase build), fetched
  2026-11-14 and rechecked 2026-11-23: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/571.138
  A combined telltale that flashes for 60 to 90 seconds at start-up and then stays on shows a
  TPMS malfunction. (The 25 percent low-pressure rule is already asked in the ase bank and is
  not repeated here.)
- US EPA, Guidance on EPA's Certification Requirements for Heavy-Duty Diesel Engines Using
  Selective Catalytic Reduction Technology, Federal Register Vol. 76 No. 109, June 7, 2011,
  fetched 2026-11-23: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2011-06-07/html/2011-13851.htm
  DEF is the liquid reductant stored in its own onboard tank and injected into the exhaust ahead
  of the SCR catalyst to reduce NOx; escalating warnings as the tank nears empty, then
  inducements such as a torque derate and a speed limit when it is empty. The 32.5 percent urea
  (ISO 22241-1) figure was seen only in a search extract of an EPA document and is not asked.
- US EPA, Regulatory Requirements for MVAC System Servicing (read for the ase build), fetched
  2026-11-14 and rechecked 2026-11-23:
  https://www.epa.gov/mvac/regulatory-requirements-mvac-system-servicing
  Only Section 609 certified technicians with certified equipment recover and recharge; venting
  is prohibited. G1 items only ask how the refrigerant type is identified and why it is
  recovered before a system is opened, not the certification and record rules the ase bank asks.
- Engineering fundamentals that every task list assumes (Ohm's law, voltage drop, battery state
  of charge, tire wear patterns, alignment angles) are textbook physics and shop practice; every
  number in a calculation item is worked in its explanation. Where a figure is set by the
  manufacturer (torque, fluid type, pressures, intervals, rotation patterns, refill and bleed
  procedures) the item says "the manufacturer's specification" or "service information" and
  never gives a number as universal.

## Could not load or confirm

- nhtsa.gov tire pages returned 403 to automated fetching; tire facts come from the CFR text above.
- USTMA tire repair procedures are not public in full; repair items ask only the principles every
  tire maker states (repair from the inside with a plug and patch, tread area only), with no hole
  size limit.
- ASE publishes no passing score; 70% is Tigertest's readiness line and the landing says so.
- The study guide's "55 total/45 scored" line conflicts with its specification (50) and the test
  page (55 scored); the landing gives the test page's 55 scored plus 10 unscored.

## Deliberately left out

- Fees, registration windows, retake waits and any question about the test itself.
- Manufacturer-specific numbers (torque values, pressures, intervals) stated as universal.
- State inspection rules (minimum legal tread depth, window tint, emissions testing).
- Technician A / Technician B items: Tigertest items are single answer, because options are
  shuffled on screen.
