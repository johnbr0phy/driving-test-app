# ASWB Masters bank sources

- ASWB, "ASWB Examination Guidebook" (2026 Social Work Licensing Exam Guidebook, footer "08/2026";
  the cover note says it reflects exams administered beginning August 3, 2026). Checked 2026-11-02 at
  https://www.aswb.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-Social-Work-Licensing-Exam-Guidebook-5.4.2026.pdf .
  Read directly (PDF text):
  - Exam format: two sections of 61 multiple-choice questions (122 in all); 12 are unscored pretest
    questions mixed in, so 110 count toward the score. Four hours on a Pearson computer, with free
    movement, flagging and answer changes within each section; an optional 10-minute scheduled break.
  - "The exam may contain both three- and four-option questions." No "all of the above", "none of the
    above" or "both A and B" options.
  - Scoring: pass/fail, reported with the number of questions needed to pass and the number answered
    correctly. The number needed to pass is statistically adjusted for each exam form, so no fixed
    percentage is published. A failed score report breaks results down by content area.
  - Masters content outline (three content areas, each with competencies and applied knowledge
    statements): I. Values and Ethics 35% (IA Ethical principles and responsibilities, IB Ethical
    service delivery, IC Diversity and social justice); II. Assessment and Planning 33% (IIA
    Assessment concepts, IIB Assessment methods and techniques, IIC Assessment practices);
    III. Intervention and Practice 32% (IIIA Practice concepts, IIIB Intervention methods and
    techniques, IIIC Practice evaluation and research, IIID Supervision and administration).
  - The 2026 blueprints come from the 2024 Analysis of the Practice of Social Work.
  - Cognitive levels: recall, application and reasoning; the Masters exam has more application and
    reasoning items than the Bachelors.
  - Retake wait of 90 days and the registration fee are in the guidebook but deliberately not used
    (not asked in the bank, and the fee is not quoted on the landing).
- ASWB, "Blueprints for the 2026 competence assessments reflect significant structural modifications,
  minor content changes", https://www.aswb.org/2026-blueprints/ (found 2026-11-02; confirms the move
  from four to three content areas).
- Prep-company blogs (not used for facts, only to cross-check): the previous Masters exam had 170
  questions (150 scored) in four content areas; guides describe the August 2026 change as 170 to 122.
- Content references used for item facts: NASW Code of Ethics (2021 revision, including the self-care
  language in the purpose section); HIPAA Privacy Rule (treatment, payment and operations; minimum
  necessary; right of access; psychotherapy notes); 42 CFR Part 2; FERPA; ADA effective
  communication; Tarasoff v. Regents (duty to protect, varies by state); ASFA (12-month permanency
  hearing, 15 of 22 months); ICWA placement preferences; DSM-5-TR (major depressive episode 2 weeks,
  persistent depressive disorder 2 years for adults, brief psychotic disorder under 1 month,
  prolonged grief disorder added in 2022); Dolgoff, Harrington and Loewenberg's Ethical Principles
  Screen; Kadushin's supervision functions; Tuckman; Erikson; Piaget; Berry's acculturation model is
  not used (it is in the NCE bank); Sue et al. (2007) microaggression taxonomy; Rothman's community
  practice models; the stages of change; Medicare hospice eligibility (prognosis of 6 months or less).

Tigertest's pass line of 70% is a readiness target, not the official pass mark.

Lanes: the NCE bank (being built at the same time) shares ethics, DSM-5-TR and theory. Items whose
fact and answer matched an NCE item (caregiver burden, tolerance, displacement, Rogers's conditions,
Tuckman's storming, structural family therapy, reframing, the miracle question, orientation, thought
process, Erikson's integrity and generativity, Piaget's conservation, enmeshment, GAD duration,
cultural humility, microaggressions, Berry's integration) were rewritten to other facts.

All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the outline above. None is copied from the
ASWB guidebook's sample questions, ASWB's practice tests or any commercial prep product.
