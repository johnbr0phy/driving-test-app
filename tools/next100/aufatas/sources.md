# Tasmania firearms safety course (Tasmania Police Firearms Services) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the Tasmanian firearms legislation, the
Tasmania Police Firearms Services pages and information sheets, and the National Firearms Safety
Code listed below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from a TasTAFE course booklet, an
assessment, a practice quiz or any prep site. No official question bank for the course is
published, so no catalogue similarity check was possible; instead every item was compared with
the other Australian, New Zealand and Canadian firearms banks (see the end of this file).

## Format

Tasmania Police and TasTAFE publish no question count, time or pass mark for the course
assessment. Firearms Services says the course determines the applicant's competency. TasTAFE's
course page (https://www.tastafe.tas.edu.au/courses/course/11357nat) sits behind a bot check and
was not opened (it was not bypassed); search extracts of it describe four hours of classroom
theory and four hours of practical work on a Tasmania Police approved range. The landing says
only that the course has theory and practical parts assessed by TasTAFE. The 80% line is
Tigertest's readiness target. Format facts are on the landing and in examFacts only, never in a
question.

## Pending law, left out on purpose

- Firearms Amendment (Strengthening Firearm Security) Bill 2026: passed Parliament in September
  2026, commences on a day or days to be proclaimed. Fact sheet read in the browser on 2026-11-23:
  https://www.parliament.tas.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0009/110223/Fact-Sheet-Firearms-Amendment-Strengthening-Firearms-Security-Bill-2026.pdf
  It changes the 14-day change of address and particulars notices to 24 hours, the 3-month and
  7-day rule for interstate residents moving to Tasmania, who must produce a firearm for police,
  lending and borrowing, Australian citizenship for licences, straight pull and lever release
  firearms (to Category C) and antique firearm permits. No question asks about any of these until
  the Act commences and the consolidated Act shows the change.
- Firearms Amendment (Miscellaneous) Bill 2026: consultation 3 July to 7 August 2026, not passed.
- Fees, penalty units and fines are left out. The Category A and B shotgun wording differs between
  the Act (lever action shotguns in Category B up to 5 rounds) and older information sheets, so no
  lever action shotgun item is asked.

## Repeats checked

Every stem and key was compared (word overlap plus same key) with the NSW, Victoria, Queensland, WA
and the in-progress South Australia firearms banks, nzfirearms, capal, crfsc and hunter. Generic
National Firearms Safety Code items that repeated another bank were replaced with Tasmanian items
(imitation firearms, chassis kits, renewals, definitions in s 3, s 158A). The remaining close pairs
are category and prohibited pistol facts that every Australian state shares; their stems name
Tasmanian law.

## Second review (2026-11-23)

Blind review of all 200 items against the Act (authorised version of 2 December 2024, still the
current version on 2026-11-23, so the Strengthening Firearms Security amendments have not
commenced) and the Firearms Regulations 2026. Eight findings, all fixed in the sources:
seven items repeated a sibling bank's fact and answer (air rifle Category A and muzzle-loader
Category B against South Australia; shotgun and rimfire combination Category A against South
Australia; prohibited pistol over .38 inch against NSW; licence age 18 against WA, Victoria and
CFSC; 90-day permit against NSW; 150 kg safe fixing threshold against Queensland), each rewritten
from a different angle with a different answer; and the s 26(2) orientation item now states the
"no more than 3 occasions in the preceding 6 months" test exactly. The renewal notice (about 6
months before expiry), the midnight-on-the-14th-day surrender rule and the earliest new card (21
days before expiry) were checked on the Tasmania Police renewing page on 2026-11-23; s 104(2)
was checked in the Act.
