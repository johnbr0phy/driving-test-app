# NSW firearms safety course (licence qualification) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the NSW Firearms Act 1996, the NSW
Police Force users guide for the longarm licence course, an NSW Government release on the 2026
reforms and the Commonwealth National Firearms Safety Code. None is copied, adapted or reworded
from a course assessment, the users guide's sample questions, a training provider's material,
a practice quiz or any prep site.

The NSW legislation and NSW Police websites block automated fetches from the build machine, so
John downloaded the official documents in his browser and the text was read from those copies.
No archive, cache or proxy service was used.

## Outline (course required by the Firearms Act, run by NSW Police approved trainers)

- NSW Police Force, Firearms (Longarms) Users Guide: Rights, Wrongs and Responsibilities,
  version two, September 2006, fetched 2026-11-16 (downloaded by John in a browser; the site
  blocks automated fetches):
  https://police.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0014/133133/Firearms_Users_Guide.pdf
  The guide for the Firearms Licence Qualification course for longarms: chapters on firearm types,
  parts, functions and ammunition; basic safety rules; licences and permits; buying, registering
  and storing; modifying; ranges and the field; legislation; responsibilities and ethics; and the
  practical component (handling, loading, malfunctions, a stuck live round, cleaning). Used only
  for safe handling, ranges, field practice, ethics and firearm basics. Its legal facts are out of
  date (it cites the Firearms Regulation 2006 and a 5-year licence term), so every legal rule in
  the bank comes from the 2026 Act below. Its chapter sample questions were not used or reworded.
  The course has no published topic weights, so the split is Tigertest's: NSW law 31 of 50,
  safe use 19 of 50.

## Format

- No question count, time or pass mark for the course is published in the documents read. The
  users guide says applicants must complete and pass the course, including a practical
  component assessed by an approved trainer. The 80% line is Tigertest's readiness target and
  the landing says so.
- Firearms Act 1996 s 11(3)(b) (a person who has never held a licence must complete the
  prescribed firearms training and safety courses) and s 10(3) (applicants are given information
  about the required course and storage rules). s 11(3)(b) is asked once (LIC-015); s 10(3) is used on the
  landing and facts only.

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it

- The current NSW Firearms Regulation could not be obtained, so nothing that only the Regulation sets is
  asked: the minor's permit minimum age, the dealer-distance rule for private sales, conveyance
  rules for longarms, ammunition limits, club participation numbers and course content rules.
  Transport advice is attributed to the users guide, not to law.
- The course's question count, length and pass mark are not published in the documents read, so
  never stated as fact.

## Deliberately left out

- Fees, penalty units and maximum penalties, firearms dealer business rules, security industry
  (category H business/employment) details beyond the basics, the commencement-pending 2025
  amendments, current events, and anything about the course or its assessment itself.
- Facts that differ between states: every legal rule is stated for New South Wales only.
- Fact-and-answer pairs already used in the Queensland (`aufirearmsqld`), New Zealand
  (`nzfirearms`) and Canadian (`capal`, `crfsc`) firearms banks were checked and avoided.
