# Queensland firearms safety course (Weapons Licensing) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official course record, the
Queensland weapons legislation and the Queensland Police Service and Commonwealth safety
documents below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from a training provider's course booklet,
assessment, practice quiz or any prep site.

## Outline (course owned by the Queensland Police Service)

- training.gov.au, 11029NAT Course in Firearms and Weapons Safety (Approved for Firearms
  Licensing in Queensland), national register record (JSON), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://training.gov.au/api/training/11029NAT
  Status current, accreditation 1 May 2022 to 30 April 2027; copyright holder and content
  enquiries: Queensland Police Service, Weapons Licensing Group. Replaces 10618NAT.
- training.gov.au unit records, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://training.gov.au/api/training/NAT11029001 (Demonstrate knowledge of weapons legislation,
  weapons and community safety, the core unit) and
  https://training.gov.au/api/training/NAT11029002 (Use Category A and B firearms lawfully, safely
  and responsibly). Other units: NAT11029003 (C), 004 (D), 005 (H), 006 (M crossbows), 007 (M
  miscellaneous). The register gives unit titles only; the course document and unit elements are
  not public, so no topic weights exist. The bank covers the core unit and the category A and B
  unit: law and licensing 30, safe use 20 of each 50 (Tigertest's split).

## Format

- The QPS weapons licensing pages (https://www.police.qld.gov.au/weapons-licensing) returned an
  Incapsula bot page and could not be read on 2026-11-15. The course format (theory and handling,
  live fire for some categories, assessment by the training provider) and any pass mark are not
  confirmed by the owner, so the landing says only that an approved course must be completed and
  that providers assess it. The 80% line is Tigertest's readiness target.
- Weapons Act 1990 s 10AA (adequate knowledge: an approved safety training course completed within
  1 year before applying) and Weapons Regulation 2016 s 13 (a course must give the knowledge for
  safe use, storage and maintenance, with an assessment that can determine it). Landing and facts
  only; never a question.

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it

- QPS web pages, licence application steps, and the course's own question count, length and pass
  mark: not readable or not published, so never stated.
- The QPS brochure's statement about disqualification after a weapons offence was not checked
  against the 2024 and 2026 amendments, so it is left out.

## Deliberately left out

- Fees, penalty units and penalties, licence application forms, firearm prohibition orders,
  security guard and dealer licences, categories C, D, H and M handling (separate course units),
  the Australian citizenship condition added to s 10, and anything about the course or test itself.
- Facts that differ between states: every legal rule is stated for Queensland only.
