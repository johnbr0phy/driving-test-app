# WA Firearm Safety Training Course (theory) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the Firearms Act 2024 (WA) and the
Firearms Regulations 2024 as in force on 15 November 2026, WA Police Force information sheets and
the National Firearms Safety Code. None is copied, adapted or reworded from the CRTAFE course
assessment or its question bank, the old Firearm Awareness Test, a training provider's material,
a practice quiz or any prep site. The assessment's questions were not seen.

## Which test (law check)

- The brief named the "WA Firearm Awareness Test". That test belonged to the Firearms Act 1973
  and the Firearms Regulations 1974, which the Firearms Act 2024 repealed (s 440, s 441).
- Western Australian Legislation, Firearms Act 2024 home page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/statutes.nsf/law_a147438.html
  Act No. 023 of 2024, assent 27 June 2024; current consolidated version 00-f0-00, currency start
  31 March 2025, marked "Current".
- WA Police Force, Implementation Q&A information sheet, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.wa.gov.au/media/122944/download
  "Western Australia's new Firearms Act 2024 and Firearms Regulations 2024 commenced on 31 March
  2025", eight new licence types, mandatory firearm safety training course, health assessments,
  new storage requirements, transitional authorities.
- Western Australian Legislation, Firearms Regulations 2024 home page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://legislation.wa.gov.au/legislation/statutes.nsf/law_s54842.html
  Current version 00-c0-00, currency start 1 July 2026. Other subsidiary legislation listed under
  the Act: commencement proclamation (SL 2024/289), Firearms Amendment Regulations 2025, and two
  2026 Police Regulations amendments (significant defence events, fees) that do not touch the
  content asked here.
- Conclusion: the Firearms Act 2024 and Firearms Regulations 2024 are in force on the build date,
  and the requirement is now the Firearm Safety Training Course, so the bank is written to it.
  The exam id, slug and landing path keep the brief's names; the landing and FAQ explain that
  the Firearm Awareness Test was replaced.

## Outline and format (WA Police and CRTAFE)

- WA Police Force, Firearm Safety Training Course information sheet (V2-0), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.wa.gov.au/media/159424/download
  First-time applicants for a firearm authority must complete the course; two parts, Part 1
  theory (online course and assessment by Central Regional TAFE, covering legislative
  requirements and safety principles) and Part 2 practical (in person with a licensed dealer or
  other approved firearms trainer: safe handling, legal obligations under the 2024 Act and
  Regulations, responsibilities of authority holders); done once, again if the authority is
  cancelled or lapsed more than three years; fees set by the providers, not WA Police;
  declaration before practical training (Act s 24, s 166; Regs 245, 246).
- WA Police Force, CRTAFE Firearms Safety Training, Theory Q&A, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.wa.gov.au/system/files/2026-01/crtafe-theory-training-q-a-v2.pdf
  Mandatory for all new firearm authority applicants and authorised persons; online; course
  material then assessment; 40 questions randomly selected from a question bank; pass at least
  80% overall across the three sections; about 2 hours; 3 attempts, then wait at least 30 days
  and re-enrol; certificate valid 12 months; 3 months from enrolment to finish.
- WA Police Force, TAFE Safety Training Course (Theory), How to Enrol, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.wa.gov.au/media/151308/download
  Cites s 166; course covers legislative requirements including safe handling; needs a USI.
- Central Regional TAFE, WAPOL Firearm Safety Training Course - Theory, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.centralregionaltafe.wa.edu.au/courses/wapol-firearm-safety-training-course-theory
  Topics: firearms legislation and licensing requirements; safe handling, operation and storage;
  firearm types and responsible ownership; transport and security; the role of firearms in the
  community. Must pass the theory test. (Fee and campus details not used.)
- WA Police Force, Firearms: current information sheets index, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/western-australia-police-force/firearms-current-information-sheets
- The three sections of the assessment are not named, and no topic weights are published, so
  the split is Tigertest's: WA law 31 of 40 and safe use 9 of 40. The landing says so. 80% is
  the published pass mark.

## Safe handling

- Commonwealth Attorney-General's Department, National Firearms Safety Code (2002), as published
  by NT Police, Fire and Emergency Services, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://pfes.nt.gov.au/file/download/35121
  Used only for handling items not already asked in the NSW, Victorian and Queensland banks:
  muzzle loader charge, open-cocked self-loaders, break-open action, feed, bolt unlocking,
  storage oiling, disposal receipt, glossary terms (clip, lands, keyhole, bedding, magnum).
  WA's storage law, not the Code's general advice, decides where ammunition may be kept.

## Could not confirm or left out

- The names and weights of the assessment's three sections: not published. The CRTAFE course
  material is behind enrolment and was not read.
- No fees, fines, penalties or infringement amounts are asked. Penalties appear only in the Act.
- The practical part (Part 2) is not modelled.
- Rules that depend on details not in the sheets (exact Schedule 6 rows for 6 or more firearms,
  business and trade licence storage, commercial carriers) are left out except level 1 and 2
  basics for 5 or fewer firearms.
- Checked against the aufirearmsnsw, aufirearmsvic, aufirearmsqld, nzfirearms, capal, crfsc and
  hunter banks; National Firearms Safety Code facts already asked there were not reused.
