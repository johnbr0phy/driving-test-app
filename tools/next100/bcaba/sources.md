# BCaBA bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the BACB documents and primary
behavior-analytic literature below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from BACB sample items,
released forms, mock exams, commercial study guides, flashcard sets or prep sites.

## Outline (Behavior Analyst Certification Board)

- BACB, BCaBA Test Content Outline (6th ed.), document "Updated 09/2024", fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/bcaba-outline-6thEd/ (redirects to
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/BCaBA-6th-Edition-Test-Content-Outline-240903-a.pdf).
  Content that appears on the BCaBA examination beginning in 2025; 150 scored questions and 25
  unscored pilot questions; 90 tasks in 9 domains with scored counts: A Behaviorism and
  Philosophical Foundations 10 (7%), B Concepts and Principles 18 (12%), C Measurement, Data
  Display, and Interpretation 20 (13%), D Experimental Design 10 (7%), E Ethical and Professional
  Issues 18 (12%), F Behavior Assessment 17 (11%), G Behavior-Change Procedures 22 (15%),
  H Intervention Development and Monitoring 20 (13%), I Supervisory Relationships 15 (10%).
  Scaled to 50 per test (one third): 3/6/7/3/6/6/7/7/5. Every question maps to a task in this
  outline (for example B.12 unconditioned motivating operations, C.10 mastery criteria, G.15
  contingency contracting, G.16 self-management, I.2 reciprocal feedback, I.4 expectations for
  supervisor and supervisee).
- BACB, Test Content Outlines page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.bacb.com/test-content-outlines-for-bacb-certifications/
  Lists the 6th edition as the current BCaBA outline.
- BACB, Recent and Upcoming Changes, fetched 2026-11-15: https://www.bacb.com/upcoming-changes/
  No new BCaBA test content outline is announced; the January 1, 2027 changes are to eligibility
  and maintenance requirements.

## Format and role (BCaBA Handbook)

- BACB, Board Certified Assistant Behavior Analyst Handbook, "Updated 10/2026", fetched
  2026-11-15: https://www.bacb.com/bcaba-handbook (redirects to
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BCaBAHandbook_261028-a.pdf).
  Exam: 4 hours; 175 multiple-choice questions, 150 scored and 25 unscored; 4 possible answers,
  only 1 correct; offered only in English at Pearson VUE testing centers; pass/fail with the
  passing score set by the modified Angoff method; up to 8 attempts in the 2-year authorization
  period, 30 days between attempts. The BACB publishes no percentage pass mark, so 70% here is a
  readiness marker. Role facts used in questions: BCaBAs provide behavior-analytic services under
  the supervision of a BCBA and may not practice without it; a BCaBA without a qualified
  supervisor on record is inactive; BCaBAs may supervise RBTs; with more than one supervisor the
  written contract must spell out each supervisor's responsibilities.

## Deliberately left out

- Fees, attempt limits, test-day rules and eligibility pathways (landing only; the pathways
  change on January 1, 2027).
- BCaBA supervision percentages (5% then 2% of service hours) and frequency rules, which the
  2027 maintenance changes may revise.
- The $10 gift limit and other facts the sibling RBT bank already asks.
- Every fact-and-answer pair in data/rbt-questions.json and data/bcba-questions.json. The bank
  was checked against both; overlapping items were rewritten (resurgence replaced by a
  multiple-schedule FCT thinning item, contextual fit replaced by social significance).
