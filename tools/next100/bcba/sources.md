# BCBA bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the BACB documents and primary
behavior-analytic literature below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from BACB sample items,
released forms, mock exams, commercial study guides, flashcard sets or prep sites.

## Outline (Behavior Analyst Certification Board)

- BACB, BCBA Test Content Outline (6th ed.), document "Updated 09/2024", fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/bcba-outline-6thEd/ (redirects to
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/BCBA-6th-Edition-Test-Content-Outline-240903-a.pdf).
  Content that appears on the BCBA examination beginning in 2025; 175 scored questions and 10
  unscored pilot questions; 104 tasks in 9 domains with scored counts: A Behaviorism and
  Philosophical Foundations 8 (5%), B Concepts and Principles 24 (14%), C Measurement, Data
  Display, and Interpretation 21 (12%), D Experimental Design 13 (7%), E Ethical and Professional
  Issues 22 (13%), F Behavior Assessment 23 (13%), G Behavior-Change Procedures 25 (14%),
  H Selecting and Implementing Interventions 20 (11%), I Personnel Supervision and Management 19
  (11%). Scaled to 50 per test: 2/7/6/4/6/7/7/6/5. Every question maps to a task in this outline.
- BACB, Test Content Outlines page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.bacb.com/test-content-outlines-for-bacb-certifications/
  Lists the 6th edition as the current BCBA outline.
- BACB, Recent and Upcoming Changes, fetched 2026-11-15: https://www.bacb.com/upcoming-changes/
  No new BCBA test content outline is announced. The January 1, 2027 changes are to eligibility
  and maintenance requirements, not the exam content.

## Format (BCBA Handbook)

- BACB, Board Certified Behavior Analyst Handbook, "Updated 11/2026", fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.bacb.com/bcba-handbook (redirects to
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BCBAHandbook_261109-a.pdf).
  The examination is currently based on the BCBA Test Content Outline (6th ed.); 4 hours; 185
  multiple-choice questions, 175 scored and 10 unscored; 4 possible answers with only 1 correct;
  all questions equally weighted; offered only in English; computer based at Pearson VUE testing
  centers; pass/fail, with the passing score set by the modified Angoff method (criterion
  referenced, set by a panel of BACB-certified subject matter experts); result given at the
  testing center; up to 8 attempts in the 2-year authorization period with a 30-day wait between
  attempts. The BACB publishes no percentage pass mark, so 70% here is a readiness marker.
  Fees are never stated by Tigertest.

## Behavior-analytic concepts

Technical definitions (reinforcement and punishment contingencies, schedules, motivating
operations, verbal operants, measurement, single-case designs, functional analysis conditions,
prompting, chaining, group contingencies, generalization tactics) follow standard
behavior-analytic usage as the outline names it, checked against these primary papers:

- Baer, Wolf and Risley (1968), Some current dimensions of applied behavior analysis, JABA,
  fetched 2026-11-15: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1310980/ (the seven dimensions).
- Iwata, Dorsey, Slifer, Bauman and Richman (1982/1994), Toward a functional analysis of
  self-injury, JABA, fetched 2026-11-15: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1297798/
  (attention, demand, alone and play conditions).
- Herrnstein (1961), Relative and absolute strength of response as a function of frequency of
  reinforcement, JEAB, fetched 2026-11-15: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1404074/
  (matching of relative response rate to relative reinforcement rate).
- Laraway, Snycerski, Michael and Poling (2003), Motivating operations and terms to describe
  them, JABA, fetched 2026-11-15: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1284457/
  (establishing and abolishing operations; value-altering and behavior-altering effects).
- Stokes and Baer (1977), An implicit technology of generalization, JABA (programming common
  stimuli, indiscriminable contingencies, training loosely, "train and hope"). The PMC page was
  rate limited when checked, so items cite only the widely documented tactic names.

## Deliberately left out

- Fees, attempt limits, test-day rules and the eligibility pathways (they are on the landing
  only, and the pathways change on January 1, 2027).
- Supervision percentages and fieldwork hour figures, which change with the 2027 requirements.
- The $10 gift limit (Code 1.12), because the sibling RBT bank asks about gift limits.
- Named commercial assessment instruments.
- Every fact-and-answer pair in data/rbt-questions.json; the bank was checked against it and
  overlapping items were rewritten (for example avoidance instead of a plain negative
  reinforcement scenario, and an 85% IOA item instead of an 80% one).
