# Sources: BC real estate trading services licensing exam (`bcrealestate`)

Every question is original, written from the primary law below. No UBC Sauder or BCFSA exam, assignment,
practice or sample question was read or used. All pages fetched 2026-11-16.

## Exam owner and format
- UBC Sauder School of Business, Real Estate Division, "Real Estate Trading Services Licensing Course" (the
  exclusive accredited pre-licensing course for a trading services representative licence under the Real Estate
  Services Act): https://www.sauder.ubc.ca/programs/real-estate/licensing-registration-courses/bc-licensing-courses/real-estate-trading-services-licensing
  Fetched 2026-11-16. Gave: final exam is three hours with 100 multiple-choice questions, one mark each; the
  passing grade is 70% (set by the regulator); the exam is written at regular province-wide sittings or at the
  UBC Computerized Exam Centre; calculator (HP 10bII+ or its app) recommended; after the exam the candidate takes
  the Residential or Commercial Trading Services Applied Practice Course run by BCFSA. Course fee, exam fee and
  dates are on that page and are deliberately not used.
- UBC Real Estate Division Student Handbook:
  https://professional.sauder.ubc.ca/re_licensing/course_resources/courses/content/student_handbook/index.cfm
  Fetched 2026-11-16. Confirms 100 questions, 3 hours, 70% minimum for every licensing exam; assignments within
  12 months, exam within two years of assignments and in no more than two attempts; says the courses need
  quantitative skills "specifically those involving mortgage finance and appraisal". No chapter list or topic
  weights are published, so the category weights in the bank are Tigertest's own.
- BCFSA, Education and Licensing Guidelines: https://www.bcfsa.ca/node/1432
  Fetched 2026-11-16. Licensing steps: licensing course and exam, applied practice course (trading services
  only), application within one year of passing. The bcfsa.ca licensing pages returned 403 to automated reads, so
  this guideline page (same site, same content) is the BCFSA source.
- Not confirmed from an official page: the exact number of Applied Practice Course components (the guideline says
  Components 1 and 2 before applying for residential). Used only on the landing, labelled as BCFSA's guideline.

## Mortgage finance and valuation
No official outline names the formulas, so these two categories are written as self-contained arithmetic and
standard method questions: every stem gives the numbers and, where a ratio is used, its definition; every
computed key is recomputed and asserted in `build.py`. Concepts used: simple interest and amortization sums,
semi-annual compounding of Canadian fixed-rate mortgages (the Interest Act s. 6 convention), loan-to-value,
gross and total debt service ratios as defined in each stem, the three approaches to value (market comparison,
cost, income) and capitalization of net operating income.
