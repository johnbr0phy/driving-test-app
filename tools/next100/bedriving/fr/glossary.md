# bedriving French (fr-BE) glossary

Written before the translation. Terms as the Belgian Code de la route uses them (Arrêté royal du
1er décembre 1975 portant règlement général sur la police de la circulation routière et de
l'usage de la voie publique, French text on code-de-la-route.be and in the Moniteur belge) and as
Wallonie.be and Bruxelles Mobilité write about the examen théorique du permis B. Belgian French,
not France French: "permis de conduire provisoire", "guide", "agglomération", "bande de
circulation", "bande d'arrêt d'urgence", "en tirette", "GSM", "feu jaune-orange", "certificat de
visite", "école de conduite", "rue cyclable", "zone résidentielle ou de rencontre".

Register: "vous" everywhere, in the questions and explanations (like the Walloon and Brussels
theory exam and the Code's own wording) and in the app copy and the landing (the French house UI
register, `i18n/fr.ts`).

Typography: decimal comma (0,5 g/l, 1,5 m, 3,5 t), thousands with a non-breaking space (1 500 kg),
"km/h", metres as "m", times as 9 h, 18 h, 19 h 30. A non-breaking space before ? ! : ; (added by
`build.py`, so the sources use a plain space). Sign codes (A1a, B9, C43, F12a...) kept exactly as
in the English bank; they are the Code's own codes. Places in their French names: Liège, Namur,
Bastogne, Louvain (Leuven), Malines (Mechelen), the Ardennes. No em or en dashes.
