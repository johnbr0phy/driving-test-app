# bedriving Dutch (nl-BE) glossary

Terms as the Belgian Wegcode (Koninklijk Besluit van 1 december 1975 houdende algemeen reglement
op de politie van het wegverkeer, Dutch text on wegcode.be) and Vlaanderen.be's pages on the
theorie-examen voor rijbewijs B use them. Belgian Dutch, not Netherlands Dutch: "rijbewijs B",
"voorlopig rijbewijs", "bebouwde kom", "autosnelweg", "gsm", "nummerplaat", "fluovestje".

Register: questions and explanations in "u", like the official Flemish theory exam and the
Wegcode's own instructions; app copy and the landing in "je", the house UI register (as in
`lib/landings/bevracht.nl.json`).

Typography: decimal comma (0,5 g/l, 1,5 m, 3,5 ton), thousands with a point (1.500 kg),
"km/u" as the Wegcode writes it, metres as "m" or "meter", times as 9 uur, 18 uur, 19.30 uur.
Sign codes (A1a, B9, C43, F12a...) kept exactly as in the English bank; they are the Wegcode's
own codes. No em or en dashes.
