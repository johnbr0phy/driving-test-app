# Sources for `bedriving` (Belgium driving theory exam, category B)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the Belgian Highway Code. No official,
GOCA or commercial practice question was opened or copied. Signs are described in words (no
images). Checked 2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam and its format
Driving licences are a regional matter in Belgium: Flanders, Wallonia and the Brussels-Capital
Region each organise the theory exam through approved exam centres (GOCA members such as
Autoveiligheid, SBAT, AIBV, Autosécurité, ACT). The Highway Code itself is federal, with some
regional variants (speed limits).

- Flanders, Vlaanderen.be, "Theorie-examen voor rijbewijs B" (read 2026-11-01):
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/rijbewijs-b/theorie-examen-voor-rijbewijs-b
  - Minimum age 17. 50 multiple-choice questions, read aloud and shown on screen, 15 seconds to
    answer each after it is read. Start with 50 points; lose 1 point per wrong or unanswered
    question and 5 points per wrong answer on questions about third- or fourth-degree offences or
    the maximum permitted speed. Pass: at least 41/50.
  - The exam is in Dutch. Since 1 January 2023 an audio translation into French, German or English
    can be added (Dutch first, then the chosen language); own interpreters no longer allowed.
  - A pass is valid 3 years. Retake not on the same day; after 2 failures, 12 hours of theory
    lessons at an approved driving school before the 3rd attempt.
  - 3 July 2026: the Flemish Government approved in principle that from 1 January 2027 the Flemish
    practical exam requires a theory pass at a Flemish centre, with no transition.
  - (Fee figures on the page are not used.)
- Wallonia, Wallonie.be, "Passer le permis de conduire théorique catégorie B" (read 2026-11-01,
  page updated 28/09/2026):
  https://www.wallonie.be/fr/demarches/passer-le-permis-de-conduire-theorique-categorie-b
  - 50 multiple-choice questions; age 17; valid 3 years, not extendable; driving-school lessons
    after two consecutive failures. French by default, German at Eupen, Dutch or English by
    appointment at the Couillet and Wandre centres. Pilot of special or adapted sessions since
    1 November 2024. Scoring not stated on the page.
- Brussels: BX1 (regional broadcaster), 4 April 2018, "Nouvelles règles du permis théorique à
  Bruxelles" (read 2026-11-01):
  https://bx1.be/categories/news/nouvelles-regles-permis-theorique-a-bruxelles-entreront-vigueur-30-avril/
  - 50 questions, 41/50 to pass, serious fault 5 points, light fault 1 point (so two serious faults
    fail); exam in French or Dutch, English or German with an interpreter; course after two
    consecutive failures.
  - Brussels Times guide (via search, 2026-11-01): theory in French or Dutch at two centres
    (Anderlecht, Schaerbeek); interpreter for German, English or sign language appointed by the
    centre. https://www.brusselstimes.com/1403706/how-to-get-a-driving-licence-in-brussels-as-an-english-speaker
- Comparison of the three regions, permis-online.be observatoire (a guide, used only to confirm
  the official figures above and for the risk perception test; read 2026-11-01):
  https://permis-online.be/observatoire/examen-permis-wallonie-flandre-bruxelles.md
  - Same 50 questions, 41/50 and 5-point serious faults in all three regions; gives running a
    red light or crossing a continuous white line as examples of third/fourth-degree offences.
  - Risk (hazard) perception test with 5 videos: separate test before the practical exam in
    Wallonia (since 1 July 2018), part of the practical exam in Brussels (since 1 November 2018)
    and Flanders (since 1 June 2017). Not modelled; the landing says so. (Guide figures.)
- Exam timing: Flanders gives 15 seconds per question after it is read; no total time is
  published by any region, so the landing gives none.

## Rules of the road
- Royal Decree of 1 December 1975, general regulation on road traffic police (Code de la route /
  Wegcode), consolidated text on the federal site codedelaroute.be (read 2026-11-01):
  https://www.codedelaroute.be/fr/reglementation/1975120109~hra8v386pu
  Articles used: 8.4 (phones), 8.5, 9.3, 9.4, 9.7 (hard shoulder), 9.8 (emergency corridor),
  10 (speed), 11 (limits, regional: built-up 30 km/h in Brussels and 50 km/h in Flanders and
  Wallonia; outside built-up areas 70 km/h in Brussels and Flanders (Flanders 90 only where
  signed), 90 km/h in Wallonia; 120 km/h on motorways and physically divided four-lane roads;
  90 km/h on motorways above 3.5 t; 60 km/h above 7.5 t on ordinary roads; mopeds 25/45),
  12 (priority to the right, rail vehicles, roundabouts, B1/B5, dirt tracks, manoeuvres),
  12bis (zip merge), 14, 16 and 17 (overtaking), 18, 19 (turning; no indicator entering a
  roundabout, indicator leaving), 20 (level crossings), 21 (motorways: minimum 70 km/h, lorries in
  the two right lanes on 3+ lanes, no U-turn, reversing or stopping), 22 (roads for motor
  vehicles), 22bis (residential and meeting zones, 20 km/h), 22ter (raised devices, 30 km/h),
  22quater (zone 30), 22quinquies and 22octies (reserved paths, 30 km/h), 22sexies (pedestrian
  zones, walking pace), 22novies (cycle streets, 30 km/h, no overtaking cyclists), 22undecies
  (school streets), 23 to 27 (stopping and parking: 1.5 m for pedestrians, 5 m before crossings
  and junctions, 20 m before lights, 15 m either side of bus/tram stops, 1 m gaps, 3 m free
  width, no parking on B9 roads outside built-up areas, blue zone 9-18 h on working days for 2 h,
  alternate parking), 28 (doors), 30 to 33 (lights: 200 m visibility, dip within 50 m, rear fog
  under about 100 m, horn), 32bis (hazard lights), 35 (belts, child restraints under 135 cm,
  airbag), 37 and 38 (priority vehicles), 39, 39bis, 40, 40bis, 40ter (pedestrians and cyclists:
  1 m in built-up areas, 1.5 m outside), 44, 51 (triangle 30 m / 100 m on motorways, safety vest),
  52 (accidents: ID over 15, report within 24 h), 61 to 64 (lights), 66 to 71 (signs), 72 to 77
  (markings).
- Alcohol: Law of 16 March 1968 on road traffic police, article 34: 0.5 g/l of blood
  (0.22 mg/l of exhaled air); 0.2 g/l (0.09 mg/l) for professional drivers since 1 January 2015.
  Federal Police, "Quelle différence entre ivresse et imprégnation alcoolique ?" (read 2026-11-01):
  https://www.police.be/5337/node/19014
  No fine or licence-withdrawal amounts are asked (they change).

## Left out on purpose
- Fines, fees, demerit or penalty figures, regional provisional-licence models and their
  accompanying-driver rules (they differ by region and change), fire extinguisher and first aid
  kit rules (regional changes under discussion), and any 2026 alcohol-sanction changes reported
  only by guides.

## Review pass (2026-11-01)
- Brussels official page, be.brussels, "Formation à la conduite et examens pour le permis de
  conduire B" (read 2026-11-01):
  https://be.brussels/fr/transport-mobilite/permis-de-conduire-et-immatriculation/obtenir-son-permis/formation-la-conduite-et-examens-pour-le-permis-de-conduire-b
  - Confirms: 50 multiple-choice questions on a computer, about 30 minutes, up to 15 seconds per
    question; 41/50 to pass; 1 point per error, 5 for a serious offence or speeding (two such
    errors fail); French or Dutch, interpreter for German, English or sign language appointed by
    the centre; age 17; pass valid 3 years; 12 hours at an approved driving school after two
    consecutive failures.
- Vlaanderen.be re-read 2026-11-01: figures above confirmed (41/50, 15 s after reading, English
  audio translation since 1 January 2023, 12 hours after two failures, 2027 Flemish-centre rule).
- Wallonie.be re-read 2026-11-01 (updated 28/09/2026): no scoring or hours stated; English by
  appointment at Couillet and Wandre confirmed. 12 hours after two consecutive failures in
  Wallonia is from guides only (permis.online, Test Achats), labelled "guides say".
- codedelaroute.be consolidated text re-read 2026-11-01: art. 11.1/11.2 regional limits confirmed
  (Brussels 70 outside built-up areas incl. four-lane roads divided only by markings; Wallonia 90,
  and 70 on a chaussée à voie centrale; Flanders 70); 12.3.1 (no priority to the right for a
  driver on a roundabout or coming from a no-entry direction); 12bis zip merge; 17.2; 21.3; 22bis;
  22novies (cycle zones: 30 km/h, no overtaking cyclists); 22undecies; 24 and 25 distances; 66.2
  (150 m, Brussels and Flanders); D9 (pedestrians, bicycles, class A mopeds) and D10; the 30 km/h
  limit on D9/D10 paths is in the Walloon text of 11.1 only. Art. 87: this regulation ceases on
  1 June 2027, replaced by a federal code and Brussels, Flemish and Walloon codes.
- Alcohol: a 0.2 g/l limit for drivers licensed under three years was announced for 2027
  (Brussels Times with Belga, 6 October 2026,
  https://www.brusselstimes.com/2348359/belgium-to-introduce-a-near-zero-alcohol-tolerance-for-new-drivers-in-2027);
  not in force as of November 2026, so the bank asks only the 0.5 g/l general and 0.2 g/l
  professional limits and says so in LIC-009.
