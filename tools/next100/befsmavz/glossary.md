# Glossary for `befsmavz` (Dutch, Belgian usage, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations neutral, third person (a klant, a verzekerde, a makelaar), as
in Belgian syllabi and the law; landing and UI copy in "je" (as `betoelating`). Belgian legal
Dutch from the wet van 4 april 2014 betreffende de verzekeringen. Typography: decimal comma,
thousands point (400.000), a space before % (25 %), no currency in stems (worked sums are plain
numbers), no em-dashes, no straight double quotes.
