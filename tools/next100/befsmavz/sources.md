# Sources for `befsmavz` (Belgium FSMA-recognised insurance distribution exam, verzekeringsdistributie, in Dutch)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Dutch from the Belgian laws below. Nothing
was copied from an official or recognised exam, a Febelfin Academy or Syntra syllabus, an insurer
academy's material or a commercial prep site. Checked 2026-11-07 unless noted.

## Exam format (official and recognised-organiser pages)
- Febelfin Academy, "What are the knowledge requirements in the context of the European Insurance
  Distribution Directive (IDD)?" (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.febelfin-academy.be/en/faq/insurances/what-are-knowledge-requirements-context-european-insurance-distribution-directive
  - Four modules: module 1 basic knowledge (two exams: the insurance market and insurance contract
    regulation; diverse legislation), module 2 non-life (four exams: liability and legal aid;
    motor vehicle and assistance; property; personal insurance other than life), module 3 life in
    general (two exams), module 4 life with an investment component.
  - Based on the implementing decision of 25 June 2019; "The new examination structure applied
    as from 1 January 2021."
  - A module is passed when all its submodule exams are passed; no fixed order; modules 2 to 4
    count only with module 1; module 4 only with module 3.
  - PCPs take module 1; responsible persons take module 1 plus the modules of the products their
    company distributes.
- Febelfin Academy course page, "Insurance distribution (IDD): distance learning with training
  platform and exam (presential)" (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.febelfin-academy.be/en/courses/insurance-distribution-idd-distance-learning-with-training-platform-and-exam-presential
  - "Each different submodule has a separate exam"; each exam "consists of 20 multiple choice
    questions (no guessing correction)"; "about 30 minutes" per submodule; a session lasts at most
    3 hours; "a minimum score of 60% per submodule".
  - Submodule titles: 3.1 applicable law and financial competency; 3.2 anti money laundering
    legislation, insurance market, pension systems, rules of conduct. Module 4 listed without
    submodules.
  - This course and exam are in English; Febelfin Academy also runs them in Dutch and French
    (French course sheets found in search, e.g.
    https://febelfin-academy.be/fr/download/api/22501-distributiondassurancesiddetudeadistanceavecplateformedentrainementetexamenenligne-proctoring-67b9345a7bce7.pdf ,
    not opened). Retake after 15 days (exam admin, landing only; never in the bank).
- FSMA communication FSMA_2019_14 of 18 July 2019 (read 2026-11-07, PDF):
  https://www.fsma.be/sites/default/files/legacy/content/NL/circ/2019/fsma_2019_14_nl.pdf
  - The KB of 18 June 2019 (BS 25 June 2019, in force 5 July 2019) moved from exams per branch to
    exams by module; "basiskennis" and "beroepskennis" levels existed only during the transition
    (basiskennis gave access only to the PCP function). So the old basiskennis/beroepskennis split
    from the brief is historical; the current system is the four modules.
- Vivium training plan for the FSMA-recognised PCP basis track (June 2022, read 2026-11-07):
  https://inform.vivium.be/documents/d/vivium-info/planning-basistraject-pcp-juni-vivium
  - Dutch submodule names: "Module 1.1 - Verzekeringsmarkt en regelgeving op het
    verzekeringcontract", "Module 1.2 - Diverse wetgevingen".
- FSMA, "Stappenplan beroepskennis" (version 08.2025, read 2026-11-07; credit side, used only for
  the PCP and responsible-person concepts):
  https://www.fsma.be/sites/default/files/legacy-mcc/nl_stappenplan_beroepskennis.pdf

## Not confirmed
- The Febelfin Academy exam regulation PDF (het-examenreglement-in-verzekeringsbemiddeling) and the
  "structuur examens" PDF both returned 404 on 2026-11-07; the figures above come from the live

- Number of submodule exams in module 4 and the topic weights inside each submodule: not
  published. The bank's 6/8/8/4/4/4/4/5/4/3 split per 50 is Tigertest's.

  Febelfin Academy's pages were read.

## Law used for the questions (in force November 2026)
- Wet van 4 april 2014 betreffende de verzekeringen, consolidated text on Justel (read in chunks
  2026-11-07): https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/wet/2014/04/04/2014011239/justel
  - Deel 4: art. 57 (voorstel binds nobody; 30 days; opzeggingsrecht 14 days non-life, 30 days
    life), 58-60 (mededelingsplicht; opzettelijk: nietig; onopzettelijk: one month), 62 (opzet;
    grove schuld only if expressly and limitatively stated), 63 (war), 65 (verval only with causal
    link), 67 (premium haalbaar), 69-72 (ingebrekestelling by exploot or aangetekende zending, at
    least 15 days; later premiums limited to two consecutive years), 74-76 (claim notice as soon as
    reasonably possible; reduction by the prejudice; refusal only with fraud), 80-81 (risk
    reduction and aggravation), 84-86 (opzegging forms; max one year, tacit renewal; opposition at
    least two months (verzekeringnemer) and three months (verzekeraar) before the due date;
    opzegging after a claim in effect after three months), 88 (three years), 90 (prior arbitration
    clause deemed unwritten), 95 (subrogation; no recourse on family members living in, staff,
    unless kwaad opzet), 96-99 (over- and underinsurance), 106 (rescue costs even above the sum),
    110-111 (changing the damaged property; transfer of real estate: three months after the
    authentic deed), 121 (new value, wear over 30 %, payment 30 days after the amount is fixed),
    123 (natural disasters in fire insurance for simple risks), 142-146 and 150-152 (liability:
    occurrence and claims-made, defence, costs and interest, direct right, recourse notice),
    155-157 (legal expenses: free choice of lawyer, fines not insurable, objectivity clause),
    162-187 (life: incontestability, wrong age, suicide in the first year, start on first premium,
    no enforcement of premium, beneficiary rules, half to the spouse and half to the children,
    acceptance by an endorsement signed by three parties, surrender, reduction, advance), 197/2
    (payment within one month), 201/1-210 (health: no exclusion or loading for a suicide attempt,
    lifelong cover, income protection at least to 65, indexes, two-year incontestability,
    chronically ill to 65, individual continuation after two years with 30 days to notify),
    61/1 and following (right to be forgotten, five years).
  - Deel 6: art. 258-268 (register categories, makelaar declaration, subagent responsibility,
    title protection, verantwoordelijke voor de distributie, conditions: knowledge and training,
    no bankruptcy in ten years without rehabilitation, professional liability insurance for the EEA,
    complaints), 279-296/2 (loyaal, billijk en professioneel; demands and needs; IPID; impartial
    analysis; remuneration and conflicts; inducements; POG; files kept five years; insurer liable
    for tied agents; insurance-based investment products: costs aggregated, suitability and
    appropriateness, suitability statement, periodic reports), 306-319 (FSMA measures and fines).
  - Deel 3, art. 41-50 (segmentation published on the website; profit sharing not guaranteed).
- Wet van 18 september 2017 tot voorkoming van het witwassen van geld en de financiering van
  terrorisme (CFI, verbod op mededeling, ten-year retention, UBO over 25 %, UBO register, PEPs,
  AMLCO, risk-based approach, life insurance beneficiaries identified at payout).
- Wet van 21 november 1989 betreffende de verplichte aansprakelijkheidsverzekering inzake
  motorrijtuigen (compulsory cover, art. 29bis vulnerable road users, Gemeenschappelijk
  Motorwaarborgfonds, Tariferingsbureau).
- Wet van 30 juli 1979 (objective liability for fire and explosion in publicly accessible premises).
- Arbeidsongevallenwet van 10 april 1971.
- Vlaams Woninghuurdecreet (tenant insures fire and water damage liability; leases from 2019).
- AVG / GDPR (special categories, 72 hours, access, data minimisation), Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.
- PRIIPs Regulation (KID, summary risk indicator 1 to 7).
- Belgian pension pillars and the VAPZ as described in the module 3.2 title (pension systems).

Fines, fees, premiums, tax rates, guarantee ceilings and other money figures from the law are not
asked. The one worked sum (underinsurance) uses plain numbers.

## Review check (2026-11-07)
Re-read on Justel (consolidated wet van 4 april 2014) and Febelfin Academy's course page:
- Art. 85: verzet tegen stilzwijgende verlenging ten minste twee maanden (verzekeringnemer) en
  drie maanden (verzekeraar) voor de vervaldag. Art. 85/1 (opzegging na één jaar voor bepaalde
  polissen, uitwerking na twee maanden) is not asked.
- Art. 173: echtgenoot en kinderen samen aangewezen, tenzij anders bedongen half om half.
- Art. 203 §1: arbeidsongeschiktheid ten minste tot 65 jaar of een jongere leeftijd waarop de
  beroepsactiviteit volledig en definitief stopt. Art. 208: twee jaar aangesloten, 30 dagen om
  de individuele voortzetting te melden.
- Art. 259 §1: een tussenpersoon kan slechts in één categorie ingeschreven worden.
- Art. 61/2: recht om vergeten te worden, vijf jaar na het succesvol beëindigen van de behandeling.
- Art. 37: precontractuele informatie bij levensverzekering in een officiële Belgische landstaal.
- Febelfin Academy course page: 20 meerkeuzevragen per submodule, geen giscorrectie, ongeveer 30
  minuten, minstens 60 % per submodule; modules 1 to 4 as listed above.
