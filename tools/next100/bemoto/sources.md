# Sources for `bemoto` (Belgium motorcycle theory exam, categories A1, A2 and A)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the Belgian Highway Code and general safe
riding practice. No official, GOCA or commercial practice question (open-exam-prep, AutoviaTest,
easy-quizzz or similar) was opened or copied. Signs and situations are described in words (the real
exam shows photos). Checked 2026-11-07 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam and its format
Driving licences are a regional matter: Flanders, Wallonia and the Brussels-Capital Region each run
the theory exam through approved exam centres (GOCA members: Autoveiligheid, SBAT, AIBV,
Autosécurité, ACT). The motorcycle theory exam is separate from the category B exam and covers A1,
A2 and A.

- Brussels, be.brussels, "Rijopleiding en rijexamen voor rijbewijs A1/A2/A" (read 2026-11-07):
  https://be.brussels/nl/transport-mobiliteit/rijbewijs-en-kentekenbewijs/rijbewijs-behalen/rijopleiding-en-rijexamen-voor-rijbewijs-a1a2a
  - 50 multiple-choice questions on a computer, with audiovisual support; 41/50 to pass.
  - Dutch or French; German or English only in booked special sessions with an interpreter
    appointed by the centre, at the candidate's expense; sign language interpreter by appointment.
  - Sit from 17 years and 9 months. Pass valid 3 years (practical exam within that period).
  - Exemptions: A1 held 2 years exempts from the A2 theory exam; A2 held 2 years exempts from the
    A theory exam.
  - Practical exam ages: A1 18, A2 20, A 24 (22 with A2 for 2 years).
  - Categories: A1 up to 125 cc, 11 kW, 0.1 kW/kg (tricycles up to 15 kW); A2 up to 35 kW,
    0.2 kW/kg; A at least 35 kW (with sidecar), tricycles over 15 kW.
  - No time limit or serious-fault scoring stated. (Fee figure on the page not used.)
- Flanders, Vlaanderen.be, "Rijbewijs A1, A2 of A behalen: stap voor stap" (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/mobiliteit-en-openbare-werken/auto-en-motor/rijbewijzen-en-rijopleiding/rijbewijs-a1-a2-of-a-voor-motoren-vanaf-50-cc/rijbewijs-a1-a2-of-a-behalen-stap-voor-stap
  - Theory exam from 17 years 9 months (A1), 19 years 9 months (A2), 23 years 9 months (A); a
    pass is valid 3 years. A guide and a Reddit thread note another vlaanderen.be page lists
    17 years 9 months for all, so the landing states Brussels' figure and Flanders' per category.
  - Practical ages A1 18, A2 20, A 24 or 22 (progressive). Provisional licence valid 1 year,
    1 month of practice before the road test.
  - Documents to carry: licence, identity card, insurance certificate, original registration
    certificate (DIV) and original certificate of conformity.
  - Question count and scoring are on a separate theory-exam page that returned 404 when checked;
    the Flemish car page (vlaanderen.be/rijbewijs-b/theorie-examen-voor-rijbewijs-b, read for
    bedriving on 2026-11-01) gives the English audio translation for the B exam since 1 January
    2023. Not confirmed for category A, so the landing says to ask the centre.
- Wallonia, Autosécurité, "Mon guide du permis de conduire catégorie A", FM2113, édition 02/2026
  (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.autosecurite.be/uploads/2026/05/2500333-FM2113-Guide-permis-Cat-A.pdf
  - Category A: motorcycle over 35 kW, motor tricycle over 15 kW. Direct access from 24 with the
    theory exam passed under 3 years earlier; progressive access from 22 with A2 for 2 years,
    exempt from the theory exam.
  - Since 1 January 2026, all theory exams must be passed in Wallonia to take the Walloon
    practical exams.
  - Code 78 for an automatic exam bike; 6 hours to remove it.
  - Provisional licence model 3 (after the private-ground test): 12 months at most, not
    extendable; own motorcycle, no guide; regulation sign at the rear; no passenger, no commercial
    goods transport; no riding 22:00 to 06:00 on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, the eve of public
    holidays and public holidays unless 24 or over; no riding abroad.
  - Exam gear: approved helmet, gloves, long-sleeved jacket, trousers or overalls, boots or ankle
    boots protecting the malleolus.
- Touring, "Hoe behaal je een motorrijbewijs (rijbewijs A)" (a guide, last update 6 February 2026,
  read 2026-11-07): https://www.touring.be/nl/node/5910
  - 50 questions on traffic law and safe motorcycle riding, at least 41/50, pass valid 3 years,
    exempt with A2 for 2 years.
- AutoviaTest, Belgium facts (guide, via the queue notes): https://autoviatest.com/en/driving-test/belgium/facts
  - About 30 minutes (other guides say 25); English at many centres "confirm with the centre".
    Not confirmed officially, so the landing gives no total time.
- Serious-fault scoring: the B exam's 5-point rule (third/fourth-degree offences and speed limits)
  is confirmed for category B (see `tools/next100/bedriving/sources.md`); no official page found
  for category A. Not stated on the landing and not modelled.

## Rules of the road
- Royal Decree of 1 December 1975, general regulation on road traffic police (Code de la route),
  consolidated text, codedelaroute.be (downloaded and read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.codedelaroute.be/fr/reglementation/1975120109~hra8v386pu
  Motorcycle articles used:
  - 2.18 (motorcycle: two-wheeled motor vehicle with or without sidecar);
  - 4.2 (officer signals: arm raised vertically, arms extended horizontally); 6.1 to 6.3
    (officers over signs over rules; working lights override priority signs);
  - 9.3.2 (motorcyclist position: right half of an unmarked two-way road, whole width of an
    unmarked one-way road, whole width of the lane; whole unit counts; moves are not manoeuvres and
    need no indicator; must not hinder someone overtaking);
  - 9.8 and 2.70 (emergency corridor: only priority vehicles, vehicles requested by the
    authorities and tow trucks);
  - 10.1 (speed to conditions); 11 (regional limits; 70 km/h on a chaussée à voie centrale in the
    Walloon text); 12.3.1 (priority to the right, roundabouts, no-entry exception, B1/B5, dirt
    tracks); 12.4 (manoeuvres incl. leaving a roadside property and changing lanes; not a
    manoeuvre to carry straight on from the end of a cycle path); 12.4bis (crossing a pavement or
    cycle path);
  - 16.2bis (filtering between lanes or queues of stopped or slow traffic is not overtaking except
    for 17.2.5; at most 50 km/h and at most 20 km/h faster than the traffic; on motorways and roads
    for motor vehicles only between the two leftmost lanes);
  - 17.2 (overtaking banned at priority-to-the-right junctions, near crests unless without crossing
    the continuous line, when the vehicle overtaken is itself overtaking a vehicle other than a
    bicycle, two-wheeled moped or two-wheeled motorcycle, at pedestrian and cyclist crossings, on
    raised devices);
  - 21.1, 21.2 (motorway access banned to mopeds, cycles and vehicles unable to reach 70 km/h);
  - 22novies (cycle streets and zones: 30 km/h, no overtaking cyclists);
  - 23.2 (solo motorcycles may park at right angles within the marking), 23.4 (motorcycles may be
    parked off the carriageway without hindering others), the paid-parking rule that several
    motorcycles in one car space pay once;
  - 30bis (two-wheeled mopeds and motorcycles: dipped beam and rear light permanently; main beam
    may not then be used); 31.1 and 31.2 (lights when parked at night);
  - 35.1.1 (children under 3 never on a motorcycle; 3 to under 8 only on a motorcycle up to 125 cc
    in an adapted child restraint; under 8 in a child restraint in a sidecar);
  - 36 (helmet for riders and passengers; gloves, long-sleeved jacket, trousers or overalls, boots
    or ankle boots protecting the ankles);
  - 43ter (motorcycle groups: two staggered rows on roads divided into lanes, never more than half
    the carriageway otherwise; over 50 riders at least two road captains, 15 to 50 may have them;
    captains at least 25, retro-reflective garment marked road captain, carry a C3 sign, may hold
    cross traffic at junctions without lights);
  - 44.4, 44.5 (no more passengers than seats; no side-saddle; passengers' feet on footrests);
  - 46.1 (load width: motorcycle without sidecar 1.25 m; two-wheeled mopeds 1.00 m), 46.2.1 (no
    front overhang), 46.2.2 (rear overhang 0.50 m);
  - 49 (a motorcycle with sidecar may tow a trailer only if the sidecar wheel has a brake);
  - 66 (danger signs A19 falling rocks, A29 cattle, A31 road works, A5 steep climb, A7 narrowing,
    A33 traffic lights, A35 low-flying aircraft, A43 level crossing without barriers, A49 rails in
    the carriageway, A51 other danger with panel); 68 (C5 more than two wheels and motorcycles with
    sidecar; C7 motorcycles; C27 width with load; C35 no overtaking vehicles with more than two
    wheels, to and including the next junction); 70 (E9b, E9i parking reserved for motorcycles);
    72.5 (bus lane: motorcycles only if shown on the F17 sign or a panel; other vehicles only to
    pass an obstacle, turn at the junction or reach a property or parking space).
- Alcohol: Law of 16 March 1968, article 34: 0.5 g/l of blood = 0.22 mg/l of exhaled air
  (professional drivers 0.2 g/l, 0.09 mg/l); see `tools/next100/bedriving/sources.md`.
- 112 (European emergency number) and 101 (police): standard Belgian emergency numbers.

## Safe riding content
Braking, countersteering, cornering lines, surfaces, weather and passenger handling are general
riding knowledge consistent with the Highway Code's speed and distance duties (art. 10.1, 18.1)
and the exam brochures above. No figures are asserted beyond the Code's.

## Left out on purpose
- Fines, fees, demerit or penalty figures; the Flemish and Brussels provisional-licence details
  (only the Walloon model 3 rules, labelled Walloon, are asked); the B-licence-to-125 cc rules;
  the 2027 novice alcohol limit (announced, not in force); tyre tread figures for motorcycles.
- Questions about the exam itself (count, pass mark, languages, exemptions, ages to sit it):
  they are on the landing and in `lib/examFacts.ts` only.
- Sibling lanes: kept to motorcycle rules; bedriving's car facts (L sign, phone holder, insurance
  certificate, 0.5 g/l limit, regional speed limits as car questions) are not repeated as the same
  stem. Stems were compared with every motorcycle bank on disk (uk, us, dk, se, fi, pt, gr, hu,
  de, nz, za, sg) and items above 0.8 similarity were rewritten.
