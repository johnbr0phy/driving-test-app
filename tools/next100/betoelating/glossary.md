# Glossary for `betoelating` (Dutch, Flemish usage, written natively)

Register: questions neutral or with "je" where the candidate acts (as on vlaanderen.be and in the
exam); a doctor addresses a patient with "u" inside quoted speech. Landing and UI copy in "je".
Flemish school terms from the leerstofoverzicht. Typography: decimal comma (9,81 m/s², 0,40 mol/L),
a space before % and units, “ ” for quoted speech, Unicode sub- and superscripts in formulas
(H₂SO₄, 10⁻⁴), the minus sign − in maths. No em-dashes.
