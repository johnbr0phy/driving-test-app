# Sources for `betoelating` (Flemish medical and dental admission exam, toelatingsexamen arts en tandarts, in Dutch)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Dutch on the public leerstofoverzicht. Nothing
was copied from past official exams, the official sample questions (voorbeeldvragen), the
ijkingstoets or a commercial course. VAARDIG texts and all study figures in them are invented
practice material (fictional data). Checked 2026-11-07 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed on official pages)
- Vlaamse overheid, "Examenonderdelen en leerstofoverzicht van de toelatingsexamens" (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/toelatingsexamens/examenonderdelen
  - KIW (Kennis en Inzicht in de Wetenschappen): "Je krijgt 10 vragen per vak en dus in totaal 40
    meerkeuzevragen voor KIW" (biologie, fysica, chemie, wiskunde), mainly insight; a formularium is
    provided.
  - GC (Generieke Competenties): EMCO (Empathie en Communicatie), "15 vragen waarbij je één juist
    antwoord moet kiezen uit vier antwoordalternatieven", (inter)personal skills; VAARDIG
    (Verbinden, Analyseren, Redeneren, Integreren), 25 questions on a short scientific text with
    figures on a health theme.
  - Leerstofoverzicht KIW 2026 (PDF, read 2026-11-07), the topic list the bank follows:
    https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1779172623/leerstof_kiw_2025_finaal_v3_0_yfxg4j.pdf
  - Formularium 2026 (PDF): constants used in the physics items (g = 9,81 m/s², R = 8,31,
    c(water) = 4,19·10³ J/(kg·K), ℓs = 335·10³ J/kg, k = 9·10⁹, µ0 = 4π·10⁻⁷, e = 1,60·10⁻¹⁹ C):
    https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1763468228/formularium2025_18.09.24_v2_jj9pbp.pdf
- Vlaamse overheid, "Het antwoordblad van je toelatingsexamen invullen" (read 2026-11-07):
  "+3 punten voor een correct antwoord", "0 punten als je de vraag openlaat", "-1 punt voor een fout
  antwoord"; two bubbles count as wrong; whole numbers only.
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/toelatingsexamens/examenonderdelen/het-antwoordblad-van-je-toelatingsexamen-invullen
  So KIW is 40 × 3 = 120 points and GC 40 × 3 = 120 points.
- Vlaamse overheid, "Verloop van het toelatingsexamen" (read 2026-11-07): KIW from 9.45, 3.5 hours
  (not before 11.30); GC from 14.30, 1.5 hours, ends 16.00 (not before 15.00); a simple calculator
  is provided in the room, no own calculator or electronics.
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/toelatingsexamens/verloop
- Vlaamse overheid, toelatingsexamens home (read 2026-11-07): 2027 dates arts Friday 2 July,
  tandarts Saturday 3 July, dierenarts Sunday 4 July; registration 1 March to 17 May 2027; on
  paper; the 2026 exam was at Brussels Expo (Heizel); a new leerstofoverzicht for 2027 will be
  published "this autumn", aligned with the doorstroomfinaliteit science courses, small changes
  possible.
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/toelatingsexamens
- Vlaamse overheid, news "Toelatingsexamen arts, tandarts en dierenarts in 2026 op papier"
  (read 2026-11-07): 2026 arts 2 July, tandarts 3 July, dierenarts 4 July, on paper at one central
  location after the fraud controversy of 2025; one exam a year.
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/toelatingsexamens/nieuws/toelatingsexamen-arts-tandarts-en-dierenarts-in-2026-op-papier
- Vlaamse overheid, FAQ 2026 (read 2026-11-07): exams in Dutch only, not translated; CLEAR was
  replaced by EMCO in GC from 2026; KIW unchanged.
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/toelatingsexamens/veel-gestelde-vragen-over-de-toelatingsexamens-2026

## Pass rule and quota
- UAntwerpen, "Wanneer ben je geslaagd op het toelatingsexamen?" (read 2026-11-07): at least 60/120
  on KIW and at least 60/120 on GC, and ranked among the first n candidates for all of Flanders;
  n = 1878 for arts in 2026; ties with place n are admitted.
  https://www.uantwerpen.be/nl/studeren/aanbod/alle-opleidingen/geneeskunde/toelatingsexamen/je-resultaat/
- UAntwerpen, "Hoe ziet het toelatingsexamen arts eruit?" (read 2026-11-07): four series of 10
  multiple-choice questions in KIW, sample with four options, simple calculator allowed; EMCO 15 and
  VAARDIG 25 questions with four options.
  https://www.uantwerpen.be/nl/studeren/aanbod/alle-opleidingen/geneeskunde/toelatingsexamen/hoe-ziet-het-examen-eruit/
- VRT NWS, 13 July 2026 (read 2026-11-07): 6,123 arts candidates, 1,881 favourably ranked (30.7%);
  tandarts 2,116 and 280 (13.2%); dierenarts 843 and 240.
  https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/13/startquota-geneeskunde-ingevuld/
- Vlaamse overheid, news "Startquota voor arts, tandarts en dierenarts ingevuld" (13 July 2026, re-read
  2026-11-07 by the reviewer): 1,881 arts, 280 tandarts and 240 dierenarts favourably ranked, matching VRT.
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/onderwijs-en-vorming/toelatingsexamens/nieuws/startquota-voor-arts-tandarts-en-dierenarts-ingevuld
- Reviewer re-check 2026-11-07: the toelatingsexamens home page links sample exams (2026) and
  questions of past exams, as the landing FAQ says; 2027 dates and the new 2027 leerstofoverzicht confirmed.
- VRT NWS, 23 May 2026 (from the brief, not re-read): 10,412 registrations, 6,941 for arts.
  https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/05/23/recordaantal-inschrijvingen-toelatingsexamens/

## Not confirmed
- No official weight of subjects inside KIW beyond 10 questions each; no published split of
  points between EMCO and VAARDIG beyond question counts (both 3 points per question).
- The tandarts exam has the same structure (EMCO for tandarts was a separate set in 2026, one
  question neutralised); this bank does not distinguish arts and tandarts.

## How Tigertest models it
- Four tests of 50: 10 biologie, 10 fysica, 10 chemie, 10 wiskunde (the real KIW) plus 4 EMCO and
  6 VAARDIG (a shortened GC block, 15 : 25 kept roughly). Equal weight, no guessing correction
  (it is explained on the landing). 70% (35 of 50) is Tigertest's readiness target: answering every
  question, the real half-points line needs 25 of 40 right per part (3c − (40 − c) = 60), and the
  quota ranks above that (about 31% of arts candidates got a place in 2026).
- VAARDIG is modelled as one short text per question, without figures (data written in words).
- Law in EMCO explanations: Belgian wet betreffende de rechten van de patiënt, 22 augustus 2002
  (inzage, weigering), beroepsgeheim.
