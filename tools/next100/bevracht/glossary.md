# Glossary for `bevracht` (Belgian Dutch, as used by GOCA, the Wegcode and EU texts)

Register: questions and explanations in "u" (like the official exam), landing and dashboard copy
in "je". Belgian usage: "rijbewijs", "voorlopig rijbewijs", "pinker" (colloquial), "gsm",
"nummerplaat", "autosnelweg", "bebouwde kom". Decimal comma (0,09 mg/l, 2,55 m, 16,50 m),
"km/u", "MTM" (maximaal toegelaten massa).
