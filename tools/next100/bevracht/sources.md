# Sources for `bevracht` (Belgium truck licence C/CE theory and driver CPC, vakbekwaamheid)

Official sources first (the source watch reads the first three https URLs). Checked 2026-11-07
unless noted. The bank is written in Dutch from these texts; no question copies an official or
commercial item. The official Belgian question bank is not public.

## The exam (format facts: landing, FAQ and examFacts only, never the bank)
- GOCA (Autocontrole), "Cat. C/C1 met het bewijs van vakbekwaamheid", Doc C-C1.CAP.NL.TH-PR,
  ACT-GOCA 16.11.2022, Brussels Hoofdstedelijk Gewest (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.autocontrole.be/sites/default/files/Doc%20C-C1.CAP_.NL_.TH-PR_13.pdf
  - Combined theory exam (rijbewijs C/C1 plus vakbekwaamheid) has three proeven:
    A. 100 multiple-choice questions, maximum 100 minutes: 50 on the licence, 50 on
       vakbekwaamheid. 2 or 3 answer options per question, on a computer screen. Pass with at
       least 80 of 100. A candidate who scores at least 40 of 50 on one of the two halves is
       exempt from that half for 3 years.
    B. 8 case studies of 5 multiple-choice questions (40 questions), maximum 80 minutes; pass
       with at least 32 of 40; a finished case study cannot be reopened.
    C. Oral test, maximum 60 minutes: 10 questions chosen by computer, written preparation
       allowed, each question scored out of 10, pass with at least 80 of 100.
  - A B licence is required for the combined theory exam; the provisional C/C1 licence only from
    18. Theory taught at an approved driving school (minimum 6 hours) or self-study; unlimited
    retakes. Passing one proef exempts it for 3 years.
  - Exam languages: Dutch, French or German; others only with a sworn interpreter appointed by the
    centre and paid by the candidate. Sign-language interpreter possible.
  - Practical exam: manoeuvres plus a road test (minimum 45 minutes) that includes setting the
    tachograph and questions on its use; for code 95 a further basic qualification practical test.
  - Leerstof vakbekwaamheid (as listed): power train for optimal use, traffic and work-accident
    risks, safety devices, crime and illegal immigrants, fuel use, physical and mental health,
    security of goods, load safety, social climate and road transport regulation, physical risks,
    emergencies, company image, economic climate of road haulage, goods transport regulation.
    Reference list: https://mobilit.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/ref1cNL.pdf
- GOCA, "Cat. C/C1", Doc C-C1.NL.TH-PR, ACT-GOCA 16.11.2022, Brussels (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.autocontrole.be/sites/default/files/Doc%20C-C1.NL_.TH-PR_13.pdf
  - Theory exam for C or C1 alone: 50 multiple-choice questions, maximum 50 minutes, 2 or 3
    options, pass with at least 41 of 50. Minimum age for the theory exam 17 years 9 months.
  - Leerstof (used for the licence categories of the bank): signs, priority, overtaking, stopping
    and parking, motorways, level crossings, tunnels, speed, licence, alcohol, defensive driving,
    first aid, distances, load safety, eco-driving, documents; drivers' hours and recording
    equipment; vehicle and transport documents; weights, dimensions and speed limiters; field of
    view; checks before departure; engines, fluids, fuel and electrical systems, transmission;
    lubrication and antifreeze; tyres; brakes and speed limiters; coupling; fault finding;
    preventive maintenance; cruise control.
  - Licence scope: C over 3,500 kg, at most 8 passengers besides the driver, trailer up to 750 kg;
    C1 over 3,500 kg up to 7,500 kg. Ages without vakbekwaamheid: provisional C or C1 18,
    definitive C1 18, definitive C 21 (a C road test passed at 18 gives C1, exchanged for C at 21).
  - Provisional licence model 3: 12 months, not renewable; 'L' sign; no driving abroad on it.
- Vlaanderen.be, "Rijbewijs C en D: stap voor stap" (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.vlaanderen.be/rijbewijs-c-en-d-stap-voor-stap
  - Confirms 50 questions / at least 41 right (C without code 95, from 17 years 9 months) and 100
    questions / at least 80 right (with code 95, from 18, B licence required), plus 8 case
    studies of 5 and an oral of 10 questions within one hour; medical exam (group 2 certificate);
    code 95 kept by 35 hours of further training every 5 years in five 7-hour modules.
- Brussels: be.brussels page on the C/C1 licence (oral part described):
  https://be.brussels/nl/transport-mobiliteit/rijbewijs-en-kentekenbewijs/rijbewijs-behalen/rijopleiding-en-rijexamen-voor-rijbewijs-cc1
  (could not be loaded on 2026-11-07; the GOCA Brussels brochure above gives the same parts).
- Autoveiligheid (Flemish centres), downloads cat. C/C1 and cat. C/C1 vakbekwaamheid:
  https://autoveiligheid.be/downloaden/cat-c-c1 (HTTP 500 on 2026-11-07; listed for the watch).
- Not confirmed from an official page: whether English is offered (GOCA lists only Dutch, French
  and German, with a sworn interpreter for others), so the landing says interpreter only.

## Rules in the bank
- Royal Decree of 1 December 1975, Wegcode / Code de la route, consolidated text on the federal
  site (read 2026-11-07):
  https://www.codedelaroute.be/fr/reglementation/1975120109~hra8v386pu
  Articles used: 11.2 (90 km/h on motorways and four-lane roads with a physical central divider
  for vehicles and combinations over 3,5 t); 11.3 (60 km/h outside built-up areas on other roads
  for vehicles over 7,5 t on pneumatic tyres); 17.2 6° (no overtaking in precipitation on
  motorways, roads for motor vehicles and four-lane roads for goods vehicles over 7,5 t, except
  slow-vehicle lanes and tractors); 18.2 (outside built-up areas vehicles over 7,5 t or longer
  than 7 m keep at least 50 m between each other); 19 (indicating, checking before turning);
  21.3 (on 3+ lanes, vehicles over 3,5 t only in the two right lanes, except to follow F13/F15);
  21.4 (on motorways no crossovers, no U-turn, no stopping or parking except on E9a parking lanes, no towing with an emergency coupling); 27.5.2 (in built-up areas no parking of vehicles over 7,5 t on
  the public road for more than eight consecutive hours); 30.1 (dip within 50 m when following);
  45 (load: not hide lights or plate, long loads secured, fasteners in good condition, open doors
  within the outline); 46 (loaded width 2,55 m or 2,60 m, height 4 m, rear overhang 1 m (Brussels,
  Wallonia), never past the front); 47 (overhang over 1 m: square panel 0,50 m, red and white,
  plus red light and orange reflectors when lights are needed); 48bis (orange-plated dangerous
  goods vehicles use motorways unless necessary; 48bis2 signs C24a/b/c ban them); 51 (triangle 30 m on ordinary roads, 100 m on
  motorways; reflective vest; 51.3 fallen load signalled like a broken-down vehicle); signs C21 (laden mass), C23 (goods vehicles), C25/C27/C29 (length,
  width, height including load), C35 and C39 (C39: no overtaking by goods vehicles over 3,5 t).
  Art. 87: the 1975 regulation ceases on 1 June 2027, replaced by federal and regional codes; the
  bank follows the text in force in November 2026 and avoids figures the regions set differently.
- Alcohol: Law of 16 March 1968, art. 34: 0,5 g/l blood (0,22 mg/l exhaled air); professional
  drivers 0,2 g/l (0,09 mg/l) since 1 January 2015. Federal Police (read 2026-11-01 by bedriving):
  https://www.police.be/5337/node/19014
- Regulation (EC) No 561/2006 on driving times, breaks and rest, as amended by Regulation (EU)
  2020/1054 (Mobility Package), EUR-Lex consolidated text:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/561/oj
  Used: 9 h daily driving, 10 h twice a week; 56 h a week; 90 h in two consecutive weeks; 45 min
  break after 4 h 30, split 15 then 30; daily rest 11 h, reduced 9 h at most three times between
  weekly rests, split 3 + 9; daily rest within 24 h; multi-manning 9 h within 30 h; weekly rest
  45 h, reduced at least 24 h, compensation en bloc before the end of the third week, at the
  latest after six 24-hour periods; regular weekly rest not in the vehicle; return home every
  four weeks; art. 9 ferry/train interruption (at most twice, at most 1 h, with a bed or
  couchette); art. 12 exception (up to 1 h, or 2 h after a 30-min break, to reach home or the
  operational centre for weekly rest).
- Regulation (EU) No 165/2014 on tachographs, as amended by 2020/1054:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/165/oj
  Used: one personal driver card, 5 years; damaged, lost or stolen card reported and replaced,
  driving on printouts for at most 15 days; records of the current day and the previous 56 days
  presented at a roadside check (since 31 December 2024); country symbol at the first suitable
  stop after a border; manual entries; four activity modes; card colours (driver white, company
  yellow, workshop red, control blue).
- Commission Regulation (EU) No 581/2010: driver card downloaded at least every 28 days, vehicle
  unit every 90 days: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2010/581/oj
- Directive 2002/15/EC (working time of mobile road transport workers): average 48 h, at most
  60 h in one week; break of 30 min after 6 h work, 45 min over 9 h; night work 10 h:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/15/oj
- Council Directive 96/53/EC as amended (dimensions): width 2,55 m (conditioned bodies 2,60 m),
  height 4 m, rigid vehicle 12 m, articulated vehicle 16,50 m, road train 18,75 m:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1996/53/oj
- Directive 92/6/EEC as amended (speed limiters, N3 and N2 goods vehicles limited to 90 km/h):
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1992/6/oj
- Directive (EU) 2022/2561 (driver CPC, codified): initial qualification and 35 hours of periodic
  training every five years, code 95 on the licence:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2561/oj
- EN 12195-1 and the European Commission's best practice guidelines on cargo securing for road
  transport (2014): securing forces 0,8 of the load's weight forward and 0,5 sideways and
  backwards; LC and STF on the strap label:
  https://transport.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2016-09/2014-cargo-securing-guidelines.pdf
- CMR Convention (Geneva, 1956): the consignment note (vrachtbrief) in three originals.
