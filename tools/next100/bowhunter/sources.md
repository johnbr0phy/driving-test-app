# Sources for `bowhunter` (Bowhunter education course test, NBEF / IHEA-USA)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the IHEA-USA bowhunter education
standards and the NBEF-approved course study guide listed below. No course quiz, unit review,
final exam, instructor test bank, state exam, past paper or commercial practice question was
used or reworded, and no training provider is named in a question. Checked 2026-11-19.

## Exam owner and content standard (official)
- International Hunter Education Association USA, "IHEA-USA Education Standards: Bowhunter
  Education" (pages 3.2 to 3.23, digital 2023 edition), hosted by the National Bowhunter
  Education Foundation at
  https://nbef.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/IHEAUSA_HunterEduStd_Bow_Digital23.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). The minimum content every bowhunter education course must teach, in five
  areas: Bowhunter Education Overview (purpose, four ways bowhunting differs from firearm hunting,
  motivations, funding by license fees and the 1937 Federal Aid in Wildlife Restoration Act);
  Equipment & Accessories (vertical and horizontal bows, arrow shafts, fletching, nocks, points,
  three broadhead types, mechanical heads for 50 pounds or more, matching equipment, sights,
  quivers, finger and arm protection); Bowhunting Safety (falls and bleeding injuries, the no dry
  fire rule, inspection, cases, tree stand types, the climbing sequence with a Prusik knot, three
  points of contact, range set-up with 300 yards of space, aerial targets with flu-flu arrows,
  hypothermia and heat exhaustion, first aid); Bow Shooting Skills, Techniques & Preparation
  (dominant eye and both eyes open, draw weight and limb bolts, wingspan divided by 2.5, distance
  estimation in 10-yard marks to 50 yards, effective range, shot angles, horizontal distance on
  uphill and downhill shots, vital organs, hunting the wind, four methods, layering, scent
  control, decoy safety); Responsibilities & Game Recovery (reasons for regulations, responsible
  behaviour, zone of shooting confidence, risks of long shots, waiting before trailing, blood
  sign, approaching downed game, tagging, field dressing, heat, dirt and moisture).
- National Bowhunter Education Foundation, home page, https://nbef.org/ (fetched 2026-11-19).
  Gave: NBEF runs bowhunter education (online course link to bowhunter-ed.com, crossbow
  education, tree stand safety videos, a crossbow inspection checklist, state coordinators) and
  links the IHEA-USA standards above.
- Bowhunter Ed, "Official NBEF Bowhunter Safety Course Online",
  https://www.bowhunter-ed.com/national/ (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: the online course is
  approved and accepted by the National Bowhunter Education Foundation; course produced by
  Kalkomey Enterprises with NBEF.

  ethics, landowner and companion courtesy, the rule of first blood, fair chase, the four Cs,
  the Bowhunter's Creed; bow types, compound draw weight reduction of 50 percent or more, draw
  length and weight (23 to 30 inches; 25 to 35 pounds for younger shooters), dominant eye
  triangle test; shafts (wood unsafe in compounds and crossbows, carbon, solid fiberglass for
  bowfishing), spine and archer's paradox (err on the stiff side, smaller number stiffer, 260 to
  500), arrow length, balance point 10 to 16 percent forward of centre, fletching (cock fletch,
  offset 120 degrees, helical 3 degrees, flu-flu, all right-wing or all left-wing feathers),

  outdoor preparedness (three primary hazards, arrow-wound first aid, duplicate arrow for medics).

## Format (what is and is not published)
- NBEF and IHEA-USA publish content standards, not a national exam. Each state or province runs
  its own course (online, classroom, field day) and its own final test, with its own length and
  pass mark. The study guide notes some states require a bowhunter education course before
  bowhunting. So the 50-question tests, the eleven-category split and the 80% readiness line are
  Tigertest's, and the landing says so.

## Left out on purpose
- Seasons, bag limits, minimum draw weights, crossbow legality, blaze orange rules, ages and which
  states require the course: they vary by state and are only ever stated that way.
- Fees, fines and course prices.
- Facts already asked in the `hunter` bank are asked from a different angle or left out (let-off,
  three points of contact, quartering-away aim, 30-minute wait, 40 F spoilage).
