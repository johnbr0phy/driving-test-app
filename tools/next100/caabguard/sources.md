# Sources: Alberta Basic Security Training (ABST) exam, `caabguard`

Every question in the bank is original, written from the rules and facts below. No provincial exam
item, sample paper, the manual's own "Check Your Knowledge" or post-test questions, or any prep
site's practice questions were used, even reworded. All sources fetched 2026-11-16 (WebFetch where
the site allowed it, otherwise curl with a browser user agent; PDFs read as text with pdftotext).

## Official: the licence and the exam (Government of Alberta, Security Programs)

- Security service worker licence (alberta.ca): https://www.alberta.ca/security-service-worker-licence
  - Fetched 2026-11-16. Licence issued under the Security Services and Investigators Act; licensed
    roles: security guard, patrol dog handler, alarm responder, loss prevention worker, body guard.
    Requirements: over 18, eligible to work in Canada, English at Canadian Language Benchmark 5, no
    criminal record, charges or investigation; complete an approved security training course
    (the Alberta Basic Security Training Course or an Accredited Basic Security Training Course,
    through an approved training provider) and score 80% or higher on the provincial final exam,
    or hold a valid licence from another Canadian province or territory. Exam rewrites and
    challenges through Security Programs. Licence valid 2 years, portable between employers; baton
    needs employer approval and a 40-hour baton course, recertified every 36 months; training
    licence 30 days, applied for by the employer. The fee is not used anywhere.
- SSIA Provincial Examination Process, Schedule A (January 2015, v2.0, open.alberta.ca):
  https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/698c1720-9dbf-44b2-9978-492b2a0f08cc/resource/6604778b-2c73-44bb-9487-3624d7131bd1/download/sched-a-examination-process-handout-january-2015-1.pdf
  - Fetched 2026-11-16. A provincially administered final exam after the approved course, pass not
    less than 80%, proctored by a Ministry or certified proctor; rewrites as many times as necessary
    for up to two years from the start of the course; challenge exams by written request to the
    Registrar. Landing and examFacts only; never in a question.
- Alberta Basic Security Training Participant Manual, third edition (Jan 2014, revised Oct 2014),
  Alberta Solicitor General and Ministry of Public Security, produced by Lethbridge College:
  https://open.alberta.ca/publications/alberta-basic-security-training-participant-manual-third-edition
  (PDF: https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/8afdb67c-38ce-4249-b1a4-d3fdc2a179ca/resource/e79e1511-cc8b-4f24-8bf5-c1c9ce4ccd95/download/abst-particpants-manual-oct-2014-2.pdf)
  - Fetched 2026-11-16 and read in full. This is the course outline and the blueprint: seven modules
    (1 Introduction to the Security Industry; 2 The Canadian Legal System and Security
    Professionals; 3 Basic Security Procedures; 4 Communication; 5 Documentation and Evidence;
    6 Emergency Response Procedures; 7 Health and Safety). Used for observe, deter, report; patrol
    by foot and vehicle; access control; crowds; substance use signs; media; traffic hand and
    whistle signals; post orders; shift start and handover; working with a partner; use of force
    decision factors; LEAPS (listen, empathize, ask, paraphrase, summarize); barriers, bias,
    discrimination, harassment; uncooperative, hostile and intoxicated persons; interviews and open
    and closed questions; notebooks (ink, no blank lines, single-line corrections initialled,
    24-hour clock, five Ws and how, objective facts); the phonetic alphabet; statements and reports;
    evidence types and scene protection; court and subpoenas; alarm sensors, transmitters and control
    panels; fire classes A to D and PASS; bomb threats (RCMP checklist, no radios within 50 m of a
    suspicious item, search public areas first, left to right, floor to ceiling); evacuation; risk
    levels; CCOHS hazard categories; shift work, sleep and nutrition.
  - Where the 2014 manual quotes law, the bank uses the current text below instead.

## Not available or not confirmed

- Alberta publishes no question count, time limit or topic weights for the ABST exam. Prep sites
  guess (about 50 questions, about an hour); the landing does not state a count.
- The 2015 exam process document is the newest the Ministry publishes; the 2-year rewrite window is
  stated "per the Ministry's exam process document" on the landing, not in questions.

## Left out on purpose

- Fees, fines and penalty amounts (they change).
- The Employment Standards Code appendix of the 2014 manual: the Code has been amended many times
  since 2014 (rest periods, general holiday eligibility, overtime agreements), so it is not asked.
- AGLC ProServe and ProTect requirements, and the manual's "excited delirium" section; not asked.
- Investigator, locksmith and business licensee rules beyond what a security worker needs.
- Ontario, BC and other provinces' rules: siblings `caguard` (Ontario) and `cabcguard` (BC).
