# Sources for `cagas3` (TSSA Gas Technician 3, G.3, Ontario)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the rules: TSSA's own policy and exam
documents, Ontario regulations on e-Laws, the TSSA Gaseous Fuels Code Adoption Document amendment
(public TSSA text), and public safety and science references. No TSSA exam item, CSA training unit
(the CSA/TSSA gas trades training modules are paid and copyrighted), CSA B149.1 code text, school
quiz, commercial prep test or app question was used or reworded. TSSA publishes no G.3 sample or
practice exam (TSSA Cert and Exam FAQs). Checked 2026-11-23.

## Exam owner: TSSA (official)
- TSSA, "Gas Technician/Oil Burner Technician Policies and Procedures", March 2025 (published
  April 2, 2025), https://tssa.org/sites/default/files/2025-04/Gas%20and%20Oil%20Policy%20and%20Procedure_G3-G2%20changes%20and%20H2%20Additions_April%202%202025.pdf,
  fetched 2026-11-23. Gave: the G.3 is the entry certificate, made under O. Reg. 215/01; the G.3
  program is modules 1 to 9 (suggested 180 hours): 1 Protect Self and Others, 2 Fasteners, Hand
  Tools and Power Tools, 3 Properties, Characteristics, Safe Handling of Propane and Natural Gas,
  4 Installation Codes, Acts and Regulations, 5 Introduction to Electricity, 6 Technical Manuals,
  Specifications, Schematics, Drawings and Graphs, 7 Customer Relations, 8 Introduction to Piping
  and Tubing Systems, 9 Introduction to Gas Appliances; a TSSA written certification exam at the end
  of each level; at least 75% in each module, 75% on the TSSA exam and 75% on the practical
  evaluation; exam and practical within one year of program completion; certificate expires on the
  second anniversary of the holder's birth date after issue; notify TSSA within 30 days of an
  address change; three failed attempts mean an accredited program before another try; the G.3
  challenge route; section 6.5 (G.2 entry).
- TSSA, "New Requirement for Fuels G2 Enrolment: Proof of G3 Work Experience for Challenge
  Certificate Holders", July 4, 2025, https://www.tssa.org/new-requirement-fuels-g2-enrolment-proof-g3-work-experience-challenge-certificate-holders,
  fetched 2026-11-23. Gave: from January 1, 2026, G.3 holders certified by challenge need 450 hours
  of G.3 work experience before a G.2 program (landing only).
- TSSA, "TSSA Certification Examination Preparation Guide", March 16, 2026,
  https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/CE-TSSA%20Certification%20Exam%20Preparation%20Guide-Apr12026-Guide_0.pdf,
  fetched 2026-11-23. Gave: exams are multiple choice, 50 to 165 questions by certificate, up to
  3.5 hours; Fuels pass mark 75%; permitted materials for G.3 (open book): Technical Standards and
  Safety Act, 2000, all Fuels Regulations and Code Adoption Documents, CSA B149.1, CSA B149.2,
  optional B149.2S1; an unsuccessful candidate receives a breakdown by area. TSSA does not publish
  the G.3 question count or time limit, so the landing says so.
- TSSA, "Cert and Exam FAQs", 14 Feb 2023, https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2023-11/Cert-and-Exam-FAQs--14-FEB-2023.pdf,
  fetched 2026-11-23: TSSA does not provide practice exams.

## Law (Ontario e-Laws, official)
- O. Reg. 215/01, Fuel Industry Certificates, https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/010215,
  current version read 2026-11-23 (page rendered in a browser; plain fetch is refused). Gave:
  s. 1 definitions of "direct supervision" (on site, in close proximity) and "general supervision"
  (may or may not be on site, readily available); s. 4 renewal and the one-year limit; s. 5 three
  successive failed attempts; s. 17 no transfer; s. 19 30-day address notice; s. 20 G.1 any input;
  s. 21 G.2 up to 400,000 Btuh; s. 22 G.3 scope: under general supervision of a G.1, G.2 or DA
  holder, with documented sign-off, install, test, activate or purge piping or tubing under
  2-1/2 in downstream of the meter or propane service valve up to the appliance control valve
  (including the pressure test tag), reactivate a previously installed appliance, clean and
  lubricate, clean, remove or replace vent connectors, venting or draft control devices; never the
  initial activation of a new or newly converted appliance; anything in the supervisor's scope under
  direct supervision; s. 53 the direct supervisor is responsible; s. 55 homeowner exemptions.
- O. Reg. 212/01, Gaseous Fuels, https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/010212, current version
  read 2026-11-23. Gave: s. 2 application and exclusions (vehicles, boats, propane storage under
  B149.2); s. 4 approved appliances; s. 6 certificate required, in-presence exception; s. 7 first
  use examined by the distributor; s. 11 duty of employer; s. 12 notify an inspector forthwith of
  CO poisoning, asphyxiation, explosion or fire, scene not disturbed; s. 13 unacceptable condition
  and immediate hazard (shut off, oral notice to distributor, written notice to user, written
  notice to distributor within 14 days, affix a notice; removing the notice); s. 14 no immediate
  hazard, up to 90 days; s. 21 contractor registration (one year, 30-day address notice, vehicles
  marked).

## Code adoption (TSSA, public Ontario amendments)
- TSSA, "Gaseous Fuels Code Adoption Document Amendment" FS-279-26, published April 2, 2026,
  effective June 1, 2026, https://www.tssa.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/Gaseous-Fuels-CAD-Amendment-Fuels-CAD-FS-279-26.pdf,
  fetched 2026-11-23. Gave: Ontario adopts CSA B149.1-25 (and B149.3-25, B149.6, NFPA 160) with
  Ontario amendments, superseding FS-225-21; the most stringent of instructions and code prevails;
  conversion from oil, propane or electric (4.3.5); fire, flood or explosion damaged appliances
  (4.5.5); access opening 550 x 900 mm; garage return air; Director approval above Table 5.1
  pressures and over 125 psig outdoors; drip pocket depth 3 in or the pipe ID; burial 15 in
  (400 mm), 24 in (600 mm) under commercial driveways; manual shut-off valve placement and the
  50 ft (15 m) tagged valve; coated pipe test at 100 psig; the pressure test tag contents and the
  sticker option; boiler low-water cut-off and flow switch; relief discharge within 12 in of the
  floor; residential boiler under 300,000 Btu/hr venting; the October 15 to April 30 natural draft
  boiler check (CO over 100 ppm upstream of the draft control is an immediate hazard, tag expires
  May 1); water heaters in bedrooms and bathrooms; unvented barn heaters; dryer exhaust 3 ft and
  10 ft from air supply openings; chimney liner within 5 days residential, 30 days commercial, no
  tile liner in an exterior chimney; outdoor B vent; Annex C and G normative; Annex P rocky
  terrain; Annex Q schedules A to C (depressurization test steps). Only facts printed in this
  public TSSA document are asked about the code; CSA B149.1 clause figures that appear only in the
  paid CSA text are not used.

## Safety, gas properties and science (official or primary)
- ILO/WHO International Chemical Safety Cards, methane ICSC 0291,
  https://www.ilo.org/dyn/icsc/showcard.display?p_card_id=0291&p_version=2&p_lang=en, and propane
  ICSC 0319, https://www.ilo.org/dyn/icsc/showcard.display?p_card_id=0319&p_version=2&p_lang=en,
  fetched 2026-11-23. Gave: methane boiling point -161 C, vapour density 0.6, autoignition 537 C,
  explosive limits 5 to 15%; propane boiling point -42 C, vapour density 1.6, autoignition 450 C,
  explosive limits 2.1 to 9.5%, liquid density 0.5, vapour pressure 840 kPa at 20 C, frostbite.
- CCOHS, carbon monoxide chemical profile, https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/chem_profiles/carbon_monoxide.html,
  fetched 2026-11-23 (colourless, odourless, harms the blood's ability to carry oxygen, symptoms).
- CCOHS, hazardous energy control (lockout), https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/hsprograms/hazardous_energy.html,
  portable ladders, https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html (4-to-1 rule,
  1 m above the landing, three-point contact, not the top three rungs), hand tools,
  https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/hand_tools/general.html, fire extinguishers,
  https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/fire_extinguishers.html (classes A, B, C, D, K), and
  WHMIS pictograms, https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/chemicals/whmis_ghs/pictograms.html, all
  fetched 2026-11-23.
- Goodman GCVC96 gas furnace installation instructions (manufacturer, Canada and US),
  https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0253/0852/8730/files/Goodman_GCVC96_Installation_Manual__1.pdf,
  fetched 2026-11-23. Used only as an example of certified installation instructions: inlet supply
  range on the rating plate, manifold taps on the valve, 24 V gas valve, conversion by kit only,
  pipe capacity read from length and size, CFH = input / heating value, no derating by lowering
  manifold pressure. Every stem that uses a figure from instructions gives the figure in the stem.
- U.S. Energy Information Administration, "British thermal units (Btu)", https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/units-and-calculators/british-thermal-units.php,
  fetched 2026-11-23: natural gas about 1,036 Btu per cubic foot (used as "about 1,000"); propane
  per cubic foot is computed from ICSC densities and a heating value near 50 MJ/kg (about 2,500).
- Unit conversions and combustion arithmetic (1 kW = 3412 Btu/h, 1 psi = 27.7 in w.c., methane
  and propane combustion equations) are computed in the explanations; NIST SI guide,
  https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811, checked 2026-11-23.
