# Ontario Hunter Education Program exam bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the Ontario government's own pages and
the Ontario statute and regulation text below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from the
Ontario Hunter Education Course manual, its exam, any instructor's or online course provider's
quizzes, or any commercial prep site or app.

## The program and what it covers (Ontario government)

- Ontario.ca, Hunting in Ontario / hunter education (Ministry of Natural Resources), fetched
  2026-11-16: https://www.ontario.ca/page/hunter-education
  Most residents must pass the Ontario Hunter Education Course, including an exam, to buy
  hunting licences and tags and to join the Hunter Apprenticeship Safety Program. Two delivery
  options: in person with a certified instructor, or an online self-directed course. Topics:
  hunting safety and equipment, hunting regulations and laws, hunter responsibilities, wildlife
  management, hunting techniques, wildlife identification. Often combined with the Canadian
  Firearms Safety Course; both can be taken separately. Proof of completion is registered at a
  participating ServiceOntario location; from December 10, 2026, online submission through the
  new licensing system is also accepted. New residents (6 months) and some non-residents with
  recognized credentials are exempt. HASP: residents 12 to 14, mentor 18+, shared firearm and
  bag limit.
- Ontario Hunter Education Program (OHEP, the ministry's program site, administered by the
  Ontario Federation of Anglers and Hunters for the ministry), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.ohep.net
  Two options offered by the ministry: instructor-led in person (one day or split) with an
  examination with the instructor, or online self-paced with an examination online. Same six
  topic areas.
- Ontario Hunting Regulations Summary 2026, chapter "Ontario Hunter Education Course" (updated
  March 9, 2026), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.ontario.ca/document/ontario-hunting-regulations-summary/ontario-hunter-education-course
  Course aims (respect regulations, prevent accidents, public safety); course plus licences
  allows bow hunting; gun hunting also needs the CFSC.

## Exam format (what could and could not be confirmed)

- Neither ontario.ca nor ohep.net publishes the exam's question count, time limit or pass mark
  for the instructor-led course. A provider listing reports 50 multiple-choice questions, open
  book; that is a guide figure only and is not used as a fact.
- HUNTERcourse.com, Official Ontario Hunter Education Course (the online course; the page says
  it is "a delegated provider for Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry" and is
  approved by the ministry), fetched 2026-11-16: https://www.huntercourse.com/canada/ontario/
  and FAQs https://www.huntercourse.com/canada/ontario/faqs.aspx
  The online course has 13 chapters, each with a quiz, and a final exam that must be passed
  "with a grade of at least 80%"; failed quizzes or exams can be retaken; Certificate of
  Completion taken to ServiceOntario. The app's 80% line follows this online-course pass mark;
  the copy says the instructor-led exam's count and pass mark are set by the course and not
  published by the ministry. The provider's course content was not read for questions.
- Ontario.ca, Hunter education (updated October 30, 2026), re-fetched 2026-11-16: from
  December 10, 2026 online submission of hunter accreditation is accepted through the new
  licensing system (FishHuntON); the licensing system is down December 7 to 9, 2026
  (https://www.ontario.ca/page/hunting-notices-and-updates). No question depends on the name of
  the licensing system.

## Left out on purpose

- Season dates, bag and possession limits that change by year and WMU, licence and tag fees,
  surcharge amounts, fines, reporting deadlines, moose tag allocation (changing in 2027) and
  paper-only tags (digital tags from 2027).
- Federal firearm licensing, storage, transport and the Vital Four ACTS and PROVE (covered by
  the CFSC banks, capal and crfsc); the PAL appears only as Ontario's own rule that gun hunters
  must carry it.
- Migratory bird hunting rules (federal), trapping licences and falconry details.
- Anything about the exam itself.
