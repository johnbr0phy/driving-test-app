# Red Seal Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic (carsrefrig): sources

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the tasks and ranges of variables of the
Red Seal Occupational Standard, Canada's federal halocarbon rules, the federal Environmental Code of
Practice and standard trade science (the refrigeration cycle, pressure-temperature relationships,
psychrometrics, basic electricity and controls). No Red Seal sample question, self-assessment item,
provincial study-guide item, textbook exercise or commercial practice question was copied or
paraphrased, and no training provider is named. Checked 2026-11-23.

## Exam breakdown (official)
- Red Seal, "Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic - Exam Information",
  https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/refrig-ac-mech/exam-information.shtml (fetched 2026-11-23):
  125 questions, all four-option multiple choice with one correct answer. Questions per Major Work
  Activity and task: A Performs common occupational skills 10 (A-1 safe and healthy workplace 8,
  A-2 continuous learning 1, A-3 communication and mentoring 1); B Performs routine trade activities
  20 (B-4 tools and equipment 5, B-5 organizes work 4, B-6 work site preparation 4, B-7 trade-specific
  activities 7); C Plans installation 16 (C-8 standard and high efficiency HVAC/R systems 6, C-9
  control systems 4, C-10 retrofits 6); D Performs installation 26 (D-11 HVAC/R systems 16, D-12
  control systems 10); E Performs commissioning 22 (E-13 commissions new and retrofitted HVAC/R and
  control system components 12, E-14 verifies system performance 10); F Performs maintenance and
  service 31 (F-15 maintains HVAC/R systems 9, F-16 services HVAC/R systems 13, F-17 maintains and
  services control systems 9). Question types: knowledge and recall 25 to 35%, procedural and
  application 40 to 50%, critical thinking 15 to 25%. Formulas and acronyms are provided at the
  sitting. This task numbering is the 2024 standard's.
- Red Seal, "Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic" trade page,
  https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/refrig-ac-mech.shtml (fetched 2026-11-23): links the new standard

  to the per-question breakdown above. Tigertest weights to the per-question breakdown on the current
  exam information page.
- Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Commission, "Refrigeration & Air Conditioning
  Mechanic Interprovincial Journeyperson Examination Information" (form dated 2026),
  https://saskapprenticeship.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Refrigeration-AC-Mechanic_Interprovincial-Journeyperson-Examination-Information_2026.pdf

## Standard (topics)
- Red Seal Occupational Standard: Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic (2024),
  https://red-seal.ca/_conf/assets/custom/docms/refrig-ac-mech/rsos.pdf (fetched 2026-11-23, read in
  full as text): task and sub-task list used as the topic list (A-1.01 to F-17.04), the ranges of
  variables (refrigerant types CFC, HCFC, HFC, HFO, HC, R-744, R-717; safety classes A1, A2L, A3,
  B2L; oils mineral, AB, POE, PVE, PAG, PAO; gases nitrogen, acetylene, oxygen; micron gauges,
  vacuum pumps, recovery units, A2L-rated equipment; glide, fractionation, bubble and dew point;
  CSA B52, CSA B51, ASHRAE Standard 34, the Environmental Code of Practice, CEC; ERV and HRV,
  economizers, chillers, cooling towers, ice machines, heat pumps, ECM and VFD motors, BAS).
- Red Seal Occupational Standard: Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic (2019),
  https://red-seal.ca/_conf/assets/custom/docms/refridgacmech/rsos.pdf (fetched 2026-11-23): the
  previous standard, compared task by task; no topic used here is absent from it except the
  2024 additions on retrofits, system integration and optimization (C-10, E-14, F-15.04).
- Skilled Trades Ontario, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems Mechanic,
  https://skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/refrigeration-and-air-conditioning-systems-mechanic
  (checked 2026-11-23): Ontario trade code 313A, compulsory trade, Red Seal endorsement available.

## Exam format (official)
- Red Seal Exam Preparation Guide, https://red-seal.ca/eng/resources/exam-prep-guide.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-23): four choices, one correct answer, pass mark 70%, writing time four hours,
  a calculator is provided if needed. No retake rule is given there (it is set by each province or
  territory), so the landing says to check with the apprenticeship authority.

## Law and federal practice
- Federal Halocarbon Regulations, 2022 (SOR/2022-110), https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-110/FullText.html
  (fetched 2026-11-23): s. 3 release prohibition; s. 14 only a certified person (valid
  environmental awareness certificate) may install, service or recover from an air-conditioning or
  refrigeration system; s. 17 leak tests on systems over 10 kg at least once every calendar year and
  no more than 15 months apart; s. 18 a leak dealt with within seven days of detection; s. 10 no
  charging before a leak test and repair; s. 9 no charging with halocarbon in order to leak test;
  s. 15 recovery into a container designed for that halocarbon; s. 16 recover everything and affix
  a notice when a system is permanently withdrawn; s. 24 releases of 100 kg or more reported within
  24 hours; s. 26 records kept at least five years. These regulations replace the Federal Halocarbon
  Regulations, 2003 (repealed 2022-05-20) and apply to federal systems; provincial ODS rules cover
  the rest, so questions name the federal regulations or say the rule "varies by province".
- Environment and Climate Change Canada, Environmental Code of Practice for the Elimination of
  Fluorocarbon Emissions from Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems, chapter 4,
  https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/canadian-environmental-protection-act-registry/publications/code-practice-eliminate-halocarbon-emissions/chapter-4.html
  (fetched 2026-11-23): venting is prohibited; dry nitrogen for pressure tests and as a brazing
  blanket; leak tests after start-up, when a leak is suspected and when parts are replaced;
  recovery cylinders grey with a yellow top or band; fill to no more than 80% of the cylinder's net
  weight at about 21 C; indirect heat only, no open flame; label the system with refrigerant, charge
  and oil after charging.
- Environment and Climate Change Canada, errata to the code of practice (June 2021),
  https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/eccc/documents/pdf/cepa/refrigerant-2015-errata-eng.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-23): deep evacuation targets stated in microns (300 microns in the corrected
  sections). The bank asks for "a deep vacuum confirmed on a micron gauge and a decay test", not a
  single number, since manufacturers' targets vary.

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it
- Ontario's written exam may differ in language and booking; the landing says to check with
  Skilled Trades Ontario or the provincial authority.
- Retake waits and fees vary by province; never stated.
- CSA B52 (Mechanical Refrigeration Code) is paywalled, so it is only referred to by name and
  purpose (machinery rooms, refrigerant quantity limits, relief devices), never by clause or figure.

## Left out deliberately
- Fees, wages, hours of apprenticeship, provincial certificate names and numbers.
- Exact refrigerant quantity limits, machinery room ventilation rates and relief settings from CSA
  B52 (paywalled, and they change with the edition).
- HFC consumption allowance dates under ODSHAR (they change with amendments).
- Anything about the exam itself (count, time, pass mark) as a question.

## Official sample questions
- Red Seal Sample Examination Questions, https://red-seal.ca/eng/questions.shtml?tid=202 (read
  2026-11-23 by the reviewer in a browser; the page loads its 20 questions from
  https://red-seal.ca/_conf/assets/json/questionnaires/202.json). The builder wrote the bank without
  seeing them. Reviewer check: every bank item compared with all 20 sample questions (normalised
  stem similarity, word overlap, shared option sets). No stem was close (highest ratio 0.61, a
  different question) and no option set was shared. Three items asked the same fact with the same
  answer as a sample question and were rewritten on a different angle: CARAC-SRV-006 (winding
  resistance sum, now identifying the common terminal), CARAC-TRD-009 (POE is hygroscopic, now what
  to do with an opened container) and CARAC-SRV-004 (TXV bulb losing its charge, now a loose bulb
  sensing room air).
