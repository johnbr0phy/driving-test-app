# Red Seal Truck and Transport Mechanic (carstruck): sources

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the tasks, sub-tasks and ranges of
variables of the Red Seal Occupational Standard for Truck and Transport Mechanic and standard trade
knowledge of heavy truck systems (engines and aftertreatment, air systems and brakes, electrical and
networks, drive trains, steering and suspension, trailers, climate control, hydraulics, hybrid and
electric vehicles). No Red Seal sample question, self-assessment item, provincial study-guide item,
textbook exercise or commercial practice question was copied or paraphrased, and no training
provider is named. Checked 2026-11-23.

## Exam breakdown (official)
- Red Seal, "Truck and Transport Mechanic - Exam Information",
  https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/trucktranspmech/exam-information.shtml (fetched 2026-11-23):
  135 questions, all four-option multiple choice with one correct answer. Questions per block and
  task: A Common occupational skills 8 (tasks 1-4: 2/3/2/1); B Engines and supporting systems 21
  (base engines 3, lubrication 2, intake 2, exhaust 3, engine management 4, fuel delivery 3, engine
  retarders 2, cooling 2); C Air systems and brake systems 17 (air 9, brakes 8); D Electrical and
  electronic systems 22 (batteries 3, charging 4, spark ignition 2, starting 4, electrical components
  5, vehicle management and electronics 4); E Drive trains 17 (clutches 2, manual and transfer cases
  3, automatic 2, automated 4, driveline 2, drive axles 3, drive train retarders 1); F Steering,
  chassis/frames, suspensions, tires, wheels and hubs 18 (steering 5, frames 2, suspension 4, hitches
  and couplers 3, tires wheels and hubs 4); G Cabs 5 (interior 3, exterior 2); H Trailers 8 (trailer
  components 5, heating and refrigeration 3); I Climate control 7 (heating and ventilation 3, air
  conditioning 4); J Hydraulic systems 8; K Hybrid and electric vehicles 4 (hybrid 2, EV 2). Question
  types: knowledge and recall under 10%, procedural and application 50 to 60%, critical thinking 35
  to 45%. Formulas (pi, area of a circle, force = area x pressure, Ohm's law, watts) and an acronym
  list are provided at the sitting.
- Red Seal, "Truck and Transport Mechanic" trade page, https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/truck-transp-mech.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-23): Red Seal in AB, BC, MB, NB, NL, NS, NT, NU, ON, PE, QC, SK, YT; NOC 72410;
  provincial titles (Truck and Coach Technician in Ontario; Heavy Equipment Technician - Truck and
  Transport Mechanic in Alberta and Nunavut); the RSOS is the basis of the exam.
- Red Seal, "Examination Weightings", https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/trucktranspmech/exam-weightings.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-23): block percentages A 6, B 15, C 13, D 16, E 12, F 13, G 4, H 6, I 6, J 6,
  K 3. This page still says 150 questions; the exam information page and Saskatchewan's 2025 sheet
  say 135, so Tigertest uses 135.
- Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Commission, "Truck Transport Mechanic
  Interprovincial Journeyperson Examination Information 2025",
  https://saskapprenticeship.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Truck-Transport-Mechanic_Interprovincial-Journeyperson-Examination-Information_2025.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-23, read as text): 135 questions with the same task counts, pass mark 70%, no
  practical examination in Saskatchewan.
- Tigertest's 50-question tests scale the 135 by largest remainder: A 3, B 8, C 6, D 8, E 6, F 7,
  G 2, H 3, I 3, J 3, K 1.

## Standard (topics)
- Red Seal Occupational Standard: Truck and Transport Mechanic (ESDC, 2022, Em15-3/39-2023E-PDF),
  https://www.red-seal.ca/_conf/assets/custom/docms/truck-transp-mech/rsos-eng.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-23, read as text): blocks A to K, tasks 1 to 41 and their sub-tasks used as the topic
  list; ranges of variables used for components and procedures (air system components: air lines,
  dryers, governors, compressors, chambers, tanks, valves, low air warning; stored energy released
  by draining tanks, caging spring brakes and fanning brakes; consumables air filters and
  desiccant; aftertreatment DOC, DPF, SCR and DEF; J1939 and CAN networks; automated transmissions;
  hitches and couplers; trailer heating and refrigeration; hydraulic components; hybrid and EV
  high-voltage safety in A-1.03, K-40 and K-41).
- Skilled Trades Ontario, Truck and Coach Technician,
  https://skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/truck-and-coach-technician (checked 2026-11-23,
  through a search extract; the page itself returned no readable text): Ontario trade code 310T,
  compulsory trade, Red Seal under the national title Truck and Transport Mechanic.

## Exam format (official)
- Red Seal Exam Preparation Guide, https://red-seal.ca/eng/resources/exam-prep-guide.shtml
  (fetched 2026-11-23): multiple choice with four options and one correct answer, one mark each,
  pass mark 70%, writing time four hours, a calculator provided if needed. No retake rule is given
  there (each province or territory sets it), so the landing says to check with the apprenticeship
  authority.

## Law
- Federal Halocarbon Regulations, 2022 (SOR/2022-110), https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-110/FullText.html
  (checked 2026-11-23): releases of halocarbon refrigerant are prohibited and recovery is done by
  certified persons. Used only for the two refrigerant handling items (reefer and cab A/C);
  provincial ODS rules cover most vehicles, so the items say venting is not allowed and recovery is
  certified, without a section number.

## Official sample questions (similarity check)
- Red Seal Sample Examination Questions, https://red-seal.ca/eng/questions.shtml?tid=243 (loads its
  20 questions from https://red-seal.ca/_conf/assets/json/questionnaires/243.json, read 2026-11-23),
  plus the three example questions on the exam information page. Used only to know which facts to
  stay away from. Builder's check: every bank item compared with all 20 sample questions
  (normalised stem similarity with difflib, word overlap of words over three letters, shared option
  texts). Highest stem ratio 0.63 (a different question, alternator voltage drop against the
  alternator 12 V 0 A item); no option set shared. Facts the samples ask were left out on purpose:
  final step before return to service, testing an ignition coil, battery load test voltage,
  alternator 0 A fault, low-power road test readings, coolant loss without external leaks, clutch
  brake adjustment, fluctuating oil pressure, ammeter connection, twin countershaft range grinding,
  fuel in oil after an injector job, oil classification for DPF engines, ignition switch run
  circuit, belt tension after running, starter armature contact, oil cooler after a bearing
  failure, antifreeze mix ratio, oil in the air reservoir, draining power steering fluid and an
  oversize brake drum.

## Sibling banks (repeat check)
- Every item was compared with the caauto (Red Seal Automotive), ase (ASE A1-A8), aseg1 (ASE G1),
  caairbrake (Ontario air brake), cdl, cdlx and the other Red Seal banks with the same stem-ratio and
  shared-key test. Items that asked the same fact with the same answer as a sibling were rewritten on
  another fact (22 items: parasitic draw timing, NTC sensor resistance, battery state of charge, ground cable first, CAN
  terminators, alternator ripple, starter clicks, short to ground, jumping out of gear, burnt ATF,
  slow pump at fast steering, airbag disable, blower on high only, condenser airflow, heater cold,
  automatic slack adjuster re-adjusting, S-cam rollers, trailer spring brakes on supply loss, hot
  oil pressure, Ohm's law, evacuation purpose, orange HV cables).

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it
- Retake waits, fees and booking vary by province or territory; never stated.
- Manufacturer figures (governor settings, pushrod stroke limits, torque, end play, build-up times)
  vary by OEM and component, so items say "OEM limit" or "specification" instead of numbers.

## Left out deliberately
- Fees, wages, apprenticeship hours, provincial certificate names and numbers.
- Jurisdictional inspection figures (CVSA out-of-service criteria, provincial inspection standards).
- Anything about the exam itself (count, time, pass mark) as a question.
