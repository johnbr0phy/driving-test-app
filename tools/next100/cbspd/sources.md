# CBSPD CSPDT bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from CBSPD's own technician exam outline and
the public federal guidance and regulations below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from the
CBSPD Technician Study Guide, CBSPD sample questions, HSPA material, past forms, or any commercial
review book, app or prep site. The bank was also checked against Tigertest's CRCST
(`data/crcst-questions.json`), CER (`data/hspa-cer-questions.json`), CHL
(`data/hspa-chl-questions.json`) and CST banks: items that repeated a fact and answer there were
rewritten (the most resistant organism, EPA registering surface disinfectants, the mechanical
monitoring key, the degassing and suction-unit items).

## Outline and format (Certification Board for Sterile Processing and Distribution, CBSPD)

- CBSPD, Technician Candidate Bulletin, C.S.P.D.T., 2026 Version, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cbspd.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Tech-CB-wo-App-2026.pdf
  Technician Examination Outline with eight weighted content areas: 1 Roles and
  Responsibilities 13%; 2 Life Science 9%; 3 Decontamination and Disinfection 19%;
  4 Preparation and Handling 16%; 5 Sterilization 18%; 6 Sterile Storage, Inventory Management
  and Distribution 11%; 7 Patient Care Equipment 6%; 8 Ethics 8%. Exam built from the 2020 Job
  Analysis: 125 scored questions plus 25 pretest questions, 150 multiple choice, 3 hours,
  computer based at PSI Services test centers (paper and pencil may still be used at some sites
  outside the U.S.), given four times a year in a one-week window. Passing SCALED score is 70;
  an official pass or fail is given at the end of a U.S. computer exam; no numeric score is
  reported to passing candidates; failing candidates get their score and a diagnostic report by
  content domain. Initial certification valid five years; recertification needs 100 points over
  the five years. Eligibility routes include 12 months of full-time SPD work, an SPD training
  course passed at 70 or higher, or 6 months of related allied health work plus 6 months of SPD
  work (as listed in the bulletin). Fees are never stated on Tigertest.
- CBSPD, Technician Candidate Bulletin, 2023 Version, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://cbspd.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tech-CB-wo-APP-923.pdf
  Same topic list, but older weights (19/11/18/20/16/9/5/2). The 2026 bulletin is the one in force
  and is the one used.
- CBSPD, Test Specifications, Technician Exam, 2020 Changes per JAS, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cbspd.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/CBSPD-Tech-New-with-125-questions-2020.pdf
  Same eight domains and percentages as the 2026 bulletin with numbered knowledge statements
  (1.K.1 to 8.K.2). It also prints question counts per domain (24, 14, 23, 25, 20, 11, 6, 2) that
  do not add up to the percentages of 125, so the percentages are used and the counts are not
  quoted anywhere.
- CBSPD, Technician certification page, fetched 2026-11-15: https://www.cbspd.net/tech/
  Links the 2026 bulletin; says the exam has 150 questions with 25 unscored pretest items.

Blueprint: the 2026 percentages of 50 are 6.5/4.5/9.5/8/9/5.5/3/4; Tigertest rounds to
7/4/9/8/9/6/3/4 per 50-question test. The 70% line on Tigertest's tests is a readiness target:
CBSPD's 70 is a scaled score, not a percent correct, which the landing says.

## Standards and guidance used in questions

- CDC, Guideline for Disinfection and Sterilization in Healthcare Facilities (2008, last update
  June 2024), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/guideline-disinfection-h.pdf
  Microbial resistance ranking; mycobacteria waxy wall; biofilms; D value and SAL definitions;
  friction and fluidics; enzymatic cleaners are not disinfectants and must be rinsed; neutral pH
  enzymatic detergents for delicate devices; alkaline cleaners corrosive; enzyme allergy; hard
  water cations; ultrasonic cavitation, endotoxin in used solution, sonication does not
  significantly kill bacteria; washer loading (hinged items open, no stacking, disassemble) and
  the 93 C for 10 minutes example; about 4-log reduction from cleaning endoscopes; OPA stains
  proteins gray, rinsing; glutaraldehyde 0.05 ppm ACGIH ceiling; MEC testing; 70% to 90% alcohol
  rinse; FDA and EPA roles for germicides and the EPA Registration Number; four parameters of
  steam, 97% dryness fraction, pressure's role; 121 C and 132 C examples; gravity and prevacuum
  air removal; steam flush-pressure pulse; Bowie-Dick purpose, uniform color change, do not use
  a failed unit; five chemical indicator classes; internal indicator visible means no external
  needed; do not use items whose indicator shows inadequate processing; CIs do not replace BIs;
  mechanical monitoring; BI organisms by method; BIs for implant loads with quarantine; single
  positive steam BI handling and recall rules; infection control risk assessment; EtO operating
  ranges and the two parameters not monitored; gas plasma by radio frequency and what it cannot
  process; dry heat at 170 C for 60 minutes; flash sterilization of implants only when
  unavoidable; large metal mass and wet packs; storage clearances (floor, 5 inches from ceiling,
  18 inches from sprinklers, 2 inches from outside walls), not under sinks, closed cabinets ideal,
  storage humidity not over 70%, dropped heat-sealed packages, event-related shelf life;
  physical barriers around decontamination; FDA treats reprocessors of single-use devices as
  manufacturers.
- CDC, Summary of recommendations (disinfection and sterilization), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/summary-recommendations.html
  Recommendations 13.a, 16.b, 16.d, 16.g, 18.d and 18.g as cited in explanations.
- CDC, Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease, infection control, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/creutzfeldt-jakob/hcp/infection-control/index.html
  High-infectivity tissues (brain, spinal cord, eyes); WHO methods beginning with 1N NaOH;
  incinerate disposables that contacted potentially prion-infected tissue.
- OSHA, 29 CFR 1910.1030 Bloodborne pathogens (eCFR current text), fetched 2026-11-15:

  https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/healthcare/hazardous-drugs/index.html

  from cases where antineoplastic drugs were given.

## Not confirmed, and how the copy words it

- AAMI ST79 and other paywalled AAMI documents were not read, so no ST79-only figure (washer test
  frequency, container weight limit, Bowie-Dick placement and time, air exchanges) is asked.
- Instrument care items that come from manufacturer care guides (BAC on tungsten carbide, bleach,
  saline, nylon brushes) are worded as what instrument makers advise; the device IFU always rules.
- Ergonomic items (heavy trays between waist and shoulder, pushing carts) follow general NIOSH
  safe-handling principles and are not tied to a numeric limit.
- Inventory items (par levels, lead time, consignment, stock rotation) are definitions, not
  CBSPD-specific rules.

## Deliberately left out

Fees, recertification fees, test-center details and any question about the exam itself; state
rules; product brand names; Trophon and other single-brand devices (named in the outline only as
examples).
