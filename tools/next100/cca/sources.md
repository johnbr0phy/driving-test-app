# Sources for `cca` (AHIMA Certified Coding Associate exam)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from public guidelines and our own summaries of
coding concepts. No AHIMA practice exam, study guide or commercial question bank was copied, and no
item reproduces a CPT code descriptor (CPT is AMA copyright) or needs a code-book look-up. Checked
2026-11-01.

## Exam format
- AHIMA, "How to Achieve the CCA Certification": https://ahima.org/certification/cca/ (checked
  2026-11-01): 105 items (90 scored, 15 pretest, randomly distributed), 2 hours, computer based at
  Pearson VUE; no moving back and forth, no flagging; passing score 300; high school diploma required,
  six months of coding experience or an AHIMA-approved coding program recommended. Fees kept off the site.
- AHIMA, CCA Exam Content Outline (Effective Date: 05/01/2022, (c) 2024 revision):
  https://ahima.org/media/oqul34x1/cca-content-outline-2024.pdf
  Domain 1 Clinical Classification Systems 30-34%, Domain 2 Reimbursement Methodologies 21-25%,
  Domain 3 Health Records and Data Content 13-17%, Domain 4 Compliance 12-16%, Domain 5 Information
  Technologies 6-10%, Domain 6 Confidentiality & Privacy 6-10%, with the task lists used for the
  category scope in spec.json.
- AHIMA, CCA 2026 Exam Required Code Books, effective 05/01/2026 (updated 01/13/2026):
  https://ahima.org/media/wejpch1q/cca_2026_codebook_list.pdf : one 2026 ICD-10-CM book and one 2026
  ICD-10-PCS book from the approved list, and the AMA CPT 2026 Professional Edition.

## Blueprint used here (per 50-question test)
Domain midpoints (32/23/15/14/8/8) scaled to 50: Domain 1 16 (ICD-10-CM 6, ICD-10-PCS 4, CPT/HCPCS
and modifiers 4, E/M 2), Domain 2 12 (payment systems 6, NCCI/medical necessity/claims 6), Domain 3 7,
Domain 4 7, Domain 5 4, Domain 6 4. Pools are four times each count, so no item repeats across tests.

## Content sources
- ICD-10-CM Official Guidelines for Coding and Reporting FY2026 and ICD-10-PCS Official Guidelines
  FY2026 (CMS / CDC NCHS; public domain).
- CMS: MS-DRG and IPPS, OPPS and APCs, Medicare Physician Fee Schedule (RBRVS), NCCI Policy Manual,
  PTP edits and MUEs, LCDs and NCDs, ABN (CMS-R-131), Medicare appeals, Medicare Advantage risk
  adjustment (CMS-HCC).
- CPT 2026 E/M guidelines as taught in coding courses, paraphrased (MDM or time; no descriptors).
- AHIMA Standards of Ethical Coding; AHIMA/ACDIS Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice.
- HHS: HIPAA Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules (45 CFR 160 and 164); 42 CFR Part 2;
  ONC information blocking rule (21st Century Cures Act); OIG compliance program guidance.
- UHDDS definitions (principal diagnosis, other diagnoses, significant procedures).
