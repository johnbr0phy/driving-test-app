# CCRN (Adult) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the sources below. No AACN sample
questions (the handbook's pages 14 and 15 were not used), no AACN practice exam or review course
items, and no prep-site or commercial question material were used, not even reworded.

## Official: AACN Certification Corporation (the exam owner)

- CCRN Exam Handbook, Direct Care Eligibility Pathway (Adult, Pediatric, Neonatal), July 2026,
  read directly (PDF text extracted), fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.aacn.org/certification/preparation-tools-and-handbooks/~/media/aacn-website/certification/get-certified/handbooks/ccrnexamhandbook.pdf?la=en
  Gave: the format ("3-hour tests consisting of 150 multiple-choice items. Of the 150 items, 125
  are scored and 25 are used to gather statistical data"); 80% Clinical Judgment and 20%
  Professional Caring and Ethical Practice; the Adult CCRN Test Plan that "applies to exams taken
  on and after November 12, 2025": Cardiovascular 13%, Respiratory 12%, Endocrine,
  Hematology/Immunology, GI, Renal/GU, Integumentary 21%, Musculoskeletal, Neurological,
  Behavioral/Psychosocial 18%, Multisystem 16%, and Professional Caring and Ethical Practice 20%
  (Advocacy/Moral Agency, Caring Practices, Clinical Inquiry, Collaboration, Facilitation of
  Learning, Response to Diversity, Systems Thinking), with every listed condition and the
  "Testable Nursing Actions" pages; the AACN Synergy Model definitions of the eight nurse
  competencies (used for the professional caring items); eligibility (1,750 hours in 2 years
  with 875 in the last year, or 2,000 hours in 5 years with 144 in the last year, U.S. RN or
  APRN licence); modified Angoff cut score; computer-based testing at PSI centers or live remote
  proctoring; results on screen; certification for 3 years; and the exam bibliography (Surviving
  Sepsis, ADA Standards of Care, ANA Code of Ethics, AACN Essentials of Critical Care Nursing,
  Urden Critical Care Nursing, AACN Procedure Manual and others), which the items follow.
- Certification Exam Policy Handbook, September 2026, read directly (PDF text extracted),
  fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.aacn.org/certification/preparation-tools-and-handbooks/~/media/aacn-website/certification/get-certified/handbooks/certpolicyhndbk.pdf?la=en
  Gave: "The passing point/cut score is not a percentage but a raw number that may change over
  time"; per-area counts on the score report; retake "up to 4 times in a rolling 12-month period";
  PSI testing centers (more than 300) and live remote proctoring.
- CCRN (Adult) certification page, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.aacn.org/certification/get-certified/ccrn-adult
  Gave: eligibility as above; fees (not used in copy); no CCRN change announced (the revised CMC
  and CSC exams launching Nov. 19, 2026 are different credentials).
- AACN Healthy Work Environments page, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.aacn.org/nursing-excellence/healthy-work-environments
  Gave: the six standards (skilled communication, true collaboration, effective decision making,
  appropriate staffing, meaningful recognition, authentic leadership).
- AACN Practice Alerts index, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.aacn.org/clinical-resources/practice-alerts
  The alerts' recommendation sections are for signed-in users only; only their open scope
  sections were read (prone positioning at least 12 to 16 hours a day in moderate to severe ARDS;
  PAOP and CVP do not predict fluid responsiveness). No item relies on a closed section.

## Clinical guidelines named in the handbook bibliography or used by it

- Surviving Sepsis Campaign: International Guidelines for Management of Sepsis and Septic Shock
  2021 (Intensive Care Medicine 2021;47:1181-1247), full text read, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8486643/
  Used for: qSOFA not alone; lactate and capillary refill; at least 30 mL/kg crystalloid in 3 h;
  MAP 65; antimicrobials within 1 hour for possible shock, 3 hours for possible sepsis without
  shock; balanced crystalloids; no starches; norepinephrine first, vasopressin added rather than
  escalating norepinephrine (in practice once norepinephrine reaches 0.25 to 0.5 mcg/kg/min); peripheral vasopressor start; hydrocortisone 200 mg a day at norepinephrine or
  epinephrine 0.25 mcg/kg/min or more for 4 hours; 6 mL/kg PBW; plateau under 30; prone over
  12 h; restrictive transfusion at 7 g/dL; insulin from 180 mg/dL; LMWH prophylaxis.
- Hyperglycemic Crises in Adults With Diabetes: A Consensus Report (ADA, EASD, JBDS, AACE, DTS;
  Diabetes Care 2024;47:1257-1275), full text read, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11272983/
  Used for: DKA diagnosis (beta-hydroxybutyrate 3.0 mmol/L or more), insulin 0.1 units/kg/h,
  0.05 in HHS without ketosis, potassium under 3.5 delays insulin, dextrose below 250 mg/dL,
  resolution (ketones under 0.6 with pH 7.3 or bicarbonate 18; not the anion gap or urine
  ketones).
- SCCM Clinical Practice Guidelines for the Prevention and Management of Pain, Agitation/Sedation,
  Delirium, Immobility, and Sleep Disruption in Adult Patients in the ICU (PADIS, Crit Care Med
  2018;46:e825-e873), recommendations page read, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/guidelines-for-the-prevention-and-management-of-pa
  Used for: BPS and CPOT, vital signs not valid for pain; light sedation; propofol or
  dexmedetomidine over benzodiazepines; delirium screening; no routine haloperidol or statins;
  dexmedetomidine for agitation preventing extubation; mobilization not barred by ventilation or
  vasoactive drugs; noise and light reduction, no propofol for sleep.
- ANA Code of Ethics for Nurses, 2025 edition (named in the bibliography), fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://codeofethics.ana.org/
  Used for the framing of the advocacy and ethics items (dignity, the patient's own wishes,
  safety); no provision text is quoted.

## Format facts for the landing (official)

150 multiple-choice items, 125 scored and 25 unscored, 3 hours; 80% clinical judgment, 20%
professional caring and ethical practice; the passing point is a raw number set by modified
Angoff, not a percentage, so Tigertest's 70% is a readiness target only; PSI test centers or live
remote proctoring; up to 4 attempts in 12 months; certification lasts 3 years.

## Left out on purpose

Fees and discounts; exact numbers of correct answers to pass (AACN publishes none); trade drug
names (AACN is moving to generic names only); resuscitation drug doses and stroke BP thresholds
(sources above not loadable); pediatric and neonatal content (separate CCRN exams).
