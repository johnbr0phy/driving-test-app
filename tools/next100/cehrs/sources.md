# CEHRS (NHA) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official test plan and the
primary laws and standards below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from NHA study guides, NHA
practice tests or preparation materials, released items, or any commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (National Healthcareer Association)

- NHA, Certified Electronic Health Records Specialist (CEHRS) Test Plan for the CEHRS Exam, based
  on a job analysis completed in 2019 (7 pages), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/test-plans/nha_cehrs-test-plan.pdf?sfvrsn=ad996ae5_2
  100 scored items and 25 pretest items, exam time 125 minutes. Domains and scored items:
  1 Non-Clinical Operations 28; 2 Clinical Operations 32; 3 Revenue Cycle/Finance 15;
  4 Regulatory Compliance 15; 5 Reporting 10. Tasks 1.A to 5.G, knowledge statements 1.K1 to 5.K7
  and the core knowledge CK1 to CK13 were used domain by domain (SOAP and SOAPIER as the plan
  expands them, POMR, CPOE scope, telehealth workflows, NCCI, EDI, EHR incentive programs,
  Meaningful Use / QPP reporting).
- NHA, Electronic Health Records Specialist certification page, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.nhanow.com/certifications/electronic-health-records-specialist
  Links the 2019 test plan as the plan for the current CEHRS exam. Eligibility: a high school
  diploma or GED (or completion scheduled within 18 months), or an EHR specialist training or
  education program within the last 5 years, or a diploma or GED plus supervised EHR specialist
  work experience (1 year within 3 years, or 2 within 5). Exam at the candidate's school, a PSI
  testing center or by live remote proctoring. Renews every two years with 10 continuing
  education credits; an expired certification can be reinstated within 1 year.

## Format (NHA Candidate Handbook, updated 10/05/2026, and 2024 pass rates)

- NHA Candidate Handbook, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/test-plans/candidate_handbook.pdf
  Scaled scores range from 200 to 500, and 390 or higher passes every NHA exam; the scale weights
  questions by difficulty, so 390 is not a fixed percentage. Computer based at PSI test centers or
  by live remote proctoring, or at a school or employer. After a failed first attempt, a 30-day
  wait; three attempts with at least 30 days between them, then one year after each further
  failure. Fees are never stated by Tigertest.
- NHA, 2024 Pass Rates for NHA Examinations, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/annual-pass-rates/nha-annual-pass-rates.pdf
  "The scaled passing score for all examinations is 390/500." CEHRS: 1,834 exams in 2024,
  68.81% passing, 4,131 active certifications at December 31, 2024.
