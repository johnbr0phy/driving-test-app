# Sources for `cemap` (CeMAP Module 1: Financial Services, Regulation and Ethics, FSRE)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from LIBF's published unit syllabuses and UK
law and FCA rules as in force in November 2026. No LIBF study text, practice exam, past paper or
commercial question bank was copied. Checked 2026-11-04.

## Who runs it and what "CeMAP 1" is now
- LIBF (The London Institute of Banking & Finance) is part of Walbrook Institute London ("Formerly
  LIBF"); the qualification is still branded "LIBF Level 3 Certificate in Mortgage Advice and
  Practice (CeMAP)".
- Walbrook, "Improvements to CeMAP from 30 September 2025":
  https://www.walbrook.ac.uk/libf/professional-qualifications/your-professional-studies/improvements-cemap-from-30-september-2025
  (checked 2026-11-04). Module 1 changed from UKFR (two units, ITFS and UKFS, one 2-hour exam of
  100 MCQs) to FSRE, two units (FRE1 and FRE2) each sat separately online in 1 hour. UKFR learners
  could stay on UKFR until it was withdrawn on 16 July 2026. New-version exams online through
  Brightspace with remote invigilation from 10 November 2025; the old version via Pearson VUE until
  12 January 2026. 70% pass mark per unit.

## Format (official)
- LIBF Level 4 Financial Services, Regulation and Ethics (FSRE) qualification specification,
  version 7, published July 2026:
  https://www.walbrook.ac.uk/hubfs/files/documents/pe/fsre-specification-2026.pdf (checked 2026-11-04).
  - Two mandatory units: FSRE: Industry, Regulation and Key Parties (FRE1) and FSRE: Skills,
    Principles and Ethical Behaviours (FRE2), 5 credits and 50 study hours each; TQT 100 hours.
  - Each unit: a one-hour electronic exam. Part 1, 25 multiple-choice questions (1 answer from 4,
    1 mark each, 25 marks); Part 2, 3 case studies with 5 linked MCQs (15 marks). 40 marks per unit,
    80 in all.
  - Unit pass mark 70%; merit 80%, distinction 90%. Qualification graded Pass/Fail.
  - Taken any time on Brightspace with remote invigilation (360-degree room scan), no booking.
  - 12-month registration; resits limited to three a week and ten in any 12 months (fee not used).
  - FSRE is the first part of the licence to practise for mortgage advisers (CeMAP) and for
    financial advisers (DipFA).
  - FRE1 syllabus used for the categories: LO1 UK financial services industry in its European and
    international context (money and markets, asset classes, government's role, the EU's role,
    the Bank of England, financial crime); LO2 the retail consumer (perceptions, needs and
    priorities, product types); LO3 legal concepts (legal entities, powers of attorney, contract
    and agency, property ownership, insolvency and bankruptcy, wills, intestacy, trusts); LO4
    development of regulation 1986 to 2021, the framework, other regulators, senior management
    oversight, auditors, trustees, external compliance support; LO5 FCA and PRA objectives,
    principles-based regulation, enforcement, the Handbook, T&C, prudential standards, COBS,
    CASS, SM&CR, regulated activities and authorisation.
  - FRE2 syllabus: LO6 advice skills (communication, information gathering, analysis, solutions,
    recommendations, insistent clients); LO7 Principles for Businesses and conduct rules, the
    Consumer Duty, MAR, culture, consumer rights and unfair terms; record keeping and notification,
    AML and proceeds of crime, data protection, complaints, FOS, FSCS, TCF and conduct risk; client
    relationships, client types, fiduciary duty, service disclosure, adviser charging, limits of
    authority, cancellation rights, vulnerable clients, advice standards, reviews, due diligence;
    LO8 ethical theories, codes of ethics, ethics versus compliance, whistleblowing, CPD.
  - LIBF publishes no weighting by learning outcome, so the per-test split (FRE1 20, FRE2 20, by
    breadth of each LO) is Tigertest's own.
- LIBF CeMAP qualification specification, published April 2026:
  https://www.walbrook.ac.uk/hubfs/files/documents/pe/cemap-qualification-specification-march-2026.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-04). Five mandatory units: FRE1, FRE2 (FSRE module), MRT1 (50 MCQs, 1 hour),
  MRT2 (40 MCQs, 1 hour), ASEW/ASSC synoptic (6 case studies x 10 MCQs, 2 hours); 70% on each.
  Meets the FCA's education standard for mortgage advisers; TQT 250 hours.
- Walbrook FSRE page:
  https://www.walbrook.ac.uk/libf/professional-qualifications/financial-and-mortgage-advice/financial-services-regulation-and-ethics-fsre/
  (checked 2026-11-04): same-day results, typically four months of study.

## Law and rules used in items (as in force November 2026)
- FSMA 2000 (s19 general prohibition; Part 4A permissions); Financial Services Act 2012 (FCA and
  PRA from 1 April 2013); Financial Services and Markets Act 2023 (secondary international
  competitiveness and growth objective; revocation of retained EU law).
- FCA Handbook: PRIN (12 Principles, Principle 12 Consumer Duty from 31 July 2023; Principles 6
  and 7 disapplied where the Duty applies), COCON, FIT, COND, TC, CASS, MIPRU, COBS 3 (client
  categories) and COBS 15 (30-day and 14-day cancellation), MCOB (regulated mortgage contract,
  ESIS, 7-day reflection period, 'independent' only with a broad range and a fee option, scope
  disclosure), DISP (8-week final response, 6 months to refer, summary resolution by the close of
  the third business day), SUP 15 notifications, SYSC 18 whistleblowing: https://handbook.fca.org.uk
- FCA FG21/1 guidance on the fair treatment of vulnerable customers (health, life events,
  resilience, capability): https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/finalised-guidance/fg21-1.pdf
- Consumer Duty: https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/consumer-duty (board report at least annually).
- SM&CR extended to solo-regulated firms on 9 December 2019; mortgage regulation from 31 October
  2004; consumer credit to the FCA on 1 April 2014; MCD changes on 21 March 2016 (ESIS fully
  replaced the KFI from 21 March 2019); RDR from 31 December 2012.
- Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (tipping off, failure to disclose); Money Laundering Regulations 2017
  (CDD, EDD for PEPs, records 5 years after the relationship ends); SARs to the NCA.
- UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (one-month
  subject access response, 72-hour breach notification, special category data):
  https://ico.org.uk
- The Information Commission took over from the Information Commissioner's Office on 30 September
  2026 (DUAA 2025 ss118-119, Commencement No. 9 Regulations, SI 2026/1015):
  https://www.gov.uk/government/news/information-commission-succeeds-the-ico-as-uks-data-protection-regulator
  (checked 2026-11-04). Items name the Information Commission.
- Regulated mortgage contract definition (RAO art 61(3)) no longer requires a first charge since
  21 March 2016. SM&CR Certification Regime still in force (PS26/6 Phase 1 changes April-September
  2026; Treasury's plan to remove certification from legislation not yet enacted, Nov 2026).
- Consumer Rights Act 2015 (unfair terms not binding on the consumer); Public Interest Disclosure
  Act 1998; Bribery Act 2010; Criminal Justice Act 1993 (insider dealing); UK MAR.
- Mental Capacity Act 2005 (lasting powers of attorney registered with the Office of the Public
  Guardian); Wills Act 1837 (two witnesses; marriage revokes a will in England and Wales);
  Inheritance and Trustees' Powers Act 2014 (spouse takes all with no issue); Partnership Act
  1890; Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000; Insolvency Act 1986 (wrongful trading).
- Bank of England remit: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy (the Chancellor sets the
  CPI target in the annual remit; the MPC sets Bank Rate).
- No money figures (FSCS or FOS limits, intestacy statutory legacy, tax rates, ISA allowances) are
  asked; they change and are left out of every Tigertest page. Law items follow England and Wales
  where Scotland or Northern Ireland differ, and say so in the stem.

## Not modelled
- The case-study format (one scenario with 5 linked questions) is written as self-contained
  "Case:" stems that each restate the facts they need.
- MRT1, MRT2 and the ASEW/ASSC synoptic (CeMAP 2 and 3) are not in this bank.
