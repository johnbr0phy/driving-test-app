# CEN (BCEN Certified Emergency Nurse) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official outline below and the
clinical guidance it points to. None is copied or reworded from BCEN practice exams, review
manuals, question books or any prep site.

## Outline (Board of Certification for Emergency Nursing)

- BCEN, Certified Emergency Nurse (CEN) Examination Content Outline, effective July 2026,
  read directly (PDF, fetched 2026-11-15):
  https://bcen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/BCEN-CEN-ECO-2025-effective-07-2026_FINAL.pdf
  (the bcen.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ link redirects here). Eleven content areas, 150
  scored items: 1 Cardiovascular 18, 2 Respiratory 17, 3 Neurological 17, 4 Gastrointestinal 14,
  5 Genitourinary, Gynecology and Obstetrical 10, 6 Mental Health 13, 7 Medical 15,
  8 Musculoskeletal and Wound 11, 9 Head, Eye, Ear, Nose, Throat 10, 10 Environment and
  Toxicology Emergencies, and Communicable Diseases 13, 11 Professional Issues 12 (prioritization:
  triage, mass casualty, patient throughput; legal and ethical issues: end-of-life care, forensic
  evidence, federal regulations, consent, continuity of care, cultural humility, abuse and neglect,
  impaired nurse and drug diversion, workplace violence). Every subtopic listed there was used to
  plan its category.
- The older outline (effective January 2022, eight areas, used by some guides) is superseded:
  https://bcen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/CEN-Content-Outline-Effective-01-2022.pdf

## Format (BCEN)

- BCEN, CEN exam page (https://bcen.org/cen/, fetched 2026-11-15): certification renews every
  four years; fees are on that page and are never stated by Tigertest.
- BCEN, FAQs About the CEN Exam (https://bcen.org/?p=12439, fetched 2026-11-15): "The CEN exam
  consists of 175 items (150 scored and 25 unscored pretest items)"; total seat time 180 minutes;
  "candidates must answer 99 of the 150 scored items correctly to achieve a passing score"
  (raw count, not curved); delivered by computer at PSI test centers or at home by live remote
  proctoring; 90-day testing window; after a failed attempt, 90 days before retesting.

## Law and clinical guidance

- 42 U.S.C. 1395dd, EMTALA (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/1395dd, fetched
  2026-11-15): medical screening exam for anyone who comes to the ED, stabilize or transfer,
  conditions for an appropriate transfer (physician certification, receiving facility accepts,
  records sent, qualified staff and equipment), no delay to ask about payment, a woman in labor is
  stabilized when the placenta has delivered, protection for staff who refuse an unstable
  transfer or report a violation.
- CDC, Sexually Transmitted Infections Treatment Guidelines, 2021, gonococcal infections in
  adults (https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/gonorrhea-adults.htm, fetched 2026-11-15):
  ceftriaxone 500 mg IM single dose (1 g at 150 kg or more), plus doxycycline 100 mg twice daily
  for 7 days when chlamydia is not excluded.
- CDC, Guideline for Isolation Precautions (2007, page updated 2023)
  (https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/index.html, fetched
  2026-11-15): standard, contact, droplet and airborne precautions; airborne infection isolation
  rooms for tuberculosis, measles and varicella.
- Other clinical facts follow widely taught emergency nursing practice: ACLS/BLS adult
  compression rate and depth, Emergency Severity Index (five levels), START mass casualty triage,
  Parkland burn formula, rule of nines, Glasgow Coma Scale, Ottawa ankle rules, and standard
  antidotes (naloxone, N-acetylcysteine, hydroxocobalamin, fomepizole, atropine and pralidoxime,
  hyperbaric oxygen for carbon monoxide). The AHA guideline pages and the AHRQ ESI page returned
  403 to automated reads on 2026-11-15, so only long-stable figures are asked (compressions 100
  to 120 a minute and at least 2 inches, ESI decision points) and no number that changed in the
  2025 AHA update is used.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test, the outline's 150 items divided by three: cardiovascular 6, respiratory 6,
  neurological 6, gastrointestinal 5, GU/gyn/OB 3, mental health 4, medical 5, musculoskeletal and
  wound 4, HEENT 3, environment, toxicology and communicable disease 4, professional issues 4.
  Pools are four times those counts (200 items), so no item repeats across tests.
- Tigertest's 66% line matches the real 99 of 150. Four 50-question tests stand in for the real
  175-item, 3-hour exam; pretest items are not modelled.
- No image items: rhythm strips and wounds are described in words. No fees, no dose that varies
  by protocol beyond common adult doses, no state-specific law (mandatory reporting and consent
  ages "vary by state").
- Written against data/nclex questions (v2), data/v2/paramedic, data/v2/emt and data/v2/nclexpn
  and checked for repeated stems with the same answer.
