# Sources for `chtheory` (Switzerland basic theory test, category B car, in English)

All 200 questions are original. The official question bank (the asa-licensed originals that
approved study apps use) was not opened or copied, nor were commercial practice apps. Signs are
described in words. Checked 2026-11-16 unless noted.

## English availability and format of the real test
- Canton of Zurich road traffic office, "Theorieprüfung"
  https://www.zh.ch/de/mobilitaet/fuehrerausweis-fahren-lernen/fahren-lernen/theoriepruefung.html
  (checked 2026-11-16): languages for the Basis-Theorieprüfung (categories A1, A, B) are German,
  French, Italian and English, with "Englisch (nur Basis-Theorieprüfung)" so English exists for
  the basic test only. The page's table gives 55 multiple-choice questions, 45 minutes, 150
  points, at most 17 errors. The learner licence is needed for practical driver training.
- Canton of Aargau, "Basis-Theorieprüfung", page published 10 Nov 2026
  https://www.ag.ch/de/verwaltung/dvi/strassenverkehr/fuehrerpruefung/theoriepruefung/basis-theoriepruefung
  (checked 2026-11-16): 50 questions, 150 points, at most 15 error points, at most 45 minutes,
  each with three answer options of which one or two are right; the 10 questions that had only
  two options are being replaced by three-option questions; holders of A1, A, B1 or B need not
  sit it. The two cantons disagree on the counts (55 and 17 against 50 and 15); both are 10% of
  the 150 points as the error limit, which is what the practice pass mark follows (45 of 50).
- Vereinigung der Strassenverkehrsämter (asa), Theorieprüfung overview
  https://www.asa.ch (checked 2026-11-16): lists "Prüfungsform" and "Prüfungsinhalte" under the
  theory exam; the exam format subpage gives the basic theory test as 50 questions, 45 minutes,
  150 points, at most 15 error points (read through a fetch summary).
- Not modelled: the real test scores each question 1 to 3 error points and has multi-answer
  items and pictures; Tigertest uses single-answer items, signs and situations in words.

## Law used for facts (all read in German from the Federal Chancellery filestore)
- Strassenverkehrsgesetz (SVG, SR 741.01), status 1 April 2025
  https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1959/679_705_685/de (art. 10, 14a, 15, 15a, 27, 28, 33
  to 37).
- Verkehrsregelnverordnung (VRV, SR 741.11), status 1 July 2025
  https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1962/1364_1409_1420/de (art. 2 to 6, 8 to 14, 15 to 25,
  27 to 31, 35 to 43, 46 to 56, 64 on loads left out).
- Signalisationsverordnung (SSV, SR 741.21), read from the admin.ch compilation
  https://www.admin.ch/opc/de/classified-compilation/19790235/201506010000/741.21.pdf with
  amendments marked up to 1 July 2025 (art. 22 to 22c, 23 to 27, 30, 35 to 40, 42, 44 to 46, 67,
  68, 73 to 79).
- Verordnung der Bundesversammlung über Alkoholgrenzwerte im Strassenverkehr (SR 741.13), state
  1 Oct 2016, 0.5 per mille or 0.25 mg per litre breath (unfit), 0.8 per mille or 0.4 mg per
  litre (qualified), read as the French text RS 741.13 from the Federal Council publication
  https://res.cloudinary.com/adminch/image/private/s--Zkxx0rHB--/v1719842679/Bundespublikationen/741-13f.pdf

## Choices made
- Right-hand traffic, priority to the right, general limits 50, 80, 100 and 120 km/h, alcohol
  0.5 per mille with a ban (0.1 per mille detection value) for probationary, learner and
  professional drivers (VRV art. 2a).
- Left out: fines, fees, penalty points, the motorway vignette and the practical test.
  The VZV (SR 741.51) was not opened; learner rules come from SVG art. 15 and 15a and VRV art. 27.
- Sign numbers in the explanations are the SSV numbers (2.49 and so on). Signs are described by
  shape and colour in words only.
