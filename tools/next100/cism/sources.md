# Sources for `cism` (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager)

All questions are original, written by Tigertest from the CISM job practice (exam content
outline) and general information security management practice and public standards. No ISACA
review manual item, QAE database item, sample question, past exam question or commercial
practice question was copied or reworded, and no prep provider is named. ISACA publishes no
open question catalogue for CISM, so there was nothing to run a similarity check against; the
stems were instead checked against the Tigertest cisa, cissp, isc2cc, secplus, cysa, sc900 and
sc200 banks for repeated fact-and-answer pairs. Checked 2026-11-23.

## Job practice (official, confirmed)
- ISACA, "CISM Exam Content Outline", https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism/cism-exam-content-outline
  (fetched 2026-11-23): 150 questions; Domain 1 Information Security Governance 18% (A Enterprise
  Governance, B Information Security Strategy), Domain 2 Information Security Risk Management 20%
  (A Risk Identification and Assessment, B Risk Response), Domain 3 Information Security Program
  33% (A Program Development, B Program Management), Domain 4 Incident Management 29% (A Incident
  Management Readiness, B Incident Management Operations), with the subtopics and 42 supporting
  tasks used for the eight drill domains.
- ISACA press release, "ISACA Updates CISM Exam Content Outline, Factoring in Today's
  Technologies, Security Responsibilities", 10 September 2026,
  https://www.isaca.org/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2026/isaca-updates-cism-exam-content-outline-factoring-in-todays-technologies-security-responsibilities
  (fetched 2026-11-23): same four domains with new weights 18/20/33/29 (the 2022 outline was
  17/20/33/30), more emphasis on security strategy and program development, new content areas
  enterprise architecture and information security architecture; preparation materials updated
  1 September 2026; updated exam available from 3 November 2026. The bank follows the updated
  outline, which is in force on the build date.

## Exam format (official)
- ISACA, "ISACA Certification Exam Candidate Guide" (copyright 2026),
  https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/-/media/fa494652c5f149289af38cef18328650.ashx
  (fetched 2026-11-23, read as PDF text): CISM is 4 hours (240 minutes), 150 multiple-choice
  questions; every question has one best answer and four options; no penalty for wrong answers;
  scores reported on a scale of 200 to 800, 450 or higher to pass; scored and unscored pretest
  items; domain results are informational only; retake policy: four attempts in a rolling 12
  months, waits of 30, 90 and 90 days; computer based at PSI test centers or remote proctoring.
- ISACA, "How do I become CISM certified?", https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism
  (fetched 2026-11-23): exam languages English, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, French and German;
  PSI test center or remote proctoring; eligibility period six months after registering. Fees are
  on the page and deliberately not used.
- ISACA, "Get CISM Certified", https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism/get-cism-certified
  (fetched 2026-11-23): five or more years of CISM work experience in at least three of the four
  domains, within the ten years before applying (the exam may be taken first); apply within five
  years of passing; experience verified by a supervisor or manager; Code of Professional Ethics;
  at least 120 CPE hours per three-year period and at least 20 a year. Waivers are not on this
  page, so the landing does not describe them.

## Standards used for facts in explanations
- NIST, "The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0", CSWP 29, 26 February 2024,
  https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf (fetched 2026-11-23, read as PDF text):
  six Functions with Govern added; Current and Target Profiles and gap analysis; Tiers.
- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, "Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity
  Risk Management: A CSF 2.0 Community Profile", April 2025, https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final
  (fetched 2026-11-23): incident response treated as part of cybersecurity risk management under
  CSF 2.0; supersedes Rev. 2 (2012).
- NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1, "Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments", September 2012,
  https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final (fetched 2026-11-23): risk as threats,
  vulnerabilities, likelihood and impact; assessment informs senior leaders' risk response.
- NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1, "Contingency Planning Guide for Federal Information Systems",
  https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final (fetched 2026-11-23): business impact
  analysis, recovery objectives and contingency plan types.
- Stable management practice used without a figure that changes: COBIT 2019 governance and
  management split; ISO/IEC 27001 statement of applicability; RTO, RPO, MTD; ALE = SLE x ARO;
  KRI, KPI and KGI; RACI; chain of custody; tabletop, parallel and full interruption tests.

## Not modelled or left out
- Fees, the application fee and waiver lists (change and are not on the official page read).
- The real exam's raw-to-scaled conversion is not published; no percent pass mark is stated.
- No item asks about the exam itself.
- No named products, officeholders or dated breaches.
