# CMA (AAMA) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-03.

## Official (Certifying Board of the American Association of Medical Assistants)

- Content Outline for the CMA (AAMA) Certification Exam, AAMA/CB/Exam Questions-Number and
  Percent/Rev: 1/26, read directly:
  https://www.aama-ntl.org/docs/default-source/about-profession-and-credential/cma-exam/exam-content-outline-effective.pdf
  200 multiple-choice questions, 180 scored and 20 pretested. Clinical Competency 106 (59%):
  A. Clinical Workflow: Patient Intake and Discharge, B. Safety and Infection Control,
  C. Procedures/Examinations, D. Pharmacology. General 38 (21%): E. Legal and Ethical Issues,
  F. Communication. Administrative 36 (20%): G. Billing, Coding, and Insurance, H. Schedule
  Appointments and Health Information Management. The detailed topic list (vital signs, terminology,
  screenings, nutrition, infection control, first aid, exam positions, surgical assisting, wound and
  ostomy care, instruments and autoclave, A&P, specimen collection, CLIA, lab panels, ECG, vision,
  audiometry, allergy, PFTs, imaging, drug classes, dosage, routes, CDC immunization resources, HIPAA,
  HITECH, PHI releases, GINA, consent, torts, legal doctrines, drug schedules, mandatory reporting,
  advance directives and PSDA, defense mechanisms, communication barriers, ADAAA, telephone
  techniques, CPT/ICD/HCPCS, fraud and abuse, claims, Medicare/Medicaid/TRICARE/CHAMPVA, financial
  terms and procedures, scheduling, registration, EHR reports) was used for every category.
- AAMA, Certification FAQ (https://www.aama-ntl.org/certification): computerized exam of 200
  multiple-choice questions; optional 5-minute tutorial; four 40-minute segments, 160 minutes of
  test time; optional breaks of 5, 10 and 5 minutes; maximum appointment 180 minutes. Scores on a
  scale of 200 to 800; "The minimum passing score (MPS) is 405", which the Certifying Board may
  change after a standard-setting study.
- AAMA, Test Center vs. Remote Proctoring (https://www.aama-ntl.org/certification/testing-options):
  taken at a PSI test center or by live remote proctoring through PSI.
- AAMA, Eligibility (https://www.aama-ntl.org/certification/eligibility): students and graduates of
  CAAHEP- or ABHES-accredited medical assisting programs, previously certified CMAs (AAMA), and other
  pathways listed there. Fees are on that page and are never stated by Tigertest.
- AAMA, Recertification (https://www.aama-ntl.org/certification/recertification): CMAs (AAMA)
  recertify every 60 months, by 60 CEUs or by exam.

Some guides still quote a passing score of 430; the AAMA page read on 2026-11-03 says 405, which
is what the landing and facts use.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test: Clinical 29 (intake 7, safety 6, procedures and A&P 6, lab and diagnostics
  5, pharmacology 5), General 11 (legal 6, communication 5), Administrative 10 (billing 6,
  scheduling and records 4). This scales the outline's 106/38/36; AAMA publishes no split inside
  each section, so the split is Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% practice line is a readiness target, not the AAMA standard (a scaled 405).
- No image items; ECG rhythms and growth charts are described in words.
- Clinical facts follow standard medical assisting references and federal sources named in the
  outline: CDC vaccine storage (2 to 8 C refrigerator, middle shelves) and immunization schedules,
  VAERS, OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens and Hazard Communication standards, GHS, CLIA, HIPAA and HITECH
  (right of access generally within 30 days), 42 CFR Part 2, GINA, the Controlled Substances Act
  schedules, the PSDA, the ADA, CPT's 3-year new/established patient rule, Medicare Parts A to D.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees or prices; dollar amounts appear only inside a
  worked coinsurance problem.
- Duplicates: written against data/ccma-questions.json, phlebotomy, cet and cna (and checked against
  every other bank on disk). Items that repeated a fact and answer were replaced while writing: eye
  flushing time, sharps fill line, GHS signal word, crash cart epinephrine, direct pressure, vaccine
  freezer, VAERS, an oral suspension calculation with the same answer as PTCB, Maslow, displacement,
  unbundling, hand rub vs soap, hands above elbows in the surgical scrub, discharge summary.

All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the outline above. None is copied from AAMA
practice exams or any commercial review.
