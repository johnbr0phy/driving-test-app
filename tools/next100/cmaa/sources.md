# CMAA (NHA) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official test plan and the
primary laws and standards below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from NHA study guides,
NHA practice tests, the CMAA Preparation Suite, released items or any commercial review book or
prep site.

## Outline (National Healthcareer Association)

- NHA, Certified Medical Administrative Assistant (CMAA) Test Plan for the CMAA Exam, based on a
  job analysis completed in 2021 (Rev01222022, 8 pages), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://info.nhanow.com/hubfs/Test%20Plans/NHA_CMAA_Test_Plan_2021.pdf
  (same document at https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/test-plans/nha_cmaa_test_plan_2021.pdf,
  fetched 2026-11-15). 110 scored items and 25 pretest items, exam time 2 hours 15 minutes.
  Domains and scored items: 1 Foundational Knowledge 10; 2 Communication and Professionalism 21;
  3 Medical Law, Ethics, and Compliance 17; 4 Scheduling 16; 5 Patient Encounter 21; 6 Billing
  and Revenue Cycle 11; 7 Medical Practice Administrative Procedures and Logistics 14. Tasks and
  knowledge statements k1 to k101 were used category by category.
- NHA, Medical Administrative Assistant certification page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.nhanow.com/certifications/medical-administrative-assistant
  Links the 2021 test plan as the plan for the current CMAA exam. Eligibility: a medical
  administrative assistant training or education program within the last 5 years, or a high
  school diploma or equivalent plus 1 year of supervised work experience in the field within the
  last 3 years. Exam at the candidate's school, a PSI testing center or by live remote proctoring.
  Certification renews every two years with 10 continuing education credits.

## Format (NHA Candidate Handbook, updated 10/05/2026)

- NHA Candidate Handbook, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/test-plans/candidate_handbook.pdf
  Exam lengths and times are those on each test plan. Scored and pretest questions are mixed and
  pretest questions do not count. Scaled scores run from 200 to 500 and 390 or higher passes every
  NHA exam; the scale weights questions by difficulty, so 390 is not a fixed percentage. Computer
  based at PSI test centers or by live remote proctoring, or at a school or employer. After a
  failed attempt a candidate waits 30 days; three attempts are allowed with at least 30 days
  between them, then one year after each further failure; each retake is paid in full (fees are
  never stated by Tigertest).
- NHA, 2024 Pass Rates for NHA Examinations, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.nhanow.com/docs/default-source/annual-pass-rates/nha-annual-pass-rates.pdf
  "The scaled passing score for all examinations is 390/500." CMAA: 9,390 exams in 2024, 62.68%
  passing.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test, scaled from the test plan's 10/21/17/16/21/11/14 of 110: Foundational
  Knowledge 5, Communication and Professionalism 9, Law, Ethics and Compliance 8, Scheduling 7,
  Patient Encounter 10, Billing and Revenue Cycle 5, Office Procedures and Logistics 6. The
  rounding is Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target; NHA's 390 is a scaled score, not a percent.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees, no penalty amounts, no state-specific rules
  (reporting duties, record retention and minors' consent are "vary by state" or "most states").
- Money appears only inside worked computations (coinsurance, out-of-pocket maximum, petty cash,
  deposit reconciliation).
- Duplicates: written against data/ccma-questions.json, data/cma-questions.json,
  data/rma-questions.json and data/cca-questions.json and scanned for repeated fact-and-answer
  pairs; matches (RACE first letter, psychotherapy-note authorization, terminal digit primary
  unit, a deductible-plus-coinsurance sum) were rewritten. The ncma builder was not on disk when
  this bank was written.
