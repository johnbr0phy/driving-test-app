# Sources for `coc` (AAPC Certified Outpatient Coder exam, formerly CPC-H)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from public CMS and CDC guidance and the
content areas AAPC publishes. No AAPC practice exam, study guide, workbook or commercial question
bank was used, and no item reproduces a CPT code descriptor (CPT is AMA copyright) or needs a
code-book look-up. Sources fetched 2026-11-19.

## Exam outline and format (AAPC)
- AAPC, "Taking the COC exam": https://aapc.com/certifications/coc/taking-the-coc-exam
  (fetched 2026-11-19). 100 questions, at least 70 must be answered, four hours in one sitting;
  70% overall to pass ("If you incorrectly answer more than 30 questions, you will not pass");
  online with a live remote proctor or at a testing center; approved books: AMA CPT Professional
  Edition, ICD-10-CM and HCPCS Level II (current year, any publisher for the last two); built-in
  calculator. Section question counts: medical terminology 7, anatomy 7, coding guidelines 3,
  payment methodologies 13 (Medicare Parts A to D, MSP, OPPS/IPPS, UB-04 and CMS-1500),
  compliance 3 (NCDs/LCDs, HIPAA, ABNs), ICD-10-CM 15, HCPCS Level II 7, CPT 13 (E/M, radiology,
  pathology/lab, medicine), surgery and modifiers 22 (procedures approved for outpatient hospital
  and ASC facilities), cases 10. Fees are kept off the site.
- AAPC, COC certification page: https://www.aapc.com/certifications/coc (fetched 2026-11-19):
  content areas (core outpatient coding, documentation and compliance, facility-based services
  including same-day surgery, endoscopy, ED and observation, revenue cycle including revenue codes
  and APC assignment); 36 CEUs every two years with annual membership.

## Blueprint used here (per 50-question test)
AAPC's 100 scaled to 50 and grouped: terminology 3, anatomy 3, guidelines and compliance 3,
payment methodologies 7, ICD-10-CM 8, CPT (E/M, radiology, lab, medicine) 6, HCPCS Level II 4,
surgery and modifiers 11, cases 5. Pools are four times each count (200).

## Facts (primary text read)
- ICD-10-CM Official Guidelines for Coding and Reporting FY 2026 (CMS / CDC NCHS):
  https://www.cms.gov/files/document/fy-2026-icd-10-cm-coding-guidelines.pdf (fetched 2026-11-19).
  Section IV A to Q (first-listed condition, outpatient surgery, observation, uncertain diagnosis,
  chronic diseases, coexisting conditions, diagnostic and therapeutic services only, preoperative
  evaluations, ambulatory surgery, general medical exams with abnormal findings), Appendix I POA
  (conditions arising in the ED, observation or outpatient surgery are POA); Section I conventions
  and chapter guidelines.
- Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 4 (Part B Hospital, OPPS), Rev. 13799:
  https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c04.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Payment window and condition code 51 (10.12), status indicators (10.1.1), APC groups and the 2 times rule (10.2),
  composite and comprehensive APCs (10.2.1, 10.2.3), packaging N, Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, J1, J2 (10.4),
  discounting (10.5), modifiers 50, LT/RT, 73, 74, 52, 53, 76, 77, FC/value code FD, PO, PN, CT,
  FX, FY, ER, CG and modifier order (20.6), C-codes (20.7), clinic and ED visits, Type A and Type B
  EDs, critical care, trauma activation G0390 with revenue code 68x (160, 160.1), same-day
  reporting (170), surgical charges and ER procedures (180.2), unlisted codes (180.3), drug
  administration (230.2), observation (290: revenue codes 0760/0762, hours rounded, start and end,
  no standing orders, recovery is not observation).
- Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 25 (UB-04 / CMS-1450):
  https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c25.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). FL 4 type of bill, FL 42 revenue codes and 0001 total, FL 44 HCPCS and
  four modifiers, FL 45 line item dates of service, FL 46 units.
- Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 30 (financial liability, ABN, MOON):
  https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/clm104c30.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Notice timing, blanket and blank notices, emergencies, EMTALA, MOON for
  observation over 24 hours delivered within 36 hours.
- Medicare NCCI Policy Manual 2026, Chapter I:
  https://www.cms.gov/files/document/01-chapter1-ncci-medicare-policy-manual-2026-final.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). PTP-associated modifiers, CCMI 0/1/9, modifiers 22, 76, 77 do not bypass,
  59 and XE/XS/XP/XU, MAI 1/2/3, surgical principles (exploration, access through diseased tissue,
  -otomy with -ectomy, approaches, conversion to open, diagnostic endoscopy before an open
  procedure).
- CMS NCCI PTP edits page:
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/national-correct-coding-initiative-ncci-edits/medicare-ncci-procedure-procedure-ptp-edits
  and MUE page:
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/national-correct-coding-initiative-ncci-edits/medicare-ncci-medically-unlikely-edits-mues
  (fetched 2026-11-19): separate practitioner and hospital outpatient files, quarterly updates,
  three MUE tables, some MUE values confidential.
- CMS three-day payment window:
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/acute-inpatient-pps/three-day-payment-window
  (fetched 2026-11-19).
- Medicare Secondary Payer Manual, Chapter 2:
  https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/msp105c02.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): working aged GHP threshold 20 employees, disability 100, ESRD 30-month
  coordination period.
- CMS HCPCS overview:
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system (fetched
  2026-11-19): Level I maintained by AMA, five digits; Level II by CMS, a letter and four digits,
  quarterly updates.
- CMS Medicare Coverage Database: https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/search.aspx
  (fetched 2026-11-19): NCDs by CMS nationally, LCDs and articles by MACs per jurisdiction.
- MedlinePlus, Understanding Medical Words appendix: https://medlineplus.gov/appendixa.html
  (fetched 2026-11-19): prefixes, roots and suffixes.
- NCI SEER Training, body terminology: https://training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/body/terminology.html
  (fetched 2026-11-19): directional terms, planes, cavities; other anatomy from the SEER modules.

## Left out on purpose
- CPT descriptors, code numbers that need a book look-up, and any question about the exam itself.
- Payment percentages that change by rule (CT, FX, FY reductions, PO clinic rates), APC rates,
  fees, and JW/JZ details (the CMS FAQ PDF could not be read as text).
- The 10 case questions on the real exam use full records; here cases are short scenarios.
