# CogAT Levels 9 to 12 (`cogat`): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from Riverside Insights' published
descriptions of the CogAT subtests. No CogAT item, sample question, practice test, released form
or commercial CogAT prep material was used or reworded. The words, numbers and patterns are
grade 3 to grade 6 school knowledge.

## Test structure (Riverside Insights, the test owner)

- CogAT Overview Brochure, "CogAT Forms 7 and 8 Testing Times and Descriptions".
  https://riversideinsights.com/hubfs/CogAT/CogAT%20Overview%20Brochure.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Gave: three batteries, Verbal, Quantitative and Nonverbal (Figural); for Levels 9-17/18
  (grade 3 and up) the nine tests and what each asks: Test 1 Verbal Analogies (two words, find
  the relationship, apply it to another pair), Test 2 Sentence Completion (read an incomplete
  sentence, choose the answer that best completes it), Test 3 Verbal Classification (three words,
  choose the word that belongs in the same group), Test 4 Number Analogies (same process as
  Verbal Analogies with quantitative concepts), Test 5 Number Puzzles ("an equation in which
  elements are missing", substitute numbers and solve), Test 6 Number Series (find the rule,
  then the next number), Test 7 Figure Matrices, Test 8 Paper Folding, Test 9 Figure
  Classification; approximately 10 minutes of testing time for each test at Level 9 and up.
  Also: separate test levels for grades K through 6 and banded levels for grades 7 to 12; two
  equivalent forms; Screening Forms of the three analogies tests in about 30 minutes; "Items from
  Level 9 (Grade 3) and above in the Verbal and Quantitative batteries are text-based"; the
  Nonverbal battery at the higher levels keeps the picture format; picture-based levels 5/6-8
  for grades K to 2; national age norms (Standard Age Score, Age Percentile Rank).
- Overview of CogAT for Parents.
  https://riversideinsights.com/hubfs/CitC/Overview_of_CogAT_for_Parents.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-14). Gave: the three batteries and their item types (Verbal: analogies, sentence
  completion, classification; Quantitative: analogies, number series, equations; Nonverbal:
  analogies, classification, paper folding); that CogAT measures reasoning, not curriculum
  mastery; the Ability Profile (median age stanine, pattern A/B/C/E, relative strength or
  weakness).
- CogAT product page. https://www.riversideinsights.com/cogat (fetched 2026-11-14). Gave: CogAT
  measures verbal, quantitative and nonverbal reasoning; links to the brochure above.
- CogAT Learning and Support hub. https://riversideinsights.com/cogat-dashboard-main (fetched
  2026-11-14). Gave: the district, teacher and family resource pages and the Ability Profile
  Finder; no format facts.

## Level band chosen

Levels 9 to 12 (grades 3 to 6): the brochure gives a separate level per grade from K to 6 and
says Level 9 is grade 3, so Levels 10, 11 and 12 are grades 4, 5 and 6. Words and numbers are
pitched across grades 3 to 6.

## Could not load or confirm

- Question counts per test at each level: the fetched Riverside documents give approximate
  times, not counts. The copy says Riverside publishes about 10 minutes per test; the Tigertest
  forms use 12 questions per test, 36 per battery in 30 minutes, and say so as our own choice.
- The number of answer choices on the real tests is not stated in the fetched documents; these
  items have four.
- The brochure describes Number Analogies as a 2 x 2 matrix. To keep each rule unambiguous in
  text, each item shows two complete pairs and a third pair to finish.

## Deliberately left out

- The Nonverbal battery (Figure Matrices, Paper Folding, Figure Classification): it is made of
  pictures and shapes at every level, and a text description would test reading, not figural
  reasoning. The landing and the dashboard say it is not modelled.
- The picture-based primary levels 5/6 to 8 (grades K to 2) and the banded levels 13 to 17/18.
- Standard Age Scores, stanines, percentile ranks and the Ability Profile: a practice form cannot
  be normed, so scores here are percent correct only.
- The Screening Form and Alternate-Verbal administration.
