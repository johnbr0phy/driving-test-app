# Sources for `cpaaud` (CPA Exam, Auditing and Attestation section)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the AICPA's published AUD blueprint and the
AICPA, PCAOB, GAO, SEC, SSARS and SQMS requirements it names. No AICPA released question, retired
simulation, commercial review course or question bank was copied. Checked 2026-11-03.

## Exam format (official)
- AICPA, "Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints effective January 1, 2026" (PDF):
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5d2r66sSSE8wJRZTqAshlE/f97ec39c394eeb61b20a5824784306df/CPA_Exam_Blueprints_2026.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-03):
  - AUD is a Core section, 4 hours, 78 multiple-choice questions and 7 task-based simulations.
  - Testlets: 39 MCQ, 39 MCQ, then 2, 3 and 2 TBSs. Score weighting MCQ 50%, TBS 50%.
  - Content areas: I Ethics, Professional Responsibilities and General Principles 15-25%;
    II Assessing Risk and Developing a Planned Response 25-35%; III Performing Further Procedures and
    Obtaining Evidence 30-40%; IV Forming Conclusions and Reporting 10-20%.
  - Groups used for the categories: I.A ethics, independence and professional responsibilities
    (AICPA Code, SEC, PCAOB, GAO, DOL); I.B skepticism and judgment; I.C nature and scope; I.D terms of
    engagement; I.E documentation; I.F communication with management and TCWG; I.G audit and assurance
    quality. II.A planning; II.B entity and environment (incl. basic economics); II.C control environment,
    business processes and IT (COSO, ITGCs, SOC 1); II.D materiality; II.E risks of material misstatement,
    fraud or error; II.F using the work of others; II.G specific areas of engagement risk (laws and
    regulations, estimates, related parties, single audits). III.A use of data and information; III.B
    sufficient appropriate evidence; III.C sampling; III.D procedures (tests of controls, details,
    analytics, confirmations); III.E specific matters (estimates, investments, inventory, litigation,
    going concern, federal awards); III.F misstatements and deficiencies; III.G written representations;
    III.H subsequent events and subsequently discovered facts. IV.A audit reports; IV.B attestation
    reports; IV.C accounting and review services (preparation, compilation, review); IV.D compliance
    reporting; IV.E other reporting (consistency, other information, interim reviews, supplementary
    information, special purpose frameworks, GAGAS).
  - Skill allocation: Evaluation 5-15%, Analysis 15-25%, Application 30-40%, Remembering and
    Understanding 30-40%.
  - Employee benefit plans subject to ERISA are eligible in every area.
- AICPA, "Summary of revisions to the Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints effective January 1, 2027":
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5CsrSRU2mFGsmWEo9FqwLy/83e5ec97beab5daff376dba518589e75/Summary_of_Changes_to_the_Exam_Blueprints_effective_01012027.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-03): for AUD, revised examples in Area III Group A Topic 2 (reliability of data and
  information), Group B (sufficient appropriate evidence, now naming evidence generated using technologies
  including artificial intelligence), Group C (sampling: methods separated from planned procedures) and a
  clarified task in Group E Topic 2 (investments in securities, focus on audit considerations). "Not
  intended to significantly change the nature or scope" of AUD.
- AICPA, "Learn what is tested on the CPA Exam":
  https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/learn-what-is-tested-on-the-cpa-exam (checked 2026-11-03).
- AICPA, "Learn more about CPA Exam scoring and pass rates":
  https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/learn-more-about-cpa-exam-scoring-and-pass-rates and
  NASBA "Score Information": https://nasba.org/exams/cpaexam/scores/ (checked 2026-11-03): 0 to 99
  scale, 75 passes, a 75 is not 75% correct.

## Not used in copy
- Exam fees and credit windows vary by jurisdiction; left out of the bank and only described as varying.
- Single audit dollar thresholds (changed by the 2024 Uniform Guidance revision) are not asked.

## Standards used in the questions (current as of 2026-11-03)
- AICPA Code of Professional Conduct: conceptual framework (threats and safeguards), independence
  (covered members, immediate family and close relatives, unpaid fees older than one year, loans from
  financial institution clients, gifts, trustee relationships, nonattest services and the SKE designee),
  contingent fees, commissions, confidentiality, acts discreditable (client records).
- SEC independence rules and Sarbanes-Oxley: prohibited nonaudit services, partner rotation (lead and
  concurring 5 years on, 5 off), one-year cooling-off, audit committee preapproval.
  PCAOB Rules 3524 and 3526.
- GAO Government Auditing Standards (independence of mind and in appearance; reports on internal control
  and compliance). DOL independence rules for employee benefit plan audits (29 CFR 2509.75-9).
- Clarified SASs (AU-C): 200, 210, 220 as amended by SAS 146, 230 (documentation completion within 60
  days, retention at least 5 years), 240, 250, 260, 265 (written communication no later than 60 days after
  report release), 300, 315 as revised by SAS 145 (inherent risk factors, spectrum of inherent risk,
  control risk at maximum when controls are not tested), 320, 330, 402 (SOC 1 Type 1 and 2, carve-out
  and inclusive methods, complementary user entity controls), 450, 500, 501, 505, 520, 530, 540 as revised
  by SAS 143, 550, 560, 570 as revised by SAS 132, 580, 600 (group audits, reference to component
  auditor; SAS 149 effective for periods ending on or after Dec 15, 2026, concepts only), 610, 620, 700
  (SAS 134 report order), 705, 706, 708, 720 (SAS 137), 725, 730, 800, 920, 930, 935.
  SAS 136 (ERISA section 103(a)(3)(C) audits).
- SQMS 1 (eight components, risk-based quality objectives, risks and responses) and SQMS 2 (engagement
  quality reviews, two-year cooling-off for a former engagement partner), effective December 15, 2025.
- PCAOB: AS 1215 (seven-year retention), AS 2201 (integrated audit, top-down approach, adverse ICFR
  opinion for a material weakness, same report date), AS 2310 as revised in 2024 (confirmation of cash
  held by third parties and receivables), AS 2415 (going concern, one year beyond the balance sheet
  date), AS 3101 (CAMs, exemptions, auditor tenure), AS 4105 (interim reviews). QC 1000 effective
  December 15, 2026 (PCAOB postponement, Aug 28, 2025:
  https://pcaobus.org/news-events/news-releases/news-release-detail/pcaob-postpones-effective-date-of-qc-1000-and-related-standards--rules--and-forms,
  checked 2026-11-03); not asked in the bank. AS 1000 14-day documentation completion: phased in by firm
  size (https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/implementation-resources-PCAOB-standards-rules/general-responsibilities-of-the-auditor-in-conducting-an-audit-(as-1000),
  checked 2026-11-03), so not asked.
- SSARS: AR-C 70 (preparation, no assurance legend), AR-C 80 (compilation, independence disclosure),
  AR-C 90 (review, inquiry and analytics, independence required).
- SSAEs: AT-C 105, 205, 206 (SSAE 21 direct examinations), 210, 215 (SSAE 19 agreed-upon procedures);
  SOC 2 trust services criteria.
- COSO Internal Control - Integrated Framework (2013): five components, 17 principles.
- 2 CFR 200 Uniform Guidance, subpart F (risk-based major program determination, opinion per major program).
