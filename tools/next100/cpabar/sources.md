# Sources for `cpabar` (Uniform CPA Examination, Business Analysis and Reporting discipline section)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the AICPA's published BAR blueprint and
the standards, regulations and frameworks it lists as references. No AICPA released question,
sample test, commercial review course or app item was used. Checked 2026-11-16.

## Exam format and outline (official)
- AICPA, "Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints", effective January 1, 2026,
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5d2r66sSSE8wJRZTqAshlE/f97ec39c394eeb61b20a5824784306df/CPA_Exam_Blueprints_2026.pdf
  linked from https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-what-is-tested-on-the-cpa-exam,
  downloaded and read 2026-11-16 (Introduction pages 2 to 3 and BAR pages BAR1 to BAR18):
  - BAR is one of three Discipline sections (BAR, ISC, TCP); a candidate takes the three Core
    sections plus one Discipline section.
  - 4 hours; 50 multiple-choice questions in two testlets of 25 and 7 task-based simulations
    (testlets of 2, 3 and 2). MCQs and TBSs are each 50% of the score.
  - Content areas: I Business Analysis 40-50%; II Technical Accounting and Reporting 35-45%;
    III State and Local Governments 10-20%.
  - Skills: Remembering and Understanding 10-20%, Application 45-55%, Analysis 30-40%,
    Evaluation not tested.
  - Area I topics used: financial statement analysis (ratios, fluctuations, data attributes,
    analytics outputs, transaction impacts); non-financial and non-GAAP measures (balanced
    scorecard, benchmarking, EBITDA, free cash flow, adjusted net income); managerial and cost
    accounting (fixed, variable and mixed costs; absorption, variable, activity-based, process
    and job order costing; variance analysis; price, volume and mix); budgeting, forecasting and
    projection (data cleaning, budgets, sensitivity, breakeven); capital structure (cost of
    capital, covenants, leverage); investment decision models (PV, NPV, Black-Scholes inputs,
    payback, EVA, IRR, make, lease or buy); risk management (COSO ERM, financial risk
    mitigation, working capital, SWOT); economic and market influences (elasticity, inflation,
    opportunity cost).
  - Area II topics used: indefinite-lived intangibles including goodwill; internally developed
    software; revenue recognition (contract interpretation); stock compensation (equity and
    liability awards); research and development; business combinations (business vs asset
    acquisition, contingent consideration, measurement period, goodwill, bargain purchase,
    noncontrolling interest, consideration transferred); consolidation (VIEs, noncontrolling
    interests, functional currency, translation adjustments); derivatives and hedge accounting;
    lessor leases and sale-leaseback; Regulation S-X, Regulation S-K, XBRL and segment
    reporting; employee benefit plan financial statements.
  - Area III topics used: government-wide, governmental, proprietary and fiduciary fund
    statements; notes, MD&A, budgetary comparison, RSI; reporting entity and component units;
    reconciliation of fund statements to government-wide; net position, fund balance, capital
    assets, long-term liabilities (net pension liability), interfund activity, nonexchange
    revenue, expenditures and expenses, budgetary accounting and encumbrances.
  - Section assumptions: questions apply to a for-profit business entity under U.S. GAAP unless
    the fact pattern says "local government", "state", "municipality" or "city".
- AICPA, "Summary of Changes to the Exam Blueprints effective 01/01/2027",
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5CsrSRU2mFGsmWEo9FqwLy/83e5ec97beab5daff376dba518589e75/Summary_of_Changes_to_the_Exam_Blueprints_effective_01012027.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-16): the only BAR change revises one Area III representative task (general and
  proprietary long-term liabilities) to make clear that OPEB plans administered through a trust
  and those that are not are both in scope, "not intended to significantly change the nature or
  scope of content eligible for testing in the BAR section". So the bank fits both the 2026 and
  the 2027 blueprint.
- AICPA, "The new CPA Exam: two years in" (Professional Insights, July 6, 2026),
  https://www.aicpa-cima.com/professional-insights/article/the-new-cpa-exam-two-years-in
  (fetched 2026-11-16): new accounting standards become testable in the later of the first
  calendar quarter after the earliest mandatory effective date or the first calendar quarter
  beginning six months after issuance. Used to decide which standards apply (see below).
- Scoring: NASBA, "Score Information", https://nasba.org/exams/cpaexam/scores/ (as for cpafar and
  cpatcp): scores 0 to 99, 75 passes, and 75 is a scaled score, not 75% correct.

## FASB standards (primary text, from FASB's storage.fasb.org PDFs, fetched 2026-11-16)
- ASU 2014-09, Revenue from Contracts with Customers (Topic 606), Section A,
  https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202014-09_Section%20A.pdf : contract existence and approval,
  contract modifications, bill-and-hold, warranties, repurchase agreements, licenses of
  functional and symbolic IP, sales- and usage-based royalties.
- ASU 2016-02, Leases (Topic 842), Section A, https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202016-02_Section%20A.pdf :
  lessor classification (sales-type criteria, direct financing with third-party residual
  guarantee), selling profit, operating lease income, sale and leaseback.
- ASU 2016-09, Improvements to Employee Share-Based Payment Accounting,
  https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202016-09.pdf : forfeiture policy election; excess tax benefits
  and deficiencies in income tax expense.
- ASU 2017-01, Clarifying the Definition of a Business, https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202017-01.pdf :
  the "substantially all of the fair value" screen.
- ASU 2017-12, Targeted Improvements to Accounting for Hedging Activities,
  https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202017-12.pdf : fair value and cash flow hedge presentation,
  documentation at inception.
- ASU 2021-08, Accounting for Contract Assets and Contract Liabilities from Contracts with
  Customers, https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202021-08.pdf : contract balances measured under Topic
  606 as if the acquirer had originated the contracts.
- ASU 2023-07, Improvements to Reportable Segment Disclosures,
  https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202023-07.pdf : significant segment expenses regularly provided to
  the CODM.
- ASU 2025-06, Targeted Improvements to the Accounting for Internal-Use Software,
  https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202025-06.pdf : effective for annual periods beginning after
  December 15, 2027, so not yet testable under the AICPA policy; the bank asks only facts true
  under the current ASC 350-40 stage model (preliminary project costs expensed) and under both
  versions (training costs expensed, paragraph 350-40-25-4). Its basis for conclusions (BC18 to
  BC19) restates the ASC 985-20 model: costs before technological feasibility are R&D expense,
  costs after feasibility and before general release are capitalized.
- ASU 2017-04, Simplifying the Test for Goodwill Impairment: the FASB PDF returned 403; the full
  text was read in PwC Viewpoint's reproduction,
  https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/fasb_financial_accou/asus_fulltext/2017/asu_201704simplifyin/asu_201704simplifyin_US/asu_201704simplifyin_US.html
  (fetched 2026-11-16): 350-20-35-8, loss equal to the excess of carrying amount over fair value,
  limited to goodwill allocated to the unit; the qualitative assessment is optional.
- ASC 985-20-35-1 (amortization of software for sale is the greater of the revenue ratio and
  straight-line over remaining life), quoted in PwC Viewpoint's software guide,
  https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/accounting_guides/software/software/chapter2/27_amort_ext_marktd_sftwr.html
  (checked via search extract 2026-11-16).
- ASU 2015-12, Plan Accounting, https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202015-12.pdf : defined benefit plan
  statement of accumulated plan benefits; net assets available for benefits.
- Other ASC rules used (goodwill and indefinite-lived intangibles not amortized and not reversed,
  ASC 730 R&D including equipment with alternative future use and IPR&D, ASC 718 grant-date
  measurement, liability awards and market and performance conditions, ASC 805 acquisition
  costs, measurement period of one year and contingent consideration, ASC 810 VIE primary
  beneficiary, ASC 830 functional currency, translation and remeasurement, ASC 815 derivative
  definition and bifurcation) are long-standing Codification rules; where an ASU above did not
  restate the paragraph, the item asks only the core rule.

## SEC regulations (eCFR, title 17, fetched 2026-11-16)
- Regulation S-X Rule 3-01 (two years of audited balance sheets), Rule 3-02 (three years of
  income and cash flow statements), Rule 3-04:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-210
- Regulation S-K Item 10(e) (non-GAAP measures: equal or greater prominence of the GAAP measure,

  Item 303: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229

## GASB standards (primary text, storage.gasb.org PDFs, fetched 2026-11-16)
- GASB Statement 34, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%2034.pdf : government-wide statements
  exclude fiduciary activities; program and general revenues; major fund 10% and 5% tests;
  general fund always major; internal service funds in governmental activities; proprietary cash
  flow categories.
- GASB Statement 54, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%2054.pdf : nonspendable, restricted,
  committed (formal action of the highest level of decision-making authority), assigned and
  unassigned fund balance.
- GASB Statement 61, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%2061.pdf : financial accountability and
  blending criteria for component units.
- GASB Statement 63, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%2063.pdf : net position components including
  net investment in capital assets.
- GASB Statement 65, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%2065.pdf : property taxes received before the
  levy period and imposed nonexchange time requirements as deferred inflows; resources received
  before eligibility requirements other than time are liabilities.
- GASB Statement 68, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%2068.pdf : net pension liability (total
  pension liability less fiduciary net position); 10-year RSI schedules.
- GASB Statement 103, Financial Reporting Model Improvements, https://storage.gasb.org/GASBS%20103.pdf :
  effective for fiscal years beginning after June 15, 2025, so testable in 2026 under the AICPA
  policy. Used: five MD&A sections and "why" detailed analyses; unusual or infrequent items as the
  last flows; proprietary subtotal for operating income (loss) and noncapital subsidies; budgetary
  comparison as RSI with original-to-final and final-to-actual variances.
- GASB Statement 33 (nonexchange transactions) returned 403 from storage.gasb.org; the
  nonexchange items rely on GASB 65's restatement of Statement 33 paragraphs 16 and 19.
- gasb.org itself returned 403 to automated requests on 2026-11-16.

## COSO
- COSO, "Compliance Risk Management: Applying the COSO ERM Framework" (2020),
  https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/3059fc_5f9c50e005034badb07f94e9712d9a56.pdf (linked from
  https://www.coso.org/erm-framework, fetched 2026-11-16): reproduces the 2017 framework's five
  components and 20 principles (Figure 1.2), severity by likelihood and impact, and tolerance as
  acceptable variation in performance.
- The COSO executive summary PDF and the 2018 ESG guidance link returned 404; the bank has no
  item that rests on the ESG guidance.

## Analysis and managerial accounting
- The blueprint lists current textbooks on finance, economics and managerial and cost accounting
  as references. The ratio, costing, variance, budgeting, CVP, cost of capital, NPV, payback, EVA,
  elasticity and discount-cost items use standard textbook formulas, and every computed key is
  recomputed in Python in its source line and checked by `build.py` (80 items).

## Modelling
- Four 50-question multiple-choice mock tests: Area I 22, Area II 20, Area III 8 (44/40/16%,
  inside the 40-50/35-45/10-20 ranges). The splits inside each area are Tigertest's. Pools are
  four times the per-test counts, so no item repeats across tests. The 75% target is Tigertest's
  readiness line.
- Task-based simulations, research tasks and the preparation of full statements from trial
  balances are not modelled; the landing says so.

## Left out on purpose
- Kept clear of `data/cpa-far-questions.json`: FAR's fund-type identification, 10-K item numbers,
  filing deadlines, EPS, quick ratio, inventory turnover, times interest earned, ROE from equity,
  EBITDA computation, DPO, basic five-step revenue items, lessee accounting, long-lived asset
  impairment and the consolidation basics (NCI in equity, intercompany loans, over 50% voting)
  are not repeated. Three items that matched FAR or CFA Level I facts (common-size income
  statement base, NPV over IRR for exclusive projects, impairment reversal) were replaced.
- No overlap with `cpa-aud`, `cpa-reg` or `cpa-tcp` (no auditing or tax items).
- Not used: ASU 2025-06's new internal-use software threshold (not yet testable); highly
  inflationary economies and the private company goodwill alternative (not confirmed in a
  primary text read for this build); ESG-specific COSO guidance (not loaded).
