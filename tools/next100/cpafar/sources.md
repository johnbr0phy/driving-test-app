# Sources for `cpafar` (CPA Exam, Financial Accounting and Reporting section)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the AICPA's published FAR blueprint and the
FASB, GASB and SEC requirements it names. No AICPA released question, retired simulation, commercial
review course or question bank was copied. Checked 2026-11-02.

## Exam format (official)
- AICPA & CIMA, "Learn what is tested on the CPA Exam":
  https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/learn-what-is-tested-on-the-cpa-exam (checked 2026-11-02).
  Lists the Blueprints effective January 1, 2026 and January 1, 2027, and the summary of changes for 2027.
  FAR testlets: 25 MCQ, 25 MCQ, then 2, 3 and 2 task-based simulations.
- AICPA, "Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints effective January 1, 2026" (PDF):
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5d2r66sSSE8wJRZTqAshlE/f97ec39c394eeb61b20a5824784306df/CPA_Exam_Blueprints_2026.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-02):
  - FAR is a Core section, 4 hours, 50 multiple-choice questions and 7 task-based simulations.
  - Score weighting for FAR: MCQs 50%, TBSs 50%.
  - Content areas: I Financial Reporting 30-40%; II Select Balance Sheet Accounts 30-40%;
    III Select Transactions 25-35%.
  - Skill allocation: Remembering and Understanding 5-15%, Application 45-55%, Analysis 35-45%,
    Evaluation not tested.
  - Content scope used for the categories: for-profit statements (incl. consolidation with
    noncontrolling interests, foreign currency transactions), nongovernmental not-for-profit statements,
    state and local government measurement focus and fund purposes, public company topics (10-Q, 10-K
    Part II Items 7, 7A, 8, 8-K, basic and diluted EPS), special purpose frameworks (AU-C 800), ratios and
    budget variances; cash, trade receivables, inventory, PP&E (incl. held for sale and impairment),
    investments (fair value, amortized cost, equity method), intangibles (incl. purchased software and
    cloud computing arrangements), payables and accrued liabilities (incl. AROs and exit costs), debt
    (incl. modification vs extinguishment, troubled debt restructuring, covenants), equity; accounting
    changes and errors, contingencies and commitments, revenue (five-step model, contract costs,
    not-for-profit contributions), income taxes, fair value measurement, lessee accounting, subsequent events.
  - Not in FAR (moved to BAR in 2024) and not asked here: business combinations beyond consolidation
    eliminations, derivatives and hedging, stock compensation, defined benefit pensions, lessor accounting,
    government-wide statement preparation.
- AICPA, "Summary of revisions to the Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints effective January 1, 2027"
  (approved by the Board of Examiners September 16, 2026):
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5CsrSRU2mFGsmWEo9FqwLy/83e5ec97beab5daff376dba518589e75/Summary_of_Changes_to_the_Exam_Blueprints_effective_01012027.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-02): for FAR, only a reference (NCGA Statements and Interpretations) was added to the
  section introduction, "not intended to significantly change the nature or scope" of FAR content.
- AICPA, "Learn more about CPA Exam scoring and pass rates":
  https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/learn-more-about-cpa-exam-scoring-and-pass-rates and
  NASBA, "Score Information": https://nasba.org/exams/cpaexam/scores/ (checked 2026-11-02): scores are
  reported on a 0 to 99 scale, 75 passes, a 75 is not 75% correct, scores are not curved.

## Not used in copy
- Exam fees (set by each jurisdiction and NASBA) and credit windows (18 or 30 months, varies by
  jurisdiction) are left out of the bank and only described as varying on the landing.

## Standards used in the questions (current as of 2026-11-02)
- FASB ASC 205-20, 220, 230, 250, 321, 323, 326 (CECL), 330 (ASU 2015-11 LCNRV), 350, 350-40 and
  ASU 2018-15 (cloud computing implementation costs), 360, 410, 420, 450, 470-10, 470-50, 470-60,
  505, 606, 730, 740 (ASU 2015-17 noncurrent deferred taxes), 805 (acquisition costs, bargain purchase,
  goodwill with NCI at fair value), 810, 820, 830, 835-30 (ASU 2015-03 debt issuance costs),
  842 (lessee), 855, 860, 958 (ASU 2016-14 net asset classes, liquidity and functional expense
  disclosures; ASU 2018-08 conditional contributions; contributed services and agency transfers).
- GASB Codification: governmental funds on current financial resources / modified accrual,
  government-wide on economic resources / accrual; fund types (general, special revenue, capital
  projects, debt service, permanent, enterprise, internal service, custodial).
- SEC: Form 10-K deadlines (60/75/90 days), Form 8-K within four business days, Regulation S-X
  Rule 10-01(d) interim review of Form 10-Q statements; AICPA AU-C 800 titles for special purpose
  frameworks.
