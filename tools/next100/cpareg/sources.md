# Sources for `cpareg` (Uniform CPA Examination, Taxation and Regulation section)

All 200 questions are original, written from the AICPA's published REG blueprint and the statutes,
regulations and uniform acts it lists as references. No AICPA released question, sample test,
commercial review course or app item was used. Checked 2026-11-02.

## Exam format (official)
- AICPA, "Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints", approved by the Board of Examiners Aug. 18, 2025,
  effective January 2026:
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/71s84dkfo3KEsoLlz4vv6G/f4314469ec5368b5b4dd0d0184f00a71/CPA_Exam_Blueprints_2026.pdf
  (linked from https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-what-is-tested-on-the-cpa-exam),
  downloaded and read 2026-11-02:
  - REG is a Core section (all candidates take AUD, FAR and REG plus one Discipline section).
  - 4 hours; 72 multiple-choice questions (two testlets of 36) and 8 task-based simulations
    (testlets of 2, 3 and 3). MCQs and TBSs are each 50% of the score.
  - Content areas: I Ethics, Professional Responsibilities and Federal Tax Procedures 10-20%;
    II Business Law 15-25%; III Federal Taxation of Property Transactions 5-15%;
    IV Federal Taxation of Individuals 22-32%; V Federal Taxation of Entities (including tax
    preparation) 23-33%.
  - Skills: Remembering and Understanding 25-35%, Application 35-45%, Analysis 25-35%.
  - Section assumptions: "Candidates will not be tested on their knowledge of specific tax rate
    percentages, amounts or limitations that are indexed to inflation." Wages are assumed not to
    include overtime or tips unless stated.
  - References: RMBCA, RULPA, RUPA, Uniform Accountancy Act, UCC, Internal Revenue Code,
    Circular 230, Treasury Regulations, P.L. 86-272, BAPCPA 2005, ACA, FCPA, UDITPA.
- The same AICPA page lists a "CPA Exam Blueprints 2027" document effective January 1, 2027. Its
  REG changes could not be read on 2026-11-02; the landing describes the 2026 blueprint only.
- One Big Beautiful Bill Act (H.R. 1, 2025) provisions became eligible for REG testing on
  July 1, 2026 (AICPA "Testing of H.R. 1 on the CPA Exam", listed on the page above; dates as
  reported by review providers, e.g. https://accounting.uworld.com/cpa-review/cpa-exam/changes/).
  No item depends on an amount the Act changed (SALT cap, standard deduction, child tax credit,
  bonus depreciation percentage, Section 179 limit, charitable floors, tips and overtime).
- Scoring: NASBA, "Score Information", https://nasba.org/exams/cpaexam/scores/ and AICPA,
  "Learn more about CPA Exam scoring and pass rates",
  https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/learn-more-about-cpa-exam-scoring-and-pass-rates
  (checked 2026-11-02): scores are reported on a 0 to 99 scale, 75 passes, and 75 is not 75%
  correct. The window to pass all four sections is set by each state board (NASBA now
  recommends 30 months; many boards adopted it), so the copy says "set by your state board".

## Modelling
- Four 50-question multiple-choice mock tests, the blueprint midpoints scaled to 50:
  I 7 (ethics 3, procedures 4), II 10 (agency and contracts 5, debtor-creditor and federal
  laws 3, business structure 2), III 5, IV 14 (gross income 5, deductions and losses 5,
  filing status, tax and credits 4), V 14 (C corporations and state tax 6, flow-through and
  exempt entities 8). 75% pass line is Tigertest's target, since the real 75 is a scaled score.
- Task-based simulations and research tasks are not modelled; the landing says so.

## Rules used (none indexed to inflation)
- Circular 230 sections 10.21, 10.22, 10.27 (contingent fees), 10.28 (client records), 10.29
  (conflicts, written consent). IRC 7701(a)(36) (return preparer), 6694 (substantial authority,
  reasonable basis with disclosure), 6107 (3-year retention), 7525 (practitioner privilege).
- Procedures: IRC 6501 (3 years, 6 years for a 25% omission, early returns deemed filed on the
  due date), 6511 (3 years or 2 years from payment), 6213 (90 days), 6651 (5% a month to 25%),
  6662 (20%), 6663 (75%), 274(d) substantiation, Form 8275, FBAR $10,000 aggregate (31 CFR
  1010.350, fixed in regulation).
- Business law: Restatement agency rules, UCC 2-205 (firm offer, 3 months), 2-209, 2-509, the
  mailbox rule, statute of frauds; UCC Article 9 attachment, automatic perfection of consumer
  goods PMSIs, buyer in ordinary course, first to file or perfect; suretyship; Bankruptcy Code
  547 (90 days, 1 year insiders), 507(a)(1) domestic support first priority, 523 nondischargeable
  support; IRS common law control test; FUTA; FCPA facilitating payments; ACA employer
  shared responsibility, 50 full-time employees including equivalents (IRC 4980H, statutory).
  RUPA, RMBCA (existence on filing), promoter liability and novation, business judgment rule.
- Property: basis (1012), conversion (Reg. 1.168(i)-4), gifts (1015), inheritance (1014 and
  1223(9)), wash sales (1091), start-up and organizational costs (195, 248: $5,000 and $50,000
  are fixed in the Code), Section 197 15 years, MACRS (27.5 and 39 years, mid-month, 40%
  mid-quarter test, 7-year 200% DB first-year rate 14.29%), order of 179, bonus, MACRS; 179
  income limit carryforward. No Section 179 dollar limit or bonus percentage is asked.
- Individuals: 101, 103, 79 ($50,000 group-term life, fixed), 117, 104, tax benefit rule, 102,
  1402 (guaranteed payments), IRS Notice 2014-21 (virtual currency is property), 1222, 72(t),
  86 (85%), 71 repeal for instruments after 2018, 691, 132; 62 adjustments, 213 (7.5% floor),
  163(h)(3), 165(h) ($100 and 10% of AGI), 199A (20%), 1211 ($3,000 / $1,500, fixed), 704(d) and
  1366(d), 469(i) ($25,000 phased out from $100,000, fixed), 183, 1091, 267, 221, 219.
- Filing status and credits: 7703, 2(a) and 2(b), 152 (age 19 or 24, over half support), 32
  (refundable), 25A (AOTC four years and 40% refundable; lifetime learning 20% of $10,000,
  fixed), 1411 (3.8%, $250,000 joint, fixed), 6654 (100% / 110% over $150,000), 21.
- Entities: 11 (21%), 243 (50%, 65%, 100%), 172 (80% limit, indefinite carryforward), 1211(a) and
  1212(a) (3 back, 5 forward), 6655 large corporation rule, 6072 due dates, 163(j) (30%),
  Schedule M-3 instructions ($10 million), 274(n) (50% meals), 162(f), 264, 1361 to 1378 (100
  shareholders, one class of stock, eligible shareholders, election by the 15th day of the 3rd
  month, consent, revocation over 50%, passive income 3 years, 5-year built-in gains period,
  AAA), 721, 722, 723, 752, 731, 732, 706(b), 704(d), Reg. 301.7701-3 (defaults, 60 months),
  501(c)(3), (c)(6), 511 to 513, P.L. 86-272, UDITPA.
