# Sources for `cpatcp` (Uniform CPA Examination, Tax Compliance and Planning discipline section)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the AICPA's published TCP blueprint and
the Internal Revenue Code, Treasury Regulations and IRS guidance it lists as references. No AICPA
released question, sample test, commercial review course or app item was used. Checked 2026-11-15.

## Exam format and outline (official)
- AICPA, "Uniform CPA Examination Blueprints", effective January 1, 2026 (approved August 2025),
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5d2r66sSSE8wJRZTqAshlE/f97ec39c394eeb61b20a5824784306df/CPA_Exam_Blueprints_2026.pdf
  linked from https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-what-is-tested-on-the-cpa-exam,
  downloaded and read 2026-11-15:
  - TCP is one of three Discipline sections (BAR, ISC, TCP); a candidate takes the three Core
    sections plus one Discipline section.
  - 4 hours; 68 multiple-choice questions in two testlets of 34 and 7 task-based simulations
    (testlets of 2, 3 and 2). MCQs and TBSs are each 50% of the score.
  - Content areas: I Tax Compliance and Planning for Individuals and Personal Financial Planning
    30-40%; II Entity Tax Compliance 30-40%; III Entity Tax Planning 10-20%; IV Property
    Transactions (disposition of assets) 10-20%.
  - Skills: Remembering and Understanding 5-15%, Application 55-65%, Analysis 25-35%.
  - Section assumptions: "Candidates will not be tested on their knowledge of specific tax rate
    percentages, amounts or limitations that are indexed to inflation."
  - Representative tasks used for the categories: equity compensation, AMT items, imputed interest
    on below-market loans, foreign earned income, kiddie tax, FSAs and HSAs, itemized vs standard,
    estimated tax, noncash charitable gifts; at-risk and passive limits; gift tax; retirement
    plans, investments, education funding, insurance, ownership and beneficiary designations,
    after-tax ROI; C corporation NOLs and capital losses, 351, distributions, liquidations, E&P,
    shareholder loans, consolidated returns, international sourcing, CFC, permanent
    establishment, branch vs subsidiary; S corporation basis, distributions, liquidations and
    allocation after a sale; partnership basis and liabilities, elections, partner transactions,
    ownership changes and 743(b); trusts (types, accounting income, DNI); 501(c)(3) status and
    UBI; entity selection, formation and liquidation; C, S and partnership planning (NOL savings,
    state apportionment, rate changes, corporate estimated tax, built-in gains, termination,
    AAA/AEP election, 704(c)); like-kind exchanges and involuntary conversions; 1231, 1245, 1250,
    unrecaptured 1250, 1244, installment sales; related parties and imputed interest.
- AICPA, "Summary of Changes to the Exam Blueprints effective 01/01/2027",
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/5CsrSRU2mFGsmWEo9FqwLy/83e5ec97beab5daff376dba518589e75/Summary_of_Changes_to_the_Exam_Blueprints_effective_01012027.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-15): "Tax Compliance and Planning (TCP) - No revisions to the TCP section."
  So the bank fits both the 2026 and the 2027 blueprint.
- AICPA, "Testing of H.R. 1: One Big Beautiful Bill Act on the CPA Exam" (Board of Examiners,
  September 18, 2025),
  https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/7E8iJ3Bqw2n90Ckm3RJokr/be209fa92afc4b6abaed980c3186757f/Testing_of_H.R._1_on_the_CPA_Exam.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-15): provisions effective in 2024 and 2025, including those for tax years
  beginning after December 31, 2025, are eligible for REG and TCP testing from July 1, 2026.
- Scoring: NASBA, "Score Information", https://nasba.org/exams/cpaexam/scores/ (checked
  2026-11-15, as for cpareg): scores 0 to 99, 75 passes, and 75 is a scaled score, not 75%.

## Modelling
- Four 50-question multiple-choice mock tests at the blueprint midpoints (35/35/15/15): Area I
  17 (individual compliance and planning 6, passive and at-risk 3, gift tax 3, personal
  financial planning 5), Area II 18 (C corporations 6, S corporations 4, partnerships 5, trusts
  and exempt organizations 3), Area III 7 (formation and entity choice 3, owner and entity
  planning 4), Area IV 8 (nontaxable dispositions 3, character of gain 3, related parties 2).
  The splits inside each area are Tigertest's. Pools are four times the per-test counts, so no
  item repeats across tests. The 75% target is Tigertest's readiness line.
- Task-based simulations and research tasks are not modelled; the landing says so.

## Left out on purpose
- No amount indexed to inflation (brackets, standard deduction, contribution limits, exemption
  amounts, the basic exclusion amount, foreign earned income exclusion amount, kiddie tax
  threshold). The one indexed figure used, the 2026 gift tax annual exclusion of $19,000, is
  named with its year and given in each stem.
- Items that duplicate cpareg facts were avoided: REG already asks gift and inherited basis,
  wash sales, MACRS, the $25,000 rental allowance, 704(d) and 1366(d) loss limits, the 80% NOL
  deduction, the 3-back 5-forward rule, S corporation eligibility, separately stated items,
  basic partnership contributions and the 501(c)(3) campaign ban. Where TCP builds on a REG
  rule, the TCP item asks the planning or computation step instead.
- Not used: rules the 2025 act changed whose details are still being interpreted (renamed GILTI,
  new HSA plan types, 529 K-12 amounts, tips and overtime, the corporate 1% charitable floor),
  and state principal and income allocations of trustee fees (the trust items state the
  allocation in the stem).
