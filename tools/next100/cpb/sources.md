# CPB (AAPC) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from AAPC's own CPB exam page and the
primary federal manuals, regulations and statutes below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from
AAPC practice exams, study guides, the CPB training course, released items, or any commercial
review book or prep site. Guidelines and concepts only: no CPT descriptors and no code-book
lookups (like the cpc, cca and ccs banks).

## Outline and format (AAPC)

- AAPC, Taking the CPB exam, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://www.aapc.com/certifications/cpb/taking-the-cpb-exam
  135 multiple-choice questions, 4 hours in one sitting, 70% to pass (95 correct); online with a
  live remote proctor or at a testing center; approved code books (AMA CPT Professional,
  ICD-10-CM, HCPCS Level II, current or prior year), an on-screen calculator. Domains and question
  counts: Types of insurance 29; Billing regulations 17; HIPAA and compliance 7; Reimbursement and
  collections 19; Claims and billing 19; Coding 10; Case analysis 34. The sub-topics listed under
  each domain set the category contents. The page names HIPAA, the False Claims Act, the FDCPA and
  the Stark Law. No dated change to the exam was shown.
- AAPC, Certified Professional Biller (CPB) certification page, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://www.aapc.com/certifications/cpb
  Four content areas (core billing knowledge; insurance and payer requirements; denials, appeals
  and collections; compliance and regulatory standards); remote or test-center delivery. Fees are
  never stated by Tigertest.

Blueprint: the seven AAPC domains scaled to 50 per test (10/6/3/7/7/4/13). Case analysis items are
short billing scenarios applying the other domains (remittance math, timely filing and appeal
dates, payer order, modifiers, collections), standing in for AAPC's source-document cases.
