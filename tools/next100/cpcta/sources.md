# CPCT/A (NHA) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official test plan and the
primary sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from NHA study guides, NHA practice
tests or Preparation Suite items, released items, or any commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (National Healthcareer Association)

- NHA, Certified Patient Care Technician/Assistant (CPCT/A) Test Plan for the CPCT/A Exam, based
  on the results of a job analysis completed in 2025 (8 pages), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://info.nhanow.com/hubfs/Products/Certified%20Patient%20Care%20Technician%20%28CPCT%29/Test%20Plan/2025/NHA%202025%20CPCT%20Test%20Plan.pdf
  100 scored items and 20 pretest items, exam time 2 hours. Domains and scored items:
  1 Patient Care 43; 2 Compliance, Safety, and Professional Responsibility 19; 3 Infection
  Control 16; 4 Phlebotomy and Nonblood Specimen Collection 12; 5 EKG 10. Tasks 1A to 1PP,
  2A to 2Q, 3A to 3I, 4A to 4P, 5A to 5I and knowledge statements k1 to k131 were used category
  by category. The Five Rights of Delegation (k64: right task, circumstance, person, direction and
  communication, supervision) and the two patient identifiers (k69: name and date of birth) are
  stated in the plan itself.
- NHA media center, "Coming soon: new CPCT/A certification exam study materials", fetched
  2026-11-16:
  https://info.nhanow.com/mediacenter/coming-soon-new-cpcta-certification-exam-study-materials
  The new CPCT/A exam (on the 2025 test plan) is available January 13, 2027, "with minimal impact
  to exam topics and content areas"; anyone who tests and fails in the 30 days before the launch
  retests on the new exam. The bank follows the 2025 plan because it is the one candidates
  preparing now will sit.
- NHA, CPCT/A Test Plan based on the 2018 job analysis (the plan for exams before January 13,
  2027), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://info.nhanow.com/hubfs/Test%20Plans/nha-2018-cpct-test-plan-1.pdf
  Same length and time (100 scored + 20 pretest, 2 hours); domains 45 / 20 / 11 / 14 / 10
  (Phlebotomy was 14 and Infection Control 11). Stated on the landing as the plan for exams
  before the change.
- NHA, Patient Care Technician certification page, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.nhanow.com/certifications/patient-care-technician
  Links the test plan; the exam has 100 (scored) questions. Eligibility: a high school diploma or
  GED (or earning one within 18 months) plus a patient care technician training program within
  the last 5 years, or supervised patient care technician work of 1 year within the last 3 years
  (or 2 years within the last 5). Taken at the candidate's school, a PSI testing center or by
  live remote proctoring. Certification renews every two years with 10 continuing education
  credits; an expired certification can be reinstated within 1 year.

## Practice standards used in questions

- CDC, Transmission-Based Precautions, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/basics/transmission-based-precautions.html
  Contact: gown and gloves on entry, removed before leaving; dedicated or disposable equipment
  such as blood pressure cuffs. Droplet: mask on entry, patient masked for transport. Airborne:
  AIIR, fit-tested N95 for staff, patient wears a surgical mask for necessary transport; airborne
  diseases include tuberculosis, measles, chickenpox and disseminated herpes zoster.
- CDC, Guideline for Isolation Precautions (2007, page updated 2023-11-27), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/index.html
  Standard precautions for all patients (all body fluids except sweat); scabies under contact
  precautions; disseminated zoster airborne plus contact; protective environment for
  severely immunocompromised patients (no fresh flowers or standing water).
- CDC, Clinical Safety: Hand Hygiene for Healthcare Workers, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/index.html
  Alcohol rub until hands are dry (about 20 seconds); soap and water when visibly soiled and in
  C. difficile outbreaks; gloves do not replace hand hygiene and are changed between patients.
- CDC, Sequence for putting on and removing PPE (poster), recorded as fetched 2026-11-15 by the
  sibling banks' builds (not re-fetched here):
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/Toolkits-PPE-Sequence-P.pdf
  Eye protection removed by the headband or ear pieces; gown rolled inside-out away from the body.
- OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
  No recapping by hand; sharps containers; red bags for regulated waste; hepatitis B vaccine at
  no cost to exposed workers; post-exposure evaluation.
- OSHA Hazard Communication standard, 29 CFR 1910.1200 (secondary container labels; SDS), fetched
  2026-11-16:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200
- MedlinePlus, Vital signs (reviewed 1/1/2025), fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002341.htm
  Adult resting heart rate 60 to 100, respiratory rate 12 to 18, temperature 97.7 to 99.1 F.
- MedlinePlus, CPR - adult and adolescent after onset of puberty (reviewed 2/10/2026), fetched
  2026-11-16: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000013.htm
  100 to 120 compressions a minute, about 2 inches deep, full recoil, 30 compressions to 2
  breaths. The AHA's own guideline pages (cpr.heart.org, ahajournals.org) returned 403 to
  automated fetches on 2026-11-16, so only figures MedlinePlus states are used.
- CMS, QRP pocket guide, Pressure Ulcer/Injury stages and definitions, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.cms.gov/files/document/pocket-guidepressure-ulcers-and-injuries-stages-and-definitions.pdf
  Stage 1: intact skin with non-blanchable redness. (NPIAP's own staging page returned 403.)
- CLSI, Order of Blood Draw: Tubes and Additives (blog, 19 March 2019), recorded as fetched
  2026-11-15 by the sibling RPT build (not re-fetched here): https://clsi.org/about/blog/order-of-blood-draw-tubes-and-additives/
  Blood culture, citrate, serum, heparin, EDTA, fluoride/oxalate.
- WHO guidelines on drawing blood: best practices in phlebotomy (2010), recorded as fetched
  2026-11-15 by the sibling RPT build (not re-fetched here):
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241599221
  Tourniquet time, puncture across the fingerprint lines, wiping the first drop, hemolysis.
- MedlinePlus, Fecal occult blood test, fetched 2026-11-16 (guaiac preparation: avoid red meat such
  as beef, lamb and liver, NSAIDs, vitamin C over 250 mg and some raw fruits and vegetables, as the
  provider directs):
  https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/fecal-occult-blood-test-fobt/
- The Joint Commission, National Patient Safety Goals (two identifiers; clinical alarm safety)
  as named in the NHA test plan (k69, k76). TJC pages return 403 to automated fetches; only the
  two facts the test plan itself states or names are used.
- Stable nursing assistant practice the test plan names (k3 body mechanics, k10 to k13 beds and
  bathing, k22 aspiration precautions, k24 positions, k41 ROM, k43 ambulation devices, k47
  transport, k49 gait belt, k61 Kübler-Ross stages, k63 postmortem care, k118 to k131 EKG basics:
  25 mm/s paper, 10 mm/mV standard, half sensitivity, lead placement, artifact types, Holter and
  telemetry). Electrode color code is the one used in the United States (RA white, LA black, LL
  red, RL green).

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test, scaled from the 2025 plan's 43/19/16/12/10 of 100: Patient Care 22,
  split by Tigertest into Basic Care and Daily Living 6, Vital Signs and Monitoring 6, Mobility,
  Procedures and Emergencies 6, Psychosocial and End-of-Life Care 4; Compliance, Safety and
  Professional Responsibility 9; Infection Control 8; Phlebotomy and Nonblood Specimens 6; EKG 5.
  The rounding (21.5 to 22, 9.5 to 9) is Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target; NHA's 390 is a scaled score, not a percentage.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees, no state-specific scope rules (what a
  technician may do "varies by state and facility" in the copy, never in a key).
- EKG strips and rhythms are described in words; no images.
- Duplicates: written against data/cna-questions.json, data/phlebotomy-questions.json,
  data/cet-questions.json, data/ccma-questions.json, data/cmaa-questions.json,
  data/rpt-questions.json, data/ascp-pbt-questions.json, data/medication-aide-questions.json and
  data/ncma-questions.json, and scanned for repeated stems and fact-and-answer pairs (stem and key
  token overlap). About 45 items that matched (bargaining with a graduation wish, cuff too small
  reads high, PPE donning order, hand hygiene breaks transmission, C. diff soap and water, TB N95,
  needlestick washing, implied consent by holding out an arm, V1 and V4 placement, 0.04-second
  box, 9 QRS in 6 seconds, Holter diary, 24-hour urine first void, basilic vein, fist pumping and
  potassium, fingerstick across the whorls, and others) were replaced with different facts.
  Core facts every patient care bank shares (contact precautions, Stage 1, implied consent in an
  emergency) remain with different scenarios.
