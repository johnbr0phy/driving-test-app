# CRFSC bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official topic list and the
federal law below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from the RCMP CRFSC student handbook, its
written test, any instructor's test or any commercial question bank or prep site.

## Course and test (RCMP, Canadian Firearms Program)

- RCMP, Safety courses, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/firearms-safety-training-transport-and-storage/safety-courses
  CRFSC is for anyone who wants to acquire restricted firearms; you must pass a written test and a
  practical test; taught by qualified instructors (contact the Chief Firearms Officer to become
  one). Topic list used for the categories, in its order: evolution of firearms, major parts,
  types and actions; basic firearms safety practices; ammunition; operating handgun actions;
  firing techniques and procedures for handguns; care of restricted firearms; responsibilities of
  the firearms owner/user; safe storage, display, transportation and handling of restricted
  firearms. No item count, weights or pass mark are published, so the blueprint counts are ours.
- RCMP, Information sheet for form RCMP 5592 (Application for a Possession and Acquisition
  Licence), fetched 2026-11-15: https://www.rcmp.ca/sites/default/files/doc/5592e.pdf
  Box 20 b): a PAL for restricted or prohibited firearms needs two courses, the CFSC and the
  CRFSC; a CFSC passed before February 1, 1999 meets both.
- RCMP, Safety course exceptions, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/firearms-safety-training-transport-and-storage/safety-course-exceptions
  CRFSC is an introductory course for anyone over 18 who wants to own restricted firearms; a CFSC
  passed before February 1999 counts as the CRFSC too.
- RCMP, Target shooters, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/licensing/target-shooters
  Residents need a valid licence and a valid Authorization to Transport (ATT); apply online, by
  phone or with form RCMP 5490; check that a club or range is approved by the CFO before joining;
  the CFO may ask a club to confirm target practice in the previous five years.
- RCMP, Canadian residents (licensing), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://rcmp.ca/en/firearms/licensing/canadian-residents
  PALs are renewed every 5 years (landing only).
- Format (guides say): College of the North Atlantic firearms FAQ, fetched 2026-11-15,
  https://www.cna.nl.ca/Programs-Courses/Continuing-Education-Firearms-FAQs : about 6.5 to 7
  hours of theory, a written multiple-choice exam and an individually scheduled practical exam,
  "Pass mark is 80% for each test". Used on the landing and in examFacts only, labelled as what
  course providers say.

## Law (Justice Laws Website, current to 2026-10-19, all fetched 2026-11-15)

- Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals Regulations,
  SOR/98-209: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209/FullText.html
  s.1 definitions (secure locking device; unloaded includes an attached magazine; unattended
  vehicle means no supervision by a person 18+ or licensed); s.6 storage of restricted (unloaded;
  locking device AND locked container/room not readily broken into, OR a vault, safe or room
  specially built or modified for restricted firearms; ammunition not readily accessible unless
  locked away); s.9(1) display in a dwelling-house (unloaded, locking device, securely attached
  to a non-portable structure, no ammunition with it); s.9(2) display elsewhere (chained or cabled
  to the structure; ammunition only in a locked container); s.9(4) may be detached for handling
  under direct and immediate supervision; s.11 transport of restricted (unloaded, secure locking
  device, locked opaque container that cannot readily be broken open or accidentally opened;
  unattended vehicle: container in locked trunk, or vehicle locked and container not visible);
  s.14(3) antique handguns in a locked opaque container; s.15 load only where discharge is
  lawful; s.16 post only within Canada by Canada Post's most secure signature service.
- Authorizations to Transport Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms Regulations, SOR/98-206:
  https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-206/FullText.html
  s.1.1 CFO issues an ATT when transport between specified places will not threaten safety;
  s.1.2 to 1.4 application by telephone with reason, dates, places; s.2 one ATT can cover more
  than one firearm; s.4 condition: a route that is reasonably direct; s.5 revoked if the licence
  is revoked or expires.
- Authorizations to Carry Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Handguns Regulations, SOR/98-207:
  https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-207/FullText.html
  Authorizations to carry only for protection of life in imminent danger where police protection
  is not sufficient, or for listed occupations (armoured car, wilderness work, licensed
  trappers); carried in a holster; one at a time.
- Firearms Act, S.C. 1995, c. 39: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/F-11.6/FullText.html
  s.12.2 (2023, c. 32) no registration certificate for a handgun to an individual (the handgun
  freeze); s.13 a registration certificate needs a licence for that kind of firearm; s.14
  serial number; s.17 restricted possessed only at the dwelling-house recorded in the Registry or
  a place a CFO authorizes; s.19(1) ATT reasons (target practice at approved clubs, repair, sale,
  moving, gun show, disposal); s.19(2.1) and (2.3) automatic ATT within the province to approved
  clubs and ranges on renewal or transfer, except for a gun collection; s.19(2.2) ATT home after a
  transfer; s.19.1 no ATT from a port of entry for a handgun without a registration certificate;
  s.20 authorization to carry; s.28 permitted purposes for acquiring restricted (protection of
  life, occupation, target shooting, collection for restricted other than handguns); s.29 clubs
  and ranges need provincial approval.
- Criminal Code, Part III, s.84(1):
  https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-46/page-14.html
  Definitions of handgun (aimed and fired with one hand), restricted firearm (handgun not
  prohibited; semi-auto centrefire with barrel under 470 mm; firearm firing at under 660 mm when
  folded or telescoped), prohibited firearm (handgun with barrel 105 mm or less or 25/32 calibre,
  with an ISU competition exception), prohibited device (handgun barrel of 105 mm or less,
  silencer, replica).
- Regulations Prescribing Certain Firearms and Other Weapons ... as Prohibited or Restricted,
  SOR/98-462, Part 4: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462/FullText.html
  A magazine able to hold more than 10 cartridges for a semi-automatic handgun commonly available
  in Canada is a prohibited device.
- Public Safety Canada, Former Bill C-21, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.publicsafety.gc.ca/cnt/cntrng-crm/frrms/c21-en.aspx
  National handgun freeze in force October 21, 2022 (sale, purchase or transfer by individuals
  and bringing newly acquired handguns into Canada); codified by Royal Assent December 15, 2023;
  exceptions for authorizations to carry and IOC/IPC handgun disciplines; licensed owners can keep
  and use registered handguns for target shooting and collection; classification unchanged.

## Handling, ammunition, firing and care

There is no public RCMP text for handgun technique (the student handbook is sold through course
providers). These items use universal, uncontested mechanics and safety practice (revolver and
semi-automatic actions, grip, stance, sight alignment, cleaning) and are written to agree with
the RCMP topic list and the Vital Four ACTS / PROVE practice the CFSC teaches. Nothing that varies
between instructors (exact stance names beyond Weaver and isosceles, hangfire wait times) is
keyed as a number.

## Kept clear of the CFSC (capal) and NZ firearms banks

Checked against data/canadian-firearms-questions.json and data/nz-firearms-questions.json: no
fact-and-answer pair repeats (for example no ACTS letter, PROVE letter, misfire or hangfire
definition, 470 mm restricted rule, 5-cartridge rifle magazine or "can most individuals buy
handguns" item). Overlapping topics are asked from the handgun and restricted-class side.

## Left out on purpose

Fees, fines and penalties; the May 2020 and later Orders in Council and the buyback; provincial
range rules and Quebec registry details; magazine-capacity items beyond the federal 10-cartridge
handgun rule; any modification, conversion, concealment or tactical content; the test itself.
