# Sources for `crisc` (ISACA Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control)

All questions are original, written by Tigertest from the CRISC exam content outline (job
practice) and from general IT risk management practice and public standards. No ISACA review
manual item, QAE database item, sample question, past exam question or commercial practice
question was copied or reworded, and no prep provider is named. ISACA publishes no open
question catalogue for CRISC, so there was nothing to run a similarity check against; the
stems and keys were instead checked against the Tigertest cism, cisa, cissp, isc2cc, secplus,
cysa, sscp, securityx, sc900, cobit, itil and cpa-isc banks for repeated fact-and-answer pairs
(stem similarity, shared content words and identical keys). Checked 2026-11-23.

## Exam content outline (official, confirmed)
- ISACA, "What is covered on the CRISC exam?" (CRISC Exam Content Outline),
  https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/crisc/crisc-exam-content-outline (fetched 2026-11-23):
  150 questions; Domain 1 Governance 26% (A Organizational Governance: strategy, goals and
  objectives; structure, roles and responsibilities; culture and ethics; policies and standards;
  business processes and resilience; asset management. B Risk Governance: ERM; lines of
  defense; risk profile; risk appetite and tolerance; risk frameworks and legal, regulatory and
  contractual requirements), Domain 2 IT Risk Assessment 22% (A Risk Identification: risk
  events; threat modeling and threat landscape; vulnerability management; risk scenario
  development and evaluation. B Risk Analysis: risk assessment concepts and standards; BIA;
  risk register; risk analysis methodologies; inherent and residual risk), Domain 3 Risk
  Response and Reporting 32% (A Risk Response: response options; risk and control ownership;
  vendor and supply chain risk; issues, findings, exceptions and exemptions. B Control Design
  and Implementation: frameworks, types and standards; design, selection, implementation and
  analysis; testing methodologies. C Risk Monitoring and Reporting: action plans; data
  collection, aggregation, analysis and validation; KRIs, KCIs and KPIs; monitoring and
  reporting techniques; emerging risks), Domain 4 Technology and Security 20% (A Technology
  and Security: technology principles; roadmaps and enterprise architecture; operations
  management; SDLC; data lifecycle; portfolio and project management; resilience and disaster
  recovery; emerging technologies. B Information Security Principles: security concepts,
  frameworks and standards; awareness and training; data privacy and protection), and 24
  supporting tasks. These subtopics are the nine drill domains.
- ISACA press release, "ISACA Updates CDPSE and CRISC Exams to Reflect Latest Risk and Privacy
  Priorities", 7 April 2025,
  https://www.isaca.org/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2025/isaca-updates-cdpse-and-crisc-exams-to-reflect-latest-risk-and-privacy-priorities
  (fetched 2026-11-23): the four CRISC domains stay the same; Risk Assessment rises to 22% from
  20% and Technology and Security falls to 20% from 22%; updated exam available from
  3 November 2025, new preparation materials from 3 September 2025. No later CRISC outline
  change was found (the November 2026 update ISACA announced is for CISM, not CRISC), so the
  bank follows this outline, in force on the build date.

## Exam format (official)
- ISACA, "ISACA Certification Exam Candidate Guide" (copyright 2026, PDF modified
  2026-08-07), https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/-/media/fa494652c5f149289af38cef18328650.ashx
  (fetched 2026-11-23, read as PDF text): CRISC is 4 hours (240 minutes), 150 multiple-choice
  questions; every question has one best answer and four options; no penalty for wrong
  answers; scores reported on a scale of 200 to 800, 450 or higher to pass; scored and unscored
  items; four attempts in a rolling 12 months, waits of 30, 90 and 90 days; PSI test center or
  remote proctoring; CRISC languages English, Spanish and Japanese; three or more years of
  experience in IT risk management and IS control, no experience waivers or substitutions.
- ISACA, "How do I become CRISC certified?", https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/crisc
  (fetched 2026-11-23): offered in English, Spanish or Japanese; PSI test centers or remote
  proctoring; eligibility good for six months from registration; five years after passing to
  apply. Fees are in the guide and deliberately not used.
- ISACA, "How to get CRISC certified", https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/crisc/get-crisc-certified
  (fetched 2026-11-23): three or more years of CRISC work experience across at least two of the
  four domains, within the ten years before applying; verified by a supervisor or manager;
  apply within five years of passing; at least 120 CPE hours per three-year period and at
  least 20 a year.

## Standards used for facts in explanations
- NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1, "Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments", https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final
  (checked 2026-11-23): threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and impact; risk assessment process.
- NIST SP 800-39, "Managing Information Security Risk", March 2011,
  https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/39/final (fetched 2026-11-23): organization-wide risk
  management, supported by the detailed assessment and monitoring publications.
- NIST, "The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0", CSWP 29, 26 February 2024,
  https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf (checked 2026-11-23): six Functions
  with Govern added.
- The IIA, "The IIA's Three Lines Model: An update of the Three Lines of Defense", September
  2020, https://www.theiia.org/en/content/position-papers/2020/the-iias-three-lines-model-an-update-of-the-three-lines-of-defense/
  (fetched 2026-11-23): management, second-line risk and compliance roles, independent
  internal audit.
- Stable practice used without figures that change: ISO 31000 risk guidelines; ISO/IEC 27001
  ISMS requirements; NIST SP 800-53 control catalog; NIST SP 800-88 media sanitization; COSO
  ERM; COBIT 2019; STRIDE, DREAD, OCTAVE, PASTA; SLE = AV x EF; ALE = SLE x ARO; RTO, RPO, MTD;
  KRI, KCI, KPI; control test methods (inquiry, observation, inspection, reperformance);
  privacy principles (data minimization, purpose limitation, privacy by design, right of
  access, pseudonymization).

## Not modelled or left out
- Fees and application fees (on the official pages, change, deliberately not used).
- The raw-to-scaled conversion is not published; no percent pass mark is stated.
- No item asks about the exam itself.
- No named products, officeholders, dated breaches or current affairs.
