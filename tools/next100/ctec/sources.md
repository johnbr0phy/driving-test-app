# Sources for `ctec` (California CTEC Registered Tax Preparer: 60-hour qualifying education course final exam)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from CTEC's own published curriculum
guidelines and registration rules and from the IRS and Franchise Tax Board publications for tax
year 2025 that those guidelines point to. No approved provider's course, review question or final
exam item, commercial course, book or app item was used, reworded or adapted, and no provider is
named. Checked 2026-11-19.

Tax year: 2025 (returns filed in 2026), the year CTEC's 2025/2026 CP23A guidelines cover. Every
yearly figure in a question names 2025 and comes from the 2025 IRS or FTB page below.

## Outline and format (official, watch these first)
- California Tax Education Council, "Qualifying Education (QE) Provider Application 2026" with
  policy CP13/CP13A and Exhibit CP23A "Qualifying Education Guidelines" (revised 07/24/2026),
  https://ctecstorage.blob.core.windows.net/public/CTEC%20-%20Qualifying%20Education%20%28QE%29%20Provider%20Application%20-%202026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave the curriculum the bank follows: Section 22255 requires at least 60
  hours, at least 45 federal (of which 2 are ethics) and 15 California; the completion certificate
  shows 43 hours federal, 2 ethics and 15 California. The CP23A topic list (I General filing
  issues and ethics, II Filing status, III Dependents, IV Tax payment, V Income, VI Exclusions,
  VII Divorce, VIII Gains and losses, IX Adjustments, X Deductions, XI Credits, XII and XIII State
  of California concerns, XIV SB 711 conformity and OBBBA non-conformity), with "required" and
  "awareness" topics. Format (landing and examFacts only, never questions): at least 5 multiple
  choice exam questions per hour, at least 4 answers each, so at least 300 (215 federal, 10
  ethics, 75 California), the student tested separately on federal, ethics and California parts,
  70% or more correct to pass, answer key never given before passing. CTEC sets no weights inside
  the federal and California parts; the bank's split per 50 (federal 34, ethics and preparer
  rules 4, California 12) follows the 43/2/15 hours and CP23A's topic counts and is Tigertest's.
- California Tax Education Council, "Continuing Education (CE) Provider Application 2026",
  https://ctecstorage.blob.core.windows.net/public/CTEC%20-%20Continuing%20Education%20%28CE%29%20Provider%20Application%20-%202026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): 20 hours of CE each year, broken down as 10 hours federal tax law, 3 hours
  federal tax update, 2 hours ethics and 5 hours California.
- California Tax Education Council, New Preparer Application, https://ctec.org/taxpreparers/new-application/
  (fetched 2026-11-19): 60-hour QE course from a CTEC approved provider, QE valid up to 18 months
  from completion, Live Scan background check, PTIN from the IRS, $5,000 tax preparer surety bond,
  registration cycle November 1 to October 31, renewal by October 31 with late renewal through
  January 15, 20 hours of CE to renew, an expired registration means retaking the 60-hour course.
  Fees are left out of questions.
- California Tax Education Council, FAQs, https://ctec.org/faqs/ (fetched 2026-11-19): who must
  register (anyone preparing returns for a fee, federal-only preparers included), the exemptions
  (California CPAs, enrolled agents, State Bar attorneys), bond details given to clients (surety
  name and bond number), CRTPs who renew with 20 CE hours by October 31 are generally exempt from
  the IRS AFTR course, B&P Code sections 22250 to 22255.
- California Tax Education Council, Media Room, https://ctec.org/media-room/ (fetched 2026-11-19):
  display the CTEC certificate, show the bond on request, fees not based on the refund amount,
  PTIN and signature on every return, refunds go to the taxpayer.
- California Business and Professions Code sections 22250 to 22259 on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov
  sit behind a bot wall and could not be read, so the bank states only the registration rules
  CTEC itself publishes (above) and cites no section number beyond 22255 named by CTEC.

## Federal law, tax year 2025
- IRS, 2025 Form 1040 and 1040-SR Instructions, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1040gi--2025.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): Form 1099-K only above $20,000 and 200 transactions, SALT limit $40,000
  ($20,000 MFS), EIC investment income limit $11,950, standard deduction worksheet ($2,000 or
  $1,600 additional amounts for age 65 or blind).
- IRS, Publication 17 (2025), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/p17--2025.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-19): $5,200 qualifying relative gross income test, IRA limit $7,000 ($8,000 at 50 or
  older), 70 cents a mile for business use in 2025, $300 educator expenses, $3,000 ($1,500 MFS)
  capital loss limit, Schedule B over $1,500, $400 self-employment filing rule, $250,000/$500,000
  home sale exclusion, 7.5% medical floor, kiddie tax $1,350 and $2,700, filing status and
  dependency tests.
- IRS, 2025 Instructions for Schedule A, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1040sca--2025.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): SALT limit $40,000, reduced when MAGI exceeds $500,000 ($250,000 MFS) but
  not below $10,000.
- IRS, "Working Families Tax Cuts: individuals and workers",
  https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-individuals-and-workers (fetched
  2026-11-19): 2025 standard deduction $15,750 single, $31,500 MFJ, $23,625 HOH; senior deduction
  $6,000 (65 by year end, phaseout over $75,000/$150,000 MAGI, SSN, joint return if married);
  tips deduction up to $25,000; overtime deduction up to $12,500/$25,000, premium part only; car
  loan interest up to $10,000, loan after December 31, 2024, final assembly in the United States,
  VIN on the return; all 2025 to 2028.
- IRS, "Working Families Tax Cuts: businesses", https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-businesses
  (fetched 2026-11-19): section 179 limit $2,500,000, 100% bonus depreciation for property acquired
  after January 19, 2025.
- IRS, One Big Beautiful Bill provisions, https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/one-big-beautiful-bill-provisions
  (fetched 2026-11-19): non-itemizer charitable deduction starts in 2026 (not 2025); clean vehicle
  credits end for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025.
- IRS, Child Tax Credit and Credit for Other Dependents,
  https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-tax-credit-and-credit-for-other-dependents
  (fetched 2026-11-19): $2,200 per child, up to $1,700 refundable, $2,500 earned income floor,
  ODC $500, phaseout over $200,000 ($400,000 MFJ), SSN rules.
- IRS, Treasury Department Circular No. 230, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/pcir230.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-19): sections 10.21, 10.22, 10.27, 10.28, 10.34.
- IRS, Instructions for Form 8867 (Rev. November 2025), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i8867.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): knowledge requirement, the four credits plus HOH, records kept 3 years.

## California law, tax year 2025
- Franchise Tax Board, 2025 California Resident Personal Income Tax Booklet (Form 540 and
  Schedule CA (540) instructions), https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-booklet.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19): standard deduction $5,706 / $11,412; personal exemption credit $153,
  dependent exemption credit $475; exemption credit AGI limits; renter's credit $60 / $120 with
  AGI $53,994 / $107,988 and six months of rent; CalEITC earned income under $32,901; YCTC child
  under six; dependent care credit only with federal AGI $100,000 or less and care in California;
  FTB 3532 for HOH; FTB 3800 kiddie tax over $2,700; estimated tax 30/40/0/30 and no estimates
  under $500 ($250 MFS); mandatory e-pay with a 1% penalty; Behavioral Health Services Tax 1% over
  $1,000,000; four-year statute; due dates April 15 and October 15, 2026; general non-conformity
  to OBBBA; no deduction for state income tax or SDI and no SALT cap; $1,000,000 mortgage limit
  and home equity interest kept; 2% miscellaneous deductions kept; no educator deduction;
  unemployment, Paid Family Leave, Social Security, California lottery and state refunds
  excluded; employer HSA contributions added back; military retirement exclusion up to $20,000
  for 2025 to 2029; alimony conformity for agreements after December 31, 2025; SDI wage limit
  removed from 2024; use tax.
- Franchise Tax Board, Publication 1031 (2025), Guidelines for Determining Resident Status,
  https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-1031-publication.pdf (fetched 2026-11-19): nine-month
  presumption, 546-day employment contract safe harbor, 45 days of return visits, $200,000
  intangible income exception.
- FTB HTML pages (ftb.ca.gov/file/...) return 403 to automated reads; the YCTC dollar amount was
  therefore not confirmed and is not asked.

## Left out on purpose
Fees, fines and penalty dollar amounts; the YCTC and CalEITC maximum credits; worker
classification (AB5) detail beyond what CP23A names; anything about the QE final exam itself;
2026 figures (the course covers 2025 returns); prep-site material.
