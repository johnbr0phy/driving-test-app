# DANB Radiation Health and Safety (RHS) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from DANB's RHS exam outline and the
federal regulations and guidance below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from DANB's or the
DALE Foundation's practice tests, review courses, the suggested textbooks, or any prep site, app
or book. Images are described in words. The 52 RHS items in the DANB CDA bank
(`data/danb-questions.json`, segment DA-RHS) were read first and no fact-and-answer pair from them
is asked again here (see "Overlap check" below).

## Outline and format (Dental Assisting National Board)

- DANB, Radiation Health and Safety exam page, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.danb.org/exams/exam/rhs
  75 questions, 60 minutes, 60-day test window; test center or remote online proctoring; also
  offered in Spanish; a component of the NELDA and CDA certifications; recognized or required in
  many states.
- DANB, RHS Exam Outline and References, effective 03/12/2025 (PDF), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://danbsfprodassets.azureedge.net/assets/docs/default-source/exam-outlines/rhs-exam-outline.pdf?sfvrsn=1749ebf2_16
  No eligibility requirements. Digital radiography only: no conventional, film-based concepts
  tested since July 7, 2022. Domains: I Purpose and Technique 50% (A landmarks, conditions and
  materials on images; B purpose of periapical, bitewing, FMS, occlusal, panoramic, cephalometric
  and CBCT images; C diagnostically acceptable image; D procedures and techniques: histories,
  patient preparation, positioning, instruments, paralleling, bisecting, panoramic, modifications
  for tori, cleft palate, shallow palate and narrow arch, error correction, care of PSP, CMOS and
  CCD receptors, patient management, viewing and mounting, legal requirements for images);
  II Radiation Characteristics and Protection 25% (physics, biology, units Gy, Sv, R, rem, C/kg,
  MPD and cumulative dose, operator barrier, position and distance, ALARA, ADA and FDA guidelines,
  leakage and retakes, filtration, shielding, collimation, PID, patient size and age, patient
  concerns, informed consent or refusal, suspected x-ray unit malfunction); III Infection
  Prevention and Control 25% (standard precautions for radiographic equipment and supplies, and
  for patients and operators, per ADA, CDC and OSHA). Computer adaptive testing; each candidate
  gets the same share of questions from each domain; scaled scores 100 to 900, 400 to pass,
  neither number nor percent correct; modified Angoff standard setting.
- DANB, 2026 Candidate Handbook (PDF), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://danbsfprodassets.azureedge.net/assets/docs/default-source/candidate-handbooks/candidate-handbook.pdf?sfvrsn=61cda93e_19
  No limit on retakes of a failed exam; candidates reapply and pay the full fee each time; state
  laws may require more education after failed attempts.
- DANB, RHS Application Packet (PDF, © 2026), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://danbsfprodassets.azureedge.net/assets/docs/default-source/national-application-packets/rhs-application-packet.pdf?sfvrsn=2c4e815e_14
  RHS exam fee $270 for traditional candidates ($265 active military), including a $75
  nonrefundable application fee. Used for the landing only.

## Federal regulation and guidance (facts in questions)

- 21 CFR 1020.31 (FDA performance standard, radiographic equipment), eCFR up to date as of
  2026-11-19, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-J/part-1020/section-1020.31
  Intraoral systems: x-ray field no larger than a 7 cm circle at the minimum source-to-skin
  distance when that distance is 18 cm or more; minimum source-to-skin distance 18 cm if operable
  above 50 kVp (10 cm if not); operator can end an exposure over half a second; visible
  indication while x-rays are produced and an audible signal when the exposure ends.
- 21 CFR 1020.30 (half-value layer table), same eCFR date, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-J/part-1020/section-1020.30
  Minimum HVL for dental intraoral systems made after December 1, 1980: 1.5 mm aluminum at 51 to
  70 kVp. Used for the concept that filtration removes low-energy photons; no HVL number is asked.
- 10 CFR 20.1003, 20.1004, 20.1201, 20.1208, 20.1301 (NRC radiation standards, the federal model
  most state rules follow), eCFR up to date as of 2026-11-19, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/chapter-I/part-20
  ALARA definition; gray = 1 J/kg = 100 rad; sievert = 100 rem; quality factor 1 for x-rays;
  occupational limit 5 rem (50 mSv) a year; lens of the eye 15 rem (150 mSv); embryo or fetus of a
  declared pregnant worker 0.5 rem (5 mSv) for the whole pregnancy; members of the public 0.1 rem
  (1 mSv) a year.
- ADA Council on Scientific Affairs and FDA, Dental Radiographic Examinations: Recommendations
  for Patient Selection and Limiting Radiation Exposure, revised 2012 (PDF), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.fda.gov/media/84818/download
  Fastest receptor; digital receptors CCD and CMOS are direct, PSP plates indirect and scanned;
  holders that align receptor and beam; staff never hold the receptor, a family member who must
  hold it wears shielding; rectangular collimation up to fivefold dose reduction over round;
  open-ended, lead-lined PID; 40 cm source-to-skin distance better than 20 cm; above 90 kVp should
  not be used, 60 to 70 kVp optimal; lower kVp gives higher contrast; filmless systems forgive
  overexposure, so set the lowest diagnostic timer setting; MPD 50 mSv a year, lifetime
  10 mSv times age; dosimeters for workers who may exceed 1 mSv a year and for pregnant operators;
  operator behind a barrier, or at least 2 m from the tubehead and out of the primary beam;
  hand-held units at mid-torso with the ring shield, stored locked; aprons hung or laid flat.
- ADA News, "Using thyroid collars during radiographic exams no longer recommended by ADA",
  February 2024 (Benavides et al., JADA, doi 10.1016/j.adaj.2023.12.002), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://adanews.ada.org/ada-news/2024/february/using-thyroid-collars-during-radiographic-exams-no-longer-recommended-by-ada/
  Routine thyroid collars and abdominal aprons no longer recommended for any patient, pregnancy
  included; restrict the beam; digital receptors; CBCT only when lower-dose imaging will not do;
  follow state law where it still requires shielding.
- ADA and AAOMR, Patient selection for dental radiography and cone-beam computed tomography,
  JADA 2026 (PDF hosted by the Rhode Island Department of Health), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://health.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur1006/files/2026-01/ADA-AAOMR-patient-selection.pdf
  Replaces the 2012 patient selection tables; imaging is prescribed by the dentist for clinical
  need; vertical bitewings for periodontal bone levels; panoramic for third molars and
  supernumerary teeth. No recall interval numbers are asked.
- CDC, Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings, 2003 (MMWR 52 RR-17),
  fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5217a1.htm
  Spaulding categories; clinical contact surfaces (including radiograph equipment); low-level
  (HIV and HBV claim) and intermediate-level (tuberculocidal) EPA-registered hospital
  disinfectants; hand hygiene before gloving and after removing gloves, alcohol rub when hands
  are not visibly soiled; dental radiology: gloves, heat-sterilize heat-tolerant receptor holders,
  digital sensors are semicritical (clean and heat-sterilize or high-level disinfect, or use an
  FDA-cleared barrier and then clean and disinfect with an intermediate-level disinfectant),
  barriers on tubehead and control panel changed after each patient.
- OSHA, Bloodborne Pathogens standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
  PPE provided at no cost; remove PPE before leaving the work area; no eating or drinking where
  exposure is likely.
- Indian Health Service Dental CDE, Chapter 3 Radiographic Techniques (PDF), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.ihs.gov/dentalcde/documents/Chapter%203%20-%20Radiographic%20technique.pdf
  Paralleling preferred; bisecting for low palatal vaults and some children; elongation and
  foreshortening come from vertical angulation; image too dark or too light causes; PA shows 2 to
  3 mm beyond the apices; bitewings show crowns and crestal bone with open contacts and a level
  occlusal plane; size 0 receptor for small children; PID close to the aiming ring.

## Left out or worded with care

- Film processing, darkroom, safelights and film speed are not asked: DANB stopped testing
  film-based concepts on July 7, 2022.
- Thyroid collars: the 2012 ADA/FDA document says to use them; the ADA's 2024 recommendation says
  routine use is no longer recommended, and some states still require them. No item keys either
  side; one item asks what the assistant does (follow state law and the office's protocol).
- Bitewing and FMS recall intervals: the 2012 tables were replaced in 2026; no interval is asked.
- State rules (who may expose radiographs, registration, survey intervals, record retention
  years) vary and are not asserted; retention items say state law sets the period.
- Fees, the passing score and exam format appear only on the landing and in examFacts, never in
  questions.
