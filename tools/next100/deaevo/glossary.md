# Glossary for `deaevo` (German, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations in "Sie" (as in IHK exam papers); landing and UI copy in
"du" (LANGUAGES.md 2.1). German typography: „ “ quotes, decimal comma, no em-dashes, no straight
double quotes. Laws cited as "§ 22 Abs. 1 BBiG", "§ 11 JArbSchG", "§ 4 AEVO" (the official short
form is AusbEignV; the IHKs and the market say AEVO, so the bank does too).
