# Einbürgerungstest / Leben in Deutschland: sources

All 198 questions are original, written directly in German from the Grundgesetz, the Staatsangehörigkeitsgesetz, the Einbürgerungstestverordnung's Rahmencurriculum and the other laws below. The BAMF's public question catalogue (Anlage 1 EinbTestV, Gesamtfragenkatalog) was used only to know which topics are covered; no catalogue question or answer option was copied, paraphrased or reworded, and no commercial app or prep site question was used. Model authorship and model language review only; no human or native-speaker review is claimed. Every source below was read on 2026-11-22.

## Land questions: decision

The real test asks three questions on the candidate's own Land (coat of arms, capital, flag, Landtag, voting age in local elections, ministries). Tigertest's bank is Land-neutral: each test has three questions on the Länder in general (number of Länder, city states, Landtag, Ministerpräsident, capitals of several Länder, Freistaaten, what the Länder decide, local elections, Bürgerentscheid, Landkreis). Modelling 16 separate Land pools would mean 16 small banks and a Land picker for three questions a test; the copy tells candidates to learn their own Land's ten questions from the BAMF catalogue as well. Coats of arms and flags are pictures and are not modelled.

## Copyright check (second review, 2026-11-22)

The reviewer downloaded the BAMF Gesamtfragenkatalog (Stand 07.05.2025, https://www.bamf.de/SharedDocs/Anlagen/DE/Integration/Einbuergerung/gesamtfragenkatalog-lebenindeutschland.pdf) and compared all 198 stems and option sets with its 460 tasks by text similarity, word overlap and shared options, then read the closest matches by hand. 40 items asked the same fact as a catalogue task in close wording or with overlapping options (for example Hitler's appointment year, the year the Wall was built, who elects the Chancellor, the flag colours, the eagle in the coat of arms, Land capitals). All 40 were replaced by items on a different fact or angle with new distractors (for example the Ermächtigungsgesetz, the Kanzlermehrheit, the top stripe of the flag, which symbols the Grundgesetz itself fixes, capitals asked from city to Land). The remaining overlaps are topics only, which the Rahmencurriculum makes unavoidable.

## Left out

- No question copies or rewords the BAMF catalogue. Picture questions (coats of arms, posters, maps) are not modelled.
- No officeholders, party strengths, fees (the 25 euro test fee is on the landing only), current affairs, or conscription status (the Wehrdienst rules were changing in 2025-2026; only Art. 12a is asked).
- No question about the test itself.
