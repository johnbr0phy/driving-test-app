# Glossary for `deheilpr` (German, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations in "Sie" or impersonal case vignettes, like official exam
questions; landing and dashboard copy in "du" (LANGUAGES.md 2.1, as `detheorie`). German
typography: „ “ quotes, decimal comma (3,5 mmol/l), thousands point (4.000 pro µl), °C, mmHg,
mg/dl. No em-dashes. Medical terms: German form first with the Latin or Greek term where the
real exam uses it ("Kieferklemme (Trismus)").
