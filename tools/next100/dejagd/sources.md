# Sources for `dejagd` (Jägerprüfung, schriftlicher Teil, Bayern, in German)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in German from the laws and official pages
below. The Bavarian Fragenkatalog with Musterlösung (published by the state for the written
exam) and the LMG's past written papers with answers were deliberately NOT opened or used: they
are question material. Nothing was copied or reworded from them, from a textbook ("Wild und
Hund", "Heintges" and similar), a course provider or an app. Species, weapons and situations are
described in words. Checked 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Why Bavaria (Land choice)
The Jägerprüfung is a Land exam; § 15 Abs. 5 BJagdG sets only its subject areas and leaves the
details to the Länder. Bavaria, the second most populous Land and the largest by area, publishes
its whole Prüfungsordnung (JFPO) on gesetze-bayern.de in a readable form, with an exact written
format (100 multiple-choice questions, six Sachgebiete with fixed counts, 100 minutes, pass with
at most 25 wrong). NRW, the most populous Land, was not chosen because its exam split is not
set out per Sachgebiet in a text we could read. The bank follows the six Bavarian Sachgebiete
and their exact 16/16/16/16/16/20 split; legal items use federal law (BJagdG, WaffG, AWaffV,
TierSchG, BNatSchG, Tier-LMHV, EU hygiene law), and the few Bavarian rules are labelled
"in Bayern" in the stem.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- Verordnung über die Jäger- und Falknerprüfung (JFPO), Bavaria, text in force from 05.08.2024,
  last amended by Gesetz vom 26. März 2026 (GVBl. S. 113), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/BayJFPO/true
  - § 10: six Sachgebiete for the written and oral parts: 1 Jagdwaffen, Jagd- und Fanggeräte;
    2 Biologie der Wildarten; 3 Rechtliche Vorschriften; 4 Wildhege, Jagdbetrieb und jagdliche
    Praxis; 5 Jagdhundewesen; 6 Naturschutz, Landbau, Forstwesen, Wild- und Jagdschadensverhütung.
  - § 12 Abs. 1: written part in the Antwort-Wahl-Verfahren (multiple choice), 100 questions:
    16 each from Sachgebiete 1 to 5, 20 from Sachgebiet 6, chosen from the Fragenkatalog mit
    Musterlösung of the supreme hunting authority; 100 minutes.
  - § 12 Abs. 2: failed when more than a quarter of the questions are unanswered, wrong or
    incompletely answered (so at most 25 of 100; pass line 75%).
  - § 13: oral part, at most three candidates, 10 to 15 minutes per Sachgebiet; failed with one
    "ungenügend" or two "mangelhaft".
  - § 14: practical part: weapon handling and rifle shooting, 4 shots at 100 m on the Rehbock
    target, at least 3 hits in rings 8 to 10, one repeat.
  - § 11: parts in the order written, oral, practical; § 15: oral and practical may each be
    repeated twice within two years of passing the written part.
- Bayernportal, "Jägerprüfung; Ablegung", fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.bayernportal.de/dokumente/leistung/765879211208
  - Run by the Bayerisches Landesamt für Maß und Gewicht (Zentrale Jäger- und
    Falknerprüfungsbehörde); four exam dates a year at up to 13 locations; register at least
    4 weeks before the written date; minimum age 15; proof of theoretical and practical training.
  - Multiple choice: one or more answers may be correct per question (catalogue description).
    Tigertest writes single-answer items only and says so.
- Bayerisches Staatsministerium für Wirtschaft, Landesentwicklung und Energie, "Reform des
  Bayerischen Jagdgesetzes 2026, Erläuterndes Faktenblatt", Stand März 2026, read 2026-11-22:
  https://www.stmwi.bayern.de/fileadmin/user_upload/stmwi/jagd-forst/jagd/2026-03-31-Reform-des-Bayerischen-Jagdgesetzes-2026-Erlaeuterndes-Faktenblatt.pdf
  - From 1 April 2026: Rehwild may be hunted without Abschussplan if the Jagdgenossenschaft
    decides so (not asked); new Rehwild seasons in Bayern (Böcke 16. April bis 15. Oktober,
    Schmalrehe 16. April bis 15. Januar, Geißen und Kitze 1. September bis 15. Januar; not asked); Wolf and Goldschakal added to Bavarian hunting law (not
    asked: the federal change was still in progress); Schalldämpfer ban removed; from 1 January
    2027 the Bavarian Jägerprüfung also proves Fallenjagd competence.
- LMG 2027 dates calendar (format only): https://lmg.bayern.de/mam/aufgaben_leistungen/jaegerpruefung/jp_27_termine_kalender.pdf

- Wildlife biology, forestry and farming items are standard hunting-course knowledge (roe deer
  Keimruhe, rut times, antler cycle, ruminant digestion, browsing and bark damage, crops). The
  Bavarian state institutes' pages were used to check them where they loaded: LWF Bayern,
  "Neues vom Reh" (PDF, binary only; not readable by the fetch tool):

## Left out on purpose
- Season dates (Jagdzeiten);
  wolf and golden jackal hunting (law in flux); Rehwild Abschussplanfreiheit (new, local);
  fees, fines, insurance sums; Falknerprüfung; anything about the exam itself.
- Multiple-answer items: the real catalogue allows several correct answers; Tigertest asks one.
- Picture questions and the oral and practical parts are not modelled.

## Copyright check at the blind review
Method: every one of the 200 items was compared by machine against 1,787 catalogue items: the
public Bavarian Fragenkatalog with solutions for all six Sachgebiete (wildtierportal.bayern.de,
sachgebiet_1.pdf and jaegerpruefung_sg2 to sg6.pdf, Stand 2017 to 2021), the LMG's written papers
2024-4 and 2025-1 to 2025-4 with solutions (lmg.bayern.de), and Niedersachsen's published
Fachgebiet 4 question set (ml.niedersachsen.de). Three measures per pair: normalised stem string
similarity (difflib ratio), content-word overlap of stem plus key against stem plus correct
answers, and shared options (option strings with similarity over 0.8). Every item with stem
similarity 0.6 or more, two or more shared options, or word overlap 0.6 with a shared option was
read by hand (61 in the first full pass). The reviewer opened the catalogues only for this check.

Result: 28 items were close rewordings of a catalogue question (same fact asked the same way,
often with the same answer wording or option set) and were rewritten with a different angle and
new distractors: WAF-009, WAF-031; BIO-001, BIO-002, BIO-004, BIO-005, BIO-007, BIO-015, BIO-016,
BIO-018, BIO-028; REC-021, REC-023, REC-024; HEG-002, HEG-003, HEG-007, HEG-024; HUN-001, HUN-002,
HUN-006, HUN-010, HUN-011, HUN-015, HUN-019, HUN-028, HUN-029; NAT-024. The remaining flags were
template matches only (for example "Was versteht man unter ..." about a different term) or shared
species and place names, with different facts. Shared facts across the syllabus remain, as in any
course book. HEG-001 (Ausschlagen mit den Hinterläufen as a Kammerschuss sign) was replaced too:
the catalogue reads the same sign, with a crooked back, as a Weidwundschuss, so a second answer
was defensible.
