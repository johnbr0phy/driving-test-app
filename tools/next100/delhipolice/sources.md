    blogs, browsers, search engines, chat, video conferencing, e-banking.

  - 16.5: cut-off marks (normalised) for shortlisting to PE&MT/DV: UR 35, SC/ST/OBC/EWS 30, ESM 25 (out of 100).
    16.6: about 12 times the vacancies are shortlisted. 16.8: PE&MT is qualifying. 16.11: final select list on

## Modelling decisions
- Full length at the real pace: two mock tests of 100 questions in 90 minutes, one section in the paper's part
  order (GK 50, Reasoning 25, Numerical Ability 15, Computer 10). Test 2 is held out of the drills.
- GK is static only (history, culture, sports facts that do not date, polity and the Constitution, economy
  institutions, geography of India and its neighbours, everyday science and Indian space milestones). No current
  affairs, no office-holders, no money figures.
- Reasoning is text only; the real paper's figure-based non-verbal items (mirror images, paper folding, embedded
  figures) are not modelled. The landing and the config say so.
- English only (the real CBE is also offered in Hindi). Score is percent correct; negative marking and cut-offs
  are explained, not applied.

## Fact checks for items
- Delhi as National Capital Territory: Article 239AA, inserted by the Constitution (Sixty-ninth Amendment) Act,
  1991; police, public order and land are outside the Legislative Assembly's powers (239AA(3)(a)).
- Delhi returns 7 members to the Lok Sabha (Delimitation Order 2008).
- First Asian Games: New Delhi, 1951. National Sports Day: 29 August (Dhyan Chand's birthday).
- Mars Orbiter Mission launched November 2013; Chandrayaan-3 landed near the lunar south
  pole on 23 August 2023.
- Calendar check: 15 August 2024 was a Thursday and 15 August 2025 a Friday.
- Article 239AA(6): the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly. Article 239AB:
  the President may suspend the operation of Article 239AA or any of its provisions.
- RBI began work 1 April 1935 and was nationalised 1 January 1949; SBI formed 1955; 14 banks nationalised 1969.
- Rohini RS-1 put in orbit by SLV-3 from Sriharikota on 18 July 1980.
- Rajya Sabha: Article 80 maximum 250; 245 seats at present (233 elected + 12 nominated).
