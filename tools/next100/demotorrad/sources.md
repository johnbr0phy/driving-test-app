# Sources for `demotorrad` (German motorcycle theory test, Klassen A, A1, A2, in German)

All 210 questions are original, written directly in German from the laws below. Nothing was
copied from the official Fragenkatalog (TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21), a past test or a commercial
learning app. Signs are described in words with their StVO number. Checked 2026-11-06 unless
noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- FeV Anlage 7, read via gesetze.legal and gesetze-im-internet.de on 2026-11-06:
  https://gesetze.legal/bund/fev/anlage_7
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_7.html
  - 1.2.2: Ersterwerb classes AM, A1, A2, A, B, L, T: 30 questions, 110 points, 10 permitted
    error points. Erweiterung (same classes): 20 questions, 72 points, 6 error points.
  - 1.2.3: failed when the permitted error points are exceeded or "zwei Fragen mit Wertigkeit 5
    falsch beantwortet worden sind"; a failed test is repeated in full.
  - 1.3: "Die theoretische Prüfung ist in deutscher Sprache abzulegen"; the text read on
    2026-11-06 lists 14 foreign languages (English, French, Greek, Italian, Polish, Portuguese,
    Romanian, Russian, Croatian, Spanish, Turkish, Standard Arabic, Kurmanji, Ukrainian). The
    TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21 page (same day) still says German and 12 foreign languages. Not
    resolved: the landing says "mehreren Fremdsprachen" and gives no count.
  - Practical test, Aufstieg: four instead of six Grundfahraufgaben after two years' Vorbesitz.
- TÜV NORD, "Theory test" (read 2026-11-06): 30 questions for a first licence (20 basic, 10
  class-specific), 10 basic and 10 supplementary when you already hold a licence; 2 to 5 penalty
  points; at most 10 (6 for an extension); two wrong 5-point answers fail; up to three video
  tasks; no fixed time limit. Motorcycle classes A, A1, A2 listed with the same rules.
  https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/services/driving-licence-and-vehicle/theory-test/
- TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21, "Fahrerlaubnisprüfung" (read 2026-11-06): German plus 12 foreign
  languages; image-selection tasks from 1 April 2025; mock tests for "Klasse A".
  https://fahrerlaubnis.tuev-dekra.de/en/
- FeV § 15 (via lexmea.de, 2026-11-06): A1 to A2 and A2 to A after at least two years' Vorbesitz
  need only a practical test, no theory test.
  https://lexmea.de/de/gesetz/fev/15
- Guides only, not used for facts: motor.com.de on the A2 licence
  (https://motor.com.de/ratgeber/motorrad-fuehrerschein-klasse-a2) and bussgeldkatalog.org on
  Fehlerpunkte. The research note's "2025 catalogue update added new A, A1, A2 questions" was not
  confirmed on an official page and is not stated.

## Not confirmed
- Cohort size (research note: probably over 150,000 a year across A, A1, A2): not checked, not
  stated anywhere.
- Official topic weights: none published. The 15 + 15 split per Tigertest test is our own (the
  real first-licence test is 20 + 10); the landing says so.
- Fees: not asked and not stated.

## Law used for the questions (in force November 2026)
- FeV § 6 (via gesetze.legal, 2026-11-06, https://gesetze.legal/bund/fev/6): A1 up to 125 cm³,
  11 kW, 0,1 kW/kg; three-wheelers in A1 up to 15 kW; A2 up to 35 kW and 0,2 kW/kg, not derived
  from a motorcycle over 70 kW; A over 50 cm³ or over 45 km/h; A includes AM, A1, A2; A2
  includes A1 and AM.
- FeV § 6b (https://gesetze.legal/bund/fev/6b): Schlüsselzahl 196 from 25 years, class B for
  five years, a Fahrerschulung, valid only in Germany ("gilt nur im Inland").
- FeV § 10 (via lexmea.de, https://lexmea.de/de/gesetz/fev/10): A1 16, A2 18, A 24 direct,
  20 with two years A2, 21 for three-wheelers over 15 kW (landing only; no age questions).
- StVG § 2a (Probezeit on the first licence only), § 24c (alcohol ban in the Probezeit and
  under 21).
- StGB § 316 with case law (absolute Fahruntüchtigkeit for Kraftfahrer from 1,1 ‰, relative
  from 0,3 ‰ with Ausfallerscheinungen; 1,6 ‰ for cyclists).
- StVO (https://gesetze.legal/bund/stvo/...): § 1, § 3 Abs. 3 (60 km/h außerorts for all Kfz
  with trailer except Pkw, Lkw and Wohnmobile up to 3,5 t), § 5 Abs. 8 (only cyclists and
  Mofas may pass waiting vehicles on the right), § 6, § 9a Abs. 1 (no stopping in a
  roundabout), § 10, § 11 Abs. 1 and 2 (blocked junction, Rettungsgasse), § 12, § 15a Abs. 4
  (motorcycles may neither tow nor be towed), § 17 Abs. 2a (Krafträder with dipped beam or
  daytime running lights by day), § 18 Abs. 1 and 5 (over 60 km/h; Krafträder mit Anhänger
  60 km/h on the Autobahn), § 20 Abs. 2 (trams at stops; Abs. 5, buses leaving marked stops, has no innerorts limit, re-read on gesetze-im-internet.de 2026-11-06, so no item asks it), § 9 Abs. 3 (pedestrians when turning), § 16 Abs. 2 (hazard lights approaching a jam),
  § 21 Abs. 1 (no passengers on Krafträder without a special seat), § 21a Abs. 2 (suitable
  helmet), § 23 Abs. 1a (devices), § 25 Abs. 2 (pushing a vehicle), § 30 (noise, idling),
  § 37 (yellow; a green arrow left behind the junction means oncoming traffic has red, wording read 2026-11-06; a green arrow in the signal itself only frees that direction), Anlagen 1 to 3 (Zeichen 103, 108, 112, 114, 117, 205 with
  Zusatzzeichen, 237, 242.1, 245, 255, 260, 274, 276, 278, 299, 306, 314, 342, 625, 721).
- StVZO § 35h (first-aid kit, not for Krafträder), Fußstützen for the passenger.
- Riding technique (Aufstellmoment, Lenkimpuls, Blickführung, Knieschluss, group riding) as
  taught in German driving schools and safety trainings; no single statute.

Bußgelder, Punkte, fees and the 2027 reform's new rules are not asked.
