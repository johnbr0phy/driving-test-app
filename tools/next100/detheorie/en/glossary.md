# detheorie English (en-GB) glossary

British English for people living in Germany who sit the test in English. Questions and
explanations address the reader as "you"; the landing and app copy use the same plain "you".
Decimal point (0.5 ‰, 1.6 mm, 3.5 ng/ml), thousands comma (3,500 kg), km/h and m. Straight
double quotes only, no German „ “. No em or en dashes.

Signs keep their StVO number and an English name in brackets: "sign 205 (Give way)",
"sign 206 (Stop. Give way.)". German words printed on a sign or plate are kept in quotes with a
gloss: "Anlieger frei" (access only), "bei Nässe" (when wet), "Fahrradstraße" (cycle street),
"Umwelt" (environment). Legal references keep the German act and use the English subsection
style: § 20(4) StVO, § 24a StVG, § 316 StGB, § 29 StVZO.
