# Sources for `detheorie` (German car theory test, Führerschein Klasse B, in German)

All 210 questions are original, written directly in German from the laws below. Nothing was
copied from the official Fragenkatalog (TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21), a past test or a commercial
learning app. Signs are described in words with their StVO number. Checked 2026-11-01 unless
noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- FeV Anlage 7 (zu § 16 Abs. 2, § 17 Abs. 2 und 3), read via gesetze.legal on 2026-11-01
  (gesetze-im-internet.de returned 503 that day):
  https://gesetze.legal/bund/fev/anlage_7
  - 1.1: the test covers the subject areas of Directive 2006/126/EC Annex II A Nos. 2 to 4; the
    catalogue is part of the Prüfungsrichtlinie; eight areas from Gefahrenlehre to Eignung.
  - 1.2.1: Grundstoff applies to all classes, Zusatzstoff to the class.
  - 1.2.2: Ersterwerb class B (also AM, A1, A2, A, L, T): 30 questions, 110 points, 10 permitted
    error points; each question "mit zwei bis fünf Punkten bewertet". Erweiterung: 20 questions,
    72 points, 6 error points.
  - 1.2.3: failed when more than the permitted error points, or "zwei Fragen mit Wertigkeit 5
    falsch beantwortet worden sind".
  - 1.3: "Die theoretische Prüfung ist in deutscher Sprache abzulegen", optional German audio;
    may also be taken in English, French, Greek, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian,
    Croatian, Spanish, Turkish or Standard Arabic (12 languages).
- TÜV NORD, "Theory test" (read 2026-11-01): 30 multiple-choice questions for class B, 20 basic
  and 10 class-specific; 2 to 5 penalty points per wrong answer; max 10; two wrong 5-point answers
  fail; up to three video tasks within the 30 questions, each clip about 15 seconds, up to five
  views; no fixed time ("You have enough time").
  https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/services/driving-licence-and-vehicle/theory-test/
- TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21, "Fahrerlaubnisprüfung" (read 2026-11-01): German plus 12 foreign
  languages (same list); about 2 million theory tests a year; image-selection tasks
  (Abbildungsauswahl-Aufgaben, an alternative multiple-selection format) from 1 April 2025.
  https://fahrerlaubnis.tuev-dekra.de/en/
- Duration: Bönninger, Kammler, Sturzbecher, Wagner, "Theoretische und praktische
  Fahrerlaubnisprüfung in Europa, Recherchebericht" (published on the TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21 site,
  read 2026-11-01): "In Deutschland besteht keine Zeitbegrenzung; die Richtzeit für die
  Aufgabenbearbeitung liegt bei 45 Minuten." Table row: "Deutschland: Keine Zeitbegrenzung,
  Richtzeit: 45".
  https://fahrerlaubnis.tuev-dekra.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Bericht_DE_Europa_Recherchebericht_komplett.pdf
  So "45 minutes" is a guide time, not a limit. The landing says so.
- Catalogue revised each 1 April and 1 October, published by TÜV | DEKRA arge tp 21; multiple
  answers can be selected in the image tasks: VerkehrsRundschau, 25 March 2025.
  https://www.verkehrsrundschau.de/nachrichten/ausbildung-karriere/fuehrerschein-das-aendert-sich-an-der-theorie-ab-april-2025-3633004
  (Bundesanzeiger notices of deletions and additions for 1 October 2022, 2023 and 2024 appear in
  search results; no notice for 1 October 2026 was found. Not asserted beyond "revised each April
  and October".)

## Planned reform (not in force on 2026-11-01)
- auto motor und sport, 8 March 2026: catalogue to shrink from 1,169 to about 840 questions,
  every question weighted equally, entry into force "in Aussicht gestellt" for early 2027.
  https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/verkehr/zeitplan-der-fuehrerschein-reform-ministerium-raet-fahrschuelern-nicht-abwarten/
- motorradonline, 21 May 2026: federal cabinet adopted the draft law on 20 May 2026; rules to
  apply from early 2027 if Bundestag and Bundesrat pass it without delay.
  https://www.motorradonline.de/ratgeber/fuehrerschein-soll-2027-guenstiger-werden-kabinett-beschliesst-reform/
- bmv.de reform page (https://www.bmv.de/SharedDocs/DE/Artikel/K/reform-ausbildung-pkw-fuehrerschein.html)
  returned 404 on 2026-11-01. Press only; the bank uses the current FeV Anlage 7 format, and the
  landing mentions the reform as planned for 2027.

## Not confirmed
- The 20/10 split is confirmed by TÜV NORD, not in the FeV text (which gives 30 in total).
- Official topic weights per test: none published. The 7/6/4/5/2/2/2/2 split is Tigertest's.
- Fees: not asked and not stated.
