# Glossary for `dkjagt` (Danish, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations neutral or in »du« where a situation is described ("Du står på
post ..."), like the official retningslinjer's examples; landing and dashboard copy in »du«.
Danish typography: » « for terms and sayings (no straight double quotes), decimal comma (5,5 mm),
thousands point (2.000 J), ranges with »til« (no dashes), units mm, m, m², km/t, J. No em-dashes or
en-dashes. Never a fee, fine or season date.
