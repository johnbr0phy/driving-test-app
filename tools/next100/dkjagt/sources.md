# Sources for `dkjagt` (Jagtprøven, skriftlig del, Danish hunting licence test, in Danish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Danish from the laws and official guidance
below. The real question pool is secret; nothing was copied from a past test, the example test
on jagttegn.dk, a textbook (Jagttegnsbogen and similar) or a commercial app. Species are described
in words (the real test shows pictures). Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- Styrelsen for Grøn Arealomlægning og Vandmiljø, »Instruks for jagtprøven« 2026 (PDF read
  2026-11-06, pkt. 3.3 and 3.6 rechecked 2026-11-07): https://sgavmst.dk/media/ttndlsbs/instruks-for-jagtproeven-2026.pdf
  - 3.3: »den skriftlige prøve består af 40 spørgsmål, hvoraf mindst 36 skal besvares rigtigt«;
    one cross per question; »ubesvarede spørgsmål tæller som fejl«; »man har 30 min«; questions
    can be read aloud and explained; no electronic media or aids.
  - 3.6: passed with at least 36 of 40; 3.7: a failed written test bars the practical test; a new
    written test at the earliest 14 days later.
  - 4 to 8: practical test (shotgun handling, distance judging at 6 game figures from 4 points,
    5 of 6 right, 5 seconds each; 4 clay targets). Table 7.3 gives the maximum shotgun distances
    used: gråand 30, ræv 25, hare 30, råvildt 20, fasan 30, grågås 25 m (used in the bank).
  - Footnote 1: Bekendtgørelse nr. 238 af 27. februar 2025 om jagttegn.
- Styrelsen for Grøn Arealomlægning og Vandmiljø, »Retningslinjer for spørgsmål, der stilles i den
  skriftlige del af jagtprøven«, revised 3 April 2025 (PDF read 2026-11-06):
  https://sgavmst.dk/media/vkffhaxl/2025_retningslinjer-for-hvilke-spoergsmaal-der-stilles-i-den-skriftlige-jagtproeve.pdf
  - Question types: factual yes/no, factual multiple choice, situation yes/no with a picture or
    description, species with a picture, hunters' terms (bilag 2).
  - Eight topics: artsbestemmelse og vildtbiologi, vildtforvaltning, jagtvåben og ammunition,
    anden jagtlovgivning, håndtering af nedlagt vildt, jagtetik og etikette, jagtteknik og
    ledelse, skudafgivelse. No weights are published: the 7/5/6/6/7/3/3/3 split is Tigertest's.
  - Bilag 1 lists the species for identification and biology; bilag 2 the glossary of terms.
- Styrelsen for Grøn Arealomlægning og Vandmiljø, »Vejledning til jagttegnsundervisning«, revised
  3 April 2025 (PDF read 2026-11-06):
  https://sgavmst.dk/media/tdjgrspz/2025_vejledning-til-jagttegnsundervisning.pdf
  (the URL in the task brief, 2025-vejledning..., returned 404; the underscore version loads)
  - Obligatory course at least 4 days (2 theory, 2 practical, at least 5 lessons of 45 minutes a
    day); age: the candidate turns 16 in the calendar year; up to 3 written and 3 practical
    attempts within 18 months of the course report; at least 14 days between written tests;
    tests held mainly in spring and autumn by Naturstyrelsen.
- Bekendtgørelse nr. 238 af 27. februar 2025 om jagttegn (retsinformation XML, read 2026-11-06):
  https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/238
  § 8 (sign-up, age, fee set on the agency's site and indexed from 1 January 2026), § 10 (written
  before practical, 3 attempts in 18 months, consent under 18), § 51 (bag report by 31 March,
  also when 0), § 52 (liability insurance). Fees are not stated anywhere in Tigertest's copy.
- Language: the instruks and its »Tillæg ... Tolkeprøver« allow an interpreter on designated test
  dates; the test itself is in Danish. Not asked in the bank.

## Law used for the questions (all read as XML from retsinformation.dk on 2026-11-06, status Valid)
- Jagt- og vildtforvaltningsloven, LBK nr. 639 af 26. maj 2023:
  https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/639 (§§ 1-4, 6, 6 a, 10, 13-20, 22-26, 28, 31, 33, 37, 39-40, 43)
- Bekendtgørelse nr. 1479 af 5. december 2023 om våben og ammunition der må anvendes til jagt m.v.
  (in force 1 January 2024): https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/1479
  (§ 1 shotgun max 2 cartridges, ≥55 cm, ≤ cal. 12, semi-auto rifle 3; råbuk with shotgun not
  16 May to 15 July; under cal. 20 only class 5; air rifle ≥5.5 mm; no pistols; § 2 shot ≤4 mm,
  no slugs, no lead shot; § 3 expanding bullets for classes 1-2, under 1% lead in centrefire;
  bilag 1 classes 1-5 with 2.000 J, 800 J, 175 J, 150 J, 200 m/s)
- Bekendtgørelse nr. 1652 af 19. december 2017 om udsætning af vildt, jagtmåder og jagtredskaber:
  https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2017/1652 (§§ 1-3, 6-12, 14)
- Bekendtgørelse nr. 470 af 17. maj 2024 om jagttid (jagttidsbekendtgørelsen):
  https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/470 (§ 3 times of day: ænder og gæs 1½ hour before
  sunrise to 1½ hour after sunset; husskade and krager from 1 hour before sunrise; § 4 no
  transfer under 1 year for ande- og vadefugle and vandhøns except skovsneppe). A jagttids-
  revision with effect from the 2026/27 season was planned (Naturstyrelsen timetable); the bank
  asks no season dates and no »har arten jagttid« for any species except fredede rovfugle.
- Bekendtgørelse nr. 827 af 24. juni 2016 om eftersøgning og aflivning af nødstedt vildt:
  https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2016/827 (§ 1: 6 dagtimer, who calls the dog handler)
- Bekendtgørelse nr. 521 af 25. marts 2021 (artsfredningsbekendtgørelsen):
  https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2021/521 (§ 29: care and 6 months)
- Våbenloven, LBK nr. 1736 af 26. august 2021: https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2021/1736 (§ 2 a)
- Våbenbekendtgørelsen nr. 2531 af 20. december 2021: https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2021/2531
  (§ 11 transport in closed case, unloaded; § 24 safe EN 1143-1 grade 0, bolted under 1.000 kg,
  ammunition in safe; § 25 temporary storage day before to day after; § 49 rifle permit 10 years;
  § 52 notify the police within 8 days of acquiring a shotgun)
- Naturbeskyttelsesloven § 3 (lakes over 100 m², moors, heaths, meadows over 2.500 m²).
- Vildtskadebekendtgørelsen nr. 1408 af 3. oktober 2022 is marked Historic on retsinformation
  (replaced): only the general rule of jagtloven § 37 (regulation when no satisfactory
  alternative) is used, no species periods.

## Teaching facts (common jagttegn teaching, labelled in explanations as rules of thumb)
- Shotgun safety distance: shot diameter in mm x 100 m; safety angle 40 degrees found with a bent
  arm (retningslinjer 2.3 names both); rifle safety distance described as »flere kilometer«, no figure.
- Biology (brunst, kimhvile, kuld, nesting) from standard Danish game biology; no figures that vary
  by source beyond ranges.
