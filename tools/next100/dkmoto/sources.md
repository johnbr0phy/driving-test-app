# Sources for `dkmoto` (Denmark motorcycle theory test, teoriprøve, categories A1, A2 and A)

All checked 2026-11-06 unless noted. Questions are original; no official, past or commercial test
item was copied. Færdselsstyrelsen publishes no question bank. Færdselsstyrelsen's "Forprøve til
kørelæreruddannelsen, kategori A" PDFs are driving-instructor entrance papers, not the student test,
and were not used.

## Exam format (Færdselsstyrelsen, the Danish Road Traffic Authority)

- Færdselsstyrelsen, "Hvor kan jeg tage en teoriprøve?" (digital theory tests, for driving schools):
  https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/koereskoler-og-koerelaerere/digitale-teoriproever
  - The digital theory test is offered for categories A, AM, B, C, D, E and LK (and combined
    tests). "Digitale teoriprøver på dansk og engelsk afvikles på samtlige digitale prøvesteder
    landet over." German digital tests only in Aabenraa. Faroese and Greenlandic remain analogue
    with an interpreter.
  - Questions are randomised and drawn from the whole teaching plan. At most five errors to pass,
    for both digital and analogue tests.
  - The page gives no question count. This confirms English for category A on an official page.
  - Re-checked 2026-11-07 (review): "Du kan tage den digitale teoriprøve på engelsk og tysk i
    kategorier A, AM, B, ..."; German only in Aabenraa, Danish and English at every digital site;
    "Besvarer du ikke et spørgsmål tæller det som en fejl" (an unanswered question counts as an
    error); "man må have fem fejl for at bestå"; 25 minutes is stated for a B test only.
- Færdselsstyrelsen, "Frigivelse og afholdelse af prøver":
  https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/koereskoler-og-koerelaerere/frigivelse-og-afholdelse-af-proever
  Randomisation key for the digital category A test (approx. % by afsnit of the teaching plan):
  1: 8, 3: 8, 4: 8, 5: 8, 6: 8, 7: 52, 8: 8. Afsnit 2 (closed practice ground) and 9 (track
  manoeuvres) are practical only. Tigertest's 25-question blueprint: afsnit 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 =
  2 each, afsnit 7 = 13 (split by sub-section 4/3/3/3, Tigertest's own split).
- Færdselsstyrelsen, "Tag kørekort til bil. Sådan gør du" (category B, checked for the shared
  test rules): https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/dit-foerste-koerekort/tag-koerekort-saadan-goer-du
  Theory test 25 minutes, digital or analogue, at most five errors; official online practice test
  25 questions with 20 to pass. Interpreters for other languages ended 1 January 2025.
- Undervisningsplan kategori AM, A1, A2 og A, section 10.2.2 (Teoriprøven) refers to the same
  section of the category B plan: test of about half an hour, random series of approved test sets,
  digital tests on a touch screen with computer-generated pictures at selected test sites. So the
  category A theory test runs under the same rules as category B.
- Question count, guides only (not confirmed on an fstyr.dk page for category A): Lovguiden
  (2026-04-14, quoting Færdselsstyrelsen; the page returned HTTP 429, read from search results)
  https://lovguiden.dk/det-offentlige/faerdselsstyrelsen/2026-04-14-test-teoriproeven-udvides-til-25-spoergsmaal-fra-21-april-2026
  and https://lovguiden.dk/det-offentlige/faerdselsstyrelsen/2026-04-14-teoriproeve-til-koerekort-test-udvides-fra-10-til-25-spoergsmaal
  say the official online practice tests for B and C grew from 10 to 25 questions on 21 April 2026;
  FDM (2025-07) https://fdm.dk/guides/regler/saadan-bliver-du-klar-til-teoriproeven and
  https://www.learndrivingtheory.com/en/denmark/practice/dk-digital-theory-test-categories describe
  25 picture questions with up to four answer options and at least one correct, five errors allowed.
  Tigertest models 25-question tests with 20 to pass (80%), matching the five-error rule.
- Paid prep exists in English and Danish (prove.dk FAQ, https://prove.dk/en/faq : "For motorcycles,
  there are 30 video lessons and 15 theory tests in English and Danish"); not used for content.

## Rules (content of every question)

- Færdselsstyrelsen, Undervisningsplan kategori AM (stor knallert) og kategori A1, A2 og A (lille,
  mellem og stor motorcykel), version 1.0 2026, in use from 1 July 2026 (students who started
  before may finish on the old rules until the end of December 2026):
  https://admin.fstyr.dk/Media/639045957573779147/Undervisnigsplan%20kategori%20AM%20(stor%20knallert)%20og%20kategori%20A1%20A2%20og%20A%20(lille%20%20mellem%20og%20stor%20motorcykel)%20-%20210126.pdf
  Every motorcycle rule and figure in the bank is from this plan. Used: front brake lever on the
  right; two independent braking systems; ABS on newer two-wheeled motorcycles; hydraulic lever
  must not sink; tread at least 1 mm in the main pattern; new tyres slippery, old tyres hard;
  chain can jump off and lock the rear wheel; forks bottoming = fault; main beam at least 100 m;
  mirror each side (newer motorcycles); windscreen too high to see 10 m ahead needs wiper and
  washer; helmet DS, FS, SS or E marked; backpack and back protector; trailer: motorcycle at least
  125 cm³, trailer at most 200 kg, 2.50 m by 1.30 m; sidecar lights (white position and red rear
  light at the outer edge, right indicators on the sidecar); categories A1 (125 cm³, 11 kW,
  0.1 kW/kg, from 18), A2 (35 kW, 0.2 kW/kg, from 20), A (24, or 22 after two years on A2);
  helmets for rider and every passenger aged 5 or over, rider responsible for ages 5 to 14;
  children under 135 cm only from age 5 with a child seat; pillion astride; sidecar: one extra
  child under 10, seat belt for children under 5; light signal instead of horn in the dark;
  always signal before lane changes on motorways; hazard lights on motorways for queues; breakdown
  on motorway: off the carriageway, reflective vest, behind the barrier; motorcycles may not tow or
  be towed; C 22,2 sign; not beside another vehicle; group riding
  staggered; position in lane, ideal line, bends, single-vehicle crashes on the outside of bends;
  meeting accidents on narrow roads, nearly half over the middle; following accidents; overtaking
  rules (7.9.5); traffic-calmed areas (pedestrian street under 15 km/h, cycle street normally max
  30 km/h, motorcycles with more than two wheels only in marked spaces); junction accident facts;
  roundabout signalling and crash types; motorway joining, leaving, combined lanes, E 44; parking
  beside another two-wheeled motorcycle allowed; parked solo motorcycle at the road edge needs no
  position light; dipping 200 to 300 m before a car or motorcycle; light rail at most the bus
  limit, 80 km/h; within 2 m of tracks stay near; afsnit 8 alcohol effects (0.2, 0.5, 0.8),
  30 to 90 minutes rising, burning from about 45 minutes, risk multipliers (10, 30, 200 times),
  drugs (cannabis 3, cocaine/heroin 10, amphetamine 30, mixed with alcohol 200), passenger
  compensation reduced with a drunk rider, speed and rural crashes, road works.
- Afsnit 3, 4 and 5 of the A plan refer to the same sections of the category B plan, which was
  read for those questions: Undervisningsplan kategori B, version 1.1, May 2026:
  https://cdn.fstyr.dk/faerdselsstyrelsen/Media/639159124710541258/undervisningsplan-kategori-b-almindelig-bil-version-11-maj-2026_pdf290526.pdf
  Used: 3.2.2 lorries (braking, clusters, turning room), 3.2.4 tractors (30 or 40 km/h, mistaken
  for lorries), 3.2.5 motorcycles (acceleration, side wind, mistaken for mopeds, sidecar pulls
  right accelerating and left braking, sidecar or rear wheel lifts, passenger not leaning), 3.2.7
  speed pedelecs (45 km/h, lights on), 4.1 to 4.7 (2 to 3 details per second, steering where you
  look, distances under 50 m, speeds over 50 km/h underestimated, 200 m, motorcyclists aged 18 to
  22, children 10 to 15 look back poorly, white cane, risk blindness, phones, medicine with
  alcohol), 5.2 (leaves, tractor soil, ice on and under bridges, roads without cycle tracks about
  twice the risk, pedestrian crossings, central reservations, narrow roads, cyclists leaving a
  cycle track give way).
- Færdselsstyrelsen modulplan kategori A, version 1.1, April 2026 (module structure, checked):
  https://cdn.fstyr.dk/faerdselsstyrelsen/Media/639142705530526264/fs-a5-modulplan-kategori-a-version-11-april-2026-digital.pdf

## Not used

- The 0.2 per mille novice limit: the plan applies it to first-time category B holders driving a
  car ("kørslen sker i almindelig bil"), so it is not asked for motorcycles. The general limit
  (over 0.50 per mille) is already in the dkteori bank and is not repeated here.
- Fines, fees, demerit points (klip), disqualification periods and lesson counts.
- The picture element and multi-answer items of the real test: Tigertest uses single-answer text
  questions and describes signs in words; the landing says so.
- The practical test (manoeuvre test and road test) and the closed-track and track exercises
  (afsnit 2 and 9), which are not in the theory test key.

## Lane check against dkteori (same country)

Items whose fact and answer repeat dkteori were rewritten while writing (indicator flash rate, ABS
lamp, triangle 50 m, flashing headlights after dark, three-lane roads, hill crest overtaking,
roundabout signalling, slip road shark's teeth, one lane at a time, built-up and rural limits,
crawler lane, solid line U-turn, C 11,2, e-scooter speed, red triangle medicine, phones, children
and elderly, slippery road sign, hazard lights on queues, daytime running lights).

## Review pass (2026-11-07)

Licence ages re-read in the A plan, 6.1 (A1 from 18; A2 from 20; A from 24, or from 22 after at
least two years on A2 and a practical test) and 6.3 (helmets from 5, rider responsible for 5 to
14; children under 135 cm only from 5 with a child seat; under 5 in a sidecar only with a belt):
all as in the bank, landing and examFacts. Rules used in the rewrites: 6.1.1 (2) motorcycles on
the motor vehicle part of the road only; 6.2.3 (3) light signal instead of horn in the lighting-up
time unless danger is imminent; 7.1.2 and 7.1.5 (impatience moving off; braking distance depends
on how hard you brake); 7.2.4 (7) ideal line; 7.3 (no continuous braking on long descents);
7.4.2 (lane changes surprise others; vibrating mirrors); 7.9.1 to 7.9.3 (overtaken vehicle is the
main other party; doubt costs time; columns with short gaps); 7.14.2 and 7.14.3 (green taken as a
free path; rushing the turn from the exposed waiting place; small vehicles beside large ones);
7.16.6 (crossing traffic at expressway junctions); 7.19 (dip as the overtaker pulls back in).
Afsnit 9 figures (braking distances at 30 km/h, wet-road speeds) are not used: afsnit 9 is not in
the theory test key.

Rewritten to avoid repeats of dkteori: hatched area kept (different fact); dropped from dkmoto
the motorway signalling rule, STOP line, lane-change arrows, merging rule, No overtaking sign,
bus lane passing, expressway oncoming traffic, red flashing level crossing light, last-exit
roundabout signal. Rewritten to avoid near-copies in other driving banks: braking distance x4,
blind spot check, lower gear downhill, pillion astride, narrow road cyclist, one-way left turn,
overtake on the left.
