# dkteori Danish (da-DK) glossary

Terms as Færdselsstyrelsen's Undervisningsplan kategori B (version 1.1, maj 2026) and
færdselsloven use them. Questions, explanations and app copy in "du", the register of the plan,
the official øveprøve and Danish learner materials. Decimal comma (1,6 mm; 0,5 promille),
thousands with a point (3.500 kg; 1.500 m), km/h, meter, times as kl. 22.00. No em or en dashes.
