# Sources for `dkteori` (Denmark theory test, teoriprøve, category B)

All checked 2026-11-01 unless noted. Questions are original; no official, past or commercial
test item was copied. Færdselsstyrelsen publishes no question bank.

## Exam format (Færdselsstyrelsen, the Danish Road Traffic Authority)

- Færdselsstyrelsen, "Tag kørekort til bil. Sådan gør du" (step 4, Teoriprøven):
  https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/dit-foerste-koerekort/tag-koerekort-saadan-goer-du
  - Færdselsstyrelsen holds the theory test at its own test centres.
  - Digital (touch screen, 16 test centres) or analogue (paper answer sheet, questions played on a
    screen) test. "Begge prøver varer 25 minutter, og du må have op til fem fejl." Same syllabus
    for both.
  - Digital: move back and forth between questions, questions can be read aloud, headphones,
    log in with MitID or a code. "Besvar spørgsmålene ved at vælge det eller de korrekte svar.
    Hvis du ikke besvarer et spørgsmål, tæller det som en fejl." Questions drawn at random from
    the whole teaching plan (undervisningsplan), so every test is different.
  - Analogue: you cannot go back to earlier questions.
  - Special test (specialprøve): 60 minutes instead of 25, after an individual assessment.
  - Official online practice test: "Du skal svare rigtigt på mindst 20 ud af 25 spørgsmål for at
    bestå øvetesten" (25 questions, 20 to pass). The real test's question count is not stated on
    the page; guides say 25 picture-based questions. Tigertest uses 25-question tests with 20 to
    pass (80%), matching the practice test and the five-error rule.
  - A passed theory test is valid for 18 months. In 2025, 67% of 177,000 theory tests were passed
    at the first attempt (not used in questions).
  - Theory test from the day you turn 16 years and 11 months, after the closed-track lessons;
    licence at 17. Under 18: accompanied 20:00 to 05:00 and Denmark only (also on
    https://www.fstyr.dk/privat/koerekort/dit-foerste-koerekort/koerekort-til-17-aarige).
  - Bookings: from 1 October 2026 new students book on minkøreprøve.dk (frigivelse page below).
  - Fees exist (not used: no money figures).
- Languages, same page ("Tag kørekort på et andet sprog"): Danish plus English, German, Faroese
  and Greenlandic. English: digital or analogue theory test at all test centres in Denmark, no
  interpreter. German: digital only in Aabenraa, analogue in Aabenraa, Haderslev, Tønder and
  Sønderborg. Faroese and Greenlandic: analogue with an interpreter. Chosen on the application
  form (P23). Interpreters for other languages were ended from 1 January 2025 (Færdselsstyrelsen
  press release via Lovguiden, 2024-11-12; the page itself returned HTTP 429, so the date is from
  the search snippet; the fstyr.dk page above confirms the current list).
- Randomisation key for the digital test, Færdselsstyrelsen "Frigivelse og afholdelse af prøver"
  (statistics for 30.04.2024 to 31.12.2025):
  https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/koereskoler-og-koerelaerere/frigivelse-og-afholdelse-af-proever
  Category B, approx. % by section (afsnit) of the teaching plan: 1: 8, 3: 4, 4: 4, 5: 4, 6: 4,
  7: 72, 8: 4. Section 2 (closed practice ground) is not tested in theory. Tigertest's 25-question
  blueprint: afsnit 1 = 2, afsnit 3 + 4 = 2, afsnit 5 + 6 + 8 = 3, afsnit 7 = 18 (split by
  sub-section 3/4/5/6, Tigertest's own split). The same page lists the sections most often
  answered wrongly (section 1, vehicle, 57% correct).

## Rules (content of every question)

- Færdselsstyrelsen, Undervisningsplan kategori B (almindelig bil), version 1.1, May 2026,
  in use from 1 July 2026 (students who started before may finish on the old rules until the end
  of December 2026):
  https://cdn.fstyr.dk/faerdselsstyrelsen/Media/639159124710541258/undervisningsplan-kategori-b-almindelig-bil-version-11-maj-2026_pdf290526.pdf
  Every rule, figure and sign meaning in the bank is from this plan, which sets out the content of
  the theory test. Figures used: tyre tread 1.6 mm; studded tyres 1 November to 15 April on all
  wheels; dipped beam 30 m, main beam 100 m, position lights 300 m, number plate light 20 m;
  3 stop lamps (older cars 2); alcohol over 0.50 per mille (0.25 mg/l breath) and 0.2 per mille for
  the first 3 years after a first category B licence; standard drink 12 g; 60 kg person 1.5 to 2
  hours per drink; 10% faster above 30 km/h up to 45% more fatal crashes; tractors 30 km/h
  (40 with tempo 40 mark), small moped 30 km/h, large moped 45 km/h, e-scooters 20 km/h;
  category B up to 3,500 kg and 8 passengers, trailer 750 kg or combined 3,500 kg; children under
  135 cm in approved restraints; driver responsible for passengers under 15; load over 1 m
  front/rear or 15 cm sides marked; warning triangle at least 50 m (100 m on motorways); towing
  gap max 4 m (mark rope over 2 m), max 30 km/h, banned on motorways; built-up area 50 km/h,
  outside 80 km/h, car with trailer 80 km/h (tempo 100: 100 km/h on motorways), motorway 130 km/h,
  motortrafikvej 80 km/h (may be raised locally to 100), motorway only for vehicles able to do
  50 km/h; police phone 114; combinations over 7 m in the two right lanes on motorways with 3+
  lanes; about 2 seconds following distance; braking distance x4 when speed doubles; play street
  under 15 km/h; cycle street normally max 30 km/h; no stopping within 5 m before a pedestrian
  crossing, 10 m of a junction, at bus stops and 12 m either side; no parking within 30 m of a
  level crossing; parking = over 3 minutes; meeting on narrow dark roads at most 40 km/h; edge posts
  orange right, white left; tunnel lane signals; light rail trains bus speed limit (not asked).
- Hand-held phone ban: undervisningsplan 4.1.5 and 4.1.7 (the fine and demerit "klip" are not
  asked).

## Not used

- Fines, fees, the number of demerit points (klip) and disqualification periods, which change.
- The picture element: the real test shows a photo or 3D-rendered picture with each question and
  can have more than one correct answer per question. Tigertest uses single-answer text questions
  and describes signs in words; the landing says so.
- Guides: open-exam-prep.com, learndrivingtheory.com, autoviatest.com (search results only)
  describe 25 picture questions, 25 minutes and 5 errors, matching Færdselsstyrelsen. Lovguiden
  (2026-04-14, via search snippet only, the page returned HTTP 429) reports that Færdselsstyrelsen's
  online practice test grew from 10 to 25 questions on 21 April 2026.

## Reviewer checks (2026-11-01)

- Novice alcohol limit: confirmed. Undervisningsplan B v1.1 (May 2026), section 4.4.1 point 5: "Er
  promillen over 0,2 for førstegangserhververe til kategori B, straffes man for spirituskørsel, hvis
  det sker inden for de første 3 år, og kørslen sker i almindelig bil." Enacted by LOV nr 567 af
  27/05/2025 (bill L 165, 2024-25, ft.dk/samling/20241/lovforslag/L165), in force 1 July 2025 per the
  search results; the general limit stays over 0.50 per mille (0.25 mg/l breath).
- Languages and interpreters: fstyr.dk "Tag kørekort til bil" re-read 2026-11-01: English digital or
  analogue nationwide; German digital only in Aabenraa, analogue in Aabenraa, Haderslev, Tønder and
  Sønderborg; Faroese and Greenlandic analogue with an interpreter. Interpreters for all other
  languages ended 1 January 2025 (Færdselsstyrelsen press release of 2024-11-12, via Lovguiden and
  DI/ATL news 2024-11-14).
- Question count: fstyr.dk still gives only 25 minutes and at most five errors. Lovguiden
  (2026-04-14, quoting Færdselsstyrelsen's press release) and FDM (2025-07) say the real category B
  test has 25 questions, with up to four answer options and at least one correct; the official
  practice test grew from 10 to 25 questions on 21 April 2026. Not confirmed on an fstyr.dk page.
- Randomisation key (8/4/4/4/4/72/4) re-read on the fstyr.dk "Frigivelse og afholdelse af prøver"
  page 2026-11-01.
- Speed limits (50, 80, 130, trailer 80 / tempo 100, motortrafikvej 80 up to 100), the right-hand
  rule, vigelinje (hajtænder) = ubetinget vigepligt (plan 7.12 point 19), and the parking and
  stopping distances were all checked against the v1.1 plan text.
