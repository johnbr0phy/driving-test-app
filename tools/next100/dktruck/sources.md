# Sources for `dktruck` (Denmark truck theory test, teoriprøve, categories C and CE)

All fetched 2026-11-15 unless noted. Every question is original: no official, past, sample or
commercial test item was copied or reworded. Færdselsstyrelsen publishes no question bank. Its
"Forprøve til kørelæreruddannelsen, kategori CDE" PDFs are driving-instructor entrance papers, not
the student test, and were not opened or used.

## Format (Færdselsstyrelsen)

- Færdselsstyrelsen, "Hvor kan jeg tage en teoriprøve?" (digital theory tests), fetched
  2026-11-15: https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/koereskoler-og-koerelaerere/digitale-teoriproever
  "Du kan tage den digitale teoriprøve på engelsk og tysk i kategorier A, AM, B, C, D, E, LK";
  Danish and English at every digital test site; at most five errors to pass. 25 minutes is
  stated for a B test only; no time is given for C. So the C test exists in English and the bank
  is written in English like `dkteori` and `dkmoto`.
- Færdselsstyrelsen, "Frigivelse og afholdelse af prøver", fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.fstyr.dk/erhverv/koereskoler-og-koerelaerere/frigivelse-og-afholdelse-af-proever
  Randomisation key for the digital category C (and C1) test, approx. % by afsnit: 1: 32, 3: 8,
  4: 4, 5: 4, 6: 16, 7: 28, 8: 8. C/E and C1/E have their own key (1: 40, 3: 7, 5: 7, 6: 20, 7: 27).
  The bank follows the C key; a few CE coupling and length items sit inside afsnit 1 and 6.
- Undervisningsplan C/D, 0.1 and 0.2: the theory test asks the candidate to find the correct
  answers among several from pictures or video sequences and questions on the law.
- Question count, guides only (not on an fstyr.dk page for C): Lovguiden (2026-04-14, quoting
  Færdselsstyrelsen; the page returned HTTP 429 to an automated fetch, read from search results)
  https://lovguiden.dk/det-offentlige/faerdselsstyrelsen/2026-04-14-teoriproeve-til-koerekort-test-udvides-fra-10-til-25-spoergsmaal
  says the official online practice tests for B and C grew from 10 to 25 questions on 21 April
  2026 and the real test allows five errors. Tigertest models eight 25-question tests at 80 %
  (20 of 25).

## Law

- Regulation (EC) No 561/2006 (driving and rest times) and Regulation (EU) No 165/2014
  (tachographs): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/561/oj and
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/165/oj . EUR-Lex returned an empty page to automated
  fetches on 2026-11-15 (checked), so every figure is taken from the restatement in undervisningsplan
  C/D 6.3 and 1.8, Færdselsstyrelsen's own teaching text of these rules.
- Færdselsloven on retsinformation.dk returned only its page header to automated fetches; the
  lorry speed limits (80 km/h on every road type, Tempo 100 emergency vehicles) and lane rules are
  taken from the plan's 7.3.5 and 7.16.5.

## Not used

- Fines, fees, klip (demerit points), disqualification periods and lesson counts.
- The picture and video element and multi-answer items of the real test: single-answer text
  questions with signs described in words.
- The D (bus) and D1 parts of the plan, afsnit 2 and 9 (closed-ground and skid-pan exercises,
  not in the key) and afsnit 10 (the test procedure).

## Lane check against dkteori and dkmoto (same country), fitruck, hugki and mtcpc

Facts and answers already in `dkteori` or `dkmoto` were left out: indicator flash rate, ABS lamp
to the workshop, dipped beam 30 m, main beam 100 m, tread 1.6 mm, braking distance x4, braking
distance and how hard you brake, reaction time, 2 to 3 details a second, distances under 50 m,
speeds over 50 km/h, children, elderly at green lights, phones, red triangle medicine, genstand,
next-morning alcohol, coffee, 0.2/0.5/0.8 per mille effects, alcohol and drug risk multipliers,
10 % speed and 45 % risk, seat belts at 10 km/h, load 1 m behind, three-lane motorway lanes for
cars with caravans, tractors' red triangle and 30/40 km/h. The EU driving-time and tachograph
rules are the same law as in `fitruck`, `hugki` and `mtcpc`; here they are asked as Danish
scenarios (one 10-hour day left after Monday, the 40 hours left after a 50-hour week, the Tuesday
22:00 deadline, the 30-hour compensation, the 8-hour ferry rule), not as copies of those stems, and
stems that came out close to `uklgv`, `iedttcv`, `rocpi`, `mtcpc` or `dkmoto` were rewritten.
