# Sources for `ea1` (IRS Enrolled Agent Special Enrollment Examination, Part 1: Individuals)

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the IRS's published Part 1 content
outline and the Internal Revenue Code, Treasury regulations and IRS forms, instructions and
publications it points to. No IRS sample question, released SEE item, PSI material, commercial
review course, book or app item was used, reworded or adapted. Checked 2026-11-16.

## Outline and format (official)
- PSI Services LLC for the IRS, "Candidate Information Bulletin, Internal Revenue Service,
  Enrolled Agent Special Enrollment Examination", REVISED October 9, 2026 (48 pages),
  fetched 2026-11-16: https://test-takers.psigov.us/api/content/bulletin/12238
  (linked from the IRS FAQ page below). It gave:
  - Part 1 Examination Content Outline with scored questions per domain: Preliminary Work and
    Taxpayer Data 14; Income and Assets 17; Deductions and Credits 17; Taxation 15; Advising
    the Individual Taxpayer 11; Specialized Returns for Individuals 11 (85 scored), with each
    domain's topic list (used domain by domain; for example kiddie tax, FBAR and Form 8938 under
    Preliminary Work, IRAs, RMDs and section 121 under Income and Assets, estate, gift and
    international information reporting under Specialized Returns).
  - Each part: 100 multiple-choice questions, 85 scored and 15 unscored experimental, 3.5 hours
    (4-hour appointment with tutorial and survey), on-screen calculator.
  - Scaled scores 200 to 800; the IRS sets the scaled passing score at 500. A pass shows only
    "pass"; a fail shows a scaled score from 200 to 499 with diagnostics.
  - Tax law tested: "For exams taken between July 1, 2026-February 28, 2027, all references ...
    are to the Internal Revenue Code, Code of Federal Regulations, IRS forms, instructions, and
    publications, as amended through December 31, 2025. Unless otherwise stated, all questions
    relate to the calendar year 2025." So the current window tests tax year 2025.
  - Window July 1, 2026 to February 28, 2027; no testing in March and April; each part up to
    four times a window; parts in any order; in person in the U.S. or remote proctored
    (international candidates remote only).
- IRS, "Enrolled agents frequently asked questions", page reviewed 26-Oct-2026, fetched
  2026-11-16: https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/enrolled-agents/enrolled-agents-frequently-asked-questions
  Confirms the three parts, 100 questions (85 scored), 3.5 hours, scaled 200 to 800 with 500 to
  pass, the 2025 law cutoff, the 2026-27 domestic window, PSI as the vendor since March 1, 2026,
  the three-year carryover of passed parts, the 24-hour wait to reschedule a failed part, and
  that international scheduling is delayed with no new start date. Its exam fee figure is not
  used anywhere in the bank or copy.
- IRS notice "Important update: Testing vendor transition", fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.irs.gov/node/776 (PSI replaced Prometric; international SEE testing delayed).

## 2025 figures used in the bank (each named with its tax year in the stem)
- IRS, "What's new: estate and gift tax", fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/whats-new-estate-and-gift-tax
  Basic exclusion $13,990,000 for 2025 deaths ($15,000,000 for 2026); annual exclusion $19,000
  for 2025 gifts; portability on a timely filed return.
- IRS, "Topic no. 503, Deductible taxes", fetched 2026-11-16: https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc503
  SALT limit $40,000 ($20,000 married filing separately), subject to a MAGI limitation, not
  below $10,000. The $500,000 MAGI start of the phase-down is from the 2025 Instructions for
  Schedule A (Form 1040), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i1040sca.pdf (read as an IRS search
  extract 2026-11-16).
- IRS, "New and enhanced deductions for individuals", fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/new-and-enhanced-deductions-for-individuals
  2025 standard deduction figures and the new tips, overtime, senior and car loan deductions
  (used only for context; no bank item asks their amounts).
- IRS, "One Big Beautiful Bill provisions", fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/one-big-beautiful-bill-provisions
  Adoption credit up to $5,000 refundable for tax years after December 31, 2024; dependent
  care credit rate change and non-itemizer charitable deduction start in 2026 (so 2025 items use
  the old dependent care rules); 25C and 25D end after 2025.
- IRS, "Residential clean energy credit", fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/residential-clean-energy-credit
  30% for property placed in service in 2025; none after December 31, 2025.

## Modelling
- Four 50-question mock tests, the bulletin's 14/17/17/15/11/11 of 85 scaled to 50:
  8/10/10/9/7/6 (Tigertest's rounding; Advising rounded up and Specialized down). Pools are
  exactly four times the per-test counts, so no question repeats across tests.
- The 70% line is Tigertest's readiness target. The real result is a scaled score with 500 to
  pass, which is not a fixed percentage.
- The real Part 1 is 100 questions (85 scored) in 3.5 hours; ours is 50 questions at the same
  pace. Unscored experimental items are not modelled.

## Left out on purpose
- Exam fees, penalty dollar amounts and indexed 2025 thresholds the outline does not need
  (tax brackets, AMT exemption, kiddie tax threshold, EITC limits, the child tax credit amount,
  saver's credit tiers, QBI thresholds), except the cited 2025 SALT cap, estate exclusion and
  gift annual exclusion, each named with its year.
- Provisions of the 2025 law that start in 2026 (gambling 90% rule, non-itemizer charitable
  deduction, new dependent care rates).
