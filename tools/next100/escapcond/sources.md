# Sources for `escapcond` (Spain Driver CPC, CAP inicial de conductores, in Spanish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Spanish from the laws below. Nothing was
copied from a community's past papers (Catalonia and others publish them), an official sample, a
CAP centre's material or a commercial test app. Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- Real Decreto 284/2021, de 20 de abril, BOE núm. 98, 24 April 2021, BOE-A-2021-6624
  (consolidated text and the original PDF, read 2026-11-06):
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-6624
  https://boe.es/boe/dias/2021/04/24/pdfs/BOE-A-2021-6624.pdf
  - Art. 4: CAP inicial in two modalities, ordinaria and acelerada; a compulsory course plus an exam.
  - Art. 5.1: ordinaria = course of 280 hours + the exam of chapter VI. Art. 6.1: acelerada = 140
    hours + the exam. No driving licence needed to take the course or sit the exam.
  - Art. 7.2: formación continua = a 35-hour course every five years (periods of at least 7 hours).
  - Art. 16.1: the exam must be passed within one year of finishing the course, on Annex I A.
  - Art. 16.3: a holder of one speciality who extends to the other does only that speciality's
    course section and "aquella parte del examen que se refiera a esta formación".
  - Art. 17: exams organised by the bodies that grant transport authorisations in each territory
    (the comunidades autónomas); called at least one month ahead; "al menos seis veces al año".
  - Anexo V, sección 1.ª: written or oral exam; "cien preguntas tipo test, cada una de las cuales
    contará con cuatro respuestas alternativas"; 25 of them on the speciality (sección 2.ª
    mercancías or 3.ª viajeros of Annex I A); at least one question per objective; time "no será
    inferior a 2 horas"; correct +1, wrong -0,5, blank or more than one answer 0; pass with "una
    puntuación no inferior a la mitad del total de puntos posibles".
  - Anexo I A: the syllabus. Sección 1.ª common (objectives 1.1 cadena cinemática, 1.2 dispositivos
    de seguridad, 1.3 consumo, 1.3 bis riesgos del tráfico, 2.1 entorno social y reglamentación:
    jornada, Reg. 561/2006, Reg. 165/2014, derechos y obligaciones en cualificación y formación
    continua, 3.1 riesgos de la carretera y accidentes de trabajo, 3.2 delincuencia e inmigración
    clandestina, 3.3 riesgos físicos, 3.4 aptitud física y mental, 3.5 emergencias, 3.6 imagen de
    marca). Sección 2.ª mercancías (1.4 carga, 2.2 reglamentación, 3.7 entorno económico).
    Sección 3.ª viajeros (1.5 seguridad y comodidad, 1.6 carga, 2.3 reglamentación, 3.8 entorno
    económico).
- Real Decreto 487/2026, de 17 de junio (BOE 18 June 2026), in force 18 September 2026 per the
  consolidated text's notes, read 2026-11-06: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2026/06/17/487
  Adds chapter IX (aula virtual: up to 166 online hours in the ordinaria course and 92 in the
  acelerada, never for practical driving or the exam), changes arts. 2, 3.1, 4, 11, 12, 18 and 21
  (art. 18: expulsion and a one-year ban for using unauthorised devices in the exam), and
  renumbers Annex I to match Directive (EU) 2022/2561 (1.3 bis becomes 1.4 and the speciality
  objectives move up one). Anexo V (format and marking) is not changed. The bank comments give
  both numberings.
- Extremadura, "Exámenes para la obtención del CAP" (read 2026-11-06): 100 test questions, four
  answers with one correct, "dos horas para realizar el examen", pass with at least half the
  possible points. https://www.juntaex.es/es/w/certificado-cap-transporte-mercancias-viajeros-examenes
- Extremadura, Resolución de 19 de diciembre de 2025 (DOE núm. 250, 30 December 2025), 2026 CAP
  exams in six llamamientos, "El tiempo para la realización del examen será de 2 horas" (read
  2026-11-06): https://doe.juntaex.es/pdfs/doe/2025/2500o/25064633.pdf
- Castilla-La Mancha, convocatoria anual 2026 (the PDF returned an HTML page when fetched on
  2026-11-06; not used for any figure):
  https://www.castillalamancha.es/sites/default/files/documentos/pdf/20251118/convocatoria_anual_pruebas_cap_2026_docm.pdf

Not confirmed, so left out of the copy: reserve questions (the research notes mention 3, the RD
and the Extremadura pages do not), the exam languages (the notes say co-official languages in
some communities), and the renewal ("promoción") route of 25 questions (art. 16.3 gives a
speciality-only part for an extension; no other 25-question route was confirmed).

## Law used in the questions
- Reglamento (CE) n.º 561/2006 as amended by Reglamento (UE) 2020/1054 (Paquete de Movilidad),
  consolidated Spanish text on EUR-Lex, read 2026-11-06: arts. 4, 6, 7, 8, 8.8 bis, 9, 12.
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/ES/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02006R0561-20240522
- Reglamento (UE) n.º 165/2014 (tacógrafos) as amended by 2020/1054: art. 34 (country symbol at the
  first suitable stopping place), art. 36 (current day and previous 56 days from 31 December 2024),
  art. 29 (replacement within seven calendar days), art. 35 (printouts, up to 15 calendar days),
  art. 33 (company keeps downloaded data at least one year), smart tachograph version 2 for vehicles
  first registered from 21 August 2023.
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/ES/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02014R0165-20200820
- Reglamento (UE) n.º 581/2010: card data downloaded at least every 28 days, vehicle unit every 90.
- Directiva 2002/15/CE (tiempo de trabajo): 60 hours in a week, 48 average over four months, 30 or
  45 minutes' break, 10 hours with night work; transposed by Real Decreto 1561/1995.
- Ley 16/1987 de Ordenación de los Transportes Terrestres (manipulación del tacógrafo as a very
  serious infringement; disposición adicional decimotercera from Real Decreto-ley 3/2022: drivers
  of goods vehicles over 7,5 t do not load or unload, with listed exceptions, from 2 September 2022).
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1987-17803
- Orden FOM/2861/2012 (documento de control in national public goods transport).
- Convenio CMR (three original copies of the consignment note) and Reglamento (CE) n.º 1072/2009 as
  amended (three cabotage operations within seven days; certified copy of the Community licence).
- Reglamento (UE) n.º 181/2011 (bus and coach passenger rights: choice of continuation or
  reimbursement over 120 minutes' delay; refreshments over 90 minutes on journeys over 3 hours; no
  refusal for disability save safety or vehicle design).
- Real Decreto 443/2001 (transporte escolar y de menores: under one hour in each direction as a
  rule).
- Reglamento General de Circulación (chaleco reflectante on leaving the vehicle on an interurban
  road; belts in buses and how passengers are told).
- Ley 50/1980 de Contrato de Seguro, art. 16 (report the claim within seven days).
- UNE-EN 12195-1 (load securing: 0,8 g forward, 0,5 g sideways and backward).
- Directiva 92/6/CEE as amended (speed limiter: N3 at 90 km/h, M3 at 100 km/h).
