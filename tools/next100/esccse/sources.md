# Sources for `esccse` (Prueba CCSE, Instituto Cervantes, in Spanish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Spanish from the Constitución española and the study text of the Instituto Cervantes Manual de preparación de la prueba CCSE 2026. The Manual's list of 300 official items was used only to know which topics the test covers; no official item, answer set or wording was copied or reworded, and no commercial app or prep site question was used. Model authorship and model language review only; no human or native-speaker review is claimed. Every source below was read on 2026-11-23 unless noted.

## Format and legal basis

- Instituto Cervantes, Guía para la prueba CCSE (PDF, examenes.cervantes.es; the host now redirects to cervantes.org, so the file was read from its Internet Archive copy of 2026-09-16): https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/guia_prueba_ccse.pdf (archived copy https://web.archive.org/web/20260916054911/https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/guia_prueba_ccse.pdf, fetched 2026-11-23). Gave: 5 tareas, 25 closed-answer items (three options with one true, or true/false); Parte 1 Gobierno, legislación y participación ciudadana 60 % (Tarea 1 10 items, Tarea 2 3 true/false items, Tarea 3 2 items) and Parte 2 Cultura, historia y sociedad 40 % (Tarea 4 3 items, Tarea 5 7 items); 45 minutes; 1 point per correct answer, no penalty for wrong ones; APTO with 15 of 25 (60 %); results about 20 days later, certificate valid four years; a NO APTO or absent candidate may register once more without paying again; held on the last Thursday of the month except August and December; the Manual holds all items that can appear in the test (300 items classified by tarea). Ley 19/2015 (disposición final séptima) and Ley 12/2015 require the CCSE for nationality by residence and for Sephardic applicants; minors and persons under guardianship are exempt.
- Instituto Cervantes, CCSE page on the current site: https://cervantes.org/es/examenes/ccse (fetched 2026-11-23). Gave: the test proves knowledge of the Constitution and of Spanish social and cultural reality, a requirement for nationality by residence; 2026 sittings on 29 October and 26 November; registration for 2027 opens 23 November 2026; the free app "CCSE Nacionalidad Española".
- Ley 19/2015, de 13 de julio, disposición final séptima (BOE-A-2015-7851): https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-7851 (checked 2026-11-23).

## Copyright check (builder, 2026-11-23)

The Manual CCSE 2026 publishes the complete official item pool. The builder extracted its 300 items (297 parsed: stems and the three or two options, true/false items included) and compared every one of the 200 Tigertest items with all of them by script: normalised stem similarity (difflib ratio), content-word overlap (Jaccard), shared options (normalised, leading articles removed) and "same key with overlapping stem words"; then read every flagged pair by hand. The first draft had 70 flags. Real matches were rewritten with a different fact or angle and new distractors: identical stems (river into the Atlantic, where the tarjeta sanitaria is issued, the TIE for foreign residents), same-fact items (euskera in the País Vasco, the teaching of co-official languages, the Defensor del Pueblo depending on the Cortes, the true/false items on torture, free compulsory schooling, decent housing, the environment, duties in a catastrophe, the right to choose where to live, academic freedom and the limits of free speech, Spain importing oil, the Aragón provinces) and every item that shared two or more options with one official item (police bodies, museums, composers, Madrid/Valencia/Sevilla city lists, age lists, Pirineos/Sierra Nevada, climates, vocational titles). After the rewrite no Tigertest item shares two options with any official item; the 46 remaining flags are question frames only ("¿Cuál es la capital de…?", "¿En qué comunidad autónoma está…?") asking a different fact from the official item, which the topic list makes unavoidable. The real Tarea 2 is true/false and the other tareas have three options; every Tigertest item has four options.

## Left out

- No officeholders (the current king and president are not asked; historical presidents to 1982 are), no population, unemployment, tourism or GDP figures, no fees (the 2026 registration fee is not asked), no current affairs.
- The Manual's statement that the minimum marriage age is 16 (article 32 as summarised there) is not used, since the Civil Code requires 18 or emancipation.
- No question about the test itself.
