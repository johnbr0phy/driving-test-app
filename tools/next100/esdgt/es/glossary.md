# esdgt Spanish (es-ES) glossary

Terms as the Reglamento General de Circulación (RGC), the Reglamento General de Conductores and
the DGT use them. Questions in "usted"; app copy and landing in "tú" (house UI register).
Decimal comma (1,5 m; 0,25 mg/l), thousands with a point as the DGT writes them (3.500 kg),
km/h, metros. No em or en dashes.
