# Sources for `esdgt` (Spain DGT theory exam, permiso B, in English)

All 200 questions are original, written from the Spanish regulations below. No DGT test, past
paper or commercial practice app item was copied (practice sites appeared in search results and
were used only for the exam format, labelled below). Signs are described in words. Checked
2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam and its format
- DGT, "Requisitos, preparación y presentación a examen" (read 2026-11-01): the practice tests
  are "similar" to the real exam in format and number of questions; you may sit your first exam
  from three months before reaching the minimum age; a passed test is kept for two years; the fee
  gives two sittings (no figure used).
  https://www.dgt.es/nuestros-servicios/permisos-de-conducir/obtener-un-nuevo-permiso-de-conducir/requisitos-preparacion-y-presentacion-a-examen/
- DGT application form Mod. 04 (Solicitud pruebas de aptitud, PDF, read 2026-11-01): theory test
  language "Castellano, lenguas cooficiales, inglés, francés y alemán: A1, A2, B y RPV.
  'Lectura fácil': A1, A2 y B." Co-official languages: Catalan (Cataluña, Illes Balears), Basque
  (País Vasco, Navarra), Galician (Galicia), Valencian (Comunitat Valenciana).
  https://sede.dgt.gob.es/export/sites/dgt/.galleries/modelos-solicitud/04/Mod.04-ES.pdf
- DGT magazine Tráfico y Seguridad Vial, test supplement (undated, read via search 2026-11-01):
  "el examen teórico consta de 30 preguntas y se aprueba hasta con 3 fallos".
  https://revista.dgt.es/Galerias/hemeroteca/revista/N-240-SUP-ESP-TEST-WOP.pdf
- Infobae, 28 August 2026 (citing the DGT to El Mundo): the theory exam does not change on
  1 October 2026 ("Por ahora sigue todo igual"); 30 multiple-choice questions, maximum 30
  minutes, up to three mistakes; risk-perception videos are being studied with no date and would
  need an amendment to the Reglamento General de Conductores.
  https://www.infobae.com/espana/2026/08/28/la-dgt-desmiente-que-el-examen-teorico-del-carne-de-conducir-cambie-el-proximo-1-de-octubre-ni-preguntas-de-desarrollo-ni-videos-de-percepcion-del-riesgo/
- Three answer options per question and the 30-minute limit: guides only (drivek.es,
  traficotest.es, examencarnet.com via search, 2026-11-01), consistent with Infobae.
  https://www.drivek.es/guias/permiso-b-en-espana-como-funciona-el-examen-teorico-y-practico/

Used in the bank and landing: 30 questions, 30 minutes, pass with at most 3 mistakes (27 of 30),
three options per question; offered in Spanish, the co-official languages, English, French and
German, plus an Easy Read version in Spanish. Tigertest's tests use four options.

## Law and rules (BOE consolidated texts, read 2026-11-01)
- Reglamento General de Circulación (RD 1428/2003, consolidated text including RD 518/2026):
  art. 18 (phones, headphones, radar detectors), 20 (alcohol: 0.5 g/l blood / 0.25 mg/l breath;
  0.3 / 0.15 for the two years after the first licence), 31-35 (lane use, emergency lanes),
  48-50 (speed limits: 120/90 for cars, 100 on separated conventional roads if signed, 90/80 with
  trailer, 30 km/h unpaved; urban 20/30/50, travesía 50, urban motorway 80), 49 (minimum 60 on
  motorways, half the generic limit elsewhere; hazard lights if below it), 51 (overtaking
  allowance repealed), 52, 54 (following distance), 57 (unsigned junctions), 59 (blocking
  junctions), 60 (narrow stretches: first in), 62-63 (reversing order; 7% gradients), 64-66
  (cyclists, pedestrians, animals), 67-69 (priority vehicles), 72 (joining), 77 (deceleration
  lane), 79 (U-turns), 82-88 (overtaking), 90-94 (stopping and parking), 95 (tunnels: 100 m or
  4 s; 150 m or 6 s over 3,500 kg), 96 (level crossings), 100, 104, 106, 107 (lights), 110 (horn),
  114-115 (doors; engine off in tunnels after two minutes), 117 (belts, children 135 cm or less),
  118 (helmets, vests), 130 (breakdowns), 133 (order of precedence of signals), 145 (regulatory
  signs), annex I (officers' signals, traffic lights, lane signals, markings: yellow lines,
  zigzag, yellow grid, hatched areas).
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-23514
  PDF read: https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2003/BOE-A-2003-23514-consolidado.pdf
- Real Decreto 518/2026, de 24 de junio (BOE 26 June 2026, in force 1 October 2026): overtaking
  cyclists, pedestrians, mopeds and VMPs outside towns at least 20 km/h below the limit with
  1.5 m, and a full lane change where there is more than one lane per direction; passing a stopped
  vehicle 1.5 m and 20 km/h below the limit; emergency lane on motorways (middle with two lanes,
  between the leftmost two with three or more); snow: no overtaking, right lane; 5 m behind
  cyclists in town; cyclists preferably in the centre of the lane; VMP riders 15+, helmet.
  https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-13889
- Reglamento General de Conductores (RD 818/2009, consolidated): art. 4 (permiso B: 3,500 kg,
  eight passengers plus driver, trailer up to 750 kg, age 18; A1 motorcycles in Spain after more
  than three years), art. 12 (validity ten years under 65, five from 65), art. 47 and annex V
  (theory exam syllabus).
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2009/BOE-A-2009-9481-consolidado.pdf
- Novice plate V-13 (white L on green, first year): Reglamento General de Vehículos annex XI as
  described by guides (autofacil.es, learndrivingtheory.com via search); colour and one-year rule
  consistent across them. https://www.autofacil.es/multa-no-llevar-l/
- V-16 connected beacon the only valid way to signal a stopped vehicle from 1 January 2026:
  DGT press release 25 November 2025 and Ministry of the Interior note.
  https://www.dgt.es/export/sites/web-DGT/.galleries/downloads/nota_prensa/2025/20251125.pdf
  https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/es/detalle/articulo/DGT-recuerda-la-obligacion-de-disponer-de-la-baliza-V-16-para-senalizar-averias-y-accidentes-en-carretera/
- Alcohol limit unchanged: the proposal to lower the general limit to 0.2 g/l was rejected in
  the Congress on 18 March 2026 (guides via search, e.g. misfaltasdeconducir.com); RGC art. 20 as
  consolidated still reads 0.5/0.25 and 0.3/0.15. Not asked as a news item.
- ITV intervals for private cars (first at four years, every two years to ten, then yearly):
  Real Decreto 920/2017, as summarised by the DGT and ITV operators (not re-read in full).
- Fatigue advice (break every two hours or 200 km) and the PAS sequence (Proteger, Avisar,
  Socorrer): standard DGT road-safety guidance, labelled as advice in the explanations.

## Deliberately left out
Fines, points and fees; the 2025 EU driving licence directive (not yet transposed); trailer
combinations above 750 kg (rules differ by combination mass and authorisation); risk-perception
video questions (not in the exam as of November 2026); the exact novice plate size.

## Review pass (2026-11-01)
Every rule re-checked against the BOE consolidated RGC (including RD 518/2026, arts 32, 85.4,
88, 118, 155) and RGCond: speed limits (art 48 table incl. 100 km/h on signed separated
conventional roads, art 50 urban 20/30/50 with reserved lanes not counted), art 62 reversing
order, art 63 steep stretches (the uphill vehicle yields only if *it* can reach a passing place
first), art 65 pedestrian priority, art 72.4 (check at the start of the acceleration lane), art 77,
art 86.2 (the overtaken driver must slow if the overtake turns dangerous), art 96, art 101/104
(motorcycles dipped beam by day), art 115 (engine off after two minutes in a tunnel), art 123
(pedestrians at night: light or reflector visible at 150 m), annex I 1.1-1.2 (officers' signals,
whistle, flags, arm angled down from a vehicle). Exam format (30 questions, three options,
30 minutes, up to three mistakes) consistent across guides found by search on 2026-11-01; dgt.es
itself states only that its practice tests match the real exam's format and length.
