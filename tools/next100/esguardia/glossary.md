# Glossary for `esguardia` (Spanish, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations in "usted" forms or impersonal (the BOE style); landing and
UI copy in "tú", like the esdgt Spanish translation. Spanish typography: opening ¿ and ¡, « »
for quotes (none needed in the bank), decimal comma (0,05), thousands point (3.240, 1:50.000),
space before % (5 %), ordinals 1.º and 149.1.29.ª. No em-dashes, no straight double quotes (the
build asserts it).

Laws are cited by their official short name and number the first time in an explanation
("Ley Orgánica 2/1986", "Ley 39/2015"); article numbers only where the article has been stable
for years. No fines, fees, sanction amounts or contraband value thresholds. No officeholders;
historical figures only in explanations (Isabel II for the 1844 founding, the Duque de Ahumada in the name of the Colegio de Guardias Jóvenes).
