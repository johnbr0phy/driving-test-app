copy in "tú" (consumer-app norm, as esdgt). Spanish typography: ¿? and ¡!, « » for quoted words,
decimal comma, thousands point (2.704), a space before % (40 %), km/h, h. No em-dashes.
Laws are named in full once per item ("la Ley Orgánica 4/2015"); article numbers only where checked.
