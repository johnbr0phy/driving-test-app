# Sources for `esvigilante` (Spain private security guard exam, vigilante de seguridad, in Spanish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Spanish from the laws and the programme
named in the official bases. Nothing was copied from a past exam, an official answer template,
an academy's test bank or a commercial app. Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed from the BOE)
- Resolución de 9 de enero de 2026, de la Secretaría de Estado de Seguridad, por la que se
  aprueban las bases de las convocatorias de pruebas de selección para vigilante de seguridad y
  sus especialidades (vigilante de explosivos) y escolta privado para el año 2026 (BOE núm. 12,
  14 de enero de 2026), BOE-A-2026-813, read 2026-11-06:
  https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-813
  (PDF: https://boe.es/boe/dias/2026/01/14/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-813.pdf)
  - Three convocatorias in 2026: 01/2026 (ten working days from publication), 02/2026
    (applications 1 to 16 April 2026), 03/2026 (applications 1 to 14 September 2026).
  - Prueba de conocimientos (vigilante de seguridad): a written cuestionario of 100 questions with
    a stem and three alternatives, only one true, in 50 minutes, on the programme of Anexo III;
    optical reading (blue or black pen). Scored with [A - E/(n-1)] x 10/P, n = 3, so each wrong
    answer cancels half a right one; blanks score nothing; 0 to 10, minimum 5 to pass.
    Answering all 100, a 5 needs 67 right (66.7 rounded up). Tigertest's passPct 67 mirrors that.
  - Pruebas de aptitud física: eliminatory, apto / no apto, sports clothing and an original
    medical certificate issued within the application period; upper-body power (pull-ups for
    men, 3 kg medicine-ball throw for women and men from 40), vertical jump, 400 m run; marks by
    age and sex in Anexo II. Not modelled.
  - Vigilante de explosivos: separate 25-question test in 15 minutes (Anexo V). Escolta privado:
    the same 100-question test plus 25 questions in 15 minutes (Anexo IV); serving vigilantes are
    exempt from the physical test and the first questionnaire. Not modelled.
  - Requisitos: mayoría de edad; EU/EEA nationality or a covering international agreement; ESO
    graduate, Técnico or equivalent; psychophysical fitness; no criminal record for intentional
    offences; diploma of the curso de formación previa (vigilante: at least 180 hours and six
    teaching weeks; explosivos 30 hours; escolta 60 hours).
  - Venues listed: Baleares, Cádiz, Granada, Madrid, Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Santiago de
    Compostela, Tarragona, Valencia, Zaragoza (the tribunal may change them). The Policía
    Nacional's Unidad Central de Seguridad Privada runs the tests; applications through the
    Policía Nacional's applicant portal (policia.es).
  - Languages: the bases mention no exam in another language; documents in other languages must
    be translated into Spanish. The task's research note said Catalonia has its own sittings: no
    official Catalan call for vigilantes was found (the Generalitat runs a separate control
    d'accés test, PCA/02/2026), and Tarragona is a venue of the national call. So the copy does
    not claim Catalan sittings or a Catalan-language exam.
  - Anexo III (programa de formación previa común, 57 temas): área jurídica (Derecho
    constitucional 2, penal 9, procesal penal 4, administrativo especial 4, laboral 3, práctica
    jurídica 2), área socio-profesional (5), área técnico-profesional (14), área instrumental
    (14). Tigertest's eight categories follow these areas; the 4/8/5/8/3/5/9/8 split is ours.
- Ministry of the Interior: the Acuerdo de 10 de septiembre de 2026 for convocatoria 02/2026
  (interior.gob.es .../Convocatoria-2_2026/Acuerdo_10_septiembre_2026.pdf) returned 403 to the
  checker; not used.
- Redopositor guide to the 2026 vigilante calls (secondary, matches the BOE: 100 questions in
  50 minutes, nine venues): https://redopositor.com/blog/es-oposiciones-proves-seleccio-per-vigilant-seguretat-ministerio-del-interior-2026/

## Law used for the questions
- Ley 5/2014, de 4 de abril, de Seguridad Privada, consolidated text (last modification
  27/05/2021), read 2026-11-06: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-3649
  Articles used: 4 (fines), 5 (actividades; investigación privada only by despachos de
  detectives), 8 (principios rectores: no intervention in meetings, demonstrations or political
  or labour conflicts; no control of opinions; client data only to judicial and police
  authorities; strike and essential services), 14 (colaboración), 21.1.d (companies guarantee their staff's training and updating), 26 (personal; vigilante de
  explosivos needs prior vigilante habilitación), 27 (habilitación, TIP, Dirección General de la
  Policía; guardas rurales, Guardia Civil), 28 (requisitos generales), 29 (formación at
  accredited centres), 30 (principios de
  actuación), 31 (protección jurídica), 32 (funciones de los vigilantes: controls without
  retaining documents, refusal allows denying entry, detention and immediate handover, no
  interrogation but noting personal data, exclusive dedication), 33 (escoltas), 34 (guardas
  rurales), 40 (servicios con armas; arms only on duty), 41 (services outside buildings, with
  or without prior authorisation), 42 (videovigilancia), 56 (prescription: leves 6 months,
  graves 1 year, muy graves 2 years), 58 (infracciones del personal; trato incorrecto leve),
  62 (sanctions; leve: apercibimiento or multa). No fine amounts in the bank.
- Real Decreto 2364/1994, Reglamento de Seguridad Privada (in force insofar as it does not
  contradict Ley 5/2014; a new regulation is still pending as of November 2026), read
  2026-11-06: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-608
  Articles used: 71 to 72 (functions, checking systems on taking over the post), 76 (handing over
  suspects and evidence), 82 (no arms outside service; armero), 83 (loss or theft reported to the
  police at once), 87 (uniform not confusable with police or military). Weapon calibre, firing
  practice periodicity and psychotechnical test periodicity were left out (later Orders may have
  changed them).
- Reglamento de Armas, Real Decreto 137/1993 (licencia C for personal de seguridad privada):
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1993-6202
- Constitución Española de 1978: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1978-31229
  (arts. 10.1, 14, 15, 16.2, 17.3, 18.3, 18.4, 19, 21, 22.5, 24.2, 104).
- Código Penal, LO 10/1995: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-25444
  (arts. 5, 11, 12, 13, 20.4 to 20.6, 21.5, 26, 29, 139, 148, 163.4, 169, 195, 197, 202, 203,
  205, 234, 253, 263, 298, 386, 402, 403, 450, 457, 512).
- Ley de Enjuiciamiento Criminal: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1882-6036
  (arts. 262, 265, 268, 490, 491, 496, 795; "investigado" since LO 13/2015).
- Ley Orgánica 3/2018, de Protección de Datos Personales, art. 22 (videovigilancia: deletion
  within one month, information sign): https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-16673
- Estatuto de los Trabajadores, RDL 2/2015 (arts. 14, 34.3, 37.1, 38):
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11430
- Ley 31/1995 de Prevención de Riesgos Laborales (arts. 16, 19, 35):
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1995-24292
- Real Decreto 393/2007, Norma Básica de Autoprotección:
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-6237
- UNE-EN 2 fire classes (A, B, C, D, F); first aid per the European Resuscitation Council

- Banco de España / BCE euro banknote checks («toque, mire y gire»):

## Lane and overlap
Written alongside `esguardia` and `espolicia` (same Constitution, criminal law and procedure).
Their banks on disk were grepped on 2026-11-06 and every shared fact-and-answer pair was rewritten
(art. 15 and art. 104 asked from other angles; no 72 hours, habeas corpus, amparo, art. 10 CP,
tentativa, agresión ilegítima, coacciones, robo vs hurto, art. 259 LECrim, Parliament elections,
Commission initiative or PRL art. 21). Kept clear in substance of the English security banks
(`security`, `uksia`, `iepsa`, `aesira`, `zapsira`, `caguard`): this bank is Spanish law and
Spanish practice.
