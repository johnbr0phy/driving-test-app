# Sources for `fihunt` (Finnish hunter's examination, metsästäjätutkinto, in Finnish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Finnish from the laws below and the
published topic list. Suomen riistakeskus keeps its exam questions and picture booklet
confidential and forbids using them as training material; none were seen or copied, and no
commercial quiz app's items were used. Species are described in words. Checked 2026-11-06; format re-read from the 2026 instruction PDF
and law facts (14 vrk, 39 cm, puoli tuntia, 1 000 ha, 7 vrk, 37 § as amended from 1.9.2026, 48 §,
metsästysasetus 5 a, 7, 9, 11, 16, 16 a, 17, 20, 22 §) re-checked against Finlex on 2026-11-07.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- Suomen riistakeskus, "Ohje: metsästäjätutkinto" to the riistanhoitoyhdistykset, dated
  1.1.2026 (asia nro 336/02.02.01.02/2025), read 2026-11-06:
  https://riista.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/OHJE_metsastajatutkinto_2026.pdf
  - 3: the exam must be passed before paying riistanhoitomaksu for the first time (laki
    616/1993 2 §); exempt: anyone who paid the fee in one of the five previous hunting years,
    Finnish citizens entitled to hunt in Åland, foreigners entitled to hunt in their own country.
  - 4: only riistanhoitoyhdistykset hold exam sessions; advance registration compulsory from
    1.1.2026.
  - 5: the optional preparatory course is recommended at 12 hours, usually over 3 to 4
    evenings; topics: metsästyslainsäädäntö, lajintuntemus, riistaekologia ja riistanhoito,
    eettinen ja kestävä metsästys, aseet ja patruunat, metsästyksen turvallisuus,
    pyyntivälineet ja -menetelmät, saaliin käsittely. Textbook: Metsästäjän opas (riistakeskus).
  - 6.1: written (paper) or electronic exam with 15 species-identification questions
    (lajitunnistuskysymystä) and 45 theory questions, based on Metsästäjän opas and current law.
  - 6.3.1: "Jokaisella halukkaalla on oikeus osallistua kokeeseen" (no age limit stated);
    dyslexia: oral exam; interpreter for those who know no Finnish, Swedish or Sámi.
  - 6.3.2 and 6.3.3: at most 1.5 hours; the species part comes first and lasts 15 minutes,
    after which the pictures are removed; the rest of the time is for the theory part; from
    1.1.2026 the exam can be taken in Finnish, Swedish, English or Sámi; the electronic exam
    runs on the candidate's own phone, tablet or computer (tutkinto.riista.fi).
  - 6.3.4: unanswered questions and questions with extra marks count as wrong.
  - 6.3.5: passed with 8 or fewer wrong answers, failed with 9 or more; minimum 52 points of 60.
  - Appendix, MMMa 1160/2014 2 §: the eight topic areas (used as the categories here); 3 §:
    60 multiple-choice questions written by Suomen riistakeskus, at most 8 wrong to pass.
- Suomen riistakeskus, "Metsästäjätutkinto" page, read 2026-11-06:

## Rules of thumb used (Metsästäjän opas teaching, not law)
- Shot danger distance in metres is about 100 x the pellet diameter in mm (3 mm: about 300 m).
- Effective shotgun range on game about 30 to 35 m; a big-game bullet can travel several km;
  a .22 LR bullet more than a kilometre.

## Left out on purpose
- Hunting season dates (they are in the decree and change; asked nowhere), quotas, permit
  numbers, fees (riistanhoitomaksu, exam fee), fines. Wolf, bear and seal quota rules.
- Anything about the exam itself (question count, pass mark, time, languages).
