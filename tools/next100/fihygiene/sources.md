# Sources: Finland Hygiene Passport test (fihygiene)

All checked 2026-11-06; the format on https://hygieniapassi.fi/en/hygieniapassitesti/English re-confirmed
2026-11-07 (40 true/false statements, 34/40 to pass, 45 minutes, Finnish, Swedish or English, translated tests on demand). Items are original: Ruokavirasto's model test questions were not opened for
writing and nothing was copied from them, from examiners' courses or from mock-test sites.

## Format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

- https://hygieniapassi.fi/english and https://hygieniapassi.fi/en/hygieniapassitesti/English
  (examiners' information site, footer names Osaamistehdas Oy): 40 true/false statements, at least
  34/40 to pass; 45 minutes in a normal test (Finnish, Swedish, English); special situation tests
  (interpreter, spoken test, other languages, dictionary) have no time limit and are arranged with
  the examiner in advance; may leave 20 minutes after the start; bring valid official photo ID;
  needed within three months of starting such work; passports issued abroad are not valid in
  Finland; passport posted by Ruokavirasto about 4 to 6 weeks after the test (page as read 2026-11-07; earlier read said 2 to 5 weeks; not used on the landing); reduced-cost renewal test after a fail
  (no fee figures used).
- Ruokavirasto regulation 7/2021 on hygiene proficiency (Livsmedelsverkets föreskrift om
  hygienkompetens, 19.4.2021, in force 21.4.2021, replaced 5/2020):
  https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/henkiloasiakkaat/hygieniapassi/maarays-hygos-ja-osaamist.-toiminnasta/livsmedelsverkets_foreskrift_om_hygienkompetens_7_2021_21_4_2021.pdf
  Section 2: 40 statements, true or false, pass at least 34 correct; 45 minutes for a normal test,
  no upper limit for a special situation test; may leave 20 minutes after the start; ballpoint
  pen, not pencil. Section 3: only Ruokavirasto-approved examiners; statements from Ruokavirasto's
  question bank (Kysymyspankki); unanswered or unclear answers count as wrong; result within one
  month. Section 4: test forms in Finnish, Swedish and/or English; special situation test notified
  to Ruokavirasto 7 days ahead; examiner translates statements in writing for other languages.
  Section 5: passport is an A4 certificate plus a bank-card-sized plastic card.
  Annex 1, the six areas (used as the blueprint): 1 basic microbiology and contamination
  (what microbes are, harm, useful microbes, temperature, acidity, moisture, oxygen,
  composition, chemical and physical contamination, easily perishable food); 2 food poisoning and
  hygienic working methods (handling and storing raw materials, semi-finished and finished
  products; preparing, heating and cooling; avoiding later contamination); 3 personal hygiene;
  4 cleaning and waste; 5 self-monitoring; 6 legislation and authorities.
- Ruokavirasto, Hygiene Passport page: https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/en/foodstuffs/hygiene-passport/
  (needed for unpackaged perishable food on food premises; customer service).
- Ruokavirasto news 26.3.2025, model test questions:
  https://ruokavirasto.fi/en/foodstuffs/hygiene-passport/uutiset/do-you-need-a-hygiene-passport--practice-with-new-model-test-questions
  (40-statement model test in Finnish, Swedish, English; areas microbes, food poisoning, hygienic
  work methods, personal hygiene, sanitation, own-checks, legislation; "approximately 10 000 test
  sessions" and "an average of approximately 65 000 Hygiene Passports" a year. Not quoted on the
  page as a cohort, only as Ruokavirasto's own figure in examFacts.)

- Ruokavirasto, The Hygiene Passport: information for food business operators and control
  authorities (dated 11.9.2026), read 2026-11-07 by the reviewer:
  https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/elintarvikkeet/hygieniapassi/the-hygiene-passport-information-for-food-business-operators-and-control-authorities.pdf
  Passport required once the employee has worked three months; the period includes similar work
  done earlier (Food Act s. 19); foreign hygiene passports, photocopies and food sector
  qualification certificates do not prove proficiency; valid until further notice; operator keeps
  records and presents them to the control authority. Confirms the landing FAQ, LAW-004 and LAW-006.

## Law and figures used in the bank

- Food Act 297/2021, consolidated (Finlex open data):
  https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2021/297/fin@
  s. 15 self-monitoring (system to identify and control hazards, results recorded); s. 16 latest
  inspection report on the website or similar and at the entrance where consumers visit; s. 19
  Hygiene Passport for people handling unpackaged easily perishable food once they have worked
  at least three months in such tasks, exemptions, operator keeps a record of staff competence
  and shows it on request, Ruokavirasto prepares the test, examiners hold it and issue the
  passport; s. 20 examiners approved by Ruokavirasto.
- Decree 318/2021 on food hygiene (MMM), consolidated:
  https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2021/318/fin@
  s. 4 registration notification (incl. description of self-monitoring, floor plan); s. 22 records
  kept at least one year after use-by / best-before or after handling, traceability at least one
  year; s. 23 temperatures for registered businesses: milk and cream 6 °C; pasteurised dairy
  products 8 °C; other perishable dairy 6 °C; fresh packed fish, vacuum/MAP processed fish,
  cold-smoked and fresh-salted fish, cooked crustaceans and molluscs, thawed unprocessed fish
  0 to 3 °C; other processed fish, kalakukko, sushi 6 °C; live bivalves 6 °C; minced meat and
  minced liver 4 °C; pasteurised juices 8 °C; other perishables incl. sprouts, cut vegetables,
  insects 6 °C; cold display once only, up to 12 °C for at most 4 hours; hot display at least
  60 °C, once only; heat-prepared bakery products delivered at room temperature may be kept at
  room temperature on the day of receipt; cooling starts immediately and reaches storage
  temperature within 4 hours; mixed chilled loads 6 °C; to consumers chilled 6 °C, hot 60 °C.
- Ruokavirasto, Ravintolatoiminnan aloittaminen (2025 restaurant guide):
  https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/elintarvikkeet/elintarvikeala/elintarvikeyrityksen-perustaminen-ja-ov/opas_ravintolatoiminnan_aloittaminen_fi_2025.pdf
  and its staff part
  https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/elintarvikkeet/elintarvikeala/elintarvikeyrityksen-perustaminen-ja-ov/opas_ravintolatoiminnan_aloittaminen_fi_2025_henkilokunta.pdf
  Separation of ready-to-eat food, raw poultry and soil-covered roots; cold water for food;
  staff food on its own shelf; fresh unpackaged fish up to 2 °C (example, not used); dishwasher
  wash 60 to 65 °C and rinse 80 to 85 °C; plastic boards, worn boards sanded or discarded, wood
  for dry foods; cleaning agents away from food; no cleaning tools in toilets; labelled cleaning
  tools; not on the floor, do not overfill cold rooms; assistance dogs in customer areas, pets at
  the restaurant's choice with a notice; cup and fork symbol; fat, heat, acid increase migration;
  bear, wild boar and seal need meat inspection; uninspected poultry or rabbit from the producer;
  acrylamide: fry below 175 °C, store unpeeled potatoes above 6 °C; 14 allergens of Annex II of
  Regulation (EU) 1169/2011; written or oral allergen information; country of origin of meat;
  delivery rules; self-monitoring stages; medium burgers as a risk dish; Oiva report in
  four smileys; deviation and corrective action; freezer space for samples. Staff part: passport
  within three months; stomach illness keeps a worker off food; gloves at least with artificial
  nails, jewellery or wounds; short nails; avoid jewellery; false eyelashes not recommended;
  piercings; protective clothing (work outfit, head covering, work shoes), change or cover when
  going outside, may be washed at home; tongs at a cash desk; six handwashing moments; plaster
  and glove on a cut. (The salmonella health statement in this part is not asked: a 2026 draft
  would remove the Communicable Diseases Act provision.)
- Ruokavirasto restaurant self-monitoring sheets:
  https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/tietoa-meista/asiointi/oppaat-ja-lomakkeet/yritykset/elintarvikeala/elintarvikehuoneistot/omavalvonta-fin-8-kypsentaminen.pdf
  (core 70 °C, poultry 75 °C, reheating over 70 °C, hot at least 60 °C, frying below 175 °C);
  omavalvonta-fin-2-jaahdytys.pdf (cool within 4 h below 6 °C, slow cooling the most common cause
  of food poisoning, measure at the centre at start and end, never outside or on a windowsill);
  jaahdyttaminen.pdf (danger zone 6 to 60 °C, measure the food not the cabinet, ice water bath or
  cold room for small amounts); omavalvonta-fin-5-lampotilojen-tarkast_muokattu-14052021.pdf
  (measure chilled and frozen goods on arrival, cold food up to 12 °C for 4 h);
  omavalvonta-fin-10-kirjanpito.pdf (what records are for).
- Ruokavirasto news 4.11.2024, updated Oiva reports:
  https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/en/foodstuffs/instructions-for-consumers/news/discover-the-updated-oiva-reports/
  (four smileys: excellent, good, to be corrected, poor; results on Oivahymy).
- Imported frozen berries: Ruokavirasto's advice to heat them before use, as reported by Kaleva
  (https://www.kaleva.fi/lidlin-myymissa-pakastevadelmissa-salmonellaa-tuot/6099237). No time
  or temperature figure used.
- Thermometer check against melting ice and boiling water: municipal self-monitoring templates
  (Tampere); worded as "often" in the bank.
- General microbiology (spores, toxins, listeria, norovirus, Campylobacter, fish tapeworm, EHEC):
  standard food microbiology, worded without figures beyond the decree's.

## Not used

Fees, test prices, cohort sizes as demand claims, the passport's validity period (not stated by
the sources read), the salmonella health statement (pending legal change), any examiner or course
provider name in the bank. UK FSA figures (8 °C, 63 °C, 75 °C for 30 s) are deliberately absent.
