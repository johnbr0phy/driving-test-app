# Sources for `fimoto` (Finland motorcycle theory test, categories A1, A2 and A)

All 200 questions are original. Commercial practice sites and driving school pages
(open-exam-prep.com, cap.fi and similar) appeared in search results; only format summaries were
read, never their questions, and nothing in the bank comes from them. Traffic picture and risk
recognition items of the real test are written here as situations in words (no images).
Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted. Written beside `fitheory` (category B) and checked against its
stems: this bank keeps to rider-specific rules and avoids repeating its fact-and-answer pairs.

## Format of the real test (official)
- Traficom regulation "Kuljettajantutkinnon teoriakoemääräys", TRAFICOM/523973/03.04.03.00/2019,
  issued 10.6.2020, in force 15.6.2020 until further notice. PDF downloaded and read in full
  2026-11-06:
  https://www.traficom.fi/sites/default/files/media/regulation/Kuljettajantutkinnon%20teoriakoem%C3%A4%C3%A4r%C3%A4ys.pdf
  - Electronic test, "Koekohtainen vastausaika on 30 minuuttia" (30 minutes per test).
  - Oral test with 60 minutes if the mother tongue is not Finnish, Swedish or English and the
    candidate masters none of them (and other listed grounds); interpreter rules for small
    language groups.
  - Categories A1, A2 and A: 20 verbal multiple choice questions (14 to pass), 50 picture
    questions on traffic situations (43 to pass), 5 risk recognition questions (4 to pass).
    "Jos oikeiden vastausten lukumäärä alittaa jossakin kokeen osassa vaaditun määrän ...
    teoriakoe hylätään": below the minimum in any part fails the test.
  - Multiple choice topic areas for AM, T, A1, A2, A, B, BE: traffic rules; the driver; the road;
    other road users; general rules and other matters; precautions when leaving the vehicle;
    mechanical aspects of road safety; vehicle safety equipment; vehicle use and the environment.
  - Extra areas for A1, A2 and A: use of protective gear; visibility of motorcyclists to other
    road users; hazards linked to road conditions; mechanical aspects of the motorcycle that
    affect road safety. The blueprint here follows these areas (Traficom publishes no weights,
    so the split is Tigertest's own).
  - Picture question areas, all categories: driving straight, right, left; giving way when
    driving straight, right, left; overtaking; changing lanes; stopping; parking.
- Traficom, "Driving examination" (English), fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/driving-examination
  (also served at https://static.traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/driving-examination)
  - Table: "A1, A2, A | 20 (14) | 50 (43) | 5 (4)"; "The theory test takes 30 minutes."
  - Oral test read aloud "in the language of the test (Finnish, Swedish or English)".
  - "A passed theory test is valid for one year."
  - Motorcycle categories: a handling test, taken separately before the driving test (valid one
    year once passed), then the driving test (initial discussion, driving in traffic, final
    discussion; 60 minutes reserved).
- Third-party guide (format only, not used for questions), consistent with Traficom's 20
  multiple choice questions: https://open-exam-prep.com/practice/fi-teoriakoe-a2
- Booking and running of tests: Ajovarma, the service of A-Katsastus for Traficom
  (https://a-katsastus.fi/en/driving-licences-and-permits/what-happens-during-the-theory-test,
  read for fitheory 2026-11-01; booking https://varaus.ajovarma.fi/en).

## Licence categories and upgrades (official)
- Traficom, "Getting a motorcycle driving licence", fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/getting-motorcycle-driving-licence
  - A1: age 16; two-wheeled motorcycles without a sidecar, up to 125 cm3 and 11 kW, power to
    weight at most 0.1 kW/kg.
  - A2: age 18; over 125 cm3, over 11 kW and at most 35 kW, power to weight over 0.1 and at most
    0.2 kW/kg, not modified from a vehicle with over 70 kW.
  - A: age 24, or 20 after holding A2 for at least two years; over 35 kW.
  - Steps: licence permit, EAS theory lessons for a first licence, driving lessons, theory test,
    handling test, driving test. Protective gear at the test: fastened helmet, riding trousers,
    jacket, gloves and boots (the bank asks only the legal helmet duty; the rest is advice).
  - Not used in the bank (durations that may change): the three-month training licence, the
    six-month examination certificate, the 15-year card validity, lesson counts (the page itself
    gives two different lesson counts).
- Traficom, "Upgrading a motorcycle driving licence category", fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://www.traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/upgrading-motorcycle-driving-licence-category
  - A1 to A2 at 18 after 2 years on A1, by driving instruction or by handling and driving test;
    A2 to A at 20 after 2 years on A2, the same two routes; or a full examination; A1 directly to
    A at 24 without two years on A2.

## Rules of the road (official, current text)
- Tieliikennelaki (Road Traffic Act) 729/2018, consolidated text from the Finlex open data API,
  dateConsolidated 2026-04-17, downloaded 2026-11-06:
  https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2018/729/fin@
  (human page: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2018/729). Facts used, by section:
  - 7 § help at an accident; 8 § police signals first; 10 § emergency vehicles.
  - 9 § 50 km/h in a taajama, 80 km/h outside unless signed (used only inside speed-choice items).
  - 17 § no driving when illness, fatigue, intoxication etc. remove the ability to drive.
  - 18 § vehicles use the carriageway; mopeds ride on the shoulder or road edge outside built-up
    areas (not motorcycles).
  - 19 § keep as far right as safely possible; rightmost lane on multi-lane roads; no needless
    lane changes. 20 § following distance. 21 § turning right: position at the right edge;
    turning left: just right of the centre line.
  - 24 § right-hand rule; turning vehicles yield to crossing pedestrians, cyclists and moped
    riders, to oncoming traffic when turning left, to pedestrians, cyclists and moped riders on
    the carriageway or shoulder when leaving the carriageway, and when leaving a roundabout;
    always yield coming from a pihakatu, kävelykatu or snowmobile route, a yard, car park, fuel
    station or off the road. 26 § give way to a bus leaving a stop where the limit is 60 km/h or
    less (same or adjacent lane). 27 § pedestrian crossings: speed to stop before it; do not pass
    a vehicle stopped before a crossing without stopping unless an island or free lane is between.
    28 § do not enter a junction where you may have to stop in it. 29 § safe space for pedestrians,
    cyclists and animals; special care near stopped school transport, buses, trams.
  - 30 § meeting; 31 § overtake on the left; on the right only a vehicle turning left, in
    parallel lanes, or as a cyclist ("polkupyöräilijä saa ohittaa muun ajoneuvon kuin polkupyörän
    oikealta": only cyclists may pass other vehicles on the right). 32 § overtaking bans (crossing,
    junction, level crossing; poor view; vehicle ahead signals to overtake; someone behind has
    started). 33 § safe gap when overtaking; the overtaken vehicle keeps right and must not speed
    up; slow drivers help others pass.
  - 35 § lateral moves only when safe. 36 § stop/park on the right, parallel and close to the edge.
    37 § "Polkupyörän tai mopon saa pysäyttää tai pysäköidä jalkakäytävälle ja pyörätielle":
    only bicycles and mopeds may park on a pavement or cycle path; other vehicles only a short,
    compelled stop. 38 § no parking beside a vehicle parked along the edge "muun ajoneuvon kuin
    kaksipyöräisen polkupyörän tai mopon": the exception is only for bicycles and mopeds;
    no parking within 30 m of a level crossing. 41 § secure the vehicle.
  - 44-46 § tunnels: no reversing or U-turn.
  - 47 § horn; 48 § direction signals (also when leaving a roundabout and moving sideways, kept
    on for the whole move); 49 § headlights or daytime lights always, headlights in darkness,
    dusk and poor visibility, full beam bans; 51 § hazard lights.
  - 56 § "Polkupyörällä, mopolla tai moottoripyörällä hinaaminen on kielletty, jos hinaaminen
    haittaa ajoa tai aiheuttaa vaaraa": towing with a motorcycle is banned if it hampers riding or
    endangers others.
  - 58 § motorways and semi-motorways: only motor vehicles, and none whose maximum or design
    speed is 70 km/h or less. 59-62 § joining, leaving, no U-turn or reversing.
  - 92 § "Kuljettajan ja matkustajan on käytettävä tyypiltään hyväksyttyä asianmukaisesti
    kiinnitettyä suojakypärää ajettaessa: 1) moottoripyörällä": rider and passenger wear an
    approved, properly fastened helmet; the rider makes sure a passenger under 15 wears one.
    93 § exemption only for a health reason, with a doctor's certificate carried while riding.
  - 98 § no hand-held communication device while riding a motor vehicle.
  - 104 § tyre tread at least 1.6 mm, "Moottoripyörän ... kuitenkin ... vähintään 1,0 mm":
    motorcycles and their trailers 1.0 mm.
  - 105 § winter tyres (main grooves at least 3.0 mm) in November to March when weather or road
    conditions require, for cars up to 3.5 t, vans and "moottoripyörä, mopo, kolmipyörä ...".
  - 106 § studded tyres allowed on motorcycles from the start of November to the end of March,
    other times when weather or road conditions require.
  - 144 § a motorcycle without a sidecar may carry at most one passenger; with a sidecar one
    behind the rider and at most two in the sidecar; a suitable seat and footrests are required;
    registered passenger numbers may not be exceeded.
  - 153 § a trailer on a motorcycle: coupling mass within the manufacturer's limit and never more
    than half the motorcycle's unladen mass.

## Drink driving (official law)
- Rikoslaki (Criminal Code) chapter 23 sections 3 and 4: drink driving of a motor vehicle from
  0.5 per mille in the blood or 0.22 mg/l of breath; no lower limit for novice drivers
  (confirmed for fitheory, 2026-11-01, Finlex Act 176/2002:
  https://www.finlex.fi/api/assets/extra/statute-book-of-finland-pdf/fin/2002/20020176.pdf).
  This bank asks only that the same limit applies to new riders (fitheory asks the figures).

## Riding technique (general road safety knowledge, no official figures)
- Braking, cornering, countersteering, look-where-you-go, slow riding, staggered group riding,
  surface hazards (spring grit, ruts worn by studded tyres, painted lines, rails), conspicuity and
  helmet care are standard rider-training content (Traficom's regulation lists protective gear,
  visibility, road hazards and motorcycle mechanics as topics). No figure from a commercial source
  is used. Reaction distance at 80 km/h (22 m in 1 s) and the four-times braking distance at double
  speed are computed (80 / 3.6 = 22.2 m; braking distance grows with the square of speed).

## Deliberately avoided
- Fines, fees, day-fine amounts; licence and test fees; durations that may change (training
  licence, examination certificate, licence validity, lesson counts); any question about the test
  itself; motorcycle trailer speed limits (Road Traffic Act annex 5.2, not in the open data file);
  lane filtering between queues on multi-lane roads (no section addresses it, so only the 31 §
  single-lane rule is asked); B licence entitlements to tricycles; helmet standard numbers;
  the 2026 night ban (it is for 17-year-olds driving category B vehicles, so it is not asked here).
