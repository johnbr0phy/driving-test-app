# fitheory Finnish (fi-FI) glossary

Terms as the tieliikennelaki 729/2018, Traficom, Ajovarma and the Finnish learner materials
(Liikenneturva, autokoulujen teoriamateriaali) use them. Questions, explanations, app copy and
landing in the "sinä" register, as Finnish learner materials and the real teoriakoe are written.
Decimal comma (0,5 promillea, 1,6 mm, 3,5 tonnia), space as thousands separator (1 500 kg),
km/h, metrit, dates 29.5.2026, times 00.00. No em or en dashes.
