# Sources for `fitheory` (Finland driving theory test, category B)

All 200 questions are original. Commercial practice apps and study sites (open-exam-prep.com,
autoviatest.com, e-koulu.com) appeared in search results; only their format summaries were read,
never their questions, and nothing in the bank comes from them. Traffic picture and risk
recognition items are written as situations in words (no images). Checked 2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Format of the real test (official)
- Traficom regulation "Kuljettajantutkinnon teoriakoemääräys", TRAFICOM/523973/03.04.03.00/2019,
  issued 10.6.2020, in force 15.6.2020 "toistaiseksi" (until further notice), read in full
  (PDF downloaded 2026-11-01):
  https://www.traficom.fi/sites/default/files/media/regulation/Kuljettajantutkinnon%20teoriakoem%C3%A4%C3%A4r%C3%A4ys.pdf
  - Taken electronically at a service point of Traficom's service provider; 30 minutes per test.
  - Oral test with 60 minutes if the mother tongue is not Finnish, Swedish or English and the
    candidate does not master any of them, if illiterate, aged 75+ after failing once, or on
    health grounds (dyslexia, other), or after cheating.
  - Three question types: verbal multiple choice, picture questions on traffic situations, risk
    recognition questions. Every part must reach its minimum or the test is failed.
  - Category B (with AM/120, AM/121, T): 15 multiple choice (11 to pass), 50 picture questions
    (43 to pass), 5 risk recognition (4 to pass).
  - Topic areas of the multiple choice questions (A, B, BE...): traffic rules; the driver; the
    road; other road users; general rules and other matters; precautions when leaving the
    vehicle; mechanical aspects of road safety; safety equipment; vehicle use and the
    environment. Picture question areas: driving straight, right, left; giving way when driving
    straight, right, left; overtaking; changing lanes; stopping; parking. The blueprint here
    follows these areas (no official weights exist).
- Traficom, "Driving examination" (English page), fetched 2026-11-01:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/driving-examination
  - Same table of question counts and minimums; test on a tablet, 30 minutes.
  - "They can read the questions aloud in the language of the test (Finnish, Swedish or English)":
    the test languages are Finnish, Swedish and English.
  - Passed theory test valid one year; theory test before the driving test; may be taken one month
    before reaching the age requirement (one month before 17 with an age exception permit).
  - Misconduct (cheating, copying, recording) = six-month ban from the theory test.
  - Driving test 60 minutes: initial discussion, driving in traffic, final discussion.
  - Reform of 29 May 2026: an oral-test requirement imposed earlier is valid until 31 Dec 2026;
    from 1 Jan 2027 the person may take a normal theory test. New interpreter exemptions no
    longer granted.
- A-Katsastus / Ajovarma (Traficom's service provider; ajovarma.fi/en redirects there),
  "What happens during the theory test", fetched 2026-11-01:
  https://a-katsastus.fi/en/driving-licences-and-permits/what-happens-during-the-theory-test
  (30 minutes, electronic, three question types, a part below its minimum fails the test).
  Booking: https://varaus.ajovarma.fi/en

## Driving Licence Act reform 2026 (official)
- Traficom, "Driving Licence Act reform: What will change?", fetched 2026-11-01:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/driving-licence-act-reform-what-will-change
  - Act in force 29 May 2026 at 00.00. Night-time driving ban 00.00 to 05.00 for all
    17-year-olds driving category B vehicles. Sign identifying an underage driver mandatory for
    17-year-olds driving passenger cars from 1 October 2026 (no design given on the page; the
    bank does not describe it). Risk recognition training up from 8 to 12 hours from
    1 October 2026. Age exception permit: applicant at least 16 years 8 months, stricter
    mobility conditions. Cheating in the theory test: six-month ban.
- Government press release (valtioneuvosto.fi, 403 to the fetcher; confirmed through search
  snippets): https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/1410829/amended-driving-licence-act-includes-night-driving-ban-for-young-drivers-to-improve-traffic-safety-for-young-people
- Short-term first licence (two years): could not be confirmed on a current official page in the
  2026-11-01 review (Statistics Finland glossary only; press reports conflict), so the item that
  asked it (FITHE-DRV-018) was replaced by "theory test one month before the licence age"
  (Traficom driving examination page, re-fetched 2026-11-01).
- Night ban covers all 17-year-olds driving category B, including those licensed before
  29 May 2026 (Traficom reform page, re-fetched 2026-11-01).
- Age 18 for category B, 17 with an age exception permit: Traficom pages above.

## Rules of the road (official, current text)
- Tieliikennelaki (Road Traffic Act) 729/2018, consolidated text from the Finlex open data API,
  dateConsolidated 2026-04-17, fetched 2026-11-01:
  https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2018/729/fin@
  (human page: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2018/729). Facts used, by section:
  - 4 § anticipation; 7 § stay at the accident scene and help; 8 § traffic controller first,
    then devices; traffic lights before give-way signs.
  - 9 § 80 km/h outside and 50 km/h inside a taajama unless signed.
  - 10 § clear passage for emergency vehicles with siren and lights; do not cut into convoys,
    supervised groups of children or processions. 11 § trains and trams get unobstructed passage;
    level crossing: do not start across when a train comes, the light says stop, the bell sounds
    or the barrier is down or moving. 12 § obstacles: warn others, call the emergency centre if
    serious.
  - 13 § pedestrians on the left edge of the carriageway, reflector in the dark "generally".
    14 § pedestrians may use all of a pihakatu or kävelykatu.
  - 17 § unfit drivers; light electric vehicle riders at least 15. 17 a § 0.5 per mille /
    0.22 mg/l (e-scooters; same figures as the Criminal Code).
  - 18 § under-12s may cycle on the pavement; mopeds on the shoulder outside built-up areas.
  - 19 § keep right; rightmost lane where two or more; pass islands on the right.
    20 § following distance; slow vehicles leave gaps outside built-up areas; no needless slow
    driving or sudden braking. 21-22 § positioning and turning. 23 § cyclists' two-stage left turn.
  - 24 § right-hand rule; turning yields to crossing pedestrians, cyclists, moped riders, to
    oncoming traffic when turning left, and when leaving a roundabout; always yield coming from a
    pihakatu/kävelykatu, a yard, car park, fuel station, a path, after crossing a pavement or
    cycle path, and from a cycle path to the carriageway; passengers at tram and bus stops.
    25 § show intent to yield by slowing or stopping. 26 § bus leaving a stop where the limit
    is 60 km/h or less. 27 § pedestrian crossings (stop for those on or about to go on; do not
    pass a vehicle stopped before a crossing without stopping). 29 § safe space for pedestrians,
    cyclists, animals; care near school transport, buses, trams, children, elderly.
  - 30 § meeting; both turning left may pass on the left; obstacle on your side: you yield.
    31 § overtake on the left; on the right a left-turning vehicle or in parallel lanes; trams
    on the right. 32 § overtaking bans (crossings, junctions, level crossings; outside built-up
    areas only junctions shown by a sign; poor view; vehicle ahead signalling to overtake; being
    overtaken). 33 § duties of overtaker and overtaken; slow drivers help, may use the shoulder.
  - 36 § stop/park on the right; left on one-way roads and, in built-up areas, on two-way roads
    if safe; parallel and close to the edge. 37 § bans incl. 5 m before a crossing, 5 m from a
    junction, roundabout, bus and cycle lanes, 3 m from a solid line, motorways except signed
    areas, tunnels, crests; stopping allowed for an obstruction, to give way or an emergency;
    short stops on a pavement only when compelled. 38 § no parking within 30 m of a level
    crossing, beside a parked vehicle, on a priority road outside built-up areas. 40 § parking
    disc: next full or half hour. 41 § secure the vehicle, doors. 42 § no storage on roads.
    43-46 § pihakatu (20 km/h, marked spaces), kävelykatu (20 km/h, access only), pyöräkatu,
    tunnels (no reversing or U-turn, stop only in an emergency, engine off if prolonged, lights).
  - 47 § horn only to avoid danger or when overtaking outside built-up areas. 48 § direction
    signals. 49 § headlights or daytime running lights always; headlights in darkness, dusk and
    poor visibility; full beam bans; fog lights. 50 § lights on parked vehicles. 51 § hazard
    lights. 52 § light electric vehicles follow cyclists' rules. 53 § animals. 55 § idling
    2 min, 4 min below -15 °C. 56 § rope 3 to 6 m, rigid bar with faulty brakes. 57 § warning
    triangle outside built-up areas, "far enough" (no figure given, so none is asked).
  - 58-62 § motorways and semi-motorways: no vehicles of 70 km/h or less; join at the start or a
    slip road, giving way; leave in good time; no reversing or U-turns; rightmost free lane;
    passing on the right without changing lane in queues; no use of crossovers.
  - 74 § lane signals, pedestrian signals. 76-82 § sign groups; variable signs may be light on
    black; additional panels of warning, priority and prohibition signs: yellow ground, red
    border. 85 § temporary longitudinal markings are yellow and take priority over white.
  - 90 § seat belts for everyone. 94 § child restraint under 135 cm, never without one under 3,
    no rear-facing seat at an active airbag. 98 § no hand-held communication device.
    99 § 25 km/h for motor-assisted bicycles, e-bikes and light electric vehicles.
    101 § towing on own wheels 60 km/h. 104 § tread 1.6 mm. 105 § winter tyres November to
    March when weather or road conditions require, tread 3.0 mm. 106 § studded tyres from the
    start of November to the end of March (other times if conditions require), on all wheels.
  - Annexes (sign pictures, vehicle speed tables) were not in the open data file; no item asks a
    sign code, a trailer speed or a sign colour not stated in the Act, apart from the general
    sign families below.
- Yellow centre lines: the 2018 Act made permanent markings white; yellow is now only for
  temporary arrangements (moottori.fi and autotalli.com reports on the reform, via search; 85 §
  above). The bank asks only the 85 § rule.
- Sign families (Vienna Convention, Finnish variants): warning triangles and prohibition circles
  with a yellow ground and red border (82 § confirms the yellow/red additional panels for these
  groups); blue mandatory circles; priority road yellow diamond; give-way inverted triangle; STOP
  octagon. Moose (hirvi) and reindeer (poro) warning signs.

## Light electric vehicles (review note)
- 15-year age limit and 0.5 per mille limit for e-scooters and other light electric vehicles: Road
  Traffic Act amendment in force 17 June 2025 (adds 17 a §; the body of 17 a § is not in the
  consolidated open-data text, but the amendment note is). Confirmed by hel.fi
  (https://www.hel.fi/fi/uutiset/sahkopotkulautoihin-promilleraja-ja-15-vuoden-ikaraja) and
  Verkkouutiset (https://www.verkkouutiset.fi/a/uudet-skuuttilait-voimaan-tana-kesana/), 2026-11-01.
- Zip merging: the 2018 Act has no merge-in-turn section, so the bank asks none.

## Drink driving (official law, confirmed through search)
- Rikoslaki (Criminal Code) chapter 23, sections 3 and 4: drink driving from 0.5 per mille in the
  blood or 0.22 mg/l of breath; aggravated from 1.2 per mille or 0.53 mg/l. Breath limit set at
  0.22 by Act 176/2002 (HE 90/2002). No lower limit for novice drivers in Finland.
  https://www.finlex.fi/api/assets/extra/statute-book-of-finland-pdf/fin/2002/20020176.pdf
  (the consolidated Criminal Code did not load from the open data API on 2026-11-01; the
  0.5 / 0.22 pair is also in Road Traffic Act 17 a §).

## Other
- Red warning triangle on medicine packs means the medicine may affect driving ability
  (Liikenneturva via moottori.fi and verkkouutiset.fi, via search 2026-11-01).
- Emergency number 112; report a collision with a large animal through 112 (standard police and
  Liikenneturva advice).
