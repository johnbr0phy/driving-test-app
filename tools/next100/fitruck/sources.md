# Sources for `fitruck` (Finland truck and bus theory test, categories C, CE, D and DE)

All 200 questions are original. Commercial practice sites (open-exam-prep.com "fi teoriakoe c",
e-koulu.com, autoviatest.com) appeared in search results; only their format summaries were read,
never their questions, and nothing in the bank comes from them. Traffic picture and risk
recognition items are written as situations in words (no images). Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Format of the real test (official)
- Traficom regulation "Kuljettajantutkinnon teoriakoemääräys", TRAFICOM/523973/03.04.03.00/2019,
  in force 15.6.2020 until further notice, read in full (PDF downloaded 2026-11-06):
  https://www.traficom.fi/sites/default/files/media/regulation/Kuljettajantutkinnon%20teoriakoem%C3%A4%C3%A4r%C3%A4ys.pdf
  - Taken electronically at a service point of Traficom's service provider; 30 minutes per test.
    Several categories may be taken on one visit; each category is assessed on its own.
  - Oral test with 60 minutes when the mother tongue is not Finnish, Swedish or English and the
    candidate does not master any of them, and in other listed cases.
  - Three question types: verbal multiple choice, traffic-situation picture questions, risk
    recognition questions. A part below its minimum fails the test.
  - Categories C, CE, D and DE: 20 multiple choice (14 to pass), 30 picture questions (26 to
    pass), 5 risk recognition (4 to pass): 55 questions, 44 correct needed in all (80%).
    (C1, C1E, D1, D1E and BE: 10 (7), 30 (26), 5 (4).)
  - Multiple-choice topic areas for C1, C1E, C, CE, D1, D1E, D, DE: driving and rest time rules;
    carriage of goods or passengers; vehicle and transport documents; action in an accident;
    precautions when changing tyres; rules on mass, dimensions and speed limiters; obstacles to the
    field of view caused by the vehicle's construction; use of road maps and route planning; load
    safety (C1, C1E, C, CE only); driver's responsibility in passenger transport (D1, D1E, D, DE
    only). C, CE, D and DE add: vehicle construction and operation; lubrication and anti-freezing;
    tyre construction, fitting, use and care; brakes and speed governors; coupling systems (CE and
    DE only); methods for finding faults; preventive maintenance and routine repairs; driver's
    responsibility for goods (C and CE only). Picture topics for all categories: driving straight,
    right and left; giving way when driving straight, right and left; overtaking; changing lanes;
    stopping; parking. The blueprint follows these areas; Traficom publishes no weights, so the
    split is Tigertest's.
- Traficom, "Driving examination" (English page), fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/driving-examination
  - Same table: C, CE, D, DE 20 (14), 30 (26), 5 (4). Test on a tablet, 30 minutes. Passed theory
    test valid one year. Oral test: questions read aloud "in the language of the test (Finnish,
    Swedish or English)". Oral-test requirements imposed before the 29 May 2026 reform are valid
    until 31 Dec 2026.
  - The page does not list test languages for each category. The regulation's oral-test rule names
    Finnish, Swedish and English as the test languages, without limiting them by category, and
    driving schools say all theory tests can be taken in those three. A category-specific
    confirmation that the C and D tests are offered in English was NOT found on a Traficom page:
    the landing says "Finnish, Swedish and English, per Traficom's rules; check the language when
    you book" rather than asserting it for C and D.
- Traficom, "Getting a driving licence for a lorry", fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/getting-driving-licence-lorry
  - C is for a lorry, CE for a vehicle combination, C1 for a smaller lorry. Minimum age C and CE 21,
    or 18 with a basic-level professional qualification as a lorry driver; C1 and C1E 18.
  - A valid category B licence is needed when taking the theory test. The theory test may be taken
    one month before turning 21; the driving test after passing the theory test and turning 21.
    The theory test is organised by Ajovarma.
  - All lorry drivers in commercial transport must have valid professional competence.
  - Re-read 2026-11-07 (review): the theory test also needs photo ID, the certificate of instruction
    and examination from the driving school, institution or instructor, and a valid driving licence
    permit; an approval document if under 21 without professional competence. Matches the landing.
  - A lorry licence is valid 5 years at a time; from 68, 2 years at a time.
- Traficom, "Getting a driving licence for a bus", fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://traficom.fi/en/drivers-and-vehicles/driving-licenses/getting-driving-licence-bus
  - D1 and D1E from 21; D and DE from 24 (exceptions under 24). Valid category B licence needed
    for instruction. Professional competence for commercial bus drivers. Licence valid 5 years at a
    time, 2 years from 68. Theory test one month before the minimum age.
- A-Katsastus / Ajovarma (Traficom's service provider), "What happens during the theory test",
  fetched 2026-11-06: https://a-katsastus.fi/en/driving-licences-and-permits/what-happens-during-the-theory-test
  (questions depend on the category; each category assessed separately; interpreter in some cases).
  Booking: https://varaus.ajovarma.fi/en

## Rules of the road and vehicle law (official, current text)
- Tieliikennelaki (Road Traffic Act) 729/2018, consolidated text from the Finlex open data API,
  fetched 2026-11-06:
  https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2018/729/fin@
  Facts used, by section:
  - 20 § following distance (also slow vehicles' gaps; not asked, fitheory asks it).
  - 26 § bus leaving a stop where the limit is 60 km/h or less: approaching vehicles give way.
  - 29 § special care near a stopped school transport vehicle, bus or tram.
  - 33 § the vehicle being overtaken keeps right and must not speed up; slow drivers help.
  - 46 § tunnels; 55 § idling (2 minutes; 4 minutes below -15 °C); 57 § warning triangle.
  - 59 § move to a deceleration lane as early as possible when leaving a motorway.
  - 61 § on a motorway or semi-motorway with three or more lanes each way, a truck or a vehicle
    combination over seven metres may use only the two right-hand lanes.
  - 90 § seat belts for driver and passengers (buses included); 91 § informing bus passengers
    (driver, conductor or guide, audiovisual means, or a pictogram visible from each seat);
    95 § child restraint responsibility (guardian; otherwise the driver, except a bus driver);
    vehicles for up to 16 passengers: safe boarding; wheelchairs and stretchers fastened;
    97 § fire extinguisher and first-aid supplies in a bus.
  - 99 § vehicle speeds in annexes 5.1 and 5.2; 100 § self-steering axle locked above 60 km/h;
    101 § towing 60 km/h; 102 § speed plate at the rear of a vehicle limited below 80 km/h.
  - 104 § same tyres on one axle; 105 § winter tyres November to March when weather or road
    conditions require: trucks, buses and cars over 3.5 t need at least 5.0 mm on driven axles
    (except steering driven axles) and 3.0 mm on other axles and trailer axles;
    106 § anti-skid devices allowed; combinations over 44 t with under 18 % of mass on driven axles
    need a traction aid device in the stud period (a single-axle differential lock does not count).
  - 107 § loading: no manufacturer mass exceeded; load may reach 1.00 m in front and 2.00 m behind;
    mirrors for wide loads; a demountable body counts as load. 108 § low compact load, centre of
    gravity low and near the centre line; centre-axle trailer nose load at most 10 % of axle mass
    or 1,000 kg. 109 § load stays in place; blocking, lashing, locking, covering; friction counts
    only when vertical movement is prevented; cover dusting loads. 110 § flag at least 300 x 300 mm,
    red or red-yellow, for loads more than 1.00 m beyond the rear; lights and reflectors in the dark.
    114 § the driver checks the loading before the trip and keeps it secure; whoever loaded or
    instructed answers for it.
  - 141 § passengers up to the registered number; public transport buses may exceed it by 30 %
    within mass limits. 142 § school and daycare transport by bus: seats only. 143 § school
    transport sign (square, reflective yellow, red border, black children symbol of sign A17,
    40 cm sides, top edge at most 150 cm, removed after the run). 148 § passengers in a truck's load
    area only on approved seats or benches.
  - 150 § coupling: one towed vehicle unless the law says otherwise; brakes and lights work;
    electric and air brakes matched; mirrors. 151 § a bus may tow a centre-axle or full trailer;
    trucks over 22 m may pull a full trailer, a dolly and semi-trailer, a B-double or a semi and
    centre-axle trailer; combinations over 22 m unladen need anti-lock brakes on every vehicle.
  - 156 § work lights may not be on when driving on the road, except for work on the road.
  - 158-159 § special transports (erikoiskuljetus) need a permit.
- Annexes to Act 729/2018 (PDF from the Finlex open data media file, original 2018 consolidation,
  fetched 2026-11-06): https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2018/729/fin@/media/6908.pdf
  - Annex 5.1: truck 80 km/h; bus 80 km/h, or 100 km/h if fitted with special safety equipment
    and with no standing passengers. Annex 5.2: car with a towed vehicle 80 km/h; car or van with a
    trailer up to 750 kg 100 km/h. A search on 2026-11-06 found no amendment raising the truck
    limit.
  - Annex 7.2: maximum height of car and trailer 4.40 m; bus width 2.55 m; most cars and trailers
    2.60 m. Masses and lengths (changed by the 2019 amendments) are NOT asked.
  - Annex 3 sign texts: C9 no buses; C22 maximum height (load included); C24 maximum mass of each
    vehicle, applying to each vehicle of a combination separately; C25 combination mass; C30 no
    overtaking by trucks (two-wheeled motorcycles and mopeds without sidecar may still be passed,
    and vehicles in a turning lane or on a joining lane); E6 bus stop (no parking within 12 m of
    the sign where there is no platform or marking; stopping allowed if it does not hinder buses);
    E9 bus lane (TAXI at the bottom for taxis); yellow continuous edge marking at bus and tram stops.
- Laki alkolukon käytöstä (Act on alcohol interlocks) 730/2016, Finlex open data, fetched
  2026-11-06: https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2016/730/fin@
  - 10 §: school and daycare transport arranged by a municipality, joint authority, school or
    institution with public support must use a vehicle with an alcohol interlock; the operator
    installs and maintains it. 11 §: a vehicle without one may be used for at most five working
    days after a breakdown, telling the customer at once.

## EU rules applied in Finland
- Driving and rest times: Regulation (EC) 561/2006 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2020/1054
  (Mobility Package): 4.5 h then 45 min (or 15 + 30); 9 h a day (10 h twice a week); 56 h a week;
  90 h in two weeks; daily rest 11 h (or 3 + 9), up to three reduced 9 h rests between weekly
  rests; multi-manning 9 h within 30 h; weekly rest 45 h, reduced 24 h compensated en bloc by the
  end of the third following week; weekly rest after six 24-hour periods; no regular weekly rest
  in the vehicle; return home or to the operating centre every four weeks; ferry or train
  interruption with a bunk or couchette; exceeding by up to 2 h after a 30-minute break to reach
  home or base for a weekly rest; goods vehicles over 2.5 t in international transport covered
  from 1 July 2026; regular passenger routes up to 50 km exempt.
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/561/oj and https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2020/1054/oj
  (long-standing text, also summarised in tools/next100/iedttcv/sources.md, CVRT guide read
  2026-11-05).
- Tachographs: Regulation (EU) 165/2014 as amended (card inserted from the start; lost card:
  apply within 7 calendar days, drive up to 15 days with signed printouts; manual records if the
  tachograph fails; smart tachograph position recording; border crossing country symbol; records
  for the current day and the previous 56 days from 31 December 2024). Downloads: driver card at
  least every 28 days, vehicle unit every 90 days (Commission Regulation (EU) 581/2010).
  Traficom issues tachograph cards in Finland.
- Working time: Directive 2002/15/EC (48-hour average, 60 in a single week; 30-minute break after
  6 hours of work, 45 minutes over 9 hours).
- Speed limiters: Council Directive 92/6/EEC as amended (trucks over 3.5 t limited to 90 km/h,
  buses to 100 km/h).
- Licence categories: Directive 2006/126/EC (C1 up to 7,500 kg; C over 3,500 kg; CE over 750 kg
  trailer; D1 up to 16 passengers and 8 m; D more than 8 passengers); code 95 for professional
  competence.
- Professional competence (ammattipätevyys): continuing training of 35 hours in each five-year
  period under the Act on Transport Services 320/2017 as amended in 2020 (HE 50/2020, in force
  13 July 2020), confirmed through Finlex search results on 2026-11-06 (the consolidated act from
  the open data API did not show the section). The initial qualification test is a separate step
  and is not modelled.
- Community licence (Regulation (EC) 1072/2009): certified copy on board in international haulage.

## Drink driving (official law)
- Criminal Code chapter 23: 0.5 per mille in the blood or 0.22 mg/l of breath for every driver;
  Finland sets no lower limit for professional drivers (see tools/next100/fitheory/sources.md).

## Deliberately avoided
- Fines, fees and day-fine amounts; masses, axle loads and lengths (changed by the 2019
  amendments; the brief says leave out changing weight limits); motorway speeds set by signs;
  the Driver CPC initial qualification test; any question about the theory test itself; facts
  already asked by `fitheory` (the 30 m level crossing rule, parking lights, the right-hand rule,
  tram stop passengers, stopped school buses, morning-after alcohol, crash-scene duty, warning
  triangle, tunnel stops, slow vehicles' gaps, crossovers).
