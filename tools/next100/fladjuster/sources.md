# Sources for `fladjuster` (Florida All-Lines Adjuster license exam, the 6-20 exam)

All 200 questions are original, written from the Florida examination content outline, the standard
ISO and NFIP forms it names, and the Florida statutes and rules it cites. No Pearson VUE sample,
past item or commercial prep question was copied, and no prep provider is named. Statute text was
downloaded from the Florida Legislature's Online Sunshine site and read, not taken from guides.
Checked 2026-11-04.

## Exam format (official)
- Florida Insurance Examination Content Outlines, effective January 1, 2026 (Pearson VUE
  publication 121003, issued by the Department of Financial Services):
  https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/121003.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-04). "Florida Adjuster's All Lines Insurance: 100 scored questions plus 10
  pretest questions. Time limit: 2 hours."
  - I. Property and liability insurance concepts, insurance contracts, and law and adjusting
    practices 14% (contracts, policy conditions, risk, property and liability concepts, contracts
    and the law; adjusting practices incl. property adjusting practices ref. 627.70131; types of
    insurers: surplus lines, reciprocals, admitted, residual market)
  - II. Casualty/liability insurance 28% (auto liability, PAP and endorsements, uninsured
    motorists, Florida no-fault PIP, business auto and garage, general liability, excess and
    umbrella, professional and EPL, homeowners liability, farm liability, boiler and machinery /
    equipment breakdown, crime and surety, workers' compensation law and policy)
  - III. Fire and allied lines, including marine, dwelling 28% (DP-1/2/3, HO-2/3/4/5/6/8,
    commercial property, flood (NFIP and other), BOP, inland marine incl. Nationwide Definition,
    ocean marine, aviation)
  - IV. Motor vehicle physical damage and mechanical breakdown insurance 15%
  - V. Health insurance 2%; VI. Residual markets 2% (FAJUA, Citizens ref. 627.351, FWCJUA)
  - VII. Selected Florida statutes and rules 11% (adjuster licensing, civil remedy, CE ref.
    626.869, expiration, change of address, refusal/suspension; conduct: salvage and motor vehicle
    title, unfair or deceptive practices, comprehensive coverage, mediation, misrepresentation,
    failure to acknowledge, department inquiries, anti-fraud, adjuster identification; ethical
    requirements and code of ethics; FIGA)
- Florida Insurance Licensing Candidate Handbook 2024 (Pearson VUE publication 121000):
  https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/121000.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-04): exam InsFL-ALJ16 "FLA All Lines Adjuster (0620, 0720, 7020)", 2 hours,
  English only (no Spanish version is listed for the adjuster exam); passing score 70% for all
  exams, computed as questions correct divided by total questions; a passing score is valid for
  one year; limit of five attempts at the same exam in 12 months; exam exemptions under
  s. 626.221 (a comparable license held at least a year in the prior home state, applying within
  90 days of becoming a Florida resident; CPCU; AIC and a list of DFS-approved adjuster
  designations); ESL candidates may request extra time. Fees listed there are not used.
- Pearson VUE Florida insurance page: https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/fl/insurance.html
  (checked 2026-11-04), source of the PDFs above and the DFS FAQ (121002).
- License type meanings (guides, not confirmed on myfloridacfo.com): 6-20 resident all-lines
  adjuster, 7-20 nonresident all-lines adjuster, 70-20 nonresident designated home state
  all-lines adjuster (WebCE, AdjusterPro license-type guides, searched 2026-11-04). The handbook
  confirms only that one exam serves 0620, 0720 and 7020.

- 631.54 (FIGA covered claim).

## Standard forms (concepts only, no form text quoted)
ISO Personal Auto Policy, business auto and auto dealers/garage forms, garagekeepers, commercial
general liability, homeowners HO-2 to HO-8, dwelling DP-1 to DP-3, building and personal property,
causes of loss, business income, businessowners, commercial crime, equipment breakdown, NFIP
Standard Flood Insurance Policy dwelling form, inland marine Nationwide Marine Definition, ocean
marine clauses (general and particular average, running down, Inchmaree, sue and labor,
warehouse to warehouse, barratry), aviation hull and admitted liability.

## Duplicate check
Every stem and key was compared by script with `data/texas-adjuster-questions.json`,
`data/property-casualty-questions.json`, `data/property-casualty-fl-questions.json` and
`data/property-casualty-tx-questions.json`; about 40 items that shared a fact and answer were
rewritten. Florida facts already asked in the FL P&C bank (7-day acknowledgment, 60-day payment,
1-year notice, 72-hour hurricane end, calendar-year deductible, catastrophic ground cover
collapse, PIP 80 percent and 14 days, UM written rejection, comparative fault, Citizens 20
percent rule, AOB, 120-day notice, CE totals, change of address) are not asked here.
