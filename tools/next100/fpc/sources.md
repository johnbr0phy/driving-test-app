# FPC (Fundamental Payroll Certification) bank sources

All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the PayrollOrg outline and KSAs and the
federal law below. None is copied or reworded from PayrollOrg's sample questions, PayTrain, The
Payroll Source, any other prep book or course, or any commercial practice test.

## Law and federal guidance (primary)

The exam tests federal law in effect January 1, 2026, so every dated figure is a 2026 figure.

- IRS, Publication 15 (2026), (Circular E), Employer's Tax Guide, https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15 ,
  fetched 2026-11-19. 2026 social security wage base $184,500 at 6.2% each; Medicare 1.45% each, no
  limit; Additional Medicare 0.9% over $200,000, no employer share; supplemental flat rate 22% and 37%
  over $1 million; backup withholding 24%; tips $20 a month; records 4 years; lookback period July 1
  to June 30 and the $50,000 test; monthly deposits by the 15th; semiweekly Wednesday/Friday rule;
  $100,000 next-day rule; $2,500 quarterly de minimis; FTD penalty 2/5/10/15%; trust fund recovery
  penalty 100%; missing W-4 treated as Single, no adjustments; exempt W-4 expires, new one by
  February 15; replacement W-4 by the first payroll period ending 30 days after receipt; lock-in
  letter copy to the employee within 10 business days; deceased worker wages; fringe benefit values
  by January 31; household workers $3,000; 1099-NEC threshold $2,000 for 2026 payments.
- IRS, Publication 15-A (2026), Employer's Supplemental Tax Guide, https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15a ,
  fetched 2026-11-19. Common law control categories, third-party sick pay, sick pay after death.
- IRS, Publication 15-B (2026), Employer's Tax Guide to Fringe Benefits, https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b ,
  fetched 2026-11-19. Group-term life over $50,000 (Table 2-2 cost per $1,000 per month, box 12 code
  C, FICA but optional FIT), de minimis rule (cash never de minimis), $5,250 educational assistance,
  2026 dependent care exclusion $7,500, 2026 qualified parking and transit $340 a month, achievement
  awards $1,600/$400, Annual Lease Value and 5.5 cents per mile fuel.
- IRS, Instructions for Form 940 (2025), https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i940 , fetched 2026-11-19.
  FUTA 6.0% on the first $7,000, 5.4% maximum credit, $500 deposit rule, credit reduction states.
- IRS, Instructions for Form 941, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i941 , fetched 2026-11-19.
- U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Fact Sheet #15 (tipped employees),
  https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/15-tipped-employees-flsa , fetched 2026-11-19: $2.13
  cash wage, $5.12 maximum tip credit, more than $30 a month in tips, tip pools.
- DOL WHD Fact Sheet #17A (exemptions), https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/17a-overtime ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: $684 a week salary level (the level enforced after the 2024 rule was vacated),
  computer employee $27.63 an hour, outside sales has no salary test.
- DOL WHD Fact Sheet #21 (recordkeeping), https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/21-flsa-recordkeeping ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: payroll records 3 years, wage computation records 2 years.
- DOL WHD Fact Sheet #22 (hours worked), https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/22-flsa-hours-worked ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: short rest breaks paid, bona fide meal periods of 30 minutes or more unpaid,
  suffered or permitted work paid, on-call, waiting, training and travel rules.
- DOL WHD Fact Sheet #23 (overtime), https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/23-flsa-overtime-pay ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: 1.5 times the regular rate over 40 hours, fixed 168-hour workweek, no
  averaging, regular rate inclusions and exclusions, weighted average for two rates.
- DOL WHD Fact Sheet #30 (CCPA garnishment), https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/30-cppa ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: disposable earnings, 25% or the excess over 30 times $7.25 ($217.50 a week),
  50/60% support plus 5% for arrears over 12 weeks, tax levies outside the limits.
- DOL WHD Fact Sheet #43 (child labor, nonagricultural), https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/43-child-labor-non-agriculture ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: minimum age 14; 14 and 15-year-olds 3 hours on a school day, 18 in a school
  week, 8 on a nonschool day, 40 in a nonschool week, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. (9 p.m. June 1 to Labor Day);
  no one under 18 in hazardous occupations.
- DOL, "Last Paycheck", https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/wages/lastpaycheck , fetched 2026-11-19:
  federal law does not require an immediate final paycheck; some states do.
- 29 CFR 785.48 (rounding), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/part-785/section-785.48 , fetched
  2026-11-19: nearest 5 minutes, tenth or quarter hour when it does not, over time, underpay.
- 29 CFR 778.601 (8 and 80), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/part-778/section-778.601 ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: hospitals and residential care, prior agreement, over 8 a day and 80 in 14 days.
- 29 CFR 778.114 (fluctuating workweek), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/part-778/section-778.114 ,

- 29 CFR 825.500 (FMLA records), https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/part-825/section-825.500 ,

- 42 U.S.C. 653a (new hire reporting), https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/653a , fetched

- IRS, General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw2w3 , fetched

  1099-MISC.
- Nacha, ACH Developer Guide, "How ACH Works", https://achdevguide.nacha.org/how-ach-works , fetched
  2026-11-19: Originator, ODFI, ACH Operator, RDFI, Receiver and the payroll direct deposit flow.
- USCIS, M-274 Handbook for Employers, Retaining Form I-9,
  https://www.uscis.gov/i-9-central/form-i-9-resources/handbook-for-employers-m-274/100-retaining-form-i-9 ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: 3 years after hire or 1 year after employment ends, whichever is later.

## Not confirmed or left out

- The SSA "Contribution and Benefit Base" page returned 403; the 2026 wage base is taken from
  Publication 15 (2026).
- The DOL page for the 2019 salary rule is dated September 2019; DOL enforces $684 after the 2024
  rule was vacated in November 2024. Items use the level only where it is the deciding fact.
- No question asks a fee, the passing score, question counts or anything about the exam itself.
- No state law: state minimum wage, state income tax, SUI rates, escheat periods, pay frequency and
  final pay deadlines vary by state and are worded that way.
- Indexed limits not in Pub. 15 or 15-B for 2026 (401(k) deferral limit, HSA limits, standard mileage
  rate) are left out or given in the stem.
- P.L. 119-21 qualified tips and overtime deductions are left out: they are income tax deductions
  claimed by the employee, and the W-2 reporting rules were still in transition on January 1, 2026.
- Pub. 15-T wage bracket and percentage tables are not reproduced; any federal income tax amount a
  calculation needs is given in the stem.
- Kept clear of facts and answers in the phr, cpafar, cpareg, cpatcp, ea1, ea2, ea3 and afsp banks.
