# Glossary for `frcaces` (French, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations use "vous" (explanations address the cariste); landing and
dashboard copy use "tu", like `frcode`. French typography: decimal comma (1,20 m, 4,5 m), a
space before units and thousands (2 000 kg, 500 mm), no em dashes. Plates, pictograms and signs
are described in words.
