# Sources for `frcaces` (CACES R489 chariots de manutention, test théorique, in French)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in French from the recommendation R489 and the
INRS documents below. Nothing was copied, reworded or consulted from the INRS "guide
d'évaluation" grids that the OTC must draw their theory questions from, from any OTC's or
training centre's test, or from any commercial CACES app, book or quiz. The R489 itself says a
training that only prepares for the test questions does not meet the training obligation; this
bank is practice on the referential, not a copy of the test. Fetched 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Owner and outline (what the bank is written to)
- INRS, "Catégories de Caces", page mise à jour le 30/07/2026, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.inrs.fr/accueil/demarche/caces-certificat-aptitude-conduite-securite/categories-caces.html
  - R489 categories 1A (transpalettes et préparateurs sans élévation du poste, levée ≤ 1,20 m),
    1B (gerbeurs, levée > 1,20 m), 2A (plateau porteur ≤ 2 t), 2B (tracteurs ≤ 25 t),
    3 (frontaux en porte-à-faux ≤ 6 t), 4 (> 6 t), 5 (mât rétractable, prise latérale d'un seul
    côté incluse), 6 (poste de conduite élevable, plancher > 1,20 m), 7 (conduite hors
    production). No option for R489 trucks.
- Cnam, recommandation R489 "Certificat d'aptitude à la conduite en sécurité des chariots de
  manutention automoteurs à conducteur porté", adopted by the CTN B, C, D, F, G and I in 2017,
  reference from 1 January 2020 (R389 applicable until 31 December 2019). The ameli.fr copy
  (r489-v3.pdf) and the INRS media page return 403 or render only by script to automated
  readers; the full text was read from the copy hosted by a training organisation, which
  carries the Cnam's own layout, annexes and adoption dates, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://evolis.org/medias/files/511_FICHIER_0.pdf
  - 3.1 aptitude médicale before the autorisation; avis must name the forklift activity; new
    medical visit when driving is a new activity.
  - 3.2 training (R4323-55, arrêté du 2 décembre 1998 art. 1); attestation de formation needed
    to sit the test; a training limited to passing the test does not suffice.
  - 3.3 CACES only for the category tested; testeur distinct from the formateur for the practical
    part; theory test written in French, questions may be read aloud; at most 12 candidates per
    testeur in the theory room; a part passed is kept 12 months; after a first CACES the theory
    part is kept 12 months for another category of the same family (extension, same end date);
    actualisation at least every 5 years; the CACES is handed to the employee.
  - 3.4 autorisation de conduite by the employer (R4323-56, arrêté du 2 décembre 1998): aptitude
    médicale, CACES or equivalent, knowledge of the site and instructions (protocole de sécurité,
    plan de prévention, plan de circulation); one per category; the employer may withdraw it at
    any time. CACES does not replace the driving licence where one is needed (Code de la route
    R221-1, quoted in the text).
  - Annexe 1: categories, excluded trucks (tout-terrain R482, gerbeurs à conducteur accompagnant
    R485, transpalettes à conducteur accompagnant R366, tracteurs de parc, chariots industriels à
    portée variable), dispenses (1B covers 1A, 2B covers 2A), équipements particuliers needing
    extra training (préparateur à poste élevable ≤ 1,20 m with 1A, double fourche with 1B, bi/tri-
    directionnels with 5, combis with 6), capacité nominale on the plate, hauteur de levée
    measured to the top of the forks.
  - Annexe 2: référentiel de connaissances (sections A to H: connaissances générales, technologie,
    types and categories, physique, stabilité, risques, exploitation, vérifications) and
    savoir-faire (prise de poste, conduite, fin de poste). This is the bank's outline.
  - Annexe 3/1: the theory test is the same for every R489 category, 100 questions at 1 point,
    themes Connaissances générales 15, Technologie et stabilité 25, Tableau / abaque de charges
    10, Exploitation 50; pass with at least 70/100 overall and at least the average on each theme.
    The bank's per-test split (8/12/5/25 of 50) follows these points.

## Content (rules a driver lives by)
- INRS ED 766, "Chariots automoteurs de manutention, Manuel de conduite", avril 2019, fetched
  2026-11-22: https://inrs.fr/dam/jcr:0747609d-ee27-4fce-87b4-36669b22f035/ed766.pdf
  - Autorisation de conduite (arrêté du 2 décembre 1998 art. 3: examen d'aptitude by the médecin
    du travail, contrôle des connaissances, connaissance des lieux); valid for the sites and
    truck types assessed; intérimaire trained by the agency, autorisation by the user company
    for the mission; external company's driver authorised by his own employer.
  - Voie publique: a chariot élévateur limited by construction to 25 km/h needs no permis but
    the autorisation; faster, a permis is needed (arrêté du 20 novembre 1969).
  - EPI (chaussures de sécurité always, gloves for manual handling, free of charge); dispositifs
    de sécurité (protège-conducteur from 1,80 m of lift, dosseret, système de retenue R4324-35,
    avertisseur, clé), plaque de capacité, porte-charge changes reduce capacity, loading rules,
    refuelling, GPL (walls never above 50 °C, cylinder exchange in a dedicated ventilated area,
    engine off), battery charging (hydrogen, ventilated room, do not discharge beyond 80 %,
    distilled water at the end of charge, never add acid, open covers while charging).
  - Prise de poste and fin de poste (forks flat on the ground or above 2 m, neutral, engine off,
    parking brake, key removed); circulation (forks about 15 cm, mast tilted back, slopes loaded
    in reverse, no U-turn on a slope, three lengths between trucks, stop more than 1 m from a
    rail, swing doors); gerbage sequences; poste élevable rules; no lifting or carrying of persons
    on a truck not designed for it (R4323-31, R4323-54); list of interdictions; daily checks,
    weekly inspection, vérification générale périodique every six months (arrêté du 1er mars 2004
    art. 22), remise en service (art. 19 and 20); fire instructions; accident causes.
- INRS ED 949, "Chariots automoteurs de manutention à conducteur porté, Évaluation et prévention
  des principaux risques lors de l'utilisation", 1re édition 2005, réimpression 2013, fetched
  2026-11-22: https://inrs.fr/dam/jcr:f70cffc4-8a0c-4074-99fd-258a181538cd/ed949.pdf
  - Accident analysis (EPICEA): pedestrians struck mostly in reverse, lateral overturn causes
    (speed, turns, forks raised, obstacles, unstable load, floor, slope), drivers trying to jump
    out are crushed; portillon and ceinture as retenue; big-bags hung from the forks; no lifting
    of persons on forks. Its Code du travail article numbers (R233-x) are the pre-2008 numbering
    and are not cited; only the risk facts are used.
- INRS ED 812, "Les chariots automoteurs de manutention", octobre 2006, réimpression 2013 without
  update, fetched 2026-11-22: https://inrs.fr/dam/inrs/CataloguePapier/ED/TI-ED-812.pdf
  - Technology only (truck types, porte-à-faux and contrepoids, mât rétractable bringing the
    load inside the polygone de sustentation, fork lengths and their usual load centres 400,
    500, 600 mm, fork wear, rallonges, équipements porte-charge reducing capacity). No legal
    reference is taken from it.
- INRS ED 4406, "Produits chimiques : apprenez à décrypter les pictogrammes de danger",
  4e édition (2023) révisée en mai 2024, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://inrs.fr/dam/jcr:3828f4a8-977f-4dcc-84b7-2573678332db/ed4406.pdf
  - The nine CLP pictograms and their meanings, for the "produits dangereux" items.

## Not loaded, and how the copy words it
- Légifrance (Code du travail R4323-55, R4323-56, arrêté du 2 décembre 1998, arrêté du 1er mars
  2004) returned 403 to automated readers. The rules are taken as R489 and ED 766 state them,
  and explanations cite the article as those documents do.
- The ameli.fr hosting of R489 returned 403; the text read is the copy named above.

## Left out on purpose
- The test itself (question count, pass mark, testeur, UT, rooms, retakes) is on the landing and
  in examFacts only, never in the bank.
- Prices, durations of training courses (set by each organisme), sanctions and fines.
- Picture questions: every pictogram, sign or plate is described in words.
- Other CACES recommendations (R482, R485, R486...) beyond knowing that they exist.
