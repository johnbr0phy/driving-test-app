# Sources for `frcode` (French driving theory test, ETG "code de la route", category B, in French)

All 240 questions are original, written directly in French from the laws and official pages
below. Nothing was copied, reworded or consulted from the official question bank of the
épreuve théorique générale, from the approved operators (La Poste, SGS, Dekra, Pearson VUE,
Bureau Veritas), from the published "questions officielles" examples or from any commercial code
app or book (Codes Rousseau, ENPC, Ornikar, En Voiture Simone and the like). Signs are described
in words (shape, colour, symbol), never shown. Fetched 2026-11-14 unless noted.

## Exam format (for the landing and spec only, never asked in the bank)
- Service-public.gouv.fr, "Permis de conduire : comment passer le code (épreuve théorique commune
  ou ETG) ?", page vérifiée le 27 avril 2026, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F33694
  - "Vous devez répondre à 40 questions à choix multiples" ; "35 réponses justes ou plus" to
    pass; taken in an approved exam centre (La Poste, SGS, Dekra, Pearson Vue, Bureau Veritas);
    "Le passage de l'examen coûte 30 €" (fee kept off the bank and the facts table); "Vous
    conservez le bénéfice du code pendant 5 ans"; from 15 (AAC) or 16; convocation and an
    identity document on the day.
- Arrêté du 20 avril 2012 fixant les conditions d'établissement, de délivrance et de validité du
  permis de conduire, consolidated text on Légifrance, fetched 2026-11-14:
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000025803494
  - Art. 2, I.A: "une épreuve théorique générale commune d'admissibilité" on the rules of the
    road and safe behaviour (vigilance and attitudes towards other users, alcohol, drugs,
    medicines, fatigue, first aid, weather and road surface, tunnels, loading, eco-driving,
    insurance and documents).
  - Art. 2, II.B (in force since 4 December 2022): passed with "un nombre de réponses justes
    supérieur ou égal à 35 sur un total de quarante questions"; the benefit is kept "à la
    condition qu'un délai maximum de cinq ans ne se soit pas écoulé". The current text gives no
    cap on practical attempts (an older version did); the landing says only "5 ans".
- Sécurité routière, "Préparation de l'examen du code de la route" and the 24-question practice
  quiz, found 2026-11-14 (pages render with JavaScript; the text was read in search results):
  https://www.securite-routiere.gouv.fr/passer-son-permis-de-conduire/preparation-de-lexamen-du-code-de-la-route
  Each official question has a photo or a video, some have several correct answers, and the
  bank was renewed from 12 September 2023 with questions seen from other users' point of view.
  The landing says ours are single-answer text items with signs described in words.
- Ten themes: the bank's categories follow the subjects of art. 2, I.A, grouped as the ten
  themes under which the ETG programme is usually presented (circulation routière, conducteur,
  route, autres usagers, réglementation générale et divers, précautions en quittant le véhicule,
  éléments mécaniques liés à la sécurité, équipements de sécurité, environnement, premiers
  secours). No official per-theme question count is published; the 8/5/5/5/4/3/3/3/2/2 split
  per test is Tigertest's.

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it
- No official per-theme question count; the split is Tigertest's and the landing says so.
- The exam time (guides say 30 minutes, 20 seconds per question) is not on service-public or
  in the arrêté, so the facts table does not give it.
- Vehicle mechanics items (ABS, aquaplaning, brake fade, oil check) state general physics and
  safety facts the programme asks for; no figure is given for them. Reaction time is given as
  "about one second" and distances are computed (90 km/h = 25 m/s).

## Deliberately left out
- Fines, fee amounts, points removed per offence, and anything about the test itself.
- Departmental 90 km/h decisions: the bank asks only the Code's 80 km/h default.
- The minimum tread depth figure (not in R314-1), the triangle distance (not in R416-19),
  tunnel rules (not read in the chapters above) and Crit'Air or ZFE rules (local and changing).
