# Sources for `frmoto` (French motorcycle theory test, ETM "code moto", categories A1 and A2, in French)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in French from the laws and official pages
below. Nothing was copied, reworded or consulted from the official ETM question bank, from the
approved exam operators (La Poste, SGS, Dekra, Pearson VUE, Bureau Veritas), from any published
"questions officielles" example or from any commercial code moto app or book. Prep sites were
used only to learn that the test exists and its common name; no fact is taken from them. Signs
and situations are described in words. Fetched 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Exam format (for the landing and spec only, never asked in the bank)
- Service-public.gouv.fr, "Permis moto : permis A2", vérifié le 1er novembre 2025, fetched
  2026-11-22: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31083
  - ETM: 40 questions à choix multiples, 35 réponses justes ou plus to pass; themes are the
    rules of traffic and of riding a motorcycle and the right behaviour of the rider; the pass is
    kept 5 years; candidates for A2 who hold A1 for at most 5 years are exempt; enrolment through
    a driving school or as an independent candidate (fee kept off the bank and the facts table).
  - A2 from 18; 35 kW, 0,2 kW/kg, not derived from a machine of more than 70 kW; at least
    20 hours of training (8 on the track, 12 on the road), 15 hours after A1; practical test off
    the road (10 minutes, 6 manoeuvres) and on the road (32 minutes).
- Service-public.gouv.fr, "Permis moto : permis A1 ou permis 125 (moto légère)", vérifié le
  1er novembre 2025, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2832
  (A1 from 16; 125 cm³, 11 kW, 0,1 kW/kg; three-wheelers up to 15 kW; ETM 35 of 40.)
- Service-public.gouv.fr, "Permis de conduire : comment passer le code (ETG) ?", vérifié le
  27 avril 2026, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F33694
  (A1 and A2 candidates take the épreuve théorique générale motocyclette, ETM, not the ETG.)
- Arrêté du 20 avril 2012 fixant les conditions d'établissement, de délivrance et de validité du
  permis de conduire, art. 2 (in force since 4 December 2022), read on Légifrance 2026-11-22:
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000025803494
  - I.A: for A1 and A2, "une épreuve théorique générale motocyclette d'admissibilité" on traffic
    and riding rules and the right behaviour: vigilance and attitudes towards other users,
    perception, evaluation and decision, alcohol, drugs, medicines, emotions and fatigue, first
    aid, weather and road surface, the risks of other users' inexperience, vulnerable users,
    specific vehicle categories and their drivers' visibility, tunnels, precautions on leaving the
    vehicle, load and passengers, environment-friendly riding (noise, fuel, greenhouse gases),
    insurance and documents. The bank's categories follow this list, grouped by Tigertest.
  - II.B: passed with at least 35 right answers of 40; kept 5 years; A2 exemption after A1.
  - I.B: an A2 holder of at least two years reaches A by a seven-hour course, without a test.
- Arrêté du 23 avril 2012 fixant les modalités pratiques de l'examen du permis de conduire des
  catégories A1 et A2, art. 1 and annexe I (in force since 1 November 2025, arrêté du
  14 octobre 2025), read on Légifrance 2026-11-22:
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000025803553
  and https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000052399762
  - Art. 1, V: mandatory gear at the exam (helmet R431-1, gloves R431-1-2, long-sleeved jacket,
    trousers or overall, boots or high shoes; rubber boots and wind jackets not allowed).
  - Annexe I, II-2: the riding competences the bank's riding items follow: looking far ahead and
    to the sides, mirrors plus a look over the shoulder, adapting speed, the "trajectoire de
    sécurité" (right-hand bend: left half of the lane with a safety cushion from the centre line;
    left-hand bend: right half of the lane with a cushion from the verge; keep speed steady in
    the bend), safety gaps, warning lights, headlight flash and horn, hazard lights at the back of
    a queue. Annexe I, I-6: braking on a short distance, counter-steering, avoidance, the
    passenger holds on with both hands, feet on the footrests.
- No official time limit or per-theme count for the ETM was found on service-public or in the
  arrêtés; the facts table gives neither, and the 7/6/5/5/6/3/3/3/2 split per test is Tigertest's.

## Law (Code de la route on Légifrance, versions in force on 2026-11-22)
- Art. R412-11-3 (créé par décret n° 2025-33 du 9 janvier 2025, in force since 11 January 2025):
  circulation inter-files on motorways and dual carriageways of 70 km/h or more, L3e and L5e of
  at most one metre wide, between the two left-most lanes, when traffic is in unbroken queues;
  sufficient lateral gap; not in roadworks, snow or ice; warn others first; at most 50 km/h, at
  most 30 km/h if one queue is stopped; no overtaking another rider between the lanes; return to
  the normal flow when one queue goes faster:
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006159599/
- Titre III, chapitre Ier "Motocyclettes, tricycles et quadricycles à moteur, cyclomoteurs et
  cycles" (R431-1 to R431-12): helmet of approved type, fastened, for rider and passenger
  (R431-1); gloves conforming to the PPE rules for rider and passenger (R431-1-2); passengers only
  on a seat fixed to the vehicle, a dual seat counting as two (R431-5):
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006159608/
- Chapitre VI "Usage des dispositifs d'éclairage" (R416-1 to R416-20): in town, even in rain,
  the exemption from dipped beam on a well-lit road does not apply to motorcycles (R416-6);
  hazard lights, triangle and vest (R416-19):
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006159603/
- General rules as cited in the frcode bank, read for this bank in their rider context: speed
  (R413-2, R413-4, R413-5 learner and probationary limits), overtaking and lateral gaps (R414-4),
  priorities (R415-5 to R415-12), alcohol (R234-1), points and probation (L223-1, R223-1),
  phone and earphones (R412-6-1):
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006159600/

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it
- The ETM time limit and the share of video questions appear only on prep sites; the landing
  does not give them. Each official item has a photo or a video; ours are text items.
- The official ETM theme list (prep sites give eight or nine themes) could not be read on an
  official page; the categories follow art. 2, I.A of the arrêté du 20 avril 2012 instead.
- A B-licence holder's right to ride a 125 after two years and seven hours of training is on
  service-public F45, which did not load; the bank does not ask it.

## Added at the second review (2026-11-22)
- Code de la route art. R412-11-3 re-read on Légifrance 2026-11-22 (in Chrome): 50 km/h,
  30 km/h if one queue is stopped (5°), no overtaking another rider between the lanes (6°),
  warn others first (3°), not in roadworks, snow or ice (2°), L3e and L5e of at most one metre.
- Code de la route art. R417-10, II, 1° (décret n° 2022-31), read on Légifrance 2026-11-22:
  stopping or parking a motorcycle, motor tricycle or moped on a pavement is "gênant":
  https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074228/LEGISCTA000006159604/
- Code de la route art. R422-3 (priority at a crossing of a road and a railway goes to the
  vehicles running on the rails; applied to tramways by the Cour de cassation unless signs or an
  officer say otherwise), as reported 2026-11-22 by Le Monde du Droit, "Priorité au tramway":
  https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/public/282-droit-public-general/90355-priorite-au-tramway.html
- Panneau B3, "interdiction de dépasser tous les véhicules à moteur autres que ceux à deux roues
  sans side-car" (arrêté du 24 novembre 1967), Cerema IISR panel list, June 2022, read 2026-11-22:
  https://equipementsdelaroute.cerema.fr/IMG/pdf/01-panneaux_iisr_juin2022_cle26b686.pdf
- Arrêté du 23 avril 2012, annexe (fiches de l'interrogation orale), read on Légifrance 2026-11-22
  (https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000025803553): fiche 5 (absence of detection in
  nearly two thirds of accidents; lights on, good position in the lane), the accident-types
  fiche (a driver turning left fails to detect the rider and cuts across), fiche 6 (jacket with
  back protector), fiche 7 (tyre wear markers, pressures, over-inflation hurts grip on wet roads,
  brakes, ABS keeps control without shortening the stop), road surface clues (markings, manhole
  covers, greasy road, gravel, leaves) and weather (strong wind pushes the bike off line).
- Sécurité routière, leaflet "long trajet", July 2026, read 2026-11-22 (a break of at least a
  quarter of an hour every two hours):
  https://www.securite-routiere.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-07/nv_depliant_long_trajet_3v-exe_bd.pdf
- Removed at review as not supported by an official text: the left-third lane position, staggered
  group riding and chain tension items (replaced by detection, left-turn and tyre-pressure items),
  and a helmet sentence in the recovery-position explanation.

## Deliberately left out
- Fines, fees, points removed per offence and anything about the test itself.
- Helmet removal by a bystander and airbag vests (no official text read for them); daytime
  lights for motorcycles beyond R416-6; the abrogated reflective-vest rule (R431-1-2, 2013-2015).
