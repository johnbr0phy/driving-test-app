Typography: Greek question mark «;» at the end of every question stem (or a colon for a
completion stem), guillemets «» for titles and quotations, dates as «25 Μαρτίου», numbers with
a full stop for thousands (2.918 μέτρα) and a comma for decimals. Monotonic accents. Register:
questions neutral and impersonal; landing and dashboard copy address the reader in the singular
(«εσύ»), like the other native-language exams' informal copy.
