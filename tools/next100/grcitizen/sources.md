# Sources for `grcitizen` (Εξετάσεις ΠΕΓΠ, Greek naturalisation knowledge test)

Official sources first (the source watch reads the first three https URLs).

1. https://exetaseis-ithageneia.ypes.gr/ : the General Secretariat for Citizenship's exam platform
   (applications, guides, the Τράπεζα Θεμάτων). Checked 2026-11-07.
2. Οδηγός εξεταστικής διαδικασίας για τους υποψηφίους που εξετάζονται γραπτά, Απρίλιος 2026
   (PDF on the platform):
   https://exetaseis-ithageneia.ypes.gr/%CE%9F%CE%B4%CE%B7%CE%B3%CF%8C%CF%82_%CE%B5%CE%BE%CE%B5%CF%84%CE%B1%CF%83%CF%84%CE%B9%CE%BA%CE%AE%CF%82_%CE%B4%CE%B9%CE%B1%CE%B4%CE%B9%CE%BA%CE%B1%CF%83%CE%AF%CE%B1%CF%82_%CF%85%CF%80%CE%BF%CF%88%CE%B7%CF%86%CE%AF%CF%89%CE%BD_%CF%80%CE%BF%CF%85_%CE%B5%CE%BE%CE%B5%CF%84%CE%AC%CE%B6%CE%BF%CE%BD%CF%84%CE%B1%CE%B9_%CE%B3%CF%81%CE%B1%CF%80%CF%84%CE%AC_%CE%91%CF%80%CF%81%CE%AF%CE%BB%CE%B9%CE%BF%CF%82%20_2026.pdf
   Read in full 2026-11-07. Confirms:
   - Three parts. Part A (written): Θεματικές Ενότητες (geography, history, political institutions,
     culture) plus the language unit «Κατανόηση και Παραγωγή Γραπτού Λόγου»; 150 minutes, may leave
     after 50. Then a 15-minute break. Part B: listening comprehension, 10 questions (12 to 15 min).
     Part C: oral production, up to 15 minutes, individually.
   - Θεματικές Ενότητες: 20 questions, 4 geography, 4 culture, 6 history, 6 institutions
     (Θεσμοί του Πολιτεύματος).
   - Written language: 18 questions plus a short text of 80 to 100 words.
   - Topics are drawn electronically on the day by the Επιστημονική Επιτροπή from the published
     Τράπεζα Θεμάτων; the committee may reorder the position of the correct answers (so the
     thematic items are closed questions with options).
   - Scoring: each correct thematic answer 2 points, maximum 40. Language maximum 60
     (written 30 = 9 + 9 + 12, listening 15 = 10 x 1.5, speaking 15).
   - Pass: at least 70/100 overall, with at least 40/60 in Greek and at least 20/40 in the
     thematic units.
3. Οδηγός εξεταστικής διαδικασίας για τους υποψηφίους που εξετάζονται προφορικά, Απρίλιος 2026
   (copy on esamea.gr): https://www.esamea.gr/storage/app/uploads/public/697/b20/35c/697b2035c8d84020047728.pdf
   Read 2026-11-07. Candidates over 62 or with a learning difficulty answer the same 20 thematic
   questions (4/4/6/6) orally before a three-member committee after 45 to 120 minutes of
   preparation; candidates with a certified disability of 67% or more answer 10 (2/2/3/3), worth 4
   points each. Same pass rule.
4. Υπουργείο Παιδείας, Φ.251/26149/Α5, 3-3-2026 (call for examiners for the sitting of 26 April 2026):
   https://alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-03/pegp.pdf . Read 2026-11-07. Confirms the four
   thematic units (Γεωγραφία, Ιστορία, Πολιτισμός, Θεσμοί του Πολιτεύματος), Greek at a level
   comparable to B1, 70 exam centres (Attica, Thessaloniki, Komotini, Ioannina, Corinth, Tripoli,
   Kalamata, Patras, Chalkida, Larisa, Volos, Mytilene, Chios, Rhodes, Mykonos, Thira, Heraklion,
   Chania) and 9 special centres. The detailed rules are the Secretary General's decision
   https://www.ypes.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/apof10787-20250226.pdf (could not be fetched as a
   PDF on 2026-11-07; a newer decision 21536/22-4-2026 is listed at
   https://www.ypes.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/apof21536-20260422.pdf , also not fetchable).
5. Τράπεζα Θεμάτων ΠΕΓΠ, Νοέμβριος 2026 (answers file for the oral candidates, language unit):
   https://exetaseis-ithageneia.ypes.gr/PAPYROS/services/CustomServices/getExamTopic/1057901 .
   Opened 2026-11-07 only to confirm that the bank is published and updated per sitting. No item
   was read for or copied into this bank.
6. Lawspot, 16-07-2026, «Εξετάσεις Πιστοποιητικού Επάρκειας Γνώσεων Πολιτογράφησης: Έναρξη
   υποβολής αιτήσεων»: https://www.lawspot.gr/nomika-nea/exetaseis-pistopoietikou-eparkeias-gnoseon-politographeses-enarxe-upoboles-aiteseon/
   Checked 2026-11-07: next sitting Sunday 15 November 2026, applications 15 July to 10 September
