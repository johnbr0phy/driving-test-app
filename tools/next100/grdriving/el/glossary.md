# grdriving Greek (el-GR) glossary

Terms from the Greek Highway Code (Κώδικας Οδικής Κυκλοφορίας, ΚΟΚ, ν. 5209/2025), the Ministry of
Infrastructure and Transport's theory test pages and the usual wording of Greek driving school
material. Written before the translation; every source file follows it.

Register: questions and explanations in the polite plural (εσείς: "πρέπει να σταματήσετε"), as
Greek official and driving school material addresses the candidate. App copy and the landing in
the singular (εσύ), the house UI register of the existing Greek pages (grcitizen, grinsure).
