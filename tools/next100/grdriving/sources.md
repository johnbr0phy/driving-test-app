# Sources for `grdriving` (Greece driving theory test, category B)

All 200 questions are original. No official question bank item, past paper or commercial app item
was opened or copied (the Ministry's question bank is not used; practice apps seen in search
results were not opened). Signs are described in words. Checked 2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Who runs the test and how
- Law 4850/2021, article 6 (theory test), via taxheaven.gr (read 2026-11-01):
  every applicant for a first licence sits a theory test of multiple-choice questionnaires (and
  case studies, as a ministerial decision sets); questions are drawn at random from the Theory
  Test Question Bank run by the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, reviewed every two years;
  the material is available in foreign languages besides Greek, in Greek with audio, and in
  Greek Sign Language. The article does not list the foreign languages.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4850/2021/arthro/6
- Law 4850/2021 draft ("Οδηγώντας με Ασφάλεια"), CNN Greece, 13 Oct 2021: accompanied driving
  from 17 on a provisional licence until 18; questionnaires in other languages, Greek with audio
  and Greek Sign Language; phones and smart devices banned in the exam room.
  https://www.cnn.gr/ellada/story/285219/adeies-odigisis-allazei-to-orio-ilikias-stoys-ypopsifioys-kamera-stin-exetasi
- NRSO (NTUA road safety observatory), accompanied driving page: ministerial decree of December
  2021 implements accompanied driving from 17. https://www.nrso.ntua.gr/nrss2030/accompanied-driving/?lang=en
- Format, GUIDES ONLY (not confirmed on a ministry page; the ministry and opengov.gr returned
  403/WAF pages on 2026-11-01): 30 multiple-choice questions, 35 minutes, pass with at most one
  mistake (29 of 30). gocar.gr, "Πόσα λάθη επιτρέπονται στις εξετάσεις οδήγησης;", 21 Feb 2025:
  https://www.gocar.gr/useful/law/41699,posa_la8h_exetaseis_odhghshs.html ; autoviatest.com Greece
  facts page (commercial, same figures): https://autoviatest.com/en-GB/driving-test/greece/facts
- Languages, GUIDE/PARLIAMENT ONLY: a Hellenic Parliament question refers to questionnaires in
  English, Russian and Albanian and to the Ministry studying more (date unclear); a February 2026
  question asks for Turkish. Tigertest copy says only "offered in English and other languages".
  https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/UserFiles/c0d5184d-7550-4265-8e0b-078e1bc7375a/13215920.pdf
- Training through a licensed driving school before the test: guides (autoviatest) and the
  structure of Law 4850/2021; no hour counts are asked.

## Rules of the road (Highway Code, Law 5209/2025, FEK A' 100/13.6.2025, applied from 13 Sept 2025)
- Speed limits, Law 5209/2025 Article 24 (taxheaven.gr, read 2026-11-01; corrected by the
  reviewer from the "Article 20" that gocar.gr gives): built-up areas 30 km/h; 50 km/h on one-way
  roads with at least two lanes separated by markings, on two-way roads with at least two marked
  lanes in each direction and on two-way roads with a central island; all unless signs say
  otherwise. Outside built-up areas cars 90, expressways 110, motorways 130; unpaved roads 40;
  ΕΠΗΟ (e-scooters) 25 km/h and barred from roads with a car limit above 50 km/h.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/24
  Start date: Article 114 par. 1 keeps the general 50 km/h built-up limit "until 1 January 2026",
  so the 30 km/h default applies from 1 January 2026 (gocar.gr's "1 November 2025" is wrong).
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/114
  The bank asks the 30 km/h default only for a single-lane town street and the 50 km/h figure
  only for a one-way street with two marked lanes.
- Law 5209/2025 published in FEK A' 100, 13 June 2025; applied in full from 13 September 2025
  (lawspot.gr commentary).
- Article 46 (alcohol, drugs, medicines; taxheaven.gr, read 2026-11-01): 0.50 g/l blood or
  0.25 mg/l breath; 0.50-0.80 g/l E3-B with licence removal; 0.80-1.10 g/l fine and 90 days;
  above 1.10 g/l at least two months' imprisonment, fine and licence and registration removed for
  180 days; refusing the test is presumed above 1.10; driving under drugs or medicines that may
  impair driving is banned. https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/46
  The 0.20 g/l limit for novice (under two years) and professional drivers is not in the text of
  Article 46 as summarised; it is given by Your Europe, ETSC and Greek guides (e-nomothesia.gr,
  testsimata.gr) as current. Kept, cited to those.
- Article 30 (junctions and priority; taxheaven.gr, read 2026-11-01): priority by signs, traffic
  lights override signs; with no signs priority to the vehicle from the right; vehicles coming
  from an unpaved road, pedestrian street, driveway, car park or filling station give way; trams
  and trains have priority; motorways, national roads and expressways have priority; no special
  roundabout paragraph. https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/30
- Article 22: on steep narrow roads the descending vehicle generally gives way (exceptions for
  convoys, heavier vehicles, buses). https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/22
- Article 31: level crossings (stop at flashing lights, never enter while barriers move, clear the
  track or warn train drivers if stalled). https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/31
- Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, "Θεωρητική εξέταση υποψηφίων οδηγών μέσω Η/Υ
  (ΜΣΘΕΥΟ)" (loaded 2026-11-01): computer test, each candidate's questionnaire drawn at random
  from the database, answer order shuffled each time, result printed at the end, four licence
  groups (car, motorcycle, lorry, bus); TestDrive practice program (also with audio). The page
  gives no question count, time or pass mark, so 30/35/29 stays "guides report".
  https://www.yme.gov.gr/metafores/adeies-odigisis/theoritiki-exetasi-ypopsifion-odigon-meso-i-y-mstheyo
- Ministry guide to the new Code for the regions (pin.gov.gr PDF, June 2025): escalating
  penalties for repeat offences (υποτροπή); phone use, seat belts, helmets; alcohol bands 0.50 to
  0.80, 0.80 to 1.10 and above 1.10 g/l with criminal proceedings above 1.10; unbelted passengers
  fined and the driver too. No fine or point amounts are used in the bank.
  https://pin.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Νέος-ΚΟΚ-Οδηγός-για-Περιφέρειες.pdf
- EU Your Europe, Greece road rules (read 2026-11-01): alcohol 0.5 g/l, 0.2 g/l for novice and
  professional drivers and motorcycle and moped riders; seat belts and child restraints mandatory;
  phone only hands-free; amber means stop unless you cannot stop safely; warning triangle, first
  aid kit and fire extinguisher as car equipment; helmets.
  https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/driving-abroad/road-rules-and-safety/greece/index_en.htm
- ETSC, drink driving in Greece: novice drivers (under two years), professional drivers and
  riders at 0.2 g/l. https://etsc.eu/issues/drink-driving/drink-driving-in-greece/
- Roundabout priority, gocar.gr and autotriti.gr explainers on Article 26 of the Code (read
  2026-11-01): priority at each roundabout is set by its signs; with a give way or STOP sign at
  the entry, circulating traffic has priority; with no signs the general priority-from-the-right
  rule applies, which the press notes differs from most of Europe. No 2025 change found.
  https://www.gocar.gr/useful/law/47921,kyklikos_komvos_proteraiothta__.html
- General rules shared with the Vienna Convention on Road Signs and Signals and the EU
  (sign shapes and colours, markings, police signals, level crossing countdown boards, category B
  up to 3,500 kg and eight passenger seats under Directive 2006/126/EC, tyre tread 1.6 mm under
  Directive 89/459/EEC, child restraints under 1.35 m under Directive 2003/20/EC, 112).
- Greek terms used once with a gloss: άδεια κυκλοφορίας (registration certificate), δίπλωμα
  οδήγησης (driving licence), KTEO (vehicle technical inspection centres), ΛΕΑ (motorway emergency
  lane), φιλικός διακανονισμός (European accident statement), αδιέξοδο (dead end).

## Left out on purpose
Fines, point totals and withdrawal periods (they changed in 2025); the exact built-up 50 km/h
exceptions beyond the one stated; minimum distances for warning triangles and parking from
junctions (not confirmed); KTEO intervals; driving school hour counts; fees; the practical test.
