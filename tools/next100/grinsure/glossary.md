# Glossary for `grinsure` (Greek, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations neutral, impersonal or third person ("ο λήπτης", "ο
διαμεσολαβητής"), like the official texts; situations about "ένας πελάτης". Landing and dashboard
copy informal "εσύ" (consumer-app norm, as in `i18n/el.ts`). Monotonic accents. Greek question
mark is ";" (ASCII semicolon, as in the platform's Greek UI); quotes « »; decimal comma and
thousands point (20.000 ευρώ); no em-dashes. Abbreviations: ΤτΕ, Γ.Ε.ΜΗ., ΕΒΠ, ΓΚΠΔ, ν. (νόμος),
π.δ. (προεδρικό διάταγμα).
