# Sources for `grinsure` (Bank of Greece insurance intermediary certification, in Greek)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Greek from the laws and EU rules below,
on the topics of Annex XIII of ν. 4583/2018. Nothing was copied from the Bank of Greece's
published question bank (the Τράπεζα Θεμάτων with answers), a past paper, a training provider's
material or a commercial app. The official bank is public and widely memorised: our wording will
not match it, and the landing says so. Checked 2026-11-07 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- Πράξη Εκτελεστικής Επιτροπής της ΤτΕ 153/2/08.01.2019 (ΦΕΚ Β' 27/15.01.2019), read via
  taxheaven on 2026-11-07 (bankofgreece.gr returned 403 to automated reads that day):
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/30155/153-2-2019
  - Art. 3: three certificates: Πιστοποιητικό Επαγγελματικών Γνώσεων Ασφαλιστικού Πράκτορα,
    Μεσίτη Ασφαλίσεων και Αντασφαλίσεων, and Επενδυτικών Προϊόντων βασιζόμενων σε Ασφάλιση. The
    first two are required for registration as an intermediary and for staff distributing
    insurance at insurers; the third for distributing insurance-based investment products.
  - Art. 5: syllabus indicatively the requirements of Annex XIII of ν. 4583/2018: sections I and
    III (non-life and life knowledge) for agents and brokers, with emphasis on arts. 3, 4 and
    27-35; section II (IBIP) for the investment products certificate, with emphasis on the
    conduct and information duties of arts. 27-40.
  - Art. 6: «Οι εξετάσεις είναι γραπτές, με ερωτήσεις πολλαπλής επιλογής»; each exam draws at
    least 60 questions from the question bank, which the Bank publishes on its website with the
    correct answers (and worked solutions where needed) and keeps updated.
  - Art. 7(5): «Επιτυχής εξέταση θεωρείται εκείνη στην οποία ο υποψήφιος έχει απαντήσει σωστά
    στο 90% των εξεταζόμενων ερωτήσεων.» Art. 7(6): only a list of successful candidates is
    published, no scores. Application online with a fee (not stated here).
  - Duration: not in the Act. Not found in any source read. Not stated anywhere.
- Exam schedule 2026, first half (insuranceworld.gr, 21 November 2025, reporting the Bank's
  announcement): Heraklion 7 Feb, Athens 14-15 Feb, Ioannina 14 Mar, Thessaloniki 4-5 Apr,
  Athens 23-24 May, Thessaloniki 27-28 Jun; agents on Saturdays, brokers and IBIP on Sundays;
  enquiries sec.certifications@bankofgreece.gr.
  https://insuranceworld.gr/tte-to-programma-exetaseon-pistopoiisis-asfaliston-gia-to-a%CE%84-6mino-2026/
- Second half 2026 (search summaries of asfalisinet.gr and aagora.gr, 2026): Heraklion 26 Sep,
  Athens 3 Oct, Thessaloniki mid-October, Athens 5-6 Dec, Thessaloniki 12 Dec. Pages returned
  403; dates not used in the bank or the landing, which says only "several times a year".
  https://www.aagora.gr/tte-exetaseis-pistopoiisis-stin-athina-stis-5-kai-6-dekemvriou/

- Review 2026-11-07: taxheaven text of Act 153/2/2019 re-read; art. 6(4) «δεν μπορούν να είναι
  λιγότερες από εξήντα (60) ανά εξέταση» and art. 7(5) 90% confirmed, no amendment noted. The
  4-year (damage) and 5-year (personal insurance) limitation of ν. 2496/1997 art. 10 confirmed
  from an Areios Pagos summary (insurancedaily.gr) and ΝΣΚ opinion 103/2014 (taxheaven.gr).

## Not confirmed (left out or labelled)
- Question bank size after the 2022 revision ("about 600 questions each for agents and brokers,
  a separate set for IBIP"): from the task research notes citing nextdeal.gr; nextdeal.gr
  returned 403. Not stated on the landing beyond "a published question bank".
- Whether any later Act amended 153/2/2019 (number of questions, pass mark). A search turned up
  wording elsewhere of 80% where the syllabus is split into units, 90% otherwise, but no text
  tying it to this exam; the landing uses the 90% of art. 7(5) and says to check the current
  announcement.
- Exam time, fee, language (questions are published in Greek; no foreign-language exam found).
- Pass rates (nextdeal.gr commentary: roughly half of candidates fail in 2024): not stated.
- No topic weights are published: the 7/8/8/8/8/7/7/7 split is Tigertest's.

## Law and rules the questions are written from
- ν. 4583/2018 (ΦΕΚ Α' 212/18.12.2018), transposing Directive (EU) 2016/97 (IDD): definitions of
  distribution and intermediaries (agent, coordinator, broker, ancillary), registration in the
  register kept at the Γ.Ε.ΜΗ. by the chambers, good repute and knowledge, CPD of at least 15
  hours a year, professional indemnity insurance, client money protection, general principle,
  pre-contract information (identity, register, advice, complaints, remuneration, holdings over
  10%), demands and needs, IPID, durable medium and paper by default, cross-selling, POG,
  IBIP conduct (conflicts, inducements, suitability and appropriateness, execution-only),
  supervision and sanctions by the Bank of Greece.
  Directive text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/97/oj
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1469 (IPID format); Delegated Regulations (EU)
  2017/2358 (POG) and 2017/2359 (IBIP conduct), amended by 2021/1257 (sustainability
  preferences).
- Regulation (EU) 1286/2014 (PRIIPs) and Delegated Regulation 2017/653: KID of at most three
  A4 sides, summary risk indicator 1 to 7, performance scenarios, costs.
- ν. 2496/1997 on the insurance contract, as amended (ν. 4364/2016 and later): parties and
  terms, duty to disclose the risk (intent vs negligence, proportional reduction), aggravation
  of risk to be declared within 14 days, premium and termination, duty to mitigate and its
  costs, under-insurance and first-loss, over-insurance and multiple insurance, subrogation,
  transfer of the insured thing, life insurance on another's life with written consent,
  beneficiaries, limitation (4 years damage insurance, 5 years personal insurance).
  Article text could not be fetched (e-nomothesia 403/503); the 14 days and 4/5 years were
  confirmed from search summaries of nextdeal.gr and a court summary. Article numbers are not
  cited in questions.
- ν. 4364/2016 (Solvency II): SCR, ORSA, home-state supervision.
- ν. 3867/2010: Εγγυητικό Κεφάλαιο Ιδιωτικής Ασφάλισης Ζωής.
- π.δ. 237/1986: compulsory motor liability; Επικουρικό Κεφάλαιο; Green Card.
- ν. 4557/2018 (AML/CFT): obliged persons incl. life insurers and their intermediaries, CDD,
  beneficial owner (over 25% indication), PEPs, enhanced due diligence, reporting to the Αρχή
  Καταπολέμησης της Νομιμοποίησης Εσόδων από Εγκληματικές Δραστηριότητες, no tipping off,
  five-year record keeping.
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and ν. 4624/2019: special categories (health), minimisation,
  data subject rights, breach notification within 72 hours, Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων.

## Kept out
- Premiums, fees, tax rates, minimum PI or motor cover amounts, guarantee fund limits.
- Officeholders and current events.
- Facts about the certification exam itself (no question asks them).
- Siblings: grcitizen (civics), grsecurity (private security) and grdriving/grmoto (road rules)
  share no topic with this bank. The English-language ieqfa, llqp, life-health, mypce, hkiiqe1
  and cemap banks cover similar principles in other jurisdictions; this bank cites Greek and EU
  law and was written in Greek, not from their stems.
