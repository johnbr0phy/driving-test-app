# Sources for `grmoto` (Greece motorcycle theory test, categories A1, A2 and A)

All 200 questions are original. No official question bank item, past paper or commercial app item
was opened or copied (the Ministry's question bank and the TestDrive program are not used; the
practice sites below were read only for format claims, never for questions). Signs are not asked
(they are `grdriving`'s lane); situations are described in words. Checked 2026-11-07 unless noted.

## Who runs the test and how
- Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, "Θεωρητική εξέταση υποψηφίων οδηγών μέσω Η/Υ
  (ΜΣΘΕΥΟ)" (loaded 2026-11-07): computer theory test in four licence groups, car, motorcycle
  (δίκυκλο), lorry and bus; each candidate's questionnaire drawn at random from the database,
  answer order shuffled, result printed at the end. No question count, time or pass mark given.
  https://www.yme.gov.gr/metafores/adeies-odigisis/theoritiki-exetasi-ypopsifion-odigon-meso-i-y-mstheyo
- Law 4850/2021, article 6 (as read for `grdriving`, 2026-11-01): multiple-choice questionnaires
  drawn at random from the Ministry's question bank; material available in foreign languages
  besides Greek, in Greek with audio and in Greek Sign Language. Languages not listed.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4850/2021/arthro/6
- Format, GUIDES ONLY (not on an official page): motorcycle (licence A) theory test of 30
  questions, pass at 29 correct (at most one mistake), the same as the car test. autoviatest.com,
  Greece motorcycle page (commercial), read 2026-11-07:
  https://autoviatest.com/en-GB/driving-test/greece/motorcycle . gocar.gr gives 30 questions,
  35 minutes, one mistake allowed for the car test (21 Feb 2025):
  https://www.gocar.gr/useful/law/41699,posa_la8h_exetaseis_odhghshs.html . No source gives the
  motorcycle test's time, so Tigertest states none.
- Language choice, GUIDE ONLY: open-exam-prep.com describes a language selection step in the
  ΜΣΘΕΥΟ theory exam and says the count, time and pass mark are not verified on Mitos/gov.gr
  (read 2026-11-07): https://open-exam-prep.com/practice/gr-theory-a . Copy says "offered in
  foreign languages; check with your Transport Directorate or driving school".
- Licence ages, NOT CONFIRMED: secondary sources conflict (autoviatest gives 16 for A; Wikipedia
  derived pages give AM 16, A1 18, A2 20; the EU directive gives A at 24 direct or 20 after two
  years of A2). No age is asked in the bank or stated as fact on the landing.
- Category limits (EU Directive 2006/126/EC, applied in Greece): AM mopeds to 45 km/h; A1 up to
  125 cc, 11 kW, 0.1 kW/kg; A2 up to 35 kW, 0.2 kW/kg, not derived from a vehicle of more than
  double its power; A unrestricted. The odigostoupoliti.eu guide confirms 125 cc / 11 kW / 0.1
  kW/kg for Greek A1:
  https://www.odigostoupoliti.eu/pote-gia-tin-odigisi-motosikletas-arkei-adeia-odigisis-aftokinitou/amp/
- Car licence holders and 125 cc bikes, GUIDE ONLY (landing note, not asked): Law 4784/2021
  art. 23 lets a category B holder aged 27+ with 6 years on B ride an A1 bike in Greece after 5
  hours of lessons and no test (code 121), per odigostoupoliti.eu, read 2026-11-07 (link above).

## Highway Code (Law 5209/2025, FEK A' 100/13.6.2025, applied from 13 Sept 2025), via taxheaven.gr, read 2026-11-07
- Article 21 (overtaking, lanes, dense traffic): overtake on the left; on the right only past a
  vehicle that has signalled and moved over to turn left; no overtaking when the driver ahead has
  signalled to overtake, just before or at a junction (except past a bicycle, moped or motorcycle
  without a sidecar, or on a priority road or controlled junction), just before or on a level
  crossing without working barriers (unless lights control it), at a marked pedestrian crossing,
  in tunnels with one lane each way, past a school bus stopped with hazard lights for pupils;
  being overtaken, keep right and do not speed up. §9: in dense traffic lane changes only to turn
  or park; motorcycles and mopeds without a sidecar may move between lanes of the same direction
  on roads with at least two lanes each way at no more than 20 km/h, other vehicles stationary,
  dipped headlights on, giving way and stopping if needed.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/21
  (Press on the draft said 25 km/h; the enacted text says 20 km/h. The bank uses 20.)
- Article 33 (motorways, expressways, tunnels): barred to vehicles that cannot exceed 70 km/h on
  a level road; on three or more lanes the left lane is for overtaking by cars or motorcycles,
  lorries, buses and combinations over 7 m in the two right lanes; emergency lane only for
  emergencies and breakdowns; tunnels need dipped or driving lights even when lit, engine off in a
  prolonged stop. https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/33
- Article 37 (passengers): no carrying that endangers anyone; not more than the registration
  allows; motorcycle or moped passenger only on the pillion seat and able to rest feet on the
  footrests; a child aged five or under on a motorcycle or moped must be in an approved restraint
  system; the driver checks belts and the passenger's helmet.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/37
- Article 38 (stopping and parking): two-wheelers without a sidecar may park side by side or at an
  angle to the kerb; no parking on pavements, squares or cycle lanes unless signed; mopeds and
  motorcycles without a sidecar exempt from placing a warning device when forced to stop on the
  road. https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/38
- Article 44 (riders): no being towed, no towing or pushing objects (cyclists excepted), no
  carrying objects that obstruct riding, no riding abreast, no leading animals, no earphones
  (rider-to-rider and instructor headsets exempt), phones and recording devices only in a dedicated
  holder; both hands on the bars except to signal; passenger only on a permanent maker-fitted seat
  or in a fitted sidecar basket; riders must keep to a bicycle/moped lane where one exists; mopeds
  barred from full-width cycle roads; riders delivering goods wear reflective clothing at night.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/44
- Article 46 (alcohol): 0.50 g/l blood / 0.25 mg/l breath for every road vehicle; no lower rider
  limit in the article text. Your Europe and ETSC give 0.2 g/l for motorcycle and moped riders;
  that figure is already asked in `grdriving`, so this bank asks no alcohol figure.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/46
- Article 78 (motorcycle lights): equipment list only, no daytime-lights rule; the bank asks
  dipped lights only where Articles 21 and 33 require them.
  https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5209/2025/arthro/78
- Helmets: the article requiring the rider's own helmet was not located on taxheaven (Article 44
  covers only cyclists' and e-scooter riders' helmets; Article 37 covers the passenger check).
  Helmet use for riders and passengers is stated by EU Your Europe (Greece road rules) and by the
  Hellenic Police's 2025-2026 helmet campaign under Law 5209/2025 (protothema, motomag).
  https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/driving-abroad/road-rules-and-safety/greece/index_en.htm
  https://en.protothema.gr/2025/07/01/campaign-zero-tolerance-in-the-non-use-of-helmets-by-the-elas-intensive-checks-throughout-the-country/
- Helmet approval: UN ECE Regulation 22 (22.05 / 22.06).

## Riding technique
General motorcycle safety practice (braking, countersteering, cornering, road position, hazards,
protective gear), written in Tigertest's words with Greek settings. Braking distance quadrupling
with doubled speed and 72 km/h = 20 m/s are arithmetic.

## Kept clear of
`grdriving` (no road sign items, no priority-from-the-right, tram, filling station, roundabout
sign, zebra, pavement parking or alcohol figure items, all of which it asks) and the Greek
siblings grcitizen, grinsure and grsecurity (no overlap of subject).

## Left out on purpose
Licence ages, fines, points and licence withdrawal periods, fees, driving-school hours, the
practical test, daytime headlight rules (not in Article 78), the B-licence 125 cc route (landing
only), and any warning-sign or sign-picture item.
