# Sources for `grsecurity` (Greece private security personnel certification, ΕΟΠΠΕΠ)

All checked 2026-11-07. The official question bank (τράπεζα θεμάτων, posted on kemea.gr) was
deliberately NOT opened or read: every item in this bank is original, written from the law and
the course outline below.

## Official and primary sources (the source watch reads the first three https URLs)

1. https://www.eoppep.gr/ : ΕΟΠΠΕΠ, the certifying body. The site did not render its security
   pages to the fetch tool; the 2026 call's application form is at
   https://eoppep.gr/images/security/aitisi_symmetoxis_security_2026_01.pdf (linked from the
   alfavita.gr notice below). Exam details below therefore come from ΕΟΠΠΕΠ notices as reprinted
   by alfavita.gr.
2. https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/2518/1997 : Law 2518/1997 «Άσκηση επαγγέλματος ιδιωτικής
   ασφάλειας» as amended (consolidated text). Articles read:
   - art. 1 (activities α to ια; «προσωπικό ασφαλείας»; para. 3 own-premises guarding),
   - art. 2 (licence conditions: Greek, co-ethnic or EU citizen, 18+, listed convictions, no
     mental illness or drug use; para. 5 single uniform type set by joint decision; para. 1B
     suspended sentences, five years),
   - art. 3 (work permit A΄ or B΄ category; issued by the Police Directorate or Security
     Directorate; personal, valid five years, renewable for an equal period; appeal within ten
     days to the General Police Director; armed staff must have completed military service),
   - art. 4 (company duties: no impression of public or police authority; uniform or an
     «ατομική βεβαίωση» with company, employee, work permit number and post; bulletproof vest for
     activity α, vest and helmet for cash transport γ; no siren or flashing light on vehicles;
     dogs only inside guarded buildings or enclosed areas; respect constitutional rights;
     monthly staff lists to the police; staff may not use the title «αστυνομικός»; staff notify
     changes to permit conditions within 15 days),
   - art. 5 (weapons: only with a licence for specific posts; kept in special company storage and
     logged; carrying banned before and after duty; every use reported at once in writing to the
     police; medical tests and training at least every three years).
   Law 5187/2025 (ΦΕΚ Α' 48/21.03.2025) arts. 158 to 160 amended arts. 2, 4 and 9 (companies'
   licence committee and obligations, administrative sanctions); nothing used here changed.
3. https://mitos.gov.gr/ (Εθνικό Μητρώο Διαδικασιών, «Ανανέωση Άδειας Εργασίας Προσωπικού
   Ασφαλείας Ιδιωτικής Επιχείρησης Παροχής Υπηρεσιών Ασφάλειας»): renewal valid 5 years;
   application at the latest one month before expiry; psychiatrist's certificate; criminal record
   checked ex officio; issued by the Hellenic Police. Fees on that page are NOT used.
4. https://elearningekpa.gr/uploads/courses/brochures/93_vasiko_programma_katartisis_prosopikou_idiotikis_asfaleias.pdf :
   ΕΚΠΑ brochure for the KEMEA «Βασικό Πρόγραμμα Κατάρτισης Προσωπικού Ιδιωτικής Ασφάλειας»
   (ΕΟΠΠΕΠ board decision ΔΠ/11556, ΦΕΚ Β' 4491/25-08-2022, amended by 15926, ΦΕΚ Β' 3151/2024):
   105 hours, ten lessons: Εκπαιδευτική Εισαγωγή; Ο Κλάδος και το Προσωπικό της Ιδιωτικής
   Ασφάλειας, Επικοινωνία και Κοινωνικές Δεξιότητες; Υγιεινή και Ασφάλεια στον Χώρο Εργασίας;
   Βασικές Πρώτες Βοήθειες; Θέματα Νομοθεσίας; Βασικές Έννοιες και Θέματα Ασφαλείας,
   Εγκληματικότητα; Αυτοπροστασία, Αυτοάμυνα, Εξοπλισμός, Μέσα, Διαδικασίες Ασφαλείας;
   Καθήκοντα στην Πρόληψη του Εγκλήματος; Διαχείριση Περιστατικών και Κρίσεων; Πυροπροστασία
   και Πυρασφάλεια. The nine categories of this bank are those lessons (the introduction left out).
   Completing the programme is the prerequisite for the ΕΟΠΠΕΠ exams; the permit sought is
   Category A.
5. https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/4620/2019/arthro/275 : Code of Criminal Procedure (ν. 4620/2019)
   art. 275: in flagrant felonies and misdemeanours police have a duty, and every citizen the
   right, to arrest; for offences prosecuted on complaint, arrest only once a complaint has been
   made, even orally, to the person entitled to arrest. Art. 242 defines «αυτόφωρο».
6. Penal Code (ν. 4619/2019): art. 22 άμυνα (necessary defence against an unjust, present
   attack on oneself or another), art. 23 υπέρβαση άμυνας, art. 25 κατάσταση ανάγκης, art. 325
   παράνομη κατακράτηση, art. 380 ληστεία. Used at concept level, numbers cited only for 22.
7. Law 3850/2010 (Κώδικας νόμων για την υγεία και την ασφάλεια των εργαζομένων): employer's
   written risk assessment, technical safety officer and occupational physician, employee duties,
   right to leave in serious and imminent danger.
8. GDPR (Regulation 2016/679), Law 4624/2019, Hellenic Data Protection Authority guidance on
   CCTV (signage before the monitored area; no cameras in toilets or changing rooms). Law
   4443/2016 on equal treatment.
9. European Resuscitation Council guidelines 2021 (first aid and basic life support): 30:2,
   100 to 120 a minute, 5 to 6 cm, back blows then abdominal thrusts, cool burns about 20 minutes,
   recovery position. Greek emergency numbers 100 (Police), 199 (Fire Service), 166 (ΕΚΑΒ),
   108 (Coast Guard). ΟΑΣΠ earthquake advice «Σκύψε, Κάλυψε, Κρατήσου». EN 2 fire classes.

## Exam format (EOPPEP notices as reprinted by alfavita.gr)

- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/449987_eoppep-oloklirothike-o-kyklos-ton-exetaseon-pistopoiisis-prosopikoy-idiotikis
  (17.06.2024): candidates answered «είκοσι (20) ισοδύναμες ερωτήσεις πολλαπλής επιλογής»
  selected by the Central Examination Committee by electronic draw.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/391766_eoppep-exetaseis-pistopoiisis-epaggelmation-eidikotitas-prosopiko-idiotikis
  (12.09.2022): theory part is a written test (no exit before 60 minutes); then the practical
  part, an oral test in groups of three before a three-member committee, about 20 minutes per
  candidate; both parts compulsory.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/464548_eoppep-bgikan-ta-apotelesmata-ton-exetaseon-pistopoiisis-prosopikoy-idiotikis
  (13.12.2024) and 459915 (17.10.2024): «ΤΕΛΙΚΟΣ ΒΑΘΜΟΣ» 1 to 9 fail, 10 to 20 pass.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/465195_eoppep-prokiryxi-exetaseon-pistopoiisis-epaggelmatikis-katartisis-gia-prosopiko
  (1st period 2025) and https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/498546_eoppep-programma-exetaseon-pistopoiisis-gia-prosopiko-idiotikis-asfaleias
  (02.12.2025, 1st period 2026: applications from 16/01/2026, indicative exam dates 14-15/02/2026
  and/or 21-22/02/2026): question bank covers both theory and practical parts; eligibility per
  Law 2518/1997 art. 2(1); KEMEA 105-hour programme required. Fees mentioned there are NOT used.
- https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/555276_pistopoiisi-eoppep-2026-bgainoyn-ta-apotelesmata-gia-prosopiko-idiotikis
  (2026 results expected 3 August 2026; recurring cohort).

Not confirmed: whether the 2026 calls still use exactly 20 theory questions, how the theory and
practical marks combine into the 1 to 20 final grade, and the theory time limit. The landing
states only what the notices above say; passPct 70 (14 of 20) is Tigertest's readiness target.
