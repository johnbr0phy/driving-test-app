# gulfmd sources (fetched 2026-11-25)

All 200 questions are original. They were written from the SCFHS SMLE blueprint and the primary
clinical guidelines below, never from an authority's question bank, the SCFHS practice test, a
Prometric item, a recall collection or any prep company's or app's questions.

## Exam outline and format (authorities' own documents)
- Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, "Saudi Medical Licensure Examination (SMLE) Examination
  Content Guideline" with the 2026 blueprint
  (https://scfhs.org.sa/sites/default/files/2026-05/Saudi%20Medical%20Licensure%20Examination%20%28SMLE%29%20Blueprint_2026_0.pdf),
  fetched 2026-11-25: computer-based, two parts of 100 questions, 120 minutes each, 30-minute
  break, up to 10% pilot items, four options one best answer, recall and scenario questions;
  passing score 560 on the 200 to 800 reporting scale (standard setting, approved by the Central
  Assessment Committee); results 2 to 6 weeks after the window; eligibility MBBS or equivalent,
  interns, or students one year from graduation; Prometric centres in Saudi Arabia and abroad;
  up to four attempts a year, two further attempts after a pass to improve the mark. Blueprint:
  Medicine 30% (cardiology, pulmonary, GI and hepatology, nephrology, infectious disease,
  endocrinology, hematology, rheumatology, neurology, oncology emergencies, critical care,
  psychiatry), OBGYN 25%, Pediatrics 25%, Surgery 20% (basic principles, general surgery,
  trauma, subspecialties), each +/-5%, with "Patient Safety, Preventive Medicine & Ethics" listed
  after Surgery (patient safety, ethics, lifestyle and preventive services, immunization,
  epidemiology and biostatistics) and no weight of its own. Topic lists with mastery levels 1 to 3
  were used to pick item topics; the reference list names Toronto Notes, Davidson, Harrison,
  Kumar and Clark, Hacker and Moore, Nelson Essentials, Illustrated Textbook of Pediatrics, Bailey
  and Love, the WHO Patient Safety Curriculum Guide, Jekel's epidemiology, the USPSTF Guide to
  Clinical Preventive Services and the SCFHS Professionalism and Ethics Handbook for Residents.
- Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, SMLE Applicant Guide 2024
  (https://scfhs.org.sa/sites/default/files/2024-07/SMLE%20Applicant%20Guide%202024.pdf), fetched
  2026-11-25: the same 30/25/25/20 weights and the 560 pass score (set in 2017); superseded by the
  2026 guideline.
- Dubai Health Authority, "Manual for DHA Professional Assessment (Oral/CBT)", version 1.1, 2025
  (https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20for%20DHA%20Professional%20Assessment-Final2025533182.pdf),
  fetched 2026-11-25: licensed professionals pass a DHA assessment (CBT and/or oral); result Pass
  or Fail only; three attempts, then blocked; CBT before oral where both apply. (The manual
  does not name Prometric.) The GP question count, time and pass mark are in DHA's CBT guideline, which was not
  found on a page that loaded.

## Could not be loaded or confirmed
- Department of Health Abu Dhabi and MOHAP pages reset the connection (not retried in a loop).
- Qatar DHP's qualifying examination page did not respond from this environment.
- Oman (OMSB), Kuwait and Bahrain (NHRA) GP exam documents were not found.
- Prep sites quote 150 questions and 60% for the DHA GP exam; that is not on an official page we
  read, so the landing shows "Check guide" for every authority except SCFHS and DHA's pass/fail
  rule, and never states a number for them.

## Blueprint used
Per 50-question test, following the SCFHS 2026 weights: medicine 16 (32%) as cardiology and
pulmonology 4, GI, nephrology and endocrinology 4, infectious disease and critical care 4,
hematology, rheumatology, neurology and psychiatry 4; obstetrics 7 and gynecology 5 (24%);
pediatrics 12 (24%) as neonatology, growth and child infections 6 and pediatric systems and
emergencies 6; surgery and trauma 6 plus patient safety, ethics and preventive medicine 4 (20%,
counting the safety section inside the 20% printed beside surgery). Every section is inside the
blueprint's +/-5%. The 60% readiness line is Tigertest's own; the SMLE is scaled.

## Clinical references for the items (primary guideline bodies)
- WHO recommendations on antenatal care for a positive pregnancy experience (2016)
  (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241549912), fetched 2026-11-25: eight contacts,
  first contact by 12 weeks; syphilis, HIV and hepatitis B testing.
- WHO recommendation on tranexamic acid for postpartum haemorrhage (2017)
  (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241550154), fetched 2026-11-25: 1 g IV over 10
  minutes within 3 hours, second dose if bleeding continues.
- WHO recommendations for care of the preterm or low-birth-weight infant and on interventions to
  improve preterm birth outcomes (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240057296), fetched
  2026-11-25: antenatal corticosteroids 24 to 34 weeks, magnesium sulfate for neuroprotection,
  erythromycin for PPROM.
- WHO fact sheet, Dengue and severe dengue
  (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/dengue-and-severe-dengue), fetched 2026-11-25:
  warning signs including persistent vomiting; paracetamol for pain, avoid NSAIDs.
- WHO fact sheet, MERS-CoV
  (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/middle-east-respiratory-syndrome-coronavirus-(mers-cov)),
  fetched 2026-11-25: dromedary camels are the primary reservoir.
- WHO fact sheet, Brucellosis (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/brucellosis),
  fetched 2026-11-25: doxycycline for about 6 weeks with streptomycin, or doxycycline with
  rifampicin; raw milk as the main route.
- WHO fact sheet, Measles (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/measles), fetched
  2026-11-25: pneumonia among the most serious complications.
- WHO guideline for screening and treatment of cervical pre-cancer lesions (2021)
  (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240030824), fetched 2026-11-25: HPV DNA as primary
  screening test.
- WHO consolidated guidelines on tuberculosis, Module 4: treatment of drug-susceptible TB (2022)
  (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240048126), fetched 2026-11-25: 6-month 2HRZE/4HR
  standard; 4-month rifapentine-moxifloxacin option.
- WHO guidelines for malaria (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/guidelines-for-malaria),
  fetched 2026-11-25: IV or IM artesunate for severe malaria.
- WHO fact sheet, Diarrhoeal disease
  (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/diarrhoeal-disease), fetched 2026-11-25:
  low-osmolarity ORS and 10 to 14 days of zinc; Plan B and Plan C from the WHO Treatment of
  Diarrhoea manual.
- WHO fact sheet, Emergency contraception
  (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/emergency-contraception), fetched 2026-11-25:
  copper IUD and ulipristal up to 5 days, levonorgestrel best within 72 hours.
- WHO Patient Safety Curriculum Guide: Multi-professional Edition (2011)
  (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241501958), fetched 2026-11-25: patient
  identification, surgical safety checklist, error disclosure, consent (the SMLE reference list
  names it).
- Surviving Sepsis Campaign: International Guidelines 2021
  (https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/surviving-sepsis-guidelines-2021),
  fetched 2026-11-25: 30 mL/kg crystalloid in 3 hours, lactate-guided resuscitation, MAP 65 mmHg,
  norepinephrine first-line; qSOFA from the Sepsis-3 definitions.
- US Preventive Services Task Force, Breast cancer screening (2024)
  (https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening),
  fetched 2026-11-25: biennial mammography 40 to 74 (the SMLE reference list names the USPSTF).
- GOLD 2025 report (https://goldcopd.org/2025-gold-report/), fetched 2026-11-25: post-bronchodilator
  FEV1/FVC below 0.70; NIV for hypercapnic acidosis.
- NICE CG174, Intravenous fluid therapy in adults (https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg174), fetched
  2026-11-25: 25 to 30 mL/kg/day maintenance water.
- NICE CG103, Delirium (https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg103/chapter/Recommendations), fetched
  2026-11-25: find and treat causes; short-term haloperidol only when de-escalation fails.
- NICE CG111, Bedwetting in under 19s (https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg111/chapter/Recommendations),
  fetched 2026-11-25: alarm as initial treatment, desmopressin alternative.
- CDC STI Treatment Guidelines 2021 and CDC Tdap in pregnancy and adult zoster recommendations
  (https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/chlamydia.htm), checked 2026-11-25: returned 403
  to this client; facts used are the long-standing published recommendations (doxycycline 7

- KDIGO AKI guideline (https://kdigo.org/guidelines/acute-kidney-injury/), checked 2026-11-25:

## Left out on purpose
Exam format, fees, attempts and booking (landing and FAQ only, never in questions); Saudi and
UAE regulations that differ between authorities or change often (consent rules for spouses,
controlled-drug schedules, sick leave limits, licensing categories); vaccine schedules by age;
drug doses that differ between national formularies except where one guideline fixes them (WHO
tranexamic acid, Parkland formula, lidocaine maximum).

## Duplicate check
Every stem and key was compared with every v1 and v2 bank in data/ (word overlap of stems, and
same key with overlapping stems). Items repeating facts already tested in PANCE, Gulf RN, Gulf
pharmacist, Poland LEK, Pakistan NLE, CCRN, CEN, Kenya and Philippines nursing and midwife and
other banks were replaced with a different fact.
