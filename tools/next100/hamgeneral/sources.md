# FCC General class (Element 3) bank sources

The questions, options and answer keys are the official 2023-2027 General class question pool
itself, written by the NCVEC Question Pool Committee, which is public domain and is exactly the
pool every Volunteer Examiner team draws the real Element 3 exam from (47 CFR 97.523). This is the
one allowed exception to the no-question-material rule, as for the Technician bank (`ham`). Every
explanation is original, written for Tigertest from the rules and theory below; none is copied
from a study guide, a license manual or a prep site.

## Question pool (NCVEC Question Pool Committee)

- NCVEC, 2023-2027 General Class Question Pool Release page, fetched 2026-11-19 through the
  Internet Archive copy of 2026-09-03 (ncvec.org itself serves a Cloudflare bot check to scripts):
  https://web.archive.org/web/20260903040827/https://ncvec.org/index.php/2023-2027-general-question-pool-release
  Lists the current file, "General Class Pool and Syllabus 2023-2027 Public Release with 6th
  Errata Feb 4 2026", and every errata: 1st (Feb 1 2023: 9 questions modified, G9C06 and G9D13
  withdrawn), 2nd (Apr 10 2023: G1A05, G1E10, G9D01 modified, G6B09 withdrawn), 3rd (Dec 1 2023:
  G1C08 and G1C10 withdrawn after the FCC rule change, G2E12 modified), 4th (Mar 6 2024: G1E09
  withdrawn), 5th (Nov 8 2024: G8C01 withdrawn), 6th (Feb 4 2026: G1A04 and G1C09 withdrawn).
- NCVEC, General Class Pool and Syllabus 2023-2027 Public Release with 6th Errata Feb 4 2026
  (docx), fetched 2026-11-19 (archive copy of the ncvec.org download):
  https://web.archive.org/web/20260903040827id_/https://ncvec.org/downloads/General%20Class%20Pool%20and%20Syllabus%202023-2027%20Public%20Release%20with%206th%20Errata%20Feb%204%202026.docx
  Syllabus effective 7/01/2023 to 6/30/2027: ten subelements, one exam question from each group,
  G1 5, G2 5, G3 3, G4 5, G5 3, G6 2, G7 3, G8 3, G9 4, G0 2 (35). Parsed to `pool.json`: 423 live
  questions after the nine withdrawals. The five G7A questions that need the G7-1 schematic
  (G7A09 to G7A13) are left out, so the bank has 418. Stems, options and keys are used as
  published; one stem that ends inside its quotation marks ("QRS?") has the question mark moved
  outside the closing quote.
- ARRL, Download or Print Amateur Radio Question Pools, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://www.arrl.org/question-pools
  Confirms the General pool is "Valid from July 1, 2023 until June 30, 2027", contains one
  schematic diagram, links the NCVEC release page, and that pools are reviewed on a four-year
  rotation (the next General pool takes effect July 1, 2027).

## Exam format and rules (FCC, 47 CFR Part 97)

- eCFR, 47 CFR 97.503 Element standards, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/api/renderer/v1/content/enhanced/current/title-47?part=97&section=97.503
  "Element 3: 35 questions concerning the privileges of a General Class operator license. The
  minimum passing score is 26 questions answered correctly."
- eCFR, 47 CFR 97.501, 97.505, 97.509 and 97.523, fetched 2026-11-19:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/api/renderer/v1/content/enhanced/current/title-47?part=97&section=97.501
  https://www.ecfr.gov/api/renderer/v1/content/enhanced/current/title-47?part=97&section=97.509
  https://www.ecfr.gov/api/renderer/v1/content/enhanced/current/title-47?part=97&section=97.523
  General needs Elements 2 and 3; exams are given by a team of at least 3 accredited VEs, and a
  General exam needs Extra or Advanced VEs; one public pool per element, at least 10 times the
  exam's question count. Used for the landing and facts only.
- eCFR, Part 97 sections cited in the G1 and G0 explanations (97.9, 97.13, 97.15, 97.111,
  97.113, 97.115, 97.119, 97.203, 97.205, 97.221, 97.301, 97.303, 97.305, 97.309, 97.311,
  97.313, 97.407, 97.505, 97.509; 47 CFR 1.1307 and 1.1310), fetched 2026-11-19 through the
  same API, e.g.
  https://www.ecfr.gov/api/renderer/v1/content/enhanced/current/title-47?part=97&section=97.301
  Every official key in G1 was checked against the current text and still matches. Where the
  pool's bracketed reference is a typo or points to a paragraph that has since moved
  (G1B05, G1C03, G1C04, G1D04), the explanation cites the current paragraph.

## Not modelled, left out

- The five G7-1 schematic questions (no figure support in the v1 engine).
- Exam fees (each VEC sets its own) and session logistics, which are on the landing only.
