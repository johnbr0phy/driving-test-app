# IIQE Paper 1 sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- PEAK Examination Centre (VTC), "Handbook for Insurance Intermediaries Qualifying Examination
  (Pencil-and-Paper and Computer Screen Mode)", effective from 1 September 2026:
  https://www.peak.edu.hk/exam/doc/IIQE_handbook_English_202609.pdf
  - 1.2: VTC is appointed by the Insurance Authority (IA) as the examination body, administered by
    the PEAK Exam Centre (Institute of Professional Education And Knowledge).
  - 2.1.1-2.1.3: Paper I Principles and Practice of Insurance is compulsory; Papers II (General),
    III (Long Term) and V (Investment-linked) are optional by line of business; Paper VI is travel.
  - 2.2.1: Paper I lasts 2 hours and has 75 multiple-choice questions.
  - 2.2.2: each paper is presented in both English and Chinese.
  - 2.2.4: pencil-and-paper mode (PPME) or computer screen mode (CSME); a separate handbook covers
    remote invigilation mode (RIME) for Papers I, II and III.
  - 2.3.1: results are PASS or FAIL; the exact score is not disclosed.
  - 2.4: a minimum score of 70% is needed to pass each paper.
- PEAK IIQE page: https://www.peak.edu.hk/exam/en/iiqe (enrolment online; syllabus, study notes,
  handbook links). Fees change (an adjustment from 1 September 2026); left out of all copy.
- Syllabus for Principles and Practice of Insurance Examination:
  https://www.peak.edu.hk/exam/doc/syllabus_P1_eng_2024.pdf
  "Candidates are recommended to study the 2024 Edition of the Study Notes for examinations to be
  held from 17 February 2025 onwards." Seven chapters, same as the Study Notes.
- Insurance Authority IIQE page (403 to scripts, title confirmed in search results):
  https://www.ia.org.hk/en/supervision/reg_ins_intermediaries/insurance_intermediaries_qualifying_examination.html
- Guides (not official) repeat 75 questions, 2 hours, 70% (about 53 of 75).

## Content source for the bank
- Insurance Authority, "Study Notes: Principles and Practice of Insurance Examination", 2024
  Edition (eighth edition, November 2024): https://www.peak.edu.hk/exam/en/doc/SN_P1_eng_2024.pdf
  - Note page vii, relative weight of chapters: 1 Risk and Insurance 12%, 2 Legal Principles 16%,
    3 Principles of Insurance 30%, 4 Core Functions of an Insurance Company 9%, 5 Structure of Hong
    Kong Insurance Industry 5%, 6 Regulatory Framework 21%, 7 Ethical and Other Related Issues 7%.
    Our blueprint per 50: 6/8/15/5/2/11/3 (Chapter 3 split 5/5/5, Chapter 6 split 5/6).
  - Chapter 1: risk definition and classification (pure/speculative, particular/fundamental), risk
    management tools, primary and ancillary benefits.
  - Chapter 2: contract types and elements, void/voidable/unenforceable, privity and Cap. 623,
    agency (ratification, actual and apparent authority, necessity, estoppel), duties, termination.
  - Chapter 3: insurable interest (child under 18; life at inception, marine at loss; assignment),
    utmost good faith (material fact, prudent insurer, duration, four breaches, remedies),
    proximate cause ("directly or indirectly"), indemnity (four methods, salvage, abandonment,
    average, excess, franchise, limits, more than indemnity), contribution (conditions, rateable
    proportion clause, non-contribution, Marine Clause), subrogation (tort, contract, statute,
    salvage; sharing proceeds after excess, limit and average).
  - Chapter 4: product development, underwriting, policy administration, claims, reinsurance,
    actuarial (appointed actuary, GL9), investment, key persons in control functions.
  - Chapter 5: Schedule 1 classes (long term A-I, general 1-17), academic classification,
    reinsurance inwards/outwards, HKFI, MIB, ECIIB, ECIRS. Market statistics (2020-2021 figures)
    are dated and not used.
  - Chapter 6: IA and IO (renamed 26 June 2017), authorisation, solvency margin, fit and proper,
    GL10, GL12, assets in Hong Kong, separate long term accounts, intervention powers, Code of
    Conduct for Insurers, ICB (6-month filing window; no appeal for the insurer); licensing regime
    from 23 September 2019, regulated activities and advice, exemptions, five licence types,
    responsible officer, section 68, multiple capacities, maximum 4 insurers (2 long term), GL23
    (HKDSE Level 2 in 5 subjects; responsible officer about 5 years with 2 in management; 15%
    controller), section 81 powers, section 90 conduct requirements, Codes of Conduct for agents and
    brokers, broker client accounts and PII, GL24 CPD (15 hours, 3 on ethics or regulations,
    1 August to 31 July).
  - Chapter 7: broker as agent of the insured, PDPO six principles, PICS, direct marketing consent,
    equal opportunity ordinances and fair discrimination, ML stages, JFIU reporting, tipping off,
    non-Hong Kong PEP enhanced due diligence, POBO "advantage" (entertainment excluded).
- Dollar figures (capital, penalties, ICB award limit, PII limits) are deliberately not asked.
  Worked examples (average, excess, subrogation sharing) use illustrative HK$ amounts only.
