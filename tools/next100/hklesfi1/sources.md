# Sources for hklesfi1 (Hong Kong LE Paper 1), checked 2026-11-05

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- HKSI Institute, Licensing Examination for Securities and Futures Intermediaries, overview:
  https://www.hksi.org/en/qualification/practising-qualifications/licensing-examination-for-securities-and-futures-intermediaries/overview/
  Paper 1 "Fundamentals of Securities and Futures Regulation": 60 multiple-choice questions,
  90 minutes; "Pass Mark: 70% for each paper"; computer-based examination at the Hong Kong
  Examination Centre or Global Examination Centres; questions in English and Traditional Chinese.
  Paper 1 is a "Local Regulatory Framework Paper", required for Representatives across the
  regulated activity types and taken by Responsible Officers with a second paper (2, 3, 4, 5, 6
  or 18 depending on the activity). The LE is designed to meet the competence requirements the
  SFC recognises. (Which papers a person needs, and any exemptions, depend on the role and the
  SFC's Guidelines on Competence: stated as "depends on your role" on the landing.)
- HKSI Institute, LE Paper 1 syllabus, Version 3.5, effective from 30 June 2026
  (Version 3.4.2 applied up to 29 June 2026):
  https://www.hksi.org/media/8902/p1-syllabus_eng-v35_clean.pdf
  Nine topics: 1 regulatory overview of the Hong Kong financial industry; 2 principles of Hong
  Kong law and the Companies Ordinance; 3 the SFO (Parts I to XVII, including IIIA, IVA, XIVA,
  XV); 4 licensing and registration and subsidiary legislation (capital, client securities,
  client money, records, contract notes, audit, regulated activities, OTC derivatives reporting
  and clearing, OFCs); 5 business conduct (Code of Conduct, FMCC, CFA Code, CRA Code, OFC Code,
  Code of Conduct for Share Registrars); 6 business operations (ICG, AML/CFT, electronic trading
  and ALPs, PDPO, compliance and governance, insurance, CRS); 7 participating in the Hong Kong
  exchanges (SEHK, traded options, futures, Stock Connect, structured products marketing);
  8 accessing public capital (Listing Rules, takeovers and buy-backs, SFC-authorised products,
  REITs, MPFA, virtual assets); 9 market misconduct and improper trading practices.
  The syllabus gives no per-topic weights or question counts: the 6/5/9/9/9/8/5/5/4 split per
  60-question test is Tigertest's own.
- Fees differ between HKSI pages (guides and pages show several figures): left out.

## Content sources for the bank (Hong Kong law as of November 2026)
- Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571), HKeL: https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap571
  Used: s.4 objectives; Sch. 1 definitions (professional investor, securities, structured product,
  CIS); Part III minority controller of a recognized exchange controller (5%); Part IIIA OTC
  derivatives; s.103 offers to the public; Part IVA OFCs; Part V licensing (ROs, accreditation,
  registered institutions, annual returns); s.156 audited accounts within 4 months; Part VII
  s.170 naked short selling, s.174 unsolicited calls; Parts VIII to XII; Part XIII MMT and
  orders (disqualification and cold shoulder up to five years); Part XIV offences and the
  double jeopardy rule; Part XIVA inside information (from 1 January 2013); Part XV disclosure
  of interests (5%, whole percentage points, 3 business days); s.378 secrecy; s.388 SFC
  prosecution of summary offences.
- Subsidiary legislation (HKeL): Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules (notice
  within one business day of a liquid capital shortfall); Client Money Rules (segregated account
  within one business day); Client Securities Rules (standing authority up to 12 months);
  Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts Rules (contract note by end of second
  business day; monthly statement within 7 business days); Keeping of Records Rules (7 years);
  Licensing and Registration (Information) Rules (changes within 7 business days); Contracts
  Limits and Reportable Positions Rules; Insurance Rules; OTC derivative reporting and clearing
  rules.
- SFC Code of Conduct for Persons Licensed by or Registered with the SFC (general principles,
  suitability clause in client agreements since 9 June 2017, discretionary accounts, telephone
  recordings kept at least 6 months, ALP qualified investors, electronic and algorithmic trading,
  corporate professional investor assessment; individual professional investors no longer get
  the exemptions): https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes
- SFC Guidelines on Continuous Professional Training (at least 10 CPT hours per calendar year
  for licensed individuals; ROs more): https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Guidelines/Guidelines-on-Continuous-Professional-Training
- SFC Fit and Proper Guidelines; Management, Supervision and Internal Control Guidelines;
  Manager-In-Charge circular (eight core functions, 2016-2017); Guideline on AML/CFT;
  Fund Manager Code of Conduct; Corporate Finance Adviser Code; CRA Code; Code on OFCs;
  Code of Conduct for Share Registrars; Code on REITs (90% distribution); Takeovers Code and
  Share Buy-backs Code (30% / 2% creeper, non-statutory).
- Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615) (CDD records kept
  5 years after the relationship ends; PEPs); OSCO (Cap. 455); DTROP (Cap. 405); UNATMO (Cap. 575);
  JFIU (Hong Kong Police and Customs and Excise): https://www.jfiu.gov.hk/
- Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) and the six Data Protection Principles; data
  access requests within 40 days; direct marketing consent; PCPD: https://www.pcpd.org.hk/
- Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) (no-par shares from 3 March 2014, private company tests,
  special resolution 75%, codified duty of care, unfair prejudice, derivative actions,
  Financial Secretary's inspectors); Companies (Winding Up and Miscellaneous Provisions)
  Ordinance (Cap. 32).
- HKEX rules and trading information (CCASS T+2, order types, auction sessions, short selling

- Main Board Listing Rules (ongoing public float rules from 1 January 2026, notifiable and connected transactions, INEDs at
  least three and one third of the board, audit committee of non-executive directors, Corporate
  Governance Code comply or explain).
- SFC on virtual assets ("same business, same risks, same rules"; VATP licensing since June 2023).

## Fact-check, 2026-11-05

  float (4 August 2025) and new ongoing public float rules from 1 January 2026 (alternative 10%
  threshold, -PF marker instead of suspension, delisting after 18 months, 12 on GEM). HKEX,
  "Exchange publishes conclusions on proposed amendments to Listing Rules relating to ongoing
  public float requirements", 17 December 2025, checked 2026-11-05:
  https://www.hkex.com.hk/News/Regulatory-Announcements/2025/251217news?sc_lang=en
- Telephone order recordings kept at least 6 months: Code of Conduct para 3.9 (raised from three
  months on 1 December 2012), checked 2026-11-05 via SFC enforcement summaries:
  https://www.charltonslaw.com/sfc-fines-hsbc-for-code-of-conduct-breaches/

  Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts) Rules, Cap. 571Q,
