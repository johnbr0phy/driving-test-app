# hknurse sources (checked 2026-11-16)

Every question in `data/hk-nursing-licensing-questions.json` is original, written from the topics
and rules below and from the standard nursing, anatomy, microbiology and pharmacology content that
the Council's own syllabus names. No Council sample or past paper was opened or used.

## Read from the Nursing Council of Hong Kong (nchk.org.hk)
- Candidates' Handbook for Nursing Council Licensing Examinations (September 2018, PDF):
  https://www.nchk.org.hk/filemanager/en/pdf/Candidates_Handbook_for_Nursing_Council_Licensing_Exam_eng.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-16, text extracted from the PDF). Appendix A, Format of the Licensing Examination
  for Part I (General) Registration, written part: one day, three papers. Paper A is a Proficiency
  Test in Medical English (40 marks, 1 hour). Paper B(I) is multiple choice, 60 marks, 1.5 hours,
  Psychology, Basic Nursing Care and System Nursing Care. Paper B(II) is 100 marks over 2.5 hours:
  multiple choice 40 marks (Anatomy and Physiology, Food and Nutrition, Primary Health Care,
  Personal and Communal Health, Microbiology, First Aid and Pharmacology), essay-type questions
  40 marks, and specialty nursing questions 20 marks (choose 2 of 4: Obstetric, Psychiatric,
  Geriatric and Community Nursing). The written examination carries a total mark of 200 and the
  passing mark is 100; candidates must pass both Paper A and Paper B. Candidates may take it in
  Chinese or English. The exam is for nurses trained outside Hong Kong approved by the Council; the
  written part must be passed before the practical part (health assessment, administration of
  medication, aseptic technique).
- A Reference Guide to the Syllabus of Subjects and Requirements for the Preparation of Registered
  Nurse (General) in the Hong Kong SAR (March 2025, PDF):
  https://www.nchk.org.hk/filemanager/en/pdf/Syllabus_for_RN(G)_en.pdf (fetched 2026-11-16, text
  extracted). Gave the topic outline used for the blueprint: general, behavioural and life sciences
  (anatomy, physiology and pathophysiology, sociology, applied psychology, fundamental pharmacology,
  microbiology, nutrition); basic nursing for safe practice (nursing process, safe environment,
  infection control, documentation, basic needs, diagnostic procedures, administration of
  medications, first aid); principles and practice of nursing (A&E, peri-operative, medical and
  surgical by body system, rehabilitative, oncology and palliative, paediatric, obstetric,
  gerontological, mental health, public health and community, communicable diseases); legal and
  ethical issues (tort, confidentiality, consent, negligence, vicarious liability, the Nurses
  Registration Ordinance Cap. 164, the Mental Health Ordinance, the Personal Data (Privacy)
  Ordinance, the Bribery Ordinance and the ICAC, the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance, and
  the Council's code); health promotion, management, research, professional development.
- Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct for Nurses in Hong Kong (updated July 2026, PDF):
  https://www.nchk.org.hk/filemanager/en/pdf/Code_of_Ethics_and_Professional_Conduct.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-16, text extracted). Four domains (Nurses and People, Nurses and Practice, Nurses and
  the Profession, Nurses and Society) and eleven standards, including "Hold in lawful confidence
  personal information obtained in a professional capacity" (standard 3).
- Nursing Council home page: https://www.nchk.org.hk (fetched 2026-11-16; the page shell only).

## Other pages read
- WHO fact sheet, Infant and young child feeding (https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/infant-and-young-child-feeding,
  fetched 2026-11-16): exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months, complementary foods from
  around 6 months.

## Could not be loaded or confirmed
- Nurses Registration Ordinance (Cap. 164) on elegislation.gov.hk loaded only a script shell on
  2026-11-16, so no section numbers or penalties are asked; only its name and purpose (from the
  syllabus) are used.
- The Council's current exam page and sample past papers were not reachable by the fetch tool, so
  only the 2018 Handbook describes the format. The Handbook is dated 2018, so the 200 marks, the
  60 and 40 mark split and the pass mark should be read as the Council's last published figures.
- The Council's Core-Competencies for Registered Nurses (General) were not read; the syllabus was
  used for scope instead.
- WHO hand hygiene and ERC/Resuscitation Council CPR pages returned errors or no figures, so
  hand hygiene moments, CPR depth (5 to 6 cm) and ratio (30:2) rest on widely published guidance
  and are worded generally. The reviewer should check them first.

## What was deliberately left out
- Paper A (Proficiency Test in Medical English), the essay questions, the specialty nursing
  choice and the practical examination are not modelled.
- No fees, fines, dates, office-holders or section numbers of any ordinance. No Hong Kong
  vaccination schedule or protocol that changes over time. Units are SI, spelling is British.

## Blueprint used (Tigertest's, labelled as such)
The Council publishes no per-topic counts for the multiple-choice papers, only the paper contents
and the 60 and 40 mark split. Per 50-question test: psychology and communication 3; law, ethics
and professional practice 3; basic nursing care and infection control 7; cardiovascular and
respiratory 4; digestive, renal and endocrine 4; neurological, musculoskeletal and sensory 4;
peri-operative, A&E, oncology and palliative 3; life-span and mental health nursing 4; first aid
3; anatomy and physiology 4; nutrition 2; public health and primary health care 3; microbiology 3;
pharmacology 3. Pools are four times those counts (200 in all).
