# CHL (HSPA) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official outline and the public
standards and guidance below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from HSPA practice exams, HSPA
lesson plans, the Sterile Processing Leadership Manual's review questions, the Purdue course, or
any commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (Healthcare Sterile Processing Association)

- HSPA, Certified Healthcare Leader (CHL) Exam Content Outline, Revised August 2025; Updated
  January 2026 (from the 2024-2025 job task analysis), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://myhspa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/HSPA_CHL_Content_Outline_2026.pdf
  Section 1 Planning and Decision Making 25% (I design quality control processes; II manage
  workplace and equipment; III develop workplace safety; IV cultivate workforce development;
  V develop financial planning; VI design quality management and process improvement systems);
  Section 2 Organizing 23% (staff management; workplace environment and processes; process
  improvement); Section 3 Leading 29% (lead and support staff; mentor and train staff; prioritize
  communication; develop leadership skills); Section 4 Governing and Compliance 23% (manage
  inventory; financial management; infection prevention; quality management and process
  improvement). Per test here: 13, 11, 15, 11.
- HSPA, CHL Eligibility Updates and Pilot Exam, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://myhspa.org/certified-healthcare-leader-chl-pilot-exam/
  From October 2026: current CRCST plus one of 12 months (2,080 hours) of SP experience, 12 months
  in a leadership role, or an associate degree or higher; pilot exam October 1 to 15, 2026.
- HSPA, Certified Healthcare Leader (CHL) page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://myhspa.org/certification/certification-overview/certified-healthcare-leader-chl/
  Secondary certification for management; CRCST must be held and kept current; annual
  recertification.
- Superseded: CHL Exam Content Outline Revised August 2021; Updated May 2022 (30/25/30/15), fetched
  2026-11-15 for comparison only:
  https://myhspa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/CHL_Exam_Content_Outline-1.pdf

## Format (HSPA Certification Handbook, revised January 2026)

- HSPA, Certification Handbook, Revised January 2026, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://myhspa.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2024-Certification-Handbook.pdf
  CHL exam: 150 multiple-choice questions, 125 scored and 25 pretest; computer based at Prometric;
  3 hours; marked review allowed; pass/fail by number of scored questions correct, passing point set
  by Angoff with the Beuk compromise, not published as a percentage; no percentage scores reported.
  References used for the exam: HSPA SP Leadership Manual 4th ed (2024), SP Technical Manual 9th ed
  (2023), ANSI/AAMI ST79 (2017), AORN Guidelines for Perioperative Practice (2023). The handbook's
  domain list still names the fourth section "Controlling"; the 2026 outline names it Governing
  and Compliance. The 70% line on Tigertest's tests is a readiness target. Fees are never stated.

## Standards and guidance used in questions

- AHRQ PSNet, Glossary, fetched 2026-11-15: https://psnet.ahrq.gov/glossary-0
  Definitions of near miss (close call), sentinel event, root cause analysis, FMEA, PDSA steps,
  Lean principles (standardized work, value stream, workflow, reducing waste), Six Sigma
  (3.4 defects per million opportunities), just culture, latent and active errors, Swiss cheese
  model, forcing function, workaround, normalization of deviance.
- AHRQ PSNet, Never Events primer, fetched 2026-11-15: https://psnet.ahrq.gov/primer/never-events
  The Joint Commission mandates a root cause analysis after a sentinel event.
- AHRQ PSNet, Root Cause Analysis primer, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://psnet.ahrq.gov/primer/root-cause-analysis
- AHRQ, Using Root Cause Analysis to Improve Quality and Performance, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ahrq.gov/evidencenow/tools/root-cause-analysis.html (fishbone diagramming).
- IHI, Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA) Tool, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ihi.org/library/tools/failure-modes-and-effects-analysis-fmea-tool
  Steps, failure modes, causes, effects; proactive, useful before a new process.
- AHRQ TeamSTEPPS, SBAR tool and Communication module, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/sbar.html
  SBAR, closed-loop communication, check-back and teach-back tools.
- OSHA, Personal Protective Equipment, 29 CFR 1910.132, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.132
  Hazard assessment before PPE selection, written certification.
- OSHA, Bloodborne Pathogens, 29 CFR 1910.1030, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
  Exposure control plan reviewed at least annually, safer devices documented annually,
  post-exposure evaluation and follow-up.
- OSHA, Workplace Violence, fetched 2026-11-15: https://www.osha.gov/workplace-violence
- FDA, MAUDE database page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/mandatory-reporting-requirements-manufacturers-importers-and-device-user-facilities/manufacturer-and-user-facility-device-experience-database-maude
  Mandatory reporters: manufacturers, importers and device user facilities.
- 45 CFR 164.502(b) minimum necessary and 164.530(c) safeguards (HIPAA Privacy Rule), CFR text
  on govinfo, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title45-vol2/xml/CFR-2023-title45-vol2-sec164-502.xml
- CDC, Disinfection and Sterilization: Summary of Recommendations, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/summary-recommendations.html
  Prions as the exception to standard sterilization.

## Could not be loaded or confirmed

- The HSPA Leadership Manual, ST79 and AORN Guidelines are sold and were not read. Questions
  name ST79 and AORN only as references HSPA lists; no ST79 figure is asserted.
- HHS and The Joint Commission pages returned 403; their facts are taken from the CFR text and
  AHRQ PSNet. CMS's Legionella memo page returned 404, so water management is asked only as a
  concept.
- Budget, depreciation, ROI, payback, FTE and par-level items are arithmetic on figures given in
  the stem; the 2,080-hour FTE is the figure HSPA itself uses for 12 months of full-time work.
