# HSPT (`hspt`): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest. No STS item, Practice Test
booklet item, HSPT Preview Sample, released form or commercial HSPT prep item was used or
reworded. Reading passages are original. STS sells a 4-page Practice Test booklet (three items per
subtest); it was not used.

## Format and blueprint (Scholastic Testing Service, Inc., the test owner)

- High School Placement Test (HSPT) overview page.
  https://www.ststesting.com/hsp/index.html (fetched 2026-11-14; ststesting.com/hsptpg9.html
  redirects there). Gave: the five subtests (verbal and quantitative skills, reading, mathematics,
  language), the Closed HSPT (a secure new edition every year, leased to high schools) and the
  Open HSPT (bought and scored locally), the Pre-HSPT for grade 7, and the optional tests in
  science (25 minutes) and Catholic religion (20 minutes), whose scores are not in the Battery
  Composite. Standard scores on a Rasch scale, cognitive skills quotient, national and local
  percentiles and grade equivalents.
- HSPT Parent's Page.
  https://ststesting.com/hspt/ (fetched 2026-11-14). Gave: taken by eighth graders for admission,
  scholarship and placement into ninth grade; no calculators, phones or other technology; the
  administrator announces the halfway point of each subtest; the raw score is the number right
  with no penalty for guessing; raw scores convert to standard scores and percentiles; schools set
  their own test dates and decide how scores are used; meant to be taken once in grade 8.
- HSPT Interpretive Manual for use with the Closed HSPT and Pre-HSPT (copyright 2021).
  https://www.ststesting.com/hp_int_sts.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14). Gave the subtest lengths and
  what each measures: Verbal Skills 60 items (synonyms, antonyms, verbal analogies, verbal
  classifications, logical reasoning), Quantitative Skills 52 (number series, number
  manipulations, geometric and nongeometric comparisons; no calculators), Reading 62
  (comprehension and vocabulary in context), Mathematics 64 (computation and problem solving in
  numbers and numeration, measurement, geometry, algebra, statistics; no calculators), Language 60
  (punctuation, capitalization, spelling, grammar, usage, composition); five multiple-choice
  subtests; composites Total Cognitive Skills, Total Basic Skills and Battery Composite; standard
  scores 200 to 800; optional 40-item science and Catholic religion tests. The sample Performance
  Profile gave the skill split this bank's blueprint follows: Verbal analogy 10, logic 11, verbal
  classification 17, synonyms 13, antonyms 9; Quantitative sequence 15, reasoning 15, geometric
  comparison 10, non-geometric comparison 12; Reading comprehension 40 (informational text 30,
  literary text 10) and vocabulary 22; Mathematics numbers and numeration 29, measurement 7,
  geometry 12, algebra 7, statistics and probability 9; Language punctuation 10, capitalization 2,
  usage 18 (incorrect 15, correct 3), spelling 10, composition 20. (These are one sample form's
  counts; each annual form may differ slightly.)
- Scholastic Testing Service 2026-27 catalog (prices effective 8/1/2026 to 7/31/2027).
  https://view.publitas.com/scholastic-testing-service-in/scholastic_testing_2022-23_catalog/page/1
  (fetched 2026-11-14 as the catalog's PDF download). Gave: Closed HSPT level grade 8, working
  time 2 hours 30 minutes; only one optional test may be given.

## Content (primary text the items are written from)

- Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (CCSSO / NGA Center).
  https://learning.ccsso.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Math_Standards1.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Grades 6 to 8 (ratios and proportional relationships, the number system, expressions and
  equations, geometry, statistics and probability) for the Mathematics and Quantitative items.
- Common Core State Standards for English Language Arts.
  https://learning.ccsso.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ELA_Standards1.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Grades 6 to 8 Reading (literature and informational text) and Language (conventions,
  vocabulary) for the Reading and Language items. Verbal Skills items use general English
  vocabulary at a grade 8 level.

## Could not load or confirm

- STS does not publish the time limit of each subtest on its pages or in the manual, only the
  2 hours 30 minutes working time. The section clocks here are Tigertest's own split of that
  time at about the real pace, and the copy says so.
- STS publishes no pass mark: schools set their own cut-offs. The score here is percent correct,
  never an STS standard score, percentile or CSQ.

## Deliberately left out

- The optional science, Catholic religion and mechanical aptitude tests, and any local test.
- Full length: each practice test is about a third of the real 298 items, at the same mix.
- Questions about the test itself (length, timing, scoring).
