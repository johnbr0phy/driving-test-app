# Glossary for `huelsoseg` (Hungarian, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations in the polite form ("Ön", "Mit tegyen?") or impersonal, as
in Hungarian first aid manuals; landing and dashboard copy in "te" (consumer-app norm, as the
German exam uses "du"). Hungarian typography: „…” quotes, decimal comma (4,0 mmol/l), en dash
in ranges without spaces (5–6 cm, 100–120), ratios written 30:2, °C, cm, perc, másodperc. No
em-dashes. Body parts and positions in everyday words with the medical term once where a
candidate meets it (agonális légzés, anafilaxia).
