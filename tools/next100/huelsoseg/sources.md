# Sources for `huelsoseg` (Hungarian road first aid exam for the driving licence, in Hungarian)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Hungarian from the sources below. Nothing was
copied from the Red Cross exam topics (tételek), the Hornyák textbook, an online exam or a
commercial practice site. Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- 31/1992. (XII. 19.) NM rendelet, read via net.jogtar.hu on 2026-11-06:
  https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=99200031.nm
  - 1. §: the first aid knowledge required by 326/2011. (XII. 28.) Korm. rendelet 13. § for a
    driving licence is certified by the county and Budapest organisations of the Magyar
    Vöröskereszt (certificate in Annex 1: "a Magyar Vöröskereszt vizsgabizottsága előtt a közúti
    elsősegélynyújtási ismeretekből sikeres vizsgát tett").
  - 2. § (1): exempt with their diploma or certificate: doctors, dentists, pharmacists, vets,
    nurses, ambulance officers and other listed health professionals; and "a közúti
    közlekedésről szóló törvény szerinti középiskolás járművezetők az online
    elsősegélynyújtási vizsgát igazoló tanúsítvánnyal" (secondary-school drivers with the online
    first aid exam certificate). The document is shown to the Red Cross, which records the
    exemption (Annex 2).
  - 2/A. §: the Red Cross notifies the road transport registry electronically on the day of a
    passed exam or an exemption.
- Magyar Vöröskereszt, Elsősegélynyújtás vizsgáztatás szabályzata (MVK-SZ-036), examinee
  extract 2022 (PDF, read 2026-11-06):
  https://voroskereszt.hu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/ESNY_vizsgaszabalyzat_vizsgazoi_kivonat_2022.pdf
  - 2 to 4 candidates in the room at once; a candidate who makes a fail-level error in the
    compulsory practical tasks or the drawn task, or whose action would worsen the casualty,
    is graded "nem felelt meg"; result "megfelelt" or "nem felelt meg", given at once.
  - A missed exam or a retake needs the exam fee paid again (fees not stated anywhere here).
- Magyar Vöröskereszt Hajdú-Bihar county, Közúti Elsősegélynyújtási Vizsga Szabályzata
  (PDF, read 2026-11-06):
  https://voroskereszthajdu.hu/ckfinder/userfiles/files/Els%C5%91seg%C3%A9lyny%C3%BAjt%C3%A1s-vizsgaszab%C3%A1lyzat.pdf
  - "a vizsga elsősorban gyakorlati jellegű, melyet az elméleti ismeretek szóbeli
    számonkérése is kiegészít"; exam board of two.
  - Tasks: compulsory practical exercises (one-rescuer CPR, examination of the casualty, care of
    an unconscious casualty in the recovery position), then an "A" and a "B" task from one drawn
    card (tétel).
  - Basis of the theory and practice: Hornyák István Ph.D., "Elsősegélynyújtás mindenkinek".
  - Pass: theory and practical tasks solved correctly, no fail-level error.
- Same rules with the list of fail-level errors (újraélesztés, életjelenségek vizsgálata,
  eszméletlenség, stabil oldalfekvés, vérzések, égés, fektetési módok, bukósisak levétele,
  Rautek-féle kimentés, hirtelen légúti elzáródás, rögzítések), a driving school's copy of the
  Red Cross information sheet (PDF, read 2026-11-06): https://teknocjogsi.hu/dlcs/voroskereszt.pdf
  The categories of this bank follow this list.
- Vöröskereszt Baranya Vármegyei Szervezete, Közúti Elsősegélynyújtó Vizsga (read 2026-11-06):
  https://voroskeresztbaranya.hu/elsosegely-vizsga
  - Theory "szóbeli vagy írásbeli formában"; practical demonstration (CPR, unconscious
    casualty, bandaging); topics: scene safety, CPR, unconscious casualty, bleeding, fractures,
    medical emergencies (heart attack, stroke, diabetic, epileptic, allergic emergencies).
  - "A vizsga nem tanfolyamköteles" (a course is optional); under 18 needs a parental consent
    form; bring photo ID and address card.
- eduline.hu, 23 Nov 2023: the exam is organised only by the Magyar Vöröskereszt; driving schools
  sell preparation courses. https://eduline.hu/campus-life/20231123_elsosegely-tanfolyam-es-vizsga-mennyibe-kerul-jogositvany
- vg.hu, Dec 2024: for secondary-school students, OMSZ staff give a four-hour practical
  demonstration in schools on top of an e-learning course and an online exam.
  https://www.vg.hu/kozelet/2024/12/jogositvany-elsosegely-oktatas-mento

  Tigertest's 4 x 50 tests and 80% (40 of 50) are Tigertest's readiness line; the landing says so.

## Content used for the questions (in force November 2026)
- Betlehem J., Bánfai-Csonka H., Kovács E., Bánfai B.: Az Európai Újraélesztési Tanács (ERC)
  2025. évi elsősegélynyújtási és felnőtt alapszintű újraélesztési gyakorlati irányelveinek
  összefoglalója és hazai adaptációs szempontjai. Orv Hetil. 2026; 167(33): 1294-1308.
  DOI 10.1556/650.2026.33623 (authors from the Magyar Resuscitatiós Társaság and the Magyar
  Vöröskereszt). https://real.mtak.hu/246229/1/650-article-p1294.pdf  Used for:
  - ABCDE with safety and responsiveness first; life-threatening bleeding before A; look,
    listen, feel for at most 10 s; agonal breathing is not normal; ACVPU (C = confusion);
    pulse oximeter limits (nail polish, cold, shock, movement).
  - Call for every unresponsive person, before the breathing check, on speakerphone; dispatcher
    support; fetch an AED alone only if it can be reached within 1 minute.
  - Hands on the centre of the chest (lower half of the sternum), at least 5 and at most 6 cm,
    100-120 a minute, full recoil, 30:2 (also for obese patients), breaths about 1 s watching
    the chest rise, pinch the soft part of the nose, compressions interrupted no more than 10 s,
    compression-only CPR for the untrained; AED pads on the bare chest, keep compressing while
    a second rescuer attaches them, nobody touches during shock, resume CPR at once after a
    shock or a no-shock advice; stop when professionals take over, the casualty clearly wakes
    (moves, opens eyes, breathes normally) or you are exhausted; do not fear causing injury.
  - Children: always start with 5 breaths; trained rescuers 15:2, untrained may continue 30:2.
  - Recovery position for reduced consciousness, not when CPR is indicated or breathing is
    agonal; watch continuously; if breathing worsens turn back and start CPR.
  - Choking: up to 5 back blows, then up to 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich), alternate; if
    unconscious call 112 and start CPR; no blind finger sweep; medical check afterwards.
  - Chest pain: rest, call, help to chew aspirin unless allergic or already taken that day
    ("Ez a dózis hazánkban 250 mg-os aszpirintablettát jelent"); help with own nitroglycerin
    spray. Women: jaw pain, nausea, breathlessness (ESC).
  - Stroke: FAST (introduced in Hungary in 2007 by the Magyar Stroke Társaság).
  - Hypoglycaemia: below 4.0 mmol/l, 15-20 g fast carbohydrate, recheck after 15 minutes,
    nothing by mouth if unconscious.
  - Asthma: help with own reliever inhaler, ideally with a spacer.
  - Anaphylaxis: signs, adrenaline auto-injector, second dose after 5 minutes if no better.
  - Bleeding: direct pressure on the wound, pressure dressing; tourniquet only for
    life-threatening limb bleeding and only for trained rescuers (no tourniquet item in the
    bank, see below); haemostatic dressings are not part of the Hungarian kit.
  - Concussion: no physical activity; neck: restrict movement manually, head squeeze or
    trapezius squeeze, jaw thrust, log-roll with a second rescuer, no cervical collars.
  - Open chest wound: leave open, no occlusive dressing; semi-sitting position.
  - Amputated part: wrap, waterproof bag, bag with ice water, never frozen, travels with the
    casualty.
  - Drowning: do not risk your life, reach from the bank with a buoyant aid; start CPR with
    5 breaths; dry the chest before the AED.
  - Hypothermia: remove wet clothes, cover, insulate from the ground.
  - Heatstroke: confusion, very high temperature; immersion cooling from the neck down is
    fastest.
  - Viper bite: rest, immobilise without compression; no tourniquet, cutting or suction.
  - Car first aid kit is compulsory equipment in Hungary.
- 1/1975. (II. 5.) KPM-BM együttes rendelet (KRESZ), read via net.jogtar.hu on 2026-11-06:
  https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=97500001.kpm
  - 58. § (1) a driver involved in a crash must stop and help; (2) police notified for death or
    injury; for a dangerous goods vehicle, uninvolved drivers also notify the police and give
    the numbers on the orange plate; (5) uninvolved drivers must give the help expected of them
    and use or hand over the vehicle's first aid kit, and a vehicle's fire extinguisher must be
    used to put out a burning vehicle.
  - 56. § (6)-(7) warning triangle and hazard lights for a broken-down vehicle; 21. § (12)
    reflective vest outside built-up areas at night and in poor visibility.
- 2012. évi C. törvény (Btk.), read via net.jogtar.hu on 2026-11-06:
  https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=a1200100.tv
  - 166. § segítségnyújtás elmulasztása (anyone who fails to give the help expected of them to
    an injured person or one in immediate danger); 239. § cserbenhagyás (a driver involved in a
    road crash leaves before checking whether anyone is hurt). No sentence lengths are asked.
- ERC Guidelines 2020/2025 first aid (for points the Hungarian summary does not give): burns
  cooled with cool running water for at least 20 minutes, blisters left intact, nothing put on
  the burn; nosebleed: lean forward, pinch the soft part for 10-15 minutes. Paediatric BLS: infant
  under 1 year, compression depth at least one third of the chest on the lower half of the
  sternum, mouth-to-mouth-and-nose for infants, neutral head position, AED usable in children;
  infant choking with back blows then chest thrusts, child over 1 with abdominal thrusts.
  https://www.erc.edu/science-research/guidelines/guidelines-2025/guidelines-2025-english
- Traditional positions in the Red Cross fail list ("fektetési módok": félig ülő helyzet,
  sokkfektetés, hasi sérült, eszméleténél lévő koponyasérült, ájult beteg): semi-sitting for
  breathlessness, legs raised for shock and fainting, supine with knees bent for abdominal
  injury, head and shoulders raised for a conscious head injury.

## Judgement calls
- Tourniquet: the Red Cross fail list (older text) counts a "szorító körülkötés" on a bleeding
  limb as a fail, while ERC 2025 allows a tourniquet for trained rescuers. No item asks about a
  tourniquet for bleeding; the only mention is as a wrong option for a viper bite (wrong under
  both).
- Pressure points and limb elevation (in the older fail list, no longer ERC advice) are not asked.
- Recovery position after trauma: ERC 2025 keeps a known trauma casualty supine; older Hungarian
  teaching turns breathing unconscious crash victims on their side. Recovery position items use
  non-trauma casualties (VIZ-026 says "Nincs sérülése"), and the trauma items use jaw thrust and
  manual head support.
- Aspirin dose: the 250 mg figure is in the explanation only (Hungarian ambulance practice per
  the Orvosi Hetilap summary); the key says "Egy aszpirint szétrágva".
- Seizure: call when it lasts over 5 minutes (common first aid teaching; also first seizure,
  repeated seizures or injury in the explanation).
- Emergency numbers 112, 104 (ambulance), 105 (fire), 107 (police): standing Hungarian numbers.
