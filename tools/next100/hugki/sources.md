# Sources for `hugki` (Hungary GKI, Driver CPC theory exams, truck and bus)

All 200 questions are original, written in English from the official GKI curriculum, EU law and
Hungarian law listed below. The KAV question bank is not public and was not used; no commercial
GKI app, past paper or provider material was opened or copied. Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam, format and pass mark (OFFICIAL)
- "Tantervi és vizsgakövetelmények az autóbuszvezetői és a tehergépkocsi-vezetői alap- és
  továbbképzési képesítésre felkészítő szaktanfolyamok számára", 2025, signed 2025-03-11 on behalf
  of the Minister for Construction and Transport, in force from 2025-03-17, published by the KAV
  (vizsgakozpont.hu). Read 2026-11-06.
  https://vizsgakozpont.hu/uploads/2025/dokumentumok/tantervi_es_vizsgakov_-_gki_-_20250311_signed.pdf
  - Theory exam subjects of the basic qualification (CA truck, DA bus, CDA combined): BSZAVSZ
    "A biztonsági szabályokon alapuló ésszerű vezetés", EASZ "Előírások alkalmazása",
    EKKKBSZLSZ "Egészségügyi, közúti közlekedési és környezeti biztonság, szerviz, logisztika",
    each a computer-based theory exam; the combined course adds DKIEGSZ / CKIEGSZ (supplementary
    bus or truck knowledge); then ET "Esettanulmány" (case study) and practical exams.
  - Each computer-based subject: "egy 40 kérdésből álló számítógépes feleletválasztós tesztet ...
    A vizsga időtartama 40 perc. Az elérhető maximális pontszám 40 pont, melyből a sikeres
    vizsgához legalább 30 pontot kell elérni." So 40 multiple-choice questions, 40 minutes,
    40 points, 30 to pass (75%). Tigertest: passPct 75 = 30 of 40.
  - Case study: written, 120 minutes, 100 points, 60 to pass. Not modelled.
  - Periodic training with exam (DT, CT) uses the same three theory subjects; DTVN/CTVN is
    periodic training without an exam.
  - Minimum course hours: DA and CA 44, CDA 66, DT and CT 35, DKA/CKA 26, DKT/CKT 17.
  - Attending the course is compulsory; basic course candidates may sit the exams once the theory
    part is certified complete; all exams must be passed within one year of starting the course,
    otherwise course and exams are repeated.
  - Syllabus of the three theory subjects (sections V.A to V.C), used for the categories:
    BSZAVK: resistance to motion, inertia, torque/power/specific fuel consumption curves, gear
    ratios and optimum revs, rev counter, exhaust after-treatment, fuels and alternative fuels,
    hydraulic and vacuum-servo brakes, dual-circuit air brakes, service brake with endurance brake,
    braking downhill, locked wheels, brake faults and checks, ABS, ASR, EBS, ESP, DBL (endurance
    brake limiter), lane keeping, adaptive cruise, brake assist, stability and centre of gravity,
    payload and volume calculation, load distribution and axle overload; bus: forces on
    passengers, safe handling, communication with passengers, children and disabled passengers;
    truck: packaging and pallets, load securing and lashing, sheets.
    EAK: accident prevention, driving and rest times (561/2006, 2002/15/EC), analogue and digital
    tachographs and the driver's duties, checks and sanctions, licence withdrawal, the driver
    penalty point system, the Vienna Convention signs, the social context of road transport,
    transport permits and contracts, consignment documents, international permits, the CMR
    convention, drivers' rights and duties for the basic and periodic qualification; bus: seat
    belts and child restraints, foreign rules, passengers' documents and border crossing; truck:
    customs goods, border crossing, the international consignment note.
    EKKKBSZLK: the place of commercial vehicles in transport, company image, the driver as the
    company's representative, mental and physical load, healthy eating, alcohol, drugs, medicines,
    smoking, fatigue, stress, hazards and accident prevention, what to do in an accident, violence,
    crime, people smuggling and illegal immigration (prevention, checklists, liability), service
    and logistics; bus and truck business organisation.
- Decree 24/2005 (IV. 21.) GKM on training and examining road drivers, in force text on
  net.jogtar.hu, read 2026-11-06. https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=A0500024.GKM
  - 33/A to 33/C §: who must hold the basic qualification (C1, C1E, C, CE licences obtained after
    2009-09-09; D1, D1E, D, DE after 2008-09-10), who may obtain it (EEA citizens normally resident
    in Hungary; non-EEA citizens with a right to work or employed by a business registered in
    Hungary); periodic qualification valid five years, periodic training every five years, with
    or without an exam.
  - The GKI card ("gépjárművezetői képesítési igazolvány") carries the EU harmonised code 95 and
    is valid for 5 years; if periodic training is completed in the 6 months before the old card
    expires, the new card runs five years from the old card's expiry. Loss, theft, destruction,
    illegibility or a change of data must be reported to the exam centre within 15 days.
  - Theory exams are computer-based (SZEV); "A rendelkezésre álló különböző nyelvű
    SZEV-vizsgaanyagok közül a vizsgázó állampolgárságára tekintet nélkül választhat" (any
    available language version may be chosen regardless of citizenship); an interpreter-assisted
    oral exam is possible only where no material exists in the candidate's official language.

## Hungarian rules used in the bank (OFFICIAL)
- KRESZ, 1/1975. (II. 5.) KPM-BM joint decree, in force text read 2026-11-06.
  https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=97500001.kpm
  - 26. § (1): cars and vehicles up to 3,500 kg 130/110/90/50; buses meeting the separate
    technical requirements 100 km/h on motorways, 70 on other roads outside built-up areas, 50 in
    built-up areas; other motor vehicles (trucks over 3,500 kg) and vehicle combinations
    80 km/h on motorways, 70 on other roads outside built-up areas, 50 in built-up areas. There
    is no separate expressway (autóút) figure for these vehicles, so 70 applies there.
  - 27. § (2): a vehicle or combination over 3,500 kg or 7 m long must, outside built-up areas,
    keep a gap behind another vehicle into which an overtaking car can safely pull in (not needed
    when preparing to overtake, with two or more lanes in its direction, or where overtaking is
    banned).
  - 48. §: seat belt exemptions on scheduled buses in built-up areas and on scheduled buses
    outside them that may carry standing passengers; how coach passengers on belted seats must be
    told to wear belts (seat sign, driver, guide or group leader, audiovisual means). 48. § (14):
    heavy trucks (over 7.5 t) outside built-up areas only on motorways, expressways and main
    roads unless the decree provides otherwise; (14a) transit network for heavy trucks with no
    Hungarian destination.
  - Built-up areas: let a signalling scheduled bus, school bus or children's bus pull out of a
    stop (slow down, stop if needed); its driver may move off only once it is safe.
  - Public bus and trolleybus drivers may signal moving off with the indicator only once all doors
    are closed.
  - "Gyermekszállítás" sign: children getting on or off a marked bus on a road not suitable for
    parallel traffic are signalled by operating all four indicators together; such a bus may not
    be overtaken or passed by oncoming traffic.
  - 52. § (6)-(7): broken-down vehicle on the carriageway or hard shoulder outside a built-up area
    marked with a warning triangle; hazard lights or a flashing amber lamp may be used as well.
  - Reflective vest for a person on the carriageway, hard shoulder or verge outside a built-up
    area at night or in poor visibility (used in the landing context only; the KRESZ bank asks it).
  - Driving only with no alcohol from drinking in the body and not under the influence of a
    substance that impairs driving (zero rule; the KRESZ bank asks it, so this bank only refers
    to drugs).
- Government Decree 190/2008. (VII. 29.) on restricting heavy truck traffic, in force text read
  2026-11-06. https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=a0800190.kor
  - Applies to trucks, tractors and combinations over 7.5 t maximum authorised mass, Hungarian or
    foreign. 1 July to 31 August: from 15:00 on Saturday (if not a working day) to 22:00 on
    Sunday, and on public holidays from 22:00 the day before to 22:00 on the day. 1 September to
    30 June: from 22:00 the day before to 22:00 on Sundays and public holidays.
  - Exemptions include live animals, fresh milk and dairy products, fresh and frozen meat, fresh
    bread, perishable food, eggs, fresh fruit and vegetables, flowers, among others.
- HU-GO distance-based e-toll (Act LXVII of 2013): vehicles over 3.5 t pay per kilometre on toll
  motorways and main roads, by on-board unit or a route ticket; buses over 3.5 t joined the
  system in 2024. hu-go.hu FAQ and article "HU-GO pre-registration for buses over 3.5 tons
  available now", read via search 2026-11-06.
  https://hu-go.hu/documents/document/faq ,
  https://hu-go.hu/articles/article/hu-go-pre-registration-for-buses-over-3-5-tons-available-now
  No rates or categories used.

## EU law used (long-standing, stable; the March 2025 curriculum names it)
- Regulation (EC) No 561/2006 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2020/1054 (Mobility Package):
  fixed week; 4.5 h then 45 min break (15 + 30 split, a pause under 15 min does not count);
  daily driving between two daily rests, 9 h (10 h twice a week); 56 h a week, 90 h in two
  weeks; daily rest within each 24 h after the previous rest, 11 h regular, 9 h reduced, split
  3 + 9; weekly rest 45 h, reduced 24 h, compensation en bloc attached to another rest of at
  least 9 h; two consecutive reduced weekly rests in international goods transport outside the
  Member State of establishment (art. 8(6)); daily and reduced weekly rest in a stationary
  vehicle with suitable sleeping facilities (art. 8(8)); multi-manning, second driver optional in
  the first hour (art. 4(o)); availability of a co-driver; art. 10 (no pay by distance or goods
  that endangers road safety; undertaking liability); art. 12 (departure to reach a suitable
  stopping place, reason noted by hand; up to 1 h, or 2 h after a 30-minute break, to reach home
  or base for weekly rest); scope over 3.5 t, extended from 1 July 2026 to vehicles over 2.5 t in
  international transport or cabotage of goods.
- Directive 2002/15/EC (working time): 30 minutes of breaks for 6 to 9 hours of work, 45 minutes
  above 9 hours; working time includes loading, cleaning, checks and administration.
- AETR agreement for journeys partly outside the EU to AETR parties (Serbia, Ukraine).
- Regulation (EU) No 165/2014 (tachographs): periodic inspection at least every two years;
  installation plaque; driver card renewal application at least 15 working days before expiry;
  workshop, company and control cards; ferry/train and out of scope entries; manual entries and
  signed printouts. Smart tachograph version 2 (Regulation (EU) 2021/1228) records border
  crossings automatically; DSRC remote early detection.
- Light commercial vehicles over 2.5 t in international haulage from 1 July 2026: RSA (Ireland)
  "Smart tachograph 2 (LCVs)" page and CMS legal update, read via search 2026-11-06.
  https://www.rsa.ie/services/professional-drivers/tachographs-information/smart-tachograph-2-(lcvs)
- Regulation (EC) No 1072/2009 as amended in 2020: cabotage, three operations within seven days,
  four-day cooling-off; vehicle return to the operational centre within eight weeks (Regulation
  (EC) No 1071/2009 as amended); certified copy of the Community licence; driver attestation for
  non-EU drivers. Regulation (EC) No 1073/2009: journey form for occasional coach services.
- CMR convention: consignment note in three originals (sender, goods, carrier); carrier's
  reservations on the apparent condition of goods.
- EN 12195-1 load securing: 0.8 g forwards, 0.5 g sideways and backwards (road); EN 12195-2
  webbing labels (LC lashing capacity, STF standard tension force).
- Euro pallet 1200 x 800 mm.
