# hukresz Hungarian (hu-HU) glossary

Terms as the KRESZ (1/1975. (II. 5.) KPM-BM együttes rendelet), its Annex 1 sign list and the KAV
"Közlekedési alapismeretek" curriculum use them, in the wording Hungarian learner books (eKRESZ,
e-titán style tananyag) use. Written before the translation; the sources follow it.

Register: questions and explanations in the impersonal or formal form (third-person verb, no
"te", "Ön" only where a subject is needed), as KAV-style questions are phrased ("Milyen
sebességgel haladhat?"). App copy and the landing in "te", like the other Hungarian Tigertest
pages (huelsoseg, hubizt). Decimal comma (0,5 g/l), four-digit numbers without a separator as
the KRESZ writes them (3500 kg, 7500 kg), times as "6 és 22 óra között", km/h, m. No em or en dashes.
