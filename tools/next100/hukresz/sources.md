# Sources for `hukresz` (Hungary KRESZ theory exam, category B)

All 200 questions are original, written in English from the Hungarian Highway Code (KRESZ). The
official KAV question bank and commercial KRESZ apps were not opened or copied. Signs are described
in words (no images). Checked 2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam, format and pass mark
- The exam is the computer-based theory exam "Közlekedési alapismeretek" taken at a centre of the
  KAV Közlekedési Alkalmassági és Vizsgaközpont Nonprofit Kft. (the transport exam centre,
  vizsgakozpont.hu / kavk.hu). The KAV publishes its exam rules as "Tantervi és vizsgakövetelmények"
  per category and "A vizsgáztatás rendje és ügyvitele".
- Format, from a driving school's legally required learner information sheet, which quotes the KAV
  curriculum and exam requirements word for word (Tanulói tájékoztató, 2025-05-01, read 2026-11-01):
  "A vizsga teljesítéséhez egy 55 kérdésből álló számítógépes, feleletválasztós tesztet kell
  sikeresen megoldani. A vizsga időtartama 55 perc, kérdésenként 1 perc a rendelkezésre álló idő.
  Az elérhető maximális pontszám 75 pont, melyből a sikeres vizsgához legalább 65 pontot kell
  elérni." So: 55 multiple-choice questions, 55 minutes (1 minute per question), 75 points
  available, 65 points to pass (86.7%).
  https://ejrok.tirek.hu/data/downloads/2025/05/20/Tanul%C3%B3i_t%C3%A1j%C3%A9koztat%C3%B3_2025.05.01.docx
- The same figures for the B95/B96 neighbours confirm the document style on the KAV site itself
  (Tantervi és vizsgakövetelmény, B96, effective 2024-10-01: 25 questions, 25 minutes, 21 of 25
  points). https://kavk.hu/uploads/2024/dokumentumok/tantervi_es_vizsgakovetelmeny__b96_b_kategoria_20241001_signed.pdf
- Confirmed on the KAV site itself (read 2026-11-01): the KAV's own curriculum and exam requirements
  for the A category course after A1/A2/A korl. (2023-08-23, signed) state the same
  "Közlekedési alapismeretek" exam word for word: "egy 55 kérdésből álló számítógépes,
  feleletválasztós tesztet ... A vizsga időtartama 55 perc. Az elérhető maximális pontszám 75 pont,
  melyből a sikeres vizsgához legalább 65 pontot kell elérni." The category B document itself
  returned 404. https://vizsgakozpont.hu/uploads/2023/dokumentumok/tantervi_es_vizsgakov_a_a1_a2_akorl_kategoria_2023_alairt.pdf
  The KAV examiner-course requirements (vb, July 2026) use the same 55-question / 75-point
  structure (with a stricter 73-point pass for examiners), which shows that questions carry
  unequal points. https://vizsgakozpont.hu/uploads/2026/dokumentumok/tantervi_es_vizsgakov_-_vb_202607.pdf
- Point weighting, GUIDES ONLY: eduline.hu (2020) reports 10 questions worth 3 points and 45 worth
  1 point (10 x 3 + 45 = 75), so you may lose 10 points. Not found in a KAV document; Tigertest
  scores every question equally and uses 87% (48 of 55) as the target. eduKRESZ also describes the
  pass rule as "at most 10 error points". https://eduline.hu/felnottkepzes/20200907_kresz-vizsga
  and https://edukresz.hu/a-jarmuvezetok-vizsgaztatasa
- Conflicting third-party figures NOT used: autoviatest.com gives 65 minutes and 82 points / 65 or
  78 to pass on different language versions.
- Learner information sheet of Autóklub (2025-08-06, read 2026-11-01): theory exam from age 16
  and 9 months; practical exam from 17; a passed "közlekedési alapismeretek" exam is valid for two
  years, after which every passed exam lapses if the licence is not completed; first aid knowledge
  is part of the process (exemptions under NM decree 31/1992).
  https://teszt.autoklub.hu/media/files/autosiskola/T%C3%A1j%C3%A9koztat%C3%B3_20250806.pdf

## Languages
- KAV exam rules (A vizsgáztatás rendje és ügyvitele, 2024-10-01, read 2026-11-01): a foreign
  candidate who does not speak Hungarian may take an oral exam with an interpreter only "if no
  question bank exists in the official language of the candidate's citizenship", i.e. some
  foreign-language banks exist; written foreign-language test sheets are also provided for.
  https://www.vizsgakozpont.hu/uploads/2024/dokumentumok/vizsgaztatas_rendje_es_ugyvitele_20241001_signed.pdf
- Which languages, GUIDES ONLY: eduKRESZ (an e-learning publisher) says the computer-based theory
  exam for motorcycle and category B can be taken in four languages: Hungarian, German, English and
  Romanian. A driving school (citromsuli.hu) says the same of English, Romanian and German.
  https://edukresz.hu/a-jarmuvezetok-vizsgaztatasa , https://citromsuli.hu/irasos-tajekoztatok/
- English and Russian test sheets for professional drivers (GKI) from September 2022 (Xpatloop,
  2022-08). Not about category B; context only.
  http://xpatloop.com/channels/2022/08/driving-tests-available-in-more-languages-in-hungary.html

## The rules: KRESZ (1/1975. (II. 5.) KPM-BM joint decree), in force text read 2026-11-01
https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=97500001.kpm
Rules used, by section:
- s3(2) no hand-held phone while moving; s4 licence, fitness, no drugs, no alcohol in the body at
  all (zero limit for every driver); s4(3) a licence issued abroad must be carried; s5 insurance,
  pre-drive checks of steering, brakes, tyres, lights and plates.
- s6 police hand signals; s7 flag men; s8 pedestrian lights; s9 traffic lights (green, amber, red,
  red and amber; flashing amber; green filter arrow; do not enter a jammed junction; priority signs
  do not apply while lights operate; lane control signals).
- s10-s17 signs: s12 priority signs; s13 mandatory signs (roundabout: island on your left; minimum
  speed); s14 prohibitions (no overtaking exceptions for two-wheelers and trams; speed limit with
  rain plate; c)-g) signs last to the next junction; end of restrictions sign; speed limit with the
  built-up area sign covers the whole area); s15 no stopping / no parking last to the next junction
  on their side; s16 warning signs (Children sign with a distance plate means 30 km/h, s26(6);
  distances 50-100 m, 150-250 m, 250-500 m); s17 information signs (residential zone).
- s18 markings (broken line, solid line, solid beside broken, deflection arrow, stop lines and the
  give-way triangle, BUSZ lane).
- s21 pedestrians (walk on the left facing traffic outside built-up areas; reflective vest at night
  outside built-up areas); s23 passengers; s24 moving off gives way.
- s25 keep right; s26 speed limits (cars 50 / 90 / 110 expressway / 130 motorway; with a trailer
  50 / 70 / 80; snow chains 50; towing 40); s27 following distance, no needless hard braking;
  s28 right-hand rule, tram from the left, dirt road gives way, emergency vehicles; s29 changing
  direction gives way, no signal needed entering a roundabout but needed leaving; s30 horn only in
  danger, or outside built-up areas to signal overtaking; s31 turning (positioning, give way to
  pedestrians, cyclists, trams and oncoming traffic; leaving a bending priority road); s32 narrow
  roads; s33 no U-turn or reversing on motorways, level crossings, one-way roads, roundabouts;
  s34 overtaking; s35 tram at a stop; s36 lanes, bus lanes, climbing lane 70/40 km/h, roundabout
  first exit from the right lane; s37 motorways and expressways (60 km/h capability, hard shoulder,
  overtaking ban for lorries over 7,500 kg 6:00-22:00, emergency corridor); s38 trams; s39 level
  crossings (at least 5 km/h average, flashing white light = may cross, stop for flashing red or a
  moving barrier); s39/A residential zone 20 km/h; s40-s41 stopping and parking distances (5 m
  junction, 5 m zebra for cars, 15 m / 5 m bus stop, 3 m to a solid line, 5.5 m on a one-way road,
  30 m level crossing, pavement 1.5 m free, parking disc); s42 blue lights and amber lights;
  s43 pedestrians; s44-s45 lights (daytime running lights or dipped beams outside built-up areas,
  main beam only outside built-up areas, fog lights, parked vehicles); s46 passengers (driver
  counts, one person on the front seat); s48 seat belts (driver exempt when reversing), children
  under 150 cm in a restraint, under 3 only in a restraint, rear-facing seat only with the airbag
  off, 135 cm on a rear seat; s54 cyclists; s56 faults and the warning triangle; s57 towing; s58
  crashes.

## New KRESZ (not used)
- A new Highway Code was published as a draft on 2026-01-20 (Index, 2026-01-20) and was planned for
  2026-09-01, then reported as slipping to 2027 or later (hegylakok.hu 2026-02; vg.hu 2026-05). No
  publication in the Magyar Közlöny was found as of 2026-11-01, so the bank follows the 1975 KRESZ
  in force. Items avoid rules the draft would change (e.g. pedestrian priority beyond crossings,
  speed limits above 130 km/h).
  https://index.hu/belfold/2026/01/20/kibeszelo-kresz-modositas-valtozasok-peto-attila-kreszprofesszor/
  https://hegylakok.hu/2026/02/uj-celdatumot-kapott-a-kresz-mar-a-2027-es-bevezetes-sem-biztos/
  https://www.vg.hu/vilaggazdasag-magyar-gazdasag/2026/05/kresz-valtozas-borulhatnak-a-tervek-uj-szabalyok

- Rechecked 2026-11-01: net.jogtar.hu shows the 1975 KRESZ with the time state "Hatály: 2026.IV.11. -"
  (open-ended), and a search found no Magyar Közlöny publication of the new code. Still not in force.

## Left out on purpose
Fines, fees, penalty point (pontrendszer) figures, the novice period rules, the practical exam,
category B general facts beyond the 3,500 kg EU definition, and anything in the draft new KRESZ.
