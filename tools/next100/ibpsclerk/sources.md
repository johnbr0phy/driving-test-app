# IBPS Clerk Prelims: sources

Exam: IBPS Common Recruitment Process for Customer Service Associates (CRP CSA, "IBPS Clerk"),
Preliminary Examination. Conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (ibps.in).
All pages below checked on 31 October 2026; the official notification was read in full on 1 November 2026.

  - Section D(I)(a), Preliminary Examination (Objective Test): English Language 30 questions, 30 marks,
    20 minutes (medium English); Numerical Ability 35, 35, 20 minutes; Reasoning Ability 35, 35, 20 minutes;
    total 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes, each test separately timed. "Candidates have to qualify in
    each of the three tests by securing cut-off marks to be decided by IBPS"; an adequate number in each
    category is shortlisted for the Mains. CONFIRMED.
  - Section D(I)(b), Main: 160 questions, 200 marks, 125 minutes (GA 40/50/20, English 40/40/35,
    Reasoning 40/60/35, Quant 40/50/35).
  - Medium: English Language in English only; the other tests in English, Hindi and the state's listed

    Konkani, Urdu, Kannada, Malayalam, Manipuri, Odia, Tamil, Punjabi, Bengali). CONFIRMED.
  - Section E: one fourth (0.25) of a question's marks deducted per wrong answer, none for a blank;
    applies to Prelims and Mains. CONFIRMED.
  - Section F: Mains cutoffs per test and total, decided by State/UT vacancies; Mains marks only in the
    final merit list. Section G: scores across sessions normalised (equi-percentile). CONFIRMED.
  - Important dates (tentative): registration 01.08.2026 to 21.08.2026, PET September 2026, Prelims
    October 2026, Prelims result November 2026, Mains December 2026, provisional allotment March 2027.
    The exact days (10 and 11 October Prelims, 27 December Mains) are from IBPS's calendar as reported by
    guides, not from the notification.
- Not in the notification: five options per question (true of past papers, per every guide), the
  section order on the day, state-wise Prelims cutoffs (past practice).

## Pattern modelled
- Cycles: CRP CSA-XV (2025; prelims 4, 5 and 11 October 2025) and CRP CSA-XVI (2026; online
  registration 1 to 21 August 2026, prelims scheduled 10 and 11 October 2026, mains 27 December 2026).
  The Prelims pattern is the same in both.
- One online objective paper, 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes, sectional timing:
  - English Language: 30 questions, 30 marks, 20 minutes
  - Numerical Ability: 35 questions, 35 marks, 20 minutes
  - Reasoning Ability: 35 questions, 35 marks, 20 minutes
- Penalty: 0.25 of the marks of a question deducted for each wrong answer; none for a blank.
- Prelims is qualifying: candidates must clear sectional and overall cutoffs; the final merit list uses
  Mains marks only. Cutoffs are published per state/UT and category (long-standing IBPS practice; no figure
  is stated anywhere in the bank or landing).
- Languages: the exam is conducted in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages (Oliveboard summary of the
  2026 notification). The bank is English only and the landing says so.

## Question-type mix
No official topic weights exist. Typical Clerk Prelims papers (coaching analyses listed above) carry:
English reading comprehension, cloze, fillers, error detection, para jumbles, sentence improvement;
Numerical simplification and approximation (the biggest block), number series, one or two DI sets and
arithmetic; Reasoning puzzles and seating (about 15 to 20 questions), inequality, syllogism, coding,
blood relations, directions, alphanumeric series, order and ranking.

Tigertest per test: English 12 reading and para jumble, 10 cloze and fillers, 8 error spotting and sentence
improvement; Numerical 15 simplification, approximation and series, 8 data interpretation (two tables),
12 arithmetic; Reasoning 8 inequalities and syllogisms, 15 puzzles (three sets of five), 12 coding,
relations, directions, letter series and ranking. Quadratic equations, common in the PO paper, are left out.
