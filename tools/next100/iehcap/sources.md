# Sources: HCAP, Hunter Competence Assessment Programme (Ireland), `iehcap`

Every question in the bank is original, written from the rules and facts below. No HCAP question
bank, sample paper, Stalker Training Manual question or prep material was read or used, even
reworded; the Deer Alliance says its MCQ bank (about 300 questions) is not published and none was
seen. All sources fetched 2026-11-16 (WebFetch where the site allowed it, otherwise curl with a
browser user agent; PDFs read as text with macOS PDFKit).

## Official: the programme (Deer Alliance HCAP Assessment Committee)

- HCAP certification procedure (the owner's format page): https://www.deeralliance.ie/certification
  - Fetched 2026-11-16. "HCAP is designed to test the knowledge and proficiency of all those who wish to
    hunt wild deer on both Coillte forest property and on private land." Assessment is a written MCQ
    examination, 40 of 50 correct to pass (item 9: pass standard 80%; questions drawn at random from a
    bank of about 300). Then a Range Test (group three shots in a four-inch bull at 100 m prone, then nine
    shots on a deer target, all in the heart/lung area) and an oral safety examination. Fees quoted on
    the page (EUR 150 / 165 / 50 repeat), the 180-day completion window, up to 50 candidates per MCQ and
    "updated 31 December 2015" are not used in questions (fees change; format facts go on the landing
    only). Current 2026 fee EUR 165 and the 2026/2027 calendar come from Deer Alliance's news posts
    (https://deeralliance.ie/blog/hcap-calendar-2027/ , listed on the home page; the posts themselves
    were not opened).
- HCAP Stalker Manual (the core text; contents list = the outline used for the blueprint):
  https://www.deeralliance.ie/hcap-stalker-manual
  - Fetched 2026-11-16. Twelve chapters: I Principles of Deer Management; II Basic Deer Biology;
    III Red Deer; IV Sika Deer; V Fallow Deer; VI Deer Stalking Aids and Techniques; VII Rifles,
    Ballistics and Zeroing; VIII Firearms Safety; IX Deer Hunting and the Law; X Carcase Handling;
    XI Forestry and Deer; XII Impacts of Deer on Nature Conservation Habitats; and thirteen
    appendices. The manual itself ((c) 2013 Deer Alliance HCAP Assessment Committee, Nolan and
    Walsh 2005) is sold in print and was not read; its four published chapters were.
- Published chapter extracts (Deer Alliance, read in full as PDFs, text of the 2005 manual):
  - Principles of Deer Management: https://www.deeralliance.ie/pdfs/principles.pdf
  - Red Deer: https://www.deeralliance.ie/pdfs/red.pdf
  - Sika Deer: https://www.deeralliance.ie/pdfs/Sika.pdf
  - Fallow Deer: https://www.deeralliance.ie/pdfs/fallow.pdf
  - Used for: species identification, biology, rut and calving, cull planning (female cull first,
    calf before accompanying hind, about 25% of the spring census), population assessment methods,
    record keeping, disturbance, high seats, shooting from vehicles, Section 42 licences.
- Deer Alliance FAQ: https://www.deeralliance.ie/faq (read; 2013 text). Used only for biology
  facts that agree with the chapters (calving dates, 240-day gestation, deer are ferae naturae, hummel,
  yeld hind). Its season dates and calibre advice are out of date and are NOT used (see below).
- About Deer Alliance: https://www.deeralliance.ie/about-deer-alliance (read). Background: formed 2003 at
  Coillte's request; HCAP launched April 2005; run by the HCAP Assessment Committee.
- Deer Alliance's own HCAP summary of the programme points (Deer Alliance home page, read): HCAP is
  developed with Coillte, NPWS, the Forest Service and An Garda Siochana. NPWS lists it as one of three
  approved courses (below).

## Official: licensing (NPWS)

- NPWS, Mandatory certification for deer hunters:
  https://www.npws.ie/licencesandconsents/hunting/deer-hunting-licences/mandatory-certification-deer-hunters
  - First-time Deer Hunting Licence applicants must show a certified deer hunting training course;
    three approved courses (Deer Alliance HCAP, Country Sports Ireland, NARGC). No syllabus is given.
- NPWS, Deer hunting licences: https://www.npws.ie/licencesandconsents/hunting/deer-hunting-licences
  - Section 29 deer hunting licences applied for online through the Heritage Licensing Portal; licence
    year 1 August to 31 July; firearm certificate documents required; all Open Season dates in the Open
    Seasons Order must be followed.
- NPWS, Deer Hunting Licence applications FAQ (v.01, 31 May 2024):
  https://www.npws.ie/sites/default/files/files/deer-hunting-licence-application-faqs.pdf
  - Applicants declare compliance with the minimum firearm requirements of S.I. 239/1977, and that they
    will hunt only on land where they have permission under section 28 of the Wildlife Act; licences
    issue online only; no fee; returns completed online; licence runs 1 August to 31 July.
- NPWS, Open Seasons Order (mammals): https://www.npws.ie/legislation/irish-law/open-seasons-order
  - Deer may be hunted only by shooting with firearms in separate seasons for male and for female and
    antlerless deer; red deer excluded from County Kerry; Deer hunters must hold a valid Deer Hunting
    Licence; S.I. 66/2024 amends the 2005 Order. The dates themselves are NOT asked: they changed between
    2013 and 2024 (Deer Alliance's seasons page still shows the old 1 September to 31 December male
    season) and the Irish Deer Management Strategy Group (below) has recommended further review.

## Primary law (Irish Statute Book)

- Wildlife Act 1976, as enacted: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/1976/act/39/enacted/en/print
  - s. 25 open season orders for exempted wild mammals; s. 28 firearms hunting only by a qualified
    person (16 or over with sporting rights or written authority); s. 29 licence to hunt with firearms
    (ordinarily resident persons; runs to 31 July; information on animals shot on request, s. 29(10));
    s. 33(3) no shotgun for protected wild animals other than hare; s. 33(2) no spring gun or tracer
    shot; s. 36 no hunting protected wild animals by mechanically propelled vehicle; s. 37 no hunting
    protected animals from one hour after sunset to one hour before sunrise; s. 38 no lamp, light or
    reflecting device in hunting; s. 42 damage permission from the Minister (out of season, to stop
    damage to crops, woodland etc.); Fifth Schedule lists "Deer species" as protected land mammals.
    The Act has been amended since (2000, 2012, 2023); only provisions the NPWS and Garda sources
    confirm as in force are asked, and no penalty levels.
- S.I. No. 239/1977, Wildlife Act, 1976 (Firearms and Ammunition) Regulations, 1977:
  https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/1977/si/239/made/en/print
  - Deer may be hunted only with the firearms and ammunition in the Schedule (centre-fire rifles, not
    less than .22 calibre, minimum muzzle energy and bullet weight stated); no other type or calibre.
    The Irish Deer Management Strategy Group (Nov 2023) recommends raising the minimum (.243, 100 grain):
    no change had been made when NPWS wrote its 2024 FAQ, but because a reform is pending the bank asks
    only the rule (centre-fire rifle only) and never the figures.
- S.I. No. 307/2009, Firearms (Secure Accommodation) Regulations 2009:
  https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2009/si/307/made/en/print
  - Schedule: one non-restricted shotgun disassembled and trigger-locked; one restricted or up to three
    non-restricted firearms in a gun safe to BS 7558 fixed to a solid structure; more firearms add alarm
    and locks to BS 3621; the top tier adds a monitored intruder alarm.
- An Garda Siochana, Commissioner's Guidelines on the Firearms Acts 1925 to 2009 (version of 12 Nov 2014):
  https://www.garda.ie/en/information-centre/freedom-of-information/freedom-of-information/the-garda-commissioner%E2%80%99s-guidelines-as-to-the-practical-application-and-operation-of-the-firearms-acts-1925-2009-amended-12-11-14-.pdf
  - Firearm certificate (FCA1) needed to possess, use or carry a firearm; certificate names the firearm
    and the amount of ammunition that may be held; valid three years; a deer rifle applicant names land
    where the species is likely and has the landowner's invitation or authority; land is assessed for
    stops, rights of way, public roads and dwellings; disentitled under 16 and "intemperate habits";
    deer rifles normally bolt action, .243 to .308, small magazine; silencers are firearms and need a
    section 7 authorisation under the Firearms and Offensive Weapons Act 1990; zeroing with
    non-expanding ammunition where a suitable range exists; secure storage and transport conditions.
    Fees and the certificate period are not asked (they change).
- An Garda Siochana, Firearms Safety/Security information sheet (August 2009):
  https://www.garda.ie/en/crime-prevention/firearm-security-english.pdf
  - Transport: case or sleeve, out of sight in a locked boot, not accessible to driver or passengers,
    never loaded, bolt removed where possible, never left in the seating area; stop and leave the
    vehicle only after reasonable steps to secure the firearm.
- Irish Deer Management Strategy Group, Final Report (Nov 2023, DAFM, NPWS, Coillte):
  https://assets.gov.ie/278490/802516b7-d01e-4b3e-b8bd-3718779925b9.pdf
  - Context for impacts (biodiversity, agriculture, road safety, forestry, TB, Lyme), Section 42
    licence use, deer management units and woodland regeneration (oak and yew). Recommendations are
    proposals only; no recommended future rule is asked as current law.

## Official: carcase handling and venison (FSAI)

- FSAI, Guidance on Hunting and Processing of Wild Game for Human Consumption:
  https://www.fsai.ie/getmedia/909877be-8c95-4e21-a43d-045af46d2545/9716_fsai-wild-game-guidance-doc-accessible.pdf
  - Fetched 2026-11-16 (also https://www.fsai.ie/enforcement-and-legislation/legislation/food-legislation/game).
    Preparation permitted as normal hunting practice: killing, bleeding, removing stomach and intestines;
    trained person examination on the spot; numbered declaration and individual tag; head and viscera must
    accompany the body if no trained person examined it or an abnormality is suspected; large wild game
    chilled to 7 degrees C or below (small game 4); no heaping in transport; skin-on large game must never
    be frozen; further processing (skinning) only in an approved game handling establishment except private
    domestic consumption; hunters supplying the market are food business operators; hunting for private
    consumption is outside food law. The small-quantity supply limits (S.I. 22/2020) are NOT asked (the
    Strategy Group recommends reviewing them).

  Strategy Group report. The reviewer should check the rifle and firearms-safety categories first.

## Deliberately left out

Season dates and the Coillte permit rules (they change and Coillte conditions vary by licence), fees and
how many questions or what pass mark the test has, the Range Test standard, calibre, energy and bullet
weight figures (reform pending), carcase supply limits under S.I. 22/2020, Section 42 licence statistics,
trophy measurement, the Hoffman pyramid arithmetic, and Northern Ireland, Scottish and English rules
(the manual lists them in an appendix; Ireland's law only here). Educational safety and wildlife-law
content only. Not affiliated with the Deer Alliance, NPWS or Coillte.
