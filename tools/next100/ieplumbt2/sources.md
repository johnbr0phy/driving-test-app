# Sources for `ieplumbt2` (Ireland plumbing apprenticeship, Phase 2 theory)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the official documents below. No SOLAS
exam paper, sample assessment, unit workbook question, past paper, prep site or commercial question
was used or reworded. Checked 2026-11-19.

## Exam owner: SOLAS (curriculum, phases and assessment)
- SOLAS / Generation Apprenticeship, "The Craft of Plumbing" programme brochure (Ver 4),
  https://content.apprenticeship.ie/f/83224/x/4d1ad0289d/plumbing-v4-a4-brochure.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-19). Gave the seven-phase structure and the Phase 2 module list the bank follows: Phase 2
  is delivered in an ETB training centre over 21 weeks with four modules, Thermal Processes and
  Mild Steel Pipework; Domestic Hot and Cold Water Services; Central Heating; Communications and
  Team Leadership. Apprentices are assessed by theory, practical and skills demonstration
  assessments and must pass all phase exams; the award is a Level 6 Advanced Certificate Craft -
  Plumbing.
- SOLAS, Craft Apprenticeship Assessment Rules (2023),
  https://www.solas.ie/f/70398/x/04d6f462bc/solas-craft-apprenticeship-assessment-rules-2023.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: phase 2 assessment schedules are devised with the collaborating
  provider; assessment types include skills demonstrations, practical assessments and theory
  examinations; an apprentice is referred if they fail to reach the pass grade of 50%; repeats,
  re-sits and referrals follow the same conditions as the original assessment.
- QQI validation panel report on SOLAS's five craft apprenticeship programmes (2015),
  https://www.qqi.ie/sites/default/files/media/file-uploads/Validation%20Report%20-%20SOLAS%2C%20five%20apprenticeship%20programmes%20leading%20to%20Advanced%20Certificate%20Craft%20Awards.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: assessment tasks, marking schemes and grading are set centrally by
  SOLAS and marked by the ETBs for Phase 2; off-the-job assessments are marked out of 100 and
  graded fail, pass, merit or distinction.
- QQI External Review Group report on the plumbing award standards,
  https://qsdocs.qqi.ie/Downloads/External%20Review%20Group%20Report%20-%20Plumbing.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-19). Background only: ETB centres provide Phase 2 training.

Could not be read: SOLAS's unit-level Phase 2 curriculum (module and unit specification) is not
published on solas.ie or apprenticeship.ie, and the old SOLAS/FÁS unit notes on local.ecollege.ie
did not resolve. Copies on third-party sites were not used. So the bank follows the four official
module titles, not unit numbers, and the per-module split of the 50-question tests is Tigertest's.
SOLAS publishes no question count or time for the Phase 2 theory papers; the copy says so.

## Building Regulations (Department of Housing, gov.ie)
- Technical Guidance Document G, Hygiene (2008),
  https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/technical-guidance-document-g-hygiene-2008-1474-kb.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: kitchen sink cold supply direct from the service pipe and other
  appliances from a cistern (1.3); cistern 212 litres up to three bedrooms and 340 litres for four
  or more (1.6); covered, not airtight, overflow giving ready warning (1.7); stopcock near the point
  of entry (1.8); frost protection (1.9); WC 6 litre maximum, dual flush reduced flush no more than
  two thirds, replacement rules (2.7, 2.9); WC separated from food areas by a door in a dwelling
  (2.5); washbasin in or next to the WC room (2.4).
- Technical Guidance Document H, Drainage and Waste Water Disposal (2016),
  https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/technical-guidance-document-h-drainage-and-waste-water-disposal-2016-173-mb-19d692ac-3.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: 25 mm retained trap seal and Table 2 trap sizes and seals; 40 mm seal
  to a gully on the ground floor; 450 mm stack connection rule; 1.5 m WC drop to a drain;
  condensate pipe at least 22 mm, acid-resistant, not copper or mild steel, air gap before an
  external trap (1.2.2.7).
- Technical Guidance Document J, Heat Producing Appliances (2014),
  https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/technical-guidance-document-tgd-partj-heat-producing-appliances-2014-213-mb-87264ff5-f.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: CO alarm siting and type (1.5.2, 1.5.3); oil fire valve shut-off
  outside the building (4.7.2); bund above 2,500 litres and 110% capacity (5.2.4, 5.2.5).
- Technical Guidance Document L, Conservation of Fuel and Energy, Dwellings (2022),
  https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/technical-guidance-document-l-conservation-of-fuel-and-energy-dwellings-2022.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: zoning over 100 m², separate time control, hot water controls with an
  auxiliary 60 °C disinfection regime.
- DHLGH and SEAI, Heating and Domestic Hot Water Systems for Dwellings: Achieving compliance with
  Part L (2019),
  https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/heating-domestic-hot-water-systems-for-dwellings-achieving-compliance-with-part-l-ener.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave the heating and hot water rules: 90% HARP boiler efficiency, return
  below 55 °C, control definitions, fully pumped circulation, automatic bypass, cleaning, flushing
  and inhibitor (BS 7593), 200 ppm hardness, commissioning and handover, boiler interlock, TRVs not
  an interlock, cylinder thermostat and zone valve, combi exemption, pipe insulation (1 m from the
  cylinder), cylinder labels, I.S. EN 12897 and BS 1566-1, electric cylinder elements, solid fuel
  heat leak and sealed-system rules, underfloor high-limit thermostat, registered gas installers.

## Water supply (Uisce Éireann)
- Uisce Éireann, Code of Practice for Water Infrastructure (IW-CDS-5020-03),
  https://water.ie/sites/default/files/docs/connections/faqs/Water-Code-of-Practice.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-19). Gave: no connection from an existing service connection, no common service pipes,
  no service connection over 15 m, existing lead service pipes replaced, I.S. EN 1717 backflow
  fittings and non-return valves.

## Safety (HSA and the 2005 Act)
- Health and Safety Authority, Fire Prevention, https://hsa.ie/topics/fire/fire_prevention/
  (fetched 2026-11-19). Gave: definition of hot work; hot work controls (only if no satisfactory
  alternative, permit, remove or protect combustibles, separate fire watcher, leave clean and safe,
  final check at least 60 minutes after); oxygen safeguards (21% to 24%, no oil or grease, open
  valves slowly, never sweeten air, nearly all materials burn); propane and butane as flammable
  gases; separate marked storage for gas cylinders; the Code of Practice for Avoiding Danger From
  Underground Services.
- Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005, section 13,
  https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2005/act/10/section/13/enacted/en/html (fetched 2026-11-19).
  Gave the employee duties used in the safety items.

## Standards used only at concept level
I.S. EN 1057 (copper tube sized by outside diameter, R220 annealed coils, R250 and R290 straight
lengths), I.S. EN 1254 (capillary and compression fittings, listed in TGD G), I.S. EN 10255 (steel
tube for welding and threading, medium and heavy series), ISO 7-1 (55° taper pipe thread, 1 in 16
taper), ISO 857-2 (soldering below 450 °C), EN ISO 3821 (hose colours). No clause text is quoted.

## Left out on purpose
Fees, allowances and phase dates; anything about the SOLAS papers themselves; gas installation
detail beyond the RGI rule (Phase 4 and 6 cover gas); below-ground drainage and wastewater
treatment beyond trap and branch rules; renewable systems (Phase 6). Irish figures are used
throughout; UK Water Regulations, Approved Documents and Gas Safe rules are not used.
