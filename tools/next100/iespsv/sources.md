# Sources: SPSV Entry Test, Industry Knowledge module (iespsv)

All checked 2026-11-05.

## Format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

- NTA, "The SPSV driver entry test":
  https://www.nationaltransport.ie/taxi/driver-licensing/applying-for-an-spsv-driver-licence/the-spsv-driver-entry-test/
  - Must be passed before applying for an SPSV driver licence (not needed for a local area hackney
    driver licence).
  - 90 multiple-choice questions in two modules: Module 1 Industry Knowledge, 54 questions on
    regulations and good practice in the SPSV industry; Module 2 Area Knowledge, 36 questions on
    the county chosen when applying.
  - Pass mark 75% in each module; both must be passed for the chosen county. Pass one and fail the
    other and you retake only the failed module, within 12 months of the original test, otherwise
    the full test.
  - Five test locations; booked through the Industry Information Line. Extra time or a reader can
    be requested. (The fee is on the page; house style leaves money out.)
  - Study material: the Official Manual for Operating in the SPSV Industry, with an audio version.
- NTA, The Official Manual for Operating in the SPSV Industry, Edition 8.1, September 2026:
  https://www.nationaltransport.ie/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/NTA_SPSV_OfficialManual_v8.1-FINAL.pdf
  Chapter 11 (Preparing for your test):
  - Industry Knowledge Module: 54 questions, 54 minutes; Area Knowledge Module: 36 questions,
    36 minutes; with the tutorial the whole sitting takes at most 1 hour 58 minutes.
  - "This Manual is the only material you need to study for this module."
  - Taken on a computer (mouse or touch screen); most questions have one correct answer, some ask
    for two. Answers can be changed within a module; skipped questions are flagged at the end of
    the module; no going back once a module is finished. Results are given immediately and a pass
    in both modules earns the SPSV Skills Development Certificate.
  - After passing, new drivers complete free online Safeguarding and Disability Awareness modules
    (Chapter 1.5, 2.2).
- Languages: neither page says which languages the test is offered in, so the landing does not
  say. Not confirmed.

## What Tigertest models

## Content (every bank item is written from the Manual, Edition 8.1)

- Ch 1 The SPSV industry: six categories, up to eight passengers; NTA set up 1 December 2009,
  Commission for Taxi Regulation joined 2011; Taxi Regulation Acts 2013 and 2016; Advisory
  Committee chosen by the Minister, advice not binding; driver licences granted by An Garda
  Síochána, vehicle and dispatch operator licences by the NTA; one category per vehicle; national
  register (s. 18) and public register on transportforireland.ie (no addresses or suspensions).
- Ch 2 Driver licensing: Class B licence held without endorsement; tax clearance for the whole
  licence; PSV 15 (new), PSV 18 (renewal), PSV 17 (licence); Carriage Office in Dublin; three
  photographs; NDLS licence history; 11 hours on any three consecutive days statement; licence up
  to five years; renew up to three months early; late renewal within 12 months; active, inactive,
  timed out; display card and smart card; lost cards to the Garda PSV Office; silver area
  stickers; stand or ply only in licensed counties, pre-booked trips from any county; Area
  Knowledge Module for each extra county; address change to the NTA in writing within 21 days
  and to the Gardaí; mandatory disqualification (life for murder, rape and some sexual offences;
  road traffic offences: the ban plus three to five years); right to refuse taxi trips over 30 km.
- Ch 3 Vehicle standards and Appendix A: NCT within 90 days (exempt under 3 months and 3,000 km);
  3,500 kg; four doors; 420 litres; windscreen 70% light, band up to 125 mm, numeral V too dark;
  permanent solid roof; side-facing seats only in limousines; 20°C inside at -5°C outside;
  WAV: one wheelchair user plus one adult, or three adults; symbol on roof sign (taxi) or
  passenger door (hackney); ramp at most 16° and 300 kg; Technical Assessor's Full Report within
  60 days; Basic Report for modified limousines; age from first registration in any country;
  new WAV licence up to 6 years, local area hackney 10, limousine no limit; change of vehicle
  for standard taxis 10 years or newer.
- Ch 4 Vehicle licensing: one vehicle per licence; licences cannot be sold; nomination by Form
  S15N for individual holders, nominee applies within 9 months; change of company control cancels
  the licence (return within 14 days); Form VL1; Conditional Offer valid 90 days with a five-digit
  provisional number; new applications currently only for WAVs, local area hackneys and
  limousines; safety equipment (2 kg dry powder extinguisher to EN3, first-aid kit, hi-vis vest,
  warning triangle, torch, pen and paper); taximeter installed before the Initial Suitability
  Inspection, verified and sealed by the Legal Metrology Service; roof sign content; insurance name
  must match exactly; lease letter for at least 12 months; licence 12 months, 6 months over 10 years
  old; renew up to 60 days early.
- Ch 5 Working as an operator: discs (green ring; yellow taxi, blue hackney, silver limousine;
  passenger number); only a Licensing Inspector removes them; driver and owner both liable for an
  unlicensed SPSV; driver-to-vehicle link (criminal offence not to notify; SPSV+ App); renting;
  ranks (local authorities with the Gardaí, customer choice, airport permits, never unattended,
  hackneys and limousines may not stand); with-flow bus lanes only while working, never contra-flow,
  not hackneys; roof sign on when available, off when hired or going to a booking, removed when
  parked off duty, lengthways over 30 km outside urban areas with agreement; cashless device linked
  to the driver, withdraw from service if broken; card problems; reasons to refuse; compliance
  officers; three area-knowledge complaints in three years; fixed payment notices paid in 28 days
  or plus 50% in 56 days.
- Ch 6 Routes: map scales, road colours, grids, legend, Eircode, GPS placement and use.
- Ch 7 Fares: National Maximum Taxi Fare Order (in force from 1 December 2024): meter on every
  journey, agreed lower fare charged, discounts noted on the receipt, printed meter receipts,
  written hackney receipts; meter start for ad hoc and pre-booked trips; initial charge covers
  500 m or 85 seconds; time charging below 21.2 km/h; standard rate 8am to 8pm Monday to Saturday,
  special rate midnight to 4am Saturdays and Sundays; one taximeter area since 2006; booking fee
  only when pre-booked; no extras for assistance dogs or luggage; tolls; hackney fares agreed in
  advance and booking documents; deposits refunded if above the fare. No money amounts are used.
- Ch 8 Customer service: lost property to the Gardaí; complaints (dispatch operator first, NTA
  outcomes, Gardaí for crimes); Equal Status Acts 2000 to 2011 nine grounds; no "cash only";
  ComReg licenses two-way radios; NTA licenses dispatch operators; assisting customers with
  disabilities (guide dog harness white and yellow, assistance dogs blue, no horn for blind
  passengers, arm above the elbow, address the passenger not the interpreter, wheelchair facing
  forward or back with brakes applied, WAV booking priority and the TFI WAV Register).
- Ch 9 Business: VAT exempt; tax return and balance by 31 October; Form 11; preliminary tax 90% /
  100%; Revenue checks up to six years; reduced motor tax for SPSV-only use; sole trader
  liability; only taxis may use "taxi" in advertising; hackney signs need written NTA approval;
  Data Protection Act 2018.
- Ch 10 Safety: 11-hour rule example; Garda high-risk times (weekend nights midnight to 4am,
  street hires); reverse into cul-de-sacs; code word; fare evasion; CCTV and data protection;
  Public Health (Tobacco) (Amendment) Act 2004, No Smoking sign; seatbelts (driver responsible
  under 17; adults 17+ responsible for themselves; children under 150 cm and 36 kg may travel in
  the back without a child seat only if none suitable is available); hands-free calls legal but
  distracting; texting an offence; collision steps; injured people and motorcyclists' helmets;
  manual handling; Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005 duties.

## Fact-check, 2026-11-05

- Fare structure (IESPSV-FAR-009 to FAR-012): Taxi Regulation (Maximum Fares) Order 2024,
  S.I. No. 479 of 2024, read 2026-11-05:
  https://www.nationaltransport.ie/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/S.I.-No.-479-of-2024-Taxi-Regulation-Maximum-Fares-Order-2024.pdf
  In operation 1 December 2024; Tariff A initial distance allowance 500 m, time allowance
  84.9 s (the Manual rounds to 85; FAR-009's explanation says so); changeover speed 21.2 km/h at
  all rates; Standard (1) 08.00 to 20.00 Monday to Saturday; Premium (2) 20.00 to 08.00, all
  Sunday and public holidays other than Christmas and New Year; Special (3) every Saturday and
  Sunday 00.00 to 04.00 and Christmas Eve 20:00 to St Stephen's Day 08:00, New Year's Eve 20:00
  to New Year's Day 08:00. Confirmed. These change with a new fare order.
- Equal Status Acts grounds (IESPSV-CUS-005): IHREC, Protected grounds, read 2026-11-05:
  https://www.ihrec.ie/your-rights/protected-grounds
  Gender, civil status, family status, sexual orientation, age, religious belief, Traveller
  community, race, disability, plus housing assistance (ESA only). Neither political opinion nor
  regional origin is a ground. Confirmed; the explanation now says "one of the protected grounds"
  rather than "one of the nine" because the Acts carry the tenth (housing assistance) ground.
- Form 11 (IESPSV-BUS-003): Revenue, Filing your tax return, read 2026-11-05:
  https://www.revenue.ie/en/self-assessment-and-self-employment/filing-your-tax-return/index.aspx
  Self-employed people file Form 11 through ROS. Confirmed. The tax-credits clause was not
  confirmed on that page and was removed from the explanation.
- Vehicle licence categories open (IESPSV-VLC-010): NTA, Applying for an SPSV vehicle licence,
  read 2026-11-05:
  https://www.nationaltransport.ie/taxi/vehicle-licensing/applying-for-an-spsv-vehicle-licence/
  The page lists only Wheelchair Accessible Taxi, Wheelchair Accessible Hackney, Limousine and
  Local Area Hackney as categories to apply for, matching Manual Edition 8.1. Confirmed; can
  change at any time.
- Advisory Committee (IESPSV-IND-008): Taxi Regulation Act 2013, s. 72(3)(a) and (6) (revised),
  read 2026-11-05: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2013/act/37/section/72/revised/en/html
  "The Minister shall appoint persons to be ordinary members"; the Minister for Transport holds
  the function today (the Manual uses the older title). Confirmed. NTA committee page:
  https://www.nationaltransport.ie/about-us/advisory-committee-on-spsvs/ (s. 72, chairperson and
  17 ordinary members).
- IESPSV-VEH-002: the unchecked sentence about private cars' first NCT at four years was removed
  from the explanation (ncts.ie refused the fetch). The key (under 3 months and 3,000 km) is from
  the Manual, Ch 3.
- IESPSV-SAF-020: explanation now states only what the Manual says (leave the helmet on); the
  general first-aid reasoning was removed.
- Languages the test is offered in: not on the NTA test page or in the Manual; the landing and
  examFacts still leave it out. Not confirmed.
- Per-chapter weights 4/6/5/5/8/3/5/5/4/3/6: Tigertest's own, as the landing, spec.json and
  WIRING.md already say (the NTA publishes no split). No source exists to confirm.
