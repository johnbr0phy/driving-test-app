# CAT 2026 (IIM Common Admission Test): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from the official CAT 2026 format below.
No official mock test item, past CAT paper, coaching institute material or question site was used,
not even reworded. CAT has no published syllabus (official FAQ Q14), so the content is the general
verbal, data and quantitative reasoning the three section names describe.

Exam: Common Admission Test 2026, conducted by the Indian Institutes of Management; convening
institute IIM Indore (Convener Prof. Aditya Billore). Official website https://iimcat.ac.in (the
portal is a JavaScript shell that loads its pages from XML files; the files below were fetched
directly). All fetched 2026-11-15.

## Format: official pages

- CAT 2026 Press Release, IIM Indore, 26 July 2026 (PDF linked from the iimcat.ac.in home page):
  https://cdn.digialm.com/per/g06/pub/32842/EForms/image/CAT2026/CAT_2026_Press_Release_26-07-2026.pdf
  fetched 2026-11-15. Gave: test on Sunday 29 November 2026 in three sessions; "The duration of the
  test will be 120 minutes"; three sections in order, Section I Verbal Ability and Reading
  Comprehension, Section II Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning, Section III Quantitative
  Ability; "exactly 40 minutes" for each section, and candidates "will not be allowed to switch from
  one section to another while answering questions in a section"; mock test on the CAT website;
  about 170 test cities; registration 3 August to 15 September 2026.
- CAT 2026 Information Bulletin, 26 July 2026 (V2), 6 pages:
  https://cdn.digialm.com/per/g06/pub/32842/EForms/image/CAT2026/CAT2026InformationBulletin_26-07-2026_V2.pdf
  fetched 2026-11-15. Gave: computer-based test on 29 November 2026 in three sessions; the 22
  participating IIMs and their programmes; eligibility (bachelor's degree with 50%, 45% for SC, ST
  and PwBD; final-year students may apply); reservation 15% SC, 7.5% ST, 27% NC-OBC, up to 10% EWS,
  5% PwBD; registration fee Rs 1350 (SC, ST, PwBD) and Rs 2700 (others); results likely by the first
  week of January 2027; score valid only till 31 December 2027; each IIM sets its own cut-offs and
  selection process. Fee and dates appear on the landing only, never in questions.
- CAT 2026 FAQs, iimcat.ac.in (XML behind the FAQ page):
  https://iimcat.ac.in/per/g06/pub/32842/ASM/WebPortal/1/1/XML/faq.xml
  fetched 2026-11-15 (also read through WebFetch). Gave: Q8 the three sections; Q9 no own
  calculator, "onscreen calculator that will be available throughout the exam"; Q10 no negative
  marks for non-MCQ questions; Q11 all questions carry equal marks and non-MCQ answers must be
  typed; Q12 the number of non-MCQs per section "varies and cannot be disclosed"; Q13 sections are
  answered in a fixed order, the same for all candidates; Q14 no defined syllabus; Q15 MCQs +3 for a
  correct answer, -1 for an incorrect one, 0 unattempted, non-MCQs +3 correct and 0 for incorrect or
  unattempted; PwBD time extension 20 minutes an hour (40 minutes for the 2-hour test) split equally
  across the three sections; one scribble pad and pen given at the centre.
- CAT 2026 home page XML (dates and document links):
  https://iimcat.ac.in/per/g06/pub/32842/ASM/WebPortal/1/1/XML/home.xml
  fetched 2026-11-15. Gave: registration 3 August to 22 September 2026 (extended; see the media
  release of 22 September 2026), admit card from 6 November 2026, test day 29 November 2026.

## Could not be confirmed on an official page, and how the copy words it

- Question count per section and in total: the IIMs do not publish it (FAQ Q12 says even the number
  of non-MCQ items "cannot be disclosed"). Tigertest's mock tests use 24, 20 and 22 questions
  (66 in all) and the landing says this is Tigertest's model, not an official figure.
- Number of options in an MCQ: not stated in the official documents. The bank uses four, and the
  landing says so.
- Scaled score and percentile method: the 2026 bulletin says only that scorecards will be on the
  website. The copy says "guides describe" a scaled score and percentile, and that Tigertest's
  percent score is not a percentile estimate.
- Para-jumble and odd-sentence items are written as typed (TITA) answers because the official FAQ
  says non-MCQ answers are typed; their share in the real paper is not published.

## Deliberately left out

- Any question about the test itself (sections, timing, marking, fee, eligibility).
- Current affairs, office-holders and facts that change; QA and DILR use made-up data only.
- Numbers or stems already used in the other India banks (the build warns on a shared stem or a
  shared set of numbers with ssccgl, ibpspo, sbipo, sbiclerk, ibpsclerk, rrbntpc and the rest).
