# INBDE bank sources

All 200 questions are original. No JCNDE practice question, past item, review-book item or
commercial question was used or reworded; the Candidate Guide's sample items (touch sensation and
nerve fibers, the indicator for surgical intervention of a carious lesion, the phrenic nerve,
spontaneous malignant transformation and Paget disease, antiarrhythmics, saliva, the site of an
acute allergic reaction) were avoided as topics.

## Outline and format (exam owner: Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations)
- JCNDE, INBDE 2026 Candidate Guide, updated 12/15/2025 (PDF, read in full, fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://jcnde.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/jcnde/files/2026_inbde_candidate_guide.pdf
  - Domain of Dentistry: 56 Clinical Content (CC) areas in three sections, Diagnosis and Treatment
    Planning (CC 1 to 15), Oral Health Management (CC 16 to 38), Practice and Profession (CC 39 to 56),
    and 10 Foundation Knowledge (FK) areas: FK1 molecular, biochemical, cellular and systems-level
    development, structure and function; FK2 physics and chemistry to explain normal biology and
    pathobiology; FK3 physics and chemistry of technologies and materials; FK4 genetic, congenital and
    developmental diseases and conditions; FK5 immune and non-immune host defense; FK6 general and
    disease-specific pathology; FK7 biology of microorganisms; FK8 pharmacology; FK9 behavioral
    sciences, ethics and jurisprudence; FK10 research methodology and analysis, and informatics tools.
  - Test specifications (rounded): Diagnosis and Treatment Planning 36%, Oral Health Management 42%,
    Practice and Profession 22%; FK totals 12/7/8/11/9/12/11/11/11/10%.
  - Items: multiple choice with one best answer of 3 to 5 options; completion, question and
    negatively worded (EXCEPT, LEAST, NOT) forms; some items use a patient box and dental charts.
  - Administration: two days at a Prometric center (second day within 7 days, same center).
    Day 1: three sections of 100 standalone items (105 minutes each) and one section of 60 case
    items; Day 2: two sections of 70 case items. 500 items, 12 hours 30 minutes in all including
    the optional tutorial, breaks and survey. Some items are unscored.
  - Scoring: reported pass or fail; scale scores 49 to 99, 75 is the minimum passing score and is not
    a percentage; no penalty for guessing; failing candidates get performance information overall,
    for the three clinical content sections and the ten FK areas.
  - Retesting: at least 60 days between attempts; Five Years / Five Attempts rule. Fees in the
    guide (examination fee $890) are not used in the bank.
- JCNDE, Foundation Knowledge for the General Dentist (PDF, fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://jcnde.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/jcnde/files/foundation_knowledge_general_dentist.pdf
  Topic lists for each FK area (for example FK2.3 critical pH, statherin, erosion from GERD and
  bulimia, tongue jewelry; FK2.4 contralateral subcondylar fracture after a blow to the mandibular
  body; FK2.5 ergonomics, carpal and cubital tunnel; FK3 radiation, materials and lasers).
- JCNDE INBDE history FAQ, https://jcnde.ada.org/inbde/inbde-history (fetched 2026-11-16): the INBDE
  replaced NBDE Part I (last given July 31, 2020) and Part II (last given 2022); first scored INBDE
  administration August 1, 2020.
- JCNDE INBDE preparation page, https://jcnde.ada.org/inbde/inbde-prepare (fetched 2026-11-16):
  links to the guide, Domain of Dentistry, test specifications and FK document.

## Clinical and legal sources
- ADA, Antibiotic Prophylaxis Prior to Dental Procedures (summarizing the AHA 2021 scientific
  statement and the ADA 2015 prosthetic joint guideline), updated July 15, 2026, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/antibiotic-prophylaxis
  Highest-risk cardiac conditions; no cephalosporin after anaphylaxis, angioedema or urticaria with
  penicillin; clindamycin no longer recommended (C. difficile); a missed dose may be given up to
  2 hours after; a patient already on an antibiotic gets a different class.
  (The AHA statement itself, ahajournals.org, returned 403, so no dose is asked.)
- ADA, Oral Anticoagulant and Antiplatelet Medications and Dental Procedures, updated
  September 28, 2022, fetched 2026-11-16:
  https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/oral-anticoagulant-and-antiplatelet-medications-and-dental-procedures
  For most patients on DOACs no change to the regimen is needed, with local bleeding control.
- ADA News, February 5, 2024, on the ADA guideline for acute dental pain in adults and adolescents
  (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://adanews.ada.org/ada-news/2024/february/new-ada-guideline-recommends-nsaids-to-manage-dental-pain-in-adults-adolescents/
  NSAIDs with or without acetaminophen first line.
- ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct, 2025 edition (PDF, fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/ada-org/files/about/2025_code_of_ethics_full.pdf
  Five principles; 2.B consultation and referral (specialists return the patient), 2.D personal
  impairment, 2.F patient abandonment, 3.E abuse and neglect, 4.A patient selection and 4.A.1
  bloodborne pathogens, 4.B emergency service, 4.C justifiable criticism, 5.A.1 amalgam removal,
  5.B.1 waiver of copayment, 5.B.4 treatment dates.
- CDC, Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings, 2003 (MMWR RR-52(17), PDF,
  fetched 2026-11-16): https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/PDF/rr/rr5217.pdf
  Occupational transmission risk of HBV, HCV and HIV; washing exposure sites; weekly spore testing
  and a biological indicator in every load with an implantable device; deferring elective care for
  active TB; sterilization versus high-level disinfection.
- CDC, Summary of Infection Prevention Practices in Dental Settings (2016, page dated May 15, 2024),
  https://www.cdc.gov/dental-infection-control/hcp/summary/index.html (fetched 2026-11-16).
- American Academy of Periodontology, Staging and Grading Periodontitis (2017 World Workshop),
  https://www.perio.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Staging-and-Grading-Periodontitis.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-16). Used for background only; the NBDHE bank already asks staging and grading.
- DailyMed, articaine hydrochloride and epinephrine injection label (published October 29, 2026,
  fetched 2026-11-16): https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=905cade1-9b37-486a-ac12-21f3262ab4e2
  Maximum 7 mg/kg; 40 mg/mL; epinephrine 0.018 mg/mL at 1:100,000; methemoglobinemia warning.
- HIPAA: HHS Privacy Rule right of access (45 CFR 164.524, action within 30 days), Breach
  Notification Rule (45 CFR 164.404, no later than 60 days after discovery; smaller breaches logged
  to HHS each year) and the business associate definition (45 CFR 160.103), from the eCFR text:
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164 (checked 2026-11-16).
- Basic science items (anatomy, histology, physiology, biochemistry, microbiology, immunology,
  pathology, pharmacology, research methods) state standard textbook facts named by the FK topic
  lists; every calculation (articaine dose, epinephrine mass, inverse square law, specificity,
  number needed to treat) was recomputed.

## Bank decisions
- Ten categories, one per Foundation Knowledge area, so a student's results map onto the areas the
  JCNDE reports. Per 50-question test: FK1 6, FK2 3, FK3 4, FK4 6, FK5 4, FK6 6, FK7 6, FK8 5,
  FK9 5, FK10 5, the guide's FK totals (12/7/8/11/9/12/11/11/11/10% of 100) halved and rounded
  to 50. This split is Tigertest's. Clinical content (the three CC sections) runs through every
  category rather than being a category of its own.
- Case-based items (about a quarter of the bank) are self-contained text scenarios opening with
  "Case:"; findings are described in words, with no charts, radiographs or photographs. On the real
  exam one case carries several items; here each item stands alone.
- Four options per item (the real exam uses 3 to 5). No negatively worded items.
- 75% is Tigertest's readiness line; the real 75 is a scale score, not percent correct.
- Kept clear of data/nbdhe-questions.json and data/danb-questions.json: items that repeated a fact and
  answer there (needlestick transmission risk, handpiece sterilization, weekly spore testing,
  rectangular collimation, glass ionomer fluoride release, hepatitis B vaccine timing, records for a
  patient with a balance, tell-show-do, HIPAA disclosure for treatment) were replaced.

## Not confirmed or left out
- Fees, dates, retake waits and anything about the exam itself are not asked.
- No antibiotic or emergency drug doses from the AHA statement (not readable), no staging, fluoride,
  blood pressure or toothpaste figures (NBDHE covers them), and no state law beyond the ADA Code and
  federal HIPAA and CDC guidance.
- Not affiliated with the JCNDE or the ADA.
