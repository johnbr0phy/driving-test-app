# RRB JE CBT 1 (`inrrn`) sources

Every question in this bank is original, written from the syllabus headings and from standard
class 10 science and static general knowledge. No RRB previous-year paper, official mock test,
coaching institute's question or app item was used, reworded or consulted for wording.

## Official pages (watch these first)

- **RRB Chandigarh, CEN 04/2026 page and the RRB portal**: https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in/ and
  https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in/ (the notice's para 16.0 names the latter as the official site for
  updates). Both, and https://rrbsecunderabad.gov.in/advertisement_category/cen-04-2026-je-dms/,
  timed out or refused connections from this environment (curl, WebFetch and Chrome), checked
  2026-11-22. Not read directly.

## Pattern followed

**Railway Recruitment Boards, Detailed Centralised Employment Notice CEN No. 04/2026, Recruitment
for various posts of Junior Engineer and Depot Material Superintendent** (77 pages). The official
PDF itself was read, from a byte copy hosted on a prep site's CDN, because the RRB hosts would not
connect: https://cdn.testbook.com/1786626977046-CEN_04_2026_JE_DMS_English-1-77.pdf/1786626979.pdf
(fetched 2026-11-22, 4.4 MB, text extracted with PDFKit; the document carries the Ministry of
Railways / Railway Recruitment Boards header and "CEN No. 04/2026 (JE/DMS)" on every page). It gave:

- Important dates: indicative notice in Employment News 25.07.2026; online applications
  14.08.2026 to 13.09.2026; CBT dates to be announced on RRB websites (not stated in the copy).
- Posts: Junior Engineer and Depot Material Superintendent, Level 6 (initial pay ₹35,400, not used
  in the copy), age 18 to 33 on 01.01.2027, 3,993 vacancies in the notice (later corrigenda are
  reported by guides to have raised it; no vacancy figure is used in the copy).
- Para 13.1, 1st stage CBT, common for all posts: 90 minutes (120 with a scribe), 100 questions,
  objective multiple choice; Mathematics 30, General Intelligence & Reasoning 25, General Awareness
  15, General Science 30, one mark each, "only indicative" with "some variations" possible. Screening
  only: the normalised score is used only to shortlist for the 2nd stage. Negative marking, 1/3 of
  the marks for each wrong answer. Minimum percentage for eligibility UR 40, EWS 40, OBC (NCL) 30,
  SC 30, ST 25 (PwBD may be relaxed by 2 marks).
- The CBT 1 syllabus headings, which the eight drill domains follow: Mathematics (number systems,
  BODMAS, decimals, fractions, LCM and HCF, ratio and proportion, percentages, mensuration, time and
  work, time and distance, simple and compound interest, profit and loss, algebra, geometry,
  trigonometry, elementary statistics, square root, age calculations, calendar and clock, pipes and
  cistern); General Intelligence and Reasoning (analogies, alphabetical and number series, coding
  and decoding, mathematical operations, relationships, syllogism, jumbling, Venn diagram, data
  interpretation and sufficiency, conclusions and decision making, similarities and differences,
  analytical reasoning, classification, directions, statement, arguments and assumptions);
  General Awareness (current affairs, Indian geography, culture and history including the freedom
  struggle, polity and constitution, economy, environmental issues, sports, scientific and
  technological developments); General Science (physics, chemistry and life sciences up to the 10th
  standard CBSE syllabus).
- Para 13.2, 2nd stage CBT: 150 questions in 120 minutes (General Awareness 15, Physics and
  Chemistry 15, Basics of Computers and Applications 10, Basics of Environment and Pollution Control
  10, Technical Abilities 100), shortlisting 15 times the community-wise vacancies, virtual
  calculator in CBT 2 only. Para "NOTE-I": calculators are not allowed in CBT 1. Not modelled.
- Medium: English, Hindi and 13 regional languages; questions shown in the chosen language and in
  English. The bank is in English only.

The same CBT 1 scheme, wording and qualifying marks appear in the previous notice, CEN 05/2025
(JE, DMS and CMA, 2,569 posts, applications 31.10.2025 to 30.11.2025), read from a third-party
text copy at https://docs.aglasem.com/view/6db938f2-8cb5-11f1-aa41-0aa932765c8b (fetched
2026-11-22), so the pattern is stable across two cycles.

Reported, not confirmed on an RRB page: CEN 04/2026 CBT 1 on 27 and 28 October 2026 (testbook news,
https://testbook.com/news/rrb-je-notification-2026-27-out/, fetched 2026-11-22) and corrigenda
raising the vacancies. Neither is used in the copy.

## Science and general awareness

Science items keep to the CBSE class 9 and 10 science textbooks (NCERT, https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php,
could not be loaded from here on 2026-11-22: certificate error); general awareness items are static
facts of Indian history, the Constitution (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ and
https://legislative.gov.in/constitution-of-india/, not loaded: socket closed), geography, the
economy, environment, culture and sports. Articles and Schedules cited: 108,
110(3), 118, 246A, 279A, 280, 352, 356, 360 and 368, and the Seventh, Eighth and Tenth Schedules. Facts that date (office
holders, current affairs, schemes, prize winners after 2023) are left out. Every number in a maths,
reasoning or science item was recomputed by script while writing, and the build asserts that each
sorted number key is the value written as the key.

## What the bank covers and leaves out

- Two full 100-question tests (Maths 30 as arithmetic 15 and other topics 15; Reasoning 25 as
  verbal 13 and logical 12; General Awareness 15; General Science 30 as physics 10, chemistry 10,
  biology 10) plus 24 drill-only items, 224 in all, four-option single answer. The split inside each
  subject is Tigertest's.
- No current affairs, no figural reasoning (mirror images, paper folding, embedded figures), Venn
  questions asked in words, data interpretation as short lists of figures.
- CBT 2 (technical abilities by engineering group, computers, environment) is not modelled.
- Negative marking is explained on the landing and in the score note, not scored.
- Indian English, rupees, Indian names.
- Duplicate check (2026-11-22) by script against every IN-region v2 bank on disk (42 exams, about
  16,700 items): same set of stem numbers, or the same non-numeric key with a similar stem. About 110
  items were rewritten in two passes. What remains shares only a template and a common answer
  (an HCF of 12, "Only conclusion I follows", a train passing in 12 seconds with other lengths and
  speeds).
