# ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) sources

All items are original, written from ISC2's own CC exam outline and from primary NIST standards and the ISC2 Code of Ethics. No item is copied or reworded from ISC2's official training, the CC self-paced course quizzes, any prep provider, question site or another Tigertest bank.

## Outline
- **CC Certification Exam Outline**, ISC2, "effective September 1, 2026", fetched 2026-11-16. https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc/cc-certification-exam-outline
  Domains and average weights: 1 Security Principles 24%, 2 Security Governance 17.3%, 3 Identity and Access Management (IAM) Concepts 20%, 4 Networking and Cloud Security Concepts 21.3%, 5 Security Operations and Incident Response 17.3%. Subtopics 1.1 to 5.5 (cybersecurity concepts and AAA, risk management lifecycle, governance and frameworks, technical, administrative and physical controls, due care and due diligence and the ISC2 Code of Ethics; GRC, BC and DR, security awareness, KRIs, dashboards and scorecards; identity lifecycle, least privilege, separation of duties, access control models; OSI and TCP/IP, IPv4 and IPv6, VPN, firewalls, wireless, ICS and IoT, segmentation, defense in depth, Zero Trust, cloud characteristics, service, deployment and shared responsibility models; data handling, encryption including quantum-resistant cryptography, logging and triage, threat actors, threat intelligence and frameworks, the IR plan and exercises, asset lifecycle and end of life, configuration and change management, red, blue and purple teaming, application testing, physical penetration testing). The page names no newer outline.

## Format
- Same outline page (fetched 2026-11-16): 100 to 125 items, 2 hours, Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT) with multiple choice and advanced item types, passing score 700 of 1000, Pearson VUE test centers, English, Chinese, Japanese, German and Spanish (Chinese only in select windows).
- **Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)** certification page, ISC2, fetched 2026-11-16. https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc
  No work experience required; ANAB accredited to ISO/IEC 17024; approved under DoDM 8140.03; exam code to be scheduled and taken within 365 days of purchase; "Peace of Mind Protection" exam-only purchase with two attempts within 180 days. The page shows no price, so no fee is stated anywhere in the copy.

## Standards and primary texts used for facts
- **ISC2 Code of Ethics**, ISC2, fetched 2026-11-16. https://www.isc2.org/ethics
  Preamble and the four canons in order (protect society, the common good, public trust and the infrastructure; act honorably, honestly, justly, responsibly and legally; provide diligent and competent service to principals; advance and protect the profession).
- **NIST CSWP 29, The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**, February 26, 2024, fetched 2026-11-16. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/cswp/29/the-nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20/final (PDF https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf)
  Six Functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, with their definitions.
- **NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3**, Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management (April 2025, supersedes Rev. 2), fetched 2026-11-16. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final
  Incident response as part of CSF 2.0 risk management. Because Rev. 2's four-phase lifecycle is superseded, no item asks for "the NIST lifecycle" by phase name; items ask only the order of work that stays true (contain before eradicating, then recover, then learn lessons).
- **NIST SP 800-145**, The NIST Definition of Cloud Computing (September 2011), fetched 2026-11-16. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/145/final
  Five essential characteristics, three service models (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS), four deployment models (private, community, public, hybrid).
- **NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2**, Guidelines for Media Sanitization (September 2025, supersedes Rev. 1), fetched 2026-11-16. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/88/r2/final (PDF https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-88r2.pdf)
  Clear, purge (including cryptographic erase) and destroy.
- Standing concepts from NIST publications, not quoted line by line: SP 800-12 Rev. 1 (An Introduction to Information Security: CIA, control types, risk), SP 800-34 Rev. 1 (contingency planning, BIA, RTO and RPO, hot, warm and cold sites), SP 800-207 (Zero Trust architecture), FIPS 203 (August 2024 post-quantum key encapsulation, ML-KEM). https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/12/r1/final https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final
- Well-known port numbers (22 SSH, 23 Telnet, 25 SMTP, 53 DNS, 80 HTTP, 443 HTTPS, 3389 RDP) from the IANA Service Name and Transport Protocol Port Number Registry. https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/service-names-port-numbers.xhtml

## Not modelled or left out
- The real exam is adaptive (CAT) and scaled 0 to 1000; Tigertest's tests are linear 100-item mocks scored as percent correct, and the copy says so.
- Advanced item types other than ordering and matching are not modelled.
- No fees, annual maintenance fees, CPE counts or retake waits in questions or copy.
- No item asks NIST SP 800-61's phase names, since Rev. 3 superseded Rev. 2's lifecycle.
- No product names, no named threat groups and no current events.
