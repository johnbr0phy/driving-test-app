# ISEE Middle Level (`isee`): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from the ISEE's published section
descriptions and the grade 6 and 7 Common Core standards. No ISEE sample question, practice test
item, online sample test, Test Innovators item, retired test or commercial prep material was used
or reworded. The reading passages are original.

## Format and outline (E3n, the test owner: the nonprofit formed by the merger of ERB and EMA)

- ISEE Quick Facts Guide 2026-2027 (version V.08.01.26).
  https://cdn.erblearn.org/www/20260801_E3n_ISEE_QuickFacts_DIGITAL.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Middle Level is for students applying to Grades 7 and 8: Verbal Reasoning 40 questions in 20
  minutes, Quantitative Reasoning 37 in 35, Reading Comprehension 36 in 35, Mathematics Achievement
  47 in 40, Essay 1 prompt in 30 (unscored, sent to the schools); 2 hours 40 minutes plus two
  10-minute breaks. Online and paper formats (as available) have the same number of questions, timing and chance to change
  answers. Scores are based only on correct answers ("Incorrect answers are not deducted"); you may
  return to skipped questions within the current section only; no calculators, rulers or books
  unless an approved accommodation; performance is compared only with students at the same grade
  level. Testing seasons fall, winter and spring/summer, once per season.
- ISEE overview and levels page (admission.org, run by E3n; erblearn.org/families/isee-by-erb
  redirects here). https://www.admission.org/assessments/isee/about (fetched 2026-11-14). Same
  Middle Level table: 161 questions, 2 hr 40 min plus two breaks; level set by the grade applied to.
- ISEE preparation page. https://www.admission.org/assessments/isee/preparation (fetched
  2026-11-14). Links the What to Expect guides and the online sample tests.
- What to Expect on the ISEE, Middle Level (ERB guide, 2021 edition).
  https://cdn.erblearn.org/www/20210712_ERB_ISEE_What_to_Expect_Guide_Middle-Level.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-14; only its descriptive pages were read for this bank, never its sample or practice
  items). Gave: Verbal Reasoning is synonyms and one-word sentence completions, four answer choices,
  synonym choices listed in alphabetical order; Quantitative Reasoning is word problems and
  quantitative comparisons (Column A and Column B with the four fixed answers: A greater, B greater,
  equal, cannot be determined), little or no calculation, built on the NCTM strands Numbers and
  Operations, Algebra, Geometry, Measurement, Data Analysis and Probability, Problem Solving;
  Reading Comprehension is six passages with six questions each on arts, contemporary life,
  history and science, in six categories (main idea, supporting ideas, inference, vocabulary,
  organization/logic, tone/style/figurative language); Mathematics Achievement uses the same
  strands, may need calculation and math vocabulary, gives US customary conversions in the
  question but expects metric conversions within a unit to be known; no calculator; each section
  carries some unscored tryout items; scores are reported as scaled scores, percentile ranks and
  stanines against same-grade applicants.

## Content standards (primary text the items are written from)

- Common Core State Standards, Mathematics, Grade 7 introduction.
  https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/introduction/ (fetched 2026-11-14): ratios and
  proportional relationships, the number system, expressions and equations, geometry, statistics
  and probability.
- Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (CCSSO PDF).
  https://learning.ccsso.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Math_Standards1.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14;
  the fetch returned the PDF, used for grade 6 and 7 domains).
- Common Core State Standards, Reading: Literature, Grade 7.
  https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RL/7/ (fetched 2026-11-14): theme, inference from
  text evidence, figurative and connotative meaning, structure, point of view.

## Could not load or confirm

- NCTM's Principles and Standards page (nctm.org) answered 402 (paywalled), so the NCTM strands
  are taken from the ISEE guide's own list and the content from the Common Core grade 6 and 7
  standards that grew out of them.
- The number of unscored tryout items in each Middle Level section: the guide's figures did not
  extract cleanly, so the copy says only that some items are unscored.
- The 2026-2027 Quick Facts Guide does not restate the question types; the 2021 What to Expect
  guide is the newest description E3n links, and its section counts match the 2026-2027 guide.

## Deliberately left out

- The essay (unscored on the real test).
- Scaled scores, percentile ranks and stanines: they compare a student with same-grade applicants
  in E3n's norm group, which a practice form cannot do, so scores here are percent correct per
  section.
- The Lower, Upper and Primary levels: this bank models the Middle Level only.
- Fees, registration, accommodations and test-day rules (landing and FAQ only, never questions).
