# Glossary for `itceli` (Italian, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations neutral and impersonal ("Quale forma completa la frase?",
"Che cosa deve fare..."), with no "tu" or "Lei" commands to the candidate; landing and dashboard
copy in "tu" (consumer-app norm), gender-neutral where possible. Italian typography: « » for
quoted texts, sentences and words (no straight double quotes), ___ for a gap, decimal comma,
24-hour times with a dot (17.42). No em-dashes or en-dashes. No real people except historical
figures (Giacomo Leopardi); places are real Italian towns, names and businesses are invented.
