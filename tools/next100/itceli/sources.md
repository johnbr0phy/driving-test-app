# Sources for `itceli` (CELI 2, level B1, written in Italian)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Italian for this bank. Every notice, email,
article and sentence was invented for Tigertest (own places, people and situations). Nothing was
copied or reworded from the CVCL sample papers ("prova d'esame: esempio 1 / esempio 2"), from the
CELI pre-test, from commercial preparation books (Edilingua, Loescher, Ornimi and others) or from
any practice site. The CILS B1 bank built in parallel was not read; settings and names here are
this bank's own. Checked 2026-11-12 unless noted.

## Exam owner and format (official)
- CVCL, Università per Stranieri di Perugia, "Esami CELI generici" (last updated 27-03-2025),
  fetched 2026-11-12: https://cvcl.unistrapg.it/pagine/esami-celi-generici
  - CELI 2 certifies Italian at CEFR level B1 for schooled adults; the candidate can manage in
    largely predictable situations of social and working life, interact on familiar topics, say
    simply what they hope and give brief reasons and explanations.
  - CELI certificates carry the ALTE Q Mark. Documents linked per level: competenze richieste,
    struttura e descrizione della prova, valutazione e punteggi, conversion table, two sample
    papers (zip). The linked PDFs on www.unistrapg.it returned a Web Application Firewall block
    or HTTP 503 to every automated fetch on 2026-11-12, and the Wayback copies could not be
    reached either, so their content below is labelled.
- CVCL, "Esami CELI immigrati" (last updated 10-06-2025), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://cvcl.unistrapg.it/pagine/esami-celi-immigrati
  - The citizenship version is called "CELI 2 i cittadinanza" (B1), valid for the language
    requirement of D.Lgs. 113/2018 for Italian citizenship.
  - CELI 2 i: written part of three components, comprensione della lettura, produzione scritta,
    comprensione dell'ascolto; objective tasks named as "scelte multiple su testi,
    completamenti, completamenti con scelta multipla, abbinamenti, riformulazioni di frasi" and
    subjective tasks (compositions, guided writing, open questions); oral part a guided
    conversation on CVCL material. Two booklets: first A and B (reading, writing), second C
    (listening).
- CVCL, "Esami CELI" overview (last updated 04-02-2025), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://cvcl.unistrapg.it/pagine/esami-celi-lingua-italiana
  - CELI certificates do not expire. CELI generici A1 to C2, CELI a (adolescenti, 12 to 18),
    CELI i (immigrati) A1 to B1.
- CVCL, "Calendario Esami CELI e DILS-PG" (last updated 01-07-2026), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://cvcl.unistrapg.it/pagine/calendario-esami-celi-e-dils-pg-000
  - 2026 CELI 2 sessions: 11 March, 9 June, 11 November; 2027: 9 March, 11 June, 16 November.
    CELI 2 i cittadinanza: several sessions a year, Italian centres only.
- Regolamento degli esami CELI, parte pubblica (Senato Accademico deliberations 134/2024 and
  154/2024), fetched 2026-11-12 from the CVCL site:
  https://cvcl.unistrapg.it/MC-API/Risorse/StreamRisorsa.ashx?guid=b682a9a2-1380-4d6c-9d54-496cbd0f4c18
  - 1.2: CELI generici from age 16. 1.10: an exam is passed when the minimum is reached in both
    the written and the oral part; the written part is "Comprensione della Lettura, Produzione
    Scritta, Competenza Linguistica -a partire dal CELI 3- e Comprensione dell'Ascolto". So
    CELI 2 has NO separate Competenza linguistica component (it starts at CELI 3). A part passed
    alone can be kept for one year.
  - 2.2.2: no dictionaries or devices. 3.2: CELI 2 oral, material given about 12 minutes before,
    a role-play and a picture to describe, at most 10 to 12 minutes.
  - 4.2: result as five grades, A (ottimo), B (buono), C (sufficiente) pass; D (insufficiente)
    and E (gravemente insufficiente) fail.
- CVCL, "CELI 2 i Cittadinanza (B1) - Criteri di valutazione" (CVCL letterhead, Palazzina
  Lupattelli), copy hosted by the exam centre Palazzo Malvisi, fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://palazzomalvisi.com/wp-content/uploads/CELI_B1_CITTADINANZA_CRITERI_VALUTAZIONE.pdf
  - Prove A (reading): A1 5 items 0-2, A2 10 items 0-1.5, A3 10 items 0-1, A4 5 items 0-1,
    total 0/40. Prove B (writing) 0/47 converted to 40. Prove C (listening) 0/40. Written total
    120, passed with 72 or more. Oral 4/20 doubled to 40, passed with 11 or more (on the 20
    scale). Whole exam passed with 92 or more of 160.
  - Lexical and grammatical descriptors refer to B. Spinelli, F. Parizzi, Profilo della lingua
    italiana (La Nuova Italia, 2010), the B1 grammar and vocabulary inventory this bank follows.

## Generic CELI 2 sheets (read only through search-result extracts of the official PDFs)
These are on www.unistrapg.it, which blocked automated fetching on 2026-11-12. Their titles and
extracts were read through search results on 2026-11-12 and are used for the landing only:
- "CELI 2 (livello B1) STRUTTURA DELL'ESAME":
  https://www.unistrapg.it/sites/default/files/docs/certificazioni/celi-2-descrizione-prova.pdf
  Extract: first booklet with parts A and B, 2 hours; part A in five tasks, A.1 short texts with
  three-option multiple choice (7 items), A.2 ten binary matches on one text, A.3 five sentences
  completed by four-option multiple choice, A.4 a text with ten three-option gaps, A.5 five
  sentences to complete; part B a form (9 items), a short notice of about 50 words and a letter or
  email; second booklet listening (part C).
- "CELI 2 (livello B1) CRITERI DI VALUTAZIONE E PUNTEGGI":
  https://www.unistrapg.it/sites/default/files/docs/certificazioni/celi-2-valutazione.pdf
  Extract: written part 120 points and oral 40 (160 in all); at least 72 in the written part and
  22 in the oral part to pass.
- "CELI 2 competenze richieste":
  https://www.unistrapg.it/sites/default/files/docs/certificazioni/celi-2-competenze-richieste.pdf
  (title only).
The landing gives no listening time and no exact item counts per task, because neither could be
read on the PDF itself. The reading, writing, listening and oral weights (40/40/40/40) are from
the CELI 2 i sheet above and the generic extract, which agree.

## Language (rules the items rely on)
- Treccani, "Congiuntivo" (La grammatica italiana, 2012), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/congiuntivo_%28La-grammatica-italiana%29/
  Subjunctive in subordinate clauses for desire, doubt, possibility; required by some
  conjunctions; imperfect and pluperfect subjunctive in second and third type conditionals.
- Treccani, "Preposizioni" (La grammatica italiana, 2012), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/preposizioni_%28La-grammatica-italiana%29/
- Accademia della Crusca, Consulenza linguistica (index), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza
  Used to check doubtful usage; items whose answer depends on a usage the Crusca calls
  acceptable both ways were dropped.
- Grammar and vocabulary kept to the B1 inventory of the Profilo della lingua italiana (book,
  not online): passato prossimo and imperfetto, trapassato prossimo, futuro, condizionale
  presente and passato, congiuntivo presente and imperfetto in common contexts, imperativo with
  pronouns, si impersonale and passivante, gerundio, direct, indirect and combined pronouns, ne
  and ci, relative pronouns (che, cui, il quale), articulated prepositions, common connectives
  (perché, quindi, però, invece, mentre, anche se, benché, affinché, prima che, appena),
  comparatives, word formation with common prefixes and suffixes.

## Modelling decisions (Tigertest's own)
- Only part A (comprensione della lettura) is modelled: short notices and messages (A.1 style),
  longer emails and articles (A.2 style, two questions per text) and the completion and
  rewording tasks (A.3 to A.5 and the "riformulazioni di frasi" the CVCL lists), which test
  grammar and vocabulary in context. They are not called "Competenza linguistica" in the copy,
  because CELI 2 has no such component (Regolamento 1.10). Writing, listening and the oral are not
  modelled; the landing says so.
- Real A.1 and A.4 items have three options; every item here has four.
- 4 tests x 50: reading 22 (10 short texts, 12 on longer texts), language 28 (verbs 8, pronouns
  5, prepositions and articles 5, connectives and rewording 4, vocabulary 6). CVCL publishes no
  item split for this ratio; it follows part A's balance of comprehension tasks to completion
  tasks (about 17 to 20 in the generic extract).
- Pass line 70%: the real line is 72 of 120 (60%) on the whole written part (reading, writing,
  listening together) plus the oral. No per-part line exists for reading alone, so Tigertest
  uses 70% (35 of 50) as a readiness target with a margin, since the objective reading part is
  usually the part candidates score highest on.

## Left out on purpose
- Fees, centre addresses, result dates, registration deadlines (they change and vary by centre).
- No questions about the exam itself.
- Usage where standard Italian accepts two forms (for example congiuntivo or indicativo after
  "penso che" in informal speech, "a me mi"): each grammar item has exactly one form accepted in
  standard written Italian.
