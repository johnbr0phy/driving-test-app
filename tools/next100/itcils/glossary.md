# Glossary for `itcils` (Italian, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations in a neutral, impersonal Italian ("Quale forma completa la
frase?", "Che cosa deve fare...?"), as in CILS instructions; landing and dashboard copy in "tu"
(consumer-app norm). Italian typography: « » around quoted sentences and passages, “ ” for a
quotation inside a passage, plain apostrophes ('), no em dashes or en dashes. Gaps are written
"___". Names, places, shops and figures in reading passages are invented.
