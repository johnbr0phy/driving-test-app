# Sources for `itcils` (CILS B1 Cittadinanza, written in Italian)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Italian for Tigertest. Every reading passage
(notices, emails, letters, short articles and instructions about everyday life in Italy) was
invented for this bank, with fictional names, places and figures. Nothing was copied or reworded
from the CILS sample papers (the "Prototipo Quaderno B1 Cittadinanza", its keys, transcripts or
audio), from past CILS papers, from commercial books such as "Percorso CILS Cittadinanza (B1)"
or from any practice site. The sample quaderno and keys were not opened. Sources fetched
2026-11-12.

    n. 132); B1 autonomous user profile; contexts: personal life, public contexts, work and
    training.
  - morphosyntax beyond earlier levels: articoli determinativi e indeterminativi; posizione
    dell'aggettivo; comparativo e superlativo; pronomi personali tonici e atoni, riflessivi;
    pronomi relativi; possessivi, dimostrativi, interrogativi; indefiniti ogni, ciascuno,
    nessuno, qualche; preposizioni articolate; forma attiva e riflessiva di verbi regolari, modali

    passato prossimo, imperfetto, infinito presente, imperativo, condizionale presente; avverbi
    frequenti; frase semplice (dichiarative, interrogative, esclamative, volitive con imperativo e
    condizionale); coordinate copulative, avversative, dichiarative; subordinate oggettive
    implicite, temporali, causali, dichiarative, relative esplicite.
  - pragmatics: common speech acts in neutral register; lexicon: Nuovo Vocabolario di Base plus up
    to 5% common lexicon.
  - Ascolto: 2 prove (3-option multiple choice; information identification), 400 to 700 words in
    all, about 30 minutes, heard twice.
  - Comprensione della lettura e Riflessione grammaticale: 2 prove (individuazione di
    informazioni; cloze), texts 350 to 500 words (letters, emails, newspaper articles,
    instructions, regulations, manuals; from newspapers, magazines, manuals, websites),
    40 minutes.
  - Produzione scritta: 1 prova, an email asking for information or solving a problem with goods
    or services, 80 to 120 words, 40 minutes.
  - Produzione orale: 1 prova, short self-presentation from a list of questions, then a dialogue
    with the examiner; about 10 minutes (1 minute presentation, 2 to 3 minutes dialogue).

## Law

- Legge 5 febbraio 1992, n. 91, art. 9.1, text in force from 13 March 2025 (Normattiva),
  fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1992-02-05;91~art9.1
  - citizenship under articles 5 (marriage) and 9 (residence) requires knowledge of Italian not
    below B1 of the QCER, shown by a school qualification or a certificate from a recognised
    certifying body; applicants who signed the accordo di integrazione or hold an EU long-term
    residence permit are not required to produce it under this article.
  - inserted by art. 14, comma 1, lett. a-bis, D.L. 4 ottobre 2018, n. 113, converted by Legge
    1 dicembre 2018, n. 132.
  - Corte costituzionale, sentenza n. 25 of 2025: the article is unconstitutional where it does
    not exempt applicants with serious limits to language learning from age, illness or
    disability, certified by a public health facility.
- Prefettura (Ministero dell'Interno), "Conseguimento della certificazione di conoscenza della
  lingua italiana al livello B1" (PDF, June 2024), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://prefettura.interno.gov.it/sites/default/files/24/2024-06/certificato_di_conoscenza_della_lingua_italiana_-ente_privato-.pdf
  - the recognised certifying bodies: Roma Tre (CERT.IT), Perugia (CELI), Siena (CILS), Dante
    Alighieri (PLIDA), plus the CPIA. It gives the conversion law as n. 136; the Gazzetta and
    Normattiva give n. 132, which the copy uses.

## Grammar and usage

Grammar items test standard Italian as described in the CILS sillabo above (auxiliary choice,
agreement of the past participle with essere and with preceding atonic pronouns, preposizioni
articolate, pronoun forms, imperative with pronouns). These are standard reference-grammar facts;
every item was checked in the review pass for a second grammatical option, and items where a
second option is acceptable in standard Italian were rewritten.

## Not loaded or not confirmed

- The page https://cils.unistrasi.it/188/357/B1_cittadinanza.htm returned HTTP 500 on
  2026-11-12; the B1 c page and its PDFs above were used instead.
- The Council of Europe CEFR pages (coe.int, rm.coe.int) returned 403 or a bot check; the B1
  profile is taken from the CILS sillabo, which states its alignment to the QCER B1 level.
- No official CILS document read states the pass mark for B1 Cittadinanza in so many words.
  By the rule for every other 12-point CILS module it is 7 of 12 per skill; the landing says
  "secondo le regole CILS per i moduli a 12 punti" and Tigertest uses a 60% readiness line.

## Modelling decisions and what is left out

- Modelled: only what fits a four-option single-answer item: reading comprehension (a short
  original passage in each stem, 60 to 120 words) and grammar, vocabulary and register at B1.
  The real reading part uses true/false items and a 6-gap cloze; ours are four-option items.
- Not modelled: Ascolto, Produzione scritta, Produzione orale.
- Grammar follows the B1 Cittadinanza sillabo, which lists indicativo presente, passato prossimo,
  imperfetto, infinito, imperativo and condizionale presente only. Futuro and congiuntivo are not
  in that list, so no item depends on them (they may appear only in passages, not as keys).
- No question asks about the exam itself, fees, dates or the citizenship procedure.
