# Sources for `itnautica` (Italian boat licence theory test, patente nautica entro 12 miglia, in Italian)

All 210 questions are original, written directly in Italian from the decrees, laws and the
international regulations below. The Ministry's "elenco unico nazionale dei quesiti" (the national
quiz database approved by decreto direttoriale 24 gennaio 2022 n. 10, replaced by D.D. 106/2022
and D.D. 131/2022) was never copied, reworded or used for wording: the topics come from the exam
programme in Allegato A of D.M. 323/2021, and the list was opened only afterwards, for the
similarity check described at the end of this file. No commercial quiz app, book or past paper was
used. Fetched 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Exam format and programme (for the landing and spec only, never asked in the bank)
- Decreto del Ministro delle infrastrutture e della mobilità sostenibili 10 agosto 2021, n. 323,
  "Adozione dei programmi di esame per il conseguimento delle patenti nautiche di categoria A, B e
  C e modalità di svolgimento delle prove", Gazzetta Ufficiale Serie generale n. 232 del
  28-9-2021, pages 34 to 39, read in the official GU PDF, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2021/09/28/232/sg/pdf
  - Art. 3: written test and practical test, at least five hours of manoeuvres certified by a
    nautical school before the exam; a failed test may be repeated once within the application's
    validity, after at least thirty days.
  - Art. 6: for "navigazione entro le 12 miglia dalla costa" the written test is the "Quiz su
    elementi di carteggio nautico" plus the "Quiz base", plus the "Quiz vela" for sail and motor.
    Quiz base: twenty questions, three alternatives, one correct; pass with at least sixteen
    right in at most thirty minutes. Carteggio quiz: five single-answer questions on reading a
    nautical chart or electronic charts; at least four right in at most twenty minutes. Quiz
    vela: five questions; at least four right in at most fifteen minutes. The exam opens with
    the carteggio quiz; the quizzes are taken in one sitting for the sum of their times; an
    omitted answer counts as wrong; every test must be passed.
  - Art. 7: practical test on a motor unit of at least 5,90 m or a sailing unit of at least 9 m.
  - Allegato A: programme (teoria dello scafo, motori, sicurezza, manovre e condotta, Colreg e
    segnalamento marittimo, meteorologia, navigazione cartografica ed elettronica, normativa
    diportistica e ambientale; teoria della vela for sail). Allegato C: quiz base distribution
    1 scafo, 1 motore, 3 sicurezza, 4 manovre, 2 Colreg, 2 meteo, 4 navigazione, 3 normativa.
    Allegato D: knots and manoeuvres for the practical test.
- Decreto direttoriale 12 maggio 2022, n. 106 (replaces Allegato A of D.D. 10/2022, the national
  quiz list, and moves its start to 1 June 2022), fetched 2026-11-22 (copy hosted by the
  Motorizzazione di Roma): https://motorizzazioneroma.eu/documenti/patenti/dd106maggio22.pdf
- MIMS Direzione Generale Territoriale del Nord-Est, Direttiva 18/2022 (19 maggio 2022) on the
  new exam programmes, the quiz software and the carteggio quizzes, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.ilportaledellautomobilista.it/gms/dettaglionormativa.downloadallegato:download/158953/Direttiva$0020n.$002018$0020del$002019$0020maggio$00202022.pdf
- MIT, Direzione Generale per la motorizzazione, decreto RD 199 del 22.04.2026 (category D1),
  which still cites D.M. 323/2021 as the rule for categories A, B and C, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/normativa/2026-04/RD%20199%20del%2022.04.2026_%20patenti%20nautiche.pdf
- MIT normativa page for patenti nautiche (lists the decrees): https://mit.gov.it/temi/patenti-mezzi-abilitazioni/patenti-nautiche/normativa
  (certificate error from the fetch tool on 2026-11-22; the documents above were read instead).

## Law (as in force on 2026-11-22)
- D.Lgs. 18 luglio 2005, n. 171, Codice della nautica da diporto, read article by article on
  Normattiva, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legislativo:2005-07-18;171!vig=
  - Art. 3: nave da diporto over 24 m; imbarcazione over 10 m and up to 24 m; natante up to 10 m
    or rowed; moto d'acqua under 4 m with a water-jet pump; length to UNI EN ISO 8666.
  - Art. 27 (text in force since 11 January 2024): natanti and moto d'acqua need no ATCN
    registration, licenza di navigazione or certificato di sicurezza; voluntary registration
    gives them the imbarcazione regime; non-CE natanti up to 6 miles (12 if approved for
    unlimited navigation or certified suitable); CE natanti within their design category and
    never beyond 12 miles; jole, pattini, pedalò, canoes, sail up to 4 m² within 1 mile; moto
    d'acqua within 1 mile.
  - Art. 39: licence needed beyond 6 miles, for moto d'acqua, and within 6 miles above the
    engine limits (30 kW / 40,8 CV, and the cylinder limits); without a licence: 18 for
    imbarcazioni, 16 for natanti, 14 for sailing natanti over 4 m² and rowing craft beyond one
    mile; categories A, B, C, D.
  - Art. 53-bis: over 0,5 g/l is "stato di ebbrezza"; bands 0,5-0,8, 0,8-1,5 and over 1,5 g/l
    with licence suspension; sanctions increased between 22 and 7. Art. 53-ter: under 21s and
    commercial users may not drink and drive at all (any rate above zero).
- D.M. 29 luglio 2008, n. 146, Regolamento di attuazione del Codice della nautica da diporto, on
  Normattiva, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:ministero.infrastrutture.trasporti:decreto:2008-07-29;146!vig=
  - Art. 25, comma 3 (checked by the reviewer on Normattiva, 2026-11-23,
    https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:ministero.infrastrutture.trasporti:decreto:2008-07-29;146~art25!vig=):
    an unit is "a motore" when sail area in m² divided by engine power is under 1 (CV) or 1,36 (kW).
  - Art. 28, comma 1, lettera a (checked 2026-11-23,
    https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:ministero.infrastrutture.trasporti:decreto:2008-07-29;146~art28!vig=):
    licences valid within 12 miles are issued by the capitanerie di porto, the uffici circondariali
    marittimi and the uffici della motorizzazione civile (the landing's "Run by" row).
  - Art. 26: patente B for navi da diporto, also valid for units up to 24 m.
  - Art. 35: minimum age 18 for categories A and C (and D2), 16 for D1, 21 for B with three years
    of category A without distance limit.
  - Art. 38: licence valid ten years, five years from age 60.
  - Art. 54: lifesaving and safety equipment per Allegato V by the navigation actually made, for
    everyone on board; within 12 miles a CE inflatable boat can replace the collective raft on
    conditions; lifejacket always worn on moto d'acqua and similar craft.
- Convenzione sul regolamento internazionale del 1972 per prevenire gli abbordi in mare (COLREG),
  official Italian text published by the Swiss Confederation (RS 0.747.363.321), fetched
  2026-11-22: https://res.cloudinary.com/adminch/image/private/s--rKesTxX4--/v1748486747/Bundespublikationen/0-747-363-321i.pdf
  Rules 5 to 19 (conduct), 21 (fanali: arcs 225, 112,5 and 135 degrees), 22 (visibility for
  vessels under 12 m), 23 (power-driven vessels, under 12 m and under 7 m at 7 knots), 25
  (sailing vessels, tricolour under 20 m, cone point down when motor-sailing), 26 (trawling green
  over white, fishing red over white), 27 (not under command, restricted, diving flag A), 30
  (anchor ball and lights), 32 to 35 (sound signals). Italy ratified it with legge 27 dicembre
  1977, n. 1085.
- IALA Recommendation R1001 Edition 2.0, The IALA Maritime Buoyage System (June 2022), fetched
  2026-11-22: https://iala.int/content/uploads/2022/05/C75-10.3.7-Revised-Recommendation-R1001-Ed2.0-The-IALA-Maritime-Buoyage-System-June-2022.pdf
  Region A lateral marks (Italy), cardinal marks and their VQ/Q white lights, isolated danger
  (black with red band, two spheres, Fl(2)), safe water, special (yellow, X) and emergency wreck.
- Directive 2013/53/EU on recreational craft, Annex I (design categories A to D: wind force over
  8, up to 8, 6 and 4; significant wave height over 4 m, up to 4, 2 and 0,3 m), transposed by
  D.Lgs. 5/2016, fetched 2026-11-22: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2013/53/oj

## Seamanship, safety, radio and meteorology
- Marina Militare, "Andar per mare", supplemento alla Rivista Marittima 2017 (official Navy
  publication on boating law and safety), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.marina.difesa.it/media-cultura/editoria/marivista/Documents/supplementi/andar_mare_2017.pdf
  Used for facts that have not changed since: VHF channel 16 for calling and distress with 24 h
  watch, channel 70 for DSC; 1530 emergency number of the Guardia Costiera; extinguisher rating
  letter B for liquid fires; Beaufort and Douglas scales; the diver's signal (red flag with white
  diagonal visible at 300 m; flashing yellow light at night); underwater fishing from sunrise to
  sunset, never with a loaded speargun on board, minimum age 16; sci nautico under D.M. 26
  gennaio 1960 (daylight, outside the bathing strip, an assistant who can swim, at most two
  skiers); marine protected area zones. Legal figures that changed later (safety equipment
  tables rewritten by D.M. 17 settembre 2024 n. 133) were not taken from it.
- Aeronautica Militare, Servizio Meteorologico, "Scopriamo che mar fa" (marine forecasts, Douglas
  sea state), fetched 2026-11-22: https://www.meteoam.it/it/in-primo-piano/scopriamo-che-mara-far--
- General nautical terminology (hull parts, propeller and rudder effects, points of sail, knots,
  compass declination and deviation, Mercator chart, nautical mile of 1852 m) is standard Italian
  usage as in the programme of Allegato A; computations (speed, time, distance, fuel endurance,
  declination with annual change) are worked in each explanation.

## Left out on purpose
- The Ministry's quiz list (copyright and the build rule above).
- Exact quantities from Allegato V (safety equipment by distance), rewritten in 2024 and
  detailed by CE marking and use; only the rules in art. 54 and general use are asked.
- Fines, fees, sanction amounts and local ordinance figures that vary by Capitaneria (bathing
  strips, speed limits near the coast, port speeds): asked only as "who sets them".
- The quiz vela's real share of the exam is modelled as 5 of 30; the practical test, the
  carteggio on paper charts 5/D and 42/D (senza limiti licence) and three-option answers are not
  modelled (ours have four options).

## Similarity check against the official quiz list (done 2026-11-22, before finishing the build)
- Downloaded the Ministry's national list, Allegato A to D.D. 31 maggio 2022 n. 131 (1469 quiz base
  and 250 quiz vela statements, V/F on three answers), from
  https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/normativa/2022-05/ALLEGATO%20A%20QUIZ%20PATENTI%20NAUTICHE%20DD%20131%20DEL%2031%20MAGGIO%202022.pdf
  (linked from https://mit.gov.it/normativa/decreto-dirigenziale-numero-131-del-31052022), fetched
  2026-11-22, plus the D1 list (D.D. 18 aprile 2025 n. 62) for a second pass. The carteggio quizzes
  are drawn on charts 5/D and 42/D and are not in the text list; ours are generic computations in
  words. Nothing from the list is stored in the repo.
- Three scripts in this folder, run on the extracted PDF text: `sim_stems.py` (each stem against
  the 928 catalogue questions that parse cleanly: word Jaccard and SequenceMatcher ratio, plus
  options found on the matched page), `sim_containment.py` (share of each stem's word trigrams found
  anywhere in the list, and options of 2+ words found verbatim) and `sim_windows.py` (the 45-word
  catalogue passage sharing most stem and option words, printed beside each item for a manual read).
- Every one of the 210 items was read beside its best catalogue passage. A close match was an item
  asking the same fact in parallel wording, or one whose options repeated those of the catalogue
  item. 68 items were close and were rewritten from a different angle with new distractors (mostly
  as worked scenarios: a natante CE at 15 miles, a 17-year-old on a moto d'acqua, 1500 m from the
  coast, licence expiry ages, 10 knots in km/h, longitude across 180°, points of sail from wind and
  heading, reciprocal and beam bearings, cardinal marks read from topmark or light, leaving a green
  mark when going out, the boom's side, the paterazzo, the winch, the two-stroke mixture) or replaced
  with a different topic from the same programme line.
- Result after the rewrite: no stem at a similarity of 0.70 or more with a catalogue question except
  one false match (`Lascare una scotta significa:` against an unrelated anchoring item), no item
  with 2 or more of its options on the matched catalogue page at a stem similarity of 0.40 or more,
  and the remaining shared facts are asked in our own wording with our own distractors.

## Reviewer checks (2026-11-23)
- Sci nautico: D.M. Marina mercantile 26 gennaio 1960 (text as reproduced at
  https://www.patente.it/normativa/decreto-ministero-della-marina-26-01-1960-n-26-sci-nautico?idc=1916,
  fetched 2026-11-23): art. 1 "in ore diurne con tempo favorevole e mare calmo", art. 2(b) the driver
  "sempre assistito da altra persona esperta nel nuoto". The decree sets no maximum number of skiers
  (that limit comes from local Capitaneria ordinances, summarised in Andar per mare), so the item
  asking it was replaced (ITNAU-MAN-022, now an anchor-holding item).
- 1530: MIT page "1530 - Emergenza in mare", https://www.mit.gov.it/index.php/come-fare-per/sicurezza/sicurezza-mare/1530-emergenza-mare
  (search extract 2026-11-23): free, from fixed and mobile phones, 24 hours a day all year. Which
  office answers a mobile call is not stated on a current official page (the 2017 Andar per mare text
  says the Comando generale), so ITNAU-SIC-018 now asks only what the MIT page states.
- ITNAU-SCA-005 (propeller walk while berthing astern) was ambiguous and was replaced with an
  engine item (oil pressure warning light).
- Similarity check repeated by the reviewer over all 210 items with the three scripts above against
  Allegato A of D.D. 131/2022: no item copies a ministerial statement or reuses a catalogue item's
  option set; the remaining shared facts (definitions such as opera viva, lascare, ancora incattivita)
  are asked in our own wording with our own distractors.
