# Glossary for `itpatente` (Italian, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations are impersonal ("si deve", "il conducente", "chi guida"),
the way Italian driving quizzes are phrased; landing and dashboard copy use "tu" (consumer-app
norm, like the German landing's "du"). Italian typography: decimal comma (0,5 g/l, 1,5 m),
thousands without separator in masses (3500 kg, as in the Codice), km/h, m. Straight double
quotes for sign text ("ALT POLIZIA", "2,5 km"). No em dashes.

Signs: always described in words (shape, colour, symbol), with the name used by the Regolamento
when the question asks what to do rather than what the sign means.
