# Sources for `itpatente` (Italian driving licence theory quiz, patente B, in Italian)

All 210 questions are original, written directly in Italian from the laws below. Nothing was
copied, reworded or consulted from the ministerial "listato" of official quiz statements, from a
past exam or from any commercial quiz app or book. Signs are described in words (shape, colour,
symbol), never shown. Fetched 2026-11-12 unless noted.

## Exam format (for the landing and spec only, never asked in the bank)
- Decreto del Ministro delle infrastrutture e della mobilità sostenibili 27 ottobre 2021 (GU n. 292
  del 9 dicembre 2021), modifiche alla prova di controllo delle cognizioni per le patenti A1, A2, A,
  B1, B e BE. Text read on a mirror on 2026-11-12, fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://www.studiocerbone.com/?p=457801
  - Art. 1 and 2: "Ciascun questionario consta di trenta affermazioni", each marked V or F;
    "La prova ha durata di venti minuti"; passed "se il numero di risposte errate è non superiore
    a tre".
- Decreto direttoriale RD prot. 439 del 13 dicembre 2021 (applies the new format from
  20 dicembre 2021): https://mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/normativa/2021-12/DECRETO_protocollo_0000439_del%20_13-12-2021.pdf
  returned HTTP 503 on 2026-11-12; the 20 December 2021 date is stated only from the MIMS circular
  38460 of 14/12/2021 as reported in the trade press, so the landing does not lean on it.
- Regione Autonoma Friuli Venezia Giulia, Motorizzazione civile, "Ammissione all'esame"
  (official regional motorizzazione page), fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://www.regione.fvg.it/rafvg/cms/RAFVG/infrastrutture-lavori-pubblici/motorizzazione-civile/FOGLIA20/
  - Exam "con il metodo informatizzato", "composto da 30 domande a risposta vero o falso",
    "della durata di 20 minuti", passed with "un numero di errori non superiori a 3"; only two
    theory attempts; retake after at least one month; the application loses validity after six
    months without a pass.
- CdS art. 121 (esame di idoneità: theory and practical test; at least one month between a failed
  test and the next; practical test repeatable no more than twice) and art. 122 (theory must be
  passed within six months of the application, no more than two attempts; the foglio rosa is valid
  twelve months; instructor at most 65 years old with a licence held for at least ten years;
  vehicle marked "P"), read on Normattiva (see below), fetched 2026-11-12.

## First aid and emergency number
- European Commission, Your Europe, "Numero di emergenza 112", fetched 2026-11-12:
  https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/safety/emergency/index_it.htm
  (112 free from any phone in the EU, reaches police, ambulance and fire service).
- The Ministero della Salute page on the NUE 112 was behind a bot check on 2026-11-12 and could
  not be read. First aid items stay at the legal duties of CdS art. 189 plus two widely agreed
  points (do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger; call 112).

## Not confirmed and how the copy words it
- The ministerial decree of 27 October 2021 was read on a law-firm mirror, not on gazzettaufficiale.it
  or mit.gov.it (both unreachable on 2026-11-12); the regional Motorizzazione page confirms the
  same 30 questions, 20 minutes, at most 3 errors.
- Languages of the computer quiz (German and French in some regions) were not confirmed on an
  official page, so the landing does not list them.
- Vehicle mechanics items (ABS, tyre pressure, aquaplaning, roof rack, brake wear) state general
  physics and safety facts that the programme (D.Lgs. 59/2011 all. II, punto 2.1.7 and 2.1.9) asks
  for; no figure is given for them.

## Deliberately left out
- Fines, point deductions, fees, the 20-point starting balance and any euro amount.
- The ministerial question list and its wording, and anything that changes often (tolls,
  regional bans, ZTL rules).
- The 150 km/h motorway option (art. 142, comma 1) as a question: it depends on the operator.
- True/false format: every item is a four-option single-answer question on the same rules.
