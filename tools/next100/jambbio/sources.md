# JAMB UTME Biology: sources

Checked 1 November 2026.

## Official
- JAMB home page, https://www.jamb.gov.ng/ : points to IBASS (Integrated Brochure and Syllabus
  System, https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng) for the syllabus and brochure, and states the 2026 minimum
  admission scores (150 universities, 100 polytechnics and colleges of education). IBASS is a
  script-rendered app that the fetch tool cannot read, so no official syllabus PDF was reached;
  the topic list below comes from guides that reproduce it (same situation as the jambeng build,
  see tools/next100/jambeng/sources.md, checked 31 October 2026).
- 2027 cycle: no 2027 UTME brochure or registration dates found on 1 November 2026 (search
  results and o3schools.com say JAMB has not announced them). The 2026 registration ran
  26 January to 28 February 2026 (legit.ng, guardian.ng reports). Nothing dated is asserted.

## Exam format (guides; agree with each other and with the jambeng sources)
- UTME: 180 objective questions in 2 hours, computer based, four subjects; Use of English 60,
  each other subject 40; every subject scored out of 100, total 400. Nigeria-admissions syllabus
  page (https://nigeria-admissions.pages.dev/subject/biology/, "Data from the official JAMB IBASS
  brochure & UTME syllabi") states 40 multiple-choice questions for Biology, scored over 100.
- No negative marking: guides only (labelled "guides agree" on the landing).
- JAMB publishes no split of the 40 Biology questions by section. The blueprint is Tigertest's
  and says so in the config's scoreNote and on the landing.
- Diagrams appearing in the paper: guides only ("guides say" on the landing).

## Syllabus (guides reproducing the JAMB UTME Biology syllabus)
- https://nigeria-admissions.pages.dev/subject/biology/ : five sections, A Variety of
  Organisms, B Form and Functions (internal structure of a flowering plant, nutrition,
  transport, excretion, reproduction, growth), C Ecology (factors affecting distribution,
  symbiotic interactions, natural habitats, ecology of populations, humans and environment),
  D Heredity and Variations, E Evolution (theories, evidence); "17 topics across 5 sections".
- https://myeduvantage.com/jamb-syllabus-for-biology/ : detail of each section, including the
  named organisms (Amoeba, Euglena, Paramecium, Chlamydomonas, Hydra, Rhizopus, mushroom,
  Loranthus, sundew, bladderwort), supporting tissues, light and dark reactions, N/P/K deficiency,
  food tests, dental formulae of man, sheep and dog, the Secchi disc and other instruments,
  continuous and discontinuous variation (PTC tasting, tongue rolling, blood groups, fingerprints;
  uses in crime detection, transfusion and paternity), Lamarck and Darwin, evidence from fossils,
  comparative anatomy, embryology.
- examkits.com, myschool.ng, ulearngo.com (search summaries, 1 November 2026): same section
  names; ulearngo groups topics differently and was not used for structure.
- Not read (403 or empty): allschool.ng, myschool.ng syllabus body.

## How the facts are used
- 40 four-option questions, scored out of 100: modelled.
- 2 hours for 180 questions: modelled as 30 minutes for 40 (pro rata), said in the directions,
  scoreNote and on the landing.
- Topic weights: Tigertest's (8/6/6/6/8/6 per test), labelled.
- 2026 minimum scores (150/100): from the jambeng sources (JAMB home page and the 2026 policy
  meeting reports), named as 2026 figures, not permanent.
- JAMB's IUPAC-style names (carbon(IV) oxide, carbon(II) oxide, sulphur(IV) oxide) as used in
  JAMB and WAEC papers; British spelling.
- Every item is original. No past paper, official sample or commercial app item was used.
  Stems were checked against every bank on disk (neet, apbio, gcsescience, hesi, teas, rrbalp,
  rrbgroupd, ssccpo, jambchem and others); near-duplicates were rewritten.
