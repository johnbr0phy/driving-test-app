# JAMB UTME Chemistry: sources

Checked 1 November 2026.

## Syllabus
- JAMB UTME Chemistry syllabus, as a 16-page PDF copy of JAMB's own document (headed "CHEMISTRY,
  GENERAL OBJECTIVES ... Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) syllabus in Chemistry"),
  hosted by myeduvantage for the 2026/2027 cycle:
  https://myeduvantage.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/syllabus-for-chemistry.pdf (downloaded and
  read in full 1 November 2026). The page that links it is
  https://myeduvantage.com/jamb-syllabus-for-chemistry/ ("JAMB Syllabus for Chemistry 2027").
  JAMB's own IBASS (https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng) is script-rendered and could not be read.
  18 topics: 1 separation of mixtures and purification; 2 chemical combination; 3 kinetic theory of
  matter and gas laws; 4 atomic structure and bonding; 5 nuclear chemistry; 6 solubility;
  7 environmental pollution (air, air pollution, water and soil pollution); 8 acids, bases and salts;
  9 oxidation and reduction; 10 electrolysis; 11 energy changes; 12 rates of chemical reaction;
  13 chemical equilibria ("no calculation will be required"); 14 non-metals and their compounds
  (hydrogen, chlorine, oxygen and water, sulphur, nitrogen, carbon); 15 metals and their compounds;
  16 organic compounds; 17 chemistry and industry (including biotechnology); 18 astronomical
  chemistry (solar system, composition of the earth). Recommended texts: Ababio, Bajah et al.,
  Ojokuku, Odesina, Uche et al. (not used for any item).
  The syllabus uses the Nigerian school names (trioxocarbonate(IV), tetraoxosulphate(VI),
  trioxonitrate(V), carbon(IV) oxide) and British spelling (sulphur), so the bank does too.
  JAMB gives no topic weights; the per-test split by drill is Tigertest's.

## Format of the UTME
- See tools/next100/jambeng/sources.md (checked 31 October and 1 November 2026): the UTME is 180
  questions in 2 hours across four subjects, Use of English 60 and each other subject 40; each
  subject scored out of 100, total 400 (guides: myschoolgist, schoolngr, ulearngo, myeduvantage).
  2026 minimum admission scores, 150 for universities and 100 for polytechnics and colleges of
  education, from the JAMB home page https://www.jamb.gov.ng/ and the reports of the 11 May 2026
  policy meeting (ICIR, NAN, Arise) listed there.
- 2027 UTME: as of 1 November 2026 JAMB had announced no 2027 registration dates or brochure
  (search of jamb.gov.ng and news, 1 November 2026; guides only project late January 2027).
- Calculator: guides (legit.ng 2024 interview with a JAMB official; 2026 guides on
  schoolregistry.ng and others) say the CBT shows a basic on-screen calculator; sources disagree
  on whether Chemistry gets it in 2026, so the landing says only "guides say" and the bank keeps
  arithmetic simple. Not in examFacts.
- No negative marking: guides agree (as for jambeng); stated as guides say.
- Review, 1 November 2026: jamb.gov.ng (home page fetched) and ibass.jamb.gov.ng (script-rendered) still
  state neither the per-subject count nor the calculator rule. A web search the same day found 2026
  guides (myschoolgist UTME guide, examready, teezab and others) that agree on 40 questions per subject
  other than English and on an on-screen calculator in the CBT for calculation subjects including
  Chemistry; no official JAMB statement was found, so both stay "guides say".
- Syllabus re-read for the review (same PDF, downloaded again 1 November 2026): topic 6(c) lists
  "Harmattan haze and water paints as examples of suspensions, fog, milk, blood, aerosol spray,
  emulsion paints and rubber solution as examples of colloids", which the Harmattan item follows;
  topic 13 says no equilibrium calculation; topic 10 limits cell potentials to simple calculations;
  topic 15(i) lists the alloys asked (steel, stainless steel, brass, bronze, type-metal, duralumin,
  soft solder, permalloy, alnico). Topic 18 objective (v), "the smallest planet that is also the
  farthest from the sun", describes Pluto, which has not been a planet since 2006; the bank asks no
  such item.

## How the facts are used
- 40 four-option questions, scored out of 100: modelled.
- 2 hours for 180 questions: modelled as 30 minutes for 40 (pro rata), said in the directions,
  scoreNote and landing.
- Every item is original. No JAMB past question, recommended textbook or commercial app's item was
  used. Relative atomic masses and constants are given in each stem, as UTME papers do.
- Checked for overlap against neet, jeemain, mhtcet, jambphys, jambbio and every other bank on disk
  (stem similarity with the same key, and shared numbers); repeats were rewritten.
