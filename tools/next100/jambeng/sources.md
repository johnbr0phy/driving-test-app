# JAMB UTME Use of English: sources

Checked 31 October 2026.

## Official
- JAMB home page, https://www.jamb.gov.ng/ : points to IBASS (Integrated Brochure and Syllabus
  System, https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng) for the syllabus and brochure; states the 2026 minimum
  tolerable admission scores, 150 for universities and colleges of nursing and 100 for
  polytechnics, colleges of education and the other institutions, and a minimum admission age of 16.
  IBASS is a script-rendered app and could not be read by the fetch tool, so the syllabus detail
  below comes from guides that reproduce it.

## News reports of the 2026 policy meeting (checked 1 November 2026)
- The 2026 policy meeting (Abuja, 11 May 2026) kept 150 as the minimum UTME score for
  universities (colleges of nursing also 150) and 100 for polytechnics and colleges of education;
  NCE candidates with four credit passes no longer need the UTME from 2026/2027. Reported by
  ICIR (https://www.icirnigeria.org/cut-off-mark-for-varsity-admission-remains-150-says-jamb/),
  NAN (https://nannews.ng/jamb-fixes-2026-cut-off-marks-sets-deadline-for-admissions/) and Arise
  (https://www.arise.tv/jamb-sets-2026-admission-cut-offs-for-universities-nursing-colleges-and-polytechnics/).
  Matches the landing and examFacts figures.
- Examkits (re-read 1 November 2026) reproduces the syllabus topic lists: Lexis and Structure
  includes question tags, punctuation and spelling, and idiomatic usage in standard British
  English; Oral Forms covers vowels, consonants, rhymes (including homophones), word stress and
  intonation/emphatic stress. No official JAMB syllabus PDF could be reached (IBASS still
  script-rendered).

## Guides (not JAMB; used only where they agree)
- Examkits, "2026 JAMB Use of English Syllabus", https://examkits.com/blog/10179-2 : three
  sections, Comprehension/Summary, Lexis and Structure, Oral Forms; topic lists; question counts
  25 / 25 / 10 (comprehension passage 5, cloze 10, reading text 10; sentence interpretation 5,
  antonyms 5, synonyms 5, sentence completion 10); idioms in standard British English; Daniel Jones
  pronouncing dictionary recommended for oral forms.
- Search summaries of myschoolgist.com, schoolngr.com, ulearngo.com, myeduvantage.com and
  others (October 2026): UTME is 180 questions in 2 hours across four subjects, Use of English
  compulsory with 60 questions, the others 40 each; each subject scored out of 100, total 400;
  The Lekki Headmaster (Kabir Alabi Garba) named as the 2026 prescribed reading text. Guides
  disagree on section weights, so the counts above are labelled "guides say" on the landing.

## How the facts are used
- 60 questions, four options, scored out of 100: modelled.
- 2 hours for 180 questions: modelled as 40 minutes for 60 (pro rata); said in the directions and
  on the landing.
- Prescribed reading text: not modelled (changes by cycle, copyrighted); replaced by two more
  original passages; the landing says so without naming a year's text as permanent.
- No negative marking on the UTME (guides agree; JAMB states no penalty in its brochure, not
  re-read here), so the landing says "no negative marking" only as guides say. Removed from
  examFacts rows to stay safe.
- Every passage and item is original. No past paper, the prescribed text or a commercial app's
  items were used.
