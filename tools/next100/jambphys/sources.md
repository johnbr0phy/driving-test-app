# JAMB UTME Physics: sources

Checked 1 November 2026.

## Official
- JAMB home page, https://www.jamb.gov.ng/ (checked 1 November 2026): states the 2026 Policy
  Meeting's minimum tolerable admission scores, 150 for universities and colleges of nursing and
  100 for polytechnics, monotechnics, colleges of agriculture, colleges of education and IEIs, and a
  minimum admission age of 16. It links to IBASS (Integrated Brochure and Syllabus System,
  https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng) for the brochure and syllabi. IBASS is a script-rendered app the fetch
  tool cannot read, and the page gives no 2027 registration dates or question counts. So the
  format and topic list below come from guides that reproduce the brochure and syllabus.
- News reports of the 2026 policy meeting (ICIR, NAN, Arise; URLs in
  `tools/next100/jambeng/sources.md`) confirm the 150 / 100 minimums. They are dated "for 2026
  admissions" on the landing and never stated as permanent.

## Guides (not JAMB; used only where they agree)
- nigeria-admissions.pages.dev, "JAMB UTME Syllabus 2027: Physics",
  https://nigeria-admissions.pages.dev/subject/physics/ (checked 1 November 2026): cites the
  official IBASS brochure and syllabus; JAMB UTME Physics has 40 multiple-choice questions; each
  subject is scored over 100 and the UTME total is 400; lists the later syllabus topics, ending
  with Conduction of Electricity Through Liquids and Gases, Elementary Modern Physics, Introductory
  Electronics and Introduction to Fibre Optics and Lasers.
- teezab.com, "JAMB Syllabus for Physics 2026/2027", https://www.teezab.com/jamb-physics-pq15/
  (checked 1 November 2026): the full numbered topic list from Measurements and Units, Scalars and
  Vectors, Motion and Gravitational Field through Electrostatics, Capacitors, Electric Cells,
  Current Electricity, Magnets, Electromagnetic Induction, Simple A.C. Circuits, Elementary Modern
  Physics and Introductory Electronics. Readers' comments suggest the page may reuse an earlier
  year's list, so it was used only where it agrees with the 2027 list above.
- Search summaries of ulearngo.com, myeduvantage.com, examkits.com, studentship.com.ng and
  schoolings.org (1 November 2026): the same topic areas; guides disagree on the number of topics
  (about 32 to 39, depending on how they split them) and give no official topic weights.
- From `tools/next100/jambeng/sources.md` (checked 31 October and 1 November 2026, same guides):
  the UTME is 180 questions in 2 hours across four subjects, Use of English 60 and the others 40
  each, computer based, each subject out of 100 and 400 in all; guides agree there is no negative
  marking.
- Search for the 2027 UTME timetable (1 November 2026): JAMB had not announced 2027 registration
  or exam dates; the 2026 cycle ran registration from late January to late February 2026 with the
  main UTME in April 2026 (legit.ng, theconclaveng.com). Not stated on the landing.

## How the facts are used
- 40 four-option questions, scored out of 100: modelled.
- 2 hours for 180 questions: modelled as 30 minutes for 40 (pro rata); said in the directions,
  scoreNote and on the landing.
- No official topic weights: the per-test split (motion 7, forces 7, heat 7, waves 7, electricity
  5, magnetism 4, modern physics and electronics 3) is Tigertest's, roughly following how many
  syllabus topics each area holds, and the landing says so.
- g = 10 m s⁻², as JAMB items usually state; given in the directions and in stems that need it.
- No negative marking is said only as "guides say".
- Every item is original. No JAMB past question, official sample or commercial app's items were
  used. Numbers and facts were checked against the neet, jeemain, mhtcet, nda, agniveervayu,
  agniveer, rrbalp, rrbgroupd, India SSC and police banks, jambchem, jambbio and the Nigeria v1
  banks; items repeating a fact or number set were rewritten.
