# JKSSB Junior Assistant (`jkssbja`) sources

Every question is original, written for Tigertest from the syllabus and the sources below. No official, past or
commercial question was used or reworded. Checked 2026-11-13.

jkssb.nic.in did not answer from this network (HTTP 503 / connection timeout on every page, also via WebFetch), and the
Wayback Machine was unreachable. The board's own PDFs were read through Google's document viewer, which fetches the
file from jkssb.nic.in itself (docs.google.com/viewer?url=...), so the text below is the board's.

## Who runs it, the post and the current recruitment

- Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board, Advertisement Notification No. 08 of 2025 dated 27.09.2025, Annexure "A"
  (post-wise break-up, qualification and criteria for selection), https://jkssb.nic.in/Pdf/ADVT08OF2025_27092025.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-13, 6.5 MB). Gave: Junior Assistant posts (Level-4, 25500-81100) in UT, divisional and district
  cadres across many departments; qualification "Graduate from any recognised university" with "knowledge of type
  writing, having not less than 35 words speed per minute"; criteria for selection "Written Examination = 80 points"
  and "Skill Test = 20 points" (the type test marks are allotted proportionately against the 20 points). The general
  conditions of the advertisement are not in this file, so negative marking was not confirmed from it.
- JKSSB notice No. JKSSB-COEOEXAM/01/2026-03 dated 24.03.2026, https://www.jkssb.nic.in/Pdf/Notice_24032026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-13): the "OMR Based Written Examination for the post of Junior Assistant advertised vide
  Notification No. 08 of 2025 dated 27.09.2025", first set for 05.04.2026, was postponed to 19.04.2026; admit cards
  from 13.04.2026.
- Why this post: Junior Assistant is JKSSB's common clerical entry post, advertised across almost every department
  (UT, divisional and district cadres) for any graduate, so its written test is the one most JKSSB candidates sit.
  Its syllabus is published on its own (below), unlike the many single-department technical posts.

## Syllabus and scheme (the outline the bank follows)

- JKSSB notice No. JKSSB-COE0EXAM(UT)/47/2023-03 dated 23.12.2025, "Syllabus for the various post of Junior Assistant
  and Junior Stenographer/ Junior Scale Stenographer of various department advertised vide Notification No. 08 of 2025
  dated 27.09.2025", Annexure "A", https://jkssb.nic.in/Pdf/Syllabus_JrAsstt_Steno_23122025.pdf (fetched 2026-11-13).
  "Syllabus for Written test (Objective Type)", Marks: 80, Time: 80 Minutes:
  - Unit I General English, 20 marks: comprehension; editing / proof reading; rearranging of jumbled sentences;
    narration; modals; articles; paragraph writing with blanks (phrases, pronouns, homonyms/homophones); clauses;
    punctuation; synonyms and antonyms; idioms and phrases; uses of prepositions; active and passive voice.
  - Unit II General Awareness with special reference to J&K UT, 20 marks: current events; political and physical
    divisions of India; Indian culture, heritage and freedom struggle; demography (Census); important rivers and lakes
    in India; weather, climate, crops, means of transport of India; J&K UT history, economy, geography (weather,
    climate, crops, rivers, lakes, flora, fauna), heritage and culture, important tourist destinations.
  - Unit III Numerical and Reasoning Ability, 20 marks: number system, percentage, average, profit and loss, ratio
    and proportion, time and work; number series, letter series, coding decoding, direction sense, blood relations,
    mathematical reasoning, speed distance and time, statements and conclusions.
  - Unit IV Basic Concepts of Computers, 20 marks: fundamentals of computer science; hardware and software; input and
    output devices; operating system; MS Word, MS Excel, MS Access and PowerPoint; e-mail and internet.
- JKSSB "Instructions for candidates appearing in Computer Based Written Test (CBT)" for Junior Assistant and related
  posts (Advertisement Nos 04 to 07 of 2020 and 01 to 03 of 2021), No. SSB/CoE/2022/517-24 dated 19.01.2022,
  https://jkssb.nic.in/Pdf/Instructions_for_Candidates_19012022.pdf (fetched 2026-11-13): "The Computer Based Test
  examination shall comprise of 80 MCQs", "The duration ... shall be 80 minutes", "There shall be negative marking and
  0.25 (1/4) marks will be deducted for each wrong answer". This is the board's most recent Junior Assistant
  instruction read; the 2026 test was on OMR with the same 80 marks and 80 minutes. The copy says 0.25 per wrong
  answer "in JKSSB's Junior Assistant instructions" and tells candidates to check their admit card.

## India General Awareness, arithmetic, reasoning and computers

- India GA is static facts only (Constitution Eighth Schedule, Census 2011 figures: sex ratio 943, Kerala highest,
  Uttar Pradesh most populous state; physical and political geography; the freedom struggle; crops and transport),
  at the level of NCERT school texts. The syllabus's "current events of national and international importance" are
  not modelled: no current affairs, schemes or office-holders.
- Arithmetic and reasoning answers are computed and asserted in jkjaArith.py and jkjaReason.py. Reasoning is text only.
- Computer items use standard Windows and Microsoft Office default shortcuts and terms.
- Items were checked against the other India v2 banks (SSC, RRB, IBPS, SBI, state police, CTET, REET, UPTET, UGC-NET,
  NDA, Agniveer, JNVST, AISSEE, CUET) with a token-overlap script, and every item that repeated another bank's fact
  and answer was rewritten.

## Modelling decisions

- One section of 80 questions in 80 minutes, the real length, in the syllabus's unit order, 20 per unit; each unit is
  two drills of 10 per test. English: grammar and usage, then vocabulary and comprehension (a 4-question passage per
  test). GA: India, then J&K. Unit III: arithmetic, then reasoning. Unit IV: computer basics, then MS Office, e-mail
  and internet. Two practice tests, Test 2 held out of the drills, 5 drill-only items per drill: 200 in all.
- The split inside each unit is Tigertest's; JKSSB publishes only the unit marks.
- Score is percent correct. Negative marking (0.25 per wrong answer in JKSSB's Junior Assistant instructions) is
  explained in scoreNote and on the landing, not applied. The type test (20 points) is not modelled.
- English only: the syllabus has a General English unit and no Urdu or Hindi unit. The paper's language for the
  other units was not confirmed from a board document, so the copy does not state it.
