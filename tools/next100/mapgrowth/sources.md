# MAP Growth 2-5 (`mapgrowth`): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from the Common Core State
Standards for grades 2 to 5. No NWEA item, sample question, practice test or commercial MAP
practice material was used or reworded. Reading passages are original.

## Outline and blueprint (NWEA, the test owner; NWEA is a division of HMH Education Co.)

- MAP Growth: Instructional Areas, CCSS (dated Monday, April 14, 2025).
  https://www.nwea.org/uploads/CCSS_2025.pdf (fetched 2026-11-13). Gave the instructional areas
  and sub-areas used as this bank's domains: Math 2-5 (Operations and Algebraic Thinking: Represent
  and Solve Problems, Analyze Patterns and Relationships; Number and Operations: Understand Place
  Value, Counting, and Cardinality, Number and Operations in Base Ten, Number and Operations -
  Fractions; Measurement and Data: Geometric Measurement and Problem Solving, Represent and
  Interpret Data; Geometry: Reason with Shapes, Attributes, & Coordinate Plane), Reading 2-5
  (Literary Text, Informational Text, Vocabulary) and Language 2+ (Writing: Write, Revise Texts for
  Purpose and Audience; Language: Understand, Edit for Grammar, Usage; Language: Understand, Edit
  for Mechanics).
- MAP Growth Technical Report for 2024-2025 (April 2, 2026).
  https://www.nwea.org/uploads/MAP-Growth-Technical-Report-2025.pdf (fetched 2026-11-13).
  Table 3.2 example blueprints: Math 2-5 Geometry 6, Measurement and Data 11, Number and
  Operations 13, Operations and Algebraic Thinking 10; Reading 2-5 13 items per area with up to 2
  passage item sets; Language Usage 2+ 13 items per area. "All test events have a maximum of 43
  items, including field test items"; up to 3 field-test items in Math and Language Usage, up to 4
  in Reading. Section 4.5 item types (multiple choice, multiselect, hot text, construction and
  generation items, all scored right or wrong). Section 5: fully adaptive, "MAP Growth 2-12
  assessments are untimed and take approximately 45-60 minutes per content area", up to four
  administrations a year. Norms: the 2025 MAP Growth norms (fall 2022 to spring 2024 sample).

## Content standards (primary text the items are written from)

- Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (CCSSO / NGA Center).
  https://learning.ccsso.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Math_Standards1.pdf (fetched 2026-11-13).
  Grades 3 to 5 domains OA, NBT, NF, MD, G.
- Common Core State Standards for English Language Arts.
  https://learning.ccsso.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ELA_Standards1.pdf (fetched 2026-11-13).
  Grades 2 to 5 Reading: Literature, Reading: Informational Text, Writing and Language
  (conventions, vocabulary acquisition and use).

## Deliberately left out

- RIT scores, norms tables and percentiles: a fixed practice form cannot place a student on the
  RIT scale, so the copy says the score is percent correct, not RIT.
- Adaptivity: each subject is one fixed form spanning grade 3 to grade 5 standards.
- MAP Growth Science and the K-2 tests; state-specific (non-CCSS) blueprints.
- Construction and generation item types other than typed numbers; hot text is written as
  multiple choice.
