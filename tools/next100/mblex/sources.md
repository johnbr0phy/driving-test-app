# Sources: MBLEx, Massage & Bodywork Licensing Examination (mblex)

All 200 questions are original. No FSMTB study guide items, MBLEx Check items, practice-exam
questions, released items, prep-site or commercial practice questions were opened for writing or
copied. Items were written from the FSMTB content outline's topics and the primary references
below, in Tigertest's own words.

## Format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

- FSMTB, MBLEx Candidate Handbook, "Effective August 2026" (2026 edition, PDF dated by its URL folder 2026/09), fetched 2026-11-14 and re-read 2026-11-15:
  https://fsmtb.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MBLEx-Candidate-Handbook-2026.pdf
  100 multiple-choice questions in 110 minutes (2-hour appointment with a 5-minute security
  agreement and a 5-minute survey); fixed-length computer-adaptive test, questions answered in
  order; all 100 must be answered or the exam is failed; no scheduled breaks; results reported
  only as PASS or FAIL, with a diagnostic report by content area on a fail; criterion-referenced
  passing standard set by the FSMTB Board (no percent published); English and Spanish; Pearson
  VUE test centers year-round; application fee $265; no FSMTB limit on attempts (a state may
  set one); a pass is not a license, which the state board or agency issues. State law sets
  scope of practice and license requirements, and these vary widely between states.
- FSMTB, MBLEx page, fetched 2026-11-14: https://www.fsmtb.org/mblex/
- FSMTB, MBLEx and Licensure FAQs, fetched 2026-11-14: https://fsmtb.org/mblex-faqs/
  (100 questions in 110 minutes, re-apply immediately after a fail, $265, English and Spanish).

## Left out on purpose

- Any single state's rules (hours of education, CE, record retention periods, waiting periods
  for dating a former client, insurance billing): they vary by state and are never asserted.
- Fees, the pass standard and other test-format facts (landing and FAQ only).
- Images: muscles, landmarks and postures are described in words.
- Disputed or fast-changing figures (exact days to avoid an injection site, cold-pack minutes).
