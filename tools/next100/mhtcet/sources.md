Checked 1 November 2026.

## Official (State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra)
- CET Cell home page, https://cetcell.mahacet.org/ (loaded 1 November 2026). Lists the 2026 press notes and the
  "Syllabus and Marking Scheme" page; no MHT-CET 2027 notice or information brochure had been posted. The only
  2027 item was a 30 September 2026 circular inviting subject experts for the Higher Education CETs of 2027-28.
- "CET Syllabus and its Marking Scheme for First Year of Engineering and Technology, Pharmacy Degree Courses,
  B.Planning and First Year of Master of Engineering and Technology (Integrated), Pharm.D PG Degree course,
  MHT-CET 2026", https://cetcell.mahacet.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Syllabus-Technical-2026.pdf (a scanned
  PDF, read page 1 as an image on 1 November 2026, and downloaded and read again by the reviewer the same day; linked from https://cetcell.mahacet.org/syllabus-and-marking-scheme/).
  Confirmed from it:
  - Questions are based on the syllabus of the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT),
    Maharashtra.
  - Approximately 20% weightage to the Std XI curriculum and 80% to Std XII.
  - "There will be no Negative Marking, however difficulty level will be at par with the JEE (Main) for
    Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry"; questions "mainly application based".
  - Three question papers of MCQs, each of 100 marks: Paper I Mathematics, 10 Std XI + 40 Std XII questions,
    2 marks each, 100 marks, 90 minutes; Paper II Physics 10 + 40 and Chemistry 10 + 40, 1 mark each, 100 marks,
    90 minutes; Paper III Biology 20 + 80, 1 mark each, 100 marks, 90 minutes (PCB group, not modelled).
  - Whole Std XII syllabus of 2025-26 (SCERT) plus these Std XI (2024-25) chapters: Physics: vectors, error
    analysis, motion in a plane, laws of motion, gravitation, thermal properties of matter, sound, optics,
    electrostatics, semiconductors. Chemistry: some basic concepts of chemistry, structure of atom, chemical
    bonding, redox reactions, elements of group 1 and 2, states of matter (gaseous and liquids), adsorption and
    colloids (surface chemistry), hydrocarbons, basic principles of organic chemistry, chemistry in everyday life.
    Mathematics: trigonometry II, straight line, circle, probability, complex numbers, permutations and
    combinations, functions, limits, continuity, conic section.

## Not confirmed from an official page in this session
- The order (Physics and Chemistry first, then Mathematics, no going back) is from guides; the CET Cell's
  marking scheme numbers Mathematics as Paper I. The mock follows the guides' order and the landing does not
  assert the order as official.
- Computer-based mode, the medium of each paper (English, Marathi, Urdu) and the "better attempt counts" rule
  are from guides and news reports, and are not stated on the landing or in examFacts as official fact
  (examFacts says "two attempts in 2026" only, which the press notes confirm).
- Whether MHT-CET 2027 will again have two attempts, and its dates and syllabus year, had not been announced by
  1 November 2026. The landing says the CET Cell publishes the syllabus each year and gives no 2027 dates.

## Modelling choices (Tigertest's own)
- Each mock is half the real length at the real pace: Physics and Chemistry 50 in 45 minutes (25 + 25, as
  100 in 90), Mathematics 25 in 45 minutes (as 50 in 90). A full mock is 75 questions in 90 minutes.
- Per test about a fifth of each subject is Std XI material (Physics 5, Chemistry 5, Mathematics 5 of 25),
  following the official 10 of 50.
- Topic split per test (Tigertest's estimate; the CET Cell publishes no chapter weights): Physics 9 mechanics and
  heat, 8 waves, optics and modern physics, 8 electricity, magnetism and electronics; Chemistry 9 physical,
  8 inorganic, 8 organic; Mathematics 12 algebra, trigonometry, vectors and geometry, 13 calculus and probability.
- Score: percent of marks, each section worth half (each real paper is 100 of 200 marks). No negative marking,
  as on the real exam. The CET Cell's percentiles are not estimated.
