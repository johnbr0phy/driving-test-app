## Written exam pattern (secondary sources; the rule book was not read)

- 100 objective questions, 1 mark each, 100 marks, 2 hours, **no negative marking**, three parts:
  General Knowledge and Reasoning 40, Intellectual Ability and Mental Ability 30, Science and Simple Arithmetic 30.
  Agreed by every guide checked:
  - Physics Wallah exam analysis (above): 40/30/30, 1 mark each, "no negative marking".
  - StudyIQ syllabus page, https://www.studyiq.com/articles/mp-police-constable-syllabus : 40/30/30, 120 minutes,
    online CBT, no negative marking, "Level of questions: Class 8th standard".
  - Careers360 (Hindi) syllabus page, https://competition.careers360.com/hi/articles/mp-police-constable-syllabus-2026 :
    40/30/30, 2 hours, 100 questions, 1 mark each, no negative marking, MCQs in Hindi; written-test shortlist of
    7 times the vacancies per category; PET out of 100 with 30 to qualify, final merit adds written and PET marks.
  - Adda247 syllabus page (search snippet only, page returned 403): same 40/30/30 table.
- **No General Hindi section** appears in any source. The batch brief expected one for MP; it is not modelled, and
  the landing FAQ says so. The paper's language: Careers360 says MCQs are in Hindi, another guide says Hindi and English; not confirmed from
  MPESB, and the landing says guides differ.
- Level: guides say class 8 (StudyIQ) or class 10 (one other guide); the copy says "about class 8 to 10".
- Qualifying marks for the written test: not found; left out of all copy.

## Syllabus followed (guides' summary of the rule book)

- GK and Reasoning: India (history, geography, polity, economy, culture, books and authors, sports), Madhya Pradesh,
  computer awareness (PW reports MP static GK, polity and computer awareness in the 2025 paper), and analogy,
  coding and series (PW). Built as static facts only: no current affairs, office-holders or schemes.
- Intellectual and Mental Ability: blood relations, coding-decoding, series and analogy, direction and ranking (PW),
  plus syllogism, arrangements, calendar and clock (Careers360 list). Text only; Venn and figure items left out.
- Science and Simple Arithmetic: biology (human body, diseases), physics, chemistry; percentage, profit and loss,
  SI and CI, simplification, algebra, probability (PW), mensuration, time and work, speed (StudyIQ, Careers360).

## Modelling decisions

- Real length 100 questions in 120 minutes: modelled at full length, two practice tests, Test 2 held out of the
  drills, 32 drill-only items: 232 in all. One section, one module, grouped in the paper's three parts.
- Per test: Part A 40 = India 14, Madhya Pradesh 12, computer 6, analogies and series 8; Part B 30 = logical 15,
  mental ability 15; Part C 30 = science 15, arithmetic 15. The split inside each part is Tigertest's; guides give only
  the part totals.
- Questions are original. Items were checked against every India bank present on 2026-11-01 (ssccgl, rrbntpc, ibpspo,
  ctet, rrbgroupd, sscgd, sscchsl, sscmts, uppolice, ibpsclerk, jnvst, inrto, and batch 2's sbiclerk, sbipo, ibpsrrb,
  biharpolice, rajpolice, delhipolice as they stood) and rewritten where the same fact and answer or the same numbers
  appeared (about 110 items rewritten).
- Review pass (2026-11-01): 38 more items rewritten for overlap with the India banks as they then stood (same fact
  asked either way round, or same numbers), plus two ambiguity fixes (Farmer : Field :: Sailor : ? had Sea as an
  option; 1 GB = ? MB had 1000 as an option).
