## The course and its assessment (official)

- Jobsplus, "Award in Private Security Guard Services" course sheet (PDF, 2 pages, created
  3 June 2025): https://jobsplus.gov.mt/media/jysd0buh/award-in-private-security-guard-services.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-07). Used:
  - Entry: 18 to 65 years of age, an MQF Level 2 qualification in Mathematics and English, and a
    clean Police Conduct Certificate.
  - 40 hours in two modules: Module 1 20 hours (including a 1-hour assessment), Module 2 20 hours
    (including a 1-hour assessment).
  - Module 1, Private Guard Legislation and Occupational Skills: adhering to the licensing
    requirements of the 'Private Guard' category in the Private Guards and Local Wardens Act;
    legal terminology; the intrinsic responsibilities of a private guard under the Act; complying
    with legislation applicable on duty; verbal and non-verbal communication and environmental
    factors; inter-personal skills with co-workers and superiors; negotiating to resolve conflict;
    customer care with third persons; stereotype judgements and how to avoid them; teamwork and
    co-ordination; personal initiative.
  - Module 2, Security Risk and Security Practices: monitoring the working environment for
    security risks; patrol equipment; surveillance with electronic security systems; security and
    fire prevention patrols; a fire safety drill; dealing with a bomb threat with co-workers and
    superiors; reports on information collected in a security investigation in line with the law;
    a flooding safety drill; searching a person or property in line with the law; initial security
    investigations or inquiries; dealing with a fire using the correct class of fire extinguisher.
  - Assessment of each module: a paper in three sections, Section A true or false, Section B
    multiple choice, Section C short answer, all to be answered; 1 hour; pass mark 45%.
  - MFHEA: Level 2 of the Malta Qualifications Framework and the EQF, 4 ECTS.
  The sheet gives no number of questions per section, so none is stated anywhere on Tigertest.
- Jobsplus, "Award in Private Security Guard Services (Refresher)" (PDF):
  https://jobsplus.gov.mt/media/kodffpxb/award-in-private-security-guard-services-refresher.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-07). 10 hours, one module, a 30-minute assessment in the same three sections,
  pass mark 45%, MQF Level 2, 1 ECTS, for holders of a Private Security Guard or Local Warden
  licence. Background for the landing only.

So: providers approved by the Commissioner of Police write their own papers; Tigertest's four
50-question mock tests (26 Module 1 and 24 Module 2 items each) and its 70% line are Tigertest's
own. The real 45% per module is stated on the landing, the FAQ and examFacts. True or false items
are written as four-option items; short answers are not modelled.

## Licensing law (official, legislation.mt)

- Private Guards and Community Officers Act, Cap. 389 (formerly the Private Guards and Local
  Wardens Act; renamed by Act XIV of 2018), consolidated text with amendments to Act XXI of 2020:
  https://legislation.mt/getpdf/6022ba10bc827214c09ac2bb (checked 2026-11-07). Used:
  art. 2 (Commissioner means the Commissioner of Police; "private guard services" (a) to (l),
  including protection of individuals from bodily harm and cash in transit, excluding in-house
  employees; community officers' services for local council bye-laws); art. 3 (no one acts as a
  private guard unlicensed); art. 4 (no one employs or permits unlicensed private guard
  services); art. 5 (an agency may not provide an unlicensed person); art. 6 (written application
  to the Commissioner); art. 10 (refusal grounds: listed convictions, discharge from the Police,
  armed forces or prison services for an offence or misbehaviour, members of those services and
  public officers, public interest, lacking prescribed qualifications); art. 11 (appeal to the
  Administrative Review Tribunal within one calendar month); art. 12 (licence valid one year from
  issue, renewable year to year; holder informs the Commissioner of any change; 12(3) a licence
  gives no new right or privilege in relation to third parties or their property); art. 12A
  (guards at a place of entertainment: refuse entry to known troublemakers, those refusing ID,
  those manifestly intent on disturbance or violent through intoxication; notify the Police
  immediately; detain a violent person until the Police arrive; escort out or detain a harasser;
  minimum force only where the person fails to comply without physical restraint); art. 12B
  (specialised private guards: refuse entry without ID; search entrants for weapons, withhold
  them and detain the person until the Police arrive; detention at cash-in-transit operations;
  minimum force); art. 13 (withdrawal or suspension); art. 21 (indemnity insurance, made by the
  employer for employees); art. 22 (identity card and uniform); art. 23 (training courses must be
  approved by the Commissioner); art. 25(d) (excessive force or abuse of powers is an offence).
  Fees, fines and the money threshold in art. 10 are not asked.
- Private Guards Regulations, S.L. 389.01, consolidated with amendments to Act XXI of 2020:
  https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/389.1/eng/pdf (checked 2026-11-07). Used: reg. 4(1) (certificate of
  an approved training course; former police, armed forces or prison service exempt); reg. 4(2)
  (five years' service or five years as a private guard for a place of entertainment or
  specialised licence); reg. 4(3) (renewal needs at least ten hours of continuing training in the
  previous year); reg. 5 (course approval by the Commissioner; First Schedule topics); reg. 7(3)
  (licence categories: Private Guard, Private Guard at a Place of Entertainment, Specialised
  Private Guard (driving), Specialised Private Guard (non-driving)); reg. 7(4) (only a specialised
  private guard (driving) guards money or property in transit); reg. 9 (uniform approved by the
  Commissioner; written exemption carried with the ID card; place-of-entertainment guards exempt);
  reg. 10 (identity card is the Commissioner's property, bears a photo, expires with a suspended,
  withdrawn or unrenewed licence, returned on expiry, worn conspicuously on duty); reg. 12
  (armoured vehicles, at least two specialised guards per cash vehicle). First Schedule syllabus:
  the science of security, security risk and probability of risk, private guard services under
  the Act, observation, investigation, inquiry, action and controls, safety drills, teamwork and
  co-ordination, initiative, disaster planning, attributes of a security person. Fees in regs. 5
  to 7 and the insurance sums in reg. 8 are not asked. The five-year card validity in reg. 10(2)
  is not asked.

## Criminal law (official, legislation.mt)

- Criminal Code, Cap. 9, consolidated: https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/9/eng/pdf (checked
  2026-11-07). Used: art. 86 (arresting, detaining or confining a person without a lawful order,
  except where the law lets private individuals apprehend offenders); arts. 223 and 224 (no offence
  where bodily harm is imposed by actual necessity in lawful defence of oneself or another; 224(b)
  defence against theft or plunder with violence); art. 355W (any person not being a police
  officer may arrest without warrant anyone in the act of committing or who has just committed a
  crime concerning sexual offences, wilful homicide or bodily harm, theft, or wilful unlawful entry
  or damage to property; must inform the Police without delay and hold the person only until the
  Police take over); art. 355X (the wider police power, including reasonable suspicion of being
  about to commit a crime); art. 355AB (no harshness, bond or restraint unless indispensable);
  art. 355AC (an arrest is unlawful unless the person is told he is under arrest and the reasons, in
  a language he understands; where a private person arrests, the reasons may be given once the
  Police take over). Note: the proviso in 355AC(2) cites "article 355V", which is the warrant
  article; the private person's power is 355W. The bank says only "a private person".

## Good practice, not from a single official source

Fire classes (EN 2: A solids, B flammable liquids, C gases, D metals, F cooking oils) and which
extinguisher suits which, 112 as Malta's emergency number and the Civil Protection Department as
its fire and rescue service, flood drill steps, bomb threat call handling, scene preservation,
notebook and report practice, patrols, CCTV and access control, communication and conflict
management. These are standard guarding practice taught on approved courses; wrong options are
plainly unsafe or unlawful.
