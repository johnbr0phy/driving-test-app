# Sources for `mtmoto` (Malta motorcycle theory test, categories AM, A1, A2 and A)

All 200 questions are original. Transport Malta publishes its own theory question bank, and some
motoring schools hand it out; it was not opened or copied, and nor were commercial practice apps
(AutoviaTest, open-exam-prep). Pictures are described in words. transport.gov.mt and the
motoring school sites answer this sandbox with a Cloudflare 403, so Transport Malta's pages were
read through search-engine extracts, as noted. The Laws of Malta (legislation.mt) load directly and
are the primary source for every rule in the bank. Checked 2026-11-07 unless noted.

## Format of the real test
- Transport Malta, "Obtaining your driving licence"
  https://www.transport.gov.mt/land/licence/obtaining-your-driving-licence-786
  (403 to direct fetch; read via search extracts 2026-11-07): the theory test "takes 45 minutes
  and is made up of 35 multiple choice questions and you need to answer 30 correctly to pass";
  applicants must hold a learner's permit; learner's permits are issued for three years and are
  applied for online at licenzji-xufiera.gov.mt; Theory Test Centre booking by phone. The page is
  general (all categories); it does not give a separate format for motorcycles. The landing says
  so: "Transport Malta gives the theory test as 35 questions in 45 minutes, 30 to pass".
- Transport Malta, form DRV 001, "Driving licence under Cat A learner's permit"
  https://transport.gov.mt/Land/Downloads/Driving/DRV-001-DRIVING-LICENCE-UNDER-CAT-A-LEARNER-S-PERMIT-3170
  (403; title and search extract only): the category A learner's permit application form exists;
  contents not used.
- Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations, S.L. 65.18, consolidated to L.N. 298 of 2025
  https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/65.18/eng/pdf (read 2026-11-07):
  - reg. 16: the driving test is a theory test plus a test of skills and behaviour; for category
    AM it is a theory test only.
  - reg. 17 and the Fourth Schedule: the theory test covers the common syllabus (road traffic
    regulations, the driver, the road, other road users, documents, accidents, vehicle safety,
    mechanical faults, the environment) and, for categories A, A2 and A1, a compulsory check of:
    (a) protective outfit such as gloves, boots, clothes and safety helmet; (b) visibility of
    motorcycle riders; (c) risk factors of road conditions, with extra attention to slippery
    parts such as drain covers, road markings and tram rails; (d) mechanical aspects with extra
    attention to the emergency stop switch, the oil levels and the chain. The bank's categories
    follow this list (Malta has no trams, so tram rails are not asked).
  - Fourth Schedule: an applicant who already holds a licence in another category is exempted
    from the common provisions. Not stated on the landing as a fact about how Transport Malta
    runs the test (not confirmed on its own pages); integrator may add it if confirmed.
  - reg. 18: only applicants who are 18 may sit the theory test, except category AM from 16.
    (Used on the landing and in examFacts as the regulation's figure; not in the bank.)
  - reg. 20: standard learner's permit at 18, except A2 (20) and A (22 with two years on A2, or
    24). Not used in the bank (ages left out, as the brief asks); not stated on the landing.
  - reg. 21: a standard learner's permit allows practice on all days except Sundays and public
    holidays, under instruction, on A1, A2, A, B1, B, BE, C1, C1E and g vehicles. Used.
  - reg. 44(1)(a): full licences in AM, A1, A2, A, B, B1 and BE are valid ten years (five years
    from age 70). Used.
  - reg. 47: licences state the conditions on which the holder may drive. Used.
  - reg. 50(d)-(f): A1, A2, A and B licences are valid for AM; A2 for A1; A for A1 and A2. Used.
  - First Schedule: AM = mopeds, light quadricycles, electric kickscooters; A1 = motorcycles up
    to 125 cc, 11 kW and 0.1 kW/kg, and motor tricycles up to 15 kW; A2 = motorcycles up to
    35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg, not derived from a vehicle of more than double its power; A = any
    motorcycle and motor tricycles over 15 kW. Used.
- Motor Vehicles Regulations, S.L. 65.11 https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/65.11/eng/pdf
  (read 2026-11-07):
  - reg. 2: "motor cycle" means a two-wheeler over 50 cm3 (internal combustion) and, or with a
    design speed over 45 km/h, including a motor tricycle. Used for the moped item.
  - reg. 80: motorcycles shall not be parked parallel to the kerb or wall but at an angle of at
    least 45 degrees. Used.
  - reg. 115(1): no holding or using a hand-held mobile device while driving a motor vehicle,
    motorcycle or e-kickscooter, or while stationary in traffic. Reg. 115(3): no in-ear or
    over-ear device in both ears while riding a motorcycle or stopped in traffic. Used (the
    phone rule only as part of the sat nav item, since mttheory asks the car version).
  - reg. 123(1): no more than one person besides the driver on a motorcycle, sitting astride on a
    proper seat securely fixed behind the driver's seat; reg. 123(2): no one may drive or be
    carried on a motorcycle or quad bike without a crash helmet. Used.
  - reg. 127: motorcycles 50 km/h in towns and villages, 80 outside (same as cars). Not asked
    (mttheory asks the car limits).
- Languages: English or Maltese, as for every Transport Malta theory test (see
  `tools/next100/mttheory/sources.md`; the official page offers reading assistance in Maltese or
  English and publishes the question bank in both).
- Highway Code of Malta, updated 25 July 2025 (Malta Daily, 25 July 2025,
  https://maltadaily.mt/malta-launches-updated-highway-code-educational-campaign): adds guidance
  on helmets, distraction and phones. The PDF returns 403 here.
- Commercial guides, for context only (not used for any rule): AutoviaTest,
  https://autoviatest.com/en/driving-test/malta/motorcycle (read 2026-11-07: 35 questions, 30 to
  pass for the motorcycle test, Transport Malta named as data source); Gordon's Motoring School,
  https://gordonsmoto.com/motoringschool (403; search snippet says it runs motorcycle courses
  including theory); open-exam-prep.com lists a Malta motorcycle theory practice set.
- Alcohol: Traffic Regulation Ordinance, Cap. 65, art. 15I (see mttheory sources): 50 mg per
  100 ml of blood for most drivers, 20 mg for probationary licence holders. Not asked as a
  figure (mttheory asks it); the bank asks how alcohol affects a rider.

## Conflicts and figures left out
- Minimum ages conflict across guides (16, 18, 20, 22, 23, 24). The bank asks none. The landing
  quotes only S.L. 65.18 reg. 18 (theory test from 18, AM from 16).
- Fees, fines, penalty points, tread depths, helmet standard numbers, daytime headlight law and
  filtering legality are not asked; no source confirmed them for Malta.
- No figure from a commercial guide is stated as an official rule.

## Kept clear of other banks
Written against `mttheory`, `mttaxi`, `ukmoto`, `nzmoto`, `zak53moto`, `sgrtt`, `moto` and the EU
motorcycle banks on disk (belgium, de, denmark, finland, greece, hungary, portugal, sweden). Items
whose fact and answer matched a sibling (112, warn traffic at a crash, press on a wound, CPR,
hay fever tablets, clockwise roundabouts, zebra crossings, white cane, return to the left lane,
overtaking at bends, rider and passenger helmets, phones in a queue, dipping and dazzle) were
rewritten to motorcycle-specific facts.

  RID-011 (INRTO). No overlap found with mttheory, mttaxi, mtguard or mtcpc.

  the side stand, so the key is now level ground and the handbook's method.

  reworded so it does not imply quad bikes are the only other vehicle; PAS-013 explanation no
