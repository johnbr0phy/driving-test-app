# Sources: Malta Taxi Driver Theory Test (mttaxi)

All 200 questions are original. No Transport Malta question bank, training provider material or
commercial practice app was opened or copied. transport.gov.mt and servizz.gov.mt answer this
sandbox with a 403, so their pages were read through search-engine extracts, as noted; the law
itself was read in full from legislation.mt. Checked 2026-11-07.

## Official sources (law, read in full)

- Taxi Services Regulations, S.L. 499.59 (L.N. 369 of 2010, as amended to L.N. 343 of 2021):
  https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/499.59/eng/pdf (PDF loads; read 2026-11-07)
  - Reg 9: no one drives a taxi without a valid taxi driver permit; noted on the driving licence;
    valid five years or a shorter period shown; renewal from three months before expiry with a
    police conduct certificate and a signed declaration.
  - Reg 11: good repute and conduct, driving licence for the category, medically fit, aged 19 or
    over (18 for an electric power assisted pedal cycle permit), the skills in reg 12; conditions
    met at all times; convictions reported in writing within 21 days (20 days after an appeal).
  - Reg 12: skills = training courses on Part 1 of the Third Schedule, a written or oral
    examination on Part 2a, a practical examination on Part 2b, the Taxi Driver and Area Knowledge
    Certificate, and at least 40 hours accompanying a taxi driver on duty in periods of at least
    four consecutive hours, at least ten of them after sunset and before sunrise; refresher course
    once every five years; holders of a certificate of professional competence exempt from the
    course, the examination and the certificate.
  - Regs 13 to 16: decisions by registered post; representations within ten days; appeal to the
    Administrative Review Tribunal within thirty days; documents within three working days;
    suspension has immediate effect; free special training before driving wheelchair accessible
    taxis; tag carried on a lanyard while on duty, produced to enforcement officers, not
    transferable, damaged tag returned for a free replacement, loss reported at once, tag returned
    within 48 hours of revocation, suspension or expiry.
  - Reg 18: licence holders may engage only permit holders registered in Part III of the Register.
  - Reg 20: new vehicle, at least 1400cc (not hybrids or EVs), leg and head room, interior
    lighting, air-conditioning, right-hand drive, taximeter visible to passengers, two-way
    communication system, tracking device, emergency button within reach of passengers and driver;
    optional surveillance camera.
  - Reg 21: taxi licence states Malta or Gozo and the taxi is used only on that island; Gozo taxis
    may go directly between Gozo and Malta International Airport or any hospital in Malta and may
    not pick up or drop off anyone else on Malta's roads; Malta taxis may not carry anyone between
    the islands; taxi licence valid one year.
  - Regs 23 to 35: renewal checks; taxis renewed until the twentieth year from first registration;
    registration marks TAXI + three numbers + M or G, electric mini cabs CABS + three numbers;
    numbers painted on the front doors, black on white (Malta), red on white (Gozo); fit and safe,
    licence suspended until remedied; manufacturer's horn only; white colour; bonnet, front doors,
    front mudguards and wheel arches stay white; only licensed taxis may use the word "taxi" or a
    roof sign; identification certificate displayed; no modification without prior written
    approval; illuminated TAXI roof sign centrally between the front doors, black on white (Malta)
    or red on white (Gozo), off when the taximeter is on, white or light yellow light; MINI CAB
    sign; wheelchair accessible taxi sign beside the word TAXI.
  - Regs 36 to 58: taximeter verified and sealed, not to be tampered with, defective meter takes
    the taxi out of service; meter started at the commencement of the hiring (when passengers
    board unless agreed), fare left displayed, fiscal receipt given; tours of agreed duration;
    Z readings kept at least six months; tracking device kept on while on duty, defective device
    takes the taxi out of service; camera captures the back seat, sticker; two-way system not for
    idle conversation; comfortable temperature unless agreed; nothing carried without the hirer's
    consent; luggage compartment not open so as to hinder the view; search after each hire and
    hand property to the Police within 24 hours; uniform; no more persons than the registration
    certificate; no free-wheeling beyond six metres; prudent driving so passengers do not feel
    unsafe; priority vehicle lanes allowed; no earphones on both ears except two-way and hands-free
    devices; no lengthy discussions; audio only if the hirer agrees; clean taxi; flammable liquid
    only in the fuel tank; no weapons; no smoking and a no-smoking notice; fire extinguisher,
    security triangle and first-aid kit; emergency button sticker "use in cases of emergency only".
  - Regs 59 to 66: no refusal without reasonable cause to any place or for up to four hours;
    attend appointed time and place; wait as the hirer directs; most expeditious route, nearest
    point; no crawling or soliciting; first three drivers on a stand stay with their taxis; only the
    first taxi on the stand (or the first with a driver in it) is hired; a wheelchair accessible
    taxi on the stand is first for a wheelchair user; a first-in-line driver who refuses loses the
    place; plying taxis keep 100 m from a taxi stand and 15 m from a bus stop or fare stage;
    priority to wheelchair users; fares no higher than the fixed fare; otherwise the meter fare, with
    an advertised or agreed discount; maximum fares up 50 percent for more than five passengers;
    tariff copy displayed; soiling charge capped; taxi stands for taxis only; booths and
    two-part pre-paid fixed-fare tickets.
  - Regs 72 to 75 (penalty points and withdrawal; not asked, house style leaves point values out);
    reg 74 driving licence forwarded to the Authority on suspension; reg 75 no new application for
    two years after withdrawal.
  - First Schedule: flat extra charge on 25 December and 1 January (amount not used).
  - Third Schedule Part 1: training subjects (vehicle and safety, passengers including elderly and
    special needs, the law on carrying passengers, emergencies: first aid and CPR, aggressive or
    drunk passengers, accidents; criminality: refusal to pay, requests for information on illegal
    activities, requests to speed or to overload; correct use of speed; fitness; risks at work;
    routes and alternate routes; prime tourist and commercial locations across Malta and Gozo;
    taximeter and equipment; customer care, politeness, personal cleanliness; languages; optional
    wheelchair accessible taxi training with the loading sequence). "Minimum duration of
    training - 46 hours".
  - Third Schedule Part 2: theory examination, written or oral, duration 45 minutes; a reader and
    scribe for candidates who cannot read and write; practical examination of 45 minutes within
    twelve months of the theory pass (route with at least one prime tourist and one prime
    commercial location, safety check, compliance check, taximeter); fail at 10 errors or any
    serious or dangerous error.
- Passenger Transport Services Regulations, S.L. 499.56 (amended to L.N. 172 of 2026):
  https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/499.56/eng/pdf (read 2026-11-07). Part III driver's permit and tag
  for other passenger transport; tag valid five years, worn on the left of the chest when not
  driving. Used only for background.
- Light Passenger Transport Services and Vehicle Hire Services Regulations, S.L. 499.68 (amended to
  L.N. 171 of 2026): https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/499.68/eng/pdf (read 2026-11-07). Reg 15: a
  light passenger transport (Y-plate) vehicle may not stand or ply for hire. Regs 39 and 40: the
  Y-plate driver's permit has its own course and examination; from L.N. 171 of 2026 an applicant
  needs a category B licence held at least two years; a taxi driver permit holder is exempt from
  the course and examination. Used for the landing FAQ only; the bank follows the taxi rules.
- Traffic Regulation Ordinance, Cap. 65: https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/65/eng/pdf (read
  2026-11-07). Art. 15I: prescribed alcohol limit 0 for buses, coaches, vehicles on priority duty
  and other vehicles carrying passengers for a fee; art. 15 allows breath tests of such drivers at
  road checks without reasonable suspicion.

## Format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

- Duration 45 minutes: Taxi Services Regulations, Third Schedule Part 2a (official, above).
- 35 multiple-choice questions, 30 correct to pass: Transport Malta, "Guidelines to become an
  approved training provider for taxi drivers"
  https://www.transport.gov.mt/Land-approved-training-providers-guidelines-4.pdf-f972
  (403 to direct fetch; read via search extract 2026-11-07: "This examination will consist of 35
  multiple choice questions, of which 30 must be answered correctly to pass").
- "The theory test consists of a multiple choice paper with 35 questions and a duration of 45
  minutes. The set pass mark is 85% and the test is available in both Maltese and English":
  Transport Malta, Taxi Driver and Area Knowledge Certificate page
  https://www.transport.gov.mt/land/approved-training/taxi-training-provider-821
  (403; via search extract 2026-11-07). 30 of 35 is 85.7 percent; Tigertest uses passPct 85,
  which gives ceil(0.85 x 35) = 30.
- Permit requirements as Transport Malta states them (category B licence, the TDAKC, medical
  fitness, fluency in speaking Maltese or English, good conduct; tag valid up to five years in line
  with the driving licence): Transport Malta, Taxis and Electric Mini Cabs
  https://www.transport.gov.mt/land/professional-transport-services/professional-drivers-operating-for-hire-or-reward/taxis-and-electric-mini-cabs-778
  (403; via search extract 2026-11-07). The regulation's own wording is "Fluency in Maltese and the
  ability to communicate in basic English"; no bank item asks about language.
- servizz.gov.mt, "Taxi - Public Transport Drivers Tag"
  https://www.servizz.gov.mt/en/Pages/Transport-and-Communications/Transport-Services/Land-Transport/Taxi/WEB795/default.aspx
  (403; listed by search with the 46-hour attendance certificate, the theory test pass report, the
  practical pass report, category B and age 19+).
- Guides, not official (read 2026-11-07, used only to cross-check): gomjablog.blogspot.com
  (January 2026, "How to get a taxi driver tag in Malta"), easy-quizzz.com Malta taxi pages (test at
  the Theory Test Centre, Pietà). Never cited on the landing.

## What Tigertest models

- Five tests of 35 at 30 to pass, single answer. Weights per test are Tigertest's (Transport
  Malta publishes no split), following the Third Schedule subject list: permit and tag 5, the taxi
  and its equipment 4, hiring and stands 5, fares 3, passengers 4, safety 5, driving 3, routes and
  places 6. The last items of each pool (25 in all) are training-only.
- The practical examination, the 40 accompanied hours and the language requirement are not
  modelled. Places and routes are described in words, no maps. No fees, fines, fare amounts or
  penalty point values.
