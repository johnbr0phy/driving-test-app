# Sources for `mttheory` (Malta driving theory test, category B)

All 200 questions are original. Transport Malta publishes its own theory question bank (English
and Maltese); it was not opened or copied, and nor were commercial practice apps. Signs are
described in words. transport.gov.mt and gov.mt answer this sandbox with a Cloudflare 403, so
official pages were read through search-engine extracts of those pages, as noted. Checked
2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Format of the real test
- Transport Malta, "Obtaining your driving licence"
  https://www.transport.gov.mt/land/licence/obtaining-your-driving-licence-786
  (403 to direct fetch; read via search extracts 2026-11-01): the theory test "takes 45 minutes and
  is made up of 35 multiple choice questions"; you need 30 correct to pass; candidates must be at
  least 18 and hold a Learner's Permit; present ID card before sitting the test; candidates who
  struggle to read can ask for assistance; Theory Test Centre phone given. Fee not used in the bank.
- gov.mt, "Getting a Driving Licence in Malta"
  https://www.gov.mt/en/Life%20Events/Pages/Getting%20a%20Driving%20License/Getting-a-Driving-Licence-in-Malta.aspx
  (403; via search extracts): multiple-choice test of driving-related questions on a touch-screen
  computer; one must be 18 to sit it; practical test needs the theory pass and a learner's permit;
  learners practise accompanied by a licensed instructor or approved tutor, Monday to Saturday
  except public holidays (the bank asks only "an authorised tutor").
- servizz.gov.mt, "Driving Licence under Category B (Learner's Permit)"
  https://www.servizz.gov.mt/en/Pages/Transport-and-Communications/Transport-Services/Land-Transport/Driving-License/WEB725/default.aspx
  (403; via search extract): eligibility "any person over the age of 18 years".
- Transport Malta form DRV 005 (category G learner's permit) says the learner permit is valid
  three years; not asserted for category B.
- Languages: Transport Malta publishes the question bank in English and Maltese (search extracts of
  transport.gov.mt question-bank downloads); guides (theorytestmalta.com/faqs, read 2026-11-01)
  say the test is taken in English or Maltese. Copy says "in English or Maltese".
- theorytestmalta.com FAQs (a commercial guide, read 2026-11-01): 35 questions drawn at random,
  45 minutes, 30 of 35 to pass, touch screen, Theory Test Centre at Gwardamanga Hill, Pietà.
  Matches the official figures above.

## Rules used in the bank
- Highway Code / Road Code: Transport Malta launched an updated Highway Code (Road Code) on
  25 July 2025 with e-scooter and pedelec rules, guidance on distraction and mobile phones, seat
  belts and helmets, and illustrations of signs and markings (Malta Daily, 25 July 2025,
  https://maltadaily.mt/malta-launches-updated-highway-code-educational-campaign). The PDF
  (https://www.transport.gov.mt/RoadCodeEN.pdf-f10821) returns 403 here.
- Left-hand traffic, British-based signs with some Italian designs, metric units: Wikipedia,
  "Road signs in Malta" (read 2026-11-01), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_signs_in_Malta ;
  Road Signs and Road Markings Regulations, amended to 2011.
- Speed limits: cars 50 km/h urban, 80 km/h rural; vans, buses, lorries and vehicles towing
  trailers 40/60 km/h. Wikipedia, "Speed limits in Malta" (citing the European Commission's Going
  abroad page), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_limits_in_Malta . Signed lower limits apply.
- Alcohol: general limit lowered from 0.8 to 0.5 g/l (50 mg per 100 ml of blood); 0.2 g/l
  (20 mg per 100 ml) for probationary licence holders and commercial vehicle drivers; 0.0 for bus
  and coach drivers. ETSC, 3 November 2018,
  https://etsc.eu/malta-cuts-drink-driving-limit-for-novice-drivers-by-75/ ; Malta Independent,
  23 December 2018 (via search). The length of the probationary period is not asked.
- Emergency number 112 (EU-wide).
- Roadworthiness test is the VRT (Vehicle Roadworthiness Test); road licence is the annual licence
  to use a vehicle on the road (Transport Malta, via search extracts).
- Traffic Regulation Ordinance, Cap. 65, https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/65/eng (loads; consolidated
  to 10 March 2026). Reviewer read the PDF (https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/65/eng/pdf, 2026-11-01):
  art. 15I prescribed limits are 22 µg/100 ml breath or 50 mg/100 ml blood for most drivers;
  9 µg / 20 mg for commercial vehicles and probationary licence holders; 0 for buses, coaches,
  vehicles on priority duty and vehicles carrying passengers for a fee. Confirms the bank.
- Motor Vehicles Regulations, S.L. 65.11, https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/65.11/eng/pdf (read by the
  reviewer 2026-11-01), used for: reg. 75 keep left, overtake on the right unless lanes are marked
  or for other just cause; reg. 79 no parking within 12 m either side of a bus stop, around a
  roundabout or traffic island, on or within 4 m of a pedestrian crossing, on the footpath unless
  signs direct otherwise, within 5 m of a corner, bend or splay; reg. 82(1) at an intersection of
  roads of equal importance the driver with the other vehicle on his right gives way; reg. 82(2)
  vehicles approaching a roundabout give way to those already on it; reg. 82(3) narrow street,
  the driver nearer the wider part reverses; reg. 84 move to the left and stop for vehicles on
  priority duty; reg. 85 slowing-down arm signal; reg. 86 stop when a person in charge of a led
  horse or driven cattle signals; reg. 90 slow and heavy traffic in the innermost (left) lane;
  reg. 123 crash helmets for rider and passenger; reg. 125 traffic lights (amber has the same
  prohibition as red unless too close to stop; flashing amber = proceed with caution; two
  flashing reds in a triangle = do not pass the stop line); reg. 127 speed limits: private cars
  50 in towns and villages, 80 outside; minibuses, goods vehicles over 3 tonnes and towing
  vehicles 40/60 (the regulation does not say whether "towing vehicles" means a car pulling a
  trailer, so the bank asks only about lorries over 3 tonnes).
- Format re-check 2026-11-01: transport.gov.mt still answers 403 (direct, the Highway-eg.pdf
  copy and the archive). A search extract of the official page reads "The test, which takes 45
  minutes, is made up of 35 multiple choice questions and you need to answer 30 correctly to
  pass", lists the Theory Test question banks in English and in Maltese, and says reading
  assistance is "restricted to the reading of the questions and possible answers in either
  Maltese or English". English is therefore supported by the official page's own wording; the
  phrase "offered in English or Maltese" also matches guides.

## Not used
Fees, fines, penalty point amounts, retake waiting times, and the probationary period length are
not asked. No figure from a commercial guide is stated as an official rule.
