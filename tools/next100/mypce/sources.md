# Malaysia PCEIA (Pre-Contract Examination for Insurance Agents) sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- Asian Institute of Insurance (Aii, formerly the Malaysian Insurance Institute, MII), programme page
  "Pre-Contract Examination for Insurance Agents (PCEIA)":
  https://aiiasia.org/programmes/pre-contract-examination-for-insurance-agents-pceia/
  Routes: AC (Basic and Life Insurance) 100 questions (Part A 50 + Part C 50), 2 hours;
  AB (Basic and General Insurance) 100 questions (Part A 50 + Part B 50), 2 hours;
  CO (Life only) 50 questions, 1.5 hours; BO (General only) 50 questions, 1.5 hours.
  Passing mark 50%. Languages English, Bahasa Malaysia, Mandarin. Delivered as a Virtual
  Computer-Based Exam (VCBE). Minimum entry SPM (5 passes including Bahasa Malaysia) or equivalent.
  Exemptions no longer recognised from 8 August 2024. (Fees are on the page; left out by house rule.)
- Aii Exam FAQs: https://aiiasia.org/exam-faqs/ (registration through iLMS, e-learning and
  interactive e-textbook included; no syllabus detail).
- Aii / MII guide book PDF: https://aiiasia.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/MII-Guide-Book-1.pdf
  (FAQ pages; text extracted; no PCEIA syllabus).
- FPAM notice, "Important notice: PCIL, PCEIA, CEILLI exemption policy change" (27 August 2025):
  https://www.fpam.org.my/important-notice-pcil-pceia-ceilli-exemption-policy-change
  (exemptions withdrawn from 8 August 2024).
- Guides (not official) say: no sectional pass requirement (50 of 100 overall); Part A has six
  chapters (risk and insurance, basic principles, legislation including the FSA, PDPA and AMLA,
  the insurance contract, law of agency, medical and health insurance); Part C covers legal
  aspects of life insurance (nomination, trusts), life products, premium rating, underwriting,
  claims and the code of practice; Part B covers general insurance (motor, fire and other classes).
  https://www.examprep.my/blog/pceia-exam-format and https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/my-pceia
  (both checked 2026-11-05). Aii publishes no per-chapter weights, so the blueprint is Tigertest's.
- Not modelled: the real route order (a candidate sits A+C or A+B, never all three); the mock tests
  mix Parts A, B and C 20/10/20 per 50. Investment-linked selling needs the separate CEILLI/PCIL
  exam (not modelled).

## Content sources for the bank
- Financial Services Act 2013 (Act 758), Bank Negara Malaysia: https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-services-act-2013
  Used: BNM as regulator; FSA replaced the Insurance Act 1996; Schedule 7 prohibited business
  conduct (misleading conduct, undue pressure and coercion); Schedule 8 (insurable interest in own
  life, spouse, child or ward under the age of majority, dependants; capacity of minors 10 to under 16
  with a parent's or guardian's written consent, carried over from the Insurance Act 1996 s.153,
  primary text not loaded: the item asks only the consent rule, not the 16-year threshold);
  Schedule 9 (consumer duty to take reasonable care not to misrepresent; non-consumer duty of
  disclosure; deliberate or reckless misrepresentation lets the insurer avoid and refuse claims;
  two-year limit on avoiding a life policy except for fraud, per study material quoting para 13);
  Schedule 10 (nomination from age 16; statutory trust for spouse, child, or parent where no spouse
  or child, for non-Muslim policy owners; Muslim owner's nominee receives as executor; trustee of
  full age, not the policy owner; an assignee has priority over the nominee, para 7).
- Prudential Assurance Malaysia, "Trust and Nomination FAQ" (August 2024, insurer explanation of
  Schedule 10): https://www.prudential.com.my/en/.galleries/PDF/trust-and-nomination-faq.pdf
  Used: who creates a statutory trust by marital status, minor nominee's trustee is the other
  parent (else the Public Trustee or a trust company), equal shares when none stated, policy owner
  cannot appoint himself trustee, consent of trustee needed to deal with the policy.
- Insurer nomination forms (Allianz, Sun Life Malaysia, Etiqa) confirm the age 16 to nominate and
  trustee age 18: https://www.allianz.com.my/content/dam/onemarketing/azmb/wwwallianzcommy/pdf/policy-self-service/NominationForm_Eng_2307.pdf
- Islamic Financial Services Act 2013 (takaful): https://www.bnm.gov.my/islamic-financial-services-act-2013
- PIDM, Takaful and Insurance Benefits Protection System: https://www.pidm.gov.my/ (no limits used).
- Ombudsman for Financial Services: https://www.ofs.org.my/ (no limits used).
- Anti-Money Laundering, Anti-Terrorism Financing and Proceeds of Unlawful Activities Act 2001
  (customer due diligence, suspicious transaction reports, tipping off).
- Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (seven principles: general, notice and choice, disclosure,
  security, retention, data integrity, access).
- Contracts Act 1950 (elements, coercion, misrepresentation, void and voidable agreements) and
  agency law (Part X, express appointment, ratification, duties, termination).
- Road Transport Act 1987 (compulsory third-party cover for death and bodily injury; report an
  accident to the police within 24 hours, s.52; insurer's right of recovery).
- BNM requirements: 15-day free-look period for life policies over one year; product disclosure
  sheet. Grace period (commonly 30 days), suicide exclusion (commonly one year), TPD waiting
  period (often six months) are stated as common practice, not law.
- PIAM own damage knock-for-knock arrangement and NCD transfer: https://www.piam.org.my/
- Institute Cargo Clauses A, B and C (2009).

No question asks about the PCEIA itself. No fees, limits, NCD percentages or tax figures.

## Fact-check, 2026-11-05 (FSA 2013 text read from BNM's PDF of Act 758)
- Act text: https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124///35ed2b4c-1995-f91d-3891-75d69d247d55
  (Laws of Malaysia, Act 758, as published by Bank Negara Malaysia), checked 2026-11-05.
- Life policy in effect more than two years during the insured's lifetime cannot be avoided for
  an inaccurate statement unless it was on a material matter and fraudulently made:
  Schedule 9 para 13(2). Confirmed.
- Minor aged 10 to under 16 may effect a life policy with the written consent of a parent or
  guardian; from 16 no consent needed to effect one (consent still needed to assign):
  Schedule 8 para 4. Confirmed.
- Nomination from age 16: Schedule 10 para 2(1). Confirmed (also the Allianz form text above).
- "Assignment revokes a nomination": WRONG. Schedule 10 para 3 says a nomination is revoked only
  by the death of all nominees, a written notice or a later nomination, and "shall not be revoked
  by a will or by any other act, event or means"; para 7 gives an assignee or pledgee priority
  over the nominee, with the balance paid to the nominee. The LLG item was rewritten to test
  para 7.
- Premium paid to an authorised agent counts as paid to the insurer: no FSA provision says this
  in those words; the item is framed as general agency law (payment to an agent authorised to
  receive it is payment to the principal), and Schedule 9 para 12 deems an authorised agent's
  acts and knowledge to be the insurer's for the formation or variation of the contract. Kept.
