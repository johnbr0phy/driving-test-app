# NBDHE bank sources

Checked 2026-11-03.

- JCNDE, NBDHE 2026 Candidate Guide, updated 9/17/2026 (read directly):
  https://jcnde.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/jcnde/files/nbdhe_candidate_guide.pdf
  (the 12/15/2025 edition is at .../files/2026_nbdhe_candidate_guide.pdf; same specifications,
  but it said the update was "anticipated in October 2026", the 9/17/2026 edition says November 2026).
  - 350 multiple-choice items covering functions delegable to a dental hygienist in a majority of
    states. Discipline-based component 200 items; case-based component 150 items on 12 to 15
    patient cases (adult and child; geriatric, adult-periodontal, pediatric, special needs,
    medically compromised and others), presented with histories, dental charts, radiographs and
    clinical photographs.
  - Test specifications before the update: Scientific Basis for Dental Hygiene Practice 61,
    Provision of Clinical Dental Hygiene Services 115, Community Health/Research Principles 24.
  - Test specifications after the update: Scientific Basis 56 (anatomic sciences incl. head and
    neck, dental anatomy, histology and embryology; physiology; biochemistry and nutrition;
    microbiology and immunology; general and oral pathology; pharmacology incl. local anesthesia),
    Provision of Clinical Dental Hygiene Services 124 (patient assessment incl. occlusal
    evaluation; dental radiography incl. emerging technologies; dental hygiene care planning incl.
    infection control, emergencies, patient education, anxiety and pain control with local
    anesthesia, patients with special needs, treatment strategies; periodontal disease
    management incl. implant care, reassessment and maintenance; preventive agents; supportive
    treatment services; professional responsibility incl. documentation and risk management),
    Research Principles and Community Health 20. Case-based areas renamed to match (patient
    assessment, dental radiography, care planning, periodontal disease management, preventive
    agents, supportive treatment services, professional responsibility).
  - Universal/National tooth numbering (1 to 32, A to T).
  - Administration: Session One, 200 discipline-based questions in 3 hours 30 minutes (optional
    15-minute break inside); optional 30-minute break; Session Two, 150 case-based questions
    (7 to 8 cases, about 75 questions, then 7 to 8 more) in 4 hours. 7 hours 30 minutes of testing
    time, 9 hours in total.
  - Scoring: reported pass or fail; scale scores 49 to 99, 75 is the minimum passing score; the
    pass decision is on the overall scale score only; no penalty for guessing; some unscored
    experimental items. Failing candidates get performance information for 13 discipline areas and
    the case area.
  - Re-examination: at least 60 days between attempts, at most four in 12 months; Five
    Years/Five Attempts rule, then one attempt every six months.
- JCNDE NBDHE page, https://jcnde.ada.org/nbdhe (read 2026-11-03): updated test specifications
  scheduled for implementation in November 2026, "not expected to substantially alter
  examination preparation"; 9-hour test time; year-round at Pearson VUE in the US and Canada; no
  longer offered in Quebec; results three to four weeks after the exam.
- JCNDE, NBDHE Case Development Guide:
  https://jcnde.ada.org/-/media/project/ada-organization/ada/jcnde/files/nbdhe_case_guide.pdf
  Cases are about 80% adult and 20% child; each case carries roughly 8 to 15 items; community
  health items appear only in the discipline-based component, as testlets.

## Bank decisions

- 200 original items, four 50-question tests. Per test: Scientific Basis 9 (anatomy 3,
  biosciences 3, pathology and pharmacology 3), clinical discipline items 21 (assessment 3,
  radiography 3, care planning 4, periodontics 4, preventive agents 3, supportive 2, professional
  2), research and community 3, case scenarios 17 (adult periodontal and geriatric 9, medically
  compromised and special needs 5, pediatric 3). This is Tigertest's split: the real exam has
  43% case items; ours 34%, with the rest moved to the clinical discipline areas the cases test.
- Case-based items are text scenarios: each stem opens with "Case:" and a short patient box (age,
  history, medications, findings). No charts, radiographs or photographs; findings described in
  words. Real cases share one patient across many items; here each item stands alone.
- 75% pass line is a readiness target; the real 75 is a scale score, not percent correct.
- Clinical figures from standard dental hygiene references and current guidance: 2017 AAP/EFP
  classification (staging by interdental CAL, grade C at 10 or more cigarettes a day or HbA1c
  7.0% or more; generalized gingivitis at 30% or more bleeding sites); 2017 ACC/AHA blood pressure
  categories (crisis at 180/120 or higher); AHA 2021 infective endocarditis prophylaxis (amoxicillin
  2 g 30 to 60 minutes before, clindamycin no longer recommended); ADA guidance on prosthetic joints
  (premedication generally not recommended); USPHS 2015 water fluoride 0.7 mg/L; probably toxic dose
  5 mg F/kg; 5% NaF varnish 22,600 ppm, 1.23% APF 12,300 ppm; ADA 2014 child toothpaste amounts;
  lidocaine with epinephrine 7 mg/kg; adult anaphylaxis 0.3 mg IM epinephrine.
- Calculation items (CAL, attached gingiva, pack-years, mg per mL, maximum cartridges, PTD,
  median) are worked in the explanation.
- Overlap: checked against data/danb-questions.json (DANB CDA) and rewrote items that repeated a
  DANB fact and answer (hypoglycemia treatment, standard precautions, mobility grade, record
  correction, informed refusal); checked data/arrt-questions.json (effective dose unit,
  radiosensitivity) and data/nce-questions.json (type I error) and replaced the repeats.
- No exam-admin questions, no fees, no commercial prep provider named. Not affiliated with the
  JCNDE or the ADA.
- Review pass (2026-11-03): rewrote items that repeated a fact and answer in arrt (hypoglycemia
  signs), ontario-psw (adult respiratory rate), cca (Notice of Privacy Practices), vtne (mg per mL
  of a lidocaine solution) and aswb-masters (formative evaluation); sharpened two stems whose
  wording gave the key away (Aggregatibacter, minocycline microspheres) and two awkward stems.
