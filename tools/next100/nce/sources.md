# NCE bank sources

All checked 2026-11-02.

- NBCC, The National Counselor Examination (NCE) Content Outline (c) 2023, linked from
  https://www.nbcc.org/exams/nce as "NCE Content Outline"
  (https://www.nbcc.org/assets/exam/nce_content_outline.pdf and
  https://sanbcccore.blob.core.windows.net/nbcc-assets/assets/exam/nce_content_outline.pdf).
  Exam form: 200 multiple-choice questions, 160 scored and 40 unscored field-test items in
  jumbled order, 3 hours 45 minutes. Job analysis of more than 16,000 credentialed counselors,
  finalized June 2019. Table 1, six domains (percent / scored items): Professional Practice and
  Ethics 12 / 19; Intake, Assessment, and Diagnosis 12 / 19; Areas of Clinical Focus 29 / 47;
  Treatment Planning 9 / 14; Counseling Skills and Interventions 30 / 48; Core Counseling
  Attributes 8 / 13. Table 3: questions aligned with the eight CACREP common core areas
  (professional orientation and ethical practice, social and cultural diversity, human growth and
  development, career development, counseling and helping relationships, group counseling and group
  work, assessment and testing, research and program evaluation). Domain 1 includes "understand
  statistical concepts and methods in research", so research and statistics items sit in nceEthics.
- NBCC, NCE handbook for state licensure candidates (revised September 24, 2024),
  https://sanbcccore.blob.core.windows.net/nbcc-assets/assets/exam/handbooks/nce.pdf :
  200-question, multiple-choice, non-sectioned exam; test session 255 minutes (5 minutes
  agreement, 10 minutes tutorial, 225 minutes exam, one scheduled 15-minute break after question
  100); 160 scored questions worth one point each, so the maximum score is 160; passing point set
  per form by a modified Angoff method (no published percentage); delivered by Pearson VUE at test
  centers or online through OnVUE; a different form for each administration. Sample questions in
  Appendix B have four options (a to d). A failed candidate may retake after a 30-day wait.
- NBCC, NCE page https://www.nbcc.org/exams/nce : "a 200-item multiple-choice examination";
  required for counselor licensure in many states; one of two exam options for the National
  Certified Counselor (NCC) credential; first used in 1983; a section "Upcoming 2027 Version of
  the NCE".
- NBCC, NCE Examination Specifications, effective July 1, 2027 (c) 2026,
  https://www.cce-global.org/Assets/exams/NCE_exam_spec_2027.pdf : from July 1, 2027 the NCE has
  170 multiple-choice questions with three responses each (30 field-test), 225 minutes with an
  optional 15-minute break halfway and no return to the first half after it, scaled scores 100 to
  500 with 360 passing on every form, and six new domains from a 2024-2025 work analysis finalized
  October 2025 (101 tasks): Professional Development and Counselor Self-Awareness 15%, Intake and
  Assessment 18%, Treatment Planning and Continuity of Care 15%, Provision of Counseling
  Interventions 20%, Indirect Client Care 12%, Legal and Ethical Compliance 20%. Spanish forms
  exist. NOT modelled: this bank follows the current (2019 job analysis) outline; the landing and
  examFacts say the change is coming.
- ACA Code of Ethics (2014), American Counseling Association,
  https://www.counseling.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/ethics/2014-aca-code-of-ethics.pdf
  (PDF text re-read 2026-11-02): standards cited by number in explanations, each checked against
  the code text: A.2.b informed consent content (incl. fees and billing), A.4.b personal values,
  A.5.c former clients five years plus documented forethought, A.10.e bartering (not exploitive,
  client requests, accepted community practice, written contract), A.10.f gifts (relationship,
  value, client's and counselor's motivation), A.11.c termination, A.11.d transfer of services,
  A.12 abandonment and client neglect (was wrongly cited as A.11.a, which is competence within
  termination and referral), B.4.a group confidentiality (importance and parameters; the code
  itself does not say "cannot be guaranteed", so the explanation no longer attributes that to it),
  B.5.b parents and guardians, B.6.g disclosure or transfer of records, C.2.b new specialty areas,
  C.2.g impairment, E.5.d refraining from diagnosis, H.1.b distance counseling laws of both
  locations, H.6.a separate professional and personal virtual presence.
- APA, DSM-5-TR (2022): diagnostic durations used (MDD 2 weeks, persistent depressive disorder 2
  years in adults, manic episode 1 week, hypomanic 4 days, cyclothymic 2 years, GAD 6 months,
  panic disorder 1 month of worry, acute stress disorder 3 days to 1 month, PTSD more than 1 month,
  prolonged grief disorder 12 months for adults and 6 months for children, brief psychotic
  disorder under 1 month, schizophreniform 1 to 6 months, schizophrenia 6 months, ADHD symptoms
  before 12, separation anxiety 4 weeks in children, substance use disorder severity 2-3 / 4-5 / 6+,
  gambling disorder as the only non-substance addiction in the main text, Cultural Formulation
  Interview, ataque de nervios as a cultural concept of distress).
- Standard counseling theory (Rogers, Ellis, Beck, Adler, Glasser, de Shazer and Berg, White and
  Epston, Linehan, ACT, Miller and Rollnick, Bowen, Minuchin, Haley, Satir), group work (Tuckman,
  Corey stages, Yalom therapeutic factors, Lewin leadership styles, ASGW Best Practice Guidelines
  on screening), development (Erikson, Piaget, Kohlberg, Gilligan, Ainsworth, Marcia), career
  (Holland, Super, Krumboltz, Gottfredson, Parsons, Schlossberg, SCCT), multicultural (Berry,
  Cross, Multicultural and Social Justice Counseling Competencies 2015, broaching), grief (Worden,
  Doka's disenfranchised grief, Walker's cycle of violence), Carkhuff's empathy scale and the
  Stanley-Brown safety planning approach.

Exam format facts (question counts, time, scoring, the 2027 change) are for the landing, FAQ and
`lib/examFacts.ts` only, never the bank. NBCC publishes no percentage pass mark; 70% here is
Tigertest's readiness target. All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the outline
above; none is copied from NBCC sample items, a past form or any commercial prep product.
Checked against the ASWB draft bank (tools/next100/aswb) built in the same batch: items sharing a
fact and answer (Berry integration, Rogers congruence, self-disclosure, mandated reporting) were
rewritten to test a different point, and the OARS item (also in the NASM bank) was replaced.
