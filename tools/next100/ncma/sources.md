# NCMA (NCCT) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from NCCT's official detailed test plan and
the primary laws and standards below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from NCCT practice
tests, NCCT review material, released items or any commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (National Center for Competency Testing)

- NCCT, National Certified Medical Assistant (NCMA) Detailed Test Plan, Effective January 2024
  (EX-0508, 4 pages), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ncctinc.com/Documents/MA%20Detailed%20Test%20Plan.pdf
  It is the plan NCCT links as "Medical Assistant Detailed Test Plan" on its forms page in
  November 2026. 125 scored items and 25 unscored pretest items, three hours; 92% standard
  four-option multiple choice and 8% alternative items (drag and drop, multi-select, hotspot).
  Content categories and scored items: 1 Pharmacology and General Medical Knowledge 18;
  2 Clinical Medical Procedures 72 (A Infection Control and Safety 15, B Patient Intake and Care
  32, C Phlebotomy 15, D ECG 10); 3 Medical Administrative Duties 15; 4 Law and Ethics 20. The
  task statements of each category, and the 30-item Essential Knowledge Base, were used
  category by category.
- NCCT, Forms and Documents page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ncctinc.com/forms-and-documents
  Lists the NCMA eligibility criteria, the detailed test plan, the comparison to the previous
  plan, the job analysis executive summary and the Candidate Handbook.

## Format (NCCT Candidate Handbook, revised January 2026)

- NCCT Candidate Handbook, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.ncctinc.com/Documents/CandidateHandbook.pdf
  NCMA: 3 hours, computer based, four-option multiple choice and innovative item types,
  125 scored and 25 pretest items. Offered in English only. Scores are reported from 200 to 720
  with every pass point scaled to 575, set by a criterion-referenced (panel) standard; results
  are shown right after the exam. Testing at an NCCT test site or by remote proctoring (ExamRoom
  system check, camera and microphone on throughout). Re-examination: 30 days after a first and
  second failure, 90 days after a third, then 180 days with an appeal and proof of remediation.
  Certification is renewed every year with continuing education. NCMA eligibility: a current
  student or a graduate within five years of an NCCT-authorized medical assistant program
  (Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeships included), one year of verifiable full-time
  work as a medical assistant, military training, or the instructor pathway. Fees are never stated by
  Tigertest.

## What could not be confirmed and how the copy words it

- eCFR (www.ecfr.gov) redirected automated requests to an unblock page on 2026-11-15, so the
  CFR sections above were read on Cornell LII's copies of the current text.
- NCCT publishes no percent passing mark; the 70% line in Tigertest's tests is a readiness
  target, and the copy says the real pass point is a scaled 575.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test, scaled from the test plan's 18/15/32/15/10/15/20 of 125: Pharmacology
  and General Medical Knowledge 7, Infection Control and Safety 6, Patient Intake and Care 13,
  Phlebotomy 6, ECG 4, Medical Administrative Duties 6, Law and Ethics 8. The rounding is
  Tigertest's.
- Alternative item types (drag and drop, multi-select, hotspot) are written as single-answer
  items; ECG strips and specimens are described in words.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees, penalty amounts or state-specific rules
  (scope of practice, minors' consent and limitation periods are "vary by state").
- Duplicates: written against data/ccma-questions.json, data/cma-questions.json,
  data/rma-questions.json and data/cmaa-questions.json (and scanned against the phlebotomy,
  cet, cna, rpt and ascp-pbt banks) for repeated stems and fact-and-answer pairs; matches
  (mitral valve, red marrow, prn, infant vastus lateralis, mastectomy arm, statute of
  limitations, sterile field border, speaking to a patient with an intellectual disability)
  were rewritten.
