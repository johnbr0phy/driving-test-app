## Modelling choices (Tigertest's own)
- Each mock is about 40 percent of the real length at the real pace: Mathematics 48 in 60 minutes (75 seconds a
  question, as 120 in 150), GAT 60 in 60 minutes (a minute a question, as 150 in 150), English 20 and GK 40 (the real
  1 : 2 split).
- Maths split per test: algebra and matrices 14, trigonometry and geometry 12, calculus and vectors 13, statistics and
  probability 9 (Tigertest's estimate of recent papers; UPSC publishes no topic weights for Paper I).
- GK per test: physics 10, chemistry and general science 10, history 10, geography 10. Current events (about 10
  percent) are not modelled, since they date within a year.
- Score is percent correct; negative marking is explained, not applied.
