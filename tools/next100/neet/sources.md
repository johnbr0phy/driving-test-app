# NEET (UG) sources

Checked 1 November 2026. All official documents were downloaded from the links on https://neet.nta.nic.in/
(the NTA NEET site, read on 1 November 2026); the PDFs are hosted on cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in.

## Official
- National Testing Agency, Information Bulletin for NEET (UG)-2026:
  https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/02/202602231394640855.pdf
  (read in full text on 1 November 2026). Confirmed from it:
  - Pattern of the test (chapter 3, section 2, "As per Letter CDN-20011/289/2024-Cordination-NMC dated 05.12.2025"):
    Physics, Chemistry, Biology (Botany and Zoology); "180 compulsory questions which must be attempted by the
    candidates in 180 minutes"; Physics 45 questions 180 marks, Chemistry 45 questions 180 marks, Biology 90 questions
    360 marks, total 180 questions 720 marks; MCQ with four options and a single correct answer or best option; 02:00
    PM to 05:00 PM. (So no optional Section B, which earlier years had.)
  - Marking: correct or most appropriate answer +4; any incorrect option marked -1; unanswered 0. Rules for questions
    with more than one correct option or dropped questions.
  - Mode: single day, single shift, pen and paper, "as was conducted in 2025". Duration three hours (compensatory time
    of one hour for eligible PwD/PwBD candidates).
  - Medium: 13 languages (Assamese, Bengali, English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi,
    Tamil, Telugu, Urdu); Hindi and regional-language candidates get a bilingual booklet with English.
  - Original date of examination: 03 May 2026 (Sunday).
  - Qualifying criteria (chapter 8): minimum marks at the 50th percentile for General and General-EWS, 40th
    percentile for SC, ST and OBC; for candidates with benchmark disabilities, 45th percentile (UR and GEN-EWS) and
    40th (SC, ST, OBC-NCL); the Central Government may lower these.
  - Re-read by the reviewer on 1 November 2026 (PDF downloaded and converted to text): the 180 / 720 pattern, +4 marking,
    13 languages, 3 May 2026 date and the 50th / 40th / 45th percentile rules confirmed. The bulletin's list of prohibited
    items includes calculators and log tables, which supports "No calculator, as in the real paper" in the Physics
    directions.
- NMC, Syllabus for NEET (UG)-2026 Examination (listed on the NTA site as "Syllabus for NEET (UG)-2026
  Examination"): https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/01/202601081066816297.pdf
  Physics units 1 to 20, Chemistry units 1 to 20 (physical, inorganic, organic, practical), Biology units 1 to 10.
  The domain split of this bank follows these units (see the header comment of each source file).
- NTA Public Notice, 15 May 2026, "Conduct of Re-examination for NEET (UG) 2026":
  https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/05/20260515960654684.pdf
  (scanned; read as an image). Re-examination on 21 June 2026 (Sunday), 02:00 PM to 05:15 PM IST including time for
  examination formalities, pen and paper, 13 languages.
- NTA Public Notice, 12 June 2026, "Student-Friendly Measures Introduced for NEET (UG)-2026 Examination":
  https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/06/202606121235115637.pdf
  (scanned; read as an image). The examination window was extended to 195 minutes, 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM, including
  formalities such as signing attendance sheets; four rough-work pages instead of two.
- NTA Press Release, 16 July 2026, result of NEET (UG) 2026:
  https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/07/20260716477215762.pdf
  (exam held 21 June 2026; marks out of 720; held in 13 languages). Used only to confirm the re-examination was held
  and scored out of 720; no candidate figures are used anywhere.
- The NTA site lists the 3 May 2026 paper as cancelled (press releases of 10 and 12 May 2026, and a fee refund for that
  sitting). Why it was cancelled is not used in any copy.

## Not confirmed / left out
- NEET (UG) 2027: no information bulletin had been published on neet.nta.nic.in as of 1 November 2026. All copy
  describes the 2026 pattern and dates it ("per the NEET (UG) 2026 information bulletin").
- Cut-off marks, number of candidates, seats and fees are not stated anywhere in the exam's copy.

## Tigertest modelling choices (not official)
- Mock tests are 100 questions (Physics 25, Chemistry 25, Biology 50, the real 1 : 1 : 2 split) in 100 minutes, about
  a minute a question like the real 180 in 180. The real paper has one clock and no subject sections; the 30 / 25 / 45
  minute split is Tigertest's.
- Score is percent correct; +4/-1 marking is explained, not applied.
- Original questions only, written to the NCERT class 11 and 12 content named in the NMC syllabus. No past NEET
  paper, NTA sample or coaching-app item was used. Every numeric answer is computed by an assert in the source file.
