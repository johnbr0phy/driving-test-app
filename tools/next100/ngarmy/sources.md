# Sources for `ngarmy` (Nigerian Army recruitment aptitude test)

All 200 questions are original. Past-question packs and practice apps turned up in search results
were not opened or copied. Checked 2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Format of the real test (what could and could not be confirmed)
- The official portal, recruitment.army.mil.ng, could not be reached from this session (DNS blocked),
  so the Army's own notices were read through news reports and job boards that reprint them.
- 91 Regular Recruits Intake (91RRI), 2026: applications 30 March to 17 May 2026; shortlisted
  candidates screened in their state of origin, 9 to 23 June 2026; "there will be no pre-selection
  Computer Based Test". Screening is described as physical assessment, credential verification and
  medical examination; at least one state's screening ran into August 2026.
  - https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/07/14/nigerian-army-recruitment-2026-application-portal-requirements-and-what-to-expect/
  - https://www.hotnigerianjobs.com/print/873959/ (eligibility as reprinted: single, Nigerian by birth,
    NIN and BVN, at least 4 passes (not credits, as the listing words it) including English in WASSCE/GCE/NECO/NABTEB/NBAIS in at most two
    sittings, state of origin, height 1.68 m men and 1.65 m women)
  - https://jobnow.ng/nigerian-army-nationwide-massive-recruitment-for-trades-non-tradesmen-women-91rri-2026/
  - https://radionigeriasouthsouth.gov.ng/delta-rallies-563-army-recruits-urges-shortlisted-candidates-to-report-for-screening/ (25 August 2026)
- 90RRI listings (screening reported for 4 to 18 December 2025) also said no pre-selection CBT and
  no zonal screening, all screening in the state of origin. The 89RRI announcement (Nairametrics,
  2 April 2025) said the opposite: a "pre-selection computer-based test at designated examination
  centers across Nigeria", then screening at the recruitment depots, 2 to 30 June 2025. Rechecked
  2026-11-01 by the reviewer; 88RRI not checked. So the copy says "90th and 91st", not "88th to 91st".
  - https://www.nigeriastartupact.ng/?p=11731 (90RRI)
  - https://nairametrics.com/2025/04/02/nigerian-army-announces-recruitment-exercise-for-89-regular-recruits-intake-in-nigeria
  - https://www.jobzilla.ng/jobs/nigerian-army-recruitment-for-trades-non-tradesmen-women-90-regular-recruits-intake-265226
- Short Service Combatant Course 49/2026: applications 9 October to 12 November 2025; shortlisted
  candidates went before a selection board at NDA Ribadu Campus (Old Site), Kaduna, 1 to 19 December
  2025. Nairametrics (24 November 2025) says the exercise includes "verification of documents,
  physical, medical, and academic tests" and "an oral interview"; no report gives the content of
  the academic tests. No source found for the builder's claim that the SSC 49/2026 notice said "no
  pre-selection CBT", so that claim was removed (reviewer, 2026-11-01).
  - https://nairametrics.com/2025/11/24/nigerian-army-releases-list-of-successful-sscc-course-49-2026-candidates
- Earlier officer intakes did use a written CBT: in 2020 the Army shortlisted 4,302 candidates for a
  "pre-selection" computer-based test for SSC and DSSC on 14 November 2020 at six zonal centres
  (Premium Times / Gazette, 10 November 2020). Subjects not given.
  - https://gazettengr.com/full-document-nigerian-army-shortlists-4302-candidates-for-pre-selection-examinations/
  - https://primeprogressng.com/opportunities/nigerian-army-releases-shortlist-for-sscc-course-49-2026-sets-selection-timetable/
  - https://nairametrics.com/2025/10/12/how-to-apply-for-nigerian-army-recruitment-for-regular-and-short-service-intake
- DSSC 29/2026: applications 7 January to 4 February 2026 (graduates, second class upper or upper
  credit HND, NYSC); selection board 23 February to 8 March 2026; successful candidates reported to
  Jaji on 27 March 2026.
  - https://nairametrics.com/2026/01/06/nigerian-army-opens-dssc-29-2026-officers-recruitment-from-january-7-2026
  - https://nairametrics.com/2026/03/13/nigerian-army-releases-list-of-candidates-for-dssc-29-2026-training/
- A general guide says officer routes (SSC/DSSC) include "a written examination at designated
  centres before the final selection stage" (withinnigeria.com above). Prep sites describe the Army
  aptitude test as English, mathematics/quantitative reasoning and general knowledge/current affairs,
  with "about 100 questions" labelled by one site as unofficial. NOT confirmed by the Army.
- Not confirmed anywhere: question count, time, subject split, pass mark, negative marking.
  Tigertest models four 50-question tests at a 70% target and says so.

## Content
English and mathematics items are general secondary-school level (WAEC credit level), British
spelling as used in Nigeria, naira. GK lane (BRIEF batch 5): Nigerian history to independence,
the armed forces, Africa/ECOWAS/AU. Static facts only; no office-holders.
- Nigerian Army history: Glover Hausas 1863 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glover_Hausas,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Army); NADCEL on 6 July marks the first shot of the civil
  war at Garkem, 1967 (https://von.gov.ng/army-to-mark-162-years-of-service-coas/,
  https://prnigeria.com/?p=165642); Armed Forces Remembrance Day 15 January; Depot NA Zaria; NDA
  Kaduna, 1964; Nigerian Naval Force 1956; Nigerian Air Force 1964; Royal West African Frontier
  Force and the 81st and 82nd (West Africa) Divisions in Burma.
- Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999: section 217 (armed forces), section 218
  and the President as Commander-in-Chief, section 214 (Nigeria Police Force).
  https://www.nigeria-law.org/ConstitutionOfTheFederalRepublicOfNigeria.htm
- Rank equivalents (Army, Navy, Air Force) and unit sizes: standard Commonwealth structure as used
  by the Nigerian Armed Forces (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_ranks_of_Nigeria).
- Pre-colonial and colonial history (Nok, Igbo-Ukwu, Sokoto Caliphate, Oyo, Kanem-Bornu, Aro, Jaja,
  Lagos 1861, Benin 1897, Flora Shaw, 1914 amalgamation, constitutions of 1922, 1946, 1951, 1954,
  NNDP 1923, Aba Women's War 1929, Enahoro motion 1953, self-government 1957/1959, independence
  1960, republic 1963, Akinkunmi's flag, Iwe Irohin 1859): standard school history, checked
  against the Wikipedia articles "History of Nigeria", "Sokoto Caliphate", "Oyo Empire",
  "Amalgamation of Nigeria", "Aba Women's riots", "Flag of Nigeria", "Iwe Irohin".
- ECOWAS: Treaty of Lagos 1975, Commission in Abuja, ECOMOG in Liberia 1990, ECOMIG in The Gambia
  2017, EBID in Lomé (https://www.ecowas.int, https://www.bidc-ebid.org). Mali, Burkina Faso and
  Niger left ECOWAS in January 2025 (Alliance of Sahel States).
- African Union: OAU 25 May 1963 Addis Ababa, AU launched 2002, Pan-African Parliament in Midrand,
  Peace and Security Council, Agenda 2063 (https://au.int). AfDB in Abidjan (https://www.afdb.org).
  MNJTF headquarters N'Djamena. UNAMID 2007 to 2020 (https://unamid.unmissions.org).
