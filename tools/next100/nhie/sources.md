# Sources for `nhie` (National Home Inspector Examination, US, v1)

All 200 questions are original, written from the EBPHI content outline and the codes it lists as
references. No EBPHI practice quiz item, manual question, past item or commercial prep question was
copied, and no prep provider is named. Checked 2026-11-03.

## Exam format (official, Examination Board of Professional Home Inspectors)
- homeinspectionexam.org now 301-redirects to https://nationalhomeinspectorexam.org/ (checked
  2026-11-03; the site answers curl but returns 403 to some fetchers).
- EBPHI, Frequently Asked Questions:
  https://nationalhomeinspectorexam.org/frequently-asked-questions-2/ (checked 2026-11-03):
  "200 multiple-choice questions. Of the 200 questions on the exam, 25 of those are pretest
  questions that are not scored. You are given four hours to complete the exam." Scale-scored
  200 to 800, 500 is the pass point, set by criterion-referenced standard setting (no fixed
  percent). Administered at more than 300 test centers in the US and Canada; candidates seeking
  licensing in Florida, Illinois, Nevada, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Tennessee or Washington must take
  it through those states' contracted administrators. "The NHIE is used in 35 states and several
  Canadian provinces for home inspector regulation." Accredited (EBPHI accreditation letter
  2023-12-12). The page lists a fee; deliberately not used in copy. References named: EBPHI
  Mechanical Systems & NHIE Content Manual (2019), Structural Systems & Business Manual (2019),
  IRC 2021, NFPA 101 Life Safety Code Handbook 2021, NFPA 70 NEC 2023.
- EBPHI, Register for the Exam:
  https://nationalhomeinspectorexam.org/register-for-the-exam/ (checked 2026-11-03): PSI in most
  states and all of Canada; Pearson VUE in Florida, Nevada and Texas.
- EBPHI, "Overview: Policies, Procedures, Content" (the content outline PDF linked as "Content
  Outline" from the Prepare page): https://nationalhomeinspectorexam.org/download/22502/
  (PDF footer "2023 Examination Board of Professional Home Inspectors", file created 2024-09-13,
  checked 2026-11-03). Four hours; scaled 200 to 800, passing 500. Content outline from the role
  delineation study:
  - Domain 1 Property and Building Inspection/Site Review 70%: Task 1 site 5%, Task 2 exterior 5%,
    Task 3 roof 6%, Task 4 structure 6%, Task 5 electrical 7%, Task 6 cooling 4%, Task 7 heating 5%,
    Task 8 insulation, moisture management and ventilation 5%, Task 9 mechanical exhaust 5%,
    Task 10 plumbing and fuel distribution 6%, Task 11 interior 4%, Task 12 fireplaces, fuel-burning
    appliances, chimneys and vents 6%, Task 13 life safety 6%.
  - Domain 2 Analysis of Findings and Reporting 20%: Task 1 describe what was inspected and methods
    4%, Task 2 limitations 4%, Task 3 defective systems 6%, Task 4 further evaluation 6%.
  - Domain 3 Professional Responsibilities 10%: Task 1 pre-inspection agreement 5%, Task 2 quality,
    integrity and objectivity 5%.
- EBPHI, Role Delineation Study summary (2022):
  https://nationalhomeinspectorexam.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/EBPHI-RDS-Summary-Report-2022.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-03): three domains kept; 19 tasks, 253 knowledge statements; new life safety
  task; alternative energy added.
- No newer outline was found on EBPHI's site as of 2026-11-03. One prep site quotes 63/25/12
  domain weights; it does not match EBPHI's own outline and is not used.

## Blueprint (Tigertest)
EBPHI 70/20/10 scaled to 50 per mock test: Domain 1 35 (site, exterior and roof 8; structure and
interior 5; electrical 4; heating and cooling 4; insulation, moisture and exhaust 5; plumbing and
fuel 3; fireplaces and life safety 6), Domain 2 10 (methods and limitations 4; defects, service life
and referrals 6), Domain 3 5. Four 50-question mocks of a 200-item, 4-hour exam (about 1.2 minutes
per item). Pass line 70% is Tigertest's readiness target: the real exam is scaled, 500 of 800, with
no published percent.

## Rules and facts used (codes the outline names)
- IRC 2021 (https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2021P2), section numbers given for the integrator's reference only
  (the bank cites the IRC by name, never by section; the builder wrote these from memory, and the reviewer re-checked each figure on 2026-11-03 against published summaries of the 2021 text, e.g. jaspector.com IRC 2021 pages and ICC CodeNotes: garage door self-closing R302.5.1, AAV at least 4 in above the drain vented P3114, stair-side guard triangle 6 in and infill 4 3/8 in R312.1.3): grade fall 6 in within 10 ft (R401.3); wood clearance to earth, 18 in joists / 12 in girders
  in crawl spaces (R317.1); anchor bolts 6 ft (R403.1.6); joist notches and holes (R502.8.1); stud
  notching 25% bearing / 40% non-bearing, boring 40% / 60% (R602.6); guards over 30 in, 4 in sphere,
  4 3/8 in at stair sides (R312.1); stairs 7 3/4 in riser, 10 in tread, 3/8 in variation (R311.7.5),
  handrails 34 to 38 in (R311.7.8.1); safety glazing 24 in arc and 60 in (R308.4.2); emergency
  escape 5.7 / 5.0 sq ft, 44 in sill, release of bars without key (R310); smoke alarms (R314.3),
  CO alarms (R315.2); garage separation 1/2 in gypsum, 5/8 in Type X under habitable rooms (Table
  R302.6), door 1 3/8 in solid wood or steel or 20-minute rated, self-closing (R302.5.1), ducts 26
  gauge steel with no garage openings (R302.5.2); roof slope 2:12 and double underlayment to 4:12
  (R905.1.1, R905.2.2), drip edge at eaves and rakes (R905.2.8.5), step and kickout flashing
  (R903.2.1, R905.2.8.3), re-cover limits (R908.3.1.1), crickets over 30 in (R903.2.2); attic
  ventilation 1/150 (R806.2); crawl space vents 1/150, 1/1,500 with Class I retarder (R408.2);
  dryer ducts 35 ft, no screws, backdraft damper, no screen (M1502); make-up air over 400 cfm
  (M1503.6); return air not from bathrooms, kitchens, garages (M1602.2); water pressure over 80 psi
  (P2903.3.1); TPR discharge (P2804.6.1); shower valves 120 F (P2708.4); drain slope (P3005.3); AAVs
  (P3114); hearth extensions 16/8 and 20/12 in (R1001.10); chimney 3-2-10 (R1003.9); masonry
  chimney clearance 2 in / 1 in (R1003.18); decorative gas logs, damper blocked open (G2432);
  vent connector clearances and 1/4 in per ft rise (G2427).
- NFPA 70 NEC 2023: working space 36 in deep, 30 in wide (110.26); service drop clearances 10/12/18
  ft (230.24(B)); overcurrent devices not in clothes closets or bathrooms (240.24(D),(E)); small
  conductor rule 15/20/30 A (240.4(D)); GFCI replacement receptacle "No Equipment Ground"
  (406.4(D)(2)); non-IC recessed luminaire 3 in (410.116(B)); PV backfed breaker at the opposite end
  of the bus (705.12(B)(3)); neutral-to-ground bond only at the service (250.24, 250.142).
- Pool barriers: ISPSC, adopted through IRC Appendix: 48 in barrier, self-closing and self-latching
  gates opening away from the pool, release on the pool side when below 54 in.
- Garage door openers: UL 325 entrapment protection (1 1/2 in obstruction, photo eyes no higher than
  6 in).
- Known problem panels (Federal Pacific Stab-Lok, Zinsco) and aluminum branch wiring are named in
  the EBPHI outline; repair by listed connectors per the CPSC aluminum wiring guidance.
- Service life items stay comparative (which lasts longest or shortest), with no year figures.
- Left out on purpose: exam fees, state licensing rules (pre-licensing hours, insurance minimums,
  report retention, statutes of limitation), which vary by state, and anything about the test
  itself.
