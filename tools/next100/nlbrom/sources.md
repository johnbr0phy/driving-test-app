# Sources for `nlbrom` (Netherlands, CBR theorie-examen bromfiets, rijbewijs AM, in Dutch)

All 200 questions are original and were written directly in Dutch from the law and the official
pages below. No CBR exam question or example question (voorbeeldvragen), no question from a
commercial theory book, app, course or mock exam (ANWB, iTheorie, theory.nl or others) and no
practice-site item was copied or reworded. Signs and situations are described in words (no
images), with the BABW code where it helps. Checked 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Format of the real exam (official, CBR)
- CBR, "Hoe gaat het theorie-examen bromfiets?" (fetched 2026-11-22):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/bromfiets/theorie-examen-bromfiets/hoe-gaat-het-theorie-examen-bromfiets
  - 50 exam questions plus 2 test questions that do not count (52 answered), after 2 practice
    questions that do not count either.
  - "Je moet minimaal 43 van de 50 examenvragen goed beantwoord hebben om te slagen voor je
    theorie-examen." (43 of 50 = 86%.)
  - 30 minutes for the ordinary exam; 45 minutes for the exam with extra time and the
    individually guided exam. Answered on a computer; result straight after the exam.
  - Bring a valid ID and the booking number; arrive a quarter of an hour early.
- CBR, "Theorie-examenplan bromfiets" (fetched 2026-11-22):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/service/nl/nl/theorie-examenplan-bromfiets
  - 50 scored questions plus 2 unscored test questions; 43 of 50 to pass.
  - Eight subjects (Ministeriële regeling eisen theorie-examen AM) with knowledge level:
    1 Gebruik van de weg (K+), 2 Voorrang en voor laten gaan (K+), 3 Bijzondere wegen,
    weggedeelten, weggebruikers en manoeuvres (K+), 4 Veilig rijden met het voertuig en reageren
    in noodsituaties (K+), 5 Verkeerstekens en aanwijzingen (K+), 6 Verantwoorde
    verkeersdeelname en milieubewust rijden (K+), 7 Wetgeving (K), 8 Voertuigkennis (K). No
    number of questions per subject is published, so Tigertest's weights are its own.
  - Chapter 3 word list (dode hoek, opgevoerde bromfiets, speed-pedelec, vizier, teerstrepen,
    los-liggende stenen, WA-verzekering, beginnend bestuurder, LEMA/EMA/EMG and others) used to
    choose topics; chapter 4 terms not translated: APK, autoweg, brommobiel, erf, snorfiets,
    speed pedelec.
- CBR, "Theorie-examen bromfiets" (fetched 2026-11-22): theory exam from 15.5 years; it shows
  you know the traffic rules and have traffic insight; it comes before the practical exam.
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/bromfiets/theorie-examen-bromfiets
- CBR, "Welk theorie-examen past bij jou?" (bromfiets, fetched 2026-11-22): ordinary exam
  (touch screen, 30 minutes); exam with extra time (15 minutes more, optional read-aloud by a
  computer voice); exam with an approved interpreter for another language, booked at least 14
  days ahead; individually guided exam (45 minutes); sign-language interpreter via the guided
  exam. No separate English-language moped exam is listed, so the copy says "in Dutch, or with
  an approved interpreter".
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/bromfiets/theorie-examen-bromfiets/welk-theorie-examen-past-bij-jou
- CBR, "Leren en oefenen" (bromfiets, fetched 2026-11-22): points to the subjects page and the
  exam plan; links to Rijksoverheid for traffic rules and signs.
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/bromfiets/theorie-examen-bromfiets/leren-en-oefenen

## Rules (primary law and official pages)
- Reglement verkeersregels en verkeerstekens 1990 (RVV 1990), version from 1 July 2026,
  https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004825/2026-07-01 (fetched 2026-11-22): art. 1 definitions
  (snorfiets 25 km/u by construction, speed pedelec an electric moped with pedal assistance above
  25 km/u, brommobiel a moped on more than two wheels with a body, brombakfiets); 2a car rules
  for brommobielen; 2b cycle rules for snorfietsen; 3 keep right, cyclists two abreast but not
  snorfietsers; 5 cycle paths (snorfiets with a combustion engine on the optional cycle path
  only with the engine off; snorfiets to the carriageway by verkeersbesluit); 6 bromfietsers use
  the fiets/bromfietspad, the carriageway when there is none, and the carriageway when on more
  than two wheels or with a trailer wider than 0.75 m; 11 overtaking on the left, only cyclists
  may overtake others on the right; 15 priority to the right, unpaved yields to paved, trams
  first; 18 turning traffic gives way; 19 stop within the distance you can see; 20-22 speeds
  (bromfiets 30 on the fiets/bromfietspad and 45 on the carriageway inside built-up areas, 40
  and 45 outside; brommobiel 45; snorfiets 25; 6 km/u on a pavement for permitted snorfietsen);
  23 no stopping (fietsstrook, solid yellow line); 27 bicycles and mopeds placed on the pavement,
  footpath or verge; 32 dipped beam at night and in seriously reduced visibility; 42-43
  motorways (60 km/u) and autowegen (50 km/u), no turning or reversing; 44-45 erf; 48 overtaking
  on the right on roundabouts; 49 pedestrians on zebras, white stick with red rings; 50 priority
  vehicles; 52 boarding passengers at trams and buses; 54 special manoeuvres give way; 55
  signalling; 56 buses leaving stops in built-up areas; 57 no unnecessary noise (also
  bromfietsers and snorfietsers); 58a children under 8 on a safe seat, a cyclist (not a
  snorfietser) may carry one person of 8 or older on the carrier; 60 helmet for driver and
  passengers of bromfietsen and snorfietsen, not on a brombakfiets; 61a no holding a mobile
  device; 61b no persons in a load space; 65 E signs; 68 lights with a bicycle symbol apply to
  bromfietsers on the fiets/bromfietspad, the sign "rechtsaf voor (brom)fietsers vrij".
  Article numbers for the hierarchy of directions, lights, signs and rules (62 to 64), temporary
  markings (63a), the lowest limit (63b) and military convoys and funeral processions (16) follow
  the nltheorie builder's reading of the same text on 2026-11-21.
- Wegenverkeerswet 1994, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0006622 (as read by the nltheorie
  builder 2026-11-21; relied on here): art. 7 do not leave the scene of a crash; art. 8 alcohol
  0.5 per mille (220 micrograms per litre of breath), 0.2 for novice drivers, novices being seven
  years from an AM licence issued under 18.
- Gemeenteblad Beverwijk 2026, 22362 (verkeersbesluit, found 2026-11-22): sign C13 "gesloten
  voor bromfietsen, snorfietsen en gehandicaptenvoertuigen, met in werking zijnde motor"; G12a is
  the compulsory cycle/moped path; mopeds are not allowed on a G11 cycle path.
  https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/gmb-2026-22362.html
- Rijksoverheid, "Voor welke soorten bromfietsen heb ik een bromfietsrijbewijs nodig?" (fetched
  2026-11-22): AM for bromfiets, scooter, speed pedelec, snorfiets and brommobiel; theory from
  15.5, lessons and practical exam from 16; AM4 for a brommobiel only; AM added automatically with
  B or A, A1, A2.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets/vraag-en-antwoord/rijbewijs/voor-welke-soorten-bromfietsen-heb-ik-een-bromfietsrijbewijs-brommerrijbewijs-nodig
- Rijksoverheid, "Regels voor snorfietsen" (fetched 2026-11-22): at least 16, AM licence, WA
  insurance, approved helmet, ride on the cycle path, carry the registration document; blue plate,
  mirror, VIN, lights and reflectors; no holding a phone; no riding under the influence of
  alcohol, drugs or some medicines; Amsterdam and Utrecht have their own carriageway rules (not
  used).
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets
- Rijksoverheid, "Helmplicht brommer, snorfiets, speedpedelec" (fetched 2026-11-22): all drivers
  and passengers wear a helmet; a brommer needs an approved moped helmet (a speed pedelec helmet
  is not allowed); on a snorfiets and speed pedelec a moped helmet or an NTA 8776:2016 speed
  pedelec helmet; E mark on the chin-strap label means ECE 22.05 or 22.06; E4 is the
  Netherlands. Snorfiets helmet duty from 1 January 2023 (Staatsblad 2022, 270).
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets/vraag-en-antwoord/verkeersveiligheid/helmplicht-brommer-snorfiets-speedpedelec
- Rijksoverheid, "Moet ik een kenteken voor mijn bromfiets hebben?" (fetched 2026-11-22): yellow
  plate for 45 km/u mopeds, blue for 25 km/u snorfietsen; RDW registers plates and documents.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets/vraag-en-antwoord/voertuigen-op-de-weg/moet-ik-een-kenteken-voor-mijn-bromfiets-brommer-hebben
- Rijksoverheid, "Verlichting en reflectie op mijn bromfiets" (fetched 2026-11-22): front one
  white or yellow dipped beam and a white reflector (white daytime running light allowed); rear a
  red light and red reflector; brake light above 25 km/u and 0.5 kW (from 2007); two amber
  indicators front and rear and hazard lights; amber side reflector; four amber pedal reflectors
  on non-folding pedals.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets/vraag-en-antwoord/voertuigen-op-de-weg/wat-zijn-de-regels-voor-de-verlichting-en-reflectie-op-mijn-bromfiets-brommer
- Rijksoverheid, "Moet mijn kind achterop in een fietsstoeltje?" (fetched 2026-11-22): under 8 a
  safe seat with support for back, hands and feet, footrests and spoke guards; a child on a
  bromfiets wears an ECE helmet, on a snorfiets a helmet too.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets/vraag-en-antwoord/voertuigen-op-de-weg/moet-mijn-kind-achterop-een-fiets-snorfiets-of-bromfiets-in-een-fietsstoeltje
- Rijksoverheid, "Mag ik met alcohol op deelnemen aan het verkeer?" (fetched 2026-11-22): 0.5
  per mille for experienced drivers, 0.2 for beginners.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/voertuigen-op-de-weg/bromfiets/snorfiets/vraag-en-antwoord/verkeersveiligheid/mag-ik-met-alcohol-op-deelnemen-aan-het-verkeer
- Rijksoverheid, "Wat is het beginnersrijbewijs?" (fetched 2026-11-22): everyone with a first
  licence is a novice; a licence at 16 gives 7 years; for AM 7 years, or 5 when B follows at 17.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/rijbewijs/vraag-en-antwoord/wat-is-het-beginnersrijbewijs
- Rijksoverheid, "Puntenrijbewijs en beginnersrijbewijs" (fetched 2026-11-22): a serious offence
  gives a penalty point; after the second the Openbaar Ministerie reports the novice to the CBR,
  which investigates driving skill.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/rijbewijs/puntenrijbewijs-beginnersrijbewijs
- Safe-riding items (blind spot of lorries, braking with both brakes, wet leaves, tar strips,
  gravel, tram rails, reaction distance arithmetic, first aid, 112 and 0900-8844) are general road
  safety from the exam plan's word list and the RVV duty of care, stated without figures beyond
  arithmetic.

## Not used, on purpose
- Fees, surcharges, fines (the helmet page names one) and penalty amounts.
- Any question about the exam itself (count, time, pass mark, language, booking, validity, the
  age to sit the theory exam).
- Picture, drag, hotspot and number-entry formats; the landing says so.
- City rules (Amsterdam and Utrecht snorfiets rules, zero-emission zones), speed pedelec details
  beyond its legal class and helmet, tyre-tread minimums, passenger seat rules for a duo seat
  (no official page found), and the exceptions in RVV art. 61b.
- Car-only content and the facts and answers in `nltheorie` (English, category B): the bank
  takes the rider's view (where a moped rides, moped speeds, snorfiets and brommobiel rules,
  helmets, AM licence and moped equipment). Kept clear of `bedriving` (Belgian rules).
