# Sources for `nlmoto` (Netherlands, CBR motorcycle theory exam, rijbewijs A1, A2 and A, in Dutch)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Dutch. No CBR exam or example question, no
question from a commercial theory book, app or course (ANWB, iTheorie, theory.nl or others), and
no practice-site item was copied or reworded. Signs and situations are described in words (no
images). Questions were written from the RVV 1990, the Wegenverkeerswet 1994, RDW and
Rijksoverheid pages, the CBR theorie-examenplan motor and the Motorplatform code it cites.
Checked 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Exam format (official, CBR)
- CBR, "Hoe gaat het theorie-examen motor?" (fetched 2026-11-22):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/motor/theorie-examen-motor/hoe-gaat-het-theorie-examen-motor
  - "Het theorie-examen bestaat uit 50 vragen." Plus 2 test questions that do not count ("In het
    examen zitten ook 2 testvragen, deze tellen niet mee voor de uitslag"), after 2 practice
    questions before the exam starts.
  - "Je moet minimaal 41 van de 50 examenvragen goed beantwoord hebben om te slagen" (82%).
  - "Voor het gewone theorie-examen heb je 30 minuten de tijd"; 45 minutes with extra time or
    as an individually guided exam.
  - Result straight after the exam; several question types, answered on a computer (the
    question-type page it links to is written for the car exam).
- CBR, "Theorie-examenplan motor" (fetched 2026-11-22; no version date on the page):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/service/nl/nl/theorie-examenplan-motor
  - 50 counting questions, 41 to pass, plus 2 unscored test questions; minimum age 17.
  - Legal basis: Directive 2006/126/EC, Wegenverkeerswet 1994 art. 111 lid 1b, Reglement
    rijbewijzen art. 61, Regeling eisen theorie-examen categorie A.
  - Eight subjects: 1 Gebruik van de weg (K+), 2 Voorrang en voor laten gaan (K+), 3 Bijzondere
    wegen, weggedeelten, weggebruikers en manoeuvres (K+), 4 Veilig rijden met het voertuig en
    reageren in noodsituaties (K+), 5 Verkeerstekens en aanwijzingen (K+), 6 Verantwoorde
    verkeersdeelname en milieubewust rijden (K+), 7 Wetgeving (K), 8 Voertuigkennis (K).
    No number of questions per subject is published.
  - Motorcycle items: 3.1.1 protective gear (gloves, footwear, clothing, helmet), 3.1.2
    visibility of riders to others, 3.1.3 slippery surfaces (manhole covers, road markings,
    stripes, arrows, tram rails), 3.1.4 mechanical parts (emergency stop switch, oil level,
    chain), and passenger safety (art. 3c of the regulation). These shaped the categories.
  - Chapter 6 lists its sources, among them "Motorplatform: Motorkleding", "Motorplatform:
    Samenspel in de file", "Rijprocedure A - motorfiets 2015", RVV 1990, Reglement rijbewijzen,
    Regeling voertuigen and the WAM.
- CBR, "Welk theorie-examen past bij jou?" (motor, fetched 2026-11-22):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/motor/theorie-examen-motor/welk-theorie-examen-past-bij-jou
  - Standard exam on a touchscreen, 30 minutes; extra time adds 15 minutes with optional
    read-aloud; individually guided exam 45 minutes.
  - Language: "Het theorie-examen voor de auto kun je doen in het Nederlands of Engels." For
    another language, or another vehicle such as the motorcycle, you book an exam with an
    approved interpreter at least 14 days ahead. The exam plan's chapter 5 mentions an
    English-language exam in general terms, but the motor page offers no English exam. Decision:
    the base language is Dutch (not switched to English via setrafik); the landing says "in het
    Nederlands; voor een andere taal kies je een examen met een tolk".
- CBR, "Theorie-examen motor" (fetched 2026-11-22): from age 17; the exam shows you know the
  traffic rules and have traffic insight.
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/motor/theorie-examen-motor

## Licence categories (official)
- RDW, "Rijbewijscategorieën" (fetched 2026-11-22):
  https://www.rdw.nl/het-rijbewijs/over-het-rijbewijs/rijbewijscategorieen
  - A1: from 18; up to 125 cc and 11 kW, power-to-weight at most 0,1 kW/kg; electric up to 11 kW;
    motor tricycle up to 15 kW.
  - A2: from 20; up to 35 kW and 0,2 kW/kg; not derived from a vehicle with more than double the
    power. With A1 for 2 years only a practical exam.
  - A: unlimited power; tricycles over 15 kW. Under 24 without 2 years of A2: code 80 first. The
    meaning of code 80 differs between RDW and Rijksoverheid wording, so code 80 is not asked.
- Rijksoverheid, "Wanneer mag ik rijles nemen en rijexamen doen voor mijn motorrijbewijs?"
  (fetched 2026-11-22): theory from 17; A1 practical 18; A2 practical 20; A direct practical 24
  for a licence without code 80. The tables give conflicting ages for the A paths, so only A1
  (18) is asked, and A2 (20) is on the landing from RDW.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/rijbewijs/vraag-en-antwoord/wanneer-mag-ik-rijles-nemen-en-rijexamen-doen-voor-mijn-motorrijbewijs
- Rijksoverheid, "Veilig rijden op de motor" (fetched 2026-11-22):
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/verkeersveiligheid/veilig-rijden-op-de-motor
  - "Op de motor moet u een goedgekeurde helm dragen." Protective clothing "is niet verplicht,
    maar wel belangrijk".
  - "Bij veel ongevallen ziet de automobilist de motorrijder niet."
  - "Motorrijders mogen in een file tussen de auto's doorrijden"; the Motorplatform code
    "Samenspel in de file" gives the rules.
  - "Rijden op een motor mag alleen wanneer de bestuurder een motorrijbewijs heeft." (used for
    the item that a B licence does not cover a 125 cc motorcycle in the Netherlands).
  - A motorcycle may be at most 4 metres long; "De Algemene Periodieke Keuring (APK) geldt niet
    voor de motor."

## Filtering code (Motorplatform, via ANWB)
- ANWB, "Vijf vragen over motoren in de file" (fetched 2026-11-22), quoting the code of conduct:
  speed difference "niet meer dan 10 km/uur"; the space between the leftmost lanes; on roads with
  more than two lanes between the two leftmost; "niet over vluchtstroken, verdrijvingsvlakken,
  puntstukken"; signal when merging back; several riders keep "een afstand van ongeveer twee
  auto's".
  https://www.anwb.nl/verkeer/veiligheid/vijf-vragen-over-motoren-in-de-file
  The Motorplatform's own text could not be loaded (the SWOV page that summarises it returned a
  bot check). The bank calls it "de filegedragscode" and treats it as a code of conduct, not law.

## Rules of the road (primary law)
- Reglement verkeersregels en verkeerstekens 1990 (RVV 1990), version from 1 July 2026,
  https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004825/2026-07-01 (read 2026-11-22): art. 1 (motorfiets is a
  two-wheeled motor vehicle; bestuurders are all road users except pedestrians), 3 (keep right;
  side by side only for cyclists and moped riders), 5 (cycle and moped paths), 11 (overtake on
  the left; right-hand exceptions), 12 (no overtaking just before or on a zebra crossing), 13
  (queues may be passed on the right; the right lane need not be kept in queues), 15 (priority
  to the right, unpaved yields to paved, trams), 16 (do not break a military column or funeral
  procession), 18 (turning traffic gives way to oncoming traffic and traffic beside or close
  behind; left-turners give way to oncoming right-turners), 19 (stop within the distance you can
  see), 20-22 (50 built-up; 80 / 100 autoweg / 130 motorway; art. 22 e: motorcycles with a trailer
  up to 3,500 kg 90 km/h), 23 (no stopping on a cycle lane, at a crossing and 5 m, tunnel, bus stop
  12 m, solid yellow line), 24 (no parking within 5 m of a junction and more), 32 (dipped beam
  at night and in poor visibility; no main beam when meeting others), 41 (daytime running lights
  allowed), 53 (towing gap), 54 (special manoeuvres give way), 58a (passengers on the seats meant
  for them), 60 (helmet for riders and passengers of motorcycles), 61a (no holding a mobile
  device while driving). Signs in Bijlage 1 (closure signs with car, motorcycle and car plus
  motorcycle; priority at narrow points; warning signs; zone and end signs; motorway sign; lane
  signals) are described in words.
- Wegenverkeerswet 1994, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0006622 (version 1 July 2026, as read for
  the sibling `nltheorie` on 2026-11-21 and rechecked in its sources): art. 5 (no dangerous
  behaviour), art. 8 (alcohol 0,5 promille / 220 µg/l; novice drivers 0,2 promille / 88 µg/l).
- Helmet approval: the ECE helmet regulation as required through the Regeling voertuigen;
  stated only as "ECE-keuringslabel".
- Protective clothing classes: EN 17092 (classes AAA, AA, A, B, C) under EU PPE Regulation
  2016/425, as described by the test houses Centexbel and Eurofins (found 2026-11-22):
  https://www.centexbel.be/en/node/1829 and
  https://eurofins.com/textile-leather/articles/safeguarding-bikers-navigating-eu-regulations-for-motorcyclist-protective-garments
- SWOV Feiten & Cijfers (named in the exam plan's sources): motorcyclists have a much higher
  risk of serious injury per kilometre than car drivers; stated only qualitatively.
- Riding technique (front brake share, weight transfer, ABS, countersteering, braking in a bend,
  engine braking, slow riding with clutch and rear brake): standard rider training as in CBR's
  "Rijprocedure A - motorfiets" named in the exam plan; stated qualitatively, no percentages.

- Code 80, the A age paths with conflicting figures, and the voluntary rider course the
  Rijksoverheid page announces.
- Picture-based, drag-and-drop, hotspot and number-entry formats.
- Belgian rules (kept clear of `bemoto` and `bedriving`); facts and answers already used by the
  Dutch siblings `nlbrom` and `nltheorie` (checked by stem and key similarity on 2026-11-22).

## Review pass (2026-11-22)
- Re-read cbr.nl "Hoe gaat het theorie-examen motor?" (2026-11-22): 50 questions, 2 unscored test
  questions, 2 practice questions, 30 minutes (45 with extra time or individually guided), pass 41
  of 50. Landing, FAQ and WIRING examFacts match.
- RVV 1990 (version 2026-07-01, wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004825/2026-07-01, read 2026-11-22):
  art. 3 (only cyclists side by side), 13 lid 2 (queues may be passed on the right), 15 lid 2,
  18 lid 1, 23 lid 1 b (no stopping on a fietsstrook or the carriageway beside it), 28, 54
  (wegrijden, achteruitrijden and other special manoeuvres give way), 58a, 60 (helmet for rider
  and passenger, approved), 61a, 73 (green arrow: lane open; red cross: lane closed; white arrow:
  warning of a red cross). Wegenverkeerswet 1994 art. 107 (no driving without a valid licence),
  stated from the act, used in NLMOT-VER-005.
- ANWB, "Verkeersborden voorrang en voorrangstekens" (search result, 2026-11-22): on the round
  narrow-point sign the black arrow (oncoming) has priority; on the blue square the white arrow
  (you) has priority. The old NLMOT-VRR-022 stem had the colours reversed; both narrow-point items
  were replaced anyway as duplicates of `nltheorie`.
- Daytime motorway limit: Rijksoverheid "Wat is de maximumsnelheid voor auto's op de snelweg?" and
  Rijkswaterstaat "Maximumsnelheden op auto(snel)wegen" (search results, 2026-11-22): 100 km/h from
  06.00 to 19.00 on most motorways since 2020 (Stcrt. 2019, 71032), 130 on some stretches since
  2025. Used in NLMOT-GBR-012 as "meestal".
- Motorcycle length at most 4 metres (Rijksoverheid page above), used in NLMOT-WET-010.
- 51 items rewritten in this pass to remove repeats of `nltheorie`, `nlbrom` and `bemoto` (same
  scenario and answer), one wrong sign description, and weak keys; ids, categories and count kept.
  Core riding facts shared by every motorcycle bank (front brake, countersteering, ABS, slippery
  markings, helmet for the passenger) stay, written in this bank's own scenarios.
