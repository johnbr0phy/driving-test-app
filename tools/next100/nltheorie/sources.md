# Sources for `nltheorie` (Netherlands, CBR theory exam for a category B car licence)

All 350 questions are original. No CBR exam or example question (voorbeeldvragen), no question
from a commercial theory book, app or course (in Dutch or English), and no practice-site item was
copied or reworded. Signs and situations are described in words (no images). Questions were
written from the RVV 1990, the BABW sign list as reflected in the RVV, the Wegenverkeerswet 1994
and official Rijksoverheid, RDW and CBR pages. Checked 2026-11-21 unless noted.

## Format of the real exam (official, CBR)
- CBR, "Hoe gaat het theorie-examen auto?" (fetched 2026-11-21):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/auto/theorie-examen-auto/hoe-gaat-het-theorie-examen-auto
  - "Het theorie-examen bestaat uit 50 vragen." You also answer 2 test questions that do not
    count, so 52 in all, after 2 practice questions before the exam starts.
  - "Je moet minimaal 44 van de 50 examenvragen goed beantwoord hebben" (44 of 50 = 88%).
  - 30 minutes for the ordinary exam; 45 minutes with extra time or as an individually guided exam.
  - Result straight after the exam; arrive 15 minutes early.
- CBR, "Theorie-examenplan auto" (fetched 2026-11-21):
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/service/nl/nl/theorie-examenplan-auto
  - 50 questions on traffic rules and traffic insight, 44 to pass, plus 2 unscored test questions
    the candidate cannot identify. Minimum age 16. Legal basis: EU Directive 2006/126/EC,
    Wegenverkeerswet 1994, Reglement rijbewijzen, Regeling eisen theorie-examen categorie B.
  - Eight subjects with knowledge levels: 1 Gebruik van de weg (K+), 2 Voorrang en voor laten gaan
    (K+), 3 Bijzondere wegen, weggedeelten, weggebruikers en manoeuvres (K+), 4 Veilig rijden met
    het voertuig en reageren in noodsituaties (K+), 5 Verkeerstekens en aanwijzingen (K+),
    6 Verantwoorde verkeersdeelname en milieubewust rijden (K+), 7 Wetgeving (K), 8 Voertuigkennis
    (K). No number of questions per subject is published.
  - Chapter 5 is an English word list for the English-language exam; chapter 6 lists terms that
    are not translated (APK, autoweg, brommobiel, erf, snorfiets, speed pedelec). The bank uses
    these Dutch terms with a gloss.
- CBR, "Onderwerpen in het theorie-examen" (fetched 2026-11-21): the same eight subjects.
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/auto/theorie-examen-auto/onderwerpen-in-het-theorie-examen
- CBR, "Soort vragen tijdens theorie" (fetched 2026-11-21): seven question types: drag (order
  1-2-3), single drag, number entry, hotspot, yes/no, multiple choice and multiple response. Most
  are on a picture. Tigertest models all items as text-only single-answer multiple choice.
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/auto/theorie-examen-auto/soort-vragen-tijdens-theorie
- CBR, "Welk theorie-examen past bij jou?" (fetched 2026-11-21): "Het theorie-examen voor de auto
  kun je doen in het Nederlands of Engels." The English exam is the ordinary exam in English
  (30 minutes; some Dutch terms appear, listed in chapter 5 of the exam plan). Extra time (15
  minutes more, with optional computer read-aloud) in Dutch or English; an exam with an approved
  interpreter for other languages, booked 14 days ahead; an individually guided exam (45 minutes).
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/auto/theorie-examen-auto/welk-theorie-examen-past-bij-jou
- CBR, "Theorie-examen auto" (fetched 2026-11-21): theory exam from age 16; it tests hazard
  recognition, knowledge of traffic rules and traffic insight; a health declaration follows.
  https://www.cbr.nl/nl/rijbewijs-halen/auto/theorie-examen-auto
- Staatscourant 2025, 40398 (Regeling, fetched 2026-11-21): adds article 3 sub c to the Regeling
  eisen theorie-examen rijbewijscategorie B: "de principes en werking van het elektronisch
  systeem", in force 1 April 2026. The explanatory note names driver assistance systems: adaptive
  cruise control, lane keeping assist, lane departure warning, autonomous emergency braking,
  blind spot warning, surround cameras; they assist and do not drive the car.
  https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2025-40398.html
  The research note said ADAS content changes "from 1 Oct 2026"; the official regulation gives
  1 April 2026, so that date is used. ADAS items are in the vehicle category.

## Format facts from press and guides (labelled, not official)
- The combined 50-question exam replaced the three-part 65-question exam (hazard recognition,
  knowledge, insight) on 7 April 2025; hazard recognition with photos was replaced by animations:
  AutoRAI, https://autorai.nl/vernieuwd-theorie-examen-b-rijbewijs-7-april-2025 and Autozine,
  https://www.autozine.nl/special/adas-bij-theorie-examen (both fetched or found 2026-11-21).
  The landing says "since April 2025" and "press reports" for the animations.
- English exam at every CBR centre for a surcharge: guide (nlcompass.com). The fee and surcharge
  are not quoted anywhere in Tigertest copy.
- A passed theory exam is valid for 1.5 years: guides only (zorgeloos-studeren.nl and others); not
  confirmed on cbr.nl, so it is left out of the landing and examFacts.
- Volumes (49,000 to 60,000 theory exams B a month in Jul-Dec 2024) and the one-in-three
  first-time pass rate come from the research notes (CBR reports to parliament, press); not used
  in copy.

## Rules of the road (primary law)
- Reglement verkeersregels en verkeerstekens 1990 (RVV 1990), version in force from 1 July 2026,
  https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0004825 (fetched 2026-11-21; the 1 Jan to 30 June 2026 version
  read too): art. 3 keep right; 4-6 pedestrians, cycle and moped paths; 11 overtaking on the left,
  right-hand exceptions (left-turners, trams, cyclists); 12 no overtaking before or on a zebra;
  13 queues may be passed on the right; 14 do not block a junction; 15 priority to the right,
  unpaved yields to paved, trams have priority; 15a level crossings; 16 military convoys and
  funeral processions; 17-18 turning, signalling, giving way when turning; 19 stop within the
  distance you can see; 20-22 speed limits (50 built-up; 80, 100 autoweg, 130 motorway; 45
  brommobiel and bromfiets on carriageway; 25 snorfiets; 90 with a trailer up to 3,500 kg, 80
  with other trailers); 23 no stopping (junction, level crossing, zebra and 5 m, tunnel, bus stop
  and 12 m, solid yellow line); 24 no parking (5 m from a junction, before an exit, priority road
  outside built-up areas, broken yellow line, loading place, permit bays, double parking); 25
  parking disc (round up to the next whole or half hour, no automatic discs, bays only); 26
  disabled bays; 27 bikes and mopeds parked on pavement or verge; 29 priority vehicles (blue
  light and two-tone horn); 32 dipped beam at night and in poor visibility, main beam rules; 34
  fog lights (rear only below 50 m in fog or snow); 42-43 motorways and autowegen (60 / 50 km/h
  minimum, no turning or reversing, hard shoulder only in emergencies, vehicles over 7 m and
  lorries in the two right lanes on 3+ lanes); 44-46 erf (pedestrians whole width, 15 km/h,
  parking in marked places); 47-48 roundabouts (overtaking on the right allowed); 49 pedestrians,
  zebra, white stick with red rings; 50 give way to priority vehicles; 52 passing a stopped bus or
  tram; 53 towing gap at most 5 m; 54-55 special manoeuvres and signalling; 56 buses leaving stops
  in built-up areas; 57 no needless noise; 58 warning triangle about 30 m, not needed with hazard
  lights; 59 seat belts, child seats under 1.35 m and under 18, rear-facing seat not before an
  active airbag, no more passengers than belts; 61a no holding a mobile device while driving any
  vehicle; 62-64 and 84 hierarchy (directions over lights over signs over rules); 63a temporary
  markings first; 63b lowest limit applies; 66 zone signs; 68-75 lights (amber, arrows, bicycle
  symbol, tram and bus lights, level crossing white and red flashing, bridge lights, red X and
  white arrow over lanes, pedestrian lights, flashing amber); 76-81 markings (solid and broken
  lines, hatched areas, lane arrows, stop line, shark's teeth, BUS and LIJNBUS); 82-83 police
  directions and stop signs.
- Wegenverkeerswet 1994, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0006622 (version 1 July 2026, fetched
  2026-11-21): art. 5 no dangerous behaviour; 7 do not leave the scene of a crash with injury or
  damage; 8 alcohol: 0.5 mg/ml blood (220 micrograms per litre of breath), novice drivers 0.2
  (88), novices being those within five years of a first licence issued at 18 or older (seven
  years for AM or T licences issued under 18).
- Signs are described as the BABW sign list shows them (A1, A2, A4, B1, B2, B3, B6, B7, C1, C2,
  D1, D2, E1, E2, E4, F1, F5, F6, G1, G2, G3, G5, G11, G12a, G13, H1, J signs, L2), without
  sign images.

## Licence, coach and vehicle rules (official pages)
- Rijksoverheid, "Wanneer mag ik rijles nemen en rijexamen doen voor mijn autorijbewijs?"
  (fetched 2026-11-21): theory from 16, lessons from 16.5, practical exam from 17 with a theory
  certificate and a health declaration; under 18 only with a coach named on a begeleiderspas.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/rijbewijs/vraag-en-antwoord/rijbewijs/wanneer-mag-ik-rijles-nemen-en-rijexamen-doen-voor-mijn-autorijbewijs
- Rijksoverheid, "Hoe word ik begeleider van een 17-jarige bestuurder?" (found 2026-11-21): coach
  at least 27, licence held at least 5 years, registered with the RDW; one to five coaches.
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/rijbewijs/vraag-en-antwoord/hoe-word-ik-begeleider-van-een-17-jarige-bestuurder
- Rijksoverheid, "Puntenrijbewijs en beginnersrijbewijs" (fetched 2026-11-21): beginner's scheme,
  a second penalty point leads to a CBR investigation; points licence for drink driving (two
  convictions in five years, the second over 1.3 per mille, invalidates the licence).
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/verkeer-en-vervoer/rijbewijs/puntenrijbewijs-beginnersrijbewijs
- RDW, "Wanneer en hoe vaak APK keuren" (found 2026-11-21): petrol and electric cars first at 4
  years, then twice every 2 years, then yearly; diesel and LPG first at 3 years, then yearly.
  https://www.rdw.nl/particulier/voertuigen/auto/apk/wanneer-en-hoe-vaak-apk-keuren
- Category B limits (3,500 kg, eight passenger seats, trailer up to 750 kg or a combination up to
  3,500 kg, code 96 to 4,250 kg): EU Directive 2006/126/EC as applied in the Netherlands.
- Compulsory liability insurance: Wet aansprakelijkheidsverzekering motorrijtuigen (WAM).
- Intelligent speed assistance on new cars from 7 July 2024: EU Regulation 2019/2144 (General
  Safety Regulation).
- Zero-emission zones for vans and lorries in Dutch cities from 1 January 2025 (cars not covered)
  and milieuzones for older vehicles: Rijksoverheid and city pages, stated without city lists.

## Not used, on purpose
- Fees, surcharges, fines and penalty amounts.
- Any question about the exam itself (count, time, pass mark, language, booking, validity).
- Picture-based, drag-and-drop, hotspot and number-entry formats; hazard-recognition timing and
  animations are not modelled, and the landing says so.
- City-specific rules (which cities have which zones, local cycle-street rules), and the daytime
  100 km/h motorway limits, which are set by signs, not by the RVV.
- Belgian rules (kept clear of `bedriving`, which also has Dutch-language users).

## Review pass (2026-11-22)
- Re-read on wetten.overheid.nl (RVV 1990, BWBR0004825, fetched 2026-11-22): art. 11 (overtake on
  the left; left-turners and trams passed on the right; cyclists overtake each other on the left
  and may pass other drivers on the right), art. 13 (queues may be passed on the right), art. 15
  and 15a, 18, 20-22 (speeds, 90 km/h with a trailer up to 3,500 kg), 23-25, 42-46, 48, 49, 53,
  54-56, 58 (triangle about 30 m), 59 (not more passengers than belts; under 18 and under 1.35 m
  in a child restraint), 61a, 71.
- CBR "Hoe gaat het theorie-examen auto?" re-fetched 2026-11-22: 50 questions plus 2 unscored,
  44 of 50, 30 minutes, 45 with extra time or individually guided. Matches the landing, FAQ and
  the WIRING examFacts block.
- Rijksoverheid "Puntenrijbewijs en beginnersrijbewijs" re-fetched 2026-11-22: second penalty
  point leads to a CBR investigation of driving skill; second drink-driving conviction within
  five years over 1.3 per mille means the licence is lost at once.
- ANWB "Voorrang voetgangers" (found 2026-11-22): turning drivers let pedestrians crossing the
  road they turn into go first (CBR's teaching, via the straight-on traffic rule).
- Sign numbers checked against ANWB's sign list: C2 no entry, C3 one-way street, G13 black
  board with white "fietspad" (non-mandatory cycle path).
- Items rewritten to stay clear of other EU and v1 driving banks (same scenario and answer),
  and number distractors rebalanced so keys sit at an end slot about half the time.
