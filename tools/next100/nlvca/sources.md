# Sources: VCA Basic Safety (B-VCA), `nlvca`

Every question in `data/vca-basic-safety-questions.json` is original, written by Tigertest from the
SSVV eindtermen and toetstermen (ETT) below. No item comes from the SSVV central item bank (which is
secret), from the official proefexamens, from wikiwijs proefexamens, from course books or from any
commercial prep. All sources checked 2026-11-22.

## Official: exam content and blueprint (SSVV, the scheme owner)

- SSVV, Examens (VCA exams page with the ETT and toetsmatrijs downloads), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://ssvv.nl/examens/
  - The Centraal College van Deskundigen VCA (CCvD-VCA) manages the eindtermen; the Centrale
    Examencommissie VCA (CEV) oversees the central item bank; recognised exam centres run the exams.
  - FAQ on the page: "Een VCA-diploma is 10 jaar geldig." After that you sit the exam again at a
    recognised exam centre. Diplomas are registered in the Centraal Diploma Register (CDR).
- SSVV, B-VCA Toetsmatrijs, versie 2026 (sheet Toetsmatrijs-Basis of VCA-ETT-Toetsmatrijzen-2026.xlsx),
  fetched 2026-11-22: https://ssvv.nl/app/uploads/2026/09/VCA-ETT-Toetsmatrijzen-2026.xlsx
  - B-VCA: 40 questions, 60 minutes, cesuur 64.5% (2,580 points), closed questions in the forms
    multiple choice, multiple response, matrix and matching.
  - Items per section (the bank's per-test blueprint): A Prepare, discuss and inspect the work 8
    (A.01 Regulations and safety rules 4, A.02 Working safely, consultation and inspections 2,
    A.03 Prevention 2); B Carrying out work 19 (B.01 Workplace 5, B.02 PPE 4, B.03 Work equipment 4,
    B.04 Specific work and circumstances 6); C Control of specific hazards 10 (C.01 Hazardous
    substances 6, C.02 Electricity and radiation 2, C.03 Fire and explosion 2); D Incidents and
    emergencies 3 (D.01 Accidents 1, D.02 Emergency situations 2).
  - VOL-VCA (sheet Toetsmatrijs-VOL): 60 questions, 60 minutes, cesuur 64.5% (3,870 points). Not modelled.
- SSVV, VCA-ETT 3.0 version 2026, English (sections, topics, final attainment levels and test criteria,
  with a B / VOL / VIL target column), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://ssvv.nl/app/uploads/2026/04/VCA-ETT-3.0-2026-Opleiding_EN.xlsx
  - The whole bank is written from the rows marked for B-VCA: Dutch and Belgian H&S law terms,
    inspectorate powers, employee and employer duties and rights, CE marking, safe-work agreements,
    safe behaviour, alcohol and medicines, housekeeping, toolbox meetings, unsafe acts and situations,
    the prevention hierarchy, RI&E and TRA, LMRA; noise, vibration, lighting, tripping, manual lifting
    (23 kg NL, 25 kg B), LOTOTO, signs and marking; PPE (otoplastics about 25 dB(A), hearing
    protection available from 80 and mandatory from 85 dB(A), P1/P2/P3, breathing apparatus below 19%
    oxygen, metal helmets banned, leather gloves not with hazardous substances); machines and powered
    tools (emergency stop, no-volt release, dead man's switch, CE marking after 1995, operators 18+),
    lifting work, forklifts and pallet trucks; openings, work at height (NL 2.5 m, B 2 m, flat roofs
    within 4 m), ladders (under 5 m, under 2 hours, under 50 N, not above wind force 6, about 75
    degrees, 1 m above the landing), scaffolds and tags, suspended scaffolds, mobile towers, man
    baskets, aerial work platforms, confined spaces (19% oxygen, under 10% of the LEL, 18+, permanent
    attendant), specific work and the "Ex" sign; hazardous substances (limit values, odour, leaks,
    classes, H and P phrases, pictograms, intake routes, source measures, medical checks mostly once a
    year, asbestos procedure, solvents, cement, quartz, CO, biological agents, 21% and 19% oxygen,
    cylinder shoulder colours); electricity (resistance, static, RCD, earthing, cable reels); fire
    (triangle, classes A to D, agents and their drawbacks, actions, burns cooled 10 to 20 minutes);
    accidents, near misses, incidents, emergency phases, drills and evacuation.
- SSVV, VCA-ETT 3.1 changes from 1 September 2026 (Wijzigingen), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://ssvv.nl/app/uploads/2026/03/VCA-ETT-3.1-miv-1-9-2026-Wijzigingen.xlsx

- SSVV news, "Wat verandert er aan de VCA-examens in 2026" (updated 29-4-2026), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://ssvv.nl/actueel/wat-verandert-er-aan-de-vca-examens-in-2026-2/
  - Renewed exams from 1 January 2026 in Dutch, then English, German and French; other languages from
    1 August 2026; the modular B-VCA (4 modules, regular education) from 1 September 2026.
  - New VOL-VCA and VIL-VCU exams are 60 questions in 60 minutes (the old ones were 70 in 75).
  - Sling spread angle and slinging loads left the VCA exams (now a separate course), so the bank has

## Official: exam rules and format at an exam institute

- PBNA, Examen VCA Basis, fetched 2026-11-22: https://www.pbna.com/en/examens/vca/examen-vca-basis/
  - 40 questions, at most 60 minutes, pass at least 65% (PBNA rounds the SSVV cesuur), 15 minutes
    extra for the read-aloud exam, diploma valid ten years. Languages listed include Dutch, English,
    Polish, Turkish, Romanian, Ukrainian and German (the page header also says "19 languages";
    landing copy says "Dutch, English and several other languages" only).
- PBNA, Reglement VCA-examinering versie 2024-01 (geldig vanaf 01-04-2024), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://pbna.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Examenreglement-VCA-2024-01.pdf
  - Section 11: maximum duration 60 minutes for Basisveiligheid VCA; section 12: read-aloud exams
    (voorleesexamen) only for B-VCA, always computer-based, 15 minutes extra (75 minutes in total).
    The rule table lists "B-VCA 40 vragen 1 uur" and the then VOL-VCA 70 questions in 75 minutes,
    since replaced by SSVV's 60 in 60 for the 2026 exams.

## How the format is used

- Real exam: 40 questions, 60 minutes (75 read-aloud), 2,580 of 4,000 points (64.5%). Tigertest:
  four 40-question mock tests at 65% (26 of 40), each with the toetsmatrijs section counts. Real
  items include multiple response, matrix and matching forms, which earn partial points; Tigertest
  uses single-answer multiple choice only and says so.
- The diploma's ten-year validity, the languages and the CDR are landing and FAQ facts only, never
  questions.

## Left out on purpose

- Exam fees, course prices and how much employers pay: never stated.
- VOL-VCA and VIL-VCU only content (TRA updates, V&G annual plans, fire watch tasks, radiation,
  electrical qualifications, accident investigation): not modelled; a VOL-VCA lane can come later.
- Pictograms and signs are described in words; no images.
- Named officeholders, named course providers in questions, and any SSVV or proefexamen item.
- No question asks about the exam itself (length, pass mark, validity, languages, CDR).
