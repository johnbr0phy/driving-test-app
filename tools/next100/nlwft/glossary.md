# Glossary for `nlwft` (Dutch, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations in neutral Dutch, third person (de klant, de medewerker) and
"u" in case items, like the CDFD toetstermen and the kandidatenbrochure. Landing and dashboard
copy in "je" (consumer-app norm, as `befsmavz`). Dutch typography: decimal comma (508,20),
thousands point (90.000), "20%" without a space, amounts written as "1.250 euro" (no euro sign in
the bank), dates as 1 april 2026. No em-dashes. Terms follow the law and the CDFD toetstermen;
Netherlands usage, not Belgian (verzekeringnemer, not verzekeringsnemer; Kifid, not Ombudsman).
