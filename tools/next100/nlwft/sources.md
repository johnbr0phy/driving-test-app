# Sources for `nlwft` (Wft Basis, vakbekwaamheidsexamen financiële dienstverlening, in Dutch)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Dutch from the toetstermen and the laws
below. Nothing was copied from the CDFD exam bank (which is secret), CDFD or institute sample
questions, a past exam, or a commercial course or e-learning. Checked 2026-11-22 unless noted.

## Official sources to watch first
- CDFD, "Initieel examen Basis" (kenmerken geldig vanaf 1 april 2026), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://cdfd.nl/initieel-examen-basis/
  - Question types: Kennis en Begrip (KB), Professioneel gedrag (PG), Vaardigheden en
    Competenties (VC).
  - Tijdsduur 120 minuten; 42 vragen, 63 punten: KB 21 vragen (21 punten), PG 2 vragen
    (4 punten), VC 19 vragen (38 punten).
  - Zak/slaaggrens 68% van het aantal punten (68% = cijfer 5,5, rounded to 6).
  - Module Basis is the first step for every beroepskwalificatie except Adviseur Zorgverzekering.
  - Fixed omslagdatum 1 April: exams up to 31 March use the law as it stood on 1 January of the
    year before; from 1 April the law of 1 January of that year.
  - Result from DUO by e-mail within two working days.
- CDFD, "Eindtermen en toetstermen module Basis, geldend voor examens vanaf 1 april 2026"
  (based on the Regeling eindtermen en toetstermen examens financiële dienstverlening Wft as
  valid on 1 April 2026), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://cdfd.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Toetstermen-Basis-260401-1.pdf
  - Taak 1 and 2, eindtermen 1a (financiële huishouding), 1b (betalen, sparen, beleggen,
    depositogarantiestelsel), 1c (consumptief en hypothecair krediet), 1d (persoonlijke
    risico's, sociale verzekeringen, pensioen), 1e (verzekeringsbegrippen, WAM, rechtsbijstand),
    1f (ordening en toezicht, zorgplicht, distributie en beloning, AVG), 1g (verzekeringsrecht),
    2a to 2d (rekenen, intake, factuur, leads, dossier), 3a to 3d (intake, informeren, klachten,
    relatiebeheer), 4a (integriteitsdilemma's). These are the ten categories of the bank.
- CDFD, "Kandidatenbrochure Initiële Wft-examens, geldig vanaf 1 april 2026, vs 2.1",
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://cdfd.nl/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Kandidatenbrochure-initieel-2.1.pdf
  - "Nee, de examens zijn alleen in het Nederlands." (Dutch only.)
  - KB: short questions, 1 point; PG: case text with one question about dilemmas, 2 points;
    VC: case with one or more questions and practice documents, 2 points.
  - Formats: multiple choice (three or four options, one correct), multiple select (point
    deduction for wrong picks), multiple dropdown (part scores), ordering (part scores),
    numeric entry. A calculator is available.
  - Table for Basis: 120 minutes, 21 (21) KB, 2 (4) PG, 19 (38) VC, 42 (63), pass 42,84 points.
- CDFD, "Advies over de inhoud van de Wft-examens vanaf 1 april 2027" (20 October 2026) and
  Bijlage 3 (wijziging toetstermen 2027), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://cdfd.nl/nieuws/advies-over-de-inhoud-van-de-wft-examens-vanaf-1-april-2027/
  https://cdfd.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Bijlage-3-Advies-over-wijziging-toetstermen-2027.pdf
  - Proposed for Basis from 1 April 2027: 1b.16 wording "gesloten (closed-end) en open
    (open-end) beleggingsfonds"; 1f.39 and 1f.40 drop the explicit AVG reference. An advice to
    the minister, not yet in force; the bank already uses "gesloten" and "open" and asks the
    AVG items in terms that survive the change.
- CDFD, "Statistieken Wft-examens april 2026 tot en met september 2026" (13 October 2026),
  fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://cdfd.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/CDFD-statistieken-Wft-examens-apr-sep-2026.pdf
  - Initieel Basis: 3.305 exams, 56,1% passed (all attempts, first tries and resits together).
- CDFD, "Instituten": recognised exam institutes (erkend tot en met 31/03/2028), fetched
  2026-11-22: https://cdfd.nl/instituten/
- DUO, "Wet op het financieel toezicht: In het kort", fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.duo.nl/zakelijk/wet-op-het-financieel-toezicht/wet-op-het-financieel-toezicht/
  - Candidates register and sit the exam at a recognised exam institute; DUO keeps the Mijn Wft
    register of adviesbevoegdheden and issues diplomas and PE certificates; registration with a
    Wft-ID instead of a BSN is possible.
- Tweede Kamer, Kamerstuk 32545 nr. 24 (pass line lowered from 70% to 68%), from the research
  notes: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-32545-24.pdf (not re-read; the current 68%
  comes from the CDFD pages above).

## Not confirmed, or left out
- The exact Wft article numbers for the beroepsaansprakelijkheidsverzekering, the inducement
  rules and the provisieverbod details sit in the Besluit Gedragstoezicht financiële
  ondernemingen (BGfo), which did not resolve in the repository; explanations name the rule
  without an article number.
- No question uses a tax rate, threshold, heffingsvrij vermogen, vrijstelling amount, AOW age,
  depositogarantie ceiling, NHG limit or other figure that changes each year. The two rates in
  calculation items (21% assurantiebelasting, 21% btw) are given in the stem as data.
- No question asks about the exam itself (count, time, pass line, language, institutes, DUO).
  Those facts are on the landing and in lib/examFacts.ts only.
- Not modelled: point weighting (PG and VC items count 2 points), multiple select, dropdown,
  ordering and numeric entry formats, and long VC cases with practice documents. Every item here
  is single best answer of four, one point each.
- The proposed 2027 toetsterm changes are noted above and not yet treated as law.
- The specialist modules (Schade, Leven, Vermogen, Krediet, Hypothecair, Inkomen, Pensioen,
  Zorg) are out of scope; they can be later siblings.
