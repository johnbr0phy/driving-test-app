# Sources for `nmls` (NMLS SAFE Mortgage Loan Originator Test, National Test with Uniform State Content)

All 200 questions are original, written from the NMLS content outline and the federal statutes and
regulations it names. No NMLS sample question (Appendix B of the handbook), past item, or
commercial prep question was copied, and no prep provider is named. Checked 2026-11-02.

## Exam format (official, NMLS Resource Center)
- NMLS, SAFE MLO Test Handbook, "1.0 Overview and Test Specifications":
  https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/testingHbk/education/mlo_testing/mlo_test_guide/mlo_testing_hbk_intro.html
  (checked 2026-11-02): "As of April 5, 2021" the SAFE MLO Test is the National Test with
  Uniform State Content: 120 items, 115 scored (5 unscored pretest items, not identified),
  190 minutes, 225-minute appointment (30 to 35 minutes of tutorial and survey). Passing score
  75% or better, set by the federal SAFE Act. A fee is listed; deliberately not used in copy.
- NMLS, "SAFE MLO National Test with Uniform State Test Content Outline":
  https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/testingHbk/education/mlo_testing/mlo_test_faq/mlo_testing_hbk_testOutline.html
  (checked 2026-11-02): Federal Mortgage Related Laws 24%, Uniform State Content 11%, General
  Mortgage Knowledge 20%, Mortgage Loan Origination Activities 27%, Ethics 18%. No revision date on
  the page; NMLS says candidates answer on current statutes and rules. Subtopics used to plan the
  categories (Reg X, Reg B, Reg Z incl. HOEPA, HPML and LO compensation, TRID, HMDA, FCRA/FACTA,
  BSA/AML, GLBA, advertising, E-Sign, Dodd-Frank, CFPB/HUD; SAFE Act and CSBS/AARMR model state law,
  licensing, sponsorship, CE, temporary authority, prohibited acts, NMLS ID in advertising;
  programs, products, terminology; application, qualification, closing, calculations; ethics,
  fraud, fair lending, predatory lending, advertising, consumer relationships).
- NMLS, SAFE MLO Testing FAQ:
  https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/testingHbk/education/mlo_testing/mlo_test_faq/mlo_testing_hbk_FAQ.html
  (checked 2026-11-02): 190 minutes; offered at Prometric test centres and online through
  Prometric's ProProctor remote proctoring (onscreen calculator and scratchpad); one passing result
  meets every state's test requirement.
- NMLS, "6.0 Preparing for the Test":
  https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/knowledge/Products/nmls/pubs/testingHbk/education/mlo_testing/mlo_test_guide/mlo_testing_hbk_testprep.html
  (checked 2026-11-02): the content outline is the only study material NMLS publishes; outline from
  a national job analysis of entry-level MLO knowledge.
- Retake waits (30 days between the first three attempts, 180 days after the third failure) are in
  the SAFE Act, 12 U.S.C. 5104(d); not used in the bank (test admin), used only on the landing FAQ.
- Third-party guides that say 125 questions are out of date or wrong: NMLS gives 120 (115 scored).

## Blueprint (Tigertest)
NMLS 24/11/20/27/18 scaled to 50 per mock test by largest remainder: federal law 12 (Reg Z 4,
RESPA/Reg X/TRID 4, ECOA and other federal 4), Uniform State Content 6, general knowledge 10
(programs 5, products and terms 5), origination 13 (application and qualification 7, closing and
calculations 6), ethics 9. Four 50-question mocks, a shorter mock of a 120-item exam at a similar
pace (190 min for 120 items is about 1.6 min each).

## Rules and facts used (current as of 2026-11-02)
- SAFE Act (12 U.S.C. 5101 et seq.) and Regulation H/G: MLO definition, exemptions (registered
  depository MLOs, own-residence seller financing, immediate family, clerical staff, real estate
  brokerage activity not paid by a lender), 20 hours pre-licensing (3 federal law, 3 ethics, 2
  nontraditional), 8 hours annual CE (3 federal law, 2 ethics, 2 nontraditional), felony rule (7
  years, or ever for fraud, dishonesty, breach of trust or money laundering; pardon not counted),
  fingerprints, credit report for financial responsibility.
- CSBS/AARMR Model State Law: independent contractor processors and underwriters licensed, no same
  course in the same or successive years, instructor CE credit 2 for 1, regulator powers, quarterly
  Mortgage Call Report, NMLS ID on advertising.
- Economic Growth, Regulatory Relief, and Consumer Protection Act (2018), section 106: temporary
  authority to originate up to 120 days.
- Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026): rescission (3 business days; 20 days to return funds; 3-year
  extension), HPML thresholds (1.5/2.5/3.5 over APOR), HPML escrow 5 years, flipped-property second
  appraisal, ATR factors, QM features, prepayment penalty limits (3 years, 2%/2%/1%), LO
  compensation and dual compensation, NMLSR ID on application, note and security instrument,
  advertising triggering terms, HOEPA counseling, valuation independence, finance charge
  exclusions, CHARM booklet timing.
- TRID (12 CFR 1026.19(e),(f), 1026.37, 1026.38): six-item application, Loan Estimate in 3
  business days, credit report fee only before intent, Closing Disclosure 3 business days before
  consummation, three re-trigger events, tolerances, 60-day cure, 10-business-day LE expiry,
  3-day revised LE for changed circumstances, coverage exclusions, CD sections A/B/C, Prepaids.
- Regulation X (12 CFR 1024): Sections 8 and 9, affiliated business disclosure, Home Loan Toolkit
  and servicing disclosure within 3 business days, escrow cushion one-sixth, initial escrow
  statement within 45 days, servicing transfer 60-day grace, 120-day foreclosure rule, notice of
  error acknowledged in 5 business days.
- Regulation B (12 CFR 1002): 30-day notice, public assistance and alimony income, appraisal copies
  and the 3-business-day notice, incompleteness notice, age.
- Regulation B final rule (91 Fed. Reg. 21,620, issued 2026-04-22, effective 2026-07-21): removes
  disparate-impact (effects test) liability from ECOA/Reg B and narrows discouragement; it does not
  affect the Fair Housing Act. Item NMLS-ETH-003 is therefore framed under the Fair Housing Act.
  https://www.huschblackwell.com/newsandinsights/cfpb-finalizes-major-regulation-b-overhaul-disparate-impact-out-discouragement-narrowed-and-spcps-restricted
  (checked 2026-11-02).
- Regulation C (HMDA): visual observation or surname for in-person applications.
- FCRA/FACTA: adverse action notice contents, Red Flags Rule. Homebuyers Privacy Protection Act
  (signed 2025-09-05, effective 2026-03, trigger leads only with consent or an existing relationship
  and a firm offer): NAMB, https://www.namb.org/understanding-the-trigger-leads-ban and National
  Mortgage Professional, https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/trigger-lead-restrictions-begin-homebuyers-privacy-protection-act-takes-effect
  (checked 2026-11-02; sources differ on March 4 or 5, so the item says "March 2026").
- GLBA privacy (opt-out for nonaffiliated sharing) and Safeguards Rule; BSA cash reporting over
  $10,000 and the SAR confidentiality rule (FinCEN 2012 rule for nonbank RMLOs); TCPA 8 a.m. to
  9 p.m.; E-Sign consent; Homeowners Protection Act 80%/78%; Flood Disaster Protection Act;
  Dodd-Frank (CFPB rulemaking, UDAAP "abusive" standard); Regulation N (MAP rule).
- Programs: FHA 3.5% at 580 and above, upfront and annual MIP, HUD county limits, 203(k), 1-4
  units owner-occupied; VA COE, no monthly MI, residual income, IRRRL, entitlement on assumption;
  USDA rural guarantee; Ginnie Mae; HECM age 62.
- Left out on purpose: dollar thresholds that are indexed each year (HOEPA and QM points-and-fees
  limits, conforming and FHA limit amounts), fees, state-specific rules, and anything about the test
  itself.
