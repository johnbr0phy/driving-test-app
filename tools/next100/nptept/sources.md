# NPTE-PT bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the FSBPT content outline and the
clinical rules below. None is copied or reworded from FSBPT's PEAT, the NPTE Demonstration
Examination, sample questions, review books or any prep company's or app's items.

Checked 2026-11-22.

## Outline (what is tested and how much)

- FSBPT, NPTE-PT Test Content Outline, effective January 2024 (Version 1.1, 8/25/2023), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.fsbpt.org/Portals/0/documents/free-resources/2024-PT-Test-Content-Outline-with-rubrics_1_1_20230825.pdf
  Measures "the knowledge required of entry-level physical therapist practitioners". 180 scored
  items. Body systems, each split into Physical Therapy Examination, Foundations for Evaluation,
  Differential Diagnosis & Prognosis, and Interventions: Cardiovascular & Pulmonary 22-27
  (7-8 / 7-9 / 8-10), Musculoskeletal 44-54 (14-18 / 15-18 / 15-18), Neuromuscular & Nervous
  39-48 (13-16 each), Integumentary 8-11, Metabolic & Endocrine 4-6, Gastrointestinal 3-6,
  Genitourinary 2-5, Lymphatic 4-7, System Interactions 8-10 (cancer, multitrauma, pregnancy,
  comorbidities, polypharmacy, psychological conditions, pain). Nonsystem: Equipment, Devices &
  Technologies 5-6, Therapeutic Modalities 4-6, Safety & Protection 5-7, Professional
  Responsibilities 4-5, Research & Evidence-Based Practice 3-5. The topic lists under each heading
  were used for every category.
- FSBPT, NPTE Development: NPTE Content page, fetched 2026-11-22 (lists the outlines "in effect
  January 2024"; no newer PT outline announced; the PT exam has five sections of 45, 225 items,
  45 of them pretest):
  https://www.fsbpt.org/Free-Resources/NPTE-Development/NPTE-Content

## Format (landing, FAQ and examFacts only, never in the bank)

- FSBPT, NPTE Candidate Handbook, version 2025.02, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.fsbpt.org/Portals/0/documents/free-resources/NPTE_Candidate_Handbook.pdf
  PT exam: 5 sections, 225 multiple-choice questions (45 per section), 5 hours of testing time,
  5 hours 30 minutes total appointment (includes a scheduled 15-minute break after section 2,
  tutorial and survey). 45 of the PT items are unscored pretest items. Sections are not separately
  timed. Some items include a graphic or video; scenario-based questions (2 to 5 per shared
  scenario, up to 40 on the PT exam) come at the end of each section. One correct answer per
  question, no penalty for wrong answers. Scaled scores 200 to 800; the passing standard is a scaled
  600 in every jurisdiction. Computer-based at Prometric, fixed dates four times a year (January,
  April, July, October). Limits: no more than three consecutive attempts in 12 months, six in a
  lifetime per level, and no testing after two scores below 400.

## Practice rules used in the bank

- CMS, Medicare Benefit Policy Manual, Chapter 15, sections 220 to 230 (outpatient therapy), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://www.cms.gov/Regulations-and-Guidance/Guidance/Manuals/Downloads/bp102c15.pdf
  The plan of care is certified for at most 90 calendar days; initial certification is timely
  within 30 days of the first treatment; the evaluation, re-evaluation and plan of care are the
  therapist's; a progress report is written at least once every 10 treatment days.
- Clinical content (special tests, outcome measures and their cut-offs, pathology, pharmacology,
  intervention parameters, modality contraindications) is standard entry-level physical therapy
  knowledge as named in the outline's topic lists. Only widely agreed values are used: Berg 56
  maximum, TUG over about 13.5 s, 10 g monofilament, ABI under 0.9 (and under 0.5 severe),
  resting BP above 200/110 as a reason to hold exercise, Ottawa ankle rules, Wells criteria
  concept, Canadian C-spine rule concept, ASIA grades, Rancho levels, Hoehn and Yahr, Brunnstrom,
  GMFCS, Glasgow Coma Scale ranges, Karvonen, spirometry patterns, ultrasound 1 and 3 MHz,
  iontophoresis polarity, levels of evidence, sensitivity and specificity (SnNout, SpPin),
  likelihood ratios.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test: cardiovascular & pulmonary 7, musculoskeletal 14, neuromuscular & nervous
  12, other systems and system interactions 9 (integumentary, lymphatic, metabolic and endocrine,
  GI, GU, system interactions), equipment, devices and modalities 4, safety, professional roles and
  evidence 4. The outline midpoints of 180 scored scaled to 50 give 6.8 / 13.6 / 12.1 / 10.3 /
  2.9 / 4.1; the small nonsystem areas are rounded up a little so each has at least 16 items.
- Tigertest's 70% pass line is a readiness target, not FSBPT's standard (a scaled 600, whose raw
  equivalent changes by form).
- PT level: examination, evaluation, differential diagnosis, prognosis, plan of care and
  intervention choices, wider than the PTA bank (which stays inside the PT's plan of care).
  Direct access and PTA supervision rules vary by state and are not asserted.
- Graphic, video and shared-scenario items are written as stand-alone text items; no images.
- Dedupe: every item was checked against `data/nptepta-questions.json` so no fact and answer pair
  repeats (no goniometer landmarks, MMT grade definitions, THA precautions, crutch stairs, cane
  side, Berg maximum, monofilament, Rancho IV/VII, pressure injury stage 3, ultrasound 1 MHz for
  4 cm, hot pack towel layers, glove-then-gown order). Overlapping topics are asked from another
  angle (a special test, a differential, a prognosis or a dosage decision).
- No exam-admin questions, no fees, no prep provider named.

## Not confirmed or left out

- No announcement of a newer NPTE-PT outline was found (FSBPT reviews outlines about every five
  years after a practice analysis; the last was 2022). If one appears, this bank must be re-weighted.
- Fees are not stated anywhere in the bank or the landing.
