# Sources for `nscacpt` (NSCA-Certified Personal Trainer exam)

All 200 questions are original, written from the NSCA-CPT Detailed Content Outline (DCO) and the
public guidelines and research listed below. No NSCA sample or practice question, NSCA study guide
item, Essentials of Personal Training review question, commercial question bank or prep course was
used. The five sample questions printed with the DCO were read only to see the item style; their
facts and numbers (a 10RM-to-1RM estimate of the same figures, the 8 to 12RM one-minute-rest
hypertrophy item, free sessions as positive reinforcement, the 100/450/40 g calorie sum, when to
give the medical history form) are not reused. Checked 2026-11-23.

Written after `nasm` (NASM CPT) and `acecpt` (ACE CPT). This bank keeps to NSCA's own outline and
framing (periodization cycles, NSCA test sequencing, %1RM load assignment, the 2025 DCO's tempo,
work-to-rest ratio, training age versus biological age, RED-S, health span) and avoids the OPT
model, the ACE IFT model and the fact-and-answer pairs already asked in those two banks.

## Exam outline and format (landing, FAQ and examFacts only; never in the bank)
- NSCA, "NSCA-Certified Personal Trainer Job Task Analysis Summary" (2025), with the new DCO:
  https://www.nsca.com/globalassets/certification/certification-pdfs/nsca-certified-personal-trainer-job-task-analysis-summary-2025
  (fetched 2026-11-23; nsca.com returns 403 to automated clients, so the PDF was read from the
  Internet Archive copy of that URL captured 2026-03-10). New DCO effective July 1, 2025: Domain 1
  Client Consultation and Assessment 32 items (initial interview, medical history and health
  appraisal, performance evaluation, basic wellness review), Domain 2 Program Planning 41 (program
  design, special populations), Domain 3 Program Execution 50 (exercise instruction, evaluation of
  exercise technique, training adaptations), Domain 4 Safety, Emergency Procedures, and Legal
  Issues 17 (safety procedures, emergency response, professional, legal and ethical
  responsibility); 140 scored items, cognitive levels 33 recall, 62 application, 45 analysis.
  Per 50-question test here: 11 / 15 / 18 / 6.
- NSCA, "How to Achieve an NSCA Certification", Certification Handbook effective July 1, 2026:
  https://www.nsca.com/globalassets/certification/certification-pdfs/nsca-certification-handbook.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-23). NSCA-CPT: 3-hour exam, 140 scored and 15 non-scored multiple-choice
  items, 25 to 35 of them video or image questions; domain table 23% / 29% / 36% / 12% (32, 41,
  50, 17); passing is a scaled score of 70 or higher; eligibility 18 or older, high school diploma
  or equivalent, current CPR/AED with a hands-on skills component (documentation within one year of
  the passed exam); Pearson VUE test centers year-round (live remote proctoring only for TSAC-F);
  retake after at least 30 days, three consecutive attempts and then a one-year wait;
  recertification on a three-year CEU cycle.
- NSCA, "NSCA-CPT Explanation Letter" (2018 JTA summary with the 2019 DCO):
  https://www.nsca.com/contentassets/53ec33293e1c4551be4153186d4b2052/nsca-cpt-explanation-letter.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-23). The superseded February 2019 DCO (32 / 45 / 43 / 20); used only to see
  what the 2025 revision moved.
- NSCA, "NSCA-CPT Exam Description" page:
  https://www.nsca.com/certification/nsca-cpt/nsca-cpt-exam-description/ (checked 2026-11-23 through
  search-result text only; the page returns 403). 140 scored and 15 non-scored questions, 3 hours,
  25 to 35 video or image items; the same as the handbook.

## Standards, guidelines and research used for facts
- American College of Sports Medicine, "Progression Models in Resistance Training for Healthy
  Adults" (position stand, Med Sci Sports Exerc 2009), university-hosted copy:
  https://sfu.ca/~ryand/kin343/ACSMresistance.pdf (fetched 2026-11-23). Exercise order (large before
  small, multi-joint before single-joint, higher before lower intensity), novice 8 to 12RM, 2 to 10%
  load increase when one or two reps over target, frequency 2 to 3 / 3 to 4 / 4 to 5 days, heavy
  1 to 6RM with 3 to 5 minutes' rest, power loads 0 to 60% (lower body) and 30 to 60% (upper body)
  moved fast, local muscular endurance 40 to 60% for 15 or more reps with short rest.
- US DHHS / ODPHP, Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 2nd edition, "Top 10 Things to Know":
  https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines/top-10-things-know
  (fetched 2026-11-23). Adults 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity a week and muscle
  strengthening on 2 or more days; youth 60 minutes a day.
- American Heart Association, "Understanding Blood Pressure Readings" (last reviewed 2025-08-14):
  https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/understanding-blood-pressure-readings
  (fetched 2026-11-23). Normal under 120/80, elevated 120 to 129 and under 80, stage 1 130 to 139 or
  80 to 89, stage 2 140 or 90 and above, severe above 180 and/or 120.
- CDC / NIOSH, "Heat-related Illnesses" (2026-03-03):
  https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/about/illnesses.html (fetched 2026-11-23). Signs and first
  aid for heat stroke (cold water immersion, call 911), heat exhaustion, heat syncope, heat cramps
  and rhabdomyolysis (dark urine, stop and seek care).
- General exercise-science facts the DCO points to (%1RM to repetition relationship, test sequence
  from least to most fatiguing, PNF techniques, grip types, five-point body contact, plyometric
  volume as foot contacts, the HIPAA privacy duty, elements of negligence, informed consent versus
  waiver) are written as long-settled textbook facts and worded without paragraph citations.

## Could not confirm, and how the copy words it
- nsca.com pages and PDFs return 403 to automated fetches. The 2025 DCO was read from an archived
  copy of NSCA's own PDF; the 2026 handbook loaded through the fetch tool. Both are NSCA documents.
- NSCA does not publish a percentage pass mark; the site's 70% line is Tigertest's practice target,
  and the landing says the real standard is a scaled score of 70.
- Real NSCA-CPT items have three options (the DCO's sample questions show A to C); this bank uses
  four, and the landing says so. Video and image items are not modelled; techniques are described
  in words.

## Left out on purpose
- No item on exam logistics, fees, CEU counts or credential validity.
- No CPR compression rate or depth item (the AHA pages could not be read); first aid items stay
  with what the CDC page states or general first aid order.
- No supplement doses, brand names or state-specific scope rules.
- Not modelled: the scaled-score conversion, video and image item formats, the cognitive-level
  split.
