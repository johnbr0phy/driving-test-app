# Sources: New Zealand firearms licence theory test (`nzfirearms`)

All checked 5 November 2026.

## Format of the real test
- Firearms Safety and Education New Zealand, "Firearms safety course options":
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/individual-licence/courses-and-education/firearms-safety-course-options
  Standard Firearms Safety Course, two parts. Theory test: 30 minutes, 30 multiple-choice questions,
  English, Simplified Chinese and te reo Māori; reader/writer or interpreter allowed; confirms understanding of
  the Firearms Safety Code, safety practices and legislation; "everything you need ... is in the Firearms Safety
  Code"; sat before the practical; a fail gets a second attempt after the practical session. Practical: safe
  handling of a deactivated firearm, the 7 rules, main parts, states of readiness, fence crossing, transit case.
  Course is free (the licence application must be paid). Community courses launch in 2027; school course for
  students 16+; Whakatūpato for isolated rural communities.
- "Firearms safety course" (who must attend, booking):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/individual-licence/courses-and-education/firearms-safety-course
  Required before the vetting interview for first-time applicants and anyone whose licence expired more than
  1 year ago; current holders are tested on safety knowledge at the vetting interview.
- Pass mark: NOT published by the regulator. Forum reports (nzhuntingandshooting.co.nz, threads 80255 and 60988)
  say 27 of 30, some adding that the first questions on the seven rules must all be right; the 2007 Arms Code
  was quoted as 28 of 30 (gunpolicy.org citation 1158). Tigertest's target: 90% (27 of 30), labelled as ours.

## Content
- Firearms Safety Code, Version 4.0, January 2025 (PDF), Te Tari Pūreke:
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2025-09/Firearms%20Safety%20Code_0.pdf
  Sections 1 to 9 (seven rules, licensing and obligations, firearm types, ammunition, states of readiness,
  preparing and zeroing, safe handling, safety equipment and first aid, ranges) and appendices. Incident figures
  (80% same party, 92% under 75 m, nearly half self-inflicted) and range table (.308 Win about 4 km maximum and
  700 m effective; .22 LR about 2.5 km and 100 m) are the code's. The regulator's page says the code is being
  updated for the Arms Act 2026 and that "some details may be out of date":
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/safety-storage-wellbeing/safety-fundamentals/firearms-safety-code
- 7 rules of firearm safety (current wording):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/safety-storage-wellbeing/safety-fundamentals/7-rules-firearm-safety
- Secure Storage of Standard Firearms and Ammunition, October 2026 (PDF):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2026-10/Secure%20Storage%20Of%20Standard%20Firearms%20And%20Ammunition%20-%20Oct%202026.pdf
  Storage inspected and approved even if no firearms held; steel safe recommended, secured to two surfaces, out of
  sight of windows; lockable steel rack for one firearm, hidden; separate locked ammunition storage; different
  keys/codes kept apart.
- Storage and transportation page (natural disasters, report theft on 105):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/safety-storage-and-wellbeing/storage-transportation-and-display/storage-and-transportation-firearms
- Using a firearm under supervision without a licence (immediate supervision definition, Arms Act 2026):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/safety-storage-wellbeing/safety-fundamentals/using-firearm-under-supervision-without-licence
- Airgun safety (Arms Act 2026: 18+, or 16-17 with a firearms licence; high-energy airguns need a licence):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/safety-storage-wellbeing/safety-fundamentals/airgun-safety
- How endorsements work (Arms Act 2026 terms: restricted firearms, restricted magazines, pistols, permits per item):
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/endorsements-permits/endorsements/how-endorsements-work

## Law
- The Arms Act 2026 (regulator summary): repeals and replaces the Arms Act 1983; Firearms Safety and Education
  New Zealand replaces Te Tari Pūreke; gang members disqualified; infringements; business licences:
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/about-us/about-regulator/arms-act-2026
- Changes for individual licence holders: all holders under the 2026 Act from 23 September 2026; Firearms
  Licensing Review Committee; blank-firing guns need a licence to buy; gang members disqualified; some changes
  from 23 February 2028:
  https://www.firearmssafety.govt.nz/changes-individual-licence-holders
- Franks Ogilvie, "Explainer: Arms Act 2026" (4 August 2026), secondary source confirming commencement and that
  the semi-automatic and high-capacity magazine bans remain:
  https://www.franksogilvie.co.nz/news/explainer-arms-act-2026
- legislation.govt.nz text of the 2026 Act did not load; section numbers are therefore never cited in the bank.

## Decisions
- Six tests of 30 (real length), 20 training-only spares (last items of each pool).
- Items that depended on 1983 Act figures likely to have changed (60-minute vehicle break, 30-day address
  notice, 5/10-year licence terms, 7-day licence production) were left out.
- "Prohibited firearm" is written as "restricted firearm" (2026 Act term) with the old term in explanations.
- No fees, no officeholders, no question about the test itself.
- Kept clear of `data/canadian-firearms-questions.json` (ACTS/PROVE, Canadian law) and
  `data/hunter-questions.json` (IHEA, US): a stem similarity scan found no near-duplicates after rewrites.
