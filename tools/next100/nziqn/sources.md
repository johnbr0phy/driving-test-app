# nziqn sources (fetched 2026-11-15)

All 200 questions are original, written from the official and primary documents below. None is
taken from the Nursing Council's published sample questions (not opened), any past or released
paper, a prep site or another Tigertest bank.

## Exam format (Nursing Council of New Zealand, Te Kaunihera Tapuhi o Aotearoa)
- Internationally qualified nurses' theoretical examination handbook, approval 13 March 2024,
  version 1.5 published October 2026, review September 2027:
  https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/IQN/Handbook-IQN-Theoretical-Exam-October2026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-15). Gave: computer-based, multiple choice "with one correct answer", scored
  correct or incorrect with no partial credit; 15 minutes for introduction and tutorial; "For
  questions requiring a calculation, a calculator is provided as part of the software"; RN exam
  120 questions in 165 minutes, Part A Medication safety 12 in 30 minutes, Part B Nursing knowledge
  108 in 135 minutes (EN exam 90 in 135: 9 in 25 and 81 in 110); Part A scope "medication and fluid
  safety questions including medicine calculations", Part B "closely linked to the Council's
  registered nurse or enrolled nurse standards of competence"; both parts on the first attempt,
  re-sit only a failed part, three attempts in all; six-month eligibility from the invitation; in
  person at Pearson test centres in New Zealand and overseas; no extra time allowances; no
  scheduled breaks, timer not paused; answers can be reviewed and changed; results within 24 hours
  as "Pass" or "Fail" with no further feedback; useful resources: RN and EN standards of
  competence, the Code of Rights, the Code of Conduct, the HPCA Act 2003.
- Competence assessment FAQs:
  https://nursingcouncil.org.nz/IQN/IQN/Competence-Assessment-Frequently-Asked-Questions.aspx
  (fetched 2026-11-15): same counts and times; tests ethical and legislative requirements to
  practise in New Zealand, medicine calculations and management of nursing care.
- Competence assessment process:
  https://nursingcouncil.org.nz/IQN/IQN/Competence-assessment-process.aspx (fetched 2026-11-15):
  the exam is the first test of competence, then the clinical competence assessment (OSCE); links
  the handbook and sample questions.

## Could not be loaded or confirmed
- legislation.govt.nz (HPCA Act, Medicines Act, Misuse of Drugs Regulations) and health.govt.nz
  returned 403 to automated reading. HPCA facts come from the Council's own pages; the controlled
  drug storage rule is as quoted in the NZNO guideline.
- No Council page gives a pass mark, a per-pou split of Part B, or the content split of Part A.
  The score is reported only as Pass or Fail per part. So the 54 Part B items per test are
  Tigertest's split across the six pou (Māori health 5, cultural safety 5, communication 8,
  assessment 10, clinical care 12, people-centred care 7, leadership 7), and the goal line is a
  readiness target, not a Council mark.
- Infection prevention guidance (Health NZ) could not be read, so infection control is not asked.
- The NZ national immunisation schedule page returned 404; no schedule items.

## Deliberately left out
- The sample questions, which the handbook says show the style only. Not opened, not used.
- Fees, re-sit costs, the OSCE and the clinical competence assessment.
- Enrolled nurse exam content; this bank is the registered nurse exam.

## Format modelled
- Part A in full: 12 calculation and medication safety items in 30 minutes, calculator on, all
  four-option multiple choice (all-number options sorted and locked). Part B at half length and
  the real pace: 54 items in 68 minutes. Two practice tests (Test 2 held out) and seven drills.
