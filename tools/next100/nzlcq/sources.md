# LCQ sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Qualification format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- NZQA unit standard 4646 version 11 (registered 2 March 2023, planned review 31 December 2027):
  https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nqfdocs/units/pdf/4646.pdf
  "Demonstrate knowledge of the Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act 2012 and its implications for
  licensed premises". Level 4, 2 credits, grade Achieved. Performance criteria 1.1 to 1.8: object
  and purpose; licensing bodies (ARLA, DLCs) and the roles of police, Medical Officer of Health and
  inspectors; licence types (on, off, club, special) and conditions (signage, designated areas, duty
  manager, temporary and acting managers, hours, prohibited persons, low-alcohol and non-alcoholic
  drinks, free water, food, transport information, responsible service); applications, renewals and
  temporary authorities; manager's certificates; categories of offences; powers of entry and police
  closure; LAPs. References: Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act 2012 and Regulations 2013.
- NZQA unit standard 16705 version 7 (2 March 2023, planned review 31 December 2027):
  https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nqfdocs/units/pdf/16705.pdf
  "Demonstrate knowledge of host responsibility requirements as a duty manager of licensed premises".
  Level 4, 3 credits, Achieved. Outcome 1 alcohol and intoxication (chemical impact, effects by age
  and gender, BAC, absorption factors, interaction with prescription and illegal drugs); outcome 2
  responsibilities (offences and penalties for minors, intoxicated and disorderly patrons; suspension
  and cancellation; duty manager and server responsibilities; benefits of a responsible environment);
  outcome 3 safe drinking environment (overcrowding and evacuation, staff training and support,
  assessing intoxication, intervention: slowing service, diverting choice, selling alternatives,
  refusal; host responsibility policy; prohibited persons: verbal communication, body language,
  licensed crowd controllers, trespass notices, police, incident book). Legislation listed includes
  the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, Fire and Emergency New Zealand Act 2017, Trespass Act 1980
  and Building Act 2004. Standard texts: Health New Zealand's On-licensed premises toolkit and the
  HPA Host Responsibility Guide (resources.alcohol.org.nz).
- ServiceIQ LCQ pages (https://www.serviceiq.org.nz/licence-controller-qualification,
  https://www.serviceiq.org.nz/lcq/get-your-lcq-online): returned 403 to the fetcher; search
  snippets (checked 2026-11-05) say the LCQ is the two unit standards 4646 and 16705, must be held
  to apply for or renew a Manager's Certificate, and can be done online through ServiceIQ (online
  profile and ID upload). Course providers (polytechnics, hospitality associations) also run it.
- NZQA external evaluation report, provider 8434 (2019):
  https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/bin/providers/download/provider-reports/8434-2019.pdf
  "Assessments have been changed from a supervised, class-based exam to a closed book, online,
  secured assessment ... pre-moderated by ServiceIQ. Students are allowed multiple attempts."
  Gaining the LCQ is one of four requirements for a manager's certificate. (One provider, 2019:
  quoted as "providers' assessments", not a national rule.)
- NOT confirmed anywhere official: a question count, time limit or pass mark. Unit standards are
  graded Achieved / Not Achieved against the performance criteria, and each provider writes its own
  assessment. Tigertest's mock tests (50 questions, 80% readiness line) are Tigertest's target and
  are labelled as such on the landing and in examFacts (as zak53 does).

## Content sources for the bank
- Ministry of Justice, "Key points for the public" (updated 13 December 2024):
  https://www.justice.govt.nz/justice-sector-policy/key-initiatives/sale-and-supply-of-alcohol/key-points-for-the-public/
  Default hours on-licences and clubs 8am to 4am, off-licences 7am to 11pm, LAPs may differ; supply
  to under-18s only by a parent or guardian, with their express consent (conversation, email or
  text believed genuine), or to a minor who is married, in a civil union or de facto, and responsibly;
  infringement offences for fake ID, using someone else's ID and lending ID to a minor; venues may not
  serve intoxicated people or let them stay; definition of intoxicated (two or more of appearance,
  behaviour, coordination, speech).
- Health New Zealand alcohol resources (formerly HPA), Intoxication Assessment Tool AL804/AL805,
  April 2024: https://resources.alcohol.org.nz/assets/Uploads/6.0-AL804-A6-Intoxication-Assessment-Tool.pdf
  Three stages Sober / Influenced / Intoxicated across speech, coordination, appearance, behaviour;
  actions monitor, intervene, deny and remove (checked via search snippets).
- Licensed premises signs, resources.alcohol.org.nz AL984 (June 2024):
  https://resources.alcohol.org.nz/assets/Uploads/10.0-AL984-Licensed-premises-signs-JUN-2024.pdf
  Accepted ID: current NZ driver licence, passport, HANZ 18+ card or Kiwi Access card. Overseas
  driver licences are not approved evidence of age (Kiwi Access FAQ, https://kiwiaccess.co.nz/).
- New Plymouth District Council, Manager's certificates (fetched 2026-11-05):
  https://www.npdc.govt.nz/services/licenses-and-regulations/alcohol-licences/managers-certificates/
  Applicant over 20, LCQ and at least six months' experience, police questionnaire, police and
  inspector report on suitability; certificates renewed every three years, renew before expiry,
  show continued involvement; temporary or acting managers when a manager is ill, absent, dismissed
  or resigns, with notice to the DLC.
- Council notices of management change (South Waikato, Wairoa, Christchurch), via search 2026-11-05:
  notice of appointment or termination of a manager to the DLC and police within 48 hours; no notice
  for a temporary or acting appointment of 48 consecutive hours or less, but record it in the
  register (s 231(3)); a temporary manager must apply for a manager's certificate within two working
  days. A first certificate runs one year (council pages), renewals three years.
- Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act 2012 (legislation.govt.nz blocked the fetcher with a WAF; points
  taken from the official summaries above and settled knowledge of the Act): object (s 4); harm;
  DLCs, ARLA, inspectors appointed by the territorial authority, police and Medical Officer of
  Health as reporting agencies; LAP contents (hours, location, density, one-way door, discretionary
  conditions); criteria for licences (suitability, LAP, hours, design and layout, amenity and good
  order, systems, staff and training); supermarkets and grocery stores limited to beer, wine, mead
  and fruit or vegetable wine, in a single area; on-licence duties on food, free water, low and
  non-alcoholic drinks and transport information; licence and manager signs; restricted and
  supervised areas; BYO and caterer's endorsements; temporary authorities; remote sellers; offences
  for sales to minors and intoxicated people, allowing intoxication, irresponsible promotions
  (excessive consumption, special appeal to minors, free alcohol, 25% discounts advertised outside);
  holdings; powers of entry; police closure. Not used: exact closure duration, licence terms,
  acting-manager week limits, fines and fees.
- NZ Transport Agency drink-driving limits: 250 micrograms per litre of breath / 50 mg per 100 ml
  of blood for drivers 20 and over; zero for under-20s (Land Transport Act 1998 as amended 2014;
  settled law, page not fetched).
- Trespass Act 1980: warning to leave (s 3) and warning to stay off for two years (s 4).
- Private Security Personnel and Private Investigators Act 2010: crowd controllers need a licence or
  certificate of approval.
- Health and Safety at Work Act 2015: PCBU duty to workers and others; notifiable injuries to WorkSafe.
- Fire and Emergency New Zealand: approves building evacuation schemes.
- NZ standard drink: 10 g of pure alcohol; litres x % ABV x 0.789 (density of ethanol); low-risk
  advice includes at least two alcohol-free days a week (Health New Zealand alcohol resources).

## Fact-check, 2026-11-05 (10 law items spot-checked in q_lic.py and q_off.py)
legislation.govt.nz and NZLII returned 403 or a bot challenge to our fetcher, so the Act was
checked through the Ministry of Justice summary and Health New Zealand (HPA) guidance:
- Ministry of Justice, "Key points for businesses", checked 2026-11-05:
  https://www.justice.govt.nz/justice-sector-policy/key-initiatives/key-initiatives-archive/sale-and-supply-of-alcohol/key-points-for-businesses/
- HPA, "Alcohol promotions on licensed premises" (April 2024), checked 2026-11-05:
  https://alcoholresources-prod.cloud.hpa.org.nz/assets/Uploads/6.0-554-AL_Promotions-on-license_APR-2024.pdf
1. Default maximum hours, on-licences and clubs 8am to 4am (LIC): confirmed (MoJ, s 43).
2. Default maximum hours, off-licences 7am to 11pm (LIC): confirmed (MoJ, s 43).
3. Supermarket and grocery alcohol kept to a single area (LIC): confirmed (MoJ).
4. On-licences must have low and non-alcoholic drinks, food and transport information, and free
   water (LIC): confirmed (MoJ).
5. Advertising a discount of 25% or more where it can be seen or heard outside the premises
   (OFF): confirmed (MoJ, HPA; s 237).
6. Promotions encouraging excessive consumption or with special appeal to minors (OFF):
   confirmed (MoJ, HPA).
7. Free alcohol (OFF): the offence is promoting or advertising free alcohol so it can be seen or
   heard from outside the premises (MoJ, HPA). FIXED: the item now says the offer is on a street
   sign and the key says "An offence, as it is seen outside" (it said "generally an offence").
8. Evidence of age documents (OFF): passport, NZ driver licence, Kiwi Access card (Sale and Supply
   of Alcohol Regulations 2013 reg 4), plus, from 28 May 2026, an approved digital ID credential
   under the Sale and Supply of Alcohol Amendment Regulations 2026 (reg 4(d), reg 5A).
   Harkness Henry, "Digital identification for alcohol purchases", 8 June 2026, checked 2026-11-05:
   https://www.harknesshenry.co.nz/2026/06/08/digital-identification-for-alcohol-purchases/
   Regulations: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2026/90/en/latest/
   FIXED: the explanation listing the documents now adds the digital credential, and the phone-photo
   item's key is now "Ask for approved ID instead" (it said "the original document", which is no
   longer the only lawful option).
9. Minor means under 18; overseas driver licences are not approved; expired documents are not
   approved (OFF): confirmed (reg 4 lists a current passport of any country and a current NZ driver
   licence only).
10. Express consent from a parent or guardian, by text if the supplier has good reason to believe
   it genuine, for supply off licensed premises (OFF, s 241): confirmed against HPA, "What the
   Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act means for under 18s",
   https://alcoholresources-prod.cloud.hpa.org.nz/assets/What-Sale-and-Supply-Alcohol-Act-means-for-under-18_s-March-2014.pdf ,
   checked 2026-11-05.
