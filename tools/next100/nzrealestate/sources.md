# NZ Real Estate Salesperson sources (checked 2026-11-16)

Every question in `data/nz-real-estate-questions.json` is original, written from the primary texts
below. No assessment item, provider quiz, past paper or prep-site question was read or used.

## Qualification format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- Real Estate Authority (REA), "Required criteria and qualifications to work in real estate" (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/real-estate-professionals/apply-for-your-licence/required-criteria-and-qualifications-to-work-in-real-estate/
  The qualification for a salesperson licence is the New Zealand Certificate in Real Estate
  (Salesperson) (Level 4); approved providers listed are REINZ, Skills Institute, Strategi, Open
  Polytechnic and Toi Ohomai. Usable if gained less than three years ago; older holders must satisfy
  the Registrar of sufficient knowledge. The earlier National Certificate is no longer offered. No
  experience requirement for salespersons. Applicants must be 18 or older, fit and proper, and
  consent to a criminal conviction history check. The page does not list unit standards or assessments.
- NZQA qualification review summary, May 2025 (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nqfdocs/summaries/2025/may25/revsummay25-12.html
  Qualification reference 3111, 50 credits, version 2 moving to version 3, developer Ringa Hora
  Services Workforce Development Council; last date of assessment for the current version 31 May 2027.
  The graduate profile now covers residential, business broking, rural, and commercial and industrial
  real estate. The summary gives no topics or learning outcomes.
- NZQA review summary, December 2020 (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/nqfdocs/summaries/2020/dec20/revsumdec20-02.html
- NOT published anywhere official that we could read: a national question count, time or pass mark.
  Providers assess their own courses. The 50-question tests and 80% readiness line are Tigertest's.
  Provider programme regulations (Unitec, via search) describe three compulsory courses:
  Fundamentals of Real Estate (15 credits), Law for Real Estate Licensees (15) and Real Estate Sales
  and Marketing (20). That is a provider listing, used only for the shape of the blueprint.

## Primary law and rules the bank is written from
- Real Estate Agents Act 2008 (version as at 28 October 2021, the REA-hosted copy; legislation.govt.nz
  returned 403 to the fetcher; fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/Real-Estate-Agents-Act-2008.pdf
  Used: s 4 definitions (real estate agency work, transaction, residential property, sole agency
  agreement, licence classes); s 6 to 9 (licensed or exempt; lawyers and conveyancers, auctioneers);
  s 12 to 15 (Authority functions, practice rules, continuing education); s 36 to 37 (entitlement and
  prohibitions); s 46 to 52 (term, no transfer, agent and salesperson licences, supervision,
  employment status, renewal); s 72 to 74, 93, 110 (unsatisfactory conduct, misconduct, complaints,
  committee and Tribunal orders; licence cancellation, suspension up to 24 months); s 121 to 125
  (name display, money received, 10 working days, account within 28 days, audit); s 126 to 133
  (agency agreements, approved guide, 5 pm next working day cancellation, 90 days, copies); s 134 to
  137 (acquisition by licensee, valuation, disclosure of benefit, related persons); s 141 to 143,
  149 to 151 (offences). Amounts of fines, compensation and levies are deliberately not used.
- Real Estate Agents Act (Professional Conduct and Client Care) Rules 2012 (SR 2012/413, REA-hosted
  copy, fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Real-Estate-Agents-Act-Professional-Conduct-and-Client-Care-Rules-2012-1.pdf
  Used: rules 5 (competence), 6 (conduct), 7 (reporting), 8 (agents' duties), 9 (client and customer
  care, agency agreements, conflicts, confidentiality), 10 (sellers' agents: appraisals, defects,
  advertising, offers, 12-month retention), 11 (buyers' agents), 12 (complaints procedures, 10
  working days). Later amendments to the Rules (for example to the Privacy Act reference) are not
  relied on; rule 9.17's privacy wording and any rule changed since 2012 are left out.
- REA, "Understanding key concepts" CPD material, version 2.0 (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/REA-CPD-Key-Concepts.pdf
  Agency work begins when work aimed at a transaction begins; House v REAA [2013] NZHC 1619 (agency
  work continues until settlement); marketing only after a signed agency agreement; rules set minimum
  standards; charge may be brought without a specific rule.
- REA, "Agency agreements" CPD material, v3.0 (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/REA-CPD-Agency-agreements-v3.0F.pdf
  Sole and general agency; agreements in writing; commission and the REA standard clauses; roll-over
  clauses; s 130 and s 131 cancellation.
- REA, "Sale and purchase agreements and lease agreements: Contractual obligations" CPD material
  (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/REA-CPD-Sale-purchase-agreements-contractual-obligations.pdf
  ADLS/REINZ forms; contents of an agreement; conditions (title search, finance, valuation, LIM,
  building inspection, engineer, sale of another property, Overseas Investment Office consent);
  conditional and unconditional; risk and insurance; GST status; signatory capacity and powers of
  attorney; Property Law Act 2007 implied lease covenants; Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017
  ss 24 to 37 (mistake, misrepresentation, cancellation, damages) and remedies on default.
- REA, "New Zealand Residential Property Sale and Purchase Agreement Guide" (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/Guides/Sale-and-purchase-agreement-guide.pdf
  What an agreement contains; buyer conditions; conditional and unconditional; deposit held in the
  agency's trust account for 10 working days; pre-settlement inspection; commission paid by the
  seller, never the buyer; tenanted property and vacant possession; guide and written acknowledgement.
- REA, "Council compliance: Essential documentation" CPD material (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://www.rea.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/Resources/REA-CPD-Council-compliance.pdf
  LIM contents and limits; property file; RMA and Building Act roles; the five activity categories;
  code compliance certificate, certificate of acceptance, safe and sanitary report, notice to fix.
- REA, "Other laws and legislation" (fetched 2026-11-16):
  https://rea.govt.nz/real-estate-professionals/obligations/other-laws-and-legislation
  AML/CFT Act 2009 applies to real estate agents from 1 January 2019 (customer due diligence, risk
  profile, compliance programme); Fair Trading Act 1986 s 14 and auction rules (ss 36X to 36ZF);
  Building Act 2004 warrant of fitness; Health and Safety at Work Act 2015; Overseas Investment Act
  (overseas persons cannot generally buy existing residential homes without consent; citizens and
  people ordinarily resident do not need consent); Privacy Act 2020; Residential Tenancies Act 1986;
  Unit Titles Act 2010 (disclosure); local council bylaws.

## Could not be loaded
- legislation.govt.nz (403): the Unit Titles Act 2010, Property Law Act 2007, Land Transfer Act 2017,
  Residential Tenancies Act 1986, Fair Trading Act 1986, AML/CFT Act 2009 and Overseas Investment Act
  2005 were NOT read in full. They are asked only at the concept level REA's own pages state. The
  Registrar's current licence fee, licence term and CPD hours were not read and are not asked.
- NZQA's own qualification record and the Ringa Hora record (no topics or unit standards listed on
  the pages read).

## Deliberately left out
Fees, commission rates and percentages, fines and compensation amounts, licence term and CPD hours,
tax rates, Overseas Investment Office thresholds, office-holders, and any question about the
assessment itself. Rules and sections changed after the 2021 Act copy (check before relying on the
Act's section numbers: explanations cite sections only where this copy shows them).
