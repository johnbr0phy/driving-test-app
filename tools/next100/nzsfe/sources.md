Web search was unavailable for this build (the session's search budget was used up), so every

## Read from the Nursing Council of New Zealand (Te Kaunihera Tapuhi o Aotearoa)
- Register as a nurse (https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/Public/NCNZ/nursing-section/Register_as_a_nurse.aspx),
  read 2026-11-05:
  - Applicants who complete an approved programme in New Zealand "can apply for registration as a
    nurse in the scope of practice for which their qualification is prescribed. You will need to
    supply: state final examination results", "evidence of support for registration from the head
    of the school of nursing", and two referees.
  - "The Nursing Council registers successful applicants as soon as practicable after they pass the
    examination. Nurses are then required to apply for an annual practising certificate."
  - The prescribed qualification lists include "a pass in a state final examination for registered
    and enrolled nurses set by the Nursing Council of New Zealand".
- Fees (https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/Public/Fees/NCNZ/Registration-section/Fees.aspx), read
  2026-11-05: lists an "Examination fees" line for registered and enrolled nurses that includes the
  New Zealand registration fee. (Confirms the exam exists; the amount is not used anywhere.)
- Registered nurse page (https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/Public/NCNZ/nursing-section/Registered_nurse.aspx),
  read 2026-11-05: links the "Standards of competence for registered nurses 2025" and the scope.
- Registered nurse scope of practice, January 2025 (PDF,
  https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/Registered%20nurse/Scope-practice-RN-2025.pdf):
  RNs "uphold and enact ngā mātāpono, principles of Te Tiriti o Waitangi, based on the kawa
  whakaruruhau framework and cultural safety"; "develop differential diagnoses"; "are responsible and
  accountable for directing and delegating to members of the healthcare team" and "provide support
  and guidance to enrolled nurses".
- Standards of competence for registered nurses (PDF NCNZ031, February 2025,
  https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/Registered%20nurse/NCNZ031-Competencies-RN-11.pdf):
  six pou: Māori health; cultural safety; whanaungatanga and communication; pūkengatanga and
  evidence-informed nursing practice; manaakitanga and people-centred care; rangatiratanga and
  leadership.
- FAQs registered nurse standards of competence (PDF,
  https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/Registered%20nurse/FAQs%20registered%20nurse%20standards%202025.pdf):
  standards in effect "1 April 2025 for all new annual practising certificates and recertification
  audit requirements" and "1 January 2026 for all other purposes"; definitions of differential
  diagnosis, kawa whakaruruhau and whakapapa-centred care (used in TIR and ASM items).

## Not found on the Council's public site (checked 2026-11-05)
The Council's public pages we could reach have no page describing the State Final Examination's
number of questions, time allowed, format (computer or paper), sittings per year or pass standard;
several old exam page addresses return 404. So:
- The four 50-question mock tests are Tigertest's format, not the real exam's.
- 70% is Tigertest's readiness target, not a Council pass mark.
- The topic split is Tigertest's, organised around the 2025 standards and the clinical content of
  a New Zealand Bachelor of Nursing; the Council publishes no blueprint we could find.

## Blueprint used (Tigertest's, labelled as such)

child, maternal and whānau health 4; leadership, safety and communication 4. Pools 20/24/24/28/36/20/16/16/16.
