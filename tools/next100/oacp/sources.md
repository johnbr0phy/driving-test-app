# Sources: OACP Certificate (Ontario police constable pre-screening), `oacp`

Every question in the bank is original. OACP and SIGMA publish no sample items; the study
materials sent after purchase were not used, nor were any prep site's practice questions
(JobTestPrep, practicetestgeeks, open-exam-prep, policeprep and the like), even reworded. Pages
fetched 2026-11-21 with WebFetch.

## Official: format of the OACP Certificate (Ontario Association of Chiefs of Police)

- Constable Selection System: https://oacp.ca/en/careers-in-policing/constable-selection-system.aspx
  - Fetched 2026-11-21. Part B online assessment (SIGMA Survey for Police Officers, SSPO): 74
    multiple-choice questions in two sections in 35 minutes. Section A, 44 questions on spelling,
    vocabulary, punctuation and grammar. Section B, 30 questions on police problem-solving; the
    quantitative section has been removed and math is limited to basic-operation word problems,
    "not the focus". Part C, a personality assessment (ESQ2), most-like and least-like statements,
    untimed, 30 to 50 minutes. Parts B and C in one proctored sitting of 60 to 90 minutes, no
    calculator, blank paper allowed. No pass/fail; results go to the police services the applicant
    applies to and are compared with the applicant pool; unanswered questions are unscored and
    wrong answers carry a penalty. Certificate valid one year, retest from ten months. PREP is no
    longer required at pre-screening. Fee $250 plus tax (landing only).
- OACP Certificate: https://www.oacp.ca/en/careers-in-policing/oacp-certificate.aspx
  - Fetched 2026-11-21. Certificate expires twelve months after issue; fitness log of 14
    consecutive days; vision and hearing medical package valid two years; proctoring vendors named
    (ProctorFree, eProctor). The two OACP pages name different proctoring vendors, so the copy names none.
- OACP jobs FAQ: https://jobs.oacp.ca/faqs/
  - Fetched 2026-11-21. Certificate $250 plus tax, valid one year, retest after ten months,
    two-week fitness log.
- OACP pilot with the RCMP: https://www.oacp.ca/en/news/rcmp-collaborate-with-oacp-to-launch-pilot-project-for-pre-screening-certificate-of-completion.aspx
  - Found by search 2026-11-21, not fetched: the RCMP piloted accepting the OACP certificate for
    Ontario applicants. Not stated on the landing beyond "check with the service".

## What the questions are written from

- Section A (spelling, vocabulary, punctuation, grammar): standard Canadian English usage. Spelling
  items avoid every word with an accepted Canadian, US or UK variant (colour, licence, cheque,
  judgment, offence, jewellery, travelled and the like), so the key is the only spelling in any
  dictionary. Punctuation items avoid contested rules (serial comma, single vs double quotes).
- Section B (police problem-solving): OACP names the section but does not describe its item types.
  Tigertest's items train the reasoning the name describes: applying a rule or service policy
  stated in the stem, reading short occurrence reports and witness statements, and basic-operation
  word problems with no calculator. Every item is answerable from the stem; none tests real Ontario
  or Canadian law, police procedure or the Criminal Code, and every policy, person and place is
  fictional.

## Left out

- Part C personality assessment (no right answers to practise), the fitness log, vision and
  hearing standards and PREP (landing only).
- The wrong-answer penalty is explained on the landing, not applied in scoring.
- Fees, vendor names and the RCMP pilot are kept out of questions.
