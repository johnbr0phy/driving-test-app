## Content
English and maths are at matric level, British spelling as used in Pakistan, rupees (Rs),
Pakistani names. Non-verbal reasoning (mirror and water images, rotation, folding, counting
shapes, cubes) is written as text, because the real section uses pictures. GK lane in this batch:
the Army's ranks, corps and institutions; everyday science and first aid; Islamic studies basics
(mainstream, non-sectarian); Pakistan's national symbols. No current affairs, office-holders,
conflicts, Kashmir or recent constitutional amendments.
