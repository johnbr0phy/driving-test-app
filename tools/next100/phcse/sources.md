# Philippine Civil Service Exam, CSE-PPT (`phcse`) sources

All checked 2026-11-01. The Civil Service Commission's own site (csc.gov.ph) answered 403 (Cloudflare) to every
request from this session, including the examination announcement pages and PDFs, so the CSC figures below come
from CSC-quoting news and review sites. Each is marked with where it came from.

## Pattern followed

**Civil Service Commission, Career Service Examination, Pen and Paper Test (CSE-PPT), Professional and
Subprofessional levels, 2026 sittings of 8 March and 9 August 2026.** Schedule set by CSC Examination Announcement
No. 08, s. 2025; the August sitting by Examination Announcement No. 03, s. 2026 (issued 20 April 2026).

- Schedule and announcement numbers: Daily Tribune, "CSC releases 2026 civil service exam schedule and application
  timelines", 27 Oct 2025, https://tribune.net.ph/2025/10/27/csc-releases-2026-civil-service-exam-schedule-and-application-timelines
  (Announcement No. 08, s. 2025; CSE-PPT on 8 March and 9 August 2026); The Summit Express,
  https://www.thesummitexpress.com/2025/10/august-2026-civil-service-exam-cse-ppt-application-schedule-requirements.html
  (Announcement No. 03, s. 2026 of 20 April; passing grade 80.00 general rating; eligibility conferred; who may take it).
- Scope per level and number of items, CSE.ph summary of the August 2026 announcement,
  https://cse.ph/?p=25655 (quoting the CSC: "For Professional level, 170 items in a 3 hours and 10 minutes time"; "For
  Sub-Professional level, 165 items in a 2 hours and 40 minutes time"; "the examinee must get an overall score of
  80.00%"), and the March 2026 scope page https://cse.ph/?p=25380:
  - Both levels: General Information (Philippine Constitution; Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public
    Officials and Employees, R.A. 6713; peace and human rights issues and concepts; environment management and
    protection), Verbal Ability (vocabulary, grammar and correct usage, paragraph organization, reading
    comprehension), Numerical Ability (basic operations, number sequences, word problems).
  - Professional only: Analytical Ability (word analogy, symbolic logic or abstract reasoning, identifying
    assumptions and drawing conclusions, data interpretation).
  - Subprofessional only: Clerical Ability (filing, spelling).
  - Language: Verbal Ability in English and Filipino; the other parts in English.
- Test proper vs questionnaire: open-exam-prep.com (a review site) says the 170 and 165 totals include 20
  Examinee Descriptive Questionnaire items on personal data, leaving 150 and 145 test items. Not confirmed from the
  CSC; the copy says "guides say" or leaves it out.
- Eligibility: CSC news pages surfaced by search (e.g. https://csc.gov.ph/26k-hurdle-august-7-civil-service-exams)
  say Professional passers get the Career Service Professional Eligibility, for first and second level positions,
  and Subprofessional passers the Career Service Subprofessional Eligibility, for first level positions only. Read
  in the search snippet only (the page itself is behind Cloudflare).
- Wrong answers and calculators: no CSC statement found. Review sites (3d-universal.com, open-exam-prep.com) say
  wrong answers are not penalised and calculators are not allowed. The landing says "study guides say" for
  calculators and does not mention a guessing penalty.
- Weights of each part: not published by the CSC. The 30/25/25/20 split of the practice tests is Tigertest's own.
- Pass rates and the fee are left out (they change each sitting; no money figures).

## General information facts

- 1987 Constitution, official text: Official Gazette, https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/constitutions/1987-constitution/
  (Article II Secs 1, 3, 6, 16; Article III Secs 12, 20; Article V Sec 1; Article VI Sec 2; Article VII Secs 4, 18;
  Article VIII Sec 4; Article IX-B Secs 2, 6; Article XI Secs 1 to 3; Article XIII Sec 17; Article XIV Sec 6;
  Article XVII Sec 2). Ratified in the plebiscite of 2 February 1987.
- R.A. 6713 (approved 20 February 1989), Lawphil, https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1989/ra_6713_1989.html,
  read 2026-11-01: Sec 3 definitions (gift; relatives within the fourth civil degree, including bilas, inso and
  balae); Sec 4 the eight norms of conduct; Sec 5(a) reply within 15 working days, 5(b) annual performance reports
  within 45 working days, 5(c) not more than three signatories; Sec 7 prohibitions continue one year after
  separation; Sec 8 SALN within 30 days of assuming office, on or before April 30 yearly, within 30 days after
  separation; Sec 9 resign within 30 days and divest within 60 days; Sec 12 CSC administers and enforces.
- Laws named in items (by number and year only): R.A. 9003 (2000), R.A. 8749 (1999), R.A. 9275 (2004), R.A. 9729
  (2009), R.A. 10121 (2010), R.A. 9262 (2004), R.A. 11313 Safe Spaces Act (2019), R.A. 9745 Anti-Torture Act (2009),
  R.A. 9208 Anti-Trafficking (2003), R.A. 11054 Bangsamoro Organic Law (2018, plebiscite 2019), R.A. 6734 (1989),
  R.A. 3019, R.A. 7160; the Supreme Court's Rules of Procedure for Environmental Cases (2010, writ of kalikasan).
  All from Lawphil and the Official Gazette.
- UN: Universal Declaration of Human Rights adopted 10 December 1948; Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989),
  ratified by the Philippines in 1990.

## What the bank covers and leaves out

- 245 original four-option items: two 100-item practice tests (Test 1 Professional shape, Test 2 Subprofessional
  shape, held out of the drills) plus 45 drill-only items (21 of them clerical, since Test 2 holds the only other
  clerical items).
- English only. The real Verbal Ability part also has Filipino items; not modelled. Figure-based abstract
  reasoning is written as letter patterns and coding instead. The personal-data questionnaire is not modelled.
- No current affairs, officeholders, pass rates, fees or fines.
- Names, prices and data tables in items are invented.
