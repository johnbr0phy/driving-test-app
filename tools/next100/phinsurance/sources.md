# Philippines Insurance Commission licensing exam, traditional life agent: sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

Official, confirmed this session:
- Amended Insurance Code, R.A. 10607 (2013), full text on the Lawphil Project:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2013/ra_10607_2013.html (downloaded and read 2026-11-05).
  Section 307: no one may act as an insurance agent, or receive commission, without a licence from
  the Insurance Commissioner; the application for an agent's licence is endorsed by the company the
  person wants to represent; the licence is renewed every three years and expires after December 31
  of the third year after issuance. Section 312: the Commissioner requires every applicant to pass a
  written examination, and may delegate its administration to an independent organisation.
  Section 313: applicants must be of good moral character, not convicted of a crime involving moral
  turpitude, and trained in the kind of insurance applied for; the exam may be waived for extensive
  education or training.
- IC Circular Letter No. 2021-01 (January 2021) and No. 2021-08 (16 February 2021), "Guidelines in the
  conduct of off-site special qualifying insurance agents' examinations" (scanned copies on the UP Law
  Center site: https://law.upd.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/IC-Circular-Letter-No-2021-01.pdf and
  https://law.upd.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/IC-Circular-Letter-No-2021-08.pdf). Confirms the
  Commission runs "qualifying insurance agents' examinations" of several types, requested by the
  insurance company for its listed examinees, with an exam fee per type (fee left out by house rule),
  paper answer sheets at proctored off-site venues. No item count, time or pass mark is stated.
- IC Circular Letter No. 2024-21 (21 October 2024), "Declaring the provisional authority to sell or
  solicit insurance business pending issuance of regular license inoperative" (Grant Thornton copy:
  https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/globalassets/_markets_/phl/media/revamp/technical-alerts/pdf-files/ic-circular-letter-no.-2024-21.pdf,
  read as page images). Supersedes CL 11-1999, 3-2009 and 33-2011; reiterates that no person can act
  as an insurance agent without a licence from the Commission; refers to the Enhanced Licensing System
  (CL 2016-04, 1 February 2016). Used in item PHINS-AGT-007.
- Search results (not opened: insurance.gov.ph returns 403 to this session) name IC CL 2021-15,
  "Conduct of insurance agents' qualifying examinations through the Online Agents' Computerized
  Examinations (Online ACE) System" (8 March 2021) and an IC press release of 7 May 2021: life,
  variable life and non-life agent exams online, proctored by video, examinees registered by the
  insurer they will represent. Rechecked 2026-11-05: a web search lists the IC's own files for these,
  https://www.insurance.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/CL2021_15.pdf,
  https://www.insurance.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/IC-Press-Release-7-May-2021.pdf and
  Licensing Division advisories 2021-001 to 2021-003 on the Online ACE System; the PDFs still return
  403 here, so the existence of Online ACE is taken from the IC's own file titles and snippets.

Guides (not official), checked 2026-11-05:
- https://open-exam-prep.com/practice/ph-ic-life-traditional and
  https://assistance.ph/insurance-commission-licensure-exam/ say the traditional life exam has
  50 multiple-choice (and true or false) items, about 1 hour, and 70% (35 of 50) to pass; one says
  70% "in each section". Older IC memoranda (Insurance Memorandum Circular 2-86, 3-93, via jur.ph
  summaries) also set 70%. The Commission's current figure could not be confirmed, so the landing and
  examFacts say "guides report" and the 70% line here is labelled Tigertest's target.
- The Commission publishes no syllabus or topic weights for the traditional life exam. The blueprint
  (principles 5, contract 8, life insurance law 8, products 8, premiums and values 6, underwriting and
  claims 5, agents 6, regulation 4 per 50) is Tigertest's.
- Variable life (VUL) is a separate exam and licence: R.A. 10607 Section 316 bars selling a variable
  contract without a licence "to act as a variable contract agent", issued only after the
  Commissioner is satisfied "after examination" (Lawphil text above, read 2026-11-05); variable
  contracts are defined in Section 238. Used in PHINS-PRD-010 and landing FAQ 3. Not modelled here.
- Item count (50) and time (about 1 hour): rechecked 2026-11-05, still only in guides
  (open-exam-prep.com above); no IC page found. Landing and examFacts keep "guides report".

## Content sources for the bank (all R.A. 10607 sections read in the Lawphil text above)
- Sections 2, 3 (spouse consent, vesting on death of an original owner), 4 (lotteries), 7 (public
  enemy), 10, 11, 12, 19, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30, 31, 33, 35, 36, 37, 41, 45, 48, 49, 50 (riders, countersign,
  electronic form under R.A. 8792), 64, 70, 71, 76, 77, 78, 79, 82, 86, 89.
- Title 5 Life insurance: Sections 181 to 186 (annuities and endowments are life contracts; parent or
  relative acting for a minor beneficiary; suicide after two years, insanity compensable; transfer by
  will; notice of transfer; sum fixed in the policy).
- Section 187 microinsurance (7.5% of the daily minimum wage cap; no peso figures used).
- Section 228 (reinsuring a whole individual life needs the Commissioner's written permission),
  Section 232 (approval of policy forms), Section 233 (individual life policy provisions: grace period
  30 days or one month, interest up to 6% a year; incontestable after two years except nonpayment and
  war service; entire contract; misstatement of age; divisible surplus; options after three full annual
  premiums; surrender charge up to one-fifth of the reserve or 2.5% of the amount insured; policy loans,
  deferral up to six months; 20-year table; automatic option; instalment table; reinstatement within
  three years), Section 234 (group life: grace, incontestability, representations not warranties,
  conversion within 30 days without evidence, not to term; five years on termination of the group
  policy; death in the conversion period; creditor groups), Sections 235 and 236 (industrial life:
  premiums monthly or oftener, four-week grace).
- Sections 247 to 251 (unfair claim settlement practices; death claims within 60 days of proof of death;
  interest at twice the Monetary Board ceiling for delay; fraudulent claims unlawful), 254 and 256
  (suspension, receivership), 307 to 315 (agents, brokers, licensing, fiduciary premiums, delivery of
  policy authorises the agent to collect), 370 to 372 (rebating, inducements, racial discrimination,
  misleading illustrations and twisting, each policy a separate offence), 374 (code of conduct),
  375 (bancassurance), 378 (Security Fund), 437 (Commissioner appointed by the President).
- Civil Code (R.A. 386), Article 2012 with Article 739, read 2026-11-05 at
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1949/ra_386_1949.html: Art. 739(1) voids donations "between
  persons who were guilty of adultery or concubinage at the time of the donation"; Art. 2012: "Any
  person who is forbidden from receiving any donation under article 739 cannot be named beneficiary of
  a life insurance policy". PHINS-LLW-008.
- Executive Order No. 192 (12 November 2015) transferring HMO regulation from the DOH to the Insurance
  Commission: https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo2015/eo_192_2015.html (search result summary,
  2026-11-05) and https://www.dof.gov.ph/hmo-regulation-transferred-from-doh-to-ic/.
- Pre-need Code, R.A. 9829 (2009), read 2026-11-05 at
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2009/ra_9829_2009.html: Section 5, pre-need companies "under
  the primary and exclusive supervision and regulation of the Insurance Commission"; Section 4(b),
  pre-need plans include "life, pension, education, interment and other plans". PHINS-PRD-030,
  PHINS-REG-004.
- Anti-Money Laundering Act, R.A. 9160, read 2026-11-05 at
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2001/ra_9160_2001.html: Section 3(a)(2) covered institutions
  include "Insurance companies and all other institutions supervised or regulated by the Insurance
  Commission"; Section 9(b) records kept five years; Section 9(c) reports to the AMLC and the bar on
  disclosing that a report was made (tipping off). Later amendments (R.A. 9194, 10167, 10365, 10927,
  11521) keep these duties. PHINS-REG-011 to PHINS-REG-013.
- Data Privacy Act of 2012, R.A. 10173, read 2026-11-05 at
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10173_2012.html: Section 7 creates the National
  Privacy Commission "to administer and implement the provisions of this Act". PHINS-AGT-024.
- Financial Products and Services Consumer Protection Act, R.A. 11765 (2022), read 2026-11-05 at
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2022/ra_11765_2022.html: Section 3(d) financial regulators
  are the BSP, SEC, Insurance Commission and CDA; Section 2 lists the rights to equitable and fair
  treatment, disclosure and transparency, protection of assets, data privacy, and timely handling and
  redress of complaints. PHINS-REG-014, PHINS-REG-015, landing FAQ 5.
- Complaints: R.A. 10607 Section 439 (Lawphil text above, 2026-11-05) gives the Commissioner power to
  adjudicate claims and complaints filed by the insured against an insurer, concurrent with the civil
  courts. PHINS-REG-016.
- IC attached to the Department of Finance: the Administrative Code of 1987 (E.O. 292) lists the IC
  as a DOF attached agency, per a Senate issuances-library document found 2026-11-05
  (https://issuances-library.senate.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2023-05/3818634624%21.pdf, search
  summary) and the DOF's own pages (https://www.dof.gov.ph/recto-lauds-ic-for-transforming-ph-insurance-industry-into-a-robust-and-dependable-sector-for-filipinos/).
  PHINS-REG-001 and the examFacts "Run by" row.
- General life insurance practice (risk, hazards and risk methods, mutual companies, contract of
  adhesion, products, riders, dividend, nonforfeiture and settlement options, net amount at risk,
  automatic premium loan, underwriting classes and documents, good health at delivery): standard life
  insurance principles not tied to a Philippine source, written fresh and worded as general practice.
  Backdating, needs analysis and reporting a false answer (PHINS-UND-010, -011, -020) are framed as
  good practice, not as cited rules. "Sliding" in PHINS-AGT-014 is a US term used only as a wrong
  option; the key, twisting, rests on Section 371.
