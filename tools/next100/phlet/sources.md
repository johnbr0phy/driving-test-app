# Philippines LET (LEPT) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-01.

## Official

- PRC, Professional Regulatory Board for Professional Teachers, Annex A, Table of Specifications
  for the Licensure Examination for Professional Teachers (LEPT), the enhanced TOS adopted by Board
  Resolution No. 11, s. 2022 and applied from the March 2023 LEPT. Read directly (PDF):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2022%20-11%20Annex%20A%20PTeacher.pdf
  - General Education (Elementary), 150 items: A. Intellectual Competencies 40%, 60 items
    (Purposive Communication in English; Malayuning Komunikasyon sa Wikang Filipino; Science and
    Technology; Mathematics); B. Personal and Civic Responsibilities 30%, 45 items (Readings in
    Philippine History and Society; The Life and Works of Rizal; Ethics); C. Practical Skills
    Development 30%, 45 items (The Contemporary World; Art Appreciation; Understanding the Self).
  - Professional Education (Elementary), 150 items: A. Teaching Profession 15%, 23; B. The Teacher
    and the School Curriculum, Methods and Strategies of Teaching, Educational Technology 30%, 45;
    C. The Child and Adolescent Learners and Learning Principles 20%, 30; D. Assessment of Learning
    15%, 22; E. Field Study and Teaching Internship (incl. action research) 20%, 30.
  - Difficulty: easy 30%, moderate 50%, difficult 20%.
- PRC, Program of the LEPT on September 24, 2023 (PDF, read directly):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/exam%20program%20sept%202023%20lept%20(prof%20teachers)%20revised.pdf
  Elementary: General Education 8:00 to 10:00 a.m., 40%; Professional Education 11:00 a.m. to
  2:00 p.m., 60%. Secondary: 20% / 40% / Specialization 40% (3:00 to 6:30 p.m.).
- Board for Professional Teachers Resolution No. 228, s. 1996 (Supreme Court e-Library,
  https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/40411): to pass, a general average
  rating of at least 75%, with no rating below 50% in any subject; from the August 1996 exam.
- RA 7836 (Philippine Teachers Professionalization Act of 1994), lawphil.net: Sec. 14, the
  elementary exam has two parts, professional education and general education; Sec. 15,
  applicants at least 18. https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1994/ra_7836_1994.html
- RA 9293 (2004), amended Sec. 26: examinees who fail by not more than five percentage points
  below the passing general average may be para-teachers on a two-year special permit, renewable
  once, assigned where professional teachers are lacking.
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2004/ra_9293_2004.html

## Guides (labelled as such where used)

- 2026 dates, from guides citing PRC Resolution No. 2113: March 15, 2026 and September 20, 2026.
  The September 2026 programme is reported by guides with the same weights and session times as
  2023 (jontotheworld.com, schoolfinderph.com, checked 2026-11-01). Not used in the bank; the
  landing says "check PRC" for dates.
- Guides report that elementary examinees with a specialization (early childhood education,
  special needs education; Board Resolution No. 20, s. 2025) take 20/40/40 like secondary, and
  that Board Resolution No. 11, s. 2025 amended the 2022 TOS. I could not read either resolution;
  the 2022 TOS area weights are used and the Professional Education areas match what guides
  report for 2026 (open-exam-prep.com). Integrator: the landing labels this.
- RA 12027 (2024) ended mother tongue as the medium of instruction in the early grades, so no
  item asks about mother-tongue-based instruction.

## Reviewer check, 2026-11-01

- Board Resolution No. 11, s. 2025 exists: the UP Law Center ONAR database lists "Professional
  Regulatory Board for Professional Teachers Resolution No. 11, S. 2025 Amending Resolution No. 11
  (S. 2022) on the Enhanced Table of Specifications for the Subjects in the Licensure Examination
  for Professional Teachers" (https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/documents/8075, title only, page now 404).
  Its text and annex could not be found on prc.gov.ph. A review site (depedclub.com/?p=32338) says it
  links the Professional Education TOS to the PPST domains; no weights given. Not confirmed whether
  the area weights changed; the landing now says the 2022 weights are shown, the table was amended
  in 2025, and to check prc.gov.ph.
- Elementary with specialization (ECE, SPED): search summaries of the PRC September 2025 program
  report General Education 20%, Professional Education 40%, Specialization 40%; the PRC PDF itself
  was not opened. Labelled "guides report" on the landing.

## Laws and standards used in items (stable facts only)

RA 4670 (Magna Carta for Public School Teachers, 1966), RA 7836, RA 9293, RA 10157 (Kindergarten
Education Act, 2012), RA 10533 (Enhanced Basic Education Act, 2013), RA 11650 (inclusive education
for learners with disabilities, 2022), RA 1425 (Rizal Law, 1956), DepEd Order No. 42, s. 2017
(PPST: 7 domains, 37 strands, 4 career stages), the Code of Ethics for Professional Teachers.
Learning theory, curriculum and assessment items follow standard Philippine teacher education
course content (Piaget, Vygotsky, Erikson, Kohlberg, Bronfenbrenner, Bandura, Tyler, Taba, Dale,
ASSURE, SAMR, TPACK, item analysis).

All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the TOS above. No item is copied from a
past LET, a PRC sample or any commercial reviewer.
