# Philippines MTLE (Medical Technologist Licensure Examination) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-05.

## Official

- PRC, Professional Regulatory Board of Medical Technology, Resolution No. 13, s. 2023, "Adoption,
  Issuance and Promulgation of the Enhanced Tables of Specifications for the Subjects in the
  Medical Technologists Licensure Examination" (signed April 2023, published in the Business Mirror
  April 24, 2023, effective immediately; applies from the August 2023 MTLE; supersedes Res. No. 12,
  s. 2009). Read directly (PDF):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2023-13%20published_medtech.pdf
- Annex A, Tables of Specifications "as of November 2022", read directly (PDF):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2023-13%20published_Annex_TOS.pdf
  Every subject is 100 items, difficulty 30 easy / 50 moderate / 20 difficult.
  - Hematology 20%: A scientific and technical components 37 (sample management 5, analytic
    techniques incl. automation, flow cytometry, molecular 32); B blood cell production, structure
    and function 20; C routine evaluation of blood cells 9; D blood disorders 9; E coagulation 20
    (hemostasis 5, tests 10, disorders 5); F quality assurance and communication 5.
  - Blood Banking and Serology 20%: A blood group systems 14; B blood processing 15; C transfusion 4;
    D blood bank procedures 10; E hemolytic diseases 5; F immunity 20; G transplantation 3;
    H serologic tests 20 (bacterial/STD 5, viral 6, fungal 1, parasitic 2, tumor markers 3, immune
    disorders 3); I molecular 3; J quality management 6. (A to E = 48 items, F to J = 52.)
  - Clinical Chemistry 20%: A technical components 16 (sample management 4, analytic techniques 6,
    reagents and laboratory mathematics 6); B quality assurance 10 and safety 5; C metabolic tests 50
    (water and electrolytes 6, NPN 8, carbohydrates 8, lipids 8, proteins 6, liver 6, enzymes 8);
    D endocrine 10; E drugs, toxicology, TDM 6; F blood gas 3.
  - Clinical Microscopy 10%: urine 57; feces 3; CSF 5; seminal fluid 6; amniotic 2; synovial 2;
    serous 4; specimen collection and handling 10; microscopes and instruments 5; QA and safety 6.
  - Microbiology and Parasitology 20%: Microbiology 70% (bacteriology 45, mycology 4, virology 8,
    molecular 3, equipment 2, epidemiology and control 2, QC/QA/biosafety 6); Parasitology 30%
    (parasites 22: nematodes 8, trematodes 3, cestodes 3, protozoa 8; techniques 5; QC/QA 3).
  - Histopathologic Techniques, MT Laws and Ethics 10%: histology and general pathology 8;
    histopathologic techniques 40; cytologic techniques 10; autopsy 2; QC/QA 5; MT laws (RA 5527) 10;
    related laws 10 (RA 11166, RA 9165, RA 9288, RA 7170, RA 10173, RA 7719, RA 10912 and one more
    listed as "RA968", which we could not identify); code of ethics and bioethics 5; laboratory
    management 8 and research 2. (A to E = 65 items, F to I = 35.)
- PRC, Program of the Medical Technologists Licensure Examination on August 15 and 16, 2026
  (approved June 11, 2026), read directly (PDF):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/RA2026/RA-111621_MEDICAL%20TECHNOLOGIST_Rosales,%20Pangasinan_8-2026.pdf
  Saturday: Clinical Chemistry 8:00 to 10:00 a.m. (20%), Microbiology and Parasitology 11:00 a.m.
  to 1:00 p.m. (20%), Clinical Microscopy 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. (10%). Sunday: Hematology 8:00 to
  10:00 a.m. (20%), Blood Banking and Serology 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. (20%), Histopathologic
  Techniques, Medical Technology Laws and Ethics 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. (10%). Non-programmable
  calculators allowed (Commission Resolution No. 1809-2024). Pen and paper (pencils, ballpens).
- PRC news article, March 2026 MTLE results released (read directly 2026-11-05): confirms a
  March 2026 sitting in NCR and 14 other centers, results posted March 11, 2026:
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/article/march-2026-medical-technologists-licensure-examination-results-released-three-3-working
  The dates March 5 and 6, 2026 (9,317 examinees, 7,838 passers) are from news reports, e.g.
  https://www.thesummitexpress.com/2026/03/mtle-results-march-2026-medtech-licensure-exam-list-of-passers.html
  The landing says only "held in March and in August" (March from this article, August from the
  PRC program above), never the exact March dates.
- RA 5527 as amended by RA 6132, PD 498 and PD 1534, PRC consolidated text (read directly
  2026-11-05): https://www.prc.gov.ph/uploaded/documents/MEDICAL%20TECHNOLOGY%20LAW.PDF
  Sec. 7 (chairman a pathologist, two registered medical technologists), Sec. 17 (weights 20/20/20/
  20/10/10) and Sec. 19 read the same as the original: "a general average of at least seventy-five
  percent in the written test, with no rating below fifty percent in any of the major subjects:
  Provided, That the candidate has not failed in at least sixty percent of the subjects computed
  according to their relative weights." The amendments did not change the pass rule, so the
  landing and examFacts wording stands. Sec. 15 still says annually in August or September;
  practice is two sittings a year.
- RA 5527 original text (lawphil.net): https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1969/ra_5527_1969.html
- RA 9288, Newborn Screening Act of 2004 (lawphil.net), Sec. 6: screening after 24 hours of life but
  not later than three days; Sec. 7 refusal on religious grounds:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2004/ra_9288_2004.html (used for background; the bank's
  newborn screening item was dropped as a duplicate of the phnle bank).

## Related laws (read directly 2026-11-05, lawphil.net)

- RA 4688, Clinical Laboratory Act of 1966, Sec. 1: operators "shall register and secure a license
  annually at the office of the Secretary of Health" (PHMT-LAW-004):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1966/ra_4688_1966.html
- RA 7719, National Blood Services Act of 1994: Sec. 2(a) and 4 promote voluntary donation, Sec. 3(f)
  defines a voluntary donor as one giving "without monetary compensation", Sec. 8 blood from
  voluntary donors only, Sec. 7 commercial blood banks phased out over two years, extendable up to
  two more (PHMT-LAW-003): https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1994/ra_7719_1994.html
- RA 9165, Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002, Sec. 36: drug testing is a screening test
  plus a confirmatory test that confirms a positive screening result (PHMT-LAW-006):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2002/ra_9165_2002.html
- RA 10912, CPD Act of 2016, Sec. 10: "The CPD is hereby made as a mandatory requirement in the
  renewal of the PICs" (PHMT-LAW-005): https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2016/ra_10912_2016.html
  The three-year PIC validity is not asked: a bill to extend it to five years passed the House in
  2026 and was pending in the Senate (SB 2391, Aug 2026), e.g.
  https://mb.com.ph/2026/08/17/mark-villar-seeks-to-extend-prc-id-validity-to-5-years
  The explanation no longer states the renewal interval.

## Laboratory science (checked 2026-11-05)

Spot-checked against official or reference sources:
- Schistosoma japonicum: Oncomelania is its snail host; egg spine "smaller and less conspicuous",
  S. mansoni "prominent lateral spine", S. haematobium "conspicuous terminal spine" (CDC DPDx,
  PHMT-PAR-001, -002): https://www.cdc.gov/dpdx/schistosomiasis/index.html
  Philippine host Oncomelania hupensis quadrasi (Acta Tropica 2020 review, Ateneo):
  https://archium.ateneo.edu/biology-faculty-pubs/80
- Capillaria philippinensis: "Transmission occurs primarily through eating undercooked fish"
  (CDC DPDx, PHMT-PAR-003): https://www.cdc.gov/dpdx/intestinalcapillariasis/index.html
- AFB grading (IUATLD/WHO, ZN): scanty 1-9/100 fields, 1+ 10-99/100 fields, 2+ 1-10 per field,
  3+ more than 10 per field (MSF TB guidelines Appendix 4, PHMT-MIC-016):
  https://medicalguidelines.msf.org/en/node/1200
- CPDA-1 red cells 1 to 6 C, 35 days; platelets 20 to 24 C (21 CFR 610.53, PHMT-BBK-009, -010):
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/610.53
- Diabetes cut-offs FPG 126 mg/dL, 75 g OGTT 2-h 200 mg/dL (ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes 2026,
  Section 2; PHMT-CHM-018, -022): https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/49/Supplement_1
- Friedewald not used at triglyceride 400 mg/dL or more (PHMT-CHM-024):
  https://gpnotebook.com/en-GB/pages/cardiovascular-medicine/friedewald-equation
- Glucose loss in unseparated blood about 5 to 7% per hour at room temperature (PHMT-CHM-021):
  https://acutecaretesting.org/en/journal-scans/preanalytic-error-in-plasma-serum-glucose-measurement-examined
- Koilocytosis reported as LSIL in the Bethesda System (PHMT-HIS-010):
  https://pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixcytologyLSIL.html

The remaining laboratory facts (Westgard rules, 20:1 bicarbonate ratio, valinomycin, Jendrassik-Grof
accelerator, red cell indices, platelet estimate factor of 20 x 10^9/L per field, 4:1 Westergren
citrate, 540 nm cyanmethemoglobin, citrate reduction above hematocrit 55%, Kidd/Duffy/Kell and
ficin facts, TRALI, Kleihauer-Betke, HBV markers, 4th-generation HIV p24, NS1, HRP-2, AFP, prozone,
HLA on chromosome 6, strip reactions, casts, crystals, CSF tube order, Light's 0.5 ratio, seminal
fructose, autoclave 121 C at 15 psi, 0.5 McFarland, BSL-3, Letulle en masse autopsy, EDTA
chelation, transformation zone) are standard textbook content (Bishop, Henry, Rodak, Harmening,
Strasinger, Mahon) consistent with the 2023 TOS topics; each was reread on 2026-11-05 and none is
time-sensitive.
