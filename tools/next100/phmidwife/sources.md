# Sources for `phmidwife` (Midwife Licensure Examination, Philippines)

All checked 2026-11-05.

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)

- PRC, Professional Regulatory Board of Midwifery, Program of the Board Licensure Examination for
  Midwives, April 29 and 30, 2024:
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/BOM%20program%2004-2024.pdf
  (downloaded and read). Day 1: Obstetrics 8-10 a.m., Fundamentals of Health Care 11 a.m.-1 p.m.,
  Infant Care and Feeding 2-4 p.m. Day 2: Primary Health Care 8-10 a.m., Professional Growth and
  Development 11 a.m.-1 p.m. Integrated: fundamentals of sociology, principles of bacteriology,
  general anatomy and physiology, general psychology, nutrition, parasitology, microbiology,
  pharmacology. Calculators of any kind prohibited.
- PRC, Revised Program of the Midwives Licensure Examination on April 14 and 15, 2026 (Tacloban copy):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/12%20-%20APR2026%20MIDWIVES%20tacloban.pdf
  (downloaded and read). Same five subjects, same times and integrated subjects; "this supersedes
  the examination program dated January 21, 2026".
- R.A. 7392, Philippine Midwifery Act of 1992 (Lawphil):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1992/ra_7392_1992.html (read):
  Sec. 2 Board of a chairman and four members under the PRC; Sec. 12 scope of examinations
  (infant care and feeding, obstetrical anatomy and physiology, principles of bacteriology,
  obstetrics, midwifery procedures, domiciliary midwifery, community hygiene and first aid,
  nutrition, ethics, primary health care, professional growth and development, family planning,
  others the Board adds); Sec. 16 general rating of 75% with no grade lower than 50% in any
  subject; Sec. 19 nurse-midwife certification after handling 20 deliveries; Sec. 21 practice
  requires a certificate of registration and license; Sec. 23 scope of practice (internal
  examination in labor except with antenatal bleeding, oxytocic drugs after delivery of the
  placenta, suturing perineal lacerations to control bleeding, IV fluids in obstetric emergencies
  when trained, vitamin K to the newborn); Sec. 25 revocation grounds including assisting or
  performing abortion, appeal to the PRC, whose decision is final; Sec. 28 repeals R.A. 2644.
- Not confirmed from an official source (left out of the landing): the number of items per
  subject (no PRC document found; a review site was the only hit), the November 2026 dates (a
  review site gives November 7-8, 2026), and passing rates. The landing says "usually twice a
  year" because PRC programs exist for April 2024, April 2026 and November 2022 (Iloilo copy of
  "exam program november 2022 mle (midwifery)", URL seen in search, not opened), and November
  results are published too (Philstar, Licensure Examination for Midwives results, 20 Nov 2024,
  https://philstar.com/exam-results/2024/11/20/2401697/licensure-examination-midwives/amp,
  search result 2026-11-05), so an April and a November sitting is the usual pattern.
- No PRC table of specifications for the MLE was found, so the five subjects are weighted
  equally (Tigertest's choice, explained on the landing).

## Content (bank)

Written from standard midwifery and public health teaching, Philippine law and DOH programs:

- R.A. 7392 (above) for the Professional Growth and Development law items.
- Other laws named in items, each title re-read on Lawphil on 2026-11-05
  (https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/raYYYY/ra_NNNN_YYYY.html; executive and presidential
  issuances under /executive/execord/ and /statutes/presdecs/):
  R.A. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991), R.A. 7875 (National Health Insurance Act of 1995,
  creates PhilHealth), R.A. 7883 (Barangay Health Workers' Benefits and Incentives Act of 1995),
  R.A. 6675 (Generics Act of 1988), R.A. 9709 (Universal Newborn Hearing Screening and
  Intervention Act of 2009), R.A. 9288 (Newborn Screening Act of 2004), R.A. 10028 (Expanded
  Breastfeeding Promotion Act, amends R.A. 7600), E.O. 51 (1986, National Code of Marketing of
  Breastmilk Substitutes: https://lawphil.net/executive/execord/eo1986/eo_51_1986.html),
  R.A. 8172 (ASIN, salt iodization, 1995), R.A. 8976 (Philippine Food Fortification Act of 2000),
  R.A. 11148 (Kalusugan at Nutrisyon ng Mag-Nanay Act, first 1,000 days), R.A. 11166 (2018 HIV
  and AIDS Policy Act, amends the Philippine AIDS Prevention and Control Act of 1998, R.A. 8504),
  R.A. 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000), R.A. 9275 (Clean Water Act of 2004),
  R.A. 8749 (Clean Air Act of 1999), P.D. 856 (Code on Sanitation of the Philippines:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/presdecs/pd1975/pd_856_1975.html), P.D. 825 (penalty for improper
  disposal of garbage: https://lawphil.net/statutes/presdecs/pd1975/pd_825_1975.html),
  R.A. 9710 (Magna Carta of Women), R.A. 11210 (maternity leave of 105 days, option of 30 more
  without pay, 15 more for solo mothers), R.A. 8187 (Paternity Leave Act of 1996: seven days
  with full pay for the first four deliveries of the legitimate spouse), R.A. 9262 (Anti-Violence
  Against Women and Their Children Act of 2004), R.A. 8353 (Anti-Rape Law, expands the crime
  of rape), R.A. 7610 (Special Protection of Children Against Abuse, Exploitation and
  Discrimination Act), R.A. 9344 (Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006), R.A. 7877
  (Anti-Sexual Harassment Act of 1995), R.A. 10354 (Responsible Parenthood and Reproductive
  Health Act of 2012), R.A. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act). All titles matched the items.
- Letter of Instructions No. 949, October 19, 1979, directs the Minister of Health to develop a
  strong primary health care system (Sec. 1.1 b): ChanRobles text,
  https://chanrobles.com/letterofinstructionsno949.html (read 2026-11-05; Lawphil has no copy).
  LOI 1000 appears only as a distractor.
- Birth registration within 30 days of birth at the local civil registrar of the place of birth
  (later is delayed registration): Philippine Embassy civil registration FAQ,
  https://vientianepe.dfa.gov.ph/civil-registration-faqs, and PSA Helpline guide,
  https://psahelpline.ph/blogs/a-guide-to-understanding-birth-registrations-and-birth-certificates
  (search results, 2026-11-05; the rule is from Act 3753 and its implementing rules). Confirmed.
- RHU headed by the municipal health officer, BHS run by the rural health midwife, birthing homes
  licensed by the DOH (Health Facilities and Services Regulatory Bureau): standard DOH structure
  under R.A. 7160 devolution; not re-opened on a DOH page this session (2026-11-05), kept as
  textbook public health organization.
- DOH programs:
  - National Immunization Program: BCG at birth, pentavalent, OPV and PCV at 6, 10 and 14 weeks,
    MMR at 9 and 12 months. DOH NIP schedule as reproduced in news and DOH-based material
    (Manila Bulletin, 13 Jan 2026, https://mb.com.ph/2026/01/13/childhood-vaccinations-a-continuing-struggle-for-herd-immunity;
    DOH NIP slide deck, https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/nippptpptx/265296125), 2026-11-05.
  - Cold chain: WHO multi-dose vial policy (WHO/V&B/00.09): reconstituted BCG, measles and yellow
    fever vaccines discarded within 6 hours or at the end of the session, whichever comes first;
    VVM discard point when the square matches or is darker than the circle. Checked 2026-11-05
    via https://content.oppia-mobile.org/mod/page/view.php?id=7854 (WHO policy summary).
  - TB: NTP Manual of Procedures 5th ed. (2014), presumptive TB = cough of 2 weeks or more:
    https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/partners/providers/pdf/NTCP_MoP2014.pdf (search excerpt,
    2026-11-05).
  - MNCHN: facility-based delivery by skilled birth attendants, TBAs refer and accompany
    (DOH A.O. 2008-0029); CEmONC adds surgery (caesarean) and blood transfusion to BEmONC
    signal functions (WHO/UNFPA EmOC signal functions). Textbook, not re-opened 2026-11-05.
  - EINC (DOH A.O. 2009-0025): dry immediately, do not wipe off vernix, dry cord care, first
    breastfeed within the first hour, routine procedures deferred until after it. Checked
    2026-11-05 against EINC summaries (secondary; https://www.scribd.com/document/680043063/EINC).
  - IMCI (WHO chart booklet 2014): general danger signs (unable to drink or breastfeed, vomits
    everything, convulsions, lethargic or unconscious); some dehydration = two of restless or
    irritable, sunken eyes, drinks eagerly, skin pinch goes back slowly; zinc for 10 to 14 days.
    https://comdis-hsd.leeds.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/50/2016/01/IMCI-English-version.pdf
    (search excerpt, 2026-11-05).
- Family planning (WHO Family Planning: A Global Handbook for Providers): Standard Days Method
  days 8 to 19 for cycles of 26 to 32 days (https://fphandbook.org/node/2629, 2026-11-05);
  vasectomy backup about 3 months (UCSF Advancing Access, https://advancingaccess.ucsf.edu/how-long-do-i-have-wait);
  DMPA every 3 months; Implanon single rod labelled 3 years (Pathfinder cue cards,
  https://www.pathfinder.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Adult-Contraception-Cue-Cards_ENG.pdf).
  Studies support longer implant use, so the item now asks what the implant is labelled for.
  LAM: baby under 6 months, amenorrhea, full breastfeeding (WHO handbook, from memory).
- Obstetric figures: oxytocin 10 IU IM within 1 minute of birth in AMTSL, and oral misoprostol
  600 µg as the alternative uterotonic where oxytocin is unavailable (WHO recommendations on
  prevention of PPH, 2012/2018, and the 2025 consolidated PPH guideline; summary at
  https://list.essentialmeds.org/recommendations/908?ln=EN, 2026-11-05). Quickening 18 to 20
  weeks (primigravida), true conjugate = diagonal minus 1.5 to 2 cm, GDM screening at 24 to 28
  weeks, late decelerations from uteroplacental insufficiency, bulging anterior fontanel and
  raised ICP, Moro fading by 4 to 6 months, newborn respiration 30 to 60 a minute, LBW under
  2,500 g (WHO definition), boiling 20 minutes = high-level disinfection and 0.5% chlorine
  10-minute decontamination (WHO/JHPIEGO infection prevention): standard texts, not re-opened.
- R.A. 7392 Sec. 16 (75% general rating, no subject below 50%) is now tested directly (PGD-033),
  read on Lawphil 2026-11-05.
- Removed: the item naming IMAP as the PRC-accredited professional organization (PGD-033). Only
  search snippets supported it (a 2011 PRC regional page, judiciary e-library); no current PRC
  APO list could be opened on 2026-11-05, so it was replaced by the Sec. 16 item above.
- The CPR rate item was removed earlier.
