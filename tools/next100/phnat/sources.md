# Sources for `phnat` (Nursing Aptitude Test, Philippines)

All 200 questions are original. Nothing was copied from any school's test or any reviewer.
Checked 2026-11-10.

## What could and could not be confirmed

The Nursing Aptitude Test (NAT) is run by each college of nursing. No school publishes an item
count, time limit, subtest split or passing score, so none is stated as fact in the bank, the
landing or `examFacts`.

- Central Philippine University, College of Nursing, BSN application advisory for SY 2026-2027:
  https://cpu.edu.ph/news/cpu-college-of-nursing-announces-application-deadline-for-incoming-bsn-students-sy-2026-2027/
  (the school's own college aptitude test comes first; eligible applicants are then told their
  Nursing Aptitude Test schedule; the NAT was slated for February 2026).
- West Visayas State University, College of Nursing admission slip:
  https://wvsu.edu.ph/files/pdf/downloads/otherforms/con-admission-slip.pdf
  (the NAT counts toward the admission rating alongside the university's admission test and an
  interview).
- Filamer Christian University, Nursing Aptitude Test registration:
  https://filamer.edu.ph/tag/nat/
  (the NAT is a requirement for BS Nursing applicants).
- An examinee report of the test (Scribd, "ERF NAT"): four subtests, namely verbal ability,
  numerical facility, science and health information, and a mental ability test. Secondary, used
  only for the list of areas. https://www.scribd.com/document/625878146/Erf-Nat-Part356

## Content references

Standard senior high school English, mathematics and science. Health items follow WHO and
Philippine Department of Health public guidance (exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months, oral
rehydration, dengue and malaria vectors, tuberculosis transmission, washing an animal bite wound
with soap and water). Vital sign ranges are the common adult textbook ranges.

## Review

Second-pass review of all 200 items on 2026-11-10: no wrong keys; three wordings fixed
(PHNAT-ARI-019, PHNAT-ALG-004, PHNAT-BIO-013). Weights are Tigertest's own: verbal 15, numerical
12, science and health 14, mental ability 9 per 50-question test.
