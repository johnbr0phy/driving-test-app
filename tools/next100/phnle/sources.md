# Sources for `phnle` (Philippine Nurse Licensure Examination, PRC)

All checked 2026-11-05.

## Exam format

- PRC, Professional Regulatory Board of Nursing, "Program of the Nurses Licensure Examination on
  Aug. 29-30, 2026" (approved May 28, 2026):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/exam%20progam%20aug%202026%20nle%20(nursing)%20signed.pdf
  - Five parts over two days. Saturday: Nursing Practice I, Care of Individuals, Families,
    Population Groups and Community (Community Health Nursing), 8:00 to 10:00 a.m.; Nursing
    Practice II, Part 1 Care of Mother, Adolescent (Well Clients), At Risk or With Problems (Acute
    and Chronic) and Part 2 Human Growth and Development, 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.; Nursing
    Practice III, Care of Clients with Problems in Surgery, Oxygenation, Fluid and Electrolytes,
    Infectious, Inflammatory and Immunologic Response, Cellular Aberrations (Acute and Chronic),
    2:30 to 4:30 p.m. Sunday: Nursing Practice IV, Care of Client with Problems in Nutrition, and
    Gastro-Intestinal, Metabolism and Endocrine, Perception and Coordination (Acute and Chronic),
    8:00 to 10:00 a.m.; Nursing Practice V, Care of Clients with Maladaptive Patterns of Behavior
    (Acute and Chronic); Care of Clients with Life-Threatening Condition, Acutely Ill/Multi-Organ
    Problems, High Acuity and Emergency Situation, 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. So each part is 2 hours.
  - Integrated across all parts: anatomy and physiology, pathophysiology, pharmacology and
    therapeutics, nutrition and diet therapy, parasitology and microbiology; fundamentals of
    nursing including professional adjustments. The nursing process is the framework. Tests ten
    BSN program outcomes at PQF level 6 (including practice according to law and ethics,
    beginning management and leadership).
  - Pencil and paper (No. 2 pencils, black ball pens); all calculators prohibited.
- PRC, "Program of the Nurses Licensure Examination", February 26-27, 2026 (same five parts):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/exam%20progam%20feb%202026%20nle%20(nursing)%20approved.pdf
- Professional Regulatory Board of Nursing Resolution No. 10, s. 2025, "Adoption, Issuance and
  Promulgation of the Enhanced Tables of Specifications for the Subjects in the Nurses Licensure

  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2025-10%20%20published%20Enhanced%20TOS%20nuse.pdf

- R.A. 9173, Philippine Nursing Act of 2002 (lawphil):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2002/ra_9173_2002.html
  - Sec. 12 written examination; Sec. 14 scope set by the Board; Sec. 15 ratings: general average

    is a chairperson and six members; Sec. 26 nurses inactive five consecutive years take one month
    of didactic training and three months of practicum; Sec. 28 scope of practice, perineal

### Guides only (not confirmed from an official source; checked 2026-11-05)
- 100 items per part, 500 in all, typically situational sets of five items (1NURSE blog,
  https://www.1nurse.com/blog/story/2025/08/26/understanding-enhanced-tos-nurses-licensure-exam/ ,
  and exam-prep sites). No PRC document read states the item count. Used on the landing only as
  "guides report"; the bank does not depend on it.
- Annex A of Board Resolution No. 10, s. 2025 (the enhanced tables with weights and item counts)
  was searched for again on 2026-11-05 and is not on the PRC site; the published PDF is the
  three-page resolution only. All splits inside a part are Tigertest's.
- Equal weighting of the five subjects is an inference: R.A. 9173 Sec. 15 requires a general
  average of 75% with no subject below 60% but sets no weights. The landing says only that
  Tigertest gives each part an equal share.

## Blueprint

Each part carries equal weight in the general average (five subjects averaged; R.A. 9173 Sec. 15
names no weights), so each 50-question mock gives 10 items to each part:
- NP I: community health nursing and DOH programmes 7, plus professional practice, law, ethics and
  leadership 3 (integrated across all parts per the program; placed here so the part keeps 10).
- NP II: maternal and adolescent care 7, human growth and development 3 (the 7/3 split is
  Tigertest's; Annex A not available).
- NP III: 10. NP IV: 10.
- NP V: maladaptive behavior 6, life-threatening and emergency care 4 (split is Tigertest's).

## Content sources (all checked 2026-11-05)

Statutes (lawphil.net, text read):
- R.A. 9288, Newborn Screening Act of 2004:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2004/ra_9288_2004.html (Sec. 6 sample after 24 hours of
  life and not later than three days; Sec. 7 refusal on religious grounds, acknowledged in
  writing). PHNLE-CHN-011, CHN-012.
- R.A. 9173, Philippine Nursing Act of 2002:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2002/ra_9173_2002.html . Sec. 3 Board of a Chairperson
  and six members (PRO-001); Sec. 26 inactive five consecutive years: one month didactic and three
  months practicum (PRO-002); Sec. 28(a) "In case of suturing of perineal laceration, special
  training shall be provided according to protocol established" (PRO-003; the explanation no
  longer says "in emergencies only", which the Act does not say); Sec. 9(f) the Board promulgates
  a Code of Ethics.
- R.A. 10152, Mandatory Infants and Children Health Immunization Act of 2011:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2011/ra_10152_2011.html . Sec. 3: hepatitis B vaccine
  within 24 hours after birth; an infant delivered with a non-health professional brought to a
  facility within 24 hours, no later than seven days. CHN-009 and the new CHN-023 (replaces an
  FHSIS "family treatment record" item that could only be checked against study aids, which
  name the individual treatment record instead).
- R.A. 11223, Universal Health Care Act (2019):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11223_2019.html . Sec. 5: every Filipino citizen
  automatically included in the National Health Insurance Program (PhilHealth). CHN-021.
- R.A. 10354, Responsible Parenthood and Reproductive Health Act of 2012:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10354_2012.html . Sec. 7: accredited public
  health facilities provide a full range of modern family planning methods. MAT-023.
- R.A. 10028, Expanded Breastfeeding Promotion Act of 2009:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_10028_2010.html . Sec. 12: paid lactation
  periods totalling not less than 40 minutes for every 8-hour working period. MAT-024.
- R.A. 11036, Mental Health Act (2018):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2018/ra_11036_2018.html . Defines informed consent of
  the service user; rights of service users. PSY item on informed consent.
- R.A. 11166, Philippine HIV and AIDS Policy Act (2018):
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2018/ra_11166_2018.html . HIV testing is confidential,
  voluntary and conducted only with the informed consent of the person. NP3 HIV item.
- R.A. 10173, Data Privacy Act of 2012:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2012/ra_10173_2012.html . Sec. 3(l): sensitive personal
  information includes information about an individual's health. PRO-011.
- R.A. 10912, Continuing Professional Development Act of 2016:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2016/ra_10912_2016.html . Sec. 10: CPD is a mandatory
  requirement for renewal of the professional identification card. PRO-012.
- R.A. 7883, Barangay Health Workers' Benefits and Incentives Act of 1995:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1995/ra_7883_1995.html (title and year; MAT-023
  distractor explanation).

Immunization and newborn care:
- WHO, "Temperature sensitivity of vaccines" (WHO/IVB/06.10, 2006), copy at
  https://vaccine.org.ua/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/who_ivb_06.10_eng.pdf : OPV is kept frozen
  at -15 to -25 C at higher cold-chain levels and shows no significant titre loss after repeated
  freeze-thaw; "Liquid formulations of vaccines containing diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus,
  hepatitis B, Haemophilus influenzae type b, IPV and their combinations should not be frozen."
  CHN-010.
- WHO, "Tetanus vaccines: WHO position paper, February 2017" (WER 92(6)):
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WER9206 : pregnant women without reliable history get
  at least 2 doses at least 4 weeks apart, a third at least 6 months later, a fourth and fifth at
  least 1 year apart. MAT-010.
- WHO Western Pacific, "Early Essential Newborn Care: Clinical Practice Pocket Guide" (2014),
  the basis of the DOH EINC protocol:
  https://platform.who.int/docs/default-source/mca-documents/policy-documents/operational-guidance/lao-mn-48-01-operationalguidance-2014-eng-eenc-clinical-practice-pocketguide.pdf
  "Within the first 30 seconds": dry thoroughly, no routine suctioning; "Clamp and cut the cord
  after cord pulsations have stopped (between 1-3 minutes)". MAT-014, MAT-015.
- Pentavalent at 6, 10, 14 weeks: the WHO tetanus position paper above lists 6, 10 and 14 weeks
  among national primary schedules; DOH NIP slide decks show the same columns. (The pentavalent
  item was dropped from the bank; no item depends on it.)
- Vitamin A 100,000 IU at 6-11 months and 200,000 IU at 12-59 months: WHO guideline doses,
  e.g. Community Eye Health Journal, "Vitamin A supplementation: who, when and how"
  (https://cehjournal.org/articles/682), and DOH Garantisadong Pambata reports (e.g.
  https://mb.com.ph/2024/1/17/pasig-lgu-holds-house-to-house-weight-height-measuring-vitamin-a-administering-for-children ).
  No current DOH circular was found; reputable secondary. CHN-020.

Communicable disease control:
- Rabies: DOH Administrative Order No. 2018-0013, Revised Guidelines on the Management of Rabies
  Exposures (14-day observation of the biting dog or cat), and the IRR of R.A. 9482 (dog observed
  14 days), summarised at https://jur.ph/law/summary/revised-guidelines-animal-bite-management ;
  DOH A.O. No. 164, s. 2002 (wash and flush the wound with soap and water, avoid suturing):
  https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocsfriendly/10/42188 . CHN-013, CHN-014.
- Vectors: Anopheles flavirostris, Philippine malaria vector (Smithsonian WRBU,
  https://wrbu.si.edu/vectorspecies/mosquitoes/flavirostris ); Aedes poicilius, principal vector
  of bancroftian filariasis in the Philippines (Southeast Asian J Trop Med Public Health 2016,
  https://www.tm.mahidol.ac.th/seameo/2016-47-5/04-691411-912.pdf ); Oncomelania hupensis
  quadrasi, snail host of Schistosoma japonicum in Leyte, Samar and Mindanao (UP Science Diliman,
  https://journals.upd.edu.ph/index.php/sciencediliman/article/view/5805 ). Filariasis mass drug
  administration: Executive Order No. 369, s. 2004 (national program, annual mass treatment),
  https://jur.ph/law/summary/establishing-national-program-eliminating-lymphatic-filariais .
  CHN-015, CHN-016, CHN-019.
- Water supply levels (Level I point source, Level II communal faucet, Level III household
  taps): DOH IRR of the Code on Sanitation, Chapter II (1995), as quoted in
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_supply_and_sanitation_in_the_Philippines . CHN-022.

Family and community health nursing:
- UP Manila College of Nursing lecture "Developing the Family Nursing Care Plan" (J.C.V. Alejo):
  https://vle.upm.edu.ph/pluginfile.php/254343/mod_resource/content/1/N12_Family%20Health%20Care%20Planning%20.pdf
  First-level assessment categories wellness state, health threat, health deficit, foreseeable
  crisis/stress point; Bailon and Maglaya's four criteria with salience as "the family's
  perception ... of seriousness and urgency". CHN-002, CHN-003, CHN-004. CHN-004 now tests
  salience; the earlier "modifiability weighs 2" item was rewritten because the weights could
  only be seen in study aids.
- COPAR phases, home visit and bag technique principles, functional community, IMCI fast
  breathing, AFP surveillance: standard Philippine CHN teaching (Maglaya, Cuevas) and WHO IMCI;
  not checked against a primary text.

Professional practice:
- Ethical principles (veracity, autonomy), elements of malpractice, informed-consent roles,
  leadership styles and care delivery models (PRO-004 to PRO-010): standard nursing ethics and
  management references; no Philippine primary text states them.

Clinical content:
- Maternal, growth and development, medical-surgical, psychiatric and emergency items are
  standard nursing references; numeric items (Naegele, Apgar, GCS, Parkland, rule of nines, drip
