# Philippines Pharmacist Licensure Examination (PhLE) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-05.

## Official

- PRC, Professional Regulatory Board of Pharmacy, Revised Program of the Pharmacists Licensure
  Examination on April 18 and 19, 2026 (dated March 31, 2026; supersedes the program of February 4,
  2026). Read directly (PDF, text extracted):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/April%2018%20and%2019%202026%20Exam%20Program%20(Pharmacy)%20revised.pdf
  - Day 1 (Saturday): Pharmaceutical Chemistry 8:00 to 10:00 a.m., 20% (Inorganic Pharmaceutical
    and Medicinal Chemistry; Organic Pharmaceutical and Medicinal Chemistry; Qualitative
    Pharmaceutical Chemistry). Pharmacognosy/Biochemistry 11:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., 15% (Plant
    Chemistry). Practice of Pharmacy 1:30 to 3:30 p.m., 17.5% (Compounding-Dispensing;
    Clinical/Hospital Pharmacy; Pharmaceutical Calculations).
  - Day 2 (Sunday): Pharmacology-Pharmacokinetics 8:00 to 9:30 a.m., 15% (Toxicology;
    Incompatibilities and Adverse Drug Reactions). Pharmaceutics 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., 17.5%
    (Manufacturing Pharmacy; Pharmaceutical Dosage Forms; Physical Pharmacy; Jurisprudence and
    Ethics). Quality Control/Quality Assurance 1:30 to 3:30 p.m., 15% (Drug Testing with
    Instrumentation, Microbiology and Public Health). Total 100%.
  - Only basic calculators (+, -, x, /, memory keys and square root) are allowed.
  - The program does not state the number of items per subject. Tigertest does not state one.
- R.A. 10918, Philippine Pharmacy Act (2016), lawphil.net:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2016/ra_10918_2016.html
  - Sec. 15: subjects of the exam; the Board, with PRC approval, may change subjects, format,
    content and relative weights.
  - Sec. 16: the exam is given twice a year.
  - Sec. 17: to pass, a general weighted average of 75% with no rating lower than 50% in any
    subject; a third-time failer must take a refresher program before retaking.
  - Sec. 5: definitions of pharmacist-only OTC medicines, pharmacy assistants, pharmacy technicians.
  - Sec. 20: Professional Identification Card renewed every three years with CPD and APO good
    standing. Sec. 29: display of the original Certificate of Registration where one practices.
  - Sec. 30 and 33: prescription drugs and pharmacist-only OTC medicines dispensed only by a
    licensed pharmacist; prescription drugs only on a valid prescription of a physician, dentist
    or veterinarian.
- Board of Pharmacy Resolution No. 01(A), s. 2011 (Supreme Court e-Library, NAR vol. 22 no. 2):
  older passing rule (75% GWA, no rating below 50% in more than one subject); superseded for this
  bank by R.A. 10918 Sec. 17. https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocsfriendly/10/56553
- R.A. 6675, Generics Act of 1988, lawphil.net:
  https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1988/ra_6675_1988.html
  Sec. 6(b) prescriptions use the generic name, brand may be added; Sec. 6(d) outlets inform buyers
  of all products with the same generic name.
- DOH A.O. 62, s. 1989 (Generics Act rules), Sec. 4, as summarized in Del Rosario v. Bengzon,
  G.R. No. 88265 (1989): violative prescriptions are not filled and are reported; erroneous ones are
  filled and reported. https://chanrobles.com/scdecisions/jurisprudence1989/dec1989/gr_88265_1989.php
  (A.O. 90 later allowed more than one drug product per prescription form; not used.)

- Legaspi and Bagaoisan, "Ehretia microphylla Tablet Formulation for Biliary and Gastrointestinal
  Colic", Acta Medica Philippina 54(1), 2020 (UP Manila NIH Institute of Herbal Medicine): the DOH's

  (COG-011, 012, 013). It names yerba buena Mentha x villosa, so the stem no longer gives a
  scientific name (COG-017).
- UP Manila news, January 2024: akapulko for superficial fungal skin infections; ulasimang bato for
  gout, tested against allopurinol; yerba buena for headache, toothache, arthritis and dysmenorrhea,
  its tablet tested against paracetamol. https://www.upm.edu.ph/?p=6309 (COG-010, 015, 016, 017)
- Philstar, "DOH launches two herbal tablets", 2001-12-03, Health Secretary Dayrit: lagundi for cough
  and asthma, sambong as a diuretic and for kidney stones.
  https://philstar.com/metro/2001/12/03/142232/doh-launches-two-herbal-tablets (COG-008, 009)

## Blueprint

Each 50-question mock follows the 2026 program weights: Pharmaceutical Chemistry 10 (20%),
Pharmacognosy/Biochemistry 8 (16%), Practice of Pharmacy 9 (18%: 6 dispensing, clinical and
hospital + 3 calculations), Pharmacology 7 (14%), Pharmaceutics 9 (18%: 6 dosage forms, physical
pharmacy and manufacturing + 3 jurisprudence and ethics), QC/QA 7 (14%). The real exam is passed on
the general weighted average with a 50% floor per subject; only the 75% average is modelled.
