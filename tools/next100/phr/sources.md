# Sources for `phr` (HRCI Professional in Human Resources, PHR)

All 200 questions are original, written from the HRCI PHR Exam Content Outline and the federal
statutes, regulations and Supreme Court decisions it draws on. No HRCI sample item, past exam item
or commercial prep question was copied, and no prep provider is named. Checked 2026-11-04.

## Exam format (official, HRCI)
- HRCI, PHR certification page: https://www.hrci.org/our-programs/our-certifications/phr
  (checked 2026-11-04): exam time 2 hours plus 30 minutes administration time; 90 scored questions
  plus 25 pretest questions (115 in all); computer-based at a Pearson VUE testing center or at home
  or office through OnVUE online proctoring. Seven functional areas: Business Management 14%,
  Workforce Planning and Talent Acquisition 14%, Learning and Development 10%, Total Rewards 15%,
  Employee Engagement 17%, Employee and Labor Relations 20%, HR Information Management 10%.
  Eligibility: one year of professional-level HR experience with a master's degree or higher, two
  years with a bachelor's degree, or four years with no degree requirement. An exam fee and an
  application fee are listed; deliberately not used in copy.
- HRCI, PHR Exam Content Outline (PDF, "took effect in 2024", (c) 2023-2024):
  https://www.hrci.org/docs/default-source/2024-document-control/hrci_phr-exam-content-outline-2024.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-04): the seven areas and their responsibilities 1.1 to 7.3 used to plan the
  categories. "Candidates are responsible for knowing the HR laws and regulations that are in effect
  at the time of their exam."
- HRCI, Certification Policies and Procedures Handbook (PDF, "Revised October 2026"):
  https://www.hrci.org/docs/default-source/pdf-documents/cert-handbook.pdf (checked 2026-11-04):
  item types are traditional multiple choice (3 or 4 options, one correct), multiple choice
  multiple response, fill in the blank, drag and drop and scenarios; pretest questions are not
  distinguished and not scored; the cut score is set by the modified Angoff method; scores are
  scaled and "the passing score for each exam type is set at 500". HRCI publishes no percentage
  passing score. (Guides say the scale runs 100 to 700; not confirmed from HRCI, not used.)
- Third-party guides (open-exam-prep.com and others) repeat the 115/90/25 and 2-hour figures and
  the seven 2024 areas; not relied on.

## Blueprint (Tigertest)
14/14/10/15/17/20/10 scaled to 50 per mock test: 7, 7, 5, 7.5, 8.5, 10, 5. The two half remainders
tie; Employee Engagement (the larger area) takes 9 and Total Rewards 7. Employee and Labor Relations
(10) is split 5/5 into EEO, leave, safety and policy law, and labor relations, complaints and
investigations. Four 50-question tests at about the real pace (120 minutes for 115 items).
passPct 70 is Tigertest's readiness line, not an HRCI figure. Multiple-response, fill-in, drag and
drop and scenario item types are written as single-answer items.

## Federal law used (as in force 2026-11-04)
- Title VII (15+ employees; BFOQ never for race or color; 180/300-day charge filing, 90 days to sue
  after a right-to-sue notice); Civil Rights Act of 1991 and Griggs v. Duke Power (disparate impact);
  Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures (1978), four-fifths rule.
- Bostock v. Clayton County, 590 U.S. 644 (2020); Groff v. DeJoy, 600 U.S. 447 (2023); Muldrow v.
  City of St. Louis, 601 U.S. 346 (2024); Ames v. Ohio Department of Youth Services, 605 U.S. ___
  (June 5, 2025, unanimous: no "background circumstances" rule for majority-group plaintiffs).
- ADA (15+; medical exams only after a conditional offer; separate confidential medical files;
  undue hardship); ADEA (20+, age 40 and over); OWBPA (21 days individual, 45 days group, 7 to revoke);
  GINA (15+, family medical history is genetic information); Pregnant Workers Fairness Act (in force
  June 27, 2023, 15+); PUMP for Nursing Mothers Act (2022, FLSA break time and a non-bathroom space
  for one year after birth).
- Equal Pay Act of 1963 (skill, effort, responsibility, similar working conditions); Lilly Ledbetter
  Fair Pay Act of 2009 (each paycheck).
- FLSA: overtime over 40 hours in a workweek; regular rate includes nondiscretionary bonuses
  (29 CFR 778.208-778.210); white-collar exemptions need salary basis, salary level and duties (the
  salary level itself is left out: the 2024 increase was vacated in Texas v. DOL, Nov 15, 2024);
  economic reality test for employee status; Portal-to-Portal Act of 1947.
- FMLA (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours; 12 weeks; 26 weeks military caregiver
  leave; eligibility notice within 5 business days, 29 CFR 825.300(b)).
- COBRA (20+ employees; 60-day election; 45 days to first premium; 18/29/36 months; 102%, 150% in the
  disability extension). ERISA (SPD; government and church plans exempt; DC vesting at least 3-year
  cliff or 2-to-6-year graded). ACA employer mandate (50+ FTEs; 30 hours a week or 130 a month).
  HSA with an HDHP; health FSA use-it-or-lose-it with a carryover or grace period; Section 125;
  Section 127 educational assistance (tax-free to an annual limit; the amount is not used).
- IRCA and Form I-9 (Section 1 by day one, Section 2 within 3 business days; retain 3 years after
  hire or 1 year after termination, whichever is later; never backdate a late Section 2, per the
  USCIS M-274 handbook; a U.S. passport is a List A document).
- FCRA background checks (standalone disclosure, written authorization, pre-adverse action notice
  with the report and summary of rights). IRS common law test (behavioral, financial, relationship).
- USERRA (escalator principle, five years cumulative service). WARN Act (100+ employees, 60 days).
- Executive Order 14173 (January 21, 2025) revoked EO 11246; Section 503 of the Rehabilitation Act
  and VEVRAA still apply to federal contractors. https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ofccp (checked
  2026-11-04).
- EEO-1 Component 1 for private employers with 100+ employees.
- OSH Act: general duty clause (5(a)(1)); fatality reported in 8 hours, hospitalization, amputation
  or eye loss in 24 hours; Form 300A posted February 1 to April 30, logs kept 5 years; Section 11(c)
  complaints within 30 days; training in a language and vocabulary workers understand.
- NLRA (Section 7 concerted activity, mandatory subjects, 30% showing of interest, TIPS), Taft-Hartley
  (union ULPs, 14(b) right-to-work), Landrum-Griffin (LMRDA, members' bill of rights),
  Norris-LaGuardia (1932), NLRB v. J. Weingarten (1975), unfair labor practice strikers versus
  economic strikers. Faragher v. City of Boca Raton and Burlington Industries v. Ellerth (1998).
- HIPAA privacy rule for plan sponsors.

## Left out on purpose
Dollar thresholds indexed or reset by rule (FLSA salary level, HSA/FSA/401(k) limits, Section 127
amount, minimum wage), fees, state-specific rules (final pay timing is "state law"), the 2024 EEOC
harassment guidance (partly vacated in 2025), NLRB doctrines in flux (Cemex bargaining orders), the
vacated FTC non-compete rule, and anything about the PHR exam itself.
