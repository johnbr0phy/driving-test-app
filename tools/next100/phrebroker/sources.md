# Real Estate Broker Licensure Examination (Philippines) sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- PRB of Real Estate Service, "Revised Program of the Real Estate Brokers Licensure Examination to
  be held on April 22, 2025" (dated April 10, 2025, signed by the Board chairperson):
  https://prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/041025%20EXAM%20PROGRAM%20REAL%20ESTATE%20BROKERS_Rev1.pdf
  One day: General/Fundamentals of Real Estate Service 8:00 to 10:00 a.m., 25%; Special and
  Technical Knowledge 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., 25%; Professional Practice 2:00 to 6:00 p.m., 50%.
  Coverage: General/Fundamentals = (1) fundamentals of property ownership, (2) Code of Ethics and
  Responsibilities, (3) legal requirements for real estate service (R.A. 9646 and IRR), (4) real
  estate laws and taxation (agrarian law, CARPER, R.A. 7160, R.A. 8424, etc.). Special and Technical
  Knowledge = (1) subdivision development (P.D. 957, B.P. 220, "P.D. 7279" [R.A. 7279], etc.),
  (2) condominium concept and other types of real estate holding, (3) legal aspect of sale,
  mortgage and lease, (4) documentation and registration. Professional Practice = (1) brokerage
  practice, (2) finance and economics, (3) urban and land use, (4) planning, development and zoning,
  (5) basic principles of ecology, (6) basic appraisal for real estate brokers. Non-programmable
  calculators only (Commission Resolution No. 1809-2024).
- Same subjects, weights, times and calculator rule in the official program of the April 16, 2026
  sitting (checked 2026-11-05):
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/exam%20program%20REB%202026%20revised.pdf
  It confirms the April 16, 2026 date. The results date (April 21, 2026) is still from news reports
  only. Neither date is used on the landing.
- Same subjects and weights in the April 18, 2024 program:
  https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/031924%20revised%20exam%20program%20apr%202024%20real%20estate%20brokers%20(res).pdf
- PRB-RES Resolution No. 04, s. 2011, Tables of Specifications (Annex A): General/Fundamentals
  100 items (four competencies of 25), Special and Technical Knowledge 100 items (four of 25),
  Professional Practice 200 items (six of about 33). Read from a copy hosted by a third-party site
  (not prc.gov.ph), so the item counts are labelled "the Board's 2011 table of specifications" and
  not asserted as current:
  https://www.foreclosurephilippines.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/03/Professional-Regulatory-Board-of-Real-Estate-Service-RESOLUTION-NO.-04-Series-of-2011.pdf
- R.A. 9646 (Real Estate Service Act), Sec. 15: pass needs an average of at least 75% in all
  subjects with no rating below 50% in any subject. Sec. 16: results within ten days of the last
  exam day. https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2009/ra_9646_2009.html
- Most recent sitting: April 16, 2026, results April 21, 2026 (news reports, e.g.
  https://www.thesummitexpress.com/2026/04/prc-releases-reble-real-estate-broker-april-2026-results-today.html;
  not checked on prc.gov.ph). PRC Resolution No. 1983 s. 2025 cancelled a September 2025 sitting
  (search result title on prc.gov.ph/node/7795, page not opened). Neither date is used on the landing.
- Blueprint here: 50-item mock tests, 13 General/Fundamentals + 12 Special and Technical + 25
  Professional Practice (the 25/25/50 weights), spread evenly over each subject's topics as the 2011
  TOS does. Only the 75% average is scored; the 50% subject floor is not modelled.

## Still textbook or practice material, not a statute
- Urban land use models (Burgess, Hoyt, Harris and Ullman), sprawl, infill, land banking,
  gentrification, townships; ecology basics; appraisal approaches and principles; listing types
  (open, exclusive, net), reservation agreements, buyer registration, comparative market analysis;
  mortgage redemption insurance and in-house financing; timeshare as a holding type. These test
  standard definitions and are not tied to a dated rule.
- The Code of Ethics and Responsibilities (PRBRES Res. No. 39 s. 2019 per unofficial copies) was not
  found on prc.gov.ph. Ethics explanations now cite only R.A. 9646 Sec. 19 and the Civil Code on
  agency (Arts. 1491, 1891, 1909); ETH-002, ETH-005, ETH-007, ETH-009, ETH-010 were rewritten so.
- No tax rates, fees, loan limits, peso penalties or yearly figures are asked. Peso amounts appear
  only inside worked calculations.
