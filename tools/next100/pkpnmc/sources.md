# pkpnmc sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Read from PNMC's own pages and documents (all read 2026-11-05)
- PNMC home page (https://pnmc.gov.pk/): PNMC "works closely with Provincial Nursing Examination
  Boards (NEBs)"; menu links to "Licensing Exams", "Examination Boards", "PNMC Act", "PNMC
  Registration". No licensing exam format on the page.
- PNMC Act page (https://pnmc.gov.pk/pnmc-act/): the original law is the Pakistan Nursing Council
  Act, 1973 (Act No. XXVI of 1973), amended as the Pakistan Nursing and Midwifery Council
  (Amendment) Act, 2023 (Act No. V of 2023). The page says the new Council adopted the former
  Pakistan Nursing Council's regulations and decisions at its first meeting on 8 August 2023.
- Amended Act 2023 PDF (https://pnmc.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/PNMC-Amended-Act-2023.pdf),
  Gazette of Pakistan Extraordinary, 21 January 2023; National Assembly notice of 19 January 2023;
  presidential assent 17 January 2023. Text extracted with pdftotext (scanned, OCR garbled). It
  lists among the Council's functions "to carry out licensure examinations for grant of licence to
  practice with PN&MC" and the proviso that "the right to prescribe drugs or medical devices may not
  be given to Registered Nurses, Nurse Specialists, Nurse Practitioners, Midwives, or Lady Health
  Visitors ... in contravention of the law and without the prior approval of the Federal
  Government". Section 11 is amended so that PN&MC, with the Federal Government, constitutes a
  nursing examination board. Used for bank items LAW-002 and LAW-003 and the landing.
- Licensing Exams page (https://pnmc.gov.pk/licensing-exams/): shows only the Family Welfare
  Workers (FWW) Licensing Examination 2022 results (pass/fail per candidate, by NEB province). No
  format, question count, time or pass mark is published there.
- Examination Boards page (https://pnmc.gov.pk/examination-boards/): four provincial Nursing
  Examination Boards (Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Sindh, Balochistan) under section 11 of the Act
  confer diplomas and licences and conduct examinations.
- PNMC registration page (https://pnmc.gov.pk/pnmc-registration-2/): "Successful completion of a
  one-year internship is a mandatory requirement for GBSN graduates to get PNC Registration";
  overseas qualifications need HEC equivalence; temporary registration for foreign nationals.
  Used for bank item LAW-005.
- PNMC online portal (https://online.pnmc.gov.pk/, /guide, /requirement-procedures): a menu item
  "Nursing Council Licensing Examination" with no live page behind it; no format, eligibility or
  pass mark published.
- Notifications (https://pnmc.gov.pk/notifications/) and Public Notices
  (https://pnmc.gov.pk/public-notices/): nothing on a nurse licensing examination as of
  2026-11-05 (latest notifications are a fee schedule revision and institution inspections,
  October 2026).
- NEB Punjab (https://nebp.edu.pk/): runs the annual nursing examinations for PNMC-recognised
  institutions (Sept/Oct 2026 session from 9 November 2026); no licensing exam format.
- Federal EPI page (https://www.epi.gov.pk/immunization-schedule/): EPI vaccinates children aged
  0 to 15 months against 12 vaccine-preventable diseases and pregnant women. The schedule itself
  is an image that could not be read here; the vaccine ages used in the bank (birth BCG, OPV-0,
  hep B; 6, 10, 14 weeks pentavalent, PCV, OPV; rotavirus 6 and 10 weeks; IPV-1 at 14 weeks;
  MR at 9 and 15 months; TCV at 9 months) are as the sibling `pknle` builder read from that image
  (Vaccination_Schedule_2023.jpeg) the same day.

## Not published by PNMC (searched 2026-11-05)
Number of questions, papers, time, delivery mode (computer or paper), pass mark, attempt limits,
eligibility rules and a topic blueprint for a registered nurse licensing exam. The Act gives PNMC
the power to hold licensure examinations, and the site has a "Licensing Exams" page and a portal
menu item, but no format. So: four 50-question mock tests, 60% as Tigertest's readiness target
(as `kenck` and `gulfrn` do), and the landing and examFacts say PNMC publishes no count or pass
mark. The web search budget ran out after the first query, so third-party guides and news reports
on the exam were not checked; none are used.

## Blueprint used (Tigertest's, labelled as such)
Built from the subjects of PNMC's nursing programmes (Generic BSN and the diploma): per 50-question
test fundamentals 7, pharmacology and dosage 4, adult health (medical-surgical) 9, critical care and
emergency 4, maternal and newborn 6, paediatrics and EPI 6, community health 6, mental health 4,
law, ethics and leadership 4. Pools 28/16/36/16/24/24/24/16/16 = 200. The PNMC curriculum documents
were not re-read this session; the split is Tigertest's.

- Paediatrics: milestones (social smile 6 weeks, pincer grasp 9-10 months), fontanelles,

## Fact-check pass (2026-11-05)
The web search budget was used up before this pass, so no search ran; every source below was opened
directly by URL on 2026-11-05. PNMC still publishes no licensing exam format (pages above), so the
4 x 50 format, the 60% readiness line and the blueprint stay Tigertest's, labelled as such.

Confirmed (kept, source added):
- EPI schedule image, https://www.epi.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Vaccination_Schedule_2023.jpeg
  (read directly, 2026-11-05): birth BCG, OPV-0, hepatitis B; 6 weeks OPV-1, rota-1, pneumococcal-1,
  pentavalent-1; 10 weeks the second doses; 14 weeks OPV-3, IPV-1, pneumococcal-3, pentavalent-3;
  9 months IPV-2, MR-1, TCV; 15 months MR-2; "12" vaccine-preventable diseases by 15 months.
  PAE-001..004, PAE-006, PAE-021 (new, TCV), COM-001 (new, 12 diseases), COM-003.
- WHO multi-dose vial policy page, https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-IVB-14.07: opened vials
  kept up to 28 days when the policy's conditions are met, otherwise discarded after 6 hours or at the
  end of the session. COM-013, COM-014.
- ICN Code of Ethics for Nurses (2021), https://www.icn.ch/sites/default/files/2023-06/ICN_Code-of-Ethics_EN_Web.pdf,
  element 1.1: "Nurses' primary professional responsibility is to people requiring nursing care and
  services now or in the future". LAW-016.
- DRAP, https://www.dra.gov.pk/about-us/drap-at-glance/what-we-do/: enforces the Drugs Act, 1976;
  registration and marketing authorisation, licensing and inspection. Pricing is not described there,
  so LAW-004 now asks about registration only.
- BTS emergency oxygen page, https://www.brit-thoracic.org.uk/quality-improvement/guidelines/emergency-oxygen/:
  94-98% for acutely ill patients, 88-92% for those at risk of hypercapnic respiratory failure.
  FUN-014, FUN-009 (new).
- MedlinePlus urine specific gravity, https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003587.htm: 1.005 to 1.030. FUN-013.
- MedlinePlus acetaminophen, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a681004.html: no more than 4,000 mg a day. PHA-015.
- MedlinePlus rifampin, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a682403.html: reddish-orange urine, sweat,
  tears; stains contact lenses. PHA-010.
- MedlinePlus gentamicin injection, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a682275.html: hearing loss and
  kidney problems. PHA-014.
- MedlinePlus insulin injection, https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000660.htm: when two
  insulins are mixed, the short-acting (clear) one is drawn up first in the worked steps. PHA-007.
- MedlinePlus digoxin, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a682301.html: "If your pulse is faster or
  slower than it should be, call your doctor." The hold-below-60 rule was not found, so PHA-008 was
  rewritten to this.
- MedlinePlus sildenafil, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a699015.html: not taken with nitrates
  such as nitroglycerin. ADU-013.
- MedlinePlus phenelzine (MAOI), https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a682089.html: serious reaction
  with high-tyramine foods such as aged cheese, and meats. MEN-003.
- MedlinePlus olanzapine, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a601213.html: weight gain, high blood
  sugar, raised blood fats. MEN-005.
- MedlinePlus clozapine, https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a691001.html: severe fall in white
  blood cells; regular blood tests. MEN-006.
- CDC hepatitis B testing, https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html: HBsAg
  means the person is infected and infectious; anti-HBs means immunity, from vaccination when HBsAg and
  anti-HBc are negative and a full series is documented. ADU-002, ADU-010 (new).
- WHO dengue fact sheet, https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/dengue-and-severe-dengue:
  warning signs including persistent vomiting, which often come after the fever goes away;
  paracetamol, not ibuprofen or aspirin. ADU-003..005.
- WHO leishmaniasis fact sheet, https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/leishmaniasis:
  spread by infected female phlebotomine sandflies. COM-008.
- WHO emergency contraception fact sheet, https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/emergency-contraception:
  LNG pills as early as possible, within 120 hours; cannot interrupt an established pregnancy.
  The old "within 72 hours" key did not match WHO, so COM-019 was rewritten.
- WHO eLENA cord clamping, https://www.who.int/tools/elena/interventions/cord-clamping: not earlier
  than 1 minute; improves infant iron status. MAT-013.
- CDC milestones, https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/2-months.html (smiles when you talk to or
  smile at her), /9-months.html (rakes food with fingers) and /1-year.html (picks things up between
  thumb and pointer finger). PAE-008 and PAE-009 were rewritten to these ages; the old 6-week and
  9-10-month keys were not found in an official source.
Reputable secondary sources (primary pages unreachable: resus.org.uk, cpr.heart.org, BNF and NICE

- Wikipedia, Cardiac arrest (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiac_arrest): Hs include hypovolaemia,

- Wikipedia, Typhoid fever: ileal perforation in the third week. ADU-006.
- Wikipedia, Wallace rule of nines: each arm 9%, genitals 1%. ADU-026.
- Wikipedia, Recurrent laryngeal nerve: at risk in thyroid surgery, injury weakens the voice. The
  bilateral-airway clause was not confirmed and was removed from ADU-028's explanation.
- Wikipedia, Rho(D) immune globulin: postpartum RhIG within 72 hours; ACOG still advises giving it
  later because partial protection remains. MAT-011, MAT-020 (new).
- Wikipedia, Postpartum bleeding: four Ts (tissue = retained placenta); oxytocin first choice;
  ergometrine raises blood pressure. MAT-008 reworded to that, MAT-009.
- Wikipedia, Lochia: rubra about 1 to 4 days. MAT-010 (3 to 4 days kept; distractors are 1 day,

- Wikipedia, Fontanelle: posterior closes within 6-8 weeks, generally by 2-3 months. PAE-010.
- Wikipedia, Hirschsprung's disease: no meconium in 48 hours, distension, bilious vomiting, rectal

- Wikipedia, Button cell: can burn through the oesophagus within hours. PAE-015.
- Wikipedia, Birth spacing: WHO recommends 24 months between pregnancies. COM-020.
- Wikipedia, Naswar: a cause of oral cancer. COM-017.
- Wikipedia, Sharps waste: emptied or discarded at about three-quarters full. FUN-017.
- Wikipedia, Blood transfusion: standard red cell unit over 4 hours. FUN-023.
- Wikipedia, Nasogastric intubation: acidic aspirate supports placement (pH cut-offs differ, 4 in this

- Wikipedia, Tardive dyskinesia; Electroconvulsive therapy (confusion and memory loss, transient);
  Flumazenil (reverses benzodiazepines). MEN-004, MEN-014, PHA-013.
- Wikipedia, Partogram: Philpott and Castle's alert line detects abnormal labour. MAT-022.
