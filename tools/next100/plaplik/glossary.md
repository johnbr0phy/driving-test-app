# Glossary for `plaplik` (Polish, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations neutral and impersonal, in the style of the statutes and of
the official paper ("sąd", "strona", "pracodawca", "kandydat"). Landing and dashboard copy address
the reader as "Ty" in the singular informal ("Ćwicz", "Sprawdź"), the norm for Polish consumer
apps. Polish typography: „ ” quotes, decimal comma, a space as thousands separator in amounts
from 10 000 (5000 zł, 10 000 zł), "§" and "art." with a space before the number, superscript
article numbers as in the Dziennik Ustaw (art. 385¹, art. 398⁵). Dates DD.MM.YYYY or "2 kwietnia
2026 r.". No em-dashes; an en dash only in ranges without spaces where the statute uses one.

Code abbreviations as Polish lawyers write them: KC, KPC, KK, KPK, KKS, KW, KPW, KP, KRO, KSH, KPA,
PPSA, USP. Each appears in full at least once on the landing.
