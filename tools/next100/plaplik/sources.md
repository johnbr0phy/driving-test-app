# Sources for `plaplik` (Polish entrance exam for the advocate and legal adviser apprenticeships, in Polish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Polish from the statutes below. Nothing was
copied or reworded from a past paper (the Ministry of Justice and newspapers publish each year's
150 questions with the answer key), from a commercial test book or app, or from any question
bank. Every key was checked against the article cited in its explanation in the consolidated
text listed here. Checked 2026-11-21 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed from the statute and the Ministry)
- Ustawa z dnia 26 maja 1982 r. Prawo o adwokaturze, tekst jednolity Dz. U. z 2024 r. poz. 1564
  (stan prawny 10.10.2024), read on the Sejm ELI service on 2026-11-21:
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/1564/text.html
  - art. 75a ust. 3: scope of the exam (criminal law and procedure, petty offences, fiscal
    criminal law, civil law and procedure, family law, commercial and company law, labour and
    social insurance law, administrative law and procedure, administrative court procedure, EU
    law, constitutional law, the system of courts and prosecution, the bar, legal advisers and
    other legal protection bodies). Used for the twelve categories.
  - art. 75a ust. 4: the advocate and legal adviser entrance exam is held once a year on a date
    set by the Minister of Justice.
  - art. 75b ust. 1, 2, 6, 7: a nine-member team appointed by the Minister each year (5 from the
    Ministry, 2 from the Naczelna Rada Adwokacka, 2 from the Krajowa Rada Radców Prawnych) sets
    one common set of 150 single-answer questions for both apprenticeships; the list of statute
    titles (wykaz) is published at least 90 days before the exam, "według stanu prawnego
    obowiązującego w dniu ogłoszenia".
  - art. 75c ust. 1: the exam announcement is published in the BIP by 30 June each year (date,
    commissions, filing deadline, fee). The fee amount is not stated anywhere in Tigertest copy.
  - art. 75h ust. 3: no statute texts, commentaries, case law or other aids, no communication
    devices.
  - art. 75i ust. 1 to 1d and 3: 150 questions with three proposed answers, only one correct;
    one answer marked on the answer sheet (A, B or C); no changes; 1 point per correct answer;
    correctness judged by the law in force on the exam day; a pass needs at least 100 points.
  - art. 75j ust. 3: appeal to the Minister within 14 days of the commission's resolution.
  - Amendment Dz. U. z 2026 r. poz. 1290 (ustawa z 4 września 2026 r., in force 17.10.2026, art.
    1 pkt 16, 41 and 42 from 01.01.2027), read 2026-11-21:
    https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1290/text.pdf
    It changes the commissions (from 2027 one per bar council district, five members, three of
    them judges) but not art. 75i: the 150 questions, three options and 100-point line stand.
    It also adds the bar's emblem and robe (art. 1 ust. 1a to 1c) and new self-government tasks;
    the bank uses none of the changed provisions.
- Ustawa z dnia 6 lipca 1982 r. o radcach prawnych, tekst jednolity Dz. U. z 2024 r. poz. 499,
  read 2026-11-21 (amendments 2025 poz. 1172, 2026 poz. 370, 731, 846 checked for the articles
  used): https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/499/text.html
- Ministry of Justice, "Ogłoszenie o egzaminie wstępnym na aplikację radcowską w 2025 r."
  (published 05.05.2025), fetched 2026-11-21: exam set for 27 September 2025 at 11:00; time for
  disabled candidates extended by half.
  https://www.gov.pl/web/sprawiedliwosc/ogloszenie-o-egzaminie-wstepnym-na-aplikacje-radcowska-w-2025-r
- Ministry of Justice, "Egzaminy wstępne na aplikacje prawnicze" (24.09.2025), fetched
  2026-11-21: "Czas na rozwiązanie testu wynosi 150 minut" (225 minutes for candidates with
  disabilities), 150 single-choice questions, at least 100 correct to pass; 2,197 candidates
  admitted to the advocate exam and 2,742 to the legal adviser exam in 2025; in 2024, 3,988 of
  5,584 who sat the four entrance exams passed (71.4%).
  https://www.gov.pl/web/sprawiedliwosc/egzaminy-wstepne-na-aplikacje-prawnicze10
  The 150-minute limit is not in the statute; it is the Ministry's figure for 2025 and the copy
  says so ("według Ministerstwa Sprawiedliwości").
- Ministry of Justice, "Ogłoszenie w sprawie wykazu tytułów aktów prawnych na egzamin wstępny na
  aplikację adwokacką i radcowską w 2026 r." (published 02.04.2026), fetched 2026-11-21: 52 acts,
  from the Prawo wekslowe (1936) and the TFUE to the ustawa o fundacji rodzinnej (2023),
  including KC, KPC, KRO, KK, KPK, KKS, KW, KPW, KP, KSH, KPA, PPSA, the Constitution, USP, the
  Prawo o adwokaturze and the ustawa o radcach prawnych.
  https://www.gov.pl/web/sprawiedliwosc/ogloszenie-w-sprawie-wykazu-tytulow-aktow-prawnych-na-egzamin-wstepny-na-aplikacje-adwokacka-i-radcowska-w-2026-r
  The bank uses only acts on this list.

## Deliberately left out
- The exam fee (art. 75d) and every other amount that changes: court fees, fines, minimum wage
  amounts, thresholds that are indexed.
- Provisions changed in 2026 or pending: the 2026 bar reform (commissions, emblem, robe,
  self-government tasks), the KSH share reforms, the KP rules on equal treatment and mobbing
  (amendment poz. 1046), the prosecution service structure, and the Constitutional Tribunal's
  current composition. No officeholder is named.
- EU treaty law (TUE, TFUE) is on the list but not modelled: the bank keeps to Polish statutes.
- Questions about the entrance exam itself (format, scoring, admission): those facts are on the
  landing only.
