# Glossary for `plbron` (Polish, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations impersonal or third person ("posiadacz broni", "osoba
ubiegająca się"), like the statutes; landing and dashboard copy in "ty" (LANGUAGES.md 2.1).
Polish typography: „ ” quotes, decimal comma (5,45 mm, 7,5 J), km, m, mm, J, mA. No em dashes.
Statute names written out in explanations ("ustawy o broni i amunicji", "Kodeksu karnego");
"KK" only after "art." in short options. Article style: "art. 10 ust. 3 pkt 1", "art. 25 § 2".
