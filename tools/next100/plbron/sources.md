# Sources for `plbron` (egzamin na pozwolenie na broń, część teoretyczna, in Polish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Polish from the statutes and regulations
below. Nothing was copied from the question banks that regional police headquarters publish
(for example the Pomeranian KWP's "Baza pytań egzaminacyjnych"), from past tests, from course
books or from commercial apps. The Pomeranian KWP page
https://pomorska.bip.policja.gov.pl/KPG/pozwolenia-na-bron/40360,Baza-pytan-egzaminacyjnych.pdf
was not opened (its certificate failed in the sandbox on 2026-11-21), so no item there was read.
Checked 2026-11-21 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed)
- Rozporządzenie Ministra Spraw Wewnętrznych i Administracji z dnia 28 lipca 2023 r. w sprawie
  egzaminu ze znajomości przepisów dotyczących posiadania broni oraz umiejętności posługiwania
  się bronią (Dz. U. z 2023 r. poz. 1475), read on the Sejm ELI service on 2026-11-21:
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/1475/text.html
  - § 2 ust. 1 to 4: organised by the authorities that issue firearm permits; a commission of at
    least three members with knowledge of the subject, named by that authority; the authority
    names the chair.
  - § 3: the exam has a theory part and a practical part.
  - § 4 ust. 1: theory scope is (1) the Ustawa o broni i amunicji and the regulations issued
    under it, (2) the Kodeks karny provisions on offences connected with weapons.
  - § 4 ust. 2: the theory part lasts 30 minutes, as a test of 20 single-choice questions.
  - § 4 ust. 3: pass with correct answers to at least 18 questions.
  - § 4 ust. 4 and 5: the answer is marked with "X" on the letter A, B or C; a mistake is
    circled, initialled and the right letter marked.
  - § 5: practical part at a shooting range (safety rules, how the weapon works, parts,
    handling a malfunctioning weapon, field stripping and assembly, loading and unloading,
    clearing stoppages, shooting test for each kind of firearm applied for); a commission member
    must be a police shooting instructor or a shooting training instructor.
  - § 7 ust. 1 and 2: failing the theory means no admission to the practical part; one resit of
    each part and one justified change of date.
  - § 8: the exam is paid, set as a percentage of the minimum wage. No amount is stated on any
    Tigertest page (house rule).
- Ustawa z dnia 21 maja 1999 r. o broni i amunicji, art. 16 ust. 1 and 3 (exam before a
  commission of the competent police authority; theory and practical parts; commission of at
  least three with one shooting instructor). Text below.
- Brief context (the landing names only the komendant wojewódzki, from art. 9 ust. 1): Wydziały Postępowań Administracyjnych of the regional
  police headquarters (KWP, KSP in Warsaw) run the exam for firearm permits; miotacze and the
  art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 4 items go to the county commander (art. 9 ust. 3).

## Doctrine used in scenario items (standard reading of the KK, no single source)
- Obrona konieczna needs a zamach by a person; an animal attack not caused by a person is
  danger for stan wyższej konieczności, while setting a dog on someone is a zamach by that person.
- Obrona konieczna has no subsidiarity condition (no duty to flee); art. 26 § 1 has one ("nie
  można inaczej uniknąć").
- "Eksces intensywny" is the textbook name for the art. 25 § 2 case of disproportionate means.

## Kept out on purpose
- No question about the exam itself (number of questions, time, pass mark, commission, fee,
  resits) and nothing on the art. 16 ust. 2 exemptions.
- No fee or fine amounts. Penalty ranges in years are quoted from the KK and the ustawa.
- No weapon construction, modification or technical how-to; handling only as the range
  regulations state it. The practical part is not modelled.
- Hunting law (Prawo łowieckie) beyond the one storage cross-reference, the 1999 transitional
  amnesty (art. 54) and the art. 29 to 31 rules for entities (świadectwo broni) beyond storage.
