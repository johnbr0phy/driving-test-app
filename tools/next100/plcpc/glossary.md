# Glossary for `plcpc` (English base, Polish translation `pl`)

The Polish bank (`data/poland-driver-cpc-questions_pl.json`, sources in `pl/t_*.py`) keeps every
questionId, category, key and option order of the English bank; stems are reworded for idiom.

Register: second person singular ("ty": "co robisz?", "musisz", "sprawdzasz"), as in Polish
driving and CPC course material, avoiding gendered past and conditional forms ("powinieneś",
"zrobiłeś"; `pl/build.py` rejects them). Typography: „ ” quotes, decimal comma (4,5 godziny,
14,6 t, 0,5 m), spaces as thousands separators (18 000 kg, 2 500 daN), times 24-hour (10:30),
km/h, m, t, m³. No em dashes and no spaced en dashes; ranges "100-120 na minutę".
