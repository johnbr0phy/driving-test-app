# Poland LEK (Lekarski Egzamin Koncowy) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the CEM thematic structure, Polish
statutes and published clinical guidelines named below. None is copied or reworded from the CEM
public question base (CEM publishes past LEK papers), past papers, or any commercial LEK question
bank, app or course. Checked 2026-11-21.

## Format and blueprint (Centrum Egzaminow Medycznych, Lodz)

- CEM, LEK in English, information page: https://www.cem.edu.pl/english_info_lek.php
  (fetched 2026-11-21). "The examination consists of 200 test tasks (multiple choice questions -
  5 possibilities of answer where only one is correct)." "Duration of the examination is 4 hours."
  "The minimal passing score is 56% of the number of valid questions (i.e. 112 if all 200
  questions are valid)." Sessions in February and September (next listed: 20 February 2027,
  date may change under art. 14a(5a) of the Act on the professions of physician and dentist).
  Applications only through SMK (smk.ezdrowie.gov.pl), deadlines 15 July (September session) and
  30 November of the previous year (February session). The English LEK carries a fee set by
  art. 14a(8); Tigertest never states it.
- CEM, LEK in English, thematic structure: https://www.cem.edu.pl/english_lek_struktura.php
  (fetched 2026-11-21). 200 questions: internal medicine 39, paediatrics (incl. neonatology) 29,
  surgery 27, obstetrics and gynaecology 26, psychiatry 14, family medicine 20, emergency
  medicine and intensive therapy 20, bioethics and medical law 10, medical certification
  (orzecznictwo lekarskie) 7, public health 8. At least 20 questions on oncology among internal
  medicine, paediatrics, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology and family medicine. No list of
  recommended English handbooks.
- CEM, LEK information (Polish): https://www.cem.edu.pl/lek_info.php (fetched 2026-11-21). Test of
  200 tasks, 5 options, one correct; structure set in par. 2 of the Minister of Health
  regulation; 4 hours; "co najmniej 56 % maksymalnej liczby punktow"; LEK result is not an
  administrative decision; electronic result certificates in SMK for sessions from 1 September
  2025; disqualification bars the next session; LEK in a foreign language charged for every
  application.
- CEM, LEK FAQ: https://www.cem.edu.pl/FAQLEK.php (fetched 2026-11-21). Says students cannot sit
  LEK (older wording; the Act now allows it after 10 semesters, see below); a pass (at least 56%) certificate
  is sent within 21 days; a candidate below 56% may ask for a result certificate.
- Ministry of Health LEK/LDEK page (from the brief):
  https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/lekarski-egzamin-koncowy-lekarsko-dentystyczny-egzamin-koncowy-ldek
  (checked 2026-11-21): the URL now lands on the gov.pl portal home page without LEK content, so
  nothing is taken from it. Every format fact above is from CEM's own pages.

Not confirmed from an official page and therefore not stated as fact: the share of each paper
drawn from the published question base, the number of candidates per session, and how LEK
scores are weighted in residency qualification; the landing does not describe either. The 2019 candidate count in the brief came from a news site and is not used.

## Polish law (texts from the Sejm ELI API, consolidated texts)

- Ustawa z dnia 5 grudnia 1996 r. o zawodach lekarza i lekarza dentysty, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 37
  (consolidated text of 4 December 2025): https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/37/text.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-21). Art. 30 duty to help when delay risks death or serious harm; art. 32
  consent (statutory representative, plus the patient's own from age 16; guardianship court
  when a 16+ minor objects); art. 33 treatment without consent in urgent cases, consult another
  doctor if possible, record it; art. 34 written consent for operations and high-risk methods;
  art. 39 conscience clause (record in documentation, employed doctors notify the superior in
  writing beforehand, art. 30 excepted); art. 40 medical secrecy, including release after death
  by a close person unless another close person or the patient objected; art. 42 certifying
  health after a personal or teleinformatic examination and analysis of records; art. 43 death
  card may depend on an autopsy in justified cases (not for dentists).
- Same act, Chapter 2a and art. 5 (fetched 2026-11-21): art. 14a(1) LEK is organised and run by
  CEM, a state budget unit under the minister of health; art. 14a(2) twice a year; art. 14a(3)
  LEK may be taken by a doctor or by a person who has completed 10 semesters of the six-year
  medical programme at a Polish university, confirmed in SMK (this supersedes the older CEM FAQ
  line that students cannot sit it, so the landing follows the Act); art. 5(4) the regional
  medical council grants the right to practise to a Polish university graduate who passed LEK in
  Polish or in the language in which they studied. The landing says LEK is required for the
  full right to practise, citing this article.
- Ustawa z dnia 6 listopada 2008 r. o prawach pacjenta i Rzeczniku Praw Pacjenta, Dz.U. 2024
  poz. 581: https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/581/text.html (fetched 2026-11-21). Art. 3
  definitions (close person); art. 17 consent from age 16 and objection to the guardianship
  court; art. 29 record retention (20 years from the end of the year of the last entry; 30 years
  for death from injury or poisoning; 22 years for children under 2; 10 years for X-rays kept
  outside the record); art. 31 objection to a doctor's opinion to the Medical Commission at the
  Patient Ombudsman within 30 days.
- Ustawa z dnia 19 sierpnia 1994 r. o ochronie zdrowia psychicznego, Dz.U. 2024 poz. 917:
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/917/text.html (fetched 2026-11-21). Art. 18 direct
  coercion (doctor decides; a nurse may decide when no doctor's decision can be had at once and
  must tell the doctor); art. 23 admission without consent (approval by the head of the ward
  within 48 hours, the hospital head notifies the guardianship court within 72 hours); art. 24
  observation admission, at most 10 days.
- Ustawa z dnia 25 czerwca 1999 r. o swiadczeniach pienieznych z ubezpieczenia spolecznego w
  razie choroby i macierzynstwa, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 854 (consolidated text of 19 June 2026):
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/854/text.pdf (fetched 2026-11-21). Art. 8 sickness
  benefit for at most 182 days, 270 days for tuberculosis or incapacity in pregnancy; art. 16 no
  benefit for the first 5 days of incapacity caused by alcohol abuse; art. 55 electronic
  certificate (e-ZLA) on the ZUS form; art. 57 letter codes A (same illness after a break of up
  to 60 days), B (pregnancy), C (alcohol abuse), D (tuberculosis), E (illness under art. 7(2)),
  and B and D omitted at the insured person's written request. The act was amended in 2026
  (sick-leave control); only provisions in the consolidated text in force on the build date are
  used, and nothing on the new control rules.
- NFZ, fast-track oncology and the DiLO card:
  https://www.nfz.gov.pl/aktualnosci/aktualnosci-centrali/masz-podejrzenie-nowotworu-sprawdz-czym-jest-szybka-sciezka-onkologiczna-i-karta-dilo,8976.html
  (checked 2026-11-21): a family doctor with an NFZ contract issues the DiLO card on suspicion of
  cancer; the electronic e-DiLO is being introduced during 2026. The item asks only which
  document starts the pathway.

## Clinical sources (guidelines named in explanations, not question material)

Clinical facts follow current European and Polish guidance as taught at Polish medical
universities: ESC guidelines (atrial fibrillation 2024, hypertension 2024, heart failure,
STEMI, dyslipidaemia, valvular disease, pulmonary embolism), ERC Guidelines 2021 (adult ALS,
basic life support and choking, newborn life support), GINA, GOLD, Surviving Sepsis Campaign
2021, European guidance on hyponatraemia and on major bleeding after trauma, Maastricht VI and
Polish H. pylori guidance, EULAR (rheumatoid arthritis, gout), ESMO (melanoma), Polish
Society of Gynaecologists and Obstetricians guidance (folic acid, aspirin, GDM criteria with the
Polish Diabetes Association), Polish infant vitamin D recommendations, the Polish Protective
Vaccination Programme (Td every 10 years), Polish recommendations on uncomplicated urinary
tract infection, Polish Lyme disease guidance, and WHO (measles and vitamin A). Units follow
Polish practice: glucose in mg/dl, urate in mg/dl, LDL in mg/dl, electrolytes in mmol/l.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test: internal medicine 10, paediatrics 7, surgery 7, obstetrics and
  gynaecology 6, psychiatry 4, family medicine 5, emergency medicine and intensive therapy 5,
  bioethics and medical law 2, medical certification 2, public health 2. This scales CEM's
  39/29/27/26/14/20/20/10/7/8 to 50; the rounding is Tigertest's. Pools are exactly 4x.
- 23 items are oncology (internal 6, paediatrics 2, surgery 8, obstetrics and gynaecology 4,
  family medicine 3), in line with CEM's "at least 20 of 200".
- The real LEK uses 5 options per item. Tigertest's v1 engine shows 4, so each item is written
  with one key and the three strongest distractors; the landing says so.
- Medical law and certification items follow Polish statutes only, cited by article as read in
  the consolidated texts above. Psychiatry includes three Mental Health Protection Act items.
- The 56% pass line is the real rule (56% of valid questions), so the practice pass mark is 56%
  of 50 (28).
- No question asks about LEK itself (format, pass mark, sessions, fees, retakes, SMK).
- Duplicates: checked against every bank in data/ by stem overlap and key. Items that repeated a
  fact and answer in pmdc-nle, pance, ccrn, ascp-mls, ascp-mlt, cen, philippines-nle, kenck,
  pnmc and the CPR and first-aid banks were rewritten while writing (HFrEF drug, hyperkalaemia,
  DKA, pancreatitis, encephalopathy, lupus antibody, aortic stenosis, croup, measles,
  appendicitis, cholangitis, obstruction, GCS, CEA, melanoma, torsion, magnesium, GDM, praevia,
  anti-D, HPV, shoulder dystocia, lithium, NMS, serotonin syndrome, INR, CPR rate and depth,
  choking, NNT, prevention level). No Polish sibling (plteoria, plaplik, plbron, plcpc) covers
  medicine.

  https://www.cem.edu.pl/english_lek_struktura.php on 2026-11-22: 200 questions, 5 options, one

  under both ACCP (vitamin K above 10) and BSH (above 8) guidance.
