# Sources for `plteoria` (Polish car theory test, prawo jazdy kategorii B, in Polish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Polish from the laws and regulations below.
Nothing was copied, translated or reworded from the official Ministry of Infrastructure question
bank (published on gov.pl with its photos and video clips), a past test, a driving school's
e-learning or a commercial app. Signs are described in words with their official code (for
example "znak A-7"). Checked 2026-11-21 unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed from the regulation)
- Rozporządzenie Ministra Infrastruktury z dnia 24 listopada 2023 r. w sprawie egzaminowania osób
  ubiegających się o uprawnienia do kierowania pojazdami, szkolenia, egzaminowania i uzyskiwania
  uprawnień przez egzaminatorów oraz wzorów dokumentów stosowanych w tych sprawach, Dz.U. 2023
  poz. 2659, text fetched 2026-11-21:
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2023/2659/text.html
  - § 19 ust. 1 i 3: computer-based in the WORD (ośrodek egzaminowania) exam room, questions drawn
    at random in real time, built from "scenariuszy, wizualizacji i opisów" (every question has a
    picture or video clip).
  - § 19 ust. 4: 32 questions: 20 "z wiedzy podstawowej" (10 of high, 6 of medium, 4 of low
    importance for road safety) and 12 "z wiedzy specjalistycznej" for the category (6 high,
    4 medium, 2 low).
  - § 19 ust. 5: one correct answer per question; 3, 2 or 1 point by importance.
  - § 19 ust. 6: basic part answered TAK or NIE, 20 seconds to read the question then 15 seconds
    to answer; specialist part chooses one of A, B, C, 50 seconds per question.
  - § 19 ust. 8: an answer can be changed only until moving to the next question or the time runs
    out; no answer scores 0. So there is no going back to a closed question.
  - § 19 ust. 9 and 10: 74 points in all; pass with at least 68 points.
  - § 19 ust. 11: 25 minutes (40 minutes for sign-language users under ust. 7).
  - § 20: a second category in the same sitting adds 25 minutes.
- Rozporządzenie Ministra Infrastruktury z dnia 21 maja 2025 r. zmieniające (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 696),
  fetched 2026-11-21: changes the practical part only (§ 12, § 23); § 19 unchanged.
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/696/text.pdf
- The 2016 predecessor (Dz.U. 2016 poz. 232, fetched 2026-11-21) had the same 32/74/68/25 rule;
  it is cited only to show the format has been stable since 2016.
  https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2016/232/ogl/pol/pdf

## Languages (confirmed from Ministry answers to Sejm interpellations)
- Ministry of Infrastructure answer to interpelacja nr 9840, 14 July 2025, fetched 2026-11-21:
  besides Polish, the theory part can be taken in English, German and sign language, and in
  Ukrainian for category B. A sworn interpreter may help only before the test starts.
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDJLHQ6/i09840-o1.pdf
- Ministry answer to interpelacja nr 13691, 17 December 2025, fetched 2026-11-21: Ukrainian is
  available for categories B and C (not D).
  https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDPGK94/i13691-o1.pdf
- Not asserted: which WORD centres offer which language on a given day (the copy says "check with
  your WORD").

## Left out on purpose
- Fines (mandaty), penalty point values and exam or licence fees: they change and are not rules a
  driver applies at the wheel.
- The old 8-month novice limits (50/80/100 km/h) and the green-leaf sticker of the former art. 91:
  replaced by the new art. 91 from 3 March 2026, so neither old nor new speed caps are asked.
- The 12-point hazard-training rule for novices (points) and anything about the exam itself.
- Picture and video scenarios: not modellable; the landing says so.
- Truck, bus and CPC rules (sibling `plcpc` lane).
