# Sources for `pmiacp` (PMI Agile Certified Practitioner, PMI-ACP)

All questions are original, written by Tigertest from the domains, tasks, enablers and the tools and
techniques list of PMI's PMI-ACP Examination Content Outline, plus the public primary texts named
below (the Agile Manifesto and its principles, the 2020 Scrum Guide, the 2025 Kanban Guide). No PMI
practice exam, PMI Study Hall item, PMI Authorized Exam Prep item, training provider question or
commercial practice question was copied or reworded. Checked 2026-11-19.

## Outline and format (official, confirmed)
- Project Management Institute, "PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP) Examination Content
  Outline", https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/agile-certified-exam-outline.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19, 19 pages). Cover dated November 2024; every page footer reads "Examination
  Content Outline - March 2026", so this is the version in force on the build date. It gave:
  the four domains and weights (Mindset 28%, Leadership 25%, Product 19%, Delivery 28%); every task
  and enabler (Mindset tasks 1 to 7: experiment early, embrace agile mindset including complexity
  theory (CAS, Stacey Matrix, Cynefin) and agile suitability tools, promote a collaborative team
  environment including inter-team coordination (scrum of scrums, team of teams), build
  transparency, foster psychological safety, shorten feedback loops (design thinking, lean
  startup), embrace change (generalizing specialists); Leadership tasks 1 to 6: empower teams
  (training vs coaching vs mentoring, emotional intelligence, self-assessment tools), facilitate
  problem resolution (root cause analysis, Ishikawa), promote knowledge sharing (lessons learned,
  communities of practice), promote agile principles, promote shared vision, facilitate conflict
  management (root cause and level of conflict); Product tasks 1 to 4: refine the backlog, manage
  increments, visualize work, manage value delivery; Delivery tasks 1 to 7: seek early feedback,
  manage agile metrics, manage impediments and risk, recognize and eliminate waste (value stream),
  perform continuous improvement, actively engage customers, optimize flow (WIP limits, shielding
  the team)); the tools and techniques list (roadmap, story maps, personas, wireframes, chartering,
  relative sizing, planning poker, wideband Delphi, affinity estimating, ideal time, information
  radiators, osmotic communication, velocity, throughput, cycle time, lead time, EVM for agile
  projects, defect rates, WIP, Kanban and task boards, timeboxing, burn charts, cumulative flow
  diagrams, Kaizen, five whys, retrospectives and intraspectives, value stream mapping, control
  limits, pre-mortem, fishbone diagrams, definition of done, continuous integration, exploratory and
  usability testing, risk-adjusted backlog, risk burn down, risk-based and architectural spikes,
  ROI/NPV/IRR, MVP, MMF, MoSCoW, Kano analysis).
  Format (pages 6 and 17 to 18): 120 items, 100 scored and 20 unscored pretest items placed at
  random; multiple choice, multiple response, drag-and-drop and exhibit items; 3 hours; one
  10-minute break after items 1 to 60, after which the first section cannot be revisited; optional
  tutorial and survey outside the 3 hours; English plus Arabic, Brazilian Portuguese, French,
  German, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese and Spanish; test centre or OnVUE
  online proctoring through Pearson VUE; up to three attempts in the one-year eligibility period;
  30 PDUs every 3 years. Eligibility (page 15): secondary diploma, 21 hours of agile training, and
  2 years of agile experience in the past 5 years, or 1 year with a GAC degree or an active
  third-party agile certification, or an active PMP. These go on the landing only, never in
  questions.
- The outline publishes no passing score, so the copy gives none and the goal line is Tigertest's.
- pmi.org's HTML certification page (https://www.pmi.org/certifications/agile-acp) returned 403 to
  automated fetches on 2026-11-19; everything above comes from the PDF PMI hosts on the same site.

## Primary texts the questions are written from
- "Manifesto for Agile Software Development", https://agilemanifesto.org/ (fetched 2026-11-19): the
  four value statements.
- "Principles behind the Agile Manifesto", https://agilemanifesto.org/principles.html
  (fetched 2026-11-19): the twelve principles (early and continuous delivery, welcome change,
  deliver frequently, daily collaboration, motivated individuals, face-to-face conversation, working
  software as the primary measure of progress, sustainable pace, technical excellence, simplicity as
  maximizing work not done, self-organizing teams, regular reflection).
- Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland, "The Scrum Guide" (November 2020, CC BY-SA 4.0),
  https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html (fetched 2026-11-19): accountabilities, events and
  timeboxes, artifacts and commitments (Product Goal, Sprint Goal, Definition of Done), sprint
  cancellation, the Scrum Team of typically 10 or fewer.
- John Coleman and Daniel Vacanti, "The Kanban Guide" (May 2025, v2025.5),
  https://kanbanguides.org/english/ (fetched 2026-11-19): Definition of Workflow, controlling WIP as a
  pull system, the service level expectation (a period and a probability, from historical cycle
  time), and the four mandatory flow metrics with their definitions (WIP, throughput, work item
  age, cycle time).
- The Cynefin Co, Cynefin wiki, https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin (fetched 2026-11-19): ordered
  systems where cause and effect are clear or discoverable through analysis, complex systems that
  can only be understood by interacting with them, chaotic systems needing immediate stabilizing
  action. Only these distinctions are asked; domain names that changed between versions of the
  framework are not.

## Left out
- PMI's Agile Practice Guide and the PMI Authorized Exam Prep materials are paid and were not read;
  no question depends on their wording.
- The Stacey Matrix and complex adaptive systems are named in the outline, but no primary text was
  read for them, so they are asked only at the level of "uncertainty and disagreement push work
  toward complexity" in one item, or not at all.
- SAFe-specific roles and events, fees, PDU rules, eligibility and anything about the exam itself.
