# Praxis PLT K-6 (5622) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-02.

## Official

- ETS, The Praxis Study Companion, Principles of Learning and Teaching: Grades K-6 (5622),
  copyright 2019 (PDF last modified February 2021), read directly:
  https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/pdfs/5622.pdf
  (redirected from https://www.ets.org/pdfs/praxis/5622.pdf)
  - Test at a Glance: time 2 hours; 70 selected-response questions and 4 constructed-response
    questions; constructed-response questions related to two case histories; computer delivered.
  - Content categories: I. Students as Learners, about 21 selected-response, 22.5%; II.
    Instructional Process, about 21, 22.5%; III. Assessment, about 14, 15%; IV. Professional
    Development, Leadership, and Community, about 14, 15%; V. Analysis of Instructional Scenarios,
    4 constructed-response, 25% (A Students as Learners 1-2, B Instructional Process 1-2,
    C Assessment 0-1, D Professional Development, Leadership, and Community 0-1).
  - Pacing: about 70 minutes expected on the selected-response section and about 50 minutes on
    the constructed-response section; the sections are not independently timed.
  - The test reflects the InTASC Model Core Teaching Standards; it may contain questions that do
    not count toward the score; some questions are specific to grades K-6 and some universal.
  - Topic lists used for the categories: I.A theorists (Bandura, Bruner, Dewey, Piaget,
    Vygotsky, Kohlberg, Bloom), learning theory terms, domains of development; I.B variables,
    exceptionalities, ADA, IDEA, Section 504, gifted, English learners, accommodations; I.C
    behavioral theorists (Thorndike, Watson, Maslow, Skinner, Erikson), motivation theory,
    classroom management, self-motivation; II.A standards, theories, scope and sequence,
    objectives and Bloom, enrichment/remediation, resources, thematic and interdisciplinary units,
    planning partners; II.B instructional models and strategies, complex cognitive processes,
    grouping, monitoring and adjusting, memory, teachable moments; II.C questioning; II.D
    communication; III.A assessment types, formats, tools, self and peer assessment; III.B
    standardized tests, norm vs criterion referenced, testing terms, holistic vs analytic,
    interpreting results; IV professional development, research, reflective practice, support
    personnel, community, collaboration, and IV.7 legislation and court decisions (equal access,
    privacy, First Amendment, intellectual freedom, mandated reporting, due process, liability,
    licensing and tenure, copyright).
- ETS, Praxis test page for 5622: https://praxis.ets.org/test/5622.html (checked 2026-11-02):
  "2 Hours", "70 selected-response questions and 4 constructed-response questions", computer
  delivered, at home (online) or at a test center. No new version of 5622 announced; a separate
  new Elementary Education test is announced for spring 2026 (different exam).
- ETS score requirements: https://praxis.ets.org/scores. ETS does not set passing scores; each
  state or licensing agency sets its own qualifying score.

## Guides (labelled as such where used)

- Scaled score range 100 to 200 and a qualifying score of 160 in many states: reported by
  guides (for example the open-exam-prep 5624 overview and state-by-state tables). Not
  confirmed from an ETS page; the landing says "guides report" and points to the state.

## Law and policy facts used in the bank (stable, federal)

- FERPA (20 U.S.C. 1232g): parents may inspect and review education records; records disclosed
  only with consent or to school officials with a legitimate educational interest.
- IDEA: IEP, least restrictive environment, Child Find, evaluation with informed parent consent,
  IEP team membership.
- Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973; Americans with Disabilities Act (effective
  communication with people with disabilities).
- Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972.
- Supreme Court: Brown v. Board of Education (1954), Engel v. Vitale (1962, school-sponsored
  prayer), Tinker v. Des Moines (1969), Lau v. Nichols (1974), Goss v. Lopez (1975), Plyler v.
  Doe (1982).
- Mandated reporting and good-faith immunity: every state's child abuse reporting law
  (details vary by state, so items say "as state law requires").
- Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. 107 (four fair use factors).
