# PRINCE2 7 Foundation (`prince2`) sources

Every question in `data/v2/prince2/questions.json` is original, written for Tigertest from the
PeopleCert documents below. No official sample paper item, PeopleCert mock exam item, prep
provider question or leaked material was used or reworded. The PeopleCert sample paper and its
rationales were deliberately not opened.

## Exam owner, format and currency

- PeopleCert, "PRINCE2 Project Management Foundation (Version 7)" exam page
  https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/project-programme-and-portfolio-management/PRINCE2-2/PRINCE2-7-foundation-3579
  fetched 2026-11-16. 60 questions, multiple choice, 60 minutes, closed book, minimum required
  score to pass 60%; languages English, Chinese, Czech, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian,
  Japanese, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Slovak, Spanish; certification renewal every
  3 years (60 CPD points, or passing another exam in the same product suite); no prerequisites
  listed. Topic list on the page: principles, practices, processes, roles and responsibilities,
  people management, project performance (including sustainability), digital and data
  management, communication, tailoring. No newer edition or retirement is mentioned.
- PeopleCert news and announcements, searched 2026-11-16 (https://www.peoplecert.org/en/news-and-announcements):
  no PRINCE2 8th edition and no retirement of Version 7 announced. A search extract of
  PeopleCert's certificate naming notice says the certificates read "PRINCE2 Project Management
  Foundation/Practitioner VERSION 7" from 15 January 2026, with the official books updated to
  v7.2; the notice page itself returned 404 on 2026-11-16, so this is not stated on the landing.
- PeopleCert, "Emboldening Project Success: Unveiling PRINCE2 7"
  https://www.peoplecert.org/news-and-announcements/PRINCE2_7
  fetched 2026-11-16: launched September 2023; people management, tailoring, digital and data,
  sustainability.

## Syllabus (learning outcomes and weights)

- PeopleCert, "PRINCE2 7 Foundation Syllabus", Official Training Materials, Copyright PeopleCert
  International Ltd. (PeopleCert's own document, hosted by an accredited training organization)
  https://2623052.fs1.hubspotusercontent-eu1.net/hubfs/2623052/Premiuminnhold/PRINCE2-syllabus.pdf
  fetched 2026-11-16 and read in full. Exam overview: closed book, 60 minutes (25% extra time,
  75 minutes, when not sitting in a native or working language), 60 questions of 1 mark, no
  negative marking, pass mark 36 marks (60%), Bloom's levels 1 and 2, question styles standard,
  missing word, list (two correct of four statements) and, exceptionally, negative. Exam
  specification (weightings): LO1 key concepts 3%, LO2 principles 8%, LO3 people 14%,
  LO4 practices 60%, LO5 processes 15%. LO4 covers the business case, organizing, plans,
  quality, risk, issues and progress practices (purpose, relationships with the principles,
  management products, key concepts, guidance and the PRINCE2 technique for each) plus the PID
  and project log. LO5 covers the purpose, objectives and context of the seven processes.
  The same PDF also holds the PRINCE2 7 Practitioner syllabus (open book, 2 h 30 min, 70 marks,
  42 to pass), which is not modelled.

## The method (primary text available to the public)

- PeopleCert, "PRINCE2 7 Foundation Quick Reference Guide", Official Training Materials
  https://www.nilc.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/PRINCE2-Quick-Reference-Guide.pdf
  fetched 2026-11-16 and read in full. Project definition and characteristics, the five
  integrated elements, the seven aspects of performance, the principles, features and benefits,
  people (ecosystems, change management, stakeholders, teams, communication), each practice's
  purpose, the roles, the four organizational levels, plan types and the planning technique,
  quality terms and procedure, risk terms, responses and technique, issue types and technique,
  baseline, change budget, tolerances and controls, and each process's purpose and objectives.
- PeopleCert, "PRINCE2 7 Glossary", Official Training Materials
  https://mypd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PRINCE2_7_Glossary.pdf
  fetched 2026-11-16 and read in full. Definitions used in keys (acceptance criteria, benefit,
  dis-benefit, change authority, change budget, concession, daily log, event-driven and
  time-driven control, exception, issue report, logs and records, management product types,
  premature closure, project assurance, project brief, project mandate, residual risk, risk
  terms, stage, team plan, tolerance, work package description and others). Two entries in this
  copy have swapped text (risk impact and risk management); neither was used.
- The official book, "Managing Successful Projects with PRINCE2 7" (PeopleCert, 2023), is sold,
  not public, and was not read. Facts that appear only there, such as the practice-to-principle
  tables, the full activity lists inside each process and who approves tailoring, are not asked
  beyond what the syllabus, guide and glossary state.

## Left out on purpose

- Fees, booking and anything about the exam itself (only on the landing).
- PRINCE2 Practitioner (scenario-based, open book) and PRINCE2 Agile.
- The detail of process activities (the syllabus tests purpose, objectives and context only).
- The syllabus's list style is written as single answer with fixed number pairs (1 and 2,
  1 and 3...), shown in a locked order. Every list item and its statements are original.
