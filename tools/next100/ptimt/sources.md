# Sources for `ptimt` (Portugal IMT driving theory exam, category B)

All 200 questions are original, written from the Portuguese law below. The IMT publishes its own
official exam questions (with pictures) at imt-ip.pt; those were not opened for copying and no
item is taken from them or from any commercial app. Signs are described in words. Checked
2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Exam format (official)
- Decreto-Lei n.º 138/2012, de 5 de julho, which approves the Regulamento da Habilitação Legal
  para Conduzir (RHLC), Diário da República 1.ª série n.º 129 (copy on the Madeira regional
  government site, read 2026-11-01):
  https://www.madeira.gov.pt//Portals/27/Documentos/Legislacao/2012_07_05_DecLei_138_2012_AlteraoCodigodaEstradaeAprovaoRegulamentodaHabilitacaoLegalparaConduzir.pdf
  (Diário da República record: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/138-2012-178550)
  - Art. 43(1)(a): categories B1 and B, 30 questions (art. 43(1)(b): A1, A2 and A, 40 questions; art. 43(4): two to four answer options, one correct) on the provisions common to all categories
    (Annex VII, part I, section II). Art. 43(2): questions supported where possible by figures or
    images of traffic situations from the driver's point of view.
  - Art. 46(a): 30 minutes for the category B test.
  - Art. 47(1): pass with at least 27 of 30 correct. Art. 47(2): a theory pass is valid one year.
  - Art. 44(2) (2012 text, replaced from 8 July 2026, see DL 114/2026 below): for the theory tests of categories AM, A1, A2, A, B1 and B, a candidate without
    enough Portuguese may ask the IMT for the test translated into their own language or, failing
    that, a translator they name who is recognised by the IMT. Art. 44(3): the translator sees the
    test in the two hours before it and translates it into the candidate's language. So English is
    available by request, not as a standard on-screen language; it is arranged through the driving
    school when booking (learndrivingtheory.com guide, below). The practical test is not translated
    (postal.pt, 6 April 2025, quoting the IMT).
  - Minimum age (RHLC art. on ages): categories A2, B, BE, C1 and C1E 18 years.
  - Annex VII part I, section II: topics of the common theory programme (road traffic system,
    reaction time, signs, rules and manoeuvres, alcohol and psychotropic substances, the driver's
    physical and mental state, fatigue, medicines, the vehicle and its safety equipment, other road
    users and defensive driving, responsibility and behaviour after an accident, adapting to roads
    and weather, licensing). The eight Tigertest categories follow these topics.
- IMT, "Carta de Condução: novas regras em vigor a 2 de novembro" (2012 page, read 2026-11-01): the
  category B theory test keeps 30 questions; theory pass valid one year.
  https://www.imt-ip.pt/pt/condutores/carta-de-conducao
- IMT, "Perguntas de exame" (read 2026-11-01): the IMT publishes the theory questions; they come
  with related images and alternative answers, only one correct; it may change them when needed.
  https://www.imt-ip.pt/condutores/obtencao/perguntas-de-exame/
- Decreto-Lei n.º 114/2026, de 8 de junho (seventh amendment to the RHLC), Diário da República
  1.ª série n.º 109, read 2026-11-01 (reviewer):
  https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2026/06/10900/0001900030.pdf
  It amends RHLC arts. 2, 3, 14-17, 24, 25, 32, 33, 35 and 44, not arts. 43, 46 or 47, so the
  30 questions / 30 minutes / 27 to pass stand. New art. 44(2): where the candidate has shown
  insufficient Portuguese and the translated test can be kept materially equivalent, the IMT may
  authorise a translated theory test on request, now for every licence category (the old text
  covered AM, A1, A2, A, B1 and B and named a translator as the fallback). Art. 6(2): the art. 44(2)
  change takes effect 30 days after publication (8 July 2026). New art. 35(13): exams may use
  technology to prevent fraud. The landing, examFacts and config comment now say "translated test
  on request" without the translator wording.
- Earlier RHLC amendments (2014-2020) were not each re-read for arts. 43, 46 and 47; every 2026
  guide found still gives 30 questions, 30 minutes and 27 to pass (autoviatest.com, 2026;
  learndrivingtheory.com).
- Decreto-Lei n.º 112/2026 (5 June 2026) amends the driving-instruction regime (tutor learning);
  press reports of a four-month wait before a resit for candidates who skip an optional school
  assessment were not confirmed from the text and are not used.

## Guides (labelled, not official)
- learndrivingtheory.com, "IMT theory exam English translation" (no date; read 2026-11-01):
  30 questions in 30 minutes, at most 3 errors; the request for an English translation goes
  through the driving school at enrolment; the printed translation is matched to on-screen
  question numbers. Fee figures on the page are not used.
  https://www.learndrivingtheory.com/portugal/procedure/imt-theory-exam-english-translation
- Generali Tranquilidade blog: 30 multiple-choice questions with 2 to 4 answers each (so the real
  test does not always have four options; Tigertest's have four).
  https://www.generalitranquilidade.pt/blog/familia/conselhos-passar-exame-de-conducao
- postal.pt, "É preciso saber falar português para tirar a carta de condução em Portugal?"
  (6 April 2025): art. 44(2) translated theory test; practical test not translated.
  https://postal.pt/nacional/e-preciso-saber-falar-portugues-para-tirar-a-carta-de-conducao-em-portugal-saiba-o-que-diz-a-lei

## Rules of the road (official)
- Código da Estrada (Decreto-Lei n.º 114/94, consolidated with every amendment to Lei n.º 24/2025,
  de 12/03), Procuradoria-Geral Regional de Lisboa consolidated text, read 2026-11-01:
  https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?nid=349&tabela=leis
  (articles 98 onward on page 2:
  https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?ficha=101&artigo_id=&nid=349&pagina=2&tabela=leis)
  Used: art. 4 (orders of authorities), 7 (hierarchy: officers, temporary signs, variable message
  signs, lights, vertical signs, markings), 13-15 (keep right, lanes, parallel queues), 14-A
  (roundabouts), 17 (children up to 10 may cycle on pavements), 18 (distance; 1.5 m to cyclists),
  19 (reduced visibility under 50 m), 20 (buses pulling out in localities), 21 (signalling),
  22-23 (horn only in danger or, outside localities, to warn of overtaking and at blind spots;
  headlight flashes instead at night in localities), 24-25 (adapt speed), 27(6) (motorway minimum
  50 km/h), 29-33 (priority to the right; leaving property, entering motorways and roundabouts;
  level crossings; trams; cycle crossings; military columns and police escorts; animal-drawn
  vehicles; narrow and steep roads: the descending driver gives way, the uphill one reverses at
  equal distance, light before heavy), 36-42 (overtaking on the left, exceptions on the right,
  1.5 m, banned places, slow vehicles 50 m, parallel queues), 43-47 (turning, U-turns,
  reversing), 48-50 (stopping and parking distances: 5 m from junctions and crossings, 50 m
  outside localities, 25 m/5 m at bus stops, 3 m from a continuous line, 10 m from level
  crossings, 5 m from fuel stations), 55 (children under 12 below 135 cm in restraints; rear
  seat; under-3 rear-facing in front with airbag off; no under-3 in cars without belts),
  60-63 (lights: dipped up to 30 m, main at least 100 m; dipped in signed tunnels by day; fog
  lights only when justified; hazard lights), 64-65 (emergency vehicles; keep the hard shoulder
  free on motorways), 67-69 (level crossings; do not block junctions), 72-74 (motorways: banned
  users, no reversing or U-turns, slip roads, lorries over 7 m in the two right lanes), 78-A
  (coexistence zones), 81 (alcohol: 0.5 g/l; 0.2 g/l for probationary and professional drivers,
  including taxi and TVDE), 82 (seat belts), 84 (phones: no continued handling, single earpiece
  or loudspeaker allowed; headphones banned), 85 (documents; five days to present), 87-89
  (breakdown, warning triangle at least 30 m behind and visible from 100 m, reflective vest,
  accident details, wait for police if anyone is hurt), 101-104 (pedestrians), 122 (probation:
  three years), 130 (probationary licence lapses after a road crime, a very serious offence or a
  second serious offence), 150 (compulsory insurance). No fines, fees or demerit point figures are
  asked.
- Speed limit table of art. 27(1) (a table not rendered in the consolidated text above): cars 50
  in localities, 90 on other roads, 100 on roads reserved for motor vehicles, 120 on motorways;
  with a trailer 50/70/80/100. Source used: Wikipedia, "Speed limits in Portugal" (citing ANSR and
  the Código da Estrada), read 2026-11-01, consistent with guides:
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_limits_in_Portugal
  No novice-driver speed rule is asked (none found in the current art. 27 text).
- Drunk driving from 1.2 g/l is a crime: Código Penal art. 292 (well known; not re-read here).
- Regulamento de Sinalização do Trânsito (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 22-A/98), as amended by
  Decreto Regulamentar n.º 6/2019 (read 2026-11-01): S5 flashing amber = pass with special care;
  S4 supplementary green arrow; S8 and S9 (level crossings: flashing or alternate red = stop,
  white lunar light = may pass); S11 flashing amber pedestrian light; S12 red crossed bars over a
  lane = lane closed; H46/H47 coexistence zone signs; markings M12, M13 and M14 (no stopping or
  parking lines, zigzag) are yellow.
  https://invicta.pt/pdf/legislacao/Decreto%20Regulamentar%206-2019.pdf
  The meaning of M12 (continuous edge line: no stopping or parking) and M14 (zigzag: no parking)
  is from the 1976 predecessor, carried into the RST (search result summary of Portaria 254/1976),
  so those two items are worded generally.
- Tyre tread minimum 1.6 mm: common EU figure as stated by guides; not checked in Portuguese text.
- 112 is the European emergency number.

## Not modelled
The real questions come with photos or drawings of traffic situations; Tigertest describes signs
and situations in words. The practical driving test is not modelled.
