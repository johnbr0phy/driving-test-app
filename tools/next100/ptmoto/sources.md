# Sources for `ptmoto` (Portugal IMT motorcycle theory exam, categories A1, A2 and A)

All 200 questions are original, written in English from the Portuguese law below. The IMT
publishes its own exam questions (with pictures) on imt-ip.pt; those were not opened for copying,
and no item is taken from them, from any commercial app or from `ptimt`. Signs and situations are
described in words. Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Exam format (official)
- Decreto-Lei n.º 138/2012, de 5 de julho, approving the Regulamento da Habilitação Legal para
  Conduzir (RHLC), Diário da República 1.ª série n.º 129, pp. 3443-3444 (copy on the Madeira
  regional government site, text extracted with pdftotext, read 2026-11-06):
  https://www.madeira.gov.pt//Portals/27/Documentos/Legislacao/2012_07_05_DecLei_138_2012_AlteraoCodigodaEstradaeAprovaoRegulamentodaHabilitacaoLegalparaConduzir.pdf
  - Art. 43(1)(b): categories A1, A2 and A, 40 questions, of which 30 on the provisions common to
    all categories (Annex VII part I section II) and 10 on the specific provisions for these
    categories (section III point I).
  - Art. 43(1)(c): categories A2 and A requested by a holder of category B1 or B: only the 10
    specific questions (section III point I).
  - Art. 43(2): questions supported where possible by images of traffic situations from the
    driver's point of view. Art. 43(4): two to four answer options, one correct.
  - Art. 45(2): sessions run every half hour for A2/A candidates who hold B1 or B.
  - Art. 46(b): 40 minutes for the 40-question test; art. 46(c): 10 minutes for the 10-question test.
  - Art. 47(1): pass with at least 36 of 40, or 9 of 10. Art. 47(2): a theory pass is valid one year.
  - Art. 44(2): translated theory test on request for AM, A1, A2, A, B1 and B (2012 text; replaced
    from 8 July 2026 by DL 114/2026, below, which opens it to every category).
  - Art. 20(1): minimum ages AM, A1 and B1 16; A2 18; A 24, or 20 with two years of A2; motor
    tricycles over 15 kW 21.
  - RHLC art. on categories (art. 3 in the 2012 text): A1 = motorcycles up to 125 cm3, 11 kW and
    0.1 kW/kg, and tricycles up to 15 kW; A2 = up to 35 kW, under 0.2 kW/kg, not derived from a
    version of more than double the power; A = any motorcycle with or without sidecar and motor
    tricycles.
  - Annex VII part I section III point I (specific programme for A1, A2 and A): 1 protective
    equipment (gloves, boots, clothing, helmet); 2 visibility (position, see and be seen,
    lighting); 3 adapting to the vehicle's instability and fragility; 4 adapting to the road
    (profile, condition, surface); 5 risk from surfaces and unstable points (manhole covers, lines
    and arrows, tram rails); 6 vehicle parts (frame, suspension, steering, instruments, controls);
    7 engine and systems (emergency engine stop switch, oil level, chain, belt and shaft);
    8 common faults, routine precautions and proper use. The four motorcycle categories of the
    bank follow these eight points.
- Decreto-Lei n.º 114/2026, de 8 de junho (seventh amendment to the RHLC), as recorded in
  `tools/next100/ptimt/sources.md` (read there 2026-11-01): amends RHLC arts. 2, 3, 14-17, 24, 25,
  32, 33, 35 and 44, not arts. 20, 43, 46 or 47. New art. 44(2): translated theory test on request
  for every category, from 8 July 2026.
  https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2026/06/10900/0001900030.pdf
- RHLC amendments between 2012 and 2026 were not each re-read for arts. 43, 46 and 47 (same
  limitation as `ptimt`).
- IMT, "Cartas de condução de motociclos" (read 2026-11-06): A1 from 16 (written consent of a
  parent or guardian under 18); A2 from 18; A from 24, or 20 with two years of A2; theory exam
  first, practical on another day; holders of a valid B licence aged 25 or over are also qualified
  for motorcycles up to 125 cm3 and 11 kW.
  https://www.imt-ip.pt/condutores/informacoes-gerais/quero-ser-condutor/cartas-de-conducao-de-motociclos/
- IMT news, "Novas perguntas das provas teóricas dos exames de condução" (23 December 2009, read
  2026-11-06): the IMT question database is split by category, including "Categoria 4 - A/A1 e
  B/B1" and "Categoria 5 - A/A1 para titulares da categoria B ou B1".
  https://www.imt-ip.pt/noticias/novas-perguntas-das-provas-teoricas-dos-exames-de-conducao/

### What the brief's research notes said, and what the law says
- The brief's forum note said candidates with no licence get 20 common plus 10 motorcycle
  questions. The RHLC says 40 questions: 30 common plus 10 specific, 40 minutes, 36 to pass.
  B and B1 holders seeking A2 or A get the 10 specific questions only (10 minutes, 9 to pass), as
  the note said.
- autoviatest.com (motorcycle page, "last updated 15 March 2026", read 2026-11-06) gives 30
  questions and 27 to pass for the motorcycle test, which is the category B figure; not used.
  https://autoviatest.com/pt-PT/driving-test/portugal/motorcycle

## Guides (labelled, not official)
- learndrivingtheory.com, "Obtain a category A motorcycle licence" (read 2026-11-06): A direct
  from 24, progressive from 20 after two years of A2, tricycles over 15 kW at 21; an A2 holder
  upgrading to A after two years skips the theory exam. Fee figures not used.
  https://www.learndrivingtheory.com/en/portugal/procedure/obtain-category-a-motorcycle-licence
- 4gnews.pt, "As motas podem passar entre os carros? O que diz a lei" (24 September 2026, read
  2026-11-06), quoting a lawyer to SIC Verifica: the Código da Estrada has no exception letting
  motorcycles ride between queues; overtaking is on the left (art. 36); weaving between cars is a
  dangerous manoeuvre. Used for PTMOTO-SPD-012 and SPD-022 (worded as the law's general rules).
  https://4gnews.pt/as-motas-podem-passar-entre-os-carros-o-que-diz-a-lei-sobre-esta-manobra/
- learndrivingtheory.com sign pages for C14a and C14c (read 2026-11-06) give two different wordings
  of which vehicles the general no overtaking sign covers, so no item asks whether it binds a
  motorcycle.

## Rules of the road (official)
- Código da Estrada (DL 114/94, consolidated to Lei n.º 24/2025), Procuradoria-Geral Regional de
  Lisboa consolidated text, read 2026-11-06:
  https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?nid=349&tabela=leis
  Used: art. 7 (order of signals: officers, temporary signs, variable message signs, lights,
  vertical signs, markings), 13 and 36 (keep right; overtake on the left), 18 (distance; 1.5 m
  when passing cyclists), 30 (priority to the right at crossroads and T-junctions), 31-33 (leaving
  property, joining motorways, trams, cycle crossings), 48 (stopping vs parking; outside localities
  off the carriageway; leave gaps and secure the vehicle), 49 (no stopping on bridges, tunnels,
  cycle tracks, pavements; 5 m from junctions; 6 m before a stop sign for vehicles on rails), 50
  (no parking blocking access to properties; in places reserved for certain vehicles), 69(2)
  (a vehicle caught in a junction with lights may leave it), 70(3) (urban car parks give at least
  5% of spaces to motorcycles and motor tricycles), 72 (motorways: no motorcycles of 50 cm3 or
  less; no stopping, U-turns or reversing), the motorway subsection applying to vias reservadas a automóveis e motociclos, 77(2)-(3)
  (reserved lanes: others may enter to reach garages, properties and parking or, where signed, to
  turn; motorcycles and motor tricycles may use them), 78-A (coexistence zones: games allowed,
  vulnerable users may use the whole width), 81 (alcohol 0.5 g/l; 0.2 g/l on probation), 82(3)
  (rider and passenger helmets of an approved model, fitted and fastened), 84 (no continued
  handling of devices), 85 (documents to carry), 86 (lenses noted on the licence must be worn),
  87 (breakdown: hazard lights, remove the vehicle), 88(1) (two- and three-wheeled vehicles exempt
  from carrying the warning triangle and vest), 89 (wait for police if anyone is hurt), 90 (riders
  may not ride hands off except to signal, feet off the footrests, be towed, lift a wheel, or ride
  side by side except on a special track), 91(1) (no passengers under 7 on motorcycles unless the
  vehicle has a rigid body), 92 (loads only in a trailer or load box; nothing that hinders
  riding), 93(2) (motorcycles ride with dipped headlights and rear position light on).
  The definition of vulnerable users (art. 1(q)) covers pedestrians and cyclists only, so no item
  calls a motorcyclist a vulnerable user.
- Speed limit table of art. 27(1) (not rendered in the consolidated text): cars and motorcycles 50
  in localities, 120 on motorways, 100 on roads reserved for motor vehicles, 90 on other roads;
  with a trailer 50/100/80/70. Wikipedia, "Speed limits in Portugal" (citing ANSR and the Código
  da Estrada), read 2026-11-06: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_limits_in_Portugal
- Regulamento de Sinalização do Trânsito (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 22-A/98, as amended by DR
  6/2019), as recorded in `tools/next100/ptimt/sources.md`: sign families (warning triangles, red
  rings, blue discs, information squares). Signs are described by shape and symbol only.

## Safety content
Protective gear, braking, cornering, countersteering, road surfaces, first aid (leave a rider's
helmet on unless they cannot breathe; firm pressure on heavy bleeding; cool burns with water;
signs of shock) and machine checks are general rider training content matching Annex VII section
III point I. 112 is the European emergency number.

## Kept clear of
`ptimt` (no repeated fact-and-answer pair: speed limits are asked for motorcycles and trailers,
not the car limits it asks; its 0.2 g/l, phone, medicine, bus pull-out, petrol station, emergency
vehicle on the hard shoulder, STOP sign, officer, insurance, probation, documents within five days
and crash-details items were replaced while writing), and checked by stem similarity against
`ukmoto`, `moto`, `zak53moto`, `nzmoto`, `sgrtt`, `ukcar` and `esdgt`; the siblings `ptcam` and
`pttvde` are in Portuguese on other subjects.

## Not modelled
The real questions carry photos or drawings; Tigertest describes them in words. The practical
tests (closed circuit and road) are not modelled. Fines, fees and penalty points are not asked.
