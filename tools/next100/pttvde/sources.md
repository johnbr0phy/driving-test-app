# Sources for `pttvde` (Exame TVDE, Certificado de Motorista TVDE, Portugal, in Portuguese)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in European Portuguese from the laws below. The
IMT publishes its own list of questions for the TVDE final assessment
(https://www.imt-ip.pt/condutores/obtencao/perguntas-de-exame/, confirmed 2026-11-07: under
"Profissionais", "TVDE - grupo de questões", one PDF of the final assessment questions); that list was not opened for
copying and no item is taken from it, from a past paper or from a commercial app. Checked
2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Official sources
- Lei n.º 59/2026, de 25 de agosto (first amendment to Lei n.º 45/2018, with the whole regime
  republished in its annex), Diário da República 1.ª série n.º 164, read 2026-11-06. In force on
  1 September 2026 (art. 7).
  https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2026/08/16400/0000300044.pdf
  Articles of the republished Lei n.º 45/2018 used:
  - art. 2(3): a service starts when a driver accepts a request on the platform and ends when the
    user leaves the vehicle; 2(4)-(6): taxi operators may also be TVDE operators, taxi vehicles may
    be registered for TVDE by the licence holder's own decision and, in TVDE service, may not take
    street hails, use taxi ranks or use taxi-only traffic rules; 2(9): a TVDE operator may not own
    or finance a TVDE driver training entity.
  - art. 3: operator licensing by the IMT; licence for no more than five years, renewable; renewal
    requested six months ahead; changes reported to the IMT within 10 days.
  - art. 5(1): service only by prior subscription and booking on an electronic platform; 5(3) the
    platform sends its contract templates to the AMT; 5(6): no street hailing, no taxi ranks.
  - art. 6: reduced-mobility vehicles on request; wait under 15 minutes, exceptionally up to 30;
    same price formula; guide dogs, wheelchairs and children's pushchairs must be carried.
  - art. 7: non-discrimination (list includes language, nationality, disability, age).
  - art. 8: refusal only for impassable or dangerous places, people behaving in a way that
    suggests danger, or requests made outside the law; luggage refused only when it would damage
    the vehicle; companion animals carried when accompanied and properly contained, unless danger,
    health or hygiene justify refusal.
  - art. 10: driver requirements (category B licence held more than three years with group 2
    endorsement; initial course passed; idoneidade; TVDE driver certificate with a unique
    registration number used on every platform; written contract); a valid taxi driver
    certificate replaces the course and the TVDE certificate (still needs the unique number and a
    platform); certificate valid five years, renewal six months ahead with idoneidade and the
    continuing course; initial course valid five years, at least 50 hours, theory and practice,
    six subjects (communication and interpersonal relations; legal driving rules; driving
    techniques; regulation of the TVDE activity; emergencies and first aid; individual driving);
    at least 80% attendance in each module plus the final assessment by the IMT or an authorised
    exam centre; 10(8): the final assessment is a test of 30 questions, passed with at least 27
    correct; continuing course 8 hours, full attendance, may be replaced by the taxi driver
    continuing course; courses only by driving schools or IMT-recognised training entities, which
    may not be TVDE operators; IMT seizes the certificate when the licence, course or idoneidade
    requirement lapses; a guia replaces the certificate while a request is pending; the driver
    carries the certificate, the guia or the taxi certificate; Código do Trabalho art. 12 applies
    to the operator-driver contract whatever it is called; working time: DL 237/2007 for employed
    drivers, DL 117/2012 for independent ones.
  - art. 10-A: the training course checks functional command of Portuguese; contents and criteria
    by portaria (not yet published when checked).
  - art. 11: idoneidade (crimes against life, physical integrity or personal freedom, sexual
    freedom, dangerous or drunk driving, crimes in the exercise of the driver activity; the
    rehabilitated are not affected; foreign criminal record when the applicant lived abroad six
    months or more in the five years before).
  - art. 12: vehicles registered with the IMT and entered on a platform by the operator; light
    passenger cars, national plates, at most nine seats including the driver; under 10 years from
    first registration, up to 12 for fully electric cars; yearly periodic inspection, the first one
    year after first registration; liability insurance covering passengers; non-removable
    identifier (dístico) with holographic or equivalent anti-fraud features, issued by the IMT,
    visible from outside, linked to the vehicle's registration, with a QR code showing only that
    the vehicle is authorised, its plate, the operator's licence number and valid insurance;
    registration valid five years, never longer than the operator's licence; advertising allowed
    as for taxis; no access to lanes reserved for public passenger transport (Código da Estrada
    arts. 76 and 77); comodato and usufruct banned except an owner-driver who alone drives the
    car, with no lending on; 12(5) the platform manager may not own TVDE vehicles. The dístico
    model is left to a portaria (not yet published when checked).
  - art. 13: at most 10 hours of TVDE driving in any 24-hour period, across all platforms;
    platforms enforce it and keep activity records for two years.
  - art. 14: the platform blocks operators, drivers and vehicles that fail the requirements; the
    operator answers for a non-compliant driver's access.
  - art. 15: price by distance and/or time tariffs or a fixed price set before booking; the
    platform's intermediation fee no more than 25% of the trip value without VAT; price formula
    shown before and during the trip; dynamic tariffs allowed when the formula and weighting
    factors are told to the user beforehand; a fixed-price alternative offered for every route;
    payment only by electronic means through the platform; electronic invoice after the trip.
  - art. 19: before and during each trip the platform shows the price and formula, a live map,
    rating buttons and a complaints button, the driver's unique number and photo, the vehicle's
    photo, plate, make and model, seats and year (and that it is a taxi in TVDE service where it
    applies), the option to choose a driver who knows Portuguese, and an emergency service that
    calls the authorities and shares the car's location; complaints go to the electronic
    complaints book; complaint records kept at least two years; user and driver rate each trip.
  - art. 19-A: optional in-car video, off by default, switched on for a given trip only with
    express acceptance by both; image only, never sound; a trip without video at no extra cost;
    signalled in the app and in the car; images encrypted and inaccessible to platform, operator,
    driver and user, reached only by judicial, police or competent administrative authorities;
    deleted within 30 days unless an incident is reported; never used for work control,
    performance, pricing or advertising; no facial recognition.
  - art. 20: the platform is jointly liable to users for the contract; records drivers' working
    and driving times; accepts only compliant operators, drivers and vehicles, checked through the
    IMT data-sharing platform (art. 20-A).
  - arts. 21-23: Portuguese law and courts for services in Portugal; alternative dispute
    resolution (Lei n.º 144/2015); supervision by the AMT and the IMT.
- IMT, "Certificação de motoristas TVDE" / "Entidades Formadoras Reconhecidas pelo IMT"
  (https://www.imt-ip.pt/?p=10228, read 2026-11-06): 50-hour initial course on modules 1 to 6 of
  Annex I of Portaria n.º 293/2018; 80% attendance per module; final exam of 30 questions, pass
  with 27 correct, by the IMT or an authorised exam centre; 8-hour continuing course (modules 1 to
  5 of Annex II, full attendance); a driver who passed the final exam does not repeat it at
  renewal; one certified without it sits it at the first renewal; taxi certificate holders need
  only a unique TVDE registration number; certificate valid five years; a lapsed certificate
  cannot be renewed. Fee figures on the page are not used. Re-read 2026-11-07: still 30
  questions, at least 27 correct, IMT or authorised exam centre, 50 h initial and 8 h continuing
  course, certificate 5 years, CMT holders exempt from the course; the page gives no time or
  language.

  (paragem; where to stop inside and outside localities), 49(1)(a), (f) (no stopping on bridges,
  in tunnels, where visibility is poor, on cycle tracks and pavements), 54 (passengers get in and

- First aid: INEM, "Manual de Suporte Básico de Vida" and INEM public guidance (112; protect the scene, alert, then help; posição lateral de

## Format facts (landing, FAQ and examFacts only, never in the bank)
- 30 multiple-choice questions, pass with at least 27 correct (Lei 45/2018 art. 10(8) as
  republished by Lei 59/2026; IMT page). 60 minutes (press reports of Portaria 344/2024).
- At the IMT or an IMT-authorised exam centre, after the 50-hour initial course at a driving
  school or IMT-recognised training entity (art. 10(7), (11)).
- Language: the course checks functional Portuguese since Lei 59/2026 (art. 10-A); no official
  source found offering the TVDE exam in another language. Copy says it is in Portuguese.
- Holders of a valid taxi driver certificate (CMT) do not take the course or the exam.
- Drivers certified before the exam became compulsory sit it at their first renewal after the
  continuing course (IMT page).
- IMT reported 829 exams and 235 fails by April 2025 (Renascença, 2 April 2025; not used as a
  current figure).
- Fees, fines and fares are never asked or quoted.

## Pending regulation (as of 2026-11-06)
- Lei 59/2026 leaves to portarias: the dístico model, the Portuguese-language check, and the IMT
  data-sharing specifications. Press (pplware, September 2026) reports the IMT saying these were
  still to be regulated. Questions state the law's principles, not unpublished details.
