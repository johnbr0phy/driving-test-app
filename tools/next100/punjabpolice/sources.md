# Sources for `punjabpolice` (Punjab Police, Pakistan: Constable and Lady Constable written test)

All 200 questions are original. Prep sites and video channels selling "Punjab Police past papers"
appeared in search results; none were opened for their questions and nothing was copied. Checked
2026-11-05 unless noted. GK lane in international batch 3 (to avoid overlap with ppsc, pakarmy
and pkpaf): Punjab's geography, districts and landmarks; policing under the Police Order 2002
and the Punjab Police Code of Conduct; criminal law basics under the PPC and CrPC; plus basic
computer skills. No Pakistan Studies, Islamic studies, Constitution, current affairs or
office-holders (ppsc's and the military banks' lanes, and dated content).

## Format: what is and is not confirmed
- NOT published by Punjab Police in any advertisement or notice read: the number of questions,
  the time, the subject split or a pass mark for the written examination.
- Prep-site descriptions of the format (100 MCQs on GK, current affairs, Islamiat, Pakistan
  Studies, Urdu, English and computer; or an older objective plus written paper) were seen only
  in search results and could not be checked against any Punjab Police source. Fact check
  2026-11-05: removed from the landing FAQ and the facts table, which now say only that Punjab
  Police publishes no format and that it can change between recruitments.
- Tigertest's model: four 50-question mock tests, 70% (35 of 50) as Tigertest's target. Weights
  are Tigertest's own: English 13, mathematics 7, reasoning 5, Punjab 7, Police Order and policing
  7, criminal law 6, computer 5 per test. Urdu, Islamic studies, Pakistan Studies and current
  affairs are left out (Urdu and Islamiat appear in guides; Tigertest is English only, and the
  Pakistan Studies lane belongs to the ppsc bank); the landing says so.
- Search results for "Punjab Police constable" are dominated by the Indian state of Punjab's
  police (punjabpolice.gov.in, a CBT with a Punjabi paper). None of that is used.

## Law used in the GK questions
- Police Order 2002 (Chief Executive's Order No. 22 of 2002, Islamabad, 14 August 2002), text as
  promulgated, read 2026-11-05: https://www.rightofassembly.info/assets/downloads/2002_Police_Order_(Pakistan).pdf
  Articles used: 2 (definitions: Provincial Police Officer, Capital City Police Officer, District
  Police Officer), 3 (attitude towards the public), 4 (duties: unclaimed property, arrest
  information to a person of choice, traffic), 7(3) (entry ranks Constable, ASI, ASP), 16 (DPO),
  21 (regions under a DIG or above; police station under an Inspector), 23 (Head of District
  Police appoints junior ranks), 24 (oath), 25 (certificate of appointment), 115 (liable to be
  called for duty), 117 (no private employment), 125 (search of suspected persons or vehicles),
  134 (inventory of unclaimed property), 151 (unauthorised use of uniform), 155 (misconduct,
  intoxication on duty), 156 (torture, vexatious arrest), 157 (delay in producing arrested
  persons), 167 (Daily Diary), 168 (Citizen Police Liaison Committees), 185 (repeal of the Police
  Act 1861). Punjab has amended the Order since 2002 (and a 2013 Punjab amendment is reported);
  the amended Punjab text could not be loaded (punjabcode.punjab.gov.pk refused the connection),
  so only articles about duties, conduct and offences, unlikely to have changed, are used, and
  no question turns on the Zila Nazim, Public Safety Commission membership or tenure rules.
- Code of Conduct for Punjab Police Officers (issued by the IGP Punjab under Article 114), read
  2026-11-05: report any bribe offered to a senior at once; minimum force; warnings and
  exhortations before declaring a crowd unlawful.
  https://Punjabpolice.Gov.Pk/system/files/Code-of-Conduct-for-Punjab-Police-Officers.pdf
- Pakistan Penal Code 1860 and Code of Criminal Procedure 1898, section texts read 2026-11-05 at
  PLJ Law Site (https://www.pljlawsite.com/html/ppc<N>.htm and /crpc<N>.htm): PPC 34, 82 (child
  under ten, as amended), 83 (ten to fourteen), 96, 120-A, 141 (five or more), 186, 300, 318
  (qatl-i-khata, the deer illustration), 337 (shajjah), 365-A, 378, 383, 391 (dacoity), 489-F;
  CrPC 46, 59, 61 (24 hours), 103 (two or more respectable inhabitants), 154 (FIR), 164, 167
  (fifteen days in the whole), 173, 550.
- Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 (PECA): name and year only.

## Fact check, 2026-11-05
- Both advertisements re-read from the official PDFs rendered as images
  (https://punjabpolice.gov.pk/system/files/Advertisement%208.1.2025_0.pdf and
  https://punjabpolice.gov.pk/system/files/Advertisement%20RYK%20and%20Rajanpur.pdf): confirmed
  age 18-22 settled / 18-25 de-excluded, SSC with 50% marks (settled), height men 5 ft 7 in and
  women 5 ft 2 in (settled), chest 33-34.5 in for men (settled; 31-32.5 in de-excluded), 1.6 km
  in 7 minutes (men) and 10 minutes (women), quotas 5% minorities, 15% women, 10% ex-servicemen,
  the sworn statement (bayan-e-halfi) that the candidate is not named in any FIR or case, the
  note that height, chest, race, written examination and interview are taken, and the issuer
  "DIG (Establishment II), Central Police Office, Lahore". The 2026 advertisement gives age on
  31.10.2026 and applications 12.10.2026 to 31.10.2026.
- Central Police Office, Lahore as Punjab Police headquarters: Punjab Police homepage ("A
  Provincial Monitoring Room has been established in the Central Police Office, Lahore"; RPO
  conferences at the CPO). https://punjabpolice.gov.pk/ (POL-026)
- CCPO Lahore and RPO rank: Punjab Police site search, news items "Govt of Punjab posted ... as
  CCPO Lahore", "In 2013 he was promoted as DIG and served as RPO Sargodha, Sheikhupura,
  Gujranwala". https://punjabpolice.gov.pk/search/node?keys=CCPO+Lahore (POL-004, POL-024)
- SDPO: Punjab Police pages "Divisional SPs, SDPOs and Police Stations - Lahore" list SDPOs over
  circles of police stations, and promoted Inspectors are "appointed DSP SDPO"; SHOs are named as
  in charge of police stations in district news. The unconfirmed Reserve Inspector description was
  removed from the POL-023 explanation (it stays only as a wrong option).
  https://punjabpolice.gov.pk/search/node?keys=SDPO
- Punjab amendments to the Police Order: Punjab Police news "Police Order 2002 amended"
  (11 May 2017) reports the Police Order (Amendment) Ordinance 2017 changed Article 11(1) (PPO
  appointment panel), which no question uses. The full amended Punjab text was still
  unreachable (punjabcode.punjab.gov.pk and punjablaws.gov.pk did not respond); the articles
  used are on duties, conduct, offences and registers, not on appointment boards or tenure.
  https://punjabpolice.gov.pk/node/5183
- PPC and CrPC: pakistancode.gov.pk returned 503 / no connection again. Section texts re-read at
