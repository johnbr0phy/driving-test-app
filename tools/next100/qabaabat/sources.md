# qabaabat sources (checked 2026-11-16)

All 200 questions are original. No QABA sample question, practice exam or prep-site item was used or
reworded, and stems were written to avoid repeating fact-and-answer pairs in `data/rbt-questions.json`
(the BACB RBT bank): where a topic overlaps (reinforcement, prompts, functions, IOA), the item asks a
different fact, a different scenario or a QABA-specific rule.

## Outline (what the exam covers)
- ABAT Job Task Analysis page (QABA), https://qababoard.com/pages/job-task-analysis-for-abat-certification/
  (fetched 2026-11-16). "ABAT Job Analysis Test Specifications", last updated June 2020 and "last
  reviewed by QABA Exam Committee August 2023": 14 domains with % and items (+/-2) of 100 scored:
  Autism Spectrum Disorder Basics 12, Education, Training & Self-Development 5, Principles of Applied
  Behavior Analysis 6, Instructional Interventions 6, Principles of working with Autism Effectively 6,
  Treating Individuals with Challenging Behaviors 8, Data Collection and Analysis 6, Positive Behavior
  Support 7, Pivotal Response Treatment (PRT) 5, Discrete Trial Training 4, Person Centered Planning 10,
  Functional Analysis 11, Philosophy, Values and Advocacy 9, Legal and Ethical Considerations 5.
  "125 Items - 100 scored and 25 unscored/pretest items."
- ABAT Competency Standards, January 2023 (PDF, QABA), http://qababoard.com/wp-content/uploads/ABAT-Competency-Standards-2023.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-16, https link from the ABAT page). Seven content areas A to G with their task lists:
  Autism Core Knowledge (DSM-5 core deficits, red flags, risk factors, terminology, differential
  diagnosis, co-morbid disorders), Legal, Ethical and Professional Considerations (scope, QABA code,
  mandated reporting, HIPAA, advocacy, IEP and IDEA), Core Principles of ABA, Antecedent Interventions
  (PBS, Premack, behavioral momentum, priming, forced choice, FCT, PECS, TEACCH, social stories, video
  modeling, visual schedules), Skill Acquisition Programming (prompts, fading, pairing, imitation, verbal
  operants, error correction, transfer trials, DTT, discrimination, shaping, chaining, NET, pivotal
  behavior, PRT), Behavior Reduction Interventions (BIP, four functions, FBA, contingency interventions,
  DRO, DRA, DRI, DRL, DRH) and Data Collection and Analysis (reliability, validity, operational
  definitions, measurement, graphs, IOA). It states that creating or modifying treatment plans is not
  within the ABAT's scope of practice.

## Format
- ABAT certification page (QABA), https://qababoard.com/pages/?p=8977 (fetched 2026-11-16): 125 questions,
  100 live and scored, 25 unscored pre-test questions not identified; 2 hours; pass point 72%; exam
  proctored online by Premier Proctoring; renew every 2 years with at least 12 CEUs; no retirement or
  change is announced.
- ABAT Candidate Handbook, last updated January 2026 (PDF, QABA), served from
  https://qababoard.com/pages/?document=abat-candidate-handbook (redirects to
  http://qababoard.com/pages/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/ABAT-Handbook-2026.pdf), fetched 2026-11-16.
  Exam table: ABAT, 2.0 hours, 125 questions, pass score 72%; candidates "immediately receive a pass or
  fail score". Requirements (18+, high school diploma, 40 hours approved coursework with 3 ethics and 5
  autism hours, 15 hours supervised fieldwork, recommendation, background attestation), ongoing
  supervision (5% of service time each month, remote through HIPAA-compliant video allowed with one
  in-person one-to-one session of at least 1 hour, up to 50% in a group), valid supervisors (QBA, QASP-S,
  BCBA/BCBA-D, BCaBA, licensed or certified professionals in scope), retake policy, renewal (at least 1
  hour of ethics), and the QABA Ethical Code of Conduct updated March 2024 (sections 1 to 10).

## Law, ethics and content sources
- QABA Ethical Code of Conduct, March 2024, in the handbook above: all items citing Code sections
  (1.2, 1.3, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 3.1, 3.4, 3.6, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5, 4.7, 4.8, 4.9, 5.2, 5.3, 6.5, 6.7, 6.10, 8.3).
- CDC, Clinical Testing and Diagnosis for Autism Spectrum Disorder (DSM-5 criteria, severity levels,
  specifiers), https://www.cdc.gov/autism/hcp/diagnosis/index.html (fetched 2026-11-16, page dated May 8, 2025).
- CDC, Signs and Symptoms of Autism Spectrum Disorder, https://www.cdc.gov/autism/signs-symptoms/index.html
  (fetched 2026-11-16): social communication signs by age (name by 9 months, gestures by 12 months,
  pointing by 18 months), repetitive behaviors, other characteristics (epilepsy, GI issues, anxiety).
- MedlinePlus Genetics, Autism spectrum disorder, https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/autism-spectrum-disorder/
  (fetched 2026-11-16): genetic factors 40 to 80 percent of risk, parental age and birth complications,
  more than four times as common in boys.
- 42 CFR 441.540, Person-centered service plan (govinfo),
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title42-vol4/xml/CFR-2023-title42-vol4-sec441-540.xml
  (fetched 2026-11-16): the process is driven by the individual, includes people they choose, convenient
  times and places, cultural considerations, conflict-of-interest guidelines, choice of services and
  providers, strengths and preferences, natural supports, backup plans, understandable plan, agreed in
  writing. Used for person-centered planning concepts only, not for program-specific review intervals.
- 34 CFR 300.320 (IEP definition) and 34 CFR 300.114 (least restrictive environment), govinfo,
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title34-vol2/xml/CFR-2023-title34-vol2-sec300-320.xml
  (fetched 2026-11-16).
- 45 CFR 164.502(b), HIPAA minimum necessary standard, govinfo,
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title45-vol2/xml/CFR-2023-title45-vol2-sec164-502.xml
  (fetched 2026-11-16), including that it does not apply to disclosures to a provider for treatment.
- Center on PBIS (U.S. Department of Education funded), What is PBIS, https://www.pbis.org/pbis/what-is-pbis
  (fetched 2026-11-16): three tiers (Tier 1 all students, Tier 2 targeted, Tier 3 individualized with FBA
  and person-centered planning).
- Behavior-analytic definitions (operant and respondent conditioning, schedules of reinforcement,
  motivating operations, DTT, PRT pivotal areas, chaining, shaping, differential reinforcement, IOA
  formulas) are the standard terms the competency standards list by name; every numeric item (IOA,
  rate, mean) was recomputed.

## Not confirmed / could not be loaded
- QABA publishes no task list under each of the 14 JTA domains. The competency standards' A to G areas
  were mapped onto them by Tigertest (see blueprint).
- hhs.gov HIPAA pages and sites.ed.gov returned HTTP 403; the CFR text on govinfo was read instead.
- CDC's autism risk-factor and causes pages returned 404, so risk factors come from MedlinePlus only.
  Vaccines and other disputed causes are not asked.
- The handbook does not state the period of the one in-person supervision session, so no item asks it.

## Deliberately left out
Fees, retake waits, question count, time, pass mark and anything about the exam itself; state ABA
licensing rules; BACB rules (RBT supervision percentages, BACB ethics code items); vaccine claims;
program-specific Medicaid review intervals.
