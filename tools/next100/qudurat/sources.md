# Qudurat, the General Aptitude Test (GAT), English version: sources

All 271 questions are original, written for Tigertest from ETEC's description of the test below.
None is taken or reworded from ETEC's sample questions, the practice questions in the Test-Taker's
Guide, the GAT Trial Test, any past paper, any prep book, course or app. The reading passages and
data sets are written for this bank; the people and figures in the data sets are invented.

## Format, sections and weights (the owner: ETEC, the Education and Training Evaluation Commission)

- ETEC, General Aptitude Test (GAT) product page, under the Qiyas Center (National Center for
  Assessment): https://etec.gov.sa/en/centers/qiyas (fetched 2026-11-25; the page's own content
  object, "General Aptitude Test (GAT)", last updated 2026-11-16). It gives: the verbal domains and
  their weights (Verbal Analogies 17%, Sentence Completion 7%, Contextual Error 10%, Reading
  Comprehension 21%); the quantitative domains and their weights for GAT for Science Tracks
  (Arithmetic 23%, Geometry 10%, Algebra 4%, Data Analysis 8%); the concepts listed under each
  domain; that verbal also covers related terms, odd-one-out and true/false statements; that
  Algebra is in the scientific version only; the FAQ answers: all multiple choice, paper-based and
  computer-based, calculators not permitted, total duration 2 hours and 5 minutes, five attempts,
  results valid for five years, norm-referenced scoring, no pass or fail, results in about a month
  (paper) or 3 to 5 business days (computer); the score-to-ranking table (81 and above top 5%,
  78 top 10%, 73 top 20%, 70 top 30%, 65 average, 60 and below bottom 30%); and the test-day rules
  (each section has its own time limit, no going back to an earlier section, no scheduled breaks).
- ETEC, "General Aptitude Test (GAT): Test-Taker's Guide" (English, InDesign PDF dated
  2026-08-03): https://media.etec.gov.sa/media/fw4be5em/gat-general-aptitude-test-gattest-taker-s-guide-en.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-25; linked from the product page as
  https://etec.gov.sa/media/fw4be5em/gat-general-aptitude-test-gattest-taker-s-guide-en.pdf, which
  serves the site shell instead of the file to a script). It gives: two versions, GAT for Humanities
  Tracks (Sharia, Business Administration) and GAT for Science Tracks (General, Health and Life
  Sciences, Computer Science and Engineering); "5 sections containing 24 questions each, for a total
  of 120 questions"; test language "Available in Arabic and English"; duration two hours and five
  minutes; the same weights and concept table as the product page; the instructions and the four
  fixed choices of comparison questions ("The first value is greater than the second value", "The
  second value is greater than the first value", "The two values are equal", "The information given
  is insufficient"); four answer choices on every item; the score-to-ranking table; who may sit it
  (second- and third-year secondary students and graduates). Its sample and practice questions
  were read for format only (four choices, comparison wording); none was used.
- ETEC, Frequently Asked Questions: https://etec.gov.sa/en/support/faq (fetched 2026-11-25). Confirms
  the GAT result is valid for five years and the test is given on paper and on computer.
- ETEC, e-services catalogue for individuals: https://etec.gov.sa/en/eservices-catalogue/individual
  (fetched 2026-11-25). Lists the GAT among the University Admission Tests ("a test designed to
  assess verbal and quantitative abilities ... for high school graduates across all tracks").

## How the bank models it

- English version, Science Tracks weights. A practice test has the real 120 questions in the real
  125 minutes, regrouped by section because the split of the five sections between verbal and
  quantitative is not published: verbal 66 (55%) in three timed parts of 22 at 23 minutes,
  quantitative 54 (45%) in two of 27 at 28 minutes. Per test: analogies and odd one out 20 (17%),
  sentence completion 9 (7%), contextual error 12 (10%), reading 25 (21%); arithmetic 28 (23%),
  geometry 12 (10%), algebra 5 (4%), data analysis 9 (8%). Counts are the weights times 120,
  rounded so each section adds up.
- Quantitative comparison uses ETEC's four fixed choices (shortened to "The first value is
  greater" and so on) in ETEC's order. Charts and Venn diagrams are given as tables or words.
- No calculator, as on the real test.

## Could not be loaded or confirmed

- The product page itself has no stable deep link that a script can load (the site is a
  single-page app that renders the GAT page from the Qiyas Center data); its content was read from
  the data embedded in https://etec.gov.sa/en/centers/qiyas.
- ETEC does not say how the five sections divide between verbal and quantitative, or the time per
  section ("the test proctor will inform you of the number of sections and the time allowed for
  each section"). The 23 and 28 minute parts are Tigertest's, at the real pace of 125 minutes for
  120 questions.
- The GAT score cannot be computed from a raw score (it is norm-referenced), so Tigertest reports
  percent correct and quotes ETEC's ranking table without converting to it.

## Left out on purpose

- The Arabic GAT (the site has no right-to-left support) and the Humanities Tracks version, whose
  quantitative weights the guide does not give and which has no algebra.
- True/false statements and "related terms", which the product page lists under the verbal areas
  but which have no weight in ETEC's domain table.
- Fees, registration dates and test-centre rules (they change and are not tested).
