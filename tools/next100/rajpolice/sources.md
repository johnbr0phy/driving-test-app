## Written exam pattern (secondary sources; the department's notice was not read)

- 150 objective questions, 2 hours, three parts: logical ability, reasoning and basic computer knowledge 60;
  general knowledge, general science, social science, current affairs and knowledge of laws on the rights of women and
  children, with government schemes and institutions of Rajasthan, 45; history, culture, art, geography, economy and
  polity of Rajasthan 45 (Utkarsh pattern table). Invictaa
  (https://invictaa.com/exams/updates/post/rajasthan-police-constable-syllabus/) gives the same 60/45/45 with the
  laws on women and children as a separate 10 within the middle 45 (35 + 10).
- Marks: guides for the 2025 recruitment give 150 marks, 1 per question (Utkarsh, Career Power). Older pages
  (Embibe, https://www.embibe.com/exams/rajasthan-police-constable/) give 75 marks at 0.5 per question with a 15-mark
  PET counted in merit, which matches earlier recruitments, not 2025. The copy says 150 questions in 2 hours and
  avoids stating total marks except as "1 mark each, per guides".
- Negative marking: a quarter of a question's marks per wrong answer (Utkarsh: "25% of the marks for a question will
  be deducted"; Embibe and Invictaa: 1/4). Not scored by the app.
  Most guides agree on 1/4 (PW, Khan Global Studies, Sarkari Talent, 2026-11-01), but Rojgar Dekho
  (https://rojgardekho.in/article/rajasthan-police-constable-syllabus-2025) and a Careers360 page give 1/3, so the
  copy keeps "guides report" and tells readers to check their notification.

## Syllabus followed (Utkarsh's summary of the notification's syllabus)

- Part A: analogy, series, puzzles, clock and calendar, statement and conclusion, word and letter arrangement,
  directions, blood relations, coding-decoding, arithmetic reasoning; basic computer knowledge and its applications,
  cyber crime. Mirror images, cubes and dice, Venn diagrams and non-verbal items are left out (text-only engine; a
  mirror-image clock is kept because it is text).
- Part B: current affairs, institutions, geography and economy, art and culture, Indian polity, general science,
  human diseases, national movement, basic computers and cyber crime, laws on women and children. Built as static facts
  only: no current affairs, awards, schemes or office-holders. The law items cover central Acts and the Constitution
  (POCSO 2012, Domestic Violence 2005, Dowry Prohibition 1961, Prohibition of Child Marriage 2006, JJ Act 2015, POSH
  2013, RTE 2009, Maternity Benefit as amended 2017, PCPNDT 1994, NCW Act 1990, CPCR Act 2005, Immoral Traffic 1956,
  Muslim Women Act 2019, 106th Amendment 2023, BNSS 2023 safeguards). Rajasthan's 50% reservation for women in
  panchayati raj (2008) is the only state rule used.
- Part C: dynasties and historic places, 1857, peasant and tribal movements, praja mandals and integration; forts and
  monuments, paintings, handicrafts, folk dances and music, fairs and festivals, folk deities, dialects; physiography,
  rivers and lakes, climate, forests and wildlife, agriculture, minerals and energy; state institutions. District
  counts and divisions (changed in 2023 and again in December 2024) and state schemes are left out.

## Modelling decisions

- Real length 150 questions in 120 minutes. Modelled at 100 questions in 80 minutes (the same 0.8 minute a question),
  40/30/30 by part, two practice tests, Test 2 held out of the drills, 32 drill-only items: 232 in all.
- Per test: reasoning 14 verbal + 14 logical, computer 12; India GK and science 20, laws on women and children 10;
  Rajasthan history 10, art and culture 10, geography, economy and polity 10. The split inside each part is
  Tigertest's.
- Questions are original. Items were checked on 2026-11-01 against every India bank in `data/v2/` (ssccgl, rrbntpc,
  ibpspo, ctet, rrbgroupd, sscgd, sscchsl, sscmts, uppolice, ibpsclerk, jnvst and the batch's sbiclerk, sbipo,
  ibpsrrb, biharpolice, delhipolice, mppolice, phcse as they stood) and about 90 items were rewritten where the same
  fact and answer or the same numbers appeared.
