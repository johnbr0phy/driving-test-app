# RD exam (CDR Registration Examination for Dietitians) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-04.

## Official (Commission on Dietetic Registration, CDR)

- Registered Dietitian Examination Test Specifications and Study Outline, January 1, 2027 to
  December 31, 2031, approved by CDR June 25, 2025, read directly:
  https://www.cdrnet.org/vault/2459/web//2027-2031%20RD%20Test%20Specifications%20and%20Expanded%20Study%20Outline.pdf
  Domain I Fundamentals of Dietetics Practice 23% (management and leadership, marketing and public
  relations, research applications, professional practice); Domain II Community and Public Health
  Nutrition 22% (guidelines and policies, needs assessment, programs and education); Domain III
  Human Nutrition and Medical Nutrition Therapy 40% (fundamentals of nutrition and physiology,
  screening and assessment, diagnosis, planning and intervention including enteral and parenteral
  nutrition, monitoring and evaluation); Domain IV Food Science and Food Systems 15% (food science,
  menu development, flow of food from procurement to service). The expanded outline was used for
  every category.
- Registered Dietitian Examination Test Specifications, January 1, 2022 to December 31, 2026
  (approved September 14, 2020), read directly:
  https://admin.cdrnet.org/vault/2459/web/4.5%20RD%20Test%20Specifications%202022-2026.pdf
  I Principles of Dietetics 21%, II Nutrition Care for Individuals and Groups 45%, III Management
  of Food and Nutrition Programs and Services 21%, IV Foodservice Systems 13%. Still in force for
  exams through December 31, 2026; the landing says so.
- RD and DTR Exam Frequently Asked Questions 2025, read directly:
  https://www.cdrnet.org/vault/2459/web//Exam%20FAQ%202025.pdf
  Computer adaptive, variable length: minimum 125 questions (100 scored + 25 pretest), maximum 145
  (120 scored + 25 pretest); 3 hours of testing time in a 3.5-hour appointment; four answer options
  per question; on-screen calculator (a simple handheld on request); no skipping, reviewing or
  changing answers; Pearson VUE test centers year-round; scaled score 1 to 50, minimum passing
  score 25; score report at the test center with sub-scores (Domains I and II, Domains III and IV
  on the 2022 outline). Fees and retake waits are on that page and are not stated by Tigertest.
- CDR RD Exam Overview (https://www.cdrnet.org/rdexamoverview), read 2026-11-04: year-round at
  Pearson VUE centers; test specifications updated every five years; in May 2026 CDR set a lifetime
  limit of eight attempts for candidates who establish eligibility on or after January 1, 2027 (not
  used in copy).
- CDR graduate degree requirement (https://www.cdrnet.org/graduatedegree): a graduate degree is
  required for RD exam eligibility for anyone first eligible on or after January 1, 2024 (read
  2026-11-04).

## Clinical and policy references used for the items

- Dietary Reference Intakes (National Academies): protein RDA 0.8 g/kg; fiber AI 14 g per 1,000
  kcal; AMDRs (carbohydrate 45 to 65%, protein 10 to 35%, fat 20 to 35%); sodium CDRR 2,300 mg
  (2019); iron RDA 27 mg in pregnancy, 18 mg women 19 to 50, UL 45 mg; EAR, RDA, AI and UL
  definitions.
- Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025-2030 (released January 7, 2026; dietaryguidelines.gov):
  the bank cites only the sodium limit (under 2,300 mg) shared with the DRI; added sugar and
  protein targets changed in this edition and are not asked.
- IOM 2009 pregnancy weight gain guidelines; AAP vitamin D 400 IU for breastfed infants.
- Academy/ASPEN malnutrition characteristics (2012) and GLIM criteria (2019); ASPEN position paper on
  albumin and prealbumin (2021).
- KDOQI Clinical Practice Guideline for Nutrition in CKD, 2020 update (0.55 to 0.6 g/kg for
  stable CKD 3 to 5 without diabetes, not on dialysis).
- ASPEN/SCCM critical care guidelines (2016, 2022): PN after 7 days for low-risk patients; stop
  PN above about 60% of needs met enterally; peripheral PN about 900 mOsm/L.
- NPIAP/EPUAP/PPPIA pressure injury guideline (2019): 1.25 to 1.5 g/kg protein.
- IDDSI framework (levels 0 to 7).
- ADA Standards of Care: 15-15 rule; metformin and vitamin B12.
- FDA Food Code 2022: two-stage cooling (135 °F to 70 °F in 2 hours, to 41 °F within 4 more).
- Federal programs: USDA FNS (WIC, SNAP, CACFP, CSFP, NSLP, SFMNP; SNAP-Ed is not asked: its funding ended September 30, 2025 under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act), Older Americans Act Title
  III; NHANES, BRFSS (CDC); Healthy People 2030 (HHS ODPHP); Hunger Vital Sign.

## Bank decisions

- Modelled on the 2027 to 2031 outline (in force from January 1, 2027, eight weeks after this
  build); the 2022 to 2026 weights are given on the landing and in the facts.
- Per 50-question test: Domain I 12 (management 6, research 3, practice and marketing 3), II 11
  (community 6, education 5), III 20 (nutrition 6, assessment and diagnosis 5, MNT 5, nutrition
  support 4), IV 7 (food science 3, foodservice 4). CDR publishes no split inside each domain.
- The 70% line is a readiness target; the real standard is a scaled 25 of 50 on an adaptive exam.
- Duplicate scan: stems compared (token overlap) against every bank in data/ and data/v2; items
  close to nbdhe (stages of change), nasm-cpt (protein RDA), uknmc (NG placement), phr (halo
  effect) and aswb-clinical (zero-based budget) were replaced.
