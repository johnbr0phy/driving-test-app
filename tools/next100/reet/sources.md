## Syllabus content
- Level 1 syllabus as summarised by Careers360 ("REET Syllabus 2026",
  https://competition.careers360.com/articles/reet-syllabus-2026) and other guides: CDP (development, heredity and
  environment, individual differences, personality, intelligence, learning theories, motivation, diverse
  learners, assessment, action research, RTE Act 2009); language comprehension of unseen passages, grammar and
  language pedagogy; mathematics content and pedagogy; EVS (family, clothes and shelter, professions, culture and
  civilisation, transport and communication, hygiene, living beings, water, earth and space, EVS pedagogy).
  Several guides list Rajasthan's fairs, festivals, dress, food, arts and crafts and tourist places under the
  Level 1 EVS heading "Our culture and civilisation"; one guide places them under Level 2. Tigertest includes
  them in EVS, as static facts only.
- Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 (6 to 14 years; Section 13 bans screening and
  capitation fees at admission).
- Standard child development and educational psychology texts for Erikson, Piaget, Pavlov, Skinner, Kohler,
  Thurstone, Gardner, Guilford, Kohlberg, Jung, Freud, Judd, the halo and Pygmalion effects, Rousseau and Tagore.
- Rajasthan facts (state flower rohida; Tal Chhapar sanctuary, Churu, blackbuck; Khejarli, 1730; Teej in
  Shravan; Kalbelia dance, snake charmers, UNESCO 2010; Ana Sagar, Ajmer; Bhavai dance; leheriya and bandhej;
  johad; Chand Baori at Abhaneri, Dausa; Bikaner camel festival; the Thar west of the Aravallis; Pakistan border).
  Reviewer checks, 2026-11-01: rohida (Wikipedia, Tecomella; declared state flower 1983), Tal Chhapar (Vajiram,
  Drishti IAS: Sujangarh tehsil, Churu, about 1,000 blackbuck), Kalbelia (indianculture.gov.in: inscribed 2010,
  5.COM; snake-charming community), Khejarli (Wikipedia, Sahapedia: September 1730, 363 Bishnois, khejri felled
  for lime burning or timber for the palace), Bhavai (Wikipedia: pots balanced on the head, dancer on a brass plate
  or glass), leheriya (Wikipedia: Rajasthani tie-dye with wave patterns), johad (crescent earthen dam that recharges
  groundwater), Chand Baori (Wikipedia, History Hit: Abhaneri, Dausa, about 3,500 steps). Ana Sagar (built by
  Arnoraja in the 12th century) and the Bikaner camel festival in January are standard references, not re-fetched.

## Originality and overlap
All 201 questions and all six passages (three Hindi, three English) are original. No REET past paper or guide
item was used. Stems, facts and numbers were compared on 2026-11-01 with every India bank in data/v2 (including
the batch-4 uptet bank as it then stood, ctet, uppolice and biharpolice for Hindi, rajpolice for Rajasthan GK,
jnvst and aissee for school maths), the US school banks and the Philippines LET bank; items sharing a fact and
answer or the same numbers were rewritten.
