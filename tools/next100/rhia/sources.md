# Sources for `rhia` (AHIMA Registered Health Information Administrator exam)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the AHIMA content outline and from
federal law and agency guidance. No AHIMA prep material (AHIMA sells 820 practice questions and two
practice exams), past exam item or commercial question bank was read or copied, and no question
needs a code book. Checked 2026-11-19.

## Exam outline (the blueprint)
- AHIMA, RHIA Exam Content Outline, effective 10/01/2023 (c) 2023:
  https://www.ahima.org/media/f5adcqxb/rhia_contentoutline_begin10-01-2023_final.pdf
  (PDF read 2026-11-19). Domain 1 Data and Information Governance 17-20% (8 tasks: record
  integrity, quality reporting data elements, data dictionary, documentation and data standards,
  data analysis for management, policies, retention and destruction, MPI integrity). Domain 2
  Compliance with Access, Use, and Disclosure of Health Information 15-18% (7 tasks: patient access
  and portals, advocacy, processing requests, monitoring access including HIE, request workflows,
  breach protocols, privacy and security initiatives such as cyber security and disaster
  recovery). Domain 3 Data Analytics and Informatics 23-26% (9 tasks: EHR end-user support,
  reports and visual displays, database management and data mining, focused audit tools, workflow
  optimization, HIE, software integrations, systems development life cycle, healthcare statistics).
  Domain 4 Revenue Cycle Management 20-23% (7 tasks: reimbursement models, coding accuracy, HHS
  documentation requirements, CDI, claims management including CDM, DNFB and A/R, coding and
  grouping, revenue integrity: coding audits, denials, fraud prevention). Domain 5 Management and
  Leadership 23-26% (9 tasks: goals, strategy and change management, contracting and outsourcing,
  HR, work design and process improvement, training, budgets, accreditation, compliance
  monitoring, project management).

## Exam format
- AHIMA, "Registered Health Information Administrator (RHIA)":
  https://www.ahima.org/certification/rhia/ (fetched 2026-11-19): 150 questions, 130 scored and 20
  pretest placed at random; 3 hours 30 minutes; computer based at Pearson VUE; candidates can move
  between items and flag them; passing score 300 (a scaled score, not a percentage); eligibility is
  graduation from a CAHIIM-accredited HIM baccalaureate, master's or post-baccalaureate certificate
  program, or a foreign program under a reciprocity agreement; schedule within 120 days of
  eligibility; 30-day wait and a new application before a retake. No newer outline is announced.
  Fees are kept off the site.
- AHIMA Candidate Guide (revised 2-24-25):
  https://www.ahima.org/media/1d0bdghm/revised-candidate-guide-2-24-25.pdf (read 2026-11-19):
  RHIA role description (managing patient health information, people, operational units and
  budgets) and eligibility.

## Blueprint used here (per 50-question test)
D1 9 (governance 5, record integrity and MPI 4), D2 8 (access and release 4, privacy, security and
breach 4), D3 12 (statistics and reports 5, informatics 7), D4 10 (reimbursement models 5, revenue
integrity 5), D5 11 (leadership and HR 6, operations, budgets and quality 5): 18/16/24/20/22%. The
outline's minimums add to 98%, so a 50-item form cannot meet every range; Domain 5 sits one point
under its 23% floor. The splits inside each domain are Tigertest's. Pools are four times each
count. Multiple choice only, like the real exam.

Claim adjustment group codes (CO, PR, OA, PI) are the X12 835 remittance standard named in HIPAA's transaction rules; only their basic meaning is asked.

Management and leadership items (Lewin's change stages, SWOT, SMART goals, job analysis and job
evaluation, Kirkpatrick levels, Herzberg's factors, conflict modes, span of control, budgets,
critical path, Gantt charts, scope creep) are kept to the standard definitions taught under the
outline's Domain 5 tasks; no item depends on one textbook's wording.

Healthcare statistics (average daily census, length of stay, occupancy, rates per 100 or 1,000,
case mix index arithmetic, A/R days, budget variance, FTEs) are standard formulas; every number in
those items is recomputed in the build script.

## Left out on purpose
Fees, penalty dollar amounts, indexed thresholds, state record retention periods (asked only as
"state law sets it"), Joint Commission standard numbers, and the pending HIPAA Privacy Rule
changes (the 2021 proposal to shorten access to 15 days is not final; items use the rule in
force). Written against `data/cca-questions.json`, `data/ccs-questions.json` and
`data/cehrs-questions.json`: no fact-and-answer pair is repeated (for example the 30-day access
and 60-day amendment clocks, Safe Harbor 18, case-mix-index definition, DNFB definition, RAC and
UPIC roles, qui tam and the OIG seven elements as named items stay in those banks).
