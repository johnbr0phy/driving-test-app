# Sources for `rhit` (AHIMA Registered Health Information Technician exam)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the AHIMA content outline and the
public federal rules it points to. No AHIMA practice exam, review book, prep test or commercial
question bank was used or reworded. No item reproduces a CPT descriptor or needs a code book.

## Exam outline and format
- AHIMA, "Registered Health Information Technician (RHIT)": https://www.ahima.org/certification/rhit/
  (fetched 2026-11-19): 150 questions, 130 scored and 20 pretest placed at random; 3 hours 30
  minutes; computer based at Pearson VUE test centers; candidates may move between items once an
  answer is selected and may flag items for review; passing score 300 (scaled). Eligibility:
  associate-level academic requirements of a CAHIIM-accredited HIM program, or an HIM program
  approved by a foreign association with an AHIMA reciprocity agreement. Fees kept off the site.
- AHIMA, RHIT Content Outline (Effective Date: 10/01/2022, (c) 2023):
  https://www.ahima.org/media/x3opwug4/rhit_contentoutline_09_2023_final-1.pdf (PDF read
  2026-11-19). Domain 1 Data Content, Structure, and Information Governance 19-25% (12 tasks);
  Domain 2 Access, Disclosure, Privacy, and Security 14-18% (6 tasks); Domain 3 Data Analytics and
  Use 12-18% (6 tasks); Domain 4 Revenue Cycle Management 19-25% (15 tasks); Domain 5 Compliance
  13-17% (4 tasks); Domain 6 Leadership 9-12% (7 tasks).
- AHIMA, RHIT Job Task Analysis crosswalk (2022): https://www.ahima.org/media/ljnh1yeo/rhit_crosswalk_2022_final.pdf
  (listed on the RHIT page, checked 2026-11-19; not needed for any fact).

## Blueprint used here (per 50-question test)
Domain midpoints (22/16/15/22/15/10.5) scaled to 50: Domain 1 11 (record content and standards 6,
MPI, identity and EHR 5), Domain 2 8, Domain 3 7, Domain 4 11 (coding, queries and CDI 6, revenue
cycle and payment 5), Domain 5 8, Domain 6 5. The splits inside Domains 1 and 4 are Tigertest's.
Pools are four times each count. Multiple choice only; the pass line of 70% is a readiness marker,
since AHIMA reports a scaled score (300 to pass) and publishes no percentage.

## Standard definitions
Hospital statistics (occupancy, inpatient service days, average length of stay, gross and net
death rates, infection and cesarean rates), descriptive statistics, chart types, quality tools
(PDSA, root cause analysis, Pareto, fishbone) and project management terms (charter, WBS, Gantt,
critical path, three-point PERT estimate) are standard textbook definitions; every computed key is
recomputed and asserted in `build.py`.

## Deliberately left out
Fees, penalty amounts, dollar thresholds, Joint Commission standard numbers, state retention and
minor-consent rules (asked only as "set by state law"), the HHS proposals to shorten the access
deadline (not final), and CPT descriptors.

## Kept apart from other banks
Written against `data/cca-questions.json`, `data/ccs-questions.json` and `data/cpc-questions.json`:
no item repeats a fact and answer from them (for example overlays, terminal digit filing, the DNFB
definition, CMI as an average of weights, NCCI, the 30-day access and 60-day amendment deadlines,
quantitative and qualitative analysis, late entries, outliers and unbundling are left to those
banks).
