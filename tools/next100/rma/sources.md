# RMA (AMT) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official outline below. None is
copied, adapted or reworded from AMT practice exams, AMT sample items, released forms or any
commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (American Medical Technologists)

- AMT, Registered Medical Assistant (RMA) Certification Examination Competencies and Examination
  Specifications (©2026, 12 pages), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/Portals/0/PDF/Get%20Cert/Prepare%20for%20Exam/RMA-Content.pdf
  Summary table: I. General Medical Assisting 65 questions (31.0%), II. Administrative Medical
  Assisting 30 (14.0%), III. Clinical Medical Assisting 115 (55%), total 210. Task areas: I.A Body
  Systems (structure and function, disorders, wellness and nutrition), I.B Medical Terminology,
  I.C Medical Law and Ethics, I.D Human Relations; II.A Insurance, II.B Clerical (scheduling, oral
  and written communication, health information, supplies, computer applications, office safety);
  III.A Infection Control, III.B Diagnostic Instruments, III.C Laboratory Procedures, III.D Minor
  Surgical Procedures, III.E Vital Signs and Measurements, III.F Physical Examinations,
  III.G Clinical Pharmacology, III.H Therapeutic Modalities, III.I First Aid and Emergency
  Response. Competencies flagged "#" are not directly assessed; no question was written on a
  flagged competency (for example suture and staple removal, ECG mounting, alternative therapies,
  culture plates, Holter monitor identification).
- AMT, Registered Medical Assistant exam page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/medical-assistant/rma-exam
  Links the handbook, content outline and reference list; exams are scheduled through Pearson VUE;
  school-based candidates get their date from their instructor.
- AMT, References for Registered Medical Assistant Certification Examination (RMA-REF-2026-01),
  fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/Portals/0/PDF/Get%20Cert/Prepare%20for%20Exam/RMA-REF.pdf
  Suggested medical assisting, terminology, A&P, law and ethics, insurance and pharmacology texts.
  Clinical facts in the bank are the standard textbook facts these references teach.

## Format (AMT Candidate Handbook, most recent revision April 2026)

- AMT Candidate Handbook, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/Portals/0/PDF/ResourceCenter/ApplicationProcess/AMTCandidateHandbook.pdf
  Medical Assistant exam time 2.0 hours. AMT exams are 100 to 230 four-option, single-best-answer
  multiple-choice items and may contain unscored pretest items. Scores are scaled from 0 to 100;
  the minimum passing score is 70, which is not a percentage. Results are given on screen at the
  end. A failing candidate retakes the whole exam and may test up to four times in all for the
  certification; a retest fee applies (fees are never stated by Tigertest).

## Law and standards used in questions

- OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
  Hepatitis B vaccine offered within 10 working days of assignment; training at assignment and
  at least annually; exposure control plan reviewed at least annually; training records kept 3
  years; engineering controls (self-sheathing needles, sharps containers) versus work practice
  controls; labels fluorescent orange or orange-red.
- CMS, Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/quality/clinical-laboratory-improvement-amendments
  CLIA requirements are based on the complexity of the test method.
- Federal facts the outline names and that are stable: HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules (minimum
  necessary, treatment, payment and operations), the Controlled Substances Act schedules and
  refill limits (Schedule II no refills; III and IV up to 5 refills within 6 months), DEA
  registration, the ACC/AHA blood pressure categories (normal under 120/80), CDC hand washing for
  at least 20 seconds, ADA fasting plasma glucose (no intake for at least 8 hours; normal under
  100 mg/dL).

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test, scaled from the 65/30/115 of 210: General 15 (body systems 6,
  terminology 4, law and ethics 3, human relations 2), Administrative 7 (insurance 3, clerical 4),
  Clinical 28 (infection control 4, instruments and minor surgery 3, laboratory 5, vital signs 4,
  physical exams 5, pharmacology 5, therapeutic modalities and first aid 2). AMT publishes counts
  only per work area; the split inside each area is Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target; AMT's 70 is a scaled score, not a percent.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees, no state-specific scope rules (scope of
  practice is "state law"), no provider or course named.
- Duplicates: written against data/cma-questions.json, data/ccma-questions.json,
  data/phlebotomy-questions.json and data/cet-questions.json and scanned against them for repeated
  fact-and-answer pairs; matches were rewritten.
