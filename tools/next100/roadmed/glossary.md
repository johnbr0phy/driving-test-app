# Glossary for `roadmed` (Romanian, written natively)

Register: questions and explanations impersonal, like Romanian grile books ("Care este...",
"Ce rol are..."); landing and dashboard copy in "tu" (consumer-app norm). Romanian typography:
ș and ț with comma below (never ş, ţ with cedilla in new text), decimal comma (0,8 s, 5,25 L/min,
9,3 kcal), units with a space (70 ml, -70 mV), subscripts in formulas (C₄H₁₀), quotes „ ”.
No em-dashes. Stems end in "?" or ":".
