# Sources for `rocpi` (Romania ARR Driver CPC initial qualification, CPI marfă / persoane)

All 200 questions are original, written from the EU and Romanian law below. ARR's own question
base (published on testare.arr.ro), the commercial "chestionare atestate" sites, apps and printed
CPI books were not copied or used as item sources. No fees, fines, tariffs or toll amounts.
Checked 2026-11-06 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam and its format
- ARR (Autoritatea Rutieră Română), communiqué "Comunicat privind susținerea unor examene în limba
  engleză / Information about exams held in English" (24 December 2024), read 2026-11-06:
  https://www.arr.ro/noutati_doc_1229_comunicat-privind-sustinerea-unor-examene-in-limba-engleza-information-about-exams-held-in-english_pg_0.htm
  - From 20.02.2025 ARR publishes the English question base as online questionnaires on
    testare.arr.ro, for transparency and so candidates can learn the interface.
  - Exams in English: CPI goods/passengers, short CPI goods/passengers ("CPI scurt"), taxi
    driver/rental driver, taxi/rental visa. Candidates sit the exam in the language of their
    training course, Romanian or English.
  - CPI and short CPI theory exams in English only in Bucharest; 2025 dates 11.04, 13.06, 08.08,
    10.10 and 12.12.2025. Practical exams in English only in Bucharest, within 7 working days of the
    theory exam. No 2026 calendar could be found (search 2026-11-06); the landing says "check the
    current dates with ARR or your training centre".
- startupcafe.ro on the same communiqué (context: drivers from Nepal, Sri Lanka and elsewhere):
  https://startupcafe.ro/examen-arr-limba-engleza-atestat-transport-soferi-sri-lanka-nepal-taxi-uber-romania-htm-29975
- Norms on the training and professional attestation of certain categories of drivers, Annex 2 to
  Order of the Minister of Transport no. 1.214/2015 (Monitorul Oficial no. 903 of 4 December 2015),
  consolidated text as reproduced on arr-atestate.ro (a training-site copy of the legal text; the
  official legislatie.just.ro page returned 502 on 2026-11-06), read 2026-11-06:
  https://www.arr-atestate.ro/norme-din-11-noiembrie-2015-privind-pregatirea-si-atestarea-profesionala-cpi-si-cpc-a-conducatorilor-auto-de-transport-marfa-si-persoane-anexa-nr-2.html
  - Art. 4(5): the CPI exam is a theory exam and a practical exam.
  - Art. 4(6)(a): a 2-hour paper of 100 questions with 4 options, one correct.
  - Art. 4(6)(b): a 2-hour paper of 6 case studies, two for each of the 3 chapters.
  - Art. 4(7): pass with at least 70% of the questions correct and at least 70% of the total
    case-study marks.
  - Art. 4(8): practical exam (driving part of at least 60 minutes and a second part of at least
    30 minutes); candidates must pass the theory exam and every part of the practical.
  - Art. 4(12): a driver who already holds a CPI for the other type of transport (goods or
    passengers) sits a 20-question questionnaire limited to the subjects of Annex 2a, plus the
    whole practical exam (the "CPI scurt" route).
  - Art. 3(3): the CPC (periodic) certificate is valid for 5 years.
  - Not confirmed from an official page this session: the time and pass mark of the short
    questionnaire, the 2026 English exam dates, fees (left out by house style).
- asistar.ro "Condiții de prezentare la examen" (training centre PDF quoting the Norms, read
  2026-11-06): documents for the CPI exam (ID or residence document, driving licence); art. 1(6):
  CPI may be obtained in Romania by EU nationals normally resident in Romania, or by non-EU
  nationals employed by a company established in Romania or with the right to work in Romania.
  https://www.asistar.ro/static/docs/CONDI%C5%A2II-DE-PREZENTARE-LA-EXAMEN-v1.pdf
- Directive (EU) 2022/2561 (codified Driver CPC directive), Annex I: the list of subjects (1
  advanced training in rational driving based on safety regulations; 2 application of regulations;
  3 health, road and environmental safety, service, logistics). The three chapters of the case
  studies match these three sections. Category weights in Tigertest's tests are Tigertest's own:
  ARR publishes no split.

## Law used in the bank
- Regulation (EC) 561/2006 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2020/1054 (Mobility Package): breaks
  (45 min, or 15 + 30 in that order), daily driving 9 h (10 h twice a week), 56 h a week, 90 h a
  fortnight, daily rest 11 h (reduced 9 h; split 3 + 9), within 24 h, weekly rest 45 h (reduced
  24 h, compensation en bloc attached to a rest of at least 9 h by the end of the third week),
  start no later than the end of six 24-hour periods, a weekly rest spanning two weeks counted in
  either week, two consecutive reduced weekly rests for international goods drivers outside the
  country of establishment with four weekly rests in four weeks of which two regular, reduced
  weekly rest in a stationary vehicle with suitable sleeping facilities, regular weekly rest not
  in the vehicle (accommodation paid by the employer), 45-minute break in a vehicle driven by
  another driver if not assisting, exceeding by up to 2 hours after a 30-minute break to reach
  base or home for a weekly rest, pay linked to distance banned where it endangers safety (art.
  10), undertaking's duty to organise work (art. 10), vehicles with a maximum authorised speed of
  40 km/h or less exempt (art. 3), vans over 2.5 t in international transport or cabotage in
  scope from 1 July 2026, AETR applying for the whole journey to or through an AETR country (art.
  2(3)). Fixed week Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00.
- AETR agreement (UNECE): contracting parties include Turkey, Moldova, Ukraine and Serbia.
- Directive 2002/15/EC (working time of mobile workers): 48 h average over four months (may be
  extended to six), 60 h maximum, breaks 30 min for 6 to 9 h of work and 45 min over 9 h, in
  periods of at least 15 min; periods of availability are not working time; loading, unloading,
  cleaning, maintenance and admin are working time.
- Regulation (EU) 165/2014 as amended by 2020/1054: smart tachograph (GNSS position at start and
  end of the day and every 3 hours of accumulated driving; version 2 records border crossings
  automatically), remote early detection, company, workshop and control cards, periodic
  inspection at least every 2 years, card renewal applied for at least 15 working days before
  expiry, repair en route if the vehicle cannot return to base within a week of the fault, manual
  records identifying the driver, slot 1 for the driver and slot 2 for the co-driver, tachographs
  in UTC, manipulation devices banned. Vehicle unit download at least every 90 days and driver card
  every 28 days (Commission Regulation (EU) 581/2010). Overspeed event over 60 seconds above the
  set speed (Annex IC). ARR issues tachograph cards in Romania.
- Romania's time zone: EET, UTC+2 in winter, UTC+3 in summer.
- Directive 92/6/EEC as amended: speed limiters on goods vehicles over 3.5 t (90 km/h) and buses
  (100 km/h) (used only in an explanation).
- Directive 96/53/EC as amended: height 4.0 m; 40 t for five- and six-axle articulated vehicles;
  44 t for a three-axle motor vehicle with a two- or three-axle semi-trailer carrying a 40-foot ISO
  container in combined transport; buses 13.5 m (two axles), 15 m (more axles), articulated 18.75 m.
- EN 12195-1 and the European Commission's best practice guidelines on cargo securing: 0.8 g
  forwards, 0.5 g backwards and sideways; LC and STF on lashing labels; top-over lashing works by
  friction; steeper lashing angles more effective; anti-slip mats; blocking and filling voids.
- ATP agreement (perishable foodstuffs), ADR instructions in writing (in the cab, in a language
  the crew can read), TIR convention (blue TIR plate), CMR convention (art. 4 to 9: three original
  copies, the second accompanies the goods; reservations) and its e-CMR protocol, Interbus.
- Regulation (EC) 1072/2009 as amended by 2020/1055: Community licence and certified copy in the
  vehicle, driver attestation for third-country drivers, cabotage 3 operations in 7 days, 4-day
  cooling-off; Community licence issued for renewable periods of up to 10 years (art. 4); driver
  attestation issued by the member state of establishment (art. 5). Vehicles back at an operating
  centre within 8 weeks of leaving it: Regulation (EC) 1071/2009 art. 5 as amended by 2020/1055. Directive (EU) 2020/1057:
  posting declarations through IMI, bilateral operations exempt.
- Regulation (EC) 1073/2009: journey form for occasional coach services; regular services under
  an authorisation.
- Regulation (EU) 181/2011 on bus and coach passenger rights: services of 250 km or more,
  rerouting or refund after a departure delay of over 120 minutes, refreshments for trips planned
  over 3 hours delayed over 90 minutes, 36 hours' notice for assistance, refusal only for safety
  or vehicle design reasons.
- Directive (EU) 2022/2561: Union code 95, periodic training in each 5-year period.
- Reviewer additions (2026-11-07): Regulation 561/2006 art. 6(5) (driving a commercial vehicle out of
  scope is recorded as other work), art. 9(3) (travelling to or from a vehicle not at home or base
  counts as other work), art. 8(6) (two consecutive reduced weekly rests: the next weekly rest is
  preceded by the compensation), art. 12 as amended (up to 1 hour to reach home or base for a weekly
  rest; up to 2 hours after a 30-minute break only for a regular weekly rest); Directive 2002/15/EC
  art. 3(a) (cleaning and technical maintenance are working time); Regulation 165/2014 art. 33(2)
  (operators keep data at least one year and give drivers copies on request) and art. 34(1) (no
  dirty or damaged charts or cards; no withdrawal before the end of the daily working period unless
  authorised), Annex IC (detailed speed for the last 24 hours the vehicle moved); CMR art. 1(4)
  (postal, funeral and furniture removal excluded) and art. 17 (liability from taking over to
  delivery); ADR 8.1.5 (crew equipment: warning vest, portable lighting, protective gloves, eye
  protection); Regulation (EU) 2020/740 (tyre label fuel efficiency classes A to E); Incoterms 2020.
- TollRO (checked 2026-11-07): Romania is replacing the rovinietă for goods vehicles over 3.5 t with
  a distance-based charge, TollRO, collected by CNAIR, scheduled for 1 October 2026 after several
  delays; buses and light vehicles stay on the rovinietă:
  https://trans.info/ro/tollro-romania-trece-la-taxarea-camioanelor-pe-kilometru-501778
  The rovinietă item (ROCPI-DOC-016) therefore asks about a coach, not a truck.
- Romania: OG 27/2011 on road transport (route licence, "licență de traseu", for regular
  passenger services); ISCTR (Inspectoratul de Stat pentru Controlul în Transportul Rutier)
  carries out road transport checks; rovinietă, the electronic road user charge for national
  roads (no amounts; trucks over 3.5 t moving to TollRO, see above); special transport authorisation for over-dimension or overweight loads;
  RO e-Transport (OUG 41/2022 as extended by OUG 115/2023): international road transport of goods
  is declared and receives a UIT code (secondary sources checked 2026-11-06, e.g.
  https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/tot-ce-trebuie-sa-stii-despre-sistemul-ro-e-transport-21813/ ).
- First aid: ERC adult basic life support (30:2); recovery position not used (it is in other
  banks).

## Romanian speed limits for C and D vehicles: left out
Secondary sources disagree on the expressway and E-road figures for categories C and D after the
2022 amendment (90, 100 or 110 km/h); see search 2026-11-06 (capital.ro, dailydriven.ro, bzi.ro).
No item asks them.

red/yellow air lines, wheel nut indicators) are asked here as worked scenarios with other numbers,
or left to the other banks. Ten near-repeats found by a similarity scan were rewritten. Review (2026-11-07): 50 more items
rewritten that repeated the fact and answer of `malta-driver-cpc`, `hungary-gki`, `finland-truck-theory`,
`uk-lgv-theory` or `ireland-truck-bus-theory` items (several identical stems), mostly as worked
scenarios with other numbers or as other facts from the same regulations.
