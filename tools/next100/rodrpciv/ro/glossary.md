# rodrpciv Romanian (ro-RO) glossary

Terms as OUG 195/2002 (Codul rutier), its implementing regulation (HG 1391/2006, "Regulament"),
the sign and marking annexes and the DGPCI (fost DRPCIV) learner questionnaires use them.

Register: questions and explanations in the formal second person ("dumneavoastră": aveți,
sunteți, trebuie să opriți), like the official questionnaires ("chestionare"), or impersonal
("conducătorul auto este obligat să..."). App copy and the landing use "tu", the house UI register
(as the existing Romanian pages, `roadmed.ro.json`).

Typography: Romanian quotes „...”, diacritics with comma below (ș, ț, not ş, ţ), decimal comma
(0,80 g/l; 1,5 m; 7,5 t), thousands with a point (1.500 kg, 3.500 kg), km/h, metri, ora 07:00.
No em or en dashes.
