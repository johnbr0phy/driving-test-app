# Sources for `rodrpciv` (Romania driving licence theory exam, category B)

All 200 questions are original, written from the Romanian traffic law below. The official DGPCI
question bank, school "chestionare" sites and practice apps were not copied (some sites were opened
only to read the text of the law they host). Signs are described in words (no images). Checked
2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Who runs the exam and its format
- Order MAI 268/2010 on the driving licence examination procedure, consolidated text to Order MAI
  110/2014 (UNTRR copy, PDF, read 2026-11-01):
  https://untrr.ro/media/wysiwyg/legislatie/ordinul-nr-268-din-8-decembrie-2010.pdf
  - Art. 5: the theory test is in Romanian; Romanian citizens of national minorities may ask for
    their mother tongue (the application form offers Hungarian and German); foreigners and citizens
    of EU, EEA states and Switzerland may ask for English, French or German.
  - Art. 10(1): the question database and tests are run nationally by the D.R.P.C.I.V. through a
    computer application; a free online self-test application is offered on its website.
  - Art. 10(2)(b): categories B, B1, C, C1, Tr, D, D1, Tb, Tv: 26 questions, 30 minutes, at least
    22 correct to pass. (a) AM, A1, A2, A: 20 questions, 20 minutes, 17 correct. (c) BE etc: 11
    questions, 15 minutes, 9 correct.
  - Art. 10(4)-(5): topics (traffic rules, signs, priority and speed; the driver; the road; other
    road users; rules and documents; accidents; mechanical elements of safety; safety equipment;
    environmental use of the vehicle).
  - Art. 11(1): three warm-up questions before the test, not scored.
  - Art. 11(2): each question has 3 answer options; one, two or three may be correct; candidates
    may skip questions and come back.
  - Annex 1 (application form): "Solicit examinarea teoretică în limba engleză / franceză /
    germană"; "în limba maternă maghiară / germană".
- DGPCI, the Ministry of Internal Affairs directorate now named Direcția Generală Permise de
  Conducere și Înmatriculări (formerly DRPCIV): https://dgpci.mai.gov.ro/ (home page loads with its
  title only; no exam page could be read).
- avocatnet.ro, "Permis auto: cum se poate obține în 2026 categoria B" (dated 10 Sept 2026, guide):
  26 questions, 30 minutes, at least 22 correct still in force in 2026; a failed candidate may
  re-sit after at least 15 days; minimum age 18; holders of B1 since 16 or 17 with a year's
  experience are now exempt from the B theory test (recent order, number not given; Order MAI
  69/2026 per search snippets, not opened). Fee figures in the article are not used.
  https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_72635/Permis-auto-Cum-se-poate-ob%C8%9Bine-in-2026-categoria-B.html
- avocatnet.ro (2011, guide): "Proba teoretica pentru permisul auto": three warm-up questions,
  re-sit after 15 days. https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_26805/Proba-teoretica-pentru-permisul-auto-tot-ce-trebuie-sa-stii.html
- avocatnet.ro act page for Order 268/2010 (read 2026-11-01): a 2023 article says the test "can be
  taken in five languages" (guide; the Order names Romanian, Hungarian, German, English and French)
  and that official practice questionnaires are in Romanian only. https://www.avocatnet.ro/act-normativ-ORDIN-nr-268-2010_690.html
- Guides say the computer stops the test after the fifth wrong answer. Not in the Order text read;
  stated on the landing only as "five wrong answers mean a fail", which follows from 22 of 26.

## Traffic law used in the bank
- OUG 195/2002 (Codul rutier), consolidated text hosted by codrutier.ro, chapters III to VI (read
  2026-11-01; this copy predates the 2022 expressway change, see below):
  https://www.codrutier.ro/codul-rutier
  - Art. 20(4): minimum age 18 for B, 16 for AM, A1, B1. Art. 24(4): foreigners and EU citizens
    resident or studying in Romania may take the theory test in an international language. Art.
    24(5): B licence valid 10 years administratively. Art. 24.1(2): any licence valid for AM; B
    valid for B1. Art. 25: beginner rules and sign for under one year.
  - Art. 36: seat belts; children under 3; phones only hands-free. Art. 37: red lights and siren,
    stop at the edge; blue lights and siren, slow, keep right, give way.
  - Art. 44(2): dipped headlights by day on motorways, expressways and European (E) roads.
  - Art. 45(5)-(6): overtake on the left; trams and left-turning vehicles on the right; trams on
    the left on one-way roads or with no room on the right.
  - Art. 49(1)-(2): 50 km/h in built-up areas, up to 80 km/h on some sections for A and B.
  - Art. 50(1)(a), (2), (4): outside built-up areas for A and B: motorways 130, E roads 100, other
    roads 90; trailer 10 km/h less; drivers with under one year, 20 km/h less.
  - Art. 57, 59: priority to the right at unmarked junctions; vehicles inside a signed roundabout
    have priority; rail vehicles have priority at unmarked junctions and lose it when turning left
    or where signs say otherwise; left-turners give way.
  - Art. 63(1): a stop of up to 5 minutes is "oprire", longer is "staționare". Art. 72(2), (7):
    pedestrian priority on marked crossings or green; 1 m of pavement for pedestrians.
  - Art. 74: motorway bans (learners, pedestrians, vehicles under 50 km/h), hard shoulder.
  - Art. 76: compulsory RCA insurance. Art. 77-79: accidents with injuries (police, 112, do not move
    vehicles); damage only: clear the road, report within 24 hours unless an agreed report.
  - Art. 78: no alcohol after an accident until tested.
- 2022 amendment to OUG 195/2002 art. 50: expressway ("drum expres") limit for cars raised from
  100 to 120 km/h (economica.net, 12 May 2022, law promulgated):
  https://www.economica.net/lege-promulgata-limita-maxima-de-viteza-in-afara-localitatilor-pe-drumurile-expres-120-km-h_583083.html
  Confirmed as in force by dailydriven.ro and others via search 2026-11-01.
- Regulation implementing OUG 195/2002 (HG 1391/2006), consolidated to 21 Dec 2020 (SNPPC copy,
  PDF, read 2026-11-01): https://snppc.ro/uploads/blog/upload/Regulament-OUG-195-2002.pdf
  - Art. 51-53, 56: traffic lights (green arrow, red with amber, amber after green, flashing amber,
    reversible lanes red X / green arrow); art. 54 white signals for trams.
  - Art. 77-81: markings (continuous and broken lines, mixed lines, continuous line between the
    two "Copii" signs, yellow kerb line and zig-zag for no parking, stop line, give-way line with a
    triangle, pedestrian and cycle crossings).
  - Art. 88-89: police officer signals; stop for school patrols and a blind person's raised cane.
  - Art. 97: children up to 135 cm in approved child seats; over 135 cm adult belt or seat;
    rear-facing seat in front only with the airbag off; no animals held by driver or front
    passenger; seat belt exceptions.
  - Art. 101, 106, 107: keep to the right lane; lane ending; choose lane at least 50 m before a
    junction; stop behind a tram at a stop without an island.
  - Art. 113: horn at least 25 m away, not in "no horn" zones. Art. 114: lights (200 m / 100 m
    dipping, alternate beams at unmarked junctions at night, fog lights, tunnels, hazard lights).
  - Art. 116: signal 50 m before in towns, 100 m outside.
  - Art. 118-120: overtaking duties, 1.5 m recommended for cyclists, overtaking bans (visibility
    under 50 m, bridges, tunnels, pedestrian crossings, level crossings and 50 m before, stopped
    tram without island, queues).
  - Art. 123: 30 km/h in towns / 50 km/h outside at unmarked junctions, on ice or packed snow,
    with visibility under 100 m, near "Copii" (07:00-22:00) and "Accident" signs.
  - Art. 126-128, 131-135: U-turn bans, narrow roads, reversing up to 50 m, buses leaving bays,
    priority cases (entering national roads, roundabouts, leaving property, cyclists, pedestrians).
  - Art. 137-139: level crossings (slow white light, rising barriers, St Andrew's cross stop,
    stranded vehicle, height gates).
  - Art. 141-144: parking (handbrake, gear, one-way streets, no stopping within 25 m of crossings,
    junction corners and stops, 50 m of level crossings, bridges, roads under 6 m).
  - Art. 147-149: documents to carry; beginner sign (yellow disc, black exclamation mark, windscreen
    low right and rear window low left); window films; worn tyres.
  - Art. 54.1 of OUG 195/2002: aggressive driving.
  - Art. 155-157: breakdowns, triangles at least 30 m, towing (bar up to 4 m, rope 3-5 m, no towing
    on ice or snow). Art. 158: residential zone 20 km/h.
- 25 m pedestrian-crossing stopping rule unchanged in 2025 (newsweek.ro, 20-21 April 2025).
- Alcohol: zero tolerance for drivers is stated from the Codul rutier (any alcohol is an offence if
  not a crime); "above 0.80 g/l in the blood is a crime" is the Criminal Code threshold (art. 336),
  stated in the explanation only. No fines, fees or penalty points are used.
- Sign names (Romanian): codrutier.ro sign lists, read 2026-11-01:
  https://www.codrutier.ro/semne-de-circulatie

## Not used or left out
- Fees (the issue fee changes on 15 Sept 2026), fines and penalty points.
- Exact regulation article numbers in explanations (laws are named instead).
- Hazard perception: Romania's theory test has no video part, so nothing is left out there; the
  real test's multiple-correct-answer format is not modelled (Tigertest items have one key).

## Review pass (2026-11-01)
- Codul rutier art. 32 (via e-juridic.manager.ro consolidated "Codul Rutier 2026", search 2026-11-01):
  red special lights are for police vehicles (stop on approach); blue for police, gendarmerie,
  ambulance, fire and others (slow, keep right, give way). Art. 37 per codrutier.ro chapter V.
  Item PRI-013 was an ambulance with red lights; now a police car, and PRI-014 an ambulance with blue.
- Codul rutier art. 30 order of precedence: police officer, priority vehicles' special signals,
  traffic lights, signs, markings, rules (used in MRK-021).
- Art. 36(3): phones banned for drivers "while moving" except hands-free; "stopped at red" removed as
  a distractor (defensible).
- Art. 49(4) consolidated text (e-juridic "Codul Rutier 2026 actualizat"): 130 motorway, 120
  expressway, 100 E roads, 90 other. codrutier.ro still shows the pre-2022 100 for expressways.
- Law 240/2025 (in force 27 Dec 2025) and Order MAI 69/2026 add theory-test exemptions (e.g. B1
  holders since 16/17 with a year's experience); search snippets give no change to 26 questions,
  30 minutes, 22 correct. Accompanied driving at 17 is a proposal, not law, as of Nov 2026.
- HG 1391/2006 (SNPPC PDF) re-read for: art. 69 (crossing without barriers signed with the St
  Andrew's cross plus STOP), 77 and 80(3) (yellow kerb line and zig-zag = no parking), 79(4)
  ("Copii" continuous line), 97 (135 cm), 107 (50 m lane choice; stop behind a tram at a station
  without refuge until doors close), 113, 114, 116, 118(2) (1.5 m recommended), 123, 127 (narrow
  roads: light reverses for heavy; going-up reverses), 128 (reverse max 50 m), 149 (beginner disc),
  157 (towing bar 4 m, rope 3-5 m, no towing on ice or snow).
- Romanian warning sign names checked against codrutier.ro/semne-de-circulatie/de-avertizare
  ('Curbă dublă' there is the first-left sign, so WRN-003 no longer names it).
