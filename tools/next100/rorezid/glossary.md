# Glossary for `rorezid` (Romanian terms used in the bank and copy)

Terms follow the Romanian editions of the bibliography (Kumar și Clark, Lawrence, Sinopsis de
medicină, Hipocrate 2021) and the Ministry of Health's competition texts.
