## Format and scoring (2025 instructions of the Comisia Centrală de Rezidențiat, kept for 2026)

Edupedu, instructions for candidates, 2025:
https://www.edupedu.ro/rezidentiat-2025-candidatii-vor-afla-rezultatele-pe-loc-dupa-finalizarea-concursului-de-pe-16-noiembrie-instructiuni-pentru-candidati-de-la-comisia-centrala-de-rezidentiat/
- 200 questions per domain, one national test per domain, identical in every centre; 4 hours,
  counted from the hand-out of the last booklet; four booklet and answer-sheet types A to D.
- 25% complement simplu: one correct answer of five, 4 points; marking more or fewer than one
  answer voids the item.
- 75% complement multiplu: 2 to 4 correct answers of five, 5 points; marking fewer than 2 or more
  than 4 voids it; no item has 5 correct answers.
- Maximum 950 points (50 x 4 + 150 x 5).
- Sheets read electronically in the room; candidates learn their result on the spot.
- Pass: 60% of the national maximum achieved in the domain (rule since 2023, when it went from 65%
  to 60%): https://www.euronews.ro/articole/rezidentiat-2023-scade-pragul-minim-de-promovare-de-la-65-la-60-din-punctajul-max
- 2025 results (Ministry figures via Edupedu): 10,147 registered; Medicină 6,956 registered,
  5,178 passed, highest score 944; Medicină dentară 1,950 / 1,467; Farmacie 1,241 / 1,067.
  https://www.edupedu.ro/rezultatele-de-la-rezidentiat-2025-au-fost-publicate-de-ministerul-sanatatii-repartitia-candidatilor-se-desfasoara-in-perioada-23-25-noiembrie/
- Language: the test and instructions are in Romanian (no other language offered).

## Book weights (guide)

The 2025 Medicină paper had 92 Kumar items (22 CS, 70 CM), 84 Lawrence (22 / 62) and 24 Sinopsis
(6 / 18): grile-rezidentiat.ro analysis, https://blog.grile-rezidentiat.ro/grilele-de-la-rezi-2025-medicina-analiza-completa/
(a prep company's count, not official). The Ministry publishes no split. Tigertest's blueprint is
100 / 80 / 20 (50 / 40 / 10%).

## Clinical content

Items are original, written in Romanian on the chapters above, with current guideline values where
a figure is asked: Sepsis-3 (lactate > 2 mmol/l in septic shock); ESC 2021/2023 heart failure
(HFrEF ≤ 40%, four pillars incl. SGLT2 inhibitors); ESC 2024 hypertension and atrial fibrillation
(CHA₂DS₂-VASc, 140/90 office threshold); ERC 2021 resuscitation (30:2, adrenaline 1 mg every 3-5
min); GINA 2024 (no SABA-only treatment); GOLD 2025 (FEV1/FVC < 0.70, LTOT at PaO₂ ≤ 55 mmHg); ESO
stroke thrombolysis 4.5 h; KDIGO 2024 CKD (3 months, G5 < 15); ADA 2025 (HbA1c ≥ 6.5%); WHO and
EACS HIV guidance (treat all; PCP prophylaxis < 200 CD4); ATLS 10th edition; Revised Atlanta
pancreatitis (lipase ≥ 3x, pseudocyst after 4 weeks); WHO TB (6-month regimen). Past papers
(published by Edupedu and the universities) and commercial grile books were not used.

## Not modelled

Five-option items, complement multiplu items (2 to 4 correct), the 4/5-point scoring and the
national ranking. Every item here has one correct answer of four, equal weight. Tests are 50
questions, a quarter of the real paper at its pace. Medicină dentară and Farmacie (separate
bibliographies) are not covered.
