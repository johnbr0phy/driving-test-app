# Sources for `rotaxi` (Romania ARR taxi and rental / ride-hailing driver attestation test)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the Romanian law below. The question
base the ARR publishes for the test (testare.arr.ro, Romanian and, since 20 February 2025,
English) was not opened or copied, nor any school "chestionare" site or practice app. Checked
2026-11-06 unless noted.

## The test and the attestation (official sources first)
- Annex 7^1 to Order of the Minister of Transport (OMT) 1214/2015, "Norme privind pregătirea și
  atestarea profesională a conducătorilor auto care efectuează transport de persoane în regim de
  taxi sau transport în regim de închiriere", as amended (published as Annex 4 to the amending
  order; UNTRR copy, .docx, read 2026-11-06):
  https://untrr.ro/media/wysiwyg/word/ANEXA_nr._7_ind_1.docx
  - Art. 3: the certificate of professional attestation for taxi or rental passenger transport is
    issued by the Romanian Road Authority (ARR) through its territorial agencies, after a
    theoretical test-grilă exam.
  - Art. 4(4): the training course is 30 hours, at an authorised driving school, on an
    ARR-approved programme that follows the annex's tematica.
  - Art. 6(1): the exam is a written multiple-choice test (probă scrisă tip grilă) in Romanian or
    English, with 20 questions ("Examinarea în limba [engleză] va fi disponibilă până cel târziu
    la data de 1 iulie 2025").
  - Art. 6(2): 70% of the questions answered correctly to pass (14 of 20).
  - Art. 6(3)-(5): result ADMIS / RESPINS, appeals within 3 days; a new test within at most 15
    days after paying the fee again; after a second fail, a new course first.
  - Art. 6(7): a pass certificate (adeverință) valid until the attestation is issued, at most 30 days.
  - Art. 7: certificate issued for an indefinite period, valid if endorsed (vizat) every 5 years
    with proof of medical and psychological fitness and a suitable licence; endorsement by passing
    the test-grilă in the last year of validity; registration through a driving school or the ARR
    online application; valid throughout Romania.
  - Tematica: (1) professional, social and fiscal law 5 h (Law 38/2003: authorisation,
    attestation, taxi vehicles, the fare device, tariffs, obligations, sanctions, authorities;
    radio rules; employment contract; the taxi driver's occupational standard; taxes); (2)
    customer service 3 h; (3) taxi practice 10 h (meter and radio, documents, local topography,
    tourist information, case studies); (4) driving and road safety 7 h (daily check, defensive
    driving in night, fog, rain, winter, skid control, even braking, vehicle fire, accidents and
    first aid, main traffic rules); (5) vehicle and passenger security 5 h (difficult and
    non-paying clients, sexual harassment, theft, armed robbery prevention and response,
    psychological after-effects); (6) a foreign language, optional, 4 h. The rental (închiriere)
    tematica has the same structure.
- ARR, "Comunicat privind susținerea unor examene în limba engleză / Information about exams held
  in English" (ARR site, December 2024; search snippet and the startupcafe.ro report below):
  https://www.arr.ro/noutati_doc_1229_comunicat-privind-sustinerea-unor-examene-in-limba-engleza-information-about-exams-held-in-english_pg_0.htm
- startupcafe.ro, ARR exams in English (read 2026-11-06, news report): the English question base
  for taxi driver, rental driver, taxi viză and rental viză attestations is published on
  testare.arr.ro from 20 February 2025; English theory exams for taxi and rental drivers and their
  endorsements start with the 1-2 March 2025 session; candidates sit the theory exam in the
  language of their course, Romanian or English.
  https://startupcafe.ro/examen-arr-limba-engleza-atestat-transport-soferi-sri-lanka-nepal-taxi-uber-romania-htm-29975
- avocatnet.ro, applications for taxi attestations only online (guide):
  https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_68450/Atestatele-de-taximetrie-nu-mai-pot-fi-ob%C8%9Binute-decat-online.html
- avocatnet.ro (2008, guide; agrees with the law on 20 questions and 70%):
  https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_11405/Atestarea-profesionala-pentru-transportul-in-regim-de-taxi-si-inchiriere.html
- Not confirmed on an official page: the test's time limit (none published that we could read).
  Fees are not used.

## Law used in the bank
- Law 38/2003 on taxi and rental transport, consolidated text "actualizată 2024" (euroavocatura.ro
  print copy, read 2026-11-06; legislatie.just.ro returned 502):
  http://www.euroavocatura.ro/print2.php?print2=lege&idItem=1865
  - Art. 1^1: definitions (fare device = taximeter + fiscal cash register; client receipt as the
    only payment document; taxi light visible from 100 m; waiting places set by the local authority
    with police approval; taxi = car up to 5 seats with the driver, or goods vehicle up to 3.5 t;
    effective working time without pauses over 15 minutes; rental transport per hour or day, paid
    in advance; switching speed; starting tariff).
  - Art. 2-4, 7: local authorities organise taxi services; life and environment first; only
    authorised carriers; individual carrier one vehicle; family association vehicles up to the
    attested members; no paid transport without an authorisation or certified copy.
  - Art. 7^1: taxi and rental transport manager (one carrier only).
  - Art. 1^1 d): authorising authority = town hall transport service (taxi), ARR (rental).
  - Art. 10-12: authorisation per vehicle, unique and non-transferable; two badges; documents on
    board (badges, registration and ITP, RAR approval copy, passenger and goods insurance, RCA,
    driver's attestation, meter verification bulletin, dispatch contract); a missing document means
    the authorisation is held until proof, withdrawn after 30 days. Art. 12^1: RAR approval
    certificate. Art. 13: local regulations, maximum tariffs, schedules.
  - Art. 17-24^1: taxi only at the client's request; permanently in the authorising locality,
    occasionally elsewhere at the client's express request with return; no fixed routes beyond the
    locality; stands first come first served; park only where allowed without stands; stop to board
    or set down except in dangerous places; bus lanes in localities without disturbing buses;
    equal access to a Free taxi; special access for reduced mobility, children under 2 and the sick;
    one client per ride; start and end of the ride; dispatcher pickup; airports (no touting; no
    tariff above the door tariff).
  - Art. 25: rental transport (contract, advance payment, documents, no taximeter, radio or taxi
    signs, badges lower left of front and rear windscreens, return to the locality, driver
    attested and employed by the carrier).
  - Art. 26-28: driver conditions (B licence 2 years, attestation, medical and psychological
    fitness, age 21, criminal record), course documents (police certificate for the last year),
    attestation issued by the ARR agency where the driver works, 70% pass, indefinite certificate
    with five-yearly endorsement, valid in all Romania.
  - Art. 30-37: taxi vehicles (4 passengers besides the driver, doors beside each seat, goods up to
    3.5 t, yellow light with TAXI, red left Busy, green right Free, lit through the shift; double
    black and white 3 x 3 cm check band; yellow body unless the council decides; badges on rear
    doors; door tariffs readable from 5 m; tariff list and photo badge inside; Free, Busy, Payment,
    Private positions; optional GPS, recorder, partition, card device; controls with the police).
  - Art. 38-50: fare device (forbidden functions, automatic locking, day 06-22 and night 22-06
    tariffs, 10 km/h switching speed, receipt contents, no rides with a faulty device, seals,
    metrological checks by BRML, electronic key, S and D modes, night tariff up to 50% above day,
    price not by number of people, hourly/distance ratio 10, starting tariff = distance tariff,
    payment up to the displayed amount if the fiscal module stops).
  - Art. 52-53: obligations of carriers, drivers, clients and dispatchers; emergency calls.
- OUG 49/2019 on alternative transport with a car and driver (ride-hailing), as approved by Law
  204/2019 (in force 1 February 2020): hotnews.ro summary of the rules (read 2026-11-06):
  https://www.hotnews.ro/stiri-esential-23635814-astazi-intra-vigoare-noile-reglementari-privind-ridesharingul-care-sunt-conditiile-pentru-soferii-care-fac-uber-bolt-clever-sau-yango.htm
  - Rides only through an approved digital platform; alternative transport authorisation and
    certified copy from the ARR territorial agency; badge at front and rear with the plate and the
    platform name; ITP every six months; RCA plus passenger and luggage insurance; no public
    transport traffic privileges; driver at least 21, ARR attestation for rental transport, B
    licence 2 years, no criminal record, medical and psychological fitness, no suspension for
    alcohol or drugs in the last year; not refusing a ride under the agreed terms; no audio or
    video without consent; only in the car registered on the platform.
  - The seat limit was raised by a 2020 law (economica.net); not asked. A 2026 law amending art. 13
    (certified copies for owned or rented cars, startupcafe.ro PDF) concerns operators; not asked.
    https://startupcafe.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/lege.pdf
- Law 448/2006 on the protection of persons with disabilities: access to transport with an
  assistance dog (stated as practice; article number not cited).
- Not used: fares, fees, fines, vehicle age limits for authorisations, and anything a local council
  sets (tariff levels, colours, schedules), which "vary by municipality".
