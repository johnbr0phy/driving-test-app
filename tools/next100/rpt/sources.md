# RPT (AMT) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the official outline and the primary
sources below. None is copied, adapted or reworded from AMT practice exams, AMT sample items,
released forms, or any commercial review book or prep site.

## Outline (American Medical Technologists)

- AMT, Registered Phlebotomy Technician (RPT) Certification Competencies and Examination
  Specifications (©2025, 4 pages), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/Portals/0/PDF/Get%20Cert/Prepare%20for%20Exam/RPT-Content.pdf
  Summary table, 200 questions: I. Obtaining Blood Samples 68 (34%), II. Specimen Collection and
  Processing 30 (15%), III. Time Management and Assignment Organization 20 (10%), IV. Professional
  Communications 11 (5.5%), V. Clerical Skills and Duties 11 (5.5%), VI. Safety Standards and
  Procedures 30 (15%), VII. Legal, Ethical, and Professional Considerations 20 (10%),
  VIII. Terminology, Anatomy, and Physiology 10 (5%). Task areas I.A to I.N (patient
  identification, containers, physiological and age-related aspects, patient preparation, site
  selection, venipuncture, tube-draw sequence, capillary and heel punctures, post care, specimen
  integrity, labeling including transfusion services, devices, venous versus arterial blood),
  II.A to II.I (non-blood specimens, time and temperature, blood cultures, department delivery,
  shipping, urine and other non-blood instructions, POCT, quality assurance), III.A to III.G,
  IV.A to IV.C, V.A to V.C, VI.A to VI.G, VII.A to VII.D, VIII.A to VIII.C.
- AMT, Registered Phlebotomy Technician exam page, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/phlebotomy-technician/rpt-exam
  Links the Candidate Handbook, content outline, references and practice exam; school candidates
  get their date from their instructor, others schedule at Pearson VUE.
- AMT, References for RPT Certification Examination (RPT-REF-2024-1), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/Portals/0/PDF/Get%20Cert/Prepare%20for%20Exam/RPT-REF.pdf
  Phlebotomy textbooks, the WHO guidelines on drawing blood (2010), and the CLSI standards GP41
  (venous collection), GP42 (capillary), GP44 (handling and processing), GP48 and GP33.

## Format (AMT Candidate Handbook, revised April 2026)

- AMT Candidate Handbook, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://americanmedtech.org/Portals/0/PDF/ResourceCenter/ApplicationProcess/AMTCandidateHandbook.pdf
  Phlebotomy Technician exam time 2.5 hours. AMT exams are 100 to 230 four-option,
  single-best-answer items and may contain unscored pretest items. Scores are scaled from 0 to
  100; the minimum passing score is a scaled 70, not a percentage. A failing candidate retakes
  the whole exam and may test up to four times in all; a retest fee applies (fees are never
  stated by Tigertest).

## Practice standards and law used in questions

- WHO guidelines on drawing blood: best practices in phlebotomy (2010), on AMT's reference
  list, publication page fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241599221
  (PDF downloaded the same day from the WHO IRIS link on that page.) Used for: patient
  identification and preparation (state full name, ask about fainting, paper under the arm, verbal
  consent), site selection (median cubital, basilic risk, avoid branching veins and existing IV
  access), 70% alcohol and why povidone-iodine is avoided, needle gauge and length table,
  hemolysis causes, not pressing the syringe plunger, arm extended after withdrawal, tube table
  (additives, PST, ACD, sodium heparin for lithium, full draw for fluoride), tourniquets as an
  MRSA source, arterial sampling (bright red rapid flow, Allen test, air bubbles, ice), sharps
  container within arm's reach, PEP not effective beyond 72 hours, bleach 1:10 and 1:100 and fresh
  daily, paediatric identification (band on the patient, not the cot), venipuncture preferred in
  term neonates, no chlorhexidine under 2 months, warming up to sevenfold, capillary site table
  (heel to about 6 months, middle and ring fingers, across fingerprint lines), finger depth 1.5 mm
  for 6 months to 8 years, posterior heel and osteomyelitis, capillary order (hematology,
  chemistry, blood bank), no second puncture with one lancet.
- CLSI, Order of Blood Draw: Tubes and Additives (blog, 19 March 2019), fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://clsi.org/about/blog/order-of-blood-draw-tubes-and-additives/
  GP41 order: blood culture, sodium citrate, serum, heparin, EDTA, fluoride/oxalate; the same
  order for syringe or tube holder; clot activator in plastic serum tubes may interfere with
  coagulation testing.
- OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens standard, 29 CFR 1910.1030, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1030
  Training at assignment and at least annually; medical records kept for employment plus 30
  years; no food or drink stored where blood is present.
- OSHA Hazard Communication standard, 29 CFR 1910.1200, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200
  and https://www.osha.gov/hazcom (fetched 2026-11-15): 16-section SDS, section 4 first aid,
  section 7 handling and storage, section 8 exposure controls; signal words danger (more severe)
  and warning; label elements.
- CDC, Transmission-Based Precautions, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/basics/transmission-based-precautions.html
  Contact PPE donned on entry and removed before leaving; droplet patients masked for transport;
  airborne diseases (tuberculosis, measles, chickenpox, disseminated zoster), AIIR or a private
  room with the door closed.
- CDC, Clinical Safety: Hand Hygiene for Healthcare Workers, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/index.html
  Sanitizer of at least 60% alcohol; soap and water when hands are visibly soiled.
- FDA, CLIA Categorizations, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/ivd-regulatory-assistance/clia-categorizations
  FDA (CDRH) categorizes tests as waived, moderate or high complexity; uncategorized tests
  default to high complexity.
- CMS, Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/quality/clinical-laboratory-improvement-amendments
  Requirements rise with test complexity.
- U.S. Department of Justice, Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.justice.gov/crt/fcs/TitleVI
  Race, color or national origin in federally assisted programs.
- EEOC, Reasonable Accommodation and Undue Hardship under the ADA, fetched 2026-11-15:
  https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/enforcement-guidance-reasonable-accommodation-and-undue-hardship-under-ada
- Standard textbook facts the AMT reference list teaches (tort definitions such as battery,
  assault, false imprisonment, slander; phases of testing; tube additives; laboratory
  departments; urine and swab collection; physiological effects of exercise, altitude,
  dehydration and smoking; basic terminology and anatomy). CLSI GP41, GP42 and GP44 themselves
  are paid documents and were not read; facts that rest only on them (heel puncture depth,
  inversion counts, serum separation times) were left out.

## Bank decisions

- Per 50-question test, scaled from AMT's 68/30/20/11/11/30/20/10 of 200: Obtaining Blood Samples
  17 (patient identification, preparation and site selection 6; venipuncture, tubes and
  equipment 7; capillary punctures and labeling 4), Specimen Collection and Processing 8, Time
  Management 5, Professional Communications 3, Clerical 3, Safety 7, Legal and Ethical 5,
  Terminology and A&P 2. The split inside work area I is Tigertest's.
- Tigertest's 70% line is a readiness target; AMT's 70 is a scaled score, not a percent.
- No question asks about the exam itself; no fees, no state rules (scope of practice, minors'
  consent and statutes of limitations "vary by state"); no provider or course named.
- Duplicates: written against data/phlebotomy-questions.json (NHA CPT), data/rma-questions.json,
  data/cma-questions.json and data/ccma-questions.json and scanned for repeated fact-and-answer
  pairs; matches were rewritten.
