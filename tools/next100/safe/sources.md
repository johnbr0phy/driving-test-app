# SAFe Agilist sources

All 208 questions in `data/v2/safe/questions.json` are original, written for Tigertest from the
exam study guide's domain and topic list and the facts stated in the public parts of the Scaled
Agile Framework articles below. No Scaled Agile practice test item, course workbook item, sample
question, past exam item, prep provider question, "dump" or leaked material was used or reworded.
The framework articles are copyrighted: nothing is quoted from them, they were read only to know
the facts, and every stem, option and explanation is in Tigertest's own words.

## Exam outline and format

- Scaled Agile support: Exam Study Guide, AI-Empowered SA - SAFe Agilist (Exam for Leading SAFe)
  https://support.scaledagile.com/en/articles/13835858-exam-study-guide-ai-empowered-sa-safe-agilist-exam-for-leading-safe
  fetched 2026-11-23. Page dated October 20, 2026. Gives: 45 questions, 90 minutes, passing score
  80%, multiple choice single select, web-based, closed book, no outside assistance, the exam
  submits when time runs out and unanswered questions count as wrong, taken from the Learning
  Plan after the course, Candidate Agreement first. First two attempts included within 60 days
  of course completion; retakes $50 unproctored, $495 proctored; waits: third attempt at once,
  fourth after 10 days, fifth and later after 30 days each. Domains (lesson, weight, topics):
  1 Adapt and Thrive with SAFe 7-9% (thriving in the age of disruption, build a Lean-Agile
  organization); 2 Build a Foundation with Mindset, Value, and Principles 21-23% (Lean-Agile
  mindset and SAFe core values, the SAFe Lean-Agile principles, empower agility with AI);
  3 Establish Team and Technical Agility 10-12% (cross-functional Agile teams, organize around
  the flow of value with ARTs, built-in quality, the continuous delivery pipeline with DevOps);
  4 Product Development Flow 25-28% (customer-centricity and design thinking, prioritize the ART
  backlog, PI Planning, execute the PI); 5 Explore Lean Portfolio Management 21-23% (define a
  SAFe portfolio, connect the portfolio to enterprise strategy, portfolio vision and flow, Lean
  budgets and guardrails); 6 Lead the Change 7-9% (lead by example, lead the change, empower
  leaders with AI). The blueprint and the eight drill domains follow this list.
- Scaled Agile support: Exam Details, Leading SAFe (SAFe Agilist 6.0)
  https://support.scaledagile.com/s/article/Exam-Details-Leading-SAFe-SAFe-Agilist-6-0
  fetched 2026-11-23. The SAFe 6.0 exam: also 45 questions, 90 minutes, 80%, single select,
  closed book; older domains (Digital Age and Business Agility 12-14%, Lean-Agile Leaders 29-33%,
  Team and Technical Agility 6-8%, Agile Product Delivery 29-33%, Lean Portfolio Management
  12-14%, Leading the Change 6-8%). Not marked retired on the page; the newer guide above is the
  one the current Leading SAFe course leads to, so the blueprint uses the newer weights.
- Scaled Agile, "New Release: AI-Native SAFe is the Operating Model for the AI-Native Organization"
  https://framework.scaledagile.com/blog/ai-native-safe-is-the-operating-model-for-the-ai-native-organization
  fetched 2026-11-23. Posted June 23, 2026: AI-Native SAFe announced; Core SAFe is not replaced
  and stays supported. The bank teaches Core SAFe as the Leading SAFe exam tests it.
- Scaled Agile, AI-Native SAFe hub (formerly AI-Empowered Agility)
  https://framework.scaledagile.com/ai-empowered-agility
  fetched 2026-11-23 (last update 23 March 2026): AI-Native SAFe framed as the operating model
  for the age of AI on Lean and Agile; shifts to outcome-driven flow, teams augmented with AI,
  faster learning and experimentation; a human-centric AI culture.

## What could not be read, and how the copy handles it

- Most framework articles stop after their introduction behind a login, so no fact was taken
  from the closed parts. Where SAFe teaches a detail that sits only behind the login (the Portfolio
  Kanban state names, the five built-in quality domains, the PI Planning agenda by hour, the
  fist-of-five confidence vote, the Lean business case template), the bank leaves it out or asks
  only what the public text states.
- The Leading SAFe course workbook is course material and was not used.
- The ART backlog and LACE articles returned 404 at the URLs tried; their facts come from the
  ART Sync, Leadership and Culture, VMO and feature articles that mention them.

## Left out on purpose

- Anything about the exam itself (questions, pass mark, fees, retakes): landing and examFacts only.
- Prices, renewal fees and course costs; named people other than the authors the framework cites
  (Kotter, Little's Law); the AI-Native SAFe early-access guidance, which changes every two weeks.
