cadre ("SBI Clerk"), Phase I Preliminary Examination. Conducted by SBI's Central Recruitment &

## Question-type mix
SBI publishes no topic weights. Typical Clerk Prelims papers (coaching analyses) carry: English reading
comprehension, cloze, fillers, error detection, sentence improvement and rearrangement; Numerical
simplification and approximation (the biggest block), number series, one or two DI sets and arithmetic;
Reasoning puzzles and seating (about 15), inequality, syllogism, coding, blood relations, directions,
order and ranking, alphabet and letter series.

Tigertest per test: English 10 reading comprehension, 10 cloze and fillers, 10 error spotting, sentence
improvement and phrase rearrangement; Numerical 15 simplification, approximation and series, 10 data
interpretation (two tables), 10 arithmetic; Reasoning 8 inequalities and syllogisms, 15 puzzles (three sets
of five), 12 coding, relations, directions, ranking and alphabet. Plus 3 drill-only items per domain.

## Originality
All passages, puzzles, tables and questions are original; nothing from SBI papers or coaching banks.
`build.py` warns when a stem without a stimulus matches, or uses the same set of three or more numbers as,
an item in any other India bank (ibpsclerk, ibpspo, ssccgl, rrbntpc, ctet, rrbgroupd, sscgd, sscchsl,
sscmts, uppolice, jnvst and the batch-2 banks present: sbipo, ibpsrrb, delhipolice, biharpolice, rajpolice,
mppolice). Every match found while writing was rewritten; none remain. A reviewer pass (1 November 2026) also ran a
fuzzy check against every India bank and the final sbipo bank and rewrote items sharing the same numbers, the
same grammar rule and sentence, or the same logic and answer. Puzzles were brute-forced for a
unique solution.
