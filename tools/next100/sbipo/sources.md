# SBI PO Prelims: sources

Exam: State Bank of India recruitment of Probationary Officers, Phase I (Online Preliminary Examination).
Conducted by SBI's Central Recruitment & Promotion Department (sbi.bank.in/web/careers).

## Official source (checked 1 November 2026)
- SBI advertisement No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09, "Recruitment of Probationary Officers", online registration
  18.06.2026 to 08.07.2026, 1,500 vacancies (1,446 regular + 54 backlog). Read as the full notification PDF
  from a mirror (https://ibsbankcareer.in/notifications/pdfs/6a351de591b7a.pdf, text extracted with pdftotext);
  the advertisement number and wording match SBI's careers page listing (https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings).
  Section 8, Selection Procedure:
  - Phase I: objective test for 100 marks, online, 3 sections with separate timings:
    English Language 40 questions, 20 minutes; Quantitative Aptitude 30 questions, 20 minutes;
    Reasoning Ability 30 questions, 20 minutes; total 100 questions, 1 hour.
    (The marks column prints only "Total Maximum Marks 100"; guides give one mark per question.)
  - "There will be no sectional cut-off in preliminary examination." Category-wise merit list on aggregate
    marks; about 10 times the vacancies in each category are shortlisted for the Main Examination.
  - Penalty (Prelims and Mains): 1/4 of the marks assigned to a question deducted for each wrong answer;
    none for a question left blank.
  - Phase II Mains: objective 170 questions, 200 marks, 3 hours (Reasoning & Computer Aptitude 40/60/50 min,
    Data Analysis & Interpretation 30/60/45 min, General Awareness/Economy/Banking 60/60/45 min, English 40/20/40 min)
    plus a 30-mark, 30-minute descriptive paper; sectional cut-offs in the Mains. Phase III psychometric test,
    group exercise and interview. Prelims marks are not added to the final merit list.
  - Tentative schedule: Prelims August 2026, Mains September 2026, Phase III October / November 2026.
- Change from earlier cycles: the 2025 advertisement (CRPD/PO/2025-26/04, 541 vacancies, prelims 4 and 5 August
  2025 per SBI's result page https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/po-pre-result-2025) used English 30, Quant 35,
  Reasoning 35. Guides confirm the 2026 change (for example https://www.practicemock.com/blog/sbi-po-prep-plan-quant-reasoning-english/,
  https://www.smartkeeda.com/sbi-po-exam-pattern, checked 1 November 2026).
- Guides report the 2026 Prelims was held on 1 and 2 August 2026 (not confirmed from an SBI page; the landing
  does not state it).

- SBI's own "Acquaint Yourself Booklet" for the 2026 Prelims (CRPD/PO/2026-27/09, dated 22 July 2026), downloaded
  from sbi.bank.in on 1 November 2026:
  https://sbi.bank.in/documents/77530/57941334/22072026_SBI%28PO%29PRE+IH+Eng+2026.pdf/5518888e-7662-47a8-e916-00b1957ca830?t=1784718598145
  confirms English Language 40, Quantitative Aptitude 30, Reasoning Ability 30, 20 minutes each, 100 marks in
  1 hour with separate timings; "Out of the five answers to a question only one will be the correct answer"; questions
  bilingual (English and Hindi) except the English Language test; 1/4 penalty per wrong answer, none for a blank;
  no sectional cut-off and about 10 times the vacancies per category shortlisted; no calculators allowed; answers
  can be marked for review. The careers page (checked the same day) also lists the Prelims and Mains results and the
  psychometric test link, so the 2026 cycle is in Phase III.

## Not confirmed / left out
- Cut-off marks: never stated; copy says only that the cut-off is set by the number shortlisted.
- Exact Prelims dates (guides say 1 and 2 August 2026): not stated on the careers page; the landing does not give them.

## Question-type mix (guides' analyses of past Prelims; Tigertest's own split per test)
- English 40: two reading passages of 6 questions, a para jumble with 4 questions, a cloze passage of 6,
  6 single or double fillers, 7 error spotting, 5 sentence improvement.
- Quant 30: 4 simplification/approximation, 4 number series, 4 quadratic equations, 8 data interpretation
  (two tables of 4), 10 arithmetic.
- Reasoning 30: 3 inequalities, 3 syllogisms, 15 puzzles (three sets of five), 9 coding, blood relations,
  directions, ranking and alphabet.
No official weights within a section are published.

## Originality
All passages, puzzles, tables and questions are original, written for Tigertest. Nothing is taken from SBI
papers, memory-based papers or coaching banks. The build warns on any stem shared with the other India v2
banks (ibpspo, ibpsclerk, ssccgl, sscchsl, sscgd, sscmts, rrbntpc, rrbgroupd, ctet, uppolice, jnvst and the
batch 2 banks present); a manual grep of numbers and sentence rules against all v2 banks led to rewriting
about 30 items that shared numbers or sentences with ibpspo, ibpsclerk, sbiclerk, rrbntpc, rrbgroupd,
uppolice, ssccgl, sscchsl, biharpolice and ibpsrrb. Every puzzle was brute-forced for a unique solution.
