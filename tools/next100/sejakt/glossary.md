# Glossary for `sejakt` (Swedish, written natively)

Register: "du" throughout (questions, explanations, landing, dashboard), as Naturvårdsverket's
pages and Jägareförbundet's course material write to learners. Swedish typography: decimal comma
(4,1 mm, 3,2 g), space before units (800 J, 45 cm, 7 °C), no em-dashes, no straight double quotes
in the bank. Animals and signs are described in words. Laws cited as "27 § jaktlagen",
"17 § jaktförordningen", "5 kap. 2 § vapenlagen", "NFS 2023:8".
