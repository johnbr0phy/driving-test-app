# Sources for `semedb` (medborgarskapsprovet i samhällskunskap)

All checked 2026-11-06. The test is new (first sitting 15 August 2026), so the format facts are
partly from press reports; those are labelled.

## Official
- https://www.uhr.se/medborgarskapsprovet/om-medborgarskapsprovet/
  UHR, Om medborgarskapsprovet (updated 22 October 2026): UHR runs the test; Migrationsverket
  decides who may take it (registration only with a letter from Migrationsverket); civics first
  (August 2026), a Swedish-language test later; next civics test planned April to May 2027, date
  and place not set; Swedish, on paper, multiple choice with four options and one right answer;
  test time and number of questions for the next sitting "not yet announced"; fee information to
  come; August 2026 test was a trial ("utprövningsprov") with generous time; UHR's five example
  questions (public sector welfare, head of state, forestry, Finland part of Sweden until 1809,
  freedom of expression) were read and NOT reused; UHR warns that other practice tests are not
  checked by UHR. Accommodations for disability exist.
- https://uhr.se/globalassets/_uhr.se/medborgarskapsprovet/utbildningsmaterial/sverige-i-fokus_.pdf
  Sverige i fokus. Utbildningsmaterial till medborgarskapsprov (UHR with Skolverket, 1st edition,
  corrected 2026-08-10, 48 pages). "Provet kommer att baseras på det här materialet." The bank
  is written from it, chapter by chapter: Landet Sverige; Sveriges demokratiska system; Så här
  styrs Sverige; Politiska val och partier; Lag och rätt; Mediernas roll; Mänskliga rättigheter;
  Arbetsmarknad och privatekonomi; Välfärdssamhället; Sveriges moderna historia; Sverige och
  omvärlden; En sekulär stat och ett mångreligiöst land; Traditioner och högtider. Corrections of
  10 August 2026 (FN founded 1945; about 1 400 medieval churches) applied.
- https://lagen.nu/2026:451
  Förordning (2026:451) om medborgarskapsprov, in force 6 June 2026 (amended by SFS 2026:1194):
  2 § lists the content (history, traditions, welfare, labour market and personal finance,
  climate and environment, democracy, constitutional laws and the legal system, rights incl.
  equality and children's rights, world religions and religious freedom, media, geography and
  geopolitics, the EU and international cooperation); 3 § UHR develops and runs the test; 4-5 §
  Migrationsverket assigns applicants; 6 § a fee from 2027 (amount not used anywhere by
  Tigertest); 7 § accommodations. No language, length or pass mark in the ordinance.
- https://www.uhr.se/medborgarskapsprovet/utbildningsmaterial/
  Material page: PDF plus one MP3 per chapter, Swedish only.
- https://www.uhr.se/medborgarskapsprovet/resultat/
  Results go to Migrationsverket automatically; no count or pass mark on the page.
- https://www.migrationsverket.se/nyheter/nyhetsarkiv/2026-05-06-nya-regler-for-svenskt-medborgarskap-fran-6-juni-2026.html
  New rules from 6 June 2026: applicants aged 16 to 66 must show knowledge of Swedish and of
  Swedish society; grades from Swedish school, komvux, folkhögskola or SFI D count; otherwise the
  citizenship test, held in Stockholm; civics part from August 2026.
- https://www.migrationsverket.se/nyheter/nyhetsarkiv/2026-09-30-medborgarskapsprovet-i-svenska-kan-flyttas-fram.html
  30 September 2026: the Swedish-language test may be postponed (UHR recommends it; the
  government decides); "Provet i samhällskunskap fortsätter att genomföras."

## Press (format of the first sitting; labelled on the landing as reported)
- https://hurbra.se/?p=15255 (15 August 2026): 60 questions, 90 minutes, paper, Swedish, four
  options with one right.
- https://hurbra.se/?p=37332 (22 October 2026): 60 questions answered, 40 scored (20 trial
  questions), 28 of 40 (70%) to pass, set by UHR on a proposal from Göteborgs universitet; 875
  sat it, about 700 passed (about 8 in 10).
- https://www.tv4.se/artikel/7f6SDFO4hJQGHdgVq0kx5e/sa-svart-blir-medborgarskapsprovet-klarar-du-testet
  (15 July 2026): 60 questions; pass mark decided after the first sitting.
- https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medborgarskapsprovet: 60 questions, 90 minutes (cites UHR and
  Migrationsverket).

## Not confirmed / left out
- The length, time and pass mark of the April to May 2027 sitting (UHR: not yet announced).
  Tigertest models 60-question tests at 70% (42 of 60) and says only 40 counted in August 2026.
- Fee amounts (never stated by Tigertest).
- Named office-holders (the King, Crown Princess, party leaders) and the list of parties in the
  Riksdag (dated by the September 2026 election) are not asked.
- The proposal to lower the age of criminal responsibility (2026) is not asked; neither is the
  age itself, since it may change.
