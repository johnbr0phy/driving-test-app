# Glossary for `semoto` (Swedish, written natively)

Register: "du" throughout (questions, explanations, landing, dashboard), as Trafikverket and
Transportstyrelsen write to learners. Swedish typography: decimal comma (0,1 kW/kg), space before
units (125 cm³, 80 km/h, 30 m), ”…” for quotes (none used in the bank), no em-dashes, no
straight double quotes. Signs and pictures are described in words.
