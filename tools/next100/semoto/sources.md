# Sources for `semoto` (Sweden motorcycle knowledge test, A1, A2 and A, in Swedish)

All 200 questions are original, written directly in Swedish from the law and the official pages
below. Nothing was copied from Trafikverket's test, a past test, a driving school's material or a
commercial theory app. Signs, pictures and situations are described in words. Checked 2026-11-06
unless noted.

## Exam format (confirmed on Trafikverket's page, 2026-11-06)
- "Provet innehåller 70 frågor med svarsalternativ - ett alternativ är rätt svar."
- "Provtiden är 50 minuter."
- "5 av frågorna är testfrågor och de räknas inte in i resultatet." Max 65 points.
- "Får du 52 poäng eller mer är provet godkänt." (52 of 65 = 80%.)
- Headphones at the computer; the questions can be heard in Swedish ("Det går att lyssna på
  svenska"). Extended time, an oral test or a test with an interpreter can be applied for. The page
  names no English or other written language version (so the landing says the test is in Swedish).
- A calculator and touch pen are provided. Result shown on screen after the test.
- The risk training (both parts) must be done and valid at the test.
- Five knowledge areas: Fordonskännedom och manövrering, Miljö, Trafiksäkerhet, Trafikregler,
  Personliga förutsättningar. No weights are published; Tigertest's 9 categories and per-test
  counts (5/8/7/6/4/4/7/6/3) are its own split of these areas.
- A passed knowledge test for A is valid one year (landing only, never in the bank).
- Hazard and photo questions on the real test are not modelled; the landing says so.

## Licence rules (Transportstyrelsen, read 2026-11-06)
- Overview: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/motorcykel/
  A1: högst 125 cm³, högst 11 kW, högst 0,1 kW/kg. A2: högst 35 kW, högst 0,2 kW/kg, not derived
  from a motorcycle with more than 70 kW. A: all two- and three-wheeled motorcycles.
- A1 page: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/valj-behorighet/motorcykel/a1-latt-motorcykel/
  Age 16; practice from 15 år och 9 månader with körkortstillstånd för grupp 1; private practice
  needs an approved handledare who rides as passenger or on their own motorcycle, only one student
  at a time when riding their own; handledarbevis carried; the green and white ÖVNINGSKÖR sign
  clearly visible from behind; the handledare is responsible and counts as the rider.
- A2 page: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/valj-behorighet/motorcykel/a2-mellanstor-motorcykel/
  Age 18; practice from 17 år och 6 månader.
- A page (above): age 24, or 20 after two years with A2; private practice from 23, or from 19 år
  och 6 månader with at least 1,5 years of A2; from 17 år och 6 månader only at a driving school;
  three-wheeled practice from 20.
- Handledare: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/handledarskap-och-ovningskorning/handledare/
  At least 24, held the category at least five of the last ten years; supervises from a
  motorcycle; one student per session on a motorcycle.
- Risk training: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/ta-korkort/riskutbildning/riskutbildning-motorcykel/
  Part 1 alcohol, drugs, fatigue and other risky behaviour; part 2 speed, safety, road and weather
  conditions, with a manoeuvring course. Valid until the licence is issued or at most five years.
  Helmet and motorcycle protective gear required. (Not asked in the bank.)

## Traffic rules (trafikförordningen 1998:1276, consolidated text read 2026-11-06)
- 3 kap. 6 §: vehicles are driven on the carriageway (körbana); cycle tracks are for cycles and
  class II mopeds.
- 3 kap. 55 §: no parking within 30 m of a level crossing, in front of a property entrance, on a
  priority road, beside a vehicle parked at the carriageway edge (not when that vehicle is a
  two-wheeled cycle, moped or motorcycle without sidecar), on a signed passing place (E18).
- 3 kap. 69-71 §§: dipped headlights; main beam when needed but not when meeting, behind another
  vehicle or on lit roads; other light in daylight instead of dipped (not in darkness, dawn, dusk
  or when the weather requires); fog light instead of dipped in fog and heavy precipitation;
  fog and dipped light together allowed on a motorcycle in daylight.
- 3 kap. 75 §: no lights needed on a two-wheeled motorcycle without sidecar stopped or parked at
  the outer edge of the road.
- 3 kap. 76 §: only white or yellow light forwards; white light rearwards only when reversing.
- 4 kap. 6 b §: a passenger only if the motorcycle is fitted for it; at most one passenger; sidecar
  as many as it is made for; the rider must be 18 (learner riders follow körkortslagen).
- 4 kap. 8 §: no unnecessary and disturbing driving near homes.
- 4 kap. 9 §: helmet for everyone riding a motorcycle; exceptions: passengers under seven with
  other suitable head protection, vehicle not moving, within parking places, petrol station or
  workshop areas, three- or four-wheeled motorcycles with bodywork when a seat belt is used; the
  rider sees that passengers under 15 wear a helmet.
- 4 kap. 15 a §: a trailer behind a two-wheeled motorcycle at most 125 cm wide and at most half the
  motorcycle's kerb weight less fuel and tools.
- 4 kap. 15 b §: no passengers in a trailer behind a motorcycle.
- 4 kap. 18 a §: winter tyre requirement (1 December to 31 March in winter conditions) lists cars,
  light lorries, light buses and their trailers, not motorcycles.
- 4 kap. 19 §: a two-wheeled motorcycle may not be towed or pushed by another vehicle; the rider may
  not tow or push another road user.
- 4 kap. 20 §: motor vehicle with a trailer coupled to a motorcycle 80 km/h (the version in force
  from 1 December 2026 keeps this row). No other speed rule for motorcycles.
- Drink-driving law (trafikbrottslagen) covers every motor vehicle; no figures are asked in this
  bank (the setrafik bank asks them).

## Course plan
- TSFS 2011:19 (kursplan, behörighet A1, A2 och A; title changed by TSFS 2012:52), read
  2026-11-06: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/TSFS/TSFS%202011_19.pdf
  Four parts: manövrering, fordon och miljö; körning i olika trafikmiljöer; resande med
  motorcykel i speciella sammanhang; personliga förutsättningar och målsättningar i livet. Used for
  topic choice (braking methods, protective gear, being seen, passengers, group pressure,
  thrill seeking, choice of motorcycle model, weather and road conditions).

## Other law
- Terrängkörningslagen (1975:1313): off-road riding with motor vehicles on bare ground is in
  general prohibited. https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/terrangkorningslag-19751313_sfs-1975-1313/
- Red warning triangle on medicine packages (läkemedel som kan påverka förmågan att köra):
  Läkemedelsverket. https://www.lakemedelsverket.se/

## Riding technique
Braking, cornering, countersteering, body position, gear, road surfaces, wind and visibility
items follow the course plan's goals and general rider training practice; no figures beyond the
computed reaction distance (90 km/h / 3,6 = 25 m/s) and the speed-squared braking rule.
