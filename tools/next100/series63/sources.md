# Sources for `series63` (NASAA Series 63 Uniform Securities Agent State Law Exam)

All 200 questions are original, written from NASAA's published Series 63 test specifications and
the public statutes, model rules and FINRA/SEC rules it lists as testable. No NASAA item (all are
NASAA copyright), commercial question bank or prep course was used. Checked 2026-11-02.

Written at the same time as `series65` (tools/next100/series65), which also tests the Uniform
Securities Act, and after `series7` and `sie`. A word-overlap scan against
`data/series-65-questions.json`, `data/series-7-questions.json` and `data/sie-questions.json` was
run and every item testing the same fact the same way was rewritten (bank exclusion from the
adviser definition, notice filing of federal covered advisers, 5-year IA records, IAR place of
business, solicitors as IARs, performance fees, fixed annuities, coordination, the 10-offeree
private placement limit, rescission offers, willful violations, churning, 10-business-day written
discretion, 25% long maintenance, call in/out of the money, call buyer's maximum loss, the
three-times insider trading penalty, front running, "risk free" retail communications, the
dealer/principal definition, quantitative suitability). Series 63 items stay at agent level; the
remaining shared rule (out-of-state broker-dealer with only institutional customers) uses a
different scenario.

## Exam format (landing, FAQ and examFacts only; never in the bank)
- NASAA, "Uniform Securities Agent State Law Examination (Series 63) Overview", dated
  September 1, 2023: https://www.nasaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/NASAA-Series-63-Exam-Study-Guide.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-02). 65 multiple-choice questions, 60 scored and 5 unscored pretest items;
  75 minutes; closed book; pass = at least 43 of the 60 scored (71.7%); assembled individually
  from a pool; administered by FINRA; tests the Uniform Securities Act of 1956 as amended by NASAA
  (not the 2002 Act). Weights: Broker-Dealers 12% (7), Agents 13% (8), Ethical Practices and
  Obligations 25% (15), Communication with Customers and Prospects 20% (12), Securities and
  Issuers 9% (5), Investment Advisers 5% (3), IARs 5% (3), Remedies and Administrative
  Provisions 11% (7). Part III lists every testable law and rule (used to pick topics).
- NASAA, "Series 63 Test Specifications", effective June 12, 2023:
  https://www.nasaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Series-63-Outline-June-2023.pdf (same weights
  and subtopics).
- NASAA Series 63 exam content outline page:
  https://www.nasaa.org/exams/study-guides/series-63-exam-content-outline/ (checked 2026-11-02):
  NASAA is updating questions affected by the May 2026 model rule amendments on investment adviser
  advertising (102(a)(4)-1, 502(b), 203(a)-2, 411(c)-1), expected early 2027. No item here tests
  adviser advertising or testimonials for that reason.
- FINRA, Series 63 page: https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/series63
  (checked 2026-11-02): a NASAA exam administered by FINRA; 60 scored + 5 unscored, 75 minutes,
  43 of 60 to pass, no corequisite. Retake waits change on January 4, 2027 (60 days, not 180,
  after a third failure). Fee listed ($147) deliberately not used in copy.

## Rules and facts used (as in force 2026-11-02)
- Uniform Securities Act of 1956 with NASAA updates and commentary:
  https://www.nasaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/1956-Uniform-Securities-Act-with-NASAA-Updates-and-Commentary.pdf
  Definitions (401: agent, broker-dealer, investment adviser, IAR, person, security, offer),
  registration effective at noon on the 30th day (202), notice of agent changes, grounds for
  denial, summary orders and the 15-day hearing, cancellation, 30-day withdrawal and one-year
  revocation window (204), consent to service of process (414), registration by notification
  (3 p.m. on the second full business day), coordination, qualification (302 to 304), stop orders
  (306), exempt securities and transactions incl. the 10-offeree limited offering (402),
  investigations, oaths and subpoenas (407), cease and desist (408), criminal penalties and the
  no-knowledge rule (409), civil liability, rescission offers (410), judicial review (411),
  interpretive opinions (412), offers in the state (414), non-waiver.
- NSMIA (1996) and Investment Advisers Act 203A, SEC Rules 203A-1 and 203A-3 (de minimis fewer
  than 6 clients; $100M/$110M SEC thresholds; IAR place of business).
- NASAA model rules: 102(a)(4)-1 (unethical practices of advisers and IARs: borrowing from
  clients, unreasonable fees, written discretion within 10 business days, disclosure of client
  identity), 202(a)-2 (IAR qualification: Series 65, or 66 + 7, or CFP/ChFC/PFS/CFA/CIC),
  203(a)-2 (records 5 years, first 2 in an office), 502(c) (advisory contract contents), 102(e)(1)-1
  (custody), Investment Adviser Information and Security Privacy Rule (2019, annual review).
- NASAA Statements of Policy on dishonest or unethical practices of broker-dealers and agents
  (1983) and in connection with investment company shares (1997); Model Act to Protect Vulnerable
  Adults from Financial Exploitation (2016: age 65+, notify APS and the Administrator, 15 business
  day delay plus up to 10, good-faith immunity); Rules for Sales of Securities at Financial
  Institutions (1998).
- SEC: Regulation Best Interest (Rule 15l-1), Section 28(e), Rules 17a-3 (36-month account record)
  and 17a-4 (6-year blotters, 3-year communications), Regulation S-P incl. the May 2024
  amendments (incident response program, notice within 30 days; compliance dates Dec 3, 2025 and
  June 3, 2026), Regulation T 50%, ITSFEA (up to three times profit gained or loss avoided).
- FINRA Rules 2040, 2111, 2121 (5% guide, proceeds transactions), 2150, 2210 (correspondence
  25 or fewer retail investors in 30 days; social media guidance in Regulatory Notices 10-06,
  11-39, 17-18), 2360, 3110 (non-supervisory branches every three years), 3210, 3240, 3280,
  4210 ($2,000 minimum equity, 25% long, 30% short), 4512 (trusted contact); Regulatory Notice
  10-22 (Reg D due diligence); FINRA By-Laws Art. V (Form U4 amendments within 30 days, U5 copy).
- SEC v. W.J. Howey Co., 328 U.S. 293 (1946); Uniform Prudent Investor Act (1994); SIPC
  (no cover for market losses, commodity futures or fixed annuities).
