# Sources for `setrafik` (Sweden, Trafikverket knowledge test, category B)

All 200 questions are original. No Trafikverket question, commercial theory app item (Teoricentralen,
Körkortonline and others) or practice-site item was copied. Signs are described in words (no
images). Checked 2026-11-01 unless noted.

## Format of the real test (official)
- Trafikverket, "Kunskapsprov B" (last updated 23 September 2026, read 2026-11-01):
  https://www.trafikverket.se/korkort/korkortsprov/personbil-och-latt-lastbil/kunskapsprov-b/
  - 70 multiple-choice questions, one correct answer each; 5 are unscored trial questions, so the
    maximum is 65 points. 52 points or more passes (52 of 65 = 80%).
  - Test time 50 minutes, starting after an instruction film. Touch screen, calculator and
    headphones (Swedish audio) at the seat.
  - Both parts of the risk education (riskutbildning) must be completed and still valid, and a
    learner's permit (körkortstillstånd) is needed to book. Valid photo ID; arrive 30 minutes
    early for a photo.
  - Five subject areas: vehicle knowledge and handling, environment, traffic safety, traffic
    rules, personal prerequisites. The page gives no number of questions per area.
  - Results on screen at the end, by subject area only (wrong questions are not shown).
  - From 19 August 2026 a passed B theory test is valid for one year (was four months), by a
    Transportstyrelsen decision. Passes before that date keep four months.
- Trafikverket, "Teoriprov och yrkesprov med stöd": extra time for B is 75 minutes instead of 50;
  oral test or interpreter can be applied for.
  https://www.trafikverket.se/korkort/ta-korkort/teoriprov-och-yrkesprov-med-stod/
- Trafikverket, "Teoriprovet på annat språk" (last updated 11 June 2026, read 2026-11-01): the B
  test is translated into 15 languages: Albanian, Arabic (MSA), BKS (Bosnian, Croatian or Serbian),
  English (British English), Finnish, French, Persian (Farsi), Russian, Somali, Sorani, Spanish,
  Thai, Tigrinya, Turkish and German. Plus Swedish (with audio), and an interpreter can be
  requested (interpreter tests for B run 100 minutes). The page says you can switch to Swedish
  during a translated test; it does not say that every language has the same questions.
  (Re-read by the reviewer 2026-11-01.)
  https://www.trafikverket.se/korkort/ta-korkort/personbil-och-latt-lastbil/teoriprovet-pa-annat-sprak/
- Questions per subject area (GUIDE, not official): Teoricentralen reports 7 vehicle, 5 personal,
  32 traffic rules, 16 traffic safety, 5 environment of the 65 scored. Used only to weight
  Tigertest's blueprint (traffic rules about half, safety about a quarter).
  https://teoricentralen.se/en/facts/how-many-questions-are-there-per-subject-area-on-the-theory-test
- Picture questions: many real items show a photo or drawing. Not modelled; the landing says so.

## Learner and licence rules (Transportstyrelsen)
- Practise driving a car from 16 with an approved learner's permit; private supervisor
  (handledare) at least 24, valid licence for the vehicle held at least five of the last ten
  years, approved supervisor permit (Transportstyrelsen handledare pages and Vi Bilägare, via
  search 2026-11-01). https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/korkort/handledarskap-och-ovningskorning/
- The introductory course (introduktionsutbildning) for private practice for B was abolished from
  1 August 2026 (Transportstyrelsen news, 2026). Not asked in the bank.
  https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/om-oss/pressrum/nyhetsarkiv/2026/introduktionsutbildningen-tas-bort--det-har-behover-du-ha-koll-pa/
- B licence minimum age 18; two-year probation (prövotid) on a new licence, with both tests to be
  retaken if it is revoked in that time (Körkortslagen 1998:488, ch. 3 and 5).
- B covers vehicles up to 3,500 kg total weight and eight passenger seats; a trailer up to 750 kg,
  or heavier if car and trailer together are at most 3,500 kg; B96 up to 4,250 kg (EU Directive
  2006/126/EC as applied in Sweden).

## Rules of the road (Trafikförordningen 1998:1276 and Transportstyrelsen guidance)
- Right-hand rule (högerregeln) at unregulated junctions; drivers entering from car parks, tracks
  and private roads give way to all traffic; huvudled (priority road) yellow diamond sign; give
  way to trams; give way to pedestrians on or about to step onto an unguarded crossing and to
  cyclists on or about to ride onto an unguarded cycle crossing (cykelöverfart); in a built-up
  area (in fact where the limit is 50 km/h or lower, 3 kap. 45 §) slow down and if needed stop so a bus can leave a stop.
- Speed: basic limit 50 km/h in built-up areas, 70 km/h elsewhere; limits in 10 km/h steps up to
  120 km/h; a car towing a trailer at most 80 km/h.
- No overtaking just before or on an unguarded pedestrian crossing.
- No stopping or parking on, or within 10 m before, a pedestrian crossing, or within 10 m of the
  nearest edge of a crossing carriageway; no parking on a huvudled; dashed yellow kerb line = no
  parking, solid yellow = no stopping, yellow zigzag = bus stop.
- Motorway (motorväg) and motortrafikled: only vehicles built for at least 40 km/h; no turning,
  reversing or stopping except in an emergency.
- Dipped headlights, or daytime running lights in daylight, whenever driving.
- Handheld mobile phone use while driving banned since 1 February 2018.
- Driver responsible for passengers under 15 using a belt or child restraint.
- Transportstyrelsen, "Så skyddar du barnen" (read via search 2026-11-01): children shorter than
  135 cm use a child restraint; a child should be at least 140 cm to sit in front of an active
  airbag (a recommendation, so the bank asks only the rear-facing seat rule).
  https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/trafikregler-och-vagmarken/trafikregler/i-fordonet/sa-skyddar-du-barnen---regler-och-tips/

## Winter
- Transportstyrelsen news, "Dubbdäcken ska vara av senast den 16 april" (2026, read 2026-11-01):
  winter tyres required on cars 1 December to 31 March when there are winter road conditions;
  studded tyres allowed 1 October to 15 April, otherwise only in winter conditions; studs release
  particles. https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/om-oss/pressrum/nyhetsarkiv/2026/dubbdacken-ska-vara-av-senast-den-16-april--om-inte-vintern-ar-kvar/
- Minimum tread depth is NOT asked: that release says 5 mm for winter tyres while the
  Transportstyrelsen tread page gives 3 mm.

## Alcohol and drugs
- Lag (1951:649) om straff för vissa trafikbrott, 4 and 4 a §§ (lagen.nu, riksdagen via search
  2026-11-01): drink driving from 0.2 per mille in blood or 0.10 mg/l in exhaled air; gross drink
  driving judged especially from 1.0 per mille or 0.50 mg/l. Same limit for all drivers. Any
  narcotic in the blood is drugged driving unless prescribed (zero tolerance). https://lagen.nu/1951:649

## Wildlife
- Polisen, "Viltolyckor" (read via search 2026-11-01): after a collision with listed animals,
  including moose, roe deer, wild boar, wolf and bear, the driver must mark the spot and tell the
  police as soon as possible, even if the animal runs off.
  https://polisen.se/lagar-och-regler/trafik-och-fordon/viltolyckor/

## Deliberately avoided
- Fines, fees, the cost of the test, demerit-like sanctions; tread depths; a passing speed for
  school buses (the 30 km/h rule was a time-limited trial, status unclear); minimum passing
  distance to cyclists; warning triangle distances; municipal idling times; environmental zone
  classes and dates; officeholders.

## Reviewer checks (2026-11-01)
- Trafikverket Kunskapsprov B re-read: 70 questions, 5 trial, max 65, pass 52, 50 minutes; passes
  from 19 August 2026 valid one year, before that four months; page updated 23 September 2026.
- Trafikförordningen (riksdagen.se, 1998:1276): 3 kap. 53 § bans stopping or parking within 10 m
  before a pedestrian crossing, in a tunnel and in a lane for buses in regular service; 3 kap. 55 §
  bans parking on a huvudled; 3 kap. 40 § bans overtaking just before or on an unguarded crossing;
  3 kap. 44 § merging lanes "under ömsesidigt hänsynstagande" (zip principle is guidance);
  3 kap. 11 § solid line may be crossed with special care to pass an obstacle; 9 kap. 1 § motorways
  for motor vehicles built for at least 40 km/h, "Mopeder klass I får dock inte framföras på en
  motorväg". https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/trafikforordning-19981276_sfs-1998-1276/
- Car with trailer: still 80 km/h (Transportstyrelsen, https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/hastighet-slap);
  a July 2026 government consultation proposes 100 km/h on motorways; not decided as of November 2026.
- Rear-facing to at least about 4 years: Transportstyrelsen "Så skyddar du barnen" and the 2017
  joint recommendation (s9109_barn_i_bil_rekommendation.pdf on transportstyrelsen.se).
