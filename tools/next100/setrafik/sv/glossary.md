# Glossary for the `setrafik` Swedish (sv-SE) translation

Written before the translation. Terms are the ones Trafikverket and Transportstyrelsen use
toward learners (trafikförordningen 1998:1276, Transportstyrelsens vägmärkesförordning pages,
körkortsboken vocabulary), and match `tools/next100/semoto/glossary.md` where the two overlap.

Register: "du" throughout (questions, explanations, landing, dashboard), as Trafikverket and
Transportstyrelsen write to learners. Typography: decimal comma (0,2 promille, 3,5 t), space as
thousands separator (3 500 kg), space before units (50 km/h, 14 m, +2 °C), ”…” for quotes,
klockslag as kl. 18, no em or en dashes. Signs and pictures are described in words.
