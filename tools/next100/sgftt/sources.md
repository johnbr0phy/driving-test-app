# Sources for `sgftt` (Singapore Final Theory Test, class 3 / 3A)

All 200 questions are original. They were written from the rule text of the Traffic Police handbook
Final Theory of Driving and do not copy any official practice set (the police e-services mock theory
test was not opened) or any commercial app. Diagrams are described in words.

## Primary source (checked 2026-11-05)
- Singapore Traffic Police, "Final Theory of Driving: The Official Handbook", "Published online by
  Traffic Police ... Published on 2.1.2026", 116 pages. Served at
  https://www.police.gov.sg/-/media/SPF/Knowledge-Hub/Traffic/FT-ENG-2126-Revised.pdf
  (linked as the English Final Theory handbook from
  https://www.police.gov.sg/Knowledge-Hub/Traffic/Traffic-Matters/Online-Learning-Portal, checked
  2026-11-05; Chinese, Malay and Tamil versions are FTTCN24, FTTMY24 and FTTTM24 on the same page).
  Chapters: Introduction; Operation of Vehicle Controls (paras 7-69); Basic Driving Techniques
  (70-167); Driving in Traffic (168-288, incl. defensive driving, expressways, emergencies,
  accidents); The Driving Test (289-292, not used for questions); Restrictions on Learner Drivers
  (293, not used); Vehicle Maintenance (294-296); Light Goods Vehicles (297-298); enforcement
  cameras (299-302, fines and points, not used); Courteous Driving (303-341).
- Handbook para 289(c): a "valid final driving theory certificate" is required on the day of the
  practical driving test (landing copy only).

## Test format
- The Traffic Police pages (Provisional Driving Licence, Singapore Driving Licence, Online Learning
  Portal, all checked 2026-11-05) give no FTT question count, time or pass mark.
- 50 multiple-choice questions, 50 minutes, 45 to pass (90%): third-party guides only
  (basictheorytestsg.com "Singapore Final Theory Test (FTT): Your Gateway to Driving", tptest.sg
  "Final Theory Test (FTT)", checked 2026-11-05). One other guide says 40 questions; the 50/45 figure
  is the common one and matches the BTT. The landing, FAQ and examFacts label it "as guides say".
  passPct 90 gives ceil(0.90 x 50) = 45.
- Class 3/3A learners take the FTT after the BTT and before the practical test; motorcycle learners
  take the Riding Theory Test instead (BTT handbook Part A para 4, see `tools/countries/sgbtt/sources.md`).
- The test is booked at the three driving centres (BBDC, CDC, SSDC) per guides and the SSDC help page
  "How do I book the Traffic Police Theory Tests (BTT, FTT and RTT) online?"
  (https://help.ssdcl.com.sg/portal/en/kb/articles/how-do-i-book-the-traffic-police-theory-tests-btt-ftt-and-rtt-online).
- The Traffic Police publish no per-topic counts. The blueprint (7/8/9/8/7/5/6 per 50) follows the
  handbook's chapters, weighted by length: controls 14%, basic technique 16%, lanes/junctions/
  manoeuvres 18%, defensive driving 16%, other road users 14%, expressways 10%, emergencies,
  accidents, maintenance and light goods vehicles 12%.

## Numbers used (all from the 2.1.2026 handbook)
- Gears: 1st from rest, change up at about 15-20 km/h, 2nd to 30-35, 3rd to 40-45; 5th (overdrive)
  above 70 km/h (paras 17, 88). Engine-brake: 3rd on a gradual slope, 2nd steep, 1st with the
  footbrake on a short steep slope (100); downhill 3rd/2nd low hill, 2nd/1st steep hill (206).
- Look ahead at least three times your speed in km/h, in metres (80(o)): 60 km/h gives 180 m.
- Eyes moved about every two seconds (171); high beam about 100 m (192).
- Vertical parking: half a car length ahead, about 1.5 m from the kerb (150); parallel parking:
  0.5 to 1 m from the kerb, full lock when the back bumper is about 0.5 m past the corner (154).
- Obstacles: 0.5 m from fixed obstacles, 1.5 m from moving vehicles (225-226); about 1 m from parked
  vehicles (220); 1 m from pedestrians facing traffic (241).
- Children below 15 (228); decline from about age 50 (229); cycling groups of more than 5 (248).
- Expressways: prohibited vehicles list incl. learner drivers, motorcycles with side-cars, e-bikes,
  forklifts (261); most limits 80 and 90 km/h (263); right outermost lane (265); no driving on the
  shoulder (267); no letting down passengers (269); breakdown, guardrails, tow (271).
- Fire Brigade 995 (277). Accidents without injury: police report not necessary (287).

## Deliberately avoided
- Everything the BTT bank already asks (signs, markings, parking distances, the three-second rule,
  the 1.5 m cyclist and back-to-traffic pedestrian gaps, headlight hours, wet-road stopping distance,
  aquaplaning response, skid correction, dazzle, tunnels, yellow boxes, uncontrolled junction rules).
- The alcohol limit (para 175 still prints 80 mg per 100 ml of blood; the 2026 Act changes it).
- Demerit points and fines (paras 290, 299-300), the driving test procedure (289-292) and the
  learner restricted areas and times (293).
- Para 201's "most countries in Europe adopt a left-hand drive system" is used as printed
  (steering wheel on the left).
