# Sources for sgm5 (Singapore CMFAS RES5, formerly Module 5), checked 2026-11-05

## Key finding: Module 5 was replaced by RES5
- MAS Notice FAA-N26, Notice on Competency Requirements for Representatives of Financial Advisers,
  page https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-faa-n26 (last revised 1 April 2024) and PDF
  https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/notices/cmg/notice-faa-n26/faa-n26-notice-on-competency-requirements-for-representatives-of-financial-advisers-1.pdf
  - Takes effect 1 April 2024; cancels FAA-N13 (para 1.1).
  - Minimum entry requirements (para 3.1): at least 21 years old; GCE 'A' Level with 3 H2 and 2 H1
    passes, IB Diploma, Singapore polytechnic diploma, or equivalent.
  - Minimum examination requirements (para 4.3, Table 1): a valid pass in RES5 plus the product
    modules (M8, M8A, M9, M9A, CM-EIP, CM-SIP; combined CM-CIS, CM-LIP, CM-LIC, CM-CMP).
  - Para 4.11: a pass in Module 5 under the cancelled notice, or SCI's non-examinable Module 5
    course, is deemed a pass in RES5. Annex B: RES5 "Rules, Ethics and Skills for Financial
    Advisory Services", administered by SCI.
  - Part 5 CPD: 6 core hours (IBF/SCI-accredited ethics or rules course, or CPF Board course),
    supplementary 24 hours (10 for MRTA/group term only), excludes job-scope research, principal
    weighs CPD in fit and proper, 183-day pro-rating and carry-over, inform principal immediately and
    complete within 12 months if unable for unforeseeable reasons, rep keeps evidence.
- SCI exam page for M5: https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/M5 (title "Rules and
  Regulations for Financial Advisory Services"; only an exam schedule, which shows "No exams
  available" on 2026-11-05).
- SCI Certification Designations (RES5 page): Module 5 counts for SCI designations only "for those
  candidates who have secured a pass by 28 March 2024".

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- SCI, RES5 exam details: https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/RES5 (content loaded from
  the page's CMS sections "Contents", "Calculator Policy", "Exam Schedule", 2026-11-05):
  - For those advising on securities, CIS units, exchange-traded derivatives, leveraged FX, OTC
    derivatives, and advising on or arranging life policies; per MAS Notice FAA-N26.
  - 27 chapters: Part I 1-13 (FAA and FAR, MAS notices FAA-N16, N03, N11, N02, N10, N12, N14, N20,
    N26, N06, N17, FSM-N23, N19, FSM-N24, MAS 307, guidelines, CIS Code, securities dealing market
    conduct, CPF); Part II 14-27 (ethics, professionalism, conflicts, fair dealing, ethical marketing,
    client relationships, fact finding, analysis, strategies, presentation, review, basic financial
    planning guide).
  - "150 MCQ Questions, 110 Questions for Part I, 40 Questions for Part II. Duration 3 hours."
  - "Candidates are required to obtain at least 75% for Part I AND at least 80% for Part II. One
    mark will be awarded for each correct answer. No mark will be awarded or deducted for any wrong
    or blank answer." Result slip only, no certificate.
  - English medium, Computer Screen Examination at SCI (9 Temasek Boulevard, Suntec Tower Two);
    closed book; held daily on weekdays; no limit on resits; 3 CPD hours on passing.
  - Calculator: own non-programmable calculator from SCI's approved list.
  - Exam schedule shows RES5 sessions on 12 to 20 November 2026.
- SCI notice on study texts, https://www.scicollege.org.sg/docs/IMP_REGULATORY.pdf (updated 30 June
  2026): RES5 study text 1st Edition (Version 1.3), released 20 July 2026, effective for exams from
  22 September 2026.
- Fees: on SCI's fee page; left out.
- Model choice: the real 150 questions are modelled as four 50-question tests at the real pace with
  the Part I : Part II share (37 : 13); one pass line of 80% (the stricter of the two). The chapter
  split per test is Tigertest's; SCI publishes only the 110/40 part split.
