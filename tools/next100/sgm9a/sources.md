# Sources for sgm9a (Singapore CMFAS Module 9A, Life Insurance and Investment-Linked Policies II), checked 2026-11-23

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the rules and definitions below. No SCI
study-text item, sample question, prep-company or app question was read or reworded (practice
sites turned up by search were not opened).

## Exam owner and format (landing, FAQ and examFacts only; never in the bank)
- SCI, M9A exam details: https://www.scicollege.org.sg/Exam/ExamDetails/M9A (fetched 2026-11-23,
  page title "Life Insurance and Investment-Linked Policies II || SCI"):
  - For new or existing representatives of financial advisers who need the knowledge to advise
    on and/or arrange investment-linked life insurance policies (ILPs); refers to MAS Notice
    FAA-N26.
  - Objectives: structured products (features, types, pros and cons, comparison with other
    investments, governance), documentation and risks of structured products and Structured ILPs,
    Structured ILPs (features, risks, performance under market conditions, suitability), exchange
    and OTC derivatives, and applications of structured funds.
  - Contents: Chapter 1 Introduction to Structured Products; 2 Risk Considerations of Structured
    Products; 3 Understanding Derivatives; 4 Introduction to Structured ILPs; 5 Portfolio of
    Investments with an Insurance; 6 Case Studies.
  - "50 multiple-choice questions", duration 1 hour, passing grade 70%, one mark per correct
    answer, no deduction for wrong or blank answers, result slip only (no certificate).
  - English medium computer screen examination, closed book, held daily on weekdays, no limit on
    resits, eBook study text only (no hard copy), 1 CPD hour on passing.
  - So M9A still exists in November 2026 as a separate paper after M9; it was not merged or
    retired. The combined alternative is CM-LIP (M9 + M9A) per FAA-N26.
- MAS Notice FAA-N26, Notice on Competency Requirements for Representatives of Financial Advisers,
  https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-faa-n26 (PDF read 2026-11-23, issue date
  28 September 2023, in force 1 April 2024):
  - Table 1: M9 for life policies that are Excluded Investment Products; M9 plus M9A for life
    policies that are Specified Investment Products (ILPs). Table 2: CM-LIP = M9 + M9A, CM-LIC =
    M8 + M8A + M9 + M9A.
  - Annex: M9A "Life Insurance and Investment-linked Policies II", administered by SCI; exempt with
    a degree in finance, financial engineering or computational finance, a degree in actuarial
    science or insurance, or the CFA.
- SCI notice on study texts, https://www.scicollege.org.sg/docs/IMP_REGULATORY.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-23, updated 3 July 2026): new versions listed for RES5, M9, CM-LIP and CM-LIC from
  22 September 2026; no new M9A version listed.
- Fees: on SCI's fee page; left out.
- Model choice: the real paper is 50 questions in 1 hour at 70%, so each Tigertest practice test is
  the real length and pass mark. SCI publishes no chapter weights; the per-test split by chapter
  (9/8/10/9/7/7) is Tigertest's.

## Content sources (Singapore rules as of November 2026)
- MAS Notice 307, Investment-Linked Policies, last revised 28 June 2021:
  https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/notices/notice-307 (PDF read 2026-11-23). Used: definitions of
  "financial derivative", "hedging", "ILP sub-fund", "NAV" and "structured products" (tailor-made
  for an ILP sub-fund, with an issuer or other entity ready to unwind at market prices so the
  sub-fund can meet redemptions each dealing day); para 7 (notify MAS 21 days before launch, one
  month before significant changes, including a change of classification between EIP and SIP);
  para 8 (changes affecting a key OTC derivative counterparty); para 12 (unit price = NAV /
  units outstanding); para 14 (capital guaranteed ILP sub-fund redeemed at maturity at the higher
  of the guaranteed amount or NAV per unit); para 51 (CIS Code appendices apply to ILP sub-funds:
  capital guaranteed, hedge, index, money market, property); para 52 (consult MAS on a novel
  structure); para 61 (derivative risks measured and managed; insurer or manager may not be the
  OTC counterparty); para 62 (credit ratings not relied on mechanistically, lowest rating used);
  para 65 (dealing at least once a month); para 66 to 68 (suspension only in exceptional
  circumstances, within 21 days, weekly review); para 93 (names: "fund-of-funds" for five or more
  underlying funds; "capital guaranteed" only if the guidelines are met; "capital protected" and
  "principal protected" prohibited in ILP sub-fund names); Annex Ab (hedge sub-funds: protection or
  guarantee effective only at maturity; credit risk of guarantor or issuer); Annex Ac (capital
  guaranteed sub-funds: guarantor's name, business, financial position and rating; guarantee gives
  no assurance of the guarantor's solvency; termination events; whether the guarantee covers 100% of
  monies or excludes preliminary charges; dilution of performance; holders to the specified date).
- Code on Collective Investment Schemes, last revised 2 July 2026:
  https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/codes/code-on-collective-investment-schemes (PDF read
  2026-11-23). Used: Appendix 1 section 3 (global exposure to financial derivatives and embedded
  derivatives at most 100% of NAV; Commitment Approach or, after consulting MAS, VaR; netting on the
  same underlying; hedging must not aim at a return and must reduce risk; currency hedges may be
  netted), section 4 (underlying exposure diversified; embedded derivative criteria; an instrument
  tailor-made for a scheme is deemed to embed a derivative; cover: liquid assets for cash-settled,
  underlying assets for physical delivery; Table 1 exposure methods, for example CDS protection
  buyer at the market value of the reference asset, interest rate swap at the notional of the fixed
  leg, FX forward at the notional of the currency leg), section 5 (OTC counterparty under prudential
  supervision; 10% of NAV for an eligible financial institution rated at least A, otherwise 5%;
  exposure measured by maximum potential loss, not notional; replacement cost plus add-on);
  Appendix 4 (capital guaranteed funds: guarantee of return of capital at a pre-determined date;
  "guarantee", "assured", "insured" or "warranty" only if the Appendix is met; a scheme guaranteeing
  income only, or relying on its investments alone, is not capital guaranteed; eligible guarantor
  AA for a financial institution, AAA otherwise; if the rating falls below A, a new guarantor within
  six months; guarantor's securities at most 10% of NAV; unconditional first-demand guarantee
  enforceable in Singapore; at least 100% of capital, or 100% less front-end loads with prominent
  disclosure; termination events).
- MAS Guidelines on Structured Deposits [FAA-G09], last revised 28 June 2021:
  https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/guidelines/guidelines-on-structured-deposits (PDF read
  2026-11-23). Used: para 7 and 8 (a structured deposit is a type of deposit; "structured
  deposit" may not label a product that is not a deposit), para 10 (variable returns, loss of
  principal if not held to maturity), para 13 (disclose the nature, the deposit-taking institution,
  guaranteed or not, best and worst case illustrations, realistic headline rates, fees, early
  termination and early redemption by the bank, principal guaranteed only if held to maturity, not
  an insured deposit under the Deposit Insurance and Policy Owners' Protection Schemes Act).
- MAS MoneySense, Understanding structured notes:
  https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/investments/understanding-structured-notes/ (fetched 2026-11-23).
  Used: a debt obligation of the issuer with embedded options or swaps; reference assets; equity,
  interest rate, credit, currency and commodity-linked notes; delivery of shares instead of cash;
  most notes not principal-guaranteed; collateral risk; issuer call and early redemption events
  (default, tax, force majeure, fall in collateral value); prospectus and Product Highlights Sheet.
- MAS MoneySense, Understanding structured deposits:
  https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/understanding-structured-deposits (fetched 2026-11-23). Used:
  deposit plus investment component; cap and participation rate; principal at maturity subject to
  the bank's credit risk; early withdrawal losses and unwinding costs; periodic valuation; callable
  deposits and reinvestment risk; not covered by deposit insurance; equity, bond, interest rate and
  credit-linked types.
- MAS MoneySense, Understanding contracts for difference:
  https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/investments/understanding-contracts-for-difference/ (fetched
  2026-11-23). Used: CFDs are OTC, unlisted Specified Investment Products needing a CKA; long and
  short; margin, margin calls, forced close-out; losses can exceed the margin; overnight financing;
  provider counterparty risk; no rights to the underlying.
- Constant proportion and option-based portfolio insurance: "Value-at-Risk-Based Portfolio
  Insurance: Performance Evaluation and Benchmarking Against CPPI", arXiv 2305.12539,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.12539 (read 2026-11-23), sections 1 and 3, citing Perold (1986),
  Perold and Sharpe, and Black and Jones. Used: floor as a percentage of the initial investment,
  cushion = portfolio value minus floor, exposure = multiple x cushion, the rest in the riskless
  asset, a higher multiple gains more on rises and falls to the floor faster, zero cushion means the
  whole portfolio is in the riskless asset, gap risk of breaking the floor between rebalancing,
  OBPI as a risky portfolio covered by a put.
- Option, futures and swap mechanics (payoffs, intrinsic and time value, margining, mark to market,
  hedging and speculation) are standard definitions; exposure conversions follow Table 1 of the CIS
  Code above. Exchange-traded margining checked against the SGX Rulebook (Clearing Rules),
  https://rulebook.sgx.com/node/6054 (opened 2026-11-23).
