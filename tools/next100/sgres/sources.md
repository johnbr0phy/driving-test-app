# Singapore RES exam sources (checked 2026-11-05)

## Exam format (landing, FAQ, examFacts only; never in the bank)
- CEA, "Prepare for the real estate salesperson examination":
  https://www.cea.gov.sg/real-estate-professionals/for-aspiring-real-estate-salespersons/real-estate-salesperson-examination/prepare-for-the-real-estate-salesperson-examination/
  Two papers, Paper 1 and Paper 2, sat separately. Each: Section A 60 MCQs (60 marks), Section B
  20 MCQs on one or two case studies (40 marks), 100 marks, 2.5 hours. Passing mark 60 for each
  paper, "subject to review by CEA". 2026 sittings in March, July and October/November (Paper 1 on
  the Saturday, Paper 2 on the Sunday, 9.30am to 12pm); 2025 and 2026 exams use the current
  syllabus, and a revised syllabus applies to exams from 1 January 2027. Policy changes announced or
  implemented within one month before an exam are not tested.
- CEA, RES Examination Syllabus 2027 (version 1.0, issued for exams from 1 January 2027):
  https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/71/25d2fc43-4e98-400d-85bd-8ffde09d2cec/RES%20examination%20Syllabus%202027.pdf
  Format table on page 3 (Section A 60 MCQs, 1 mark each; Section B 20 MCQs on one or two case
  studies, 2 marks each; 2.5 hours per paper). Candidates must complete an RES course of at least
  65 hours with an Approved Course Provider before the exam. Paper 1 tests Competency Units 1 (Real
  Estate Agency Industry Overview and Basic Land Law Concepts) and 2 (Dealings with Interests in
  Land); Paper 2 tests Units 3 (Regulation of Real Estate Agency Industry and Real Estate Marketing)
  and 4 (Property Transactions). Recommended training hours per topic used for the blueprint:
  Unit 1 11 h (1.1 1.5, 1.2 1, 1.3 1.5, 1.4 2, 1.5 1, 1.6 1, 1.7 1.5, 1.8 1.5); Unit 2 23 h (2.1 5,
  2.2 3, 2.3 3, 2.4 3, 2.5 1.5, 2.6 to 2.11 1 each, 2.12 2.5); Unit 3 24 h; Unit 4 15 h.
- CEA, "Anticipated FAQs on revision of RES Examination Syllabus" (version as at 27 Aug 2025):
  https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/71/e331bb2c-1bcd-46d7-85f1-8f2d8c448c68/RES%20FAQs%20(Version%20as%20at%2027%20Aug%202025)v1.pdf
  New units 3.2 (professional practice, ethics and conduct), 3.4 (PMLPFTF obligations) and 3.9
  (green buildings and leases); foreign worker housing removed; no change to format or duration
  from 2027; the RES course certificate is valid two years from course completion; a pass in one
  paper (a "modular pass") stays valid within two years of course completion; the syllabus was
  last revised in January 2017.
- Not confirmed from CEA: exam language (assumed English, not stated on the landing), calculator
  rules. Left out.

## Content sources for the bank (2027 syllabus topics)
- Syllabus learning outcomes above (all eleven categories map to its units; see spec.json).
- CEA regulations page and Estate Agents (Estate Agency Work) Regulations 2010: no dual
  representation (reg. 5: no salesperson may have both vendor and purchaser, or landlord and
  tenant, as clients for the same property), no holding or handling of transaction monies (reg. 7),
  no referral to moneylenders:
  https://www.cea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/legal/regulations/estate-agents-(estate-agency-work)-regulations-2010.pdf
  https://www.cea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/consumers/no-dual-representation.pdf
- CEA, Preventing money laundering, proliferation financing and terrorism financing:
  https://www.cea.gov.sg/regulatory-matters/preventing-money-laundering-proliferation-financing-and-terrorism-financing/
  CDD before facilitating a sale or purchase, enhanced CDD, STR to STRO, tipping off under CDSA
  s. 48. Record retention period not quoted (not confirmed).
- SLA via ask.gov.sg: an accepted caveat is valid five years from lodgment, extendable:
  https://ask.gov.sg/sla/questions/cmbt7ovw40063s5f67vmjkddg
- URA, Renting Property: minimum stay of three consecutive months for private homes; no
  short-term accommodation. Occupancy caps (6, or 8 for larger units until 31 Dec 2028) left out
  as time-limited: https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/renting-property/
- HDB, renting out a flat (eligibility): Plus and Prime flats may not be rented out as a whole
  even after the 10-year MOP; whole-flat rental for others after MOP with approval, citizen owners
  only: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/business/estate-agents-and-salespersons/renting-out-a-flat-or-bedroom/eligibility
- HDB, Option to Purchase and resale terms: Intent to Sell registered at least 7 days before the
  OTP; valid HFE letter when granted the OTP; seller may not grant another option during the option
  period: https://www.hdb.gov.sg/cs/infoweb/residential/buying-a-flat/buying-procedure-for-resale-flats/plan-source-and-contract/option-to-purchase
  The option period length and option fee amounts are not used (guides only, and money figures).
- CCCS FAQ on the Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act: sale and purchase of property largely
  excluded; claims against estate agents for their services are covered:
  https://www.cccs.gov.sg/resources/faqs/consumer-protection
- Land Titles (Strata) Act s. 84A collective sale consent: 90% of share value and strata area for
  developments under 10 years old, 80% for 10 years or older (syllabus 4.3.1; long-standing rule).
- Not used, as they change often: stamp duty and ABSD rates, SSD holding period, LTV, TDSR and MSR
  percentages, CPF housing limits, option fees, CPD credit counts, penalties in dollars.
