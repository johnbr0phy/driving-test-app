# sgsnb sources (checked 2026-11-16)

Every question is original. No SNB sample question was used or reworded (the five samples in the test
plan PDF, on breakthrough cancer pain, a quality improvement mindset, pelvic floor exercises for stress
incontinence, the most serious trauma finding and therapeutic communication, were avoided as topics).

## Outline (what the exam covers)
- SNB RN Licensure Examination test plan and sample questions, April 2026 (PDF, official):
  https://file.go.gov.sg/snb-rn-le-test-plan-and-sample-qns.pdf (fetched 2026-11-16, fetched via
  https://go.gov.sg/snb-rn-le-test-plan-and-sample-qns). Four core competencies and their categories:
  1 Legal and Ethical Nursing Practice (1.1 accountability and legal obligations, 1.2 ethical nursing
  practice, 1.3 culturally appropriate care); 2 Professional Nursing Practice (20 categories: quality
  improvement and holistic care, health promotion and client education, safe environment, critical
  thinking, nutrition/fluids and electrolyte imbalance, communication, medical emergencies, end of
  life and palliative care, mental health, respiratory care, elimination, assessment and re-assessment,
  pre and post operative care, wound care, diabetes care, client safety and comfort, infection control,
  medication and pharmacology, elder care, mobility); 3 Collaborative Practice and Teamwork
  (collaboration, delegation and supervision); 4 Continuing Professional Education and Development.
  The plan gives no weights, counts or pass mark.

## Format
- SNB Licensure Examinations page, https://www.snb.gov.sg/for-professionals/becoming-a-nurse-or-midwife/apply-for-registration-enrolment/snb-licensure-examinations/
  (fetched 2026-11-16): the LE is Computer-Based Testing from 28 November 2024, run at Pearson VUE test centres.
- SNB LE CBT FAQ (PDF), https://file.go.gov.sg/frequently-asked-questions-snb-le-cbt-8-may-2025.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-16): applications are made by the employer's HR through SNB's NRS portal for
  a candidate employed by an eligible Singapore healthcare provider; two attempts per candidate;
  Authorisation-to-Test usually valid 4 months; all LEs at Pearson VUE centres.
- Pearson VUE SNB page, https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/snb.html (fetched 2026-11-16): multiple choice,
  60 minutes with no extension, on-screen calculator. It states no question count or pass standard.

## Law and ethics
- Code for Nurses and Midwives, SNB, effective 2 April 2018 (PDF, official):
  https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/78/3283b320-e71e-4d71-81ee-b5e22821d097/code-for-nurses-and-midwives-april-2018.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-16). Ten principles (people's values and rights; autonomy; confidentiality and privacy;
  advocacy; responsible and accountable practice; competency; records; professionalism and public trust;
  self-regulation, including a valid Practising Certificate; collaboration), the glossary (accountability
  to people, employer and SNB; professional misconduct), Appendix 1 professional boundaries and Appendix 2
  social media guidelines. All LEG items rest on this text.
- Nurses and Midwives Act (sso.agc.gov.sg) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching, so no question asks a
  section number, penalty or fee. The Code itself says it supplements the Act.

## Clinical content
Clinical items (observations, infection control, drugs, calculations, emergencies, surgical, diabetes,
mental health, end of life) test universal nursing knowledge named by the test plan categories. They use
standard published guidance (WHO Five Moments for hand hygiene, ABCDE, ERC/resuscitation compression rate
and ratio, usual adult physiological ranges) with no country-specific figures, and every calculation was
recomputed. Singapore generic drug names, SI units, British spelling.

## Not confirmed
- Number of questions, section weights and the pass mark are not published by SNB or Pearson VUE; the
  landing and examFacts say so. 50-question tests and the 70% line are Tigertest's.
- The SNB core competencies of the RN (2023) PDF could not be loaded, so nothing relies on it.

## Deliberately left out
Fees, fines, registration or renewal figures, the Nurses and Midwives Act section numbers, the PDPA and
other Singapore statutes, MOH guideline numbers, and anything about the exam itself.

safety, infection control and comfort 5; medications and pharmacology 7; fluids, nutrition and
elimination 4; emergencies and respiratory 5; surgical, wound and diabetes 5; mental health, elder and
end of life 5; teamwork, delegation and CPE 4. Pools 24/20/16/20/28/16/20/20/20/16.
