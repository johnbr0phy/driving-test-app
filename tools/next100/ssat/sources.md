# SSAT Middle Level (`ssat`): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest. No official SSAT question, the
free Mini-Practice Test, the Official Guide or Online Practice items, released forms or any
commercial SSAT prep item was used or reworded. Reading passages are original. Quantitative
items are written from the grade 5 to 7 mathematics the test owner lists (arithmetic,
elementary algebra, geometry); verbal items from plain English vocabulary.

Level modelled: the **Middle Level SSAT** (students in grades 5 to 7 applying for grades 6 to 8).
The Upper Level shares the same format and timing (grades 8 to 11), but its vocabulary and math
are harder; this bank is written for the Middle Level only.

The SSAT is owned and run by E3n (Enrollment Management & Educational Records Group, Inc., the
non-profit formerly The Enrollment Management Association). ssat.org now redirects to
admission.org, "brought to you by E3n".

## Outline and format (the test owner)

- What's on the Middle Level SSAT? (admission.org help centre).
  https://www.admission.org/help/whats-on-the-middle-level-ssat (fetched 2026-11-14). Sections in
  order: Writing Sample 25 minutes (not scored), Quantitative 25 questions in 30 minutes, Reading
  Comprehension 40 questions in 40 minutes, Verbal 60 questions (30 synonyms, 30 analogies) in 30
  minutes, Quantitative 25 questions in 30 minutes, Experimental 16 questions in 15 minutes (6
  verbal, 5 reading, 5 quantitative, not scored); two ten-minute breaks; total 3 hours 10 minutes;
  no calculators. Quantitative measures "arithmetic, elementary algebra, geometry, and other
  concepts". Reading passages 250 to 350 words, narrative (novels, poems, short stories, essays)
  and argument (a clear point of view), from fiction, humanities, science and social studies;
  questions on main idea, details, inference, word meaning from context, author's purpose, tone
  and attitude, evaluating opinions and arguments, and predictions.
- What's on the Upper Level SSAT? https://www.admission.org/help/whats-on-the-upper-level-ssat
  (fetched 2026-11-14). The same sections, counts and times for grades 8 to 11 (used only to say
  the two levels share a format).
- About the SSAT. https://www.admission.org/assessments/ssat/about-the-ssat (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Middle Level grades 5 to 7, Upper Level grades 8 to 11; verbal, math and reading; non-scored
  writing sample; an unscored experimental section; "virtually unspeeded".
- SSAT Candidate Handbook for Testing Year 2026-2027 (V1, August 3, 2026 to present).
  https://5232910.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/5232910/website_files/ssat/SSAT%20Candidate%20Handbook.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-14, text extracted locally). Middle Level for grades 5-7 applying to grades
  6-8; "five multiple choice sections and an unscored writing sample"; section times (Writing 25,
  Break 10, Quantitative 30, Reading 40, Break 10, Verbal 30, Quantitative 30, Experimental 15);
  scoring: 1 point for each correct answer, a quarter point lost for each wrong answer, nothing
  for omitted questions; "eliminate 2 of the 5 answer choices" (five choices per question);
  calculators prohibited; testing year August 3, 2026 to July 20, 2027.
- SSAT Score Reports. https://www.admission.org/assessments/ssat/score-reports (fetched
  2026-11-14). Middle and Upper Level: a point for each correct answer, a quarter point subtracted
  for each incorrect answer, nothing for omitted questions.
- How should I interpret the SSAT score report?
  https://www.admission.org/help/how-should-i-interpret-the-ssat-score-report (fetched
  2026-11-14). Scaled scores per section and total, percentiles against first-time test takers in
  the same grade over three years, equating across forms.
- Guessing on the SSAT: good or bad? (admission.org advice, September 5, 2025).
  https://www.admission.org/advice/guessing-on-the-ssat-good-or-bad (fetched 2026-11-14). Five
  answer choices per question; the quarter-point penalty.
- What items are prohibited during the SSAT?
  https://www.admission.org/help/what-items-are-prohibited-during-the-ssat (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Calculators and rulers are not allowed.

## Content standards (what the math items are written from)

- The owner publishes no topic list beyond "arithmetic, elementary algebra, geometry, and other
  concepts". The items stay within grade 5 to 7 mathematics: whole numbers, fractions, decimals,
  percents, ratios and rates, order of operations, number properties, simple equations and
  expressions, patterns, perimeter, area, volume, angles, coordinates, mean, median and simple
  probability. Common Core grade 6 overview used as a check on grade level:
  https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/introduction/ (fetched 2026-11-14; an unofficial
  mirror of the CCSS text, used only to confirm what grade 6 covers).

## Deliberately left out

- The Writing Sample (unscored) and the Experimental section (unscored).
- SSAT scaled scores and percentiles: a practice form cannot place a student on the SSAT scale or
  against the norm group, so the score here is percent correct. The quarter-point penalty is
  explained on the landing and in `scoreNote`, not applied.
- The Elementary Level (grades 3 and 4) and the Upper Level's harder content.
- Length: each practice test is about half the real scored length at about the real pace
  (Quantitative 13 and 12, Reading 20, Verbal 30: 15 synonyms and 15 analogies).
