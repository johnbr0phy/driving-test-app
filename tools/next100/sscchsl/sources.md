# SSC CHSL Tier 1 sources

Checked 31 October 2026. Re-checked 1 November 2026 by the reviewer: both PDFs below downloaded and read
(the notice is 88 pages); every figure listed here matches the text.

- Staff Selection Commission, Notice: Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2026, uploaded
  7 September 2026. https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_chsl_2026.pdf
  - Posts: Lower Division Clerk / Junior Secretariat Assistant, Data Entry Operator, DEO Grade 'A' (para 1).
  - Applications 07.09.2026 to 07.10.2026; Tier I and Tier II schedule "to be notified later".
  - Para 13.8, Scheme of Tier-I: four parts "(Not in sequence)", each 25 questions / 50 marks:
    I English Language (Basic Knowledge), II General Intelligence, III Quantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic
    Skill), IV General Awareness. "60 Minutes (with sectional timer of 15 minutes for each subject)"; 80 minutes
    (15 + 5 = 20 per subject) for candidates eligible for a scribe.
  - 13.8.1: objective multiple choice only; questions in English, Hindi and any language opted by the candidate
    (Annexure-XV) for Parts II, III and IV.
  - 13.8.2: negative marking of 0.50 marks for each wrong answer.
  - 13.6: metric system of weights and measures.
  - 13.10: indicative Tier-I syllabus (English topics incl. cloze and comprehension passages; General Intelligence
    verbal and non-verbal types incl. emotional and social intelligence; Quantitative Aptitude: number systems,
    arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistical charts; General Awareness: history,
    culture, geography, economic scene, general policy, scientific research, current events).
  - 17.1: minimum qualifying marks in Tier-I: UR 30%, OBC/EWS 25%, all other categories 20%.
  - 17.2 to 17.6: candidates shortlisted for Tier-II on Tier-I performance; category-wise cut-offs per post group;
    final merit from Tier-II Sections I and II only.

state). Static facts only: no current affairs and no current office-holders.
