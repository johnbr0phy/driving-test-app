# SSC CPO (Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs) Paper-I sources

Checked 1 November 2026. Reviewer re-downloaded the notice PDF on 1 November 2026 (69 pages) and confirmed
paras 12.1, 12.2, 12.9.1.2 ("India and its neighboring countries"), 16.2, 16.3 and 16.8 as summarised below.

## Official
- Staff Selection Commission, Notice: Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2026
  (to be uploaded on ssc.gov.in on 10.09.2026; 69 pages). Downloaded and read in full for the figures below.
  https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_capf_2026.pdf
  - Online applications 10.09.2026 to 30.09.2026; correction window 08.10.2026 to 10.10.2026; "Schedule of Computer
    Based Examination: To be notified later" (cover page).
  - Posts: Sub-Inspector (GD) in CAPFs, Sub-Inspector (Executive) Male and Female in Delhi Police, Sub-Inspector (Fire)
    Male in CISF, all pay Level-6 (para 1). Age limit 20 to 25 years as on 01.08.2026 for all posts except SI (Fire) in
    CISF (para 5.1).
  - Para 12.1: the exam is Paper-I, PST/PET, Paper-II and DME, all mandatory. Paper-I: Part I General Intelligence and
    Reasoning 25 questions / 25 marks, Part II General Knowledge and General Awareness 25/25, Part III Quantitative
    Aptitude 25/25, Part IV English Comprehension 25/25; 1 hour "with sectional timer of 15 minutes for each part".
    Paper-II: Parts I to III 20 questions / 40 marks each in 1 hour (sectional timer 20 minutes each) and Part IV English
    Comprehension 100 questions / 200 marks in 1 hour.
  - Para 12.2: objective multiple choice; questions in Hindi and English in Parts I, II and III of both papers (Part IV
    is English); negative marking 0.25 marks per wrong answer in Paper-I and 0.50 in Paper-II.
  - Para 12.3: scores normalised across shifts. Para 12.6: NCC bonus marks (C 5%, B 3%, A 2% of maximum marks).
  - Para 12.9: indicative syllabus for Paper-I and Paper-II (reasoning, GK and GA including India and its neighbouring
    countries, quantitative aptitude topics, English comprehension), used for the topic lists in the domain files.
    Note in the notice: Paper-II is harder than Paper-I.
  - Para 16.2: minimum qualifying marks in Paper-I and Paper-II (without NCC bonus): UR 30%, OBC/EWS 25%, all other
    categories 20%. Para 16.3: Paper-I shortlists for PST/PET, and the Commission may fix cut-offs in each part of
    Paper-I. Para 16.8: final selection on Paper-II marks (plus NCC bonus) and post preferences.
- Pattern change: guides report that until the 2025 cycle Paper-I was 200 questions (50 per part, 200 marks) in
  2 hours with 0.25 negative marking (e.g. https://www.sscadda.com/ssc-cpo-exam-pattern, read 1 November 2026). The
  2026 notice cuts it to 100 questions in 1 hour with 15-minute sectional timers, which this bank models at full
  length.

## Facts used in questions
- Constitution of India (Articles 76, 109, 118, 124, 148, 280, 315, 324, 352, 356, 360; Parts III, IV, IV-A; Seventh,
  Eighth, Ninth and Tenth Schedules; 42nd, 52nd and 61st Amendments).
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, in force 1 July 2024
  (Ministry of Home Affairs).
- Border guarding roles of the SSB (Nepal 2001, Bhutan 2004), BSF, ITBP and Assam Rifles: Ministry of Home Affairs
  border management pages.
- NCERT history, geography and science texts for the remaining static facts.
