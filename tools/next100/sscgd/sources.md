# SSC GD Constable (CBE) sources

Checked 31 October 2026.

- Staff Selection Commission, notice of the Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and
  Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026 (published 1 December 2025, applications 1 to 31 December 2025,
  25,487 tentative vacancies in BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles and SSF). https://ssc.gov.in
  Notice PDF (74 pages, F. No. HQ-C-3007/10/2025-C-3), read on 1 November 2026 from the copy hosted by the Khowai
  district administration (Government of Tripura, NIC S3WaaS), linked from
  https://khowai.nic.in/notice/notificaton-of-constable-gd-in-central-armed-police-forces-capfs-and-ssf-and-rifleman-gd-in-assam-rifles-examination-2026/ :
  https://cdn.s3waas.gov.in/s3e5841df2166dd424a57127423d276bbe/uploads/2025/12/17646730408096.pdf
  (ssc.gov.in itself did not serve the PDF to this environment). It confirms, in para 12 (Scheme of Examination):
  one objective paper of 80 questions, 2 marks each, Part A General Intelligence and Reasoning, Part B General
  Knowledge and General Awareness, Part C Elementary Mathematics, Part D English/Hindi, 20 questions and 40 marks
  each, 60 minutes for the paper (no per-part times are given); CBE in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages;
  0.25 marks off per wrong answer (12.1.2); scores normalised (notice of 02.06.2025) with NCC bonus marks added
  (12.1.5); matriculation level (12.1.8); no calculators (12.1.10). Para 14.2: minimum qualifying marks UR 30%,
  OBC/EWS 25%, all other categories 20% (before NCC bonus). Para 14.3: NCC bonus 5% (C), 3% (B), 2% (A) of maximum
  marks. Para 14.4: shortlisting for PET/PST state-wise, area-wise and category-wise, about 8 times the vacancies.
  Vacancy table: 25,487 (23,467 male, 2,020 female). Tentative CBE window on the cover: February to April 2026.
  The indicative syllabus (12.1.11) says reasoning questions are "principally of non-verbal type" and that general
  awareness includes current events; this bank is text only and static, as noted below.
  Before the PDF was reached, the scheme was taken from the sources below, which agree with it.
- Scheme of the Computer Based Examination as reproduced by:
  - Free Press Journal, "SSC GD Constable Recruitment 2026: Registration Starts For 25,487 Vacancies", 2 December 2025:
    https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/ssc-gd-constable-recruitment-2026-registration-starts-for-25487-vacancies-know-selection-process-exam-pattern
  - Oliveboard, SSC GD exam pattern: https://www.oliveboard.in/ssc-gd-exam-pattern/
  - Physics Wallah, SSC GD exam pattern: https://store.pw.live/blogs/ssc-exams/ssc-gd-exam-pattern
  Agreed figures: 80 multiple-choice questions, 160 marks, 60 minutes, four parts of 20 questions and 40 marks
  (Part A General Intelligence and Reasoning, Part B General Knowledge and General Awareness, Part C Elementary
  Mathematics, Part D English or Hindi), 2 marks per question, 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer, matriculation
  level, offered in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages, scores normalised across shifts. Syllabus topics per
  part as listed by Physics Wallah (analogies, similarities and differences, spatial visualisation, arithmetical
  reasoning, coding-decoding, blood relations, series; India and its neighbours, history, culture, geography,
  economics, general polity, the Constitution, scientific research, sports; number systems, decimals and fractions,
  percentages, ratio and proportion, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, mensuration, time and
  distance, time and work; comprehension and basic English).
  - Conflict noted: one Careers360 page (https://competition.careers360.com/articles/ssc-gd-constable-2026) gives
    25 questions per part, 90 minutes and 0.50 negative marking. Every other source, and the earlier notices, give
    20 per part, 60 minutes and 0.25; that page appears to mix in the SSC CGL pattern and is not used.
- SSC notice dated 11 April 2026, "Schedule of Constable (GD) ... Examination, 2026": the CBE was scheduled from
  27 April 2026 to the end of May 2026 in four weekly windows. (Copy fetched from
  https://cdnc.heyzine.com/flip-book/pdf/51817e832bdec79986c455ab41b78307febb1a18.pdf.)
- Minimum qualifying marks in the CBE: UR 30%, EWS and OBC 25%, all other categories (SC, ST, ex-servicemen) 20%,
  applied before NCC bonus marks: para 14.2 of the 2026 notice, and SSC's earlier result write-ups, for example
  https://ssc.nic.in/SSCFileServer/PortalManagement/UploadedFiles/Write_up_CTGD_Exam2022_pdf_file_08042023.pdf
  and https://doc.ssc.nic.in/SecAudit/SSCFileServer/PortalManagement/UploadedFiles/WriteUp_GD2021_25032022.pdf.
  NCC bonus marks differ between years' notices (2026: 5, 3 and 2 percent of maximum marks for C, B and A
  certificates), so the landing does not give figures.
- Selection stages after the CBE: Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test, detailed medical examination,
  document verification (Oliveboard, Free Press Journal). PET running standards are not repeated on the landing.
- General Knowledge facts checked against the Constitution of India (Articles 1, 15, 19, 24, 44, 52, 72, 124, 148,
  280, 343; Schedules 8 and 10), NCERT history, geography and science texts, and well-established sports facts.
  Static facts only: no current affairs and no current office-holders.

Not modelled: Hindi as the Part D language (the bank is English only), and spatial visualisation, figure series
and the other non-verbal questions the notice says make up most of Part A (text only), and current events in Part B. Every question is original; none is copied from SSC previous-year papers, from another
Tigertest bank (the ssccgl, rrbntpc, ibpspo and ctet banks were checked for overlapping facts) or from a coaching app.
