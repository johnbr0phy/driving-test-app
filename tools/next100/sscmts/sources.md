# SSC MTS and Havaldar sources

Checked 31 October 2026.

- Staff Selection Commission, Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar (CBIC and CBN) Examination, 2025,
  official notice (uploaded 26 June 2025, F.No. E/15/2025-C-2), fetched directly as a PDF on 1 November 2026 by the
  reviewer: https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_mts_2025.pdf
  (87 pages). Confirmed from it:
  - Para 14.5: one computer-based exam in two sessions on the same day, both mandatory. Session 1: Numerical and
    Mathematical Ability 20 questions / 60 marks, Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving 20 / 60. Session 2: General
    Awareness 25 / 75, English Language and Comprehension 25 / 75. 45 minutes each (60 for candidates eligible for a
    scribe); Session 1 closes automatically and Session 2 starts at once.
  - Para 14.5.4: questions in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages for Session 1 and the General Awareness section.
  - Para 14.5.5: no negative marking in Session 1; one mark deducted for each wrong answer in Session 2.
  - Para 14.5.6: multi-shift scores are normalised.
  - Para 14.6: indicative syllabus (GA "up to 10th Standard"; English includes a simple comprehension paragraph).
  - Para 14.7: Havaldar PET walking 1,600 m in 15 minutes (men), 1 km in 20 minutes (women), plus PST. Not modelled.
  - Para 18.2: Session 2 is evaluated only for candidates who qualify in Session 1.
  - Para 18.3: minimum qualifying marks in Session 1 and in Session 2: UR 30%, OBC/EWS 25%, all others 20%.
  - Paras 18.5 to 18.11: separate category-wise, State/UT-wise and age group-wise (18-25, 18-27) cut-offs; merit list
    prepared solely on Session 2 (normalised) performance.
  No 2026 notice was found at the predictable URLs (Notice_of_adv_mts_2026.pdf returned 404 on 1 November 2026); the
  2025 pattern is used.
