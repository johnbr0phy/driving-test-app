# STAAR Algebra I EOC (`staaralg1`): sources

Every question in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from the Texas Algebra I TEKS as
TEA's assessed curriculum lists them. No released STAAR test, sampler item, interim item, scoring
guide example or commercial prep item was used or reworded.

## Blueprint and assessed curriculum (Texas Education Agency, the test owner)

- STAAR Algebra I Blueprint, effective as of Academic Year 2022-23 (TEA Student Assessment
  Division, Fall 2022).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/2022-staar-algebra-i-blueprint-0.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Five reporting categories with standards, questions and points: 1 Number and Algebraic Methods
  (readiness 2, supporting 11; 9-11 questions, 9-14 points); 2 Describing and Graphing Linear
  Functions, Equations and Inequalities (3 and 8; 10-12, 10-16); 3 Writing and Solving Linear
  Functions, Equations and Inequalities (5 and 7; 12-14, 12-18); 4 Quadratic Functions and
  Equations (4 and 4; 9-11, 9-14); 5 Exponential Functions and Equations (2 and 3; 5-7, 5-9).
  41 one-point questions (multiple choice and non-multiple choice) and 9 two-point
  non-multiple-choice questions: 50 questions, 59 points. Readiness standards about 55-70% of the
  points, supporting 30-45%.
- STAAR Algebra I Assessment, Eligible Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEA, January 2014;
  the current assessed-curriculum link on TEA's STAAR Mathematics Resources page).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/2014-algebra-i-assessed-curriculum.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  The TEKS text of every student expectation, by reporting category, with its readiness or
  supporting label: A.10 and A.11 and A.12 (category 1), A.3 and A.4 (category 2), A.2 and A.5
  (category 3), A.6, A.7 and A.8 (category 4), A.9 (category 5). Process standards A.1 are
  assessed inside every category. This is the primary text the items are written from.
- STAAR Mathematics Resources (TEA).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/staar-mathematics-resources (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Lists the Algebra I assessed curriculum, blueprint, performance level descriptors, graph paper
  and reference materials as the current documents.
- STAAR Algebra I Reference Materials (TEA).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/staar-algebra-1-reference-materials.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Factoring (perfect square trinomials, difference of squares), properties of exponents (product,
  quotient, power of a power, rational and negative exponents), linear equations (standard,
  slope-intercept and point-slope forms, slope formula), quadratic equations (standard and vertex
  forms, quadratic formula, axis of symmetry). The practice reference panel restates these in
  Tigertest's own layout.

## Format, item types, calculator, scoring

- Accompanying Guide to New Question Type Samplers: Mathematics (TEA, STAAR redesign).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/staar-redesign-scoring-and-reporting-for-new-question-types-guide-math.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  The redesign effective 2022-23 caps multiple-choice questions at 75%. EOC math may use equation
  editor, text entry, graphing, number line, inline choice, hot spot, drag and drop, match table
  grid and multiselect questions, worth 1 or 2 points (partial credit possible on 2-point items).
- STAAR Calculator Policy (TEA).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/staar-calculator-policy.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Calculators are required for Algebra I; each student must have access to a graphing
  calculation device throughout the test; tools are available in the online platform; CAS,
  polynomial root finders, simultaneous equation solvers and inequality graphing must be disabled.
- STAAR Test Administrator Manual (TEA).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/test-administration/staar-test-administrator-manual.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Students are expected to finish within three to four hours; a session must finish in the same
  school day and may not exceed seven hours of working time (with stated exceptions).
- Raw Score Conversion Tables index and Spring 2026 Algebra I table (TEA).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/student-assessment-results/raw-score-conversion-tables (fetched 2026-11-14).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/student-assessment-overview/2026-staar-spring-eoc-algebra-i-rsss.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  Spring 2026: Approaches Grade Level (scale score 3550, the passing standard) from raw score 19,
  Meets from 31 (scale 4000), Masters from 41 (scale 4345), of 59 points. Cuts move a little with
  each form, so the copy calls any percentage an estimate.
- STAAR Assessed Content, TEA Assessment Development Division (November 4, 2025), and House Bill 8
  Frequently Asked Questions (TEA, August 2026).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/test-administration/2025-tac-staar-assessed-content.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/hb-8-faq-update.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14).
  HB 8 (89th Legislature, 2nd Called Session, 2025) replaces STAAR with the Student Success Tool
  from the 2027-28 school year: optional beginning- and middle-of-year EOC tests and a required
  end-of-course test. 2026-27 is the last STAAR year. Recorded in lib/examFreshness.json.

## Deliberately left out

- Scale scores and performance levels: a percent score is shown, with the Spring 2026 raw cuts
  only as an estimate in the score note.
- Graphing, number line and hot spot item types (described in words or tables instead); 2-point
  partial credit (every item here is all or nothing).
- Regression items that need a graphing calculator's fit (A.4A, A.4C, A.8B, A.9E) are written
  with the data and the fitted model given, so they can be answered with the scientific
  calculator on the platform.
