# STAAR Biology EOC (`staarbio`): sources

All items and shared data sets are original, written by Tigertest from the Biology TEKS and TEA's assessed
curriculum. No STAAR released test question, rationale, scoring guide item, TEA practice test item,
interim item or commercial prep item was used, reworded or paraphrased. Figures and graphs are described in
words or given as tables.

## Blueprint and assessed curriculum (fetched 2026-11-14)
- Texas Education Agency, "Biology STAAR Blueprint", effective beginning with the Spring 2026
  administration, updated fall 2025: https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/biology-blueprint-2026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-14; the older /student-assessment/staar/ path returns 404)
  - Strands: Biological Structures, Functions, and Processes 12-14 questions, 13-16 points; Mechanisms of
    Genetics 7-9 questions, 8-11 points; Biological Evolution 7-9 questions, 8-11 points; Interdependence
    within Environmental Systems 5-7 questions, 6-9 points. Total 33-35 questions, 40 points. Sub-scores
    are not reported for the strands.
  - 1-point questions (multiple choice and non-multiple choice) 26-30 questions, 26-30 points; 2-point
    questions (non-multiple choice) 5-7 questions, 10-14 points.
  - Question types: drag and drop, hot spot, inline choice, match table grid, multipart, multiple choice,
    multiselect, short constructed response (SCR), text entry, and cluster question sets. A cluster set is
    a stimulus (scenario with a graphic and/or data display) and 3-5 questions, each scored independently.
- Texas Education Agency, "Biology Assessment Eligible Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills", effective as
  of the 2025-2026 school year: https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/2026-staar-bio-assessed-curriculum.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Eligible student expectations, by strand. Structures, Functions, and Processes: B.5A (S), B.5B (R),
    B.5D (S), B.6A (S), B.6B (S), B.6C (R), B.11A (S), B.11B (R), B.12A (S), B.12B (R). Mechanisms of
    Genetics: B.7A (S), B.7B (S), B.7C (R), B.8A (S), B.8B (R). Biological Evolution: B.9A (S), B.9B (R),
    B.10A (S), B.10B (R), B.10C (R), B.10D (S). Interdependence: B.13A (S), B.13B (S), B.13C (S),
    B.13D (R). (R readiness, S supporting.)
  - Not eligible, so not written: B.5C (homeostasis through cellular transport) and B.7D (PCR, gel
    electrophoresis, genetic engineering).
- 19 Texas Administrative Code §112.42, Biology (One Credit), Adopted 2020 (effective April 28, 2021,
  46 TexReg 2729), Chapter 112 Subchapter C, August 2024 update:
  https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch112c.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Full text of the knowledge and skills statements 5 to 13 that the assessed curriculum lists; every item
    is written to one of the eligible student expectations above (the `skill` label names it).

## Format and scoring (fetched 2026-11-14)
- Texas Education Agency, "2026-2027 STAAR Test Administrator Manual":
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/test-administration/staar-test-administrator-manual.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Students are expected to complete assessments within three to four hours; they must finish within the
    same school day (unless approved for an extra day or a health-related interruption) and may test up to
    seven hours. Calculators are required for Biology (an embedded calculator tool online, or an allowed
    handheld calculator).
- Texas Education Agency, "Raw Score Conversion Table, Biology, Spring 2026":
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/student-assessment-overview/2026-staar-spring-eoc-biology-rsss.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - 40 raw points. Approaches Grade Level scale score 3550 (raw 10 on the Spring 2026 form), Meets 4000
    (raw 17), Masters 4570 (raw 26). The Approaches 2026-and-after standard comes from the Spring 2026
    standard setting. Raw cuts change by form, so the landing gives the scale scores and calls the raw
    figures "on the Spring 2026 form".
- Texas Education Agency, "House Bill 8: Student Assessment":
  https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/student-assessment-overview/house-bill-8-student-assessment
  (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Beginning in the 2027-28 school year STAAR is replaced by a new instructionally supportive program
    (which may be called the Student Success Tool); the page lists Biology SST blueprints. Recorded in
    lib/examFreshness.json; the bank follows the current STAAR blueprint.

## What is modelled and left out
- Two practice tests of 35 questions (the top of the 33-35 range), one point each, by strand 13/8/8/6
  (the middle of each range), untimed like the real test. Three cluster sets of 3 or 4 questions per test.
- Formats: multiple choice and multiselect only. Drag and drop, hot spot, inline choice, match table grid,
  text entry and short constructed responses are not modelled, and 2-point scoring is not modelled, so the
  score is percent correct, not a scale score.
- No item uses B.5C or B.7D (not assessed). No item asks about the test itself.
