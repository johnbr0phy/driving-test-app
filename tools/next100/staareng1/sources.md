# STAAR English I (`staareng1`): sources

Every question and every passage in this bank is original, written for Tigertest from the TEA
blueprint, test design and assessed curriculum below. No released STAAR test, released test
question, rationale, practice test, interim item or prep-site item was used or reworded. Reading
passages (fiction, poetry, drama, literary nonfiction, informational, argumentative and paired texts)
and the student drafts used for revising and editing were written for this bank; any person named in
them is invented. US spelling throughout.

## Blueprint and test design (owner: Texas Education Agency, Student Assessment Division)
- TEA, "STAAR English I Blueprint, Effective as of Spring 2023" and "STAAR English I Test Design"
  (August 2022): https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/staar-english-i-blueprint-schematic-pdf.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Reporting Category 1, Reading: 14 readiness and 15 supporting standards, 29 to 31 questions,
    32 to 34 points. Reporting Category 2, Writing: 7 readiness and 7 supporting standards, 21 to 23
    questions, 30 to 32 points.
  - Item types: 48 one-point questions (multiple-choice and non-multiple-choice), 3 two-point
    questions (non-multiple-choice), 1 extended constructed response worth 10 points; 52 questions,
    64 points in all. Readiness standards are about 55 to 75% of the points, supporting 25 to 45%.
  - Base test reading section: two single reading passages and one paired reading passage, 29 to 31
    items. Writing section: two revising passages, two editing passages and one extended constructed
    response (a written response to a single or paired reading passage), 21 to 23 items including the
    response. About 6,000 words of reading at most; a field-test section of 13 items does not count.
  - Eligible genres: fiction, drama, poetry, literary nonfiction; informational, argumentative,
    correspondence and persuasive, whose topics come from subject-area TEKS up to grade 8 (mostly
    social studies and science).
- TEA, "STAAR Redesign": https://tea.texas.gov/student-assessment/assessment-initiatives/staar-redesign (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Redesign in force from 2022-23: online delivery, new non-multiple-choice question types, a cap
    of 75% of points on multiple choice, cross-curricular informational passages, and an extended
    constructed response written from a reading passage in the informational, argumentative or
    correspondence mode.

## Eligible standards (TEKS)
- TEA, "English I Assessment: Eligible Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills", SST draft revised
  August 2026: https://tea2.tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/sst-english-i-assessed-curriculum.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Lists the eligible English I TEKS by reporting strand with readiness and supporting labels;
    reading (2)(A)-(C) vocabulary, (4)(C),(E)-(H) comprehension, (5)(C)-(D) response, (6)(A)-(D)
    literary elements, (7)(A)-(E) genres (poetry, drama, informational and argumentative structure),
    (8)(A)-(G) author's purpose and craft; writing (9)(B)(i)-(ii) and (9)(C) revising, (9)(D)(i)-(vi)
    editing (sentence boundaries, verb tense and voice, pronoun-antecedent agreement, capitalization,
    punctuation including commas, semicolons, colons and dashes, spelling). Genres for revising and
    editing: fiction, literary nonfiction, correspondence, informational, argumentative, persuasive.
  - Its "Key Updates" table says the label changes (2.B, 6.B, 8.A, 8.D, 8.F to supporting) take
    effect with the Student Success Tool in Fall 2027, so the standards assessed are the same and
    only readiness labels change. The 2023 STAAR assessed curriculum URL
    (https://tea.texas.gov/student-assessment/staar/2023-english-i-assessed-curriculum.pdf) returned
    404 on 2026-11-14; the bank's categories follow the strands, not the labels.
- 19 Texas Administrative Code Chapter 110, Subchapter C, §110.36, English Language Arts and
  Reading, English I (adopted 2017), SBOE text:
  https://sboe.texas.gov/sites/default/files/Ch%20110%20ELAR%20English%20I-IV%20Amended%200621.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - The primary text of the standards above.

## Scoring
- TEA, "State of Texas Assessments of Academic Readiness Raw Score Conversion Table, English I,
  Summer 2026": https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/student-assessment-overview/2026-staar-june-eoc-english-i-rsss.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Raw score out of 64. Approaches Grade Level scale score 3775 (27 raw points on that form),
    Meets 4000 (35), Masters 4606 (53). Raw cuts change from form to form; the scale cuts do not.

## What changes next
- TEA, "DRAFT SST RLA English 1 Summative Blueprint, effective beginning with the Spring 2028
  administration": https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/sst-english-i-summative-blueprint.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - The Student Success Tool replaces STAAR: reading 18 to 21 questions, writing 10 to 12, 30 to 31
    questions and 38 points, with one 6-point extended constructed response; question types extended
    constructed response, inline choice, multipart, multiple choice and multiselect. A draft; the
    landing mentions it as coming, and the bank models the STAAR blueprint in force for 2026-27.

## Not confirmed, and how the copy words it
- The time allowed: TEA's test administrator manuals set it and none was read for this build. The
  practice tests use Tigertest's own pace and the copy says so; no time limit is claimed for STAAR.

## Left out on purpose
- The extended constructed response (the essay, 10 of 64 points) and the two-point
  non-multiple-choice items scored by hand or by rubric: not modelled; the landing says so.
- Drag-and-drop and match-table items are modelled as multiple choice, inline choice (dropdowns),
  multiselect and hot text (select a sentence).
- No question about the test itself (counts, times, scores, retests).
