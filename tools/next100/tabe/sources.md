# TABE (`tabe`): sources

All 200 questions and all of their passages, drafts and data tables are original, written for Tigertest from the
TABE 13&14 Level D blueprints below. No TABE practice item, sample item, released form, TABE Edge item, locator item
or commercial prep material was used or paraphrased. DRC's TABE 13&14 practice items page was not opened.

## Test owner (checked 2026-11-14)
- TABE home page, Data Recognition Corporation (DRC): https://tabetest.com (fetched 2026-11-14). Lists TABE 13&14 as
  the current product, with its blueprints, practice items, testing times, FAQs and reference sheets.

## Content outline: TABE 13&14 Level D blueprints (fetched 2026-11-14)

(High, Medium, Low). Copyright 2026 DRC, revisions dated January and February 2026.

## Format (fetched 2026-11-14)
- DRC, "TABE 13&14 Maximum Allowable Testing Times" (TB_TstTms_121224):
  https://tabetest.com/PDFs/TABE_13-14_Test_Max_Test_Times.pdf
  Reading Level D 39 items, Part 1 50 minutes and Part 2 50 minutes; Mathematics Level D 40 items, Part 1 45 minutes
  and Part 2 15 minutes; Language Level D 33 items in 50 minutes. Same times at Levels E, M, D and A. Locator:
  Reading 16 items in 35 minutes, Language 16 in 20, Mathematics 16 in 10 + 10 minutes.
- DRC, "What's New with TABE 13&14?" (TB_WhtsNw_010825): https://tabetest.com/PDFs/Whats_New_w_TABE.pdf
  Reading has multiple-choice and evidence-based selected-response items; Mathematics has multiple-choice and
  gridded-response items; Language is multiple choice only; one answer document for Levels E, M, D and A; the online
  Math test uses the Desmos calculator (11&12 used the DRC INSIGHT calculator); shorter Reading passages.
- DRC, "TABE Frequently Asked Questions" (TB_FAQ_060525): https://tabetest.com/PDFs/TABE_13-14_FAQs.pdf
  The Locator decides the level per content area; any subtest can be given alone; all levels are on one scale;
  DRC does not set or recommend cut scores for screening; 60 to 80 hours of instruction suggested before retesting
  on the same form.
- DRC, TABE 13&14 brochure (TB_Brchr_091725): https://tabetest.com/PDFs/TABE_13-14_Brochure.pdf
  Reading, Mathematics and Language aligned to CCR standards; approved for NRS reporting by OCTAE; paper, large
  print, Braille, audio and online (DRC INSIGHT) delivery.
- DRC, TABE 13&14 Mathematics reference sheets: https://tabetest.com/PDFs/TABE_13-14_Online_Mathematics_Reference_Sheets.pdf
  Level D sheet: rectangle perimeter and area, circle circumference and area, triangle area, rectangular prism
  volume, the Pythagorean Theorem, 1 foot = 12 inches, 1 hour = 60 minutes, 1 minute = 60 seconds. The practice
  section's reference panel lists the same formulas in Tigertest's words.
- U.S. Department of Education (OCTAE), "Tests Determined To Be Suitable for Use in the National Reporting System
  for Adult Education", Federal Register notice 2025-07974, published 2025-05-08:
  https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2025-07974.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  TABE 11/12 (Forms 11 and 12, paper and computer) approved for Reading, Language and Mathematics at all ABE levels
  only during a sunset period ending June 30, 2026. So by the build date TABE 11&12 has sunset for NRS reporting and
  TABE 13&14 is the form in use; the practice test is written to the 13&14 blueprints and the landing says so.

## Could not be confirmed
- Which Mathematics part allows the calculator on TABE 13&14. DRC confirms the online Desmos calculator but the
  documents read do not say which part it is for. The copy says a calculator is provided online and that the
  program will say when it may be used; the practice items need no calculator.
- Score tables (scale scores, NRS educational functioning levels, grade equivalents). DRC publishes no
  raw-to-scale table here, so Tigertest reports percent correct only and never a scale score or NRS level.
- The exact split of the 39 Reading items between the two parts, and how many are evidence-based two-part items.

## Left out on purpose
- Level L, E, M and A content: the practice test is Level D (AE-CCR level D, about grades 6 to 8) only, labelled so.
- Writing extended responses: Language 13&14 is multiple choice only, so revision and organization items stand in
  for Text Types and Purposes.
- Gridded response is modelled as typed numeric answers; the two-part evidence items as pairs of single items on
  the same passage.
- Anything about the test itself (cut scores, retest rules, levels) is on the landing, never in a question.
