# TExES PPR EC-12 (160) bank sources

Checked 2026-11-02.

## Official

- Texas Educator Certification Examination Program (Pearson), test page for 160 Pedagogy and
  Professional Responsibilities EC-12:
  https://www.tx.nesinc.com/TestView.aspx?f=HTML_FRAG/TX160_TestPage.html
  Computer-administered test with 100 selected-response questions; 5 hours total appointment
  time (15-minute CAT tutorial and compliance agreement plus 4 hours 45 minutes of testing);
  passing score 240; by appointment year round at test centers in Texas and nationwide. No
  retirement notice on the page as of 2026-11-02.
- Study guide, Section 3: Overview and Exam Framework (160):
  https://www.tx.nesinc.com/Content/StudyGuide/TX_SG_obj_160.htm
  Some questions may be unscored. Domain I Designing Instruction and Assessment to Promote Student
  Learning 34% (competencies 001-004); Domain II Creating a Positive, Productive Classroom
  Environment 13% (005-006); Domain III Implementing Effective, Responsive Instruction and
  Assessment 33% (007-010); Domain IV Fulfilling Professional Roles and Responsibilities 20%
  (011-013). Competencies: 001 human development; 002 student diversity; 003 goals, objectives
  and coherent instruction and assessment; 004 learning processes and motivation; 005 classroom
  climate; 006 organization, routines and behavior; 007 communication; 008 active engagement;
  009 technology; 010 monitoring, feedback and flexible response; 011 families; 012 professional
  growth and the educational community; 013 legal and ethical requirements and the structure of
  education in Texas.
- TEA, Chapter 235 Classroom Teacher Pedagogy Standards EC-12 FAQ (updated May 2025):
  https://tea.texas.gov/texas-educators/preparation-and-continuing-education/program-provider-resources/ch-235c-ppr-ec-12-faq.pdf
  The new 19 TAC Chapter 235 Subchapter C standards (effective May 18, 2025) repeal and replace
  the grade-banded PPR standards; exams launched after that date, beginning with Core Subjects
  EC-6 and the Texas Test of Educator Proficiency (T-TEP), are informed by them. "edTPA remains an
  option alongside the PPR."
- TEA, Texas Test of Educator Proficiency page:
  https://tea.texas.gov/educators/certification/educator-testing/texas-test-educator-proficiency
  Timeline still reads "September 2027: T-TEP launches as the required pedagogy assessment
  (Pending SBEC Action)".
- TEA Educator Preparation Programs newsletters, April 29, 2026
  (https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXTEA/bulletins/414bcdd) and July 15, 2026
  (https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXTEA/bulletins/41fa4b9): after the April 24, 2026
  SBEC meeting, "Revised launch date of 2029 for the Texas Test of Educator Proficiency (T-TEP),
  pending SBEC action". The landing says T-TEP is planned to replace the PPR, its date has moved
  (TEA's newsletters list 2029), and the PPR is offered until then.
- Rule history: a 2023-24 Texas Register filing of the 19 TAC 230.21 figure
  (https://regulations.justia.com/resources/v2024100900/tx/admin/2024/202304859_2.pdf) listed
  "160 PPR EC-12 TExES (last operational date 8/31/2026)". That date was tied to the Texas-specific
  performance assessment that later failed; the testing program still offers 160 in November
  2026 with no end date posted. Not stated on the landing as a date; the landing tells readers to
  check TEA.

## Content sources for the bank (rules a teacher lives by)

- Texas Family Code section 261.101: a professional must report suspected abuse or neglect no
  later than the 48th hour after first suspecting it, may not delegate the report, and reports to
  law enforcement or DFPS. https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/FA/htm/FA.261.htm
- Educators' Code of Ethics, 19 TAC Chapter 247 (section 247.2): no falsifying records, no gifts
  or favors that impair professional judgment, confidentiality of student and colleague
  information, no inappropriate electronic communication with students.
- 19 TAC Chapter 74 subchapter A (section 74.4), English Language Proficiency Standards, for all
  teachers of English learners; 19 TAC Chapter 89 (LPAC; ARD committee).
- Texas Education Code 11.251 (campus-level planning and decision-making committee); TEC Chapter 8
  (20 regional education service centers); SBOE adopts the TEKS (TEC 28.002); SBEC governs
  certification (TEC Chapter 21).
- Federal: IDEA (least restrictive environment), Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act, FERPA,
  17 U.S.C. 107 (fair use factors).
- T-TESS, the state's recommended teacher appraisal system (TEA).
