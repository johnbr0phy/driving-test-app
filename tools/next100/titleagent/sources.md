# Title insurance agent exam bank sources

All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the state outlines, policy forms and federal law
below. None is copied from a state or vendor sample question, a pre-licensing course or any commercial
prep product.

There is no national title agent exam. Each state insurance department sets its own, usually through
PSI, Pearson VUE or Prometric. The bank covers the general title insurance knowledge the three outlines
below share, the way the real estate bank covers the national portion only. Every state-law topic in
them (licensing, appointment, continuing education, rate filing, trust account and unfair trade practice
statutes) is left out or worded as "varies by state".

## State outlines and format (exam owners)

- Alabama Department of Insurance, "Title Insurance Agent Examination Content Outline" (revised
  7/24/2023 for examinations beginning January 1, 2024), https://aldoi.gov/PDF/Licensing/2023TitleOutline.pdf ,
  linked from https://aldoi.gov/Licensing/ExamOutlines.aspx , fetched 2026-11-19. Four parts:
  Regulatory Compliance 50 (Alabama Title Insurance Act 10, ALDOI regulation 8, GAO report 2, CFPB 3,
  RESPA 5, affiliated business arrangements 3, HUD SOP 1996-4 2, Gramm-Leach-Bliley 9, rules of agency
  5, ethics 3); Industry Information 40 (history 1, title insurance 101 and forms 2, ALTA 2021
  commitment 7, ALTA 2021 owner's policy 20, ALTA 2021 loan policy 10); Title Search and Examination 35
  (search 10, source of title 10, liens and encumbrances 10, surveys and legal descriptions 5);
  Settlement / Escrow 25 (settlement process 8, contracts 4, TRID 4, UPL 4, cybersecurity 5). 150
  questions, 105 correct to pass, 180 minutes.
- PSI, "Ohio Title Insurance Agent, Series 11-37" content outline, in the Ohio Department of Insurance
  candidate handbook updated 8/1/2023,
  https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/insurance.ohio.gov/Agent/Documents/PSI%20Candiate%20Handbook%20updated%208.1.23%5B51%5D.pdf ,
  fetched 2026-11-19. 100 questions, 2 hours. Insurance regulation 10%, general insurance 10%, real
  property 35%, title insurance 20%, title exceptions and procedures for clearing title 25%. The handbook
  states "The minimum passing score is 70%."
- PSI, "Pennsylvania Producer's Examination for Title Insurance, Series 16-10" outline effective
  3/1/2026, https://proctor2.psionline.com/media/programs/PAINS/16-10xx.pdf , linked from the
  Pennsylvania Insurance Department candidate booklet
  https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/7286 , both fetched 2026-11-19. 60 items, 80
  minutes. Insurance regulation 12%, general insurance concepts 3%, real property 10%, title insurance
  23%, title exceptions and clearing title 21%, real estate transactions 31%. The booklet adds 5 to 10
  unscored experimental items. It does not print a passing score.

## Federal law (primary)

- 12 CFR 1024.14 (RESPA Section 8), https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/14/ ,
  fetched 2026-11-19: kickbacks, thing of value, unearned fees, permitted payments in (g).
- 12 CFR 1024.15 (affiliated business arrangements), https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/15/ ,

  appraiser exception, return on ownership only.
- 12 U.S.C. 2608 (RESPA Section 9), https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/2608 , fetched
  2026-11-19: seller may not require a title insurer; liability three times the title charges.

  fetched 2026-11-19: lender's title premium at the full rate, owner's premium calculation with a

## Not confirmed or left out

- Real property, deed, estate, recording-statute and lien basics (fixtures, freehold estates, joint
  tenancy unities, warranty and quitclaim deeds, race-notice and notice statutes, inquiry notice,
  rectangular survey sizes, lis pendens, easement types) are the standard common-law topics the Ohio and
  Pennsylvania outlines name. Where a rule differs between states (tenancy by the entirety, dower and
  homestead, redemption periods, judgment lien terms, tax lien priority, good funds, what a closer may
  prepare) the item says it varies by state.
- Wire fraud items follow the cybersecurity topics in the Alabama outline in general terms (verify by a
  known phone number); no agency's advisory text is quoted.
- ALTA Best Practices 5.0 (10-08-2026) is members only, so its pillars are not asked.
- Left out on purpose: every state's licensing, appointment, CE, fee, rate filing, trust account, CPL
  statute and penalty rules; dollar limits; the FinCEN residential real estate reporting rule (its
  effective date was changed); the 2007 GAO report and HUD SOP 1996-4 details.
- Kept clear of the real-estate, life-health, property-casualty and NMLS banks: nine items that repeated
  a fact and answer from those banks (acreage, TRID timing, special warranty deed, sufferance, equitable
  title, reinsurance, express and apparent authority, prepayment penalty) were replaced.
