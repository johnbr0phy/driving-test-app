# TOEFL iBT Reading (`toeflread`): sources

All questions and all passages are original. Every Complete the Words paragraph, daily-life text
(notices, emails, text messages, schedules, menus, posts) and academic passage was written for
Tigertest on a general-knowledge topic in the style ETS describes, and every question is answered
from its text alone. The organisations, people, shops and courses in the daily-life texts are
invented. No ETS sample, practice test, lesson-plan text, TOEFL Practice Online item, official guide
item or commercial prep item was used or paraphrased; the ETS sample tasks quoted in the overview were
read only to see the task format and none of their texts, topics or words are reused.
American spelling, as on the TOEFL iBT.

## Format and task types (owner: ETS)
- ETS, "TOEFL iBT Test: 2026 Update Test Blueprint and Specifications Document":
  https://www.eu.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-specifications-2026.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Reading: 50 items, 35 raw points (items include unscored pretest items), machine scored, max 1
    point per scored item. Router stage 18 to 21 minutes, then a lower or upper module of about
    9 minutes ("two-stage adaptive"); total estimated time is the router plus one module.
  - Task types and target items: Complete the Words 30 (B1 to C1+, academic language use: "use
    word-ordering rules, lexical knowledge, and reading comprehension to construct meaningful text");
    Read in Daily Life 5 to 15 (A1 to C1, short non-academic texts, 2-item and 3-item sets);
    Read an Academic Passage 5 to 15 (B1 to C2: main ideas, key details, inferred meanings, idea
    relationships, rhetorical structures).
  - Texts range from 15 to 50 words (easier) up to about 200 words; all texts stay visible while the
    questions are answered; stimuli include signs, menus, emails, social media posts and extended
    academic passages.
- ETS, "Updated TOEFL iBT Test Overview" (Korea site copy of the same PDF):
  https://www.kr.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-overview.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Reading is first, then Listening, Writing, Speaking. Reading (multistage): Complete the Words,
    Read in Daily Life, Read an Academic Passage; 50 items; approx. base time 30 minutes.
  - Complete the Words: paragraph-length (about 70 to 100 words) academic texts; following an
    intact first sentence, the second half of every second word is deleted and the test taker
    supplies the missing letters; 10 words with missing letters per text.
  - Read in Daily Life: short non-academic texts (poster, sign or notice, menu, social media post or
    web page, schedule, email, chain of text messages, advertisement, news article, form, invoice,
    receipt), about 15 to 150 words, followed by two or three multiple-choice questions; questions
    on non-linear formats, main purpose, informal and idiomatic language, inference, telegraphic
    language, skimming and scanning.
  - Read an Academic Passage: short expository passages from history, art and music, business and
    economics, life science, physical science and social science, about 200 words, no background
    knowledge needed, followed by five questions on factual information, vocabulary in context,
    inferences, the relationship between ideas, and the purpose of all or part of the text.
- ETS, "TOEFL iBT Reading Section":
  https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content/reading.html (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - The three task types and what each measures; all information needed is in the text; no prior
    knowledge of the topic is needed.
- ETS, "TOEFL iBT Test Content":
  https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content.html (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Reading 50 items, approx. base time 30 minutes; "As the test adapts, test time and items may
    vary"; four section scores and an overall score on the 1 to 6 scale; overall is the average
    of the four rounded to the nearest half band; a comparable 0 to 120 score for a two-year
    transition period after January 2026.
- ETS, "Understanding Your TOEFL iBT Scores":
  https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - From January 21, 2026, score reports use the 1 to 6 scale in half-point steps, aligned to the
    CEFR. Comparison table of the old 0 to 30 Reading score to the 1 to 6 band (for tests taken
    before January 21, 2026). Unofficial Reading and Listening scores are shown at the end of the
    test. No table from raw Reading points to the 1 to 6 band is published.
- ETS, "Teacher Frequently Asked Questions for TOEFL iBT Enhancements":
  https://www.eu.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/teacher-faq.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Reading and Listening are multistage adaptive: one module, then a second module matched to the
    test taker's level; no skipping or going back between stages; the score counts the questions
    answered correctly in both stages and their difficulty, converted by IRT equating.
- ETS, "TOEFL iBT Lesson Plans: Reading":
  https://www.eu.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-lesson-plan-reading.pdf (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Complete the Words: 10 words, a short paragraph. Read in Daily Life: up to 3 questions on a 15 to
    150 word text. Read an Academic Passage: up to 5 questions on about 200 words, expository text
    typical of secondary or higher education. Key skills: main ideas, scanning for details, word
    meaning from context.
- ETS blog, "Inside the TOEFL iBT Updates: Validity by Design":
  https://www.ets.org/toefl/blog/ibt-validity-by-design.html (fetched 2026-11-14)
  - Complete the Words is a C-test: the second half of every second word is deleted and students
    fill in the missing letters to recreate the original words.

## How Tigertest models it
- Two practice tests of 50 items in 30 minutes: a router module of 35 items in 21 minutes (two
  Complete the Words paragraphs, four daily-life sets of 3, 3, 2 and 2 questions, one academic
  passage) and a second module of 15 items in 9 minutes (one Complete the Words paragraph and one
  academic passage), easier or harder by router accuracy (60%). ETS gives the totals per task and
  per stage time, not the split of tasks between stages, so that split is Tigertest's.
- Complete the Words is shown as ETS describes (intact first sentence, then the second half of every
  second word removed, the gap marked with one underscore per missing letter; one-letter words and
  numbers are skipped), but each gap is answered by choosing the whole word from four words of the
  same length that start with the same letters. On the real test you type the letters.
- Every item is scored; the real section includes unscored pretest items and counts difficulty.
  ETS publishes no raw-to-band table for the 1 to 6 scale, so the score is percent correct, and an
  easier second module caps the estimate (Tigertest's own rule, like an adaptive test that weights
  harder items).

## Left out
- Listening, Writing and Speaking; the 0 to 120 comparable score; band predictions.
- Typed answers for Complete the Words (choices instead).
