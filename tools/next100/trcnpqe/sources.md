## Official (Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria)

- TRCN, PQE Curriculum Guide, https://trcn.gov.ng/pqe-curriculum (page and its script bundle
  `/_next/static/chunks/app/pqe-curriculum/page-3e9710422d5c2009.js`, read directly).
  - Format panel: Duration 60 min; Questions 60; Format Computer-based; Schedule May & Nov.
    "Exams are conducted in batches in the May and November diets"; candidates get date and venue
    by SMS and email at least two weeks before.
  - Eligibility: minimum of NCE, PGDE or PDE in Education.
  - "Five core areas of the streamlined PQE syllabus", each 20%: Pedagogy, Literacy, Numeracy,
    ICT, Safeguarding.
  - Pedagogy subtopics: Foundations of Learning 12% (developmental stages, factors affecting
    learning, contextualisation, social reconstructionism, SEL; National Policy on Education
    (2014), National Teacher Policy 2025, TRCN Act 31 of 1993 (CAP T3 of 2004), foundations,
    psychology and philosophy of learning); Instructional Planning 20% (curriculum design, Bloom's
    taxonomy and SMART objectives, scheme of work and lesson plan, selecting resources; NERDC
    curriculum); Teaching Methodologies 20% (scaffolding, teacher- vs learner-centred,
    cooperative and inquiry learning, question types, feedback, multigrade teaching, gradual
    release); Classroom Management 16% (rules and routines, non-verbal communication and
    proximity control, conflict resolution, Positive Behaviour Support, record keeping);
    Differentiated Instruction 16% (content, process, product; learning styles; inclusive
    education).
  - Literacy: Reading Comprehension 30% (texts incl. memos, circulars, charts, timetables,
    figurative language), Grammar and Structure 25% (parts of speech, subject-verb agreement,
    question forms, pronouns, active/passive, modals, sentence types, "common Nigerian English
    interference patterns"), Vocabulary 20% (morphology), Writing Skills 10% (writing process,
    paragraphs, punctuation, reports), Professional Communication Skills 15% (instructions,
    emails, memos, parent messages, register).
  - Numeracy: Core Mathematical Concepts (Arithmetic) 26% (place value, number bases, properties
    of operations, HCF/LCM, squares and roots, fractions/decimals/percentages, rates and
    proportion); Geometry and Measurement 13%; Data Handling and Algebra 13% (charts, mean,
    median, mode, probability, patterns, linear, simultaneous and quadratic equations); Teaching
    Strategies for Numeracy (CRA sequence, model drawing for word problems, growth mindset,
    diagnosing errors; no weight shown).
  - ICT: Core Digital Competencies 35% (OS, cloud and file management, search, email and video
    conferencing, collaborative tools, credibility; National Policy on ICT in Education 2019);
    Leveraging Mobile Technology 25% (apps, blended and flipped models, troubleshooting, mobile
    learning); ICT and AI Integration in Teaching 20% (LMS such as Moodle or Google Classroom,
    multimedia design, ICT for assessment, blended and remote learning); Digital Citizenship and
    Safety 20% (cyberbullying, online predators, copyright and plagiarism, data protection,
    digital footprint).
  - Safeguarding: Foundational Principles and Policies 26.3% (child protection vs safeguarding;
    Child Rights Act 2003; National Policy on Inclusive Education 2023; National Policy on
    Safety, Security and Violence-Free Schools; Minimum Standards for Safe Schools); Recognising
    Signs of Abuse and Reporting Mechanisms 31.6% (types and indicators of abuse, online abuse,
    confidentiality, do no harm, informed consent, referral, documentation); Prevention and
    Awareness 26.3% (safe school culture, personal safety curriculum, staff training,
    safeguarding, evacuation and risk assessment plan, school-community collaboration);
    Professional Ethics and Conduct 15.7% (TRCN Code of Conduct, physical and digital boundaries,
    investigation of misconduct).

## Laws and policies named in items (static facts only)

- TRCN Act No. 31 of 1993 (CAP T3, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 2004): creates TRCN. Text
  checked 2026-11-01 at https://placng.org/lawsofnigeria/print.php?sn=506 (1993 No. 31, dated
  4 May 1993): s. 9(1) establishes "a tribunal to be known as the Teachers Disciplinary
  Committee" which determines cases referred by the Teachers Investigating Panel; s. 9(3) sets up
  a State Teachers Investigating Panel in each state and one for the FCT; s. 10 lets the
  Committee reprimand or order the name struck off the register, appeal to the Court of Appeal.
  TRCN's curriculum guide calls the Committee the "Teachers Tribunal"; items use the statutory
  name and mention the common one. The Act itself sets no degree: s. 5 requires a qualifying
  examination accepted by the Council.
- Universal Basic Education Act 2004: free, compulsory nine-year basic education (six years
  primary, three years junior secondary); establishes the Universal Basic Education Commission
  (UBEC).
- National Policy on Education (2014): NCE as the minimum teaching qualification.
- Child Rights Act 2003: a child is a person under 18.
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
- NERDC develops the national curricula.
