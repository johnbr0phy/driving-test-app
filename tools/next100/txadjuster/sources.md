# Sources for `txadjuster` (Texas All-Lines Adjuster license exam, Pearson VUE)

All 200 questions are original, written from the Pearson VUE content outline, the standard
insurance forms it names (the 165-line Standard Fire Policy, ISO homeowners, dwelling, personal
auto, business auto, commercial property, BOP and crime forms) and the Texas statutes and rules it
cites. No Pearson VUE sample, past item or commercial prep question was copied, and no prep
provider is named. Checked 2026-11-03. Statute and PDF text was downloaded and read, not taken
from guides.

## Exam format (official)
- Pearson VUE, Texas Insurance Content Outlines, publication #124401, "Content Outlines:
  Effective September 1, 2026":
  https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/124401.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-03). "ALL LINES ADJUSTER CONTENT OUTLINE (150 scoreable questions)", no
  pretest count in the heading (the handbook says that means no pretest items):
  - I. General property and casualty insurance product knowledge pertinent to adjusters 40%:
    A Standard Fire Policy (coverages, limitations, proof of loss, loss requirements and
    inventories, appraisal, replacement costs); B Auto liability (personal, commercial);
    C Personal lines homeowner coverage (dwelling and contents, HO forms and coverages, general
    property forms, ISO HO-2 to HO-6); D Commercial lines (commercial property and causes of loss,
    CPP, boiler and machinery, BOP, cargo, commercial crime); E Inland marine (personal,
    commercial and installation floaters); F Ocean marine (P&I, running down clause, average,
    general and particular average); G Additional coverages (business interruption, time element,
    law and ordinance, valuable papers, garagekeepers, aviation, umbrella, yacht/boatowners);
    H Bonds and professional liability (E&O).
  - II. Insurance terms and related concepts 40%: peril, waiver/non-waiver, insurable interest,
    estoppel, proof of loss, depreciation, deductible, absolute/strict and vicarious liability,
    valued policy, appraisal, ACV and fair market value, robbery, burglary and theft, agreed value,
    replacement cost, indemnity, damages, subrogation, other insurance (primary/excess, pro rata),
    arbitration, constructive total loss, loss, warranties and representations, salvage, risk,
    hazard, liberalization, binder, reservation of rights, policy provisions and contract law
    (declarations, insuring agreement, conditions, exclusions, appraisal, definition of insured,
    duties, insurer obligations, mortgagee rights), proximate cause, coinsurance, occurrence,
    cancellation, nonrenewal, vacancy and unoccupancy, negligence, endorsements,
    admitted/nonadmitted.
  - III. Texas statutes and rules pertinent to P&C adjusting 20%: A licensing (TIC 4101.001,
    .051-.054, .057, .101; DHS adjuster; exemptions 4101.002; CE 4101.059 and TAC 19.1001-.1018;
    denial, renewal, expiration 4101.061; termination 4101.202-.203; notification 4001.252,
    TAC 1.502, TIC 4005.101-.102; Commissioner powers, records, hearings, penalties, cease and
    desist 83.051-.054); B marketing practices (TIC 542.151-.154, claims practices 542.001-.014
    and 542.051-.061, TAC 21.201-.205, misrepresentation TAC 21.4, defamation 541.053);
    C adjuster functions and prohibited conduct (4101.001-.002, 4102.163); D workers'
    compensation (Labor Code definitions, who is covered, employer election, average weekly wage,
    waiting period, compensable injuries, benefits, Subsequent Injury Fund).
- Pearson VUE, Texas Insurance Licensing Candidate Handbook, publication #124400 (October 2024),
  https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/124400.pdf
  (checked 2026-11-03): exam table lists "Adjuster - All Lines - English" (InsTX-ALAdj16) and
  "Adjuster - All Lines - Spanish" (InsTX-ALAdj36), 150 minutes each (fee listed, not used).
  Scores are scaled 0 to 100 with 70 passing; the scaled score is not a percent correct. No
  penalty for wrong answers. A candidate who fails can schedule again within one day, with no
  limit on attempts. A completed license application must reach TDI within 12 months of
  passing. Exemptions: CPCU or AIC designation, or a TDI-approved adjuster course completed
  within 12 months before applying.
- Pearson VUE Texas insurance page: https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/tx/insurance.html
  (checked 2026-11-03), source of the two PDFs above and the Spanish outline (#124402).
- TDI, "Adjuster: all lines": https://www.tdi.texas.gov/agent/adjuster-all-lines-apply.html
  (checked 2026-11-03): exam by Pearson VUE, ESL (Spanish) request form, exemptions (approved
  course in the last 12 months, CPCU or AIC, reciprocal nonresident), apply through Sircon within
  one year of passing.

Exam format facts are used only on the landing, FAQ and `lib/examFacts.ts`, never in the bank.
The pass line in the app is 70 percent of 50, the house convention for Texas's scaled 70.
Third-party guides that call the 70 a raw 70 percent (105 of 150) are wrong per the handbook.

## General forms (standard texts)
- 165-line Standard Fire Policy (1943 New York form): 60-day proof of loss, inventory, appraisal
  (20 days, umpire 15 days, court of record, award by two, costs shared), option to repair on 30
  days' notice, payment 60 days after proof, theft excluded, explosion/riot unless fire ensues,
  increase of hazard and 60-day vacancy suspension, neglect, removal five days, no ordinance
  allowance, examination under oath, mortgagee 10 days' cancellation notice and 60-day proof.
- ISO HO 00 03 / HO 00 05 / HO 00 04 / HO 00 06, DP forms, PAP, CA 00 01, CP 00 10, BP 00 03,
  crime forms, common policy conditions, MCS-90.
