# UGC-NET Paper 1: sources

All checked 1 November 2026.

## Official

- **NTA, UGC-NET December 2026 Information Bulletin** (revised edition of 16 October 2026, official PDF
  https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s301eee509ee2f68dc6014898c309e86bf/uploads/2026/10/202610161905649093.pdf,
  linked from https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/; read in full as text on 1 November 2026). Confirms every pattern
  fact below unchanged from December 2025: CBT only (3.1); two sections in one sitting, no break, Paper I 50
  questions for 100 marks and Paper II 100 for 200, 3 hours (180 minutes), all compulsory (3.2); English and
  Hindi, English version final (3.4); 2 marks per question, no negative marking (3.5); 6% slots, 40% / 35%
  aggregate of both papers to be considered (11.3); three categories, 70% NET weightage for PhD admission,
  one-year validity for categories 2 and 3. Dates: registration 8 to 28 October 2026 (extended to 31
  October, fee to 1 November by public notice of 01.11.2026), city intimation 23 November, admit card 7
  December, exam 14 to 18 December and 21 December 2026. Syllabus: the bulletin points to
  https://www.ugcnetonline.in/syllabus-new.php (unchanged Paper I syllabus below).
- **NTA, UGC-NET December 2025 Information Bulletin** (Public Notice and bulletin of 7 October 2025; copy of
  the NTA PDF hosted at
  https://www.adda247.com/jobs/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2025/10/07182912/UGC-NET-December-2025-Notification-PDF-1.pdf,
  read in full as text; the NTA site itself is https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in). Used for:
  - Mode: Computer Based Test only (section 2.1).
  - Pattern (section 2.2): two sections in one sitting, all objective multiple-choice, no break. Paper I: 50
    questions, 100 marks, assesses "teaching/research aptitude ... reasoning ability, reading comprehension,
    divergent thinking and general awareness". Paper II: 100 questions, 200 marks, on the chosen subject. Total
    duration 3 hours (180 minutes) without any break; all questions compulsory.
  - Medium (section 2.4): English and Hindi only, except language papers; the English version is final.
  - Marking (section 2.5): 2 marks per question, 2 marks for each correct response, no negative marking, no
    marks for unanswered or marked-for-review questions.
  - Three categories (section 1.3): (1) JRF and Assistant Professor, (2) Assistant Professor and admission to
    PhD, (3) admission to PhD only; for categories 2 and 3 NET marks carry 70% weightage in PhD admission
    and are valid for one year. Result in percentile with normalised marks for multi-shift papers.
  - Result procedure (section 6.3): slots equal to 6% of candidates who appeared in both papers, allocated by
    reservation; to be considered a candidate must have appeared in both papers and secured at least 40%
    aggregate in both papers together (General/EWS) or 35% (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, PwD/PwBD, third gender);
    subject-wise and category-wise cut-offs follow.
  - Eligibility: master's degree with 55% (50% for reserved categories); four-year undergraduates with 75%
    may apply (not used in the questions).
- **UGC NET Bureau, Paper I syllabus, "General Paper on Teaching & Research Aptitude", Code 00**
  (https://www.ugcnetonline.in/NTA_All_R_Syllabus/00-Paper-I/Paper-I_English.pdf, linked from
  https://www.ugcnetonline.in/syllabus-new.php). Ten units: I Teaching Aptitude, II Research Aptitude, III
  Comprehension ("A passage of text be given"), IV Communication, V Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude, VI
  Logical Reasoning (including Indian logic: pramanas, anumana, vyapti, hetvabhasas), VII Data
  Interpretation, VIII ICT, IX People, Development and Environment (EPA 1986, NAPCC, Montreal, Rio, CBD,
  Kyoto, Paris, ISA), X Higher Education System. Note (i): "Five questions each carrying 2 marks are to be
  set from each Module." This sets the 5-per-unit blueprint used for both practice tests.

## Facts used in questions (stable, checked)

- UGC (Promotion of Academic Integrity and Prevention of Plagiarism in Higher Educational Institutions)
  Regulations, 2018: similarity up to 10% is Level 0 (minor, no penalty).
- Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016: sharps in white translucent puncture-proof containers; yellow,
  red and blue categories as stated in the explanation.
- Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000: silence zone of not less than 100 metres around
  hospitals, educational institutions and courts.
- International Solar Alliance: launched by India and France at COP21, Paris, 2015; headquarters Gurugram.
- NAPCC (2008): eight national missions. SDGs: 17 goals, 169 targets (2015). MDGs: 8 (2000 to 2015).
- Cartagena Protocol (biosafety, 2000), Nagoya Protocol (access and benefit-sharing, 2010), Sendai
  Framework (disaster risk reduction 2015 to 2030), Rio 1992 (Agenda 21).
- UGC set up 1953, statutory under the UGC Act 1956; Section 3 (deemed universities), 2(f), 12B. NAAC 1994,
  Bengaluru. AICTE statutory 1987. IGNOU 1985. RUSA 2013. NIRF 2015. NSQF 10 levels. NPE 1986 modified 1992.
  NEP 2020: GER 50% by 2035; HECI with four verticals (NHERC, NAC, HEGC, GEC); exits with certificate,
  diploma, degree, degree with research. M. C. Mehta v. Union of India (1991) on environmental education.
- Shodhganga (INFLIBNET, theses), e-PG Pathshala (INFLIBNET, PG e-content), NDLI (IIT Kharagpur), NPTEL
  (IITs and IISc), SWAYAM four quadrants, SWAYAM Prabha DTH channels, National Academic Depository via
  DigiLocker, Academic Bank of Credits.
