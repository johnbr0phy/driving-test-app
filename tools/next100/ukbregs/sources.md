# ukbregs sources (fetched 2026-11-12)

All 200 questions are original, written from the Building Regulations 2010 and the Approved Documents
listed below. No sample questions, past papers, course quizzes or commercial question banks were
used. No British Standard text was read or quoted: where an Approved Document names a standard
(BS 5839-6, BS EN 14604, BS EN 50291-1, PAS 24, CIBSE TM59), the question asks only what the
Approved Document itself says about it.

## The "exam"
There is no single official Building Regulations exam. This is a "know the regs" test for builders,
site managers, students on construction and building control courses, and building control trainees.
The 70% pass mark is Tigertest's readiness line, and the category weights are Tigertest's split by
practical importance on site (fire, structure and Part L weighted up; Parts D, P, Q, R, S and
procedure lightly). Building inspector law, the Building Safety Regulator, gateways and the RBI code
are left to `ukrbi`; BS 7671 detail to `uk18ed`; unvented hot water (G3) to `ukg3`; Water Fittings
Regulations to `ukwater`; gas safety law to `ukgas`.

## The law
- The Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), Schedule 1, legislation.gov.uk:
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/schedule/1 (fetched 2026-11-12). Text of requirements A1 to T1.
- Regulations 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 38: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/4 (and /3, /5, /7, /9, /38),
  fetched 2026-11-12. Building work, material alteration, "no more unsatisfactory", material change of use,
  materials and workmanship, the 18m external wall rule and its exemptions, fire safety information.
- Schedule 2 (exempt buildings): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/schedule/2 (fetched 2026-11-12).
  Class 6 small detached buildings (15m² any; 30m² if 1m from boundary or non-combustible), Class 7 extensions.

## Could not confirm, and how the copy words it
- The gov.uk collection page lists AD Q as dated 22 November 2024, but the only PDF on its page is the 2015 edition;
  the bank uses that text. AD T's listed date (3 November 2025) is the date of the revised PDF.
- AD L and AD F 2026 editions take effect 24 March 2027. Until then the 2021 editions apply; the facts asked are the
  same in both, except the Future Homes Standard date item, which is stated as a commencement date.

## Left out on purpose
- Money: the £2,000 gigabit cost cap and the £3,600 EV cost cap, fees, fines.
- Part G3 (unvented hot water, the 48°C bath limit), Water Fittings Regulations, gas safety law, BS 7671 detail,
  building control procedure, competence and the higher-risk regime.
- Figures due to change with the Future Homes Standard that differ between editions (notional dwelling
  specification, primary energy factors).
