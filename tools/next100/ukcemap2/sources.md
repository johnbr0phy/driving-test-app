# Sources for `ukcemap2` (CeMAP Module 2: MORT, units MRT1 and MRT2)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from LIBF's published unit syllabuses and from
UK law, FCA rules and official guidance as in force in November 2026. No LIBF study text, sample
exam, past paper or commercial question bank was read or copied. Checked 2026-11-19.

## Outline and format (official)
- LIBF CeMAP qualification specification, published April 2026 (Walbrook Institute London, formerly LIBF):
  https://www.walbrook.ac.uk/hubfs/files/documents/pe/cemap-qualification-specification-march-2026.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-19). CeMAP is five mandatory units. Module 2 is MORT, two units:
  - MORT: Mortgage Law, Practice and Application (MRT1): 1-hour exam of 50 standalone MCQs (50
    marks); pass 35/50 (70%), merit 40/50, distinction 45/50. Learning outcomes LO1 regulatory and
    legal definitions of mortgages and regulated second charge loans; LO2 buying at auction; LO3
    types of borrower and who cannot borrow; LO4 MCOB duties of lender and adviser; LO5 property
    market, interest rates, lending sector, buy-to-let and consumer buy-to-let, MCOB; LO6
    affordability, suitability, risk, term, ethical advice, plausibility checks; LO7 additional
    security, higher lending charges, guarantors and surety; LO8 fees and charges; LO9 property
    defects and new-home guarantee schemes; LO10 factors affecting value (tenure, easements,
    covenants, insurability, flooding, subsidence and heave, listed buildings); LO11 valuations and
    RICS Home Survey reports.
  - MORT: Mortgage Products and Post Completion (MRT2): 1-hour exam of 40 standalone MCQs (40
    marks); pass 28/40 (70%), merit 32/40, distinction 36/40. LO1 raising additional money (further
    advances, remortgages, second charges, bridging, lifetime mortgage and home reversion drawdown);
    LO2 transferring and amending mortgages (new lender, moves, conversions, adding or removing a
    party, redemption, overpayments, porting); LO3 debt consolidation; LO4 non-payment and arrears;
    LO5 lenders' remedies, subrogation, MCOB on default; LO6 repayment methods, rate types, product
    types, government schemes, shared ownership and equity share, Islamic home finance.
  - Exams on Brightspace with remote invigilation, any time, no booking; 18-month registration (12
    per module); at most three attempts a week and ten in 12 months per unit.
  - LIBF publishes no weighting by learning outcome. Each 50-question mock here is 28 MRT1 + 22
    MRT2 (the 50:40 ratio of the two real units), split inside each unit by the breadth of each LO.
    This split is Tigertest's.
- Walbrook CeMAP course page:
  https://www.walbrook.ac.uk/libf/professional-qualifications/financial-and-mortgage-advice/certificate-in-mortgage-advice-and-practice-cemap/
  (fetched 2026-11-19).

## Law, rules and official guidance used in items (as in force November 2026)
- FCA Handbook, MCOB, read section by section on https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/mcob13/mcob13s3
  and the other MCOB sections below (fetched 2026-11-19; the site renders by script, so it was read in
  a browser): MCOB 4.4A (initial disclosure: product range, remuneration, alternative finance; list of
  lenders within five business days); 4.7A (suitability factors, no 'least worst' product, cheaper
  product explanation, interest-only, retirement interest-only, bridging, shared equity, debt
  consolidation, 4.7A.19 and 4.7A.21); 4.8A (execution-only: right to buy, debt consolidation and
  shared equity barred; HNW and no-extra-borrowing variation exceptions; porting); 6A.3 (binding
  offer conditions, reflection period, draft agreement); 11.6 (affordability: net income,
  committed, essential and quality-of-living spending, no equity or price-rise basis, no
  self-certification, retirement, 1% and five-year rate stress, Bank of England forward rates, FPC,
  second charge stress, credit-impaired consolidation, interest-only strategies, bridging, roll-up
  exemption, annual review); 11.9 (modified affordability for switching with no extra borrowing);
  12.3 and 12.4 (early repayment and shortfall charges, Rule of 78, payment allocation); 13.3 to 13.6
  (arrears policy, two direct debit attempts, options to consider, debt advice, 15 business days,
  MoneyHelper sheet, quarterly statements, 8am to 9pm, local authority housing, repossession sale,
  shortfall notice six years or five in Scotland, surplus, expired-term customers).
- Regulated Activities Order 2001 arts 61, 61A and 63B (regulated mortgage contract, related
  persons, exclusions, administering, home reversion plans):
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/544/article/61 (fetched 2026-11-19).
- Mortgage Credit Directive Order 2015 art 4 (buy-to-let and consumer buy-to-let definitions):
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/910/article/4 (fetched 2026-11-19).
- Law of Property Act 1925 ss1, 87, 101, 103, 105, 109, 136 and Land Registration Act 2002 ss27,
  48, 49 (legal estates, minors, charge by way of legal mortgage, power of sale, receivers,
  proceeds, assignment, registration, priority, tacking):
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20/section/101 (fetched 2026-11-19).
- Administration of Justice Act 1970 s36; Statute of Frauds 1677 s4; Insolvency Act 1986 s360;
  Building Societies Act 1986 s5; Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 ss1, 11; Planning (Listed
  Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 ss7, 9 (all on legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-11-19).

  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mortgage-charter/mortgage-charter (fetched 2026-11-19).

  https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy (fetched 2026-11-19).
- RICS consumer guides: Home surveys (2023), Property auctions (June 2024), Subsidence, Flooding,
  Spray foam insulation: https://www.rics.org/consumer-guides (fetched 2026-11-19).

  https://www.nhbc.co.uk/homeowners/buildmark-cover (fetched 2026-11-19).
- Flood Re eligibility (homes built before 2009; no homes let outside the family):
  https://www.floodre.co.uk/can-flood-re-help-me/ (fetched 2026-11-19).

  definitions for capped, collared, cashback, flexible, offset, self-build and foreign currency
