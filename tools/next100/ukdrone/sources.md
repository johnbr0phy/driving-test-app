# UK Flyer ID and A2 CofC theory bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the UK Civil Aviation Authority's
Drone and Model Aircraft Code, the CAA's class mark and A2 Certificate of Competency pages, and
the UK regulation text in the CAA regulatory library, all listed below. None is copied, adapted or
reworded from the CAA's Flyer ID theory test, any Recognised Assessment Entity (RAE) A2 CofC
exam, sample question, prep book, app or website. The FAA Part 107 bank
(data/part107-questions.json), the NZ Part 101 bank (data/nz-drone-questions.json) and the
Canada drone bank were checked so no fact-and-answer pair repeats.

Scope decision: the bank covers the Flyer ID theory test (the Drone Code, Over People A1 and Far
from People A3) in seven categories and the A2 CofC theory subjects (meteorology, UAS flight
performance, operating principles and ground risk mitigations) in one set of three. The A2 exam
needs a Flyer ID first, so it builds on the same rules; one bank serves both steps.

## Flyer ID test format and the Drone Code (rules from 1 January 2026)

## A2 Certificate of Competency (Near People, A2)

  technical and operational mitigations for ground risk; online or paper; awarded by RAEs;
  self-directed practical training; valid five years.
- CAA, Introduction to more advanced flying (Near People A2), fetched 2026-11-22:

  50m; no intentional overflight; Flyer ID, Operator ID and A2 CofC.
- CAA regulatory library, UK Regulation (EU) 2019/947 UAS.OPEN.030 (SI 2025/1106 text), fetched 2026-11-22:

- CAA regulatory library, GM1 UAS.OPEN.030(1) (30m minimum, the 1:1 rule), fetched 2026-11-22:

## Weather (A2 meteorology)

- Met Office, Fog, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/types-of-weather/fog
  Fog below 1,000 metres visibility, mist above; radiation fog on clear calm nights clearing after
  sunrise; advection fog when moist air crosses a colder surface.
- Met Office, Thunder and lightning, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/types-of-weather/thunder-and-lightning
  Unstable air, heavy rain and hail, divide the flash-to-thunder seconds by three for kilometres.
- Met Office, Wind, fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/types-of-weather/wind
  Air moving from high to low pressure.
  Urban wind effects (funnelling between buildings, eddies and turbulence downwind of buildings,
  wind stronger aloft than at the surface) are the AMC's listed sub-topics, written at concept
  level with no figures. Battery items (C-rate, capacity, memory effect, swelling, cold reducing
  capacity) are concept-level as the AMC lists them; the Drone Code's point 15 gives the cold
  battery fact.

## Format facts (landing and examFacts only, never in the bank)

Flyer ID: 40 questions, pass mark 30 (75%), open book, free, at least 30 minutes, valid five years.
A2 CofC: at least 30 questions, 75% overall, 75 minutes, closed book, taken at an RAE after
self-practical training, certificate valid five years. Tigertest's practice tests are 40
questions at the Flyer ID length, with a 75% line.

## What could not be loaded

- The Drone Code PDF (March 2026) returned 404 to automated reads; the section web pages it
  mirrors were read instead.
- The RAE exams themselves are set by each provider from the AMC subject list; no official split
  per subject is published, so the A2 weights here are Tigertest's.

## Deliberately left out

Registration fees, fines, course prices, named RAEs or training providers, Specific category
details (GVC, PDRA, operational authorisations) beyond the point that flying outside the Code
needs CAA authorisation, Flight Restriction Zone dimensions (the Code points to maps), the 2028
changes to legacy A2 aircraft and Remote ID beyond one dated fact, and weather or battery numbers
not in an official text (wind limits are the manufacturer's).
