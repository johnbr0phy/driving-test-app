# Sources for `ukfood3` (Level 3 Award in Food Safety (Supervising), Catering, UK)

All 200 questions are original. No awarding body's exam paper, sample questions, course book or
prep site item was copied or reworded; the specification's sample questions were read only to see
the style and were not used. The sources below fixed the topic coverage, figures and legal points.
All checked 2026-11-23.

## Qualification structure and assessment
- Highfield Level 3 Award in Food Safety for Catering (RQF), qualification specification v1.2,
  August 2021, qualification number 603/4942/6, fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://centres.highfieldqualifications.com/Assets/DownloadArea/QualificationDownloads/[25082021_1546]_L3_FSC_(RQF)_Qualification_specification_v1.2_Aug21.pdf
  One unit, Food Safety for Catering (M/617/7267), 20 GLH, TQT 25, 3 credits, learners 14+.
  Assessment: multiple-choice exam, externally set and marked, 45 questions in 90 minutes, pass
  66% (30/45), distinction 80% (36/45). LO1 compliance with legislation and management procedures
  (FSMS, HACCP, verification, enforcement, inspections, complaints, due diligence, purchase to
  service controls); LO2 good practice on personal hygiene, training, cleaning and waste, pests,
  microbiological, chemical, physical and allergenic contamination, premises design, temperature
  control and thermometers. The category list follows these outcomes.
- Highfield Level 3 Award in Food Safety for Catering fact sheet (April 2024; regulated by Ofqual
  and Qualifications Wales, 25 hours TQT, 20 GLH, multiple-choice exam), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://centres.highfieldqualifications.com//Assets/DownloadArea/QualificationDownloads/[15042024_1505]_L3_Award_FS_Catering_FS.pdf
- RSPH Level 3 Award in Supervising Food Safety and Hygiene (Catering), Ofqual 603/2396/6: the
  RSPH site returned errors to automated fetches, so its format (a specimen paper with a
  multiple-choice section and scenario questions) is taken only from search results and is not
  stated as fact on the landing. The landing gives Highfield's format as the example and says other
  awarding bodies set their own papers.

## Law (England, with Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland differences)
- Food Safety and Hygiene (England) Regulations 2013, contents (regs 6 to 10, 12, 16 to 19,
  Schedule 4), fetched 2026-11-23: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2996/contents
- Reg 6 hygiene improvement notices (grounds, matters, measures, at least 14 days; failing to
  comply is an offence), fetched 2026-11-23: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2996/regulation/6
- Reg 8 hygiene emergency prohibition notices and orders (imminent risk, magistrates' court,
  notice lapses after 3 days without an application, ends with the authority's certificate,
  compensation), fetched 2026-11-23: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2996/regulation/8
- Reg 9 remedial action notices apply to establishments approved under Regulation 853/2004 (so
  they are used only as a distractor), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2996/regulation/9
- Reg 19 penalties (summary: fine; indictment: up to two years, a fine or both), fetched
  2026-11-23: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2996/regulation/19
- Schedule 4 temperature control (8°C chill holding and its exemptions: canned food until opened,
  food that must ripen or mature at ambient; under 4 hours display tolerance; breakdown and
  defrosting defence; 63°C hot holding and the 2-hour rule; scientific assessment defences),
  fetched 2026-11-23: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2996/schedule/4
- Retained Regulation (EC) 852/2004 Article 5 (HACCP principles (a) to (g), review on change,
  records in proportion to size), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/852/article/5
- Retained Regulation 852/2004 Annex II Chapter XII (supervision and training to suit the work;
  adequate HACCP training for those who develop and maintain the procedures), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/852/annex/II/chapter/XII
- Food Safety Act 1990 (s.9 seizure and detention, s.14 nature, substance or quality demanded,
  s.15 falsely describing or presenting food, s.20 fault of another person, s.21 due diligence,
  s.32 powers of entry): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/16/contents (checked 2026-11-23)
- GOV.UK food safety management systems (HACCP principles, records as evidence), fetched
  2026-11-23: https://www.gov.uk/food-safety-management-systems
- Food hygiene rating scheme (0 to 5 meanings; three elements: food handling, physical condition,
  management of food safety; display compulsory in Wales and Northern Ireland and the rating must be
  given verbally on request; appeals, right to reply and re-rating visits), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/food-hygiene-rating-scheme/food-hygiene-rating-scheme

## Guidance
- Safer Food, Better Business (packs, diary with the 4-weekly review), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/safer-food-better-business-sfbb/safer-food-better-business-sfbb
- FSA introduction to PPDS allergen labelling (three tests for PPDS; name and full ingredients
  list with the 14 allergens emphasised; food packed after ordering is non-prepacked and needs
  information by any means including orally; food packed by one business for another is prepacked;
  distance selling information before purchase and at delivery; 1 October 2021), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69a03d0cc497bac082bc7710/introduction-to-allergen-labelling-changes-prepacked-for-direct-sale-ppds_0.pdf

  food): https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/allergen-guidance-for-food-businesses (checked 2026-11-23)
- FSA fitness to work guidance (report diarrhoea, vomiting, infected skin, discharges from eye, ear
  or nose, household contacts; clearance for typhoid, paratyphoid and some E. coli infections):
  https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/fitness-to-work (checked 2026-11-23)
- FSA E. coli O157 cross-contamination guidance (separation of raw and ready-to-eat, two-stage

- Gluten-free limit (20 mg/kg; very low gluten 100 mg/kg), retained Regulation (EU) 828/2014:
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/828/annex (checked 2026-11-23)

## Scotland and Northern Ireland
- CookSafe (Food Standards Scotland) and Safe Catering (FSA in Northern Ireland) named as the
  HACCP-based packs there; the Scottish reheat figure (82°C) is left to the Level 2 bank.

## Left out on purpose
- Fines and penalty amounts other than the two-year maximum on indictment; inspection frequencies
  (the Food Law Code of Practice was revised and its rating scheme is not asked); FHRS appeal
  periods; registration timing (asked in the Level 2 bank).
- Facts the Level 2 bank (`ukfood`) already asks with the same answer were rewritten to a
  supervisory angle or replaced. A similarity check against ukfood, foodmgr, foodhandler, aufss and
  fihygiene (stem ratio over 0.6, or key ratio over 0.85 with a stem ratio over 0.35) was run on
  2026-11-23 and every close fact-and-answer match was rewritten.
- The real exam's own format (45 questions, 90 minutes, 30 to pass) is on the landing only.
