## (c) Landing FAQ
1. **Who needs an unvented hot water certificate?** Anyone installing an unvented cylinder in England or Wales without building control inspecting it first. Approved Document G says installers can show the skills through a competent person scheme or a current unvented skills card, and schemes ask for this certificate.
2. **What does the assessment cover?** System types, the inlet group (strainer, pressure reducing valve, check valve, expansion relief valve and vessel), the energy cut-out and T&P relief valve, the tundish and D1/D2 discharge pipe rules, temperature control and legionella, commissioning, Benchmark paperwork and fault finding.
3. **Do I need to do discharge pipe sums?** Yes. Expect to size D2 from Table 3.1 of Approved Document G: take the straight-pipe allowance for the size, subtract the resistance of each elbow, and go up a size if the run is too long.
4. **Is the practical assessment covered here?** No. Tigertest practises the knowledge paper only. The practical (fitting or inspecting a system) is done at the training centre.
5. **How long does the certificate last?** BPEC certificates are valid for five years, and you can reassess in the 12 months before expiry. Check your awarding body's rules.
