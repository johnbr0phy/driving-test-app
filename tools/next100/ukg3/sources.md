# ukg3 sources (checked 2026-11-12)

All 200 questions are original, written from the primary text below. No BPEC, LCL Awards or
City & Guilds sample papers, past papers, course manuals or commercial question banks were used,
and no official question was reworded. Paid British Standards (BS EN 12897, BS EN 1490,
BS 6283-2, BS 8558, BS EN 806) were not read: they are named only where Approved Document G
itself names them, and no clause numbers or values are taken from them.

## The qualification and its format (exam owner)
- BPEC, Domestic Hot Water Storage Systems (Unvented): https://bpec.org.uk/qualification/domestic-hot-water-storage-systems/ (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Two manuals: England and Wales (Approved Document G3, Part L1 and Part P) and Scotland (Building Standards). Four modules: vented and unvented systems and their operating principles; design and installation; maintenance; compliance with other Building Regulations. Three assessments, one practical, all must be passed for the certificate and competency card. Certificate valid 5 years, reassessment within 12 months of expiry. Entry: NVQ/SNVQ Level 2 or 3 plumbing/heating, enrolment on one, or several years' experience. The page publishes no question count, time or pass mark.
- Providers (format only, "guides say"): one college lists a multiple-choice paper of about 1 h 20 min, a short written paper of 30 min and a practical job sheet of 40 min; another a practical visual assessment and a multiple-choice theory paper. LCL Awards' Hot Water Systems and Safety (formerly "Unvented G3") is a one-day course covering systems up to 500 litres and 45 kW. LCL's own specification page could not be found or fetched; no pass mark is published by any owner, so the bank uses a 70% readiness line (Tigertest's).
- Blueprint: no owner publishes topic weights. The nine categories follow BPEC's four modules and the order of Approved Document G Section G3, with Tigertest's split: law 6, system types 5, inlet controls 7, safety devices 8, discharge 8, temperature 5, installation 4, commissioning 3, maintenance 4 per 50.

## Law and guidance (England)
- The Building Regulations 2010, Schedule 1 Part G (G3): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/schedule/1 (fetched 2026-11-12)
  G3(1) heated water to the listed appliances; G3(2) resist temperature and pressure in normal use and foreseeable malfunction, adequately supported; G3(3) stored water never over 100°C and safety discharges conveyed safely and visibly; G3(4) bath water not over 48°C in dwellings erected or formed by a material change of use; G3(3) not applicable to industrial-process-only systems.
- Approved Document G, 2015 edition incorporating 2016 and 2024 amendments: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/sanitation-hot-water-safety-and-water-efficiency-approved-document-g (fetched 2026-11-12); PDF https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/66f6c6ce3b919067bb4828cc/ADG_with_2024_amendments.pdf
  Read in full for Section G3 (paragraphs 3.1 to 3.80, Diagram 1, Table 3.1 and the worked example) and the key terms (unvented, vented, direct, indirect, primary thermal store, unit, package, tundish, relief valves, non-self-resetting cut-out, expansion vessel).
- May 2024 amendment booklet: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/667ad2d94ae39c5e45fe4c0c/AD_G_amendment_booklet.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12). The 2024 changes (in force 1 October 2024) touch G4 only (hot tap on the left, toilet types); Section G3 is unchanged.
- The Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999, Schedule 2 paragraphs 17 to 23 (hot water services): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1148/schedule/2 (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Para 17 temperature control plus T or T&P valve on unvented heaters over 15 litres; para 19 safe and conspicuous discharge; para 22 valves close automatically, expansion valve close to the vessel with no intervening valve and opening at not less than 0.5 bar above normal pressure; para 23 T&P valve directly on unvented vessels over 15 litres, its pressure element opening not less than 0.5 bar above the greater of the vessel's maximum working pressure and the expansion valve setting. Only these hot-water paragraphs are used; general water fittings content is left to the sibling ukwater bank.
- HSE HSG274 Part 2, The control of legionella bacteria in hot and cold water systems: https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg274part2.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Store at least 60°C, 50°C at outlets within one minute (55°C in healthcare), cold below 20°C (within two minutes), calorifier return not below 50°C, whole calorifier at 60°C for one hour a day, solar cylinders, expansion vessels (cool area, cold pipe, near the incoming supply, vertical, isolation and drain valve, minimal retained volume), hydraulic accumulators, TMVs (38 to 46°C, type 2 and type 3).

## Manufacturer practice (commissioning, maintenance, faults)
- Ideal Heating, heat pump DHW cylinder installation and servicing instructions (issue 01 04/25): https://idealheating.com/_gatsby/file/fddbfc488d4841eaa24abb6b86d10212/GPL015_IU_Iss01_04_25%20HP%20DHW%20Cylinder%20PP%20Lite_V4_WEB.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Combination inlet group (strainer, PRV with integral check valve, expansion relief valve), balanced cold to mixers, pre-charge checked with the water side at zero, manual operation of relief valves, 12-month service, hard water above 200 ppm, Benchmark service record, cold intermittent discharge points to the expansion vessel, hot continuous discharge: heat off but water left on, fault-finding sequences.
- Ideal Heating, mains pressure hot water storage system commissioning checklist (Benchmark layout): https://idealheating.com/_gatsby/file/d7c457fc3aded6db9c8e5023375a7590/Logic%20Air%20Cylinder%20Commissioning%20Checklist.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Completed by the commissioning engineer and handed to the customer; records static inlet pressure, strainer cleaned, hard water and scale reducer, registered operative ID.
  Manufacturer figures (pre-charge 3 bar, T&P 95°C/6 bar) are not asked as fixed numbers; questions say "the manufacturer's figure".

## What was left out or worded carefully
- Scotland (Building Standards Technical Handbook) and Wales (its own Approved Document G) differ; every question follows England's Approved Document G and says so where it matters.
- AD G still cites withdrawn standards (BS 6700, BS 7671 17th Edition) and old regulation dates; those references are not asked.
- The name of the approved commissioning procedure guide is not asked (AD G's wording is out of date).
- No fees, no card validity, no assessment format, no provider names in stems.
