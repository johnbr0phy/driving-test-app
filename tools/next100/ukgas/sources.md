# Sources for `ukgas` (Gas Safe ACS core domestic gas safety, CCN1, knowledge)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from the law, the HSE Approved Code of
Practice, the industry unsafe situations procedure and the ACS assessment criteria listed below. No
ACS assessment paper, certification body question, past paper, training provider question bank or
commercial practice test was read or copied, and no provider is named in a stem. Checked 2026-11-12.

## Exam owner and scheme (assessment criteria, official)
- IGEM, Standard Setting Body: https://www.igem.org.uk/technical/standard-setting-body.html
  (fetched 2026-11-12). IGEM manages the Standard Setting Body; the Strategic Management Board is
  the Scheme Owner for ACS, the "Nationally Accredited Certification Scheme for Individual Gas
  Fitting Operatives". (Energy & Utility Skills ACS links now redirect here.)
- IGEM, Assessment: https://www.igem.org.uk/technical/standard-setting-body/assessment.html
  (fetched 2026-11-12). Competence for Gas Safe registration is shown by a qualification or by ACS;
  work is assessed as a core competence (CCN1, core domestic gas safety) plus appliance
  competences (e.g. CENWAT for boilers); certificates are renewed every 5 years by ACS
  reassessment or the Group Competency Scheme.
- IGEM, CCN1 Safety Assessment Criteria, Initial and Re-assessment, Domestic Natural Gas, CCN1
  Issue 20.1 (ACS.SMB.004AC Table 1), linked from
  https://www.igem.org.uk/technical/standard-setting-body/assessment-criteria-documents.html
  via https://www.igem.org.uk/resource/ccn1-safety-assessment-criteria-initial-domestic-natural-gas.html
  (fetched 2026-11-12). The 16 competence sections (legislation and standards; gas emergency
  actions; products and characteristics of combustion; ventilation; pipework; tightness testing
  and purging; checking operating pressure at the meter outlet; unsafe situations, notices and
  labels; emergency isolation controls; burner pressures and gas rates; gas safety devices;
  chimney standards; chimney inspection and testing; open, balanced and fanned flues; re-establish
  supply; re-assessment appliance checks) gave the categories. Normative documents named: HSL56,
  BS 5440-1 and -2:2023, IGEM/G/11, IGEM/UP/1B, IGEM/UP/17, IGEM/G/5, IGEM/G/13, Approved
  Documents B, F and J, Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (Amendment) Regulations 2022.
  Tightness test figures used (criteria section 6a and 6b, which follow IGEM/UP/1B): let-by test at
  7 to 10 mbar for 1 minute, a rise over 0.25 mbar suggests let-by; test pressure 20 to 21 mbar, 1
  minute stabilisation, 2 minute test, no perceptible movement (0.25 mbar water gauge, 0.2 mbar
  electronic gauge reading to one decimal place); no drop allowed on new installations; MP
  installations above 75 mbar isolated at the MIV; MP without MIV tested at 18 to 19 mbar and an
  ECV letting by there stops the test and is reported to the gas emergency call centre; air may be
  used if natural gas is not available; leak detection fluid on joints once gas is on;
  combustion analysis probe at highest steady CO2 or lowest steady O2, CO/CO2 ratio recorded.
  Issue 16 (2022, https://www.euskills.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/CCN1-Issue-16.pdf, fetched
  2026-11-12) also gave a 21 mbar meter outlet pressure and a 19 to 23 mbar range; Issue 20.1
  removed both ("criteria removed"), so no meter outlet pressure figure is asked.
- BPEC Certification, ACS page: https://bpec.org.uk/qualification/acs/ (fetched 2026-11-12, a
  UKAS-accredited certification body): assessments "normally consist of a combination of
  practical and theory examinations"; certificates normally valid for 5 years. No question
  count, time, open-book rule or pass mark is published by the scheme or this body; the 70% in the
  spec is Tigertest's readiness line.

## Primary law and HSE guidance
- Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998 (SI 1998/2451), latest revised text:
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2451 and data.xml (fetched 2026-11-12). Extent
  England, Wales and Scotland (Northern Ireland has its own 2004 Regulations). Regulations 2, 3,
  4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12 to 19, 22, 23, 26 to 37 and 36A used.
- HSE L56 (fifth edition, 2018), Safety in the installation and use of gas systems and appliances,
  ACOP and guidance: https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/l56.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12). Gas Safe
  Register as the only approved body (para 93), competence (paras 81 to 85), appropriate fittings
  and ECV not a seal (paras 23 to 24, 115), search methods (para 122), tightness test scope
  (para 125), joints tested before coating (paras 214 to 216), meter on escape route and 95°C
  thermal cut-off (paras 165 to 166), bonding advice in writing (para 202), sleeves sealed at one
  end (para 209), LPG voids (para 211), flexible connections (paras 110, 232), used appliances
  moved (para 234), shut-off devices (para 237), flue terminals and birds (para 254), boundary
  (para 259), unsafe appliances and no power to disconnect (paras 280 to 289), CO alarms (para 22),
  regulation 36A (paras 304 to 314), escapes and CO advice (paras 318 to 334), Appendix 3 flue
  and appliance checks (flue flow then spillage test, one appliance per flue, sweeping solid fuel
  flues).
- HSE, Carbon monoxide: https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/domestic/co.htm (fetched 2026-11-12):
  properties, symptoms, warning signs, what to do, 0800 111 999 and 0800 300 363.
- HSE, Domestic gas FAQs (updated 16 March 2023): https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/domestic/faqs.htm
  (fetched 2026-11-12): ID and AR both labelled DANGER DO NOT USE, NCS removed from the procedure,
  landlord records and 28 days, CO alarm rule in England from 1 October 2022.
- MHCLG, Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations Q&A (as amended by SI 2022/707):
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/smoke-and-carbon-monoxide-alarms-explanatory-booklet-for-landlords/the-smoke-and-carbon-monoxide-alarm-england-regulations-2015-qa-booklet-for-the-private-rented-sector-landlords-and-tenants
  (fetched 2026-11-12): rooms with a fixed combustion appliance, gas cookers excluded,
  bathrooms included.
- Approved Document J (2010 edition incorporating 2022 amendments, England):
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6336e58be90e0772dc9651a2/ADJ_2022.pdf (fetched
  2026-11-12): paragraphs 3.1 to 3.5 (14 kW gross = 12.7 kW net), 3.9 (5 m³), 3.11 (100 cm² for
  a DFE in a recess with a throat), Diagram 33 note 6 (0.045 kW per m³ example), Table 5 (125 mm
  and 175 mm flues), 3.43 to 3.45 (CO alarm in the room, BS EN 50291-1, 300 mm from walls,
  150 mm from ceiling, 1 m to 3 m from the appliance). Diagram 32 (vent areas per kW) is an
  image that could not be read, so no per-kW vent sizing figure is asked.

## Unsafe situations procedure
- IGEM/G/11 Edition 2 with amendments July 2022 and June 2024, Gas industry unsafe situations
  procedure (Communication 1887), free download from IGEM:
  https://www.igem.org.uk/asset/3F9BFBCA-29A5-4100-91CCCCD53A8CD157 (fetched 2026-11-12). ID and AR
  definitions, ID and AR actions and turn-off order, warning notices and signatures, ESP attends
  ID within an hour, gas incidents, RIDDOR 11(1) and 11(2) (14 days), Stay Energy Safe, and Table
  1 examples used for classifications (failed tightness test, smell after a pass, 10 ppm CO
  re-entry, fly screens and closable vents AR, 90% vent rule, redundant chimney not ventilation,
  flueless cooker under 20 m³, park home skirt, mechanical ventilation interlock, flue into a
  conservatory ID, concealed flue without hatches AR, draught diverter missing AR, disabled
  safety devices ID, open-flued appliance in a bathroom ID, flexible hose on a flued appliance AR,
  open-ended pipe ID, ECV let-by AR, built-over PE service with no label, MP and LP definitions,
  propane 37 mbar and butane 28 mbar).

## Gas properties
- UKHSA, Methane incident management (October 2024):
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/671fbcc4ae0462c448fc40d1/UKHSA_Incident_Management_Methane_.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-12): specific gravity 0.55, LEL 5%, UEL 15%, odourless with odorant added.
- UKHSA, LPG incident management (October 2024):
  https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6707da17e84ae1fd8592f173/Incident-management-liquified-petroleum-gas.pdf
  (fetched 2026-11-12): propane specific gravity 1.6, LEL 2.1%, UEL 9.5%, stenching agent.
- General combustion chemistry (methane + 2 O2, air about 21% oxygen, so about 9.5 volumes of air)
  is computed in the explanation. The flame-chilling, vitiation and blocked primary air items
  follow the ACS criteria's "causes of incomplete combustion at burner, combustion space, heat
  exchanger and flue" and HSE's yellow-flame warning sign; they assert nothing beyond that.
