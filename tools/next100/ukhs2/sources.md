# Sources for `ukhs2` (Level 2 Award in Health and Safety in the Workplace, UK)

All 200 questions are original. No awarding body's exam paper, specimen paper, course book, prep
site or app item was copied or reworded; no question material was read. The specifications below
fixed the learning outcomes and the exam format, and HSE guidance and the legislation fixed every
figure and legal point. All checked 2026-11-23.

## Qualification structure and assessment
- RSPH Level 2 Award in Health and Safety in the Workplace, qualification page (Ofqual
  600/0082/X, Qualifications Wales C00/0303/2; "a 40-minute multiple-choice examination", 7 GLH,
  9 hours TQT; regulated by Ofqual, CCEA and Qualifications Wales), fetched 2026-11-23:
  https://www.rsph.org.uk/qualification/level-2-award-in-health-and-safety-in-the-workplace.html
  The page links the specification PDF ("Specification - RSPH Level 2 Award in Health and Safety
  in the Workplace", v1.07, January 2020), downloaded 2026-11-23 through the page's signed storage
  link (the link expires, so it is not listed). It gives the unit Health and Safety in the
  workplace (H/601/9199) with four learning outcomes: 1 roles and responsibilities (employer and
  employee duties under the 1974 Act, COSHH and RIDDOR; consequences of non-compliance, enforcement
  officers, improvement and prohibition notices; training and competence; communicating
  information and safety signs), 2 risk assessment (hazard and risk, the process, COSHH
  assessments, pregnant women and young people), 3 common hazards and controls (slips, trips and
  falls, manual handling, repetitive movements, machinery, DSE, hazardous substances, electricity,
  noise, vehicles, fire and the fire triangle, dust and fumes; the risk control hierarchy with PPE
  as the last resort), 4 accidents and incidents (human, occupational and environmental causes,
  actions after an incident, emergencies and first aid, recording and reporting). Assessment: a
  multiple-choice exam of 25 questions in 40 minutes; a candidate who meets the outcomes scores at
  least 16 out of 25. The eight categories and the 64% pass line follow this.

## Repeats
Every stem and key was compared with the cscs, ukfaw, uksia, ukfood, ukcare, osha, osha30,
auwhite, iesafepass, auwah, ukaplh and (open PR) ukfood3 banks by word overlap and identical
keys. 33 items that asked the same fact with the same answer were rewritten from a different
angle (for example the 1 in 4 ladder rule as a distance sum, a hazard picked out of a list instead
of defined, a hospital visit by a shopper under RIDDOR). The remaining overlaps are on different
facts or different answers.
