# ukplumb sources (fetched 2026-11-12)

All 200 questions are original, written from the documents below. No City & Guilds sample tests, past papers, EPAO question material or commercial question banks were used, even reworded. Paid British Standards (BS EN 806, BS 6700, BS EN 12056, BS 8000, BS 7593, BS EN 442) were not read: they are named only where an Approved Document or the C&G range names them, and no clause or figure is quoted from them.

## The apprenticeship (Skills England, formerly IfATE)
- Plumbing and domestic heating technician ST0303 v1.2 standard: https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeship-standards/st0303-v1-2 (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Level 3; v1.2 from 7 April 2025. Core knowledge K1 to K18 (health and safety, installation and testing of cold, hot, heating, sanitation and rainwater systems, scientific principles, legislation, commissioning, decommissioning, servicing, fault finding, sizing, electrical control and limits of competence); options for gas, air source heat pumps and solar thermal, and non-domestic. Mandatory Level 3 Diploma in plumbing and domestic heating.
- ST0303 v1.2 end-point assessment plan (16 July 2025): https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/media/tm0oky4o/st0303-plumbing-v12_for-publication-16_7_25.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Knowledge test paper 1 (core): 60 multiple-choice questions, four options, 120 minutes; pass 36/60, distinction 50/60. Paper 2 by option: ACS CCN1 and CENWAT (gas), 2a heat pumps and 2b solar thermal (30 questions, 60 minutes, pass 18/30 each), non-domestic 30 questions.

## Split and pass mark
- Category weights are Tigertest's split, built from the C&G 6035-02 test specification sizes (H&S 42, science 40, processes 40, cold 40, hot 40, heating 40 plus L3 606 43, drainage 40 with rainwater 7, electrical 40) and L3 301 (27), cut to 50: safety 6, science 5, processes 6, cold 6, hot 6, heating 8, drainage 5, rainwater 2, electrical 3, environmental technology 3.
- passPct 60: the ST0303 paper 1 pass (36 of 60) and the C&G "approx. 60%" on every Level 2 test.

## Not confirmed, left out
- Notch and hole limits for joists: the current Approved Document A no longer gives them (council notes and secondary sources only), so no figures are asked.
- Underground pipe cover depths (often quoted as 750 mm to 1350 mm) are in Regulator's guidance not read here; left out, and they belong to ukwater.
- Ladder 1 m above a landing: HSE's page gives 1 m above the working position, which is what the question uses.
- Copper tube tables, clip spacings, pipe sizing and fluid category detail (BS, WRAS guide, paid) not asked. Gas content left to ukgas, unvented to ukg3, BS 7671 detail to uk18ed.
