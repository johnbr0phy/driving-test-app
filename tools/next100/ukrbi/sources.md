# ukrbi sources (checked 2026-11-12)

All 200 questions are original. No competence-scheme sample questions, past papers, assessment material or commercial question banks were used, and no paid British Standard was read or quoted. Every rule below was read in the primary text on gov.uk or legislation.gov.uk on the date given.

## The outline: BSR registration, classes and the competence framework (England)
- Register as a building inspector (gov.uk, BSR), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/register-as-a-building-inspector
  Registering is required to carry out regulated building control activities (assessing plans, inspections, advising building control bodies) in England or Wales; criminal offence to work unregistered; classes 2 and 3 need a BSR-approved scheme assessment, class 1 does not; registration lasts 4 years (from 6 April 2024 for earlier registrants). Last updated 23 April 2025.
- Registered building inspectors (gov.uk, BSR), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/registered-building-inspectors
  Classes 1 to 4, building categories A to H with their heights, supervision rules (the building control body provides the supervisor), class 4 technical manager (no mandatory assessment), assessments repeated at least every 4 years, changes notified within 28 days, England review within 21 days then the First-tier Tribunal, permissible work on existing HRBs. Last updated 24 September 2025.
- Registered building inspectors: professional codes and standards (collection), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/registered-building-inspectors-professional-codes-and-standards
  Change history: 16 Apr 2025 class 3H = all buildings; 18 Jun, 4 Jul, 18 Sep and 7 Oct 2025 permissible work for class 2 and 3G; 22 May 2026 the regulatory authority named as the Building Safety Regulator, not HSE. No other 2026 change. The version used is the one in force on 2026-11-12.
- Building inspector competence framework (BICoF), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/registered-building-inspectors-professional-codes-and-standards/building-inspector-competence-framework-bicof
  Nine subject areas in three families (technical: law, technology, building services; delivery: functions and activities, plans assessment and enforcement, inspections and enforcement; management: management and core skills, safety management, ethics); levels A Awareness, B Appreciation, C Understanding, D Comprehensive; class 2 level C plus ethics D; class 3 level D in the six technical and delivery areas, Health Technical Memoranda and Building Bulletins, HRB work in multi-disciplinary teams under BSR direction; legislation named (BSA 2022, Building Act 1984, Fire Safety Act 2021, RRFSO 2005, Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022, Defective Premises Act 1972, Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, CDM 2015).
- Code of conduct for registered building inspectors (England), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/registered-building-inspectors-professional-codes-and-standards/code-of-conduct-for-registered-building-inspectors
  Six principles; standards 1 to 5 (breach reporting to the building control authority, independence, insurance, financial propriety, price fixing, conflicts, whistleblowing records 15 years, CPD on data protection, AML and EDI, terms of engagement in writing before work, confidentiality); Annex 1 reporting (14 days; 28 days for insolvency matters; changes of details 28 days); Annex 2 engagement letter contents; Annex 3 conditions under s58C; Annex 4 classes and the HRB definition (18 m or 7 storeys, 2+ residential units, or a care home or hospital during design and construction); Annexes 5 and 6 permissible work.
- BSR becomes standalone body (gov.uk news, 27 January 2026), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/bsr-becomes-standalone-body-in-landmark-step-towards-single-construction-regulator
  BSR left HSE on 27 January 2026 and is an executive non-departmental public body sponsored by MHCLG.
- Building control bodies: professional codes and standards (collection), fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/building-control-bodies-professional-codes-and-standards
- Operational standards rules, fetched 2026-11-12: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/building-control-bodies-professional-codes-and-standards/operational-standards-rules
  Rules 1.1 to 4.5: risk-based approach, records kept at least 15 years, annual evaluation, speak-up policy, outsourcing oversight, staff stopped when not competent or registered, statutory consultees told in writing with reasons, inspection reports for site and remote inspections, evidence time-marked and geo-tagged, reports recorded and enforcement records updated within 5 working days, contraventions resolved before completion or final certificates, RBCA cancels its initial notice when contraventions cannot be resolved.

## Format and weights
BSR publishes no assessment format, question count, pass mark or topic weights: competence is assessed by BSR-approved independent schemes, each with its own method. The category split here is Tigertest's, built around the BICoF subject areas and the code of conduct; the 70% line is a Tigertest readiness target, not an official pass mark. The copy says so.
