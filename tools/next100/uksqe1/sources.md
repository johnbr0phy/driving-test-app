# SQE1 (FLK1 and FLK2) bank sources

All 200 questions are original, written for Tigertest from the SRA's SQE1 Assessment
Specification and Functioning Legal Knowledge (FLK) listed below, the SRA Standards and
Regulations, and the primary law of England and Wales the FLK names. None is copied, adapted or
reworded from the SRA's published SQE1 sample questions (original and pre-tested sets), any
training provider's materials, prep book, app or website. The sample question PDFs were opened
only to read their format notes on pages 1 to 3 (session length and question count); their
questions were not read for writing. The CeMAP 1 (data/cemap-questions.json), UK personal licence
and Water Regulations banks were grepped so no fact-and-answer pair repeats.

## Official SRA sources (the outline and the format)

- SRA, SQE1 Assessment Specification, applicable for assessments from 1 September 2026, updated
  April 2026 (PDF), fetched 2026-11-22:
  https://sqe.sra.org.uk/docs/default-source/pdfs/sqe1-assessment-specification-(from-1-september-2026).pdf?sfvrsn=9a3f672d_2
  FLK1: Business Law and Practice; Dispute Resolution; Contract Law; Tort; Legal System of England
  and Wales, Constitutional and Administrative Law and EU Law; Legal Services. FLK2: Property Law
  and Practice; Wills and the Administration of Estates; Solicitors Accounts (in the context of
  property and probate); Land Law; Trusts Law; Criminal Liability; Criminal Law and Practice.
  Ethics and Professional Conduct examined pervasively; taxation only in business, property and
  wills; single best answer, five options, one correct; closed book; law cut-off four calendar
  months before the first assessment in a window; changing tax rates and thresholds are given in
  the question; case names needed only where they name a principle (Rylands v Fletcher, CPR Part
  36, section 25 notice); England and Wales differ on some topics; SRA warning notices on NDAs and
  SLAPPs can be examined.

## Left out on purpose

- No money figures that change: no tax rates, nil-rate band, annual exemption amounts, court fees,
  statutory legacy, SDLT bands or interest rates. Tax items test reliefs and principles; where a
  figure is needed it is given in the stem, as the SRA does.
- No short residential leases (not examinable in Property Law and Practice from September 2026)
  and no IHT anti-avoidance (removed in 2026).
- No item depends on law that changed or is pending after the cut-off for the January 2027
  window (law at about September 2026), such as sentencing reforms still before Parliament.
- Format facts (questions, sessions, pass mark) appear only on the landing and in examFacts.
- Five-option items are written with four options (the Tigertest engine's format); the landing
  says so.
