# ukwater sources (Water Regulations, checked 2026-11-12)

Every question in this bank is original, written from the primary law and official guidance below. No awarding body's sample, assessment pack or past questions, no WRAS Water Regulations Guide items and no commercial practice tests were used.

## Format and outline (awarding bodies)
- City & Guilds 3345 product page: https://www.cityandguilds.com/qualifications-and-apprenticeships/building-services-industry/plumbing/3345-water-regulations-unvented-hot-water-systems (fetched 2026-11-12)
  3345 is current (page last updated 26 Aug 2026). 3345-20 (Water Regs) closed to registration 31 Dec 2023, last certification 31 Dec 2026; learners now register on 3345-21 (last registration 31 Aug 2027, certification 31 Aug 2028). Assessed by online multiple choice. No pass mark on the page.
- City & Guilds 3345 qualification handbook v1.8: https://cityandguilds.com/-/media/productdocuments/building_services_engineering/plumbing/3345/3345_level_3/centre_documents/3345_l3_award_qualification_handbook_v1-8-pdf.ashx (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Level 3 Award in Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations and Water Byelaws in the UK, unit 301/304: 50 multiple-choice questions, 105 minutes, open book (The Water Regulations Guide, 2nd edition, allowed). Test specification by outcome: 1 regs 2, 2 terminology 1, 3 materials 2, 4 water fittings 7, 5 system design 9, 6 cross connection 2, 7 fluid categories 1, 8 backflow requirements 1, 9 backflow guidance 11, 10 cold 1, 11 hot 4, 12 WCs and urinals 4, 13 baths, sinks, showers, taps 2, 14 appliance consumption 1, 15 outside use 2. The bank's blueprint follows this, grouped into eight categories (see spec.json); fluid categories and cold water are weighted up a little because the outcome counts are tiny, so the split is "Tigertest's split based on the C&G 3345 test specification".
- BPEC Water Regulations & Byelaws (WRWB): https://bpec.org.uk/water-regulations-england-and-wales-and-water-byelaws-scotland-and-ni/ (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Covers the 1999 Regulations, the Scottish Byelaws 2014 and the NI 2009 Regulations. Open book, 52 multiple-choice questions, 104 minutes, pass 42 of 52 (80%); 37 to 41 allows a second attempt on the questions missed. Content updated to a common UK industry training and assessment strategy (article dated 5 April 2023). passPct 80 is taken from BPEC, the only awarding body found that publishes a mark.
- LCL Awards: no official specification could be fetched. Provider pages say open book, online multiple choice. Not relied on.

## Primary law, England and Wales
- Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999, SI 1999/1148, contents: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1148/contents/made (fetched 2026-11-12)
- Regulations 1 to 6 as made and as revised (XML data): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1148/regulation/5 (fetched 2026-11-12). Gave: definitions (approved contractor, material change of use, regulator, supply pipe); reg 2 scope, one-month temporary supply exclusion, pre-1999 fittings; reg 3 waste, misuse, undue consumption, contamination, erroneous measurement; reg 4 quality and workmanlike (CE route omitted from 31 Dec 2020 by SI 2019/558); reg 5 notification table (230 l bath, 12 l/min pump, 750/1350 mm, 10,000 l pool), ten working days, deemed consent, approved contractor exemptions 2, 4(b), 4(g); reg 6 contractor's certificate.
- Schedule 1, fluid categories: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1148/schedule/1/made (fetched 2026-11-12)
- Schedule 2, requirements for water fittings: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1148/schedule/2 (fetched 2026-11-12). Paragraphs 1 to 31: definitions, materials, 1.5 times pressure, concealed fittings and dezincification, 25°C, stopvalves, test pressure and Tests A and B, flushing and disinfection, identification, backflow, cisterns, hot water devices (15 l, 100°C, 0.5 bar), WCs (6 l, two thirds, 1.2 l/s, 300 mm), urinals (10 and 7.5 l/h, 1.5 l), drinking taps, plugs, appliance limits (27, 48, 4.5), troughs and ponds.

## Regulator's specification (Defra and Welsh Government)
- Specification on the Prevention of Backflow, dated 6 May 2026: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69f2092ab0c3a4023e5d6e2f/_1_Specification_on_the_Prevention_of_Backflow.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12)
  Air gap definition (20 mm or twice bore, 15°), Types AA to AUK3, DC, BA to LB and Tables 1 and 2 with maximum fluid category for back pressure and back-siphonage; tap gaps 20/25/70 mm; DC 300 mm WC, 150 mm urinal; DA/DUK1 on a 300 mm Type A upstand; BA/CA relief outlets to a Type AA air gap.
  It comes into effect on 6 May 2027. Until then the earlier regulator's specification applies. The types and categories used in this bank are the same in both; the new Type AUK4 (interposed flushing cistern, 150 mm) is deliberately not asked until it is in force.
- Government response, preventing backflow from toilet cisterns: https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/water-fittings-preventing-backflow-of-water-from-toilet-cisterns/outcome/summary-of-responses-and-government-response (fetched 2026-11-12). Confirms AUK4 naming and the 12-month lead-in.

## Industry guidance (Water Regs UK, owned by the UK water undertakers)
- Guidance index and paragraph pages, e.g. https://www.waterregsuk.co.uk/guidance/schedule-2-paragraph-15-backflow-protection/ and https://www.waterregsuk.co.uk/guidance/schedule-2-paragraph-9-system-design-and-installation/ (fetched 2026-11-12). Gave: CR brass and gunmetal; concealed vs accessible basement; permeation, drain tap and weir overflow ingress examples; lead solder; design to 20°C; stopvalves isolate a zone; BS EN 806-2 (tests, storage), BS EN 806-4 (flushing), BS 1710 (marking); 25°C England and Wales vs 20°C Scotland and NI for expansion; Building Regulations meet the unvented safety requirement; material change of use examples; Scotland and NI notify Scottish Water and NI Water.
- Regulation 6 guidance: https://www.waterregsuk.co.uk/guidance/regulation-6/ (fetched 2026-11-12)
- Scotland requirements page: https://www.waterregsuk.co.uk/regulations/requirements-scotland/ (fetched 2026-11-12). Byelaw 5 notification to Scottish Water, ten working days; Schedule 2 paragraph 9 at 20°C.
- WaterSafe: https://www.watersafe.org.uk/advice/general_plumbing_advice/water_fittings_regulations_and_byelaws (fetched 2026-11-12). Approved contractors' schemes: APHC, CIPHE, SNIPEF, WIAPS. Landing only.

## Scotland and Northern Ireland
- Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws Extension Order 2024, SSI 2024/139: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2024/139/pdfs/ssi_20240139_en.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12). Byelaws 2014 made 13 May 2014, in force 12 July 2014, extended to 12 May 2029.
- Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009, SR 2009/255, Schedule 2 and regulation 5: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/255/schedule/2 (fetched 2026-11-12). 20°C in paragraphs 9 and 15(2).
- Scottish Water's byelaws booklet could not be fetched (host unreachable). Scottish facts are taken from Water Regs UK's Scotland page only, and are limited to 20°C and notification.

## Temperatures (HSE)
- HSG274 Part 2, Legionnaires' disease, hot and cold water systems: https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/priced/hsg274part2.pdf (fetched 2026-11-12). Cold below 20°C (within two minutes at outlets), hot stored at 60°C, 50°C at outlets within one minute (55°C healthcare), remove redundant pipework and deadlegs.

## Left out
- Paid British Standards (BS EN 806, BS 8558, BS EN 1717) are only named, never quoted.
- Fees, penalty levels, WRAS product approval processes, the content of the Water Regulations Guide, and Type AUK4 (in force 6 May 2027).
- Unvented cylinder G3 detail beyond Schedule 2 paragraphs 17 to 24 (the ukg3 bank covers it).
- Exam format, pass marks and certificate validity (landing only).
