All checked 2026-10-31.

  2026-10-31) lists the 2025 direct recruitment of Constable Civil Police and equivalent posts
  (आरक्षी नागरिक पुलिस एवं समकक्ष पद, सीधी भर्ती-2025): PET centre and date change notices of 7 and 8 October 2026 and

- Advt. 07/2025, 32,679 posts (Constable Civil Police, PAC, jail warder and others), notified 31 December 2025,

  careers360, https://competition.careers360.com/articles/up-police-constable-exam-date-2026-out (dates, 300 marks,
  offline mode); karmasandhan, https://www.karmasandhan.com/up-police-constable-exam-date/ (dates).

## Written exam pattern (secondary sources; the board's notice was not read)

- 150 objective questions, 300 marks (2 each), 2 hours; General Knowledge 38, General Hindi 37, Numerical and Mental
  Ability 38, Mental Aptitude / IQ / Reasoning 37. Agreed by every guide checked:
  - Physics Wallah exam analysis of the 24 August 2024 sitting (2023 recruitment),
    https://www.pw.live/state-govt-jobs/exams/up-police-constable-exam-analysis-24th-august-2024-difficulty-level
  - Physics Wallah syllabus page, https://www.pw.live/exams/state-govt-exams/up-police-constable-syllabus
    (also the syllabus topic list used for the domains)
- Negative marking: most guides (PW exam analysis, biharhelp, mycoaching) give **0.5 mark per wrong answer**; some
  aggregator pages for Advt. 07/2025 give 0.25 (or "1/4", which may mean a quarter of the 2 marks, i.e. 0.5), and
  some say there is none for the 2025/2026 cycle; Oliveboard's pattern page (fetched 2026-11-01) says it is "to be
  confirmed" for the next cycle. Reviewer re-check on 2026-11-01: uppbpb.gov.in /Home/Notice and
  /Home/DirectRecruitment list no advertisement PDF for the constable recruitment, its notice PDFs are scanned or in
  a legacy Hindi font that does not extract, and the application portal https://upcons2025.com is behind a
  Cloudflare challenge. Still unconfirmed, so the copy says guides disagree and "check your notification", and the
  app does not score it.
- Language: the GK, numerical and reasoning parts are bilingual (Hindi and English), per PW; General Hindi is in Hindi.
- Qualifying or minimum marks: not confirmed; left out of all copy.
- Selection after the written exam (document verification and physical standard test, physical efficiency test):
  described in general terms only.

## Syllabus followed (PW summary of the notification's syllabus)

- General Knowledge: India and neighbouring countries, science and technology, Indian history, culture, constitution,
  economy, agriculture, geography of India and the world, environment, plus Uttar Pradesh (education, culture,
  social practices). Built as static facts only: no current affairs, awards, office-holders or schemes.
  The police and law items (State List, 112, Article 22, BNS/BNSS/BSA from 1 July 2024, NHRC, 1930 cyber helpline)
  are static facts from the Constitution and central Acts.
- General Hindi: passage-based questions, synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, sentence correction, idioms and
  proverbs, plus the grammar commonly set (sandhi, samas, gender, number, karak, prefixes and suffixes, alankar, ras,
  authors). Passage and letter-writing items are not modelled.
- Numerical and Mental Ability: number system, simplification, decimals and fractions, HCF and LCM, ratio, percentage,
  profit and loss, discount, interest, partnership, average, time and work, time and distance, mensuration.
- Mental Aptitude, IQ and Reasoning: analogies, classification, series, coding-decoding, blood relations,
  directions, ranking, syllogism, calendar, clock, arrangements. Figure, Venn and space-visualisation items are left
  out (text-only engine).

## Modelling decisions

- Real length 150 questions in 120 minutes. Modelled at 100 questions in 80 minutes (25 per subject, the same
  0.8 minute a question), two practice tests, Test 2 held out of the drills, 32 drill-only items: 232 in all.
- Per test: GK 15 India + 10 UP/police/law; Hindi 13 shabd + 12 vyakaran; numerical 15 arithmetic + 10 numbers and
  mensuration; reasoning 13 verbal + 12 logical. The split inside each subject is Tigertest's; the board publishes
  only the subject totals.
- Questions are original. Items were checked against the ssccgl, rrbntpc, ibpspo and ctet banks and the batch's
  other India banks present on 2026-10-31 (rrbgroupd, sscgd, sscmts, jnvst), and rewritten where the same fact or the
  same numbers appeared.
