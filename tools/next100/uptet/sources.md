# UPTET Paper 1 sources

Checked 1 November 2026. The commission's own site (upessc.up.gov.in) returned HTTP 503 or reset the connection on
that date, and updeled.gov.in's UPTET page (DefaultTET.aspx) only says "Check Schedule Activity is Closed". The
commission's information booklet itself was read on review (below); the result figures come from news reports.

## Official information booklet (read 1 November 2026)
- UPESSC, Prayagraj, "Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test (UPTET)-2026, Adv. No. 01/UPTET/2026, Information Booklet"
  (32 pages, Hindi in Kruti Dev encoding), read from the copy at
  https://rojgarwithankit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/UPTET.pdf (an exam blog's copy of the commission's PDF; the
  commission's own site was down). Confirms:
  - Section 1: published 20-03-2026; registration and fee from 27-03-2026 to 26-04-2026; fee reconciliation and
    corrections to 01-05-2026; exam district notified 22 June 2026; admit cards 30 June 2026; exam 2, 3 and 4 July 2026.
  - Section 7: Paper 1 (Classes 1 to 5), all compulsory: Child Development and Pedagogy 30, Language I Hindi 30,
    Language II (English, Urdu or Sanskrit) 30, Mathematics 30, Environmental Studies 30; 150 MCQs, 150 marks.
  - Section 8: 2 hours 30 minutes (150 minutes); every question multiple choice with four options; no negative
    marking; 150 questions of 1 mark each; medium Hindi and English except the language subjects.
  - Section 9: more than one shift allowed, with score normalisation.
  - Section 10: certificate for 90 of 150 (60%) and above for general candidates; minimum 55%, that is 82 of 150, for
    SC, ST, OBC, EWS, dependants of freedom fighters, ex-servicemen and candidates with disabilities. Passing gives no
    right to a job.
  - Section 12: certificate valid for life for all categories from the result date (UP government letter
    392/68-4-2021-9(16), 16 June 2021).
  The earlier guide figure of applications closing on 3 May 2026 is not in the booklet (it may be an extension); the
  copy does not use it.

## Who runs it, and the 2026 cycle
- Careers360 news, "UPTET result 2026 out for 19 lakh; 80.07% pass paper 1, 71.85% clear paper 2" (26 August 2026),
  https://news.careers360.com/upessc-up-gov-in-uptet-result-2026-out-updeled-gov-in-direct-link-pass-percentage-paper-1-2-sarkari-exam
  Conducting body: Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC). Exam held 2, 3 and 4 July 2026 in five
  shifts at 955 centres in 60 districts; result 26 August 2026; Paper 1: 7,13,648 appeared, 5,71,435 passed (80.07%);
  results normalised across shifts under point 9 of the TET notification. Scorecards on updeled.gov.in and upessc.up.gov.in.
- Search results (Careers360, Career Power, NewsX, Mahendras, March 2026): notification 20 March 2026, applications
  27 March to 3 May 2026. Provisional answer key 8 July 2026, objections to 14 July 2026 (guides).
  Earlier reports gave 29 and 30 January 2026 as dates; the later sources and the result report confirm July.
- Special TET for serving teachers (after the Supreme Court's 2025 ruling that in-service teachers must hold a TET):
  registration 5 September to 4 October 2026; guides give the exam date as 3 or 11 November 2026 (conflicting).
  Not mentioned on the landing as a fact; it is a separate sitting for working teachers.
  https://news.careers360.com/up-tet-special-2026-dates-out-otr-starts-today-exam-dates-apply-upessc-gov-in-grievance-facility-5-days-supreme-court-order

## Paper 1 pattern (guides citing the 2026 notification)
- Careers360, "UPTET Exam Pattern 2026" (updated 22 March 2026), https://competition.careers360.com/articles/uptet-exam-pattern
  Paper 1 (Classes 1 to 5): offline OMR, 150 one-mark MCQs, 2 hours 30 minutes, no negative marking. Five sections of 30:
  Child Development and Pedagogy; Language I (Hindi); Language II (English, Urdu or Sanskrit, candidate's choice);
  Mathematics; Environmental Studies. Same five-section split on Utkarsh, Adda247, Oliveboard and Testbook syllabus pages.
- Qualifying marks (guides; now confirmed by the booklet as 90 and 82 of 150): 60% for general candidates, 55% for
  reserved categories. Testbook, "UPTET passing marks 2026", https://testbook.com/news/uptet-passing-marks-2026/ and
  Careers360, https://competition.careers360.com/articles/uptet-passing-marks-2026 .
- Certificate validity: lifetime, approved by the UP Chief Minister in 2021 following the central (NCTE) decision.
  https://news.careers360.com/uptet-2021-cm-approves-extension-of-tet-certificate-lifetime . Not used on the landing beyond
  "valid for life" in examFacts, labelled as the 2021 decision.

## Syllabus content used for the questions
- Syllabus topic lists (Adda247 UPTET syllabus in Hindi, https://www.adda247.com/teaching-jobs-exam/uptet-syllabus-in-hindi/ ;
  PW, https://www.pw.live/teaching/exams/uptet-syllabus): Hindi includes अपठित अनुच्छेद, वर्णमाला (स्वर, व्यंजन), वर्णों के
  उच्चारण स्थान, संधि, समास, पर्यायवाची, विलोम, लिंग, वचन, वाच्य, विराम चिह्न, अलंकार, रचनाकार and Hindi pedagogy;
  Maths and EVS follow NCERT Classes 1 to 5 content with pedagogy.
- Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009: Section 12(1)(c) (25% seats), Section 17 (no physical
  punishment or mental harassment), Section 21 (SMC, three-fourths parents or guardians), Schedule (PTR 30:1 primary).
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, Section 31 (free education, ages 6 to 18, benchmark disabilities).
- National Education Policy 2020 (5+3+3+4; foundational stage ages 3 to 8). NCF 2005 (EVS integrated with language and
  mathematics in Classes 1 and 2). NCERT EVS source book (assessment indicators). NCERT learning outcomes.
- Uttar Pradesh state symbols: state tree Ashoka, state flower Palash (UP government portal, up.gov.in, as listed by
  guides). State bird and animal are left out (used in the uppolice bank).
- Standard child development and pedagogy texts for Piaget, Vygotsky, Erikson, Kohlberg, Bruner, Gardner, Spearman,
  Thorndike, Pavlov, Skinner, Bandura, Kohler, Maslow, Miller, Ebbinghaus, Polya, Armstrong, Kilpatrick, Froebel, Montessori.

## Originality and overlap
All 200 questions and the six passages (three Hindi, three English) are original. No UPTET past paper, official sample or
coaching app item was used. Stems and keys were compared (token overlap plus same key) against every bank in data/v2 and
the Philippine and India v1 banks on 1 November 2026, including the sibling reet and aissee banks being written in the same
batch; about 40 items that repeated a fact and answer (mostly with reet, also biharpolice, nswsel, sscgd, ssccgl, rrbntpc,
sscmts, aissee, ocnsw) were rewritten. reet was still changing, so re-run the comparison after the batch lands.
