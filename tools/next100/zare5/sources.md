# Sources for `zare5` (RE5 Regulatory Examination for Representatives, South Africa)

All checked 2026-11-05. WebSearch was unavailable this session (the shared search budget was used up),
so only the pages and PDFs below, fetched directly, were read. The FSCA's own website was reachable

## Exam format (confirmed)

1. Preparation Guide for Regulatory Examinations (RE 1 and RE 5), "17 January 2026 - Version 1",
   header "Preparation Guide: Regulatory Examinations" (states that the exams are developed and
   delivered "under the direction and management of the FSCA"):
   https://www.moonstone.co.za/wp-content/uploads/library/Regulatory%20Exam%20Library/RE%20Preparation%20Guidelines/RE1%20and%20RE5%20Preparation%20Guideline.pdf
   - Section 8.11 table: RE 5, 50 questions, each question = 2%, duration 2 hours, pass mark 66%
     (RE 1: 80 questions, 1.25% each, 2 hours 30 minutes, 65%).
   - 3.1: multiple choice on the qualifying criteria; closed book; no limit on attempts; one
     national version per exam; one right answer per question; no negative marking; "Examinations
     will be done in paper format as indicated on their registration page."
   - 2: "Currently there is one examination body: Moonstone" (registration at faisexam.co.za).
   - 3.3: four options per question; styles include direct, negative, most/best/least and Roman
     numeral (more than one statement correct). Every task is covered at least once in an exam.
   - RE 5 layout: Level 1 30% (15), Level 2 40% (20), Level 3 20% (10), Level 4 10% (5) questions.
   - Random selection from a central question bank: two candidates do not get the same questions.
   - Qualifying criteria: Board Notice 105 of 2008, amended by BN 151/2008, BN 64/2009, BN 95/2009,
     BN 44/2010, BN 60/2010, BN 194 of 2017, FAIS Notice 86 of 2018.
   - "RE 5 has 8 tasks that will be tested."
   - Languages are not stated; left out of the landing.
2. RE5 task and qualifying criteria map, "Re5 Task QC.pdf", "14 January 2025 - Version 1":
   https://www.moonstone.co.za/wp-content/uploads/library/Regulatory%20Exam%20Library/RE%20Preparation%20Guidelines/Re5%20Task%20QC.pdf
   The eight tasks (with the number of qualifying criteria):
   1. Demonstrate understanding of the FAIS Act as a regulatory framework (4): the Act and
      subordinate legislation, financial services and products, applying product knowledge, the
      compliance officer.
   2. Contribute towards maintaining an FSP licence (9): licence requirements, display of licences,
      Medical Schemes Act accreditation, undesirable practices, offences.
   3. Define the role of the key individual (3).
   4. Adhere to the specific Codes of Conduct (14): general and specific duties, conflicts of
      interest, disclosures (provider, representative, product, fees and commission), the advice
      process, custody of products and funds, complaints, termination of agreements.
   5. Comply with regulated record keeping requirements (2): FAIS and FIC Acts.
   6. Comply with the FIC Act and the Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Control Regulations (2).
   7. Dealing with complaints submitted to the Ombud for FSPs (1), including the Ombud Council
      rules for the Ombud for FSPs, 2024.
   8. Operate as a representative (12): role, fit and proper (honesty, integrity, good standing,
      qualifications, experience, regulatory exams, CPD), advice versus intermediary services, the
      register, supervision (FAIS Notice 86 of 2018), debarment (FAIS Act s14, FSR Act s153 and
      s230, Guidance Note on debarment 1 of 2019).
3. Moonstone regulatory exam body page (states it operates "in terms of a mandate from the FSCA";
   exams at a venue with an invigilator; results within 20 working days):
   https://www.moonstone.co.za/services/regulatory-exam-body/
4. FSCA home and Financial Services Professionals pages (reachable; no exam detail):
   https://www.fsca.co.za/ and https://www.fsca.co.za/Financial-Services-Professionals

## Blueprint used here

No per-task weighting is published. Tigertest's split per 50-question test, led by the number of
qualifying criteria per task: Task 1 6, Task 2 6, Task 3 3, Task 4 13 (disclosure 7, advice and
complaints 6), Task 5 3, Task 6 5, Task 7 3, Task 8 11 (representative 7, debarment 4).
Four tests of 50 like the real length, 66% line. Four content sets.

## Content: law checked against official text on 2026-11-05

WebSearch stayed unavailable (shared budget used up), so these were fetched directly. Each is the
official text or the text published by the body concerned.

5. FAIS Act 37 of 2002, as amended to the FSR Act (Sabinet consolidation, 25 June 2018), published by
   the FAIS Ombud:
   https://www.faisombud.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/FINANCIAL-ADVISORY-AND-INTERMEDIARY-SERVICES-ACT-37-OF-2002.pdf
   (checked 2026-11-05). Confirmed: s8(8)(a) display a certified copy of the licence in every business
   premises (LIC-005, reworded from "licence conditions" to "the FAIS Act"), s8(8)(b) licence status in
   documents and adverts (LIC-004); s14(1) "is or was" a representative, s14(3)(a)(ii) copy of the
   written debarment policy and procedure (DEB-007 reworded from "guidelines"), s14(3)(c) notify of
   rights and Tribunal reconsideration (DEB-008), s14(4)(b)-(c) remove from register, protect clients
   (DEB-009, DEB-011), s14(4)(d)-(e) notify the Authority within 5 days, reasons within 15 (DEB-010),
   s14(5) debarment of a former rep must start within six months (DEB-016); s17(2) compliance officer
   approved by the Authority, approval withdrawn if not fit and proper. s17 says nothing about an
   external compliance officer, so FWK-022 was replaced by the withdrawal-of-approval rule; s18 records
   for five years incl. complaints (REC-001, REC-004, ADV-020); s20(3) informal, economical,
   by reference to what is equitable (OMB-002, OMB-009: "no charge" removed, Rule 9 allows costs);
   s27(3)(a) three-year time bar (OMB-005), s27(3)(b) court proceedings (OMB-006); s34(3) notice
   in the Gazette with 21 days for representations (LIC-014), s34(4) stop the practice (LIC-015),
   s34(5)-(6) direction to rectify within 60 days. s34 does not say "reimburse clients", so LIC-017
   now keys "Put right what it caused".
6. General Code of Conduct (BN 80 of 2003 as amended by BN 43 of 2008), published by the FAIS Ombud:
   https://www.faisombud.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/General-Code-of-Conduct-for-Authorised-Financial-Services-Providers-and-Representatives.pdf
   (checked 2026-11-05). Confirmed: s3(1)(a) plain language, timeously, monetary terms (DIS-003,
   DIS-023 to DIS-025); s4(1) and s5 oral information confirmed in writing within 30 days (DIS-004,
   narrowed to the provider details of s5); s4(1)(d)(i) more than 10% of the supplier's shares
   (DIS-014); s4(1)(d)(ii) more than 30% of total remuneration in the preceding 12 months (DIS-015);
   s4(4) comparisons must make the differences clear (new DIS-012); s10(1)(b) written confirmation of
   receipt of funds taken into safe custody without the mediation of a bank (ADV-016, reworded);
   s14(1)(a) adverts not fraudulent, untrue or misleading, s14(1)(b)(iv) past performance warning
   (DIS-027, DIS-028); s16(2) complaints recorded for five years (ADV-020); s19(1)(c) receipt
   promptly acknowledged in writing (new ADV-015); s19 proviso: client told the Ombud may be
   approached within six months (new ADV-021); s19(2) full redress without delay (new ADV-022);
   s21 waiver null and void (DIS-026).
   This copy predates the 2010 conflict of interest amendments and the later complaints
   amendments, which could not be fetched (FSCA pages 404). So the immaterial financial interest
   item (old DIS-012) and the "expression of dissatisfaction", "reportable complaint",
   "compensation payment" and "goodwill payment" items (old ADV-015, ADV-020 to ADV-022) were
   replaced with the confirmed rules above. The other conflict of interest items (DIS-006 to
   DIS-011, DIS-013) were not re-read against current text.
7. Ombud Council Rules for the Ombud for Financial Services Providers, 2024:
   https://www.faisombud.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Ombud-Council-Rules-for-the-Ombud-for-Financial-Services-Providers-FAIS-Ombud-2024.pdf
   (checked 2026-11-05). Made by the Ombud Council under FSR Act s201 (OMB-012). Rule 4(c): the
   respondent failed to resolve the complaint within six weeks (OMB-004); Rule 4(d): complaint to the
   Ombud within six months of the final response (ADV-021); Rule 7(2)(e) pending court proceedings;
   Rule 9 costs may be awarded against a complainant in some cases; Rule 10(2) complaints about
   unauthorised persons referred to the Authority (OMB-010 replaced: no rule on referring to another
   ombud scheme was found).
8. FAIS Ombud, How to complain, pre-requisites and appeals pages:
   https://www.faisombud.co.za/how-to-complain/pre-requisites/ (3 years, court proceedings) and
   https://www.faisombud.co.za/how-to-complain/appeals/ (reconsideration by the Financial Services
   Tribunal under FSR Act s230; OMB-008, DEB-013) (checked 2026-11-05).
9. Financial Sector Regulation Act 9 of 2017, published by the FAIS Ombud:
   https://www.faisombud.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/FSRA-Financial-Sector-Regulation-Act-9-of-2017-ENG.pdf
   (checked 2026-11-05). s153 debarment by the responsible authority (DEB-012), s230 reconsideration.
10. Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements for FSPs, BN 194 of 2017, Government Gazette 41321,
    15 December 2017: https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201712/41321bn194.pdf
    (checked 2026-11-05). Confirmed: categories I to IV (Category II discretionary, III administrative,
    IV assistance business; FWK-024); s26(6) regulatory exams passed before appointment and Table A
    (RE 5 for representatives in every category); s28(3) product specific training not required of
    Category II, IIA and III; s28(4) and s29(2) key individuals per class of business (KEY-009
    reworded from "licence categories"); s29(1) class of business and product specific training before
    rendering services (REP-008, REP-009); CPD cycle 1 June to 31 May and 6/12/18 hours (REP-011).
    Not found in BN 194: "pass the RE within two years of first appointment" (s26(6) says before
    appointment; any supervision exemption in FAIS Notice 86 of 2018 was not fetched), so REP-010 now
    asks which exam a representative writes; and "no services until CPD is compliant", so REP-012 now
    asks for the CPD cycle dates.
11. Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001, as amended (FIC):
    https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Financial-Intelligence-Centre-Act-2001-Act-38-of-2001.pdf
    (checked 2026-11-05). Confirmed: Schedule 1 item 12 (FSPs advising on the investment of financial
    products, excluding non-life insurance, reinsurance and medical scheme business; FIC-002);
    s21F foreign politically exposed person needs senior management approval (FIC-011, term updated
    from "foreign prominent public official"); Schedule 3A lists the head of a national or provincial
    department as a domestic politically exposed person, while a "prominent influential person"
    (Schedule 3C) is a company officer supplying the state, so FIC-010 was wrong and now keys
    "Domestic politically exposed person"; s21G enhanced measures if higher risk; s23 records five
    years from termination of the relationship or from the transaction (REC-009, REC-010); s24 records
    kept by a third party with free and easy access, the institution stays liable (REC-008); s29
    proposed transactions and transactions only enquired about (FIC-014), no minimum amount (FIC-015).

Other law the tasks name (Medical Schemes Act broker accreditation, Long-term Insurance Act policy
definitions, FAIS Notice 86 of 2018) was not re-read this session.

No rand amounts, fees or the immaterial financial interest limit appear in the bank.
