Known limits to disclose after implementation: no validated malnutrition-screening instrument scoring; MDS coverage is limited to selected Section K instructions; limited laboratory interpretation, disease-specific diet breadth, advanced equipment/system design, procurement law, complex capital financing and marketing. Public sources support selected competencies, not replacement of CBDM's textbooks. Clinical questions implement existing plans and do not ask the CDM to prescribe.

Final depth pass replaces 41 items (18 nutrition, 9 personnel, 6 foodservice, 6 safety, 2 business), plus SAF-046 factual correction and NUT-005 wording correction. Stable IDs and headline counts remain. This is selected substantive coverage, not a claim that all outline subdomains are tested.
