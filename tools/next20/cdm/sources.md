Every question is original, written from primary standards and rules. No official sample questions, past questions, question pools or commercial practice items were used.

- CBDM Exam Content Outline, effective March 1, 2025: https://www.cbdmonline.org/exam-prep/exam-content-outline (fetched 2026-11-17). Five domains: Nutrition 20%, Foodservice 22%, Personnel and Communications 20%, Sanitation and Safety 24%, Business Operations 14%. The practice blueprint reproduces these percentages exactly.

## Scope and omissions

The 200-item bank is a focused study supplement across the five weighted domains, not an exhaustive replacement for the two ANFP textbooks or a 160-item timed simulation. The textbooks were not purchased or reproduced. It emphasizes screening and care-plan implementation, standardized production, staffing and communication, food safety and operational arithmetic. It does not model full medical nutrition therapy, detailed disease-specific nutrient prescriptions, all employment-law exceptions, every pathogen, all equipment/culinary techniques or jurisdiction-specific law. Fees, changeable workforce statistics, sample questions and official pool material are excluded. CDM eligibility and certification activation are not taught as subject questions. No individual question is evidence of professional clinical competence.

The format and source facts were checked 2026-11-17. Independent model review is separate from authorship; no human or native professional review is claimed.
