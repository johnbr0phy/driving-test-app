# EPA 609 source gate, 2026-11-18

The 100-question original bank is based on primary narrative/regulatory text, never official samples, review questions, or test pools. No question material was used. The sample gate was accepted for expansion; full independent review is pending. Editorial question-and-answer panels encountered inside the training manual are excluded from the objective inventory.

- EPA, Section 609 Technician Training and Certification Programs. https://www.epa.gov/mvac/section-609-technician-training-and-certification-programs (fetched 2026-11-18; page updated 2026-11-02). Required subject areas and approved providers, including MACS. EPA supplies no national percentage blueprint here.
- Current 40 CFR Part 82 Subpart B, particularly 82.40(a)(2). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-B (fetched/read through web tool 2026-11-18, page states current through 2026-11-16). Required topics, applicability, equipment, recordkeeping, and incorporated service standards. Direct Python HTTP fetch encountered an access challenge; it is not recorded as a successful source cache.
- MACS Certification Training Manual, revised January 2025, linked as the current manual by the approved provider. https://macsmobileairclimate.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/MACS-609-CertTrainingManual-2025.pdf (fetched 2026-11-18). Cover, foreword, contents and printed pages 5-18, 20-28, 31-37, 42-52 inspected for scope and candidate procedural objectives. Printed pages 19 and 29-31 contain editorial Q&A; no objectives are derived from those panels. Printed pages 38-41 contain historical SNAP listings/fitting tables, which are not treated as current law.

## Current law and official guidance

All fetched/read 2026-11-18:

- EPA Regulatory Requirements for MVAC System Servicing. https://www.epa.gov/mvac/regulatory-requirements-mvac-system-servicing Updated 2026-04-08. Handling, certification, equipment registration, records, disposal, fittings, current AIM transition distinction between new vehicles and servicing existing ones.
- EPA Section 608 and Section 609 Overlap. https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-and-section-609-overlap Updated 2026-11-16. MVAC-like equipment, R-22 buses, cargo exclusion and purchase boundaries. Its small-can shorthand says fewer than 2 pounds; use the dedicated sales page's precise two-pounds-or-less rule and current primary regulation before authoring any boundary item.
- EPA Refrigerant Sales Restriction. https://www.epa.gov/section608/refrigerant-sales-restriction Updated 2026-03-23. Certification-aligned purchase authority, employer purchases, DIY substitute-can exception and wholesaler records.
- EPA Acceptable Refrigerants and their Impacts. https://www.epa.gov/mvac/acceptable-refrigerants-and-their-impacts Environmental effects, SNAP use conditions, new-system versus retrofit approval and new-vehicle transition rules.
- EPA Certified Equipment. https://www.epa.gov/mvac/certified-equipment Equipment standards and certification listings. Not a source of model-specific equipment operation instructions.
- EPA Unique Fittings and Label Colors. https://www.epa.gov/mvac/unique-fittings-and-label-colors Refrigerant-specific connection and label requirements. A long memorization bank of obscure legacy fitting sizes is not planned.
- EPA Choosing and Using a Retrofit Refrigerant for a CFC-12 MVAC. https://www.epa.gov/mvac/choosing-and-using-retrofit-refrigerant-cfc-12-mvac Updated 2026-01-20. Required labels, recovery, cutoff switches and barrier hoses.

## Limits and exclusions

This is refrigerant handling/recovery certification preparation, not a general automotive diagnosis qualification. No primary exam weightings were found. No claim of complete official test simulation is supportable. Exclude question/test material, obsolete SNAP phaseout tables, fees/fines, pending changes, unrelated stationary refrigeration procedures, general engine/HVAC diagnostics, repeated reverse formulations, and equipment certification-laboratory test-fixture minutiae. Specific medical first-aid prose and questionable manual rounding examples are not candidate objectives.

Source-cache manifest and raw files remain outside the repository at `/Users/johnbrophy/tigertest-next20-progress/epa609-research`. `fetch_sources.py` reproduces downloads with hashes and explicitly records cache failures. `inventory.py` regenerates `objective-inventory.json`; its entries are candidate objectives, not validated questions or complete evidence mappings.

The selected design is 100 original questions and four 25-question tests, following the question count of MACS online/home delivery. The former 200-item default is intentionally not used. Current 40 CFR 82.36(a)(10) was separately fetched/read on 2026-11-18 to confirm dual-refrigerant J3030 equipment; (a)(9) instead covers J2851 contaminated recovery. No future transition dates are tested.
