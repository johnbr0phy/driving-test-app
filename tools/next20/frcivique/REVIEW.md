# Examen civique: review record (2026-11-18)

All reviews are model reviews. The French was written and reviewed by models only; no human or native-speaker review is claimed.

## Build
- Research from official sources only (research.md in the session scratchpad, summarised in sources.md): arrêté du 10 octobre 2025, CESEDA, service-public.gouv.fr, the formation-civique study sheets.
- Five writers, one per theme, wrote 240 original questions from the official sheets and primary texts. The ministry's lists of knowledge questions were read for coverage only and never copied.
- Each pool is six blocks; tests 1-2 at about A2 (carte de séjour pluriannuelle), 3-4 B1 (carte de résident), 5-6 B2 (naturalisation).
- A check pass fixed answer-pattern tells ("public", "droits", "seulement"), the key-longest rate (27% to 20%) and one stem.

## Round 1
- Blind key review (keys hidden, options shuffled, official sources): 240 of 240 matched. Flags: INS-021 (second answer arguable: family benefits go to the tribunal judiciaire), DRS-029 (direct or indirect discrimination arguable), HIS-025/026 (ordinals), DRS-033 (stem gave the key). All rewritten.
- Options-only review: 75% (ceiling 82%), 8 surface-cue hits, all rewritten. The reviewer also showed that in the situation items the key was never the hedged option ("si", "sauf", "seulement"). About a quarter of situation keys are now correct conditional rules and some wrong options are sweeping principled statements.

## Round 2 (fresh reviewers)
- Blind key review: 239 of 240 matched; the miss was DRS-029 again, so it now asks whether the rule is permitted (Service-Public's own example) rather than which kind of discrimination it is. HIS-009 said the Code civil was promulgated by an emperor (it was promulgated in March 1804, before the Empire); now "Napoléon Bonaparte". PRS-032 wording fixed.
- Options-only review: 74% (ceiling 82%), 5 surface-cue hits (under the 3% limit): PASS. The 5 were rewritten anyway (DRO-030 now uses other articles of the Charte as wrong options).

## Round 3 (changed items only)
An independent reviewer answered the 8 changed items blind: all correct, no second answer or factual error. Two option-form weaknesses (DRS-032, PRS-035) and one filler option (DRS-029) were fixed.

## Known limits
- Model French review only.
- Values items (laïcité, equality, discrimination) can partly be answered by someone who knows basic French values without the question; that is the content of the real exam's situations.
- Not modelled: which nationalities the bilateral agreements exempt; whether the 12 situations differ by mention.
