# Examen civique: sources

All 240 questions are original, written in French from official texts and the ministry's study sheets. The ministry's published lists of knowledge questions (CSP, CR, naturalisation) were read only to check coverage; no listed question was copied or closely reworded, and no commercial question bank was used. Model authorship and model language review only; no human or native-speaker review is claimed. Every source below was read on 2026-11-18.

## Levels

Each pool is six blocks in test order. Blocks 1 and 2 are written at about A2 (carte de séjour pluriannuelle), 3 and 4 at B1 (carte de résident), 5 and 6 at B2 (naturalisation), following the operator's statement of the language level per mention. `item-source-map.json` gives each item's notion, level and sources.
