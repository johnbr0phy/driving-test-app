## Format limits and deliberate exclusions

Official theory: 107 prompts (91 QCM + 16 QRC), 230 minutes, coefficients 3/2/3/2/1/3/3. Weighted mean at least 10/20 AND each domain at least 6/20, except English at least 4/20. Separate practical admission includes at least twenty minutes driving, minimum 12/20. App: four 50-question single-answer practices, study target 35/50 (70%), no official-score conversion. QRC production/spelling, official minima/weighting, independent scores, oral interaction and practical driving are not reproduced. Whether official QCM permit multiple correct choices remains unconfirmed from accessible instructions: copy describes our single-answer format without asserting official single-answer equivalence.

Excluded: official items, fees, fines, changing tax/social thresholds, platform pay floors, pending implementing rules, photos/video assessment and independent medical advice. Current code overrides misleading summaries: register updates fifteen clear days, six-month leases qualify, electric/hybrid exceptions retain general seat limits. Historical transport compilation and stale ONISR overview are not fact authorities. Coverage supports chosen objectives, not a claim to exhaust every syllabus detail. Full-source retrieval logs remain outside the repository in the coordinating research directory.
