# Sources for `barber` (barber state board written theory exam)

All 200 questions are original. No commercial question bank, past paper or the NIC sample
questions were copied; the outline fixed the topics and the references fixed the facts. A
near-duplicate scan against `data/cosmetology-questions.json` was run and the overlapping stems
rewritten from a different angle. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Test format and outline
- NIC (National-Interstate Council of State Boards of Cosmetology), National Barber Theory
  Examination Candidate Information Bulletin, effective June 1, 2025, revision date September 1,
  2025 (mirror read 2026-10-30: https://prov-testing.github.io/nic_cib/barber_national_theory_english.html).
  - 60 items, 50 weighted (scored); 90 minutes.
  - Domains: Scientific Concepts 35% (infection control and safety; human anatomy and physiology,
    skin and hair disorders; basic chemistry of barbering products), Implements and Equipment Used
    in Barbering 10%, Hair Care Services 40% (consultation and documentation; draping, shampoo,
    scalp treatments; haircutting, outlining, neck shave; wet, blowdry, thermal and natural hair
    styling; hair enhancements; head shaving; preliminary tests, haircolor, lightening, foiling,
    relaxing and curl reduction, chemical waving), Facial Hair and Skin Care Services 15%
    (consultation, draping, the shave service, facial hair design, facials, electrotherapy and
    light therapy).
- NIC also publishes a Barber 1 (No Chemical) theory bulletin with different weights (Scientific
  Concepts 40%); not modelled here.
- Pass mark: NIC reports a scaled score; the passing score is set by each state board (75 in most
  NIC states, per the cosmetology build's research; not re-verified board by board).

## References named in the NIC bulletin (reference date September 1, 2025)
- Milady Standard Barbering, 7th edition (2026) and 6th edition (2017).
- Pivot Point Fundamentals: Barbering (2022).
Facts were written from general knowledge of these standard texts plus EPA, OSHA (Bloodborne
Pathogens and Hazard Communication standards, formaldehyde hazard alert on hair smoothing
products) and FDA (no haircolor on brows or lashes) guidance.

## Weighting (per 50-question test)
Infection 8, anatomy/disorders 7, chemistry 3 (Scientific Concepts 18 = 36%); implements 5 (10%);
consultation/shampoo 5, haircutting/head shaving 7, styling 3, chemical services 5 (Hair Care 20 =
40%); shaving/facial hair design 4, facials/electrotherapy 3 (Facial Hair and Skin 7 = 14%).
Pools are 4x per test, so no item repeats across tests.

## Universal content only
Training hours, fees, license renewal, laws and rules and pass marks vary by state and are not
asserted. Clipper guard lengths (number 2 = about 1/4 inch) follow the common standard set; the
explanation tells candidates to check the maker's chart.
