# Nova Scotia Class 7 learner's knowledge test (cans) sources

Read October 30, 2026.

## Official
- Nova Scotia Driver's Handbook, PDF served from https://novascotia.ca/sns/rmv/safe/handbook.asp and
  https://www.novascotia.ca/documents/drivers-handbook (same file, `drivers-handbook.pdf`): "Reprinted
  with revisions, 2003 ... 2013 and 2017", with 2019 insert pages (restricted individual stage since
  April 1, 2015, learner licence valid 2 years, slow down and move over at 60 km/h, yield to transit
  buses at 60 km/h or less). Chapters used: 1 licence and demerit tables, 2 rules of the road,
  3 signs, markings and work zones, 4 safety, 5 adverse conditions and emergencies, 6 impairment.
- Take a driver knowledge test: Learner's Licence (class 7):
  https://www.novascotia.ca/take-driver-knowledge-test-learners-licence-class-7
  Two parts (Rules of the Road Test, Road Sign Recognition Test), 20 multiple-choice questions each,
  at least 16 correct on each part, 30 minutes per part, alone with no materials; online in English
  and French on a desktop or laptop with webcam, or in person at the RMV or Access Nova Scotia;
  retake only the failed part, no wait online, next day in person; age 16+, written consent at 16 or
  17; after passing: vision test, ID, consent, licence fee, photo.
- Learner's Licence (RMV permit directory): https://novascotia.ca/sns/paal/rmv/paal378.asp
  Valid 2 years (since April 1, 2015); supervising driver in the front seat with a regular licence,
  not in GDL; no other passengers (examiner and dual-brake instructor exceptions); zero BAC;
  at least 12 months before the road test, 9 with an approved course (25 h classroom + 10 h driving);
  4 or more demerit points = 6-month suspension.
- Newly Licensed Driver (Class 5N): https://novascotia.ca/sns/paal/rmv/paal379.asp
  Zero BAC; one front-seat passenger, no more passengers than belts; midnight to 5 a.m. curfew unless
  a supervising driver is in the front seat or a work exemption is held; 6 points = 6-month
  suspension; exit needs 2 years and an approved driver improvement course.
- Traffic Safety Act changes, in force October 19, 2026:
  https://novascotia.ca/changes-to-traffic-safety-legislation/ (child restraints: rear-facing to at
  least 2, harness to at least 4, booster 4 to 9 and under 145 cm; at least 1 m when passing a
  bicycle; "stop, look, open, close" dooring rule; curb parking distance 15 cm to 30 cm; school bus
  stop not required across a divided highway). Act text:
  https://nslegislature.ca/sites/default/files/legc/statutes%20HTML/traffic%20safety.htm
  (crossing guard paddle: stop no closer than 5 m from the crosswalk). The new regulations
  (N.S. Reg. 222-242/2026) are listed at https://novascotia.ca/just/regulations/regsbyact.htm#traffic
  but their text was "being prepared" on October 30, 2026.

## Choices
- Format: five 40-question tests, each 20 sign items and 20 rules items, mirroring the real two
  20-question parts; 80% pass (16 of 20 per part). The engine scores the 40 together; the landing
  tells users to check 16 of 20 on each half.
- Where the 2017 handbook and the Traffic Safety Act differ, the bank avoids the item or uses the
  current rule: no item on the curb distance (15 vs 30 cm), the school bus on a divided highway, the
  collision-report dollar threshold or vehicle inspection frequency; child seats and the cyclist gap
  use the Act.
- Learner wait (12/9 months) and supervisor rule use the current RMV pages, not the handbook's older
  6/3 months.
- No dollar amounts. Signs are described in words (no images).

## Reviewer check (October 30, 2026)
The new regulations were readable as filed PDFs (https://novascotia.ca/just/regulations/regs/2026-NNN.pdf):
- Using the Road Regulations (230/2026): 80 km/h default, 50 km/h in an "urban area" (homes and
  businesses fronting the road for 100 m) and parks (s. 64); school area limit is the posted sign and
  applies only when a child is outdoors on the road or within 30 m of its centre line (s. 66); no
  50 km/h within 30 m of a railway crossing any more; stop no closer than 5 m from the nearest rail
  (s. 19); stop sign with no line or crosswalk: before the cross road, no more than 1.5 m back
  (s. 40); left of a solid single line allowed to pass if clear, never with 2+ lanes your way, double
  solid only to pass a cyclist or pulled-over slow vehicle (s. 32); flashing green ball "may proceed"
  (s. 45); right on red, left on red one-way to one-way (s. 50); failed signal = stop (s. 42);
  overtaking a car stopped at a crosswalk allowed only after yielding to pedestrians (s. 21(3));
  emergency vehicles: 60 km/h and move over, same roadway only (s. 92), fire vehicle 150 m and hose
  only on a fire official's direction (s. 94); transit bus yield at 60 km/h or less (s. 95); high
  beams off within 150 m oncoming, 60 m following (s. 76); nighttime = 1/2 h after sunset to 1/2 h
  before sunrise (Interpretation Regs 221/2026); commercial vehicles 60 m apart above 60 km/h (s. 39);
  no parking within 5 m of a hydrant, 15 m of the nearest rail (s. 147); rear-facing seat never in
  front of an active frontal airbag (s. 98).
- Drivers Licence Regulations (226/2026): learner supervision and passengers (s. 6), 5N curfew and
  passengers (s. 5), zero alcohol/drugs for learners, 5N and novice drivers (s. 7); suspension at 10
  (fully licensed), 6 (5N/6N), 4 (Class 7/8) points, 6 months (ss. 21-22); points removed 24 months
  after conviction (s. 25). Interpretation Regs s. 16: novice driver includes the first 2 years after
  the newly licensed stage (the old "restricted individual" class no longer exists).
- Driver Licensing Requirements Regulations (236/2026): 12 months, 9 with a Class 5 driver education
  course (25 h theory + 10 h in-vehicle), Class 7 valid 2 years, Class 5 after 24 months of 5N plus
  an approved course.
- Act s. 221: after hitting an unattended vehicle, leave a written notice and report to police
  within 24 hours; s. 34 crossing guard paddle; s. 190 hand-held devices; s. 211 every passenger
  belted, driver responsible under 16.

## Not verified
- Handbook-only advice (stopping distances, 4-second rain gap, rest every 150-200 km, 30 m from a
  vehicle fire, 20 m from a downed line) is not in the regulations; kept as safe-driving advice.
- Sign descriptions come from the handbook; the Traffic Control Devices Regulations PDF is a scan
  that could only be partly read.
