# Saskatchewan Class 7 learner (cask) sources

Read October 30, 2026. The handbook PDF was downloaded from sgi.sk.ca and its text extracted in the
session scratchpad (`cask-src/handbook.txt`); it is not committed.

## Official
- Saskatchewan Driver's Handbook, "A guide to safe driving", 2026-27 edition (SGI, print code
  SLB143 07/2026; text "accurate as of September 2025", online version at sgi.sk.ca/handbook):
  https://sgi.sk.ca/documents/37148/138037/drivers_handbook.pdf
  - 2.3 Graduated Driver Licensing: Learner 9 months (16, or 15 in high school), Novice 1 6 months,
    Novice 2 12 months incident-free (restarts from the loss or conviction date); restrictions table
    (supervisor in front seat at all hours; novice: one non-family passenger, family only from
    midnight to 5 a.m.; all stages: passengers limited to seatbelts, no cellphone hand-held or
    hands-free, no right-hand drive, zero BAC, cannot supervise).
  - 2.5 Licence Class 7: Basic and Sign knowledge tests; supervisor holds Class 5 or greater (Canada
    or U.S.), valid licence 365 days in the preceding three years, not a novice, seat nearest the
    driver. Parental consent under 18 cannot be withdrawn.
  - 2.8 Mandatory driver education: high school program (30 h class, 6 h car) or 6 h + 6 h with an
    SGI-certified instructor.
  - 2.10-2.11 Tests: knowledge tests in two parts (driving situations and rules of the road; sign
    identification), rewrite only the failed part, results valid one year, once per day; vision test;
    Class 7 held nine months; written tests in Regina and Saskatoon without appointment.
  - 3 Rules of the road (80 km/h unposted, 60 km/h past stopped emergency vehicles, tow trucks and
    plows, 5 m from a school bus, signalling 30 m / 150 m, stopping positions 3 m / 10 m, right-of-way,
    roundabouts, DDI, RCUT, headlights 200 m / 100 m, parking lots).
  - 4 Traffic signs and signals (shapes, colours, regulatory, warning, construction, work zone
    fines within 3 km, railway crossings, crossroad delineators, signals).
  - 5-6 Driving skills, winter driving, emergencies, animals, collisions, power lines.
  - 7-8 Drinking, drugs and driving; Traffic Safety Act (48 hours to produce licence, impoundments,
    new driver zero tolerance and roadside suspensions).
- SGI Graduated Driver Licensing page (same content as 2.3):
  https://sgi.sk.ca/handbook/-/knowledge_base/drivers/graduated-driver-licensing-program

## Not verified (not in the handbook, not on a reachable official page)
- Question count and pass mark. SGI publishes neither in the handbook; sgi.sk.ca's learner and quiz
  pages are client-rendered and returned no text to a direct fetch. Prep sites report 80% per part,
  and some report 40 questions with 32 to pass. The bank uses 80% as a practice target and the
  landing says SGI does not publish the figures.
- Fee: prep sites give $25 per attempt. Not used anywhere.

## Choices
- Pass mark 80 (practice target, labelled as such).
- Signs (14) + lights, markings and railway crossings (7) = 21 of 50, mirroring the separate Sign
  part; the rest follows the handbook's rules chapters.
- No dollar amounts, demerit or SDR point values are asked (house style), even though the handbook
  lists them. Impoundment is asked only as "the vehicle is impounded", not its length.
- Sign designs are described in words from the handbook's shapes and colours and the standard
  Canadian symbols; the landing tells readers to study the handbook pictures.
