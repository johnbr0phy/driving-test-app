# Sources for `cpc` (AAPC Certified Professional Coder exam)

All 200 questions are original, written by Tigertest from public guidelines and our own summaries
of coding concepts. No AAPC practice exam, study guide or commercial question bank was copied, and
no item asks the candidate to look up or reproduces a CPT code descriptor (CPT is AMA copyright).
Items test the rules and concepts a coder applies with the code books open. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Exam format
- AAPC, "Taking the CPC Exam": https://aapc.com/certifications/cpc/taking-the-cpc-exam
  (checked 2026-10-30): 100 multiple-choice questions, one sitting, 4 hours; 70% overall to pass
  (70 of 100); open code book (AMA CPT Professional Edition, ICD-10-CM and HCPCS Level II, current or
  prior year, print or eBook); online calculator; testing center or live remote proctor; $425 one
  attempt or $499 two attempts (fees kept off the site). 17 areas plus 10 cases:
  terminology 4, anatomy 4, compliance and regulatory 3, coding guidelines 7, ICD-10-CM 5, HCPCS
  Level II 3, CPT 10000 to 60000 series 6 each, radiology 6, pathology and laboratory 6, medicine 6,
  E/M 6, anesthesia 4, cases 10. Failing candidates are told areas scored 65% or less.
- AAPC, "How long is the exam": most AAPC exams are 100 questions in 4 hours.

## Blueprint used here (per 50-question test)
Halved from the AAPC counts: terminology 2, anatomy 2, compliance 2, guidelines 4, ICD-10-CM 3,
HCPCS 2, each surgery series 3 (18), radiology 3, lab 3, medicine 3, E/M 3, anesthesia 2, cases 3.
Cases are short scenarios testing rules (modifier choice, repair addition, outpatient uncertain
diagnoses, sequencing) instead of full medical records that need the code books.

## Content sources
- ICD-10-CM Official Guidelines for Coding and Reporting, FY2026 (CDC NCHS / CMS; public domain):
  Excludes1/Excludes2, placeholder X, 7th characters, hypertension presumed links, diabetes default
  type 2 and Z79.4 + Z79.84 together (since FY2022), chapter 15 priority, adverse effect sequencing,
  fracture defaults, Section IV outpatient uncertain diagnoses, external cause codes.
- CMS HCPCS Level II code set and modifier lists (public domain): structure, sections (A, E, J, L,
  K, Q, G, S), GA/GY/GZ, F and T digit modifiers, TC, QW, anesthesia modifiers AA/QK/QX/QZ/QS.
- CMS Medicare Claims Processing Manual ch. 12 (global surgery package, day before major surgery;
  anesthesia time, base and time units, medical direction; consultation codes not paid since 2010)
  and the NCCI Policy Manual (PTP edits, MUEs, incidental appendectomy, surgical endoscopy includes
  diagnostic).
- CPT conventions and section guidelines as taught in coding courses, paraphrased: symbols, Category
  II and III, unlisted codes, modifiers, repair classification and addition, lesion sizing, Mohs,
  graft units, fracture treatment types, arthroscopy, spinal fusion per interspace, central venous
  access, venous CABG, 2023 anterior abdominal hernia codes, obstetric package, epidural codes with
  imaging guidance (2017), "with contrast", ultrasound recorded images, panels, surgical pathology
  levels, vaccine product plus administration, infusion/push/hydration time rules, moderate sedation
  (10 minutes, 2017), 2021 office E/M (MDM or time, two of three elements), 2023 ED (MDM only) and
  merged hospital inpatient/observation codes, MDM high risk for drug therapy needing intensive
  monitoring for toxicity, critical care under 30 minutes.
- OIG General Compliance Program Guidance (November 2023): seven elements. False Claims Act, Anti-
  Kickback Statute, Stark Law, HIPAA Privacy Rule minimum necessary standard.

## Not verified against a 2026 CPT book
We do not have the 2026 CPT Professional Edition. Items rely on long-standing CPT guidelines that did
not change in the 2024-2026 editions as far as public summaries show (CPT 2026 changes were mainly
new codes, remote monitoring and digital medicine). Re-check items CPC-NRV-002 (epidural codes and
imaging), CPC-DIG-005/006 (hernia), CPC-EM-011 (inpatient/observation) and CPC-MED-009/010
(sedation) against each new CPT edition.
