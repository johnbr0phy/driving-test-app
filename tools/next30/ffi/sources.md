# Sources for `ffi` (Firefighter I written certification)

All 200 items are original, written from the standards and public guidance below. No
certifying agency's test bank, IFSTA or Jones & Bartlett textbook text, or provider is copied or
named in any stem.

- **NFPA 1010, Standard on Professional Qualifications for Firefighters, 2024 edition** (issued
  Dec 2023, effective Jan 2024). It consolidated NFPA 1001 (2019), 1002, 1003 and 1005;
  Firefighter I JPRs are now Chapter 6. Content outline for the bank. Adoption varies: some states
  still certify to NFPA 1001 (2019); Firefighter I content is essentially the same.
  (Checked by web search 2026-10-30: Oregon DPSST NFPA 1010 Firefighter guide and task book,
  Iowa IAC 661-251 referencing the 2024 edition.)
- **NFPA 470** (hazmat awareness/operations, now consolidated) for the few awareness items; DOT
  Emergency Response Guidebook colour sections (orange guides, yellow ID index, blue name index,
  green protective distances); 49 CFR placard size 273 mm (10.75 in).
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134** respiratory protection: two-in, two-out, IDLH, facial hair and annual
  fit testing; 19.5% oxygen-deficient threshold.
- **NFPA SCBA and PASS requirements** (formerly NFPA 1981/1982, consolidated into NFPA 1970 in
  2025): low-air alarm at 33% of rated pressure (since the 2013 edition of 1981; NFPA 1970-2025 restates it by volume, 31% remaining on 4,500 psi, per MSA and 3M compliance notices), so the bank says "about one-third"; PASS full
  alarm after about 30 s without motion. Stems say "current NFPA SCBA standard" without a number.
- **US DOT** cylinder rules: composite SCBA cylinders hydrostatically tested every 5 years,
  15-year service life.
- **NFPA 291** hydrant colour code (AA light blue >= 1,500 gpm, A green 1,000-1,499, B orange
  500-999, C red < 500).
- **UL 711** extinguisher rating (1-A = 1.25 gal water equivalent).
- **UL FSRI / NIST** ventilation and flow-path research (coordinated ventilation, VEIS door
  control, transitional attack).
- Widely taught fire-service practice that every Firefighter I curriculum covers: nozzle
  pressures (50 psi smooth-bore handline, 80 master stream, 100 fog), ladder climbing angle
  ~75 degrees and the working-length/4 rule, 10 ft from power lines, hydraulic ventilation 85-90%
  of the opening, ICS span of control 3 to 7, LUNAR mayday, water facts (8.34 lb/gal,
  7.48 gal/cu ft, 1,700:1 steam expansion).

Real exam format: each state agency or IFSAC/Pro Board-accredited body sets its own written
exam; many use about 100 multiple-choice items and a 70% pass mark, plus a practical skills
exam. The bank models four 50-item mock tests at 70%. Weights are ours (no national blueprint
is published), following the Chapter 6 JPR groupings.

Not verified against the paywalled NFPA 1010 text itself: exact Chapter 6 JPR numbering. The bank
cites no paragraph numbers.
