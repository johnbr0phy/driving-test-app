# NASM-CPT sources (checked 2026-10-30)

- NASM, "How many questions are on the CPT exam and how long do I have": 120 questions, 20 of them
  unscored research items, 2-hour limit, passing scaled score of 70.
  https://support.nasm.org/how-many-questions-are-on-the-cpt-exam-and-how-long-do-i-have-to-complete-it
  and https://support.nasm.org/how-many-attempts-am-i-provided-for-my-nasm-personal-trainer-certificate-exam-and-what-is-the-passing-score
- NASM, "Updated NASM CPT exam blueprint" (CPT7, based on the 2019 job analysis):
  https://www.nasm.org/docs/pdf/cpt7-nasm-exam-blueprint.pdf . Domain weights used:
  1 Basic and Applied Sciences and Nutritional Concepts 15%, 2 Client Relations and Behavioral
  Coaching 15%, 3 Assessment 16%, 4 Program Design 20%, 5 Exercise Technique and Training Instruction
  24%, 6 Professional Development and Responsibility 10%. The PDF itself would not load in our fetch
  tool; the first four weights were read from NASM's own search snippet and the order and last two
  from two independent 2026 prep guides. Verify if the PDF is reachable.
- NASM exam information page (eligibility: high school diploma or GED, current CPR and AED):
  https://www.nasm.org/certified-personal-trainer/exam-information
- NASM recertification (every two years, 2.0 CEUs including 0.1 for CPR/AED):
  https://www.nasm.org/certification/ce-requirements
- Content written from the NASM Essentials of Personal Fitness Training, 7th edition topics (OPT
  model, overhead squat compensations, Corrective Exercise Continuum, acute variables) as widely
  taught; no textbook or NASM practice-exam text is reproduced. Blood pressure categories: 2017
  ACC/AHA guideline. Activity minimums: Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 2nd edition.
  Maximum heart rate: Tanaka formula, 208 - 0.7 x age.

## Not verified / deliberately avoided
- Cardiorespiratory zone and stage definitions (sources disagree between a three- and four-zone
  reading of the 7th edition), so items only use VT1 and the talk test.
- Exact SMR hold time (30 s in older editions, some 7th-edition summaries say 30 to 60 s); the item
  asks "about 30 seconds" against clearly wrong options.
- Fees (house style).
