# Sources for `nzroad` (New Zealand learner licence theory test)

All 200 questions are original. NZTA publishes sample Road Code questions on its website; those
were not opened or copied. Topics and facts come from the rule text of the Official New Zealand
Road Code (NZTA, online edition, checked 2026-10-30) and the drive.govt.nz licence pages.
Signs and markings are described in words (no images).

## Test format (checked 2026-10-30)
- drive.govt.nz, "Doing the learner licence test": "The test has 35 multi-choice questions."
  "You need to get at least 32 questions right out of 35 to pass." "You're given 30 minutes."
  https://drive.govt.nz/learner-licence/doing-the-learner-licence-test
- drive.govt.nz, "Preparing for and booking the test": at least 16; eyesight check; proof of
  identity; computer-based multi-choice test on the Road Code; offered in Arabic, Chinese,
  Filipino, Gujarati, Hindi, Korean, Maori, Punjabi, Samoan, Thai and Tongan; fail twice on the
  same day = wait 10 working days. https://drive.govt.nz/learner-licence/preparing-for-and-booking-the-test
- A search snippet of drive.govt.nz says each question has two or four options; this bank uses
  four throughout.
- NZTA publishes no per-topic counts. The blueprint (5/6/7/6/5/3/3 per 35) follows the Road Code's
  chapters with give way rules weighted heaviest.

## Licence conditions
- drive.govt.nz learner page: supervisor holds a full licence for the class for 2+ years, front
  passenger seat; L plates front and back; passengers only if the supervisor agrees.
- drive.govt.nz restricted page: alone 5 am to 10 pm; supervisor 10 pm to 5 am; listed passenger
  exemptions (spouse or partner, dependants, parent/guardian...); alcohol under 20 zero, 20+
  250 mcg/L breath and 50 mg/100 mL blood.
- Demerits: 100 points within 2 years = suspension (3 months).

## 2025-2026 changes checked
- Graduated licensing changes start 25 January 2027 (NZTA "proposed changes to graduated driver
  licensing", 1News 3 Feb 2026, MTA): car learner stage 12 months for under 25s (6 with approved
  course or logged hours), restricted 12 months for under 25s, full licence practical test removed,
  hazard perception moves into the restricted test. The zero alcohol limit for all learner and
  restricted drivers needs separate legislation and is not in force. The bank avoids stage
  durations and asks only rules in force on 2026-10-30.
- From 1 October 2026 (news reports, 1News 12 Sep 2026, Driven): minimum 1.5 m passing gap for
  cyclists, horse riders, pedestrians and scooter users; give way to a bus pulling out from a stop
  on roads of 60 km/h or less. Reviewer (2026-10-30) confirmed via NZTA (nzta.govt.nz "New lane
  use rules", read through search; the page itself blocks automated fetches), 1News 12 Sep 2026 and
  newswire 1 Oct 2026: 1.5 m minimum when passing non-motorised road users travelling the same way
  in the same lane; give way to a bus leaving a bus stop, the roadside or a bus lane after it has
  signalled for at least 3 seconds, on roads of 60 km/h or less. The rule text itself was not read.
  Stems no longer carry the date (it is in the explanations).
- Roadside oral fluid drug testing began 15 December 2025 (NZ Police release).

## Numbers used (and where)
- 50 km/h urban, 100 km/h open road, 90 km/h towing (NZTA towing page).
- Two-second rule, four seconds in wet/icy (Road Code following distance page).
- Signal at least 3 seconds (Road Code).
- Headlights 30 min after sunset to 30 min before sunrise, or visibility under 100 m (Road Code
  night driving). Dip when meeting or following; the Road Code gives no distance, so none is asked.
- 20 km/h past a stopped school bus, either direction (Road Code via ODT).
- Parking: 6 m from an intersection, 6 m from the approach side of a pedestrian crossing, 1 m from
  a driveway, 50 cm from a fire hydrant (Road Code "where not to park").
- Crashes: injury, police within 24 h; damage-only and owner not found, police within 60 h
  (NZ Police FAQ).
- Tyre tread 1.5 mm; child restraint under 7 (7-year-olds if available); driver responsible for
  passengers under 15 (NZTA child restraints page).
- Hand-held phone banned while driving including in queues and at lights (NZTA).

## Deliberately avoided
- Flush median distance (Road Code says "a short distance"; 50 m is not in the rule).
- Headlight dipping distance, restricted (R) plate rules, fees, stage durations, the adult
  alcohol limit for learners after the planned change.
