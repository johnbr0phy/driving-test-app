# Sources for `police` (police officer entrance written exam, US, generic)

All 200 questions are original. No commercial or vendor question bank (NPOST/Stanard, FrontLine
National/NTN, PELLETB, LEAB, civil service study guides) was copied, and no vendor is named in any
item. Every reading passage describes a fictional department's procedure written for this bank;
passages test reading, not any agency's real policy. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Format (what the bank models)
- There is no single national police exam. Agencies use a vendor test (for example NPOST, FrontLine
  National, the California POST PELLETB, or a city civil service exam) or their own. The common core
  across them is reading comprehension, writing (grammar, spelling, vocabulary, clarity), incident
  report writing, basic math, and judgment or reasoning (situational judgment, deductive and
  inductive reasoning, spatial orientation). Pass marks are set by each agency or civil service
  board; 70% is a common cut-off and is used here as a marker, not an official pass mark.
- California POST, "Applicant Preparation Guide for the POST Entry-Level Law Enforcement Test
  Battery (PELLETB)": https://post.ca.gov/portals/0/post_docs/publications/Applicant_Preparation_Guide_for-the-POST_Entry-Level-Law-Enforcement-Test-Battery.pdf
  Writing (spelling, vocabulary, clarity) and reading sections. Per the search results checked
  2026-10-30, administrations on or after 1 January 2025 use a version without the CLOZE sub-test;
  the exact item counts differ between secondary sources and were not confirmed from POST itself.
  Reviewer 2026-10-30: the CLOZE removal itself was also not found on post.ca.gov (only a third-party
  prep site states it), so it appears only as "reported" in the config comment and nowhere in items or the landing.

## Weighting (per 50-question test)
Reading 10, grammar/spelling/vocabulary 10, report writing and clarity 8, math 8, situational
judgment and ethics 8, reasoning 6. Reading and writing together are 56% because they dominate
every vendor format; no vendor's own weights are copied.

## Deliberately excluded
- Memory and observation sections (study a picture, answer from memory): need images and a timed
  study screen the v1 engine does not have.
- Any state's criminal law, arrest powers or use-of-force statute; items are universal judgment.
- Physical ability tests, oral boards, psychological and polygraph steps (described on the landing
  only as later steps).

## Facts used in items
- Naloxone: second dose after 2 to 3 minutes without response (product labeling, FDA-approved
  nasal naloxone).
- 88 ft/s = 60 mph (1 mph = 1.4667 ft/s).
- Recording police in public: generally protected in the US (federal circuit decisions); the item
  says "generally".
