# RBT bank sources

- BACB, RBT Test Content Outline (3rd ed.), effective for examinations beginning January 1, 2026 (document
  "Updated 09/2024"). Checked 2026-10-30 at
  https://www.bacb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/RBT-3rd-Edition-Test-Content-Outline-240903-a.pdf .
  75 scored questions plus 10 unscored pilot questions; 43 tasks in 6 domains with scored counts:
  A Data Collection and Graphing 13, B Behavior Assessment 8, C Behavior Acquisition 19,
  D Behavior Reduction 14, E Documentation and Reporting 10, F Ethics 11.
- BACB, RBT Ethics Code (2.0) (core principles; competence; supervision; confidentiality; public
  statements and testimonials; multiple relationships; gifts worth no more than 10 US dollars, stricter employer policy first; confirmed in secondary copies of the code, BACB PDF not re-read).
- BACB RBT Handbook (Updated 10/2026), read directly by the reviewer on 2026-10-30: ongoing supervision of
  at least 5% of behavior-analytic service hours each calendar month, at least two face-to-face real-time
  contacts per month, one observing service delivery, one individual; supervisors are RBT Supervisors and/or
  RBT Requirements Coordinators. Exam: 85 questions (75 scored, 10 unscored), 4 options each, 90 minutes,
  pass/fail with the passing score set by the modified Angoff method.
- 2026 program changes (application fee, 2-year recertification, 12 PD hours) are deliberately not
  asked in the bank because they changed recently.
- Technical definitions follow standard behavior-analytic usage (measurement, reinforcement
  schedules, prompting, chaining, differential reinforcement, extinction effects, punishment).

Exam format (BACB, Pearson VUE): 85 multiple-choice questions (75 scored, 10 pilot) in 90 minutes.
The BACB reports pass/fail against a modified-Angoff passing score and publishes no percentage; this site uses 80% as
a readiness marker, not an official pass mark.

All 200 items are original, written for Tigertest from the outline above. No item is copied from
any published question bank.
