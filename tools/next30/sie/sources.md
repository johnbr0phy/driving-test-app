# Sources for `sie` (FINRA Securities Industry Essentials)

All 200 questions are original, written from FINRA's published content outline and the public
rules and statutes it covers. No commercial question bank, prep course or FINRA sample item was
copied. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Exam format
- FINRA, "Securities Industry Essentials Exam (SIE)":
  https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/qualification-exams/securities-industry-essentials-exam
  (checked 2026-10-30): 75 questions, 1 hour 45 minutes, passing score 70, age 18+, no firm
  association required, results valid four years. Outline weights: Knowledge of Capital Markets
  16% (12), Understanding Products and Their Risks 44% (33), Understanding Trading, Customer
  Accounts and Prohibited Activities 31% (23), Overview of the Regulatory Framework 9% (7).
  The outline PDF on that page sits in a 2025-10 folder (current revision, October 2025).
- Unscored pretest items: prep providers (e.g. ExamFX) report FINRA cut them from 10 to 5
  effective 2025-10-27 (80 items in total, same 1h45). Not confirmed on a FINRA page, so the
  landing and facts say only "75 scored plus a few unscored pretest questions".
- Fee: FINRA lists $100 (deliberately not used in copy).

## Rules and facts used (current as of 2026-10-30)
- T+1 regular-way settlement since 2024-05-28 (SEC Rule 15c6-1 amendments); ex-date normally
  equals record date under T+1.
- Regulation T initial margin 50%; FINRA Rule 4210 maintenance 25%.
- Regulation SHO locate requirement; Rule 10b-10 confirmations; FINRA Rule 2231 quarterly statements.
- FINRA Rules 2010/2020, 2090, 2111, 2150 (guarantees, sharing), 2165 (financial exploitation holds),
  2210 (communications: retail = more than 25 retail investors in 30 days), 3210, 3240, 3270, 3280,
  4512 (trusted contact), 5% markup policy (guideline).
- Regulation Best Interest (June 2020).
- Securities Act of 1933 (20-day cooling-off, commercial paper 270 days, Rules 144/147, Reg A, Reg D),
  Securities Exchange Act of 1934, Investment Company Act of 1940, ITSFEA 1988 (treble civil penalty),
  ERISA 1974, Bank Secrecy Act / USA PATRIOT Act (CTR over $10,000, CIP elements, SAR confidentiality), OFAC SDN list.
- Form U4/U5 30-day deadlines; Regulatory Element CE annual since 2023 (FINRA Rule 1240 as amended).
- REIT 90% distribution; letter of intent 13 months (90-day backdating); 403(b) eligibility; 60-day rollover.

## Deliberately avoided
- Dollar figures that change (FINRA gift limit, contribution limits, simplified arbitration threshold),
  RMD ages, Reg A Tier 2 dollar cap (the item asks only about the 12-month cap structure).
