# Sources for `ukseru` (London private hire SERU assessment)

All 200 questions are original practice questions based on the published SERU guide, the PHV
Driver's Handbook. No commercial question bank, TfL knowledge check or past paper was copied.
Tigertest has no connection with TfL. Checked 2026-10-30.

## Study material (the only content source)
- Transport for London, "Private Hire Vehicle Driver's Handbook: Safety, equality and regulatory
  information for London private hire vehicle drivers", version 1.0, 30 September 2021
  ("Summary of amendments: none"). TfL links it from
  https://tfl.gov.uk/info-for/taxis-and-private-hire/safety-equality-and-regulatory-understanding-requirement
  (tfl.gov.uk blocked automated fetching on 2026-10-30; the text was read from a copy of the same
  v1.0 PDF). No newer version was found as of 2026-10-30; a third-party prep site also called
  v1.0 the current version in January 2026. The handbook says TfL will name the version to read
  for a given test if it is amended: re-check before each content update.
- Sections (= categories here): 1 London PHV Driver Licensing; 2 Licensing Requirements for PHVs;
  3 Carrying out Private Hire Journeys; 4 Staying Safe; 5 Driver Behaviour; 6 Driving and Parking
  in London; 7 Safer Driving; 8 Being Aware of Equality and Disability; 9 Safeguarding Children and
  Adults at Risk; 10 Ridesharing. The glossary is not tested (the handbook says so), and nor are
  linked websites.

## Assessment format (not fully published by TfL; landing and config hedge it)
- TfL / handbook: computer-based Safety, Equality and Regulatory Understanding assessment at a TfL
  assessment centre, based on the handbook; passing it also provides the English reading and
  writing evidence (speaking and listening are a separate B1 test). TfL's page describes
  multiple-choice and sentence-completion questions.
- Secondary sources only (Uber UK and Addison Lee driver blogs; serupractice.com 2026 guide):
  37 questions (18 multiple choice, 17 sentence completion, 2 reading and understanding) in
  45 minutes, or 36 questions (22 MCQ, 12 drop-down, 2 reading) in 60 minutes; pass mark 60%.
  These disagree on count and time, so the mock tests use 36 questions and 60% (22 of 36) and the
  landing says "about 36 to 37 questions in 45 to 60 minutes". Attempt limits and fees also vary
  by source and are left out (house style avoids money; TfL's fee table listed the PHV SERU first
  attempt at GBP 40 from 9 March 2026).

## Weighting
- TfL publishes no per-section counts. Per 36-question test: licensing 6, vehicles 2, journeys 5,
  staying safe 4, behaviour 3, driving and parking 5, safer driving 4, equality 4, safeguarding 2,
  ridesharing 1, roughly in proportion to each section's length in the handbook.

## Facts deliberately avoided (may have changed since the 2021 handbook)
- Congestion Charge and ULEZ exemptions for wheelchair accessible PHVs (TfL changes these
  charges and discounts often), charge amounts and hours. Items keep to "pay the daily charge unless
  exempt" and the Euro 4 petrol / Euro 6 diesel ULEZ standards, which still apply.
- Helpline phone numbers, fees, attempt limits.
