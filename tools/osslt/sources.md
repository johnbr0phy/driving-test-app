# OSSLT sources

All questions and passages are original, written for this bank. No EQAO
released test, practice test or sample item was copied or paraphrased.
Passages are fiction or general-knowledge texts set in Canada; the people,
schools, organizations and news events in them are invented (real places:
Sudbury, Thunder Bay, London, Kingston, Hamilton, Brantford, Winnipeg,
Algonquin Park, Huntsville).

## Official sources (checked 2026-10-29)

- EQAO, *Ontario Secondary School Literacy Test Framework* (Framework_369Xe_1019,
  copyright 2025): https://www.eqao.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/framework-osslt.pdf
  (linked from https://www.eqao.com/framework-osslt/, "revised 09.10.26").
  Used for: the three reading skills (explicit, implicit, connections); the
  three writing skills (main idea with supporting details, organization,
  conventions); online, two sessions, Session A about 65 min and Session B
  about 75 min; 37 operational questions = 35 selected-response + 2
  open-response; reading = information paragraph (6 SR), news report (5 SR +
  1 open response), dialogue (5 SR), real-life narrative (7 SR), 23 SR in
  all; writing = 12 SR + opinion essay; field-test items not scored; raw
  points 55 SR / 23 OR; scored by item response theory; outcome successful
  or not yet successful.
- EQAO, OSSLT assessment page: https://eqao.com/the-assessments/osslt/
  Used for: written for the first time in the second year of secondary
  school (Grade 10) by students working toward the OSSD; scale score 200 to
  400, minimum 300 for a successful outcome, not points out of 400; OSSLC
  eligibility after two opportunities with at least one unsuccessful, earlier
  at the principal's discretion, mature students may enrol directly; EQAO
  online practice test.

## Choices that are ours, not EQAO's

- Writing split 4 main idea / 3 organization / 5 conventions per test: EQAO
  gives 12 writing SR items but no split by skill.
- Timing 45 min reading + 20 min writing for the 35 SR items: EQAO times
  sessions including the open responses, which are not modelled.
- Readiness line 75%: our estimate. The real cut is 300 on a 200-400 IRT
  scale that includes the two open responses, so no percent maps to it.
- Formats: single, choose two (multi), order (drag and drop on the real
  test), cloze (drop-down menu) and highlight (select text), standing in for
  the selected-response formats the framework lists (single-selection,
  multiple-selection, drag and drop, drop-down menu, checklist).

## Facts inside passages (general knowledge, checked 2026-10-29)

- Maple sap: flows with freeze-thaw cycles; about 2% sugar; about 40 L of sap
  per litre of syrup; budding ends the season (Ontario/Quebec producer and
  extension material, widely published).
- Wetlands: flood control, filtering, carbon storage; most pre-settlement
  wetlands in southern Ontario lost (Ontario government and Ducks Unlimited
  Canada estimates of about 72%).
- Leaf colour: chlorophyll, carotenoids present all season, anthocyanins made
  in fall, brightest with sunny days and cool nights above freezing.
- Teen sleep: melatonin onset shifts about 1 to 2 hours later in adolescence;
  Canadian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines for Children and Youth recommend 8 to
  10 hours for ages 14 to 17 (https://csepguidelines.ca/).
- Lake-effect snow: warm open lakes, cold northwest air, snowbelts east of
  Lake Huron and Georgian Bay, less common once ice forms.
- Three Sisters: corn, beans and squash grown together by Haudenosaunee
  farmers; beans climb the corn and add nitrogen; squash shades the soil.
- Ontario graduation: 40 community involvement hours for the OSSD. Whether
  help for family members counts is set by each board's list, so the
  dialogue has the counsellor say "Our board doesn't count favours for
  family" rather than state a provincial rule.

## Basics skills used (shared library, no new skills)

reading.json: mainIdea, findingDetail, inference, wordsInContext,
authorPurpose, factOpinion, toneWords, causeEffect, sequenceEvents.
writing.json: topicSentences, addingSentence, deletingSentence, keepOnGoal.
skills.json: transitions, commaSplices, listIntroCommas, apostrophes, itsIts,
subjectVerb, pronounCase, verbTense, commonlyConfused.
No item tests the serial comma (optional in Canadian usage) or -our/-or
spelling (both are accepted on the real test).
