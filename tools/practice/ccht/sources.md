# Certified Clinical Hemodialysis Technician

All 200 questions are original. The NNCC specifications define the examination scope. Clinical, regulatory, and equipment claims are traced to the primary guidance in the item ledgers below. Sources fetched 2026-11-28.

- [NNCC test specifications](https://www.nncc-exam.org/sites/default/files/cchtSpecifications_2021_08272021.pdf), activity list and domain and cognitive distributions.
- [NNCC CCHT credential](https://www.nncc-exam.org/certification/ccht), examination format, timing and standard score.
- [CMS State Operations Manual, Appendix H](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/guidance/manuals/downloads/som107ap_h_esrd.pdf), survey guidance for end-stage renal disease facilities, revised August 17, 2026.
- [NNCC examination development](https://www.nncc-exam.org/sites/default/files/development/cchtExamDevelopment.pdf), unscored pilot items and criterion-referenced scoring.

Each 50-question practice test covers 25 clinical items (12 preparation and access, 13 treatment monitoring), 11 technical, 8 environment and 6 role responsibilities. Practice sets use a raw target of 37/50. NNCC reports the official result as a standard score.
