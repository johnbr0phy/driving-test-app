# FAA Airframe knowledge test sources

All 200 questions are original. The bank follows the civilian Aviation Maintenance Technician Airframe knowledge test (AMA). General and Powerplant are separate written examinations.

## Outline and real test

- FAA, Aviation Mechanic General, Airframe, and Powerplant Airman Certification Standards, FAA-S-ACS-1, Area II, fetched 2026-11-27. The 15 subject areas and knowledge elements define the scope. https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/Aviation_Mechanic_Certification_Standards.pdf
- FAA, companion guide to the mechanic ACS, September 2024, pages 13 and 14, fetched 2026-11-27. The published knowledge-area ranges allow 5 to 15 percent for metallic structures and inspection and 5 to 10 percent for each of the other 13 areas. The practice forms choose whole-question counts inside those ranges. https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/amt_acs_companion_guide.pdf
- FAA Airman Knowledge Testing Matrix, revised 25 September 2026, page 6, fetched 2026-11-27. The AMA row lists 100 questions, two hours and a passing score of 70%. https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/testing_matrix

The app uses 50-question practice forms with four options per item. It covers the written knowledge domains. The oral and practical examination and the official testing supplement figures are outside this bank. Aircraft-specific maintenance limits and service procedures depend on the applicable approved data.
