# Texas Life Agent sources

The Texas Department of Insurance licenses life agents. Pearson VUE publication 124401, effective September 1, 2026, sets the tested topics for InsTX-Life01. Printed pages S1 to S3 describe life policies, provisions, underwriting, retirement and tax concepts, and two Texas law domains. Exam logistics come from the current candidate handbook. The following three official sources were checked 2026-11-27 Eastern time.

- [Texas Life Agent content outline](https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/124401.pdf)
- [Texas insurance candidate handbook](https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/124400.pdf)
- [Texas Department of Insurance life agent licensing](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/agent/life-agent-apply.html)

All items are original. Each question ID below maps to its official source and locator. Amounts and scenarios illustrate the rule in the cited text. The UTC date was November 28 when the following references were checked.
