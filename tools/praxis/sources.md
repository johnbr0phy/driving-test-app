# Praxis Core (`praxis`) sources

All 200 items and all 16 stimuli (reading passages, data tables and revision drafts) are original,
written for Tigertest to the ETS content topics. No ETS sample question was copied or paraphrased.

## Official (checked October 30, 2026)
- ETS, *The Praxis Study Companion: Core Academic Skills for Educators: Writing (5723)*,
  https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/pdfs/5723.pdf
  - 100 minutes: a 40-minute selected-response section (40 questions) and two 30-minute essays.
  - Selected response: usage, sentence correction, revision in context, research skills.
  - I. Text Types, Purposes, and Production: 6 to 12 selected response + 2 essays, 60%.
  - II. Language and Research Skills for Writing: 28 to 34 selected response, 40%.
  - Usage directions: four underlined portions plus "No error"; no sentence has more than one error.
- ETS, *The Praxis Study Companion: Core Academic Skills for Educators: Mathematics (5733)*,
  https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/pdfs/5733.pdf
  - 90 minutes, 56 questions; select one of five, select one or more, numeric entry (integer, decimal or fraction).
  - On-screen four-function calculator.
  - I. Number and Quantity 20 (36%); II. Data Interpretation and Representation, Statistics, and
    Probability 18 (32%); III. Algebra and Geometry 18 (32%). Content topics I.A to I.I used for praxisNumber.
- ETS, *The Praxis Study Companion: Core Academic Skills for Educators: Reading (5713)*,
  https://praxis.ets.org/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-ets-praxisLibrary/default/pdfs/5713.pdf
  (the PDF is encrypted and its text could not be extracted here; figures below are from ETS search
  snippets and prep providers, consistent with each other)
  - 85 minutes, 56 selected-response questions.
  - Key Ideas and Details 17 to 22; Craft, Structure, and Language Skills 14 to 19; Integration of
    Knowledge and Ideas 17 to 22.
  - Passage types: paired passages, long passages, short passages, brief statements, visual or quantitative information.
- ETS, Score Requirements, https://praxis.ets.org/scores : passing scores are set by each state or agency.

## Secondary (used only for the "many states" passing-score line on the landing and in WIRING facts)
- Prep-provider state tables (Magoosh, Achievable) agree that the most common cutoffs are Reading 156,
  Writing 162 and Math 150, with exceptions (for example Math in Mississippi and Pennsylvania).

## Facts in passages
- Svalbard Global Seed Vault: about minus 18 C; first withdrawal 2015 by researchers from the Syrian
  gene bank (ICARDA), replanted in Lebanon and Morocco.
- Standard time: railroads adopted four zones in November 1883; Congress made them law in 1918.
- Year-round daylight saving time in the US: enacted January 1974, ended in October 1974.
- Lyceum movement: Josiah Holbrook's 1826 proposal; thousands of local lyceums by the mid-1830s.
- Octopus vision: a single visual pigment in most species; light-sensitive opsins reported in octopus skin.
- Urban honeybees and wild bees: a Paris study (Ropars et al., 2019) found wild pollinator visits fell where
  hive density rose; the passage names no city.
- Light pollution: about a third of humanity cannot see the Milky Way (Falchi et al., 2016).

## Format modelled
Half-length sections at the real pace: Reading 28 in 43 min, Writing selected response 20 in 20 min,
Math 28 in 45 min. Essays not modelled. Five options throughout, as on the real tests.
