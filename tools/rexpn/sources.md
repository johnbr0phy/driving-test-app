# REx-PN sources (all checked 2026-10-30)

All 210 questions are original. No item, stem or rationale is copied from NCSBN sample items, the test plan's sample items, CNO or BCCNM material, or any commercial question bank. The sources below set coverage, weights, format and exam facts only.

## Blueprint and format
- NCSBN, *2022 REx-PN Test Plan* (cover: "Effective January 2022"; file revised 2025):
  https://www.ncsbn.org/public-files/2025_rexpn_test-plan_final.pdf
  (listing page: https://www.ncsbn.org/publications/2022-rexpn-test-plan, marked "Effective 2022 (Revised)")
  - Client Needs ranges: Management of Care 18-24%, Safety and Infection Control 10-16%, Health Promotion and Maintenance 6-12%, Psychosocial Integrity 8-14%, Basic Care and Comfort 6-12%, Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies 14-20%, Reduction of Risk Potential 8-14%, Physiological Adaptation 6-12%. Target distribution chart: 21/13/9/11/9/17/11/9%. Our 90-item tests use 19/12/8/10/8/15/10/8.
  - Exam length 90 to 150 items, 30 unscored pretest items, 4-hour limit including tutorial and breaks, CAT with 95% confidence rule, maximum-length and run-out-of-time rules, no going back.
  - Item formats: multiple choice, multiple response, fill-in-the-blank calculation, exhibit, graphics. No NGN case studies, so the bank uses single, multi (SATA), numeric and exhibit-style single items with a table in `passage`.
  - Scoring: "Effective January 2026, all items will be scored dichotomously ... except for multiple response, select all that apply items. These items will be scored polytomously for partial credit." The app scores all or nothing; the scoreNote says so.
  - Terminology used in items: client, unregulated care provider (UCP, e.g. personal support worker), delegation, assignment, health care provider.
  - Related content lists per category (e.g. Indigenous health knowledge and practices, continuous subcutaneous infusions, central venous access devices, TPN, lab values list: ABGs, BUN, creatinine, glucose, A1C, Hct, Hgb, INR, platelets, potassium, PT, PTT/aPTT, sodium, WBC).
- NCSBN, *REx-PN Academic Webinar Series Part 1: Practice Analysis and Test Plan* (2025 slides), same distribution and the January 2026 partial-credit note:
  https://ncsbn.org/public-files/webinars/2025_REx-PN_Webinar_Test-Plan.pdf

## Exam facts
- College of Nurses of Ontario, *About the REx-PN* FAQ: 90 to 150 questions, 60 to 120 scored plus 30 pretest, four-hour limit, CAT, cannot go back, English and French, RPNs in Ontario and LPNs in BC, 60-day wait between attempts, no limit on attempts:
  https://www.cno.org/en/become-a-nurse/entry-to-practice-examinations/rpn-exam/faq-rexpn/
- BC College of Nurses and Midwives, *Who needs to take the REx-PN*: fee 350 CAD plus GST per attempt, paid to Pearson VUE, non-refundable; no attempt limit with a valid application; 60-day wait:
  https://www.bccnm.ca/LPN/applications_registration/exam

## Canadian clinical content (facts in items, checked against current Canadian guidance)
- Health Canada: vitamin D 400 IU daily for breastfed infants; folic acid 0.4 mg multivitamin before conception.
- Canadian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines (CSEP): 150 min/week moderate to vigorous activity for adults.
- Diabetes Canada Clinical Practice Guidelines: hypoglycemia below 4.0 mmol/L, treat with 15 g fast-acting carbohydrate and recheck in 15 minutes; A1C target 7.0% or less for most adults.
- Canadian Task Force on Preventive Health Care: colorectal screening 50 to 74 with FIT every 2 years (provincial programs vary, 1 to 2 years).
- PHAC / PIDAC routine practices and additional precautions: influenza droplet and contact; TB airborne with N95; MRSA contact; C. difficile soap and water; disseminated zoster airborne and contact; PPE removal starting with gloves.
- Heart and Stroke Foundation: one nitroglycerin dose, call 911 if not better in 5 minutes.
- Criminal Code (MAID): provided by physicians and nurse practitioners; other nurses may give information and pass on requests.
- Provincial law is stated as varying (substitute decision-maker ranking, immunization schedules, car seat stages, elder abuse reporting in long-term care); child protection reporting is a duty in every province.
- SI reference ranges used: K 3.5-5.0 mmol/L, Na 135-145 mmol/L, Hgb 120-160 g/L (women) and 140-180 g/L (men), WBC 4-11 × 10⁹/L, platelets 150-400 × 10⁹/L, pH 7.35-7.45, PaCO2 35-45 mm Hg.
