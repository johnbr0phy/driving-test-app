# RRB NTPC CBT 1 (`rrbntpc`) sources

## Pattern followed

**Railway Recruitment Boards, Centralised Employment Notices CEN 05/2024 (NTPC graduate posts) and CEN 06/2024
(NTPC undergraduate posts)**, section on the scheme of examination for the First Stage CBT. This is the pattern used
for the CBT 1 sittings of 2025 (graduate level from 5 June 2025) and kept in the next cycle's notices, CEN 06/2025 (graduate, CBT 1 held March 2026) and CEN 07/2025
(undergraduate, CBT 1 May to June 2026), per secondary reporting checked 2026-10-30 by the reviewer (the 2025
notice PDFs themselves were not read).
Official notices are published on each RRB website (for example https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in, https://rrbapply.gov.in).
Cross-checked 2026-10-30 against secondary summaries (IIT Kanpur SATHEE RRB NTPC exam-pattern page,
careers360 news of the 2025 CBT 1 schedule), which agree on every figure below.

- First Stage CBT, common to graduate and undergraduate levels: 100 questions, 90 minutes (120 minutes for eligible
  PwBD candidates using a scribe).
- General Awareness 40, Mathematics 30, General Intelligence and Reasoning 30; one mark per question.
- Negative marking: 1/3 mark deducted for each wrong answer. Modelled in copy only (landing FAQ, section directions,
  `scoreNote`); the app scores percent correct.
- Minimum qualifying percentage: UR 40, EWS 40, OBC (non-creamy layer) 30, SC 30, ST 25 (PwBD may be relaxed by
  2 percentage points if seats are short). CBT 1 is a screening stage: candidates for CBT 2 are shortlisted on
  normalised CBT 1 marks, and CBT 1 marks are not counted in the final merit.
- Syllabus (CEN annexure): Mathematics: number system, decimals, fractions, LCM and HCF, ratio and proportion,
  percentage, mensuration, time and work, time and distance, simple and compound interest, profit and loss,
  elementary algebra, geometry and trigonometry, elementary statistics. General Intelligence and Reasoning:
  analogies, number and alphabetical series, coding and decoding, mathematical operations, similarities and
  differences, relationships, analytical reasoning, syllogism, jumbling, Venn diagrams, puzzles, data sufficiency,
  statement and conclusion or course of action, decision making, maps and graphs. General Awareness: current
  events, games and sports, art and culture of India, Indian literature, monuments, general science and life
  science (up to class 10 CBSE), history of India and the freedom struggle, physical, social and economic geography
  of India and the world, Indian polity and governance, space and nuclear programmes, UN and international
  organisations, environment, computers, abbreviations, transport, Indian economy, famous personalities,
  government programmes, flora and fauna, important government bodies.

## What the bank covers and leaves out

- Two full 100-question tests (40/30/30, grouped by subject: GA, then Maths, then Reasoning; the order of sections on the real paper is not modelled as fixed) plus 32 drill-only items,
  232 in all, four-option single answer like the real CBT.
- GA is static facts only (history, culture, sports trophies, geography, Constitution and institutions, banking
  and tax basics, class 10 science, computer basics). No current affairs, no office-holders, no award winners of a
  given year, no government schemes that may be renamed.
- Reasoning is text only: no Venn diagram figures, mirror images, paper folding or graphs.
- Indian English, rupee amounts with Indian digit grouping (3,60,000), Indian names.
- Every item is original. The bank was checked against the parallel SSC CGL bank (`data/v2/ssccgl`) and items
  testing the same fact or the same numbers were rewritten so the two banks do not overlap.

## Facts checked

Constitutional articles and amendments (Articles 18, 21, 53, 58, 74, 108, 109, 112, 159, 280, 324; 42nd, 44th,
61st, 73rd amendments) against the Constitution of India text (Legislative Department, legislative.gov.in).
Banking dates (RBI 1935, bank nationalisation 1969 and 1980, SBI 1955, NABARD 1982). Geography facts are standard
NCERT class 9 to 11 content (Contemporary India, India: Physical Environment). Science to NCERT class 10.
Calendar items were computed: 1 January 2026 is a Thursday and 15 August 2026 a Saturday.
