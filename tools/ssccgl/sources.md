# SSC CGL Tier 1 sources

- Staff Selection Commission, Combined Graduate Level Examination notice (2025 cycle notice, June 2025; the 2026 cycle,
  Tier 1 scheduled 30 September to 30 October 2026, keeps the same pattern): Tier 1 is one computer-based paper,
  100 objective questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes (80 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates), four parts of 25
  questions (General Intelligence and Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension),
  0.50 marks deducted per wrong answer. Syllabus annexure of the same notice for topic coverage. https://ssc.gov.in
- One secondary source found by web search gave the Tier 1 duration as 2 hours; the notices and every other source
  say 60 minutes. Verify against the current notice on ssc.gov.in if in doubt.
- General Awareness facts checked against the Constitution of India (Articles 17, 21A, 32, 51A, 64, 80, 83, 84,
  108, 118, 155; 42nd, 73rd, 86th and 101st Amendments) and NCERT history, geography and science texts.
  Static facts only: no current affairs and no current office-holders.

Every question is original. No item is copied from SSC previous-year papers or from another Tigertest bank.
Rupee amounts and Indian names in word problems; Indian English spelling.
