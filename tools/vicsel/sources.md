coaching items were used or adapted; the ACER sample-question ZIP was not
downloaded.

## Not published (modelling choices)
- Number of questions per section and number of options per question. Tigertest uses
  40 four-option items per section (20 per strand) to fill the real 60 and 55 minutes.
- Weighting of sections. Tigertest averages the two section percentages.
- The reasoning tests may include shape and figure items; Tigertest's are text only.
- Earlier years (before ACER) used a different provider and timing (five 30-minute
  parts per some coaching sites); ignored in favour of the current ACER format.

## Facts in reading texts
- Great Ocean Road (R2-B): about 240 km, Torquay to Allansford; construction began
  1919; about 3,000 returned servicemen; full route officially opened 1932; toll for
  some years; widely called the world's largest war memorial.
- Superb lyrebird (R1-B): wet forests of south-eastern Australia including east of
  Melbourne; winter breeding; males display on cleared mounds with the tail thrown
  forward; mimicry of many species; machine sounds recorded mostly from birds near
  people.
- Honey bees (R0-A): brood kept near 34 to 35 °C; shivering to warm; fanning, water
  evaporation and clustering outside to cool.
